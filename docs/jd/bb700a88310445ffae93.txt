Role reports to: Sr Manager, Neutrogena HOBA Brand Growth Location: North America, United States, New Jersey, Summit Work Location: Hybrid What you will do Neutrogena is a dermatologist-recommended skincare brand and leader in the Skin Health & Beauty category. This is a rare opportunity to shape the next chapter of one of America’s most iconic brands. The Associate Manager, Health of Business Analytics (HOBA) Brand Growth serves as a strategic business partner to Neutrogena Marketing, Customer Strategy, Finance, and Commercial teams by transforming complex data into actionable business insights. As part of Neutrogena's center of excellence for reporting and analytics, this role is responsible for delivering trusted performance reporting, identifying key business drivers and risks, and enabling fact-based decision making across assigned categories. This individual will own end-to-end analytics for designated Neutrogena businesses, providing weekly performance storytelling, competitive intelligence, and scalable reporting solutions that support POS growth, share improvement, and strategic business planning. Key Responsibilities Deliver business performance reporting and actionable insights for assigned Neutrogena categories. Analyze POS, share, retailer, and competitive trends to identify growth opportunities and risks. Develop scalable reporting tools and dashboards that improve decision-making. Partner cross-functionally to support planning, forecasting, and business reviews. Translate complex data into clear, compelling stories that drive action. Lead executive-ready business reviews and performance narratives to leadership, influencing strategic priorities and action plans What we are looking for Required Qualifications A minimum of a Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, Analytics, Finance, Economics, or related field. A minimum of 3 years of experience in marketing, business analytics, brand management, category management, sales strategy, business intelligence, or related functions. Proficiency in Excel and PowerPoint. Desired Qualifications Experience in Mass Beauty with fluency in KPIs of Mass & omni classes of trade In-depth knowledge of beauty markets, consumer trends and competitive landscape, with the ability to translate insights into actionable strategies. Ability to synthesize large, complex datasets into concise business stories. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Excellent communication and presentation skills. Strong demonstration of all behaviors of our Leadership Imperatives. Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to build strong relationships with internal and external stakeholders.

Senior FP&A Analyst Position Summary The Senior FP&A Analyst is a key finance business partner supporting financial visibility, planning, and performance management across the business. This role helps drive the financial operating rhythm through monthly P&L analysis, weekly revenue pulse forecasting, Risks & Opportunities (R&O) tracking, scenario planning, and support for core planning processes including AOP and LRP. This position partners closely with commercial, operations, and finance leaders to deliver timely analysis, actionable insights, and leadership-ready reporting. The Senior FP&A Analyst also supports month-end close activities, revenue analysis, journal entry preparation, Monthly Operating Review (MOR) and Quarterly Business Review (QBR) materials, bridge analysis, and integration-related activities to help improve forecast accuracy, business decision-making, and overall financial performance. Key Responsibilities Support month-end close activities, including coordination of financial submissions, review of results, and preparation of select journal entries Perform detailed revenue analysis to understand business performance, trends, pacing, and key drivers across product, customer, channel, and region Support monthly financial close through detailed P&L analysis, including variance analysis versus prior year, forecast, and plan Deliver clear and actionable insights on business performance, including trends in revenue, margin, volume, mix, and operating costs Prepare and maintain weekly revenue pulse forecasts to provide visibility to near-term performance and support proactive decision-making Support the identification, quantification, and tracking of Risks & Opportunities (R&O) across the business Partner with cross-functional stakeholders to develop scenario analyses and assess the financial impact of changing business conditions Support core planning processes including Annual Operating Plan (AOP) and Long-Range Plan (LRP) through data consolidation, modeling, analysis, and presentation development Prepare materials and reporting for Monthly Operating Reviews (MOR) and Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR) for finance and business leadership Provide bridge analysis to explain period-over-period changes in revenue, gross margin, SG&A, and other key financial metrics Support integration efforts by assisting with financial reporting alignment, performance tracking, and analysis tied to business transitions or acquisitions Collaborate with commercial, service, operations, and functional teams to gather inputs, validate assumptions, and improve forecast quality Help enhance finance processes, reporting tools, and forecasting methodologies through standardization, automation, and continuous improvement Ensure accuracy, consistency, and timeliness across financial data, reporting packages, and leadership presentations

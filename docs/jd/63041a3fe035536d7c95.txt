About This Role Provide strategic and operational leadership for Carrier's global cloud platforms, enabling secure, scalable, and cost-efficient digital infrastructure services across the enterprise. Accelerate application delivery and business innovation through reusable platform services, infrastructure automation, self-service capabilities, and modern cloud engineering practices. Establish cloud platforms as enterprise products that improve developer experience, strengthen governance, and create a consistent operating model across AWS, Azure, and GCP environments. Key Responsibilities: Lead the global Cloud Platform organization responsible for AWS, Azure, and GCP platform engineering, cloud governance, identity, networking, automation, and self-service infrastructure capabilities. Define and execute the enterprise cloud platform strategy, driving standardization, security, reliability, operational excellence, cost optimization (FinOps), and developer velocity through automation and platform engineering practices. Manage and develop a global team of cloud engineers, architects, and FinOps professionals while partnering closely with Cybersecurity, Cloud Operations, Application Teams, and external service providers to deliver business outcomes. Required Qualifications: High School Diploma/ GED 10+ years of progressive experience in cloud infrastructure, platform engineering, DevOps, or enterprise technology leadership, including experience leading globally distributed teams. Cloud architect certification (AWS Solutions Architect Professional, Azure Architect, and/or GCP Professional Cloud Architect). Preferred Qualifications: Bachelor or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or related field. Deep expertise in public cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, and/or GCP), Infrastructure as Code, cloud security and governance, automation, CI/CD, FinOps, and platform engineering operating models. Experience leading platform transformations, cloud migrations, and enterprise-scale cloud governance programs. Strong executive communication, vendor management, financial management, and stakeholder engagement skills.

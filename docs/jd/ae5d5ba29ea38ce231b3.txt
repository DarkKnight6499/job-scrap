Financial Analyst I
PJM Interconnection
Location: Audubon, PA

Flexible Work Arrangement: Hybrid - Audubon, PA This position is an entry-level financial analyst in the Budget & Analysis department. The job responsibilities include financial analysis and reporting, budgeting and forecasting, maintaining project portfolios and assisting with calculations of PJM’s pricing allocation model. This person will conduct budgeting and forecasting for at least one operating division at PJM. This requires the development of forecasting methodologies and ongoing communication with division management. Financial analysis and reporting will be conducted on activity and anticipated activity for the division, providing detailed insight into variances and cost trends. Additionally, this person will assist with other Budget and Analysis departmental duties including monthly general ledger close, preparing and/or entering journal entries, and coordination of project funding within SAP. Essential Functions Assists in the development of the monthly financial reporting used by PJM Executive Team to monitor company and divisional financial performance. Assists with preparing forecasts and budgets for operational expenditures. Assists in development of financial analysis, reporting and presentation information to PJM management, Finance Committee, Audit Committee and PJM’s Board of Directors on current financial position and company performance on goals and long-range financial plans. Provides project financial data for preparation of budget and project presentation materials for the Executive Team, Finance Committee, Members Committee and PJM’s Board of Managers. Assists with analyzing project funding requests ensuring appropriate levels of Authorization for Capital (AFC) have been completed. Ensure data integrity and accuracy for project portfolio funding requests. Analyzes project postings for accuracy, identifying and investigating unusual postings to ensure proper correcting journal entries are posted when necessary. Maintains and updates all project system and pricing model desk procedures and internal controls documentation. Characteristics & Qualifications: Required Bachelor of science (or working towards) in Accounting, Finance, Business Administration Ability to efficiently use Microsoft Office Suite (MS-Word, MS-Excel and MS-PowerPoint) Ability to produce high-quality work products with attention to detail Ability to communicate effectively and operate in a fast-paced team environment Preferred SAP knowledge, specifically CO and PS modules Knowledge of MS-Excel pivot tables Experience in Hyperion Planning Experience in quantitative and qualitative analysis Equivalent working experience (0-3 years)

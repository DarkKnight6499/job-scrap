The Equity Exotics Trader is responsible for actively managing and risk-managing a proprietary and client-facilitation trading book comprising complex equity exotic derivatives and structured products. The primary objective is to generate sustainable, risk-adjusted trading revenue, maintain rigorous risk controls across complex non-linear Greeks, and partner closely with Structuring, Quantitative Analysis, and Institutional/Wealth Sales to expand Citi’s global equity exotics franchise. Key Responsibilities: 1. Book Management & Trading Execution Risk Management & Hedging: Manage and dynamically hedge higher-order and non-linear risk exposures across structured and exotic equity derivatives books (including Autocallables, Reverse Convertibles, Clicquets, Accumulators/Decumulators, Barrier Options, Volatility/Variance Swaps, Quanto structures, and other Light Exotics. Greeks & Parameter Management: Actively monitor and manage complex market parameters, including: First- and second-order Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Vega, Volga/Vomma, Vanna, Charm, Speed, Color). Volatility surfaces, skew/smile dynamics, forward volatility, and term structure. Correlation risk, cross-gamma, dividend risk, repo/borrow curves, and interest rate sensitivities. Pricing & Market Making: Provide competitive, rapid, and accurate pricing for bespoke structured payoffs and flow exotics to Sales, Structuring, Private Banking, and Institutional clients. Revenue Generation: Deliver on budgeted net trading revenue targets in the context of established value-at-risk (VaR), Stress VaR, Comprehensive Capital Analysis and Review (CCAR), and desk-level sensitivity limits. 2. Quantitative Analytics & Platform Evolution Model Collaboration & Calibration: Partner with Quantitative Research / Financial Engineering (Strats) to refine, backtest, and calibrate pricing engines, Local Volatility (LV), and Stochastic Local Volatility (SLV) models. Analytics & Automation: Develop tactical algorithmic/Python pricing scripts, automated hedging tools, and scenario stress-testing suites to enhance desk efficiency and real-time P&L attribution. Infrastructure Optimization: Collaborate with Technology to improve trade booking workflows, risk aggregation systems, and straight-through processing (STP). 3. Governance, Controls & Risk Management Regulatory & Policy Compliance: Adhere strictly to all internal policies, Volcker Rule requirements, Market Abuse Regulations (MAR), FRTB guidelines, Booking Policy, and mandatory trading protocols. Control Function Engagement: Interface proactively with Market Risk Management, Model Risk Management (MRM), Credit Risk, Product Control, Legal, and Compliance to ensure proper validation, booking, and governance for all exotic payoffs. Culture & Conduct: Foster a culture of responsible finance, robust supervisory controls, operational discipline, and transparency; actively escalate potential control deficiencies, model breaks, or reputational risks. Taking accountability to analyze and own trade outcomes to ensure best practice for franchise and client development. 4. Franchise & Client Development Sales & Structuring Partnership: Work collaboratively with Equity Structuring and Institutional/Private Wealth Sales teams to identify market trends, anticipate client payoff preferences, and deliver innovative structured investment and hedging solutions. Counterparty & Market Interaction: Maintain strong inter-dealer broker and exchange relationships to source correlation, dividend, and bespoke option liquidity efficiently. Market Commentary: Provide actionable market intelligence, skew/volatility insights, and trade ideas to internal stakeholders and key client accounts. Qualifications & Competencies: 1. Experience & Industry Knowledge: Experience: Professional trading experience within Equity Derivatives (4–7 years for VP level consideration, 3-4 years for AVP level consideration) or 8-10 years of experience in another applicable field for VP or AVP level consideration. Experience running an Equity Exotics or Structured Products trading book with strong risk management. Product Expertise: Deep technical mastery of non-linear payoff structures (Autocallables, Cliquets, Barriers, Dispersion, Quartiles, Range Accruals, Volatility/Variance derivatives, Quanto payoffs) across single-stock, basket, and index underlyings. Risk Acumen: In-depth knowledge of exotic risk management techniques, pinning risks, jump/gap risk dynamics, and multi-asset hybrid correlation behavior. 2. Technical & Quantitative Skills: Exceptional mathematical, probabilistic, and quantitative aptitude (stochastic calculus, numerical methods, Monte Carlo simulations, PDE solvers). Advanced programming and data analysis proficiency in Python (and/or C++, R, SQL, VBA). Proficient in market data systems and analytics platforms (Bloomberg OVME/DLIB/VOLS, Reuters, and proprietary equity risk engines). 3. Professional Skills & Licensing: Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to clearly articulate complex quantitative risk profiles to both internal control partners and client-facing teams. High level of composure, commercial acumen, and decision-making agility through a risk management lens in high-volatility, fast-paced trading environments. Licensing: Relevant regulatory registrations and trading licenses as required by jurisdiction (e.g., Series 7, 63, 57 in the US). Education: Bachelor’s Degree: Degree in a quantitative discipline (Financial Engineering, Mathematics, Physics, Computer Science, Econometrics, Quantitative Finance, or Engineering).

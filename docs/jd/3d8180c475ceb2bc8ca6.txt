Description Job Description: The Credit Card Risk Strategy Analyst leads the development, implementation, and ongoing monitoring of quantitative credit strategies for Huntington’s consumer credit card acquisition programs. The role is responsible for account approval strategies, initial credit line assignment, and risk-based pricing strategies. This position also monitors the credit risk profile and performance of newly acquired accounts to ensure alignment with established portfolio loss and risk-adjusted return objectives. Job Responsibilities: Lead the development, implementation, and ongoing oversight of consumer credit card new account origination strategies, including segment-level performance reporting and change management. Design, execute, and evaluate policy experiments and A/B tests, translating analytical findings into actionable credit policy and strategy enhancements. Manage forecasting, monitoring, and reporting frameworks that provide timely visibility into credit risk performance and emerging portfolio trends. Partner cross-functionally with Credit Administration, Underwriting, Fraud, Model Risk Management, Product, and Legal/Compliance to advance business priorities and ensure alignment with risk and regulatory requirements. Demonstrated experience managing the full analytical lifecycle, including project development, execution, presentation, implementation, and post-implementation monitoring. Demonstrated understanding of credit card profit-and-loss (P&L) drivers and financial performance. Analyze data using SQL/SAS to review performance, conduct comprehensive analyses, propose strategy changes, and validate implementations. Basic Qualifications Bachelors degree 7+ years experience in analytics role in financial services. 5+ years experience with SQL/SAS Preferred Qualifications MBA/MS or Ph.D in Statistics, Economics, Finance, or Business Analytics Experience with Tableau, Snowflake, or comparable data visualization tools, and Microsoft Excel.

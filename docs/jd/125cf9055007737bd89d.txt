Summary: We are seeking an Advisory Consultant to join the team within Highland Capital Brokerage, a member of Osaic, Inc. This position is primarily responsible for the sale of annuity products to registered investment advisors, independent broker dealers, registered reps as well as independent agents and agencies. An Advisor Consultant is tasked with recruiting and educating new producers on how to incorporate annuities into their practice. This position will shepherd the producer from the initial sales concept through the point of sale with in-depth product knowledge and extraordinary customer service. In addition to recruiting new producers, there will be a heavy emphasis on advisor attrition and reoccurring producers. Education Requirements: Bachelor’s degree preferred, high school diploma (or equivalent) in combination with significant practical experience will be considered in lieu of degree. Minimum of high school diploma or equivalent is required.

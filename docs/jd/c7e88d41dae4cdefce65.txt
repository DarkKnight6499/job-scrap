Job Summary: Provides timely and accurate assistance with monthly closing and supporting analysis Reviews and processes information to create journal entries in the general ledger Prepares, records, analyzes and reports accounting transactions and reviews for completeness, accuracy and compliance with GAAP, SOX, and company financial policies Review variances to determine their cause and identify corrective action. Participates in operational excellence initiatives to support achievement of organizational goals Supports the annual budget and standard cost process, strategic review process, and monthly/quarterly forecasting process Supports the annual physical inventory process Prepares balance sheet account reconciliations Works with operations to develop and load financial forecasts and budgets Delivers timely, accurate and relevant financial reports to management in order to facilitate management level understanding of financial impacts Completes special projects and ad-hoc requests as needed Qualifications: Bachelor’s Degree in Accounting or Finance 2 - 4 years’ experience in general accounting, finance or cost accounting in a manufacturing environment Knowledge of GAAP (Generally accepted accounting principles) High level of proficiency in MS Office products, Oracle or equivalent ERP/manufacturing application, OneStream, Tableau and Dataiku experience a plus Exhibit a positive attitude and maintain poise under pressure Proven track record of working independently and managing multiple projects or deliverables simultaneously. Problem-solving capability, detail orientation, and sensitivity to tight time frames.

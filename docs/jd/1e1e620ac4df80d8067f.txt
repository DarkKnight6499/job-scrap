Now Hiring: Director / Senior Manager, Enterprise Risk Management We're seeking an experienced Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) leader to play a pivotal role in developing, implementing, and enhancing a company-wide risk management program within a highly regulated financial services environment. In this role, you will partner closely with executive leadership to identify, assess, monitor, and report key enterprise risks while helping shape a strong risk culture across the organization. This position has significant visibility with senior management, executive committees, and Board of Directors. Role Responsibilities: ✅ Lead the development and evolution of the Enterprise Risk Management Framework and Risk Appetite Statement ✅ Design and manage the Enterprise Risk & Control Self-Assessment (RCSA) Program ✅ Develop executive-level risk reporting, dashboards, KRIs, and risk registers ✅ Drive implementation of Integrated Risk Management tools and reporting capabilities ✅ Partner with business leaders to identify, assess, mitigate, and monitor enterprise-wide risks ✅ Lead enterprise risk training programs, workshops, and Risk Champion initiatives ✅ Present risk insights, emerging risks, and recommendations to Executive Risk Committees and senior leadership ✅ Oversee risk governance documentation, policies, frameworks, and procedures ✅ Support regulatory reporting and risk management submissions ✅ Mentor and develop a high-performing risk team while fostering a culture of accountability and continuous improvement ✅Risk and Controls Self-Assessment Procedure, etc.)- Support adoption and application of risk policies and procedures throughout the organization in a manner that practically supports each relevant function People Management Role Requirements Bachelor’s degree in risk management, Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, Economics, or a related field required; advanced degree (MBA, Master's, or equivalent) preferred. 12+ years of progressive experience in enterprise risk management, operational risk, compliance, audit, financial services, or related risk disciplines, with significant leadership responsibility. 5+ years of experience leading enterprise-wide risk management programs within a complex, highly regulated organization. Proven track record partnering with executive leadership teams and presenting risk matters to senior management, executive committees, and Boards of Directors. Proven ability to influence and build consensus across business lines, corporate functions, and the three lines of defense. Demonstrated success leading, developing, and mentoring high-performing teams and fostering a strong risk culture.

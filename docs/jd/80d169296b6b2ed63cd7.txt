Job Description: Join Baker Tilly as a Director in our fast-growing Financial Advisory Services team within the Office of the CFO practice. This is a leadership role within a rapidly scaling team focused on building a market-leading Treasury and finance transformation offering. We partner with CFOs, private equity sponsors, and executive teams to design, build, and operate high-impact finance functions that drive better, faster decision-making. Our team supports a diverse client base—from high-growth, PE-backed portfolio companies to large enterprises—across industries including consumer products, manufacturing and distribution, technology, life sciences, healthcare, media and entertainment, and professional services. We deliver a broad suite of services spanning interim Treasury leadership, function stand-up, carve-out support, cashflow/liquidity optimization, modernize operating models, and implement enabling technology. As a Director, you will operate at the intersection of client delivery, capability building, and business development, helping shape the future of our practice while serving as a trusted advisor to senior finance leaders. This role is focused on ongoing Treasury, finance transformation, and value creation—not transaction execution—often working with clients post-investment.

Job Description: At Regions, the Capital Markets Business Systems Analyst will conduct business process analysis, needs assessments and preliminary cost/benefits analysis in an effort to align information technology solutions with business initiatives. Assignments may be broad in nature and require originality and ingenuity and have appreciable latitude for independent action or decision. The analyst should have the ability to work with minimal supervision with a high attention to detail in a fast paced environment and may be assisted by junior or intermediate level personnel.

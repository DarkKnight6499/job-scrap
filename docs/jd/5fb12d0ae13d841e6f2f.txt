As a Business Analyst, you will provide analytical and strategic support for commercial opportunities involving natural gas infrastructure, market fundamentals, financial analysis, and project development. You will collaborate across business development, commercial, engineering, and finance to evaluate opportunities, identify potential synergies, and support business and management decisions. You will develop and maintain a centralized opportunity-tracking system to increase visibility into commercial opportunities across multiple business groups, improve collaboration, and provide actionable insights to stakeholders. In this role, you will support commercial opportunities throughout their lifecycle, helping organize and analyze information as opportunities move from initial identification and evaluation toward executable energy projects and transactions.

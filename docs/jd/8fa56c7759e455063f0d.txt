Senior Analyst, Risk and Compliance
London Stock Exchange Group
Location: USA-New York-28 Liberty Street
Link: https://lseg.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/USA-New-York-28-Liberty-Street/Senior-Analyst--Risk-and-Compliance_R0123669

The senior analyst will be part of the Risk and Compliance team within the Pricing and Reference Services focusing on supporting operational risk, audits, internal controls, issue management, and business continuity initiatives. This role will also collaborate closely with central risk teams within LSEG to ensure the business unit meets LSEG policy requirements and risk initiatives. Additionally, the senior analyst will manage ad-hoc projects and data analysis relating to the quality program. The role is an individual contributor and will partner with senior risk officers and members of the business management team as required. Role Responsibilitie s Support all core risk activities, including but not limited to, risk control assessments, issue management, external assurance, control testing, and vendor due diligence, and periodic control recertification activities Assist in control testing including obtaining evidence and challenging information provided to support the control design and operating effectiveness Support in the reporting of KRIs (Key Risk Indicators) and KCIs (Key Control Indicators) to assist in risk reporting Analyze and interpret data to provide regular reports and recommendations to stakeholders, identifying data quality issues, control deviations, trends, and other areas requiring review Support the end-to-end delivery of SOC1 audit and other audits by coordinating audit activities across internal stakeholders and external auditors Track audit requests, evidence submissions, recertification activities and due dates, ensuring timely follow-up, progress against established deadlines, and escalation where required. Review audit and control evidence for quality and completeness before submission Communicate effectively with management and cross-functional stakeholders on findings and ongoing issues, adapting communication style as appropriate to different teams and levels of seniority Qualification Requirements 2+ years of Ops risk, compliance, audit or controls related work in financial services or data vendor A bachelor’s degree in a related field or relevant certification with applicable experience Knowledge of financial markets data with demonstrated ability to analyze data sets in an efficient and effective manner utilizing standard tools Ability to communicate effectively and collaborate with teams across the firm, building productive working relationships across different functions, working styles, and personalities Able to work independently to plan and execute on projects, manage multiple priorities and deadlines, and collaborate as part of a team Experience with data analysis and visualization tools such as MS Excel, PowerPoint, Power BI or other Desirable Professional certifications such as CISA, CRISC or similar Experience in programming in Python, SQL and other data languages preferred; familiarity with AI-enabled tools, automation is a plus Previous IT Audit, 2nd line Technology Risk or 1st line technology risk assurance in financial services Career Stage: Senior Associate Compensation/Benefits Information: LSEG is committed to offering competitive Compensation and Benefits.

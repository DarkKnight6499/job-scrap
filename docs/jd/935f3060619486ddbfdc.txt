Learning Operations Associate
New York Life
Location: New York,New York,United States
Link: https://careers.newyorklife.com/careers/job/44997425

Location Designation: Hybrid - 1 day per week The Learning Operations Associate role is a key enabler of a modern, high-performing Learning & Development team. This role combines strong execution with a digital and AI-first mindset to help deliver seamless learning experiences, generate actionable insights, and continuously improve how learning gets done. The ideal candidate is curious, tech-savvy, data-minded, and highly organized—someone who enjoys finding smarter ways to work and is excited to leverage AI-first platforms, automation, and emerging technologies to increase the impact and efficiency of L&D. What You’ll Do Power Learning Operations Coordinate and support the end-to-end execution of learning programs, experiences, and events. Manage learning calendars, enrollment, communications, materials, and program logistics. Maintain accurate learning records, resources, and program documentation. Provide responsive support to learners, facilitators, and key stakeholders. Leverage AI & Technology Use AI-first platforms and digital tools to streamline workflows, automate routine activities, and enhance the learner experience. Apply AI to accelerate activities such as communications, content creation, research, analysis, and reporting. Recommend opportunities to make learning operations simpler, faster, and more scalable. Turn Data into Insights Track engagement, participation, completion, learner feedback, and other key learning metrics. Develop reports and dashboards that translate learning data into clear, actionable insights. Identify trends and opportunities to strengthen programs and improve the learner experience. Use data to help the team understand what is working, where attention is needed, and where learning can have greater impact. Drive Continuous Improvement Look for opportunities to simplify, standardize, automate, and improve L&D processes. Maintain trackers, workflows, templates, and operational resources that enable the team to work effectively. Partner across the L&D team to test new approaches and scale successful practices. Bring curiosity, creativity, and a continuous-improvement mindset to how learning is delivered. What You Bring Bachelor’s degree preferred. 2 years of experience in Learning & Development, HR, operations, program management, or a related field. Comfort using data to identify trends, generate insights, and inform decisions. Interest and demonstrated fluency in AI, automation, and emerging workplace technologies. Ability to quickly learn and leverage new digital platforms and tools. Strong written communication and stakeholder management skills. A proactive, solutions-oriented mindset with a passion for improving how work gets done. Pay Transparency Salary Range: $51,500-$73,500 Overtime eligible: Nonexempt Discretionary bonus eligible: Yes Sales bonus eligible: No Actual base salary will be determined based on several factors but not limited to individual’s experience, skills, qualifications, and job location.

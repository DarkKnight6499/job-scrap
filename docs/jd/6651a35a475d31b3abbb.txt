Sourcing Project Specialist ($84,900-$120,567) Position Summary Provides leadership and direction for large, complex projects. Develops negotiation strategies and leads negotiations with suppliers representing all internal stakeholders. Executes contracts and monitors supplier performance to optimize project success. Mentors, coaches and oversees development of associate sourcing project specialists. Essential Responsibilities SOURCING - Sources for high-value, complex goods and services in support of significant company projects (up to $50M). Leads the process for defining technical bid requirements and specifications and specialized terms and conditions required by individual capital projects. Leads bidding events and bid evaluation teams to analyze proposals and award business. Seeks opportunities to consolidate sourcing activities to encourage supplier competition. Seeks opportunities for cost savings and cost avoidance PROJECT MANAGEMENT - Creates and manages a project plan for the sourcing of goods and services and controls the necessary supply chain resources in support of the plan. Interacts with business unit personnel to integrate the sourcing and business project plans. Maintains relationships with key business unit stakeholders and communicates sourcing project status. Establishes metrics for evaluating and documenting specific supplier services and contractual requirements and monitors performance. NEGOTIATIONS - Develops negotiation strategies and leads negotiations representing company interests in major project purchases associated with capital projects. Facilitates the resolution of disputes and contract adjustments with suppliers and internal stakeholders. SUPPLIER MANAGEMENT - Establishes and oversees the process to track and monitor supplier performance, commitments and progress milestones to ensure full value of the project has been achieved. Identifies opportunities in collaboration with a category manager to support project needs with key suppliers in alignment with category strategies. Manages tier I and II supplier relationships as assigned RISK CONTAINMENT - Applies understanding of underlying principles of contract law to administer all project contract agreements and contractual forms, Defines and manages the risk elements of the contract and assembles all necessary documentation for obtaining project-specific insurance. Limits company liability by ensuring all required documents and notices have been obtained and are satisfactory. MENTORING - Mentors associate project specialist in sourcing event management, negotiation strategy, program standardization, supplier management, project management and risk containment. Minimum Requirements Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain Management, Engineering, Project Management, Business, Finance or equivalent experience required. Requires five of purchasing, sourcing or logistics experience. Project management experience is a plus. Significant contract knowledge, negotiation and project management skills required. Requires considerable knowledge and expertise in sourcing and supply chain management principles. Must pursue and maintain Certified Professional in Supply Management (CPSM) certification or equivalent. Proficiency in Microsoft Office suite and significant experience with purchasing and sourcing systems is required. Must be results-oriented and customer focused. Strong interpersonal, relationship, and presentation skills are required. Up to 20% domestic travel is required. Sr SC Capital Projects Specialist ($97,600-$138,600) Position Summary Develop, manage and execute large-scale engineering, procurement, & construction (EPC), engineered equipment, and construction services contract project sourcing strategies aligned with business stakeholder priorities. Utilize construction project contract expertise to procure construct EPC/turnkey contracts. Implement strategies to achieve project savings targets. Develop strategies and negotiate commercial terms and contracts with suppliers. Manage risk mitigation and post award contract management plans, including KPIs and supplier reviews to optimize successful project execution. Essential Responsibilities Execute contracting and procurement strategy/tactics for large-scale capital projects, sourcing for high value, complex goods and services. Utilize should cost models and project justifications. Lead the process to define technical bid requirements, specifications, and specialized terms & conditions. Execute bidding events. Lead bid evaluation teams to analyze proposals and award business. Coordinate with Category Management to leverage scale for recurring purchase opportunities and value capture. Apply expert contract knowledge, diligence, and integration of all contract components to ensure efficient project delivery management. Lead the development, refinement, and application of the project delivery contracting strategy for assigned projects. Ensure contractor training and compliance with corporate policies and procedures. Deploy contract negotiation strategies and plans to facilitate negotiation of contracts representing company interests in purchases associated with large-scale capital projects. Analyze, verify, and negotiate modifications, changes, or additional work orders that were not part of the original contract or purchase scope. Resolve disputes and contract adjustments with critical suppliers and internal stakeholders. Create and manage project plans for sourcing goods and services. Integrate sourcing and business project plans to develop enterprise-wide standardization of technology and equipment specifications. Deepen relationships with stakeholders and communicate sourcing project status. Execute post award contract management plans, including KPI development. Review with suppliers that contractor commitments and progress milestones are tracked in accordance with contractual terms & conditions and full project value is achieved. Manage post award contract management plans. Manage supplier performance to ensure commitments and progress milestones are met. Collaborate with Category Managers to support project needs in alignment with category strategies. Manage critical supplier relationships. Develop and lead capital project risk management strategies. Apply contract law knowledge to administer project contract agreements and contractual forms. Manage contract risk and assemble documentation to obtain project specific insurance. Limit company liability by ensuring proper documentation. Minimum Requirements Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain Management, Engineering, Construction Management, Business, or related technical/analytical field. Requires five years of advanced contracting, sourcing, or logistics experience. Project management experience required. Expert contract knowledge, negotiation and project management skills required. Requires advanced expertise in sourcing and supply chain management principles. Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite and significant experience with purchasing and sourcing systems is required. Must be results-oriented and customer focused. Strong interpersonal, relationship, and presentation skills are required. Up to 20% travel; driver’s license required. Preferred Qualifications Previous Nuclear experience preferred Experience using SAP and working with COUPA MBA and Certified Professional in Supply Chain Management (CPSM) or other supply chain certification strongly preferred.

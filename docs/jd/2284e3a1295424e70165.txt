You will use enterprise data—spanning customer, account, digital, telephony, financial, and operational sources—to help build automated and repeatable analytics solutions. You’ll work in an agile delivery model to iterate, incorporate feedback, and deliver incremental value. You will partner closely with cross-functional teams across servicing and product organizations to align on success measures, test approaches, and performance tracking.

Job Description: The Financial Resource Management (FRM) team is responsible for optimizing the firm's financial resources across its derivatives businesses, including capital, and counterparty risk. As part of the xVA Desk, the team partners closely with Sales, Trading, Structuring, Quantitative Analytics, Risk Management, Treasury, and Technology to ensure that derivative pricing appropriately reflects the economic cost of the bank's resources while supporting client activity and business growth. The Associate role provides broad exposure across all asset classes and offers a unique opportunity to contribute to derivative pricing, structuring, resource optimization, and strategic initiatives that influence the firm's derivatives business. The successful candidate will work with senior members of the desk to develop solutions that optimize capital, funding, liquidity, and counterparty credit resources while enhancing the bank's xVA framework and pricing capabilities. Key Responsibilities: Support the pricing and structuring of derivative transactions across all major asset classes, ensuring accurate incorporation of xVA and financial resource costs. Contribute to the development and enhancement of the bank's xVA framework, methodologies, and analytics across rates, FX, credit, commodities, and equities businesses. Analyze the impact of capital, funding, liquidity, balance sheet, and counterparty credit constraints on derivative transactions and client portfolios. Assist in identifying optimization opportunities that improve the efficient use of the bank's financial resources while supporting business objectives. Partner with Sales and Trading teams to evaluate transaction economics and develop resource-efficient client solutions. Develop strong relationships across Front Office businesses and become a trusted partner to traders, salespeople, structurers, quants, and risk managers. Collaborate with Quantitative Analytics, Technology, Risk, Treasury, and Product Control teams on strategic initiatives and model enhancements. Support the development of analytical tools and reporting infrastructure used to measure and manage xVA-related impacts.

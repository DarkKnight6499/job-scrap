The VP, Underwriting will be a “hands-on” technical expert focused on targeting a wide variety of commercial risks throughout the U.S. across. Reports to the Vice President – Midwest Region and has Underwriting responsibility across the 12 Midwest States: IL, IN, IA, KY, MI, MN, MO, ND, OH, SD, WI and WV Responsibilities · Leads leaders and a team that underwrites a profitable book of small, middle market and complex risks, employing the appropriate analytical diagnostics to assess acceptability. · Demonstrate expertise in analyzing

About This Job The main purpose of this job is to be responsible for analyzing commercial credits by conducting comprehensive financial analysis on companies of diverse industries and sizes. You’ll work with seasoned Commercial Bankers and Portfolio Managers to prepare Credit Approval Summary packets for loan committees for all East Region markets requiring a high degree of accuracy. These packets are utilized by senior management for annual review of credit relationships, reaffirming loans due for renewal, and for approving new loans. In addition, the Credit Analyst will be responsible for financial statement spreading and other projects as assigned. Preparation of Credit Approval Summary packets requires an understanding of financial statements and key financial metrics such as cashflow and leverage. Daily work includes research of loan documentation and an understanding of the various bank systems. Credit Specialists works directly with Relationship Managers and Portfolio Managers in the Commercial line of business, including Commercial Banking, Business Banking, and Private Banking. The position provides an opportunity to learn credit analysis and an introduction to Commercial Banking.

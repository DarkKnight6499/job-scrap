Job Description Summary About This Role Government contracts demand precision — and the people who manage indirect rate compliance are the ones who make it possible. As a Senior Analyst focused on Indirect Government Rates, you will own and continuously improve the end-to-end indirect rate process across two legal entities, ensuring GE Vernova Hitachi Nuclear Energy’s (GVH) government cost submissions are accurate, defensible, and audit-ready. This is a role where your technical depth in government cost accounting directly shapes compliance outcomes. You will serve as the primary liaison with external auditors and consultants. You will partner with internal stakeholders in Finance, Human Resources (HR), and Operations, to drive lasting improvements to the processes and controls that uphold GVH’s government contracting obligations. Job Description Who You Are You are a detail-oriented government cost accounting professional with significant experience in indirect rate development and a track record of navigating complex regulatory environments with confidence. You communicate clearly, work independently, and bring a continuous improvement mindset to established processes. Required Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field — or equivalent experience (see Education note below) At least 5 years of experience specifically in government accounting or government cost reporting At least 8 years of experience in financial accounting, financial planning and analysis (FP&A), or a related finance function Preferred Proven experience developing and improving indirect rate processes in a government contracting environment, with working knowledge of FAR, CAS, and related regulations Experience interfacing directly with DCAA, DOE, DCMA, or other U.S. government agencies in an audit or compliance context Advanced proficiency in Excel, including complex financial modeling, pivot tables, and large dataset analysis; experience with enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems such as SAP, Oracle, or Hyperion Professional certification such as Certified Public Accountant (CPA) or Certified Management Accountant (CMA) Education A formal education and subsequent Bachelor's or Master's degree in Accounting or Finance is nice to have, but we are most interested in your total experience and professional achievements.

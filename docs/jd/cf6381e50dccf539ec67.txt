The Wealth Management National Sales organization, within Investment Solutions, supports the Firm’s Financial Advisors by managing the field sales support of non-banking products and the business development functions for our branch network. The Morgan Stanley Concentrated Equity Team is a specialized group within Field and Client Business Development, dedicated to educating and engaging advisors and clients on our comprehensive suite of concentrated equity solutions. The Concentrated Equity Director will report to the Head of Concentrated Equity and act as a subject matter expert, collaborating closely with specialist teams across Investment Solutions, the Global Investment Office (GIO), MSIM and external partners. This role will take a holistic, product-agnostic approach to advisor engagements, supporting the Firm’s Advisory, Capital Markets, and Alternative Solutions businesses. The Director will play a key role in FA/PWA understanding and adoption of concentrated equity strategies.

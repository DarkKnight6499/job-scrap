Introduction to the team Expedia Technology teams partner with our Product teams to create innovative products, services, and tools to deliver high-quality experiences for travelers, partners, and our employees. A singular technology platform powered by data and machine learning provides secure, differentiated, and personalized experiences that drive loyalty and traveler satisfaction. We are a dedicated team of investigators within Enterprise Technology, Fraud & Security, specializing in the manual review of potentially fraudulent or abusive transactions. Our mission is to assess risk and prevent fraud by carefully analyzing individual transactions and making informed decisions based on available data. We embrace AI as part of how we work, and we expect our investigators to use approved AI tools thoughtfully to strengthen the quality and efficiency of their reviews. As a Fraud & Risk Investigator, you’ll play a vital role in safeguarding our platform by identifying suspicious activity and helping to ensure a safe experience for all users. This position encompasses all the responsibilities of the Fraud & Risk Investigator I position but with additional emphasis on quality and the responsible, effective use of AI. In this role, you will : Monitor multiple queues to assess and analyze high-risk transactions for potential fraud or abuse, using AI-assisted insights to inform, not replace, your independent judgment. Leverage approved AI tools, in line with Expedia Group’s responsible AI and data privacy guidelines, to summarize case details, research linked activity, and surface fraud-risk insights, critically validating all AI-generated output before acting on it. Manage and respond to incoming communications across various channels from both internal teams and external customers. Communicate emerging fraud trends and insights effectively with peers and leadership, fostering a collaborative and informed environment. Provide feedback on AI tool performance, flagging inaccurate or incomplete output, to help improve how AI supports our investigations. Utilize standard SQL queries to extract data and generate routine reports to support fraud detection efforts outside the queues, using AI tools where appropriate to speed up query writing and analysis. Work in a hybrid environment to foster collaboration, enhance communication, build strong relationships, and support a positive team culture. Minimum Qualifications: 1+ year of professional experience in a detail-oriented, investigative, or analytical role, such as fraud prevention, payments or banking, compliance, loss prevention, quality assurance, or travel or customer operations. Proven track record of consistently meeting or exceeding productivity and quality goals. Demonstrable ability to make sound, independent decisions with minimal supervision, including knowing when to question or override AI-generated recommendations. Ability to document findings and explain decisions clearly and concisely, in writing and verbally. Experience working in a fast-paced, production or queue-based environment, with comfort managing multiple tasks autonomously and adapting to rapidly changing AI-enabled tools and workflows. Strong interest in learning generative AI tools to improve research, analysis, and productivity, with a commitment to verifying AI output and protecting customer data. Proficiency with Microsoft 365 (Outlook, Teams, Excel) and strong internet research skills. Willingness to work flexible hours, including weekends, holidays, and non-standard shifts; may occasionally require extended hours or work on unscheduled days. Preferred Qualifications: Experience in fraud prevention, fraud investigation, or risk review, ideally in the airline, travel, hospitality, or online marketplace industry. Experience investigating chargebacks or payment fraud. Experience using generative AI tools in a professional setting to improve research, analysis, or productivity, and the ability to give examples of how. SQL experience, or experience building trackers and analyzing data in Excel. Experience with case management or ticketing platforms, including case logging, queue management, and taking a case from open to close.

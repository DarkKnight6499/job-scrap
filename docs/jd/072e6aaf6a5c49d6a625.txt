Job Description: Job Title Technology Support Specialist Corporate Title Associate Location New York, NY Overview As a Technology Support Specialist, you will provide hands-on technical support for the trading floor and help maintain the stability, performance, and reliability of critical trading technology. You will partner with Traders, Operations, and Technology teams to diagnose issues, improve workflows, and minimize disruption in a fast-paced financial markets environment. Your work will support the applications, hardware, and infrastructure that enable efficient trading operations, while helping the team proactively identify risks and adopt new technologies. What We Offer You A diverse and inclusive environment that embraces change, innovation, and collaboration A hybrid working model, allowing for in-office / work from home flexibility, generous vacation, personal and volunteer days Employee Resource Groups support an inclusive workplace for everyone and promote community engagement Competitive compensation packages including health and wellbeing benefits, retirement savings plans, parental leave, and family building benefits Educational resources, matching gift and volunteer programs What You’ll Do Provide technical support for trading floor operations, diagnosing and resolving software, hardware, Windows, and network-related issues to minimize downtime and maintain system performance Support and maintain financial markets and productivity applications, including Bloomberg, FactSet, Eikon, Tradeweb, and Microsoft Office 365 Monitor trading systems and proactively identify, troubleshoot, and resolve potential issues before they affect business operations Partner with traders, Operations, and Technology teams to understand technical needs, address incidents, and improve support workflows Maintain the integrity, security, and reliability of the trading environment by following established technology controls, risk standards, and company policies Contribute to process improvement through documentation, scripting, automation, and the adoption of new support technologies and practices Skills You’ll Need Proven experience providing technology support within financial services, preferably in a trading floor or financial markets environment Working knowledge of trading systems, financial markets, and related technologies, including Bloomberg, FactSet, Eikon, or Tradeweb Hands-on experience troubleshooting software, hardware, Microsoft Windows, Microsoft Office 365, and basic networking issues Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to prioritize and resolve technical issues in a time-sensitive environment Clear written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain technical concepts to both technical and non-technical stakeholders Proven ability to leverage AI tools to enhance productivity, optimize workflows to solve business problems, while applying critical judgment to ensure responsible and ethical use of data and AI outputs Skills That Will Help You Excel Experience using scripting or automation tools to streamline technology support activities Familiarity with Information Technology Infrastructure Library processes and service management principles Understanding of risk management, information security, and compliance requirements within a financial trading environment Proactive and detail-oriented approach, with

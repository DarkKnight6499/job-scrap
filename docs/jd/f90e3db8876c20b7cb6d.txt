Work Schedule Standard (Mon-Fri) Environmental Conditions Office Job Description When you’re part of the team at Thermo Fisher Scientific, you’ll do meaningful work that makes a positive impact on a global scale. Join 120,000 colleagues who bring our Mission to life each day to enable our customers to make the world healthier, cleaner and safer. You’ll find the resources here to achieve your career goals and help take science a step beyond by developing solutions for some of the world’s toughest challenges, like protecting the environment, making sure our food is safe or helping find cures for cancer. What will you do? We seek a Finance professional with excellent analysis and communication skills to support the Customer Channels Group as a Financial Analyst II. Your position will report to a Commercial Finance Manager and will support the Commercial Team as they implement contracts, pay customer incentives, and build relationships with recognized Global companies. Helping you do this, is a team of three Finance professionals who meet weekly and work collaboratively to solve issues together. Key Duties and Responsibilities: Forecast, track and initiate payment of customer incentives Book month-end customer incentive accruals Monthly variance analysis of actual customer performance versus expectations Perform special projects and ad-hoc reporting as required by finance and business leadership. Work with appropriate internal departments to accurately implement new contracts. Prepare several different types of standard reports to support our customers and Sales Team Problem solve multi-system variances for accuracy Collaborate with other members of the team to share knowledge and help teammates were needed Ensure accuracy, quality and integrity of financial data Ad-hoc reporting, as the need arises Perform other related duties as assigned How will you get here? Minimum Education and Experience: Bachelor’s Degree in Finance or related fields Strong Microsoft Excel skills Minimum of 3 years of relevant work experience Strong interpersonal skills with the proven ability to build and maintain relationships and communicate professionally to all levels of an organization. Excellent organization and time management skills with an eye for detail Knowledge, Skills and Abilities: Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to translate and articulate business impact in financial terms. Ability to perform effectively under pressure and to handle multiple assignments Ability to work as part of team environment and individually Self-motivated, with the willingness and ability to learn new concepts Ability to communicate effectively (written and verbal) at all levels in the organization Embody the Thermo Fisher Scientific values of Integrity, Intensity, Involvement, and Innovation Strong Excel and modeling skills, experience with MS PowerPoint, Cognos and PowerBI highly valuable.

The Associate III position in Leveraged Finance is a credit underwriting and portfolio management role focused on completing acquisition, recapitalization, restructuring, and refinancing transactions for new and existing customers of Citizens. The position requires constant interaction with internal colleagues from Commercial Banking, Credit Risk Management, and Capital Markets. The candidate will be working as part of a deal team on financing opportunities including underwriting, obtaining internal credit approval, and completion of the documentation and closing process. Associates will be directly involved in establishing and performing underwriting due diligence, identifying key transaction risks and mitigating factors, and obtaining credit approval for proposed transactions. Day-to-day activity will involve supporting Portfolio Managers with portfolio management, including, but not limited to: Quarterly Portfolio Reviews, Asset Quality Reporting, Covenant Tracking, Financial Statement Analysis, and completion of various internal modeling tools. The Associate will be assigned to one or more Industry sectors, depending on need. Preference for Associates with leveraged finance skills and prior experience underwriting Lead Arranger, Sole Bank, Club or Participant transactions. Primary responsibilities include Assisting in the completion of new and existing loan underwriting due diligence, including preparation of deal screening memos, identification of risks and mitigants, new credit approval requests, preparation of three-statement financial models, in-depth financial analysis, and completion of comparable debt and enterprise value analysis Conducting industry research, preparing written summaries of industry issues, risks, opportunities, and the competitive environment, and communicating questions or observations Strong knowledge and utilization of internal risk and return models and loan systems Monitoring for the receipt and accuracy of client reporting including financial statements, and covenant compliance certificates and completing quarterly analysis and annual reviews Participation in Team Meetings, Capital Markets calls, and broader Commercial Banking calls.

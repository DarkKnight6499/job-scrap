First Hawaiian Bank is seeking a Retail Management Associate to join the B.R.I.D.G.E. Development Program. The Retail Management Associate (RMA) is a full-time participant in the bank’s 12-month B.R.I.D.G.E. Program, a structured leadership development experience designed to prepare individuals for future Assistant Branch Manager (ABM) roles. RMAs rotate through key branch functions, complete structured coursework, receive mentorship, and work on real-world projects that build foundational technical skills and leadership behaviors. Upon successful completion, RMAs may be considered for ABM placement based on demonstrated readiness and organizational needs.

Analyst, Investment Performance Portfolio Analytics
TIAA
Location: 2 Locations (West Hartford CT   75 Isham Road)
Link: https://tiaa.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Search/job/West-Hartford-CT---75-Isham-Road/Analyst--Investment-Performance-Portfolio-Analytics_R260900133

Gather, calculate and analyze investment performance data for internal groups (operations, portfolio/product management, marketing, compliance, investment management) and external groups (clients, prospects, consultants). Ensure reports and analyses are completed in an accurate and timely manner. Engage in complex analyses or interpretations on topics such as portfolio/benchmark performance results, attribution. Must effectively manage deadlines, client expectations, and staff where required and ensure best practice is maintained across the department. Key Responsibilities and Duties Calculate, review and verify performance data ensuring accuracy and timeliness. Interact with appropriate individuals/groups to resolve errors as necessary. Maintains databases to ensure the accuracy and integrity of static and time series data across relevant portfolios, benchmarks, or other performance data. Ensure procedures are followed and adequate controls in place. Provide technical expertise related to performance calculations. Review any calculations prepared outside of base systems. Assist in the creation, maintenance, and validation of investment performance track records, ensuring performance results, benchmarks, and disclosures are presented accurately and in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements, including the SEC Marketing Rule. Prepare, interpret and investigate anomalies in attribution/contribution reports used in the analyses of performance. Understand various attribution models. Regular direct interaction with clients to research, analyze and resolve complex queries and analyses. Attend and lead meetings with internal and external clients as necessary. Ensure performance data has been represented in a complete and accurate manner within Senior Management reports, third-party databases, sales materials and regulatory filings. Exhibit comprehensive knowledge in performance and maintain current knowledge with regulatory and industry guidelines. Take initiative and successfully execute and complete special high-profile projects. Identify and implement process improvements and streamline efficiencies. Serve as technical expert on investment performance data. When necessary, provide ad hoc commentary on investment performance, general research on industry trends or peer group data. Knowledge of the NREIF/PREA Reporting standards and how they relate to calculating private real estate investment performance. Educational Requirements University (Degree) Preferred Work Experience No Experience Required Physical Requirements Physical Requirements: Sedentary Work Career Level 5IC Preferred Qualifications 1+ years of transferable experience in the investment management industry Bachelor’s degree; concentration in Mathematics, Finance, Accounting, Computer Science, Business, or similar is preferred. Strong knowledge of Fair Value GAAP Accounting, particularly how it applies to private fund investment performance. Must possess a high aptitude of analytical and mathematical skills which are required for calculating, reviewing and disseminating performance information. Data aggregation and relational database analytics skills are required, demonstrated though knowledge of excel pivot tables, data visualization software such as Tableau (preferred) or Power BI. Ability to manipulate data, and construct data pipelines in Python/R Experience using Kiro or other agentic AI development tools to improve data pipelines, reporting processes, validation routines, or analytics workflows. Must show superior customer service by generating and responding to client requests in a timely manner as well have the interpersonal skills to interact with both internal and external clients Must be able to communicate effectively both in writing and orally. Must also be proficient in the use of third-party financial industry databases and Microsoft Office. The ability to cooperate in a team environment is also critical. Pursuit or completion of CIPM, CAIA, and CFA designations encouraged. Prior experience in Private Equity Real Estate. Knowledge of NCREIF Data and Query Tool. Working knowledge of Yardi GL Accounting systems. Experience building models in a production environment with Anaplan.

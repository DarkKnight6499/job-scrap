You will partner directly with business leadership, Client Services, Product, Compliance, Legal, regional stakeholders, technology and other Risk teams to proactively identify emerging risks, strengthen governance, enhance controls, and enable sustainable growth across increasingly complex cross-border operating environments. This individual will bring strong enterprise and operational risk expertise, strategic thinking capabilities, and a deep understanding of payments, regulatory frameworks, financial crime risks, and global payment ecosystems. The ideal candidate is viewed by executive leadership as a thought leader capable of translating complex risk, regulatory, and operational challenges into actionable business insights while enabling responsible growth across a dynamic global payments landscape.

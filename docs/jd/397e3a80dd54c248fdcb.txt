The VCAT (Vulnerable Client Alert Team) Associate Investigator supports VP Investigators within the Vulnerable Adult Investigations (VAI) team by conducting high-volume, routine investigations involving scams, elder/vulnerable adult financial exploitation, subpoena-related matters, and IC3 complaint submissions. The role focuses on cases that do not require customer outreach, key responsibilities include reviewing accounts and transactions, identifying and remediating risk, documenting cases to meet SLAs, preparing and filing Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) when warranted, completing required regulatory reporting/assessments, and escalating matters to VP Investigators as appropriate. The Associate reports to the Team Lead and partners with internal stakeholders and external agencies (e.g., Adult Protective Services, state regulators, and law enforcement) when required. As a Global Security Investigator - Associate in Global Security (GS) you will protect the firm’s people and assets and support the safety and soundness of JPMorgan Chase’s operations worldwide. You will help reduce risk related to elder and vulnerable adult financial exploitation by managing a high volume of routine investigations—primarily scams and exploitation cases—while also supporting subpoena-related investigative work and IC3 complaint submissions when warranted. You will support senior investigators within VAI and perform investigations as part of VCAT, without direct customer contact. Reporting to the Team Lead, the Associate is expected to exercise sound fraud/risk judgment, maintain clear and defensible documentation, and consistently meet regulatory requirements and SLAs, including SAR drafting and filing.

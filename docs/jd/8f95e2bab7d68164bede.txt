As Senior Audit Manager - Enterprise-wide Risk Management (ERM) and Governance Audit , you will lead audit coverage of ERM and Governance in the US. The ERM and Governance Audit team is responsible for audit coverage of the management of the ERM function and Corporate Governance across HSBC Group, including the management of operational and resilience risks. This is achieved through both direct audit coverage and providing subject-matter expertise to monitor and direct the audit coverage across Global Internal Audit.

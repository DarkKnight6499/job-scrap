Job Location CINCINNATI GENERAL OFFICES Job Description As a Retail Media Trader, you will: Manage North America retail media campaigns across leading retailer media networks, owning campaigns from activation through ongoing optimization to deliver business and media objectives. Develop and execute retail media strategies aligned to brand, category, customer, and commerce objectives, translating business priorities into effective media plans and activations. Own campaign planning, activation, and optimization across retail media platforms, including keyword, product, audience, and targeting strategies while managing budgets, pacing, and performance. Use real-time data, reporting tools, and platform insights to identify trends, opportunities, and risks, delivering incremental and profitable growth for P&G and retail partners. Monitor and analyze campaign performance to develop actionable recommendations, improve efficiency, and drive continuous optimization against key business objectives. Partner with Brand, Sales, Analytics, Commerce, IT, and other cross-functional teams to align retail media investments with broader eCommerce and omnichannel strategies. Create standardized reporting, communicate performance results, and present actionable insights and recommendations to internal and external business partners. Manage media investments with financial discipline, supporting forecasting, budget allocation, pacing, and performance optimization to maximize business results. Identify, test, and scale new capabilities, formats, technologies, and opportunities across retail media platforms to drive continuous improvement and innovation. Maintain high standards of campaign quality, reporting accuracy, governance, and operational excellence while managing multiple priorities and deliverables. Job Qualifications Required Bachelor’s Degree. Experience in retail media, digital commerce, paid search, digital advertising, performance marketing, media buying, or a related field. Experience planning, activating, optimizing, or analyzing digital advertising and media campaigns. Strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret performance data and translate insights into action. Experience using reporting tools, spreadsheets, and data visualization tools to monitor performance and communicate results. Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to collaborate and influence across multiple stakeholders. Experience with Microsoft Office suite, including Excel, PowerPoint, and Word. Preferred Experience working within consumer goods, retail, eCommerce, or omnichannel business environments. Familiarity with retail media networks, retail search, audience-based advertising, onsite media, offsite media, display advertising, or programmatic media environments. Experience managing agency, retailer, or external partner relationships. Experience with campaign planning, budget management, forecasting, and performance optimization. Experience identifying business insights from data and translating those insights into recommendations and action plans. The ideal candidate is experienced in retail media, digital commerce, paid media, or performance marketing and has a passion for using data and analytics to drive business growth. You are comfortable working within media platforms and performance reporting tools, possess strong problem-solving and communication skills, and can translate complex data into actionable business recommendations. You operate with agility, attention to detail, and a continuous learning mindset while building strong partnerships across functions and delivering meaningful business results.

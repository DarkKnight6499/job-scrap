What this job involves: JLL is seeking a Senior Property Associate to join the Property Management Team for one of its most prominent clients. This is an on-site role for a portfolio of Class A Buildings in Chelsea. As a Senior Property Associate, you play a critical role to ensure smooth and safe operations while serving as a key liaison between tenants, property managers, vendors and other stakeholders. You will also provide accounting and financial support, maintain positive tenant relations and provide superior customer service. You will lead specialized projects, mentor new team members and own critical administrative functions. This position provides an outstanding opportunity to launch and grow your career in commercial property management, where you'll work alongside experienced professionals who are committed to your growth and development. What your day-to-day will look like: Provide exceptional customer service to tenants by responding to inquiries, processing service requests, and coordinating resolution of maintenance issues Support property management team with administrative tasks including data entry, document preparation, and maintenance of property records and databases Manage vendor coordination and work orders to ensure timely completion of maintenance and repair activities Help prepare monthly reports, financial summaries, and property performance updates for senior management and clients Support lease administration activities including tenant move-in processes, document filing, and communication coordination Lead tenant events, building communications, and community engagement initiatives to enhance tenant experience All other tasks which may be required to comply with Property Management Agreements, local ordinances, or as necessary to ensure property operations On-site presence required Required qualifications: 2 + years of commercial real estate or property management Experience with Yardi, Kardin and/or MRI property management software 2+ years of experience in a customer service or customer-facing position Bachelor's degree in Business, Real Estate, Communications, or related field Strong written and verbal communication skills Excellent organizational skills with ability to manage multiple tasks and prioritize effectively in a fast-paced environment Positive attitude with eagerness to grow within the commercial real estate industry This position does not provide visa sponsorship. Candidates must be authorized to work in the United States without sponsorship.

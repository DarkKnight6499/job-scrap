Job description Group Description: Evercore's Private Capital Advisory (PCA) team is a leading global secondaries market advisor, focusing on providing strategic advisory services to financial sponsors across a variety of portfolio and fund management objectives, advising limited partners on liquidity solutions for their private asset portfolios, and employing various forms of structured ABS technologies to create innovative capital and liquidity solutions for private markets issuers and investors. What is the Position? PCA is seeking a highly motivated Data Analyst to join the PCA Operations team and support its technology and data initiatives. Reporting to PCA’s COO and working day-today with the Operations team member who leads these initiatives, the Data Analyst will help translate the team’s technology priorities into practical solutions by automating manual processes, integrating systems, and building reliable reporting and analytics. The ideal candidate combines strong technical and analytical skills with a foundation in data analytics and information systems, is intellectually curious and detail-oriented, and is able to manage multiple projects at once while continuing to learn and grow within a collaborative team. The Data Analyst will partner closely with PCA’s Analytics team, senior bankers, and Evercore’s Technology team to gather requirements, deliver solutions, and support their adoption across the global PCA team. This is a highly visible role offering broad exposure to PCA’s business and the ongoing evolution of its operating platform. Key Responsibilities Partner with the PCA Operations and Analytics teams to identify high-impact opportunities for process automation and system integration across PCA Design and implement automated workflows and reporting pipelines to improve process efficiency, reduce manual effort, and enhance data timeliness and accuracy Develop and maintain data validation and governance frameworks, including automated quality checks and exception reporting, to ensure the integrity of PCA’s data Gather business requirements, map data flows, and coordinate with stakeholders to support change management for new analytics tools, dashboards, and systems Develop documentation, training materials, and best practices to help bankers adopt new data tools and integrate them into their day-to-day workflows Collaborate with PCA’s Analytics team to ensure data-driven insights are incorporated into deal execution and client materials Track usage and business impact of PCA’s data tools through dashboards, analytics, and management reporting, helping measure the value created by data initiatives Collaborate with Technology, Data, Legal, Compliance, and other cross-functional teams to deliver scalable, secure, and compliant data solutions Prepare presentations, analyses, and recommendations for PCA senior leadership to support decision-making Qualifications We are looking for a highly motivated, detail-oriented individual who is intellectually curious and eager to apply data and technology to complex business problems. Core Requirements: 1–3 years of experience in banking, consulting, or analytics is required Demonstrated interest in AI, business transformation, and technology-enabled process improvement Self-starter with a collaborative mindset, intellectual curiosity, and a willingness to learn new technologies Key Attributes: Comfortable working with bankers at all levels of seniority Excellent written and verbal communication skills Strong analytical and problem-solving skills Exceptional attention to detail and organizational ability Ability to manage multiple priorities effectively under pressure Collaborative, team-oriented mindset Expected Base Salary Range: $100,000 - $140,000 In addition to a competitive base salary, employees may be eligible to receive a discretionary bonus delivered in the form of cash and/or deferred equity.

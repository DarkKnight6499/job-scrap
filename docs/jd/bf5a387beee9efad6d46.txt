Join JPMorganChase's Firmwide Regulatory Reporting & Analysis (FRRA) team, where you'll play a critical role in ensuring the accuracy and integrity of U.S. regulatory filings that underpin the firm's fortress balance sheet. This is an opportunity to build deep expertise in regulatory reporting while collaborating with controllers, technology partners, and senior stakeholders across the organization. If you thrive in a fast-paced, detail-driven environment and want to make a tangible impact on how one of the world's largest financial institutions meets its regulatory obligations, this role is for you. As a External Regulatory Reporting Analyst within the Firmwide Regulatory Reporting & Analysis (FRRA) External Reporting Production team, you will be responsible for end-to-end processing and reporting activities for Investment Securities and Securitization across key U.S. regulatory reports including FR Y-9C, Call Report, and CCAR. You will manage critical BAU activities — from data sourcing and validation to adjustments processing and reconciliations — while partnering closely with line of business controllers, financial control, product controllers, and Risk & Finance Technology teams. You will also identify and execute process improvements to existing tools and procedures, support business users of the FRI application, and contribute to strategic initiatives that strengthen the firm's regulatory reporting infrastructure.

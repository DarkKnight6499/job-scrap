Position The High Yield Junior Research Associate will have the opportunity to gain financial skills as well as learn the intricacies of fundamental research. Working directly with experienced Research Analysts, this individual will be responsible for creating and maintaining financial models, conducting due diligence on potential investment opportunities, and completing ad hoc tasks (e.g., creating client presentations) related to the investment and portfolio management process. A successful Junior Research Associate will become increasingly responsible for the sourcing, analysis, diligence, and maintenance of investments in an increasingly independent manner. Responsibilities Partner with and support senior research professionals in all aspects of due diligence processes for current and prospective investments Conduct due diligence on company and industry trends through reviewing SEC filings, quarterly calls, industry conferences and other industry-specific resources Analyze, build, and update financial and valuation models Maintain deep knowledge of the industries and companies being followed and make or change recommendations as necessary Effectively generate and communicate investment ideas to senior investment personnel Write research reports, synthesize data, and share findings (relevant investment themes, events, and breaking news) to senior management Search for new investment ideas through internal screens, industry reports, industry conferences and other relevant sources Present during team meetings, solicit feedback, and seek continuous improvement of presentation skills Qualifications Bachelor’s degree 0-2 years’ research and valuation experience working in high yield investments Knowledge of accounting, valuation, and the fixed income market Exceptional oral and written communication skills including the ability to explain complex information succinctly and efficiently Ability to work well under pressure and handle tight deadlines Purpose-Driven: Starts with Why – in service of our communities, our clients, and each other Accountable: Operates with autonomy and drives towards results Emotionally Intelligent: Demonstrates self-awareness and interpersonal skills Grit: Persists in the face of adversity Lifelong Learner: Employs a growth mindset and strives to become more Additional Information Full-time Employee Location: Waltham, MA Less than 5% travel required Salary Range: $75,000 -

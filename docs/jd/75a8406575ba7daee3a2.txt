What you will do: The Credit Investment Analyst conducts fundamental investment analysis on loan and bond capital structures. The Analyst works closely with a Senior Analyst to evaluate issuer and industry trends, determine relative value within issuer capital structures and industry sectors, and communicate investment recommendations to Portfolio Managers. The fundamental credit investment research conducted by the Analyst is an integral component of the portfolio construction process. The Analyst role involves regular interaction with the management of issuing companies, industry participants & consultants, and other market participants. Analysts benefit from our comprehensive investment capabilities across the Fixed Income universe, including interest rates, currencies, structured products, macroeconomics, and investment strategy. The Analyst role offers increased responsibility as the Analyst’s experience, capability, and confidence grows. This role is a hybrid work arrangement requiring working in our Newark, NJ office 3 days a week. What you can expect: Financial statement analysis, modeling, and forecasting Industry data collection and trend analysis Identify industry initiatives and market trends that impact the business Assess business and financial strategy Monitoring of market movements through daily meetings with portfolio managers, economists, and strategists Formulate and maintain forward looking investment recommendations Determine credit quality by assigning and maintaining independent internal ratings What you will bring: Bachelor’s Degree Required: Majors include Finance, Accounting, and/or Economics, and a minimum GPA of 3.0 Strong analytical, leadership and problem-solving skills Strong written, oral communication, and interpersonal skills Proficient in MS Office applications (Excel, Word, PowerPoint) Leverages AI tools (e.g., Claude Financial, Copilot, etc) to accelerate research, data synthesis, and report drafting.

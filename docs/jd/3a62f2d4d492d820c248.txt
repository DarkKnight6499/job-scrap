What You'll Be Doing The Bank Data Analyst II – CECL & Credit Risk will work as a member of Stifel's Analyst team within the Credit Risk Data Analytics group. The Credit Data Analyst II is responsible for automation and servicing of systematic data extraction from the Core systems and integration of data into applications used by the business. The Credit Data Analyst II will also act as a subject matter expert for assigned raw data sets as well as maintain documentation surrounding them. The Credit Data Analyst II will employ data science methodologies to ensure loan portfolio data integrity to be utilized in credit loss forecasting and management reporting as well as supporting discovery or solutions for firm data driven initiatives and developments. This position is expected to interpret requests, work with business users as well as the portfolio delivery team to further define requirements, analyze the data requested to ensure proper use and then translate those requirements into queries that will provide the objective data sets. What We're Looking For Responsible for the monthly production of loan impairment calculations and portfolio analytics Master all aspects of processing within Impairment Studio with the help of Moody's internal support team Perform in-depth analysis and act as subject matter expert for assigned raw data sets and document data dependencies, sources and outputs that impact the allowance process Support Portfolio Managers and /Underwriters with finalizing PD / LGD calibrations integrating Moody's Credit Lens and Commercial Mortgage Metrics platforms in an end-to-end Credit Risk Management process Work with Bank's 3rd party consultants and Stifel IT on future aspects of the final/ new implementation packages and the department's data management strategy Develop analyses to support management review of the broad loan portfolio incorporating PD/LGD metrics down to the loan/facility level Improve the accuracy and consistency of loan risk ratings, and ImpairmentStudio outputs through automation and documentation of internal processes, providing data integrity and reconciliation accuracy and consistency metrics through periodic reporting Documentation of existing processes and models to a required standard as defined by the internal & external auditors and other governance bodies Proactively identify and recommend process and practice improvement within the team. Maintain strong communication with stakeholders, following up as needed to ensure all requests are being actively addressed and/or status is being communicated.

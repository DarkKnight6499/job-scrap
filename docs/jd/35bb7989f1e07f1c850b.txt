Who we are looking for Global Liquidity Management (GLM) is part of the Global Treasury within the Finance organization and is responsible for managing the firm’s liquidity risk. A primary focus of GLM is to analyze, measure, and manage the firm’s liquidity position and risk profile to ensure that State Street and its international entities can meet all contractual and contingent financial obligations through normal market cycles as well as periods of stress. Through meaningful, timely, transparent, multidimensional and globally harmonized measures, GLM provides key stakeholders with the insight needed to understand the firm’s liquidity position, associated risks, and the factors that influence liquidity. As part of GLM, the Assistant Vice President of Collateral and Intraday Liquidity Management will be responsible for analyzing, reporting, and proactively optimizing the firm’s collateral inventory and intraday liquidity usage. This role works closely with all aspects of the Liquidity Management and Portfolio Trading teams to provide critical insights and support collateral mobilization and substitution actions. Why this role is important to us The team you will be joining plays an important role in the overall success of the organization. Across the globe, institutional investors rely on us to help them manage risk, respond to challenges, and drive performance and profitability. In this role, you will help develop cutting-edge solutions that are both practical and scalable. You will contribute to building resilience across the organization and executing day-to-day deliverables with excellence. This role offers significant visibility and ownership, with the opportunity to drive initiatives that support key Treasury and liquidity management objectives.

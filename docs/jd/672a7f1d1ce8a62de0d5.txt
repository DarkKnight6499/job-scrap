Purpose Contributes to the overall success of the US Treasury in New York ensuring specific individual goals, plans, initiatives are executed / delivered in support of the team’s business strategies and objectives. Ensures all activities conducted are in compliance with governing regulations, internal policies and procedures. The Associate supports the measurement, monitoring, and reporting of liquidity risks across GBM and Business Banking activities. The role performs liquidity analytics, stress testing, forecasting, and reporting activities while supporting the maintenance of methodologies, governance processes, and management reporting. What You'll Do Champions a customer focused culture to deepen client relationships and leverage broader Bank relationships, systems and knowledge. Lead liquidity risk analysis and oversight for GBM and Business Banking activities Support execution of ILST scenarios and liquidity stress testing exercises Analyze business-driven liquidity risks, funding requirements, and balance sheet trends Prepare liquidity forecasts, scenario analyses, and management reporting Monitor key liquidity metrics and investigate material variances or emerging risks Support the development and maintenance of liquidity risk methodologies and assumptions Conduct data validation, controls, and analytical reviews to ensure reporting accuracy Assist with governance materials, committee reporting, and regulatory documentation Partner with stakeholders across Treasury, Finance, Business, and Risk functions to address liquidity risk and reporting requirements Support automation, process improvement, and analytical enhancement initiatives Understand how the Bank’s risk appetite and risk culture should be considered in day-to-day activities and decisions. Actively pursues effective and efficient operations of his/her respective areas in accordance with Scotiabank’s Values, its Code of Conduct and the Global Sales Principles, while ensuring the adequacy, adherence to and effectiveness of day-to-day business controls to meet obligations with respect to operational, compliance, AML/ATF/sanctions and conduct risk. Champions a high-performance environment and contributes to an inclusive work environment. What You'll Bring Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Accounting, Risk Management, Data Analytics, or a related discipline. 5+ years of experience in Treasury, Liquidity Risk Management or business aligned function Strong understanding of liquidity risk management concepts, including liquidity stress testing, funding risk, balance sheet management, and regulatory liquidity frameworks. Experience performing quantitative analysis, scenario modeling, forecasting, and management reporting. Knowledge of regulatory liquidity requirements such as ILST, LCR, FR 2052a, Reg YY, or related U.S. banking regulations is preferred. Proficiency in Excel and data analytics tools; experience with SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Python, or similar tools is an asset. Demonstrated ability to synthesize complex data into clear insights and recommendations for senior management. Strong stakeholder management, communication, and presentation skills, with the ability to work effectively across Treasury, Finance, Risk, and Business teams. Proven ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced, highly regulated environment. Interested?

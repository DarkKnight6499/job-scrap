Senior Underwriting Officer – Environmental Casualty Product Line Manager

Description As a member of North America Specialty (NAS) Environmental’s Management team, you will meaningfully contribute to the growth and profitability of our Environmental Casualty products through consistent engagement with Underwriters, Underwriting Managers, other Product Line Managers and other key business stakeholders (Engineering, Claims, Legal, Actuary, Finance). The role requires extensive technical knowledge of underwriting concepts, practices, procedures and techniques, as well as strong insights into the environmental casualty marketplace, including experience working with or at key environmental carriers or brokers Manage complex referrals and support timely, consistent underwriting decisions. Monitor premium, rate, retention, profitability, loss trends, capacity, and aggregation exposure. Develop and maintain underwriting guidelines, forms, and product enhancements. Support EPIC, excess casualty, auto, and environmental contractors’ workers’ compensation strategy. Provide technical guidance, coaching, and training to underwriters. Occasional travel and marketing responsibilities, including client and broker visits to support your assigned production teams and regions. Strengthen broker and distribution relationships and support renewal and new-business execution. Qualifications Bachelor's degree or equivalent training plus at least 7 years of relevant environmental casualty production underwriting experience with progressively increasing levels of responsibility. Requires at least 3 years of previous people management experience. Requires an advanced knowledge of environmental casualty underwriting concepts, practices, procedures and techniques, including, but not limited to coverage issues, product lines, marketing, product competition within the marketplace. Knowledge/Underwriting experience for workers compensation for environmental contractors. Exceptional interpersonal skills with the ability to effectively interact with brokers, clients, colleagues, and other internal departments. Places a high value on collaboration and possesses strong verbal and written communication and organization (multi-tasking) skills. Strong negotiation, analytical and decision-making skills.

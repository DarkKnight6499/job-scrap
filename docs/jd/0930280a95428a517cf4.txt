YOUR IMPACT GSFO is in search of an Associate to support the rollout, execution, and ongoing optimization of advanced wealth planning capabilities for the firm’s most strategic UHNW clients and family office relationships. This individual will serve as a planning specialist and trusted partner to Private Wealth Advisors and other internal subject-matter experts. The Associate will assist with the development of complex, integrated financial analyses that help clients understand their consolidated net worth, liquidity needs, cash-flow outlook, wealth-transfer objectives, and key planning trade-offs. OUR IMPACT Wealth Management Across Wealth Management, Goldman Sachs helps empower clients and customers around the world to reach their financial goals. Our advisor-led wealth management businesses provide financial planning, investment management, banking and comprehensive advice to a wide range of clients, including ultra-high net worth and high net worth individuals, as well as family offices, foundations and endowments, and corporations and their employees. Private Wealth Management (PWM) Goldman Sachs Private Wealth Management (PWM) specializes in creating comprehensive wealth management plans for high net worth individuals and families, as well as select institutions, including foundations and endowments. PWM Teams work one-on-one with clients to advise and deliver customized strategies drawn from our deep investment experience, diverse wealth management capabilities and global reach. Our Private Wealth Advisors (PWAs) deliver an unparalleled investment platform inclusive of the full product and service offerings of Goldman Sachs and beyond, which may include tax and estate planning, philanthropic planning and private banking and lending services. As client needs evolve, this close relationship allows the team to offer complementary services and new opportunities by leveraging the resources of Goldman Sachs and beyond. Goldman Sachs Family Office (GSFO) works in coordination with Private Wealth Advisors to develop strong ties with clients to deliver a full suite of wealth planning and family office services. HOW YOU WILL FULFILL YOUR POTENTIAL Serve as a wealth-planning resource for the firm’s most sophisticated client situations, including those involving substantial private-business interests, complex ownership structures, concentrated assets, private investments, leverage, philanthropy, and multigenerational wealth-transfer goals. Act as a subject matter expert, assisting advisors in building maintaining complex, nested-entity balance sheets that consolidate assets, liabilities, ownership interests and cash flows Assist advisors in developing advanced, dynamic cash-flow models incorporating investment income, operating business distributions, liquidity events, capital calls and distributions, debt service, taxes, lifestyle spending, philanthropy, insurance, and planned wealth transfers. Assist advisors in developing advanced tax-efficient wealth transfer analysis — including the optimization of irrevocable trusts, lifetime gifting programs, and pre-transaction planning. Participate in client meetings to explain planning analyses, assumptions, scenarios, and trade-offs in a thoughtful and client-appropriate manner. Serve as an escalation point for the most technically complex client situations and advisor requests; assisting advisors in building models while also advancing their own skills Identify recurring advisor needs and work with Product and Engineering to translate them into enhancements to tools, processes, data infrastructure, and reporting capabilities. Train and mentor Private Wealth Advisors, analysts, associates, and planning professionals on the interpretation and application of advanced financial models.

As a Sr. Financial Analyst, Stores Finance – Store Allocation & Workload , you will lead the governance, allocation, and financial management processes for Target Stores’ sales, payroll, and workload plans, along with providing analysis and insights to leaders across FP&A, Stores (Field & Store Operations), and other HQ partners. Own the planning, forecasting, governance, allocation, and performance management of Target Stores’ monthly and annual sales, payroll, and workload plans, translating enterprise financial goals into actionable store-level plans. Partner with headquarters and field teams, including Store Operations, HR P&D, and broader FP&A, to shape strategies and establish financial goals and forecasts for store workload & payroll. Drive financial results and accountability across partner teams through effective reporting, tools, and actionable insights. Monitor business performance, respond to emerging needs, and elevate financial risks and opportunities to senior leadership. Apply curiosity, initiative, and collaboration to solve complex business challenges and pursue opportunities that position stores for success while maintaining financial discipline. Drive Target’s monthly store sales & payroll planning processes, leveraging workload trends and business insights to effectively allocate to the right stores You will gain direct knowledge of the people, process, and technology required to deliver $100B+ of annual sales through Target’s stores while delivering on the exceptional guest experience that Target is known for. A successful candidate will have a strong data and analytics background, be a connector that is comfortable working with a diverse set of business partners, be highly organized and accountable, and be naturally curious to uncover areas of process opportunity and expense savings.

At Freddie Mac, our mission of Making Home Possible is what motivates us, and it’s at the core of everything we do. Since our charter in 1970, we have made home possible for more than 90 million families across the country. Join an organization where your work contributes to a greater purpose. Position Overview: Join our dynamic Multifamily Asset Management Guarantor & External Relations team as a pivotal player, where your primary mission will be to evaluate and enhance the performance of our securitized portfolio of multifamily properties. Your role will involve assessing the credit quality and performance of commercial real estate assets, aiming to maximize recovery for investors and deliver outstanding service to borrowers, all while adhering to government regulations and company policies. You'll keep a keen eye on portfolio performance by analyzing Watchlist, delinquency, and maturing loan reports, and you'll effectively communicate your insights to both internal and external stakeholders. Apply now and support our mission to #makehomepossible! Our Impact: Our team is at the forefront of ensuring the robust performance of securitized multifamily loans, safeguarding the interests of investors and Freddie Mac. We delve into data analysis across our global portfolio to deliver critical intelligence to our internal stakeholders, enhancing the performance of multifamily assets. We also spearhead special projects and produce insightful ad hoc reports. Your Impact: Conduct thorough assessments of loan-level risk by analyzing operating statements, rent rolls, and servicer property inspections. Vigilantly monitor portfolio performance through detailed reviews of delinquency reports, maturing loan data, and other vital sources. Offer astute analysis of trends, whether at the individual property level or across the global portfolio. Contribute to the development of comprehensive reports related to property performance. Scrutinize monthly statements to ensure guarantor payments are accurate and subordination levels are maintained. Engage in special projects that challenge and expand your expertise. Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience, preferred. 0-2 years of relevant experience in the real estate, banking, or finance industry. In-depth knowledge of real estate property fundamentals. Proven ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously. Advanced proficiency in MS Excel, including creating macros, tables, and charts, required Experience with MS PowerPoint and MS Word. Keys to Success in this Role: Be a collaborative team player, adept at building and nurturing relationships with both internal and external stakeholders. Excel in multi-tasking, prioritization, and adaptability. Maintain a meticulous attention to detail and organizational prowess.

Revolution Medicines is a global, commercial-state oncology company dedicated to discovering, developing and delivering innovative medicines for patients with RAS-addicted cancers. Leveraging its differentiated RAS(ON) tri-complex inhibitor platform, the company is advancing a broad, integrated portfolio of oral RAS(ON) inhibitors designed to directly target the active, cancer-driving state of RAS. Founded on rigorous scientific inquiry and a willingness to challenge long-held assumptions, Revolution Medicines is committed to changing the trajectory of disease for patients with RAS-addicted cancers worldwide. Our people are united by a shared way of working: follow the science, challenge assumptions, act with urgency and hold ourselves to a high standard of rigor—all in service of patients. The Opportunity: We are seeking a trademark and copyright attorney to join our IP team. In this newly created role, you will share responsibility for RevMed’s expanding global trademarks and domains practice, spanning clearance, prosecution, portfolio management, and enforcement. Execute global trademark strategy; determine trademark filing, prosecution and portfolio maintenance priorities in alignment with commercial objectives, and direct outside counsel to implement those decisions. Required Skills, Experience and Education: 6-10 years of relevant legal experience at a law firm and/or in an in-house role. J.D. degree from an ABA accredited law school and a member in good standing of the California Bar, or eligible for in-house registration in California. Admitted to practice before the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office. Demonstrable experience in global trademark portfolio management and enforcement. Strong organizational and time-management skills, with the ability to prioritize and manage multiple time-sensitive projects simultaneously. Ability to multi-task, prioritize options, anticipate challenges and execute on goals as a member of an interdisciplinary team is extremely important. Collaborative, flexible, positive and pragmatic work style with excellent written and verbal communication skills; able to simplify the complex and adapt to audiences with varying levels of legal fluency. Thrives in and enjoys a creative and collaborative team setting, and driven by a desire to be innovative in a high energy environment. Demonstrates flexibility, adaptability, and a willingness to contribute beyond the core responsibilities of a role. Preferred Skills: Experience working in a multinational corporation or commercial-stage startup. Experience advising biotech or pharma companies strongly preferred. Undergraduate or advanced degree in a life sciences discipline.

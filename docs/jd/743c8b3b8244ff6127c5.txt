AECOM's Adaptive Program Management Technologies team lives at the intersection of construction program management, digital transformation, and emerging technologies including data analytics and AI-enabled solutions. We are looking for talented candidates from construction management, civil engineering, computer science, information systems, and related disciplines who are interested in applying technology to improve program and project delivery. AECOM is seeking an Entry-Level Digital Solutions Business Analyst for Project Management Information Systems (PMIS) to support the Adaptive Program Management Technologies team. This early career position provides an opportunity to develop business analysis skills, gain experience with a wide range of PMIS platforms, and participate in the full software development lifecycle for custom digital solutions. This position offers flexible work arrangements and may be performed remotely, hybrid, or on-site at any AECOM office. The successful candidate will support our growing portfolio of projects that deliver technology solutions for clients managing capital improvement programs. Working alongside senior team members, the Business Analyst I will contribute to the deployment of integrated solutions that address complex program and project management challenges. Solutions may include commercial-off-the-shelf applications, custom software developed by AECOM, AI-enabled capabilities, or integrated combinations of these technologies. This role will provide exposure to the implementation and configuration of leading PMIS platforms such as e-Builder, Kahua, Procore, InEight, and other emerging technologies. The Business Analyst I will help bridge the gap between business needs and technology solutions by translating client requirements into system configurations, process workflows, and functional requirements. Under the guidance of senior team members, they will apply business analysis techniques and critical thinking to evaluate existing processes, identify opportunities for improvement, and support solution design and implementation. The Business Analyst will participate throughout the solution delivery lifecycle, including discovery, requirements gathering, design, testing, implementation, training, and rollout activities. Projects vary in size, complexity, and technology platforms, providing significant opportunities for professional growth and career development.

Technology Business Analyst
Guidehouse
Location: US - VA, McLean; US - DC, Washington
Link: https://guidehouse.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/External/job/US---VA-McLean/Technology-Business-Analyst_44521

Job Family : Systems Analysis (SYS) Travel Required : Up to 10% Clearance Required : Ability to Obtain Public Trust What You Will Do: Partner with business and technology teams to understand needs, define priorities, and translate them into clear requirements. Analyze business processes, systems, data flows, and pain points to identify opportunities for improvement. Create user stories, functional requirements, process maps, workflow diagrams, and acceptance criteria. Facilitate discovery sessions, stakeholder interviews, requirements workshops, and solution reviews. Work closely with product managers, engineers, designers, QA, and project managers throughout delivery. Support testing by developing test scenarios, validating results, and coordinating issue resolution. Identify risks, dependencies, and opportunities to improve technology solutions and business outcomes. Communicate complex technical concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences. What You Will Need: THREE (3+) years of experience as a technology business analyst, systems analyst, product analyst, or in a similar role. Experience gathering, documenting, and managing business and technical requirements. Strong understanding of software development lifecycles, Agile practices, and product delivery. Ability to analyze complex problems and turn them into practical, well-defined solutions. Experience creating process flows, data flows, user stories, use cases, and functional specifications. Strong communication, facilitation, organization, and stakeholder management skills. Comfort working across multiple teams and managing competing priorities. Experience with tools such as Google Workspace, Jira, Confluence, Azure DevOps, Visio, or similar platforms. What Would Be Nice To Have: Experience working with APIs, integrations, cloud platforms, databases, or enterprise systems. Familiarity with data analysis, reporting, or business intelligence tools. Experience in a technology, SaaS (e.g., Salesforce, ServiceNow, Appian), federal government, or other highly regulated environment. Knowledge of product management, UX research, service design, or change management practices. Experience supporting system implementations, process automation, or digital transformation initiatives. Business analysis certification such as CBAP, CCBA, or PMI-PBA. Experience working with distributed or cross-functional teams. Experience or knowledge of Data Accountability Broker Submission (DABS) and Financial Assistance Broker Submission (FABS) functions.

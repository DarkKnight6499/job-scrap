Responsibilities The Medical Professional Allied Healthcare Enterprises Leader is responsible for the strategic leadership, underwriting management, and profitable growth of Berkley Healthcare's Medical Professional Liability portfolio, including Senior Care, Physician, and Medical Facilities business. Working collaboratively with Actuarial, Product Development, Claims, and senior leadership, this role develops underwriting and product strategies, establishes portfolio management standards, and drives profitable growth initiatives. The position provides technical and people leadership while overseeing the underwriting and management of complex Medical Professional Liability risks. Key functions include but are not limited to the following: - Lead the strategic development, management, and profitable growth of Berkley Healthcare's Medical Professional Allied Healthcare portfolio, including Senior Care, Physician, and Medical Facilities business segments. - Develop and implement underwriting and product strategies for products in the Allied Healthcare segment, including underwriting appetite, guidelines, pricing frameworks, policy forms, distribution strategies, and growth objectives. - Partner with Actuarial, Product Development, Claims, and senior leadership to establish and maintain rates, underwriting authority, risk appetite, underwriting guidelines, and portfolio management strategies. - Provide technical underwriting leadership for complex and specialized Medical Professional Allied Healthcare risks, including risk selection, pricing, coverage determinations, and referral authority decisions. - Monitor portfolio performance and implement strategic actions to achieve growth, profitability, retention, and loss ratio objectives. - Establish and strengthen key broker, customer, and distribution partner relationships to support business development and market expansion objectives. - Develop, train, and lead underwriting staff while fostering a culture of technical excellence, accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement. - Maintain expertise in healthcare, insurance, legal, regulatory, and industry developments to identify emerging risks, market opportunities, and product enhancements. - Provide leadership and oversight for the Allied Healthcare segments including, Medical Facilities, Senior Care and Physician underwriting portfolios, including underwriting performance, talent development, and execution of business strategy. Qualifications Education - BA/BS/BBA degree from an accredited university or college. Qualifications - Minimum 12-15 years of progressive medical professional liability underwriting experience, including leadership responsibility. - Demonstrated leadership experience managing underwriting professionals and driving portfolio performance. - Comprehensive knowledge of medical professional products, including coverages, policy forms, and industry standards. - Experience developing or enhancing insurance products, underwriting guidelines, pricing strategies, or policy forms. - Strong customer service orientation, with a commitment to delivering high-quality client experience. - Demonstrated ability to develop and execute strategic initiatives that drive profitable growth. - Self-motivated and independent, while remaining accountable to team objectives and company goals. - Demonstrated ability to take calculated risks and apply sound business judgment in underwriting decisions. - Willingness to travel occasionally, less than 25 percent, as required by the role. - The Company is an equal employment opportunity employer.

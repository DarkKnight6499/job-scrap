Portfolio Specialist I
United Community Bank
Location: Lawrenceville, GA, US
Link: https://careers-ucbi.icims.com/jobs/2193/portfolio-specialist-i/job

Overview A Portfolio Specialist I provides credit and sales support to Commercial Banking Relationship Managers. What You’ll Do Work with RMs to manage all aspects of the local loan portfolio including annual reviews, past dues, borrowing base monitoring, covenant compliance, CARs, collateral exceptions, and other portfolio management activities Actively participate and assist in all aspects of the loan process including joint calling, underwriting, and preparation of credit approval packages Coordinate with Credit Analysts in the preparation of LARs, credit memos, financial spreads, and related documents Serve as secondary contact on client relationships Identify cross-sell opportunities with new and existing clients Prepare commitment letters and review loan documents Assist lenders in prospecting efforts Spread, analyze, and interpret complex business and personal financial statements and tax returns Analyze relationships, clients, and prospects globally, not just on individual requests Identify strengths and weaknesses of individual requests and make recommendations to address and mitigate perceived risks Prepare and present credit analysis packages through the credit approval chain with limited supervision Requirements For Success Bachelor’s Degree with course work in finance and accounting preferred 1 - 2 years of work experience related to various aspects of commercial lending preferred Possess a general knowledge of credit policy, banking regulations, and loan documentation issues Possess knowledge of various loan structures and have the ability to apply this knowledge in the formulation of sound credit recommendations Excellent interpersonal skills Excellent communication skills; both verbal and written, one-on-one and in groups Ability to develop and maintain a cohesive and cooperative working relationship with co-workers Good personal organization and time management skills Understand and work effectively within the credit culture of the company Must allocate time effectively and independently prioritize work load to meet timelines Ability to work with in-house financial reporting applications, including Navigator Participate in all required compliance training, including Bank Secrecy Act/anti-money laundering training, as well as internal and external training programs, online training, meetings, and seminars/conferences, etc Conditions of Employment Must able to pass background and credit check. FLSA Status: Exempt SUPERVISORY RESPONSIBILITY This position has no direct reports.

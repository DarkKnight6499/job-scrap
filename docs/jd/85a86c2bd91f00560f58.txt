Key Exciting Responsibilities Partner directly with global customer leadership to translate operational metrics into high-impact financial strategies. Build advanced Business Intelligence and predictive models to evaluate the financial impact of self-service automation and customer support economics. Spearhead global monthly and quarterly financial forecasting alongside annual budgeting cycles. Execute monthly close activities, conduct detailed variance analysis, and ensure absolute spend tracking accuracy. Leverage modern generative AI tools to accelerate data analysis, automate routine workflows, and solve complex financial challenges. Essential Skills & Experience Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Computer Science, or a related field. 3 to 5 years of experience in corporate finance or FP&A. Strong financial modeling skills in Excel and Google Sheets with exceptional attention to data integrity and variance analysis. Practical experience using generative AI tools for workflow automation and data analysis. Proven ability to act as a systems thinker, communicating complex financial insights to non-financial stakeholders. Bonus Points For Experience using AI Tools: Gemini or Claude Experience within a fast-growing SaaS, HaaS or enterprise technology environment. Familiarity with Customer Experience (CX) metrics and unit economics. Hands-on exposure to enterprise BI and EPM tools like Domo or Adaptive Planning.

SUMMARY OF THE ROLE The Sr. Analyst, Portfolio Optimization supports the optimization and valuation of an LNG portfolio by analyzing supply and sales contracts, shipping assets, and market opportunities to maximize portfolio value. The role partners closely with commercial, trading, and operations teams to evaluate cargo allocation, shipping strategies, contract optionality, and new business opportunities, while providing scenario analysis, risk assessments, and actionable insights that support commercial decision-making and profitability. Candidates should have LNG industry experience, strong analytical and optimization skills, and the ability to translate complex quantitative results into clear business recommendations. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES Portfolio Optimization & Analytics Run LNG portfolio optimization model, including: Long‑term supply and sales contracts (FOB, DES, SPA optionality) LNG assets (liquefaction, shipping) Market access, pricing indices, and temporal flexibility Optimize decisions across: Cargo allocation and destination selection Contract nominations, deferrals, and diversions Shipping deployment and fleet utilization Quantify and value contractual and operational optionality embedded in the portfolio Commercial & Trading Support Support LNG commercial teams with: Portfolio‑level pricing and valuation Incremental value analysis for new deals and amendments Scenario planning and stress testing under price, demand, freight, and operational scenarios Work closely with trading, shipping, and commercial operations teams to understand value drivers and constraints, and proactively provide forward‑looking insights on portfolio exposure, value drivers, and trade‑offs Evaluate portfolio impacts of: New assets or contracts Market dislocations and basis shifts Regulatory or operational constraints Translate complex optimization results into actionable commercial recommendations EDUCATION, SKILLS, EXPERIENCE, & COMPETENCIES Basic Requirements: Strong understanding of LNG value chains, including contracts, shipping, and physical operations 5+ years in LNG portfolio optimization, LNG shipping, trading support, or analytics Ability to communicate complex results clearly to non‑technical stakeholders Familiarity with risk metrics, scenario analysis, and valuation frameworks Degree in operations research, applied mathematics, economics, or similar.

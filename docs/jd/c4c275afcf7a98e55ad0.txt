About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking an Operational Accounting Associate Manager within Loss and Recovery Operations , part of Fraud and Claims Management Operations. The team supports critical recovery accounting activities through accurate financial processing, reconciliations, reporting, and operational controls, while identifying opportunities to strengthen processes, mitigate risk, and improve operational effectiveness. Learn more about the career areas and lines of business at wellsfargojobs.com . The Operational Accounting Associate Manager will lead a production-focused team and oversee day-to-day operations, with accountability for operational performance, controls, risk management, and team member development. This leader will also drive process improvement and transformation efforts, partner with Technology and key business stakeholders on system and process enhancements, and use reporting and performance metrics to identify trends, resolve issues, and strengthen the overall control environment.

The Market Risk Technology Business Analyst connects business stakeholders and technology teams, turning complex market risk requirements into practical, scalable solutions. Sitting within Citi's Market Risk function, this role plays a direct part in how regulatory frameworks such as FRTB are implemented across trading and risk systems. The right candidate will translate market risk concepts into clear, actionable requirements that shape business planning and technology delivery. Responsibilities Act as the Market Risk Technology Business Analyst, bridging business users and technologists on complex market risk projects. Define system scope and objectives for market risk initiatives, including FRTB SA and IMA implementation, to align technology delivery with regulatory requirements. Evaluate business processes and industry standards to resolve complex system issues and recommend practical solutions. Manage system change processes from initial requirements through to implementation, providing analytical input at every stage. Work with business stakeholders to define functional specifications and support day-to-day operational needs. Identify risks and technology-related impacts, and communicate findings clearly to both business and technical audiences. Assess risk in business decisions to safeguard Citi's regulatory compliance and reputation. Operate with a high degree of independence, exercising sound judgment on project priorities. Required Qualifications & Skills 10 or more years of experience in investment banking or capital markets, with deep exposure to market risk technology. Hands-on experience implementing FRTB SA and IMA regulatory rules. Subject matter expertise in financial products and market risk concepts. Practical experience with use case methodologies and artifact development. Strong data analysis skills and intermediate to advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office, flowcharting, and Visio. Clear and concise communication skills combined with strong interpersonal and diplomatic ability, self-motivation, and the capacity to manage multiple priorities under pressure with attention to detail. Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience. Beneficial Skills & Qualifications Master's degree or MBA with a finance specialization. Professional certification such as CFA or FRM. What We Offer Join a team where your market risk expertise directly shapes how Citi manages regulatory change and technology delivery, with the autonomy to make a real impact and the support to keep growing. Shape the technology response to global regulatory requirements such as FRTB, working alongside experienced market risk professionals. Work in a hybrid model with three days in the office and two days working remotely each week. Access structured learning and development opportunities to build on your market risk and technology expertise. Collaborate with teams across Citi's global markets business, gaining exposure to a wide range of projects and systems. Benefit from wellbeing, financial planning, and family support programs designed to support your life outside work.

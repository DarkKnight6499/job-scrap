Job Description Summary The AVP – FP&A supports financial planning and analysis activities, including forecasting, budgeting, reporting, strategic projects, and business performance insights. This role provides analytics, reporting, and P&L development for shared services leaders. The role offers the opportunity to work with executives and the broader finance organization to drive business performance, shape financial plans, and optimize organizational structures in support of strategic goals. What You’ll Do Partner across the organization to build new processes and enhance financial reporting capabilities. Own high visibility projects focused on profitability analysis, cost attribution, P&L creation and development and more. Coordinate and manage forecast and budget processes for shared service groups, subsidiaries and business lines. Deliver accurate and timely financial reporting and analysis to business leaders; identify trends, risks, and opportunities. Support the holding company and intercompany financial analysis and serve as a liaison with other functional teams. Assist in developing and evaluating strategic initiatives, including financial modeling and due diligence for new investments or business opportunities. Provide analytical support on cross-functional projects and ad hoc initiatives. Utilize tools such as Workiva, Power BI, Workday and Adaptive Planning to support reporting and analysis. Qualifications +7 years of relevant financial experience, ideally in FP&A, corporate finance, controllership or consulting. Experience with intercompany financials, annual budgeting, working with large data sets, and KPI-driven forecasting is preferred. Experience with Workday, Adaptive Planning, OneStream, or other reporting and forecasting tools is desired. Experience building financial models for product revenue, expense drivers, and long-term project costs in support of executive decision-making and strategic objectives. Experience partnering with procurement, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and controllership teams. Proven ability to collaborate effectively with business leaders and cross-functional partners. Comfortable working independently while exercising excellent judgement and problem-solving skills in a fast-paced and dynamic environment. Advanced Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint skills.

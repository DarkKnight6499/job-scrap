Position Information Hiring Manager: Director Department: IPG - COO Department Overview The Investor Partners Group (“IPG”) is a ~175-person department dedicated to developing and nurturing investor client relationships. IPG integrates new business development, client service, product specialists and product development to create a Gold Standard investor client experience. Position Responsibilities This individual will assist the IPG COO Team, Co-Presidents and senior business leaders with key initiatives, business challenges, cross-functional priorities and expanding business capabilities. Responsibilities include, but are not limited to: Supporting project leaders in the execution of critical initiatives; assisting with establishing governance and supervisory frameworks in partnership with Global Product Group, Sales and Planning & Operations Teams Supporting the development of business plans, budget forecasts and resourcing plans including quarterly, annual and 3-year plans Working with the IPG COO Team to ensure all projects are clearly defined, prioritized and completed on time, on budget and with excellent quality Preparing key financial analyses and reporting in partnership with the Strategic Accounting & Finance Team Assisting with preparation of presentations, analyses and recommendations for senior management Managing project plans, tracking milestones and supporting execution of key initiatives and expansion efforts Helping to drive process improvement through AI-enabled solutions Providing leverage to the IPG COO Team and sub-departments Becoming an expert on Golub Capital and the alternative asset management industry Keeping abreast of industry best practices and how those might be implemented at Golub Capital Candidate Requirements Qualifications & Experience: Bachelor’s degree required; MBA and / or CFA a plus 4+ years of experience at a top-tier investment bank, investment management firm or consulting firm Financial services and / or asset management industry experience is required Strong Excel and PowerPoint skills are required Ability to synthesize and present complex information in a clear, concise and compelling manner Strong project management skills and ability to multitask Adept at interacting with senior executives, department heads and external partners Demonstrated experience in working with high-performing teams; ability to establish relationships and work effectively with a variety of business partners Ability to maintain exceptional attention to detail while managing multiple responsibilities in a fast-paced environment Highly motivated with the ability to work independently, set priorities and meet aggressive deadlines Excellent communication, presentation and interpersonal skills Demonstrated ability to effectively leverage AI tools in day-to-day work, with strong critical thinking to assess accuracy, limitations and appropriate use of AI generated outputs Enthusiastic about working in office and creating a Gold Standard hybrid work culture Critical Competencies for Success Our Gold Standards define key behaviors and competencies across 4 dimensions: Leadership, Achieving Results, Personal Effectiveness and Thinking Critically. These behaviors and competencies drive our ability to win together. Leadership: Role models in this area consistently focus on the right goals and priorities and continually develop themselves and others. Always team players, they influence and engage with others to contribute to a supportive and inclusive culture where all feel welcome. Achieving Results: Role models in this area are high achievers who develop careful plans and deliver consistently and effectively. They hold themselves and others accountable for delivering high quality results, and they remove barriers to ensure others can contribute and grow. Personal Effectiveness: Role models in this area build strong relationships, treat others with respect and communicate effectively. They are driven to exceed expectations and are adaptable to changing circumstances. Thinking Critically: Role models in this area understand our business, rely on analytical reasoning and seek diverse perspectives to solve problems. They are forward thinking, anticipating issues and addressing them in advance. The department-specific competencies define the knowledge, skills and abilities that are needed to successfully perform the functional or technical work of this role. Industry Knowledge: Demonstrates an understanding of the Firm’s position in the industry, specifically with regard to direct lending and asset management, as well as the competitive landscape. Analytics: Surfaces, sources, analyzes and interprets data to identify trends and inform strategy and business decisions. Data and Content Stewardship: Follows appropriate policies and procedures to enter and manage data and content, including evaluating issues with data and content and taking appropriate steps to address them. Relationship Management: Creates ways to engage others, cultivate trust and build long-term win-win partnerships with investors, business partners and internal teams.

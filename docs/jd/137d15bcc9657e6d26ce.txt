Job Summary This entry level role provides technical data, reporting, application and process support across Land. The position strengthens the Land Core technical team by supporting Land data queries and reporting in Snowflake while helping expand the practical use of AI, automation and modern data tools across Land applications and workflows. The role works closely with experienced Land Business Analysts, Land functional teams, Accounting, IT, and adjacent teams to translate business questions into reliable data outputs and technology-enabled solutions. Job Duties & Responsibilities Support Land data requests by gathering, validating, analyzing and presenting data from Snowflake and other approved enterprise data sources Develop and maintain SQL queries, recurring reports, data extracts and quality-control routines Document important Land data structures, query logic, business rules and reporting dependencies Research discrepancies across Land source systems, Snowflake and downstream reporting and coordinate resolution Assist with Land data cleansing, reconciliation, conversions and system implementation activities Build and maintain

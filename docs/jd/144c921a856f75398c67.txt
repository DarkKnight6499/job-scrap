The Opportunity This position is based out of our Princeton, NJ location within the APOC Point of Care division. In this role, you'll be part of the Abbott Point of Care Finance team, supporting our growing US Commercial business. You will serve as a trusted finance partner to US Commercial leadership, helping drive fact-based decision making, develop accurate P&L forecasts, and provide insights that help solve business challenges and drive business performance. This position will manage one Financial Analyst and help establish a best-in-class FP&A function to support our expanded US Commercial organization. In addition to leading financial planning and analysis activities, you will oversee select accounting and month-end close responsibilities related to revenue recognition.

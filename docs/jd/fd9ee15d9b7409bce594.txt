Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all . Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever. We are a high-impact team driving Micron’s investment strategy and AI initiatives at the intersection of technology, venture capital, and the semiconductor ecosystem. We partner closely with business and engineering leaders to identify emerging opportunities, shape strategy, and translate innovation into real outcomes. If you’re excited about influencing where AI and infrastructure are headed, this is a seat at the table! This role sits at the core of our venture and AI strategy efforts. You will source and evaluate investments, guide strategic initiatives, and connect insights from the AI ecosystem to executive decision-making. It is both analytical and highly collaborative, requiring strong judgment, technical fluency, and the ability to influence senior stakeholders.

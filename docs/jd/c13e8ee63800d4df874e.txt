We are seeking a highly motivated professional to join our Hedging Product Control team within the Treasury/Chief Investment Office (TCIO). As a Product Control Senior Associate you will work closely with multiple teams across front office, middle office, technology and policy to support the firm’s structural interest rate risk management hedging activities.

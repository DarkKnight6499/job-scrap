As a Portfolio Strategy & Transformation Leader, you will lead the Consumer Markets Division’s (CMD) Portfolio and Program Management Office (PPMO) and play a critical role in translating strategy into disciplined execution and measurable business outcomes. You will oversee a complex portfolio spanning strategic transformation, business-critical initiatives, AI-enabled programs, and ongoing investments that support the strength and evolution of Pacific Life’s life insurance and annuity businesses. This is an opportunity to lead at the center of one of Pacific Life’s most established and consequential businesses. The PPMO is where the division’s strategic priorities become actionable programs, where investments are prioritized and resourced, and where diverse teams come together to deliver meaningful outcomes for customers and distribution partners. As a member of the Strategic Management & Services leadership team, you will serve as a trusted advisor to senior business and technology leaders, bring transparency to complex decisions, and help shape how Pacific Life operates in an increasingly connected enterprise environment. You will lead an established organization of approximately 35 portfolio, program, project, and agile professionals, including five to six direct reports, while building the capabilities required to support the division’s future growth and transformation.

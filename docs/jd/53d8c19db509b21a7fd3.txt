Job Description: Position Overview Terex Corporation is a global manufacturer of lifting and material processing products and services that deliver lifecycle solutions to maximize customer return on investment. The Company reports in four business segments: Environmental Services Group, Specialty Vehicles, Aerial Work Platforms, and Materials Processing. Terex delivers lifecycle solutions to a broad range of industries, including the construction, infrastructure, manufacturing, shipping, transportation, refining, energy, utility, quarrying and mining industries. Terex offers financial products and services to assist in the acquisition of Terex equipment through Terex Financial Services. The Business Analyst will serve as a liaison between the Terex Specialty Vehicles E-One Business Unit and the IT organization in order to provide technical and data analytical solutions to meet business needs. The ideal candidate will have expertise in Information Technology, Manufacturing Processes and Business Administration for the role of Sr. Business Analyst. IT business analysts are responsible for enhancing the quality of ERP, IT products and services, analyzing data to inform business decisions, and finding technological solutions to business needs, among other duties. Responsibilities: Liaising between the E-One BU and the IT department. Acting as an information source and communicator between IT org and the BU. ERP discovery and process mapping. Planning and coordinating User Acceptance Testing, and performing end to end regression testing. Understanding strategic business needs and plans for growth. Enhancing the quality of Erp implementation, IT products and services. Utilizing IT data for business insights. Analyzing data to drive business decisions. Promoting the use of AI in the BU to enhance and optimize business processes and reporting. Analyzing business needs. Finding technological solutions to business requirements. Sourcing and evaluating new business technology. Producing reports for IT and supplier analytics. Experience and Skills Degree qualified, preferably specializing in Bachelor's and College Degree in Finance, Business, Business/Management, Business/Administration, Marketing, Computer Science, Economics, Accounting, Engineering, Management or equivalent experience Experienced in leading ERP systems such as Oracle R12, D365 and Baan. Experienced in manufacturing processes for supply chain, production planning, inventory, quality control and after-market services. Minimum of 15 years of business systems analysis experience reflecting increasing levels of responsibility. Ability to work across legacy and modern systems to deliver business success. Excellent problem-solving, presentation and listening skills Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Skilled at presenting technical information in business language. Attention to detail. Action and results oriented Ability to work well with people from many different cultures and disciplines with varying degrees of technical experience over geographically dispersed locations Willingness to take ownership of issues and ensure all interested parties are kept informed of progress in order to deliver solutions Technical Requirements Required: Highly skilled and experienced in Baan, D365 and Oracle R12 ERPs, Excel, PowerPoint and Project. Required: Good understanding of AI. Required: System integration experience. Integrating ERPs with payment, HR and other 3rd party systems. Additional skills: SQL, Azure DevOps, Access.

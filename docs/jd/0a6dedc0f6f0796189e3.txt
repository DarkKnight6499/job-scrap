Position Description PIMCO is seeking a Product Analyst or Associate to join the Product Strategy Group, focusing on multi-sector fixed income strategies. The Product Strategy Group serves as a bridge between investment expertise, business strategy, and client engagement across a broad range of PIMCO investment strategies. In this role, you will gain exposure to financial markets, product development, portfolio analysis, and client communication while partnering with colleagues across Portfolio Management, Client Management, Legal & Compliance, and other areas of the firm. This is an early-career opportunity designed for candidates with a strong interest in investment management and, ideally, 1–2 years of relevant industry experience. The role is specifically focused on coverage of multi-sector fixed income products. Responsibilities include supporting sales and marketing initiatives, helping to build and maintain strategy-specific collateral, conducting portfolio analysis and commentary, tracking market and competitor trends, and contributing to clear client communication. Through this experience, Analysts/Associates develop a deep understanding of PIMCO’s platform, investment capabilities, and client needs while building a strong foundation for long-term career growth. Responsibilities In this role, you will: Build deep command of PIMCO's investment process, portfolio positioning, and market outlook — enough to become the most credible advocate in the room for the strategies you cover Represent the investment team internally: be a highly valued resource for Account Managers and Client Management on your assigned strategies, while building exposure across PIMCO's full platform Own, alongside Portfolio Management and Marketing, a consistent suite of product and client-service materials, and actively seek out feedback to ensure ongoing quality and relevance Develop a strong understanding of business, stakeholder and client needs and articulate business value and product intent to internal leadership. Track the competition and bring a point of view to how we grow the platform Apply and help develop AI-powered tools to improve the productivity and quality of recurring workflows Construct attribution, performance, and risk reporting that shows how portfolios are positioned, and why they performed the way they did Write market updates, portfolio commentary, and portfolio construction materials that clearly communicate PIMCO's investment views Partner on RFPs and DDQs, drafting and reviewing content that represents our strategies accurately Qualifications Bachelor's degree, preferably in Finance, Economics, Accounting, Mathematics, Engineering, applied science, or another related discipline Zero to two years of relevant experience; internship experience in investment management or alternatives is preferred Strong mathematical, quantitative, and analytical abilities, with a detail-oriented skill set Excellent written and verbal communication skills Proficiency with Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint; reasonable proficiency with Bloomberg preferred Demonstrated curiosity and experience using new and emerging technologies and AI tools to solve problems and drive better outcomes Strong time management and organizational skills, with the ability to multi-task in a fast-paced environment Intellectual curiosity, high energy, and a self-starter mindset Ability to work effectively and professionally with all levels of personnel both internally and externally; strong team player Enthusiasm for the financial markets, investing, and macroeconomics Interest in pursuing the CFA designation (or active status in the program) a plus PIMCO follows a total compensation approach when rewarding employees which includes a base salary and a discretionary bonus.

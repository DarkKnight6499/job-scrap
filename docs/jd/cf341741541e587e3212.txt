Job Description We aspire to be the premier research-intensive biopharmaceutical company. At our company, we are at the forefront of research to deliver innovative health solutions that advance the prevention and treatment of diseases in people and animals. We use the power of leading-edge science to save and improve lives around the world. Join us in our mission to promote transparency, accountability, and ethical use of data and AI. The Associate Director, Governance Risk and Compliance for Data, Data Products and Digital Solutions will lead the development and implementation of governance, compliance, privacy, and responsible AI practices across data, data products, and digital solutions. This role partners with cross-functional teams to embed practical controls and standards throughout the data and AI lifecycle, ensuring solutions are developed and used in alignment with company policies, enterprise standards, and applicable regulations. The position supports the operationalization of data governance frameworks, AI stewardship, privacy-by-design principles, and risk management processes that promote transparency, accountability, and ethical use of data and AI. The ideal candidate brings strong experience in data governance, compliance, privacy, and/or AI governance within a complex or regulated environment. This individual will help develop policies, standards, guidance, and training while collaborating with technical and business stakeholders to drive adoption and continuous improvement. The role requires strong communication, influence, and problem-solving skills, along with the ability to translate governance requirements into practical, scalable solutions that support innovation while managing risk. Responsibilities Lead governance and compliance practices for data, data products, and digital solutions across the lifecycle, from design through deployment and retirement. Develop and maintain policies, standards, guidance, and controls that support consistent, ethical, and compliant use of data and AI-enabled solutions. Partner with business, technical, privacy, legal, and compliance stakeholders to embed governance requirements into delivery processes and decision-making. Support responsible AI practices by ensuring transparency, accountability, documentation, and appropriate oversight of AI use cases and outputs. Assess governance, privacy, and compliance risks; track issues and remediation actions; and support continuous improvement of controls and processes. Provide guidance, training, and enablement to teams to promote awareness of governance expectations and strengthen adoption. Support data quality, stewardship, access, metadata, and documentation practices to improve trust, usability, and control of data assets. Contribute to governance reporting, audit readiness, and enterprise risk management efforts related to data, products, solutions, and AI.

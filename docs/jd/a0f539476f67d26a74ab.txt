Your Role The Senior Risk Analyst, Investment Risk Strategy & Execution will support the planning, coordination, and execution of strategic initiatives across Investment Risk. Working closely with senior leaders and business partners, the role will help drive successful delivery of key programs by providing structured problem solving, project management, stakeholder coordination, reporting, and process improvement support. The subject matter native to the global Investment Risk team is Invesco’s data environment, most notably how AI will be applied to Risk, Performance and Attribution information. Strong candidates should be “quant-aware”, with a base level of familiarity with data-oriented projects. The successful candidate will be comfortable operating across a variety of topics and initiatives, often balancing strategic thinking with hands-on execution. The role requires strong analytical and communication skills, the ability to build relationships across the organization, and the maturity to interact effectively with stakeholders at multiple levels of the business. This position offers significant exposure to senior leadership and provides an opportunity to develop expertise in asset management, business transformation, organizational effectiveness, and strategic program delivery. You Will Be Responsible For: Support the planning and execution of strategic initiatives across Investment Risk, helping drive progress against key business priorities. Lead workstreams and smaller projects, coordinating stakeholders, tracking milestones, managing dependencies, and ensuring timely delivery of objectives. Develop and maintain project plans, status reporting, dashboards, executive updates, governance materials, and other management reporting tools. Analyze business challenges, identify root causes, and develop practical recommendations to improve processes, operating models, and organizational effectiveness. Gather and synthesize information from multiple stakeholders to develop clear insights, recommendations, and action plans. Support governance forums, leadership meetings, and working groups by preparing materials, documenting decisions, tracking action items, and ensuring effective follow-through. Develop presentations, executive summaries, briefing materials, and other communications for senior leaders and key stakeholders. Partner with business, risk, operations, and technology teams to support delivery of transformation initiatives and operational improvement efforts. Identify opportunities to improve efficiency through process simplification, automation, reporting enhancements, and adoption of new capabilities. Build strong working relationships across Investment Risk and related business functions to drive collaboration, alignment, and successful execution. Support organizational change efforts by helping stakeholders understand objectives, progress, impacts, and expected outcomes. Ensure all activities are conducted in accordance with Invesco's Conduct Principles and risk management expectations. The Experience You Bring: 3–7 years of experience in management consulting, project management, business transformation, corporate strategy, financial services, asset management, banking, or a related field. Bachelor’s degree in business, Finance, Economics, Management, Engineering, or a related discipline. Demonstrated experience supporting strategic initiatives, transformation programs, operational improvement efforts, or complex cross-functional projects. Strong problem-solving and analytical capabilities, with the ability to structure ambiguous situations and develop actionable recommendations. Excellent written, verbal, and presentation communication skills, including the ability to tailor messaging for different audiences and levels of seniority. Strong interpersonal skills and relationship management capabilities, with the confidence and maturity to engage effectively with stakeholders across the organization. Experience creating executive-level presentations, dashboards, management reporting, and business communications. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft PowerPoint and Excel. Experience with Microsoft Copilot, Power BI, workflow automation tools, SQL, Python, or related technologies is preferred but not required. Demonstrated ability to manage multiple priorities, shift effectively between topics, and perform in a dynamic and evolving environment. Strong business judgment, situational awareness, and executive presence, with the ability to navigate complex stakeholder environments and read the room appropriately. Intellectual curiosity, high personal accountability, strong attention to detail, and a proactive approach to identifying and solving problems.

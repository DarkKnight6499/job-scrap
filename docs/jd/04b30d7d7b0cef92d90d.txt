Join our Corporate Functions Finance team within the broader Corporate Finance & Treasury department, where you will play a key role in providing financial insights and supporting strategic decision-making. As a Senior Financial Analyst, you will collaborate with Senior Leaders across multiple Corporate Functions, serving as a trusted finance partner and reporting to the Senior Manager, Corporate Functions Finance. Key Responsibilities: Lead corporate functions budgeting and forecasting processes, ensuring data-driven insights inform financial strategy. Collaborate with functional leaders to enhance forecasting accuracy, streamline processes, and support strategic decision-making. Drive continuous improvements in financial reporting, including the alignment and standardization of dashboards, reports, and key performance indicators (KPIs) such as return on investment (ROI). Provide monthly, quarterly, and annual OpEx and CapEx commentary for the corporate functions supported, delivering actionable insights to leadership. Ensure accuracy of monthly, quarterly, and annual financial data, inclusive of accruals and spend trend monitoring. Support ad-hoc financial projects to drive efficiency, cost optimization, and strategic initiatives. Qualifications & Skills: Bachelor’s degree in Finance or Accounting required; Master’s degree or MBA preferred. 3-5 years of relevant experience in Finance and/or Accounting with a focus on Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) and budgeting. Strong Excel skills and financial modeling experience required. Detail-oriented with strong interpersonal, communication, and organizational skills—must be comfortable working across all levels of the organization. Experience with multidimensional databases such as Oracle, OneStream, Power BI is a plus. Additional details : This role is hybrid between Princeton, NJ and remote.

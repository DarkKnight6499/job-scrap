Job Description: About Sixth Street Asset Based Finance Sixth Street, a leading global investment firm dedicated to developing innovative investment strategies and fostering growth across various stages of business, is seeking an Associate for its market leading Asset Based Finance team. The Sixth Street Asset Based Finance group is at the forefront of asset/platform investing and origination, working across diverse financial markets, including residential and commercial mortgages, consumer assets, commercial equipment, renewables and energy finance, infrastructure debt and transportation. The Asset Based Finance team is a critical strategic capital partner and investor to companies and management teams and a solutions provider at scale. The Role This is a full-time Investing Associate role based in New York. As an Associate on the Asset Based Finance team, you will have responsibility for analyzing, underwriting and executing corporate finance transactions across industries. The Asset Based Finance group is at the forefront of FIG private equity investing, working across industries, geographies, and capital structures.

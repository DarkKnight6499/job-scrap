Corporate Access Associate The Role We are seeking a highly qualified Corporate Access Associate to join our Corporate Access team. This individual would report directly into the Head of US Broker Relations & Corporate Access and manage the expansion of our internal sell side offering for our investment professionals. What you’ll do This candidate will support 25+ Portfolio Managers and teams on our internal platform in the US & EMEA to coordinate sell side events. They will oversee the organization and submission of all sell side corporate access and conference requests as well as maintain the coordination surrounding those events. There will be a lot of autonomy in this role where each day the individual will be responsible for ensuring teams are kept informed of upcoming events, communicating with the sell side and management teams, allocating meetings using a variety of factors and managing our internal meeting tracking system. They will also be expected to liaise with various employees to ensure accuracy and completeness of information internally. We leverage both internal and external portfolio manager teams around the world, seeking to capitalize on inefficiencies and opportunities within the markets. We draw from decades of experience and a significant investment in proprietary technology, infrastructure and risk analytics to invest across four main strategies: Quant, Tactical, Fundamental Equity and Discretionary Macro & Fixed Income.

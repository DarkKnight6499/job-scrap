Job Title: Vice President, Portfolio Manager- Lender Finance Location: CityScape What you'll do: As Vice President, Portfolio Manager - Lender Finance you'll be responsible for analyzing, underwriting, and coordinating the closing of large corporate loans & structured products ($25mm+). The Portfolio Manager will work with Directors and MD’s to ensure credit risks are monitored and identified. Join a growing team of 20 with $10B of AUM. Gather and analyze necessary information to present along with Originators, to Senior Management or Loan Committee for approval. · Analyze portfolio investment performance for buy/hold/sell recommendations · Prepare portfolio scorecards and recommend marks on collateral · Coordinate processing of approved loans; ensure loans are processed according to agreement, customer needs and conform to Bank lending policies; assist in solving problems in your portfolio. · Handle various other underwriting, portfolio management and upkeep tasks and responsibilities related to new loan requests and/or portfolio. What you'll need: 6+ years of related experience in Portfolio Management or similar field. Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience required. Advanced knowledge of Fixed Income investing. Advanced knowledge of commercial credit and loan structuring. Credit analyst training completed. Advanced speaking, excel and writing communication skills. Progress toward or completion of CFA Compensation: Salary range for new hires is generally $139,530.00 - $172,360.00 for Los Angeles, CA. Salary range for new hires is generally $139,530.00 - $172,360.00 for San Diego, CA. Salary range for new hires is generally $148,830.00 - $183,850.00 for New York, NY. Benefits you’ll love: We offer all the important things you'd want — like competitive salaries, an ownership stake in the company, medical and dental insurance, time off, a great 401k matching program, tuition assistance program, an employee volunteer program, and a wellness program.

The Deposit Pricing and Analytics team is responsible for setting the interest rates on all deposit accounts (e.g., checking, savings, and CDs) within the retail line of business at JPMorganChase, which has the largest deposit base among all banks in the United States. As a Quant Analytics Senior Associate on the Deposit Pricing and Analytics team you will be responsible for deposit-based strategic analytics to help inform pricing strategy. You will also be responsible for end-to-end analytics that answer challenging, open-ended questions that drive product decisions and inform on deposit trends.

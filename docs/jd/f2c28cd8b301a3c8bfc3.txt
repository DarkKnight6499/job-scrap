Try new things, learn new skills and discover what you excel at—all from Day One. Job Description Are you detail-oriented, analytical, and energized by work where accuracy matters? Join our Structured Finance team as a Structured Finance Analyst , where you'll play an important role in supporting complex financial relationships and ensuring high-volume payment activity is completed accurately and on time. What you'll do In this role, you'll manage responsibilities associated with an assigned portfolio of Structured Finance accounts. Your day-to-day work may include: Preparing and processing high-volume wire payments and other financial transactions Reviewing information for accuracy and ensuring payments are completed correctly and on schedule Using Excel-based processes that include formulas, macros, and manual data entry Researching exceptions and troubleshooting issues before escalating them Communicating with clients, calculation agents, and internal partners to address questions or issues Analyzing requests to ensure they align with established procedures before taking action Taking on additional account relationships and responsibilities as your knowledge develops Supporting projects and helping teammates as a knowledgeable Grade 10 team member What will help you succeed We're looking for someone who can stay organized and thoughtful in a fast-paced environment. You'll be well positioned for success if you bring: Strong attention to detail and the ability to double-check your work Working knowledge of Microsoft Excel. Advanced Excel expertise is a plus, but not required Strong analytical and problem-solving skills Clear, professional communication skills The ability to stay composed and make thoughtful decisions when priorities become urgent Curiosity and a willingness to understand the reasoning behind processes rather than simply completing individual steps Initiative to troubleshoot issues, ask good questions, and take ownership of your work Candidates do not need to come directly from Structured Finance or banking operations. The hiring manager is open to a broad range of backgrounds when candidates can demonstrate transferable skills, including experience from banking, financial services, client-facing roles, retail, hospitality, or recent education Basic Qualifications - Associate's degree or technical education, or equivalent work experience - Three or more years of related experience Location St. Paul, MN- West Side Flats Monday- Friday: 8am-4:30pm This role requires working from a U.S Bank location three (3) or more days per week.

Lead Financial Analyst (Hybrid)
Stryker
Location: Cary, Illinois; Portage, Michigan
Link: https://stryker.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/StrykerCareers/job/Cary-Illinois/Lead-Financial-Analyst--Hybrid-_R575159

Work Flexibility: Hybrid Make an impact that matters within the Sage Business Unit. In this role, you will turn financial data into insights that influence business decisions, resource allocation, and long-term performance across a dynamic, growing business. You will partner with functional leaders to strengthen financial planning, forecasting, and performance management while improving the scalability and effectiveness of FP&A processes. What You Will Do Partner with cross-functional teams, including Sales, Marketing, Operations, and Shared Services to provide actionable insights that influence strategic priorities and capital allocation. Analyze expense budgets, including SG&A and centralized functions, to identify cost optimization opportunities and ensure alignment with business goals. Contribute to the annual budgeting, quarterly forecasting, and long-range planning processes to ensure financial targets align with organizational strategy. Develop and implement KPIs, dashboards, and trend analyses to monitor business performance and drive accountability across teams. Drive process improvement initiatives to enhance efficiency, accuracy, and scalability in FP&A operations, including automation and system optimization projects. What You Will Need Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, or related field. Minimum 4 years of experience in FP&A, financial planning and analysis, corporate finance, financial analysis, business finance, or a related finance function. Experience analyzing financial and operational data using Microsoft Excel, including complex financial models and multidimensional datasets. Preferred Qualifications CPA or MBA. Experience in FP&A within a large, publicly traded organization. Familiarity with SG&A management, consolidated planning, and capital allocation. Experience with enterprise planning systems (e.g., SAP, Hyperion, Oracle). Hybrid: This role will follow a hybrid work arrangement, with an expectation to work onsite 1–2 days per week in either Cary, IL or Portage, MI , with flexibility to work remotely on other days.

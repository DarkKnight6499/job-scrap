The Senior Structuring Analyst will support the financial planning, performance, and analysis of energy storage projects within a regulated utility environment. This role is in the Battery Energy Storage Business Development Program Management team and will work cross functionally with other organizations to support overall company objectives. The ideal candidate will have a combination of strong financial analytical skills, attention to detail, and a growing understanding of the renewable energy and utility markets. Responsibilities: Perform business unit financial planning and analysis, including budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis, long-range planning, and performance reporting. Track and explain changes in project financial performance, including cost movements and schedule impacts. Prepare clear, concise financial materials for internal stakeholders, including executive summaries, approval memos, dashboards, and recurring financial reports. Perform internal benchmarking and comparative analysis Support rate case financial requests Coordinate financial reporting alignment Build, update and maintain financial models for Battery Energy Storage investments. Evaluate project economics, including cash flows, capital costs, operating costs, tax assumptions, revenue requirements, customer impacts, and other financial drivers relevant to regulated utility decision-making. Review model outputs for accuracy, reasonableness, and consistency with approved assumptions; complete sensitivities and scenario analyses to evaluate project cost competitiveness, and key value drivers under changing assumptions. Support internal transaction approvals by preparing financial analyses, summaries, business cases, and presentation materials for management review. Assist in evaluating project alternatives, including project acquisition, equipment procurement options, and capital deployment considerations.

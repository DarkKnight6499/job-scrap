The Role This position features a hybrid work arrangement. This role does not provide visa sponsorship now or at any time in the future. Candidates must have authorization to work without requiring employer-sponsored work authorization. Kyndryl is seeking a Business Analyst to support the North Carolina DMV the Modernization Program , a large-scale transformation initiative focused on modernizing critical DMV operations and services. This role will serve as the bridge between business stakeholders and technical teams, ensuring business objectives, processes, and operational needs are effectively translated into technology solutions. The Business Architect will analyze and document business capabilities, processes, information flows, stakeholder impacts, and organizational requirements while helping drive the successful implementation of the MAX solution. Key Responsibilities Lead business process analysis, requirements gathering, and stakeholder engagement activities. Facilitate workshops, interviews, and working sessions with business and technical teams. Document current-state and future-state business processes, workflows, and operational models. Translate business requirements into functional specifications and solution recommendations. Conduct gap analyses and assess business impacts, dependencies, risks, and opportunities. Collaborate with project teams to ensure alignment between business objectives and technical solutions. Support User Acceptance Testing (UAT), including test planning, execution support, defect tracking, and validation. Assess stakeholder impacts and support organizational change initiatives. Help define and document business architecture components, including business capabilities, information flows, organizational roles, and process frameworks. Provide strategic recommendations regarding business transformation and modernization efforts.

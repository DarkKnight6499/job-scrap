Job Overview: We are looking to expand our 30+ person credit research team by adding an Associate Research Analyst to assist Senior Analysts covering companies with Loan-Only capital structures. This individual will be part of the Global Credit Research Team and will interact with investment professionals across our taxable fixed income mandates. We’ll trust you to: Conduct detailed industry and company fundamental analysis for Loan-Only capital structures as directed by the Senior Analysts Build and maintain industry and company financial models Assist Senior Analyst in constructing company and industry research notes, providing background information, graphs and data tables Develop and refine research knowledge and skills under the guidance of the supervising Senior Analyst and Director of Research Communicate and develop relationships with issuer management teams, sell-side professionals, legal and restructuring professionals, and private equity sponsors Analyze and help develop credit documents such as loan agreements, amendments, and security agreements You'll need to have: 2-3 years of financial modeling experience Experience with high yield/leveraged loans, private credit/direct lending and the healthcare sector would be helpful Excellent quantitative, communication, and investment management skills BA/BS degree required Why Lord Abbett?

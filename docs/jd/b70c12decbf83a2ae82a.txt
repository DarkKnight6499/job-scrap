UMB Commercial Banking & Lending provides a comprehensive solution to commercial organizations of all sizes. On this Credit Analyst team, you gain the knowledge and skill to understand UMB’s Commercial lending philosophy. You will gain broad understanding of loan processes and gain the skills needed to grow your career in UMB’s Commercial Lending team. As the Credit Analyst you will analyze commercial borrower's credit standing and account relationships with UMB and their specific industry through the creation of spreads and write-ups. How you will spend your time: · Produce formal credit write ups which summarize · analyze a commercial borrower's credit and account relationship with UMB · Work on the more complex loan write ups Produce reports pertaining to various aspects of credit · Present loan proposals to Discount Committee on a rotating basis to · assist during peak times · Must be flexible as area can be deadline oriented · Other duties as assigned. We are excited to talk with you if: · You have completed a bachelor’s degree in business or finance with 6 hours of accounting at a collegiate level. · OR any combination of education and experience that would provide an equivalent background Bonus Points if you have: · Demonstrates proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel, Windows · Demonstrates comprehension and application of financial analysis skills · Must have gained the relevant knowledge and experience to be able to handle the most complex credit write ups with minimal guidance and minimal errors · Demonstrates ability to work with all levels of associates in various areas of the Bank, by phone and in person · Demonstrates strong written & verbal communication skills This position was posted on 11/02/2026.

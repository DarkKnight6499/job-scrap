Job Description: The Role Customers increasingly expect to use their financial information across a connected ecosystem of applications and services. As a Senior Business Analyst, you will help deliver capabilities that enable customers to share account information with authorized third-party applications. You will work with product managers, architects, engineers, and business stakeholders to translate customer and business needs into clear requirements and scalable data-sharing solutions. Candidates must be able to leverage data to inform decisions, synthesize and clearly communicate insights, and apply a customer-first mindset with ownership, curiosity, and a commitment to continuous learning to deliver impactful results. Gather, analyze, and document business, system, data, and API requirements Translate customer needs into user stories, acceptance criteria, process flows, and visual models Analyze API contracts, data models, payloads, and integration requirements Collaborate with architects and engineers throughout solution design and delivery Identify dependencies, risks, and impacts across systems and teams Support testing, business validation, and release-readiness activities Bring customer and business insights into product decisions The Expertise and Skills You Brin g Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience 5 plus years of business analysis, systems analysis, product analysis, or related experience Experience working with APIs, digital products, data platforms, or system integrations Familiarity with REST APIs, JSON, OpenAPI/Swagger, Bruno, or similar API tools Ability to create process flows, context diagrams, and sequence diagrams Working knowledge of modern application architecture, databases, and cloud platforms Basic understanding of OAuth and customer-permissioned access Strong analytical, communication, facilitation, and stakeholder-management skills Experience working in an Agile environment using Jira and Confluence Financial services, financial-data sharing, data aggregation, or fintech experience is a plus The Team You will join a collaborative product and technology team that builds standards-based APIs and integrations to help customers use their financial information across authorized third-party applications and services. This is an opportunity to work at the intersection of customer experience, APIs, financial data, and fintech while helping shape the future of an increasingly connected financial ecosystem.

While reporting directly to the Sr. Manager, Core FP&A, as the Financial Analyst, you will be responsible for the annual planning, forecasting, financial reporting, and various types of financial analysis. Additional responsibilities include long-range strategic planning, special project assignments, and financial process improvements. What you'll be doing: Act as a key contributor to the annual budgeting and forecasting processes working cross functionally with the sales team, manufacturing team, and corporate finance Conduct financial analyses and special project work for the division, including preparation of various pro formas, discounted cash flows, ROI’s as they related to major expansion/acquisitions/divestitures and business valuations Support the division level activities related to the financial close process Assist with / coordinate Internal Audit and SOX requirements in the division Develop and implement approaches and templates to improve financial reporting, forecasting, planning, and analytics Undertake statistical analysis and analytical/industry research Assist in the review of capital projects checking the accuracy and reasonableness of financial projections and compliance with Corporate and Division capital policies Assist with training of new manufacturing and sales personnel in Sonoco accounting and financial systems Other duties as assigned This position can be located in Hartsville, SC or Charlotte, NC.

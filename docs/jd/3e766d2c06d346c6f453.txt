Job Description Join Visa Treasury's Digital Assets and Innovation team to help reshape how money moves with digital assets like stablecoins and next-gen payment rails. In this role, you'll own operations end to end, develop treasury roadmaps, and guide decisions, partnering with Risk, Compliance, Legal, Settlement Ops, Finance, and Product to vet ideas and design and launch pilots with external partners. You'll keep a close pulse on the crypto ecosystem and turn research into clear recommendations. We're looking for someone who brings big ideas and takes ownership, yet stays grounded in treasury fundamentals like liquidity, settlement, reconciliation, and controls. This role is perfect if you're ready to roll up your sleeves, move fast, and help define the next chapter of money movement at Visa. Key Responsibilities: Run daily treasury operations across digital assets and traditional payments, including cash, liquidity, settlement, and reconciliation. Execute treasury activities across blockchains, custodians, wallets, banks, exchanges, and payment providers. Participate in a rotating weekend and after-hours support model for critical treasury operations and incident response. Monitor fiat and digital asset liquidity, funding needs, and operational risk controls. Support stablecoin operations, including minting, redemption, custody transfers, wallet funding, and exception management. Own settlement, reconciliation, operational controls, audit readiness, and issue resolution processes. Improve runbooks, procedures, controls, and escalation frameworks. Track KPIs and operational metrics; provide actionable insights to leadership. Assess opportunities and risks across digital asset use cases, focusing on liquidity, efficiency, settlement, and working capital impacts. Monitor developments across blockchain, stablecoin, custody, payments, and regulatory ecosystems. Create executive-ready presentations, strategy memos, and business recommendations. Lead pilot execution from planning through launch, including governance, testing, vendor diligence, and operational readiness. Partner with Risk, Compliance, Legal, Finance, Product, Engineering, and Operations teams to build scalable solutions. Support selection and evaluation of custody, wallet, banking, payment, and blockchain infrastructure providers. This role is a Hybrid position to be located in our Foster City, CA office location. This role requires weekend operations.

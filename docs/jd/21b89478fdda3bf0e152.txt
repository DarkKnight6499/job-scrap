Job Description Purpose of the role To support the development of audits aligned to the bank’s standards and objectives by working collaboratively with colleagues, providing accurate information and recommendations, and complying with policies and procedures. Accountabilities Audit development and delivery support, including financial statements, accounting practices, operational processes, IT systems and risk management. Identification of operational risks to support the delivery of the Barclays Internal Audit (BIA) Audit Plan through risk assessments. Assessment of internal control effectiveness and their capability to identify and mitigate risk aligned to regulatory requirements. Communication of key findings and recommendations to stakeholders, including the Audit Owner, senior managers and directors. Identification of regulatory news and industry trends/developments to provide timely insight and recommendations for best practice. Join us as an Internal Audit AVP -Credit Risk and play a key role in delivering high-quality internal audits aligned with Barclays’ Credit Risk strategy. In this role, you’ll execute end-to-end audits, evaluate the design and effectiveness of internal controls, and identify potential risks and areas for improvement. You’ll collaborate with business stakeholders, communicate audit findings, and support continuous monitoring activities. This is an excellent opportunity to contribute to a collaborative team within a global banking environment. To be successful as an Internal Audit AVP -Credit Risk, you should have: Internal audit experience, including audit planning, execution, and reporting Active Certified Internal Auditor (CIA) certification Experience assessing control design, operating effectiveness, and risk mitigation Understanding of internal audit methodologies and risk frameworks Experience managing stakeholders and communicating audit finding Ability to independently execute audits and manage multiple priorities Other highly valued skills include: Experience in retail credit risk or consumer banking Familiarity with 1LOD, 2LOD, and 3LOD risk and control frameworks Experience working within large global financial institutions Knowledge of banking regulations and regulatory expectations Experience working within financial services or banking environments Excellent written, verbal, and presentation skills You may be assessed on key critical skills relevant for success in this role, such as risk and controls, change and transformation, business acumen, strategic thinking and digital and technology, as well as job-specific technical skills. This role is based out of Wilmington, DE.

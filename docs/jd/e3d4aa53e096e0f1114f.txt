Use Your Power for Purpose A position within Pfizer Global Supply – GTEL Laboratory Operations Center function providing GMP compliant services to PGS within GTEL ES & MDCP. The applicant must have awareness of cGMPs, good lab practices and good housekeeping practices. The candidate must also have experience with laboratory equipment and demonstrated compliance to procedures. What You Will Achieve The Sr. Associate Metrologist will: Have primary oversight for service providers and/or perform occasional validation, qualification, calibration/preventative maintenance for laboratory instrumentation used for GMP testing. Be responsible for generating and/or reviewing documentation in support of the metrological activities stated above. Including maintaining these records and filing according to Pfizer policy Be responsible for updating and maintaining calibration database Be responsible for scheduling and managing calibration/maintenance service contracts with outside vendors Perform/document investigations for non-conformances associated with laboratory equipment. Assist with troubleshooting equipment and equipment software applications as well as make recommendations to Laboratory Team Leaders and Analysts. Ensure compliance to Quality Agreement between GTEL ES MDCP and other sites/entities Be responsible for providing support during regulatory inspections and on-site audits. Here Is What You Need (Minimum Requirements) Applicant must have a bachelor's degree with at least 2 years of experience; OR a master's degree with 0+ years of experience; OR an associate's degree with 6 years of experience; OR a high school diploma (or equivalent) and 8 years of relevant experience. Experience with laboratory equipment such as balances, calipers, etc… Strong written and oral communication skills Demonstrated proficiency with a CMMS software Demonstrated computer literacy in Microsoft Office or equivalent programs, familiarity with document management systems and databases Familiarity with GMPs and applicable regulations, guidelines (e.g. CFR, EMA and ICH publications) and standard operating procedures Ability to work independently and effectively in a team environment with colleagues of diverse backgrounds Ability to complete work within reasonable timelines and to a high-quality standard Ability to commit to the Pfizer Values & work in a regulated environment OTHER JOB DETAILS Last Date to Apply for Job: October 23, 2026.

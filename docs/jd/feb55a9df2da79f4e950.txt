Position Summary The Senior Member Credit Analyst (the Analyst) is responsible for monitoring and assessing the financial condition of the Bank’s member institutions. The Analyst also participates in the credit underwriting process by reviewing credit requests and ensuring that such requests are compliant with the Bank’s risk guidelines. In addition, the Analyst produces routine or ad-hoc credit reports and supports broader sector credit reviews. This role will have a hybrid work schedule in our Boston office in accordance with the Bank’s Hybrid Work Program. More time will be expected in the office to support onboarding initially.

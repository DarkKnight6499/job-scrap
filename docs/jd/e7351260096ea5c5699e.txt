The Senior/Lead Business Analyst, Financial Close, Reconciliation & Reporting supports Markel's Oracle Enterprise Performance Management (EPM) applications — primarily Financial Consolidation and Close (FCCS), Account Reconciliation (ARCS), and Profitability and Cost Management (PCM) — as well as Finance's broader enterprise reporting capabilities delivered through Oracle Financial Data Integration (FDI) and Power BI. Reporting to the Manager, Oracle EPM & Reporting, this role partners closely with IT, the Oracle R2R Product Owner, and Finance stakeholders to support the day-to-day operation, enhancement, and continuous improvement of the close, reconciliation, and reporting landscape. This is fundamentally an accounting and process role rather than a systems role. While the Oracle EPM suite and Finance's reporting tools are the platforms through which the work is delivered, sound accounting judgment is the core competency required for success in this position. Candidates with accounting backgrounds are encouraged to apply; training on the underlying systems will be provided. Job Responsibilities Apply accounting judgment to evaluate the reasonableness of consolidation results, reconciliation variances, and cost allocation results, identifying and escalating discrepancies before they affect the close. Partner with FP&A to ensure PCM allocation methodology and outputs align with how profitability results are analyzed and presented. Serve as a liaison between Finance teams and the IT functional team, gathering, documenting, and translating business requirements for FCCS, ARCS, PCM, and reporting initiatives and enhancements. Develop subject matter expertise across the Oracle EPM suite, with a specific focus on FCCS, ARCS, and PCM, and maintain a working understanding of how these applications integrate with upstream and downstream financial systems. Support the reporting layer connecting Oracle EPM and ERP applications to downstream reporting via Oracle FDI and Power BI, maintaining working knowledge of data mappings, dimension crosswalks, and load rules within FDI, and data models, measures, and refresh logic within Power BI, to help ensure the accuracy, completeness, and timeliness of data flows. Contribute to the enhancement and expansion of Power BI dashboards and reporting capabilities to improve visibility, insight, and decision-making for Finance stakeholders. Coordinate and support end-to-end testing efforts for FCCS, ARCS, PCM, and reporting changes, including defining test scenarios, tracking timelines across stakeholder groups, and driving issues to closure. Act as a point of contact for production issues across FCCS, ARCS, PCM, and reporting tools, applying accounting knowledge to triage root causes and coordinating resolution with the appropriate technical teams. Identify and support opportunities to enhance close, reconciliation, and reporting processes through automation, leveraging Oracle's native capabilities within the EPM suite (e.g., intelligent matching in Account Reconciliation, automation and AI features within FCCS and Narrative Reporting, and allocation automation within Profitability and Cost Management). Stay current on emerging Oracle AI and automation capabilities across the EPM and reporting landscape and help assess their applicability to Finance processes. Serve as a representative on EPM- and reporting-related meetings and initiatives, building effective relationships with key stakeholders across Finance and IT. Support the development of best practice guidance, conduct training, and partner with the Finance team on enhanced use of Oracle EPM and reporting tools. Ensure all key process controls and appropriate governance documentation are in place and kept up to date for FCCS, ARCS, PCM, FDI, Power BI, and reporting-related processes. Support responses to external audit queries and coordinate the delivery of supporting information requests. Proactively identify process gaps and inefficiencies and contribute recommendations for improvement.

Bonus Type Discretionary Summary As an FP&A Analyst, you’ll play a critical role in helping the bank understand the why behind performance. This role partners closely with Finance leadership, business leaders, and technology teams to analyze profitability, costs, and financial drivers across the organization. The ideal analyst is intellectually curious, technically strong, and motivated by turning complex data into clear, actionable insights that influence decision‑making at the enterprise level. This role is based in Tulsa, OK and offers strong exposure to senior finance leaders, meaningful analytical projects, and long-term career growth within FP&A. Job Description The FP&A Analyst supports profitability and cost analysis initiatives by developing insights that help leaders understand financial performance at the product, segment, and customer level. This role contributes to forecasting, modeling, and reporting efforts while partnering across Finance, business units, and Technology to ensure accurate, insightful analysis. You’ll work on high‑impact projects tied to net interest margin, earnings drivers, and customer profitability, while continuing to strengthen your technical and analytical skillset in a collaborative, fast‑paced environment. Team Culture This team values curiosity, collaboration, and continuous improvement. Analysts are encouraged to ask questions, bring forward ideas, and deepen their technical skills while gaining exposure to senior leaders across Finance and the business. The environment is supportive but fast‑moving, with a strong emphasis on learning, accountability, and producing high‑quality analysis that drives real business outcomes. How You'll Spend Your Time Analyze profitability, costs, and financial performance across products, customers, and business segments Build and maintain financial models to support forecasting, scenario analysis, and sensitivity analysis Partner with Finance leadership, business stakeholders, and Technology to support enterprise‑wide financial initiatives Support attribution analysis (e.g., net interest margin, earnings per share, profit margin drivers) Prepare clear, concise analysis and presentations for senior leaders, including CFO‑level discussions Work with large data sets using Excel and data analytics tools to identify trends, risks, and opportunities Contribute to ongoing enhancement of reporting, tools, and analytical processes Education & Experience Requirements T his level of knowledge is normally acquired through completion of a Bachelor’s or Advanced degree in Business Administration, Finance, Data Analytics, Computer Science or related discipline and 6-8 years of related work experience or equivalent combination of education and experience. Previous related experience in Banking preferred. Advanced proficiency in BI visualization tools (PowerBI, Tableau, Qlik) and SQL. Strong SQL Skills Ability to work with large amounts of data and effectively communicate trends and insights Must be able to define, shape, and drive projects to completion independently while effectively collaborating with others Exceptional analytical and problem-solving skills Excellent interpersonal and communication skills, work ethic, and the ability to work well individually as well as in cross-functional project teams Ability to present complex insights to executive/senior stakeholders. Familiarity with analytical tools (Alteryx, Python, R) and ability to implement advanced analytics solution s.

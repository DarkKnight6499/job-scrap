Your opportunity Your responsibilities include the preparation and analysis of the U.S. GAAP Group consolidated financial results, as well as the readying and distribution of periodic financial statements and reports for either internal or external audiences. You will ensure that all reports and disclosures comply with applicable government regulations, professional standards, and organizational policies. Additional responsibilities include proactively addressing reporting and accounting data issues and recommending operational improvements to management. This role offers significant exposure to U.S. GAAP consolidation accounting, regulatory reporting, subsidiary reporting, and financial reporting processes. The successful candidate will have the opportunity to develop advanced technical accounting and reporting expertise while building toward expanded responsibilities and future career progression within the Financial Reporting team. Prepare the Group’s U.S. GAAP consolidated financial results in SAP Group Reporting for the parent company and global subsidiaries, including the review and analysis of consolidation entries and elimination adjustments, including investment, equity, dividend, and intercompany eliminations. Prepare and submit various financial reports to upper management, including monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements. Assist with the preparation and review of other regulatory and statutory filings as needed. Partner with external auditors in their audit/review of Group and certain subsidiary financial documents. Report required financial information to the U.S. Census Bureau and the Bureau of Economic Analysis. Ensure accuracy and completeness of all reports and processes with adherence to SOX controls. Carry out other duties as assigned.

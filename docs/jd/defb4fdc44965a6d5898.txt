Job Description: At Regions, the Credit Products Specialized Industries Associate supports Credit Products Specialized Industries Managers in identifying and mitigating risks in existing and proposed credit products. As a member of the First Line of Defense, this role analyzes new commercial credit opportunities, underwrites new or renewed credit exposure, and manages a portfolio of loans and other credit relationships in conjunction with a manager. Primary Responsibilities Prepares credit approval packages on new and renewed credit products including determination of the appropriate risk rating as well as risk factors that could change the risk rating, analysis of financial performance, performance vs. projections, covenant compliance, relationship profitability (RAROC), and any other significant risk drivers Analyzes guarantors as needed to support credit underwriting Assists Credit Products Specialized Industries Managers with the review of pre-closing loan documents and closing due diligence Manages financial reporting requirements, covenant requirements, past dues and documentation exceptions Ensures credit files are complete with all up to date information Provides tickler implementation and maintenance Monitors portfolios and servicing activities in conjunction with Credit Products support Validates the appropriate risk rating for a company and proactively identifies risk rating changes Identifies opportunities for exposure reduction or new opportunities within assigned portfolio Participates in research, preparation and execution of customer and prospect calls Participates in identifying financial solutions that meet the client’s needs and the Bank’s risk acceptance criteria Ensures compliance with internal bank commercial loan policy, credit risk management policy, and external regulatory requirements; documents any exceptions to policy and related rationale/mitigants This position is exempt from timekeeping requirements under the Fair Labor Standards Act and is not eligible for overtime pay. This position is incentive eligible. Requirements Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting or a related field Two (2) years of underwriting experience or financial services analysis and accounting experience Completion of a major corporate credit training program (or the equivalent corporate banking/corporate finance experience) Ability to competently complete credit packages and credit services agreements with management oversight Skills and Competencies Ability to identify and voice issues; self-initiates discussions Ability to perform quality analysis and display competency in completion and oversight of client portfolio management activities Excellent verbal and written communication skills Strong analytical and problem solving skills Well organized, detail-oriented, and ability to manage and/or lead multiple projects under pressure Works collaboratively with multiple stakeholders This position may be filled at a higher level depending on the candidate's qualifications and relevant experience.

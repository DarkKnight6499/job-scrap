Job Description Ready to take your career global? Make your mark at one of the biggest names in payments. We’re looking for a Sr. Associate, Content Writer, to join our ever-evolving Marketing team and help shape the future of global commerce. What you’ll own Writes publications for release to internal audiences with adjusted content for maximum effectiveness based upon intended communication method (internet, intranet-LAN, white paper, ad, etc.). Edits routine material to produce an error free publication, correcting grammar and ensuring clear messaging on websites, brochures, flyers, advisements, etc. Submits and edits graphics, headlines, and website links to support publication content and adhere to marketing specifications. Performs initial review of disclosures and releases and escalates missing or incomplete documents to more experienced editors to assist with validation of disclosures and signed releases for use of third party information or content referencing third party including trademarks, copyrights, business names, and photo releases, etc. Updates marketing library to ensure content is accurate and the most recent concerning new publication releases, and assists with audits of publications to ensure adherence to company standards or regulations. What you’ll bring Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, Journalism, Business, Advertising, or a related field, or an equivalent combination of education and relevant professional experience. Experience developing, writing, editing, and/or publishing content in a large corporate, enterprise, or global business environment. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with demonstrated ability to create clear, compelling, and audience-focused content across multiple channels. Proven ability to manage multiple projects, priorities, and deadlines while maintaining a high level of accuracy and attention to detail. It’s a bonus if you have Typically requires a minimum of 4 years of relevant professional experience in marketing, communications, content development, or related disciplines. Experience with marketing technologies, project management, copywriting, content creation, editing, and the administration of digital platforms, tools, and software. Demonstrated ability to manage content and marketing initiatives through planning, execution, and delivery while partnering with cross-functional stakeholders. Experience within the financial services, banking, fintech, or related regulated industries is preferred. Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities, meet deadlines, and maintain a high level of quality and accuracy.

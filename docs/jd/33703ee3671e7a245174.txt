Job Description Our Manufacturing Operations teams are the people that make our products. We work in the manufacturing plants with a “Safety First, Quality Always” mindset striving for continuous improvement. We work in the local plant connected to our global manufacturing network to ensure the highest quality of raw materials, intermediates and finished products. General Summary. The Vaccine Manufacturing Facility (VMF) is seeking a Transformation and Business Support Lead, who will lead an organization and portfolio of projects that will ensure the successful execution of strategic projects that are aligned with critical facility transformation milestones in addition to overseeing general business support activities. General Profile Responsibilities Reporting to the Director, Operations, the incumbent will assist in strategy development and will independently manage project assignments. Effectively manage a team of engineers and scientists supporting drug product facility change controls as well as other strategic initiatives, as requested Unite Quality, Operations, Technology Operations, Maintenance, Digital Manufacturing and other supporting functions behind a single integrated execution plan. Provide end-to-end technical support to assigned areas of focus throughout the project lifecycle including but not limited to quality risk management, strategy development, development of standard operating procedures and master batch records, process development, and process performance qualification. Review and approve project documents as necessary. Lead, facilitate and participate on cross-functional teams to collaboratively address issues and achieve project milestones. Supports team safety, environmental, and compliance objectives. Escalates and consults on an as-needed basis with next level manager on more complex decisions impacting critical project milestones. Establish and lead a business support team focused on strengthening core operational processes, including quality systems, change management, process optimization, documentation, and overall operations effectiveness. People Management The VMF Business Transformation & Support lead will directly oversee a team of project managers, ensuring their efforts are aligned to the department’s critical milestones and long-term objectives. This includes setting clear expectations, providing coaching and feedback, fostering collaboration across silos, and ensuring accountability to critical project milestones. The role requires developing talent, aligning individual goals to organizational priorities, and maintaining strong engagement and motivation during a period of intense transformation and delivery pressure. QUALIFICATIONS Required. Bachelor Degree in Engineering, Science or related field Minimum 8 years of experience with strong preference for individuals with two or more of the following experiences: Manufacturing Operations, Technical Operations, Quality Operations, or Site Engineering Services with supervisory responsibility with exempt personnel.

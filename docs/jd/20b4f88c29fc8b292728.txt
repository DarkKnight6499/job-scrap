Technology Risk Officer The Technology Risk Officer serves as a subject matter expert within the second line of defense (2LoD), responsible for the challenge, oversight, and support of enterprise IT and cybersecurity risk management programs. This role partners closely with first line of defense (1LoD) technology and controls teams — including, but not limited to: Cybersecurity, Corporate Technology, Nuveen Technology, Wealth Technology, and the Enterprise Vendor Program Office — to monitor, escalate, and report on significant risks and control weaknesses. Key Responsibilities and Duties Risk Assessment & Oversight: This role will support 2LoD targeted risk assessments to confirm control effectiveness and identify opportunities to strengthen controls, ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and availability across the technology environment. This includes evaluating application and infrastructure-specific control gaps, identifying thematic issues, and developing remediation plans. Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA): This role supports the implementation and ongoing maturation of the RCSA program by coordinating with 1LoD IT risk and control partners to ensure appropriate identification of technology risks and control weaknesses. The officer collaborates with business-aligned control teams to ensure RCSAs reflect a comprehensive view of the technology control environment that critical business functions depend upon. Issue Management: The Technology Risk Officer provides governance and 2LoD challenge across the Issue Management Program, including reviewing issue rating criticality, evaluating action plans for adequacy in addressing identified control gaps, assessing risk-accepted issues for adherence to requirements. Policy, Standards & Regulatory Alignment: This role provides 2LoD support and challenge of TIAA’s Information Technology Policy and IT Standards, including evaluating proposed policy changes that could affect technology teams. Reporting & Risk Appetite: The Technology Risk Officer provides support to management for producing risk management committee materials, metrics reporting and other related analysis, as needed. Relationship Management: This role builds and maintains effective relationships with 1LOD/2LOD/3LOD teams and control partners to ensure understanding of risk program expectations and guiding issue and action plan owners as needed.

Job Summary: We’re looking for a Claims Operations Associate I to join our growing Claims Operations team. This role offers the opportunity to work closely with Claims professionals across the organization while supporting both day-to-day operations and strategic transformation initiatives. You’ll help keep critical processes moving, collaborate on projects designed to improve how we work, and contribute to the continued growth and modernization of the Claims function. The role reports to the Director, Claims Operations. This is a hybrid position based in Warren, NJ working 3 days onsite, 2 remote. Responsibilities: Support the Claims organization with initial claims intake and set up, financial processes, customer service, data management, vendor management, and special projects as needed. Oversee payment queues for assignment Processing payments and other financial transactions Ongoing claim file maintenance including diary management Create and maintain spreadsheets and databases Assist colleagues with a variety of operational functions as needed, including processing and indexing of mail as well as special projects Customer service support including answering calls and responding to email inquiries as needed Perform quality control and oversight of outside vendors Provide support to Claims professionals Qualifications, Education & Experience: Bachelor’s degree or equivalent work experience Property and casualty insurer and Claims-related experience are desired, but not required Knowledge, Skills & Competencies: Ability to stay focused and complete work Issue resolution, problem solving, and creativity Strong organizational and task completion skills Strong work ethic Strong verbal and written communication and interpersonal skills, as well as a positive attitude Excellent customer service skills, including follow-through on and communication about action items, task completion and/or issues which remain unresolved Ability to work well in a professional, team-oriented environment Computer proficiency in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel Suite skills and PowerPoint) Leverage AI and emerging technologies to improve claims processes, efficiency, informed decision-making, and customer outcomes. The base salary range for this position is $43,000-$55,000 annually. The offered rate of compensation will be based on individual education, experience, qualifications and work location.

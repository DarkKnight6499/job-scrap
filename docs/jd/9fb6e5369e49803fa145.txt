A Day in the Life: In the position of Sr Analyst, Treasury - Systems and Operations, you will provide hands-on support for the SAP S/4HANA Helios implementation and help optimize Treasury operations through available technologies, including SAP, AI, Power BI, and Power Automate/Flows. You will support core Treasury operations, bank account management, and cross-functional initiatives. This role requires a practical Treasury professional who is comfortable working in SAP and can contribute effectively from the start. The Impact You'll Make: Identify, document, and implement Treasury process improvements and automation opportunities using SAP, AI, Power BI, Power Automate/Flows, and other available technologies; develop reporting and controls that improve visibility, accuracy, and efficiency. Support Treasury operations, including payment execution and payment support, intercompany transactions, dividend payments and distributions, and hedge settlements, ensuring timely execution, accurate documentation, and issue resolution. Support bank account management and governance activities, including account openings and closures, signatory and user updates, bank resolutions, KYC requests, documentation, access reviews, and maintenance of accurate bank account records. Support Treasury workstreams for the SAP S/4HANA Helios implementation, including requirements gathering, process design, testing, data validation, issue resolution, training support, and post-go-live stabilization. Serve as a hands-on Treasury subject matter resource for SAP processes and related integrations, translating operational needs into practical system solutions. Collaborate cross-functional and across departments with Corporate Finance, Accounting, Legal, Tax, International Finance, and MNST subsidiaries to coordinate Banking activities, resolve issues, and deliver project and operational objectives. Ensure systems, processes, and operational activities comply with Treasury policies, internal controls, SOX requirements, information security standards, and documentation expectations. Who You Are: Education: Bachelor's Degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Information Systems, Business, or a related field. Between 3-5 years of experience in corporate Treasury, Finance operations, Treasury systems, or a related function. SAP Experience Desired: +1 years of hands-on SAP experience, with demonstrated comfort navigating SAP finance or Treasury processes; SAP S/4HANA implementation, testing, or deployment experience strongly preferred. Computer Skills Desired: Advanced Excel and strong analytical skills. Working experience with Power BI and Power Automate/Flows preferred; practical interest in applying AI to process improvement and analysis. Additional Knowledge or Skills: Knowledge of payments, intercompany transactions, dividends, hedge settlements, bank account management, and Treasury controls. Strong process orientation, documentation and testing discipline, problem-solving ability, attention to detail, and cross-functional collaboration skills.

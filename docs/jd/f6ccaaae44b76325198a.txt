Role Overview: The PXM Business Analyst supports the delivery of PIM solutions by eliciting, documenting, and validating requirements related to product data, content, and business processes. This role focuses on detailed analysis , traceability, and clarity, ensuring that business needs are well understood and accurately reflected in the solution design. The Business Analyst works under the guidance of the PXM Functional Lead and in close collaboration with technical teams to translate requirements into actionable user stories, acceptance criteria, and functional documentation. This role is critical to maintaining alignment between stakeholders, delivery teams, and the final implemented solution. The work: Elicit and document business requirements through interviews, workshops, and process reviews Analyze current‑state product data flows and identify gaps, inefficiencies, and improvement opportunities Document functional requirements, use cases, and acceptance criteria Support product data attribute definition, field mapping, and taxonomy documentation Create and maintain user stories and backlog artifacts in collaboration with delivery teams Support data migration and integration analysis by documenting source‑to‑target requirements Assist with UAT planning, test case development, and defect triage Ensure requirements traceability from discovery through delivery and validation Support change management and training documentation efforts Drive AEO and GEO strategies for clients using PIM data for LLM syndication Stay up to date on industry trends related to Agentic Commerce and leverage of GenAI and Agentic solutions to automate the product content supply chain Here’s what you need: 3+ years of experience in business analysis fundamentals with understanding of PIM concepts , product data structures, and content lifecycles 2+ years of full lifecycle implementation experience using various SDLC methodologies 1+ year Exposure to PIM platforms such as Salsify, Akeneo, Syndigo, inRiver, Stibo STEP, or Informatica P360 (hands‑on or project-based) Bonus points if: Solid understanding of AEO and GEO strategies and how to adapt product data for LLM’s Familiarity with attributes, hierarchies, variants, and localization Knowledge of GDSN concepts, Digital Shelf Analytics, Enhanced Content, Retailer schemas & validation rules Experience with multi-lingual and multi-market implementations Experience with GenAI or Agentic AI integration within PIM platforms Experience with AEO, GEO Compensation at Accenture varies depending on a wide array of factors, which may include but are not limited to the specific office location, role, skill set, and level of experience.

Global Investment Research – Equity Research Associate, Business & Information Services You will join a collaborative, high-performing Equity Research team focused on the Business & Information Services sector. The team covers approximately 30 public companies across information services, professional services, business services and related industries. Associates play a meaningful role in the research process, including financial modeling, fundamental analysis, company earnings, thematic research and direct engagement with institutional investors and corporate management teams. The team is based primarily in San Francisco and has developed a strong culture of collaboration, mentorship and long-term career development. Associates are encouraged to take ownership of their coverage responsibilities while working closely with teammates across the broader coverage universe, fostering both deep company expertise and broad sector knowledge. The role is well suited for intellectually curious individuals who enjoy fundamental research, thrive in a team-oriented environment and are interested in building a long-term career within Equity Research. Job Summary and Responsibilities Work closely with members of the Equity Research team to conduct fundamental research within the Business & Information Services sector, supporting coverage of approximately 30 public companies. Develop, maintain and enhance detailed financial models, earnings analyses, industry databases and company forecasts. Serve as a lead associate on assigned stocks under coverage, with responsibility for financial modeling, earnings analysis, research production and day-to-day coverage maintenance while collaborating across the broader coverage universe to maintain backup coverage and shared sector expertise. Conduct independent primary and secondary research using company filings, industry sources, proprietary data, expert insights and other research tools to identify actionable investment opportunities and risks. Monitor company developments, industry trends, competitive dynamics and macroeconomic events, proactively identifying investment implications and bringing new ideas, observations and insights to the team. Contribute to the development of research reports, thematic projects, client presentations and other thought leadership initiatives. Communicate investment views and research findings with the Equities Sales force, institutional investors, company management teams and internal stakeholders. Take initiative in identifying emerging topics, investigating new research questions and developing differentiated insights that enhance the team’s research product and value to clients. Contribute to a highly collaborative, team-oriented culture that emphasizes intellectual curiosity, continuous learning, strong judgment and long-term professional development.

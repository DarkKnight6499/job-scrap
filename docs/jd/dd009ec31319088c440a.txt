How This Role Impacts Live Oak and its People We are on a mission to be America’s small business bank. Our deposits help fund loans for small businesses across the country. As a Deposit Quality Assurance Associate, you help fuel the growth of small businesses. The Deposit Quality Assurance Associate supports the compliance, risk, and governance functions of the Deposits team as it undergoes a multi-year expansion of products, services, and technology. The Deposit Quality Assurance Associate is a designated essential employee and may be required to be available after hours, and to work either in the office or remotely during inclement weather or a disaster scenario.

This position offers the opportunity to play a central role in advancing the Bank's community development strategy while supporting investments that create affordable housing, drive economic development, and deliver meaningful community impact. What You'll Do Underwrite and analyze community development investment opportunities, including LIHTC multi-investor funds, direct NMTC investments, CDFI loan pools, EQ2 investments, SBIC funds, and other mission-driven investments. Perform detailed financial, credit, and risk analysis of fund managers, syndicators, developers, sponsors, and underlying assets to assess investment viability and identify key risks. Independently prepare comprehensive investment opportunity and credit approval packages that clearly articulate transaction structure, sponsor qualifications, financial performance, investment risks, CRA impact, and recommendation rationale. Independently analyze credit and financial information of property and individuals to determine the degree of risk associated with the extension of credit. Complete annual reviews, portfolio monitoring reports, risk rating assessments, and covenant compliance analyses for existing investments. Oversee documentation and ongoing monitoring of asset and client performance. Work with external and internal business partners on the review of partnership agreements, operating agreements, subscription documents, fund documents, legal opinions, and other transaction-related closing documentation Review credit approval memorandums and attorney-prepared closing documents for new and renewed investments to ensure compliance and adherence to the approval guidelines. Interact with lending and investment staff, deal teams and clients in a welcoming and professional manner. Qualifications Bachelor's degree (Business, Finance, Accounting, or Economics preferred). Strong understanding of general credit and risk principles, and banking policies/procedures; formal bank credit training preferred. Minimum 3 years of experience in commercial real estate credit, investment underwriting, tax credit finance, or community development finance. Working knowledge of Low-Income Housing Tax Credits (LIHTC), New Markets Tax Credits (NMTC), CDFIs, or affordable housing finance. Strong analytical and business writing skills Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple transactions and portfolio responsibilities simultaneously. Good interpersonal skills with internal partners, counsel, clients and senior management. Accounting and finance knowledge, as well as knowledge of real estate fundamentals and concepts.

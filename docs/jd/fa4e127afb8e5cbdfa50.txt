Job Description Summary Design, deliver, and administer specialized training in life, annuity, and health underwriting covering analysis techniques, risk assessment, modeling, and tools and technology used to interpret and apply criteria for accurate risk assessments. Implement career development and engagement programs to improve the employee experience. Job Description Responsibilities Apply best practices in curriculum design and delivery to department staff and stakeholders in alignment with industry and company requirements. Develop clear and measurable learning criteria for different levels of underwriting knowledge and technical expertise. Keep current on regulatory rules, product offerings, and technology advances. Share insights through group and individual training. Evaluate the effectiveness of training/identify gaps and collaborate with management on ways to enhance the learning experience. Assess team needs to identify and create opportunities for career development and employee engagement. Participate in cross-functional projects/programs requiring collaboration with Underwriting.

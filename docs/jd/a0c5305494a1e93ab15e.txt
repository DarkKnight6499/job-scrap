Role Summary/Purpose: The VP, Enterprise Risk Analytics will provide enterprise-wide analytical leadership, support, and capabilities across the Risk organization. This leader will design and develop advanced analytical tools, techniques, and reporting solutions that generate actionable risk management insights and enable Risk colleagues to identify, assess, monitor, and manage risk more efficiently and effectively. The role will oversee analytical and reporting initiatives, guide the design of analyses and project plans, and translate findings into clear, strategic recommendations. This leader will manage a team of Risk Analytics professionals who support Synchrony’s second line of defense oversight responsibilities and broader risk strategy. Reporting to the SVP, Risk Data Governance, they will engage regularly with executive and senior leadership, as well as cross-functional business partners across the organization. As a people leader, this role is critical to attracting, developing, and retaining top analytical talent; building a strong future talent pipeline; fostering collaboration and cross-training across teams; creating meaningful career pathways; and establishing and executing a team engagement strategy. Essential Responsibilities: Lead a global organization of Risk Analytics professionals and support the planning and execution of oversight activities while providing mentorship, guidance, engagement, career pathing and development opportunities Develop enterprise-wide, forward-looking analytics and early-warning capabilities that identify material risk trends, concentrations, interdependencies, emerging risks, and changes in the Company’s risk profile Understand, leverage and promote value of data and analytics to create bigger impact and value to the business Drive extended usage of analytics and AI to drive more efficiencies and effectiveness within risk management processes and activities (e.g. replace traditional sampling with full file analysis where possible, etc.) Lead the AML Analytics arm of the team which drives the use of analytics and full-file testing to strengthen BSA/AML/OFAC compliance, improve data quality and monitoring, and provide senior leaders with insights and solutions that proactively identify and reduce financial crime risk. Establish and maintain risk-based standards for the development, documentation, testing, peer review, data lineage, change management, ongoing validation, and quality assurance of risk analytics and critical risk reporting.

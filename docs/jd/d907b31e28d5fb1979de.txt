Job Description: Our Opportunity Chewy is seeking an Associate Director, Team Member Experience & Engagement to join our Culture & Development team within Human Resources. This leader will play a critical role in shaping how Team Members experience Chewy, from their first day through all the key moments in their journey. In this role, you will be responsible for leading and scaling enterprise onboarding, Team Member experience programs, and culture initiatives. Translating strategy into meaningful, consistent, and inclusive experiences across our diverse workforce. The ideal candidate is a strategic thinker, collaborative partner, and results-driven people leader who can balance enterprise consistency with the unique needs of our different business units. Through strong partnerships across HR and the business, you will build scalable solutions, leverage Team Member insights, and drive measurable improvements in engagement, connection, and the overall Team Member experience. This position reports to the Director, Culture & Development and can be based at our corporate offices in Plantation, FL; Bellevue, WA; Minneapolis, MN; or Boston, MA.

Program Summary KBR's Army PBL program is a comprehensive logistics initiative aimed at enhancing the operational readiness of the U.S. Army while minimizing total ownership costs. Through a performance-based approach, KBR provides maintenance, supply chain management, technical support, training, and documentation services. This includes preventive and corrective maintenance, efficient supply chain operations, and expert technical assistance. By leveraging advanced analytics and monitoring tools, KBR ensures proactive maintenance and optimization of equipment performance. The program's focus on tailored support enhances the reliability, availability, and effectiveness of Army equipment, ultimately contributing to mission success and military readiness.

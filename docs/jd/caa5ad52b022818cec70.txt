Reinsurance Actuarial Senior Associate Director Preferred Location: Philadelphia, PA Additional Locations, Chicago, IL, New York, New York, Bloomington, MN As part of an industry-leading team, you will help empower results for our clients by delivering innovative and effective solutions as part of our Aon Reinsurance Solutions business group. The position is based in the U.S. but preferred office location is Philadelphia, PA. This position offers a hybrid workstyle (1-3 days in office) with a fully virtual option for highly qualified candidates. Your impact as a Senior Associate Director As a Senior Associate Director, your primary role will be to support clients as requested by supervisors or brokers, across virtually any client project or internal research assignment with a focus on P&C risk modeling. Senior Associate Directors are expected to be masters in completing standard actuarial analyses, while also participating in more advanced analyses that support revenue growth. You will become familiar with Aon’s tools and approach and present Aon’s solutions and services to our clients. You will be responsible for producing and presenting high-quality actuarial analyses with keen attention to detail to help clients make strategic reinsurance buying decisions, while also building relationships internally and externally. You should have an aptitude for problem solving, be able to manage multiple project deadlines at once, and effectively communicate the results of your analyses to clients and brokers.

Senior Risk Specialist, Compliance Governance Analyst The Compliance Governance, Oversight and Validation (CGOV) team within Compliance & Ethics is seeking a collaborative, analytically-focused risk management professional to support our well-managed governance processes and protocols. This role is ideal for an individual who is adaptable to change, who embraces bold ideas and who is intellectually curious; and who is proficient in utilizing Google Suite, AI tools, Data solutions, and advanced risk methodologies to drive organizational effectiveness and maintain business integrity. You will work closely with cross-functional teams to carry out key governance activities, collect and analyze data, and implement meaningful process improvements. This position offers the opportunity to serve as a strategic partner to senior leaders by implementing and supporting Compliance Management Program methodologies , assessing risks, and serving as an ambassador for innovative, AI-driven workflows. This role requires highly motivated Risk Management professionals with excellent organizational and communication skills. General Responsibilities may include but are not limited to: Coordinate Compliance Processes and Governance: Develop, coordinate, and mature internal processes and program governance, ensuring the efficiency and sustainability of key programs and initiatives. Monitor day-to-day CGOV operations to ensure adherence to standards and methodologies, while proactively identifying and implementing process improvements and redesign initiatives. Establish and Manage Engagement Structures: Set up and maintain milestone meetings, folder organization, communication channels, and meeting minutes to ensure effective collaboration and communication. Create meeting templates, coordinate template distribution, manage content inputs, and provide meeting facilitation support. Resolve Impediments and Support Compliance Organizational Priorities: Resolve or escalate impediments to deliver on top priorities in a timely and efficient manner, and assist in managing key deliverables across various initiatives. Audit/Exam Support: Assist with Compliance related audits and exams, engage in pre-planning, prepare and distribute status reports, track requests, timelines, milestones, and progress for key stakeholders. Design, implement and monitor relevant audit/exam remediation activities. Dashboard Governance: Manage intake requests for new or decommissioned Compliance dashboards; create and monitor new dashboards. Facilitate Reporting and Analytics: Support the gathering of insights and data, prepare written reports, and deliver oral presentations on identified issues and recommended remediation actions. Perform end-to-end outcome analysis, including the creation of relevant reporting updates to help leadership understand risk implications. Stakeholder Communic ation & Change Management: Communicate the results of assessments, process outcomes, refresher training, and issue management to ensure leadership remains informed on Compliance priorit ies and impactful change. Documentation: Support C&E Policies, Standards and Procedures (PSP) governance and create and maintain job aids to memorialize relevant processes and protocols. Tool Advocacy: Leverage and integrate AI tools—such as Gemini, Google Workspace, and NotebookLM—into daily workflows and serve as an ambassador to demonstrate their value to others. Monitoring & Quality Assurance: Design, implement and execute monitoring and quality assurance activities as needed to ensure consistency with expectation outlined in the Compliance Management Program. Collaboration: Participate in cross-functional project teams to provide input and partner with groups across the organization on larger strategic initiatives. Ideal Candidates: Are critical thinkers who seek to understand the business, regulatory, process, risk and control environment Use data analysis to drive insights and propose solutions Foster an environment that promotes trust and belonging Possess a relentless focus on quality and timeliness Develop collaborative relationships within and outside of immediate team, based upon shared objectives Do the right thing and lead by example Communicate in a clear and compelling way to influence individuals at all levels Basic Qualifications: High school diploma, GED, or equivalent certification At least 3 years of Compliance, Audit, or Risk Management experience At least 1 year of Financial Services experience At least 1 year of experience using Google Suite Preferred Qualifications: Bachelor’s Degree or Military experience At least 2 years of experience in Project Management, Process Management, or Relationship Management Process Management, Project Management or Risk Management Certification Proficient in use of query and data visualization tools (Tableau) Experience using AI tools (Gemini, Workspace Studio, Gemini Notebook, etc.) At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position.

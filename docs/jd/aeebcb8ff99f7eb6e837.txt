Proven ability to implement AI-powered solutions to solve business challenges. Demonstrates a growing awareness of AI risk management and a commitment to responsible and ethical AI use Education Minimum of an undergraduate degree in a quantitative discipline from a leading university Advanced degrees or professional certifications are strongly welcomed — data science qualifications, the Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) designation, project management credentials such as Project Management Professional (PMP), or equivalent certifications that demonstrate a commitment to excellence Responsibilities This is a newly created, high-profile role sitting at the heart of Moody's most urgent and exciting transformation — reimagining how credit intelligence is produced, delivered, and experienced through AI, digitalization, and structured project execution across four active workstreams. Support project management activities across three strategic workstreams within the Digitalization & Analytical Tools team — coordinating timelines, stakeholder accountability, and cross-functional dependencies to keep high-priority initiatives on track and on target Help shape and advance the digitalization strategy for Credit Strategy & Guidance, identifying bold opportunities to leverage Generative AI, automation, and modern data technologies to transform analytical insights Support the assessment of Credit Strategy & Guidance's GenAI and AI-driven analytical solution readiness — working with Moody's proprietary data and content as the foundation for next-generation tools Support the rollout of the Credit Strategy & Guidance data governance strategy — partnering directly with data owners, data stewards, and Technology teams to build a trusted, high-quality data foundation for AI deployment Define and monitor performance frameworks for analytical data and content, measuring engagement, quality, and risk to ensure continuous improvement and accountability across digital initiatives Serve as a critical connector across Moody's Ratings, Moody's Ratings Technology, and Moody's Ratings Research — bridging credit expertise and technology innovation to accelerate the delivery of AI-powered insights About the Team This is your opportunity to join one of the most strategically important teams at Moody's. The Digitalization & Analytical Tools team is a newly expanding function within the Digital Content, Data & Analytics group — a sub-team of Credit Strategy & Guidance — and sits at the center of Moody's transformation agenda. Operating as a project-driven environment, the team actively manages three concurrent workstreams spanning AI readiness, data governance, research digitalization, and analytical capability building. Credit Strategy & Guidance connects the dots from macro to sector to issuer and instrument, across regions and asset classes, providing thought leadership in analytics, models, research, market engagement, and regulatory oversight. By joining this team, you will not just witness that transformation — you will help architect it, working alongside some of the brightest analytical and technology minds in global financial markets, right from our New York City headquarters.

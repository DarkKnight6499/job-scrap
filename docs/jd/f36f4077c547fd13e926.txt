Job Description: The Special Assets Group (SAG) manages the Bank’s criticized, classified, and distressed commercial credit portfolio. SAG’s primary objective is to maximize recoveries, minimize loss severity, and execute disciplined, risk-based resolution strategies that protect the Bank’s interests while preserving long-term enterprise value where appropriate. The (SAG) Corp/Comm Senior Portfolio Officer is responsible for managing and resolving the Bank’s most complex criticized, classified, and distressed commercial credit relationships. The Sr. Portfolio Officer develops and executes resolution strategies, leads negotiations with borrowers and stakeholders, and provides subject matter expertise on complex credit situations. This role partners closely with senior leadership, Credit Risk, Legal, and line-of-business partners to drive resolution outcomes that maximize recoveries, minimize losses, and protect the Bank’s interests. Manage a portfolio of highly complex criticized, classified, and distressed commercial credit relationships through all stages of the resolution process. Develop and execute credit resolution strategies designed to maximize recoveries, minimize loss severity, and protect the Bank’s interests. Assess borrower performance, collateral support, guarantor strength, industry conditions, and legal considerations to determine the optimal path to resolution. Lead negotiations involving restructurings, repayment plans, settlements, discounted payoffs, collateral dispositions, note sales, and other recovery-focused solutions. Prepare and present strategy recommendations, credit approvals, and portfolio updates to senior leadership and risk management partners. Serve as the primary point of contact for borrowers, sponsors, attorneys, consultants, participating lenders, and other stakeholders associated with assigned relationships. Manage bankruptcy, litigation, foreclosure, receivership, and other legal recovery actions to maximize recoveries and minimize losses. Monitor portfolio performance, recovery trends, and risk migration to identify emerging issues and drive timely action. Support portfolio forecasting, reserve discussions, criticized asset reporting, and other portfolio management activities. Coach and mentor junior associates on the team to help develop credit and corporate workout skills. Serve as a resource on complex credit situations and contribute to initiatives that strengthen SAG processes, risk management practices, and portfolio performance.

About the Role Hut 8 is developing large-scale energy and digital infrastructure, with a growing pipeline of data center campuses and power projects. We are seeking a FP&A — Development professional to own the financial picture of our development portfolio across the full project lifecycle — from initial estimate through project completion — and to answer the question that matters most: how did we actually perform against what we underwrote? This is an analytically demanding, high-ownership role. You will publish recurring reporting on the development portfolio, build and maintain cost estimates and estimate-to-actual analyses, and serve as a trusted finance partner to both the Corporate Development team and the Energy Origination (EO) team. You'll be expected to work in modern tooling — including visualization platforms and AI tools — to make reporting faster, sharper, and more decision-ready. Key Responsibilities Portfolio Analysis & Reporting Publish recurring reports on the development portfolio — budgets, forecasts, commitments, spend, and variances — on a consistent weekly/monthly cadence. Perform portfolio-level analysis across projects: capital deployment, cost trends, benchmarking across sites, and scenario/sensitivity analysis to support leadership decisions. Build clear, decision-ready dashboards and visualizations in Power BI, Tableau, or AI tools; continuously improve reporting so insights are self-serve and current. Cost Estimating & Project Lifecycle Ownership Own the financial view of the full development lifecycle: develop and maintain cost estimates at each stage, track them through execution, and close the loop with estimate-vs-actual analysis at project commercialization. Serve as cost estimator for development-stage projects, working with Development, Construction, and Procurement to build defensible, well-documented estimates. Feed lessons learned from completed projects back into estimating assumptions, benchmarks, and unit-cost libraries to improve accuracy over time. Business Partnership Act as a finance partner to the Corporate Development team and the Energy Origination (EO) team — supporting planning cycles, reforecasts, capital approvals, and ad hoc analysis. Reconcile project data between PMIS and NetSuite to ensure a single, accurate source of truth for project financials. Support monthly close, accruals, and capital reporting in partnership with Accounting. Tools, AI & Continuous Improvement Actively use AI tools to accelerate analysis, reporting, and documentation — and identify new ways to automate manual workflows. Improve and standardize templates, models, and reporting processes across the development portfolio. Travel to project sites (~10% of time) to stay grounded on site progress and validate assumptions behind the numbers. Qualifications 2+ years of experience in FP&A, project finance, cost estimating, project controls, or portfolio analytics — ideally supporting real estate development, data centers, energy, or large capital projects. Strong analytical skills with demonstrated experience in portfolio analysis, cost estimating, budgeting and estimate-vs-actual (lookback) analysis. Excellent communication skills; able to publish polished reporting and present findings to corporate and operations stakeholders. Comfort operating with ownership and autonomy across the full lifecycle of a project. Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Engineering, Construction Management, or a related field (or equivalent experience). Willingness to travel ~10% to project sites. Systems & Tools Visualization: Power BI, Tableau, or other tools — able to build and maintain dashboards independently. PMIS: Experience with project management information systems preferred (e.g., Procore, Kahua, e-Builder, Unifier, or similar). ERP: NetSuite preferred; comparable ERP/accounting platforms acceptable. Excel: Advanced proficiency — complex models, pivot tables, lookups. AI: Demonstrated, practical use of AI tools in day-to-day analytical and reporting work.

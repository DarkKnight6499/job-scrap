POSITION SUMMARY: The Senior Analyst, FP&A – Network Consolidation & Management Process supports the end-to-end financial consolidation of the Network P&L and manages the operational cadence of the Finance management process within CVS Health’s Pharmacy Services business. Reporting to the Manager, FP&A Fees, Credits, GTs, Legislative & Network Consolidation, this role aggregates and reconciles revenue, COGS, fees, and credits across all network segments into a complete and accurate Network financial view. The Sr. Analyst also o wns calendar management and process coordination for close, budget, and forecast cycles, ensuring timely and high-quality outputs across the team. PRIMARY DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES: 30% - Supports the consolidation of the Network P&L – aggregating and reconciling revenue, COGS, fees, and credits across all network segments into an executive-ready financial view. 25% - Manages the Finance management process calendar, coordinating deliverable timelines across the Network Finance team for monthly close, budget, and forecast cycles. 25% - Prepares consolidated reporting packages and management presentations, ensuring accuracy and reconciliation across all Network P&L components. 15% - Identifies and resolves consolidation variances and reconciling items across network segments; escalates material issues to the Manager. 5% - Supports ad-hoc analytical requests and special projects as directed by the Manager.

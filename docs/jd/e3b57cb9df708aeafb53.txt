Your Role Develop, enhance and support post-NAV reporting products and tools, working from defined requirements and with guidance from more senior team members. Support existing reporting technologies by investigating issues raised through the IT Ticketing System, developing an understanding of the underlying processes and code, and implementing appropriate solutions with support where required. Contribute to process improvements and automation using technologies including Excel, Power Query, VBA, Python and other relevant development tools. Complete assigned development and support activities to agreed requirements, communicating progress and raising questions or blockers promptly. Test your own development changes in line with established testing processes, ensuring work is accurate and complete before independent review by more senior team members. Maintain appropriate technical, process and user documentation for changes you develop or support. Work with senior team members and end users to understand defined business requirements and translate these into technical changes and solutions. Participate in technical discussions and development reviews, applying feedback and established development standards to continuously improve the quality of your work. Communicate clearly with team members and end users when investigating issues, delivering changes or providing support. Develop your technical knowledge through hands-on experience, training and ongoing learning across the technologies and reporting products used within the team. Use AI tools and emerging technologies, where appropriate, to support development, troubleshooting and problem-solving, while maintaining appropriate oversight and attention to accuracy. About You Relevant professional or educational experience working with data-processing, reporting, automation or development tools, with evidence of strong technical capability and an ability to learn new technologies. Strong Microsoft Excel skills, together with experience in programming, automation or data processing. Experience with VBA and Power Query is beneficial but not essential. Experience with at least one programming language, or evidence of strong programming fundamentals and an ability to learn new technologies. Basic knowledge of SQL and relational data concepts. Strong learning agility, with an interest in developing your technical skills and working with new technologies. A proactive approach to learning and problem-solving, with the confidence to investigate unfamiliar issues, ask questions and seek guidance when required. Good analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to investigate technical issues logically and work towards appropriate solutions. Strong attention to detail, with a focus on accuracy, consistency and quality. Good written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with both technical colleagues and business users. Experience within asset management, audit, hedge funds, fund administration or another financial institution would be an advantage, but is not required. Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Management Information Systems, Commerce, Accounting, Finance, Economics or a related discipline, with relevant programming coursework or experience. An interest in AI and emerging technologies and a willingness to learn how they can be used effectively and responsibly within development activities. Ability to travel as necessary.

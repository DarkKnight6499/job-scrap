Structured Finance Operations Manager
United Community Bank
Location: Greenville, SC, US
Link: https://careers-ucbi.icims.com/jobs/2493/structured-finance-operations-manager/job

Overview The Structured Finance Operations Manager (Operations Manager) has responsibility for the leadership, oversight, and continuous improvement of the Structured Finance Group’s operational infrastructure, collateral monitoring processes, borrowing base administration, portfolio servicing functions, operational risk controls, and ABL technology platforms. The Operations Manager assists in the formulation, recommendation, implementation, and ongoing enhancement of operational policies and procedures. The Operations Manager maintains a thorough understanding of all collateral analysis and operational functions and provides leadership, support, and backup coverage for key departmental responsibilities as necessary. The Operations Manager also serves as a strategic partner to the other members of the ABL team as well as Relationship Managers, Portfolio Managers, Credit Officers, Treasury Management, Loan Operations, and other internal stakeholders to support growth, risk management, and operational excellence across the Structured Finance portfolio.

INVESTMENT RISK MANAGER – CORPORATE CREDIT This is a HYBRID position which requires 3 days per week in our Ft. Lauderdale office. About the Department Investment Risk Management provides independent oversight and forward-looking guidance across fixed income. The team partners with investment professionals, senior leaders, and client-facing colleagues to strengthen portfolio construction and risk governance. This senior role covers public and private credit, including collateralized loan obligations. You will gain broad exposure to global credit strategies while influencing investment, client, and governance decisions. How You Will Add Value You will lead monthly and ad hoc credit risk reviews. You will evaluate concentration, liquidity, leverage, spread duration, and downside exposure. You will develop stress tests for defaults, recoveries, refinancing, and liquidity shocks. You will connect issuer-level risks with macroeconomic themes and funding conditions. You will assess mandates, risk budgets, guidelines, benchmarks, and portfolio peers. You will provide credible challenge during client, investment, and governance discussions. You will improve risk dashboards, attribution, monitoring, and model-output validation. What Will Help You Be Successful in This Role Experience, Education & Certifications Bachelor’s degree: a technical field is preferred. Minimum of 7 years of relevant investment or credit risk experience. Demonstrated deep corporate credit expertise and strong investment judgment. Advanced credentials are strongly preferred, including an MBA, CFA, and/or FRM. Technical Skills Apply advanced credit, capital structure, and credit derivatives knowledge. Strong proficiency with Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, Aladdin, Bloomberg PORT, and/or comparable platforms. Experience with Structured Query Language, Python, or visualization tools is preferred. Soft Skills Communicate complex risks clearly to senior investment professionals and clients. Exercise sound judgment, attention to detail, and independent ownership. Prioritize effectively while managing risk coverage from beginning to end.

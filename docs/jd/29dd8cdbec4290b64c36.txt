The Compensation Forecasting & Reporting professional for Apollo Global Management is responsible for the monthly business segment compensation forecast update, operational support in preparing the annual compensation budget and long-term plan as well as ad hoc reporting and other projects. The position partners closely with the Strategic Finance, Human Capital, and Compensation Finance teams to define analytics necessary to inform the business on all matters pertaining to compensation expense budgeting and forecasting processes. The position sits within Finance.

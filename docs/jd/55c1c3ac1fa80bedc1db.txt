As a Senior Underwriting Consultant - Internal Auditor, you will be part of the US Life Underwriting Risk Management and Audit team, providing independent and objective quality assurance of underwriting decisions and processes. This role helps ensure underwriting guidelines are applied consistently, risk is managed appropriately, and opportunities for continuous improvement and training are identified. The ideal candidate brings deep life insurance underwriting expertise, strong critical thinking skills, and the ability to evaluate complex underwriting decisions while collaborating effectively with business partners and stakeholders. The role is remote and can be based anywhere in the continental US. Position Responsibilities: Conduct independent audits and quality reviews of life insurance underwriting decisions, documentation, and processes. Evaluate underwriting assessments against established guidelines, procedures, and best practices. Assess medical, non-medical, financial, compliance, retention, and reinsurance decisions for appropriateness and consistency. Identify trends, gaps, and opportunities for process improvement and underwriter development. Recommend training and educational initiatives based on audit findings. Participate actively in team discussions, providing objective feedback and professional opinions on audit results. Collaborate with Underwriting, Medical, and Risk Management teams. Contribute to innovation, continuous improvement, and the evolution of the underwriting audit program. Support targeted audits of specialized case types and emerging business initiatives. Required Qualifications: Minimum 5 years of life insurance underwriting experience. Expert knowledge of medical, financial, and non-medical underwriting principles and practices. Strong understanding of life insurance risk assessment and underwriting guidelines. Strong analytical, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills. Excellent written, verbal, presentation, and interpersonal communication skills. Ability to work independently while managing multiple priorities. Strong attention to detail and organizational skills. Ability to influence and collaborate effectively with stakeholders at all levels. Preferred Qualifications: Experience auditing, reviewing, or coaching underwriting decisions. Knowledge of reinsurance treaties, retention limits, and marketplace practices. Experience with compliance and quality assurance programs. Strong digital literacy and proficiency with Microsoft 365 applications, including Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and Teams; comfortable adopting new technology and leveraging digital tools to improve efficiency and reporting.

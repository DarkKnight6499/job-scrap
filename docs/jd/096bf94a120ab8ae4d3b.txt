POSITION SUMMARY: Oceanview Life and Annuity Company is seeking an analytical and highly motivated Associate, Strategy and Financial Planning to report directly to the CFO and work closely with the CEO on financial planning, forecasting, and strategic initiatives, including mergers and acquisitions (M&A). The Associate will maintain and enhance the Company's financial projections, develop budgets and scenario analyses, and evaluate financial performance to provide actionable insights to the CFO, CEO, senior management, and Board of Directors. The role will also play an integral part in evaluating M&A opportunities, capital raising, new business initiatives, competitive research, and other high-priority strategic projects. This is an opportunity to directly influence Oceanview's strategic direction and key business decisions while gaining broad exposure to its financial and operational activities. The Associate will have regular interaction with executive leadership and collaborate with teams across Oceanview and Bayview Asset Management, with additional opportunities for exposure to Controller and Treasury functions. RESPONSIBILITIES: WHAT YOU'LL DO Financial Planning, Forecasting & Analysis · Maintain and update the Company’s financial projections and underlying financial models, ensuring accuracy, consistency, and alignment with business assumptions. · Support annual budgeting and periodic forecasting processes, including coordination of inputs and analysis of key financial drivers. · Develop alternative scenarios, sensitivity analyses, and financial forecasts to evaluate potential business outcomes and strategic decisions. · Analyze actual financial results against budgets and forecasts, identify key variances, and communicate findings and implications to senior management. · Prepare financial analyses and decision-support materials for the CFO, CEO, and Board of Directors. Corporate Strategy, M&A & Special Projects · Support the evaluation of potential mergers, acquisitions, partnerships, capital raising opportunities, and new business initiatives through financial modeling, research, and analysis. · Conduct market, industry, product, and competitive research to inform strategic planning and business development decisions. · Partner with cross-functional project teams to assess strategic alternatives, financial implications, and execution considerations. · Participate in discussions with senior management regarding business performance, strategic priorities, and growth opportunities. Executive Reporting & Cross-Functional Collaboration · Develop and review presentations, analyses, and supporting materials for internal leadership, the Board of Directors, and external stakeholders. · Translate complex financial information into clear, concise, and actionable insights for financial and non-financial audiences. · Collaborate with Finance, Accounting, Treasury, and other teams to validate assumptions, gather information, and improve planning processes. · Identify opportunities to enhance financial models, reporting processes, and analytical capabilities as the business grows. WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR · Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, or a related field. · 1–3 years of relevant experience in corporate finance, financial planning and analysis, accounting, investment banking, equity research, or a related analytical role. · Strong foundation in financial statements, accounting principles, and financial modeling, with the ability to build and interpret integrated financial projections. · Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and strong working knowledge of Microsoft PowerPoint. · Demonstrated ability to analyze financial information, assess assumptions, and develop well-supported recommendations. · Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to present findings clearly to senior stakeholders. · Ability to work independently, manage multiple priorities, and escalate key questions or risks appropriately. · Experience with M&A analysis, valuation, insurance, or financial services is a plus. SKILLS FOR SUCCESS · Strong analytical curiosity, sound judgment, and a solutions-oriented mindset. · High level of accuracy and attention to detail when working with financial data and models. · Ownership mentality and enthusiasm for contributing to a growing organization and a lean, collaborative team. · Ability to move between detailed analysis and broader strategic considerations. · Comfort interacting with executives and colleagues across multiple functions and levels of the organization. · Adaptability, initiative, and the ability to perform effectively in a fast-paced environment. · Professionalism, integrity, and discretion when handling confidential business and financial information.

Job Description: Job Title: Quant Trader- E- Trading Municipal Bonds Corporate Title: Director Location: New York, NY Overview The Municipals e-Trading team that is part of the Rates Trading division is looking to hire a senior quant researcher focusing on automated electronic trading of Municipal bonds. The focus of the role is on automated pricing, trading, and hedging for Municipal bonds both in the customer (dealer to client) and interdealer (dealer to dealer) markets. The role is highly technical, quantitative, and focused on idea generation and data analysis, requiring coding skills and a strong analytical background including statistics. Experience in automated market making in flow products is essential, domain specific knowledge in Municipal bonds is an advantage.

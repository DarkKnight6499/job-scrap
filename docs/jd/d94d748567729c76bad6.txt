Who You’ll Work With: The Senior Associate, Transaction Management will be responsible for the diligence review and closing of both flow and bulk residential loan acquisitions for AB CarVal Loan Portfolios. This individual will work closely with a wide variety of parties both within AB CarVal and externally. This position will report to the Director, Investment Operations. The role will be located in Minnesota. What You’ll Do: Transaction Management – 70% Responsible for loan re-underwriting, analysis, presentation and submission process for settlement Collaborate with the AB CarVal Loan Portfolios commercial team and external partners Ensure compliance with closing schedules and document submissions Assist with maintenance of office pipeline reports Assist in managing the timely receipt and delivery of all third-party reports and documents Provide day-to-day operational input on residential loan transactions Quickly evaluate complex situations that arise and use good judgement to propose solutions Business Partnering – 30% Make judgement-based decisions related to the operations function and assume responsibility for the impact to the business Understand key areas of the residential loan investment transactions/business and anticipate potential issues; proactively communicate solutions to others within the team or management What We’re Looking For: Required Bachelor’s degree in Real Estate or Finance, or equivalent education and related training 5+ years of direct transaction management experience Excellent organizational skills and drive to succeed Demonstrated ability to meet deadlines Maturity and self-confidence to simultaneously handle a variety of assignments Ability to work harmoniously with internal/external workers/customers Excellent written and verbal communication skills Prior history of working in a high-volume loan production office Who We Are: AB CarVal is an established global alternative investment manager and part of AllianceBernstein’s Private Alternatives business. Since 1987, AB CarVal’s team has navigated through ever-changing credit market cycles, investing $155 billion in 5,850 transactions across 82 countries. Today, AB CarVal has approximately $20 billion in assets under management. The firm has a 38-year history, strong track record and an established reputation in asset-based finance with a team of approximately 74 investment professionals in the US, Europe and Asia. A key differentiator is the firm’s robust global network of established relationships with operating platforms and partners. Whether you're producing thought-provoking research, identifying compelling investment opportunities, infusing new technologies into our business or providing thoughtful advice to our clients, we are fully invested in you.

AVP, Nuveen Green Capital Accounting Controller The AVP / Controller will be the senior accounting professional managing accounting operations and supervising a team of accounting professionals in record keeping, reporting, and other accounting functions, including treasury, for Nuveen’s rapidly growing Green Capital sustainable commercial real estate lending business. This job will partner directly with Nuveen senior management as well as the Nuveen Green Capital investment, asset management and capital markets teams to manage the ongoing growth and expansion of the accounting process, systems, and team. Key Responsibilities and Duties Coordinates the day-to-day general accounting activities within Nuveen Green Capital to ensure the timely preparation of US GAAP financial statements. Manages the ongoing, monthly, quarterly, and annual journal entry process through accounting close, including the preparation of ad hoc journals for asset and debt investment transactions. Enforces the preparation of timely and accurate US GAAP work papers and supporting schedules to satisfy all investment accounting requirements for internal and external audits. Oversees the reconciliation of balance sheet, profit & loss, bank accounts, etc. to confirm that statements are correctly recorded. Reviews of accounting reports such as financial statements, cash flow reports, budget reports, etc. for internal and external audiences. Works closely with internal and external auditors, and other financial professionals ensuring that practices are in line with the overall goals of the organization. Manages various complex accounting calculations in Microsoft Excel as well as the Netsuite accounting environment. Oversees strategic initiatives and other reporting needs to include system upgrades, process improvements, implementation of new products, and asset management initiatives. Maintains investment accounting policies, procedures and control processes to ensure that policies are clear and understood by all team members Monitors performance of team through regular, timely feedback as well as the formal performance review process to ensure delivery of exceptional services and engagement, motivation, and development of team.

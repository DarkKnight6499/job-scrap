Fitch Ratings’ US Public Finance Local Government group is currently seeking an Analyst, or Senior Analyst, either in our New York, Austin, Chicago or San Fransico office. About the Team The US Public Finance Local Government Rating Group is responsible for assigning new and monitoring existing ratings on local government and special purpose district issuers throughout the United States. Security ratings are assigned to general obligation, lease revenue and dedicated tax and revenue bonds.

Associate Director, CEO's Office, EMEA
Standard Chartered
Location: New York, US
Link: https://jobs.standardchartered.com/job/New-York-Associate-Director%2C-CEO's-Office%2C-EMEA/1372594057/

Job Summary The Associate Director, CEO’s Office will be responsible to support the Europe, Middle East and Africa (EMEA) clusters and work closely with the Chief of Staff, EMEA in driving projects, and be able to assess and deliver multiple strategic deliverables across the EMEA CEO Office’s book of work. The role works closely with the Cluster CEOs and their Chiefs of Staff, Finance, Risk, Compliance, HR, CABM and COO teams as well as the other global governance teams. The role is inherently varied and reflects the changing demands and responsibilities of the CEO’s Office for Europe, Middle East & Africa. Key Responsibilities • Support the CEO’s office for EMEA to deliver all regional priorities across strategy, workflow efficiencies, cluster synergies, risk management & governance, and monitoring & transparency. • Support risk management and process improvements across EMEA • Support project delivery across EMEA • EMEA regulatory, structural and org knowledge • Provide critical business insights through data analysis. • Liaise with technology and operations teams to support process improvement, cost transparency, and digital enablement. • Support in providing oversight of regulatory compliance, risk controls, and audit readiness across EMEA. • Support preparation of papers / slides / speaking notes for submission for various meetings. • Coordinate budget reconciliation, and stakeholder communications to ensure alignment with group and business priorities. • Organise regular meetings for CEO’s top team, townhalls or other meetings to ensure close communication and alignment across the clusters. Regulatory & Business Conduct • Display exemplary conduct and live by the Group’s Values and Code of Conduct. • Take personal responsibility for embedding the highest standards of ethics, including regulatory and business conduct, across Standard Chartered Bank. This includes understanding and ensuring compliance with, in letter and spirit, all applicable laws, regulations, guidelines and the Group Code of Conduct. • Effectively and collaboratively identify, escalate, mitigate, and resolve risk, conduct and compliance matters. Key stakeholders • CEO, US & Americas and EMEA • Cluster CEO’s for Europe, Middle East & Pakistan, and Africa • Head, CIB Conduct & Geographical Projects and Gov. • Cluster Chiefs of Staff for Europe, Middle East & Pakistan, and Africa • Cluster Management Teams • Country Functions (HR, Finance, Legal, Risk, Compliance, Risk & Control, IMO, etc) Skills and Experience • Effective Communication • Planning, Tactical & Strategic • Project Management • Data Gathering & Reporting • Risk Management • Organisational Governance Qualifications • 4 to 5 years of Experience in at least two of operations, first-line risk, compliance or legal in FI’s. • Good understanding of Corporate & Investment Banking, and Wealth & Retail Banking • Good understanding of the SCB Network/Geographies • Understanding of Risk, Compliance, Governance and related matters • Prior experience in Project / Change Management preferred • Strong execution skills, ability to manage ambiguity, work on multiple projects and under tight timelines. • Take Initiative and drive tangible business outcomes.

We’re actively seeking a talented Senior Real Estate Portfolio Manager to join our Real Estate Investments team in Newport Beach, CA. As a Senior Real Estate Portfolio Manager you’ll play a key role in Pacific Life’s growth and long-term success by monitoring the credit quality and performance of commercial real estate loan assets to provide the greatest recovery to investors and superior service to borrowers. You will work with a team to review property financial statements, financial covenants, and various other reports to determine loan performance and identify broader portfolio trends. You will actively manage all loan servicing transactions for assigned loan portfolio. Frequent communication with borrowers associated with assigned loan portfolio is expected to maintain investor relationship. How you’ll help move us forward: Read loan documents and mortgage loan investment summaries to understand nature of complex loans to properly analyze and monitor the loan, as well as detect key requirements of the loan. Determine reporting requirements, loan covenants, and triggers. Set up associated ticklers in loan servicing system, create templates to perform the tests, and perform the tests at required intervals. Lead the servicing for you portfolio, including monthly payment collection, payoff/prepayment statement preparation, lease and property management agreement reviews, reserve requests, and analysis of complex loan transactions. Collect and analyze financial statements for Pacific Life portfolio loans. Determine and document causes for variances in property performance via discussions with borrowers and co-lenders (for participated loans). Monitor property rent rolls for early lease terminations and expiration of significant existing tenants. Recommend and assign risk rating classifications and monitor overall trends within assigned portfolio of loans and submarket(s). The experience you bring: 4-year college degree preferred 2-4 years' experience in commercial real estate or commercial loan servicing. Experience reviewing and interpreting property, borrower, and guarantor financial statements, and commercial mortgage loan documents. Strong knowledge of Excel and Word, and the ability to quickly learn new program applications. Excellent organizational skills, with the ability to manage multiple projects at once and to meet deadlines under pressure. Strong customer service, verbal, and written communication skills. Strong logical and analytical skills (ability to independently research, identify issues, and problem solve). Analyzes possible solutions using existing methods and prior experiences.

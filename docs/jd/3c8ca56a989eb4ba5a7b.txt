Job Summary As an Associate Medical Underwriter, you will evaluate evidence of insurability for employee benefit applicants and make risk-based underwriting decisions that balance customer needs with sound risk management. In this role, you will review medical information, apply underwriting guidelines and contract provisions, and partner with internal teams, field representatives, employers, and employees to deliver a positive customer experience. You'll exercise independent judgment, analyze medical and insurance-related information, and contribute to projects that support continuous improvement and team goals. Key Responsibilities Review medical evidence and determine insurability for employee benefit applicants. Apply underwriting guidelines, contract provisions, and regulatory requirements to risk decisions. Analyze medical histories, exam results, and related documentation to assess risk. Communicate underwriting decisions and information clearly to internal and external partners. Participate in projects and process improvement initiatives that support team performance and customer experience. Skills and Background You'll Need Education: High school diploma or equivalent required. Experience: 2+ years of experience in claims, employee benefits, insurance, or a related field required. Basic understanding of risk analysis and medical underwriting philosophy, and an interest in learning about and analyzing medical information. Ability to interpret and apply underwriting guidelines, insurance contracts, and applicable regulations. Strong written and verbal communication skills with a customer-focused approach. Experience reviewing, analyzing, or interpreting medical information preferred. Medical underwriting experience preferred.

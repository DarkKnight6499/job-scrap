The Analyst/Senior Analyst will be part of the Data Strategy & Analytics team dedicated to helping American Express leverage AI, advanced analytics, and data-driven insights to solve complex business challenges and transform risk management and data capabilities. Partner with business stakeholders to identify high-value data use cases, define success metrics, and translate business needs into analytical solutions. Analyze large, complex datasets to generate actionable insights and identify opportunities using AI and advanced analytics applications. Develop dashboards, metrics, and reporting frameworks to measure data initiative performance, business impact, adoption, and operational effectiveness. Evaluate emerging AI technologies, tools, and external partnerships to identify opportunities that strengthen business capabilities and innovation. Drive continuous improvement by identifying process automation opportunities using AI, predictive analytics, and intelligent workflow solutions. Communicate analytical findings and strategic recommendations to business leaders through clear presentations and executive-ready documentation. Work effectively in a dynamic, cross-functional, and global environment while managing multiple initiatives and priorities.

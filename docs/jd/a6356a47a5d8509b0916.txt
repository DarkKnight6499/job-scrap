The Capital Markets team is the funding engine for Upstart’s AI lending marketplace. We partner with leading institutional investors across a diverse range of financing and funding solutions including securitizations, whole loan sales, co-investment partnerships and other structured transactions. The Institutional Capital team has built a durable capital franchise with a track record of over $10+B of funding capacity closed. We own Upstart’s whole loan relationships from lead generation to deal close across forward flow and back book transactions. Upstart’s funding engine starts with this team. We connect product innovation and borrower demand with scalable, risk-adjusted capital. As the Capital Markets Manager - Institutional Capital, you will play a critical role in shaping our overall funding strategy. You are comfortable driving deals from inception through execution, bring deep expertise in structured finance, and approach established concepts with first-principles thinking. You have cultivated a strong industry network and are a genuine, trusted partner to both internal and external stakeholders. You thrive in nimble, fast-paced environments, with an exceptional ability to bring clarity and structure to complexity. We are looking for someone who shares our passion for harnessing financial innovation and capital markets to expand access to credit at a massive scale.

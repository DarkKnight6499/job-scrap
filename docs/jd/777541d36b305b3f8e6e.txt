Job Description Senior Analyst, BioCatch FP&A Position Summary The Senior Analyst, BioCatch FP&A will play a key role in supporting the financial planning, forecasting, reporting, and strategic decision-making activities of the BioCatch business. This individual will partner closely with BioCatch leadership, Visa finance teams, and cross-functional stakeholders to deliver actionable financial insights, drive operational excellence, and support growth initiatives. In addition to core FP&A responsibilities, this role will serve as a critical contributor to the FP&A integration of BioCatch into Visa, helping to align planning processes, reporting frameworks, forecasting methodologies, and performance management practices. The ideal candidate is highly analytical, detail-oriented, proactive, and comfortable operating in a fast-paced, dynamic environment. Key Responsibilities Financial Planning & Forecasting Support the monthly forecast, annual operating plan, and long-range planning processes for the BioCatch business. Build and maintain financial models for revenue, operating expenses, headcount, and investment planning. Analyze actual results against forecast and budget, identifying key drivers, risks, and opportunities. Support scenario analysis and sensitivity modeling to inform business decisions. FP&A Integration Leadership Serve as a key finance representative supporting the post-close integration of BioCatchs FP&A processes into Visa's finance operating model. Lead workstreams related to planning, forecasting, management reporting, and KPI alignment. Partner with Corporate Finance, Accounting, HR, Strategy, and other teams to standardize financial reporting and planning processes. Help develop and implement new reporting frameworks, forecasting tools, and management dashboards. Drive process improvements and identify opportunities to automate and streamline financial reporting activities. Support integration-related analyses, synergy tracking, and executive reporting requirements. Business Performance Analysis Monitor business performance across revenue, expenses, profitability, customer growth, retention, and operational metrics. Develop insightful analyses and recommendations that help leadership drive business results. Create executive-level reports, dashboards, and presentations that clearly communicate financial performance. Partner with business leaders to understand performance drivers and emerging trends. Management Reporting Prepare monthly and quarterly business reviews for BioCatch and Visa leadership teams. Consolidate financial results and deliver accurate and timely reporting packages. Support variance analysis and preparation of executive commentary. Ensure consistency and accuracy across all financial reporting deliverables. Strategic Finance Support Support business case development for product investments, strategic initiatives, pricing decisions, and growth opportunities. Conduct ROI analysis, investment evaluations, and financial impact assessments. Assist with post-acquisition value creation tracking and performance measurement. Provide analytical support for leadership reviews and strategic planning discussions. Cross-Functional Partnership Partner with Product, Sales, Operations, HR, Accounting, and Strategy teams to understand business performance and financial implications. Act as a trusted finance partner by delivering data-driven insights and recommendations. Support finance transformation and continuous improvement initiatives across the organization. Qualifications Qualifications Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, or a related field. 3-6 years of experience in FP&A, corporate finance, investment banking, consulting, accounting, or related analytical roles. Strong financial modeling, forecasting, and analytical skills. Advanced proficiency in Excel and financial analysis tools. Experience preparing management reporting and executive presentations. Strong problem-solving and communication skills. Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment. Preferred Qualifications Experience supporting software, SaaS, cybersecurity, fraud, risk, identity, fintech, or technology businesses. Experience supporting acquisitions, integration efforts, or finance transformation initiatives. Familiarity with subscription business models, ARR, retention, and growth metrics. Experience with enterprise planning and reporting systems. MBA, CFA, CPA, or progress toward a professional finance designation is a plus.

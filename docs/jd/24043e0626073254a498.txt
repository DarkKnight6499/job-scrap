Job Description: H&W Sr. Business Analyst The Role As a Sr. Business Analyst you will join a team of passionate Health and Welfare professionals committed to crafting business requirements that are crucial to our ability to serve as a directed record keeper for our plan sponsors. You will serve as a requirements expert and are responsible for providing business analysis support for client advised projects to ensure that the systems are functioning according to the detailed business requirements. Your interactions with the team will model Fidelity leadership principles to sustain an innovative culture and build a positive environment for your team. The Expertise and Skills You Bring We’re looking for candidates with a Bachelor’s degree or equivalent years of industry experience, 5-7 years of Business Analysis experience, at least 5 years of direct experience within Health and Welfare Annual Enrollment and 5-7 years of benefits administration, outsourcing administration (H&W) or similar experience. Interested candidates should be customer obsessed; and demonstrate the ability to balance customer service and sound business judgment. Eligible candidates would possess the following essential skills: Strong organizational, time management, facilitation, and prioritization skills The ability to critically analyze complex business requirements/product issues Proven understanding of Fidelity’s end-to-end business process life cycle, data process flows, and plan level provisions is desired In-depth understanding of core product mechanics, record keeping systems and regulatory environment Strong critical thinking skills Strong technical skills and an ability to apply them in an analytical process Demonstrates strong communication skills (written and verbal) Ability to develop, follow, and execute documented procedures Experience in Agile preferred, but not required Advanced skills in Microsoft Office applications The Team As a valued member of the Project Delivery Team, you would partner with the Client Delivery Portfolio Manager and Healthcare Program Managers to understand the goals and the work for each project milestone.

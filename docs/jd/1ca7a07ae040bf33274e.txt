J.P. Morgan Natural Capital is seeking an Associate-level Investment Specialist / Investor Relations professional to support fundraising, business development, and client service across the Natural Capital platform. As the Global Business Development team’s dedicated Investor Relations professional, you will help execute investor engagement, prepare fundraising materials, and support the platform’s rebrand and upcoming fund launch. About the Natural Capital Team J.P. Morgan Natural Capital is a leading investment manager within J.P. Morgan Asset Management’s global Alternatives platform, with more than four decades of experience managing sustainable forests and related natural assets. As of December 31, 2025, the platform oversaw approximately USD 10.9 billion in assets under supervision and 1.5 million acres (approximately 600,000 hectares) globally. You will work alongside a team of approximately 145 colleagues across the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, and Latin America. Our multidisciplinary team combines investment expertise with on-the-ground asset management, offering exposure across the natural capital investment lifecycle and the opportunity to support investment, environmental, and community outcomes. Job Summary As an Associate supporting J.P. Morgan Natural Capital, you will help drive high-quality investor engagement and responsiveness through the creation and maintenance of marketing and due diligence content, coordination of RFP/DDQ processes, management of investor queries, and support for client meetings and fundraising pitches. You will work closely with the Investment Specialist team, portfolio management, JPMorgan Asset Management & Wealth Management distribution channels, and partner teams globally. You will also collaborate with Product Strategy, Marketing/Communications, Market Insights, Sustainable Investment, and subject matter experts to develop and refresh intellectual capital and client materials, including fact sheets, data room content, and messaging aligned to the rebrand and product roadmap.

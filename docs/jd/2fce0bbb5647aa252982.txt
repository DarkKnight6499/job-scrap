Purpose Contributes to the overall success of the FICC Index team and the Global Analytics and Financial Engineering team ensuring specific individual goals, plans, initiatives are executed / delivered in support of the team’s business strategies and objectives. Ensures all activities conducted are in compliance with governing regulations, internal policies and procedures Accountabilities Design, develop, and maintain a robust cross-asset index back-testing and simulation framework capable of supporting futures, equities, fixed income, FX, commodities, and multi-asset investment strategies. Build and enhance the infrastructure of the QIS Index platform, including calculation engines, risk decomposition tools, performance attribution, and portfolio analytics capabilities. Develop quantitative libraries and services to support index construction, rebalancing, execution modeling, transaction cost analysis, and strategy evaluation. Partner with Structuring, Trading, and Research teams to implement and productionize systematic investment strategies and custom client solutions. Create analytical tools for portfolio construction, factor analysis, scenario analysis, stress testing, and risk management. Design automated workflows for trade decomposition, hedge generation, booking integration, and operational monitoring of QIS products. Integrate market data sources and pricing services required for strategy research and index calculations. Develop dashboards and monitoring tools to support production processes, data quality controls, and daily operational oversight of QIS strategies. Collaborate with technology teams to deliver scalable, production-grade solutions and improve release and deployment processes. Provide technical expertise and quantitative support to stakeholders across Trading, Risk Management, Product Control, Audit, and Technology.

We are passionate about our business and our culture, and are seeking individuals with that same drive. About the Group: Moelis & Company’s Capital Structure Advisory (CSA) Group advises clients on complex capital structure matters, including liquidity enhancement, capital structure optimization, liability management, recapitalizations and restructurings. The Group works with publicly listed companies, financial sponsors and sponsor-owned businesses, institutional investors, boards and special committees, creditors, governments and other stakeholders. The Group helps clients evaluate and execute alternatives across both out-of-court and in-court situations, combining capital structure expertise with financial analysis and transaction execution. Working closely with the Firm’s industry bankers, capital markets specialists and M&A advisors, the team delivers integrated advice tailored to each client’s financial position and strategic objectives. Representative advisory areas include: Liquidity and capital structure: Liquidity enhancement strategies, capital structure optimization, capital raising and debt management advisory Liability management: Debt exchanges, consent solicitations, debt repurchases and negotiated recapitalizations Restructuring: Out-of-court, in-court and cross-border restructurings Asset monetization and special situations: Asset sales and acquisitions, including Section 363 sales, as well as expert testimony and litigation support For more information, please refer to: Moelis Capital Structure Advisory. Role Overview: We are seeking an experienced Analyst to join our Capital Structure Advisory team in Dallas, Texas. You will support assignments involving companies facing liquidity constraints, refinancing needs and complex capital structure challenges, as well as creditors and other stakeholders evaluating their alternatives. Your work will include financial modeling, liquidity and credit analysis, valuation, and research into debt instruments and restructuring transactions. You will prepare client materials and work closely with Associates and senior bankers to evaluate alternatives and support transaction execution. Key Responsibilities: Financial analysis and modeling: Build and maintain operating forecasts, cash flow projections and liquidity analyses to assess financial performance, funding requirements and downside scenarios Capital structure and credit analysis: Analyze debt instruments, maturities, interest obligations, collateral and covenant provisions to support assessments of debt capacity and refinancing risk Valuation and recovery analysis: Prepare enterprise valuation, capital structure waterfall and creditor recovery analyses under alternative transaction and restructuring scenarios Strategic alternatives: Support the evaluation of liability management transactions, recapitalizations, capital raises, asset sales and in-court and out-of-court restructurings Company and market research: Research company performance, debt trading levels, credit markets and precedent restructuring transactions to inform client recommendations Transaction materials: Prepare client presentations, financial analyses and supporting materials that clearly communicate capital structure issues and available alternatives Execution support: Assist with due diligence, data rooms, information requests and process trackers;

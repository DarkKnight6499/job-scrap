Role Summary: The Risk Manager protects the firm’s capital and operational baseline by proactively identifying, quantifying, and mitigating high-stakes uncertainties inherent in complex, multi-million dollar civil infrastructure projects. Civil Construction Focus Areas: ERM Frameworks: Design and deploy project-level Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) frameworks to address geotechnical uncertainties, supply chain disruptions, and labor constraints. Quantitative Modeling: Conduct financial modeling and probabilistic schedule/cost risk analyses (such as Monte Carlo simulations) to quantify risk exposure on major civil bids. Mitigation & Registers: Partner with project directors to maintain robust risk registers, constructability review inputs, and proactive contingency drawdown plans. Key Qualifications & Preferred Experience: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Civil Engineering, Construction Management, Risk Management, or Finance. 5+ years of experience. Deep understanding of construction contracts, alternative delivery methods (Design-Build, P3), and professional liabilities. The FTA Plus: Familiarity with FTA Risk Assessment methodologies (e.g., FTA Project Management Oversight guidelines for capital projects) and managing federally mandated contingency reserves.

About the Department The Franklin Innovation Research, Strategies and Technologies (FIRST) team serves as Franklin Templeton's hub for innovation, helping the firm identify, develop, and advance opportunities at the intersection of investment management and emerging technologies. The team plays a key role in shaping the firm's digital assets strategy and bringing innovative solutions to market, while fostering collaboration across business, investment, and technology functions. Through thought leadership, strategic initiatives, and experimentation with next-generation technologies, FIRST helps to position Franklin Templeton at the forefront of the evolving financial services landscape. The Emerging Solutions team within FIRST manages the earliest stage ideas through to MVP execution, identifying high-potential, innovative opportunities arising from industry trends, client demand, and technological change, and develops them into validated business cases and scalable offerings. Working in close partnership with investment, distribution, technology, and strategy teams as well as external partners, Emerging Solutions enables these initiatives to be implemented by FIRST and the broader organization. Through this work, the team helps Franklin Templeton to navigate a rapidly evolving industry landscape and lead in the development of next-generation solutions and capabilities.

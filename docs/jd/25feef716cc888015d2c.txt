About the Team Our sales and partner management teams are the heart and soul of DoorDash, helping us partner with top merchants (think restaurants, grocers, convenience stores, retailers) across the country to grow their revenue and help them operate even better. As DoorDash grows both in scale and scope of offering, the strength of our sales engine and organizational structure must grow with it. The Pre-Sales S&O team focuses on the acquisition of SMB restaurants across the United States, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand. About the Role As a Senior Associate on Sales Strategy & Operations, you'll take end-to-end ownership of the Canada SMB Inbound, Self-Serve Merchant Onboarding (SSMO), and Sales Development Representative (SDR) functions — the core engine behind restaurant acquisition for Canada. You will own the strategy, goaling, and forecasting infrastructure for these channels, manage relationships with stakeholders across sales, marketing, and vendor partners, and be accountable for day-to-day performance and ongoing improvement of the business. You'll be expected to operate with significant autonomy — identifying opportunities, shaping strategy, and leading initiatives that produce measurable outcomes — and you'll also help the team use AI and automation to scale its own productivity, building tools and workflows that create leverage well beyond your own workload. You will report into the Canada Pre-Sales RevOps Lead on our Pre-Sales S&O team in our Sales Strategy & Operations organization. You're excited about this opportunity because you will… Own – Take full ownership of the Inbound, SSMO, and SDR functions for Canada, including goaling, forecasting, and pacing infrastructure Build – Design and encourage adoption of new sales products, processes, and tools (including AI-powered workflows) that scale team productivity Strategize – Partner with Sales leadership to build effective, cost-efficient go-to-market strategies and structural changes to the sales motion Analyze – Build and maintain models (walk-to-goal, forecasting, ROI) that evaluate performance and surface opportunities for improvement Influence – Work across Merchant S&O, Sales, Analytics, Planning, and other Sales S&O teams to scale business impact, and represent Canada's needs in broader regional/global conversations We're excited about you because… You have a Bachelor's degree and 3+ years of experience in management consulting, tech, investment banking, corporate strategy, or business operations;

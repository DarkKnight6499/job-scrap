Requisition #: 76795 The Role at a Glance We are excited to bring on a Request for Proposal Associate to join our Operations team supporting Workplace Solutions! The Request for Proposal team plays a critical role in supporting Lincoln Financial Group’s Group Protection business by partnering with sales, underwriting, and client service teams to deliver accurate and timely Request for Proposal (RFP) responses. As an Associate, you will apply product & services knowledge to complete basic RFP responses that accurately and favorably represent LFG's products & services. You will position RFP responses based on discussions with selling team members to understand the client's needs. If this sounds like a role for you, please read on! What you'll be doing You will support proposal and renewal activities by partnering with teams across Underwriting, Client Services, Implementation, and other key business areas. You will gather, assess, and organize information needed to support broker and customer requests. You will educate brokers on Lincoln's products, service model, policies, and processes while helping connect them to the right resources. You will provide document, print, and proposal support for the Request for Proposal (RFP) team and broader Group Protection organization. You will identify opportunities to improve processes, reduce inefficiencies, and enhance the customer experience. You will contribute to special projects, business initiatives, and change management efforts while staying current on industry trends and best practices. What we’re looking for Must-have experience (Required): 4 Year/Bachelor's degree or equivalent work experience (4 years of experience in lieu of Bachelor's). 0 - 1+ Years experience with RFPs/RFIs that directly aligns with the specific responsibilities for this position Ability to communicate effectively (verbal/written). Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook Nice-to have experience (Preferred): Experience responding to RFPs, RFIs, questionnaires, or other client-facing business communications. Familiarity with employee benefits, insurance, financial services, or related industries. Experience working with proposal management, content management, CRM, or workflow platforms (e.g., Salesforce, SharePoint, Qvidian/Responsive, or similar technologies). Familiarity with AI-enabled productivity tools, such as Microsoft Copilot, and an interest in leveraging emerging technologies to improve efficiency, quality, and customer outcomes. Demonstrated curiosity, adaptability, and a continuous improvement mindset, including a willingness to explore new technologies, automate routine tasks where appropriate, and identify opportunities to enhance processes while maintaining a human-in-the-loop approach to review and decision-making.

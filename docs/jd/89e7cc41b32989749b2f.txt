Job Description: J ob Title: Quantitative Strategist - Rates Corporate Title: Vice President - Director Location: New York, NY [ALL ROLES TO BE CONSIDERED] Overview GSA concentrates Deutsche Bank's quantitative and modeling expertise within a single unit. With group-wide responsibility for model development, GSA takes a cross-business and cross-functional approach to solving quantitative analytics challenges and establishing common development standards across the bank. This role will support the Agency RMBS and CMO business, partnering closely with Trading, Structuring, and Technology to develop and enhance mortgage analytics within the strategic Kannon platform. The successful candidate will contribute to the development of pricing, risk, prepayment, and cashflow analytics used across the mortgage trading desk, delivering intraday and end-of-day risk, valuation, and P&L information for securitized products.

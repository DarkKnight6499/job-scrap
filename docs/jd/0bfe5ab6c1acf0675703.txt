We’re seeking a future team member for the role of Specialist, Treasury II. This role is located in Pittsburgh. In this role, you’ll build a strong foundation for a career in Corporate Treasury by developing a deep understanding of the bank’s balance sheet, liquidity profile, regulatory framework and key business drivers. You’ll make an impact in the following ways: • Develop hands-on expertise in preparation of FR 2052a, LCR and NSFR, connecting regulatory requirements to the bank’s balance sheet, funding profile and broader Treasury strategy. • Analyze significant liquidity movements and emerging trends, investigate underlying business drivers, and translate findings into insights for Treasury stakeholders. • Identify reporting and data gaps, strengthen controls, and help improve the quality, integrity and efficiency of Treasury information used for decision-making. • Partner on regulatory and reporting enhancements, testing changes in the QA environment and helping ensure solutions are robust before they reach production. • Prepare analysis for supervisory and regulatory inquiries, senior management and ALM committees, gaining exposure to how liquidity matters are communicated and governed at senior levels. • Work across Corporate Treasury, Risk, Finance, Technology and the businesses on initiatives that broaden your understanding of the firm and build the skills needed to grow into a well-rounded Treasury professional. To be successful in this role, we’re seeking the following: • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent combination of education and experience, ideally in finance, economics, accounting, business analytics or information systems. • 2–3 years of work experience in liquidity, external reporting, finance or a related area preferred. • Interest in developing expertise in bank balance sheets, liquidity management and regulatory frameworks, including FR 2052a, LCR and NSFR. • Axiom, SQL and Power BI experience preferred but not required; curiosity and an appetite to build analytical and technical skills are important. • Strong analytical mindset and attention to detail, with the ability to connect data movements to underlying business activity. • Clear written and verbal communication, with the ability to work across multiple partners and contribute effectively in a fast-paced Treasury environment.

To learn more about CIBC, please visit CIBC.com What You’ll Be Doing As a member of the Corporate Banking team, you’ll work in a fast-paced organization where you’ll make a meaningful difference in our clients’ lives. As Associate, Agency Services, Project Finance & Infrastructure, you will be responsible for managing all client-facing and lender-facing activities (inter alia, the processing of waivers, extensions, consents, amendments, refinancings) on transactions in our portfolio where CIBC is acting as Administrative Agent or is a bilateral lender to a client. The Agency Services team will serve as the main point of contact for deal stakeholders (sponsors, borrowers, consultants, lawyers, lenders etc.) while closely working with the Portfolio Management team and Deal Execution Teams. How You’ll Succeed The Associate will be responsible for providing dedicated coverage of agented or bilateral transactions in conjunction with the New York Portfolio Management and Deal Teams and the Toronto based CPS (Agency) team Serve as the main point of contact for deal stakeholders as set out above Co-ordinate the lender group in responding to Borrower requests such as amendments, waivers or consents Co-ordinate and direct the relevant advisors on a transaction (e.g. independent engineer, legal advisor, insurance consultant) as needed to respond to Borrower requests Distribute required information and reports to lenders as needed Organize and participate in all lender / sponsor calls and updates Discuss and answer credit and deal structure related questions from other lenders. Determine whether required lenders have approved or declined borrower requests Assist Portfolio Management Agency team leader in various reporting and presentations to CIBC senior management Who You Are CIBC possesses a strong culture that enables it to attract, integrate and retain exceptional Corporate and Investment Banking professionals and clients.

About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Lead Risk Analytics Consultant to join the Unsecured Reporting & Analytics team within the Unsecured CRO organization. This role will drive communication, collaboration, and alignment across the organization by transforming complex data and analysis from multiple sources into clear, actionable insights for senior leadership. The successful candidate will serve as a strategic thought partner to senior leaders, developing concise and influential narratives that strengthen risk oversight, inform business decisions, and drive organizational action. In this role, you will: Serve as a trusted advisor to senior leaders by developing executive presentations and communications that drive clarity, alignment, and informed decision-making. Aggregate, analyze, and synthesize data, reports, and insights from multiple sources into clear and compelling business narratives. Translate complex analytical findings, risk assessments, and business issues into concise, actionable insights for executive audiences. Develop executive communications and presentation materials aligned with strategic priorities, business objectives, and risk oversight needs. Identify key themes, emerging risks, opportunities, and business implications to support timely decision-making. Create high-impact presentations that communicate risk insights, business performance, and strategic recommendations. Partner with stakeholders across Risk, Business, Finance, Analytics, Operations, and Corporate Functions to ensure consistent and aligned messaging. Influence decision-making across a complex, matrixed organization through effective data-driven storytelling and executive communication. Connect data and analytics to business strategies and risk outcomes to support enterprise-level discussions and actions. Required Qualifications: 5+ years of Risk Analytics experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education. Desired Qualifications: Exceptional executive communication, presentation development, and storytelling skills. Proven ability to synthesize large volumes of complex information and distill key messages and insights for senior leadership. Strong understanding of enterprise risk management, including risk disciplines and governance frameworks. Demonstrated ability to connect disparate data sources, identify meaningful insights, and develop compelling business narratives. Advanced PowerPoint and executive presentation skills, with the ability to translate complex analysis into concise, visually compelling communications. Strong business acumen and strategic thinking skills, with the ability to connect analytics, risk considerations, and business outcomes. Excellent stakeholder management, collaboration, and influencing skills, with the ability to build effective partnerships across organizational levels. Proven ability to work independently, manage competing priorities, and consistently deliver high-quality work in a fast-paced environment. Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to deliver executive-ready materials under tight deadlines.

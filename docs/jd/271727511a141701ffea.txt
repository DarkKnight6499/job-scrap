ROLE OVERVIEW Nscale is seeking a Development Associate to support the development of new AI data center sites across the Americas. Reporting to the Director, Data Center Development, you will work alongside development managers to advance projects from site control through due diligence, entitlement, and permitting to Final Investment Decision (FID). This is a high-exposure role at the center of Nscale’s development engine — you will run diligence workstreams, coordinate consultants, and build the analysis and reporting that keeps a fast-moving portfolio on track, developing the full development-lifecycle toolkit needed to grow into a development manager role. DEVELOPMENT SUPPORT & COORDINATION Support development managers advancing a portfolio of AI data center sites from origination through diligence and FID Maintain project schedules, milestone trackers, budgets, and risk registers across assigned projects Coordinate day-to-day with external consultants and with internal Energy and Power, Design, and Preconstruction teams Prepare meeting materials, agendas, and follow-ups to keep project workstreams moving DUE DILIGENCE & ENTITLEMENTS Manage and track due diligence deliverables — title, survey, geotechnical, environmental, zoning, and utility studies — and summarize findings and risks Support entitlement and permitting efforts, tracking applications, hearing dates, conditions, and jurisdiction requirements Assist with site-control documentation and closing checklists in partnership with Legal Research jurisdictions, utilities, incentives, and site conditions to support project decisions ANALYSIS & REPORTING Build and maintain project dashboards, status reports, and inputs to FID / capital approval packages Support development budget tracking, invoice review, and cost reporting with Finance Perform ad-hoc analysis on sites, markets, schedules, and costs as projects demand Help standardize templates, playbooks, and reporting across the development team QUALIFICATIONS Bachelor’s Degree in Real Estate, Engineering, Construction Management, Finance, Urban Planning, or a relevant discipline, or equivalent combination of education and experience 1–3 years of related experience in real estate, infrastructure, construction, finance, or consulting; data center exposure a plus Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage many moving pieces across concurrent projects Strong analytical and writing skills; able to distill complex information into clear, concise summaries Proficiency in Google Suite and Microsoft Office tools;

Oversee daily operations, manage performance, allocate resources effectively, and ensure compliance with company policies and strategic objectives. Manage and grow a high-quality excess casualty insurance portfolio working through your network of wholesale broker partners. Position requires the ability to leverage your network of industry relationships and grow portfolio utilizing Berkley Custom’s proprietary playbook and broad underwriting appetite.

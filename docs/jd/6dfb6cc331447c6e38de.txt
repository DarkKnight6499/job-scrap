Job Description: As part of the broader Global Markets Credit team, Global Counterparty Credit (GCC) provides best-of-class service to business partners in TD Bank Financial Group, including TD Securities. GCC is a dynamic team responsible for the new business due diligence and ongoing monitoring of counterparties across the Financial Institutions, Non-bank Financial Institution and Funds sectors. Global Markets Credit contributes to the Group's strong risk culture by identifying, assessing, and mitigating counterparty credit risks. The Vice President – GCC Hedge Funds is a member of a team of credit analysts that evaluate and facilitate the credit requirements for TD Securities' Global Markets Hedge Fund business within the Groups' risk appetite and strategies. The Vice President works closely with key stakeholders, including Global Markets business lines, Relationship Managers, Global Markets Documentation, Credit Risk Management/Risk Management and Market Risk Control in assessing trading, derivative, and financing transactions. Hedge Fund and derivative product experience is essential for a successful candidate. Depth & Scope: Responsible for the prudent management of a portfolio of hedge fund counterparties that are active users of capital markets products, including derivatives, financing products and margin financing via prime brokerage and synthetics. Evaluate transaction and counterparty risk to support growth of the Equities (Prime Brokerage, Stock borrowing/lending, OTC Derivatives), Fixed Income (Repo/Reverse Repo, Interest Rate Derivatives) and FX businesses. Provide well-reasoned assessments of risk/return profiles associated with transaction and client relationships. Oversee and monitor the ongoing performance of a portfolio of hedge fund counterparties to ensure compliance with all terms and conditions of the credit authorization and trading documents and ensure that counterparty risk ratings for the assigned portfolio remain accurate. Negotiate and structure credit terms contained in relevant trading and/or lending agreements to ensure appropriate risk mitigating terms are incorporated as required per trading/lending product policies. Develop working relationships and coordinate with Global Markets business lines, Credit Risk Management/Risk Management, Global Markets Documentation, Legal, Compliance and Operations to structure transactions to mitigate counterparty and various other risks as applicable. Conduct market, industry, and counterparty due diligence as part of comprehensive credit assessment. Keep apprised of industry trends and derivative product developments through participation in industry forums and conferences. Education & Experience: Undergraduate degree in Business (Accounting or Finance) or Mathematics/Economics. 5-10 years a counterparty or credit risk function at a large Financial Institution covering hedge funds, private equity, and institutional family offices. Experience with Prime Brokerage underwriting and equity derivatives products is required. Experience with legal agreements including ISDAs, MCAs, GMRAs, MRAs and PBAs. Strong Excel and modeling skills.

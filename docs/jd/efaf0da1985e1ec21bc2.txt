Job Description: Enterprise Valuation is seeking an experienced Senior Actuary to support Fixed Annuity Valuation, with primary responsibility for BMA (Bermuda Monetary Authority) reporting. This role will own key valuation and financial reporting activities, analyze and communicate financial results, drive valuation and process enhancements, and provide actuarial expertise across strategic initiatives and cross-functional projects. This role is on-site 4 days per week and work from home 1 day per week in Newport Beach, CA, Omaha, NE, or Charlotte, NC. If you are not currently located near one of our offices, we offer comprehensive relocation assistance. Responsibilities Own the quarterly and annual valuation and financial reporting for fixed annuity products under BMA regime, ensuring timely and accurate delivery of results. Perform in-depth analysis of valuation results, including attribution analysis, financial drivers, and quarter-over-quarter movements, and communicate key findings to management and stakeholders. Coordinate and prepare responses to requests from auditors, regulators, rating agencies, and other stakeholders, providing appropriate actuarial analysis and supporting documentation. Identify and implement process and analytical enhancements, including opportunities to automate processes, strengthen controls, and improve the understanding of valuation results. Support and coordinate Model Risk Management (MRM) initiatives, including model documentation, peer reviews, model governance requirements, and adherence to best-practice guidelines. Lead the testing and validation of model enhancements and modifications, including analysis of financial impacts and confirmation that appropriate controls and processes are maintained in alignment with MRM and ICFR guidance. Develop and maintain valuation databases, analytical tools, and reporting capabilities to support financial analysis, management reporting, and ad hoc requests Provide technical guidance and peer review to other actuarial team members and contribute to the development of actuarial talent and best practices within the team. Perform ad hoc analyses and contribute to special projects and strategic initiatives as needed. Factors for success Bachelor’s degree in Actuarial Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Finance, Economics, or a related field. FSA designation with 4+ years of relevant actuarial experience. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills. Demonstrated initiative, ownership, and ability to manage multiple priorities. Strong technical skills with experience in actuarial valuation and financial reporting. Ability to analyze complex results and communicate key insights to management and stakeholders. Strong collaboration skills and ability to work effectively across functions. Experience with Prophet is a plus.

About The Role We are seeking a Senior Research Analyst who will be based in either Washington D.C., New York City, or Chicago office within the Corporate Positioning & Insights practice, serving on the primary research team. Senior Research Analysts provide support for both research logistics and analysis, and often manage smaller projects. Responsibilities include writing first drafts of survey questionnaires or discussion guides, managing survey fielding or focus group logistics, proofing materials, and generally playing a plays a hands-on role in the design, execution, analysis, and delivery of primary research engagements, managing defined workstreams and smaller projects. What You’ll Do Develop questionnaires, discussion guides, analysis plans and other research materials, applying sound research judgment and working closely with senior team members to refine and finalize research outputs. Manage the day-to-day execution of primary research projects, coordinating timelines, fieldwork, vendors, and deliverables, while proactively identifying and resolving issues, to ensure work is completed efficiently and to a high standard. Take ownership of research findings, identifying key themes, trends, audience differences, relationships, and unexpected findings, and determining the insights and implications most relevant to the research objectives. Develop client presentations and reports that synthesize research findings into clear, compelling, data-driven stories, using effective structure and visualization to communicate actionable insights and implications. Conduct and support focus groups and in-depth interviews where appropriate. Independently manage defined workstreams and smaller projects, while supporting senior team members on larger, more complex, and global engagements. Support and provide guidance to junior team members on research execution, analysis, and development of project materials.

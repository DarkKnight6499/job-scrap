Job Description: The Role Fidelity Service Bureau is seeking a Quantitative Strategist to help drive the evolution of our electronic trading products through research, analytics, and data-driven insights. This role combines quantitative analysis, product management, and client engagement. You will evaluate algorithm performance, analyze trading outcomes, and develop insights that improve execution quality for Fidelity Service Bureau clients. Working closely with other quantitative strategists, technologists, product managers, and sales teams, you will identify opportunities to enhance the Fidelity Service Bureau trading platform and deliver innovative solutions to complex execution challenges. You will leverage large-scale trading data, transaction cost analysis, and market microstructure research to support both the ongoing optimization of our algorithm suite and the strategic direction of the product. The Expertise and Skills You Bring We are seeking a candidate who combines strong quantitative and technical skills with strong proven collaboration and communication. Success in this role requires the ability to independently analyze complex datasets, develop actionable insights, and communicate findings clearly across technical and business audiences. The ideal candidate is intellectually curious, data-driven, and motivated by solving challenging problems in a fast-paced environment. Preferred qualifications include: Bachelor's or master’s degree in computer science, Mathematics, Engineering, Statistics, Finance, or a related quantitative discipline Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to independently investigate complex questions Experience working with large datasets using Python, q/KDB, SQL, R, or similar analytical tools Experience analyzing time-series data and developing data-driven recommendations Knowledge of UNIX/Linux environments and data processing workflows Working knowledge of equity market structure, electronic trading, execution algorithms, and institutional trading workflows Strong verbal and written communication skills with the ability to explain complex concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences Experience evaluating algorithmic execution and transaction cost analysis (TCA) is a plus FINRA Series 7, 63, and 57 licenses required within six months of hire The most successful candidates are intellectually curious, highly analytical, and motivated by using data to improve trading outcomes. They enjoy combining quantitative rigor, market intuition, and technical expertise to solve complex execution challenges. The Team The Quantitative Strategist will join a team of experts responsible for delivering and optimizing equity trading solutions for asset managers and hedge funds. You will work closely with various teams including sales, product owners and other quantitative strategists to drive revenue. Teamwork, communication, and organization skills are essential for a successful candidate. In close collaboration with fellow quantitative strategists and technologists, you will research, develop, and deploy novel trading algorithms to improve trading efficiency for our clients. You will also leverage your data and programming expertise to drive scalable analytics efforts across the trading platform.

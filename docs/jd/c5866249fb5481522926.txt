The Team: Upstart’s Capital Markets team plays a pivotal role in securing loan funding capital for ou r AI lending marketplace. We collaborate with leading institutional investors across a diverse range of financing and funding solutions, including securitizations, whole loan sales, co-investment partnerships and other structured transactions. Within Capital Markets, the Structured Products team oversees Upstart’s securitization, pass thru, and financing programs. Upstart has been an active issuer in the asset-backed securities (“ABS”) markets since 2017 with over $9 billion in aggregate issuance to date. The team is the primary point of contact with major investment banks and other lenders and investors on Upstart’s capital markets offerings, while ensuring alignment across the business. As the Capital Markets Manager, Structured Products, you'll help shape and execute the financing strategy that supports Upstart's AI lending marketplace. You'll lead complex warehouse, securitization, and other structured financing transactions while partnering closely with investors, banks, legal counsel, and internal stakeholders to deliver scalable funding solutions. You have a strong track record within structured finance and are energized by building relationships across Capital Markets participants. You will leverage your technical expertise to review asset- and bond-level cashflow modeling to support structuring discussions and lead negotiations across our Structured Products offerings. How you’ll make an impact Drive the execution of Upstart's securitization issuances, warehouse facilities, and other asset-backed financing transactions. Build and deepen strategic relationships with lenders, investors, and other key capital markets participants. Partner closely with internal and external legal counsel to negotiate, review, and execute financing agreements. Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders—including Legal, Accounting, Data Analytics, Finance, and Operations—to structure and execute financing transactions. Present transaction updates, financing strategies, and market insights to senior leadership. Coach and mentor junior team members while contributing to the continued growth of the Structured Products team.

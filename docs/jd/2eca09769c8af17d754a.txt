Work you’ll do US Cyber Data Protection team is responsible for securing and protecting confidential data of Deloitte US Member Firm, our Clients and our Employees. Team’s core mission is to implement consistent cyber security controls to protect Firm’s data and data entrusted to us by our Clients, to build their trust and protect our brand. We are seeking an experienced, strategic Cyber Engineering Associate Director to join the US Cyber Data Protection team in the Deloitte Technology US Organization within Deloitte Services LP. The US Cyber Engineering Associate Director, Data Protection is a senior leader of the US Cyber Data Protection team and serves as the team’s final technical escalation point and strategic owner for a portfolio of large, complex cyber security technology services. The role leads cross-functional initiatives spanning the entire US Member Firm, requiring in-depth technical knowledge, sound judgment and decision-making. The Associate Director is accountable for the delivery, adoption and ongoing support of these services for 181K+ US/USI Member Firm professionals, for shaping the long-term Zero Trust and data protection strategy with Cyber Leaders, and for building and developing a high-performing team. This role will directly lead nine (9) Deloitte US and USI professionals, provide oversight of the wider US Cyber Data Protection team, and serve as Service Owner for the following US Member Firm Cyber Data Protection services: (1) Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA) Proxy (2) Web Application Firewall (WAF) (3) FSI Enhanced Controls Adoption (4) Data Classification & Rights Management (DCRM) (5) Key and Certificate Management. The role will also assist in leading the team’s emerging services and new Zero Trust initiatives as the portfolio grows. Role will require demonstrated ability and comfort with executive level reporting, issue escalation, and effectively communicating solutions to Business Leaders, CIO Leadership Team, Confidentiality and Privacy Stakeholders, BISOs, FSI Practice Leaders and PPMDs, and Executives in the Deloitte US Member Firm. Position US Cyber to align with Technology and Cyber Leadership direction and lead new Zero Trust initiatives and cross-functional programs across the US Member Firm. Lead migration of internet facing applications to modern cloud-delivered ZTNA platforms while maintaining secure and reliable access. Lead end to end onboarding of Firm’s internet facing applications to multiple WAF providers across multi-cloud environments.

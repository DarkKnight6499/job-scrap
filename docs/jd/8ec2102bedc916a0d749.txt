We're fully remote, with team members across the U.S. About the role Ondo's user research team is small but mighty, and works horizontally across the whole product suite, not inside a single pod. We're hiring a senior quantitative user researcher to build and lead quantitative research across the portfolio. You'll set your own UXR strategy with minimal guidance, take on the hardest decision-shaping questions whichever team they come from, and answer them with evidence that holds up at scale. What You'll Do Own the research intake and quant roadmap. Field research questions from product, design, GTM and leadership. Turn vague asks into answerable questions, decide which need quantitative evidence (and which don't), and prioritize where evidence most reduces risk on our biggest bets. Answer the big questions before launch. Use MaxDiff and conjoint to show which features, benefits and messages actually drive decisions. Design pre-launch experiments (stimulus tests, matched cohorts, vignettes, threshold studies) with decision rules set before fielding, and pretest instruments with cognitive interviews. Build measurement and segments that last. Adapt or develop validated scales for trust, perceived security and comprehension, track them across products and markets, and turn our user profiles into statistically validated, sized segments. Get data you can trust, and connect it to behavior. Design sampling, translation and fraud controls for a global, crypto-native audience, and partner with data science to link survey data with product data. Evaluate AI-assisted experiences. Design human evaluation for AI-assisted finance features: rubrics, rater reliability, and whether user trust matches how reliable the system actually is. Be leadership's partner for evidence. Present findings as recommendations with effect sizes, confidence and what would change the call. Give quick, right-sized answers to ad-hoc leadership questions, whether that's existing data, a fast pulse survey or a full study. Set the standards that let PMs and designers run sound lightweight surveys or data-driven validation themselves. Requirements 7+ years of UX or applied research experience, including at least 5 years leading quantitative studies end to end, from study design through recommendation. Experience leading quantitative research studies for financial products (e.g., brokerage, trading, banking, payments, wealth or lending). A self-starter who operates with minimal guidance: you scope ambiguous problems, set your own priorities, and drive studies to a decision. Deep survey science expertise: sampling and weighting, instrument design, bias control, data-quality screening, and programming in Qualtrics or similar. Hands-on experience designing and analyzing MaxDiff and/or conjoint studies. Strong applied statistics: regression, power analysis, and experimental and quasi-experimental design. Fluency in R or Python for analysis and visualization. You have an existing AI-assisted research setup, from drafting survey instruments to writing and reviewing analysis code, and you proofread/validate every output with rigor. A track record of quantitative work that changed a product or business decision, and the ability to explain uncertainty clearly to executives and non-researchers. Nice to Have Experience with crypto, onchain or tokenized-asset products. A master’s or PhD in a quantitative field such as statistics, survey methodology, behavioral economics, or marketing science. Depth in advanced methods, such as hierarchical Bayes and simulators, psychometric scale development, segmentation, pricing research, or SQL and multilevel modeling. Experience evaluating AI or LLM-powered products, such as human evaluation design, rubrics or rater reliability. Research across markets in LATAM, APAC or EMEA, including translating survey instruments. Responsible AI-native research workflows, such as using LLM tools to speed up instrument design, coding of open-ended responses or analysis.

THE ROLE Serve as the lead portfolio management representative for the US Core and SMID Value strategies to internal and external clients from within the investment team. Act as a true investment professional who can credibly stand in for the portfolio managers with clients and consultants across all distribution channels, while serving as the strategy expert to position and support assigned capabilities with the objective of retaining and growing assets. WHAT YOU WILL DO Serve as an integral member of the investment team in partnership with the US Core and SMID Value portfolio management groups: participate in investment discussion, attend company meetings and industry conferences where relevant. Develop deep knowledge of assigned strategies, investment processes, portfolio construction, positioning and performance. Monitor existing holdings, including risk metrics and portfolio characteristics. Assume primary responsibility for portfolio management functions other than stock selection or portfolio construction, including negotiating client guidelines for new accounts and managing customized portfolios and cash in clone portfolios. Work with portfolio managers to define and refine investment philosophy and style; develop materials that validate style attributes, track metrics for consistency of approach, and help set client expectations and retain assets when style is out of favor. Articulate investment style, process and portfolio positioning effectively to clients, consultants and prospects, providing consistency of message and discussing specific holdings and performance attribution. Work with distribution partners to develop long-term strategy for each product, supporting asset retention, new business opportunities, competitive positioning and distribution effectiveness. Provide industry intelligence and client insights to senior management to contribute to product enhancements, customized mandates or new solutions. Investment thought leadership: Produce differentiated investment commentary, market perspectives and content that reinforces and supports the strategy. Contribute to sustainability initiatives and client communications, including engagement examples that illustrate our integrated approach to material ESG issues. WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR MBA and/or CFA Charterholder preferred. 8–12+ years of investment management experience, preferably as an Institutional Portfolio Manager or in investment product management, with experience working with institutional clients and consultants. Broad knowledge of the investment environment, economic trends, capital markets and competitive trends, and a strong understanding of active investment processes. Strong written, verbal and presentation skills, including the ability to translate performance attribution into clear commentary. Ability to work effectively with investment, distribution, marketing, legal, compliance and operational teams at all levels, and to operate independently in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment. High level of attention to detail and project management skills; proactive and diligent in responding to business and client needs. Travel required.

You will conduct qualitative and quantitative chemical analysis on raw materials, solutions, and components following approved Standard Operating Procedures and test methods in an accurate and timely manner. What you'll be doing Analyze organic or inorganic compounds to determine chemical or physical properties using chromatography, spectroscopy, or spectrophotometry techniques. Maintain laboratory instruments to ensure proper working order and troubleshoot malfunctions when needed. Prepare test solutions, compounds, standards or reagents for laboratory personnel to conduct tests. Follow laboratory safety procedures to ensure compliance with standards or to make improvements as needed. Monitor operational procedures in technical environments to ensure conformance to standards. Purchase laboratory supplies, such as chemicals. Operate lab equipment such as pipettes, burets, balances, hot plates, etc. Review samples received, logs samples, and enter data into computer database (LIMS). Responsible for general housekeeping of laboratory and test suites. Sustain a clean and safe work environment using 6S principles. Perform routine laboratory and safety audits. Use sophisticated laboratory instrumentation and computer systems to collect and record data. Perform advanced chemical assays requiring precise analytical skills and understanding of chemistry principles. Complete all testing, including special project/ protocol testing in a timely and appropriate manner. Maintain data integrity and ensure compliance with company SOPs and specifications, FDA, GLP, QSR and cGMP regulations. Investigate deviations and write exception documents. Work under minimum supervision. What you'll bring B.S. degree in Natural Sciences: Chemistry preferred. Minimum 1 years of experience in a Chemistry Laboratory or Master's Degree in Natural Sciences with relevant experience in a Chemistry Laboratory, preferably in the pharmaceutical or medical device industry. Digital literacy/knowledge of Microsoft office applications (Word, Excel). Attention to detail and strong organizational skills. Must have a basic understanding of laboratory instrumentation. Good documentation skills and knowledge of GDPs (Good Documentation Practices). Able to manage multiple tasks/priorities in a timely manner. Familiarity with LIMS (or equivalent system) and TrackWise. Must be proficient with at least three of the following lab techniques HPLC, GC, AA, Flame Photometry, FTIR, UV-Vis Spectroscopy.

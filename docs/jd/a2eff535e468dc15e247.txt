This individual will partner closely with Product Managers, business stakeholders, data engineers, and technology teams to analyze complex datasets, identify opportunities for product enhancements, measure product performance, and inform strategic decision-making. The ideal candidate combines strong analytical and technical skills with a solid understanding of product management principles. They are naturally curious, highly data-driven, and capable of translating business questions into meaningful analyses that influence product direction and customer experience. Primary Responsibilities: Support product strategy and lifecycle management, including product roadmaps, release planning, implementation, and ongoing performance monitoring. Partner with business, technology, and cross-functional teams to translate user and business needs into requirements, prioritize work, and deliver product enhancements. Serve as product owner for assigned features or components, supporting backlog management, sprint and PI planning, daily standups, and issue/risk resolution. Engage stakeholders and leverage feedback, analytics, and user insights to identify opportunities and prioritize product features and improvements. Support product delivery and adoption through testing, documentation, release planning, training materials, and change management/readiness activities. Research industry trends and emerging technologies to inform product decisions and continuously improve the product and user experience. “I am the person Capital Group is looking for.” You hold a Bachelor's degree in Business, Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, or a related field.

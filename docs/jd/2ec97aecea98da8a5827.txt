Excellent business communication skills are required. Provide day-to-day application support to trading systems, which includes answering user queries, and monitoring applications. Interact closely with regional and global business users and other groups in technology to ensure issues are addressed and communicated in a timely manner. Troubleshoot production issues to identify root causes and track issues to their completion. Work closely with Developers and business groups to plan system rollout, application updates. Provide rotational after hours (may include evenings and weekends) support and/or ON-call support to correct any critical/Time sensitive issues impacting the applications. Provide expert technical knowledge of applications management in relation to system components, functionality, interfaces, data flows and business rules. Provide reporting to technology partners, business and management on the application availability such as Start of the day checks, Intraday, End of the day, weekly Incident governance, problem and change tracking. Possesses technical and/or business functional knowledge of systems, timing and dependencies. Conducts independent analysis and assessment to resolve strategic issues. Proactively monitors system performance and identifies operational improvements, in ensuring smooth and consistent customer and business partner delivery. Supports deployment activities, managing implementation issues to resolution. Develops or helps to develop the knowledge required for the operation. Adheres to ITIL best practices regarding Incident, Problem, Change Management or other related Issue logging and recording of requests, incidents, problems, and changes Broader work or accountabilities may be assigned as needed. Experience: 4-7 years of work experience in IT or business environment and/or BS/BA or MBA/MS in computer science, engineering, information systems, math or business . Capital markets, Fixed Income IT Support, rates and/or credit products, or similar high-pressure business experience is required, with experience supporting trading or operational workflow applications Additional skills: eTrading technical troubleshooting: OMS, Pricing, execution routing, post trade feed ITIL service operations methodologies and have experience with Service management tools like Service now and JIRA Excellent communication and problem-solving skills Ability to work independently or within a team environment and handle multiple issues simultaneously. Understanding of Information Technology operating processes used for systems to ensure effective delivery including but not limited to IT Operations mandatory operating standards for monitoring, logging, and alerting.

Build our future together: The Digital Health Technology Associate Manager (DHT AM) is responsible for implementing digital tools and technologies — electronic COA (eCOA), eConsent, wearables, sensors, spirometry, ECG, and similar — in individual clinical research studies, from study design through close-out, in collaboration with the study team. A typical day includes managing project timelines, translation, specifications, and UAT activities (either direct execution or oversight), ensuring data collection complies with Regeneron SOPs and ICH/GCP. This role applies to both internally sourced studies and studies outsourced to CROs, and includes responsibility for managing vendor activities and training/educating stakeholders.

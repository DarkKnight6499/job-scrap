The Vehicle Overdue Associate will protect company assets by managing overdue rental cases from first contact through vehicle recovery. You will work across multiple channels — phone, email, and coordination with field personnel — to resolve situations efficiently and professionally. What You’ll Do: Contact renters, corporate sales accounts, and field locations via inbound and outbound calls and emails regarding overdue cases. Document all case activity accurately in the Case Management database, including contact attempts, notes, and status updates and contract extensions. Update Wizard and/or Compass system with all relevant details for overdue vehicles. Issue certified demand letters and initiate repossession orders when warranted; follow up with repo companies on recovery progress. Prepare and forward documentation to field security personnel once all in-house recovery options have been exhausted. Coordinate with field personnel and security managers to escalate vehicles to missing/alarm status and obtain police reports. Build and maintain strong working relationships with field teams to ensure timely and effective communication.

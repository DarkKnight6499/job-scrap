Role Description: The Securitized Products Research Analyst will focus primarily on analyzing Residential and Commercial Mortgage Backed Securities (MBS/RMBS/CMBS) and Collateralized Loan Obligations (CLOs). This role involves modeling and analysis of various datasets, contributing to research reports, and servicing institutional clients. Key tasks include analyzing prepayment/default trends for various mortgage products, recommending relative value trades, monitoring ownership structure of various sectors, monitoring policy developments, and analyzing corporate credit and leveraged loans.

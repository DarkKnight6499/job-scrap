Join a team that plays a critical role in supporting trust and investment operations through accurate asset valuation, trading oversight, and transaction processing. This position is responsible for calculating fund net asset values, maintaining investment records, and ensuring the timely and accurate processing of investment-related activities that support client accounts and portfolio management. Calculate and validate daily net asset values for collective and unitized funds, ensuring accurate pricing, accruals, and transaction activity. Monitor and support equity and fixed income trading activities, reviewing trade confirmations and resolving discrepancies to facilitate timely settlement. Establish and maintain security records within trust accounting systems to support pricing, income collection, tax reporting, and account administration. Process and monitor domestic and international wire transfers, ensuring accuracy, timely execution, and resolution of exceptions. Reconcile held-away account activity, update account holdings, and verify position accuracy for client reporting purposes. Coordinate and process class action filings and settlement payments on behalf of client accounts. Maintain accurate security pricing by researching and obtaining valuation information from third-party sources and identifying missing or stale prices. Support securities lending and depository transfer activities through ongoing monitoring, reconciliation, and transaction processing. Education & Experience Required Bachelor’s degree in Business, Accounting, Finance, or a related field, or an equivalent combination of education and experience 2+ years of experience in trust, investments, finance, or accounting Preferred Experience working with investment operations, trust accounting, trading, or fund administration activities Experience reconciling investment accounts and researching security pricing Why You’ll Love Working Here This role offers the opportunity to work across a broad range of investment and trust operations functions while contributing directly to the accuracy and integrity of client account management. You'll gain exposure to trading, fund accounting, securities processing, and portfolio support while collaborating with experienced professionals in a dynamic financial services environment.

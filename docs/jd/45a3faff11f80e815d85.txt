Model Risk Management & Validation Policy Analyst (Multiple Openings)
CME Group
Location: Chicago - 20 S. Wacker
Link: https://cmegroup.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/cme_careers/job/Chicago---20-S-Wacker/Model-Risk-Management---Validation-Policy-Analyst--Multiple-Openings-_34923

Support the day-to-day implementation of the model risk management practices, including the internal and third-party model validation processes and review model changes. Support the drafting of model risk management policies and procedures. Refine and maintain the model inventory. Work with third-party validators to respond to their inquiries and review identified findings for severity and accuracy. Support the review and oversight of model validation responses and related remediation activities by working with model developers and users. Develop knowledge of CME Clearing’s risk models and best practices in model risk management. Telecommuting permitted on a hybrid schedule as determined by the employer. MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS Master’s degree in Financial Math, Financial Engineering, or a related quantitative field and 24 months of experience as a Financial Risk Specialists or related occupation. Must have two (2) years of experience with/in: - Programming languages, including at least one of the following: C++, C#, Java, or Python; - Utilizing model risk management best practices, including building validation tools; - Knowledge of financial markets and derivatives products, including risk management methods (e.g., VaR scenario analysis); - Exercising risk management methods (including value-at-risk, stress testing, and scenario analysis); and - Developing database queries and scripts for data analysis. Must have one (1) year of experience with/in: - Working at a bank, major financial institution, or as a management consultant for the financial services industry.

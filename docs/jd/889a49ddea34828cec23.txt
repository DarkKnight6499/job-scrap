The Opportunity The Compliance & Operational Testing Analyst Lead serves as a regulatory subject matter expert supporting the implementation of Continuous Monitoring within the FLOD Bank Testing Program. This role translates complex consumer protection regulations into scalable, data-driven monitoring requirements and partners across Compliance, Risk, Business, Data, Analytics, and Technology to develop automated monitoring and testing capabilities. The ideal candidate brings experience with continuous monitoring, automated or compliance testing, and developing monitoring requirements, business rules, or control testing methodologies. Strong candidates will have expertise across multiple banking products, such as Credit Cards, Deposits, Payments, Consumer Lending, or Real Estate, along with experience building monitoring frameworks from the ground up and using data to identify compliance risks, emerging trends, and monitoring opportunities. We offer a flexible work environment that requires an individual to be in the office 4 days per week. This position can be based in one of the following locations: San Antonio, TX, Plano, TX, Phoenix, AZ, Colorado Springs, CO, Charlotte, NC or Tampa, FL Relocation assistance is not available for this position. What you'll do: Develops the steps needed to test and/or analyze compliance with complex policies/procedures and laws/regulations. Serves as a subject matter expert in a specific subject/business area and provides guidance to team members on complex issues. Leads and manages a grouping of tests to assess operational risk mitigation and compliance requirements. Reviews and executes work papers to support conclusions of test findings. Provides input to leader on allocation of resources to complete testing activities. Performs complex qualitative analysis of testing data and develops reports and recommendations for leaders. May develop executive briefs and present to key internal and external stakeholders. Builds, develops, and maintains relationships with the business and internal control partners (Audit, Risk & Compliance, Legal) to facilitate collaboration between all lines of defense for testing. Ensures risks associated with business activities are effectively identified, measured, monitored, and controlled in accordance with risk and compliance policies and procedures. What you have: Bachelor's degree; OR 4 years of relevant education and/or experience. Experiences that will support your success: Certified Regulatory Compliance Manager (CRCM) certification or equivalent professional certification demonstrating advanced expertise in consumer banking regulations. Deep knowledge of consumer protection regulations, including Regulation Z, Regulation B, Regulation E, FCRA, SCRA, UDAAP, and other applicable banking laws and regulations. SME knowledge in one or more of USAA’s Everyday Banking products (Credit Cards, Deposits & Payments) Significant experience in Compliance Testing, Operational Testing, Compliance Risk Management, Internal Audit, or related regulatory oversight functions within the banking or financial services industry. Strong understanding of consumer banking products at USAA, associated operational processes, and applicable regulatory requirements. Demonstrated experience interpreting regulatory requirements and translating them into actionable business requirements, testing methodologies, and monitoring solutions. Experience leading complex, cross-functional initiatives. Strong analytical, problem-solving, organizational, and written communication skills. Demonstrated ability to influence stakeholders, drive alignment, and deliver results within a matrixed environment. What sets you apart: Experience implementing Continuous Monitoring, Continuous Controls Monitoring, Automated Testing, Compliance Testing, or Quality Assurance programs. Experience leading complex cross-functional initiatives across Compliance, Risk, Business, Data, Analytics, and Technology. Experience developing monitoring requirements, business rules, or control testing methodologies. Deep knowledge of two or more banking product areas, including Credit Cards, Deposits, Payments, Consumer Lending, and Real Estate. Experience building monitoring frameworks or automated compliance testing capabilities from the ground up. Strong analytical skills with the ability to leverage data to identify compliance risks, emerging trends, and monitoring opportunities.

Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Risk Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Regulatory Risk & Compliance - Health Industries - Payer Compliance - Senior Associate you will help clients navigate regulatory requirements, strengthen compliance processes, and interpret complex payer obligations within our Risk and Compliance practice. As a Senior Associate you will analyze intricate issues, manage assigned workstreams, build meaningful client connections, and support junior team members while developing your personal brand and technical skills.

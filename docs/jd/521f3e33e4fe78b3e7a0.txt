The Global Risk & Compliance organization (GRC) is an independent risk management function, led by the Chief Risk Officer, with the objective of ensuring that American Express operates in a safe, sound, and fully compliant manner within all applicable regulatory expectations. GRC creates and maintains the overall risk management framework, performs independent risk management assessments, and monitors applicable risks. GRC colleagues are passionate about supporting the Company's goals of growth and progress by fostering a culture of risk awareness and proactivity around regulatory matters. By partnering closely with business units across the enterprise, we help deliver maximum value to our shareholders and customers through effective risk management and oversight activities. The Opportunity: GRC's Enterprise Risk Management & Risk Oversight organization is seeking a Manager to support the implementation, adoption, and ongoing governance of the Enterprise Change Risk Management Policy and associated governance programs. The role will provide dedicated capacity across Change Risk Management, New Products Governance, Project Governance, and related initiatives, while serving as a flexible, consulting-style resource for strategic priorities led by the VP, Change Risk Management & Strategic Initiatives. This role combines risk management, governance, program execution, communications, stakeholder engagement, change enablement, and knowledge management. The successful candidate will be a strategic thinker who can translate complex governance requirements into practical solutions and help build sustainable infrastructure that supports consistent execution across the enterprise.

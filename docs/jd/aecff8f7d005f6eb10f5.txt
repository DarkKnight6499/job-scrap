Job Description: Position Title: Senior Finance Analyst (L9) Business Unit: TD Securities Division/Region: TD Securities USA (New York) Report Location: Hybrid Hours: M – F 8:30 – 5:00 Overview TD Securities US Finance is part of the broader TD Securities Finance organization and one of three regional finance groups supporting TD Bank Group’s Wholesale Banking segment. The team plays a key role in the United States by supporting financial, management, liquidity, and regulatory reporting. As a primary control and support function in the region, TD Securities US Finance also provides business information for projects and strategic initiatives and serves as a liaison among teams and business segments in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Singapore. Specific Accountabilities Include: Support and assist in regulatory report production for TDS as required by U.S. regulators, including the Federal Reserve Board (FRB), Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council (FFIEC), and U.S. Department of the Treasury. Research financial, accounting, audit, operational, and reporting matters; interpret applicable guidelines, standards, policies, and analysis. Serve as a senior technical resource by working independently, providing subject-matter and process guidance, coaching colleagues, and building stakeholder alignment on projects and activities. Partner with Finance teams to enhance processes, including report production, system queries, process mapping, specialized tracking, analysis, and transactional support. Understand data attributes and sources to identify issues to improve reporting processes, by using current or AI enhanced tools. Assist in automation of workflows and implementation of new databases and systems, as needed (i.e., Axiom). Interpret data flows and internal data architecture to optimize data management for regulatory reporting team. Support governance requirements and partner with Compliance and 1B to maintain effective data and process governance, key controls, and analytics. Continue developing expertise in regulatory concepts, practices, data, and reporting processes. Support other key departmental projects and initiatives assigned by the manager. Maintain procedures and supporting documentation, including gathering required information from source systems and business partners. Analyze business and accounting movements to support regulatory reporting variance analysis. Specific Requirements / Accreditations: Bachelor’s degree in business, accounting, finance, or a related field; accounting proficiency is preferred. 3+ years of financial services experience, preferably in capital markets. Proven analytical skills and sound judgment in a dynamic business environment. Ability to translate complex, high-volume reporting requirements, and data into simple, consistent, and sustainable processes. Demonstrated data aggregation and reconciliation skills. Strong understanding of process and data governance, with exceptional attention to detail. Highly motivated, with a proven track record of delivering results. Curious, intuitive, and logical thinker with a strong interest in identifying and implementing process improvements. Demonstrated time-management and organizational skills, with the ability to meet tight deadlines and adapt to changing priorities. Understanding of Wholesale Banking and Capital Markets businesses, products, and related balance sheet and profit-and-loss movements. Collaborative team player who can also work effectively and independently. Advanced Microsoft Excel and Access skills are required; knowledge of Alteryx is an asset. Demonstrated ability to communicate effectively with senior leaders and collaborate laterally with business partners. Familiarity with regulatory reporting requirements would be an asset.

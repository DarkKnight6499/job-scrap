COMPANY OVERVIEW KKR is a leading global investment firm that offers alternative asset management as well as capital markets and insurance solutions. KKR aims to generate attractive investment returns by following a patient and disciplined investment approach, employing world-class people, and supporting growth in its portfolio companies and communities. KKR sponsors investment funds that invest in private equity, credit and real assets and has strategic partners that manage hedge funds. KKR’s insurance subsidiaries offer retirement, life and reinsurance products under the management of Global Atlantic Financial Group. References to KKR’s investments may include the activities of its sponsored funds and insurance subsidiaries. TEAM OVERVIEW The Insurance Strategy & M&A team at Global Atlantic is responsible for driving strategic growth initiatives, including inorganic expansion, corporate development, and strategic projects for the insurance business. The team works closely with senior leadership across Global Atlantic and KKR to evaluate and execute transactions and initiatives that enhance the company’s competitive positioning and long-term value creation. Key areas of focus include (i) evaluating and executing acquisitions, partnerships, and reinsurance transactions, (ii) leading strategic initiatives across Global Atlantic’s insurance and asset management platform, (iii) assessing new business lines and growth opportunities, and (iv) supporting long-term strategic planning and capital deployment decisions. POSITION OVERVIEW Global Atlantic is seeking an Associate to join the Insurance Strategy & M&A team in New York. This role sits at the intersection of corporate development, strategy, and investing, with significant exposure to senior leadership and key decision-makers across both Global Atlantic and KKR. The Associate will play a key role in evaluating and executing M&A and strategic initiatives, including acquisitions that enhance the insurance business of KKR, reinsurance transactions, and internal strategic projects. This is a highly dynamic role suited for candidates interested in shaping the growth trajectory of a scaled insurance business.

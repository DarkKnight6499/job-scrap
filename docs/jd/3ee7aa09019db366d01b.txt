Recovery and Resolution Planning (RRP) is responsible for the design, development and maintenance of the firm’s recovery and resolution plans and related capabilities. The plans require development of central strategies to support timely and effective execution of various financial, legal and operational actions during periods of unprecedented stress. Development of the plans requires coordination with stakeholders across the organization, including technology, finance, treasury, operations and businesses, to ensure execution readiness. RRP sets the requirements for and reviews plan documentation to ensure that it meets regulatory guidelines and is fit for purpose to guide management and board throughout both the planning and execution phases. RRP is also responsible for identifying required capabilities necessary to support plan development and execution and for establishing the framework to support capabilities testing. Finally, RRP oversees and manages the governance process associated with plan approval and submission along with regulatory engagement for plan review and challenge. We are seeking an Assistant Vice President to join the Recovery & Resolution Planning team within the Finance organization. This team is responsible for developing and maintaining recovery and resolution preparedness, including strategies, capabilities and plans, that support regulatory requirements and strengthen firm resilience during periods of financial stress. The successful candidate will support strategic planning initiatives, coordinate regulatory submissions, and partner across Finance (including Treasury), Risk, Operations, Technology, and the businesses. The role offers significant exposure to senior leadership through regulatory and strategic deliverables and provides the opportunity to influence enterprise-wide resilience, governance, and regulatory preparedness programs.

Risk Manager At Capital One, we dare to dream, disrupt, and deliver a better way. We bring ingenuity, simplicity, and humanity to an industry ripe for change. Do you want to join the team that paves the way for transformation by managing risk for our customers, communities, and associates? The Risk Management Business Risk Office is a critical function responsible for mitigating risk for the entire Risk Organization. Our work is at the heart of risk management for the company and covers all categories of risk, governing policies and standards, risk assessments, and issue and event management. We partner with executives and their teams to identify and monitor risks in an open, collaborative environment where new ideas and solutions are welcomed and rewarded. If you’re a creative and analytical thinker interested in having a real impact in the growth of an exciting and innovative company, this role is for you! The Risk Management Risk (RMR) Office is seeking a highly motivated Risk Manager to apply their risk, communication, analytical, and project management skills in support of our mission. Through this work they will gain a broad, general understanding of how Capital One manages risk. Responsibilities Include, but are not limited to, the following: Partner collaboratively with our Risk Advisors and business areas, risk data management team, and other risk management partners, and business risk offices Support internal audits and exams as needed Participate in and execute other special projects for the team on a periodic basis Demonstrate effective communication and influence across multiple functional areas and levels of the organization, fostering accountability and collaboration. Provide clear, actionable recommendations to ensure our process and controls are executing as intended. Solving problems and engaging in critical thinking to continuously improve our risk practices. Basic Qualifications: Bachelor's Degree or military experience At least 5 years of experience working in risk management, audit, or quality assurance programs At least 3 years of experience with querying, analyzing and working with data and scripting languages such as Python, R, Spark, or SQL At least 2 years of experience working with process and project management and improvement practices Preferred Qualifications: 6+ years of experience working in risk management, audit, or formal quality assurance programs 4+ years of experience working with at least one scripting language such as Python, R, Spark, or SQL and querying, analyzing and working with data languages 3+ years of experience working with process and project management and improvement practices Experience working in the financial services industry Experience with Governance, Risk, and Compliance software tools At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position.

Background The Commercial Real Estate (CRE) industry is at an inflection point driven by several disruptive macroeconomic factors including on-going market volatility, demographic shifts, rapid evolution in technology and data analytics and the implications of ESG regulations. These factors are contributing to increased complexity and cost in owning, managing and occupying real estate assets. Investors are requiring faster, data-driven insight, consistent reporting, and an integrated view of risk, return and operational performance. JLL is evolving its Advisory approach to key clients by drawing together current complementary skill sets into a newly formed team focussed on Strategic Value Advisory within the Value & Risk Advisory group. The team will deliver advice to clients alongside the Valuations business through three key Advisory service pillars – Investor, Credit and Asset (including Asset Management Services). The Strategic Value Advisory offer is powered by JLL’s market-leading “quants-driven” approach, combining proprietary analytics, predictive modelling, and execution capability to deliver measurable asset and portfolio performance for clients and compelling commercial outcomes for JLL. Within JLL Asset Management Services, we are reimagining how Asset Management outsourcing and support is conceived and executed: building a data-enabled, capital-aware and globally consistent platform that connects real estate operations directly to investment strategy in a streamlined and scalable way. The Asset Management Services offer combines established strategic Asset Management expertise with JLL’s “quants-driven” approach to optimise asset-level and operational performance for clients. The Role The Associate Director, Asset Management Services – Global Solutions role will support the delivery and development of client mandates and the global solutions platform, working closely with the Executive Director, Asset Management Services and wider Asset Management Services leadership team. This role will focus on contributing to data-led analysis, client deliverable preparation, and operational support across the Asset Management Services platform. It will provide exposure to complex, multijurisdictional client mandates while building foundational expertise in asset management advisory, operating model design, and analytics-driven performance optimisation. The Associate Director will work with guidance from senior team members while developing increasing independence on defined workstreams. Key responsibilities include: Client Service Delivery Support the preparation and execution of client deliverables across Asset Management Services mandates, working under the direction of the Asset Management Services leadership team. Contribute to analysis and reporting for asset performance optimisation, governance frameworks, and risk monitoring workstreams. Assist in the development and documentation of Asset Management Operating Models, incorporating data, technology, and scalable solutions. Coordinate inputs from internal stakeholders to ensure timely and accurate delivery of client materials. Support execution of existing client initiatives, including data compilation, analysis, and preparation of client-facing outputs. Maintain consistency of quality across deliverables, applying standard templates, tools, and processes established by the team. Analytical and Advisory Support Utilise JLL’s data-powered intelligence platforms—such as Asset Beacon, Horizon, and Debt Management Systems (DMS)—to compile and present performance data and portfolio insights. Support financial modelling, benchmarking, and scenario planning workstreams under the guidance of senior team members. Prepare analytical outputs and presentation materials that contribute to client engagement and asset-level decision-making. Identify and flag data anomalies or inconsistencies, escalating to senior colleagues as appropriate. Resource Co-ordination and Collaboration Assist in co-ordinating inputs from internal teams across Innovation, JBS, JLLT, Research, Property Management and external partners. Maintain transparent tracking of project milestones, workplan progress, and document management across active mandates. Contribute to the delivery of integrated client solutions in support of the other Strategic Value Advisory teams, reflecting a OneJLL approach. Technology and Platform Enablement Support the use and ongoing standardisation of the Strategic Value Advisory technology stack, contributing to automation and efficiency improvements. Assist with data ingestion, quality checks, and preparation of structured datasets across platforms. Engage with JLL’s data-first, tech-enabled delivery model and develop working knowledge of key systems and tools used across the platform. Stakeholder Engagement and Compliance Contribute to the preparation of client presentations, workshop materials, and supporting documentation. Develop working relationships with internal colleagues across the Value & Risk Advisory group and wider JLL business. Ensure all activities comply with JLL’s ethical, legal, and operational standards. Candidate Requirements 5-7 years’ experience in Commercial Real Estate, Asset Management, real estate advisory, or a related analytical field. Demonstrable analytical capability, with experience in financial modelling, data analysis, or quantitative approaches to performance assessment. Experience contributing to multi-stakeholder projects or client deliverables, with a track record of reliable, accurate output. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to present findings clearly to internal and client audiences. Collaborative mindset and willingness to work across teams, disciplines, and geographies. Degree in real estate, finance, economics, or a related field; progress toward a professional qualification (MRICS, CFA, or equivalent) is advantageous. Performance Metrics Timely and accurate delivery of analytical outputs and client deliverables across active mandates. Demonstrated contribution to existing client initiatives, including quality of analysis and supporting materials prepared. Evidence of growing independence on defined workstreams, with increasing capability to manage tasks with limited supervision. Quality and consistency of materials produced in support of client engagements and internal initiatives.

Position Summary and Role Impact: The Enterprise Risk Management (“ERM”) team is responsible for implementing the risk governance program, risk oversight, and to provide an independent view of risks faced by CardWorks, its clients and their customers. The Third-Party Risk Analyst II supports the overall direction of Third-Party Risk Management (“TPRM”) activities by identifying and assessing potential risks with current and potential third parties. Essential Functions: Identify and assess potential risks in current and potential third parties and provide written summaries of findings (background checks, legal cases, business reports, complaints, news, internet searches, etc.). Assist in the oversight/ coordination of third-party risk management and the respective oversight and performance assessments. Work with the vendor relationship owners throughout the organization for current and potential third parties to obtain necessary information for risk assessments (financials, audit reports, questionnaires, policies, etc.). Assess, in writing, key risks that may arise from the Company’s outsourced activities and options for controlling these risks. Assist in preparing TPRM reporting for management and committees, including board of directors. Identify issues and make recommendations for improving the assessment/coordination process. Adjust processes to adhere to changes in the regulatory environment. Create and maintain operational procedures for TPRM processes. Escalate suspected violations of law, regulations, or non-compliance with Company policies. Adherence to the Company’s internal controls policies and procedures. Perform other duties as assigned by VP of Enterprise Risk Management and/or reporting manager.

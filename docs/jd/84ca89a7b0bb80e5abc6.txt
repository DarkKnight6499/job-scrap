Job Duties: Associate, Banker-CSG with Goldman Sachs Bank USA in Dallas, Texas. Maintaining and executing front-end risk bookings of collateral, funding requests, borrowing base calculations, approval of cash distributions. Identify potential issues with new and existing deals, and work to enact comprehensive solutions, coordinating with various global teams across multiple internal groups. Collaborate with product controllers to ensure the accuracy of Profit and Loss (PnL) figures, and books and records. Set up and integrate new Private Credt ABL, Private Equity NAV, Private Equity Capital Call deals into existing workflows. Check borrowing bases against legal documents and internal systems. Interact with fundamentals team, ensuring that collateral assets are properly modeled and prices are correctly marked. Work with the engineering and strats teams to ensure risk is correctly reflected in the appropriate firm systems. Coordinate with external parties (agents, clients, and trustees) to facilitate accurate transaction reporting. Act as a point of contact for back and middle office operations teams. Job Requirements: Master’s degree (U.S. or foreign equivalent) in Business Analytics, Math, Finance, Accounting, Financial Engineering, or related field and one (1) year of experience in the job offered or in a related role OR Bachelor’s degree (U.S. or foreign equivalent) in Business Analytics, Math, Finance, Accounting, Financial Engineering, or related field and three (3) years of experience in the job offered or in a related role. Prior employment must include one (1) year of experience (with a Master’s) OR three (3) years of experience (with a Bachelor’s) with: working with US GAAP accounting and financial statements; reviewing loan credit agreements; producing client reports using market data analyses, leveraging electronic data sources including Bloomberg, FactSet or Capital IQ; derivative documentation (International Derivatives and Swaps Association (ISDA), Credit Support Annex (CSAs); presenting portfolio and individual collateral obligation risk summaries to senior management and risk management on loan operations; processing trustee files, borrowing bases, and fundings; and market data applications and Microsoft Excel to track volumes, workflow, and projects, including utilizing Pivot tables to manage large datasets, and V-lookups to minimize manual processes and manage risk. ©The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc., 2026.

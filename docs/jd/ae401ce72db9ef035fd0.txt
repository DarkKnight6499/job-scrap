The Senior Business Analyst will serve as the primary business-analysis contributor and a key liaison among business stakeholders, Product Management, customers, and software development teams. This individual will independently own and drive complex discovery and analysis activities for assigned software products and capabilities. The Senior Business Analyst will also work across Global Marketing, Research & Development, Information Technology, Quality, Operations, Regulatory, and Business Development to deliver software solutions that provide measurable value to customers. The Senior Business Analyst serves as a key liaison between business stakeholders and technical teams, driving the successful delivery of software solutions across Global Marketing, R&D, IT, Quality, Operations, Regulatory, and Business Development. What You’ll Work On Lead requirements discovery and analysis activities with business stakeholders, product managers, subject matter experts, customers, and technical teams. Analyze business processes, system interactions, user workflows, and operational data to identify gaps, risks, dependencies, and improvement opportunities. Elicit, document, manage, and maintain business, functional, non-functional, and system requirements. Develop user stories, use cases, process flows, requirements specifications, acceptance criteria, and other analysis artifacts. Collaborate with Product Management, Architecture, UX, Engineering, Quality, and Regulatory teams to define scalable and compliant software solutions. Manage requirements traceability, scope, and change control throughout the SDLC. Assess and communicate the impact of requirement changes on project scope, timelines, risks, quality, and cost. Facilitate stakeholder alignment by resolving competing priorities, clarifying business objectives, and recommending data-driven solutions. Support Agile product planning, backlog refinement, prioritization, release readiness, and post-release activities. Lead User Acceptance Testing (UAT) planning and execution to validate solutions against business requirements. Partner with Quality Assurance teams to ensure comprehensive test coverage and alignment with approved requirements. Identify risks, dependencies, and compliance considerations while ensuring adherence to quality system and regulatory requirements. Independently manage requirements and analysis activities across multiple concurrent initiatives and product releases. Build strong partnerships across business and technical teams while contributing best practices for business analysis and product delivery.

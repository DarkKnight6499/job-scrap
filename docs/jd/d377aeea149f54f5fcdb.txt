Requisition #: 76808 The Role at a Glance We are excited to bring on a motivated Web/Mobile Business Analyst to our Group Protection Digital and Data Enablement pillar in a remote or hybrid environment, depending on your proximity to one of our key cities. Background Details: In this role, you will help carry out the GP Digital Strategy through dedicated agile pods working on prioritized projects for our My Lincoln Portal platform. You will consult/analyze and deliver on project specific assignments and act as a resource to applicable internal stakeholders. You will also consult and collaborate with business and IT stakeholders to define and validate business solutions that meet their needs, goals, and objectives. In this role, you will report directly to our Director, Product Owner role. What you'll be doing You will be responsible for elicitation and documentation of GP Web/Mobile requirements: planning, prioritization, scope, effort estimations and traceability to downstream and upstream teams/applications. You will consult, collaborate, and serve as a resource to team members and key internal and/or external stakeholders to elicit more complex business issues, determine root cause(s), and capture business needs. You will partner directly with UX resources to document requirements that reflect best-in-class user experiences methodologies. You will drive consistent/effective communication with agile pod and peer teams. You will identify and recommend functional and navigational improvements that significantly improve our customer experience. You will act as liaison between Business and IT partners ensuring clear understanding of user stories and business requirements for design. You will create system integration requirements by liaising with upstream and downstream application teams to identify dependencies, integration points, data exchanges, and end-to-end requirements. You will create and maintain Data Dictionaries to document data definitions, sources, transformations, and lineage across applications, ensuring clear understanding and traceability of data throughout the ecosystem. What we’re looking for Must-Haves 3-5+ years of experience in business analysis. Project management skills including the ability to coordinate and balance multiple projects in a time-sensitive environment, under pressure, and while meeting deadlines. Digital (Web/Mobile) product experience. 4-year Bachelor's Degree or equivalent work experience (4+ years of experience aligned with the duties of the role). Ability to define problems, analyze complex information, collect data, establish facts, and draw valid conclusions and solutions. Strong collaborator and communicator. Demonstrated project management leadership skills with the capacity to manage and drive multiple efforts of work simultaneously in a time-sensitive environment. Nice-to-Haves User Acceptance Testing (UAT) experience. Agile methodology experience.

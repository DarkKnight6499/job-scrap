The Opportunity Responsible for supporting Corporate Treasury’s Capital Management function and business partners across the organization. The Senior Treasury Analyst will support a broad range of capital management activities, including regulatory capital planning and forecasting, stress testing, governance, senior management reporting, and strategic analyses. The role requires a high degree of independence and analytical ownership, with a significant focus on Basel III Endgame capital rules. The Senior Analyst will lead complex analyses and workstreams, drive process improvements, provide forward-looking insights, and partner across Treasury, Finance, Risk, Technology, and other enterprise teams. The Work Itself The Work Itself Execute Ally’s regulatory capital planning, forecasting, and stress testing processes to meet internal and external requirements Lead analyses supporting Basel III Endgame implementation, including assessment of risk-weighted asset and capital impacts in alignment with applicable capital rules, identification of data and process requirements, and execution readiness Develop, review, and support governance of capital forecasts, stress testing outputs, and related analyses, ensuring accuracy, consistency, appropriate controls, and regulatory alignment Partner across Treasury, Finance, Risk, Technology, and other business teams to source inputs, resolve issues, and align on capital assumptions, methodologies, and regulatory treatments Prepare executive and Board level materials communicating Ally’s actual and projected regulatory capital position, key risks, emerging trends, and strategic considerations Support compliance with internal policies, external capital requirements, audit standards, and governance processes, including responses to regulatory, audit, and internal review requests Monitor capital metrics, regulatory developments, and financial market conditions;

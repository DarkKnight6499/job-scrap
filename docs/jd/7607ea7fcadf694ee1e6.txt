Department Profile The Fixed Income Division is comprised of Interest Rate and Currency Products, Credit Products and Distribution. Professionals in the Division assess and actively manage risk, trade securities, and structure as well as execute innovative transactions in the fast-paced and constantly changing global markets. Morgan Stanley aligns its municipal investment banking, underwriting, sales, trading, lending, and M&A advisory in one integrated organizational group under the umbrella of the Municipal Securities Division. Background on the Team Within Municipal Securities, the Syndicate / Client Financing team is made up of a group of experienced municipal market professionals focused on providing exceptional marketing and execution services to the full complement of our borrower and investor clients. Within this multi-faceted group, the Analyst/Associate will concentrate on the marketing of municipal new issue to both clients and internal stakeholders to broaden distribution and enhance connectivity with the universe of municipal investors. The role will also focus on designing and implementing innovative data-driven approaches needed to support new issue structures/pricing levels and track investor feedback ahead of transaction executions. Qualified senior Analyst / Associate candidates will benefit from unique exposure to the municipal market through underwriting, market making, and structuring activities. Qualified candidates will work with Public Finance investment bankers, internal risk functions, municipal issuers, investors, sales, trading, and other capital markets professionals. Primary Responsibilities Provide the analytical, quantitative and operational support necessary to execute bespoke financing structures Develop and maintain marketing materials for existing and new product offerings that appropriately reflect current marketing conditions and technicals Work closely with Public Finance bankers and the salesforce force to identify both client needs and design structures to meet those needs Develop and participate in presentations to municipal and not-for-profit borrowers, as well as domestic and international institutional investors Work and collaborate with other desks and market makers at Morgan Stanley and externally to support trading activities, including short-term and long term products. Qualifications Undergraduate degree Developed understanding of municipal markets.

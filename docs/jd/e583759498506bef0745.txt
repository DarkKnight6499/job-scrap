ROLE SUMMARY: As part of LSEG Enterprise AI, we are hiring a Senior Associate Product Manager to support the strategy, roadmap and delivery of foundational platform capabilities that enable secure, scalable and governed AI expansion across LSEG's product portfolio. This is a great opportunity for an early-career product manager to develop their craft across a broad range of platform and enablement areas, supporting LSEG teams to build AI-powered products on a common enterprise platform. WHAT YOU'LL BE DOING: Supports the definition of vision, strategy and roadmap for LSEG's Enterprise AI Platform, working closely with stakeholders including product managers & engineering teams. Assists in owning product direction for one or more platform capability areas (e.g. platform services, developer tooling, agent capabilities, governance and enablement), as assigned. Participate in hands-on frontier AI products, prototyping, building and validating with customers Partners with Engineering to help deliver secure, scalable and reliable platform capabilities, tracking delivery progress and surfacing blockers. Helps maintain platform standards, documentation and reusable services that support AI product development across LSEG. Supports adoption of common AI infrastructure and tooling, including gathering feedback from internal teams and users. Works with Security, Architecture, Governance and Risk teams to help ensure platform features comply with enterprise policies and Responsible AI standards. Engages with product teams across LSEG to gather requirements, document use cases and support roadmap prioritisation. Helps define and track success metrics for platform adoption, operational performance and developer experience. Assists in coordinating with industry partners and cloud providers on specific platform initiatives. Contributes to LSEG's Enterprise AI strategy by supporting delivery of foundational platform capabilities. WHAT YOU'LL BRING: Bachelor's degree in Finance, Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems or equivalent experience. Some understanding of or strong interest in AI platforms, LLM ecosystems, APIs and enterprise software is desirable. Early career experience in Product Management, Business Analysis, Engineering or a related field (internships and graduate schemes considered). Experience or coursework involving software platforms, APIs or cloud-native services is a plus. Strong communication skills and the ability to work effectively with cross-functional teams. Curiosity, willingness to learn quickly, and comfort operating in a fast-changing environment. Preferred: Exposure to financial data, analytics systems or enterprise-scale data workflows. Familiarity with basic BI tools, metrics dashboards or monitoring stacks. Prior experience working in or alongside technology teams in a large organisation.

Given the increase in natural disasters and potential for increased credit losses, we are seeking someone with deep property and flood insurance industry knowledge. As a valued colleague on our Servicing and Insurance Policy team, you will contribute to the development and maintenance of the Single-Family servicing and insurance policies and implement changes that align with Fannie Mae's strategy. In this role, you will ensure policies reflect the appropriate risk tolerance and compliance with all relevant regulatory guidance, as well as provide guidance on internal policies, business standards, and regulatory interpretation. You will provide expertise to internal and external customers and industry participants. Apply technical understanding of insurance industry practices and principles and review and analyze policies to ensure alignment with the evolving industry, regulatory, and economic landscape. Research regulatory interpretations and leading industry practices and make recommendations for new policies and standards to manage the credit book. Evaluate and monitor emerging risks and regulatory changes to ensure our policies remain current and risk appropriate. Evaluate market trends or other relevant data in developing policy conclusions, including preparing reports or analyses for management or larger groups with findings and recommendations. Consider, where applicable, relevant enterprise systems and processes to develop optimal policy guidance. Ensure clear and consistent communication of Fannie Mae’s policies through authoring formal policy communications. Provide policy support and expertise on broad corporate initiatives that further Fannie Mae’s strategic mission and enable innovation. Respond to policy inquiries and policy-related customer engagement requests.

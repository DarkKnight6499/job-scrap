Summary The Credit Business Analyst supports strategic initiatives, process improvement, AI and automation enablement, and operational effectiveness across Credit Administration. This position partners with business stakeholders to evaluate processes, document requirements, strengthen controls, and identify responsible uses of AI, automation, and workflow tools that improve efficiency, consistency, and decision-making. The analyst independently manages assigned analysis, documentation, reporting, issue tracking, and project-support activities. Duties & Responsibilities Analyze Credit Administration processes, policies, procedures, and business needs. Facilitate stakeholder discussions; document current-state activities; identify gaps, risks, bottlenecks, and root causes; and recommend practical improvements that strengthen efficiency, consistency, controls, and the team member experience. Develop and maintain clear, accurate business and operational documentation, including business requirements, process maps, procedures, job aids, decision logs, issue documentation, and project materials. Translate complex information into concise, business-friendly language for varied audiences. Identify, evaluate, and advance opportunities to use AI, automation, and workflow tools across Credit Administration. Partner with business stakeholders to define use cases, expected benefits, business requirements, risks, controls, and measures of success; support prioritization, implementation readiness, responsible adoption, and ongoing process improvement for approved solutions. Partner with Credit Administration leaders, lending teams, Operations, Risk Management, Compliance, Internal Audit, and other business areas to support cross-functional initiatives. Coordinate discussions, clarify business needs, document decisions, and help drive timely resolution of open items. Support project planning and execution by maintaining milestones, risks, issues, dependencies, decisions, and action items. Prepare status updates and management-ready materials, monitor assigned deliverables, and escalate material concerns or barriers in a timely manner. Develop, maintain, and analyze operational reports, inventories, dashboards, metrics, and other management information. Validate data accuracy, identify trends and performance drivers, and present actionable insights that support business decisions and continuous improvement. Support audit, exam, issue-remediation, policy, change-readiness, and special-project activities. Gather and organize evidence, track corrective actions, update procedures and communications, safeguard confidential information in accordance with Bank policies, and complete other assigned responsibilities. It is the responsibility of this role to take ownership of all tasks and challenges that they encounter in the operation of their assigned position. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions. Minimum Experience Four (4) to seven (7) years of progressively responsible experience in business analysis, process improvement, project delivery, operations, or a related function. Experience should include independently analyzing business processes, documenting requirements and procedures, preparing business recommendations, and supporting cross-functional initiatives. Self-motivated, detail-oriented, adaptable, and able to work effectively both independently and in a team-oriented environment.

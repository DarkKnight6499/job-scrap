Associate Analyst / Analyst, Middle Office (Contract Management)
Cheniere Energy

POSITION OVERVIEW The Associate Analyst / Analyst, Middle Office (Contract Management) reports directly to the Director, Contract Management and will focus on daily activities related to processing trading activity, trade reporting, and providing contract management analysis. RESPONSIBILITIES AND ESSENTIAL DUTIES Assist in monitoring transactions and amendments to ensure trade accuracy in compliance with underlying documentation. Assist in the contract review and coordination of approvals for execution of enabling agreements. Process daily reconciliation reports and identify parties requiring tax certificates or deals and/or counterparties requiring action to resolve reconciliation deficiency. Maintain a master inventory of subscriptions including annual budget allocations for multiple departments, process updates to users lists, and prepare invoices with appropriate departmental allocations. Coordinate all necessary documentation required for updates to Oracle records and monitor timely processing of invoices. Effectively communicate and coordinate with external Customers and internal Front Office and Middle Office teams to complete the confirmation process including investigation of differences in transaction terms. Liaison with external Customers gathering/distributing documents in support of master reference data collection and customer vetting. Maintain contract documentation for all commercial business including quarterly reporting requirements to various teams throughout the company. Regulatory reporting support. Assist on any ad hoc projects or requests and cross-train to help handle daily functions of the broader Middle Office team to create efficiencies within the group. The duties and responsibilities described are not a comprehensive list and additional tasks may be assigned to the employee as needed. KNOWLEDGE AND SKILLS Ideal candidate possesses knowledge of and has experience within Mid Office Operations in a commodity trading environment. This knowledge includes an understanding of the techniques and analytical tools used to validate and process commodity transactions, contracts and confirmations. Skills: Excellent verbal and written communication skills. Interest and understanding of natural gas markets. Ability to communicate with people of different professional backgrounds. Must work well cross-functionally with all internal stakeholders. Self-motivated and ability to handle multiple responsibilities. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Strong technical skills in MS Excel and SharePoint. EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE Education and Certifications: Bachelor’s degree required, preferably in Business, Accounting, Finance, or related field. Experience: Associate Analyst: Zero (0) to two (2) years’ relevant work experience. Understanding of gas marketing activities, including transportation, storage, financial transactions and trading platforms is preferred. OpenLink Endur experience is a plus. Analyst: Minimum two (2) years’ relevant work experience, preferably in a commodity trading and/or marketing business. Understanding of gas marketing activities, including transportation, storage, financial transactions and trading platforms is preferred. OpenLink Endur experience is a plus.

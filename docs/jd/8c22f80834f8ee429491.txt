Responsibilities The Liquidity Analyst collaborates with the Liquidity team, Treasury Management, Commercial Banking, and Corporate Treasury to support liquidity optimization, enhance deposit stability, and inform balance sheet decisions. This role applies analytical expertise to evaluate deposit trends and liquidity dynamics, translating complex data into clear, actionable insights for business partners. Working across teams, the Liquidity Analyst contributes to strategic initiatives by supporting analysis that informs pricing, product, and portfolio decisions, while advancing ONB’s priorities around organic growth, client experience, and sales effectiveness. The role supports deposit growth and portfolio optimization efforts by analyzing deposit performance, assisting with earnings credit rate evaluations, and partnering with others on the Liquidity team, Relationship Managers, Treasury Management Consultants, and Product teams to develop client-focused liquidity insights. The Analyst helps build and maintain dashboards, reporting, and models, and contributes to analysis of deposit flows across commercial segments. Additionally, the Liquidity Analyst monitors key liquidity and funding trends, identifies emerging risks and opportunities, and escalates insights to senior team members to support informed decision-making.

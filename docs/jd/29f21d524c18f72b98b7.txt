Assoc Financial Analyst
Regeneron
Location: RENSS - GLOBAL VIEW
Link: https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/RENSS---GLOBAL-VIEW/Assoc-Financial-Analyst_R51756

Summary: Responsible for assisting in the preparation of budgets and forecasts for the development and commercialization of all of Regeneron’s drug candidates. Essential Duties and Responsibilities include, but are not limited to, the following: · Assist in the preparation of all IOPS sections of annual budgets and quarterly reforecasts: · Operating expenses, drug supply, outside expenses, headcount and FTEs · Provide support in report generation and analysis across multiple elements of IOPS Finance · Provide support for explaining variances between actual and budgeted results · Support financial analyses and presentations as assigned by either IOPS Senior Financial Analysts or IOPS Finance Senior Management · Seek ways to continually improve IOPS business knowledge as well as existing forecast models, reports, etc. Education and Experience: · Requires BS/BA in Finance, Accounting or Business Administration with 0-3 years of relevant analytical experience · Work experience or internship in a manufacturing and/or science environment is a plus · Experience with Hyperion, Oracle, SAP or similar financial systems is a plus Does this sound like you?

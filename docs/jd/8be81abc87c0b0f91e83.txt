Job Description: As part of our journey to build and mature a next-generation Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) platform, the Associate Risk Analyst in Corporate Risk will support the enhancement, maintenance, and ongoing administration of enterprise risk and compliance applications. This role partners with business and technology teams to deliver application improvements, support user needs, and contribute to risk management and compliance initiatives. The role also supports the analysis of risk and compliance data, helping to develop reports, dashboards, and insights that enable informed decision-making. The ideal candidate is analytical, detail-oriented, and eager to learn how technology and data can improve risk management processes. This position provides the opportunity to gain experience in business analysis, data analytics, reporting, project support, and GRC platform administration. The Expertise and Skills You Bring Bachelor's degree in Business, Information Systems, Risk Management, Finance, or a related field 2-5 years of experience in risk, compliance, business analysis or financial services Advanced experience with Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) platforms such as Archer, IBM OpenPages, or similar applications is a plus Experience with SharePoint and design tools are a plus Experience analyzing and interpreting data to identify trends, risks, and opportunities for process improvement Familiarity with reporting and visualization tools such as Tableau, Power BI, DOMO, or similar platforms Experience with Excel, SQL, Snowflake, or other data analysis tools is a plus Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills Strong attention to detail and commitment to delivering high-quality work Excellent written and verbal communication skills Ability to work optimally in a collaborative environment with business and technology partners Adaptability and willingness to learn in a dynamic, fast-paced organization The Team Corporate Risk is within the Corporate Services Business unit that focuses on the support and maintenance of the enterprise GRC application. Central to the team’s mission is close collaboration with partners throughout the Firm – including Business Leaders, Legal, Compliance, Audit, and Security – in pursuit of our common goal of protecting the Firm, its associates, and customers.

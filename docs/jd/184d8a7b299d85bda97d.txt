JPMorgan Chase & Co. (NYSE: JPM) is a leading global financial services firm with assets of $2.5 trillion and operations worldwide. The Corporate Responsibility (CR) department supports the firm’s business objectives by advancing a more durable and safe financial system and an economy that works for all, so the firm can best serve its customers, clients and communities around the world. The department includes sub-departments focused on policy advocacy and lobbying, philanthropy, sustainability, impact finance, community engagement and data analysis and research, and this role sits within CR’s Strategy / Chief Operating Office, partnering with CR leaders to solve strategic problems and promote cross-CR operating and management rhythms. As an Associate in the Corporate Responsibility COO team, you will help solve operations and execution challenges critical to Corporate Responsibility and to the firm, and help build and run programs, forums, and operating rhythms that enable CR teams to deliver impact for our customers, clients, and communities.

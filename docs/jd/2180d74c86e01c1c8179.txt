Job Description As a valued colleague on our team, you will act as project lead for the development and implementation of all components of financial models supporting investment and capital market activities. You will act as a point of contact for the development and implementation of financial models for one or all of the following areas: derivatives valuation, interest rate modeling, potential future exposure (PFE) for derivatives, and hedge accounting optimization. THE IMPACT YOU WILL MAKE This position will be a key contributor for models that have direct business impact. This position will be responsible mainly in interest rate modeling areas, including curve construction, term structure, derivatives valuation and mortgage rate modeling. Apply advanced skill, knowledge, and/or experience while leading teams in translating complex mathematical, business, and financial modeling logic into software code, as well as design and execute modeling application systems via distributed computing both on premise and on external cloud to achieve efficiency, scalability, and modularity. Lead the design and execution of test cases for modeling and analytical software applications to ensure they meet business needs and model requirements. Consider multiple interrelated systems, processes, or models to assess model implementation quality and production control risk. Develop system requirements for model and analytical applications, such as computing power, storage, network, and security. Recommend technology platforms and architecture designs for model applications to ensure robust and scalable execution. Lead the evaluation of emerging technologies and industry best practices of model and analytical system implementation. THE EXPERIENCE YOU BRING TO THE TEAM Minimum Required Experiences: 4 years Shows curiosity and adaptability in learning and responsibly applying new technologies, including artificial intelligence, to reimagine how we work. Desired Experiences: Advanced degree in a highly quantitative field.

Description Summary : The Vice President, Equity Capital Markets (ECM) is responsible for supporting the origination, structuring, and execution of equity and equity-linked transactions. Positioned between senior coverage officers and junior team members, this individual plays a key role in managing client relationships, leading transaction workstreams, and mentoring junior team members. This individual will work closely with senior leaders and junior talent to manage the full lifecycle of transactions—from pitch development and investor engagement to execution and closing. The Vice President will work across a broad range of offerings including IPOs, follow-on offerings, ATM’s, forward ATM’s, convertible securities, and baby bonds. The role requires strong technical expertise, market insight, and the ability to collaborate across product and industry teams to deliver integrated capital markets solutions. Duties and Responsibilities : Lead execution of equity and equity-linked transactions, including IPOs, secondary offerings, and convertibles. Prepare and review client materials such as pitch books, offering memorandums, investor presentations, and internal approval documents. Coordinate with internal teams (Sales & Trading, Research, Legal, Compliance) to ensure seamless execution and regulatory compliance. Support senior bankers in managing client relationships and developing strategic financing solutions. Participate in client pitches, roadshows, and investor meetings. Provide market insights and transaction structuring advice to clients and internal stakeholders. Maintain CRM systems and databases to track deal pipelines and client interactions. Contribute to idea generation and business development efforts. Analyze market trends, investor sentiment, and comparable transactions to identify opportunities. Assist in pricing strategy and syndication planning. Supervise and mentor Analysts and Associates, ensuring high-quality work product and professional development. Support recruiting, onboarding, and training initiatives for junior team members. Partner with industry coverage, M&A, and Debt Capital Markets teams to deliver comprehensive client solutions. Ensure alignment with internal risk, legal, and compliance teams throughout the transaction lifecycle. Performs other duties as assigned.

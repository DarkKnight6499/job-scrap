Overview The Mortgage Business Analyst is responsible for supporting mortgage operations, system enhancements, workflow automation, and regulatory compliance initiatives within a fast-paced banking environment. This role serves as a key liaison between business users, operations teams, compliance, IT, and third-party vendors to deliver scalable solutions that improve efficiency, accuracy, customer experience, and operational controls.

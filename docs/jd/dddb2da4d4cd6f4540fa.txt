Antares Capital

Job Description The Associate, Finance is a critical role at Antares ensuring the accuracy of financial data recorded in various systems and ultimately reported to senior management on a monthly basis and to various stakeholders via quarterly financial statements. This position will be reporting to the MD, Legal Entity Controller and will be responsible for reconciling subledger and general ledger activity and maintaining oversight of balance sheet and income statement general ledger accounts. An ability to manage multiple tasks while working under strict month end reporting deadlines is essential. Our ideal candidate is independent, motivated, detail orientated able to analyze data efficiently and convey conclusions effectively. Responsibilities Prepare timely and accurate account reconciliations for month end close and quarterly financial reporting deadlines. Liaise with various teams across the organization in gathering detailed supporting documentation in order to prepare adjusting journal entries for various general ledger accounts. Prepare disclosures for quarterly financial statements. Explain budget versus actual variances and financial trends. Liaise with auditors and gather audit support as needed during interim reviews and annual audits. Assist with cross team initiatives within the organization and overall process improvements as it relates to the Finance team which may include enhancing or developing internal controls. Access appropriate data from various financial systems, assist with system conversions, create new and enhance existing internal financial reports using primarily Microsoft Dynamics and OneStream software. Maintain files, accounting documents, records, reports and any other pertinent data in accordance with department policies and procedures. Qualifications Bachelor's degree in accounting, finance or business-related discipline. 4-7 years of accounting related professional experience. CPA preferred Skills Experience using Microsoft Dynamics, OneStream software, and fund accounting tools a plus. Ability to analyze and evaluate data, forming accurate conclusions and translating insights into action. Proficient in MS PowerPoint, Excel, and Word. Ability to effectively manage time as demonstrated by efficiently organizing and prioritizing tasks.

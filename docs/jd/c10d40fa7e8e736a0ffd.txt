Purpose The Corporate Banking Associate supports senior team members in the development and marketing of existing and prospective investment grade and non-IG clients across the Industrial sector (A&D, Building materials, Chemicals, Packaging and other Industrials) The individual will work closely with other team members including, other associates, associate directors (vice presidents), directors and managing directors to manage client relationships located in the United States. The associate will be responsible for handling credit related activities, transaction execution and relationship management, including: completion of credit presentations, financial models, marketing and pitch materials performing due diligence, negotiating documentation, and monitoring compliance and industry trends. What You'll Do: Champion a client-centric culture by leveraging banking systems and sector knowledge to deliver value-added solutions for the clients. Evaluate and analyze the financial needs of corporate clients, including the development of financial models. Analyze lending opportunities, prepare marketing materials and presentations, and generally support client pitches and deal execution efforts. Financial and written analysis of companies and industries, including the development of financial/valuation models, pro-forma financial statements and comparable company analysis, etc. Prepare credit presentations and recommendations for review by team members and Global Risk Management; liaise directly with Global Risk Management. Assist in the management of an assigned loan portfolio: coordinate account management with team members, handle customers’ requests for waivers, amendments and restructurings of credit facilities. Support the negotiation of loan terms and documentation with borrowers/other banks, culminating in the closing of transactions. Work closely with the Bank's specialized product groups (GTB, Treasury Services, Capital Markets, etc.) to tailor products to clients and promote effective marketing and delivery of these products to customers and prospects. Work with other Bank Syndicate agents on syndicated deals in order to present information clearly and concisely in credit presentations. Understand how the Bank’s risk appetite and risk culture should be considered in day-to-day activities and decisions. Ensure the adequacy, adherence to and effectiveness of day-to-day business controls to meet obligations with respect to operational risk, regulatory compliance risk, AML/ATF risk and conduct risk, including but not limited to responsibilities under the Operational Risk Management Framework, Regulatory Compliance Risk Management Framework, AML/ATF Global Handbook and the Guidelines for Business Conduct. Champion a high-performance environment and contribute to an inclusive work environment. What You'll Bring: Degree in Business or related field. 2+ years of banking experience in Investment/Corporate/Commercial Lending/Leverage Finance or related environment. Strong financial modeling and accounting skills. Familiarity with industrial sectors preferred Excellent computer skills: Microsoft (Excel, Powerpoint, Word, Sharepoint, etc.); Financial platforms (Factset, CapIQ, BBG), Credit & Risk systems Strong written and communication skills for both internal stakeholders and client interactions.

What we are looking for: Under the supervision of the Portfolio Manager, the Portfolio Analyst will assist in the management and trading of portfolios. In addition, they will assist with market analysis, communications (both internal and external) and collaborate with internal business partners to ensure client needs are being met. Your responsibilities: Work closely with traders and portfolio managers in the day-to-day management of accounts. Track portfolio cash. Work with portfolio managers to ensure portfolios are being managed according to guidelines and firm’s strategies. Assist team in allocating, booking and funding trades. Communicate with internal and external relationships on portfolio additions and withdrawals and tracking of these flows. Provide support for client queries and reports. Update systems and files used to monitor portfolios and strategies. Must have requirements to be successful in this role: Bachelor’s degree in business or science related field preferred. Minimum of 2+ years of experience in portfolio management, portfolio analysis, investment operations, trading support, or a related asset management/investment management environment. Strong technical skills are mandatory, including experience with Python, SQL, artificial intelligence tools, and workflow/process automation. Knowledge of Charles River, Perform, Advent, and Bloomberg is an asset. Ability to work effectively and accurately in a fast-paced environment. Team player with strong interpersonal skills Proficiency with Microsoft Excel, Word and PowerPoint Basic understanding of bond-related concepts and terminology. Fast learner with strong work ethic Additional Information: Fiera Capital adheres to a hybrid working environment with 4 days in-office per week. The salary range for this position is $90,000 - $110,000. This role is also be eligible for additional compensation, including bonuses and employee benefits. This salary range is for New York.

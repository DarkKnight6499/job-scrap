In this multi-faceted role, you will work across the organization, collaborating closely with Product, Engineering, Sales, Finance, Risk, and Operations to deliver a seamless, high-performance transaction banking experience. HOW YOU WILL FULFILL YOUR POTENTIAL Strategic Planning & Execution: Work with senior leadership to develop, refine, and execute TxB’s growth strategy, translating complex business objectives into clear, concise presentations for internal committees (e.g., Board, Operating Group) and external partners. Market & Competitor Intelligence: Conduct deep-dive research on the Transaction Banking and Payments landscapes to uncover key industry shifts, evaluate competitor positioning, and identify potential strategic partnerships or product enhancements. Cross-Functional Collaboration: Facilitate strategic alignment working across product managers, engineering teams, sales coverage, and control functions (Risk, Compliance, Legal) to drive key business initiatives. Performance Management: Define, track, and analyze key performance indicators (KPIs) to monitor business growth, product adoption, and financial performance, providing data-driven insights to senior executives. Industry Knowledge: Strong understanding of the Transaction Banking landscape, including Liquidity, Deposits, Commercial Payments (e.g., ACH, Wire, RTP, SWIFT), and Digital/API-led Products. FinTech Acumen: Solid understanding of the current Transaction Banking landscape, emerging payment technologies, and digital treasury trends. Stakeholder Management: Proven ability to develop, foster, and manage deep relationships with both internal cross-functional partners and external stakeholders in a matrixed environment. Communication & Presentation: Extremely strong written and verbal communication skills. Leadership Presence: Confidence and maturity to interact effectively, present recommendations, and build credibility with senior leaders and business executives.

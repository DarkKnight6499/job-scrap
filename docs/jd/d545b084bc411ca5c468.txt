Job Type General Description This position supports the governance, oversight, analysis, and reporting of Popular’s operational losses and related operational risk activities. Responsibilities include reviewing and reconciling operational losses, analyzing trends and key risk indicators; monitoring root causes, control deficiencies, and remediation, and preparing information for management and governance committees. The position also supports the evaluation and creation of annual Operational Risk capital stress test and scenario analysis. The analyst works closely with business units and other stakeholders, provides appropriate review and challenge of information submitted by the First Line of Defense, and delivers guidance and training to promote consistent and timely reporting. This position will support mainly the operational risk management process and propose to minimize operational risks from evaluation of key risks indicators and evaluation of controls effectiveness, identifying control failures, facilitating risk remediation, and monitoring the first line of defense in an effort to minimize risk exposures and strengthen the overall control environment. This position reports to the Operational Risk Senior Manager. Essential Duties and Responsibilities Operational Losses Governance, Data Management, and Reporting Promotes strong risk and control culture and reinforce awareness of operational losses requirements across Popular. Review operational and potential loss events for completeness, accuracy, timeliness, proper classification, and adequate support. Reconcile Operational Losses data with accounting records; research and resolve discrepancies with the relevant stakeholders. Prepare recurring reports, dashboards, scorecards, KRIs, and Power BI reporting within established deadlines; analyze trends, recurring events, recoveries, and emerging exposures. Support enhancements, testing, and issue resolution for systems, reports, interfaces, and data sources used in the Operational Losses process. Coordinate the annual KRI calibration and threshold review for Operational Risk. Coordinate the annual OLRS user access certification and approval hierarchy updates with IT/InfoSec. Coordinate the annual Corporate Operational Losses Awareness Training, for the business units and other stakeholders. Material Events, Root Cause, Remediation, and Recoveries Coordinate the collection, monitoring and reporting of material loss events that meet management or committee-reporting thresholds. Support root-cause analysis and review contributing factors, control gaps, and corrective actions for significant or recurring events. Monitor remediation commitments and escalate overdue or unsupported matters, preserving First Line ownership while providing Second Line oversight and challenge. Coordinate potential insurance and captive recoveries, and support reporting of gross losses, recoveries, and net losses. Capital Stress Testing and Scenario Analysis Support the annual Operational Risk capital stress-testing process, including preparation, validation, and adjustment of historical loss data. Support loss estimates, add-ons, and low-probability/high-severity scenarios; coordinate inputs with subject-matter experts, Capital Planning, Treasury, Finance, and Insurance. Document and challenge scenario assumptions, calculations, and results, maintaining audit trail. Prepare summaries, work papers, and submission packages for management and governance forums. Governance, Training, and Support Prepare Operational Losses materials and executive summaries for the Operational Risk Committee (ORCO), Risk Management Committee, Senior management Team, and working groups by the required due date. Develop and deliver training and guidance to business-unit liaisons on event reporting, classification, root cause, and remediation. Review and recommend updates to Operational Losses policies, procedures, and job aids. Support responses to internal audits, regulatory reviews, quality-assurance observations, and other reviews related to Operational Losses, reporting, capital stress testing, or scenario analysis. Perform other related duties as assigned consistent with the position's responsibilities. Education Bachelor’s Degree of Business administration, Accounting, Finance, or Industrial Engineer, Data Analytics, or a related field. Experience Preferably, three to five years of experience in Operational Risk Management, Operational Losses, financial or risk data analysis, internal controls, banking, finance, accounting, or a related area. Experience supporting management reporting, reconciliations, root-cause analysis, corrective-action monitoring, capital stress testing, scenario analysis, or governance processes is preferred.

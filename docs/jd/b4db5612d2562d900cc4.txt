Senior Associate Strategic Planning & Ops Duties: Work with global cross-functional teams to define project deliverables, milestones, tasks, identify and manage risks; Create roadmaps, reports, and dashboards for project tracking; Apply Agile principles in all project management activities; Develop necessary plans including communication plans, stakeholder analysis, implementation plans, sustainment plans and project schedules; Schedule and facilitate team meetings including setting agendas, writing meeting minutes and tracking action items to ensure completion; Build and maintain strong relationships with CfOR leaders to understand business objectives and priorities; Identify process gaps root cause and generate creative solutions; Communicate status and issues effectively to appropriate parties, both within CfOR and across Amgen; Manage initiatives to improve and support CfOR capabilities and effectiveness; Help increase efficiency across CfOR with innovative ideas in using technology including Smartsheet Automations and formulas, ChatGPT, and CoPilot to replace manual operations; Build and maintain positive collaborations, communicating, seeking advice, and facilitating critical business decisions. May telecommute. Requirements: Master’s degree (or foreign equivalent) in Computer Science, Information Technology, Project Management, or a related field & 5 years of experience in the job offered or a data-related occupation. Position requires 5 years of experience in the following: Agile project management and Agile frameworks including: Scrum and Kanban; Smartsheet and Excel; Understanding market dynamics across therapeutic areas; Synthesizing and analyzing cross-functional information to guide decision-making; Pharmaceutical product lifecycle management, including early-stage development and commercialization; Miro, Visio, SharePoint, OneNote, MS Teams, MS Project, and Office 365; Project leadership in pharmaceutical or health industries; Coding in languages including: Python and Jquery for non-interventional study management application development and enhancement; and Collaborating with global partners for project execution.

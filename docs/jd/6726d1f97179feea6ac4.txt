Job Description Ares' Legal & Compliance Department oversees the Firm's global Legal, Regulatory and Compliance functions and programs. Under the leadership of the Chief Compliance and Regulatory Officer, the Compliance and Regulatory functions are comprised of teams that operate collaboratively with a global focus, including Regulatory, which oversees global regulatory matters relating to the Investment Advisers Act of 1940, Investment Company Act of 1940 and other regulatory regimes. Ares is seeking a talented professional to join our Compliance team to serve as a key team member to drive the creation, enhancement and management of key portions of the Firm’s compliance program. In this role, you will provide guidance to business teams and other partners such as technology, finance and accounting, and corporate strategy. Acting in partnership with other professionals across Legal & Compliance, you will drive initiatives that support and enable the strategic direction of the business, and that are designed to identify opportunities to enhance program effectiveness and mitigate compliance risks. The ideal candidate will possess a strong background in investment adviser compliance and other relevant areas, with experience in finance, accounting and/or regulatory reporting viewed as particularly valuable. They will have a proven track record of operating with rigor, trustworthiness and entrepreneurship within the context of an evolving regulatory and business landscape. They will also demonstrate an ability to operate within a dynamic and fast-changing environment, fostering and maintaining relationships across the firm’s various functional groups to ensure the continued enhancement and success of the business along with its governance and compliance framework. Primary Functions & Responsibilities Actively engage departmental senior leadership and other Compliance colleagues to drive key initiatives across the Investment Adviser Compliance function. Partner across Compliance, Regulatory Reporting, Finance & Accounting, Technology and other functions to advance data capabilities, systems, workflows and other initiatives that contribute to the formation and achievement of the enterprise-wide data strategy. Drive key initiatives supporting the robustness of the firm’s SEC and CFTC compliance programs considering emerging regulatory trends and business requirements. Provide consistent and ongoing Compliance guidance to the business; partner with and advise the respective Compliance leads and their client groups on regulatory policies and procedures, including those that are designed to detect and mitigate potential conflicts of interest; and proactively provide trends and analyses relating to Compliance-related activities. Partner with Compliance colleagues to advise key business stakeholders, including Finance & Accounting and relevant investment groups, on maintaining and updating applicable compliance policies, procedures and related processes. Support the firm’s key governance activities related to valuation, reporting, management fee calculations and expense allocation in addition to other finance and accounting-adjacent regulatory initiatives, as well as other areas as needed. Identify and help advance practical applications of emerging technology, including Generative AI, to enhance the efficiency, quality and scalability of role-relevant Compliance processes, acting in partnership with the Compliance Strategy team, Technology and other stakeholders, and operating within the context of appropriate risk management and governance. Demonstrate initiative and creativity in independently identifying and recommending ideas and solutions to business contacts, senior leaders and Compliance leadership. Develop, maintain and broaden effective working relationships across the organization and within the Compliance Department. Identify potential areas of vulnerability and risk and elevate key findings along with recommendations to senior leadership.

As a Finance Manager, you will consult with function leaders to understand their objectives, develop and analyze their plans, and provide them with the essential financial insights they need to make effective business decisions. A Finance Manager is an established, independent individual contributor, whose work has a direct impact on the team's outcomes. This role will be based in New York, and it is a hybrid role with a requirement of working at least three days a week in the office. We will count on you to: Collaborate with manager and team members to prepare budgets, allocations, forecasts, monthly variance reports, and conduct expense analysis for Corporate Function budgets. Review and analyze financial planning data and reports to provide actionable recommendations to management. Support the month-end close process by evaluating recurring and ad hoc reports, developing a comprehensive understanding of P&L structures, and analyzing variances against forecasts, budgets, and prior year results Ensure compliance with financial regulations, policies, and internal controls. Analyze data extracts from MMC’s data warehouse to support key strategic initiatives. Assist in creating financial presentations utilizing various formats, including charts, graphs, and other business intelligence visualization tools Research, identify, and explain expense and operational variances to highlight risks and opportunities for improvement. Collaborate with manager to define and deliver standardized expense reporting and allocation templates. Generate and deliver regular and ad hoc reports to business partners. Manage and execute special projects, including report standardization, financial model development, and the integration of the latest business intelligence tools to enhance recurring activities. Oversee month-end and year-end closing processes. Manage corporate allocations to business units and deliver variance analyses with explanations. Ad-hoc Analysis Develop expertise in reporting and analytic tools and data sources, including, Microsoft Office, Hyperion Interactive Reporting, Essbase, iProcurement. Support quantitative analysis and projects relating to operating and staffing models. Project Management Support Provide business analytic and/or project management support for Corporate Functions. Assist with the development and maintenance of project plans and timelines. Coordinate between business units, operations and finance to accomplish assigned project objectives. Assist in the coordination of communication and key initiatives. What you need to have: A Bachelor’s Degree in Finance, Accounting or a related field.

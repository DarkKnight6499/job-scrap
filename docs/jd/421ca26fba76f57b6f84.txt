We are seeking an Associate to join our dynamic team in Boston, MA providing support across a wide range of projects and industries, with a primary focus on distressed real estate projects. This role offers a unique opportunity to engage in a variety of tasks, ranging from low-to-mid-level responsibilities to higher-level strategic initiatives within our firm. The ideal candidate will demonstrate a strong understanding or quick ability to grasp our work, with a background in finance preferred. Approximately 40% of the role involves on-site management of properties, requiring hands-on operational oversight. Our clients often find themselves in situations of distress that require expert advice swiftly. These situations, whether they present as a crisis or an opportunity, may necessitate restructuring, workout, turnaround, or even wind-down strategies. During these critical times, our clients look to us to provide a steadying hand for the best outcome. The position is based in the Boston area with expectations to work in the office on a hybrid schedule. Job Responsibilities Support the reporting manager in project management and financial analysis across a wide range of projects and industries, including real estate and operating companies. Execute and review detailed financial modeling, build and analyze cash flow forecasts, and conduct comprehensive financial statement reviews. Assist in the preparation of assessments and court reporting. Conduct industry research and contribute to client engagements with due diligence. Collaborate effectively within project teams and interact with senior-level client personnel. Manage physical on-site property operations and ensure operational efficiencies. Maintain strict adherence to project timelines and deliverables. Contribute to the development and enhancement of internal processes and procedures. Demonstrate proactive problem-solving and decision-making skills in a fast-paced environment. Uphold the highest ethical standards and maintain credibility in all interactions.

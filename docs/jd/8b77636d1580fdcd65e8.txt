Role Summary

Global X ETFs is seeking a highly motivated professional to join the Quantitative & Fixed Income Investment (QFI) Team. This is a buy-side ETF portfolio management role; the PM Associate will work alongside senior Portfolio Managers and Traders in the day-to-day management of a growing suite of ETFs spanning equities, fixed income, and derivatives in both index-based and actively managed strategies. The role operates in a trading-desk environment and combines continuous intraday portfolio monitoring and implementation with the development of reporting, analytics, and automation. The PM Associate will regularly collaborate with Trading & Capital Markets, Portfolio Administration, Risk, Compliance, Technology, and Product Development.

The PM Associate is expected to manage concurrent market-driven priorities, exercise judgment under time constraints, and communicate and escalate issues promptly. The ideal candidate is self-directed, takes ownership of recurring responsibilities through to completion, and brings a strong foundation in portfolio management or trading, ideally within the ETF ecosystem.



Key Responsibilities

 - Support the ETF portfolio management and investment process across a suite of funds with distinct investment objectives, including:

 - Monitor positions, cash, fund flows, exposures, performance, and scheduled events throughout the trading day

 - Prepare trade recommendations across equities, fixed income, and derivatives, including futures, swaps, and listed and FLEX options, in support of portfolio rebalances, creation/redemption activity, and cash management

 - Investigate portfolio and operational exceptions, communicate findings, and escalate issues to the team in a timely manner

 - Build, maintain, and enhance the reporting, analytics, and operational infrastructure used to manage the funds, using Excel, Python, SQL, and AI-enabled automation

 - Support new product and fund enhancement initiatives by evaluating portfolio implementation, operational, structural, and governance considerations



Qualifications

 - 2-5 years of professional experience in a time-sensitive investment, trading, or operational environment, ideally in portfolio management or trading within the ETF ecosystem

 - A track record of owning recurring processes with daily or intraday deadlines and following them through to completion

 - Advanced Excel skills and demonstrated working proficiency with Python or SQL; experience using AI tools for research, analysis, and workflow automation a plus

 - Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with excellent attention to detail and concise, effective communication

 - Experience with Bloomberg and OMS/EMS platforms such as AIM, EMSX, Aladdin, or Charles River a plus

 - Bachelor’s degree required; CFA charterholder or progress toward CFA designation a plus

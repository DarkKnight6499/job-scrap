The Hartford’s Small Business team provides industry-leading coverage for small businesses across the United States, including Spectrum Business Owner’s Policy, Workers’ Compensation, and Commercial Auto. The Associate Director, Product Management – Alternative Distribution will manage a portfolio of strategic distribution relationships across multiple channels, with accountability for Partner Agency performance, financial results, and execution of key business initiatives. This role will develop and implement strategies to drive profitable growth, strengthen competitiveness, and optimize portfolio performance while leading cross-functional efforts across Product, Underwriting, Pricing, Digital Experience, Marketing, Legal/Compliance, and Transformation. The ideal candidate brings strong financial acumen, product management expertise, and Partner Agency and Channel management capabilities, with the ability to translate data, market insights, and business opportunities into actionable strategies that improve top- and bottom-line results. Key Responsibilities Achieve or exceed revenue, income, growth, and profitability targets for assigned Channels and Partner Agencies. Develop a strategic vision and tactical plan to profitably expand market share within Alternative Distribution Channels and Partner Agencies. Address day-to-day needs of functional partners supporting your assigned Channels and Partner Agencies. Use countrywide data, channel insights, and market trends to anticipate shifts in competitiveness, conversion, and profitability. Partner with cross-functional teammates to develop, customize, launch, and improve products and capabilities across distribution platforms. Maintain competitiveness in non-ICON platforms with levers that include Product presentment, Pricing options, and classification and underwriting policies. Build effective working relationships across Product, Pricing, Underwriting, Agency Sales, Data Analytics, Claims, Service, Digital Experience, Marketing Communications, Legal/Compliance, and Transformation. Monitor market dynamics, customer needs, and partner/channel performance to lead improvements to business offerings and processes. Cultivate external relationships that support business performance and strategic goals. Required Capabilities and Experience 7+ years of experience in Commercial Lines or Personal Lines Product Management, Product Development, Pricing, Underwriting, or a comparable role. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, including the ability to challenge current approaches and identify improved solutions. Ability to conduct competitor analysis and develop an in-depth understanding of market dynamics, underwriting initiatives, rate levels, policy forms, economic trends, and emerging issues. Financial acumen and ability to understand the business impact of product, pricing, underwriting, and channel decisions. Ability to evaluate loss cost models and support innovative, effective risk pricing strategies. Strong presentation, communication, interpersonal, and relationship management skills, with emphasis on customer focus, collaboration, and influence. Solution-oriented mindset with ability to think strategically, implement change, and balance long-term priorities with short-term business needs. Strong organization, documentation, attention to detail, and decision-making skills. Team-oriented approach with demonstrated ability to build strong internal and external relationships. Drive-for-results mentality, with ability to manage multiple priorities and make decisions in a business context. Independent thinker who can effectively challenge and advocate to ensure sound product, pricing, and business decisions. Knowledge of state laws and regulatory considerations preferred. Ability to travel up to 10% for market-related or partner-related meetings.

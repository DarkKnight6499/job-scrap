We are currently looking for a Treasury Analyst 2 to join our team in Franklin, TN. This position supports Nissan’s capital markets team in the U.S., with a focus on compliance and reporting, new debt issuance, and liquidity planning. A Day in the Life: The responsibilities listed are not intended to be an exhaustive list of all duties for this role. The position will also provide strategic support to Nissan’s treasury department, including but not limited to the following functional areas: Securitization Compliance, including Reg AB Investor Relations Strategic Funding and Liquidity Analysis Financial Modeling Competitive Research Cross-functional Projects Additional support may be required for DOA compliance and transaction due diligence. This position will report to the Manager responsible for compliance and strategic funding. Additional tasks include: Completes monthly reporting procedures for NMAC’s asset-backed securities. Provides audit, internal compliance and due diligence support. Evaluates debt financing and liquidity management strategies. Enhances current and future state analytical capabilities by improving data management practices and developing financial models that support strategic business decisions. Performs cash flow and liquidity analysis for entities within the MC-A region. Conducts quantitative analysis and develops financial models in support of treasury-related activities. Provides competitive benchmarking research for other OEMs and sales finance captives. Participates in cross-functional projects, assisting with communication across multiple entities and internal teams. Reads and understands requirements detailed in certain legal documents related to treasury-related activities. Participates in preparation of disclosure documents, coordinates due diligence, and develops materials for rating agencies and investors. Who We’re Looking For Required: Bachelor’s degree with 3+ years of financial and/or accounting experience. Fundamental understanding of treasury operations and data management. The ability to work on-site in Franklin, TN. Ability to travel up to 25%. Desired: Master’s degree and CFA or CTP certifications. Experience with analytical research tools including Bloomberg and EDGAR. Strong oral and written communication skills. Attention to detail to ensure information provided to management is accurate. Ability to balance multiple priorities and meet deadlines while demonstrating strong teamwork and communication skills.

What You Can Expect: Payment Operations Oversee payment processes across multiple rails (SWIFT, Fedwire, ACH, etc); coordinate electronic funds transfers with subsidiaries/business units. Advise on payment regulatory considerations Cash Management Oversee daily cash positioning and short-term liquidity forecasting, ensuring sufficient liquidity for operational and strategic needs Treasury Systems & Process Improvement Optimize Treasury Management System and processes including prioritization of opportunities Drive automation and process enhancements for efficiency and accuracy. Enhance and streamline reporting and visibility to cash accounts. Reporting & Analytics Provide insights to senior management for strategic decision-making. Support ad-hoc reporting and projects Leadership Lead and mentor a team of treasury professionals. Delegate tasks appropriately and foster a collaborative, high-performance environment. Champion a culture of continuous learning and professional growth within Enterprise Finance. Drive adoption of automation, AI, advanced analytics, and digital enablement to enhance financial processes and decision-making. What You’ll Need: Bachelor’s degree in finance, Accounting, or related field (CTP or CFA preferred). 5+ years of treasury experience, ideally in a multinational environment. Proficiency with Kyriba Strong knowledge of cash management, payment regulations. Excellent leadership, communication, and stakeholder management abilities.

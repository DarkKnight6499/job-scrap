The Senior Risk Officer will play a critical role in advancing and strengthening the Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) Program and Framework. This position is responsible for leading strategic initiatives that enhance the effectiveness, efficiency, and regulatory alignment of the organization's third-party risk management capabilities while supporting ongoing program maturity objectives. This role will serve as a key contributor to the implementation of TPRM artificial intelligence and automation initiatives designed to significantly improve operational efficiency, reduce the cost of performing third-party due diligence activities, and shorten due diligence cycle times. The position will partner across Risk, Technology, and Business Lines to identify opportunities to leverage emerging technologies that improve risk assessment quality, consistency, and scalability. In addition, the Senior Risk Officer will lead efforts to further align the methodologies, and lifecycle processes with evolving regulatory expectations and industry best practices. Responsibilities include evaluating and enhancing key processes, supporting framework development, and defining business requirements for the TPRM system of record to streamline workflows, improve execution capabilities, enhance data quality, and strengthen reporting and management information. The Senior Risk Officer will serve as a key resource during internal and external examinations, including responses to Audit, Second Line Quality Control reviews, and regulatory inquiries. The individual will be expected to provide subject matter expertise and support evidence requests. Responsibilities & Qualifications Duties & Responsibilities: Lead the implementation of TPRM artificial intelligence and automation initiatives to improve operational efficiency, reduce due diligence costs, and accelerate review cycle times. Drive enhancements to the TPRM Program and Framework to ensure alignment with evolving regulatory expectations and industry-leading practices. Develop and maintain third-party risk methodologies, standards, and lifecycle processes that improve consistency, effectiveness, and risk oversight. Define and prioritize business requirements for the TPRM system of record to improve workflow automation, reporting, data quality, and user experience. Enhance fourth-party risk management and concentration risk oversight through improved dependency mapping, monitoring, and reporting capabilities.

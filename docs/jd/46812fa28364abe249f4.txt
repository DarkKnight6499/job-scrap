We are looking to add a Risk Finance Leader to Aon! Are you passionate about helping organisations make better risk and capital decisions? Do you have the vision to build the future of risk financing across one of the world's leading professional services firms? Join Aon as Head of Risk Finance Consulting and play a pivotal role in defining and delivering our client-facing risk capital strategy across the region. Our global Risk Finance team helps large multinational organisations quantify, finance, and optimise their risk exposures to improve resilience, capital efficiency and business performance. This is a hybrid role with the flexibility to work both virtually and from any US Aon office. Aon is in the business of better decisions At Aon, we shape decisions for the better to protect and enrich the lives of people around the world. As an organization, we are united through trust as one inclusive team and we are passionate about helping our colleagues and clients succeed. What the day will look like As Head of Risk Finance Consulting, you will set the strategic direction for Aon's risk finance proposition across the region. Combining client engagement, business growth, innovation, and leadership, you will help large multinational organisations better understand, quantify, and finance their risk exposures, connecting clients to specialist broking expertise to execute on complex and innovative deal structures. Key responsibilities include: Owning the Risk Financing strategy and growth agenda across the region. Partnering with colleagues across Commercial Risk Solutions, Industry Practices and Client Leadership teams to bring innovative risk financing solutions to clients. Supporting business development activity, including major client pursuits and tenders, by articulating the value and differentiation of Aon's risk financing capabilities. Driving the development and deployment of next-generation analytics tools and solutions, including Risk Financing Analytics (RFA). Providing thought leadership and delivering risk insights that help clients make better business decisions. Building, leading, mentoring and developing a team of highly skilled risk financing professionals. How this opportunity is different This is a opportunity to influence the future direction of Aon's risk capital capabilities across the region. You will work closely with senior leaders to shape strategy, drive innovation, and accelerate growth. You will collaborate with experts across risk consulting, broking, analytics, and industry practices, helping clients navigate some of the most significant and sophisticated risks facing organisations today. Combining strategic leadership, client engagement, innovation and commercial impact, this role offers outstanding visibility and the opportunity to build solutions that support some of the world's leading businesses. Skills and experience that will lead to success Significant experience in risk financing, analytics, capital modelling, or risk consulting gained within a consulting, insurance, reinsurance or related professional services environment. Demonstrated success leading, mentoring, and developing high-performing teams across multiple geographies. Strong partner management and influencing skills, with experience engaging senior executives, clients, and business leaders. Highly numerate with a degree in a quantitative field; a postgraduate qualification or relevant professional designation would be helpful.

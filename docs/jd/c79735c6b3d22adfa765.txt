Bring your accounting expertise to the center of one of JPMorganChase’s fastest-evolving businesses. As an Associate on the Card & Connected Commerce Controllers team, you’ll help power Chase Media Solutions by owning the end-to-end accounting for revenue and receivables—turning card-linked offer performance data into accurate, decision-ready financial outcomes. In this role, you’ll apply revenue recognition standards with precision, generate invoices, execute intercompany transfers, and actively manage receivables to keep the business moving. You’ll be trusted to independently investigate variances, perform deep-dive data mining, and elevate reporting through thoughtful analysis and strong technical execution. You’ll also work cross-functionally with Sales, Business Operations, and Finance in a highly collaborative, high-visibility environment—building a broad, practical understanding of the full Card & Connected Commerce P&L while contributing to a business that’s growing and changing quickly. Job Responsibilities Assist with P&L reporting and month-end close processes; Revenue Recognition, Invoicing, Receivables Management.

Position Summary The Associate Vice President serves as the senior techno-functional leader for the firm’s core finance operations spanning Accounting, Accounts Receivable, Billing, and Payroll, together with the reporting and data integrity that underpin them. Sitting at the intersection of finance and technology within the Finance Process & Operations (FPO) team, the role owns the process and systems landscape for these functions and sets the strategy for how AI, automation, and emerging technology create value across them. As the AI and technology champion for these functions, the AVP defines where automation and AI deliver the highest return, maintains a prioritized portfolio of use cases, and establishes the governance needed to adopt new technology safely. The role pairs this forward-looking mandate with deep accountability for data integrity and controls - ensuring accuracy, auditability, and compliance across record-to-report and order-to-cash processes. The AVP is a trusted strategic partner to finance leadership, translating business goals into a technology and process roadmap and representing these functions in firmwide initiatives. The role leads a team of FPO staff and consultants through agile delivery, manages risk and exposure across the domain, and communicates progress and trade-offs to senior leadership. Success requires the judgment to balance strategic transformation with the day-to-day realities of finance operations, and the influence to drive outcomes across a broad set of stakeholders.

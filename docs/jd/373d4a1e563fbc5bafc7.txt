The Team you are joining: The Capital Controls & Planning team is a complementary function that bridges Development and Delivery teams with Finance and Accounting. Operating in conjunction with but independently from Regional Project Controls, the team provides an enterprise-level layer of oversight focused on financial integrity, standardization, and performance visibility across construction programs. The team ensures accuracy, consistency, and predictability in cost and financial data across regional development and construction projects. By partnering cross-functionally, they establish and enforce standards, enhance data quality, and enable reliable reporting that supports informed decision‑making at both regional and corporate levels. Through proactive governance and insights, the team helps drive financial discipline, improve forecast confidence, and strengthen alignment between project execution and financial outcomes. Summary : Ada Infrastructure is seeking a highly skilled and experienced Capital Controls & Planning Regional Lead with a background in real estate, construction support and/or financial controls to lead the function in the US. In this role, you will act as a business partner to key stakeholders in the Ares Digital Infrastructure business including Regional Leadership, Development teams (spanning Design, Development, Delivery and Project Controls), Investments, Portfolio Management & Growth (“PMG”) and Fund Accounting.

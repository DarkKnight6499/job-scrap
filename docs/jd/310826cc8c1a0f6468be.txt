Sr. Associate, Capital Markets & Risk As a Senior Associate on the Securitization Investor Reporting team, you will gain valuable exposure to capital markets while developing a unique perspective into Capital One’s securitization programs and their financial reporting performance. Our team is responsible for preparing regulatory reporting required to support our securitization programs. Your responsibilities will include delivering results to senior leadership, preparing regular analysis to support our Capital Markets Funding team, engaging in cross-functional initiatives including technology modernization, and driving automation. You will also work with an outstanding team to identify process efficiency and implement solutions with exposure to senior management and various departments across the Company. Responsibilities: Become a subject matter expert of our auto and credit card securitization reporting Assist in the preparation and review of our monthly Form 10-D filings and supporting reports Analyze and summarize monthly results, presenting key findings and performance metrics to senior leadership and business partners Respond to internal and external audit requests, including in support of the annual Regulation AB audit process Calculate daily and monthly wires to the trustee Build cross functional relationships to stay attuned to business changes and key events that will impact financial results Participate in key corporate projects such as system upgrades, process automations, and enhancements Own BAU processes and help evolve our processes into the next generation by implementing emerging technology tools to reimagine the future Perform cross-team support and ad hoc analysis and reporting as needed, such as for our Form 10-Q and 10-K filings, Earnings and others Basic Qualifications: Bachelor's degree or Military experience At least 2 years of experience in Financial Services ​ Preferred Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, or Economics 2+ years of experience in the use of Excel, SQL, Python or a combination 1+ year of experience in Capital Markets Operations Strong problem-solving skills At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position. ​ The minimum and maximum full-time annual salaries for this role are listed below, by location.

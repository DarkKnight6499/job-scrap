Who We Are Looking For We are seeking a quantitative risk professional to join Corporate Audit as Model Risk Audit, Assistant Vice President. The role will lead and execute audit engagements focused on models, quantitative tools, analytics, and artificial intelligence used across State Street. The successful candidate will bring strong hands-on experience developing, validating, implementing, or reviewing models within a professional financial-services environment. Candidates coming from Model Risk Management, Model Validation, Model Development, Quantitative Risk, or Model Risk Audit will be considered based on their technical experience, leadership potential, and ability to develop audit-management skills. Under the direction of senior audit management, the Assistant Vice President will execute model-risk audits across planning, risk assessment, testing, stakeholder discussions, issue development, and reporting. The individual will also monitor emerging model-risk themes, help develop the quantitative capabilities of audit colleagues, and support broader departmental initiatives. Why This Role Is Important to Us Corporate Audit provides independent assurance over the governance, risk management, and control processes supporting State Street’s global businesses. Models and quantitative analytics influence critical decisions across financial risk management, financial crime prevention, Treasury, Markets, and Investment Management. As the use of advanced analytics and artificial intelligence expands, independent assessment of model design, implementation, validation, monitoring, and governance becomes increasingly important. In this role, you will combine quantitative model expertise with audit and risk-management skills to evaluate whether significant models are appropriately designed, implemented, validated, monitored, and governed. What you will be responsible for As a Model Risk Audit Manager, Assistant Vice President you will: Lead or support model-risk audits covering two or more areas such as credit risk, market risk, capital, liquidity, counterparty risk, financial crime, pricing, Treasury, Investment Management, Markets. Plan and execute audits according to the Corporate Audit’s risk-based audit methodology and internal standards, industry practices and regulatory guidance (e.g. SR 11-7/26-2 on Model Risk Management, ECB guide to internal models, SR 15-18, etc.) Assess the design and effectiveness of controls across the model lifecycle, including development, validation, implementation, change management, monitoring, use, and governance. Review and assess model documentation and implementation details, model validation and ongoing monitoring results to assess the effectiveness of the model risk management control design and execution both on an individual model basis and in aggregate. Draft clear audit workpapers, findings, memoranda, and reports that explain the risks, root causes, business impact, and recommended actions. Communicate audit progress, conclusions, findings, and remediation expectations to audit management and key stakeholders. Identify recurring or emerging model-risk themes through audit results, management-identified issues, validation findings, and ongoing risk monitoring. Develop an understanding of State Street’s products, services, data, IT application / data infrastructure, risk-management processes, and regulatory obligations. Build effective relationships across Model Risk Management, Model Validation, business teams, technology teams, and the three lines of defense. Support knowledge sharing and cross-training to strengthen quantitative and model-risk capabilities across Corporate Audit. Coordinate and facilitate global operation of the model risk audit team to meet team and individual priorities What We Value Strong quantitative judgment and the ability to critically evaluate complex model methodologies, assumptions, data, code, and results. Intellectual curiosity and the ability to understand unfamiliar models, products, and risks. Leadership potential and the willingness to develop end-to-end audit-management capabilities. The ability to translate complex quantitative concepts into clear risk and business implications. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to discuss technical conclusions with quantitative and nontechnical stakeholders. Sound judgment and the confidence to provide constructive challenge. The ability to manage multiple priorities, work independently, and deliver high-quality work in a fast-paced global environment. A collaborative approach and the ability to build effective relationships across functions and lines of defense. Qualifications Master’s degree or higher in quantitative finance, statistics, mathematics, economics, financial engineering, computer science, or a related quantitative discipline. Five or more years of professional experience in model development, model validation, model risk management, quantitative risk, quantitative research, Internal Audit, or a related field within a financial institution or consulting organization. Hands-on professional experience developing or reviewing a model across its lifecycle. Technical depth in at least two model or risk areas, such as credit, market, capital, liquidity, counterparty, pricing, financial crime, Treasury, investment management. Working proficiency in Python, R, or a comparable statistical programming language, including the ability to review, run, modify, and analyze model code and outputs. Ability to assess model risks and evaluate the design and effectiveness of related controls. Strong analytical, problem-solving, organizational, project-management, presentation, and communication skills. Leadership potential and the ability to develop the skills needed to manage audit engagements through planning, testing, findings, stakeholder discussions, and reporting. Experience leading or supporting model validation, model-risk assessments, regulatory reviews, or audit engagements is a plus. Knowledge of model-risk regulations and guidance, including SR 11-7, SR 15-18, or comparable regulatory expectations, is a plus. Experience with State Street-relevant financial products, such as custody, credit facilities, securities finance, derivatives, foreign exchange, or investment management, is a plus. Experience with artificial intelligence, machine learning, or financial-crime models is a plus. CFA, FRM, or a comparable professional designation is a plus. If you have hands-on experience developing, validating, or reviewing models and are interested in applying that expertise within an independent assurance role, we encourage you to apply.

Position Summary HarbourVest is seeking a Senior Associate to join our Finance Transformation team as a Workday Financials technology specialist. Collaborating with the Vice President, Workday Financials Product Owner, this role will support and improve HarbourVest's Workday financial technology ecosystem through reporting, analytics, process improvement, and platform administration. The Senior Associate will support team members across Accounting, Finance, Tax, Treasury, Data Governance, and Technology to help users better understand, use, and optimize Workday Financials and related platforms. Success in this role requires a solid understanding of accounting and financial reporting concepts, curiosity about how systems operate, and a desire to improve business processes through technology. This is an excellent opportunity for an experienced Workday Financials user, analyst, administrator, accountant, auditor, or financial reporting professional looking to broaden their expertise across Accounting Center, Adaptive Planning, Prism Analytics, AI-enabled capabilities, and broader Finance Transformation initiatives. What This Role Is (and Isn't) This is a Workday Financials administration and reporting role that combines an understanding of how financial processes operate within Workday with building reports, tools, and solutions that improve finance and accounting operations. The Senior Associate will help users understand system behavior, investigate transaction activity, support reconciliations, and translate business requirements into effective Workday solutions. This is not an FP&A role, a Staff Accountant role, or a position responsible for preparing journal entries, performing reconciliations, or completing month-end close activities. Key Responsibilities Workday Financial Reporting Build, maintain, and improve custom reports in Workday Financials, including advanced, matrix, and composite reports, as well as financial statement reporting. Develop dashboards and worklets that give Accounting, Finance, and leadership timely visibility. Create and maintain management and close reporting packages in OfficeConnect. Reconcile report output to the general ledger and ensure accuracy, consistency, and auditability. Translate reporting requests from Accounting and Finance into clear specifications, then build and test the solution. Handle report sharing, user access, and documentation. Workday Financials Support Support Financial Data Model (FDM) maintenance, including worktags, hierarchies, and reference data. Maintain Account Posting Rules, Elimination Rules, and Accounting and Consolidation Details to align with company accounting policies and GAAP. Support Workday Accounting Center administration, including understanding how operational transactions are transformed into accounting and financial reporting outcomes. Support the evaluation, testing, and enhancement of Workday Financials business processes, including workflow steps, approvals, and notifications. Gather requirements, document enhancement requests, and support release testing and user acceptance testing for Workday updates and new functionality. Solve user issues and escalate to IT or Workday support as needed. Help evaluate, test, and implement emerging Workday AI capabilities and other productivity tools to improve efficiency, user experience, and business processes. Workday Adaptive Planning Support Support Adaptive Planning operations, including sheets, dimensions, versions, and data loads. Maintain Adaptive reports and OfficeConnect packages and keep Adaptive aligned with Workday Financials data. Partner with FP&A on model enhancements and requests. You will support the model build, while FP&A will continue to lead the planning content. Data Quality and Governance Maintain data standards across reports and dashboards so definitions stay consistent. Follow change management, testing, documentation, and control practices that support the control environment.

Company Details Berkley Specialty Property operates in New York, Georgia, and California and writes commercial property business throughout the United States. The company serves a broad range of occupancy classes and focuses on understanding the unique needs of its clients through empowered underwriting, efficient claims handling, strong policy wordings, and technical support services. Berkley Specialty Property is committed to delivering long-term, sustainable insurance solutions. Responsibilities The AVP, Underwriting is responsible for supporting the development and implementation of underwriting strategy within the Berkley Specialty Property underwriting team. This role executes and monitors underwriting activities across assigned commercial property business and collaborates with field and staff functions to support profitable growth, underwriting discipline, and effective portfolio management. Responsibilities Support the development and implementation of underwriting strategy. Execute underwriting activities across assigned commercial property accounts and portfolios. Monitor underwriting performance and adherence to underwriting guidelines. Partner with field and staff functional teams to achieve business objectives. Support profitable growth through disciplined underwriting practices. Contribute to effective portfolio management and risk selection. Build and maintain productive broker relationships. Represent Berkley Specialty Property professionally and effectively in the marketplace. Exercise sound underwriting judgment and business decision-making. Resolve complex and unique business challenges through creative problem-solving. Qualifications Four to ten years of commercial property underwriting experience, preferably within the U.S. Excess and Surplus (E&S) insurance market. Demonstrated success in commercial property underwriting with proven results. Strong knowledge of underwriting principles and insurance industry practices. Experience underwriting commercial property lines of business. Broad understanding of related insurance disciplines and functions. Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities within established deadlines. Ability to exercise independent judgment and make sound business decisions.

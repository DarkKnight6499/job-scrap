The Opportunity Join MassMutual’s LTC Inforce Management Team and help drive the success of our stand-alone and hybrid Long-Term Care products. As a Quantitative Consultant, you’ll work in a fast-paced, collaborative environment, partnering across the organization to support key initiatives including inforce repricing, annual rate certifications, illustration testing, dividend analysis, and wellness programs. This is an exciting opportunity to apply your analytical expertise, influence business decisions, and make a meaningful impact on a highly visible business. The Team The LTC Inforce Management Team is responsible for inforce management for our stand-alone LTC and hybrid products. This team consists of both actuaries and non-actuaries that work collaboratively with many areas throughout the organization. Team members are expected to work collaboratively with both internal and external partners, form an opinion, advocate for our policyowners and the company, and drive action.

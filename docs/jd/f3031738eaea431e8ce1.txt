Press space or enter keys to toggle section visibility As an Associate within Business Operational Risk, you will provide independent operational risk oversight and advisory support to the Specialized Asset Finance business across the Americas. You will partner with front-office teams and corporate support functions to identify emerging risks, assess the effectiveness of controls, and help drive proactive risk management practices. This role involves reviewing new business initiatives, transactions, and change activities to ensure operational risks are appropriately identified and managed before implementation. You will also lead risk assessments, incident reviews, control assurance activities, and governance reporting while supporting the ongoing execution of the firm's operational risk management framework.

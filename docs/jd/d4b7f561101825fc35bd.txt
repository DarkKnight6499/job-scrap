The Role Fidelity Capital Markets (FCM) is seeking a product associate to help support the Fixed Income Product Team’s digital fixed income trading experience! The role is foundational in a career defining the features, functions, and capabilities of our electronic offering. The purpose of the role will be to monitor trading systems, manage product and production incidents, perform remediation analysis, and represent the product team in resolving incidents. The group is looking for someone who has familiarity with fixed income trading, trade processing, and brokerage systems. Additionally, this person will work closely with internal business partners, product management, technology, and operations staff to decompose, analyze, and improve existing trading processes.

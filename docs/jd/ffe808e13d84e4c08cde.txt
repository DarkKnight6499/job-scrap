Skills and Competencies Strong analytical and modeling skills, including the ability to perform cash flow analysis and assess transaction structures and legal documentation. Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to clearly present and defend independent credit opinions. Sound judgment and the highest ethical standards, with a professional, proactive, and collaborative working style. Highly organized and efficient, with the ability to manage multiple priorities and meet tight deadlines. Strong teamwork skills and the ability to work effectively with colleagues holding diverse perspectives. Ability to work independently with minimal supervision while contributing to broader team objectives. Demonstrated proficiency in artificial intelligence concepts, with hands-on experience using AI tools to streamline workflows and enhance operational efficiency. Education Bachelor’s degree in economics, finance, statistics, applied mathematics, financial engineering, or a related field with three or more years of relevant work experience Responsibilities This role is responsible for leading the surveillance and credit analysis of U.S. RMBS transactions, producing high-quality research, and contributing to Moody’s thought leadership and analytical innovation. Act as lead analyst for surveillance of U.S. RMBS transactions, analyzing credit and legal risks and formulating rating recommendations. Perform cash flow modeling and detailed analysis of transaction structures, collateral performance, and legal documentation. Lead rating committees and present clear, well-supported views on transaction credit risk. Produce publishable research, including transaction reports, sector commentary, and in-depth analytical pieces. Communicate effectively with internal and external stakeholders, including issuers, arrangers, investors, and other market participants. Participate in issuer and investor meetings, operational reviews, and industry conferences, representing the RMBS surveillance team. Contribute to analytical, operational, and digital enablement initiatives, including projects involving GenAI and other emerging technologies. Support the RMBS new ratings team as needed. Mentor and support the development of less experienced analysts. About the Team The U.S. Consumer RMBS Surveillance Team within Moody’s Structured Finance group is responsible for monitoring all active U.S. RMBS ratings. The team delivers timely, high-quality research on market developments and emerging risks, contributes to industry dialogue through conferences and investor and issuer engagement, and plays a key role in advancing analytical excellence and thought leadership across the structured finance market.

Responsibilities Located in our Urbandale, Iowa office, the Commercial Underwriting Manager is responsible for managing an assigned commercial underwriting function and staff, driving profitable underwriting results and managed growth through effective leadership, development, and engagement. This role will: Assist in the development and implementation of macro (regional and enterprise) and micro (state, city, rural area, agent level) strategies for long-term success in construction, manufacturing, distribution across middle market, and/or small commercial property and casualty. • Manage an assigned commercial underwriting function and staff through delegation of underwriting authority, ensuring adherence to the Letter of Authority and other underwriting standards and guidelines.• Monitor risk acceptability, pricing adequacy, and classification accuracy of accounts to ensure profitability and managed growth of the business; consult with underwriters and Home Office Underwriting on risk acceptability, coverages, pricing, terms, and conditions.• Underwrite accounts exceeding the authority level of underwriting staff by analyzing applications, risk services reports, Motor Vehicle Reports (MVRs), financial reports, and other relevant information; determine accurate classification of business, policy contract forms, and pricing of risk; refer accounts to Home Office Underwriting as needed based on established guidelines.• Develop a high-performing team of construction, manufacturing and distribution, middle market and/or small commercial underwriting professionals through strong leadership and execution, including coaching, feedback, and performance management.• Demonstrate genuine care and provide regular, specific constructive feedback to staff and leaders to develop their skill sets and performance for current and future roles.• Contribute to the organization's success by developing business unit and individual performance goals, measuring results, and providing recognition and acknowledgment of key achievements.• Engage agency partners through selective travel, participation in distribution-related events, and correspondence on key accounts; provide leadership and oversight in the selection and management of the distribution network of independent agencies.• Conduct quarterly underwriting file quality reviews and share results with the Regional Manager; develop and implement action plans in response to file quality reviews.• Monitor and report on key performance indicators for the portfolio, including pipelines, quotes, new business, total book of business, mix of business, retention, loss performance, and rate attainment.• Partner with leaders in other business units (Home Office Underwriting, Claims, Risk Services, Customer Experience, IT, Analytics) to enhance the effectiveness of critical business processes and products.• Perform other related duties as assigned by management.• Directly supervise Commercial Underwriters and provide overall direction, coordination, and evaluation of the unit.• Carry out supervisory responsibilities in accordance with organizational policies and applicable laws, including interviewing, hiring, and training employees; planning, assigning, and directing work; appraising performance; rewarding and disciplining employees; and addressing complaints and resolving problems.

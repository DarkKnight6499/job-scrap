Investment Banking Associate - Industrials
Citi
Location: Chicago Illinois United States

Citi is looking for an Investment Banking Associate to join our Industrials coverage team, playing a key role in advising clients on mergers, acquisitions, and capital markets transactions. In this position, you will serve as a strategic advisor to corporate clients — helping them raise capital, evaluate complex transactions, and navigate significant business decisions. Sitting within Citi's Institutional Banking business, this role offers the opportunity to work on high-impact mandates while contributing directly to the growth of one of the world's most connected financial institutions. Responsibilities Advise corporate clients on mergers, acquisitions, and capital raising strategies, acting as a trusted partner across the full transaction lifecycle. Execute M&A and capital markets transactions, applying deep financial expertise to support deal structuring, analysis, and execution. Develop and review detailed financial analyses, including valuation models, transaction assessments, and corporate structure evaluations. Guide and mentor junior analysts by providing structured feedback, managing information flow, and sharing relevant credit and exposure context to support their development. Build and develop client relationships by tracking business development opportunities and contributing to the growth of Citi's Industrials franchise. Monitor industry trends and gather market intelligence to support client advisory work and identify new business opportunities. Evaluate business decisions with a clear focus on risk, ensuring all activities align with regulatory requirements and uphold the highest standards of ethical conduct. Required Qualifications & Skills 5 to 8 years of experience in investment banking or a closely related financial advisory role. Demonstrated ability to evaluate corporate transactions and structures, with hands-on experience supporting M&A execution and capital raising processes. Skilled in building and interpreting financial analyses, including valuation models and transaction-related financial assessments. Clear and confident communicator, with the ability to present complex financial concepts concisely in both written and verbal formats. Series 79 and Series 63 licenses, current or ability to obtain upon hire. Bachelor's degree in Finance, Business Administration, or a closely related field. Beneficial Skills & Qualifications Master's degree in Business Administration (MBA) from an accredited institution. Prior experience in client development or business development activities within a banking or financial services environment. Familiarity with Industrials sector dynamics, including the ability to track and analyze industry trends to inform client advisory work. What We Offer At Citi, you will have the opportunity to work on complex, high-profile transactions alongside experienced professionals in a collaborative and performance-driven environment. We offer a strong platform for career progression, global reach, and the chance to make a genuine impact for clients across the Industrials sector. A hybrid working model offering 3 days in the office and 2 days working remotely, supporting a healthy balance between collaboration and flexibility. Access to structured learning and professional development programs to help you continue growing as an advisor and leader. Exposure to a global network of clients, colleagues, and deals, providing broad experience and long-term career optionality. Mentoring opportunities and a team environment that values the development of individual talent alongside collective performance. Competitive compensation and financial wellbeing benefits reflective of your contribution and seniority. Wellbeing support and programs designed to help you sustain high performance across all areas of your professional and personal life.

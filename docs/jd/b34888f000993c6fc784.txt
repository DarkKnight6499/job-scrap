Senior Associate, Business Strategy - Listings, Capital Markets & ETFs
Intercontinental Exchange
Location: New York, New York
Link: https://careers.ice.com/jobs/13992

The Senior Associate is a key analytical partner to NYSE senior leadership across Listings, Capital Markets, and ETFs. The role leads business performance reporting, supports forecasting and budgeting, and drives strategic projects and analysis to support business growth. The ideal candidate is analytically strong, detail-oriented, and knowledgeable about capital markets, with the ability to independently own workstreams from analysis through recommendation. Responsibilities Business Reporting Lead recurring performance reporting for the Global Listings business, partnering with cross-functional teams to translate KPIs into clear insights for NYSE leadership. Partner with Finance on forecasting and the annual budget process, providing business inputs on listings activity and the IPO and ETF pipelines. Support the annual listing fee billing cycle for corporate and ETF issuers, partnering with Finance to ensure accuracy and serving as a resource on fee schedules. Market Research & Analysis Monitor capital markets activity, industry trends, and macroeconomic indicators, providing market insights and commentary for internal and leadership updates. Develop industry and peer trading analyses that reinforce the NYSE value proposition and support issuer pitches and leadership discussions. Build and maintain datasets and management dashboards, automating reporting workflows to improve efficiency and reduce manual effort. Strategic Projects Drive strategic projects from initial brainstorms through project management, presentation of concepts, and execution. Support team initiatives and internal operations, including Salesforce and workflow improvements. Knowledge and Experience Bachelor's degree in finance, accounting, economics, or a related quantitative field. 2+ years of experience in strategy, financial analysis, consulting, capital markets, or a related analytical role. Understanding of capital markets, including IPOs, ETFs, and market structure. Advanced Excel and PowerPoint skills. Experience with market data platforms such as FactSet, Dealogic, or PitchBook is a plus. Strong analytical skills and attention to detail, with a high degree of accuracy when working with data. Excellent written and verbal communication skills; able to present findings clearly to senior stakeholders. Intellectually curious and self-motivated, with the ability to manage multiple priorities New York Base Salary Range The expected base salary for this role, if located in New York, is between $100,000 - 120,000 USD.

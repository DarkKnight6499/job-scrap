The Global Card and Intelligent Payables product team is committed to driving digital payment adoption and creating innovative solutions that help our clients capture and manage spend. This role will sit within the Commercial Card Product Development team, which plays a pivotal role in managing and shaping the commercial card platforms that define how our corporate clients engage with our products. It will be aligned to mobile wallet and global travel products and be responsible for creating and managing digital product strategies, managing relationships with partners who provide our digital card solutions, being the subject matter expert for virtual cards, working with sales to identify opportunities for solution expansion that include virtual cards, and working with B2B product teams to create cross functional solutions. You will reimagine the future of our platforms, ensuring they meet both business goals and compliance standards, while driving revenue and enhancing client experience. The individual must be comfortable working on technical projects, leading multi-discipline project teams, teaching non-technical partners, communicating value internally and externally, conducting stakeholder impact assessments creating business cases and documenting business requirements, and working with partners to create commercialization materials. Key Responsibilities: - Define and execute the platform strategy, roadmap, and commercialization plans for mobile wallet, virtual card, and travel payment solutions, aligning investments to client needs, market opportunities, and business objectives • Lead the development and enhancement of digital payment capabilities, including mobile wallet provisioning, tokenized payments, virtual card issuance, and travel-related payment experiences • Serve as a subject matter expert for virtual card and travel payment solutions, partnering with Global Product, Sales, and Operations to identify opportunities to expand client adoption and wallet share • Manage strategic relationships with payment networks, wallet providers, travel technology partners, and third-party vendors to deliver new capabilities and optimize product performance • Partner across card, payables, fraud, risk, operations, and technology teams to design digital payment solutions that improve client experience and drive business growth • Develop business cases, product requirements, and stakeholder impact assessments to support new products, enhancements, regulatory changes, and platform modernization initiatives • Monitor product performance, client feedback, market trends, and competitive offerings to prioritize investments, enhance commercialization strategies, and identify opportunities for innovation.

Job Description Summary As a key investment professional within the Investment Strategy team of the Chief Investment Office (CIO), support the design, management, and oversight of customized insurance investment portfolios. Partner across disciplines to translate client objectives, balance sheet constraints, liability considerations, and regulatory requirements into actionable portfolio strategies and investment decisions across the Transamerica general account. Job Description Responsibilities: Assist in managing the General Account liquidity position, including cash forecasting and planning to support ongoing funding and liquidity needs. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to evaluate, structure, and implement portfolio optimization initiatives, including portfolio transfers, derivative unwind transactions, and other strategic actions. Lead the review and approval process for portfolio transfer proposals, ensuring transactions are optimized and aligned with established investment and risk management guidelines. Support the benchmark-setting process by contributing to the development and maintenance of tactical asset allocation targets for investment portfolios. Partner with investment strategy and portfolio management teams to lead and execute large-scale strategic initiatives across asset classes and product portfolios. Contribute as both a collaborative team member and independent lead on strategic investment initiatives, business process improvements, and other ad hoc assignments. Prepare investment-related presentations, analyses, and communications in support of business development efforts and client Request for Proposal (RFP) responses. Manage and collaborate on quarterly investment reporting processes, ensuring accurate and timely analysis, documentation, and communication of portfolio activity. Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in business, finance or relevant field Six years of fixed income trading or portfolio management experience, preferably within the insurance industry Broad knowledge of most asset classes/sectors, such as corporates, public and private mortgage-backed securities, commercial and residential mortgage loans, and public and private high yield Broad knowledge of fixed income portfolio management techniques Familiarity with asset-liability management for life insurance companies and life insurance products Strong analytical and quantitative skills, including experience evaluating portfolio risk, duration, liquidity, and asset allocation tradeoffs Advanced proficiency in Excel and experience developing financial models and portfolio analytics Experience working collaboratively with portfolio managers, actuaries, risk professionals, and senior investment leadership to support strategic decision-making Ability to communicate complex investment concepts and portfolio recommendations to both technical and non-technical stakeholders Preferred Qualifications: Actuarial experience MBA, CFA, and/or FSA Compensation: The Salary for this position generally ranges between $135,000 - $175,000 annually.

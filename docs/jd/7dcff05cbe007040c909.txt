Position Summary Responsible for the development and execution of the Enterprise Risk Management Framework to enable the Bank to identify, assess, monitor, and report enterprise-wide risks within the approved risk appetite. Leads and mentors risk management team members in the execution of enterprise risk programs, partners with business and risk leaders to support risk-informed decision-making, and supports alignment with enterprise strategy. Responsible for supporting the ongoing maintenance of the Risk Appetite Statement, aggregating and presenting comprehensive enterprise risk insights to executive and board-level stakeholders, and driving continuous improvement of enterprise risk management practices across the organization. Essential Duties and Responsibilities Directs the creation, review, and continuous improvement of Enterprise Risk Management policies, standards, frameworks, risk taxonomy, risk appetite, and related governance documents across the organization. Establishes and oversees enterprise-wide processes for identifying, assessing, prioritizing, and aggregating material risks, including strategic and emerging risks, risk assessments, and risk appetite monitoring. Oversees enterprise risk response and mitigation monitoring processes. Partners with risk owners and subject matter experts to evaluate risk response strategies, monitor action plans, and escalate material exposures or risk acceptance decisions through established governance channels. Monitors, analyzes, aggregates, and reports the Bank's enterprise risk profile, risk appetite performance, key risk indicators, strategic and emerging risks, and significant changes in exposure to executive management and the Board, providing actionable insights and recommendations. Designs and executes enterprise risk management communication and training strategies. Promotes consistent understanding of risk appetite, risk ownership, escalation expectations, and enterprise-wide risk management practices. Builds consensus among senior leadership and cross-functional partners; drives enterprise-wide risk initiatives through influence and strategic partnerships and serves as a trusted advisor on enterprise risk matters. Selects, implements, and optimizes GRC systems to support enterprise risk taxonomy, risk assessments, risk appetite, KRIs, issues, reporting, and governance; leads tool adoption, integration, and data quality standards. Designs and delivers enterprise risk presentations to executive leadership, board committees, regulators, and other key stakeholders; communicates complex risk themes clearly and credibly. Leads advanced enterprise risk analysis, develops aggregation and reporting methodologies, identifies interdependencies and emerging trends, and translates risk information into strategic insights for leadership. Develops departmental documents and activities in compliance with applicable laws, regulations, policies, and procedures as applicable to this position, including completion of required compliance training. Performs other duties and responsibilities as assigned. Qualifications To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily.

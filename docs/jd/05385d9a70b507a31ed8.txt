Here are just a few of the many awards we’ve recently received: Best Places to Work 2026, 2025, 2024, 2023 - Presented by BuiltIn WealthTech of the Year 2025 - Presented by US FinTech Awards The World’s Top 250 Fintech Companies 2024 - Presented by CNBC ABOUT THIS ROLE As an Associate Solutions Architect at Apex, you will drive cross functional technology solutions on our strategic Ascend platform, working with clients, sales, business operations partners and other product managers to envision and design compelling and operationally efficient outcomes. This role offers exceptional learning opportunities in enterprise-scale financial services architecture, allowing you to master complex trade execution, custody, and clearance systems while contributing to high-impact regulatory compliance and client experience initiatives. You will progress from coached execution to leading increasingly visible and complex enterprise solutions as you develop deep expertise in financial services technology that will disrupt our industry. Duties/Responsibilities Solution Design & Delivery: Gather and interpret business requirements from clients and stakeholders to drive architectural decisions for assigned platform components. Own the end-to-end development, documentation, and implementation of solutions that enhance trade execution, custody, and clearance systems. Project & Feature Execution: Independently develop execution plans, comprehensive documentation, and test scenarios for assigned features and initiatives. Analyze current processes to identify optimization opportunities and lead collaboration with product managers and subject matter experts. Team Coordination & Leadership: Coordinate cross-functional teams and mentor new members within assigned strategic domains. Champion quality, innovation, and accountability initiatives, establishing agile feedback mechanisms and tracking goals and performance metrics for delivered solutions. Stakeholder Engagement: Build and manage relationships with product management, development, and business operations teams. Present project statuses, risks, and recommendations to senior management, and proactively lead client interactions and technical discussions regarding platform capabilities. Continuous Improvement & Issue Resolution: Independently identify process and platform issues, taking ownership of timely escalation, resolution tracking, and communication. Facilitate knowledge sharing and collaboration, establishing subject matter expertise and visibility across the organization. Education and/or Experience Bachelor's degree in Finance, Computer Science, Engineering, or related field (or equivalent work experience) required 3+ years of experience in Financial Services technology or related field, with demonstrated interest in solution architecture and agile development environments. Required Skills/Abilities Basic to intermediate knowledge of Financial Services, with eagerness to learn Clearance, Custody and Trade Execution processing requirements. Willingness to understand processing differences versus traditional investment products. Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with demonstrated ability to work effectively in team environments and learn from senior colleagues. Good listening skills and developing ability to ask clarifying questions and challenge assumptions appropriately. Solid written and oral communication skills and ability to adapt messaging for different audiences. Strong problem-solving abilities and attention to detail, with eagerness to learn proactive risk identification and mitigation within agile environments. Basic understanding of user experience, web development, or API standards, with strong interest in learning best practices and incremental delivery. Ability to make decisions with imperfect information and lead without authority while being comfortable with ambiguity and complexity.

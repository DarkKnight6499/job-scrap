Financial Analyst (Chicago)
Fitch Ratings
Location: Chicago, IL, US

Bixby Research & Analytics is currently seeking a Financial Analyst based out of our Chicago, office. Bixby Research and Analytics (“Bixby”) is the leading private information provider in the $1.5+ trillion leveraged loan market. Since 2018, Bixby's SaaS platform has been revolutionizing the way institutional investors consume information for their private investments. Bixby's market moving news and expansive financial data keeps hedge funds and asset managers ahead of their competition. Bixby’s platform is relied upon each day as a trusted source of information for some of the largest, most-awarded institutional investors in the credit market. What We Offer: This is a unique opportunity to join a highly experienced research team within a rapidly growing, fast paced company. Compensation is competitive. Develop deep knowledge of the leveraged loan market, which continues to grow in size and weight Access to the resources of Fitch, including career advancement opportunities (dependent on exceptional performance) We’ll Count on You To: Bixby is looking for an energetic, entrepreneurial self-starter that will work alongside senior research staff. An ideal candidate will have excellent attention to detail, prior experience in finance and / or accounting, strong interpersonal skills, and an ability to adapt to the dynamic needs of a growing business. Support senior research staff to maintain real-time coverage across broad universe of companies. Compile detailed financial models with ability to process detailed reconciliations, restatements, and acquisitions. Review company documents to identify news, financial and non-financial data in real time. Ability to prepare and review own work product to ensure accuracy and completeness in a time-sensitive environment. What You Need to Have: Bachelor’s degree required 2-5 years of accounting and / or finance experience required. Comprehensive understanding of cash flows and the linkage between financial statements (BS, CF, IS) with ability to build and maintain financial models and interpret financial ratios and metrics is required. Detail oriented with established skills in accuracy, organization, and experience working in a high-volume, time-sensitive environment. Highest level of integrity to keep sensitive business information confidential. What Would Make You Stand Out: Accounting, finance or economics degree strongly preferred. Certified Public Accountant (CPA) and / or pursuit of Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) certification are strongly preferred. Prior experience at Big Four accounting firm, investment bank, investment management or leveraged lending strongly preferred. Strong understanding of credit with experience in leveraged loans and / or high yield bonds is preferred.

About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking an Associate to support CIB Banking’s Energy & Power Portfolio Management team with a focus on Oil and Gas. The role will be responsible for supporting credit underwriting, portfolio monitoring, financial analysis, risk rating assessment, and transaction execution for a portfolio of loans to upstream companies. Wells Fargo’s Oil and Gas business is an integral part of the Energy and Power group providing financing, capital markets and advisory services. The group is a market-leading franchise with top-ranked positions in debt and equity capital markets and a longstanding commitment to serving clients across North America. In this role, you will: Own the day-to-day management of an assigned portfolio of loans with complex capital structures and significant financing requirements. Support credit underwriting, including building and reviewing complex projection models and performing detailed financial & credit analysis. Monitor borrower performance and provide ongoing market intelligence on upstream credit trends, regulatory developments, financing markets, and emerging risks affecting the Oil and Gas portfolio. Support all credit monitoring work including borrowing base redeterminations, annual reviews, status updates and focus reviews for the assigned portfolio, accounting for all risk factors and forming and defending risk rating recommendations with oversight from the Portfolio Manager. Lead coordination and execution of loan closings ensuring satisfaction of all booking, legal documentation and compliance requirements. Oversee coordination with operational teams for all day-to-day activity for the loans, including draws and issuances of Letters of Credit, and support resolution of items escalated. Review and interpret credit policies and procedures and incorporate requirements into credit analysis, observations and monitoring. Manage communication between Borrowers and the syndicate of lenders for transactions where we act as Administrative Agent. Review the work of Analysts — including models, credit write-ups and monitoring deliverables — before submission to the Portfolio Manager. Direct, train and develop Analysts on the team, including allocating workstreams and providing performance feedback. Represent the bank in maintaining relationships with clients. Collaborate and consult with peers, internal partners and stakeholders, including clients. Support regular reporting to credit and senior management on the portfolio, in collaboration with more senior staff.

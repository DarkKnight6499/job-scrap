The AES CE Operations team is hiring a Manager with extensive renewable asset management experience to lead teams of 2-6 Sr. Portfolio Asset Managers, Portfolio Asset Managers, and Analysts. The Manager will provide leadership, coaching and development to their team responsible reporting, oversight, and coordination for a region greater than 2GW of financed renewable energy assets. This individual will be responsible for fleet decision making, Senior Leadership presentation, and other experienced leadership deliverables. The Portfolio Asset Management team is critical in the operation of a large fleet of renewable and energy storage projects across the United States to ensure optimal financial performance of the company’s rapidly growing fleet. Team responsibilities will include project reporting and coordination of financial, commercial, compliance management, risk management and mitigation, and administrative tasks as well as coordination and oversight of the operations and maintenance of the assets.

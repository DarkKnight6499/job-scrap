Join us and transform the lives of patients while transforming your career. Sr Associate Global Distribution HUB Americas (GDHA What you will do Let’s do this. Let’s change the world. In this vital role you will be The Senior Associate, Global Distribution HUB Americas (GDHA), reports to the GDHA Manager. Under general supervision, this role supports GDHA HUB business processes and continuous improvement. The position contributes to cross-functional projects, provides accurate information and analysis to support decision-making, oversees compliance activities, and develops strategic and tactical processes that strengthen the Corporate Transportation value proposition. Execute GDP/GMP and other applicable business processes within GDHA HUB with minimal supervision, including processing Stock Transport Orders (STOs). Manage and prioritize outbound distribution schedules. Coordinate outbound distribution activities, including preparing shipping documentation, booking couriers, and creating documentation for reusable shipping solutions. Track the distribution budget. Develop and maintain key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics for area management. Develop and maintain Amgen training materials related to GDHA HUB. Develop and maintain applicable Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), forms, and other business documents. Provide training to new staff. Represent GDHA HUB by leading or facilitating cross-functional meetings that develop and implement process improvements and business solutions. Provide support for Minor and Major deviations/CAPAs, root-cause assessments, and Atomic records.

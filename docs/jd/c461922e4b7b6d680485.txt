What you will be doing: Record-to-Report (RTR) is a key function within the Chief Accounting Organization under the Chief Financial Officer (CFO), supporting the accuracy, integrity, and timely reporting of the company’s financial results. RTR oversees critical accounting and close processes while driving greater standardization, simplification, automation, and control across the end-to-end financial reporting lifecycle. Our focus is to transform RTR into a globally consistent, efficient, and scalable organization, leveraging technology and process optimization to reduce manual effort and improve the experience for our teams and stakeholders. Ultimately, our goal is to maintain accounting excellence while creating capacity for greater insight, agility, and strategic value. The RTR Strategy & Optimization Co-op will support strategic transformation initiatives focused on simplifying, standardizing, and automating RTR processes while gaining hands-on experience in finance transformation, project execution, and continuous improvement. Key Responsibilities: Support the R2R Strategy & Optimization team with day-to-day activities related to process improvement initiatives. • Assist in documenting current accounting and reporting processes, including process flows, procedures, and supporting materials. • Help gather, organize, and analyze information to identify process improvement opportunities. Contribute to defining the AI strategy for accounting, including the prioritization of key initiatives through year end. • Support project tracking by helping maintain timelines, action items, meeting notes, and status updates. • Assist with preparation of presentation materials, summaries, and reports for team and stakeholder meetings. • Help develop and update process documentation, playbooks, toolkits, and training materials. • Participate in meetings with cross-functional teams such as Accounting, Finance, IT, Tax, Treasury, and Legal to capture notes and follow-up actions. • Support data collection and basic analysis for metrics related to close and reporting processes. • Assist the team with testing, implementation, and rollout activities for process or system improvement initiatives. • Conduct research on leading practices, process improvement ideas, and relevant technologies to support team priorities. • Provide general administrative and project support to the R2R Strategy & Optimization team as needed. Qualifications: Currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree in: Finance, Business, Accounting, Or a related discipline At least one prior co-op experience, preferred not required.

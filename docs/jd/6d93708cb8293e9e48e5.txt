This leader will help clients address one of the fastest-growing challenges in cybersecurity: securing and governing identities used by applications, services, cloud workloads, APIs, automation platforms, DevOps pipelines, machines, bots, and AI agents. The ideal candidate combines executive-level client advisory skills, strong business development and market-facing capabilities, architect-level technical expertise , experience leading complex cybersecurity and identity programs, and passion for shaping emerging identity capabilities within the AI era.

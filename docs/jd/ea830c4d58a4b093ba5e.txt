A Typical Day The Property Preservation Associate III is responsible for supporting in-depth loss analyses, vendor management, and property timeline optimization for escalated and high-risk loans within Property Preservation. The Property Preservation Associate III will:: Manage high-risk and complex loan portfolios, including post-sale FHA properties, 203K loans, and government-backed/conventional loans, to ensure preservation milestones and conveyance timeframes are met Lead vendor management efforts by evaluating, negotiating, and approving repair bids against investor/insurer requirements to minimize servicing advances and company loss exposure Conduct quality reviews of completed vendor repair work and photos to confirm compliance with budget approvals and workmanlike standards Evaluate daily reports, line-item insurance claims, and property inspections to identify damage root causes and establish corrective repair pathways Coordinate response workflows for consumer complaints, city code violations, receiverships, and demolition orders by performing research and escalating risk indicators to leadership Perform other related duties as required and assigned Demonstrate behaviors which are aligned with the organization’s desired culture and values What You’ll Bring This is a work from office position Ability to review photos of homes and property to determine condition and type of repairs required to place the home in conveyance condition by FHA, VA, USDA, FNMA and FHLMC guidelines Ability to meet and exceed goals in a fast-paced environment by utilizing daily reports to ensure compliance with established guidelines in Property Preservation Ability to review bids, photos, inspections on a daily basis to track status of ongoing vendor work on 200 plus loans in your work queue Candidate must be highly organized, highly ethical with a high attention to detail Proficient in Microsoft Office and G-Suite Workplace Strong attention to detail with the capacity to independently problem solve, when necessary Delivering timely and accurate information in a fast-paced environment Excellent critical thinking, problem solving, mathematical skills and sound judgment Mortgage

Job Summary: The Director of Finance is responsible for directing all financial functions of the organization, ensuring financial integrity, strategic alignment, and operational efficiency across all departments. Let’s talk job responsibilities: Establish and maintain internal controls to monitor assets, capital investments, cost management, and liquidity. Partner with senior leadership on accounting and financial reporting, including monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements. Analyze and interpret financial metrics such as asset ratios, break-even analysis, profit margins, operating costs, and overall financial performance. Provide financial insights and communicate with cross-functional teams including Operations, Marketing, HR, and IT regarding budgets, ROI, and project investments. Ensure accurate and timely financial reporting in compliance with applicable regulations (e.g., GAAP, FASB, SEC). Prepare for audits and ensure documentation meets all regulatory and compliance standards. Lead and direct finance teams, including oversight of subordinate managers and staff. Oversee personnel management activities such as hiring, performance management, and employee development. Contribute to organizational strategy through financial planning, goal setting, and decision support in partnership with executive leadership. Develop and implement departmental policies, procedures, and practices that significantly impact the organization. Your experience and skills: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, or a related field. 10+ years of progressive experience in finance, accounting, or a related discipline. Prior leadership or management experience required. Extensive knowledge of financial principles, reporting standards, and regulatory requirements.

We’re actively seeking a talented Senior Financial Analyst II to join our Financial Planning and Analysis team in Newport Beach, CA. As a Senior Financial Analyst II you’ll move Pacific Life, and your career, forward by analyzing Life Insurance and Annuity earnings results compared to Plan. This role requires innate curiosity to understand the underlying stories and business drivers of our results, combined with technical expertise to leverage Pacific Life's financial systems for maximum impact and efficiency. You will fill an existing role that sits on a team of 5 people in the Consumer Markets Division (Annuities and Life Insurance). Your colleagues will include Financial Analysts, Actuaries, and other Finance professionals. This team collaborates extensively with Valuation, Sales, Expense, and Investment areas to craft and communicate a comprehensive business driver story of results for internal management. How you’ll help move us forward: Facilitate the quarterly close process for GAAP and STAT Internal Management reporting requirements for Life, including: Provide timely, relevant, and accurate findings/research to Consumer Markets Division (“CMD”) Finance, mainly around variances to Plan by business driver Collaborate with Actuarial, Sales, Experience Studies, and Expense Management teams to understand business drivers for robust forecasting Analyze CMD historical trends by product line to validate Plan assumptions and identify anomalies in current period results Participate in the annual financial Plan cycle and develop an understanding of how our Plan results are produced by Actuarial models compared to historical results Facilitate continuous process improvement for CMD Finance by evaluating current procedures and recommending value-added improvements Use best practices and knowledge of internal or external business issues to improve services and solve long-term problems. Work with experts (both within Pacific Life and externally) to consider options and identify solutions that best align with business strategies. Develop solutions to complex problems with minimal supervision, requiring the regular use of ingenuity and creativity Provide guidance to staff regarding procedures, systems, and analyses in order to develop talent around CMD Finance. The experience you bring: 8+ years financial reporting and analysis experience, preferably in the Life Insurance and Annuity industry Proven leadership, organizational, interpersonal, analytical, and problem-solving skills. Demonstrates organizational, time management, project management, oral and written communications, and multi-tasking skills Adaptable to using/understanding various system functionalities including Microsoft Office products. Excel proficiency is a non-negotiable requirement, though VBA programming is not required. Strong team building, partnership and collaboration skills. Demonstrated ability to prioritize/communicate conflicting demands in a fast-paced environment.

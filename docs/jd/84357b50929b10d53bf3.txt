The Employee Health & Benefits (EH&B) Underwriter supports the EH&B sales and service departments in placement of new and renewal business, including but not limited to preparing all aspects of Medical & Dental underwriting projections, bid specifications, and quote analysis; assists in negotiation of final rates, and prepares recommendations and analyses for client presentations. This position will develop and maintain relationships with local insurance carrier contacts and will be an internal resource for technical assistance for all levels of Sales and Service. Some key day to day responsibilities include: Preparing and evaluating claim experience and utilization reports. Assisting in the review of contracts as well as preparing RFP specifications and identifying markets for proposals. Following industry development, changes, and legislative updates within all types of underwriting methodology, including but not limited to Medical, Dental, Life, Short- and Long-Term Disability.

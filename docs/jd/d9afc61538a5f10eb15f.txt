Key responsibilities UBS Business Solutions US LLC is seeking an Associate Director, Financial and Regulatory Reporting Specialist in Morrisville, NC. Are you an innovative thinker? Are you focused on the details, even when under pressure? Do you enjoy delivering enhanced change capabilities across a range of business functions? We’re looking for an Associate Director, Financial and Regulatory Reporting Specialist to: • Prepare and submit monthly, quarterly, annual, and ad-hoc regulatory and financial reports in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements such as US GAAP and IFRS. Ensure compliance with all reporting standards, policies, and deadlines set by regulatory bodies such as the Federal Reserve and other relevant authorities. • Act as a liaison with regulators, internal and external auditors, and senior leadership to provide insights, address queries, and support audits and reviews. Analyze financial data and trends to provide meaningful insights and variance analysis. • Provide strategic financial analysis and business insights to support forecasting, budgeting, and long-term financial planning activities. • Partner with Senior leadership and business units to identify risks and opportunities, and drive data-informed decision-making. Develop and deliver executive-level presentations for management and regulatory review meetings. Collaborate with cross-functional teams including Finance, IT, and the Front Office to enhance reporting quality, data integrity, and process efficiency. • Identify opportunities to automate and streamline reporting processes.

Job Description: The Role In the role of an Investment Risk Management Analyst , you will work closely with other members of a small team. You will define relevant risk metrics , produce reporting and participate in discussions that would provide assurance that investment managers are taking appropriate types and levels of risk for each client mandate. It is crucial that these risks are measured accurately, monitored continuously, and assumed deliberately in accordance with clients’ mandates. The Expertise You Have Degree in an analytical / quantitative field such as economics, finance, mathematics, statistics, computer science, or equivalent is required. A related master’s degree would be a plus. Progressive years of analytical experience required. Interest in pursuing the CFA or FRM charter would is a plus . The Skills You Bring Strong statistical analysis, Programming/coding experience in Python. Required proficiency in the use of Snowflake, SQL Developer and Power BI, (Tableau is a plus not required). Excellent communication, presentation, independent thinking, innovation, and problem-solving mindset. Ability to multi-task, prioritize effectively and meet deadlines with high quality deliverables. Understanding the basics of risk management (risk types, measurement, analytics). Knowledge of major financial market instruments including derivatives. A team player with the ability to thrive in a small-team environment. The Value You Deliver Contributing to reporting on the utilization of financial derivatives across the organization. Assistance with risk measurement for liquid alternative and private/illiquid alternative products. Assuring detailed risk reports are accurately produced , and reviewed by relevant individuals on appropriate cycles (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, etc.) . Participating in regular independent risk reviews of investment products . Engaging with key internal business partners and third-party vendors to ensure that data elements and processes critical for the efficient functioning of the Investment Risk team are maintained . The Team The Asset Management Independent Investment Risk Management team (IIRM) conducts objective investment risk assessment, measurement, and management of funds. The team issues risk reports, conducts risk research, actively participates in risk discussions and educates the investment platform on risk matters. The team engages internally with Chief Investment Officers, Portfolio Managers, Quantitative Analysts, Heads of Businesses, Portfolio Managers, Boards of Trustees, and other control functions. External interactions are with third party risk service providers, clients, and consultants.

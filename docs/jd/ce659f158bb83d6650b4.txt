Position Summary The Carlyle Group seeks to hire an Associate as a Product Specialist for Global Private Equity within the Client Solutions Group. The position will support Senior PE Product Specialists in strategically managing fundraise processes and handling investor requests across Carlyle’s growing US Buyout business. Periodic interaction with existing limited partners and potential investors is expected. This successful candidate must be able to prioritize multiple tasks, demonstrate attention to detail, and work under pressure in a deadline-oriented environment.

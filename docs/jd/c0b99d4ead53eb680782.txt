Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all . Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever. As an FP&A Finance Manager at Micron, you will play a pivotal role in crafting our financial success. This outstanding opportunity allows you to work alongside our world-class teams, including Finance, Corporate Strategy, Business Units, Engineering, and Operations leadership. You will lead the efforts in consolidating, analyzing, and delivering quarterly Board of Director materials for Finance, ensuring our strategies are flawlessly accomplished. Responsibilities Consolidate corporate business plans: Collaborate with business and finance leaders to review and refine plan assumptions, ensuring alignment and accountability. Prepare executive and board reporting: Provide timely financial reports and analyses for executive and board review, including investor communications. Monitor financial performance and enable decisions: Track key financial metrics and provide relevant financial analysis to support strategic, value-creating decisions. Drive cross-functional process improvement: Partner across functions to improve FP&A processes, efficiency, and business performance. Systems Transformation & Process Excellence Partner with Finance Systems, IT, and business collaborators to develop balanced solutions that improve corporate planning systems. Champion automation, AI adoption, and advanced analytics to improve reporting quality and efficiency. Operational Efficiency Drive process improvement, simplification, and reporting optimization while maintaining high accuracy and Finance reporting standards. Follow disciplined planning calendars, meet deadlines, and ensure consistent benchmark definitions. Minimum Qualifications 8+ years of Finance experience and a bachelor’s degree in finance, economics, accounting, or a related field. 5+ years of experience in corporate FP&A, financial statement analysis, and forecasting and planning. Expertise in advanced financial modeling, financial systems, and modeling tools. Strong communication, interpersonal, and negotiation talents, with the skill to articulate complex financial information clearly to executives and the board. Strong analytical and process orientation, with the ability to prioritize various tasks and meet tight deadlines in a fast-paced environment. Knowledge of AI-enabled efficiency, automation, and analytics tools. Preferred Qualifications Graduate-level qualification in Finance or Business. Outstanding business competence and financial skill with a deep understanding of P&L, cash flow, and balance sheet dynamics.

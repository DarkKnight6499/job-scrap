Job Description: Job Title Associate Trader – CMO Derivatives Desk Corporate Title – Associate Location New York, NY Position Type Full-Time Position Overview We are seeking a motivated and analytical Associate Trader to join our CMO Derivatives trading desk. The role provides hands-on exposure to mortgage derivatives markets, trade execution, relative value analysis, risk management, and desk strategy development. The candidate will work closely with senior traders to support daily trading activities and contribute to idea generation and customer engagement. What We Offer You A diverse and inclusive environment that embraces change, innovation, and collaboration A hybrid working model, allowing for in-office / work from home flexibility, generous vacation, personal and volunteer days Employee Resource Groups support an inclusive workplace for everyone and promote community engagement Competitive compensation packages including health and wellbeing benefits, retirement savings plans, parental leave, and family building benefits Educational resources, matching gift, and volunteer programs What You’ll Do Support trade execution, risk management, P&L reporting for the CMO Derivatives desk. Analyze market trends, prepayment dynamics, customers flows and relative value opportunities. Develop and maintain trading tools, analytics, and process automation using Python and Excel/VBA. Partner with risk, operations, technology, and compliance teams to support efficient desk operations. Skills You’ll Need Bachelor's degree in Mathematics, Finance, Economics, Engineering, Physics, Computer Science, or a related quantitative field with strong academic performance Proficient experience in securitized products, fixed income, or a related analytical role Strong quantitative and analytical skills with an interest in mortgage markets Familiarity with Python and Excel/VBA Excellent communication and interpersonal skills and ability to work in a fast-paced trading environment Proven ability to leverage AI tools to enhance productivity, optimize workflows to solve business problems, while applying critical judgment to ensure responsible and ethical use of data and AI outputs Skills That Will Help You Excel Exposure to Agency MBS, CMOs, CMO Derivatives, CMBS, RMBS, interest rate derivatives, or other securitized products Familiarity with Bloomberg, Yield Book, Intex or similar fixed-income analytics platform Expectations It is the Bank’s expectation that employees hired into this role will work in the New York, NY office in accordance with the Bank’s hybrid working model.

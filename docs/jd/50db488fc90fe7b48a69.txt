About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Senior Commercial Lending Servicing Specialist to join the Loan Trade Support team within the Corporate Investment Banking Lending Operations (CIBLO) division. This role will support syndicated loan trading and servicing activities by facilitating trade settlements, reviewing and validating loan trade assignments, researching and reconciling transaction activity, and ensuring accurate processing across applicable lending systems. The position will partner closely with Loan Trading, Agency Servicing, Member Servicing, and other internal stakeholders to support timely trade execution, accurate money movement, and effective risk and control management. Learn more about our career areas and lines of business at wellsfargojobs.com .

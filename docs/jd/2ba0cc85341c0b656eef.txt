About the team Zillow Group is looking for a Transaction Management Associate to assist with our asset sale efforts for the Mortgage segment. This position reports directly to the Transaction Manager, Mortgage Capital Markets and is responsible for working across functional teams to maintain controlled operational processes that allow for seamless and continuous placement of loans originated by Zillow Home Loans and realize targeted gain on sale margins. The Transaction Management team develops, negotiates, and formalizes contracts with new servicing buyers and ensures that all existing contractual obligations for all investors/services are met. About the role - Coordinate with Capital Markets trade desk and loan delivery teams for loan and servicing sales. Troubleshoot transaction issues and provide recommendations for operational improvement. - Review closed loan files for accuracy, completeness, and alignment with investor, agency, and regulatory requirements, escalating high-risk issues and recommending corrective actions. - Reconcile investor purchase advice and compare the execution relative to expectations. - Monitor post-closing pipeline and key quality metrics, identifying trends, process gaps, and root causes, and partnering with leaders to implement improvements that enhance efficiency and reduce defects. - Monitor collateral reports, communicate with custodians, and raise collateral concerns. - Interpret evolving post-closing and secondary-market guidelines, translating requirements into clear, repeatable procedures and job aids for the broader team. - Serve as a primary point of contact for escalated post-closing questions, investor suspense items, and exception requests, ensuring clear, timely communication and follow-through. Who you are - Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, or a related field. - 3+ years of experience in mortgage operations, with significant exposure to post-closing, funding, shipping, or secondary-market delivery. - Demonstrated experience reviewing closed loan files for completeness and compliance with investor or agency requirements in a high-volume environment. - Strong analytical skills and attention to detail. - Excellent communication and presentation skills. - Ability to work effectively in a team environment to improve results. - Highly proficient in MS Office applications. - Ability to multi-task and excel in a fast-paced, constantly evolving and data driven organization.

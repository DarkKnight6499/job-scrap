IT Business Analyst, Mid The Opportunity: Business analysts play a critical role in translating business needs into actionable solutions, ensuring teams stay aligned, informed, and working toward meaningful outcomes. We are seeking a forward-thinking analyst who anticipates requirements, understands business drivers, and supports project teams in delivering impactful results. This role involves partnering closely with stakeholders, contributing to modernization initiatives, and enabling continuous improvement across the project lifecycle. In this position, you will apply analytical expertise and domain knowledge to drive clarity, maintain momentum, and ensure decisions are grounded in data and well‑defined requirements. You will support planning efforts, assess business implications at each phase, and foster seamless communication among technical and functional team members, particularly within Agile delivery environments. This position is located in Atlanta, GA. If you're ready to shape high‑impact solutions and grow within a passionate, mission‑driven team, we invite you to take on this challenge. Join us. The world can’t wait. You Have: 3+ years of experience in business analysis supporting IT modernization or transformation efforts 3+ years of experience supporting process modernization using platforms such as Power Apps, Power Automate, Power Pages, or Dynamics 365 3+ years of experience working in a professional environment with technical and functional team members Experience working within Agile delivery environments Ability to navigate conflict among stakeholders and lead requirements gathering and status update meetings with clients Ability to obtain and maintain a Public Trust or Suitability/Fitness determination based on client requirements Bachelor's degree Nice If You Have: Experience with Azure DevOps or products such as JIRA Knowledge of Cloud Platforms and Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM or Power Platform Knowledge of requirements and the software development lifecycle Possession of strong interpersonal skills Master's degree Vetting: Applicants selected will be subject to a government investigation and may need to meet eligibility requirements of the U.S. government client.

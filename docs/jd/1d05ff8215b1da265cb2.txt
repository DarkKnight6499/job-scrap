Join us and transform the lives of patients while transforming your career. Regulatory Policy and Intelligence Associate Director What you will do Let’s do this. Let’s change the world. The Regulatory Policy and Intelligence team advances important policy issues, aligned with Amgen’s goals and priorities, by engaging externally with regulators, industry trade associations, consortia and other stakeholders, along with internal subject matter experts across Amgen. In this vital role, you will lead the development and articulation of Amgen's positions on regulatory policies that impact Amgen's business within a defined portfolio, exercising independent judgement and driving cross-functional alignment. You will report to the Amgen’s U.S. Lead for Regulatory Policy and Intelligence. Primary Responsibilities: Foster U.S. trade association relationships and provide stewardship over the company’s regulatory and R&D-related trade association engagements. Staff company executives in leadership roles in domestic and international forums. Develop and lead enterprise-wide regulatory reform initiatives; implement strategies to shape policy outcomes, the external environment and identify related internal business needs for implementation. Lead assigned policy initiatives from analysis and position development through external engagement and implementation, in partnership with policy leads and management. Additional Responsibilities: Partner with policy leads and subject matter experts, including Government Affairs, R&D, Operations, and other relevant stakeholders, to develop advocacy materials on priority policy issues. Prepare presentations and other communications to support alignment on positions and strategies across the Amgen organizations and regions. Investigate policy research questions on key policy topics and determine the implications of emerging regulatory developments. Analyze regulatory guidances and other regulatory policy documents to inform positions and impact assessments. Plan and help manage projects for agreed upon policy objectives under the direction of and in partnership with policy lead(s) and management.

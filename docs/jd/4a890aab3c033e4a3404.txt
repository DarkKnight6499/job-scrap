When you join the growing BILH team, you're not just taking a job, you’re making a difference in people’s lives. Contributes to the Hospital’s mission by performing pre-analytical processes to prepare specimens for testing. This clinical-support staff position will have an impact on patient care and patient and physician satisfaction by accurately and expeditiously handling their aspect of the laboratory testing process. Job Description: QUALIFICATIONS Education Required: High School Diploma or equivalent Experience Preferred: At least one year of medical laboratory, clerical or secretarial experience. JOB FUNCTIONS As part of the Laboratory team, this position: Receives specimens from multiple locations, data inputs accurate documentation of the patient and the required lab test order. Verifies appropriate specimen accompanies test requisition. Verifies that the specimen is properly labeled with two patient identifiers, date and time of collection, initials of person obtaining the sample Attach printed labels to specimens from physician offices if necessary Completes the order, producing labels and essential documentation. Verifies insurance information and follows through with Hospital registration and/or physician practices to ensure appropriate and efficient billing. Investigate test requisitions with missing information from physician office or off-sites Issues ABN form to patients when tests do not meet medical necessity Preparing specimens for shipments and/or courier to deliver to reference laboratory. Interacts with reference labs, physician offices, clinicians, and others, providing superior customer service and clinical information within their scope of practice. Orders and stocks supplies ensuring adequate reference lab and other materials are on hand to optimize sufficient levels of inventory given limited storage space Answers the phone in a professional manner, answering questions about lab results and researches test status when necessary. Runs reports and addresses the data by providing results, checking on outstanding tests, and other follow through required to improve lab efficiency and physician/clinician satisfaction. Perform the pre-analytical steps in specimen processing including, but not limited to, sorting, centrifuging, aliquoting and generating work lists Perform tests that are waived by the CLIA and other regulatory agencies.

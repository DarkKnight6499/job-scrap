The Business Process/Service Quality area is responsible for enhancing and maintaining the quality of business processes and services within an organization. Developing documentation to support analysis and improvement including business requirements, functional specifications, process maps, gap/problem analysis. Planning implementing quality assurance and compliance processes. Supporting development of training and/or change management materials and activities to support new processes and procedures.

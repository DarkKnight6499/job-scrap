Cherry Bekaert's Finance & Accounting Outsourcing practice is continuing to grow and is seeking experienced accounting professionals to join our team. As a Senior Associate, you will work with a variety of middle-market clients, helping deliver accounting, financial reporting, and operational accounting services while expanding your technical and client service skills in a collaborative environment. As a Finance & Accounting Outsourcing (FAO) Senior Associate, you will: Perform day-to-day accounting activities for multiple client engagements, including general ledger, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and bank reconciliations. Support month-end and year-end close processes, including journal entries, account reconciliations, and financial statement preparation. Prepare accurate and timely financial reports and management reporting packages. Maintain accounting records and ensure compliance with established processes and internal controls. Assist with budgeting, forecasting, and other client accounting projects as needed. Collaborate with clients and internal team members to resolve accounting issues and improve processes. Leverage accounting systems and technology to support efficient service delivery. Manage multiple client priorities while delivering high-quality client service. What You Bring to the Role: Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field. 3+ years of accounting or finance experience. Strong understanding of accounting principles and financial reporting. Experience working with accounting software and ERP systems. Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and other financial reporting tools. Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills. Exceptional attention to detail and commitment to accuracy. Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines. Strong communication and collaboration skills. Ability to work independently while contributing to a team environment.

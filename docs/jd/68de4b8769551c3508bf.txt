Introduction to the Role Are you ready to turn complex manufacturing operations into simple, digital experiences that safeguard quality and accelerate medicines to patients? Join AstraZeneca in Frederick, Maryland, where you will partner with manufacturing, quality, supply chain, engineering, facilities, technical operations, and IT teams to solve real operational challenges through robust, scalable technology. This hybrid role is based at our Frederick Manufacturing Center and requires working on-site an average of at least four days per week. Occasional domestic and international travel may also be required. This is a hands-on opportunity for a curious problem solver who thrives in the details of processes, systems, data, and integrations. You will shape solutions alongside technical teams and suppliers, investigate and resolve issues, support testing and deployment activities, and ensure technology delivers measurable value in a regulated, safety-first environment. Can you seamlessly move from process maps to system logs to help unblock production, improve reliability, and enable operational excellence? Accountabilities Business Partnering & Prioritisation Engage site and global stakeholders to understand priorities, challenges, and business needs. Translate business requirements into a clear, outcomes-focused technology roadmap that improves safety, reliability, and efficiency across biologics manufacturing operations. Process & Requirements Design Lead workshops and design sessions to document current and future-state processes. Produce high-quality requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, and delivery artefacts that support successful implementation. Solution Shaping & Option Assessment Partner with Product Owners, Solution Architects, IT Support teams, and external suppliers to evaluate solution options. Recommend solutions that are scalable, practical, supportable, and aligned with enterprise standards. Operational Insight & Issue Resolution Develop a detailed understanding of systems, integrations, data flows, and operational controls. Investigate incidents, perform root-cause analysis, and drive timely resolution to protect manufacturing operations. End-to-End Delivery Excellence Contribute across the full delivery lifecycle, including solution design, test planning, user acceptance testing (UAT), defect triage, deployment readiness, and hypercare support. Ensure consideration of GxP requirements, data integrity, cybersecurity, validation, and quality expectations throughout delivery. Continuous Improvement & Value Realisation Measure business outcomes and technology performance. Capture lessons learned and continuously improve solutions to drive adoption and maximize long-term value.

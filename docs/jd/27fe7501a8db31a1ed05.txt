The Role The Associate Broker's primary responsibility is to support Brokers & Senior Brokers to effectively manage through the renewal process and deliver results for assigned clients. Day-to-day, an Associate Broker will actively run Willis's suite of Core Analytics, assist in the preparation of marketing submissions, analyze loss and exposure information, prepare strategy or proposal presentations for clients, review quote documents, and manage the post bind process. Outside of the renewal process, Associate Brokers are actively involved in project work and assisting with new business opportunities. Each Associate Broker is assigned to specific client engagements, covering a broad range of industries, program structures, and program complexity. Throughout these engagements, Associate Brokers are front and center, actively participating in client and carrier conversations. Training & Development is extremely important to the Large & Complex Casualty team. In this role, Associate Brokers have the opportunity to learn and develop their skillset through structured training sessions and direct involvement throughout the renewal cycle. Structured training includes periodic Casualty Catalyst sessions, monthly educational Associate Broker meetings, Mock New Business exercises, in-depth coverage training, and more.

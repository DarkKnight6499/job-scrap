The Team: Upstart’s FP&A team plays a critical role in shaping business decisions, ensuring financial discipline as products scale, and driving the Company’s business and financial planning process. This role will explain and forecast performance, identify opportunities and risks, and inform financial decisions for the Unsecured Lending organization. Unsecured Lending is a complex and strategically important part of Upstart’s business with multiple products, financial drivers, and performance dynamics to manage across varying stages of maturity. The organization includes Upstart’s biggest product as well as the newest and fastest growing product that introduces a subscription monetization model. As a Senior Financial Analyst at Upstart, you will drive all aspects of FP&A which includes forecasting and business planning, performance analysis, and executive reporting. You will build reliable financial models, translate business and financial results into actionable insights, and partner across the organization to support informed decision-making. If you are naturally curious, love data, are well-versed in financial modeling and analysis, and are an excellent communicator, we’d love to hear from you.

Financial Analyst The Financial Analyst will report directly to the Sr Manager, Accounting, and will be responsible for many cross functional accounting areas at PacStar, a manufacturing company. This position requires a high level of accounting knowledge specific to inventory costing, controls and analysis, sales order entry and processing, and overall general accounting functions. This is a hands-on role within the accounting team that will be expected to have a working knowledge of sales order processing including quote to cash, full cycle accounting operations, G/L reconciliations, US GAAP, and compliance within the manufacturing industry. PacStar is implementing SAP/S4 Hana with a go live target date of late 2027, and this role will be involved in the implementation and transition to this new system. This position requires someone with a high attention to detail, an effective cross functional team player that is able to manage within timelines in a dynamic work environment. PacStar is a rapidly-growing organization and over time this position will be responsible for expanding areas and needs within the accounting team.

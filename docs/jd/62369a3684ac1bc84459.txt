Job Description The Corporate Trust Financial Management Analyst 3 manages a portfolio of corporate and municipal bond clients and supports their financial activity from beginning to end. This role is responsible for monitoring accounts, reviewing governing documents, processing payments, and helping ensure client transactions are completed accurately and on time. The position is a good fit for someone who is organized, detail-oriented, comfortable managing several priorities, and interested in financial operations. Because each client and transaction may have different requirements, the analyst must be able to review documents, locate important information, and determine the appropriate action. Key Responsibilities: Manage the day-to-day financial activity for an assigned portfolio of Corporate Trust clients. Complete standard daily reports and monitor cash positions, account activity, and items requiring attention. Review and interpret governing documents to identify payment requirements and other transaction instructions. Process invoice payments, recurring payments, requisitions, taxes, insurance, and other deal-related disbursements. Respond to customer questions and requests in a timely and professional manner. Collect and prepare funds for scheduled debt-service payments. Execute debt-service activity, with increased payment volume around the first and fifteenth of each month. Maintain accurate records and ensure assigned work is completed by established deadlines. Use Excel and other internal systems to track account and transaction information. Balance recurring responsibilities with changing client and business needs. Schedule and Location: Location: West Side Flats in St. Paul, Minnesota Schedule: Monday through Friday during normal business hours The employee should generally be available until at least 4:00 p.m. to help address business needs that may arise later in the day. Basic Qualifications: - Associate's degree or technical education, or equivalent work experience - Two to three years of related experience What Will Help You Succeed: Strong organizational and time-management skills. Ability to manage multiple assignments in a high-volume, deadline-driven environment. Strong attention to detail and accuracy. Ability to read, understand, and apply information from detailed financial or governing documents. Basic Microsoft Excel skills. Ability to work independently while also collaborating with team members. Professional communication and customer-service skills. Willingness to learn complex transactions and processes. This role requires working from a U.S Bank location three (3) or more days per week.

HarbourVest is seeking an experienced valuation professional to join its Evergreen Valuations team. This individual will play a key role in the daily valuation of evergreen fund investments. This candidate will be responsible for daily valuation activities across a diversified portfolio of private market investments, including primary fund investments, secondary investments, direct co-investments, and structured or credit-related investments. The role calls for sound valuation judgment, curiosity, and an understanding of private market investment structures. The candidate will be comfortable working in a collaborative, fast-paced environment while applying consistent valuation practices and controls. This position will work closely with Fund Accounting, Investment Teams, Portfolio Analytics, Treasury, Operations, Risk, and other internal team members to identify events, evaluate portfolio changes, and ensure that valuations are supported, documented and reviewed. Key Responsibilities Daily Valuation and NAV Oversight Perform daily valuation activities for investments held within HarbourVest’s evergreen funds. Assess portfolio activity and market developments that may affect investment values, including capital calls, distributions, new investments, realizations, financing activity, foreign exchange movements, public market changes, and material company-specific or fund-specific events. Review daily valuation movements and review changes that exceed established thresholds or appear inconsistent with portfolio activity, market conditions, or investment performance. Ensure valuation adjustments are appropriately calculated, supported, approved, and communicated within the daily NAV timetable. Valuation Analysis Build and maintain valuation methodologies for private equity, venture capital, private credit, infrastructure, real assets, and other private market investments. Evaluate the appropriateness of reported GP NAVs and determine whether adjustments are vital based on reporting lags, subsequent events, market movements, investment performance, financing structures, or other available information. Analyze valuation inputs such as public market indices, comparable companies, credit spreads, foreign exchange rates, transaction activity, operating performance, and capital structure changes. Controls and Oversight Maintain a operating framework around the daily valuation process, including documented review procedures, approval requirements, exception reporting, and customer concern protocols. Develop valuation review guidelines and other controls designed to identify unusual movements. Maintain valuation documentation that supports methodologies, assumptions, judgments, adjustments, and conclusions. Reporting and Coordination Serve as a valuation contact for Fund Accounting and other organizations involved in the daily NAV process. Partner with Investment Teams to obtain and assess information regarding portfolio developments and valuation-impacting events. Prepare daily reporting on valuation movements, exceptions, outstanding information, and key areas of judgment. Support investor reporting and respond to valuation-related questions from internal teams. Coordinate the resolution of valuation issues within the daily deadlines. Operational Improvement and Team Leadership Help design and enhance valuation processes that support the growth of HarbourVest’s evergreen product platform. Look for opportunities to automate data collection, valuation calculations, exception monitoring, and management reporting. Partner with technology and data teams to improve valuation systems, dashboards, workflows, and data quality. What You'll Bring Experience supporting investment valuation, private markets, fund accounting, financial reporting, audit, transaction advisory, or related work Familiarity with private market investment structures and valuation approaches Comfort working with data and financial analysis in Excel or similar tools Experience partnering with colleagues across multiple teams CPA, CFA, ASA, or other professional designation is valued but not required Key Competencies Strong analytical and valuation thinking. Ability to make decisions using incomplete, delayed, or conflicting information. Capacity to perform successfully under deadlines and handle valuation matters simultaneously. Understanding of business impact, issue, and risk in a daily NAV environment. Ability to explain valuation matters to both technical and nontechnical stakeholders.

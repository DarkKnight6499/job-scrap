Collaboration with the Supervisory Analyst, Compliance and other stakeholders to ensure Research is produced and delivered to Clients and internal stakeholders in a compliant manner. As a Senior Analyst within Credit Research, you will support the analysis of investment-grade pharmaceutical and healthcare companies, helping to produce timely and actionable research for institutional investors. Working closely with senior analysts and the broader research team, you will evaluate company performance, industry developments, and market trends that influence investment decisions across fixed income markets. To be successful as a Senior Analyst - Credit Research you should have experience with: Financial analysis and modelling, including advanced Excel skills. Assessing company performance, financial statements, and industry trends to support investment recommendations. Producing research, insights, or analytical reports for internal or external stakeholders. Some other valued skills may include: Experience with fixed income, credit markets, or corporate bond analysis. Familiarity with Bloomberg or other financial market data platforms. Knowledge of the pharmaceutical, healthcare, or broader life sciences sectors. You may be assessed on the key critical skills relevant for success in role, such as risk and controls, change and transformation, business acumen, strategic thinking, digital and technology, as well as job-specific technical skills.

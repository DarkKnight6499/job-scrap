About the Team The New Verticals Finance & Strategy team supports DoorDash’s expansion into categories beyond restaurants, which is a critical growth driver for the overall company. We enable our businesses to grow faster while becoming more profitable. We do this by creating quarterly and annual plans, holding our stakeholders accountable to goals, and providing strategic insights on our business to understand past trends and identify the best path forward. About the Role We're hiring a Senior Associate to help lead the day-to-day financial reporting and closing processes for our New Verticals business. In this role, you'll own recurring reporting cycles, including monthly close, weekly business reviews, and cross-team planning processes, partnering closely with Accounting, Data Engineering, and business teams to keep our financial reporting accurate and on time every cycle. This is a highly detail-oriented, execution-focused role: you'll spend most of your time working directly in the numbers, with real opportunities to build automation and process improvements along the way. You will report into a Manager on our New Verticals Finance team in our Finance & Strategy organization. You’re excited about this opportunity because you will… Own the monthly close and weekly reporting process for New Verticals, ensuring our financial data ties out accurately and on time every cycle. Build and maintain financial models and reporting tools that the broader Finance team relies on every week. Partner with Accounting, Data Engineering , and cross-functional business teams to investigate and resolve reporting discrepancies before they affect business decisions. Help modernize recurring reporting processes, including opportunities to use AI-powered tools to increase efficiency. Play a foundational role on a growing team, with room to take on more scope as you build expertise in the business. We’re excited about you because… You have 4+ years of experience in FP&A, corporate finance, financial reporting, investment banking, or a similar analytical finance role, with day-to-day ownership of a recurring reporting or close process. You have a track record of identifying and resolving data or reporting discrepancies independently. You have experience partnering with cross-functional teams to drive a process or project to a deadline. You have advanced proficiency in Excel and/or Google Sheets, including building and maintaining complex financial models. You have experience writing SQL queries or using AI tools to independently investigate and validate financial or operational data, or equivalent experience with a comparable query language.

Role Overview The Vice President, Finance Strategy & Execution, is a leadership role responsible for defining, shaping, and executing the future-state Finance operating model. Positioned outside of regular Finance production work, this role is focused on change, and how Finance evolves as a functional and strategic partner to support Treasury and the wider Finance organization. The VP will serve as a transformation change leader, agent, strategist, and architect, working closely with Treasury, Technology and enterprise strategy teams. The role integrates strategic vision with pragmatic execution, helping to ensure Treasury evolves towards its strategic Target Operating Model (TOM) while maintaining strong control, integrity, and flexibility. This position is foundational to build a modern and functional Finance Strategy & Execution organization—one that helps design the future of the Finance organization, positions Finance competitively throughout the firm and develops Finance leaders. Core Responsibilities 1. Strategic Leader & Change Driver Help shape and engineer the future of Treasury, defining the enterprise-wide operating model. Drive the design of the strategic vision and realization of complex Finance transformation initiatives, with clear accountability for outcomes and value realization. Translate enterprise and Treasury strategy into clear, multi-year transformation agendas. Act as a thought partner to the various Treasury teams in a complex and continuously evolving environment. Demonstrate ability to apply fit-for-purpose methods aligned to initiative complexity and risk. Actively manage risks, issues, and remediation actions across a portfolio of initiatives. Strengthen organizational capability to sustain long-term transformation and innovation. 2. Transformation Architect & Solution Mindset Contributes to the overall transformation architecture, ensuring coherence and compliance across the operating model. Define solution intent, design principles, and target-state outcomes while ensuring solutions remain executable. Ability to translate business requirements into functional and technical solutions. Lead cross functional working sessions focused on future-state design, decision-making, and adoption. Drive integration and simplification across core Finance and Treasury platforms to reduce complexity and manual intervention. Ensure solutions are designed for scale adaptability, and long-term sustainability. Serve as a trusted advisor and partner for Treasury and Finance, articulating the strategic rationale, design choices, and trade-offs underlying transformation decisions. 3. AI & Automation Enablement Integrate the AI-enabled Finance vision, embedding advanced analytics and intelligent capabilities into the operating model. Identify and scale high-impact use cases for AI, including predictive forecasting, anomaly detection, and intelligent reconciliation. Shape the data architecture required to enable enterprise-grade analytics and decision intelligence. Shift Finance from a reactive mindset to forward-looking, insight-driven, and performance focused organization. Reimagine Finance processes through an automation-first lens, eliminating manual effort and redesigning workflows. Embed AI fluency and digital capability into Finance ways of working and talent models. Continuously evaluate emerging technologies to sustain competitive advantage. Required Qualifications 10+ years of experience across Treasury, Finance and Transformation work or similar advisory roles within complex financial institutions. Deep knowledge across Treasury/Finance and their processes Proven experience designing and leading large-scale Treasury transformations, not simply managing delivery. Demonstrates ability to operate credibly at both high-level strategic and detailed architecture levels.

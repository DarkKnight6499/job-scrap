If you like making complex data clearer, safer, and easier for teams to use, this role is for you. JPMorgan Wealth Management needs passionate Data Owner Associates to support product data end-to-end—help make it trusted, well-governed, and ready to power analytics and delivery. You’ll support a single product’s data enablement, publishing, and governance—working alongside a VP Data Owner to keep priorities moving and turn data into reliable outcomes. As a Data Product Owner on the US Wealth Management Data & Analytics team, you support end-to-end enablement, publishing, and governance for all data created and used by an aligned product under the direction of a VP Data Owner. You help define and document product data—business definitions, metadata, classifications, and quality expectations—so it is discoverable, understood, and fit for purpose. You assist with monitoring, issue management, and cross-functional coordination to improve data quality and reduce data risk across the lifecycle. Support delivery of data pipelines and products by tracking dependencies, coordinating handoffs, and reinforcing Agile execution across workstreams. Document business definitions, metadata, and classifications so critical data is discoverable and consistently understood Maintain data governance artifacts such as inventories, controls evidence, and procedures to support ongoing compliance and audit readiness Perform data profiling and validation to identify anomalies, gaps, and trends impacting downstream use Partner with technology and data delivery teams to support root cause analysis and remediation execution Communicate updates, risks, and decisions through well-structured documentation and stakeholder routines.

Liquidity Modeling & Analysis Consultant Liquidity Management | Treasury | Finance Full-Time Boston, MA, Springfield, MA, or New York, NY The Opportunity As a Liquidity Modeling & Analysis Consultant, you will work in a fast-paced, innovative and collaborative environment focused on providing the company with detailed analytics on liquidity positioning, identifying liquidity risks, and creating playbooks to mitigate them. This individual contributor role will report directly to the Head of Liquidity Modeling & Analysis within MassMutual’s Treasury organization and will have the opportunity to: Improve our modeling capabilities from static snapshots to build a dynamic, forward looking forecasting engine capable of navigating the unique complexities of life insurance cash flows Participate in the design of contingent liquidity planning frameworks to codify our response to liquidity stress Collaborate across multiple teams including Investment Management, Actuarial, Enterprise Risk Management, Financial Planning & Analysis, Controllership, and more The Team The Liquidity Modeling & Analysis Consultant is a member of the Liquidity Management Team within the Treasury organization, which is a dynamic team of individuals with diverse backgrounds that are focused on: · Monitoring our daily cash and liquidity positioning, forecasting our cash flow needs, and maintaining our bank relationships to raise funding · Designing models to forecast our liquidity risks and developing the funding options and execution strategies required to response to stress events · Achieving a balance of maximizing value for our policyholders while continuing to operate within our liquidity constraints · Serving as a strategic partner to our colleagues in Investment Management, Actuarial, and Enterprise Risk Management The Impact Your role within the Liquidity Modeling & Analysis team will contribute to key insights regularly delivered to our senior management on management of key liquidity risks.

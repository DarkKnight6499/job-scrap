Role profile: LSEG is in the process of launching and developing new Post Trade services which expand beyond the traditional core clearing businesses! As part of the effort to launch new products and services within the new Post Trade Division, LSEG is seeking to hire a Product Manager, based in New York, to develop and implement new services within the existing Equities OTC business. The ideal candidate will have detailed Equities OTC derivatives knowledge covering trade booking, life-cycle events, confirmations and settlement. Experience in product ownership, change management experience and familiarity with Agile methodology is preferable Key responsibilities of the role: Within a wider product team, the individual will focus on the Contract & Trade Life-cycle service with key responsibilities being; product enhancement, customer engagement, solution design, roadmap planning in collaboration with stakeholders and the Business Leads Builds and maintains a client engagement model to drive Product development. Owns product strategy, design, business requirements, feature and functionality trade-offs for elements of the Equity Derivative TradeAgent business in partnership with Agent Business Stakeholders. Contributes to product roadmaps, communicating the vision to their teams, senior leaders and stakeholders. Acts as the product lead on project delivery teams, providing direction on scope, approach and helping formulate go-to-market tactics. Executes on go-to-market strategies with Business Lead and Sales org to successful launch & follow up. Develops a client training plan for product workflows. Conducts research on market evolution, competitors, client requirements and strategies. Engages with Member Integration and Operations teams to establish Product/Function Champions. Ownership for the creation and maintenance of technical specifications and associated documentation. Works closely across divisions to market and manage new products. Responsible to help develop, mentor and coach colleagues to enhance the overall capability in the business and fostering the talent within the function. Crucial experience and skills required: Asset Classes Detailed understanding and experience of OTC equity derivatives market, market infrastructure & contract life-cycle. Detailed understanding of ISDA definitions & legal confirmation processes for Equity Derivative asset class. Analysis Skills & decision making Strong analytical skills, with the ability to organise complex data, assess multiple options, and make sound, evidence-based decisions in a timely manner. Problem solving Defines a problem and evaluates and identifies the best solution to overcome the problem.

MANAGER, CREDIT RISK OPERATIONS


HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

You’ll own credit risk operations across Airwallex’s global product portfolio, helping assess prospective and existing merchants while balancing commercial growth with robust risk protection. You’ll evaluate exposure across cards, bank payments and local payment methods, and use data-driven insights to strengthen credit decisioning, monitoring and early-warning frameworks.

 - Own credit risk exposure across Airwallex’s global product portfolio and payment acceptance channels.

 - Drive KYB processes that support a diversified, high-quality merchant portfolio.

 - Define and monitor global credit risk performance KPIs, reporting and dashboards to improve visibility into credit performance.

 - Build and improve standard operating procedures for credit risk vetting, investigation and ongoing monitoring.

 - Partner with Business Development, Legal, Compliance and Transaction Risk to balance commercial goals with risk mitigation.

 - Strengthen risk awareness across global stakeholders and surface emerging risks across products and geographies.



You’ll work from our San Francisco office on Mondays, Wednesdays and Thursdays.




WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR


ESSENTIALS

 - Strong analytical and quantitative skills, with demonstrated experience assessing counterparty or merchant credit risk.

 - Deep understanding of credit fundamentals, including financial statement analysis, cash flow assessment, collateral structures and exposure management.

 - Practical experience with ongoing credit monitoring, limit setting and early-warning frameworks for merchant portfolios.

 - Strong understanding of payment flows and how settlement timing, reserves and chargebacks affect credit exposure.

 - Experience identifying and escalating emerging credit risks across payment products and geographies.

 - Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain credit findings clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders.

 - Proven ability to manage multiple priorities with precision and attention to detail.

 - An ownership mindset: you take accountability, drive clarity and execute with urgency.


PREFERRED

 - Advanced proficiency in data analysis tools such as SQL.

 - Familiarity with card brand monitoring, compliance and KYB/KYC requirements.

 - Experience managing credit exposure for merchants or partners within fintech, payments or financial institutions.

 - Background in regulatory compliance or operational risk management.

 - A track record of defining and reporting KPIs to improve process performance.

 - Deep understanding of payment processing systems and associated credit, fraud and operational risks.


YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

 - You're comfortable making decisions with incomplete information and moving quickly anyway.

 - You own the outcome and you don't wait for permission to fix what's broken.

 - You connect dots across functions and hold the big picture without losing grip on the details.

 - You say what you mean. You're direct, but you lead with respect.

 - You've operated in a compliance-heavy environment and know how to move fast without cutting the corners that matter.

The Opportunity This role will be responsible for executing key components of the Technology Risk and Controls Assurance program, including independent control testing, issue validation, and risk reporting for the Technology Business Line Testing team. The role will work closely with multiple stakeholders including Ally Technology and Cyber leadership, IPRM, business leaders, and technology project and operations teams to conduct technology and cybersecurity risk assessments, interpret data and analytics outputs, and lead control remediation and uplift efforts. The Work Itself Partner with Ally Technology and Cyber Leaders working cross-functionally while conducting individual control tests Independently develop, complete, and document tests in accordance with Ally policies and procedures and industry best practices Interpret data literacy and analytics outputs — including automated testing results and control monitoring dashboards — to identify anomalies, assess sample adequacy, and provide consultation to management regarding emerging control practices Participate in uplift initiatives will be essential to the function's contribution to the enterprise and its continuing role within Ally business Proactively identify emerging risks and remain informed of emerging risk trends and industry-leading mitigation practices Complete assigned validations of findings and issue management activities with quality and in accordance with Ally standards Contribute to control uplift initiatives, special projects, and continuous improvement efforts that strengthen control operations, reduce deficiencies, and support evolving regulatory and cybersecurity expectations. Provide coaching, guidance, and support to team members and contractors, as needed. Apply current cybersecurity risk assessment practices in control testing, consistent with evolving industry and regulatory baselines The Skills You Bring 3+ years of work experience in audit, assurance, or equivalent line of service; technology/cyber focus strongly preferred Bachelor's degree or comparable combination of education and experience Working knowledge of technology control frameworks (e.g., FFIEC, NIST SP800-53, CRI Profile, etc.) Relevant certification (CIA, CISA, CISSP, CRISC, CPA, etc.) is a plus Detail-oriented, deadline driven, self-directed and organized individual Desire to creatively identify problems that potentially were not known to exist and provide solutions and/or recommendations Strong written and verbal communications skills Ability to work with structured datasets and dashboard/reporting tools (e.g., Excel-based data models, Power BI, or similar) to interpret testing results and identify control exceptions — direct coding or scripting experience not required Proven ability to excel in highly interactive team environment Working knowledge of GRC Platform (e.g., Archer) and Microsoft Office applications Minimum Qualifications 3+ years of experience High school Diploma or GED equivalent How We'll Have Your Back Ally's compensation program offers market-competitive base pay and pay-for-performance incentives (bonuses) based on achieving personal and company goals.

What You'll Be Doing Under administrative direction, the Research Associate III is primarily responsible for providing information, data gathering, analysis, modeling, and organizational support to the Research Analyst and senior Research staff. A level III Associate has mastered the functions of a level II Associate and can perform all of the functions of an associate, including effectively filling the role of the Research Analyst in his or her absence. A Research Associate III is developing the ability to react to new information, assist in making judgment calls on stocks, and take a proactive role in helping build the franchise. What We're Looking For Build and maintain Excel-based financial models, including income statements, balance sheets, cash flow statements, and discounted cash flow analysis. Formulate mathematical or simulation models of problems, relating constants and variables, restrictions, alternatives, conflicting objectives, and their numerical parameters. Analyze information obtained from management in order to conceptualize and define operational problems. Perform validation and testing of models to ensure adequacy; reformulate models as necessary. Building and updating comp sheets. Managing the databases and electronic data services (i.e. Bloomberg, FactSet, etc.) Must be able to clearly state and defend the senior level Research Analysts investment thesis and outlook for each of the stocks under coverage. Able to clearly apply, defend and/or develop valuation methodologies for the stocks under coverage and the broader sector. Ability to react to new information and what it means to the stocks. Proactively generate ideas that enhance research. Respond to questions from institutional sales and clients regarding covered companies, and industry questions and data requests. Generate report ideas and fully develop written product. Meets the special projects demands of the Research Associate VP. Continues to build overall development of job knowledge. Some travel by car and/or air in conjunction with local, regional and/or national travel, up to 25%.

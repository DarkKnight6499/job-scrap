About You You bring a passion for commercial real estate and a strong interest in multifamily investment sales. You are intellectually curious, financially minded, and motivated to build a long-term career in a fast paced, entrepreneurial environment. The ideal candidate is highly driven, collaborative, and comfortable working both independently and as part of a high-performing team. Strong communication, analytical, and relationship-building skills are essential. In this role, you will… Heavily involved in underwriting and analyzing properties, cold calling and prospecting for new business, and following up on leads and opportunities to support deal execution. Maintain company databases to track prospects and business opportunities. Bring a great attitude in learning the business and local marketplace by attending local business and industry events, networking, and participating in client meetings. Go through training to learn the “Colliers Way” of doing business. Coordinate supporting materials for offer packages and review proposals, financials, and related documents. Prepare and distribute presentations, reports, proposals, spreadsheets, correspondence, and other documents. Eventually transition to independently managing real estate sales transactions from start to finish. What you’ll bring Highly motivated with a strong work ethic, competitive mindset, and desire to build a long-term career in commercial real estate. 1+ years of relevant sales experience (both via phone and in-person canvassing), quality internship experience is considered. Licensed Real Estate practitioner with the State of North Carolina (or in process of obtaining). Excellent interpersonal quantitative, analytical, writing, and communication skills. Well organized with excellent time management skills. Desire to advance in the commercial real estate industry. Prior experience using CRM programs. Spreadsheet applications experience (Microsoft Excel).

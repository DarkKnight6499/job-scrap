Build your wealth management experience while making a meaningful contribution to new client relationships. Join the JPMorgan Wealth Management Client Specialist Team, where you will work alongside experienced wealth management professionals and gain hands-on exposure to prospect development, client engagement, and the wealth management sales process. You contribute across the sales cycle by developing prospect profiles, preparing for and following up on prospect meetings, coordinating with internal partners, managing the sales pipeline, and supporting client and prospect events. You help Client Specialists understand clients' wealth and planning needs and connect them with appropriate wealth management services and resources. Through this role, you will gain hands-on experience in private wealth management, sales, and relationship development while helping the team build a robust pipeline and win new wealth management business.

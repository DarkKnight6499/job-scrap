The Opportunity Join a high-impact finance team supporting the growth of the ID NOW Infectious Disease business within Abbott's Developed Markets U.S. Commercial organization. This role serves as a key business partner across Commercial, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Finance, leading demand forecasting and financial planning activities that drive informed business decisions, optimize inventory planning, and support long-term growth strategies. What You'll Work On Lead the sales revenue and volume forecasting process for the ID NOW product portfolio, incorporating market share trends, seasonality, distributor inventory levels, sell-out data, and other key business drivers. Develop, maintain, and continuously improve complex forecasting models to support short-, mid-, and long-range business planning. Prepare and review financial forecasts, evaluate key assumptions, and identify business risks and opportunities. Partner closely with Commercial, Marketing, Corporate Accounts, Supply Chain, and Finance teams to align demand plans with business objectives. Communicate forecast plans, assumptions, and demand outlooks across the organization to support effective operational and financial decision-making. Monitor forecast performance through key performance indicators and drive continuous forecast accuracy improvements. Perform root-cause analysis on forecast variances and implement corrective actions to enhance future forecasting outcomes. Analyze financial results, identify trends and performance drivers, and provide actionable insights to leadership. Prepare business unit reporting, financial templates, variance analyses, and executive-level presentations. Support ad hoc financial analyses and strategic business initiatives. Drive process simplification and continuous improvement efforts across forecasting and financial planning activities. Ensure compliance with corporate financial policies, controls, and procedures. Lead, coach, and develop team members while fostering a collaborative, high-performing culture. Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Finance or Accounting 5+ years of progressive experience in finance, forecasting, financial planning & analysis (FP&A), demand planning, or related business functions. Experience developing and managing complex forecasting models using multiple business and market inputs. Strong analytical and financial modeling skills with the ability to translate data into meaningful business insights. Demonstrated ability to partner effectively with cross-functional stakeholders and influence decision-making. Experience analyzing financial performance, forecasting trends, and communicating business risks and opportunities. Strong verbal and written communication skills, including presentation and financial storytelling capabilities. Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced and dynamic environment. Advanced proficiency in English. Proficiency with Microsoft Excel and financial reporting tools. Preferred Qualifications MBA, CPA, CMA, or other relevant advanced degree or professional certification. Experience supporting commercial healthcare, diagnostics, medical device, life sciences, or related industries. Knowledge of Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP) processes and demand forecasting methodologies. Experience working with distributor-driven business models and inventory-based forecasting. Experience creating executive-level reporting, dashboards, and presentations. Demonstrated success leading process improvement initiatives and driving operational efficiencies. Prior people leadership, mentoring, or team development experience. Experience working within a global, matrixed organization.

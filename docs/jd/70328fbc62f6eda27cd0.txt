Underwriting Operations Assistant
W. R. Berkley
Location: Minneapolis, MN, US

Company Details We're a member company of W. R. Berkley Corporation, an A. M. Best A+ rated Fortune 500 holding company. Berkley is comprised of individual operating units that serve a defined insurance market segment. BTU A global insurance provider, offering property, casualty, professional and cyber insurance coverage for technology firms and businesses with technology exposure. Responsibilities The Underwriting Operations Assistant supports the new business underwriting process by preparing submissions, performing rating activities, validating data, and maintaining workflow accuracy. This entry-level developmental role ensures submissions are complete, accurately documented, and ready for underwriting review. Responsibilities Review new business for completeness, accuracy, and readiness for underwriting review. Use AI-enabled tools and source documentation to validate submission information, rating inputs, supporting data, and AI-generated outputs; correct inaccuracies and document discrepancies, overrides, or feedback according to established procedures. Ensure accurate and timely rating, booking, issuance, and policy or endorsement production by entering and validating data based on underwriting instructions and established guidelines. Manage assigned workflows and maintain accurate files, documentation, and workflow records while meeting service, quality, and turnaround expectations. Provide timely and professional service by obtaining information needed to complete transactions and responding to questions related to policy status, billing, or transaction processing. Communicate with Underwriting, Underwriting Services, IT and billing staff to coordinate work, resolve questions, escalate exceptions, and support timely transaction completion. Support process improvement and system application testing by identifying opportunities to improve rating accuracy, AI workflows, data quality, and submission readiness from initial review through deployment. Build knowledge of underwriting, insurance operations, company products, AI-enabled tools, and workflow systems through training and daily work. May perform other functions as assigned. Qualifications Insurance, administrative, data entry, customer service, or other detail-intensive experience preferred. Comfort working with technology, AI-enabled tools, rating platforms, and workflow systems preferred. Ability to critically review AI-generated information, compare it with source documentation, and identify inaccuracies or unsupported conclusions. Ability to follow detailed rating procedures while recognizing when a situation requires escalation. Strong attention to detail, organization, and time management skills. Strong communication, critical thinking, and problem-solving abilities. Comfort adapting to evolving technology, processes, and system functionality.

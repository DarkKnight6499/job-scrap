Job Summary: We are seeking a detail-oriented self-starter to join Allegion’s Channel Finance team. The Senior Financial Analyst will provide support to Americas Channel Finance with responsibility for tracking revenue performance and assisting with strategic channel initiatives. The position will also play a critical role in producing timely and accurate financial information that is in compliance with GAAP and applicable company policy (FICAP). This role will help drive transformation within the organization by enhancing the team’s analytics capabilities to provide meaningful information to the business to drive decisions. This position will have exposure to the Sales Leadership Team, Americas Leadership Team, and Finance Leaders. What You Will Do: Support channel finance efforts in tracking revenue performance of sales teams and strategic initiatives Provide variance explanations and actionable insights in relation to

About this role Forward Deployed Business Analyst – BlackRock Transformation Office (BTO) The BlackRock Transformation Office (BTO) is a global group of change professionals that delivers strategic transformation to help make BlackRock greater than the sum of its parts. We drive 1BLK outcomes by continuously connecting and being students of the firm’s people, processes and technology. We are a versatile team with varied experiences and backgrounds, motivated by the challenge of finding solutions that benefit BlackRock and our clients. In BTO, we focus on developing our people, strengthening leadership and delivery skills, and broadening firm and industry knowledge. Projects and roles consider both successful delivery and individual development objectives and career aspirations. Your role and impact The Forward Deployed Business Analyst will work in cross-functional teams to reimagine, innovate and scale solutions that drive growth and operating models within the firm. This is not a traditional business analysis role centered on gathering requirements and handing them to technology. The role combines business analysis, product discovery, business architecture and hands-on prototyping to turn ambiguous problems into tangible solutions that business users and engineers can test and refine together. Successful candidates will combine the structured thinking of a business analyst, the prioritization instincts of a product owner, the enterprise perspective of a business architect and the bias to action of a hands-on builder. Your responsibilities In every role at BlackRock, you'll be expected to apply sound judgement and critical thinking to solve complex problems, adapt as the business evolves, and combine the curiosity to explore new approaches and technologies with the rigor to challenge the results. The scope of this role also includes the following responsibilities: Take ownership of complex, ambiguous business problems and rapidly translate them into testable solution hypotheses with measurable outcomes. Build structured business context covering terminology, entities, relationships, decision logic, data dependencies, controls and exceptions for use by subject matter experts, AI tools and engineering teams. Decompose business processes into explicit logic, controls and exception paths for implementation by technology teams. Create functional prototypes using AI-enabled development capabilities to discover requirements, challenge assumptions and validate future-state processes, data, policies and controls. Shape the target operating model, including ownership of data, governance, controls, roles and exception management. Partner continuously with business experts, product managers, engineers, architects, data specialists and control partners from discovery through implementation. Develop measurable project objectives, drive consensus, and communicate plans, progress and outcomes effectively to stakeholders and management across levels. Your experience 3–5 years of relevant work experience in transformation, product delivery, business architecture, technology-enabled change or a comparable discipline. Experience in business architecture and operating-model design, connecting solutions to business capabilities, roles, governance, policy ownership, controls and exception handling. Proficiency in prototype-led problem solving and rapidly converting ambiguous business problems into functional prototypes that stakeholders can see, test and challenge. Expertise using AI-enabled development environments to accelerate analysis, prototyping, requirements definition and testing, including critically evaluating generated outputs, maintaining traceability and understanding where human judgment remains essential. Experience with iterative delivery and hands-on ownership through cycles of analysis, experimentation and solution shaping. Experience in financial services or a similar industry.

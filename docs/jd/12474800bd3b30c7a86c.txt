Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We’re hiring a Manager Quality Risk Management (QRM) to join our team at our new highly automated drug substance manufacturing facility in Saratoga Springs. This position leads the implementation of the QRM Governance process globally at Regeneron Industrial Operations & Product Supply (IOPS) sites, ensuring compliance with global regulations and standards set forth by governing regulatory and accrediting agencies. They will also support other departments and project teams in meeting quality and business objectives as they relate to GxP risk management.

A Brief Overview The Associate Director, Client Experience will help lead our client experience strategy, regional, events, and business initiatives. This position will act as an expert in experience execution while helping drive the collaboration between the Client Experience team, other CMS functional teams and the business. What you will do Client Experience Strategy & Planning: In coordination with the greater Client Marketing strategy and business/regional strategies, This person will plan, manage, and lead client experience engagements in collaboration with cross functional teams, such as business development colleagues, and senior executives. This person will serve as the overall lead on large projects and initiatives for the client engagement team, such as industry sponsorship opportunities, business development communication and RGA promotional project planning. Impactful Experiences: Responsible for elevating engagements through unique and innovative experiences that support the RGA purpose and strategy. Responsible for developing engagement initiative experiential plan, engaging the support team, and managing the other resources needed for the execution of tasks, including key and timely communications, planning meetings, establishing clear objectives and roles, coordination with external parties, as applicable, as well as timeline and deadline management. Identify most effective internal and external resourcing options for efficient execution. Audience and Objective Focused: Working with senior leaders and the business development team to clarify target audience and primary objectives for client engagement initiatives, and support strategy alignment with relevant cross functional teams. Lead engagement roadmap before, during, and post event, including leveraging most effective and efficient communication means for target audience such as event platform and/or app. Global Collaboration and Enablement: Support client experience collaboration and enablement across regional teams helping to identify and address shared challenges and opportunities, contributing to business guidelines and best practices, ensuring insight into regional events and experiences, and leveraging resources that enable efficiencies, experience consistency and an elevated client experience. Internal Communications and Reporting: Leads and manages all internal communications and coordination. Lead stakeholders and colleagues through planning, preparation, execution, and follow-up activities. Identify and communicate ROI efforts for colleagues through qualitative and quantitative reporting methods. Process Optimization: As part of client experience team, support team resource management and execution throughout the year. Contribute to evolution of best practices and compilation of most efficient and effective experiential resources to maximize efforts and evolve and elevate our client experience. Relationship Building: Act as a Client Experience liaison to the business teams and leadership, delivering on a variety of business and elevated client experience goals. Develops and maintains constructive, beneficial working relationships with internal and external clients. Continues to increase working knowledge of internal functions and activities as related to Client Marketing. Acts as an RGA industry-facing representative, building and maintaining relationships with vendors, partners, associations, and industry bodies. Act as on-site backup as needed.

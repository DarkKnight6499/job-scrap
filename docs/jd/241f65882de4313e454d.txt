Job Posting Title: Merchandise Planning Intern, Spring 2027 Req ID: 10160398 Job Description: Job Description About the Role & Program Step into the fast-paced world of merchandise planning! As a Merchandise Planning Intern, you’ll dive headfirst into hands-on projects, sharpening your analytical skills as you help shape assortment strategies designed to maximize sales and drive results. This role isn’t just about crunching numbers; you will collaborate closely with planners and cross-functional teams, learning the art and science behind creating winning product mixes. If you’re fueled by curiosity and eager to make a real impact, this internship offers the perfect launchpad for your career in retail strategy and planning. This internship is a full-time, six-month commitment in Orlando, FL, from January 2027 to June 2027. What You Will Do You’ll assist with quarterly and annual assortment planning, analyze item and category performance, and identify stock issues. Working with the Planner, you’ll communicate solutions and help launch products. Day-to-day tasks also include supporting special projects and running routine analyses to help the team maximize sales and minimize risk. On any given day, you might: Review weekly sales and share recommendations to boost business Communicate assortment changes to guide future orders Work with a Merchandise Assistant to ensure product launches are on schedule and report any issues Coordinate with Location Strategy & Allocation to verify that in-stock items are set up and selling in all locations Help prepare presentations for leadership on program results and strategies Manage planning tasks like updating locations, changing set/exit dates, revising POs, handling RTVs, and price changes Visit stores regularly to spot ways

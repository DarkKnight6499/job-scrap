As the Associate Booking Producer, you will work as part of NBC News’ Booking and Editorial Unit, on the News and Politics team, responsible for booking guests across all platforms of NBC News, including TODAY, Weekend TODAY, Nightly News, Weekend Nightly News, Meet the Press, Dateline, Specials, as well as our streaming network NBC News NOW and all digital platforms. Responsibilities: Manage short- and long-term bookings across multiple NBC News platforms. Pitch story ideas and work on assigned bookings as they are launched by editorial teams and shows. Coordinate guest travel and live shot logistics in partnership with production teams and arrange editorial guidance and pre-interviews with segment producers. Support the team in on set and remote guest coordination for tape and live segments, helping to book and organize guests across various broadcast and NBC News NOW shows. Work with the team to give direction to bookers in the field, as well as coordinate and schedule guests and analysts, often during breaking news. Responsible for compiling and sending out our daily booking note which details all booking efforts and secured guests as well as organizing the team’s bookings into comprehensive handovers for the next day. Must have at least 2 years of experience working in production or booking at the network level. Must have a Bachelor’s degree. Interested candidates must submit a resume/CV through nbcunicareers.com to be considered. This job is a Tuesday to Saturday schedule, must be able to work additional hours beyond scheduled shift with little or no notice if needed. Must be willing and able to work flexible hours, under tight deadline pressure, including breaking news. Must be willing to travel with little notice, and for extended periods. Must be willing to work in New York, NY. Must be willing to take a background investigation. Must have unrestricted work authorization to work in the United States. Desired Characteristics: A genuine news junkie who gets their information from a wide range of sources be it broadcast, newsletters, podcasts, social media or anything in between. Someone who starts the day by checking news alerts, morning shows, and socials to know the stories everyone will be talking about. Strong journalistic writing skills, research skills, and editorial judgment. Understands how to work with people in serious, sometimes sensitive and stressful situations. A self-starter who thrives in a fast-paced environment where the day we started at 9AM may be totally different by noon. Strong interpersonal skills. Ability to multi-task and prioritize assignments. Strong knowledge of all social media platforms. Knowledge of NBC News Policies and Guidelines and Practices. Demonstrated strong knowledge and interest in news and current events. Additional Requirements: Hybrid : This position currently has a hybrid schedule, which requires contributing from the office a minimum of four days per week.

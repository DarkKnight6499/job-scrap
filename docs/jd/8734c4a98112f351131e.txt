How This Role impacts Live Oak and its People The Senior Portfolio Analyst (“SPA”) serves as an integral part of the bank’s credit risk management and is responsible for collecting and analyzing financial data, completing financial spread analysis, testing financial covenants, and completing loan reviews, advances, renewals, and amendments. Additionally, SPA provides support to more senior members of the Sponsor Finance team, preparing diligence materials and providing valuable feedback on portfolio performance. What You’ll Do at Live Oak Collect and analyze financial data for a portfolio of accounts throughout the life of the relationship to prepare financial spreads and analyses, to ensure accuracy of financial statements, to monitor financial performance, liquidity, and earnings trends, and to accurately calculate credit metrics and track covenant compliance to ensure clients remain compliant with all conditions set forth in the loan documentation. Evaluate and assess financial performance and the associated trends and credit metrics by sensitizing financial performance and monitoring key performance indicators and risks as well as qualitative factors to adequately assess borrower risk and ensure the relationship is appropriately risk rated at all times in accordance with the bank’s risk rating policy. Establish and maintain direct client relationships through regular communication, management meetings and site visits, and conduct onboarding of new clients. Proactively identify underperformance and root causes, and assess whether the underperformance has resulted in, or may result in a default(s) or event of default under the loan documents. Engage the deal team and credit team, and other internal parties as appropriate, to guide underperforming client accounts, and to determine and carry out the appropriate response to underperformance to protect the bank’s interests. Underwrite and manage the approval process for loan modifications, amendments, waivers, consents, renewals and advance requests, including multi-advance facilities, all in accordance with bank procedures and policy, coordinating with the client as well as legal counsel, closing, credit and operations departments. Assist the deal team in negotiations and review of legal documentation with legal counsel to ensure documentation correctly reflects the approval. Collaborate with Analysts on underwriting more complex modifications and amendments or facility increases. Facilitate, process and closely monitor multi-advance requests and revolving credit facilities. Prepare diligence and presentation materials for ongoing loan reviews and present to internal stakeholders on specific loans and the related credit risk, recommending appropriate action and/or proactive strategies. Monitor and ensure that client files remain up to date, including the creation of files for new clients, setting up reporting and covenant dashboards, and assisting with various reporting requirements. Manage operational complexities and inquiries from both external and internal stakeholders and closely monitor the operations function for a portfolio of accounts at origination of a new facility, for collection of payments or at the time of an amendment, modification or advance request, alongside the operations department. Lead or contribute to cross-functional and strategic bank initiatives, including system/process implementations and improvements to portfolio monitoring tools, reporting dashboards, and risk mitigation strategies Take ownership of responsibilities and goals, pursue them with enthusiasm, and take pride in helping clients and teammates achieve theirs. Mentor teammates by providing guidance on financial and credit analysis and risk assessment. Facilitate third-party engagements, as necessary, including valuations, appraisals, and field exams. Exercise sound judgment, seek guidance when appropriate and communicate effectively to set and meet realistic expectations. Complete additional tasks as assigned or as needed to support the deal team and/or client. Maintain a high standard of regulatory awareness including BSA, AML, CIP, and OFAC.

Investment Accounting Associate II, Mortgage Loans and Real Estate Investment Controllership Full-Time/Boston, MA or Springfield, MA The Opportunity As an Accounting Associate II on our Investment Accounting team, you will help ensure accurate and timely monthly, quarterly, and annual accounting close cycles for our mortgage loan and real estate asset classes. You will account for investment transactions in compliance with applicable regulations and support monitoring, governance, process improvements, and transaction controls—including activities performed by third-party accounting service providers. You will also contribute to initiatives that improve efficiency and add value while building your project management expertise and deepening your knowledge of investment product accounting and its financial impacts The Team The Investment Controllership team is a high-profile group within the Controllers organization. Our accountants oversee a range of asset classes and investment activities, and the team continues to grow alongside our assets, strategic initiatives, and special projects. We combine strong business acumen with a commitment to delivering timely, accurate financial information. We work with agility, collaborate to improve our processes, ask thoughtful questions, identify issues with precision, and take accountability for making things right. The Impact Your responsibilities supporting the mortgage loan and real estate investment areas will include: Review and analyze monthly, quarterly, and annual reports prepared by the third-party administrator. Review financial statements, footnote schedules, regulatory filings, and management reports prepared by the third-party administrator. Analyze trends and results, partnering with peers and the Financial Reporting team to develop clear commentary that explains how performance affects financial results. Help prepare, analyze, and present monthly financial analysis reports to senior management. Support annual regulatory financial statement audit requests and participate in discussions with auditors. Identify opportunities to learn, grow, and strengthen team performance. Manage competing priorities, adapt quickly, and improve established processes to achieve goals efficiently. Identify and implement accounting improvements that increase efficiency and business value. Lead increasingly complex projects and continuous improvement efforts that may extend beyond the team’s core responsibilities. Collaborate effectively with Accounting Policy, the Chief Investment Office, Tax, Financial Reporting, Capital Management, and business unit controllers. Implement new statutory and GAAP investment accounting requirements and establish accounting processes for new investments and investment-related transactions. Develop, document, and maintain a strong internal control framework that supports accurate, timely financial reporting. Recommend improvements, monitor investment accounting controls—including work performed by vendors—and ensure adherence to policies and procedures. Actively participate in implementing the Company’s new ledger.

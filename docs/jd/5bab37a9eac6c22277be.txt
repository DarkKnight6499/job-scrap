Join Thermo King as a Digital Portfolio Leader ! In this high-impact role, you will lead the strategic vision, product roadmaps, and commercial execution for Thermo King’s fastest-growing segment. You are accountable for leading all aspects of Fleet intelligence offering strategy, new offer development, and commercialization to drive profitable growth by expanding recurring subscriptions and achieving sustainability objectives aligned with Trane Technologies’ vision. Leading the Digital Product Growth Team (PGT), you will spearhead portfolio expansion across transport refrigeration units (TRUs), auxiliary power units (APUs), and electrified climate systems. You will optimize end-to-end connectivity processes—streamlining commissioning, and device activation while architecting automated digital workflows that reduce fleet downtime and guarantee cold chain compliance. Operating across commercial and technical boundaries, you will benchmark the competition, arm sales teams with high-impact value-selling tools, and empower our premier dealer network with digital diagnostic workflows and recurring margin models. Key Responsibilities: 1. Digital Portfolio Strategy & Platform Lifecycle Management Direct the end-to-end product lifecycle for the TracKing® telematics platform and next-generation connected software applications across trailer, truck, bus HVAC, and APU product categories. Own the Multi-Generation Product Plan (MGPP) for the TracKing® platform, cloud applications, diagnostics, and mobile technician tools across transport refrigeration units (TRUs), APUs, and electrified platforms. Expand the digital portfolio into high-value domains: predictive asset health, automated cold chain temperature verification, fleet decarbonization metrics, and remote multi-temp control. Define connectivity and API strategies to enable seamless data exchange with third-party Fleet Management Systems (FMS) and customer enterprise platforms. 2. Competitive Benchmarking & Market Intelligence Lead continuous technical and commercial benchmarking against competitive OEM connected offerings and emerging third-party IoT transport platforms. Conduct deep-dive competitive teardowns to identify feature gaps, integration advantages, and pricing shifts, converting findings into proactive roadmap enhancements. 3. Profitable Growth & Commercial Value Realization Deliver on portfolio P&L targets by scaling annual recurring subscription revenues, factory-fit telematics attach rates, customer contract renewals, and gross margins. Build robust business cases and capital allocation justifications for digital product investments, balancing rapid commercial payback with long-term platform scalability. Formulate comprehensive pricing strategies, value-based bundling structures, and market positioning across new and existing digital offerings to optimize customer lifetime value and drive subscription adoption. 4. Sales & Commercial Enablement Track and optimize core commercial metrics: annual recurring subscription growth, telematics attach rates, renewal rates, churn, and gross margins.Equip commercial sales teams with consultative value-selling toolkits: Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) models, fleet ROI calculators, and competitive battle cards.Serve as the executive digital subject matter expert in key enterprise fleet pursuits, and Dealer/customer advisory councilsLead sales training programs that transition commercial teams from selling standalone refrigeration equipment to delivering integrated Fleet intelligence solutions. 5. Dealer Enablement Partner closely with Thermo King dealer principals, service managers, and technicians to drive digital adoption across dealer service bays and field operations. Institutionalize customer and dealer feedback loops through structured advisory councils, fleet site visits, and operational business reviews. Develop a keen understanding of their underlying needs and determine optimal strategies to make them more successful. Investigate, identify, and systematically eliminate operational and technical roadblocks impeding implementation speed; lead active change management programs across the dealer network to commercialize both current/existing offerings and newly launched solutions. 6. Product Growth Team (PGT) Leadership & Governance Lead the Digital Product Growth Team (PGT) utilizing standard work routines, visual management frameworks, and executive KPI scorecards. Drive cross-functional execution across software engineering, controls hardware, cybersecurity, field service, marketing, operations, and finance. Coordinate with the marketing and sales teams to develop and implement a marketing strategy and plan (including targeted programs and promotions) for each new or updated product/service or extension.

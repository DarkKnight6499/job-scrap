Job Purpose The Manager, Financial Planning & Analysis – Anaplan is responsible for product design, strategy, roadmap, development, maintenance and enhancement of Anaplan business applications. Partnering with the AVP, Financial Planning & Analysis, this role works closely with Actuarial, ALM, Controllership, Treasury, JET, Investment Accounting, Risk, PPM and Institutional teams. This role leads the forecast and planning technology architecture initiatives, with a focus to evolve and strengthen planning processes and capabilities. Essential Responsibilities Manages and supports analysts through sprint planning, prioritization, and day-to-day execution. Provides clear direction, acceptance criteria, and feature requirements to the analyst. Coordinates development timelines, dependencies, and release schedules. Monitors progress and review deliverables to ensure alignment with product goals and user expectations. Mentors and guides team members while fostering accountability and collaboration. Defines the product vision and strategy aligned with the business’ goals. Gathers and prioritizes stakeholder and business requirements. Creates and maintains a product roadmap and enhancement backlog. Manages product development timelines and releases. Supports integration strategies between Anaplan and enterprise systems such as Oracle. Monitors platform adoption, user support, training, and continuous improvement efforts. Supports forecast process, including partnership with other departments involved in the forecast and implementation of new capabilities. Maintains and enhances corporate-level modeling (debt, shares, shareholder distributions, etc.). Manages the enterprise business plan consolidation model and tools, including model documentation, testing, quarterly maintenance and rollover processes as well as model enhancements. Seeks out and develops additional Anaplan capabilities supporting downstream processes including actuarial and business process with a goal to enhance overall timeliness and controls. Supports growth opportunities and facilitates cross-business unit initiatives impacting the forecast and planning architecture. Partners with other members of FP&A and business partners to further automate management reporting and analytics capabilities. Perform ad-hoc research and analysis. Other Duties Supports various special projects as required. Performs other duties and/or projects as assigned. Knowledge, Skills and Abilities Experience or knowledge with designing, developing, and implementing applications using Anaplan framework. Experience or knowledge with designing, developing, and implementing end user solutions using PowerBI. Experience with financial statement analysis and an understanding of corporate finance concepts and processes. Experience managing or demonstrated aptitude to manage a team, preferably in finance. Deep analytical and modeling skills with broad relevant experience. Ability to effectively navigate and drive projects in a complex corporate environment. Comfortable presenting to and interacting at all levels of the organization. Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Strong project management skills and ability to effectively manage multiple concurrent projects. Ability to build collaborative relationships with stakeholders from various backgrounds and influence consistently without authority. Strong PC skills including advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint. Qualifications Bachelor's Degree required. 5+ years Anaplan experience required. 7+ years of Corporate Finance experience preferred. 5+ years PowerBI experience or other visualization tools preferred. 1+ years of management experience preferred. Insurance/financial services industry experience preferred. Anaplan Model Builder certification required. Anaplan Model Builder certification preferred.

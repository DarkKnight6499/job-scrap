Overview This is a hybrid role with the expectation that time working will regularly take place inside and outside of a company office. With more than 40 years of experience supporting the innovation economy, our teams work closely with clients across technology, life sciences, healthcare, venture capital, private equity, and other innovation-driven sectors, providing specialized banking solutions, industry insights, and meaningful connections that help businesses scale and thrive. Responsibilities & Qualifications As a Treasury Management Sales Advisor in our Innovation Banking group, you will play an integral role in our clients’ growth journey. You are an experienced relationship builder with a customer-focused approach to sales. With a deep understanding of your clients’ business models and their current and emerging needs, you will be a trusted advisor providing timely, strategic, and scalable payments solutions. Internally, you will collaborate with cross-functional teams to enhance each client’s experience and best position our multifaceted value proposition. In this role you will: • Sell the full suite of FCB's payments products and services with a focus on technology forward payment solutions. • Assess your customer needs and recommend effective strategies; negotiate pricing to optimize profitability; and provide market-competitive solutions for clients. • Review client portfolios to determine gaps in cash management products or opportunities to expand existing relationships. Be recognized as trusted advisor in payments products and services, including having your finger on the pulse of emerging technologies. • Establish and maintain a high level of client satisfaction across your assigned portfolio. • Develop sales strategies to meet sales goals and product penetration goals. • Partner with senior Managers in the Commercial Bank to achieve cross sell goals. Qualifications: Bachelor's Degree and 2 years' experience in Treasury Sales or HS diploma/GED with 6 years' expereince in Treasury Sales. Preferred Qualifications: Like people! A history of developing and cultivating relationships with clients of all sizes. Our team focuses on solving clients’ challenges. Creating strategic and positive client experiences that help us build long-term customers. Curiosity and self-motivation are two key characteristics of our most successful salespeople. Related experience in the payments/FinTech industry, with either a product or sales focus. Strong aptitude for payment and technology solutions. Skilled in crafting effective client facing and internal communications. The ability to effectively communicate your ideas and thoughts via written or verbal channels. Strong organizational and time management skills are required. Skilled in Word, Excel, and PowerPoint enabling you to download & sort information, look for analytic value and present ideas in a simple but effective format.

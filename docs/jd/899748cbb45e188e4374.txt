Company Overview:
Chevy Chase Trust is a privately owned investment management firm headquartered in the Washington, D.C. metropolitan area, serving individuals, families, endowments and institutions since 1999. With more than $46 billion in assets under management and a team of over 130 professionals, the firm specializes in investment management, financial and estate planning and fiduciary services.
The firm is known for its Global Thematic Equity Investment approach which focuses on long term secular trends stemming from economic, demographic and technological change and their impact on industries and companies. In addition, our in-house team provides highly customized financial and estate planning and trust services to address the complex needs of each client.


Position Summary:
The Associate Compliance Officer provides support to the Compliance Department in the administration of compliance functions for Chevy Chase Trust & ASB Capital Management. This position requires a high degree of self-initiative, critical thinking ability, written and verbal communication skills, and ownership of the results. The Associate Compliance Officer demonstrates an ability to develop competencies and knowledge regarding the compliance systems and associated legal requirements, specifically the Investment Advisers Act and fiduciary laws and regulations for trust companies. The Associate Compliance Officer applies this knowledge to the operations and systems of Chevy Chase Trust and ASB Capital Management.

Duties & Responsibilities of the Associate Compliance Officer: The Associate Compliance Officer supports the Compliance Department in its oversight of each firm’s corporate compliance program. Specifically, the Associate Compliance Officer is primarily responsible for: Day-to-day administration of pre-clearances under the firm’s Code of Ethics. Day-to-day administration of the firm’s investment guidelines monitoring systems. Performance of periodic and ad hoc testing. Implementing and monitoring policies and procedures for each firm’s compliance with securities and fiduciary laws and regulations. This includes conducting follow-up with the other staff to ensure compliance with the firm’s policies and participating in the daily administration of compliance throughout the firm. These duties also include day-to-day monitoring activities for each firm’s equity, fixed income, and real estate investment management functions as well as oversight of various trust services, anti-money laundering procedures, privacy and cybersecurity monitoring, and ERISA compliance. Assisting in the delivery of responses to regulatory exams and external and internal audits. Assisting in the annual compliance program review for each firm. Assisting with regulatory filings for each firm. Assisting with the facilitation of continuing education, a requirement to be fulfilled by the individual investment adviser representatives of each firm. Assisting with the production of meeting materials and reporting to various Committees of each firm. Monitoring and, as necessary, coordinating compliance activities of various departments to remain abreast of compliance matters and identify trends. Identifying potential areas of compliance vulnerability and risk. Serving as backup for cash and security guideline administration in the trade order management system.

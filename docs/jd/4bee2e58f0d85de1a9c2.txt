Role Overview: Join the in-business portfolio management team within the Credit Financing business. This role is designed for a professional with a strategic mindset towards data. You will be a central stakeholder in the portfolio's data lifecycle, responsible for supporting data integrity, data strategy, and execution of data-centric projects. Work Profile Specifics: The role will support the strategy, maintenance, execution, governance of data across the portfolio. Key responsibilities include: Portfolio Data Strategy & Integrity: Act as the primary steward across data supporting the portfolio. Design and implement strategies for data modeling, storage, and hygiene across our suite of applications Data Maintenance and Quality: Spearhead the assimilation of complex financial data (e.g., Borrowing Bases) into internal systems. Develop, support and manage processes for seamless data ingestion, validation, and reporting Client & Stakeholder Data Engagement: Proactively interface with clients, originations, and underwriting teams to resolve data quality issues, ensuring the accuracy and integrity of information from its source Risk Management through Data: Leverage portfolio data to proactively assist in managing Credit Risk. Support broader portfolio management team in monitoring key credit quality indicators and initiate remediation actions based on data-driven insights Automate & Optimize Reporting: Support automation of portfolio management reporting, including borrowing base monitoring, valuation, and covenant checks. Identify and implement opportunities to enhance control frameworks and improve operational efficiency through technology Strategic Technology Initiatives: Collaborate with cross-functional technology partners to design, develop, and execute strategic initiatives that enhance data infrastructure and support long-term business objectives Qualifications: 5-8 years of experience in a related role Knowledge of Credit Financing and Securitizations is preferred but not required Proficiency in data scalability and integration, including ingestion, validation, and reporting workflows Aptitude in data management, with ability to track, action, and drive process as applicable Awareness of regulatory frameworks, including but not limited to; Basel Capital Calculations and reporting requirements Familiarity with technology-driven process improvements, task automation, and control optimization Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with a track record of driving efficiency and accuracy Strong written and oral communication skills, as well as demonstrable ability to comprehend legal documentation (including loan facility agreements) Key Competencies: A passion for data integrity, governance, and strategy Analytical Rigor.

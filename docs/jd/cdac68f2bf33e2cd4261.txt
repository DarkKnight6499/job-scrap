Operations Analyst - Treasury Management
SEI Investments
Location: USA - PA - Oaks
Link: https://seic.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/SEI_Global_Services/job/USA---PA---Oaks/Operations-Analyst---Treasury-Management_R0038835

The Treasury Management Analyst is responsible for performing various cash, disbursement and reconciliation activities for the SEI Wealth & US Operations Trust Platforms within SPTC. This individual will be a primary contact for internal and external clients, supporting and assisting in exception & problem resolution with regards to demand deposit accounts, money movement, settlement transactions and payment system issues. The analyst will be required to be a self-motivated, goal-oriented individual with the ability to manage multiple tasks in a time sensitive delivery schedule. What you’ll do: Perform various banking activities such as managing wire transfers, reviewing bank activity reports and resolve any discrepancies that may occur along the way. Reviewing, analyzing, and entering client information and transactions into multiple banking and platformsystems General client account maintenance Respond to client inquiries in a timely, accurate and professional manner. Participate in various projects and team initiatives Perform research & analysis of DDA exceptions Implement solutions as assigned. Interact effectively with multiple operational processing teams. Maintaining the highest level of confidentiality, quality and privacy. Manage a time-sensitive delivery schedule as defined in the client specific SLAs. Processing error-free transactions such as (but not limited to) domestic and international wires, client settlements, IT’s, adjustments and returns accurately and in a timely manner Perform accounting functions within the various financial systems, bank reconciliations, audit inquiries, and backup of other core treasury functions, e.g. cash position and electronic payments, as assigned. Other duties as assigned What we need from you: Undergraduate degree and/or equivalent work experience required. Preference in: Accounting, Finance or Business related field. Knowledge of Microsoft Office products including Word, Excel, Access and Outlook. What we would like from you: The ability to demonstrate effective communication skills to clients, service partners, and internal teams both in written and verbal communication. Effectively coordinate, manage and complete multiple duties and assignments concurrently and within acceptable timelines. Provide excellent customer service to both our internal and external clients and service partners, responding timely and professionally to client inquiries as well as handle problems Must be able and willing to work effectively independently, as well as in a team, in an open floor environment Someone who will embody our SEI Values of courage, integrity, collaboration, inclusion, connection and fun.

We’re actively seeking a talented Project Portfolio Management Consultant to join our Portfolio and Program Management team in Newport Beach, CA. This role is hybrid. We believe in empowering our employees to get work done both in and out of the office. As a Project Portfolio Management Consultant, you’ll move Pacific Life — and your career — forward by leading portfolio management practices that ensure the right initiatives are prioritized, monitored , and executed in alignment with strategic objectives , business value, and capacity. This P5 consultant role is recognized as an expert-level individual contributor within and beyond the function. You will fill a role within the Workforce Division, partnering with project, program, portfolio, product, finance, strategy, business, and technology leaders to strengthen portfolio governance, forecasting, roadmap visibility, and decision-making discipline. How you’ll help move us forward: Lead portfolio management rhythms and governance forums that help senior leaders prioritize investments, track health, and make timely trade-off decisions. Translate divisional and functional strategies into integrated portfolio roadmaps that connect demand, funding, capacity, dependencies, milestones, risks, and expected outcomes. Support the end-to-end portfolio lifecycle, including planning, initiation, prioritization, resource and capacity inputs, execution monitoring, benefits realization, and value tracking. Translate Workforce Division strategies into actionable portfolio initiatives that advance growth, innovation, operational excellence, and measurable business outcomes. Maintain awareness of business, market, and operational trends to help identify portfolio opportunities, risks, and trade-offs. Partner with business, finance, strategy, technology, product, program, and project leaders to ensure project and program work is prioritized, monitored , and executed in support of strategic objectives and business value. Develop and continuously improve portfolio management standards, practices, templates, reporting, and operating routines to advance PPM maturity across the organization. Analyze portfolio performance, capacity, financials, risks, issues, and dependencies to surface insights, options, and recommendations for decision-makers . Drive transparency across portfolio health, value realization, and delivery confidence through concise executive-ready communications, dashboards, summaries, and decision materials. Act as a trusted advisor and thought partner to stakeholders, applying broad business perspective and portfolio expertise to solve complex, cross-functional challenges. Coach and guide colleagues with less experience, helping teams adopt consistent practices while adapting approaches to business context and delivery needs. The experience you bring: 10+ years of experience in portfolio management, program management, project management, strategic planning, governance, or related business transformation roles. 4-year degree or equivalent experience. Demonstrated expertise in managing or enabling complex portfolios of initiatives across business and technology teams in a matrixed environment. Proven ability to interpret internal or external business issues and recommend process , product, service, or operating model improvements with broad business impact. Strong knowledge of portfolio and project management methodologies, techniques, and best practices, with the ability to apply them pragmatically across business contexts. Strong executive communication, facilitation, prioritization, and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence decisions without direct authority. Experience with portfolio governance, integrated roadmapping , demand intake, prioritization, capacity planning, dependency management, financial stewardship, risk management, and value tracking. Strong analytical skills, including the ability to synthesize quantitative and qualitative inputs into clear insights, options, and recommendations. Experience using portfolio, project, and collaboration tools such as Planview, Smartsheet, Azure DevOps, Jira, Confluence, Microsoft Project, SharePoint, Teams, OneNote, PowerPoint, or similar platforms. Experience in regulated industries such as insurance, financial services, healthcare, or similar environments preferred. What makes you stand out: Project Management Professional (PMP)®, Program Management Professional ( PgMP )®, Portfolio Management Professional ( PfMP )®, or PMI Agile Certified Practitioner (PMI- ACP)® . SAFe ® Agilist (SA), Lean Portfolio Management, Certified ScrumMaster (CSM), Certified Scrum Professional (CSP), or similar Agile / Lean certification. Experience in implementation and roll out of Portfolio Management and Project Portfolio Management tools such as Planview, Clarity or similar platforms. Experience implementing or maturing portfolio governance, cross-portfolio planning, value management, and executive portfolio reporting practices. Consulting experience or demonstrated ability to advise leaders, shape ambiguous problems, and build coalitions around pragmatic solutions. Demonstrated ability to build a compelling narrative, facilitate working sessions, influence senior leaders, and help drive decisions to conclusion in ambiguous environments. Advanced executive storytelling, data visualization, and decision support capabilities. What Success Looks Like: Portfolio decisions are grounded in clear strategy, capacity, value, risk, dependency, and funding insights. Roadmaps and governance routines remain current, useful, and actionable — not theoretical. Leaders have timely visibility into portfolio health, trade-offs, and decisions needed to maintain momentum. Teams use consistent portfolio practices while adapting execution approaches to business context . Risks, issues, and dependencies are surfaced early and managed through transparent ownership and escalation paths.

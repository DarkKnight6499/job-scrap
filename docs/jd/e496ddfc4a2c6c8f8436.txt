What you will be doing: As a member of Vertex’s and Corporate Responsibility (CR) team, the co-op will help research and advance key projects to support Vertex’s Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) priorities. The work will span multiple subject areas, including but not limited to environmental sustainability, access to medicines, and mandatory and voluntary external reporting. The wide scope of the role will allow the co-op to gain experience and exposure to a variety of functional areas across the business Key Responsibilities: Support data collection for Vertex’s annual Corporate Responsibility Report. Support mandatory external disclosures including country-specific disclosures. Assist with the development and entry of Vertex’s annual CDP submission. Track current and emerging global ESG regulations, standards, and reporting frameworks – including the coordination of submission requirements and due dates across Vertex global entities. Benchmark industry including positions taken on select topics, and providing recommendations when appropriate. Prepare presentation decks to support key meetings. What you will need to succeed: Graduate or undergraduate students pursuing a degree in sustainability, business, government or communications OR with demonstrated interest in health/medicine/public policy. Excellent research and data analysis skills. Organized, efficient, clear communication and writing style. Creative. Demonstrated ability to collaborate and work with a variety of colleague. Project management experience to support various workstreams is a plus. Experience with process documentation is a plus. Legal authorization to work in the United States, now and in the future.

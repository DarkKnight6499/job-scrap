Department Profile: Executive Financial Services (“EFS”) supports transactions involving restricted or control stock, often involving Rule 144 Affiliates and Corporate Insiders. These transactions include Rule 10b5-1 Trading Plans, sales under Rule 144. EFS conducts due diligence with Issuer counsel prior to the transaction, negotiates and implements 10b5-1 contracts (including trade execution pursuant to the plan) and facilitates SEC filings, clearing and settlement of restricted trades. Position Summary: Located in our US offices (Sandy, Utah; Purchase, NY; Alpharetta, GA, or Tempe, AZ) and reporting to the Executive Services Team Lead, the Executive Services Specialist fulfills the role of being the face of Shareworks by Morgan Stanley to our Executive and C-Suite level customers. You will interact daily with Shareworks’ executive clients, building and maintaining strong relationships. Shareworks has been successful in building trust and respect with our customers through dedicated relationship management, supported by delivering our promise of “Everyday Excellence.” We are looking for enthusiastic, outgoing individuals who share our passion for customer service excellence.

What You'll Do: The Associate, Financial Risk Oversight provides critical support to department leadership by monitoring OCC’s adherence to its risk tolerances, developing risk reporting, monitoring and challenging the effectiveness of OCC’s financial risk management framework, and supporting default management and recovery and wind-down oversight activities. The role will involve close collaboration across teams within the broader Corporate Risk Management function as well as other key OCC stakeholders, including Compliance and first-line Financial Risk Management. Primary Duties and Responsibilities: To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each primary duty satisfactorily. Assist the department in implementing risk management oversight activities to assess, monitor, and report on the adequacy and effectiveness of OCC’s management of financial risk Assist with enterprise risk assessments (ERAs) on first-line Financial Risk Management in coordination with the Operational Risk Management (ORM) team, pursuant to OCC’s enterprise-wide Risk Management Framework (RMF) Assist ORMC with maintenance of risk inventories, taxonomies, and other elements related to financial risk management and compliance activities Develop and leverage an understanding of OCC’s enterprise risk and assist business units with governance issues and concerns Verify, analyze, create, and monitor key performance, risk, and control indicators to support management and board-level reporting across business units Identify, develop, and implement tools or methods to track and monitor financial risk Track and communicate identified audit and exam issues as well as business area self-identified issues and findings to other Corporate Risk Management teams Understand OCC plans for mitigation of default and non-default losses, and assist in planning and testing of the tools that may be used during those loss events Understand OCC quantitative and qualitative reporting and assist in reviewing data to make sure it is accurate and consistent Assist with developing and maintaining department-level policies, procedures, job aids, and controls Support management with special projects and other duties assigned Supervisory Responsibilities None Qualifications: The requirements listed are representative of the knowledge, skill, and/or ability required. Founded in 1973, OCC is dedicated to promoting stability and market integrity by delivering clearing and settlement services for options, futures and securities lending transactions. As a Systemically Important Financial Market Utility (SIFMU), OCC operates under the jurisdiction of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC), and the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System. OCC has more than 100 clearing members and provides central counterparty (CCP) clearing and settlement services to 23 exchanges and trading platforms.

The Health, Safety & Wellbeing Associate Director – Enterprise and Real Estate Business , will serve as a deputy and reporting to the US Corporate Head of Health, Safety and Well-being. This role will serve as an individual contributor , in a strategic leadership role responsible for advancing Health, Safety, and Wellbeing (HSW) performance across real estate and enterprise accounts and portfolios within the US and Canada. This position as a strategic and operational leader, you will shape and deliver program that protect people, ensure regulatory compliance, and embed a harm‑free culture across all areas of the business. You will partner closely with senior leaders, engineering, project managers, cost leaders, procurement, learning and development, operations, and site EHS teams to ensure consistent application of policies, effective risk reduction, and continuous improvement worldwide. This position focuses on supporting business transformation, operational excellence, risk governance, leadership engagement, and the development of scalable HSW programs that drive measurable business outcomes. This role requires a strong construction safety consulting background, excellent leadership capabilities, and experience managing client relationships, compliance programs, audits, and health and safety advisory services throughout the project lifecycle. The successful candidate will serve as a trusted advisor to executive stakeholders, lead complex HSW initiatives across multiple business units, and support business growth through client engagement, thought leadership, and strategic consulting services. Responsibilities This position follows a hybrid work model, requiring employees to work both remotely and from the office.

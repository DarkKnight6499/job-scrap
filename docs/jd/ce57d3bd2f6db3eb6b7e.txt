About the Team DoorDash Labs is an independent team within DoorDash. We explore robotics and automation to transform last mile logistics in the long term. If you want to work on commercializing autonomy and robotics in a service used by millions of people, then we want to talk to you! About the Role We’re looking for an Inventory Procurement Associate to support the sourcing, purchasing, and tracking of components critical to our robotics, autonomy, and field systems. This role is responsible for ensuring inventory accuracy, managing supplier relationships, and scaling procurement and supply chain workflows across hardware programs. You’ll work closely with engineering, sourcing, and operations teams to ensure timely and accurate delivery of components—from fast prototyping through production and early-stage deployment. This is a hands-on, operationally focused role within a fast-moving environment, with responsibilities spanning procurement, inventory management, and warehouse operations. The Inventory Procurement Associate will report to the Supply Chain team in our Autonomy Operations organization. You’re excited about this opportunity because you will… Source, negotiate, and purchase parts, tools, and materials for R&D, production, and field operations Build and maintain relationships with suppliers to ensure quality, competitive pricing, and on-time delivery Track and manage inventory across labs, warehouses, and deployment sites Receive, label, store, and issue materials used in R&D, production, and field builds Plan and kit materials for prototype, NPI, and production builds Submit and monitor purchase requests for a wide range of hardware components (mechanical, electrical, embedded systems, PCBAs, cables, packaging, consumables, etc.) Maintain organized records of POs, receipts, invoices, and part usage Conduct regular cycle counts and resolve inventory discrepancies Ensure proper storage practices for sensitive and high-value components Coordinate domestic and international shipments, ensuring compliance with customs, tariffs, and export regulations Support onboarding of new suppliers and ensure compliance with documentation requirements Help maintain procurement system workflows (Coupa, SAP, NetSuite, or similar) and resolve basic system-related issues Identify opportunities to streamline procurement, inventory, and warehouse processes Maintain and upkeep warehouse storage areas, implementing best practices in material handling, storage, and documentation Collaborate with cross-functional partners to forecast material needs and prevent shortages We’re excited about you because… You have 3–5 years of experience in procurement, inventory management, production, or hardware operations (preferably within robotics, aerospace, automotive, or manufacturing environments) You have experience with ERP and procurement systems (Coupa, SAP, NetSuite, or similar) and ideally MRP systems for production You are familiar with hardware development cycles, BOMs, and have supported engineering or R&D teams You have strong vendor management and

Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism SAP Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a SAP Business Process & IT Controls Senior Associate you will support client needs by analyzing SAP compliance and security requirements, helping implement access and control solutions, and contributing to the delivery of consulting services that help clients optimize operational efficiency and maintain alignment with regulatory expectations within our Business Application Consulting practice. As a Senior Associate you will build meaningful client connections, navigate complex situations, deepen your technical skills, and help guide junior team members while developing a broader understanding of how your work supports project objectives and business priorities. In this role at PwC, you will apply critical thinking to break down control issues, interpret data and feedback to inform recommendations, use a range of tools and methods to address client challenges, and uphold professional and technical standards as you support SAP control and security workstreams. Responsibilities - Analyzing SAP business process and IT control requirements to identify compliance gaps and security risks - Supporting the design and implementation of SAP governance, risk, and control solutions across client environments - Reviewing process documentation, control narratives, and audit evidence to validate control operation and support remediation efforts - Applying SAP GRC and security concepts to help clients strengthen access management, segregation of duties, and control monitoring - Interpreting regulatory and corporate governance requirements to shape control design and compliance activities - Developing clear work products, status updates, and client-facing materials for assigned workstreams - Collaborating with team members to break down complex issues, identify root causes, and recommend practical actions - Mentoring junior team members by reviewing tasks, sharing technical guidance, and supporting their development What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 3 years of experience of experience delivering SAP compliance, security, and governance solutions, including auditing, advisory, and implementation engagements What Sets You Apart -

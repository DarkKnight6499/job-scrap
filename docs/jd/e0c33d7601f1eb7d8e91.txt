Job Posting Title: Disney Consumer Products 3D Modeler Intern, Spring 2027 Req ID: 10160429 Job Description: About the Role & Program Disney Consumer Products (DCP) brings the magic of Disney’s beloved stories, franchises, and characters to families and fans worldwide. From toys to apparel, books to games, our global organization has a profound impact on The Walt Disney Company and the global markets in which we operate! With a portfolio of renowned franchises, including Disney, Pixar, Marvel, 20th Century Studios, National Geographic, and Star Wars, we are a worldwide industry and category leader, delivering innovative products that entertain and inspire! As a 3D Modeling Intern, you will be responsible for producing character artwork for three-dimensional product applications and working on projects at all levels of complexity with the team. You'll work under general supervision as part of a team that meets expectations under pressure while maintaining high quality standards. If selected, you'll join a dedicated, collaborative, hard-working group of 3D modelers who bring characters and stories to life in 3D. This exciting internship is a full-time, six-month commitment in the Orlando, FL area from January 2027 to June 2027. What You Will Do · Development of 3D character models for the development of consumer products across multiple properties and product types · Preparing computer files accurately for appropriate manufacturing processes in accordance with client/vendor specifications · Archiving all project files within the standards established by the 3D Team · Operating and maintaining 3D printers for rapid prototyping and high-resolution 3D outputs for manufacturing · Contribute to new product ideas and participate in brainstorming sessions · Responsible for maintaining high character art standards Please Apply If You Have/Are · Demonstrate high-quality 3D modeling skills in ZBrush · Possess strong creative digital literacy in Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop · Understand the principles of cartooning and cartoon animation · Have a baseline knowledge of Character Art · Previous exposure to multiple mediums (sculpting, painting, and traditional/digital drawing) · Proven Strong concepting skills · Passion for Disney Characters and Storytelling and how they translate to products · Fresh and inventive creative problem solver · Understanding and awareness of marketplace trends · Able to present creative concepts in both formal and informal settings · A Portfolio submission that clearly demonstrates the ability to translate 2D concepts to 3D It Would Also Be Great If You Have · Skill in Cinema 4D · Proficiency in hard surface modeling in one of the following (Maya, SolidWorks, Rhino3D) Education Junior or Senior year preferred Pursuing a bachelor’s degree in Animation, Fine Arts, Graphic Design, or a related art field Required Materials · A portfolio review is required for this position .

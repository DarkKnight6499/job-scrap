The role requires strong writing skills, broad equity market expertise, the ability to create and help deliver research insights on behalf of the department and the team to enhance the firm's research offering and support institutional client relationships. Key Responsibilities Partner closely with the Head of US Product Management and senior Research management to execute strategic content initiatives across the US Equity Research platform. Lead the daily equity research product management process, overseeing the production and distribution of content supporting the Equity Morning Call and broader Sales & Trading organization. Utilize Bloomberg, Macro/Fixed Income teams and alternative data sets to identify emerging investment themes and inform content development. Partner with the analysts and Research leadership to produce differentiated, timely, cross-sector and thematic content, market outlook, screens and top ideas pieces to increase the visibility, relevance, and client impact. Collaborate with Research, Sales, Trading, Compliance and Data teams on publication, distribution and broader global research initiatives Support and enhance departmental infrastructure including publishing workflows, research publication metrics, analyst performance dashboards and Top Picks performance. Mentor junior team members and contribute to the ongoing development of the Research Product Management function. Qualifications Bachelor's degree required; advanced degree and/or CFA designation preferred. 7+ years of experience in Equity Research, Strategy, Research Product Management, Financial Journalism, or a related function within financial services. Exceptional writing and editing skills , with an ability to identify and distill complex investment and market topics into concise, compelling content. Strong understanding of equity research workflows, compliance requirements, content publishing processes, and institutional client engagement. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with experience creating client-facing and executive-level presentations. Proven passion for financial markets, equities, and investment research. Advanced proficiency with Bloomberg, Excel, Claude/CoPilot. Unwavering attention to detail and ability to operate effectively in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment.

About the Role: Grade Level (for internal use): 12 Company Name: IHS Global Inc. Job Title: Lead Research Analyst Location: 55 Water Street, New York, NY 10041 (telecommuting permitted anywhere within the United States) Job Duties: IHS Global, Inc. seeks a Lead Research Analyst responsible for the production of commodity market research analysis, including the long-term quarterly Ammonia Outlook report. Present S&P Global Commodity Insights analysis to the industry, including attending conferences and client meetings to discuss market developments and forecasts for the ammonia supply chain. Participate in webinars to present analysis on commodity markets to clients. Create and maintain models and databases used for ammonia intelligence. Build the data and knowledge bank for the complete supply chain of ammonia. Partner with the wider team to contribute to other reports/initiatives: short-term ammonia report, low-carbon short-term report, low-carbon long-term report. Provide training and knowledge transfer on ammonia for other team members. Job Requirements: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Statistics or a physical sciences field related to the industry vertical (such as Chemistry or Geography) plus 4 years of experience in the job offered or related occupation. 4 years of experience must include: Data analysis and market research relating to the Ammonia supply chain, including downstream markets; collaborating with cross-functional teams (natural gas, hydrogen, fertilizers, petrochemical applications of Ammonia) and presenting findings to senior management or clients; presenting research findings or case studies at industry conferences, showcasing expertise and contributing to thought leadership; delivering presentations to clients, effectively communicating insights and recommendations tailored to their needs including workshops and seminars; and preparing data visualizations and writing reports. Position requires 10% domestic and 5% international travel to attend client meetings and industry conferences. The anticipated base salary range for this position is $126,152 to $175,650 . Final base salary for this role will be based on the individual’s geographic location, as well as experience level, skill set, training, licenses & certifications. In addition to base compensation, this role is eligible for an annual incentive plan. IHS Global Inc. is part of S&P Global Inc., and this role is eligible to receive additional S&P Global benefits.

Who we are looking for State Street Markets (“SSM”) is looking for an Officer to join the SSM Model Risk team in Boston. The SSM Model Risk team provides solutions to SSM business units, including Financing Solutions, FX Sales & Trading, Portfolio Solutions, GlobalLink, State Street Associates and Market Surveillance, allowing business units to address Model Risk requirements from internal oversight functions and external regulators. Work arrangement This role can be performed in a hybrid model, where you can balance work from home and office to match your needs and role requirements. Why this role is important to us The team you will be joining is a part of State Street Markets. As a leading provider of trading and lending solutions to the world’s institutional investors, we deliver the industry’s most innovative platforms, financing and portfolio solutions. Our capabilities are backed by proprietary, high-value research, insights and indicators that power clients’ investment decisions, accelerate performance and help investors stay ahead of shifting markets. Join us if making your mark in the capital markets industry from day one is a challenge you are up for. What you will be responsible for As Markets Quantitative Analyst, Officer, you will: Support model owners in the execution of engagement-specific statistical and financial modeling and analyses, and in the preparation of final model deliverables. Support the Markets Risk and Capital Optimization team on capital- and liquidity-related quantitative analyses. Develop customized solutions to perform independent model testing and document the development methodology and implementation process. Establish appropriate benchmarking and back-testing techniques based on the model class, purpose, horizon, hedging implications, and other relevant factors. Identify key model risks by stress-testing model inputs and parameters. Demonstrate model stability across alternative specifications and input ranges, including extreme values and boundaries of model effectiveness. Collaborate with model owners to design and implement ongoing monitoring plans, including performance metrics, thresholds and escalation plans. Develop first-line-of-defense documentation covering model development, implementation and ongoing monitoring. Work with model owners and developers to update models in line with internal model validation requirements. Conduct post-implementation reviews of SSM proprietary trading algorithms, including code and strategy changes, compliance with the FX Global Code, and independent testing of selected components. Communicate with quantitative professionals and developers to understand strategy changes and justify modeling assumptions and results to internal model validation and external regulators. Prepare and deliver engagement status updates to key stakeholders and manage workflows to ensure timely deliverables. What we value These skills will help you succeed in this role: Strong understanding of quantitative analysis methods in relation to financial institutions. Knowledge of financial markets, including securities lending, equities and derivatives, FX or electronic trading, is a plus. High-level understanding of capital- and liquidity-related regulations. Ability to multitask and operate in a fast-paced, deadline-oriented environment. Strong verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to explain complex concepts, facilitate discussions and resolve conflicts among stakeholders with competing interests. Education & Preferred Qualifications Graduate degree in a quantitative discipline, such as Financial Mathematics, Financial Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics or a related field. 0-3 years of experience in the model risk field preferred. Advanced programming skills in at least one supported statistical programming environment, including Python, R or MATLAB. Intermediate programming skills in VBA and other languages; Java experience is a plus.

Job Description: Job Title: Quantitative Strategist – Rates Corporate Title: Associate Location: New York, NY Overview Group Strategic Analytics (GSA) brings together Deutsche Bank’s quantitative modelling and analytics expertise across businesses. The team develops and delivers strategic analytics, pricing, risk, and P&L capabilities used across the Bank’s trading businesses. We are looking for a Quantitative Strategist to join our Rates business in New York, working closely with Trading, Structuring, and Technology. You will develop quantitative analytics and market data capabilities supporting Fixed Income trading, with particular focus on interest rates products, curves, volatility, pricing, risk, and P&L.This is a highly quantitative, hands-on role offering exposure to the trading desk and the opportunity to combine financial modelling, software development, and markets knowledge to solve real-time business problems.

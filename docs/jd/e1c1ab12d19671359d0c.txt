Your Team, Your Impact The DCE Connectivity Hardware organization is one of Marvell's most strategically critical engineering groups, responsible for the full silicon lifecycle of the world's highest-performance electrical and optical connectivity products. The team designs and delivers advanced retimers, gearboxes, Active Electrical Cable (AEC) solutions, PCIe switches, Copper PHY, coherent-lite DSP, and Co-Packaged Optics technologies — products that sit at the physical layer of every major AI cluster, cloud data center, and carrier network on the planet. The organization spans a globally distributed team of world-class engineers across North America, Europe, and Asia, working at the leading edge of CMOS process technology — from 5nm through 3nm FinFET and into 2nm GAAFET. With deep expertise across digital design, design verification, physical design, DFT, and silicon validation, the group operates with the rigor and velocity required to deliver first-silicon success on the industry's most complex, high-speed ASICs. As AI-driven bandwidth demand continues to reshape the data infrastructure landscape, this team is defining what comes next — pushing the boundaries of 200G/lane SerDes, Co-Packaged Optics, and uLED integration to architect the connectivity fabric of the future. What You Can Expect Marvell is seeking a visionary Associate Vice President (AVP), Engineering to lead a critical engineering function within the DCE Connectivity Hardware organization. This role sits at the intersection of advanced CMOS digital design, high-speed SerDes, silicon validation, and multi-generational product execution — spanning 200G/lane PAM4 retimers and gearboxes, PCIe Gen7 and beyond, Active Electrical Cable (AEC) solutions, Copper PHY, coherent-lite DSP, and emerging Co-Packaged Optics and uLED technologies. You are not simply managing an engineering team — you are shaping the execution engine that delivers industry-leading connectivity silicon from architecture through tape-out and into high-volume production on the world's most advanced process nodes, including 3nm FinFET and 2nm GAAFET. This role reports directly to the VP of Engineering, DCE Connectivity, and plays a central role in defining execution strategy, technology roadmap, and cross-site engineering delivery for products that set the pace of the industry.

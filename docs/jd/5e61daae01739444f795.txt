Overview The AVP is responsible for performing the Operational Risk (OR) Framework management. The purpose of this job specifically includes managing the OR framework, policy and procedure, while also analyzing and consolidating operational risk data from teams and generating Branch-wide operational risk management reports to the Head Office. Other responsibilities include but not limited to supporting the risk management governance of the department including: facilitate monthly ORC meetings, Q&A the meeting material and supporting documents; facilitate regulatory and audit meetings and affairs.

The Difference You Make: The Corporate & Investment Banking Structured Trade Finance Associate develops, analyzes, and executes specialized financing solutions for international trade and commodity transactions with key risk mitigants. Key duties include structuring deals with instruments (prepayments, inventory finance), modeling cash flows, negotiating with banks, and managing risk through insurance or collateral. Deal Structuring: Design and implement tailored financing solutions (e.g., prepayments, inventory finance, borrowing base facilities). Risk Mitigation: Analyze and mitigate risks using instruments like insurance guarantees and letters of credit. Financial Modeling & Analysis: Create complex working capital models, forecast trends, and analyze collateral (inventory, receivables). Execution & Documentation: Negotiate with lenders/counterparties and prepare legal documentation.

What the day will look like Aon’s Life, Health & Benefits Strategy Consulting team, part of Aon Advisory, is hiring an Experienced Strategy Associate. We work with many of the most prestigious companies across the global life, health and benefits, property & casualty, insurtech, and (re)insurance markets. As a Strategy Associate, you will be a key member of high‑impact project teams, helping clients address their most important strategic questions.

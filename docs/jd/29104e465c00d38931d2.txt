Line of Business: Product Management Job Description: Department Overview: The Product Manager, Deposits & Liquidity (US), supports the management, enhancement, and development of TD's U.S. commercial deposit and liquidity product portfolio within Global Transaction Banking Product. Working closely with Sales, Treasury Management, Operations, Servicing, Implementations, Risk, and Technology partners, the role is responsible for day-to-day product management activities, product development support, and the delivery of initiatives that enhance client experience, operational effectiveness, and business growth. The successful candidate will help ensure products remain competitive, compliant, customer focused, and aligned with evolving market and customer needs. Supports the management and ongoing enhancement of commercial deposit and liquidity solutions, including operating accounts, sweeps, liquidity pooling structures, virtual account management, escrow solutions, and related cash management capabilities, ensuring products meet client needs, risk requirements, and business objectives.

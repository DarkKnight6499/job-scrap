The Corporate Strategy & Planning team serves as TIAA's internal strategy function, comprising three core capabilities: Strategic Consulting, Competitive Intelligence, and Strategic Planning. The CS&P Associate Development Program offers early-career professionals the opportunity to build a strong foundation in internal strategy consulting while contributing to enterprise-wide strategic initiatives across all of TIAA's businesses – including retirement, insurance, wealth, and asset management/Nuveen as well as key functional areas such as marketing and technology. Associates in this 2-year program receive structured mentorship, hands-on training, and progressively increasing responsibility as they develop the skills of a strategy consulting professional, working alongside experienced team members on high-impact projects that shape TIAA's strategic direction. Key Responsibilities and Duties • Partners with business leaders across TIAA's business units and functional areas, under the guidance of senior team members, to address enterprise-wide and business-specific strategic challenges • Supports and increasingly leads components of consulting project workstreams, developing skills in timeline management, stakeholder coordination, and delivery of high-quality outputs • Learns to structure and solve complex business problems across diverse strategic topics, including growth strategy, operating model design, market positioning, and organizational effectiveness • Conducts rigorous analysis, including market sizing, competitive benchmarking, financial modeling, interview synthesizing, and scenario planning, building core analytical capabilities that support strategic recommendations • Develops comfort working through ambiguity, gaining experience with incomplete information and incorporating risk assessment into decision-making • Contributes to the development of executive-ready materials that synthesize complex analyses into clear, actionable insights, with growing exposure to senior audiences including the Executive Committee and Board of Trustees • Builds relationships with engagement managers and business partners, establishing a foundation of credibility and trust as a developing strategic resource • Participates in formal training, mentorship, and feedback cycles designed to accelerate professional growth, and contributes to continuous improvement of team capabilities and infrastructure Educational Requirements • University (Degree) Required • MBA Preferred Work Experience • 5+ Years Required;

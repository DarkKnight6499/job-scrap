Job Overview Global Network Banking (GNB) serves a select number of the world's largest and most prominent multinational corporations that demand the highest standards of relationship coverage and strategic banking partnership. The Corporate Banking Associate independently manages a portfolio of multinational clients operating in and out of the United States and serves as the primary relationship owner for those clients. The Associate is responsible for developing deep client relationships, understanding strategic priorities, and delivering the full breadth of Citi's global capabilities to help clients optimize working capital, enhance treasury efficiency, manage risk, and support growth. This role offers early ownership of client relationships and direct exposure to senior decision-makers across global organizations. Associates work alongside experienced bankers and product specialists while developing the skills required to become a trusted advisor to some of the world's leading companies. As the lead bank to many of the world's most global and sophisticated corporations, Citi provides a unique platform for Associates seeking broad exposure, accelerated development, and a long-term career in corporate banking. Responsibilities Serve as the primary relationship owner for an assigned portfolio of multinational clients, maintaining responsibility for overall client satisfaction, relationship growth, and strategic engagement. Develop a thorough understanding of each client's business model, operating footprint, treasury structure, and financial objectives to identify opportunities where Citi can create value. Deliver Citi's global capabilities to help clients improve working capital efficiency, streamline treasury operations, manage financial and operational risk, strengthen liquidity, and support cross-border growth. Coordinate the delivery of Citi's global platform by bringing together expertise across banking, markets, services, and risk management to address client priorities and challenges. Identify opportunities to deepen relationships and expand Citi's role as a strategic banking partner across the client's organization and geographic footprint. Build relationships with client treasury, finance, and corporate development teams, while gaining exposure to senior decision-makers including Treasurers, CFOs, and other executive stakeholders. Support senior Bankers on larger or more complex client relationships, financing transactions, and strategic initiatives. Conduct strategic financial and credit analyses, evaluating company filings, business plans, and financial forecasts to inform coverage and deal strategies. Exercise sound credit judgment, risk discipline, and strict adherence to regulatory compliance and internal governance policies. Contribute actively to team growth objectives and a collaborative, high-performance culture. Qualifications Minimum 3–5 years of relevant experience in Corporate Banking, Investment Banking, Treasury Sales, or related financial services. Foundational understanding of corporate banking, lending, treasury & liquidity solutions, capital markets, and credit fundamentals. Strong analytical, financial modeling, and problem-solving skills. Clear and concise written, verbal communication, and presentation skills. Proactive client-service mindset with the ability to collaborate effectively across multi-disciplinary deal and product teams. Strong organizational and time-management skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a dynamic environment. Series 7 and 63 licenses preferred (or willingness to obtain upon joining).

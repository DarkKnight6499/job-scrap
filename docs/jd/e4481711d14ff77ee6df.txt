Research Associate
Neuberger Berman
Location: New York, NY
Link: https://nb.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/NBCareers/job/New-York-NY/Research-Associate_R0012791

The Quality Equity Group creates quality-focused, long-term oriented long-only, large cap equity portfolios for institutional clients. The team’s philosophy and process has delivered long-term outperformance vs. SP500 and peers by focusing on high quality franchises with enduring sustainable competitive advantages, financially sound business models, rational valuations and high integrity management and governance. The team is seeking a research professional who will have independent coverage and work closely to support the senior research analysts and portfolio manager in the group to drive performance. Responsibilities: Follow company activities, results, and valuations to make trading recommendations around current positions. Generate new investment ideas to add performance and/or control risk. Conduct primary analysis, maintain dialogue with company management teams, industry sources, and sell-side analysts. Build and maintain in-depth fundamental models for each company; integrate those models into the team’s valuation framework and research management system. Assist the team in identifying areas of opportunity and/or risk in order to improve overall performance. Support the team in marketing efforts internally and with clients/gatekeepers. Travel as needed for site visits, competitor analysis, and investment conferences. Qualifications: 2-5+ years of experience in financial analysis required. In depth knowledge of one or two sectors/industries (the exact ones are of less importance) and exposure to international equity markets. Foreign language or work experience a major plus, as is work experience outside of financial firms/ financial analysis. Ability to articulate (verbally and in writing) a clear and concise investment recommendation. Openness to debate major issues is critical to success in this highly collaborative environment. Equally critical is the ability to move on after if/ when the team has a different point of view. Advanced degree and/or CFA designation preferred. Must obtain Series 7 & 66 licenses within 6 months of employment if currently not held. Applicants must be authorized and have the right to work in the country where the role is located without the need for current or future sponsorship.

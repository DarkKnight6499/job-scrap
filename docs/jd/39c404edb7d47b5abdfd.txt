Trust Associate Fiduciary Trust International is hiring a Trust Associate to work in our New York City office located at One Madison Avenue. The Trust Associate partners with the firm’s Trust Officers, Relationship Managers, and Portfolio Managers in managing the wealth and financial needs of our High-Net-Worth clients. Associates interact directly with High-Net-Worth clients, manage trust agency and some smaller trust relationships, and manage follow-up and/or ongoing client deliverables. Responsibilities: Work with Trust Officers, Relationships Managers, and Portfolio Managers on HNW family relationships Manage an existing book of trust agency and some trust accounts, including: general trust administration; evaluation and preparation of discretionary payment requests; participation in annual review and remediation process; and trust terminations Act as a key point of contact for clients and their accountants, attorneys, and other advisors Review trust documents to ensure all transactions are in compliance with the trust agreement and state laws Ensure compliance with internal policies and procedures Partner with Trust Officers on large, complex relationships to understand client goals and provide timely and accurate responses to inquiries Attend client meetings and investment reviews Qualifications: Education, Experience, Skills: Bachelor’s degree Completion of trust certification (CTFA) or similar qualification 3-5 years of experience in financial services with trust administration preferred Detail-oriented with the ability to multi-task and meet deadlines Ability to work in a team-oriented environment while balancing the needs of the client Effective interpersonal skills and relationship building skills Ability to maintain confidentiality and handle client account information with discretion Strong oral and written communication skills Skilled in Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint Strong analytical skills with the ability to think strategically as it relates to client needs Self-starter with strong problem solving skills Ability to practice good judgment while making basic business decisions Ability to handle various projects and deadlines and prioritize each task appropriately Compensation : Franklin Templeton offers employees a competitive and valuable range of total rewards—monetary and non-monetary — designed to support their well-being and recognize their time, talents, and results.

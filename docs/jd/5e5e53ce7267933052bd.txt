Following our acquisition by U.S. Bancorp in 2026, we combine BTIG’s high-touch, client-first approach with the scale and stability of the nation’s fifth largest commercial bank. Job Description BTIG is seeking an experienced Associate or Vice President to support the Investment Banking team in the M&A and the Strategic Advisory group in New York. The candidate will be involved in all facets of BTIG's investment banking activities, including equity and debt, public and private market offerings, merger and acquisition advisory, and other investment banking advisory services. The ideal candidate will demonstrate the potential to take on a significant role in deal execution by providing day-to-day support to senior bankers and clients while also providing professional guidance to junior team members. BTIG offers a unique environment which combines a highly entrepreneurial platform focused on growth companies with the analytical rigor and transaction experience of a bulge bracket investment bank.

Job Description This intern will report to the Director of Finance Transformation and will support the Data Analytics team in advancing data governance, requirements gathering, and AI use case development. This intern will assist the team in advancing strategic initiatives while gaining exposure to enterprise data practices. Responsibilities Support migration of global finance systems to US-based systems. Contribute to systems mapping and data flow exercises by analyzing large datasets, identifying trends, and generating actionable insights. Collaborate with cross-functional teams on analytics initiatives. Perform data extraction, transformation, and visualization, as well as supporting predictive modeling and performance measurement initiatives. Engage in continuous learning opportunities about emerging finance technologies and systems, while offering support across finance transformation projects. Contribute to business analysis by comprehending financial workflows and participating in project planning. Participate in professional development to refine skills in finance and technology. Qualifications Currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree in information systems, data analytics, or other IT-type degree. Internships are not available for students who have completed their college education or who will graduate prior to the conclusion of the internship. Strong interest in financial systems, data analysis, automation, and process improvement. Willingness to learn new technologies and work in a team-oriented environment. Strong attention to detail and organizational skills. Basic to intermediate experience with Excel and SQL. Preferred Qualifications Prior relevant internship experience or an interest in the IT field. Exposure to Python, Alteryx, AI, Cloud-technologies. Passion for learning and keeping pace with the latest advances in data science and machine learning. Working Conditions Interns will work hybrid in Cedar Rapids, IA.

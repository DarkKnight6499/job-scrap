Become a part of our caring community The Associate Director, Formulary Operations Management is responsible for the development, implementation, maintenance, and governance of pharmacy formularies and utilization management strategies across lines of business. This role partners with clinical, operational, regulatory, analytics, and product teams to ensure formulary decisions are accurately implemented, compliant with regulatory requirements, and aligned with organizational goals. The Associate Director, Formulary Operations Management requires a solid understanding of how organizational capabilities interrelate across departments and serves as a key leader in translating formulary strategy into operational excellence. The Associate Director, Formulary Management leads formulary operations implementation and maintenance activities, ensuring alignment with regulatory requirements, business objectives, and member needs. This role collaborates with internal and external stakeholders to implement formulary strategies, oversee utilization management initiatives, support regulatory submissions, and ensure accurate implementation of formulary and coverage decisions. Associate Director is accountable for formulary operations workflow, quality, capacity, training, reporting, issue resolution, and people leadership. This role will work standard Eastern Time business hours. Key Responsibilities: • Lead formulary development and maintenance activities across applicable products and lines of business. • Oversee operational implementation of formulary changes, including coordination with clinical, configuration, product, and operational teams. • Support the development and execution of utilization management programs, including, but not limited to, prior authorization, step therapy, quantity limits, and coverage • Evaluate formulary performance and operational impacts through data analysis and reporting. • Ensure compliance with applicable state, federal, and client formulary requirements. • Develop and maintain policies, procedures, and supporting documentation for formulary operations. • Partner with cross-functional stakeholders to support formulary submissions, audits, regulatory reviews, and implementations. • Lead strategic projects and initiatives related to formulary optimization, operational excellence, and process improvement. • Lead, develop, and support a high-performing team by setting clear expectations, coaching associates, supporting career development, and fostering accountability, engagement, and collective success.

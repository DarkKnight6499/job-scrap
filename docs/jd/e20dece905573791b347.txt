L&G's Asset Management business is a major investor across public and private markets worldwide, with $1.533 trillion in AUM.* Our clients include individual savers, pension scheme members and global institutions, who invest alongside L&G’s own balance sheet. Our ambition is to be a leading global investor, innovating to solve complex challenges for our clients using the power of L&G. This is rooted in our investment philosophy and processes, which are focused on creating value over the long term. We believe that incorporating financially material sustainability criteria, when relevant to our clients, can generate value and drive positive change. We're seeking an experienced ETF Capital Markets professional to play a pivotal role in the launch and growth of our U.S. ETF business. This is an exciting opportunity to join a new function in the US and help build the capital markets infrastructure behind a new ETF platform. You'll work across the full ETF ecosystem—partnering with custodians, authorized participants (APs), market makers, exchanges, trading teams, and operational partners—to ensure successful fund launches, strong liquidity, and a best-in-class investor experience. From day one, you'll be a key contributor to ETF launches, helping establish operational processes, liquidity strategies, trading oversight, and external relationships. As the platform grows, you'll become the firm's capital markets expert, guiding creation/redemption activity, monitoring trading quality, and supporting asset growth across the ETF range. Responsibilities Overseeing the ETF Capital Markets function as the firm launches L&G ETFs in the US Manage day-to-day primary and secondary market activity. Work closely with internal teams such as portfolio management, trading, and operations, to support ETF trading and liquidity. Coordinate with internal and external stakeholders to resolve any issues related to trading. Monitor ETF market quality, including volume, creations/redemptions, premiums/discounts etc. Serve as a technical subject matter expert in all areas related to ETF trading. Support relationship management efforts and oversee client service functions. Maintain relationships with authorized participants, market makers, trading platforms, and exchanges. Develop and enhance internal ETF tools and processes. Coordinate execution of key initiatives and projects within the ETF Capital Markets platform across functional groups.

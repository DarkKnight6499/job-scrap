Position Summary: The Operations Specialist – Funds Transfer Pay Processing is responsible for the processing of manually initiated wire transfer instructions as well as repairing electronic instructions that do not straight through process. These wire transfers may be in USD or a foreign currency. The position entails understanding industry wide payment formatting conventions (SWIFT) as well as BBH’s own processing systems and requirements. The specialist will need to be able to interpret instructions in order to differentiate between the various types of money movements (ex. payments from expects). Timeliness and accuracy are key traits needed to succeed in this position. Principal Responsibilities: Process Transactions The specialist will use Keystone to process instructions received via fax. Since all transactions require duality, they will either be the person entering the item or verifying the item in Keystone. The specialist will also need to continuously go into repair queues within Keystone to fix items which did not STP and enable them to be processed by the system. The specialist will be required to interpret fax instructions as they are received in various formats to be able to process them in Keystone. Not only do clients use different fax templates, but the specialist will need to be able to identify between payments, book transfers, expects and amendments. They will need to be able to accurately pull information from the fax. These faxes range from straight forward with little interpretation needed to complex where a knowledge of other departments or industry practices may be needed. The specialist must be able to work in a team environment as all instructions require two people to complete the item. Communication within the group is key to get instructions processed before market deadlines. While the specialist may concentrate on either USD transactions or non-USD transactions most of the time, they will need to be flexible as resources are moved around to accommodate volumes and ensure items are processed in timely manner. The specialist must process items accurately and within established timeframes to be successful. Authentication All specialists must adhere to Funds Transfer and Firm policies related to accurately authenticating all instructions received Completely checking signatures and who is sending in instructions, callback documentation accuracy, accepting valid instructions Problem Resolution When unclear instructions are received via fax or electronically within Keystone, the specialist should research the item and look to resolve the issues. The specialist should feel comfortable escalating items to their supervisor as appropriate to reduce risk and ensure the correct steps are taken to resolve the issue. Communications From time to time, the specialist will receive emails or phone calls from other BBH departments. They need to communicate professionally and provide quality answers. Process Improvement Specialists should question inefficient processes and make Risk & Control All specialists must adhere to firm policies particularly those related to Funds Transfer and department procedures. The specialist needs to ensure that transactions are reviewed in a timely manner but without sacrificing quality or putting the firm at risk.

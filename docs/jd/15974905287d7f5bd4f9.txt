You will work across a modern, cloud-based analytics stack, partnering with product owners, data engineers, and business domain experts to turn raw data into governed, well-designed analytical experiences. Our strategic architecture is a business enabler for AI-driven transformation, built on foundational data storage and access, elastic scaling for high-performance workloads, and rapid business intelligence engineering. You will be part of that capability, with real ownership of the experiences our users touch every day.

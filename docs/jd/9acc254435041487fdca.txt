As a Security Operations Senior Associate in CTC Detection and Response Operations, you will play an important role in safeguarding the organization's digital assets and infrastructure by proactively detecting, assessing, and responding to threats, vulnerabilities, and security incidents. Drawing on your knowledge of security principles, practices, and theories, you will collaborate with cross-functional teams to develop a coordinated approach to security and educate employees on best practices, policies, and procedures. Your work will have a direct impact on departmental outcomes, as you plan and ensure progress, identify gaps in information, and conduct analyses to solve complex cybersecurity problems. By utilizing your advanced analytical, technical, and problem-solving skills, you will contribute to the continuous improvement of our cybersecurity posture and help maintain the integrity, confidentiality, and availability of sensitive data and systems. Job responsibilities Analyze security alerts and lead in-depth investigations by reviewing system logs, endpoint telemetry, network traffic, and other data sources to determine impact, scope, and root cause. Execute incident response actions (containment, eradication, recovery) to mitigate cyber threats, and document investigative steps and outcomes. Develop, maintain, and continuously improve threat detection and response playbooks and standard operating procedures (SOPs), incorporating industry best practices, regulatory requirements, and lessons learned. Leverage security tools and technologies (e.g., SIEM, Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR), and network packet capture/inspection platforms) to support detection, investigation, and response. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver improvements to security operations—tools, processes, and procedures—driving efficiency and resiliency. Use enterprise-authorized AI capabilities to accelerate triage, evidence synthesis, and incident reporting, validating outputs and handling data in accordance with sensitivity and security requirements.

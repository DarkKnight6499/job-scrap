Together we win! THE OPPORTUNITY The Portfolio Operations Associate is expected to independently monitor the receipt and performance of all insurance associated with the loan portfolio. This includes, but is not limited to, Property (Commercial and Residential), Master Condo, Business Personal Property, Liability, Liquor Liability, Professional Liability, Business Interruption, Worker’s Compensation, Life, Errors & Omissions, Disability, and Pollution Insurance. The individual will identify deviations from the agreed-upon coverages and take the appropriate remedial steps. The Portfolio Operations Associate will work closely with a Loan Officer to assess client and collateral risk on the loan portfolio, which consists of SBA 7(a), 504, and Express loan programs, as well as conventional commercial loans. Perform and complete various projects and assignments as needed. Performs all duties in accordance with the Company’s policies and procedures, all U.S. state and federal laws and regulations, wherein the Company operates. HOW YOU’LL MAKE A DIFFERENCE Create insurance ticklers for all loans in the portfolio as required. Monitor third-party tracking activities as required. Send notifications to guarantors and insurance agents requesting updated insurance information. Directly contact guarantors and insurance agents in order to obtain current insurance information. Accept insurance certificates through mail, email, fax, and other methods. Evaluate and process insurance certificates in a timely manner. Obtain proper assignment language through insurance agents and agencies. Negotiate proper coverages with guarantors and insurance agents. Obtain force-placed insurance coverages when required and where available. Update the loan system with accurate and up-to-date insurance information. Evaluate and create ticklers for acquired portfolios. Assess fees and charges to a loan for force-placed insurance or other items as needed. Process and administer insurance claims according to Bank policies and procedures. Save insurance certificates and related information to the document management system. Inform loan officers of changes to collateral, business status, or other items as discovered. Provide accurate information to loan officers as required to maintain current risk assessments. Prepare weekly reports and summaries to keep management informed of insurance deficiencies and variances. Inform loan officers of needed changes to loan structure or other items as discovered. Maintain a complete understanding of SBA SOPs related to insurance, taxes, financials, and other requirements. Understand the basic requirements related to SBA liquidations. Assist in maintaining insurance, taxes, financials, and other items so that SBA guarantees are fully honored. Prepare for and participate in multiple audits from internal, external, and regulatory groups. Complete NAGGL or other SBA-related training programs as recommended by the manager. Contribute to team efforts by accomplishing related results as needed, including special projects. Follow all established policies and procedures. Perform other duties and projects as assigned. WHAT YOU’LL BRING Minimum two or more years’ lending/insurance experience required. High School diploma or equivalent required. Associate’s degree or higher preferred. SBA lending experience preferred. General understanding of corporate finance and accounting concepts. Advanced Excel spreadsheet skills are required in order to perform the essential functions listed above. Proven ability to use Microsoft Office Suite, including Outlook, Excel, Microsoft Word, and PowerPoint. Ability to communicate orally and in writing in a professional manner when dealing with clients, peers, and Company contacts. Ability to work independently and collaboratively. Ability to interface effectively and professionally with senior-level management. Ability to multitask several projects at once. Excellent attention to detail and accuracy. Excellent organizational and time management skills.

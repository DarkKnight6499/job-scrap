Line of Business: Risk Management Job Description: Department Overview: The Risk Identification Catalog & Measurement (RICM) team is part of Enterprise Risk Management. The RICM team is responsible for the quarterly identification and assessment of Top, Emerging and Material risks. In addition, the team performs Risk Event Measurements on Significant risks to determine potential risk impacts across the Combined US Operations. Each of these Risk Events are developed via engagement with Business & Risk Subject Matter Experts, with impacts utilized for Enterprise Stress Testing purposes. The Group Risk Specialist provides a comprehensive and diverse range of risk management expertise to business leaders. Leads and supports the implementation of appropriate risk frameworks and leading related management activities to mitigate enterprise and/or business-wide risk exposures for key business segment of the Bank or functional area. May provide oversight, commentary, and recommendations based on overall strategy and industry best practices.

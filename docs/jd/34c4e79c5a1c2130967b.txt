The Difference You Make: The Quantitative Risk Modeling team is responsible for the development, implementation, monitoring, and governance of quantitative credit risk models that support Santander's commercial lending portfolios. The team partners closely with Risk, Credit, Business Line, Data, Technology, and Model Risk Management stakeholders to enhance the bank's risk assessment framework, improve portfolio management, and meet regulatory expectations. Position Summary The Associate, Risk Modeling is responsible for supporting the development, implementation, monitoring, and enhancement of quantitative risk models that inform credit risk assessment, portfolio management, allowance methodologies, and strategic decision-making. Develops, enhances, implements, and monitors quantitative risk models and analytical frameworks. Performs data analysis, data engineering, and statistical modeling to support risk measurement and credit decisioning. Evaluates model performance and stability through ongoing monitoring, back-testing, sensitivity analysis, and benchmarking. Identifies opportunities to improve model methodologies, predictive performance, and operational efficiency. Partners with business, credit, risk, and technology stakeholders to translate business requirements into scalable analytical solutions. Supports model documentation, testing, implementation, and governance processes throughout the model lifecycle. Contributes to the development of innovative risk analytics capabilities leveraging emerging technologies, automation, and advanced data techniques.

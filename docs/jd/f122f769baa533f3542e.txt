A Brief Overview Provides strategic leadership and oversight for financial planning and analysis functions by coordinating budgeting and forecasting processes, providing guidance on financial strategy, and collaborating with stakeholders to align financial goals with business objectives. Ensures accurate financial reporting, drives process improvements, and contributes to strategic planning and risk assessment. What you will do Directs operations for the financial planning processes, including budgeting, forecasting, and long-term strategic planning. Conducts in-depth financial analysis to evaluate the financial performance of business units or initiatives, analyzing profitability, cost drivers, pricing strategies, and investment opportunities. Assists with the development of the company's strategic plans by providing financial analysis and insights, assessing financial feasibility and impact of proposed strategic initiatives and investments. Prepares financial models and sensitivity analysis to support decision-making. Contributes to monthly and quarterly reporting packages and formal business reviews, and participates in leadership presentations. Ensures quality financial summaries and presents complex dashboard information to management. Collaborates with business leaders to identify areas of improvement, cost-saving opportunities, and revenue growth strategies. Allocates resources to improve financial planning and analysis inefficiencies, implement automation or standardization initiatives, and enhance the accuracy and timeliness of financial reporting.

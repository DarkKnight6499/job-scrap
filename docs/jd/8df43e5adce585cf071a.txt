Your opportunity Collaboration: Partner with key stakeholders on the Central Research Team to support the management of our Research Suite of products. Data Strategy: Design, maintain, and enhance scalable data pipelines and cloud-based infrastructure that support both research and production workflows, ensuring reliable access to high-quality financial data and analytics. Risk & Factor Analysis: Proactively decompose portfolio performance and positioning into factor exposures to assist in risk management and portfolio construction. Model Development: Design, evaluate, and implement alpha models to assist in our stock selection process and refine our portfolio construction methodologies. Operational Execution: Support recurring analyses, documentation, quality-control processes and project coordination while helping to reduce operational friction. Technical Expertise: Advanced programming and data engineering capabilities, with demonstrated experience architecting scalable cloud-based research platforms, data pipelines, and analytical solutions that support both exploratory research and production investment processes. Financial Data: Experience working with institutional investment data platforms and point-in-time financial datasets, including Bloomberg, FactSet, S&P Capital IQ/Compustat, or comparable research and market data sources. Investment Acumen: Demonstrated interest in fundamental investing, including financial statement analysis, valuation, and the application of data-driven insights to investment decision-making. Nice to have skills Education: Advanced degree in Financial Engineering, Statistics, Computer Science, Mathematics, Finance, or a related discipline preferred. Certifications: CFA Charterholder, CFA candidate, or demonstrated interest in fundamental security analysis preferred. Communication: Exceptional qualitative skills.

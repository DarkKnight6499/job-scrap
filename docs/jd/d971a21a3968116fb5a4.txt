The Impact You will have in this role : The Scrum Master Associate Director will play a key role in enabling cross-functional technology teams to deliver high-quality solutions within an Agile environment. You will guide teams in applying Scrum and Agile practices, remove delivery obstacles, and strengthen collaboration across Product Owners, Business Analysts, Developers, and Testers. In addition to Scrum facilitation, you will bring strong project management discipline around planning, timelines, budgets, risks, dependencies, and reporting, while driving continuous improvement and consistent Agile practices across the organization. Your Primary Responsibilities : Guide assigned teams in the effective use of the Scrum framework and support the adoption and continuous improvement of Agile practices. Facilitate Scrum ceremonies, including sprint planning, daily stand-ups, backlog refinement, sprint reviews, and retrospectives. Partner closely with Product Owners, Business Analysts, Developers, Testers, and other stakeholders to ensure alignment on priorities, scope, and delivery commitments. Create and maintain Jira projects, boards, workflows, dashboards, and reporting to provide visibility into team progress and delivery. Track team commitments, milestones, risks, issues, and cross-team dependencies, driving timely resolution and escalation where appropriate. Develop and maintain detailed project plans, timelines, milestones, and budgets to support successful project execution. Track project financials, including budget, forecasts, and actuals, and report on variances as appropriate. Identify opportunities to improve team effectiveness, delivery predictability, and Agile maturity. Drive consistent Agile standards, processes, and best practices across assigned teams. Provide clear and timely status updates, metrics, and delivery reporting to stakeholders and leadership. Support technology initiatives including software application development, digital transformation, and cloud migration. Ensure projects and teams operate in accordance with applicable financial services regulations, data security standards, and internal governance requirements.

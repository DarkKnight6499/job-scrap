Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We’re hiring a Manager Quality Assurance (QA) Validation to join our team at our new highly automated drug substance manufacturing facility in Saratoga Springs. This position leads the laboratory and analytical equipment validation function. They are responsible for qualification of laboratory and analytical instrumentation supporting quality control (QC) and manufacturing science functions, with secondary oversight of manufacturing equipment, utility qualification, and cleaning/sterilization validation processes. They will participate in and defend validation data during FDA and other regulatory inspections.

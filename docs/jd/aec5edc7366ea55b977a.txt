Role Description: Support the Americas Wholesale business as their finance counterpart, primarily focused on expense analysis. Providing monthly expense reporting metrics, researching drivers for material variances, and offering insights on budget overruns or areas of improvement. Leveraging available business intelligence, the general ledger, accounts payable, transaction database, or global counterparts, to be able to tell a story and provide a complete picture for the business. Consolidate regional revenue commentary, on a weekly, monthly and quarterly basis, working directly with product control. The analyst will take ownership of the Regional Headquarters commentary, particularly Franchise Revenues, being able to explain the drivers of cross regional sales. Liaise with business managers providing them with both standard monthly management reports as well as responding to ad-hoc requests as needed. Partner with the team through the annual budget cycle, building supporting analysis, validating business submissions, and loading the final plan. Participate in firm’s daily revenue flash reporting to senior management. Build checks and controls to ensure accuracy in reports and implement automation/efficiencies into existing processes as needed. Assist with ongoing projects and initiatives within the finance division. Opportunity to take on transformation and automation work. Candidates with an interest in SQL, Power BI, or data transformation tooling will be given the exposure and support to apply them to real reporting problems, beyond the core responsibilities of the role. Skills, experience, qualifications and knowledge required: 1+ years of Financial Planning & Analysis experience or within Finance in the financial services sector Proficiency in Microsoft Excel required. Comfort with Powerpoint, Word and Outlook a plus. Bachelor’s degree in finance, Accounting or related field General Accounting Knowledge Experience with reporting tools (Hyperion / Essbase) or general ledger (preferred) Ability to work independently and communicate summary findings Strong organizational skills and ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously while maintaining attention to detail and meeting deadlines. Intellectual curiosity and eagerness to learn, with demonstrated ability to quickly grasp new concepts and business processes.

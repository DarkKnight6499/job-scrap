Your Impact As a member of the Capital Call Financing Underwriting team, you will play an integral role in evaluating, structuring, executing, and monitoring credit solutions for private equity sponsors and fund managers. You will analyze fund sponsors and limited partner collateral, evaluate the risk and economics of proposed transactions, develop financial and borrowing base models, and prepare credit recommendations. You will partner with clients, Credit Risk Management, Private Wealth Management teams, and other Goldman Sachs Bank USA stakeholders to deliver solutions that address client needs while remaining within the Bank’s risk parameters. Depending on experience and level, you will support or lead the underwriting process, advise on transaction structures, and manage ongoing portfolio responsibilities. More experienced professionals will independently lead complex transactions, provide subject-matter expertise, influence underwriting standards, and mentor junior team members. Key Responsibilities Credit Analysis, Underwriting and Structuring Support or lead the underwriting of capital call, subscription, and other fund-level credit facilities. Evaluate the credit, business, and economic implications of proposed transactions using financial, analytical, and technical judgment. Conduct due diligence on private equity funds and sponsors, including track records, financial performance, investment strategies, limited partners, private placement memoranda, and limited partnership agreements. Analyze funds across strategies such as buyout, real estate, infrastructure, and other alternative investments. Evaluate the quality and diversification of limited partner collateral and assess the implications for the facility’s borrowing base and overall credit profile. Build and maintain borrowing base certificates, credit models, financial analyses, and stress scenarios. Evaluate complex or structured loan proposals and develop credit solutions that address client objectives while remaining within acceptable risk parameters. Document proposed loan structures, risk considerations, mitigants, and recommendations for credit approval and file documentation purposes. Transaction Execution and Partnership Partner with clients, Credit Risk Management, Private Wealth Management teams, Legal, Operations, and Goldman Sachs Bank USA stakeholders to facilitate timely credit approval and execution. Coordinate transaction diligence, credit approvals, legal documentation, policy reviews, and closing requirements. Ensure that loan documentation accurately reflects approved terms and that appropriate risk ratings are assigned to each transaction. Communicate underwriting requirements, transaction considerations, risks, and outstanding items clearly to internal and external stakeholders. Manage multiple transactions and priorities while maintaining strong organization, accountability, and attention to detail. Portfolio and Risk Management Monitor ongoing compliance with covenant, collateral, and borrowing base requirements. Review fund, sponsor, and limited partner developments that could affect transaction performance or credit quality. Ensure that credit approvals, policy exceptions, and risk mitigants are appropriately documented. Maintain complete and accurate credit files consistent with internal underwriting standards and regulatory expectations. Support the broader Goldman Sachs Bank USA loan portfolio and interactions with banking regulators as needed. Product Expertise and Underwriting Standards Develop and maintain a strong understanding of Capital Call Financing products, fund structures, and relevant market practices. Apply consistent underwriting practices and ensure adherence to standards prescribed in the Lending Guide. Stay informed about developments affecting private equity sponsors, fund finance structures, limited partner collateral, and alternative asset strategies. Contribute to the development and enhancement of underwriting practices, portfolio monitoring, and transaction execution processes. Leadership and Team Development Contribute to a collaborative, client-focused culture grounded in sound credit judgment, disciplined execution, and effective risk management. Share product knowledge and transaction insights with junior colleagues and cross-functional partners. At the Vice President level, independently lead complex or structured transactions and advise stakeholders on credit solutions and risk considerations. At the Vice President level, mentor junior team members and provide guidance on fund analysis, financial modeling, transaction structuring, and credit documentation. At the Vice President level, help promote consistency in underwriting standards and contribute to broader business and process-improvement initiatives.

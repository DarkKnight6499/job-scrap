Position Summary The Neuroscience Discovery Biology team focuses on understanding the cellular and molecular mechanisms underlying neurodegenerative diseases to support the discovery of novel therapeutics. Our team uses a variety of cellular models alongside molecular and functional assays to investigate disease-relevant pathways and evaluate potential therapeutic strategies. The co-op will gain hands-on experience in early-stage drug discovery, contributing to experimental research, data analysis, and collaborative scientific discussions. Key Responsibilities Execute and troubleshoot cell-based experiments to investigate disease biology and therapeutic mechanisms. Culture and maintain mammalian cells, including iPSC-derived cellular models. Perform cellular and molecular assays, including qPCR and immunoassays, to assess functional cellular responses. Analyze and interpret experimental data and draw meaningful scientific conclusions. Maintain accurate laboratory records, documenting experimental procedures and results. Present findings and collaborate with scientists to support experimental planning and ongoing drug discovery efforts. Qualifications & Experience Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Neuroscience, Biology, Biochemistry, or a related field. Previous laboratory experience through academic research, coursework, or internships. Foundational knowledge of cell biology, molecular biology, and experimental design. Preferred: experience with mammalian cell culture and aseptic technique. A plus: familiarity with qPCR, ELISA, or other cell-based assays. Interest in neuroscience, neurodegenerative diseases, and drug discovery.

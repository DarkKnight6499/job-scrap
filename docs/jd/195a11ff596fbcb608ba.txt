About the Permian Straddling West Texas and Southeastern New Mexico over an area roughly the size of Kansas, the Permian is the most prolific oil and gas basin in America and the engine behind the country’s energy renaissance. Nationally, it provides about 40% of oil and 15% of gas production and plays a critical role in making the U.S. a leading energy exporter. A key asset for ExxonMobil, the Basin accounts for about 40% of the Corporation’s net oil and gas production in the U.S. in 2021, with significant future growth potential. Production reached more than 550,000 barrels per day in 2022 and is expected to surpass 800,000 barrels per day by 2027. Many of these molecules travel down to ExxonMobil’s facilities along the Gulf of Mexico, where they are transformed into high-value finished products such as jet fuel and lubricants. The Permian Basin business unit also has the distinction of being the first at ExxonMobil to unveil and execute a plan for net zero Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse gas emissions in operated assets by 2030, as part of the Corporation’s broader 2050 net zero ambition. About 800 employees of ExxonMobil’s Unconventional business line work and live in Midland, Odessa, Carlsbad and the surrounding areas, all of which offer easy access to national parks and unrivaled outdoor recreational opportunities. As a major operator in the Permian Basin, ExxonMobil invests heavily in local community development through the Permian Strategic Partnership, an industry consortium that works to enhance quality of life by improving infrastructure, healthcare, housing, education and workforce development. What you will do Manage large portfolio of reclamation / remediation projects – responsible for strategic planning, financial and safety stewardship, schedule management, and field execution coordination Evaluate contractor proposals, approve expenditures, and drive cost-effective project delivery while maintaining business and regulatory objectives Develop work plans/strategies that align with expectations for cost efficiency, waste minimization, and risk mitigation. Ensure site-specific risks are assessed, actions prioritized and risk management plans developed. Review and Approve Consultant Reports, Workplans, and Deliverables for submittal to stakeholders Steward and evaluate contractor/consultant activities and field work execution in alignment with ExxonMobil Operations Integrity Management System (OIMS), controls, and safety requirements. Develop and coordinate processes for maintaining compliance with regulatory, landowner, and permit requirements Collaborate with internal departments (wells, land, operations, etc) as needed to facilitate compliance requirements Participate in regulatory engagement, either directly, through a consultant, or through a designated EM contact in alignment with the regional engagement strategy, advocate on paths forward and maintain positive relationships Utilize Project Management tools such as Path to Closure, Cost and Schedule, Vulnerability & Risk, Bridging Agreements, Access Agreements, etc. Track and report KPIs (e.g., waste volumes, closure progress, spend) to support portfolio-level stewardship.

Summary: As a key member of the Franchise, Products & Experiences, Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) team, the Senior Financial Analyst, Global Consumer Products will be responsible for providing financial reporting, analytical, and operational support to the Consumer Products Business. The primary focus of this role is the consolidation of global forecasts, budgets, and financial results, as well as the preparation of reporting and analysis of overall business performance. The successful candidate must be able to effectively multitask and balance priorities on competing initiatives and deadlines, while demonstrating strong analytical skills and exceptional attention to detail. Responsibilities include: Planned job responsibilities are as follows but can change as team priorities evolve: Support Finance Manager in the consolidation and analysis of the Global Consumer Products P&L during financial cycles across Forecast, Budget, Long Range Plan, Quarter-end close and Headquarters pacing. Support Finance Manager with ‘Other Revenue’ across Forecast and Quarter Close, updating reporting and variance analysis. Own assigned components of the Global Consumer Products P&L, including consolidation, reporting, variance analysis, and forecast accuracy, translating financial results into clear business insights, identifying key performance drivers, emerging trends, risks, and opportunities. Key contributor for the monthly and quarterly close processes, partnering closely with Controllership to ensure timely and accurate financial reporting and deliverables. Support Finance Manager with cost-related deliverables including Overhead expenses and T&E actualization. Perform detailed variance analysis and regularly reconcile for accuracy and completeness. Identify, lead and implement opportunities to automate, standardize, and improve financial and operational process improvements. Act as a liaison with other departments within FPP, including Controllership, Regional FP&A, IT, Contract Operations and other cross-functional stakeholders. Collaborate with Regional Finance teams to ensure companywide financial goals and objectives are being met in a timely and efficient manner. Lead/assist special projects and other ad hoc analyses as assigned. Develop and maintain financial models to evaluate business performance and financial risks/opportunities. Qualifications/Requirements: 3+ years of professional experience in FP&A and/or finance-related fields combined Bachelor’s degree in finance, accounting or a related field CPA, CA, CMA or other professional accounting/finance designation a plus Strong accounting and financial reporting knowledge required Experience in Consumer Products, Media & Entertainment or Financial Consolidations a plus Advanced Microsoft Excel proficiency (Power Query) preferred Strong analytics and problem-solving skills, exceptional attention to detail, and high standards in quality and accurate reporting Experience with SAP preferred (S4/SAC) Desired Characteristics: Excellent communication skills, both verbal and written Consistently displays persistence, accountability, and flexibility Continuous improvement attitude, ability to seek out and implement best practices Ability to work independently as well as part of a team in this fast-paced environment Strong analytics and problem-solving skills, with demonstrated initiative Ability to multitask and prioritize work assignments and work well under pressure Exhibit strong analytical, technical, and research skills Must be accurate, highly organized, with great attention to detail Hybrid : This position currently has a hybrid schedule, which requires contributing from the office a minimum of four days per week.

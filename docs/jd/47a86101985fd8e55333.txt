Management Level: 84 Work Shift: 1ST SHIFT (United States of America) The Associate Technical Services is an entry-level position that both leads and helps facilitate the daily activities of the R&D Technical Services Department. The candidate will lead and assist other teams with the hands-on development ingredient substitution and innovation related projects from concept to commercialization including bench top and pilot plant work. The successful candidate will lead technical project teams and provide technical support to other functional groups within Tyson Foods. This candidate must also be familiar with critical steps in executing experiments and collecting data and in basic formulations and computer software used by the Technical Services group. This person will lead/support the Technical Services managers. Key Responsibilities Product & Process Development The position will assist in launching new products changes using current line processes or working with corporate and operations to implement the needed changes to launch a product packaging format etc. Maintains completes the manufacturing instructions for the product specification system. Lead the Technical Services team in the utilization of the tools for project management. Verify that during first production that operations is following the correct manufacturing instructions. Manages product and process development projects. Cross-Functional Collaboration Assists supply chain in understanding plant capabilities through plant testing to verify product performance labor and yields; Leads project teams through rolling and hard cut offs for the Bill of Material changes Specifications and communication with the Plant Operations team. Helps facilitate Product Transfers by assisting the plants in qualifying Finished Goods components and packaging and verifying the specifications are complete and the Bills of Materials are available prior to the qualification runs. As an innovations team member work with team members to ensure that test designs are completed and communicated to plant operations for a successful tests. Interacts with Corporate R D Marketing Engineering Purchasing and Manufacturing to move projects from the concept stage to pilot plant test market and then to successful launch Additional Responsibilities Applies knowledge of research methods food science and process technology to develop new or improved products, improve existing processes and generate cost savings Provides service to manufacturing and technical services for troubleshooting and problem resolution. Collect data run tests and conduct experiments to develop resolution to problems. Documents summarizes and recommends status and next steps. Education Bachelors degree in Food Science Meat Science Biology Chemistry or other related discipline. Experience 1 year experience in Quality, Food Safety, R&D or plant operations Communication Skills Good communication and interpersonal skills Computer skills: Computer Skills Good grasp of the use of Microsoft Office tools and willingness to learn enterprise wide software. Special Skills Strong working knowledge of Quality Control and HACCP principles and product development formulations. Self-motivated with the ability to translate technical knowledge to a variety of listeners. Supervisory Report to Senior Manager. Travel Travel will be about 10% of time.

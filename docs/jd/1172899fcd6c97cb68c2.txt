Principal Risk Associate - Business Continuity Management As a Commercial Risk Business Continuity Associate you will apply your skills to one of our highest profile Risk Management programs by partnering across the Commercial Bank’s Business, Technology, and Third Party landscapes to support planning and recovery solutions while ensuring compliance with Enterprise Policy requirements and protecting our customers, shareholders, and associates. You will partner with first line peers and senior leaders in virtually every Commercial Bank line of business, including across both the second and third lines of defense, to drive Program Governance, Oversight, and Compliance. Commercial Business Continuity Risk professionals are experienced, well-rounded, motivated, and intellectually curious individuals that operate within a highly dynamic and collaborative team environment to deliver value-added risk services to our business partners. Commercial Risk Associates at Capital One succeed with excellent analytical, organizational and communication skills, as well as high attention to detail and sound judgement. They are experienced in balancing the needs of multiple stakeholders while working autonomously to execute on a number of different priorities simultaneously. If you are interested in high exposure opportunities, are curious and eager to learn new things, love problem solving and have a deep reverence for risk management, this may be the role for you. Responsibilities Support cross functional risk management activities, focusing on Business Continuity, Operational Resiliency, and Crisis Management, as well as providing surge support to Commercial’s Third Party Management Team Identify opportunities to strengthen efficiency and execution of the performance and compliance goals of the Commercial Business Continuity Program Manage a robust Business Continuity control suite, including providing LOB challenge in Annual Quality Reviews tied to Commercial’s most critical business processes Provide ongoing Business Continuity Plan administration support to Plan Owners and Managers to help facilitate approvals across Commercial’s entire portfolio of BCPs Contribute to crisis event reporting to assess potential Bank, Financial, and Third Party impacts, and complete post event After Action Report (AAR) requirements Leverage project management and execution skills to support Commercial Business Continuity Projects, including tracking, reporting, and presenting to senior leadership Monitor and report on key risk indicators (KRIs), including providing thematic analysis and timely escalation of threshold breaches to support proactive remediation Coordinate development, maintenance, and change management of business continuity and crisis policies and procedures Provide audit and exam management support Basic Qualifications: Bachelor’s Degree or Military experience At least 1 year of experience in Risk Management, Consulting, Quality Assurance, Project Management, or Process Management Preferred Qualifications : 2+ years

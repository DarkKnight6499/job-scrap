Job Title: Senior Specialist, Financial Planning & Analysis Job Description: Senior Specialist, Financial Planning & Analysis The Role: Entegris is seeking a skilled Senior Financial Analyst to join our Corporate Financial Planning and Analysis (FP&A) team. The ideal candidate will be critical in driving Entegris’ forecasting and financial performance. As part of the Corporate FP&A team you will collaborate across Finance and with functional business partners to analyze and communicate key drivers of company results and operational variances. This candidate will play a key role in aggregating data, analyzing financials, reporting results, forecasting future periods, and influencing spend targets. This role in particular partners closely with Information Technology, Finance, Human Resources, and the Law Department and offers close exposure to Entegris’ executive leadership team (ELT). The ideal candidate possesses strong business and financial acumen, analytical and problem-solving capability, technical systems expertise, communication skills, and an ability to work independently to solve problems and drive outcomes. What You will Do: Advance “AI-Native” in the Finance function by creating agents and leveraging Copilot to automate routine administrative tasks; streamline legacy financial models and processes using Power BI, Power Query. Develop and maintain robust financial models to standardize reporting, analyze key business trends, and communicate clear and actionable results. Support the Annual Operating Plan (AOP) and Strategic Long-Range Plan (SLRP) with template development, input collection, financial analysis, and slide creation. Partner closely with business partners to communicate results, understand drivers, and influence decision making aligned with operating plan targets and key metrics. Become acquainted with functional drivers, leading indicators, and measure business results against key performance indicators (KPIs). Assist with ad-hoc analyses, reporting, model creation, and project management with an inquisitive mindset and “drive-to-completion” approach. Collect financial and non-financial inputs to develop supportable and accurate forecasts of headcount, operating expense, and capital expenditures. Aggregate high-volume and complex data from disparate systems across Workday, SAP, BPC, Accolade, and other sources.• Forge relationships with the broader Finance organization and become a trusted, strategic business partner to Entegris functions and the ELT.

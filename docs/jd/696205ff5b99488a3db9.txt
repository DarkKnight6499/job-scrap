Job Description RBC Real Estate Capital Partners RBC Real Estate Capital Partners (RECP) is a leading provider of debt capital to premier, institutional real estate sponsors. RECP’s mandate is to originate debt secured by stabilized and transitional assets across all major commercial real estate asset classes, in both single asset and portfolio investments. RECP acts as principal, deploying RBC Capital Markets’ balance sheet by originating senior mortgage, mezzanine, loan-on-loan, and revolving facilities. RECP focuses on core and major regional markets in the US, UK, Europe, Australia, and Canada. With a small team of dedicated professionals in Dallas and London, over the past decade RECP has participated in over $42 billion of mortgage, mezzanine and loan-on-loan financings. What is the opportunity? The Associate will be responsible for the day-to-day management of a sub-portfolio of 20-25 loan positions held within the Real Estate Capital Partners (RECP), Dallas loan portfolio. In addition to the specific loan level management activities, the Associate will work with the Head of Asset Management and RECP Regional Head to prepare and provide portfolio level reporting, analysis and updates to key internal and external stakeholders including, but not limited to, RECP Management Committee, Global Risk Management, Global Credit, Boards and Committees, and Regulatory Bodies. The Asset Manager will be the primary point of contact for back-office functions with respect to specific loan positions and underlying collateral. What will you do?

Job Title Risk & Controls Manager - Fixed Term Contract: Fixed-term role until September 2027 As Manager, Risk & Compliance , you will help strengthen and harmonise the risk and compliance environment by carrying out a gap analysis of existing controls and supporting the implementation of an enhanced Group model. This role will focus on building and embedding a risk and compliance framework aligned to regulatory requirements, monitoring and testing programmes, and consistent execution across the business. What you will do Complete a gap analysis of the existing controls, with a view to implementing an enhanced Group model Lead the build-out, harmonisation and embedding of risk and compliance frameworks aligned to regulatory requirements and monitoring and testing programmes Enhance the reporting suite to support monitoring, governance and escalation Work closely with first line teams to embed controls into processes, including customer care, merchant onboarding, system readiness and AMLA requirements Support training, documentation and consistent execution of the framework across the business Escalate matters to the Head of EMEA Risk and Compliance and other senior stakeholders where required What you will need to have Strong technical expertise in operational risk and control frameworks within payments and/or financial services Deep experience in RCSA, control design, process mapping, and governance, risk and compliance systems Familiarity with EU regulatory expectations, including safeguarding, AMLA, AML, outsourcing and DORA Ability to work hands-on in complex, multi-stream programmes with high interdependencies What would be great to have Experience supporting implementation of a Group-wide risk and control framework and/or harmonising local practices to enterprise standards Experience developing reporting, dashboards or management information for risk and control monitoring and escalation Thank you for considering employment with Fiserv.

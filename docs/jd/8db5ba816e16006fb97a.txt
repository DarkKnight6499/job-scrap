Overview Are you interested in exploring career paths in investment finance? In Spring 2027, join us at Susquehanna to learn more about what a day in the life looks like for our Sell Side Research, Derivatives Sales, and ETF Sales teams. Through panel discussions, meet and greets with employees, and interactive gaming sessions, you’ll learn about different aspects of investment finance, and will get a sense of our unique culture. This opportunity is open to students who are planning to graduate in the winter of 2028 or the spring of 2029. What we're looking for We are looking for students with a background and demonstrated interest in any of the following fields/majors: Finance Economics Math/Applied Math Statistics Computer Science Engineering Please submit your resume in order to be considered for the program. About Susquehanna Susquehanna is a global quantitative trading firm powered by scientific rigor, curiosity, and innovation. Our culture is intellectually driven and highly collaborative, bringing together researchers, engineers, and traders to design and deploy impactful strategies in our systematic trading environment. To meet the unique challenges of global markets, Susquehanna applies machine learning and advanced quantitative research to vast datasets in order to uncover actionable insights and build effective strategies. By uniting deep market expertise with cutting-edge technology, we excel in solving complex problems and pushing boundaries together.

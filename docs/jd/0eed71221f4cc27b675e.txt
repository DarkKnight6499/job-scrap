About The Role & Team The Banking Operations team plays a critical role in facilitating the movement of client funds across Webull's platforms, ensuring that deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and other banking transactions are processed accurately, efficiently, and securely. Working closely with internal departments, banking partners, and external vendors, the team oversees daily payment operations, resolves transaction-related issues, and supports the development and launch of new banking products and capabilities. We are seeking an experienced Senior Banking Associate to oversee the day-to-day operations of our Canadian banking infrastructure, including EFT, Interac e-Transfer, wire transfers, bill payments, and foreign exchange (FX). In this role, you will be responsible for ensuring seamless payment processing, managing transaction reconciliations and operational risks, and driving the implementation of new money movement products and vendor integrations. This is an exciting role to collaborate with cross-functional teams and external partners to enhance operational efficiency and support the continued growth of Webull's banking capabilities across Canada and other international markets.

Job Description: Job Title Capital Management Strats Corporate Title Vice President Location New York, NY Overview As a Capital Strat, you will develop analytics, forecasting tools, and scalable front-office solutions that support capital, expected credit loss, and financial resource management. You will partner across Front Office, Treasury, Credit Risk Management, Finance, and Technology to deliver robust modelling, planning, and scenario analysis capabilities with strong controls. Your work will directly support capital efficiency, senior management decision-making, and the Bank’s broader objective to allocate constrained resources effectively. Deutsche Bank is investing heavily in digital capabilities and long-term platform modernization, which means you will have the opportunity to shape solutions that have measurable impact across the franchise.

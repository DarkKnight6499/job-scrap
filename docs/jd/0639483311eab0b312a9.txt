Company Profile: Morgan Stanley is a leading global financial services firm providing a wide range of investment banking, securities, investment, and wealth management services. The Firm's employees serve clients worldwide, including corporations, governments, and individuals from more than 1,200 offices in 43 countries. In the Firm Risk Management division, we advise businesses across the Firm on risk mitigation strategies, develop tools to analyze and monitor risks and lead key regulatory initiatives. What you’ll do in the role: Morgan Stanley Services Group Inc. is seeking an Associate, Risk / Policy Mgmt in New York, New York to identify, evaluate, and monitor credit risk associated with lending, counterparty trading, and related activities conducted by the Firm. Assess credit risk of new loan originations and continuously monitor existing Firm borrowers to protect the Firm from exposure to credit losses. Conduct credit analysis and due diligence for corporate lending transactions for US Financial Institutions, process transaction approval packages, and present credit recommendations to senior officers. Review the integrity of financial models and credit base/downside case scenarios to ensure accuracy of credit risk calculations and assessments. Propose and evaluate credit protections and terms to be incorporated in legal documentation. Monitor existing credits for warning signs of potential deterioration of credit profile and recommend credit actions. Review portfolio management and credit transaction approvals. Telecommuting permitted up to 2 days per week. What you’ll bring to the role: -Requires a Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Finance, or a related field of study. Employer will accept a 3-year or 4-year degree. -Requires three (3) years of experience in the position offered or three (3) years as an Associate, Analyst, or a closely related occupation. -Requires three (3) years of experience with the following skills: Investment banking services, corporate lending, acquisition financing, and other capital markets products; Leveraged lending underwriting standards, and key structural terms of credit facilities including lender protections; Key credit risk concepts including probability of default, loss given default, regulatory classification, and credit ratios; Composing credit memos by summarizing key considerations around credit ratings, financial performance, and company strengths and weaknesses; Financial institutions including industry dynamics and key players in real estate investment trusts or insurance, investment and wealth management, and alternative asset managers; Financial statement analysis; Financial modeling and balance sheet stress scenarios; Business, financial, and legal due diligence processes; Microsoft Excel including pivot tables, charts, and summarizing large data sets; and Microsoft Power Point and Word.

Health Risk Strategist (RN) | Stop Loss | Population Health | Case Management | Remote/Hybrid Responsibilities The Health Risk Strategist (HRS) reports to the AVP, Clinical Health Risk Management and serves as a strategic resource supporting Berkley's Health Risk Management initiatives. The HRS partners with clients and internal stakeholders to identify health risks, evaluate cost containment opportunities, and implement population health and clinical management strategies that improve outcomes and optimize healthcare spend. A key responsibility of this role is to work collaboratively with the Clinical Consultant, Cost Containment RNs to ensure identified risks lead to clinical interventions, referral activity, member engagement, and measurable outcomes. By strengthening the connection between risk identification, clinical solutions, and member engagement, the HRS helps clients better understand the value of health risk management programs while improving overall program effectiveness. The HRS also supports innovation through the evaluation and adoption of technology-enabled solutions, including AI and analytical tools, such as Copilot, to improve efficiency, standardize analyses, enhance reporting capabilities, and reduce manual preparation efforts. This position can either be fully remote (if not within commutable distance to the office) or based in one of our offices: Hamilton Square, NJ Marlborough, MA Our offices offer a hybrid work schedule with 4 days in the office; and 1 day remote where it makes sense to do so.

Partners with transportation operations, maintenance teams, and leadership to promote a culture of safety, reduce risk, and improve compliance performance through coaching, training, auditing, and data analysis. Responsibilities: Support implementation of transportation safety, compliance, and risk management programs across assigned operations. Assist transportation leaders and drivers in understanding and applying company policies, procedures, and regulatory requirements. Conduct safety and compliance audits, reviews, and assessments to identify risks and opportunities for improvement. Monitor FMCSA, DOT, and related regulatory compliance metrics and communicate findings to operational leadership. Analyze driver, vehicle, and safety performance data to identify trends and recommend corrective actions. Deliver safety training, coaching, and communication programs to drivers, managers, and transportation team members. Maintain accurate compliance records, reports, and documentation required by regulatory agencies and company policies. Support regulatory reviews, inspections, and audits by providing requested documentation and operational support. Collaborate with transportation operations, maintenance, human resources, and other business partners to achieve compliance and safety objectives.

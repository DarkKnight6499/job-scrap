Position Summary & Overall purpose: The Jr. Asset Management Associate will work directly with the SVP, Broker Dealer Operations & Solutions and Senior Asset Management Associate, Team Lead to successfully service existing clients and onboarding new clients who are placing their trust in Lenox Advisors to take care of their total investment needs. The Jr. Asset Management Associate shall demonstrate proficiency in all client service processes, systems, and procedures. Special projects may be assigned at the discretion of Lenox leadership. This is a hyrid role reporting to our NYC office 2-3 days a week. When trained and required, support AMA team to onboard new investment clients. Learn to understand the relationships within the firm and LAM team including Relationship Managers, Asset Managers, neighboring departments, MMLISI, and vendors as well as the end client.

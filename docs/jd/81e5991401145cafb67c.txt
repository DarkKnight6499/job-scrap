Senior Credit Analyst Associate
Intesa Sanpaolo
Location: New York, US
Link: https://jobs.intesasanpaolo.com/job/New-York-Senior-Credit-Analyst-Associate/1453708433/

. Overview You will join the Credit Officer International Network within the Corporate and Investment Banking Division at the New York Hub. You will be responsible for independently assessing corporate credit proposals, safeguarding portfolio credit quality, applying the Bank’s credit policies and delegated lending authorities, and providing clear recommendations to the relevant decision-making bodies. Knowledge of the fundamentals of structured finance and project finance transactions would be considered an asset. What you'll be doing Key responsibilities include: Analyze corporate credit proposals originated by the business units Validate historical financial data from annual reports, Form 10-K, Form 10-Q and interim results; assess forecasts, liquidity, leverage and debt-service capacity Review financing structures, security packages, guarantees, collateral, covenants, market trends, regulatory factors, business plans and sensitivity analyses, challenging key assumptions Evaluate external ratings, default indicators, peer analysis, legal contingencies and transformative transactions Confirm RWA, CRA, EWS classification, internal rating, ESG score, group exposure and approval level; complete the Pratica Elettronica di Fido Prepare clear credit opinions identifying strengths, risks and mitigants; resolve proposals within delegated limits and support higher approval bodies Assess amendments and waivers, monitor deteriorating positions and recommend risk-mitigation actions. Who we are looking for If you want to turn your credit expertise into a senior career and you have the following characteristics, we are waiting for you: 3–5 years of relevant credit analysis are required In-depth credit analysis, underwriting and credit risk management knowledge Financial statement analysis and projection modelling, including liquidity, leverage and sensitivity assessment Knowledge of U.S. GAAP and IFRS accounting principles Secured and unsecured loan documentation, guarantees, collateral and financial covenants U.S. capital markets, syndicated loans, distressed debt, banking regulation, workouts and bankruptcy fundamentals Microsoft Excel and Word proficiency Undergraduate degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics or a related field Preferred: postgraduate degree in Business Administration, Accounting or Finance, or completion of a major-bank credit training program.

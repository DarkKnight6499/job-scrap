Job Description The Analyst, Corporate Insurance supports the development, administration, and optimization of the company’s global insurance programs. This role provides analytical, operational, and technical expertise across insurance placement, claims management and contractual risk transfer. The analyst works closely with the VP of Insurance and cross‑functional partners in Legal, Finance, Procurement, Operations, HR, and Compliance to ensure the company’s insurance portfolio effectively protects people, assets, and operations worldwide. Key Responsibilities Insurance Program Support & Analysis Assist in the renewal, placement, and administration of global insurance programs, including General Liability, Auto, Umbrella/Excess, Workers’ Compensation, Professional Liability, Cyber, Property, and specialty lines. Collect and analyze underwriting data, exposure values, and loss histories. Assist in preparing renewal submissions, schedules, and benchmarking reports. Support evaluation of program structure, retentions, and limits. COI Review & Contractual Risk Transfe r Review Certificates of Insurance (COIs) to ensure compliance with corporate requirements, including GL, Auto, Umbrella, WC, Professional Liability, and required endorsements. Maintain COI tracking and follow up with vendors on deficiencies. Support Legal and Procurement in reviewing insurance‑related contract language. Assist in developing and updating enterprise insurance requirements. Claims Support & Loss Analysi s Assist with reporting, tracking, and resolving claims. Coordinate with carriers, TPAs, and internal stakeholders to gather documentation and monitor claim progress. Support initiatives to reduce claim frequency, severity, and total cost of risk. Compliance, Governance & Process Improvemen t Ensure compliance with internal policies, regulatory requirements, and audit standards. Support internal and external audits related to insurance and claims. Identify opportunities to streamline workflows, automate COI review, and enhance data quality. Assist in evaluating new technologies and tools for insurance operations. This role is a Hybrid position to be located in our Foster City, CA office location.

About this role: Wells Fargo Wealth and Investment Management (WIM) Operations is seeking a Securities Operations Associate Manager in the Check Processing department to lead a team handling check processing for the firm. The team is part of Wealth and Investment Management, and this leadership role closely partners with front office partners, Legal, Risk, Compliance and Control teams. Additionally, this leader plays an integral role in issue management, establishment of check processing target operating models and resolution of escalated items.

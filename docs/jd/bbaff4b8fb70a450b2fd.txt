Solutions for Today’s Challenges. Vision for Tomorrow’s Opportunities. Join William Blair, the Premier Global Partnership. We are seeking an entry-level Associate I, PWM Capital Markets to join the Capital Markets team within Private Wealth Management. The role will focus primarily on fixed income while also supporting broader Capital Markets and Corporate Executive Services (CES) activities. The ideal candidate will be able to assist with fixed income securities, market dynamics, options-based strategies, exchange funds, direct indexing, and client service processes. This position collaborates closely with senior team members and Wealth Advisors to support solutions aligned with clients’ investment objectives and risk tolerances, including third-party manager proposals, onboarding, and ongoing servicing needs. Essential Responsibilities: Support fixed income activities, representing approximately 60–70% of the role, while assisting with broader Capital Markets and CES responsibilities for approximately 30–40% of the role. Assist with entering fixed income and equity trades and related trade support activities, including gathering required information, verifying trade details, and coordinating with team members to support accurate and timely execution. Engage with high-net-worth clients and Wealth Advisors to understand investment needs, risk tolerance, financial goals, and service requirements. Support fixed income strategies, including asset allocation, duration management, and credit quality analysis. Monitor and analyze fixed income market trends, interest rate movements, economic indicators, and other relevant market developments. Support Capital Markets and CES activities, including corporate share buyback account opening and money movements, as well as providing backup coverage for functions on the team outside of fixed income. Coordinate proposal requests, onboarding, and ongoing servicing with third-party managers and solution providers, including options overlay managers, exchange fund providers, and direct indexing providers. Support the preparation of client reports, presentations, and related materials. Additional responsibilities as requested. Qualifications: Bachelor's degree required. Advanced degree or professional designation preferred. 1+ years of experience within the financial services industry required. SIE, Series 7, 63, and 52 required and must be acquired within 180 days of employment. Ability to carry out processes related to fixed income, Capital Markets, and CES investment solutions and client service activities. Demonstration of basic analytical skills, including interpreting basic financial data, market trends, interest rate movements, and economic indicators. Basic understanding of financial analysis software and tools, including Bloomberg and FactSet. Client-focused orientation with a commitment to delivering exceptional service to clients, Wealth Advisors, and internal partners. Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to convey complex concepts clearly and coordinate effectively with team members and third-party managers. Experience utilizing Microsoft Excel, Word, and PowerPoint.

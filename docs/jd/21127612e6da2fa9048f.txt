You will influence through product expertise, desk judgment, quantitative creativity, and the ability to partner closely with Treasury, Finance, Model Risk Management, product teams, and desk stakeholders. Success in this role requires someone who can bridge desk intuition and analytical rigor while delivering tools and insights that change how the business operates.

Job Descriptio n: CashPro is Bank of America's digital banking platform, serving hundreds of thousands of users globally across payments, receivables, reporting, liquidity, trade, and administration capabilities. CashPro continues to invest in industry-leading security solutions that help protect clients, strengthen authentication, and combat evolving cyber threats. The Security Product Associate supports the strategic execution of the CashPro Security roadmap by helping drive key initiatives, governance activities, vendor partnerships, and cross-functional delivery efforts. This role partners closely with Product, Technology, Operations, Client Protection, Fraud Prevention, Commercialization, and other internal stakeholders to help deliver security capabilities that protect clients while providing a seamless user experience. The role provides broad exposure to product management, cybersecurity, and financial services while developing expertise in CashPro's security strategy and roadmap. • Support execution of the CashPro Security roadmap, ensuring initiatives are tracked, coordinated, and delivered on time. • Partner with Product, Technology, Risk, Operations, Client Protection, and other stakeholders to drive alignment and execution. • Coordinate governance routines, leadership reporting, and executive presentations for key security programs. • Support vendor management activities, including relationship management, performance tracking, financials, and market research. • Develop communications, presentations, and educational materials to support awareness, adoption, and stakeholder engagement. • Conduct research on cybersecurity, authentication, fraud prevention, digital identity, and industry trends to inform product strategy. • Gather, analyze, and summarize data, client feedback, and market insights to support business cases and strategic recommendations. • Facilitate meetings, document decisions and action items, and ensure timely follow-through on commitments. • Maintain portfolio management tools, including AgilePlan, Jira, roadmaps, and initiative tracking. • Assist with commercialization, change management, and rollout activities for new security capabilities and enhancements. • Build strong partnerships across CashPro, GPS, and enterprise teams to advance the CashPro Security strategy.

Director, Community Finance Capital Officer (Originations) - West Coast Global Enterprise Affairs works with many external and internal stakeholders to envision and create a world of greater socioeconomic opportunity through advocating for an inclusive society, building thriving communities, and creating financial tools that enrich lives. Our teams include Communications, Community Impact and Investment (CI&I), Community Finance, CRA Strategy and Program Management, the Government and Political Affairs Group, Regulatory Relations, and Strategy and Engagement. Through our teams, we have unparalleled access to a diverse group of motivated changemakers similarly focused on the ingredients for a more perfect future. The Opportunity and Role: Capital One’s Community Finance team originates debt and equity to support affordable housing development. Community Finance provides debt financing, and invests in low income housing credits through proprietary and multi equity funds. The Capital Officer team is primarily responsible for managing relationships with developers and other industry stakeholders. Capital Officers market our products, understand the competitive landscape and market needs, identify opportunities that align with Capital One’s overall strategy, and elevate market intelligence to key partners in External Affairs and across the company. We are seeking a passionate, motivated, emotionally intelligent Director to lead and inspire a team of associates focused on sourcing strategy-aligned multi-family housing development opportunities. Your team will enhance our understanding of communities and Capital One’s opportunities to promote socioeconomic mobility. Through your knowledge, influence, and people leadership, you will build upon our strong industry reputation while challenging the status quo and ushering our team into its next chapter of impact and innovation. We are looking for a leader who is: Wired for Collaboration and Connection. You thrive in a matrixed organization because you know that together we can do better. Obsessed with building and cultivating relationships. You love the chance to share your perspective, to hear the perspectives of others, and to figure out how teams can collaborate. Intellectually Curious, with a strong Point of View (POV). You ask why, you explore, you’re comfortable with ambiguity, you’re hungry to learn and a seeker of new challenges. You use this curiosity to develop and then refine, and refine, and refine your POVs. A Do-er. You’re biased toward action, you try things and sometimes you fail. You can get around your own roadblocks and you remove obstacles for your team to stay focused on enterprise goals.

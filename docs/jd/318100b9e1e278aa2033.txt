The EV & Energy Services team brings together machine learning researchers, data scientists, operations researchers, engineers, and business leaders to develop innovative solutions for smart cities infrastructure, EV charging orchestration, driver behavior modeling, battery health management, and next-generation mobility services. This internship will support our Mobility Services initiatives by developing data-driven simulation and machine learning tools used to study high-utilization mobility fleets. Projects may involve modeling fleet operations, battery health, charging behavior, maintenance requirements, vehicle uptime, and business performance. This is an exciting opportunity for students who are passionate about applying advanced analytics and machine learning to real-world challenges in transportation, sustainability, and future mobility. A Day in the Life: Contribute to the development, training, validation, and optimization of machine learning and simulation models for mobility service applications. Analyze vehicle, charging, fleet, and battery-related datasets to identify operational trends, risks, and opportunities for improvement. Support the development of simulation tools used to evaluate fleet utilization, charging strategies, maintenance planning, battery health, and service availability. Collaborate with researchers, data scientists, engineers, and business stakeholders on feature engineering, data processing, model evaluation, and KPI development. Communicate findings through reports, dashboards, visualizations, presentations, and demonstrations to technical and business audiences. Explore opportunities to contribute to patents, academic publications, open-source projects, and other intellectual property initiatives.

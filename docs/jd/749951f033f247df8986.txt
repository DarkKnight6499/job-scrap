Job Summary: The Standard’s Individual Annuities division is currently looking to add an Actuarial Associate to our team. This role will play a support role in creating the models, data processes, and analytics needed to support our VM-22 implementation. This role is ideal for someone that is process oriented, enjoys solving new problems, effectively explains results, and wants to develop their modeling skill set. This role will further support the development and refinement of our processes to implement VM-22 in pricing, FP&A, ALM, and governance frameworks. As part of this team, you will have opportunities to support other modeling initiatives in our near-term future state, potentially including strategic initiatives such as reinsurance to a Bermuda affiliate, strategic asset allocation, or ALM reporting. Key Responsibilities: Applies understanding of functional area to support enhancement of AXIS models and processes supporting VM-22 valuation. Support key element of VM-22 model implementation, such as model improvements, including structure, assumptions, and runtime optimization. Develop of working understanding of VM-22 in order to effectively support governance needs (change management, controls, validation readiness). Partner with valuation, pricing, FP&A, and investment management to ensure consistency and usability of results. Analyze model output to identify drivers, limitations, and opportunities for improving model performance and scalability (automation, workflow, cloud integration where applicable) Partners with the core VM-22 team as well as other members of the actuarial community of practice. * Note: This role may be filled at either of the levels noted below based upon applicable experience and career path: Actuarial Associate III: 3+ yrs. hands-on actuarial experience & ASA required.

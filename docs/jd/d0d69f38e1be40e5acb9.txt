Meet the Team Millennium's Global Risk Management Department is responsible for identifying, measuring, monitoring, managing and reporting on the risks associated with Millennium's portfolios at the Firm-wide and Portfolio Manager levels, with a focus on the market, credit and liquidity risks the Firm faces in the course of its business. At the Portfolio Manager level, the team establishes and monitors trading parameters, risk guidelines and performance metrics across three regions and multiple product classes, and also monitors aggregations of risk up to the full Firm-wide level. Within Risk Management, the Quantitative Strategies team is a collaborative and entrepreneurial investment team that develops quantitative investment strategies across asset classes, including equities, fixed income, commodities, credit and FX, and across a broad set of instruments spanning futures, forwards, options, swaps and cash products. The team works across the full research lifecycle: idea generation, data sourcing, signal development, model implementation, backtesting, portfolio construction and live strategy refinement. The team is launching a Quant Analyst Program designed to develop early-career quants over 18–24 months into strong, cross-asset systematic researchers through formal technical training, asset class rotations, cross-functional exposure and supervised contribution to the quantitative strategies platform, with senior quant mentorship and quarterly development goals throughout. What You’ll Do ·Complete a structured 18–24 month development program combining formal technical training in backtesting methodology, signal design, portfolio construction, risk and volatility modeling, and transaction cost modeling. ·Rotate through asset class research across equities, FX, rates, commodities, and options/volatility, learning market structure, common alpha sources, implementation constraints, and risk frameworks in each. ·Conduct original research and contribute new signals and features across quant themes, including equity risk premia, carry, momentum, value, flows, volatility, and dispersion. ·Build and improve shared research infrastructure, including data pipelines, backtesting tools, risk and volatility models, transaction cost models, and performance diagnostics. ·Analyze strategy performance with attention to robustness, implementation, transaction costs, liquidity, and risk exposures, following the team's standards for reproducible, peer-reviewed research. ·Gain hands-on exposure to trading, operations, compliance, technology, and risk to understand how ideas become investable strategies. ·Partner closely with senior quants, portfolio managers, and technologists to move ideas from research into shadow and production. ·Present research in regular team seminars and take ownership of an independent research project, with scope increasing over the program.

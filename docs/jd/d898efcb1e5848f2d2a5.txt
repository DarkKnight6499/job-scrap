Job Title - Sr. Business Analyst – Senior Analyst - ACS Song Management Level 10-Sr. Analyst Location: Kochi Must have skills: Requirement Analysis Good to have skills: Business Process Tools Experience: 3-5 years of experience is required Educational Qualification: Graduation (Accurate educational details should capture) Job Summary You’ll capture user requirements and translate them into business and digitally enabled solutions across a range of industries. Roles and Responsibilities Analyzing as-is and design to-be processes to define business, solution and system requirements. Organizing and facilitating workshops with clients to elicit and understand their requirements. Interacting with various (client) stakeholders to, gain a deep understanding of their needs and represent that knowledge to the rest of the team, while using analysis techniques like story mapping, process modelling and data- and document analysis. Managing the requirements life cycle Facilitating effective communication between the team and acting as a point of contact for clarifications on product backlog items Iteration planning, backlog refinement, creating user stories and story acceptance criteria is also part of your scope. Stakeholder management and being an interface between your team, the customer and the project manager. Identifying various solution options to satisfy our client’s requirements and helping them and our teams make the right decision by carefully weighing business value and technical complexity. Creating and detailing user stories based on criteria set by client or your practice Collaborating with key external and internal project stakeholders who have a direct impact on their role and deliverables (e.g., decision-makers, Subject Matter Experts) Ensuring that requirements and designs at an implementation level are aligned to one another, and managing the changes to related requirements Retaining requirement accuracy and consistency throughout and beyond the change during the entire requirements life cycle, and supporting the reuse of requirements in other solutions Professional and Technical Skill Experience in producing implementation-ready product backlog items based on the elicited requirements Experience in understanding initial product backlog/ inception documentation to be able to form a complete picture of project scope Skilled in engaging with clients, vendors, and internal teams to align expectations and deliverables. Excellent verbal and written communication for reporting, presentations, and team coordination. Ability to mentor, motivate, and manage cross-functional teams across geographies. Proficiency in JIRA, MS Project, or similar platforms. Understanding of development, testing, deployment, and maintenance phases. Knowledge of automation pipelines, version control, and deployment strategies. Relevant application knowledge of any commerce platforms like SAP Hybris, commerce tools or similar could complete your profile.

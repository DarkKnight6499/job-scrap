BASIC FUNCTIONS The Senior Analyst, Business Delivery is an individual contributor responsible for prioritized operational delivery and initiative execution within Service and Fulfillment. You will translate leadership priorities into clear deliverables, coordinate execution activities, manage risks and dependencies, and ensure initiatives are delivered on time. The role combines process ownership, light project management, change management, and data-driven decision-making to enhance quality, consistency, customer outcomes, and operational efficiency. This position collaborates with Service leadership, BPCs, Technology, Fulfillment, and Reporting to support effective execution, ensure operational readiness, and confirm delivery outcomes. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES • Operational Delivery & Process Ownership o Lead end‑to‑end operational delivery of complex, cross-functional technical and non-technical initiatives, demonstrating independent ownership of execution, readiness, and delivery outcomes. o Translate initiative scope and priorities into clear, actionable execution plans, including tasks, owners, timelines, and success criteria. o Independently coordinate and execute operational activities required to deliver outcomes (e.g., logistics, requirements gathering, pilots, sequencing, UAT, cutover planning, operational readiness), serving as a primary point of accountability. o Proactively identify, assess, and manage complex operational risks, constraints, and dependencies using analysis from multiple sources; escalate issues with clear, well‑reasoned mitigation options and recommendations. o Apply in‑depth functional and operational SME knowledge, as well as awareness of best practices to assess issues, define solutions, and guide execution decisions. o Create and maintain operational artifacts such as process documentation, current state and future state process workflows, checklists, and readiness documentation to support successful execution and handoff; identify recurring risks and escalate optimization opportunities. o Partner with operational leaders to confirm ownership, accountability, and long‑term sustainability post‑delivery; influence alignment across teams without direct authority. o Validate delivery success through measurable operational outcomes including continuity, adoption, quality, timeliness, and efficiency. o Lead and adapt execution approaches in response to emerging issues, shifting priorities, or increased complexity, with minimal guidance. • Analytics, Reporting & Insights o Define expected KPI improvements during the project and establish ongoing measurement and tracking to assess whether performance gains are realized (i.e. quality, cycle time, rejections) o Partner with Reporting to develop and improve dashboards (Power BI) that measure initiative outcomes using OKRs, KPIs, and performance metrics; act as a knowledgeable consumer and translator of data for decisionmakers. o Prepare and deliver insights, analysis, and presentations that explain complex or sensitive performance information, risks, trends, and recommendations to leadership and stakeholders. • Stakeholder Management & Alignment o Serve as a trusted operational partner to Business Enablement and Service leadership to confirm priorities, success criteria, and delivery expectations across initiatives. o Proactively manage stakeholder expectations by clearly communicating progress, risks, tradeoffs, and decisions; build consensus and alignment across diverse perspectives. o Lead and facilitate working sessions, reviews, and decision checkpoints; act as a resource and advisor to colleagues and related teams to remove blockers and enable progress. • Change Management, Training & Communications o Independently develop and deliver communications and job aids; provide input on training materials that support adoption and sustained use of new workflows and standard work. o Identify training and change impacts early; partner with leaders and peers to design targeted reinforcement strategies. o Support employee engagement and continuous improvement activities that reinforce change, improve execution maturity, and promote operational excellence.

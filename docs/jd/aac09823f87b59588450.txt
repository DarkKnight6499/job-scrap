As a Senior Associate, Valuation, you will: Perform valuations of business enterprises, debt instruments, equity securities, and hybrid or structured securities using methodologies including discounted cash flow, comparable transactions, and comparable public companies Analyze historical financial and operating results, evaluate business trends and risks, and challenge assumptions underlying future financial projections Construct and review valuation and financial models, perform financial statement analysis, and assist in developing discount rates including WACC, IRR, and WARA Perform purchase price allocations for financial reporting and/or tax purposes under U.S. GAAP Review investment memoranda, investor presentations, credit and equity agreements, and financial models to understand portfolio companies and securities under analysis Conduct market and industry research, develop industry growth estimates, and help prepare valuation reports and other client deliverables Present analyses and conclusions to internal management, client points of contact, boards of directors, and fiduciaries while directing engagement activity and providing review and

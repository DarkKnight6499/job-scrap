About the Role:

Join Crusoe’s Cloud Finance team, playing a key role in driving Financial Planning & Analysis in support of our dynamic and growing Cloud business. You will work on financial analysis across revenue, cost, and capital investment workstreams. Your work will directly influence strategic financial decisions, drive operational efficiency, and enhance our finance function.



We’re seeking a results-driven professional with 4+ years of experience who is inspired to play a hands-on role in defining strategic financial targets and driving implementation amidst rapid growth



What You'll Be Working On:

 - Cloud Deal Analysis: Conduct financial analysis and modeling for customer deals — evaluating pricing, contract terms, ROI, and margin impact to support the sales team and ensure deals meet financial thresholds

 - Capital Investment Analysis: Support financial modeling for infrastructure investment decisions including GPU purchases, colocation site evaluations, and build-vs-buy analysis for data center opportunities

 - Cost & Unit Economics: Support GPU total cost of ownership (TCO) analysis and cost benchmarking across the deployment portfolio

 - Competitive Intelligence: Help maintain competitor financial models, track industry developments, and translate competitive data into insights that inform pricing, investment, and strategic decisions

 - Capital Raise Support: Support creation of models, presentations, and diligence materials for debt and equity fundraising events

 - Process Improvement: Drive process improvements to enhance financial modeling and decision making efficiency

 - Ad-Hoc Analysis: Conduct special projects and perform ad-hoc financial analyses as needed; serve as a point of contact internally and externally for finance inquiries



What You'll Bring to the Team:

 - 4+ years of experience in investment banking, venture capital, financial planning & analysis, or corporate finance

 - Excellent skills in financial modeling and forecasting

 - Strong interpersonal, written, and oral communication skills

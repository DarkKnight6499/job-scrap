Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Managed Services Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Managed Services - Guidewire ClaimCenter - Sr. Associate you will help clients improve insurance operations by analyzing needs, supporting Guidewire ClaimCenter implementation and optimization, and contributing to training and production support within our Managed Services practice. As a Senior Associate you will navigate complex workstreams, build meaningful client connections, mentor junior team members, and use critical thinking, data insights, and Guidewire knowledge to move client priorities forward. In this role at PwC, you will focus on helping clients use Guidewire software to streamline claims processes, address system issues, and support ongoing enhancements that align with business objectives. Responsibilities - Managing Guidewire ClaimCenter support workstreams across incident resolution, defect analysis, and enhancement requests - Analyzing client issues, tracing root causes, and translating business requirements into practical application solutions - Configuring and supporting Guidewire ClaimCenter functionality across claims operations, workflows, and integrations - Monitoring production performance, identifying recurring issues, and recommending process or system improvements - Coordinating testing activities, validating fixes, and documenting outcomes for release readiness - Building client relationships by responding to service needs and explaining technical findings in clear language - Mentoring junior team members on Guidewire processes, delivery tasks, and issue triage methods - Collaborating across teams to prioritize backlog items and align delivery with operational goals - Leveraging application support tools and automation capabilities to improve service delivery and reduce manual effort What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 3 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for a Master's or Masters of Business Administration degree in at least one of the following fields of study: Actuarial Science, Business Administration/Management, Business Analytics, Commerce, Computer and Information Science, Computer Applications, Computer Engineering, Computer Engineering & Accounting, Data Processing/Analytics/Science, Economics, Electrical Engineering, Engineering, Engineering and Business, Finance, Information Technology, Management Information Systems, Mathematics, Mechanical Engineering, Operations Management/Research, Risk Management, Software App, Statistics -

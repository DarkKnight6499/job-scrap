P&C) Specialism Assurance Management Level Manager Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Risk Modeling Services P&C Manager you will lead actuarial and risk modeling work that helps clients assess financial risk through statistical modeling, data analysis, and clear recommendations. As a Manager you will guide teams, coach junior staff, manage workstreams, and bring structure to complex assignments while using technology, business knowledge, and sound judgment to support client needs within our Assurance practice. In this role at PwC, you will oversee model development and review, help align deliverables to timelines, support client conversations, and contribute to work that helps organizations navigate regulatory and risk challenges. Responsibilities - Leading property and casualty risk modeling workstreams for insurance clients by reviewing actuarial assumptions, model structure, and deliverable timelines - Developing and interpreting financial risk models using statistical analysis software, actuarial science methods, and complex data analysis - Guiding team members through model implementation, validation activities, and documentation for pricing, reserving, and capital analysis - Reviewing analyses and deliverables for accuracy, consistency, and alignment with client objectives and technical guidance - Managing project plans, budgets, and milestone tracking across multiple engagements while addressing scope changes and issue resolution - Coaching analysts and senior associates on statistical modeling, financial modeling, and risk analysis techniques - Communicating model findings, assumptions, and limitations to client stakeholders through presentations and written reports - Identifying opportunities to improve modeling methods, automate repeatable tasks, and apply digital tooling to enhance delivery What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree in one of the following fields of study: Actuarial Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Economics, Applied Mathematics, Mathematical Statistics - At least 4 years of experience - At least one of the following: Fellow of Society of Actuaries or equivalent, certification requirement may be waived if the selected individual has more experience in the relevant field What Sets You Apart - Experience in property and casualty risk modeling - Background in model validation or model development - Applying actuarial methods to insurance risk analyses - Managing project timelines, budgets, and deliverables - Mentoring teams and reviewing work for learning - Using statistical software and digital tools Travel Requirements Up to 20% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $99,000 - $252,450.

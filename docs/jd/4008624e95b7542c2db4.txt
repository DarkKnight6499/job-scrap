Performs key Accounts Payable functions, including payment processing, corporate credit card administration, supplier record maintenance, and expense report review. Collaborates with segment accountants to ensure Accounts Payable transactions are processed accurately and efficiently. JOB RESPONSIBILITIES: • Create, review, and process invoices for payment • Support administration of the corporate credit card programs • Collaborate with internal Accounts Payable teams to maintain accurate supplier records • Assist associates with corporate credit card expenses and reporting • Coordinate supplier communications related to purchase order processing • Reconcile general ledger accounts by researching and resolving discrepancies • Support the annual preparation of supplier Forms 1099 • Identify and communicate solutions that improve processes • Document projects, report progress, and follow through to completion **Hourly pay range this for this position is - $23-$25 EDUCATION: Associate’s or bachelor’s degree; accounting-related field preferred EXPERIENCE: One or more years of accounting experience preferred COMPETENCIES: • Working knowledge of fundamental accounting principles • Proficiency with computers and workplace technology • Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines • Customer-focused approach when working with diverse stakeholders • Strong communication and teamwork skills • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills • Ability to work independently and demonstrate initiative Our benefits package includes medical, dental, vision, HSA and FSA, generous earned time off, 401K/student loan repayment, life insurance & AD&D insurance, employee assistance program, employee stock purchase program, tuition reimbursement, performance-based incentive pay, short- and long-term disability, and a robust wellness program.

The Loss Prevention Impound Associate is responsible for researching, analyzing, and managing all activities related to impounded company vehicles across ABG Division. This role ensures timely recovery, accurate documentation, and full financial recoupment of impound-related expenses. The specialist serves as a key liaison between law enforcement agencies, impound vendors, field operations, and internal departments, while also supporting broader Loss Prevention functions as needed. What You’ll Do: Research, analyze, and upload impound information into the Compass Workforce Management System and Allstate Omni portal with a high degree of accuracy Evaluate, obtain, and provide all required documentation to expedite the recovery of vehicles from impound lots. Process & place impounded vehicles on and off impound alert hold Researching & responding to inquiries from our vendor(s) in a timely manner Contact law enforcement agencies regarding Police holds and provide updates to area security managers and field operations. Review and work various exception reports Analyze and close rental contracts associated with impound events, ensuring all related charges are accurately applied and recovered Process incoming impound lien mail notifications and ensure timely entry into the appropriate systems Participate in cross-training within the Loss Prevention department and support additional functions as operational needs arise. undefined Perks You’ll Get: Access to Medical, Dental, Vision, Life and Disability insurance Eligible to elect other voluntary benefits including: Group Legal, Identity Theft, Accident Insurance, FSA, additional life insurance coverages 401(k) Retirement Plan with company matched contributions Full training to learn the business and enhance professional skills Employee discounts, including discounted prices on the purchase of Avis/Budget cars Access to an Employee Assistance Program for services including counseling, financial and legal consultation, referrals for care service and more What We're Looking For: College Degree or equivalent work experience. 3 years car rental security loss prevention experience is preferred but not required.

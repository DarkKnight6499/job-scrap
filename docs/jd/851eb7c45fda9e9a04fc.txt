The Associate Capital Markets (Equity) reports to the Senior Director Capital Markets and assists with evaluating, analyzing and securing third party debt for Nuveen Real Estate's direct equity investments including acquisition, development and refinancing opportunities. In addition to placement of secured third party financing solutions for the US equity business, the Associate Capital Markets (Equity) assists in all aspects of securing third party debt for fund level facilities including subscription lines, repo-warehouse lines, Private Placements, Net Asset Value (NAV) facilities and secured/un-secured term/revolving facilities. Key Responsibilities and Duties Facilitates and supports the due diligence process between the capital markets deal team lead, investments and asset management teams and prospective lenders for all equity financing opportunities. Prepares the initial draft financing offering memorandums for in-house or third party broker lead financing requests. Assists senior team members in the financial analysis of lender quotes and hedging strategies to determine optimal leverage outcomes for the equity business. Participates in discussions with the relevant portion of the equity business with any financial inputs required for Investment Committee memos. Facilitates senior team members with the loan or facility closing process with internal and external stakeholders. Facilitates senior team members with any required ad hoc analysis of financial and operating performance to assist with investor, client and lender due diligence for sector fund products. Interfaces with key internal groups including debt capital markets, research, legal, insurance, asset management, portfolio management, accounting and treasury. Prepares analysis and helps monitor market dynamics and trends while seeking the input of the Nuveen Real Estate research team and other professionals in analytic activities.

We are seeking a highly organized, proactive, and professional Administrative Assistant to support the Board Relations team within the Office of the Corporate Secretary. This role requires exceptional stakeholder management, strong communication skills, and the ability to effectively manage competing priorities. Proven experience managing complex executive calendars and schedules, travel coordination, meeting planning, documentation, data entry, and internal/external communications. Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint); experience with collaboration tools such as SharePoint and Teams. Strong time management, organizational, and prioritization skills with exceptional attention to detail. Excellent verbal and written communication skills and the ability to interact professionally with a wide range of stakeholders. Professional demeanor with the ability to work independently and collaboratively across teams.

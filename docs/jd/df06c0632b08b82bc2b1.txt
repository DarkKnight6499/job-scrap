Job Description Position Overview Our Digital Manufacturing Operations Team supporting our vibrant West Point site is seeking a Process Automation Engineer Associate to join our team. This role will provide automation system support and expertise to enable our Manufacturing Division in the reliable and compliant supply of quality vaccine products to customers and patients across the globe. Responsible for problem resolution, implementation of process control, data collection techniques, and troubleshooting in compliance with SDLC, GMP, safety, and environmental regulations. Actively support and participate in a collaborative and respectful work environment, including significant interaction with Manufacturing, Quality, and Engineering groups. Supports a broad spectrum of automated systems within GMP Manufacturing Operations. Key Functions Work independently and as a team member while demonstrating Integrity, Precision, Accomplishment, Motivational Ambition, and Respect. Comply with Global Policies, Procedures, Guidelines, regulatory requirements, and current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP) in the performance of day-to-day activities and all applicable job functions. Provide automation system support and expertise to assist in the manufacture of quality vaccine products and support site priorities. Support all automation system components associated with manufacturing processes, including shop floor support of manufacturing activities. Design, implement, test, and close change controls related to automation corrective actions and continuous improvement initiatives. Build relationships with functional counterparts and business stakeholders to proactively identify and resolve technical issues. Lead or participate in investigations of automation incidents and issues to prevent recurrence, including development and implementation of corrective actions. Maintain awareness of emerging automation technologies and industry trends and provide recommendations for adoption where appropriate. Provide off-shift, weekend, evening, remote, or on-site automation support for 24/7 manufacturing operations as required. Education BS in Engineering, Computer Science or related field or High School Diploma and with 2 years of experience (GMP Manufacturing and/or Manufacturing Automation) Experience, Skills & Knowledge Required Strong verbal and written communication skills and interpersonal abilities, with the capability to effectively interact with colleagues at all organizational levels and external partners. Ability to work effectively in a team environment while managing individual responsibilities. Preferred Experience in the pharmaceutical or biotechnology industry. Knowledge of and/or experience with process control systems. Experience supporting aseptic processing and/or sterile manufacturing operations. Technical and operational experience with computer systems, automation technologies, and programming.

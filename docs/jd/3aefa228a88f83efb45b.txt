Aon is looking for an Investment Consulting Asset/Liability Management Specialist Entry-level role within Aon’s Investment Analytics & Strategy (IAS) team. The Asset/Liability Management (ALM) group within the IAS Team provides advisory investment consulting services, primarily in the areas of asset/liability modeling and asset allocation, for institutional investors with specific liability or cash flow requirements. Detailed Responsibilities: Uses technical, analytical, and communication skills to support ALM consultants and broader teams in all aspects of asset/liability modeling and investment‑related analysis Takes accountability and ownership of assigned modeling studies, from data preparation through model runs, validation, and documentation Supports ALM work for multiple pension and retiree-medical plans, balancing recurring studies with ad hoc analytical requests Prepares, validates, and reconciles asset and liability data inputs for ALM models, ensuring consistency with actuarial valuations and investment records Sets up and runs ALM models to evaluate alternative investment and risk‑management strategies Analyzes model results to assess the impact of different investment strategies on funded status, contribution requirements, and key risk metrics over time Performs reconciliations and reasonableness checks on ALM results Summarizes ALM findings in charts and exhibits, and prepares concise written interpretations of results for internal and client‑facing materials Participates in internal meetings with senior ALM and client‑team members to review results Technical Skills and Knowledge: Excellent technical and analytical skills High level of accuracy and attention to detail Basic knowledge of capital markets and investments and/or a keen interest in developing this knowledge Proficiency with specific software packages (e.g., Microsoft Office; Excel skills strongly preferred) Demonstrates clear written and verbal communication skills Actuarial knowledge is beneficial, but not required Education: Bachelor’s degree in relevant areas (e.g., business, finance, economics, actuarial, math, statistics) How we support our colleagues In addition to our comprehensive benefits package, we encourage an inclusive workforce.

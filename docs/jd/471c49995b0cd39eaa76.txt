What You Will Do : Operational Oversight : Support and advise operations management team on safety, compliance, and sustainability programs, ensuring alignment with EH&S goals. Ensure projects are appropriately defined in EHS SMART to support achievement of EHS goals. Monitor and execute strategies for EH&S initiatives to ensure sustained compliance with local regulations, government standards, and RTX/Collins Aerospace requirements. Ensure regulatory permit applications and agency correspondence are submitted on a timely basis. Lead and facilitate onsite reviews of EH&S Management System elements. Participate in and support Central EH&S audit teams, as needed. Incident Management : Coordinates and facilitates incident investigations as needed, ensuring lessons learned are implemented. Strategic Leadership : Develop and execute long-term strategies to minimize occupational injuries and ensure compliance with local regulations, government standards, and RTX/Collins Aerospace requirements. Lead initiatives to standardize and optimize EH&S tools, processes, and systems across the Riverside, CA site. Stakeholder Collaboration : Build and sustain relationships with site operational stakeholders, integrating EH&S into business processes. Coordinate with site leader, and Central EH&S teams to address concerns and initiatives. Collaborate with the Asia-Pac and EMEA field service leads and the Central EH&S organization to align global policies with local standards and requirements.

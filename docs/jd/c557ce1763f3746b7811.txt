What's in it for you: The Associate, Corporate Strategy is a key member of the Corporate Strategy team, providing analytical and program management support for strategy projects. This leader in this role will lead highly visible and prioritized strategic initiatives to support executive decision-making on consequential topics for Trane Technologies. Typical projects include market sizing, competitive analysis, portfolio optimization, M&A strategy, growth and/or operational improvement projects as well as functional and transformation projects. Based in our Davidson Headquarters, this role reports directly to the Vice President of Corporate Strategy and will have an Enterprise-wide scope of responsibility. What you will do: Support all elements of the company-wide strategic planning process, including the analysis of macro and micro economic factors, strategic benchmarking, current/future portfolio review, and competitive analyses. Support Trane Technologies strategic growth initiatives execution, working with business units to help them define and refine business strategy. Conduct on-going market assessments to identify, evaluate and prioritize specific strategic alternatives and initiatives to drive growth and shareholder returns. Build analytical models and perform market and financial analyses to evaluate opportunities. Drive externally focused orientation, particularly with regard to awareness and insightful analysis of global economic, market & transaction trends, and identification & exploration of adjacencies to our core businesses to identify new opportunities and specific M&A prospects. Support other global initiatives and projects assigned by Executive Leadership Team. Limited overnight travel will be required, less than 20%. What you will bring: Bachelor’s degree in science or engineering or business administration Strong ability to build trusted relationships with stakeholders across functions, collaborating effectively to deliver business-focused solutions and shared outcomes. Highly accountable with a strong sense of ownership, consistently delivering high-quality work and driving results with limited direction. Demonstrated ability to synthesize complex information, prioritize competing demands, and execute against commitments aligned with broader business and strategic objectives. Strong organizational and problem-solving skills, with the ability to navigate complex business environments, connect disparate inputs, and drive workstreams to completion. Comfortable operating in ambiguity, rapidly learning new business contexts, and applying structured problem-solving and analytical approaches to identify innovative opportunities and solutions. Strong analytical capabilities, including experience leveraging data, market insights, and business performance metrics to develop recommendations and support business decision-making.

Responsibilities Position Summary The Treasury Management Consultant (TMC) is responsible for growing Treasury Management revenue, deposits, and client relationships within an assigned Commercial Banking portfolio and territory. The TMC serves as a trusted advisor by delivering customized treasury, liquidity, payments, fraud mitigation, and working capital solutions that help clients optimize cash flow, improve operational efficiency, and reduce financial risk. This role is focused on SPECIALTY/GNP clients. The TMC partners closely with Relationship Managers and internal product specialists to develop and execute client strategies, expand share of wallet, and drive profitable growth. Seen as a trusted advisor, the TMC works closely with Commercial clients to understand their business, optimize cash flow and minimize financial risk. The TMC partners with, informally/formally mentors, and aides in the development Treasury Management Analysts (TMAs) to support a strong talent pipeline. Through coaching, collaborative client engagement, and exposure to consultative sales activities, the TMC helps TMAs build treasury management expertise, sales acumen, and readiness for future TMC roles. The TMC leverages TMAs as strategic partners to enhance client outcomes while developing the next generation of Treasury Management talent.

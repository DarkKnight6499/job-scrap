JOB DESCRIPTION We are looking for a motivated and career-oriented candidate with a proactive approach and strong analytical, Excel, and data set management skills. As a Senior Financial Analyst, Digital & Ad Tech, you will step into a critical position responsible for financial modeling to support the labor capitalization and software development process. The Senior Financial Analyst will work on monthly budgets and forecasts, key financial reporting, ad hoc analysis, tracking of KPIs, and creating dashboards and tools to review operational performance and initiatives. This is a high-visibility and challenging position rich in data analytics. The Senior Financial Analyst will be responsible for providing key budget/forecast and actuals analysis to understand the Digital Division's financial performance. This analysis is key to the management team’s decision-making and interpretation of financial results.

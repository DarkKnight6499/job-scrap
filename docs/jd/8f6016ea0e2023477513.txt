About the Team Global Private Equity Analytics sits at the intersection of Finance, Operations, IT, Investor Relations, and Capital Markets. We build the metrics, tools, and data products that power decision-making across the platform — from Power BI and Excel reporting to enterprise applications built on our central data lake. Everyone on this team, from associate to leadership, takes a hands-on approach to reaching our goal of an ecosystem of dynamic, scalable, explainable solutions. The Role We're looking for a Senior Associate to join our data function. This person will be a critical link between raw financial/operational data and the LPs, deal teams, senior leadership, and Investor Relations. You'll spend meaningful time both delivering against existing processes and improving them. What You'll Do LP Data Delivery: Support fundraising and LP engagement by delivering accurate, timely data and analysis in response to investor requests, leveraging existing tools and data infrastructure. This includes bespoke deliverables, held to a high bar for accuracy and turnaround time. Process Improvement: Identify repeatable, manual, or error-prone workflows and build tools to fix them — whether that's a better Power BI model, Alteryx workflow, a script, or an application or workflow built with Claude Code — with the goal of faster, more automated analytics. Enterprise Tooling: Contribute to enterprise-level applications built with agentic coding tools, working alongside the broader analytics team to extend what our data platform can do. Data Quality & Governance: Partner with the Data Governance team to design and maintain an interpretable, well-documented data lake that stakeholders outside our team, including non-technical users, can leverage and trust. Cross-Functional Partnership: Work directly with Finance, Investor Relations, Capital Markets, IT, Data Governance, and Investment Professionals to understand upstream data and downstream needs.

Pzena Investment Management, LLC is a registered investment adviser and broker-dealer managing approximately $85 billion in global equity assets. We provide equity investment management services for institutional investors, leading corporate, public and individual clients on a world-wide basis by way of separate accounts, sub-advised mutual funds, and other commingled vehicles since our firm founding in 1995. Our team of over 170 employees is mostly based in our headquarters in Manhattan and we have additional offices in London, Dublin and Melbourne. We are a growing company, in the size of our team, our locations, and our assets under management. We seek partners, not just employees, at every level in the firm and are looking for an exceptional new team member to grow with us. At Pzena, we invest in your career and have a strong track record of internal mobility. Position Overview: As a member of Marketing & Client Services, you will have critical interaction with all members of the team in supporting new business and servicing existing clients. You will be responsible for completing RFPs/DDQs and responding to a variety of client/prospect support requests to help grow our business. This role will report directly to the RFP Manager. Core Responsibilities: Proactively address Request for Proposals (RFPs), Due Diligence Questionnaires (DDQs), recurring client reporting, and ad hoc client/prospect requests Review, edit, and approve RFPs, DDQs, client reporting, and ad hoc client/prospect requests Lead on-going projects such as the implementation and maintenance of new tools, AI skills, and RFP library and consultant database governance Draft responses to new/unique questions for prospects or clients Provide day-to-day support to junior staff Articulate in writing our investment philosophy, process, and outlook; and access relevant performance and other attribution data Understand and analyze performance attribution and assist with reporting and commentary as needed Complete ad hoc projects to support sales and management team as requested Strategically develop best-in-class communications materials Partner with investment team, operations, legal, and other subject matter experts throughout the firm to coordinate accurate RFP/DDQ responses Assist with improvement and maintenance of standard language in RFP and consultant databases Build an in-depth understanding of the firm’s products and strategy and use financial acumen as it relates to RFP materials Duties, responsibilities, and activities may change at any time. Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree required 5+ years of experience in financial services industry Must have asset management marketing and/or RFP experience Exceptional writing skills Proficiency with Microsoft Word and Excel Knowledge of investments and financial products Experience with Responsive or similar proposal management software (preferred) Competencies: Diligence & Attention to Detail: demonstrate accuracy and thoroughness; monitor own work to ensure quality control of all work product. Planning & Organizing: proactively prioritize and plan tasks; use time efficiently; maintain strong organizational habits. Interpersonal Skills & Teamwork: a collaborative team player who is professional, respectful, honest, open to new ideas, and willing to pitch in wherever needed to get the job done well. Communication: communicate clearly and professionally across all formats; produce polished and complete documents; present data effectively. Dependability: consistently a productive and positive team member; respond to direction and developmental feedback and solicit feedback to improve performance. Initiative: take ownership and accountability, seek out additional responsibilities, and work toward goals with growing independence. Results Oriented: understand and analyze work product; apply critical thinking to ensure accuracy of deliverables. Problem Solving: identify the cause and effect of issues and analyze internal and external information to arrive at appropriate solutions.

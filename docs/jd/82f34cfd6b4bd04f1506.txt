SUMMARY The Associate Commercial Relationship Manager serves as an entry level commercial banking position with functionality ranging from business development utilizing a small business platform to supporting senior bankers as an apprentice relative to portfolio management and experience with complex credit functions. This role drives shareholder value through a collaborative effort to generate quality earnings through interest income growth, via maintaining margin and credit quality, and non-interest income through collaboration with business partners while simultaneously understanding the importance of expense control. This position maintains an average loan portfolio size of up to $20MM, revenues to the bank of at least $800K, and manages at least 75 bank relationships, or may provide support to a Commercial Relationship Manager II or Senior Commercial Relationship Manager with a loan portfolio exceeding $50MM. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Achieves individual annual production and growth goals for loan, deposits, and fees to ensure the bank meets its overall financial targets and conforms to credit terms. Initiates and develops new business through outside business development activities either through monitored calling efforts utilizing a small business banking platform or collaboration with a Commercial Relationship Manager II or Senior Commercial Relationship Manager. Ensures ongoing contact with new clients to enhance client’s experience with First Interstate Bank. Develops a comprehensive understanding of the client’s needs based on the review and analysis of personal bank business financial data gathered through relationship reviews and personal meetings. Uses internal customer sourcing software to generate leads, track activities and make referrals to requisite business partners and bankers. Actively participates in community organizations to source business development opportunities and demonstrates First Interstate Bank’s commitment to the communities we serve. Builds client and prospect referral pipeline via identification and development of internal and external center of influence. Develops and maintains an in-depth knowledge of products and services as well as knowledge of competitors and competitive products. Proactively promotes bank products and services and keeps clients informed of all the financial solutions First Interstate Bank offers to meet their needs. Assembles cross-functional team to respond to customer requests when not able to address requests personally. Works on complex problems requiring in-depth evaluation of client information in conjunction with Commercial Relationship Manager II and Senior Commercial Relationship Manager. Maintains follow up on existing loans to ensure compliance with internal procedures and other required terms. Research and studies industrial, commercial, economic and financial situations relevant to existing and new business. Actively engages, demonstrates and fosters the First Interstate Bank Mission, Vision, and Values by executing the Service Commitments. Maintains compliance with all bank regulations for assigned job function and applies to designated job responsibilities. Follows all Bank policies, procedures, and compliance regulations and completes all required annual training.

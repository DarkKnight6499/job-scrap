About the Role: OnDeck, an Enova company, is a leading small business lender dedicated to helping entrepreneurs access fast, simple, and transparent financing. By combining cutting-edge data and technology with a human touch, we deliver an exceptional lending experience tailored to small business owners. We are looking for an Underwriting Manager to oversee the production, quality, and performance of our small business loan decisioning. In this role, you will lead a team of Underwriting Supervisors and Team Leads, focusing on developing these front-line managers, setting operational strategy, and driving high-level team performance. Reporting to the Senior Manager of Underwriting, this position is ideal for an experienced leader ready to scale their impact by managing through others. What You'll Do: Utah Office Culture & Site Leadership: Serve as the culture champion and leader for the Utah office, fostering a collaborative, high-energy environment, driving local employee engagement, and building a strong sense of community across teams. Leadership Development & Coaching: Lead, coach, and develop a team of Underwriting Supervisors and Team Leads; empower your front-line managers to effectively drive their respective pods of production underwriters. Operational Strategy & Execution: Establish consistent workflows, quality standards, and throughput targets across all pods, translating high-level organizational strategy into clear daily execution. Risk & Escalations: Serve as the senior escalation point for high-complexity decisions exceeding direct report authority, partnering closely with Credit & Risk to ensure policy clarity and alignment. Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work alongside Sales, Product, Compliance, and Operations to streamline the borrower experience and optimize automated/manual decisioning intersections. Workforce Planning & Bench Building: Own capacity modeling, scheduling, and talent acquisition to build a resilient leadership pipeline for the growing Utah site. What We're Looking For: 5+ years of underwriting experience in small business or commercial lending 7+ years managing people, with at least some experience managing other managers or team leads Strong command of SMB credit fundamentals; cash flow analysis, financial statement review, and risk assessment Proven ability to develop leaders: setting clear expectations, giving direct feedback, and building capability over time Has an operational mindset; comfortable with metrics, process design, and driving accountability at scale Strong communicator who can translate between frontline execution and senior leadership expectations Experience working in a high-volume, technology-enabled lending environment Background in fintech or online lending platforms is preferred Familiarity with automated decisioning and where manual underwriting intersects with it Exposure to fair lending or SMB-specific compliance requirements (Reg B, etc.) Compensation: The budgeted annual salary range for this position is $102,000 to $148,000.

Director, Climate Risk Consulting This is a hybrid role with the flexibility to work both virtually and from our New York City office. Aon is in the business of better decisions At Aon, we build decisions for the better to protect and enrich the lives of people around the world. As an organization, we are united through trust as one inclusive team and we are passionate about helping our colleagues and clients succeed. Aon’s Climate Risk Advisory offers physical and transition risk diagnostics, consulting, and risk transfer solutions worldwide. The team includes more than 30 climate experts from various regions, solutions, and industries. It is deeply integrated within Aon’s core business, using over 100 modelers, 40 full-time scientists and engineers, and thousands of industry and regional specialists. The advisory team provides customized solutions and climate insights to help clients improve their decisions about climate and catastrophe-related risks. What the day will look like This role acts as a collaborator with regional practice leaders in Aon’s Climate Risk Advisory. It aims to promote excellence, coordination, efficiency, and effectiveness across client engagements and central initiatives. The individual will lead all aspects of project execution, manage several ongoing workstreams, and develop positive relationships with clients, account executives, producers, and the modelers, scientists, and consultants within the wider Aon team. As a trusted partner to practice leadership, the successful candidate will coordinate priorities, support communication, and address operational challenges. This will ensure consistent, high-quality delivery, engage colleagues and clients, and help expand the Climate Risk Advisory’s solution suite.

Wealth Management Virtual Engagement Associate (VEA) Program This application is for candidates who are interested in roles with anticipated start dates throughout 2027. Position Overview: The VEA Program is designed to give you firsthand experience working with the top Financial Advisors (FAs) in the industry at a leading global financial institution. In the VEA Program there are several different roles that are designed to support Financial Advisors in areas of business development, including the Client Engagement Associate, Financial Planning Associate, Workplace Engagement Associate, and Wealth Solutions Associate. Upon hire, you will be placed in one of these roles based on your experience, education, credentials, and business needs. The goal of the program is to give you a strong foundation in several systems/tools, business areas, and skills. The VEA Program is structured as a two-year and three-month commitment (including the licensing and training period) and is designed to build a strong foundation across Wealth Management and client engagement. The length of the program enables you to earn your licenses, be trained in the role, gain additional professional designations, network, and capture valuable experience working with different FAs to understand how they run their practice. The insight you will gain is unique, and if successful, will propel you into a fruitful career at Morgan Stanley. Many successful associates continue their journey beyond the initial program period by gaining experience in new areas, broadening their expertise, and pursuing additional opportunities within the VEA organization. The VEA Program operates in-person, across six office locations: Alpharetta, GA | Chicago, IL | Dallas, TX | New York Metro | Tempe, AZ. This is not a remote position. The Experience You Could Gain: The VEA Program includes a rigorous licensing period, followed by training and assessments designed to prepare you to succeed in the role. Through participation in the VEA Program, you could gain experience in: Partnering with Financial Advisors to understand their business goals and tailor client engagement strategies to support growth. Building and executing digital and targeted client outreach strategies , including social media/website content and segmented client communication campaigns. Developing structured communication cadences that increase meaningful client touchpoints and deepen relationships over time. Analyzing client relationships and engagement data to identify opportunities and deliver tactical recommendations to enhance the client experience. Supporting workplace engagement initiatives , including communicating the value of a financial advisor partnership to workplace participants. Applying financial planning tools and resources to gather client information, build/analyze plans, and communicate results and next steps clearly. Educating clients on Morgan Stanley wealth solutions and supporting end-to-end onboarding to drive adoption of relevant capabilities (e.g., cash management and self-directed solutions). Developing product and solutions fluency across advisory programs and select investment/insurance solutions, with exposure to complex implementation and client-facing reporting. Creating and presenting business insights (e.g., reports and readouts) for FAs and leadership, and sharing best practices to continuously improve strategy.

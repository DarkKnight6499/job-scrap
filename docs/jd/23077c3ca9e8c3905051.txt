The Risk Control Consulting Director-Healthcare serves as an enterprise leader responsible for advancing risk control strategy, technical excellence and underwriting support across Allied Healthcare and Life Science business segments. This individual contributor is responsible for the assessment, evaluation, and delivery of complex risk control consulting services addressing the most challenging clinical, operational, regulatory, professional liability, and product liability exposures. This role will also provide a centralized source of expertise for Life Sciences, particularly in the area of product liability involving pharmaceutical manufacturers, biotechnology firms, medical device companies, and other life sciences organizations. JOB DESCRIPTION: Job Description Essential Duties & Responsibilities Performs a combination of duties in accordance with departmental guidelines: Performs onsite or virtual risk evaluation and consultative risk improvement services on the most complex and/or sophisticated accounts. Controls loss exposure and costs and ensures underwriting profit through risk determination for underwriting. Assesses customer's program needs and develops and executes a customized strategy and supporting implementation plan. Manages the delivery of direct evaluative and consultative services. Partners with underwriting and other areas to provide information for risk evaluation and assessment on the most complex and/or sophisticated risks. Participates with senior management in the development and implementation of risk control policy, marketing and business strategy. Develops and maintains business relationships with internal and external business partners. Provides account coordination of risk control services for multinational and global insured accounts. Identifies opportunities for new products and services. Provides expertise in specialized area such as allied healthcare, facilities and providers. Mentors less experienced risk control staff and other functional areas. May Participates in the development and presentation of education and training programs. Stays up to date on risk control issues and regulations, industry activity and trends. May represent the company in industry trade groups.

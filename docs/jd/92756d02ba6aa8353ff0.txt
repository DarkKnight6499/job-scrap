Position Overview The Technical Business Unit Associate plays a key role in coordinating between leadership, program management, and engineering teams within the NyproMold Business Unit (BU). This position supports daily BU operations and serves as a technical customer-facing team member, working closely with senior principal tooling engineers to operational clients servicing their asset lifecycle spares management needs. In parallel, the role will support the operational needs of other books of business within the Business Unit by coordinating internal activities, maintaining business processes, and helping improve overall organizational efficiency. This is an ideal opportunity for those looking to gain hands-on experience in business operations, project coordination, and strategic development within a fast-paced, dynamic manufacturing environment, with ample room for growth, creativity, and professional development. Key Responsibilities Support the Sr. Business Unit Director and Technical (BU) Managers & Coordinators in daily operations. Support asset lifecycle management by communicating with clients to understand spare component needs, interpreting technical requirements, coordinating internally across relevant teams, and assisting with proposal development. Over time, develop the knowledge and experience necessary to independently manage spares within the BU. Develop a strong understanding of mold basics, metrology/CMM inspection, and application through training and hands on use. Build on an established business plan aimed at achieving spares revenue & profit contribution objectives. Identify, target & engage customers that fit account profile. Coordinate with leadership, program managers, and engineers to help manage project plans, reports, BOMs, and customer-specific requirements. Maintain regular communication with project teams to ensure transactions align with customer satisfaction and project performance goals. Contribute to business development, including market opportunity identification and growth strategies. Support NyproMold’s marketing initiatives, materials, and website to enhance global brand recognition and footprint along with tracking budget. Assist in project management and customer relationship initiatives (NDAs, UX, NPS, Customer Website Portal, etc.). Update company presentations, forecasts, quote records, team spending. Handle general office administrative tasks such as but not limited to: data entry, health & safety, visits/travel, documentation, customer swag, etc. Monitor ongoing project activities, address challenges, and escalate issues as needed. Lead or assist with other projects as assigned by the BUD & leadership and deemed necessary.

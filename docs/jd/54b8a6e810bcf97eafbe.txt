This position has the opportunity to interface with and have a positive impact on multiple areas of Wintrust's business We hold ourselves accountable to high standards, share wins, operate ethically, and have fun Position Overview: The Advanced IT Business Analyst is a mid-level business analysis position and serves as a key subject matter expert for organizing and coordinating strategic business analysis services. This role will be key to understanding and documenting capabilities and business, functional and technical requirements needed to address business challenges. Business Analysts work closely with Business resources and stakeholders, internal Technology teams, and various vendors and third-party consultants and implementers. The ideal candidate will bring strong experience working within Agile Scrum environments and possess hands-on knowledge of Salesforce and nCino platforms , with the ability to support business process analysis, requirements gathering, solution design, and system administration activities. This includes experience with Salesforce user administration, data and field analysis, system configuration research, and supporting business processes within the nCino platform. Experience within commercial banking or financial services environments is highly preferred. This role requires a collaborative mindset, extensive Agile and Scrum knowledge, and the ability to influence and guide stakeholders while supporting enterprise initiatives that leverage Salesforce, nCino, and other critical business platforms. What You’ll Do Partner with business stakeholders to gather, analyze, and document business, functional, and technical requirements for new initiatives, enhancements, and strategic projects. Serve as a liaison between business and technology teams, ensuring requirements are clearly defined, communicated, and aligned with business objectives. Create, refine, and manage user stories, acceptance criteria, and JIRA backlog items while partnering with development teams throughout the SDLC. Support Salesforce and nCino initiatives by analyzing business processes, gathering requirements, and assisting with platform administration, workflows, and system enhancements. Participate in Agile delivery teams, facilitating requirements discussions, sprint activities, and cross-functional collaboration to drive successful project outcomes. Ensure compliance with Enterprise PMO governance standards, project documentation requirements, and established delivery methodologies. Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to support project implementation, testing, issue resolution, and continuous process improvement efforts.

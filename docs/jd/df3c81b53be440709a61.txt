Responsibilities Berkley Luxury Group is seeking an Underwriting Specialist to join their team. This position can be located anywhere in the Midwest or in MD/DC and will be responsible for growing the book of business in that region. The Underwriting Production Specialist is a key member of the underwriting team reporting to the VP of Underwriting. The primary goal of this position is to profitably grow a book of business by actively partnering with brokers and working effectively with colleagues throughout the organization. This person will plan and budget for the book of business, develop and maintain agency relationships, and execute underwriting decisions using underwriting rules, designated authority and will have strong knowledge of insurance laws and regulations.

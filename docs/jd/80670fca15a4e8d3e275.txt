Job Classification: Investment Management - Investment Operations PGIM Custom Harvest , a division of PGIM Global Wealth, is a pioneer in the development of tax‑beneficial investment strategies using exchange‑traded funds (ETFs) for clients seeking improved after‑tax outcomes. We provide full exposure to a client‑selected benchmark while generating tax alpha through customized SMA portfolios of ETFs. PGIM Custom Harvest manages $7.1 billion on behalf of a wide range of RIAs and wealth managers. What You Will Do PGIM Custom Harvest is launching a fixed income SMA platform and is seeking a Senior Fixed Income Trader to help build and lead this new capability. This is a hands-on role with end-to-end accountability. You will be responsible for trading and managing individual client fixed income portfolios, establishing trading processes, and contributing to the growth of the fixed income SMA business. What You Can Expect Execute trades across a range of fixed income securities (U.S. Treasuries, agencies, investment-grade corporates, and other relevant sectors) for individual client SMA accounts Manage day-to-day portfolio activity, including cash flows, position adjustments, and ongoing risk monitoring Develop and refine trading processes, workflows, and dealer relationships Collaborate with portfolio managers, operations, compliance, and technology partners Maintain a strong focus on best execution, risk management, and fiduciary standards Support the scaling of the fixed income SMA business over time What You Will Bring 7-10 years of fixed income trading experience with a minimum of 3-5 years within an SMA, managed accounts, wealth management, or institutional fixed income platform. Proficiency with industry platforms and tools (Bloomberg, Tradeweb, MarketAxess, etc.) CFA designation is a plus Series 7 and Series 63 (or Series 66) preferred; willingness to obtain if required Ability to build processes, relationships, and infrastructure in a developing business environment Strong communication and collaboration skills; comfortable working across teams and with external counterparties Disciplined approach to risk management with a clear client-first mindset Salary Range Disclosure Prudential is required by state specific laws to include the salary range for this role when hiring a resident in applicable locations.

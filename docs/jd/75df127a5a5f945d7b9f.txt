Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Financial Due Diligence Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Financial Due Diligence Senior Associate you will support transactions by analyzing financial information, focusing on earnings quality, assets, cash flows, and other key deal issues within our Deals practice. As a Senior Associate you will build meaningful client connections, manage assigned workstreams, and develop deliverables while deepening your technical skills, guiding junior team members, and navigating complex situations with professional judgment. In this role at PwC, you will help clients assess the financial profile of mergers, acquisitions, and divestitures, interpret findings to inform decisions, and contribute to work that sits at the center of deal strategy and execution. Responsibilities - Analyzing financial statements, earnings quality, cash flow, and working capital trends for buy-side and sell-side diligence engagements - Evaluating historical and projected performance to identify transaction risks, value drivers, and key deal issues - Building financial models and supporting valuation analysis using transaction data, management reports, and market information - Reviewing quality of earnings adjustments, balance sheet items, and accounting policies to interpret business performance - Coordinating diligence workstreams, tracking deliverables, and responding to client questions across transaction timelines - Developing concise findings, exhibits, and presentations that summarize insights for internal review and client discussions - Mentoring junior team members, reviewing assigned work, and helping prioritize tasks across multiple engagement streams - Strengthening client relationships by anticipating needs, adapting to shifting transaction conditions, and communicating practical recommendations What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 3 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for at least one of the following fields of study: Accounting, Actuarial Science, Analytics/Data Science, Business Administration/Management, Computer Science/Information Systems, Economics, Finance, Health Administration/Public Health, Mathematics/Statistics, Risk Management/Insurance - Demonstrating financial due diligence skills across buy-side or sell-side transactions - Interpreting financial statements, earnings quality, cash flow, and deal drivers - Utilizing data analysis and modeling tools to inform transaction insights - Applying critical thinking to complex deal issues and valuation concepts - Building client relationships while anticipating team and client needs Travel Requirements Up to 60% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $77,000 - $202,000.

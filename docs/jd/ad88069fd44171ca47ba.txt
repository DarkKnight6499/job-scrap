The Opportunity From strategy to execution, the Government & Infrastructure (G&I) practice of Ernst & Young LLP provides a full range of consulting and audit services to help our Federal, State, Local and Education clients implement new ideas to help achieve their mission outcomes. We deliver real change and measurable results through our diverse, high-performing teams, quality work at the highest professional standards, operational know-how from across our global organization, and creative and bold ideas that drive innovation. We enable our government clients to achieve their mission of protecting the nation and serving the people; increasing public safety; improving healthcare for our military, veterans and citizens; delivering essential public services; and helping those in need. EY is ready to help our government build a better working world. Within G&I Risk Consulting, we help government organizations understand and manage risks that can affect mission outcomes, financial performance, operations and public trust. Our teams build and operate the programs that strengthen governance and controls, improve resilience, prevent fraud and financial loss, manage grant and relief funds, enhance compliance, and enable better risk-informed decisions. Across our work, we use data, AI, automation and technology platforms to improve how risks are identified, assessed, monitored and managed. Our Enterprise Risk, Controls & Resilience team helps government organizations govern risk, strengthen controls and stay resilient. Our work spans internal controls management (including OMB Circular A-123 and Federal Managers' Financial Integrity Act requirements), internal audit, integrated and enterprise risk management (ERM), enterprise resilience, third-party risk management (including vendor risk management), and ethics and regulatory compliance. We design, transform and operate these programs, using AI, data analytics and Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) platforms to automate control testing and monitoring, so that government organizations can achieve and sustain clean audit opinions and make risk-informed decisions. As a Risk Consulting Manager in our Enterprise Risk, Controls & Resilience team, you may be assigned to one or more government agencies as your primary clients. You will lead engagements or major workstreams, own engagement quality and economics, develop our people and play an active role in growing the practice by shaping offerings, building client relationships and leading pursuits. You will be a key team member in the development and growth of our G&I Risk business, which is a priority for our firm. Your key responsibilities Client delivery and engagement leadership • Lead internal controls (including OMB Circular A-123, audit readiness and control remediation), internal audit, integrated and enterprise risk management, third-party risk, ethics and regulatory compliance, or enterprise resilience engagements or major workstreams, from planning through closure. • Serve as a direct point of contact for agency leaders, such as CFO, Chief Risk Officer, internal audit and program leaders, including status reporting, managing expectations and resolving issues. • Advise on control and risk program design, including governance, risk appetite, A-123 and state internal control assessment strategy, audit-readiness roadmaps, internal audit transformation, ERM and third-party risk operating models, compliance programs and resilience programs. • Lead the design, build and operation of third-party risk management programs, including governance and policy, risk appetite for vendor relationships, inherent risk tiering, risk-based due diligence standards, contract requirements, ongoing monitoring and offboarding, and advise leaders on critical vendor concentration and fourth-party dependencies. • Own engagement economics: budget-to-actuals, contract terms, burn rates, staffing mix, change requests and margin. • Design the delivery approach, including which testing, documentation, vendor assessment and reporting work is delivered through our Service Delivery Center (SDC), and set requirements, quality standards and acceptance criteria for that work. • Review and approve deliverables to make sure they meet EY quality and risk management standards and working paper documentation standards. • Hold difficult conversations with team members and clients professionally and on time. Practice and people development • Shape offerings, methodologies and accelerators in internal controls, internal audit, ERM, third-party risk, compliance and resilience, including AI-enabled testing, controls monitoring and third-party risk assessments delivered as a managed service; contribute to thought leadership. • Delegate, supervise and review the work of multiple Staff and Seniors, and provide timely feedback and career advice. • Serve as a formal counselor to Staff and Seniors, including coaching and participating in the firm-wide performance review process. • Recruit, interview and onboard talent. Business development • Identify, shape and qualify new opportunities, for example from audit results, oversight findings, vendor-related incidents or new mission requirements, with existing and new clients. • Lead proposal and orals development (solution, staffing model, pricing inputs and win themes) with Senior Managers and Partners. • Build account knowledge: client mission priorities, audit and risk posture, budget cycles, contract vehicles and upcoming needs.

The Difference You Make: The Quantitative Risk Modeling team is responsible for the development, implementation, monitoring, and governance of quantitative credit risk models that support Santander's commercial lending portfolios. The team partners closely with Risk, Credit, Business Line, Data, Technology, and Model Risk Management stakeholders to enhance the bank's risk assessment framework, improve portfolio management, and meet regulatory expectations. Position Summary The Sr. Associate, Risk Modeling conducts robust researches and validations of a wide variety of risk models against established standards, develops benchmarks, and challenges and replicates models where applicable. The incumbent manages models of advanced complexity and leads special projects and/or programs. Reviews and assesses overall model health within a given framework, identifies potential problems and works with stakeholders to resolve. Conducts robust researches and validations of a wide variety of models against established standards, develops benchmarks, challenges and replicates models where applicable. Assesses model performance and evaluates model assumptions and weaknesses. Collaborates with model owners and develops to understand the business context for models. Supports senior model risk personnel in their communications with risk committees, auditors, regulators, and senior management regarding model risk and its potential effects on the risk profile to the organization. Improves researching new modeling methodologies and new approaches to the management of model risk. Prepares risk model reports, describing the results of the validation analyses and list the recommendations for addressing any issues identified. Assists in developing and providing training and support to management and employees regarding model development strategies and programs. Prepares model development related reporting for internal and external stakeholders. Provides effective communication to model, business and model risk owner groups. Improves the model development standards which have been aligned to the Model Risk Management Framework within the company. Proactively seek improvements to the credit models that increase the percentage of new loan application volume that can benefit from automated decisioning versus manual underwriting.

Purpose Contributes to the overall success of the FICC Index team and the Global Analytics and Financial Engineering team ensuring specific individual goals, plans, initiatives are executed / delivered in support of the team’s business strategies and objectives. Ensures all activities conducted are in compliance with governing regulations, internal policies and procedures Accountabilities Develop and maintain components of a cross-asset index back-testing and simulation platform supporting systematic strategies across equities, fixed income, FX, commodities, futures, and multi-asset portfolios. Contribute to the enhancement of the QIS Index platform by building and supporting calculation engines, portfolio analytics, risk reporting, and performance attribution tools. Assist in the development of quantitative libraries and services used for index construction, rebalancing, execution modeling, transaction cost analysis, and strategy evaluation. Work closely with Structuring, Trading, Research, and Technology teams to implement, test, and deploy systematic investment strategies and bespoke client solutions. Build analytical tools to support portfolio construction, factor analysis, scenario analysis, stress testing, and risk management activities. Support the automation of workflows related to trade decomposition, hedge generation, booking processes, and operational controls for QIS products. Develop and maintain integrations with market data providers, pricing services, and internal data platforms used for strategy research and index calculations. Create monitoring dashboards and data quality controls to support production processes and the daily oversight of live QIS strategies. Participate in the delivery of scalable, production-grade solutions by collaborating with technology teams on testing, deployment, and release management activities. Provide technical expertise and quantitative support to stakeholders across Trading, Risk Management, Product Control, Audit, and Technology. Champions a customer focused culture to deepen client relationships and leverage broader Bank relationships, systems and knowledge.

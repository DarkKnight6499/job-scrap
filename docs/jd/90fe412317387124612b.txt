About the Role Within Corporate Treasury, Deposits Coverage & Strategy (“DCS”) is a unique opportunity for individuals at all levels to oversee the firm’s deposit portfolio and directly contribute to executing the firm’s funding strategy. Working closely with the Global Treasurer and other members of senior management, DCS is responsible for the design and execution of the firm’s deposit strategy as well as the primary point of contact for all deposit business lines through Treasury’s global “business coverage” model. The team is also responsible for managing the firm’s deposit sweep portfolio, and partnering with senior divisional resource managers to raise deposits aligned with the firm’s strategic objectives. The team seeks to optimize the firm’s funding mix to minimize cost in a risk-conscious manner and is a key participant in decision making regarding the allocation of firm liquidity. DCS ‘ approach to asset-liability management is both holistic and practical; individuals who excel in this role are equally adept at detailed trade-level recommendations and strategic thinking and are expected to develop deep knowledge of the funding products raised within Treasury, as well as the underlying business and client activity supported by that funding.

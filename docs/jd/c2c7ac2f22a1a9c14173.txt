The purpose of this job is to provide risk analysis and daily reporting support of Trading exposures, monitor exposures against PFJ set risk limits, and bring to the attention of the Market Risk Manager any breach of risk limits. Review, analyze, interpret, and provide daily risk exposure reporting; identify and resolve issues related to market risk Monitor Trading activity and report any breaches in positions or risk limits. Monitor exposure against PFJ set Risk Limits and report any breaches to Head of Market Risk. Monitor overnight risk system processes each morning to make sure there were no issues with source system data extracts, including generated log files. Monitor intra-day trading activity such as new trades and potential P&L changes due to changes in market conditions outside normal trading activities. Analyze and understand positions being taken by Traders and design ad hoc reports to communicate major P&L movements and potential market impact on these positions. Identify areas for improvements and continual development of risk systems Articulate factors driving day-to-day P&L changes.

USON Overview The US Oncology Network is a leader in community-based oncology care, bringing together independent oncology providers and supporting practice business operations so providers can focus on patient care. Role Overview The Senior Financial Analyst - Revenue Cycle Controllership supports the financial integrity, visibility, and performance of US Oncology revenue-cycle operations. The role owns recurring close, reconciliation, planning, forecasting, reporting, and analysis for an assigned portfolio of Regional Business Offices and may support headquarters revenue-cycle expenses. The analyst partners with US Oncology Finance, Accounting, Revenue Cycle Operations, Finance Systems, and USON practices to explain results, identify risks and opportunities, strengthen controls, and improve decision-making. Key Responsibilities Financial Close, Reconciliations & Controllership Perform monthly close activities, including journal entries, account reconciliations, accruals, account substantiation, and review of supporting documentation. Validate balance-sheet and income-statement activity, investigate variances and reconciling items, and coordinate timely resolution with Accounting and operational owners. Maintain clear close documentation, support control execution, and contribute to audit readiness and consistent application of McKesson policies. Revenue Cycle Financial Reporting & Analysis Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual reporting and variance analysis versus budget, forecast, prior year, and other relevant scenarios. Analyze Regional Business Office and headquarters revenue-cycle costs, including payroll, technology, vendor, and other operating expenses. Translate financial and operational results into concise, decision-oriented summaries that explain key drivers, risks, opportunities, and recommended actions. Forecasting, Budgeting & Long-Range Planning Support monthly forecasts, annual budgets, and long-range plans for assigned Regional Business Offices and applicable headquarters revenue-cycle expenses. Build driver-based models and scenario analyses that connect staffing, productivity, service volumes, technology, and operating assumptions to financial outcomes. Maintain forecast accuracy, document assumptions, and identify actions to optimize performance and mitigate emerging risk. Practice & Business Partnership Partner with USON practices, Revenue Cycle Operations, Finance, and Accounting to articulate financial performance and provide transparent explanations of costs, metrics, and trends. Prepare and present executive-ready materials and business cases for Finance and operational leaders. Respond to ad hoc questions with well-supported analysis, clear source traceability, and practical recommendations. Controls, Data Quality & Issue Resolution Identify data-quality, mapping, process, or control issues affecting close, reporting, reconciliations, or management decisions. Coordinate root-cause analysis and corrective actions across PeopleSoft, SAP, Essbase, FDW, iReserve, billing-system, and reporting stakeholders as applicable. Escalate significant issues promptly and track resolution through sustainable closure. Process Improvement, Automation & Standardization Drive simplification and standardization of recurring close, reconciliation, forecast, and reporting activities. Use advanced Excel, reporting tools, automation, and AI-enabled capabilities to improve speed, accuracy, scalability, and insight. Document standard work and support implementation of new processes, systems, and operating-model changes. Team Contribution & Professional Development Operate as a self-directed, dependable team member who manages multiple priorities and delivers accurate work on schedule. Build strong working relationships, share knowledge, and contribute to a culture of accountability, inclusion, thoughtful challenge, and continuous improvement.

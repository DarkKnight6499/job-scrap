The Difference You Make The Senior Financial Risk Analytics Engineer plays a critical role in supporting the Bank’s reserving, stress-testing, underwriting, and financial forecasting activities. This position is responsible for designing, developing, maintaining, and enhancing data workflows, analytical solutions, and credit loss models that support regulatory compliance, risk management, and strategic decision-making. Working closely with Finance, Risk, IT, Data Management, and business partners, this role transforms complex business requirements into scalable analytical and technology solutions that drive actionable insights across the organization. Position Summary Research, scope, and manage the business requirements, workflows, data needs, resource requirements, and implementation plans for CECL credit loss models supporting stress-testing, reserving, and underwriting activities. Design, develop, code, implement, and maintain analytical models and supporting infrastructure. Develop and maintain expert knowledge of Webster’s data systems, databases, tools, and reporting environments. Design, build, and maintain robust data workflows and automated processes that support forecasting, reserving, and risk management activities. Collaborate with IT, Data Management, Finance, Risk, and business partners to develop and enhance analytical solutions and reporting capabilities. Create user-friendly reports, dashboards, and data visualizations to support decision-making and performance monitoring. Anticipate, identify, and resolve data quality, workflow, and reporting challenges. Maintain comprehensive workpapers, model documentation, testing results, and change management records in accordance with governance, audit, and regulatory requirements. Author reports documenting model design, development, implementation, testing methodologies, and ongoing model enhancements. Build and maintain strong relationships with Lines of Business, Finance, Risk, IT, and Data partners to ensure analytical and software development requirements are effectively delivered. Lead and/or participate in Model Risk Management, Executive Management, Audit, Regulatory, and cross-functional planning meetings. Support initiatives across one or more key areas including Research & Development, Quarterly Production, and Credit Scorecard development and maintenance.

As a Business Strategist, Officer you will contribute to the continued growth of State Street Investment Management’s global Retirement Business by supporting new business initiatives, product rationalization and innovation, and work with senior members of the US Retirement team in executing/monitoring the business strategy. You will report to the Head of Retirement Research. The US Retirement team sits within State Street Investment Management’s Chief Business Office team. Job Functions Support the US Retirement business in executing its strategy through responsibilities such as: Monitor the performance of business strategy on a quarterly basis, reporting on what has gone well, what needs to improve and what’s next to Retirement team leadership Monitor and update team on industry trends and competitive developments with the goal to identify new sales opportunities and product innovation Support, and ultimately lead, projects needed to drive business growth and enhance business and product operations (e.g. IncomeWise strategic initiative) Manage all procurement and on-going related processes for the Retirement business Build and maintain presentation materials to support interactions with executive management and other internal Retirement partners Provide support as part of the Retirement team sales process (e.g. RFPs, sales presentations, competitor comparisons, etc.) Support and contribute to on-going retirement research About You Bachelor’s Degree. 1 - 5 years’ financial services experience Enthusiasm for the industry, a positive attitude, and a drive to succeed Excellent communication, organizational, and interpersonal skills Strong attention to detail, with the ability to analyze large datasets and conduct comprehensive industry research Strong critical thinking skills with ability to frame business issues and apply data analysis to support strategic decision-making Ability to learn quickly and demonstrate industry and product knowledge Ability to build relationships within our team, across our organization, and with our clients Familiarity with using AI to streamline operational processes Strong aptitude for multi-tasking and producing required reports within short timelines Salary Range: $75,000 - $123,750 Annual The range quoted above applies to the role in the primary location specified.

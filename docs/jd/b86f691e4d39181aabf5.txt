This position is not eligible for visa sponsorship. CNO Financial Group’s 40|86 Advisors is hiring a Structured Securities Analyst. Under technical direction, a Structured Securities Analyst will be responsible for conducting research and analysis leading to investment recommendations in the firm’s fixed income portfolio. The individual will be primarily responsible for ensuring that our portfolios adhere to investor requirements as is expected to learn about fundamental credit analysis, portfolio and risk management, and structured credit to contribute to the investment process. Core responsibilities and deliverables: Assist with identifying attractive investment opportunities (ABS, RMBS, Commercial Mortgage-Backed Securities (CMBS). Support the monitoring of existing positions through servicing data, sector research, and market participant discussions. Uses vendor tools such as Intex and Bloomberg to generate and evaluate cash flows. Evaluating pricing and performance attribution; supports the communication of sources of return. Ongoing evaluation of capital markets trends that impact the securitized sector. Following capital markets and investment trends that impact fixed income markets. Interacting with other members of the investment team including Portfolio Managers and Traders to help facilitate an effective investment decision making process. Continuously develops skills with internal and external company supported resources. Skills, Knowledge and Abilities: Knowledge of investment and financial principles and practices. Analytical and inquisitive. Good research capability & ability to provide input accordingly. Ability to thrive in a fast paced environment and work under tight deadlines. Strong Excel and database capabilities. What you need (Minimum requirements): Bachelors degree in Finance, Economics, or Accounting, or equivalent, required. Typically up to three years investment experience. Strong initiative and work ethic; willing to learn, ask questions, and accept guidance and feedback. Professional presence and strong written and oral communication skills. What will set you apart (preferred experience): Interest in pursuing CFA designation in the future. Knowledge of VBA is a plus.

Job Description The TAA Revenue Operations team is seeking a Associate Director of Sales Strategy to serve as a strategic business partner to the Divisional General Managers and Sales Leadership. Reporting into Director Revenue Operations TAA division, this role is responsible for driving go-to-market execution, sales productivity, and ACV acceleration through strategic planning, business insights, operational governance, and performance management. The Associate Director will lead the operating cadence for the business, translating strategic priorities into actionable plans while ensuring accountability, predictability, and execution across the sales organization. This individual will leverage analytics, technology, and process optimization to improve commercial performance and accelerate Annual Contract Value (ACV) growth. Key Responsibilities Go-to-Market Strategy & Planning Partner with Divisional GMs and Sales Leaders to develop and execute segment growth strategies. Lead annual and in-year sales planning, including capacity modeling, territory design, coverage optimization, and quota setting. Develop data-driven market opportunity assessments to align resources with growth potential. Drive account prioritization, segmentation, and scoring strategies to maximize sales productivity. Business Performance & Governance Own the operating cadence for the business, including forecasting, Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs), account reviews, and strategic planning sessions. Drive accountability through rigorous inspection of pipeline health, forecast accuracy, and execution against growth objectives. Establish key performance indicators and dashboards that provide visibility into business performance and leading indicators. Analyze trends and performance drivers to identify risks, opportunities, and actions required to achieve bookings targets. Analytics & Insights Generate actionable insights through advanced analytics and data-driven decision making. Develop reporting frameworks that connect top-of-funnel performance to customer acquisition, expansion, retention, and customer value realization. Improve the quality of business decisions through disciplined data management and analytical rigor. Revenue Operations & Transformation Lead cross-functional initiatives that improve sales productivity, operational efficiency, and revenue velocity. Partner with Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, Finance, and Technology teams to streamline processes and improve execution. Recommend and support incentive plan design that aligns seller behavior with business objectives. Drive adoption of technology, automation, and AI-enabled capabilities to simplify workflows and increase customer-facing selling time. Qualifications 7-10 years of progressive experience in Sales Strategy, Revenue Operations, Business Operations, Consulting, or related commercial functions. Proven experience partnering with senior sales leaders and executives to drive business performance and transformation. Strong understanding of sales planning, territory management, forecasting, pipeline management, and go-to-market strategy. Demonstrated success leading complex cross-functional initiatives in a matrixed environment. Exceptional analytical, financial, and problem-solving skills with the ability to translate data into business action. Experience leveraging AI, automation, and modern technology solutions to improve decision-making and operational effectiveness. Strong executive presence with outstanding communication, presentation, and stakeholder management skills. Experience building and leading high-performing, geographically dispersed teams. Demonstrated ability to balance strategic thinking with hands-on execution.

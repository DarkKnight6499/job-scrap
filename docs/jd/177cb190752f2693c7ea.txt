As a Portfolio Manager focused on Commercial Real Estate, you will support the underwriting, credit monitoring, and ongoing management of commercial real estate lending relationships. This role plays a key part in evaluating credit opportunities, managing portfolio risk, and helping deliver high-quality financial solutions to clients while ensuring adherence to lending policies and regulatory requirements. . Key Accountabilities Analyze borrower, guarantor, property, and market financial information to evaluate new loan requests, renewals, modifications, and annual reviews. Monitor portfolio performance and maintain timely and accurate risk ratings for commercial real estate relationships. Review loan repayment activity, property performance, and the financial condition of borrowers to identify and manage risk. Ensure portfolio administration and client relationship management activities comply with lending policies, operating procedures, and regulatory requirements. Minimize portfolio exceptions by proactively managing reporting requirements, covenant compliance, relationship reviews, and past due items. Partner with Relationship Managers to support client relationships and coordinate with Credit, operations, and other internal business partners. Assist with due diligence and analysis for prospective client opportunities and relationship expansion efforts. Participate in loan approval processes, present credit recommendations as appropriate, and contribute to special projects that support team objectives. Education & Experience Required Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, or an equivalent combination of education and experience. 3+ years of Commercial Real Estate lending, underwriting, portfolio management, or credit approval experience. Preferred 3+ years of experience working with complex credit relationships. Sales experience. Formal credit training. Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) designation or progress toward obtaining the designation by start date. Access to reliable transportation and the ability to lawfully drive, if applicable.

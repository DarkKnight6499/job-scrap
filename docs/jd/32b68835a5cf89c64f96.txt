Risk Manager, Investment Risk The Risk Manager, Investment Risk, will serve as a senior subject-matter leader within the ALM, Credit and Market Risk team, providing independent risk oversight and perspective across The Hartford’s Alternative Asset and Corporate Credit portfolios, with a focus on Private Equity/LP Funds, Private Credit, and public and private Corporate Credit. The individual will be accountable for developing and communicating independent, forward-looking risk views and translating analysis into actionable risk conclusions for senior management. This role will set the analytical direction for assigned portfolios, lead the development of risk assessment and stress-testing capabilities, and provide technical oversight and development for a junior analyst. The individual will be expected to move beyond summarizing portfolio and sector information by independently evaluating underlying assumptions, connecting risks across sectors and asset classes, and identifying implications for The Hartford’s investment portfolio and risk profile. The role will also leverage AI-enabled tools, analytics, and automation to enhance risk identification and analytical capabilities. This position is based in Hartford, CT on a full-time, hybrid schedule (T, W, Th in office; M, F remote). Responsibilities: Own the assessment of risk across The Hartford’s Alternative Asset and Corporate Credit portfolios, developing independent, forward-looking risk views and identifying key vulnerabilities, concentrations, and emerging risks. Lead the development and enhancement of risk assessment frameworks to identify key risk drivers, deteriorating trends, concentrations, and downside risks and translate findings into actionable conclusions. Drive enhancements to the measurement, aggregation, and monitoring of portfolio exposures, with a focus on identifying risks and portfolio implications that may not be apparent through individual sector analysis. Lead the development of stress testing and scenario analysis to evaluate tail risk, collateral sensitivity, asset performance, concentrations, and correlated risks across the portfolio. Lead market surveillance and thematic risk analysis, translating market, sector, and portfolio developments into forward-looking risk insights. Leverage AI-enabled tools, advanced analytics, and automation to enhance risk identification, surveillance, analytical capabilities, and early-warning indicators. Partner with investment teams and business partners to provide independent risk perspectives, assess key assumptions, and identify areas requiring deeper analysis or heightened monitoring. Advise senior leaders on investment risk implications arising from market events, portfolio developments, and ad-hoc analyses, providing clear, timely conclusions and recommendations. Drive scalable surveillance, reporting, and analytical capabilities that improve the quality, consistency, efficiency, and transparency of risk insights. Provide hands-on technical leadership and support the development of a junior analyst, ensuring the quality and accuracy of risk analysis and deliverables and providing guidance on the interpretation of results. Qualifications: Minimum of 7+ years of experience in investment risk, market risk, credit risk, or a related analytical role; portfolio management or rating agency experience preferred. Strong analytical skills with proficiency in Excel, Bloomberg, BlackRock Aladdin, Tableau, or similar tools. Experience using AI-enabled tools, advanced analytics, or automation preferred. Excellent communication skills with senior leaders and key business partners. Strong attention to detail and ability to synthesize complex information into clear, actionable conclusions. Solid understanding of fixed income markets, corporate credit, Alternative Assets, and investment risk drivers. Demonstrates curiosity, initiative, sound judgment, and ownership. Ability to work independently and collaborate effectively in a team environment. Degree in a quantitative discipline and/or credentials such as CFA or FRM preferred.

Financial Analyst I Role Summary The Financial Analyst I supports the Central BU Operations Finance team through financial reporting, variance analysis, planning support, data management, and finance operations. This is an entry-level role ideal for an analytical, detail-oriented finance professional looking to build expertise in FP&A, financial systems, and operational finance within a global technology organization. The role partners with finance team members to support reporting, planning processes, financial analysis, and continuous improvement initiatives that drive efficient and scalable finance operations. Key Responsibilities Financial Reporting & Analysis Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports, dashboards, and scorecards. Perform variance analysis and identify key financial trends and drivers. Compile, reconcile, and analyze data from multiple systems. Support month-end and quarter-end reporting activities. Planning & Forecasting Support Assist with forecasting, annual operating plans, and long-range planning processes. Support data collection, consolidation, validation, and reporting. Prepare templates, schedules, and planning reports. Ensure planning assumptions are accurately reflected in reporting systems. Systems & Data Management Utilize finance systems including SAP, Anaplan, Power BI, and related tools. Maintain reporting databases, financial models, and planning templates. Support reporting enhancements, system testing, and process improvements. Leverage AI-enabled tools to improve reporting efficiency and analysis. Continuous Improvement Document finance processes and reporting procedures. Support automation, standardization, and operational excellence initiatives. Identify opportunities to improve efficiency and strengthen controls. Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Business, Business Analytics, or related field. Less than one year of relevant experience (internships/co-ops included). Strong analytical, quantitative, and problem-solving skills. Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and Office applications. Ability to work with large datasets and communicate insights clearly. Strong organizational, written, and verbal communication skills. Demonstrated initiative, curiosity, and attention to detail. Preferred Qualifications Internship or co-op experience in finance, accounting, analytics, consulting, or operations. Exposure to financial reporting, forecasting, budgeting, or variance analysis. Familiarity with SAP, Anaplan, Power BI, or similar financial systems. Interest in automation, technology, and AI-enabled business processes. Why Join ADI? This role offers an excellent opportunity to launch a career in FP&A and operational finance while gaining exposure to ADI's planning processes, financial systems, analytics tools, and industry-leading technologies. You'll work alongside experienced finance professionals and build a strong foundation for future growth within the Finance organization.

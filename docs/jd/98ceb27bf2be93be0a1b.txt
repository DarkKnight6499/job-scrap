Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Customer Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Pricefx Technical Analyst you will support the delivery of technology solutions within our Software and Product Innovation practice, with a focus on Pricing, Promotions & Revenue Management. You will help translate business needs into functional and technical solutions, support implementation activities, and contribute to client service workstreams that require careful problem solving and clear communication. This role combines software delivery knowledge with analytical thinking, giving you exposure to end-to-end project execution and the chance to build your personal brand while working through complex client needs. As a Senior Associate you will analyze complex situations, manage assigned workstreams, and develop deliverables with a client-focused mindset. You will use critical thinking to break down issues, interpret data to inform recommendations, and guide junior team members while maintaining professional and technical standards. In this role at PwC, you will build meaningful client connections, deepen your understanding of the business context, and navigate ambiguity while contributing to technology implementations and delivery activities. Responsibilities - Implementing Pricefx solutions by translating business requirements into functional and technical deliverables - Supporting client-facing delivery activities across pricing, promotions, and revenue management workstreams - Analyzing business processes and system configurations to identify gaps, dependencies, and improvement opportunities - Developing user stories, test scenarios, and release materials to support solution delivery and deployment - Performing data review, validation, and troubleshooting across pricing platforms and integrated systems - Coordinating project tasks, timelines, and risks to keep workstreams moving through each delivery phase - Interpreting feedback from clients and internal teams to refine solution design and address emerging needs - Applying SDLC, software testing, and technology integration knowledge to support implementation activities - Collaborating across functional teams to resolve technical issues and advance implementation milestones - Mentoring junior team members by reviewing deliverables and sharing practical implementation approaches What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 3 years of experience What Sets You Apart - At least one of the following: Pricefx Config Engineer Certification and/or Project CE — Certified Configuration Engineer - Developing and maintaining Pricefx ETL pipelines and data warehouse processes using data modeling and ETL architecture best practices - Enhancing Pricefx functionality through REST APIs, Groovy, JavaScript, and SQL within a SAFe agile environment - Using Git, XML, JSON, and related APIs to support Pricefx data integration and interchange - Building meaningful client connections and anticipating team needs - Managing ambiguity while analyzing complex pricing and revenue challenges -

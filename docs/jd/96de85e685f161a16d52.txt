Global Clearing Product Control – Associate
JPMorgan Chase
Location: Brooklyn, NY, United States
Link: https://jpmc.fa.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/210801005

Join a dynamic team at the forefront of global derivatives clearing, regulatory reform, and market innovation. Be part of a business that shapes the future of financial markets and supports clients across a full suite of products. As a Global Clearing Product Control – Associate in our Corporate & Investment Bank, you will play a key role in Profit & Loss and risk reporting for the Global Futures & Options and Over-the-Counter Clearing business. This position offers exposure to regulatory reform, cross-functional collaboration, and leadership in a rapidly evolving industry. You will gain expertise in a critical area within the bank and markets, supporting both daily operations and strategic initiatives. Job responsibilities Own daily Profit & Loss and month-end close processes for the Clearing Desk. Analyze key drivers of Profit & Loss and provide detailed attribution and commentary explaining daily and monthly P&L movements to desk heads, business managers and senior finance management. Compile and distribute Management Information Systems to the business, including monthly financials and ad-hoc requests. Partner with Front Office, Treasury desk, Operations, Risk, and Technology teams to identify, investigate, and resolve control, reporting, and P&L issues in a timely manner. Build strong partnerships with the Clearing Desk to understand business strategy, investment and revenue-generating activities, and the resulting financial and balance sheet impacts. Ensure adherence to Corporate & Investment Bank product controller standards through robust controls, reconciliations, and issue resolution. Required qualifications, capabilities and skills At least 2 years of experience in Product Control, Treasury Finance, Markets Finance, Financial Control, or related functions. Bachelors in Finance or Accounting or an equivalent degree. Knowledge of U.S. Treasuries, repo, reverse repo, securities financing transactions, or interest rate products. Advanced quantitative and analytical skills with attention to detail. Excellent written and verbal communication and presentation skills. Ability to multi-task and execute on various deliverables in a fast-paced environment. Ability to implement and maintain effective controls. Self-motivated, results-driven, and able to forward operational change. Proficient in Microsoft Excel, Outlook, and PowerPoint. Preferred qualifications, capabilities and skills Experience with Alteryx and VBA. Understanding of clearing, collateral, margin, or exchange-traded derivatives markets.

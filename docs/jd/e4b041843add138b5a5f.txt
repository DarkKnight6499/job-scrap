About You and This Role Dow's Polyurethanes Systems House has an opportunity available for an Associate Operator to join our team in Marietta, GA. In this role, your primary purpose is to complete production batches safely, on time, and within specification. You will work within and across Zones on the manufacturing floor. Filling customer orders through the block scheduling process is the primary goal of all technicians on the manufacturing floor. Responsibilities Safely and Accurately Complete batches per production / block schedule and standard Operating Discipline. Execute assigned work using batch ticket and detailed procedures. Assume ownership of assigned work. Follow batches through to completion. Check QC progress, packaging needs to keep batches moving. Overtime may be required to complete batches. Status of Batches should be noted on block schedule, (i.e., Complete, Ready to Package, Incomplete). Reason for exceptions to block schedule should be noted in log. Accountable to Production Coordinator for daily activity. Job Performance is monitored by the Technical Adviser, Production Coordinator, Production Engineer, (and others), but Managed by the Operations Leader. Perform job duties to satisfactory levels in support of the business and the team. This includes prompt arrival at start of shift, appropriate breaks, and adequate communication between shifts to ensure safe and accurate completion of batches. Cooperates with associated functions to support the business: QC Lab, Site Logistics, EH&S and R&D. Knows, understands, and contributes to Plant goals. Schedule Vacation and Time off per the site policy. Keep Production Coordinator informed of all attendance issues. Complete Safety and Compliance Checks as directed. Use proper PPE and perform Housekeeping as required. Attends/completes training as directed. Once Red Tag User (LO/TO) and Safe Work Permitting (SWP) trained, performs LO/TO and SWP duties as needed. All employees are expected to participate as team members in their area of work. Intervenes with unsafe behavior and coach teammates to do the same. Dow does Not Tolerate Unsafe Acts. Coach teammates to correct behavior and if necessary, reports unsafe acts immediately to EH&S Delivery and leadership. Responds to Emergencies, Leak, or Spill as directed by the Response Leader. Participates in Root Cause Investigations and volunteers for corrective actions. Works with the Production Coordinator/Production Engineer to properly document Operating Discipline through procedures and training documentation. Performs initial troubleshooting to best of ability before involving Production Coordinator / Supervision. This is a hands-on role that requires repetitive tasks, troubleshooting and extreme care and safety. Due to the complexity of the role the person hired will be expected to stay in the role for a minimum of 2 years. Required Qualifications A minimum of a High School Diploma or GED equivalent. Current, valid US driver's license. Must be able and willing to work an 8-hour permanent shift: First shift is Monday - Friday (6AM - 2:30 pm). Second shift is Monday - Friday (2pm - 10:30pm). Third shift is Sunday - Thursday (10pm - 6:30am). Work overtime as needed. A minimum requirement for this U.S. based position is the ability to work legally in the United States. No visa sponsorship/support is available for this position, including for any type of U.S. permanent residency (green card) process. Physical Requirements Able to meet the following physical demands of the job, with or without reasonable accommodations. Work outside in all types of weather, including extreme heat and cold, rain, etc. Wear required Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) such as respirators, protective clothing, face masks, goggles, boots, etc. The respirator will require a clean-shaven face. Lift (up to 50lbs), push, and pull. Frequent bending, reaching, and lifting. Stand and walk for extended periods of time. Work in tight and enclosed spaces. No known sensitivity to chemicals. This role does involve elevated work greater than or equal to four feet above the ground. Must be able to wear and use personal protective equipment (PPE), including harnesses with a total weight capacity of 285lbs. Preferred Qualifications 2 or more years of experience in a manufacturing or industrial role OR 3 or more years of relevant military experience. Associate's degree or higher in Chemical Technology, Manufacturing Processing, or Chemical Processing. Previous experience in a chemical or petrochemical manufacturing environment. Hazmat experience. Fort Lift Truck experience/certifications. Familiarity with LOTO and Safe Work Permit Writing. Working with machines and HMIs. Skills Safety Mindset: Demonstrates a strong commitment to safe work practices, follows PPE requirements, identifies and addresses unsafe behaviors, and supports a culture of safety in daily operations. Operating Discipline: Follows established procedures, batch tickets, work instructions, and operating standards to safely and accurately complete production activities. Teamwork & Collaboration: Works effectively within an empowered team environment, partnering with operations, quality, logistics, EH&S, and other functions to achieve production goals. Problem Solving: Performs initial troubleshooting, identifies issues impacting production, and supports root cause investigations and corrective actions to improve performance. Self-Management: Takes ownership of assigned work, works independently with minimal supervision, and follows tasks through to completion while maintaining productivity and accountability. Communication: Communicates clearly across shifts and work groups, documents production activities accurately, and shares information needed to ensure safe and efficient operations. Attention to Detail: Maintains a high level of accuracy when executing batches, monitoring quality and packaging status, documenting exceptions, and completing compliance requirements.

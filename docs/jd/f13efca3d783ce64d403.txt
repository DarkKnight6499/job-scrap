Job Description: GTB is a key growth business within TDS that provides the opportunity to make an impact with top tier organizations. We offer innovative solutions and treasury advisory services on large and complex liquidity, payments, and trade needs. Over the last decade, we have seen unprecedented levels of innovation. The business constantly changes with macroeconomic conditions, interest rate environments, and Foreign Exchange ("FX") movements. Accordingly, GTB continues to invest and innovate to support our clients' evolving needs. GTB is comprised of three business lines: Trade & Working Capital ("TWC"), Corporate Cash Management ("CCM"), and Financial Institutions ("FI"). TWC is made up of Global Core Trade Finance ("GCTF") and Structured Trade & Export Finance ("STEF"), which is comprised of Export & Agency ("EAF") Finance and Working Capital Solutions ("WCS"). Working Capital Solutions: Helps companies optimize cash flow cycles, reduce funding gaps, and improve financial stability, enabling growth and operational efficiency Develops client centric solutions to help them manage short-term liquidity needs via trade payable (Supply Chain Finance, Reverse Factoring / Forfaiting) and trade receivable financing (Factoring / Forfaiting). Clients principally include Fortune 500 companies with head offices located in North America and Europe.

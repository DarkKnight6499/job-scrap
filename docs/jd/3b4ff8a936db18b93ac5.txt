Job Description Analyst/ Associate, Collateral Analyst What is the opportunity? We are seeking an Analyst or Associate level Collateral Analyst for our financing team, primarily to support the residential mortgage loan business. This role is central to ensuring portfolio integrity, maintaining compliance with our credit framework, and supporting the operational and risk management infrastructure that underpins our warehouse lending activities. This role will serve as a key liaison between collateral management, operations, IT development, and risk management. There will also be an opportunity to participate in the growth of the business by helping to build our capabilities in new products and market sectors. What will you do? Collateral Intake and Credit Box Compliance Assist in reviewing all incoming loan collateral against our pre-defined credit box by evaluating loan characteristics and supporting documentation. Portfolio Surveillance Conduct ongoing surveillance of the active loan portfolio, monitoring for credit deterioration, undue concentration risk, and data defects using monthly servicing data and periodic credit updates. Processing Servicing Data Assist in validating and reconciling loan balances, paydowns, and status changes across the portfolio. Process Automation Identify manual workflows and screening logic that are candidates for automation, working closely with the IT development team to audit current processes and assist in the design and testing of automated solutions. Risk Management Assist in the identification, measurement, and monitoring of key risk exposures at the portfolio level, including counterparty, market, and credit risk. What do you need to succeed? Must-have Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Business, or related field 2–3 plus years of experience in credit analysis, loan portfolio management, mortgage origination, or related finance function Strong Excel proficiency and comfort with data manipulation, validation, and reconciliation Ability to work with structured data; familiarity with databases or SQL is a plus Excellent attention to detail and a commitment to data accuracy and process discipline Clear written and verbal communication skills Demonstrated ability to work across teams and manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment Nice-to-have Experience in whole loans and/or securitization analytics (RMBS, or other structured product types) Familiarity with or background in mortgage/warehouse lending Knowledge of credit rating methodologies or portfolio risk analytics Experience with business process automation or requirements gathering for IT projects Proficiency in Python, R, or other data analysis tools What’s in it for you?

Job Description Summary At Raymond James Investment Management, marketing plays a critical role in driving growth across our U.S. Wealth and Institutional businesses. We are seeking a highly motivated Campaign Manager to lead the strategy, planning, execution, and optimization of integrated marketing campaigns that support distribution priorities and business growth. This individual will serve as the central orchestrator of multi-channel marketing initiatives, from campaign ideology and planning through in-market execution, performance measurement, and optimization. The ideal candidate is a self-starter who excels at managing complex projects, coordinating cross-functional teams, and leveraging data, technology, and AI-enabled tools to deliver measurable business outcomes. The Campaign Manager will translate business objectives into audience-centric marketing programs across digital, content, email, social media, webinars, events, and sales enablement channels. This role will partner closely with Sales, Product Marketing, Digital Marketing, Marketing Operations, Compliance, Data, and external vendors to deliver campaigns that drive engagement, lead generation, pipeline growth, and business impact across financial advisors, intermediary platforms, and institutional investors.

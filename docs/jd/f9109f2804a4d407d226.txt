Join a market-leading Trade & Working Capital team where you'll advise senior executives, originate complex financing solutions, and drive strategic growth for clients through innovative trade and working capital strategies. As a Sales Originator within Trade & Working Capital (T&WC), you will serve as a trusted advisor to clients and internal partners, leading strategic engagements that identify and deliver tailored working capital and trade finance solutions. Products and structures include Supply Chain Finance, Receivables Finance, Sales Finance, Inventory Finance, Export Agency Finance, Letters of Credit, Trade Loans, and Bills of Exchange/Promissory Notes. Contribute to strategic account planning and go-to-market execution by partnering with Product, Commercial Banking, Payments Sales, and Investment Banking teams to drive client coverage and business development efforts. Act as the primary relationship manager between Trade & Working Capital and assigned clients, ensuring a coordinated and seamless client experience across the organization. Originate and develop new business opportunities by delivering strategic insights and presenting tailored financing solutions to senior executives, including Chief Financial Officers, Treasurers, and Chief Procurement Officers. Develop a deep understanding of client objectives, industry trends, and market dynamics to uncover opportunities and strengthen long-term partnerships. Identify emerging client needs and market developments, providing strategic input to support product innovation and business growth initiatives.

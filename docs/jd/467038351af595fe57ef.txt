About the Role:

Crusoe is a vertically integrated AI infrastructure company. We source power, manufacture modular data centers, build hyperscale campuses, and operate our own cloud. That puts us in four fast-moving markets at once: AI compute, power, data center construction, and manufacturing. Something important changes every week. A competitor raises, a hyperscaler signs a power deal, a GPU generation slips, a new model is released, a state rewrites its interconnection rules. Someone has to see it first and analyze  what it means for us.



The Strategy team sits in the Office of the President and Chief Strategy Officer. We write the position papers the executive team decides from, and we incubate new programs until they are ready for a permanent owner. Every one of those calls rests on a view of the market. You will build that view, and the system that keeps it current.



Your Mission:

Build the intelligence function that helps Crusoe see around corners.



You will develop a differentiated view of the markets, competitors and technologies shaping Crusoe's business, then translate that information into clear implications and recommendations for the company.



The role combines research, judgment and building. You will develop original market, innovation and competitive insights, partner across Crusoe to understand the questions that matter most and build AI-enabled systems that make high-quality intelligence faster, more reliable and easier to maintain.



Success is not measured by the volume of research produced. It is measured by whether the work helps Crusoe make better decisions, identify important changes earlier and solve meaningful business problems.



What You'll Be Working On:

Market and competitive intelligence

 - Build and maintain a differentiated view of the markets that matter to Crusoe, including AI compute and cloud, hyperscalers, neoclouds, semiconductors and accelerators, power and energy, data center infrastructure, manufacturing and other adjacent markets.

 - Develop competitive intelligence on key companies and ecosystems: strategy, customers, partnerships, capacity, economics, technology, geographic expansion and other indicators of how competitors are evolving.

 - Build structured market maps and datasets that allow Crusoe to understand how companies, customers, technologies and value chains connect.

 - Identify the developments that could materially affect Crusoe and distinguish meaningful signals from the much larger volume of industry noise.

 - Maintain rigorous sourcing and update standards so the underlying intelligence is trusted and auditable.

Original insight and strategic analysis

 - Turn fragmented information into clear, actionable insights for Crusoe. Identify what is changing, why it matters and what the company should consider doing differently.

 - Work cross functionally to translate business pain points into clear strategic questions, size opportunities, compare alternatives and test assumptions using both quantitative and qualitative analysis.

 - Synthesize external research with insights from customers, partners, experts and teams across Crusoe.

 - Write concise position papers and strategic briefs that articulate the conclusion, supporting evidence, implications for Crusoe, key uncertainties and recommended next steps.

 - Own recurring intelligence products for leadership that elevate the developments most relevant to Crusoe rather than simply summarizing industry news.

 - Act as a research and thought partner on live strategic questions, including market entry, product expansion, partnerships, M&A screens, competitive responses and emerging technology opportunities.

 - Build relationships across Crusoe to tap into the company's collective expertise and ensure external intelligence reflects what teams are seeing on the ground.

AI-powered intelligence infrastructure

 - Turn recurring research and intelligence needs into repeatable workflows using AI research tools, agents, APIs, data sources and light scripting.

 - Build systems that can monitor markets and companies at a scale that would be impossible through manual research alone.

 - Design AI workflows with trust and verification in mind. Maintain traceability to primary sources, test outputs for accuracy and understand where automated systems are likely to fail.

 - Build tools that augment judgment rather than replace it: systems that make analysts faster while preserving clear ownership of conclusions and recommendations.

 - Document and share workflows so they can be reused and improved across the Strategy team.

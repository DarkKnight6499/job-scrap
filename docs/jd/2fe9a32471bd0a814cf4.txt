Marsh’s US Financial Planning & Analysis team is seeking candidates for a Senior Manager, FP&A - Central Zone Advisor position. This position is based in New York, NY and is hybrid, with an expectation of working in the office at least three days per week. This a leading finance position role that works closely with the Central Zone (“CEZ”) distribution team, and its business executives. CEZ, a business with more than $600MM of revenue, is the second largest zone within the Marsh US, and larger than most countries in global Marsh in terms of revenue. As the lead finance partner for CEZ Leadership, this position is responsible for: 1) providing relevant and consistent management and performance information to the Zone, 2) assessing key business issues, and 3) providing advice to support growth, productivity, and profit improvement. In addition, the person who holds this role will be required to ensure that information and advice is delivered timely and accurately, company policies and procedures are followed, and that communication between finance and the business is seamless and strong.

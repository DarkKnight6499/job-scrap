Impact You'll Make As an Associate, you will conduct forensic acquisition and analysis of digital devices and data to support investigations, incident response, and legal matters. You will work with limited supervision, produce defensible findings, and help mentor junior team members as we build out the Digital Forensic & Cyber Risk practice. What You'll Do Acquire and preserve evidence from computers, mobile devices, cloud accounts, and removable media using forensically sound methods Analyze file systems, registry artifacts, browser and email data, logs, and application data to reconstruct user activity Maintain chain of custody and detailed case documentation Write clear, accurate reports and summaries for technical and non-technical audiences Support incident response, insider threat, fraud, and eDiscovery matters Provide testimony or technical declarations when required Assist in reviewing junior examiners’ work and validating tools and methods What You'll Bring 2-4 years of hands-on digital forensics or incident response experience Bachelor’s degree in cybersecurity, computer science, digital forensics, or equivalent experience Proficiency with at least one major forensic suite (EnCase, FTK, Magnet AXIOM, Cellebrite, or X-Ways) Working knowledge of Windows, macOS, and Linux file systems and artifacts Understanding of evidence handling, forensic imaging, and write-blocking Strong written and verbal communication skills Ability to work independently and manage multiple cases Certifications such as GCFE, GCFA, CFCE, EnCE, ACE, or CCE preferred. Mobile forensics and cloud forensics experience (M365, Google Workspace, AWS) preferred. Basic scripting skills (Python, PowerShell) preferred. Familiarity with memory forensics and malware triage preferred. Prior experience writing expert or court-ready reports preferred. How You'll Thrive Exercise integrity and sound judgment when handling sensitive, confidential matters. Communicate clearly, collaborate across disciplines, and respond effectively to evolving priorities. Bring curiosity, analytical rigor, and a solutions-oriented approach to complex issues.

ABOUT THE ROLE The SMA Reconciliation Operations Associate is responsible for ensuring timely and accurate reconciliation of portfolios and resolving trade, currency, and corporate action discrepancies with our custodians. This role will also be responsible for client and third-party reporting and other duties as assigned and ensuring processes and procedures are accurately documented. The ideal candidate is a self-starter with exceptional attention to detail and the ability to work independently under tight deadlines. The expected shift is approximately 8:30AM-5:00PM EST. PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES Reconciling client portfolios against custody statements/downloads/websites Resolving variances in a timely manner Ability to understand risk and to escalate as appropriate Documentation of written processes and procedures Must be comfortable working with large datasets in MS Excel. Must be able to manage and organize datasets of 100,000+ rows of data.

Meet the Team Millennium’s Equity Capital Markets team sits within Execution Services and supports the firm’s activity across a broad range of equity and structured capital markets transactions. The team evaluates and executes opportunities in public and private markets, including initial public offerings, primary and secondary equity offerings, block trades, convertible bonds, equity-linked transactions, private investments in public equity, SPACs, private placements, and other private-company capital solutions. Beyond transaction execution, the team represents Millennium in the market and develops relationships with public and private companies, investment banks, financial advisors, sponsors, and other market participants. The team works closely with trading teams, portfolio managers, and internal control and support functions across the firm.

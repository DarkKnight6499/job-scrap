The PROPEL program is a 12-month professional development experience designed to equip recent graduates with the essential skills, training, and experience needed to succeed at Cisco. It provides a supportive environment where you will learn how Cisco powers the internet and connects people, devices, and systems around the world. You will spend your first six weeks in immersive onboarding training, followed by two 6-month rotations (the first rotation will be a quota-carrying rotation) within the Recurring Revenue Acceleration organization. Through these roles, you will gain hands-on experience and work alongside expert teams to support our customers. By the end of your first year, your performance and the connections you build will help pave the way for a transition into a full-time role within the organization. Meet the Team As a member of the PROPEL cohort, you will join a dynamic community of early-career talent dedicated to accelerating growth and driving innovation within Cisco’s Recurring Revenue Acceleration (RRA) organization. You will collaborate with a diverse group of associates, mentors, and senior leaders who are passionate about the LAER (Land, Adopt, Expand, Renew) model. By participating in associate-led committees and cross-functional rotations, you will work alongside teams like Renewals, Strategy & Planning, and Services Acceleration, gaining firsthand experience in the tools and technologies that power Cisco’s customer success organization. Each associate will complete their first rotation in a Renewals Manager or Renewals Specialist role (quota-carrying); the second rotation can be either quota-carrying or non (Go to Market, Strategy, Enablement, Operations, etc.). Your Impact In the PROPEL program, you will directly influence Cisco’s recurring revenue success by transforming technical knowledge into actionable business outcomes. Leveraging your ability to learn quickly and adapt, you will support the full renewal lifecycle—from identifying upsell opportunities and analyzing data to streamlining processes through AI-driven insights. Your efforts will help us bridge the gap between sales and delivery, ensuring our partners and customers receive seamless support. By proactively engaging with your cohort, building internal networks, and championing innovation, you will play a key role in optimizing the tools and strategies that keep Cisco at the forefront of the industry. Program Milestones Built on a model of growth, collaboration, and real-world experience, the program offers a clear progression: Weeks 1–6: Onboarding: A comprehensive deep dive into Cisco tools, culture, and foundational skills. Months 2–7: Rotation 1: A 6-month period of hands-on work experience in a quota-carrying Renewals Manager or Renewals Specialist team in RRA. Months 8–12: Rotation 2: A second 6-month period in a new area to broaden your skills and organizational impact in RRA. Post-Program: Career Transition: A dedicated pathway to transition into a full-time role in RRA. Minimum Qualifications Completion within the past 3 years, or current enrollment with expected completion within 12 months, of a certification or relevant degree program (e.g., Associates, Apprenticeship, Boot Camp, or Certification in a specialized program + 1 year of relevant experience, High School Diploma + 2 years of relevant experience) or Bachelors + 0 years of relevant experience. Relevant fields include: Business, Supply Chain, Communications, Mathematics, Finance, Industrial Engineering, Statistics, etc. Preferred Qualifications Business Operations Interest: A genuine curiosity regarding the business and operational side of the technology sector, with an eagerness to understand how internal processes drive market success. Adaptability: The ability to thrive in a fast-paced and ever-evolving work environment, where priorities may shift and new challenges frequently emerge. Proactive Learning: Intellectual curiosity and a self-starting mindset, with the capacity to learn new concepts quickly and work with a high degree of independence. Analytical Mindset: A results-oriented approach to tasks, with a focus on leveraging data to inform decisions and achieve organizational objectives. Collaborative Communication: Strong interpersonal skills that allow you to communicate effectively across diverse teams and act as a constructive problem-solver in group settings. Self-Starter: Proven track record of initiating and leading projects, such as starting organizations, conferences, competitions, or publications, showcasing entrepreneurial spirit and initiative. Leadership Experience: Demonstrated leadership abilities through roles in student organizations, or significant involvement in extracurricular activities. For quota-based incentive pay, Cisco typically pays as follows: .75% of incentive target for each 1% of revenue attainment up to 50% of quota; 1.5% of incentive target for each 1% of attainment between 50% and 75%; 1% of incentive target for each 1% of attainment between 75% and 100%; and Once performance exceeds 100% attainment, incentive rates are at or above 1% for each 1% of attainment with no cap on incentive compensation. For non-quota-based sales performance elements such as strategic sales objectives, Cisco may pay 0% up to 125% of target. Cisco sales plans do not have a minimum threshold of performance for sales incentive compensation to be paid.

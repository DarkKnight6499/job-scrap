Job Description Role Summary: Each Integrated Account Management (IAM) Customer Manager (CM) at our company delivers best-in-class, end-to-end contracting services, providing affordable patient access through customer and internal relationships that they develop. The Utilization Based Contracting Team (UBC) manages payer contracting relationships that are central to our company commercial and government business. The UBC CM connects and interprets customer, field, and headquarters perspectives to deliver customer contracts. In close partnership with the designated National Account Director (NAD), this role relies on deep customer knowledge, close collaboration with internal stakeholders, and analytical acumen to serve as the internal-facing customer advocate. The CM manages the end-to-end contract lifecycle from development through implementation in coordination with field personnel for our health plan and PBM contracts. Incumbents in this role must have advanced proficiency in both cross-functional (e.g., communication, project management) and domain-specific (e.g., access strategy, financial acumen) competencies. Primary Responsibilities: Manages end-to-end contract process by drafting, revising, and negotiating utilization-based contracts for pharmaceutical and medical benefit products. Serves as internal-facing counterpart to NAD to drive activities such as: Implementation and negotiation of contracting initiatives. Evaluation/review of account performance and segment trends to optimize net sales for our company products. Balance improved customer performance with our company business objectives. Identification and execution of contracting opportunities that meet profitability and access objectives. Engagement with our company Senior Leadership to conduct business reviews, suggest strategic approaches to navigate customer relationships, and identify opportunities to optimize internal business objectives. Development and implementation of Customer Account Plans. Establishes and maintains meaningful relationships with internal and external contract decision makers. Drives collaboration among our company stakeholders (e.g., CCE, Finance, Payer Marketing) to work toward agreements that are mutually beneficial to the Customer and Our company. Interprets legal language and understands its implications to develop Terms & Conditions (T&C), including identifying business-related areas of risk and exposure, in partnership with commercial attorneys or other internal counsel. Understands rules and contracting processes; uses that knowledge to identify creative solutions to successfully negotiate with internal our company teams and/or Customers. Coordinates financial, trend, or data modeling analytic results and implications to support negotiations. Oversees internal evaluations and analyses of customer contracts. Utilizes data analytics techniques to inform inputs/outputs of sophisticated analyses; can draw logically sound, data-driven conclusions about contract scenarios. Accurately applies appropriate analogy models and customer understanding to best recommend discounting options with customer financial modeling and profitability metrics. Monitors marketplace trends and regulatory changes that could influence contracts. Proactively gathers and analyzes product level information from other CMs and collaborates with Payer Marketing to guide payer marketing strategies. Demonstrates awareness of and sensitivity by creating experiences for all customers and coworkers. Utilizes strengths and acceptance in team members, customers, or other individual stakeholders (e.g., in background or experience) to improve own performance.

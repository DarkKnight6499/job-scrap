Job Title : Financial Analyst Reporting To: Site Finance Manager - Avionics Work Schedule: Onsite – Mineral Wells, TX Moog is looking for a Financial Analyst to partner with cross-functional teams to support financial planning, reporting, cost accounting, and strategic decision-making for a growing aerospace manufacturing operation. This role is responsible for budgeting, forecasting, financial analysis manufacturing cost accounting, and process improvement indicatives while supporting accurate financial reporting, revenue recognition, and business performance. This is an onsite role in Mineral Wells, Texas and reports to the Site Finance Manager – Avionics for Moog’s Military Aircraft Group. This position requires a minimum of 3 years of Finance or Accounting experience supporting a manufacturing or production environment. Candidates must be authorized to work for any employer in the United States. Key Responsibilities Partner with Operations, Supply Chain, Engineering, and Program Management to analyze financial and P&L performance and identify opportunities to improve profitability Support month-end and year-end close activities, including journal entries, account reconciliations, and corporate reporting requirements Support revenue recognition and related accounting activities for both point-in-time and over-time customer contracts in accordance with corporate accounting policies and U.S. GAAP Assist with annual budget, rolling forecast and long-range plan (LRP) process Develop financial models and scenario analyses to evaluate business performance, forecast results, and support strategic initiatives Support manufacturing cost accounting, including project costing, overhead allocation methodologies, and customer profitability analysis to improve financial visibility and decision-making Lead continuous improvement initiatives by identifying opportunities to automate, standardize, and streamline finance processes and internal controls Support special projects including ERP enhancements, costing improvements, profitability analysis, and new business/program implementations This job requires access to U.S. export-controlled information.

Job description Overview The Data Services & Strategy group (“DSS”) at Evercore manages all third-party content contracts and market data subscriptions for the firm. With approximately $80 million in spend under management, it is one of the largest indirect spend categories at Evercore. The team administers 500+ contracts with over 17,000 user subscriptions, covering multiple business lines in every Evercore geography (North America, EMEA and APAC). DSS is seeking a Market Data & Operations Associate to support its data, reporting, and financial operations. This role requires strong analytical capabilities, advanced Excel skills, and high attention to detail, along with the ability to collaborate effectively with internal stakeholders (business groups, IT, Finance) and external vendors. This is a fast-paced, demanding role that follows the firm’s monthly accounting cycle with hard-stop deadlines for many of the group’s deliverables. The ideal candidate is highly organized and able to manage multiple competing priorities under tight timelines.

About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Lead Credit Portfolio Consultant within the Business Banking and SBA Construction Portfolio Services Division. This role is focused on managing credit and construction risk throughout the life cycle of commercial real estate construction projects, from underwriting and pre-closing due diligence through construction, funding, and project completion. The Business Banking & SBA Construction Portfolio Services Division is responsible for: Evaluating construction risk and project feasibility during the underwriting and pre-closing process Reviewing and analyzing commercial real estate construction due diligence, including project plans, budgets, permitting, and other requirements necessary for successful project completion Coordinating commercial real estate loan closings with a focus on credit and collateral quality Providing subject matter expertise and guidance related to commercial real estate construction loans Monitoring construction progress, draw requests, and project funding after closing Identifying and addressing risks that could impact construction progress, project completion, or credit quality Ensuring construction loans remain in compliance with applicable credit policies and requirements Providing commercial real estate construction guidance and training to business partners The team works directly with Relationship Managers, Senior Lead Commercial Banking Portfolio Managers, Independent Risk Management, and other Wells Fargo team members to identify, evaluate, and mitigate credit and construction risks related to real estate collateral.

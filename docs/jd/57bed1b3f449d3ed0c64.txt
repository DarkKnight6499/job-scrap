Job Description Job Description Role Summary The Senior Associate, Administrative Assistant, will provide proactive, high-quality, and reliable administrative support to the Intellectual Property legal group, a part of the Offices of the General Counsel. The individual in this role will coordinate and perform multiple administrative tasks in a dynamic environment, and closely collaborate with administrative associates and paralegals in the Intellectual Property legal group. Responsibilities and Primary Activities Prepare, edit, and assist with various documents, memos, correspondence and reports, under guidance and supervision of attorneys (such as various internal patent-related memos, legalizations of Powers of Attorneys, copyright applications, and PowerPoint presentations). Perform advanced clerical duties. Manage multiple calendars and scheduling tasks. Review and approve invoices for compliance with billing guidelines; create matters within billing system; assist with financial matter management and budget planning. Track weekly schedules for team members. Manage outside visitors and meetings. Coordinate travel and process expense reports. Assist in maintaining and updating team websites, SharePoint sites and other web resources. Sort and review correspondence on a daily basis; organize and maintain electronic and physical filing systems in accordance with records retention policies. Coordinate and perform multiple tasks while understanding the project principles and technical skills related to own work assignments. Communicate and interact effectively with internal and external stakeholders. Reliably handle confidential and sensitive legal issues. Coordinate special projects within the Intellectual Property legal group. Consistently demonstrate Company Leadership behaviors. Required Education: High School Diploma or equivalent. Experience and Skillset: 8+ years of administrative assistant experience or 4+ years of paralegal experience Advanced computer skills using MS Office Suite (Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, SharePoint, OneNote, Teams, etc.), enterprise communication and travel solutions, and other company software and hardware tools. Experience processing expense reports, travel reservations, and invoices. Experience scheduling meetings and managing calendars. Ability to apply independent judgment, creativity, and problem-solving capabilities. Ability to handle and prioritize multiple work assignments. Experience with financial and budget tasks. High-level of attention to detail and organization is required. Strong communication and people skills. Collaborative, inquisitive, adaptive, and flexible. Enthusiastic, self-motivated team player with a strong work ethic. Documented experience as a strong performer in current and past positions. Preferred Education: Advanced education or certifications (e.g. Certified Administrative Professional, Paralegal Studies Certificate, Project Management Professional Certification, Professional in Human Resources Certification) Experience and Skillset: 3+ years administrative experience supporting executives (Senior Director, Executive Director, Vice President, or above). Experience as an intellectual property administrative associate in a corporate or law firm setting. Concur and Tymetrix360 ebilling system. Intellectual Property specific systems (e.g., Clarivate’s First to File and TIPMS).

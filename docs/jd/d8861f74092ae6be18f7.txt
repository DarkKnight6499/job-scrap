Role purpose Support the end-to-end delivery of US HSBC North America Holdings (HNAH) and Group stress testing activities, including Comprehensive Capital Analysis and Review (CCAR) and HSBC Global (Group) stress tests, within Treasury Finance. The role is responsible for high-quality execution across analysis, review and challenge, documentation, process controls, process improvement, governance support and submission readiness. You’ll work closely with Finance, Treasury, Business and Risk stakeholders across HNAH and Group to ensure stress test outputs are accurate, well-controlled, explainable, and regulator-ready.

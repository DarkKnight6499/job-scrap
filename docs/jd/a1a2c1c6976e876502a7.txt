Job Description The Position Our IT team partners with the business to deliver innovative solutions that help everyone be more productive. The Research & Development Division IT team is seeking a Product Manager – PV Reporting and Surveillance , reporting to the Product Line Lead for Clinical Safety. You will lead the strategy, processes, and systems for pharmacovigilance (PV), ensuring regulatory compliance and high quality standards. The ideal candidate combines digital technology, business strategy, user experience, and agile delivery skills with an entrepreneurial drive, and can guide peers and influence decision-makers to turn stakeholder needs into real solutions. What will you do? Partner with cross-functional teams to develop and implement PV strategies that ensure patient safety, quality, and compliance. Own the product strategy, roadmap, and prioritized backlog of changes. Manage PV systems, including adverse event reporting and signal detection tools. Stay current on global PV regulations (e.g., ICH guidelines) and ensure processes and systems comply. Maintain quality standards and controls that ensure data accuracy and integrity. Work with clinical, regulatory, and safety teams to integrate PV into the overall product development plan. Partner with business technology teams to deliver new PVQC systems and changes on time. Manage stakeholders effectively. Qualifications, Skills & Experience Required Senior-level experience in enterprise, business, information, systems, application, or solution architecture. 5+ years of experience in a health authority–regulated environment. Knowledge of pharmaceutical R&D and Global Safety Case Intake, Processing, Management, and Reporting. Proven ability to communicate with executives, business leaders, and global IT peers while balancing conflicting priorities and tactical and strategic needs. Experience setting product strategy and execution plans across matrixed IT and business organizations. Strong vendor relationships that influence platform roadmaps. Agile development, testing, and implementation experience in hosted and cloud environments, including architecture, information, and UI design. Ability to analyze business needs and coordinate vendors and stakeholders to deliver timely, quality support. Strong project management, leadership, and pragmatic problem-solving skills. Self-motivated and able to work with little direction in a complex, fast-changing environment. Nice to have Understanding of PV, medical affairs, clinical operations, and product quality complaint processes and systems. Working knowledge of Veeva Vault Safety, Vault Safety Docs, Workbench, and Signal. Knowledge of IT regulatory requirements such as 21 CFR Part 11, Annex 11, GLP/GCP, and Computer Systems Validation. Familiarity with risk-based monitoring and other PV industry applications. What we offer Exciting work in a great team, global projects, international environment.

Other job-related duties may be assigned as required. Team & Role Overview This role sits within the LMS Optimization and Pricing team within Citi Services. The primary objective of the team is to maximize the financial performance, liquidity efficiency, and balance sheet productivity of Citi’s global deposit franchise through actionable commercial strategies, analytical tools, targeted optimization campaigns, disciplined pricing governance, and regulatory capital frameworks. We operate at the critical intersection of business strategy, treasury modeling, and technology execution. The team collaborates extensively with global and regional stakeholders—including Product Heads, Sales Teams, Balance Sheet Leads, Corporate and Business Treasury (Liquidity Management / GLRM), Market Managers, Risk Management, Controllers, and Technology partners. We leverage deep subject-matter expertise in client liquidity behaviors, regulatory metrics (RLAP, LCR, NSFR), client-level cost of funds (CoF), and Services data architecture. As the LMS Liquidity Analytics & Optimization Lead , you will lead the strategic agenda on deposit optimization and liquidity frameworks. You will own the analytical and technical enhancement of our proprietary data-mart, interface directly with Treasury governance bodies, drive multi-million-dollar revenue and liquidity optimization workstreams, and provide executive-level pricing advisory for key franchise relationships. What You'll Do As a senior leader within this global team, you will drive core optimization workstreams, shape regulatory liquidity models, lead cross-functional remediation programs, and oversee end-to-end technology. Your primary responsibilities will include: Liquidity & Balance Sheet Optimization: Drive global optimization initiatives to deliver annualized revenue targets and liquidity value across the Services deposit franchise. Treasury Liquidity Frameworks & Governance Advocacy: Represent LMS in high-level Treasury forums. Advocate for and lead model enhancements with Global Liquidity Risk Management (GLRM) and Corporate Treasury. Lead rapid response analytics for macroeconomic stress testing to model deposit behavioral impacts for Board presentations. Stakeholder Management: Partner with global clusters and sales teams to optimize RLAP/LCR/NSFR and maintain target performance thresholds. Project Management: Oversee the lifecycle of analytical projects, from data acquisition to final delivery and follow-up. Technology Implementation: Oversee business architecture and functional requirements for end-to-end analytics technology. Including data acquisition and analysis to generate actionable insights and support strategic initiatives. Data Quality Management: Lead the escalation and follow-up process for data quality issues to ensure the integrity of underlying data. Metric Development: Design, develop, and implement new metrics to track performance and drive business outcomes. Stakeholder Training, Enablement & Talent Development: Lead comprehensive global training curriculums for cluster balance sheet leads, product managers, and sales teams on liquidity models, Clearview tools, and optimization levers. As an integral part of the broader Pricing and Optimization team, you will also contribute to a range of activities spanning subject matter expertise on client liquidity value, client pricing, client financials, and Services data architecture. What We'll Need From You Education & Professional Experience: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Quantitative Finance, Financial Engineering, Computer Science, or Data Analytics; Master’s degree / MBA / CFA / FRM strongly preferred. 7–10+ years of progressive experience in Treasury, Liquidity Risk Management, Balance Sheet Management, Product Management, or Financial Analytics within a Tier-1 global financial institution. Regulatory Liquidity (Essential): Deep subject matter expertise in regulatory liquidity metrics, including RLAP (Resolution Liquidity Adequacy and Positioning) , LCR (Liquidity Coverage Ratio) , NSFR (Net Stable Funding Ratio) . Thorough understanding of regulatory reporting data architectures, specifically Fed FR 2052a (Complex Institution Liquidity Monitoring Report) and Short-Term Wholesale Funding (STWF) calculations. Quantitative Analytics & Systems Architecture: Advanced quantitative analytical skills with the ability to interrogate large-scale, complex transactional and balance sheet datasets. Proven project management experience to lead implementation of tools and solutions that support business objectives, data marts, and business intelligence suites. Strategic Advisory & Executive Communication: Exceptional ability to synthesize highly complex, technical liquidity and quantitative modeling concepts into concise, high-impact executive narratives and presentations for senior management, governance committees (FLRC/LIMRF), and Board-level reporting. Demonstrated commercial acumen to balance regulatory risk constraints with revenue maximization. Leadership & Stakeholder Alignment: Proven leadership capability to drive consensus across competing priorities, manage project scopes, and influence global stakeholders across 7+ regional clusters without direct line authority.

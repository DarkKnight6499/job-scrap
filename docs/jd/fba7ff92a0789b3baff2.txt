Job Description: The Business Banking Credit Portfolio Officer role is responsible for supporting credit underwriting and monitoring for new and existing clients. Key responsibilities include completing credit analysis, evaluating risks, and writing financial analysis using various tools and systems. Job expectations include analyzing financials, projections, industry data, covenant compliance, collateral, and policy requirements to ensure alignment with the bank’s risk appetite. This role reports to the Commercial Credit Manager that supports the market. Responsibilities: Analysis of client financial information, identification/mitigation of potential risks, compliance with regulatory requirements and evaluation of collateral in determination of credit worthiness Complete monitoring activities via analysis of clients’ financial performance and collateral evaluation to ensure appropriate requirements are met Listen in to client meetings to develop a deeper understanding of the client's business, management team, and past and future performance Review completed client requests to ensure compliance with policy and procedures Understand how credit data is used internally and provided to regulatory partners Assist with periodic portfolio reviews Supports the credit monitoring process for existing clients. Prepares credit analysis to facilitate the decisioning of a client's credit worthiness. Skills: Relationship Building Financial Analysis Credit Documentation Underwriting Analytical Thinking Loan Structuring Active Listening Attention to Detail Collaboration Written Communication Issue Management Organization Data and Trend Analysis Research Required Qualifications: 2+ years of solid Commercial Banking experience Experience in financial analysis, structuring, underwriting and portfolio management Analytical/technical skills, including financial accounting, modeling and loan structuring Strong communication skills; ability to communicate vertically, horizontally, and externally Industry knowledge across multiple sectors Minimum Education Requirement: High School Diploma / GED / Secondary School or equivalent Shift: 1st shift (United States of America) Hours Per Week: 40 Pay Transparency details US - IL - Chicago - 110 N Wacker Dr - Bank Of America Tower Chicago (IL4110) Pay and benefits information Pay range $70,000.00 - $95,800.00 annualized salary, offers to be determined based on experience, education and skill set.

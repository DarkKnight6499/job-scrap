About the Role The Director of Operational Risk leads the bank's enterprise non-technology operational risk program. The role provides independent oversight and effective challenge of first-line risk management. It works closely with the lines of business, technology, and operations to keep operational, data, payment, and process execution risks within the Board-approved risk appetite, consistent with OCC Heightened Standards (12 CFR Part 30, Appendix D) and interagency guidance. Happen Bank has a foundation in place for operational risk; this role will be responsible for its next stage of growth and maturity. The role’s first priorities will be building and operationalizing programs for process and execution risk, payment risk and data risk – and then extending oversight across Deposits, Personal Loans and other products (over time). The role’s end goal is to deepen the existing capabilities and provide the Bank a stronger, independent view of operational risk and to work with business leaders to better understand and manage their operational risks.

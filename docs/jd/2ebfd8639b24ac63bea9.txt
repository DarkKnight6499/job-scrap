Marsh Agency helps people and organizations manage risk and prepare for the future through insurance, benefits, retirement, and wealth management strategies. As a trusted partner to our clients, we believe in hands-on proactive service, data-driven insights, and understanding how global events can impact local decisions. Within the Graham Division, the Associate Consultant supports the Captive Practice through data analysis, financial modeling, loss projections, reinsurance evaluations, and preparation of client-facing proposals and presentations. The Associate coordinates projects and communications among clients, prospects, internal teams, and external partners while helping identify new business opportunities and supporting the growth of the Captive Practice. Essential Duties and Responsibilities include, but are not limited, to the following: Captive Analysis and Data Management Compile, organize, clean, and maintain client, prospect, premium, loss, claims, and exposure data used by the Captive Practice. Assist with historical premium and loss analyses to support the evaluation of captive feasibility, retained layers, deductible structures, and potential program outcomes. Support loss pick analyses and projections used in captive pro forma models, prospect presentations, client proposals, and strategic recommendations. Assist in preparing excess and reinsurance estimates for prospective and existing clients, including estimated premiums and other program components. Support the development and maintenance of captive financial pro forma models using Graham Company’s tools, national resources, and established methodologies. Prepare financial comparisons of reinsurance options, deductible structures, retained layers, and individual cost components as part of quote reviews. Communicate findings clearly and identify key considerations for review by Captive Practice Leader. Proposal and Presentation Support Prepare client and prospect presentations, proposals, reports, and other deliverables, translating technical captive concepts and financial results into clear, client-appropriate materials. Participate in prospect and client meetings, supporting preparation, presentation delivery, and follow-up. Support the preparation of strategic recommendations that align with client objectives, risk profiles, and business needs. Client Service, Project Delivery, and Practice Support Support the evaluation of prospective captive clients by gathering relevant information, coordinating internal analysis, and assessing against Graham Company’s underwriting and revenue criteria. Assist with new client onboarding, project execution, and ongoing client service activities to help ensure a seamless client experience. Coordinate timelines and deliverables with Service Teams and external partners. Serve as a liaison to Production and Account Management regarding client and prospect information needs, project requirements, and status updates. Communicate with external stakeholders, including captive managers, actuaries, reinsurers, and other key contacts, to gather information, confirm requirements, track timelines, and support project execution. Build professional working relationships with clients, prospects, Producers, service teams, and external partners. Conduct research and preparation related to new business opportunities, cross-selling initiatives, client reviews, and prospect discussions. Help identify relevant industry trends, market developments, regulatory changes, and emerging captive opportunities for consideration by the Captive Practice. Professional Development and Other Responsibilities Develop knowledge of captive insurance structures, alternative risk financing, reinsurance, financial modeling, and related industry practices. Required Education and Experience Bachelor’s degree in Risk Management, Insurance, Business, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience. Five (5) or more years’ experience in an insurance brokerage or agency environment, focused on commercial insurance. Professional underwriting, actuarial, or insurance product management experience.

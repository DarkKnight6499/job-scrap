About The Role The Treasury Analyst will support the current Treasury team to fulfill its global tasks. This role provides support to a variety of topics, including the following activities: Cash Management: Compile daily cash data to support cash positioning, funding, and foreign exchange decisions across all 32 countries that FTI conducts business in Prepare Treasury wire forms for funding and foreign exchange payments Assist with intercompany settlement activities Reporting: Extract and analyze Treasury-related data; compile outputs and translate numbers into clear insights and draft recommendations to support Treasury and Finance decision‑making Build clear models, charts, and dashboards to communicate insights using Excel, Power BI, and PowerPoint Support other recurring and ad-hoc Treasury reporting Cash analytics, forecasting, and planning: Perform cash lookbacks comparing actual results to forecast, summarize variances, and identify key drivers Execute 13-week cash forecasting and longer-term cash planning, document assumptions and highlight risks or opportunities Track DSO and collections performance against targets, summarize trends, and identify key drivers and exceptions Operational bank account management: Support banking access management, issue resolution, and bank KYC Resolve operational issues (e.g., rejected payments/receipts, check inquiries) with banks Other: Participate in special projects as needed (e.g., M&A support, process improvements, Treasury technology enhancements), including analysis, documentation, and implementation support Perform other duties as directed by senior Treasury staff and/or the Treasurer What You’ll Do The right candidate will ensure to perform the required tasks accurately and timely, have a strong interest in making recommendations whenever they see a way to improve existing processes, and have an innate curiosity and motivation to expand their Treasury knowledge and experience. They will also bring a passion for data analysis and visualization—turning complex data into clear, decision-ready insights. This role will provide thorough training on the company’s specifics and require that the candidate is willing and able to understand complex structures and dependencies. We are looking for a responsible and self-driven addition to our team. Over time, you will take increasing ownership of recurring reporting, forecasting components, and stakeholder interactions, with guidance from senior Treasury leadership. This is an ideal role for a recent graduate (1-3 years) with first exposure to operational Treasury, Finance, Accounting, or Banking.

What this job involves: The Corporate Strategy team is tasked with identifying and delivering transformative revenue growth opportunities as part of our enterprise Accelerate2030 strategy. Initiatives we work on span global markets and across all our current and adjacent service lines in commercial real estate - the largest asset class in the world. We work together with Corporate Development, Research, Corporate Finance, Business Leaders and our Executive Leadership Team (Global Executive Board) to recommend JLL’s path forward. This is a unique opportunity for top performers who are seeking a high-impact, high-visibility position to directly impact the most important strategic decisions for JLL, one of the largest commercial real estate companies in the world! The Corporate Strategy (Senior) Associate: Market Analytics will spend their time working cross-functionally with other members of the Corporate Strategy team, Research and Corporate Finance, as a key contributor to programmatic and ongoing enterprise strategy efforts. You will conduct market sizing & share analyses, gap and opportunity analyses across the top 50 markets globally and across all key service lines to support strategic and capital allocation decisions. What your day-to-day will look like: Developing and updating market size, gap and opportunity analyses for commercial real estate services as part of ongoing strategy efforts Synthesize information and form an opinion/perspective to understand how the market analysis relates to client and business opportunities Partner cross-functionally across other JLL teams (e.g., Research, Corporate Finance, Business Line Strategy Functions, Corporate Development) to drive projects to success Build relationships, collaborate, and communicate effectively with stakeholders and senior leaders across the firm Simplifying the complex while driving quantitative rigor in decision-making Instil a client-driven, fact-based and objective approach to analysis, decision-making and prioritization Opportunity for

Position Summary TCW’s Sustainable Investment Team is composed of analysts that have fundamental asset class, sectoral, and thematic coverage, while the Quantitative SI Analyst handles data, analytics, and portfolio construction algorithm development. The team works closely with fundamental investment analysts, product specialists, client services, compliance, and marketing professionals. The quantitative SI Analyst primarily collaborates with the Sustainable Investment Analysts to amplify and systematize their work but may also have independent relationships with quantitative counterparts in different business lines (e.g., Quantitative Investment Research, Portfolio Analytics Group, Risk Management). The Quantitative Analyst also collaborates with Technology in order to oversee the sustainable investing data infrastructure and integration of analytics into various tools and platforms. The Quantitative SI Analyst position is a core member of the Sustainable Investment team and reports to the Head of Sustainable Investments. The Analyst is responsible for identifying, selecting and overseeing the stack of data and tools that comprise the team’s data and analytics platform. The Analyst serves as the team’s primary interface with Technology and oversees the architecture and implementation of the data and analytics platform. The Analyst presents results of the team’s work in a clear and understandable manner to a wide range of internal and external stakeholders, while staying current on relevant sustainability research literature and market developments.

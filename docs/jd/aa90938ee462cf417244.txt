We work with clients to reimagine work, the workforce, and the workplace across the enterprise and to transform their HR functions with AI and emerging technology. With the rapid pace of change in today’s world, you will help clients answer questions like: How do I access, develop, and motivate my workforce? What should my AI strategy be for the HR function? Work You’ll Do As a Senior Consultant, Strategy, Growth, and Transformation on the Organization, Workforce, & Change team, you will: Support the organizational change management (OCM) point of contact and project manager for assigned organizations transitioning from legacy systems to Oracle Cloud HCM. Lead organization-level OCM project plans, schedules, resource plans, and dedicated support teams; coordinate with the OCM Hub Lead to align work with program priorities, resources, and risk management. Support stakeholder analyses, change impact assessments, and readiness assessments; direct development of role-based training, organization-specific communications, and delivery plans. Support stakeholder engagement, readiness validation and go/no-go activities, and cutover and transition activities through go-live; monitor post-go-live adoption, reinforcement, and stabilization. Maintain formal risk and issue logs; drive mitigation planning; make delegated schedule and scope trade-off decisions; report plan, progress, readiness, and risk status to leadership; and align activities with the overall program’s wave deployment schedule.

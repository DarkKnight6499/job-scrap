What You Will Achieve In this role, you will: Serve as Quality Assurance oversight for the validation program at the Andover, MA Pfizer site. Primary focus on validation documentation review and approval including specifications, protocols, other testing documents, summary reports, higher level validation plans, program documentation and strategies. Review and approval of change controls including assessment for validation impact and identification of appropriate testing to ensure maintenance of the validated state. Provide support to project teams on compliance and technical issues related to validation to ensure compliance with company policies and government regulations. Review and approve investigations, commitments, and procedures, and maintenance work orders as they relate to validation. Exercises judgement in resolving simple to moderate quality validation issues. Participate in continuous improvement initiatives. Maintain a constant state is inspection readiness.

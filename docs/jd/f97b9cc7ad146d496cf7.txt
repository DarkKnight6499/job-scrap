Summary of the Job: Sr. Specialist duties will include but are not limited to Collateral processing, review of loan documentation while creating and maintaining a positive relationship with peers, business partners, and customers. About This Role: Key Accountabilities: Review consumer purpose loan documents based upon specific standards Respond and Process e-mail and phone requests in a timely manner while providing a quality customer experience through delivery of accurate information Process lien filings and releases accurately and timely Monitor liens that require ongoing maintenance Review insurance documents and escalate issues Demonstrates regular and predictable attendance, punctuality and adherences to agreed upon schedule of availability. Understand and adhere to all bank policies, laws and regulations applicable to the role The Ideal Candidate for This Role: Knowledge, Skills, Experience Qualifications: 2 or more years of loans or credit experience Ability to read and interpret loan related documents such as memo’s, notes, deeds/mortgages, title policies, security agreements, and more Ability to evaluate processes and procedures to make improvements An understanding of credit and retention policy Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple deadlines as required Self-motivated, results orientated person with problem solving skills Experience in the use of Microsoft Office programs, Foundation, and First Work Candidates must possess unrestricted work authorization and not require future sponsorship. Compensation: Compensation range (base pay): $41,116.00-$65,787.00 This role may have a specific starting pay within this range. Final compensation offer to candidate may vary from posted hiring range based upon work experience, education, and/or skill level. As part of our team, you'll experience the energy and relationship-building of face-to-face collaboration while still enjoying the flexibility of remote workdays. We provide the tools and technology to ensure seamless transitions between work environments, supporting your productivity wherever you are.

Financial Analyst
Aristocrat
Location: Las Vegas, NV, US
Link: https://aristocrat.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/AristocratExternalCareersSite/job/Las-Vegas-NV-US/Financial-Analyst_R0022917

Aristocrat is a pioneer in its field and committed to outstanding performance. Innovation shapes every part of our business as a company built on ideas. Our Financial Analysts help convert Aristocrat’s Reporting Center of Excellence from traditional, static reporting into a dynamic, driver-based model. This opportunity allows you to join a team providing reliable financial data and forecasting tools to assist business leaders in achieving their financial objectives. If you are proactive, motivated, and like working with many internal partners in a fast-moving environment, this position suits you well! What You'll Do Run and maintain existing monthly reporting packages to ensure consistent operating rhythm. Support the development of new reports and work closely with the automation team to deliver improved information and insights. Implement reporting guidelines and automations into existing reports. Build timely and insightful data sources to facilitate decision making. Collaborate with Enterprise D&D Finance to identify reporting gaps and build solutions. Support the Reporting COE to fill in capacity gaps as needed. Document processes to build a balanced reporting environment (SOPs). Participate in other ad hoc projects as needed. What We're Looking For Strong analytical, financial modeling, and problem-solving skills. Experience analyzing financial data, forecasts, trends, and important metrics to support business decision-making. Comfortable building reports in Excel and/or Power BI, validating data, and formatting for an executive audience. Ability to identify process improvement and automation opportunities. Excellent organizational, communication, and collaborator management skills. Self-motivated with strong accountability, attention to detail, and ability to work independently. Proven track record to prioritize multiple tasks and adapt to changing business needs. Collaborative great teammate capable of working effectively across functions, cultures, and global teams. Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience in Data Science, Finance, Analytics, or similar. 2+ years’ experience in finance-related data reporting, business intelligence, or related fields. Highly proficient in Power BI or a similar BI dashboard tool, Excel including pivot tables, macros, formulas, modeling, and charts, and Copilot. Preferred experience (not required) in data transformation tools, SQL, database interaction, IBM Planning & Analytics, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Python, and R.

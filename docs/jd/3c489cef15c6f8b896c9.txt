The Associate Director, Employment Conditions Product and Process Design serves as the accountable business owner for all Employment Condition activities within the People Services (PS) domain. This role fills a critical ownership gap by establishing clear process and product stewardship for employment condition topics that span the full BMS employment lifecycle and are administered through People Services. This leader is responsible for driving global process design, governance, and operational excellence — from initial framework development to system enablement — ensuring alignment with cross-functional partners including HR Business Partners, Legal, Finance, Centers of Excellence for Compensation and Benefits, IT, and internal PS stakeholders. The role is central to maintaining business continuity, optimizing the employee experience, and strengthening PS capabilities at scale.

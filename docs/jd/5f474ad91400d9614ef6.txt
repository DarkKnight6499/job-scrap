Job Description Brookfield is seeking a highly motivated Vice President to join the Real Estate Secondaries Portfolio Management team in New York. This individual will be responsible for investment management, oversight of business plan execution and dispositions across a growing portfolio of global investments. The role requires a combination of real estate experience, portfolio oversight, relationship management, and strategic initiatives, with close collaboration across internal stakeholders and third-party GPs in global markets. Key Accountabilities Support underwriting for investments and portfolio actions, including expected return analysis, performance attribution, and sensitivity work. Responsible for managing relationships with third-party GPs, operators, consultants, and service providers to facilitate business plan execution. Drive asset management oversight by monitoring investment performance, updating business plans, and identifying opportunities to enhance value across the portfolio. Responsible for dispositions, coordinating with legal, GPs and internal sector teams to determine timing and execution for the greatest Assist with hold/sell decisions, recapitalization analysis, dispositions, refinancing, and other capital events, including related IC materials and execution support. Prepare quarterly reporting, investor-facing analyses, and ad hoc materials for senior stakeholders, Investor Relations, and external partners. Work closely with Brookfield's real estate platform, capital markets, finance, tax, legal, and other internal teams to ensure investment oversight across the portfolio lifecycle. Contribute to the development and maintenance of scalable reporting tools, automation, and process improvements across valuation and portfolio management workflows. Candidate Profile Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, Real Estate, or a related field. At least 8 years’ experience in real estate investments and/or investment mangement, portfolio management, private equity, investment banking, or related investing roles; exposure to secondaries preferred. Exceptional analytical, financial modeling, and valuation skills; strong grasp of IRR, MoC, discounted cash flow, and sensitivity analysis. Excellent Excel and PowerPoint skills; familiarity with Argus, Power BI, Anaplan, or similar reporting tools is a plus. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to present clearly to senior stakeholders and external partners. Highly organized, detail-oriented, and proactive, with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment. Self-directed and collaborative, with leadership capabilities, strong judgment, and the maturity to work with limited supervision. Unquestionable ethics and integrity, with a willingness to do what it takes to get the job done.

Job Purpose The Senior Treasury Analyst, Cash Management performs a variety of treasury-related activities, which include daily cash management and daily collateral management activities for the Jackson Treasury department. Essential Responsibilities Supports daily cash management (operating cash and requests from other groups) and related cash moves. Ensures cash availability for short-term payments. Reviews inquiries from banks and other relevant parties. Prepares monthly Cashflow Forecast for senior management and assists with other reporting as needed. Researches cash discrepancies, working with both management and other departments. Develops and prepares reporting for senior management. Liaises with investment manager. Takes lead on various special projects as required. Supports Collateral team activities when needed, including Futures, OTC, and preparation of both internal and external collateral transfers. Acts as a subject matter expert by answering questions and responding to requests. Coaches, mentors, and trains less experienced staff. Other Duties Performs other duties and/or projects as assigned. Knowledge, Skills and Abilities Solid knowledge of accounting principles and procedures. Accuracy and attention to detail. Strong written and verbal communications skills. Ability to build and maintain internal and external relationships. Strong PC skills, including advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel. Knowledge of banking platforms. Understanding of Jackson products and systems. Ability to work independently and as part of a team. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Qualifications Bachelor's Degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics, or equivalent experience required. 5+ years of treasury, accounting, or finance experience required.

Role Summary: The Vice President – Global Treasury will serve as a key leader responsible for shaping and driving the organization’s worldwide treasury strategy. This role will oversee and optimize global cash management, capital structure, and liquidity strategies, while safeguarding the company’s financial health through robust risk management and financing strategies. The VP of Global Treasury will play a critical role in managing banking relationships, executing debt transactions, investing corporate funds, forecasting cash flow and ensuring compliance with internal controls and adherence to regulatory standards. Responsibilities: Develop, implement, and enforce global treasury policies and procedures to ensure consistency and compliance across the business Oversee global cash flow forecasting, ensuring adequate liquidity to support operational and strategic needs Actively participate in debt issuance, refinancing, credit facility negotiations, and other capital markets activities to optimize the company’s capital structure Maintain proactive dialogue and enhance relationships with credit agencies to optimize credit ratings Manage relationships with banking partners and treasury service providers, ensuring competitive rates and high service levels Conduct routine business reviews with service providers to identify opportunities and address emerging risks or trends Direct the development and execution of hedging strategies (foreign exchange and interest rate), monitor financial exposures, and ensure compliance with regulatory and internal guidelines Oversee all credit card processing operations, including monitoring fraud and chargeback activity, and implementing risk mitigation strategies to maintain secure and efficient processes Evaluate emerging payment trends and recommend innovative payment solutions Proactively identify opportunities to optimize and automate treasury processes and controls across all locations, enhance policies, reporting tools, and data quality, and maintain operational excellence Provide oversight of corporate travel and expenses (T&E) and purchasing card (P-Card) programs, evaluating and driving expansion of these programs globally Deliver regular reporting and actionable insights to executive leadership regarding treasury performance and strategic initiatives Collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams to achieve organizational objectives and drive treasury-related projects Foster a culture of innovation, accountability, and continuous improvement within the treasury function Identify and support the

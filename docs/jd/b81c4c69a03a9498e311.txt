Job Description: Position: Vice President, Liquidity Risk Management Level: L10 Position Summary: TD is looking for a Vice President to join TD's Liquidity Risk Management team. This role will be part or the liquidity risk strategy team that is tasked with providing independent risk management oversight of the Liquidity Management team. This team member will be responsible for ensuring that all significant liquidity risk pertaining to the firm's treasury function are thoroughly understood, analyzed, and measured against the bank's risk appetite and applicable regulations. Key Accountabilities: Provide oversight of Liquidity Management, including independent reviews of liquidity position reporting, governance documents, liquidity risk policies, & limit calibrations. Maintain a culture of risk management and control, supported by effective processes in alignment with the established risk appetite for Liquidity Risk. Develop thorough understanding of the bank's liquidity/funding strategies, collateral risks, line of business activities, and drivers of liquidity usage. Ensure the compliance of liquidity risk management processes with US & Canadian regulations. Understanding that regulatory compliance alone is not sufficient and that capabilities should be commensurate with the size, scale, and complexity of the bank. Contribute to the annual Independent Liquidity Evaluation including review of processes, procedures, and controls to ensure that the business, control, and infrastructure functions are meeting the organization's effective risk management requirements. Liaise closely with other business partners including multiple first-line of defense Treasury teams, other second-line of defense risk partners, regulators, and Internal Audit. Facilitate, and/or implement actions and remediation plans to address performance, risk, and governance issues that may arise from self-identified issues, internal audit, or regulators. Keep current on emerging issues, trends, and evolving regulatory requirements and grow knowledge of the businesses and related products.

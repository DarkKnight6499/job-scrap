What you will be doing: Vertex is seeking a curious and motivated laboratory co-op student to support our Structural Biology and Biophysics team. This hands-on opportunity is designed for students who are interested in learning about laboratory-based drug discovery research, protein science, and biophysical characterization. Working alongside experienced scientists, the co-op student will receive guidance and training while contributing to experiments that help characterize proteins, assess molecular interactions, and support the advancement of potential therapies. The role is based on-site , with laboratory work being a core component of the position. Key Responsibilities: Support daily laboratory activities and research projects within the Structural Biology and Biophysics team. Learn and perform routine laboratory techniques under the guidance of experienced scientists. Prepare buffers, reagents, samples, and assay plates by following established procedures. Support biochemical and biophysical assay activities, including nephelometry-based experiments used to evaluate properties such as sample solubility, aggregation, or precipitation. Collect, organize, and document experimental data using laboratory notebooks, electronic systems, spreadsheets, and reports. Follow Vertex laboratory safety, quality, documentation, and compliance requirements. What you will need to succeed: Pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree in biochemistry, biology, chemistry, biotechnology, biophysics, molecular biology, pharmaceutical sciences, or another related scientific field. Available to work on-site for the full duration of the co-op assignment. Interest in laboratory research, protein science, structural biology, biophysics, or drug discovery. Completion of introductory college coursework with a laboratory component in biology, chemistry, biochemistry, or a related field. Comfortable following detailed procedures and asking questions when clarification is needed. Ability to keep clear, accurate records of work performed. Effective written and verbal communication skills. Ability to work independently on assigned tasks and collaboratively as part of a team. Prior experience in structural biology, biophysics, or pharmaceutical research is preferred. Training and mentorship will be provided. Prior exposure to structural biology and/or biophysics through coursework, academic research, laboratory projects, internships, or scientific literature review is beneficial. Interest in structural biology, biophysics, protein science, biochemistry, or drug discovery. Exposure to laboratory techniques such as protein purification, protein assays, molecular biology, analytical chemistry, or biophysical characterization. Familiarity with nephelometry, turbidity measurements, microplate-based assays, plate readers, or laboratory automation is beneficial but not required. Experience using Excel or similar tools to organize, analyze, and graph scientific data. Familiarity with electronic laboratory notebooks, scientific documentation, or presenting project results. Demonstrated curiosity, reliability, and enthusiasm for developing laboratory and scientific problem-solving skills. Legal authorization to work in the United States, now and in the future.

Acting with Integrity Communicating Effectively Pursuing Self-Development Serving Customers Supporting Change Supporting Organizational Goals Working with Diverse Populations What does it take to be successful in this role? • Strong practical understanding of quantitative finance models, including stochastic calculus and their application to real-world investment and risk management challenges. • Deep orientation toward probabilistic thinking; Bayesian mindset. • Solid understanding of the economic rationale and financial implications underlying quantitative models and investment strategies. • Familiarity with major fixed-income asset classes, fixed-income analytics, bond mathematics, and vanilla foreign exchange and interest rate derivatives. • Proficiency in developing and implementing quantitative models using Python; experience with C++ and/or C# is an advantage. • Knowledge of source control and collaborative development practices using Git/GitHub, as well as experience with data platforms and databases such as Snowflake. • Takes ownership of problems and seeks to add value, not just complete assigned tasks. • Systematic thinker who acts to increase, not decrease, the organization and clarity of information. • Ability to seek conceptual understanding, not just technical precision. • Comfortable with explaining quantitative ideas to non-quants, and strives to break-down technical ideas into intuitive, digestible bits. • Intellectual curiosity, with the ability to generate and brainstorm ideas with colleagues. • Team player. Education & Experience Required Bachelor's degree Financial Engineering, Mathematical Finance, Mathematics, or similar quantitative discipline. A minimum of 2 years of related quantitative analysis, financial modeling, or similar experience with a Bachelor's degree, or a minimum of 0 to 1 year of related quantitative analysis, financial modeling, or similar experience with a Master's degree. Or an equivalent combination of education and experience Education & Experience Preferred Master’s degree Financial Engineering, Mathematical Finance, Mathematics, or similar quantitative discipline. Python, C++, SQL or similar experience is preferred. Travel 10 percent or less Principal Duties & Responsibilities • Maintains and enhances asset valuation and net investment income (NII) forecasting models that support FP&A's financial planning, forecasting, and strategic decision-making processes. • Partners with FP&A and other key stakeholders to contribute to long-term initiatives that expand model capabilities and align analytical solutions with evolving business requirements. • Builds strong working relationships across investment, finance, and risk functions by translating complex quantitative concepts into actionable business insights for senior stakeholders. • Delivers timely, actionable analyses using existing quantitative tools and models to inform business and investment decisions. • Develops and calibrates models concerning hedging strategies and optimization in FX, IR, credit, and equities domains. • Supports strategic and tactical asset allocation decisions through economic scenario generation, portfolio optimization, and analysis from both total return and income perspectives. • Communicates complex modeling methodologies, assumptions, and results to non-technical stakeholders, ensuring transparency and business understanding. • Documents model methodologies, key assumptions, calibration processes, and governance standards to support model integrity and regulatory compliance. • Remains current with emerging quantitative and financial modeling techniques, while proactively identifying opportunities to improve data quality, analytical processes, and the production of financial, accounting, and investment reporting. • Performs other duties as assigned.

Job Description The Senior Financial Analyst will provide financial reporting and analytical support for Payments, partnering closely with Finance teams across each business line as well as Corporate Finance. Key responsibilities include preparing weekly sales reporting and insights for executive leadership and supporting the development of executive-level materials for quarterly earnings, forecasts, month-end results, business line reviews, annual planning, and other strategic priorities. This role requires strong communication and organizational skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across teams, manage competing priorities, and deliver high-quality work in a fast-paced environment with tight deadlines. Key Responsibilities Prepare executive-level presentation materials supporting internal and external financial reporting, including annual and long-range plans, forecasts, SEC reporting, financial results, and business line and operating reviews. Manage weekly sales reporting by consolidating data from multiple sources, analyzing trends and performance drivers, and translating results into meaningful insights for executive leadership. Support the preparation of quarterly earnings materials and other key financial reporting, ensuring information is accurate, timely, and clearly communicated. Analyze financial and business performance, identifying key trends, drivers, risks, and opportunities to support leadership decision-making. Partner across Payment Services, Corporate Finance, and other key stakeholders to support financial reporting, analysis, and broader business priorities. Identify and leverage AI tools to streamline financial reporting and analysis, automate routine activities, and generate more meaningful insights for decision-making. Perform ad hoc financial analysis and reporting and support cross-functional projects that enable informed business and leadership decision-making. Review financial reporting and executive materials to ensure accuracy, consistency, and quality. Basic Qualifications Bachelor's degree, or equivalent work experience Typically six or more years of related experience Preferred Skills/Experience Working knowledge of financial analysis techniques and general accounting procedures Well-developed mathematical and analytical skills Advanced MS PowerPoint, Excel and Essbase skills Thorough knowledge in financial analysis, forecasting, and planning Result orientated, ability to recognize needs, solve problems, anticipate issues, and develop solutions using strong analytical, business and financial acumen Self-motivated, able to set priorities effectively, multi-task, pivot easily as priorities change and follow through to meet the demands of multiple constituencies Strong technical skills related to data mining and visualization tools Master's degree preferred *This role requires working from a U.S. Bank location three (3) or more days per week.

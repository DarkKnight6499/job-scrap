Job Description Summary This role is responsible for managing a large portfolio of vendors (200+) and contracts, leading negotiations, and ensuring vendors deliver against performance, cost, and risk expectations. The ideal candidate thrives in a fast-paced environment with competing priorities and brings a strong balance of commercial acumen, risk awareness, and relationship management. Job Description Essential Duties and Responsibilities Manage a portfolio of vendor contracts and service-level agreements (SLAs), including negotiation, execution, and ongoing oversight Lead complex contract negotiations, including pricing, terms, and performance standards, ensuring alignment to business needs and risk considerations Monitor vendor performance, ensuring service delivery meets contractual obligations and business expectations; proactively address issues and drive remediation Identify, assess, and manage vendor-related risks, escalating concerns as appropriate Partner closely with business stakeholders to understand evolving needs, prioritize initiatives, and deliver solutions on time Manage departmental budgets related to vendor spend, including forecasting, tracking, and cost optimization opportunities Coordinate the end-to-end vendor lifecycle, from vendor selection and onboarding through payment and ongoing management Develop and deliver clear, tailored communications and reporting for stakeholders across varying levels of the organization Manage multiple projects simultaneously in a dynamic environment with shifting priorities Meet with suitable vendors to assess their products, inquire about their services, negotiate pricing, and communicate any product or service-related concerns. Conduct research on available vendors to determine which vendors offer the best pricing and product quality.

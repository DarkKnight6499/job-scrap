Associate Director, Product Management, UpToDate Core Experience & Access The Role Make the core UpToDate experience easy to access, use, and evolve for clinicians, care teams, and enterprise customers. At Wolters Kluwer Health, the UpToDate suite supports millions of clinical decisions every day. As customer expectations, enterprise access models, clinical workflows, and digital product experiences continue to evolve, we are focused on delivering intuitive, reliable, and customer-ready experiences across core UpToDate web and mobile products. As Associate Director, Product Management, UpToDate Core Experience & Access, you will own product strategy and execution for a meaningful area of the core UpToDate experience. This includes improving registration, login, access, identity, and entitlement experiences; advancing web and mobile product improvements; managing important customer, accessibility, operational, and roadmap priorities; and helping shape how the core UpToDate experience evolves over time. This is a senior individual contributor role for a product leader who can operate across user experience, technical systems, enterprise customer needs, and product strategy. You will partner closely with engineering, UX, architecture, security, analytics, customer-facing, commercial, implementation, and product leadership teams to define priorities, make tradeoffs, and deliver measurable improvements for users and customers. What You’ll Do Own product strategy, roadmap priorities, and measurable outcomes for core UpToDate experience and access-related capabilities. Develop a deep understanding of how clinicians, care teams, administrators, and enterprise customers access and use UpToDate across web and mobile experiences. Identify opportunities to improve registration, login, authentication, SSO, account, identity, and entitlement workflows. Partner with engineering, architecture, security, implementation, and customer-facing teams to support scalable, customer-ready approaches to access, account, identity, and entitlement experiences. Lead discovery and prioritization for core product improvements that enhance usability, accessibility, reliability, customer readiness, and overall product quality. Manage roadmap priorities that balance product evolution with important customer requests, accessibility needs, operational improvements, and platform dependencies. Help shape consistent, intuitive product experiences across core UpToDate web and mobile platforms. Translate complex user, customer, technical, and business needs into clear product requirements, decision frameworks, roadmap priorities, and success metrics. Partner with UX, analytics, engineering, commercial, customer success, implementation, support, and other product teams from discovery through launch and iteration. Use customer conversations, product analytics, stakeholder input, experimentation, and post-launch feedback to inform product direction and measure impact. Make product decisions that balance user value, customer needs, technical feasibility, implementation complexity, accessibility, security, cost, risk, and business impact. Communicate product priorities, rationale, risks, dependencies, and tradeoffs clearly across cross-functional teams and leadership. Demonstrated ability to own a meaningful product area, set roadmap priorities, and deliver measurable outcomes across multiple capabilities or workstreams. Strong product discovery and execution skills, including customer research, requirements definition, prioritization, roadmap development, experimentation, launch planning, and metrics-driven decision-making. Experience translating complex user, customer, technical, operational, and business needs into practical product plans and intuitive product experiences. Technical fluency with product areas that involve user access, account experience, authentication, identity, permissions, entitlements, enterprise administration, or implementation complexity.

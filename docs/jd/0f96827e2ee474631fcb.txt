Citi’s Strategic Equity Solutions (“SES”) Advisory business partners with leading alternative asset managers to develop and execute strategic capital and liquidity solutions across the private markets ecosystem. Working with sponsors across private equity, infrastructure, private credit and real estate, the Advisory team originates, structures and executes a broad range of transactions, including continuation vehicles, GP-led secondary transactions, NAV-based financings, preferred equity investments and other bespoke strategic capital solutions. We are seeking an experienced Analyst or Associate to join our New York-based Advisory team. This individual will work closely with senior bankers and clients throughout the lifecycle of complex transactions while helping expand one of Citi’s fastest-growing advisory businesses. As a lean, entrepreneurial team within Citi’s global Investment Banking and Markets platform, professionals are given early responsibility, direct client exposure and the opportunity to make meaningful contributions to both transaction execution and the continued growth of the business. This role offers significant client exposure, meaningful transaction responsibility and the opportunity to work alongside senior decision-makers at many of the world’s leading alternative asset managers. Key Responsibilities Support the origination and execution of continuation vehicles, GP-led secondary transactions, NAV financings, preferred equity investments and other strategic capital solutions. Build sophisticated financial models, valuation analyses and transaction structures at both the portfolio company and fund level. Develop client presentations, strategic analyses, marketing materials and investment committee documentation. Conduct financial, commercial and structural due diligence across complex private markets transactions. Coordinate execution processes with clients, legal counsel, accounting firms, financing providers and other advisors. Support investor marketing efforts, including preparation of marketing materials, management presentations, investor due diligence and transaction execution. Work closely with Sponsor Coverage, Investment Banking, Capital Markets, Financing and other product partners across Citi to deliver integrated client solutions. Monitor market developments across GP-led secondaries, NAV financing, private capital formation and broader strategic capital solutions. Contribute to internal market intelligence, analytical tools, thought leadership and business development initiatives supporting the continued expansion of the Advisory platform. Qualifications 2–5 years of experience in investment banking, secondaries advisory, private equity, infrastructure investing, private credit, valuation advisory or another relevant financial advisory role. Strong financial modeling, valuation and analytical skills. Exceptional written and verbal communication abilities. Ability to manage multiple workstreams in a fast-paced, team-oriented environment. Strong attention to detail, sound judgment and excellent project management skills. Demonstrated interest in private markets, alternative asset management and strategic advisory. Preferred Experience Experience with one or more of the following is highly desirable: GP-led secondary transactions. Continuation vehicles. NAV financing. Preferred equity. Private equity. Infrastructure. Private credit. Real estate. Fund finance. What We Offer This role provides a unique opportunity to work directly with senior decision-makers at many of the world’s leading alternative asset managers on some of the most innovative transactions in the private markets. Because the Strategic Equity Solutions Advisory business continues to expand, successful candidates will have the opportunity to do more than execute transactions. Team members will help shape processes, develop analytical capabilities, contribute to business development initiatives and assume increasing responsibility as the franchise grows.

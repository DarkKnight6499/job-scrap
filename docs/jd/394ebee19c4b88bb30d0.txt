An aspiring Oscar-worthy director who can get the best shot and coach senior leaders to show up in the best ways on camera? Do you understand how to connect people to the deeper purpose in the work they do everyday through visuals and creative design? If so, this role could be for you! About the Role We’re seeking a Multimedia Specialist to join the Card Communications team who will be responsible for delivering best-in-class videos and design graphics which help us tell the Card story. These creative elements will be featured in both local and Card-wide communication vehicles and will cover some of our most important moments that matter throughout the year. You’ll inform and engage associates across the organization, helping them stay connected to our business priorities and feel engaged as part of our inclusive culture. You’ll partner closely with key stakeholders to uncover compelling story ideas, use strong creative judgement to consult on the most effective ways to bring those stories to life, deeply understand our associates’ preferences by analyzing performance data and generating actionable insights, and manage the entire end-to-end video production process from scripting and preparation to filming and editing. We’re looking for someone who can partner with communicators to shape creative strategies and concepts for executive and associate storytelling, capture and edit content into polished pieces, and bring fresh thinking to how we use multimedia and creative design to keep associates inspired and informed. You will have the opportunity to frequently see your work showcased to an internal audience of more than 30,000 associates while working side-by-side with smart, talented people who will challenge you to grow and excel.

Machinery Breakdown Risk Engineering Consultant
Zurich Insurance
Location: Virtual, KS, US
Link: https://www.careers.zurich.com/job/Virtual-Machinery-Breakdown-Risk-Engineering-Consultant-KS/1377076257/

Zurich is seeking a Machinery Breakdown Risk Engineer (Mid-Senior or Senior level). The ideal location for this qualified individual is within the MO, KS, IA and NE areas. This is a field role with approximately 20-25% overnight travel. This is a work from home role and includes a company car. As a Machinery Breakdown Risk Engineer you are responsible for: Providing jurisdictional inspections and machinery breakdown risk assessment services for customers and business partners. Provides intermediate machinery breakdown consultative services to customers, including identifying exposures with significant loss potential, investigation of cause/effect of major losses and hazard/machinery breakdown management programs. Provides basic level training for customers, service design and coordination, marketing, and loss investigations. Provides Account Engineering and coordination services to the Chicago and Cleveland based underwriting team. Supports the Machinery Breakdown Sales and Marketing team. Final candidates will also be subject to a Motor Vehicle record background check, because this position includes a company car. The role will be filled at the Mid-Senior or Senior level. The hiring manager will determine the appropriate level based upon the selected applicant’s experience and skill set relative to the qualifications listed for this position. Basic Qualifications: Mid-Senior Risk Engineering Consultant: Bachelors Degree and 3 or more years of experience within Boiler and Machinery in the Risk Engineering and Safety area OR High School Diploma or Equivalent and 5 or more years of experience within Boiler and Machinery in the Risk Engineering and Safety area OR Senior Risk Engineering Consultant: Bachelors Degree and 6 or more years of experience within Boiler and Machinery in the Risk Engineering and Safety area OR High School Diploma or Equivalent and 8 or more years of experience within Boiler and Machinery in the Risk Engineering and Safety area OR Zurich Certified Apprentice, including an Associates degree and 6 or more years of experience within the Boiler and Machinery Risk Engineering and Safety area AND Working in a team environment Preferred Qualifications: Advanced knowledge within the boiler and machinery industry segment Advanced experience in the assessment of risk, based on what is required by Machinery Breakdown specific competency standards National Board of Boiler and Pressure Vessel Inspectors Commission Card Navy Nuclear School Insurance industry experience Advanced Microsoft Office skills (Microsoft Outlook, Excel, Teams, and Word) Strong communication and organization skills Experience working in a team environment Your pay at Zurich is based on your role, location, skills, and experience.

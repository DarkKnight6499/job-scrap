Help shape how we manage liquidity across a global financial institution. In this role, you will turn balance sheet and market dynamics into clear insights and actionable strategy. You will work with leaders across Treasury and key stakeholders to support resilient funding and liquidity outcomes. If you enjoy solving complex problems with data and sound judgment, you will find meaningful impact here. As a Senior Associate in the Liquidity Management Strategy team, you will monitor firmwide and legal entity balance sheets and liquidity positions, including currency exposures, to help guide our liquidity strategy. You will support forecasting and internal stress measures that inform how we manage liquidity under business-as-usual and stressed conditions. You will partner closely with stakeholders to translate complex analytics into clear messages and decision-ready materials. You will join a collaborative team that values thoughtful analysis, ownership, and strong risk awareness.

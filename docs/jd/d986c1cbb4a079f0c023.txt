About the role: As a Statement Processing Associate I, you’ll perform bulk file, cycle set-up and manual statement rendering duties for various banks. Role location: Onsite at our Canton, MA facility Schedule: Monday–Friday, 10:00 a.m.–6:30 p.m. EST Opportunities for overtime Must work one weekend per month What you will be doing: Preparing cycles for manual and/or automated enclosing by sorting rejects, working direct fine sort reports and manually filing rejects Handling special processing for various banks Manually rendering enclosure and non-enclosure statements using appropriate marketing inserts and envelopes Preparing bulk items to be sorted at statement cycle time Filing bulk file items within stationary and movable storage units Performing physical searches using systems to determine item locations for rejects and lose items Working snag statements before sending them to customers Kicking off image statements at selected data centers, resolving snags and completing the process by sending statements to print Preparing truncation for pick-up and delivery to an alternate storage site for storage or shredding Performing necessary quality checks throughout the statement/notice process Answering questions and addressing concerns from internal and, when applicable, external customers What you will need: Ability to lift and carry trays and envelopes weighing up to 40 pounds Considerable knowledge of statement processing Experience operating mail machines Ability to perform calculations using a calculator Proficient written and verbal communication skills when working with employees and external customers/clients What we offer you: At FIS, you can learn, grow and make an impact in your career.

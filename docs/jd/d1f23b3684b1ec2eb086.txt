We’re actively seeking a talented Project Portfolio Management Director to join our Portfolio and Program Management Office (PPMO) for our Consumer Markets Division, located in Newport Beach, CA. This role follows a hybrid schedule of four days in the office. If you are not currently located near the office, we offer relocation assistance to well-qualified candidates. The Project Portfolio Management Director leads portfolio planning, governance, and execution discipline across the Consumer Markets Division. This role connects strategy to execution by strengthening portfolio planning, prioritization, capacity management, resource allocation, investment governance, and benefits realization practices. The Director is a people leader for a team comprised of portfolio and planning experts. Key Responsibilities: Enterprise Portfolio Planning: Align CMD’s strategy, priorities, capacity, and funding with Enterprise to deliver the highest value outcomes for CMD and PL strategic objectives. CMD Cross-Portfolio Planning: Integrate program planning and roadmaps to determine dependencies, sequencing, and trade-off decisioning. Reporting & Data-Driven Insights: Deliver decision-ready insights through executive-ready recommendations, and governance forums, dashboards, newsletters, and centralized repositories. Annuities & Life Program Increment (PI) Planning: Partner with release train engineers to align priorities and capacity against quarterly planning events across divisional and company level forums. PPMO Standards & Playbooks: Strengthen portfolio and program delivery standards, methodologies, reporting practices and execution disciplines while preserving the agility needed to respond to changing business needs and aligning to Enterprise portfolio standards and COE. Stakeholder & Relationship Management : Partner with product, underwriting, operations, distribution, technology, finance, program, and project leaders to drive decisions and remove execution friction. What Success looks like: Portfolio decisions are faster, clearer, and grounded in strategic value, capacity constraints, critical risk mitigation, dependencies and trade-offs. CMD roadmaps, program planning and delivery governance materials are current, consistent, and decision-ready. Portfolio risks, issues, dependencies, sequencing conflicts, and capacity constraints are surfaced early with clear ownership. Leaders have reliable insights and recommendations across functional, divisional and company levels for portfolio trade-off discussions. PPMO standards, playbooks, and planning routines are practical, adopted, and continuously improved. Connect Annuities & Life PI Planning with ways of working across the divisional functions in order to maximize investment returns and prioritize the highest value work. Required experience: 10+ years in portfolio management, program management, project management, strategic planning, governance, or business transformation, including leadership of matrixed and direct teams. 4-year degree or equivalent experience Experience managing complex business and technology portfolios in a matrixed environment.

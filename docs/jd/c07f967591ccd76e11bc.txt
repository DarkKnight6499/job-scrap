We are currently looking for an IT Business Analyst to join our team in Franklin, TN . In this role, you will support one or more products within the value stream by translating business needs into clear requirements, aligning solution priorities with Product Managers, and ensuring proposed approaches follow organizational standards. You will also partner with internal and outsourced development teams to clarify expectations, validate deliverables, and maintain quality and timely execution while contributing to continuous improvement across the product lifecycle. A Day in the Life: Serve as a liaison across departments, vendors, and technical teams, translating business requirements into technical specifications, facilitating communication, and guiding or coordinating other analysts when needed. Collaborate with technical teams on technical design and implementation for assigned products. Oversee code quality, security, and performance through reviews and automation. Manage incidents and production support, working with multiple third‑party vendors, interpreting technical issues, reviewing logs or source logic, and ensuring timely resolution and effective maintenance. Lead requirements gathering and documentation, using interviews, workshops, workflow analysis, and enterprise methodologies to produce business requirements, functional specs, use cases, interface designs, and testing artifacts. Apply strong analytical, technical, and managerial skills, including familiarity with programming environments, Agile delivery, project and risk management, and the ability to evaluate information, reconcile conflicts, and drive clarity. Collaborate and communicate effectively with diverse stakeholders, influencing decisions, building consensus, challenging assumptions, and interacting professionally with executives, managers, developers, and subject matter experts. Support organizational goals across domains and regions, working autonomously, adapting to shifting priorities or off‑hours needs, contributing to feasibility studies and cost analyses, and engaging in multiple initiatives simultaneously. Who We’re Looking for: Required: Bachelor’s degree required plus 5+ years of overall IS/IT experience. 5+ years of experience working in an application development environment. 5+ years of experience working with third-party vendors. Hands‑on experience with a diverse technology ecosystem, including: Mainframe systems Big Data platforms Snowflake AWS Cloud Tableau and Power BI Java Machine Learning tools and frameworks Strong experience with Salesforce and Agentforce. Ability to work effectively in Agile/Scrum environments, participate in sprint activities, and manage work through JIRA. Ability to work on-site in Franklin, TN. Desired: Advanced technical degree preferred. Prior experience working with offshore teams. Project management experience. Proven ability to lead and support multiple quality analytics initiatives across complex, enterprise‑level environments. Skilled at coordinating across organizations, optimizing processes, and supporting analytics and operational objectives.

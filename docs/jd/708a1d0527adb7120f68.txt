Job Description Summary The Engineering EHS Risk Manager will serve as the primary EHS subject-matter expert and risk advisor for GE Vernova Gas Power Engineering in Greenville, SC, with support across North America and global Engineering locations as needed. This role will lead Engineering EHS risk management, MOC, compliance support, incident learning, and EHS operating rigor for Engineering-owned activities, while partnering closely with Site EHS, Facilities, Global Supply Chain, contractors, and business EHS teams. The role reports to the GE Vernova Gas Power Engineering Business Operations leader and requires strong technical judgment, influence, and practical leadership to help Engineering work safely and compliantly. Job Description Job Responsibilities: Govern Engineering EHS Management of Change for equipment, labs, test operations, Standard Operating Procedures, chemicals, work methods, facilities interfaces, and other changes that may create EHS risk. Facilitate or support HAZOP, FMEA, or equivalent risk assessments for Engineering Labs Facilitate or support MOC reviews, ensure appropriate risk assessment and approvals, verify controls before use, and track actions through closure. Interpret applicable OSHA, EPA, NFPA, company, state/local, and relevant international EHS requirements for Engineering activities and coordinate with specialists where needed. Partner with Engineering teams to embed EHS early in project planning, lab changes, test operations, prototype work, equipment installation, commissioning, and contractor activities. Review higher-risk Engineering work before execution and escalate when risks, controls, approvals, or ownership are unclear. Lead or support investigations of Engineering incidents, near misses, and unsafe conditions; ensure root causes, corrective actions, lessons learned, and effectiveness checks are completed. Plan and conduct risk-based EHS reviews, audits, and compliance assessments for Engineering labs, test areas, equipment, and work activities. Support Power segment EHS reviews, audits, and compliance assessments as appropriate. Establish and support Engineering EHS operating mechanisms, including KPIs, leading indicators, training status, audit findings, MOC actions, compliance actions, and leadership review cadence. Clarify ownership boundaries between Engineering, Site EHS, Facilities, Global Supply Chain, contractors, Business Operations EHS SME’s and other organizations while ensuring Engineering risks are identified, escalated, and closed. Influence and coach Engineering leaders, managers, and employees to make practical, risk-based decisions that support safe and compliant execution. Requirements: Bachelor’s degree in Occupational Environment, Health and/or Safety, Engineering or equivalent Minimum of 5 years of EHS experience in an industrial, engineering, manufacturing, laboratory, test, energy, aerospace, or similarly technical environment Eligibility Requirements: This is a 100% onsite position in Greenville, SC (this is not a remote position) Desired Characteristics: Experience with PSM, PHA, HAZOP, FMEA, What-if Analysis, LOPA, or equivalent hazard-analysis methods Ability to build simple EHS operating mechanisms such as risk registers, leading indicators, compliance calendars, training matrices, audit plans, and MOC trackers Strong communicator and influencer with the confidence to challenge risk while helping teams find practical solutions Preferred certification such as CSP, CIH, CHMM, PE, ISO 45001/14001 auditor qualification, or equivalent EHS credential GE Vernova offers a great work environment, professional development, challenging careers, and competitive compensation.

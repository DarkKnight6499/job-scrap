Summary The Enterprise Risk Analytics & Reporting Sr Analyst supports enterprise risk reporting, dashboards, and data visualizations used for Executive Management and Board-level committee oversight. This role partners with Risk Management and first line stakeholders to translate data and reporting needs into clear, accurate, and actionable insights. The position helps strengthen enterprise risk reporting through data validation, reporting automation, documentation, stakeholder support, and continuous improvement of reporting solutions. Duties & Responsibilities Support the development of quarterly risk committee reporting packages, including the creation, update, and enhancement of data visualizations that communicate key risk trends and insights for Executive Management/Board-level committee oversight. Translate stakeholder reporting and data visualization requirements into new or enhanced dashboards, reports, and visualizations within Archer, Power BI, or other reporting platforms; proactively identify opportunities to improve usability, clarity, automation, and insight beyond initial stakeholder requests. Stakeholders include both second line and first line of defense partners. Perform recurring data validation, table refreshes, and reporting data quality checks to support accurate, complete, and timely reporting outputs. Coordinate with Data Engineering and other technical partners to troubleshoot data issues, support source system or table updates, and improve ETL processes, data model refreshes, and recurring data workflows to enhance reporting reliability, efficiency, and scalability. Provide stakeholder education, reporting guidance, and ongoing support to promote effective use and understanding of dashboards, reports, and data visualizations; respond to reporting questions, troubleshoot issues, and help stakeholders interpret data outputs and insights. Document reporting actions, design decisions, processes, controls, and overall strategy to support transparency, continuity, and repeatability; maintain clear documentation that enables others to understand, support, or replicate reporting and data visualization approaches. It is the responsibility of this role to take ownership of all tasks and challenges that they encounter in the operation of their assigned position.

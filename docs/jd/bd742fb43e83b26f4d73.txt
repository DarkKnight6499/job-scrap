What you'll do: As an Analytics Engineer II on our Regulatory Reporting team, you'll play a hands-on role in building the data platform that powers Underdog's prediction markets business. This is a zero-to-one opportunity. We're scaling fast and building the regulatory reporting infrastructure from the ground up. You'll work across engineering, product, compliance, and senior leadership, getting first-hand exposure to how a federally regulated prediction markets exchange runs and what it takes to keep it running. The end goal is a fully automated reporting platform: purpose-built dbt models, automated validations, and pipelines that run without manual intervention. You'll build toward that from day one while doing the hands-on investigation work that teaches you exactly what needs to be built and why. Run daily validation across our regulatory reporting submissions, verify accuracy, flag breaks, and own triage when something looks off. Investigate data issues end-to-end: get into the raw data, follow the problem wherever it goes, document findings, and hand the team something specific to act on. Build and maintain dbt models purpose-built for regulatory reporting, slim, tested, connected directly to raw data, and resilient to upstream changes. Implement automated test cases and validations that catch recurring issues before they become problems. Support pre-launch submission validation, audit requests, and legal and state data pulls as the function grows. Own the feedback loop with product and engineering: monitor upstream changes, anticipate their impact on reporting, and raise issues before they hit a deadline. Participate in the on-call rotation with the broader team. Who you are: Builder: You want to build a platform, not run a checklist. You think in systems and automation, and you take pride in making things more reliable over time. Strong SQL: You're comfortable writing complex queries, getting into raw data, and following a problem wherever it goes. Engineering-minded: You think in pipelines and you want to build things that don't need babysitting. You have hands-on experience with dbt. Reliable under deadline: You understand what it means to own something with a hard cutoff. You stay calm, move fast, and communicate early when something looks off. Excited by the scope: You want a role where your work touches engineering, product, compliance, and leadership. You thrive when you can see how everything connects. Bonus points if you have: Experience in a federally regulated environment at a prediction markets platform, fintech, crypto exchange, or neobank. Experience in the sports industry, particularly fantasy sports or sports betting. Background in reconciliation, trade-level reporting, or data validation work. Hands-on experience with Airflow or similar orchestration tooling. Experience with data contracts, schema registries, or similar approaches to managing upstream data dependencies. Any exposure to prediction markets, derivatives, or exchange operations. Experience in a role with on-call responsibilities or SLA-driven data work.

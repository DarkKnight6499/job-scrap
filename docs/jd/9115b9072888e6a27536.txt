About the Role: Grade Level (for internal use): 15 The Team The Vice President, Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A), Energy is a senior finance leader responsible for shaping the financial strategy, performance management agenda and decision support for S&P Global Energy. The role will serve as a trusted advisor to the Energy CFO and senior business leaders, translating business strategy into financial plans, challenging assumptions, and providing forward-looking insights that improve growth, profitability, investment discipline and execution. This leader will own the division FP&A agenda across planning, forecasting, performance analysis and strategic financial planning, while building strong connectivity with Enterprise Finance & Strategy and other corporate partners. The VP will lead and develop a high-performing global team and create the financial transparency, operating rigor and analytical capability required to enable senior leaders to make informed decisions. Responsibilities and Impact Enterprise FP&A Leadership & Financial Strategy Own the end-to-end FP&A agenda for S&P Global Energy, including annual budgeting, forecasting, long-range planning, management reporting, performance reviews and scenario analysis. Partner with the Energy CFO and senior leadership to translate strategic priorities into actionable financial plans, performance targets and resource allocation choices. Shape the financial performance narrative for the division, identifying underlying business drivers, emerging risks and opportunities, and the actions required to improve outcomes. Provide an independent, enterprise-minded finance perspective, constructively challenging assumptions and ensuring decisions balance growth, profitability, investment requirements and long-term value creation. Lead financial risk and opportunity assessment and establish mechanisms to surface material performance issues early and drive accountability for corrective actions. Drive divisional M&A activities, including acquisitions, divestitures, winddowns, and internal business transfers. Drive transformation within the finance organization through the streamlining of workflows and the introduction of AI enabled processes. Executive Decision Support & Business Partnership Serve as a trusted strategic finance advisor to senior Energy leaders, bringing rigorous analysis and judgment to business strategy, portfolio choices, investments and operating decisions. Lead the preparation and delivery of decision-ready financial insights for executive forums, including Quarterly Finance and Business Reviews and other senior leadership reviews. Evaluate strategic business cases for organic and inorganic growth opportunities, including ROI, NPV, revenue potential, cost efficiencies, integration considerations and synergy opportunities. Partner across Product, Commercial, Strategy and other functions to assess growth opportunities, improve economic outcomes and strengthen linkage between business priorities and financial commitments. Conduct market, competitor and benchmarking analysis where relevant to inform strategy, performance expectations and investment choices. Revenue, Profitability & Performance Management Provide leadership and oversight of revenue and expense planning, forecasting and performance analysis across the Energy portfolio. Build a driver-based understanding of financial performance, connecting commercial and operating indicators to revenue, margin, expense and cash outcomes. Partner with business leaders to assess revenue performance and growth opportunities using relevant measures such as retention, wallet share, pricing, product mix and customer growth. Establish clear performance metrics, management dashboards and forward-looking analytics that improve transparency, forecast quality and accountability. Drive disciplined trade-off decisions across investments, productivity opportunities and cost actions to support sustainable financial performance. Workforce, Investment & Resource Allocation Lead financial oversight of Energy workforce planning, partnering with People and business leaders to align headcount, location strategy, capabilities and investment with strategic priorities. Establish next generation headcount analytics and insights across full time employees and 3rd party support Advise senior leadership on allocation and reallocation of resources across businesses, products and initiatives based on strategic importance, financial return and execution capacity. Provide financial leadership for major transformation, productivity and investment programs, establishing financial baselines, benefits tracking and clear accountability for outcomes. Operating Excellence, Controls & Transformation Strengthen FP&A operating rhythms, planning methodologies, management reporting and governance to improve speed, consistency and decision usefulness. Champion automation, advanced analytics, AI-enabled capabilities and modern planning tools to increase FP&A effectiveness and shift capacity toward higher-value insight and partnership. Partner closely with Controllers and other Finance teams to maintain strong financial governance, policy alignment, reporting integrity and compliance with applicable requirements. Create scalable processes and standards while retaining the flexibility required to support the differing economics and strategic priorities across the Energy portfolio. Team Leadership Build, lead and develop a high-performing, globally connected FP&A organization with strong commercial acumen, analytical rigor and executive-level business partnership capabilities. Set clear priorities and standards for the team, fostering accountability, collaboration, continuous improvement and an enterprise mindset. Develop finance talent and succession pipelines, creating opportunities for broader business exposure, capability building and career growth. Lead effectively across matrixed teams and stakeholders, creating alignment around priorities, roles and financial outcomes.

Position Summary: Working within the Global FP&A team, the Financial Analyst will be focused on the International Digital business segment with forecasting, financial reporting and other related processes. This position reports to the Senior Manager of Global FP&A, supporting the overall FP&A team and works closely with the Business Intelligence, Marketing, Controllership, and Film HQ teams. The candidate should display an aptitude for financial systems; prior experience in SAP/BW/BPC or Essbase is preferred but not required. This person ideally has a finance/accounting background with strong Excel skills, is detail orientated, and thrives in a fast-paced environment. Responsibilities: Support monthly pacing forecast and annual budget process; help to consolidate and upload financial data to studio Film HQ. Support monthly accrual process. Support monthly and quarterly closing processes. Prepare and manage financial reporting as necessary. Work with team to drive business process improvements. Build effective business partner relationships with internal stakeholders. Support the Senior Management Team with ad-hoc data and financial analysis requests. Basic Requirements: Bachelor’s Degree in Business, Finance, Accounting or related field. Minimum 1 year of experience in Finance related role preferred. Proficient in MS Excel and PowerPoint. Database systems experience preferred. Desired Characteristics: Interest and/or experience in media and entertainment. Possess an aptitude for financial systems, solid analytical abilities, outstanding process improvement capabilities, and strong interpersonal skills. Exceptional attention to detail and high standards for quality and accuracy. Solid organizational skills and ability to prioritize multiple and competing priorities. Excellent communication skills and a proactive approach to building working relationships in a matrix environment. Ability to plan and organize work in order to meet tight deadlines in a fast-paced environment. Possesses a high level of personal and professional integrity.

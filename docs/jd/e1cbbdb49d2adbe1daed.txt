To be successful in this role, we’re seeking the following: • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, Business, or related field. • Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills. • Attention to detail and ability to manage multiple priorities. • Experience with financial systems, investment operations, or accounting processes preferred.

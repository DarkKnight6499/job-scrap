The Business Analyst is responsible for preparing and distributing accurate EGM and Operational statistical summary reports based on the predefined report schedule. The Business Analyst will proactively, and as requested, prepare analysis and recommendations to improve the revenue of existing or growing product base. What You'll Do Prepare and distribute EGM reports based on a predefined schedule. Connect with sales staff and other departments. Conduct statistical analysis on accumulated game performance data. Analyze and offer recommendations to sales staff on denomination mix, model types, and game types to ensure maximum revenue. Accompany the Sales Account Manager as requested to customer locations, including but not limited to routine visits and annual account reviews. Manage relationships with other departments is vital in achieving company goals. Follow State and Tribal Gaming Regulations and company policies and procedures. Provide guidance to Finance & Data Analysts. What We're Looking For Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Sales, Marketing, or Business. Or a minimum of five years of job-related experience or an equivalent combination of education and experience.

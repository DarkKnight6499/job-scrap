Citi's Asset Backed Securitization and Financing business is one of the most active and diversified structured finance platforms in the market, operating across a broad range of asset classes including auto, equipment, consumer, solar, with a focus on the esoteric products. The team sits within Citi's Spread Products Business group and works across the full securitization lifecycle — from new-issue structuring and deal analysis through to ongoing surveillance and performance monitoring. The business partners closely with originators, underwriters, and investors globally, leveraging deep analytical capability and market expertise to deliver best-in-class structured finance solutions at scale. We are looking for a Vice President/ Director to join the Financing and Securitization Analytics team in a structuring-focused role, where you will own the development and management of cashflow models across a large and varied ABS portfolio, support deal structuring workflows. This is a role for a structured finance professional who combines strong quantitative and modelling skills with the ability to work across deal teams, mentor analysts, and contribute to both the analytical and operational infrastructure of a high-performing business. Key Responsibilities Lead the structuring and execution of ABS transactions, including warehouse facilities and term securitizations, across Core (Auto, Personal Loan, Equipment) and Esoteric (Aviation, Data Center, Triple Net Lease) asset classes. Develop, maintain, and enhance detailed cash flow, collateral, and credit models using Excel, Intex, and related analytical tools. Conduct rigorous quantitative analysis, including bond math, scenario analysis, and stress testing, to assess deal economics, structural features, and credit risk. Coordinate execution with internal teams including credit risk, capital markets, syndicate, sales, trading, legal, and operations. Interface with external parties such as rating agencies, legal counsel, accountants, and investors throughout the lifecycle of each transaction. Support capital markets execution through preparation of rating agency materials, offering documentation, and investor marketing presentations. Review and provide feedback on transaction documentation to ensure alignment with deal structures, credit assumptions, and regulatory requirements. Identify new asset classes, subsectors, and structuring opportunities in collaboration with coverage teams and origination partners. Required Qualifications 6+ years of experience in ABS structuring, securitized products, or structured finance, with exposure to non‑traditional collateral. Strong quantitative and analytical skills, with advanced proficiency in Excel and Intex; VBA experience preferred. Deep understanding of ABS deal structures, securitization mechanics, and market conventions. Proven experience working with rating agencies, external counsel, and institutional investors on transaction execution. Strong judgment and attention to detail in evaluating deal structures and credit risk. Excellent communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively across teams and with clients. Preferred Qualifications Highly organized, detail‑oriented, and capable of managing multiple live transactions concurrently. Creative, solutions‑oriented approach to structuring and problem‑solving in less standardized asset classes. Series 7 and 63 licenses preferred.

Kroll is seeking an Associate-level Quality Control Specialist within its Discovery & Analytics Solutions practice. This role performs first-line validation and documented quality checks across eDiscovery and sensitive data review workflows, escalates exceptions, and maintains quality metrics within a structured delivery environment. Key Responsibilities Perform first-line quality control checks against established protocols, checklists, matter requirements, and acceptance criteria. Validate data loads, coding consistency, document counts, work-product completeness, and deliverable readiness. Document QC results, exceptions, corrective actions, and approvals in matter trackers and quality records. Escalate discrepancies, recurring defects, and potential risks promptly to the appropriate Senior Associate, Manager, or project lead. Support sampling, reconciliation, rework validation, and final-release checks. Maintain QC metrics and assist with periodic reporting on defect types, trends, and remediation status to support quality monitoring and continuous improvement. Follow established security, confidentiality, defensibility, and data-handling requirements. Participate in training and continuous-improvement initiatives. Essential Skills & Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Business, Legal Studies, Information Technology, Data Analytics, or a related field. Two or more years of experience in Sensitive Data Review, eDiscovery, litigation support, quality assurance, document review, or a related operational role is preferred. Strong attention to detail and ability to follow documented procedures consistently. Familiarity with eDiscovery concepts and platforms is preferred; experience with Relativity is a plus. Strong written communication, organization, and issue-escalation skills. Ability to manage repetitive, detail-intensive work while maintaining accuracy and confidentiality. With nearly a century of trusted expertise, we combine deep domain knowledge with cutting-edge technology to help clients address their most complex investigations, litigation, regulatory, and data challenges.

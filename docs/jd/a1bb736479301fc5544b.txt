Primary Purpose of Position The primary responsibility of the Principal CES Project Structuring is to lead the financial analysis necessary to evaluate capital investment opportunities and other energy services and operations transactions. Working in coordination with Sales, Engineering, and other functional teams within the business and broader Corporate functions, along with an extensive network of third-party lenders and investors, the Principal CES Project Structuring utilizes financial modeling expertise, experience in finance, tax, and accounting structuring, and external market research and networking to develop the optimal product offerings to meet our customer's needs and maximize returns. The Principal works in close coordination with Senior Leadership, Commercial leads, Legal and external financiers to develop new commercial and financing strategies and structures to grow originations expand the business through the identification and development of new potential sources of project financing, incentives, rebates, tax credits and deductions and any other source of funding. Also, the Principal manages, coordinates, and reports to Senior Leadership on strategic project funding and commercial initiatives of the business. Primary Duties and Accountabilities Lead financial analyses to evaluate and structure capital investment opportunities and energy services and operations transactions, and communicate recommendations. Actively monitors, manages, and optimizes financial performance of CES's financing and operating energy asset portfolios. Prepares financing and commercial agreement documentation in accordance with specific regulatory requirements and contractual requirements. Leads the Distributed Energy's deal approval efforts in concert with Constellation's and Constellation's approval processes and manages relationships with internal functional groups to secure timely input and sign off Establishes and maintains key lender and investor relationships to maximize capital structure flexibility and achieve the lowest cost of capital for funding a project. Collaborate with senior leadership to develop and implement long-term business strategies.

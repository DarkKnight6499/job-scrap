Job Description As a valued contributor to our team, you will advise the team on methods, models, and analytical templates used for assessing and identifying potential risks that may threaten our reputation, safety, security, and/or financial success, as well as work with management and team members in communicating and collaborating with key stakeholders across the enterprise, and understand business objectives. THE IMPACT YOU WILL MAKE Technology Operational Risk – Advisor role will offer you the flexibility to make each day your own, while working alongside people who care so that you can deliver on the following responsibilities: Advise business and technology partners on operational risk matters, with an emphasis on business & tech resiliency and technology development. Deep understanding of risk management frameworks, RCSA, process risk and controls is essential. Lead risk-based technology assessments, evaluate control design and effectiveness, and communicate practical recommendations to stakeholders. Assess resilience risks, dependencies, recovery capabilities, and remediation plans across critical business services and supporting technologies. Apply knowledge of artificial intelligence to evaluate emerging technology risks, governance considerations, and responsible adoption practices. Use data analytics to identify risk trends, develop insights, monitor key indicators, and support management decision-making. Build productive relationships across business, technology, risk, 2LoD, and control teams, and present complex findings clearly to technical and nontechnical audiences. THE EXPERIENCE YOU BRING TO THE TEAM Minimum Required Experiences 6 years of relevant experience in technology operational risk, business resiliency, technology resiliency, risk management, technology controls, or a related field. Demonstrated experience conducting technology risk or control assessments and translating findings into clear, actionable recommendations. Working knowledge of business continuity, disaster recovery, operational resilience, technology dependencies, and recovery planning. Knowledge of artificial intelligence concepts, associated risks, and governance considerations. Proficiency in data analytics, including interpreting data, identifying trends, developing risk insights, and communicating results. Strong interpersonal, communication, and collaboration skills, with the ability to influence stakeholders and explain complex topics to varied audiences. Shows curiosity and adaptability in learning and responsibly applying new technologies, including artificial intelligence, to reimagine how we work. Desired Experiences 10 years+ of relevant experience in technology operational risk, business resiliency, technology resiliency, risk management, technology controls, or a related field. Bachelor’s degree or equivalent practical experience in risk management, information systems, business, data analytics, or a related discipline. Experience with governance, risk, and compliance platforms, business intelligence tools, or data visualization tools. Familiarity with recognized resilience, technology risk, cybersecurity, or artificial intelligence risk management frameworks. Relevant certification such as CBCP, CRISC, CISA, CISSP, or a comparable risk, resilience, technology, or analytics credential.

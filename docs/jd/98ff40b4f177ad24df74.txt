Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism CMAAS (Capital Markets and Accounting Advisory Services) Management Level Senior Manager Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Deals - Capital Markets Accounting Advisory Services you will advise clients on complex transactions, technical accounting, financial reporting, and capital markets topics that can influence value during deals and other transformational business events. As a Senior Manager you will lead large projects, provide strategic direction, and use sound judgment to guide teams through complexity while building trusted client relationships and supporting outcomes across diverse stakeholders within our Deals practice. In this role at PwC, you will apply your knowledge of transaction structures, accounting advisory matters, and financial reporting issues across public and private deal activity, while also helping coach teams, communicate clear points of view, and support work that spans diligence, execution, and post-deal value creation. Responsibilities - Advising clients on technical accounting, financial reporting, and transaction accounting issues across mergers, divestitures, capital raising, and initial public offerings - Leading complex deal accounting workstreams from planning through delivery, coordinating issue resolution and milestone tracking - Developing accounting analyses, position papers, and financial statement support for business combinations, carve-outs, restructuring, and other transformational events - Interpreting generally accepted accounting principles, international accounting standards, and regulatory guidance to address transaction structures and reporting questions - Reviewing technical accounting positions, disclosure considerations, and reporting impacts for public and private company transactions - Guiding teams through complex accounting advisory engagements, providing coaching and direction on work quality and deadlines - Building client relationships across finance, legal, and deal teams to gather information and translate technical findings into clear recommendations - Identifying process improvements and innovative tools that improve delivery efficiency, modeling, and financial reporting support What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 7 years of experience - At least one of the following: an active CPA License What Sets You Apart - Preference for at least one of the following fields of study: Accounting, Economics, Finance - Demonstrating capital markets accounting advisory experience across deal events - Applying technical accounting and financial reporting knowledge to transaction readiness - Crafting clear, client-facing messages that tell a holistic story - Applying systems thinking to identify accounting issues and opportunities - Coaching teams through ambiguity, change, and complex deadlines - Modeling professional standards, conduct, and independence requirements Travel Requirements Up to 60% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $124,000 -

Pay Range: $78,419.00 - $117,628.00 Scheduled Weekly Hours: 40 What You’ll Be Doing The Asset Liability Management (ALM) Analyst supports the Credit Union's asset/liability management, liquidity risk management, and capital planning functions through financial analysis, forecasting, and reporting. Working under the guidance of senior teammembers, the analyst assists in the development and maintenance of balance sheet, net interest income (NII), net economic value (NEV), and liquidity forecasts used to assess the impact of changing economic conditions on the Credit Union's financial position. This role is responsible for gathering and validating data, supporting risk analyses, preparing management reporting, and collaborating with teammembers to provide meaningful insights that support balance sheet management and strategic decision-making while building expertise in asset/liability management and financial risk management practices. Assists in monitoring and reporting interest rate risk and liquidity risk exposure using Asset/Liability Management ("ALM") software under established scenarios and assumptions. Reviews reports and performs analysis to help identify key drivers and support the validation of results. Assists with running simulations and generating reports on balance sheet and various interest rate risk metrics. Supports the maintenance of input assumptions used in ALM models, including prepayment speeds, deposit decay rates, and deposit betas. Obtains, organizes and maintains data used in the ALM software’s monthly processes and assist with data reconciliation. Maintains ALM model account attributes and other mo[SK1.1]del inputs under established procedures. Supports budget and forecast processes by assisting with cash flow model execution and report preparation. Works closely with teammembers to support ALM analyses, reporting requirements and departmental objectives. Assists in the monitoring and analysis of liquidity positions, funding sources, and cash flow trends to support the Credit Union's liquidity risk management framework. Supports the preparation of liquidity forecasts, stress-testing analyses, and management reports used to evaluate funding adequacy under a range of operating and economic scenarios. Demonstrates a basic understanding of economic conditions, interest rate movements and their impact on financial institution balance sheets and profitability. Performs routine data mining, analysis, and reporting assignments as assigned.

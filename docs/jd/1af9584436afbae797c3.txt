Job Description: Director, Digital Assets Accounting Operations - Geneva & Private Funds The Role As Director of Digital Asset Accounting, focusing on the Geneva and Geneva World Investor platforms, you will provide strategic and operational leadership across the accounting function supporting Fidelity’s digital asset investment products. You will oversee all aspects of accounting operations, ensuring accurate, timely, and controlled delivery of core processes including digital asset pricing, books and records maintenance, NAV production, and financial reporting. You will lead teams responsible for supporting accounting activities for blockchain-enabled investment products, including wallet and custodian reconciliations, digital asset valuation, and lifecycle event processing (e.g., staking rewards). The role requires strong ownership of daily deliverables in parallel with managing forward-looking initiatives that enhance scalability, automation, and control. Operating with a high degree of autonomy, you will partner closely with senior stakeholders across Accounting, Pricing, Technology, Product, Custody, and Risk to ensure operational readiness and solutions for new digital asset products and structures that are introduced. You will play a key role in evolving the operating model to support a rapidly expanding and increasingly complex digital asset platform. In addition to leading day-to-day operations, you will drive strategic initiatives focused on modernizing infrastructure, integrating blockchain-derived data into accounting processes, and improving transparency and efficiency across workflows. The role requires the ability to define complex problems, synthesize insights, and deliver recommendations that align with broader business priorities. The Expertise and Skills You Bring Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, or a related field and 10–12 years of experience in asset management operations, fund accounting, or alternative investment accounting, with leadership experience overseeing complex functions and large-scale operational teams.

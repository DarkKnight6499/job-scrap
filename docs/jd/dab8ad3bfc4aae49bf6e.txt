About this role Company: BlackRock Financial Management, Inc. Job Title: Associate, Application Engineer III Location: 400 Howard St San Francisco, CA 94105 Job Duties: Build scalable financial desktop applications and web applications that are performant, scalable, and distributed, capable of displaying real-time data updates, fixed income bond calculations, and key metrics. Utilize fixed income and equity trading domain strategies, including FIX protocol) to build order and execution workflows. Develop client-facing financial applications for fixed income trading using React, Typescript, HTML, SCSS, Redux, and Jotai for front-end components and GraphQL, Java, and AMPs together to build and maintain backend RESTful and streaming APIs. Build pure React components for use in internal component libraries. Partner closely with product management to determine functional and non-functional requirements, design product roadmaps with specific client commitment deadlines, and develop release timelines and post-release checkout and deployment steps. Translate wireframes from Figma into working fixed income workflows that match the look and feel of other similar financial applications. Collaborate with quality assurance teams to design automated, manual, and dev testing workflows, including building suite of unit tests, component tests, and integration tests using Cypress and Cucumber. Deploy production code to live client environments. Provide operational support including solving client issues, participating in client calls, and using/building Grafana dashboards for telemetry monitoring. Mentor junior engineers from a technical and professional perspective, including allocating project work and providing career mentorship and guidance. Organize meetings according to the Agile methodology and SDLC, including weekly sprint planning meetings, retrospectives, ticket grooming, and project scoping. Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Data Science or a related field and three (3) years of experience as an Associate, Analyst or a related role. Must have three (3) years of experience with: React and Typescript; State management frameworks including Redux and Jotai; HTML, CSS and Sass; Git, AquaDataStudio, ADO, and Agile methodologies; Java 17, SQL, and GraphQL; Data streaming tools including RESTful APIs, WebSockets, Faraday, and AMPs; Frontend testing frameworks including Cypress and Cucumber; Financial products and tools icluding FIX messaging and protocol, TradeWeb, and MarketAxess; Knowledge of US, EMEA, and APAC bond trading markets for corporate, treasury, and MUNI bonds, including anonymous trading, straight-through processing ("click to trade" workflows), and portfolio trading; CI / CD pipelining and deployment tools including SonarQube, Jenkins, JFrog Artifactory, and Grafana; UX and design tools including Figma and Miro; and Production support and debugging tools including Vim, Splunk, MobaXTerm, and Baretail.

Senior Risk Analyst - Credit Portfolio Intelligence Advisory (Hybrid)
First Citizens Bank
Location: Raleigh, North Carolina; Morristown, New Jersey
Link: https://jobs.firstcitizens.com/jobs/36204

Overview This is a hybrid position that must sit in our Raleigh, NC or Morristown, NJ offices requiring 3 days in office and 2 days remote. This position is responsible for delivery of trusted report and analytical solutions based on a wide-range of use cases for portfolio monitoring and enablement of identification of emerging risks within Credit Administration. This individual will partner with key stakeholders within Credit Administration and various internal lines of defense (e.g. Business Units, Credit, Enterprise Risk Management, Credit Review, Internal Audit) as well as external stake holders (e.g. External Auditors, Regulators) to create relevant & meaningful analytical content and empower these stakeholders with a deep understanding of the underlying business segments. Responsibilities & Qualifications Duties & Responsibilities: Advising – Create, develop, and nurture a “trusted advisor” atmosphere and brand. Becoming ingrained in the business segment operations allowing for the anticipation of reporting and analytics needs as well as providing insights. Liaison between stakeholder and Business Intelligence & Risk Reporting to collect requirements, design, create, and enhance reports and Tableau dashboards that satisfy stakeholder desires. Reporting – Create ad-hoc and recurring reports, presentations materials, and memos by identifying, analyzing, and interpreting, complex datasets; researching and understanding economic conditions and external events; and combining internal stress testing results and analytics in a clear, concise, and understandable manner enabling data-driven risk management decision making. Meetings and Presentations – Attend various Credit Administration meetings to gain relevant knowledge of emerging risks and changes in credit appetites, policies, procedures and standards. Growth and Improvement - Continuously enhancing knowledge & skills regarding analytical tools, techniques, and methodologies. Proactively looking for opportunities to enhance current analytical capabilities and develop new analysis and reporting to better support Credit Administration and the various Business Segments. Skills: Data Mining Data Gathering and Reporting Risk Mitigation Risk Identification Analytical Reasoning Qualifications: Bachelor's Degree and 4 years' experience OR High School Diploma or GED/Equivalent and 8 years' experience in Risk management, or financial analysis, or statistical modeling Preferred Qualifications: Experience in Banking and Finance Industry, preferably within credit analysis, underwriting or credit risk oversight Experience using Microsoft Office products (PowerPoint, Word, Excel, Outlook, and Teams) Experience With Business Intelligence software (Microsoft Power BI, Tableau, SQL and SAS) Additional Information This job posting is expected to remain active for 7 days from the initial posting date listed above.

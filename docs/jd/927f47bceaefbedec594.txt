Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Valuation Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Deals Valuation - Financial Analytics & Derivatives (FAD) - Senior Associate, you will help clients evaluate financial transactions within our Advisory practice, applying financial analytics to value derivatives and other financial instruments, assess deal risks, and inform decisions about mergers, acquisitions, and divestitures. As a Senior Associate, you will manage assigned valuation workstreams, analyze complex questions, develop connections with clients, and guide junior colleagues as you anticipate the needs of your team. In this role at PwC, you will turn valuation analysis into clear insights that help clients understand the worth and potential risks of a transaction. You will draw on valuation methods and technology resources, consider the broader business context, and review deliverables while maintaining professional and technical standards. When a transaction presents ambiguity, you will ask questions, interpret feedback, and work with colleagues to determine a path forward. This role offers opportunities to deepen your technical skills and develop your approach to mentoring others through varied client engagements. Responsibilities - Developing quantitative models to value derivatives, options, swaps, convertible bonds, fixed income securities, and commodity instruments for transaction and financial reporting decisions - Applying option pricing, Monte Carlo simulation, and financial engineering techniques to assess complex deal structures, market assumptions, and valuation risks - Analyzing balance sheets, cash flows, cost of capital, peer groups, and industry trends to assess business, equity, and asset values in mergers and acquisitions - Evaluating fair value measurements, purchase price allocations, goodwill impairment, and legal entity valuations using financial statement models and documented valuation methods - Testing model inputs and outputs through sensitivity analysis, market data comparisons, and mark-to-market reviews, documenting limitations and questions for further investigation - Using Bloomberg Terminal, MATLAB, Python, VBA, and Alteryx to source market data, automate calculations, and review outputs from AI-assisted valuation workflows - Presenting valuation findings to clients and deal teams, explaining key assumptions, transaction implications, and potential effects on value creation and capital allocation - Managing assigned valuation workstreams, coordinating schedules and budgets, reviewing junior colleagues’ work, and incorporating stakeholder feedback into deliverables for complex transactions What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 3 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for a Master's degree in Quantitative Finance or similar - Valuing derivatives, swaps, and convertible bonds using option pricing and Monte Carlo simulation for transaction analyses - Building financial and cash flow models to assess deal structures, capital allocation, and business value - Applying market data analysis and sensitivity testing to validate valuation models for complex financial instruments - Leveraging Bloomberg Terminal, MATLAB, Python, or VBA to streamline quantitative analysis and review modeling outputs - Assessing fair value measurements and purchase price allocations for financial reporting in acquisitions -

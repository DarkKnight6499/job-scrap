Principal Associate, Risk Specialist Do you like working in the spotlight? Are you ready to work on the front line of a top 10 Bank? Can you build relationships as well as develop and implement innovative solutions? As a Principal Risk Associate on the Global Payment Network Data Governance team, you will work with a team of professionals dedicated to ensuring that the Network’s most critical data is well-managed, high quality, and adheres to enterprise data management policies. You will play a pivotal role in maintaining the integrity of our data ecosystem, enabling the organization to make data-driven decisions with confidence while mitigating risks across the data lifecycle. Responsibilities: Data Governance & Policy Adherence: Enable the implementation and oversight of data management practices to ensure compliance with policies, procedures, and best practices Data Quality Management: Implement and execute programs to ensure the quality, accuracy, and reliability of Card’s most important data Tooling & Ecosystem Mastery: Proactively learn and master the nuances of the various tools used to support the data management ecosystem to drive automation, efficiency, and better risk oversight Problem Solving & Innovation: Apply intellectual curiosity and strong analytical skills to solve complex data management challenges and identify opportunities for process transformation and automation initiatives Stakeholder Collaboration: Partner with Accountable Executives (AEs), Performing Data Stewards (PDSs), and tech teams to ensure data-driven initiatives align with the broader risk and well-managed agenda Risk Insights & Reporting: Proactively synthesize data and reporting to provide actionable business insights to senior leadership regarding the health and compliance of the data ecosystem Basic Qualifications: Bachelors Degree or Military experience At least 3 years of Risk Management, Data Management, or Financial Services experience At least 3 years of experience supporting, partnering and interacting with internal business clients Preferred Qualifications: Masters Degree At least 4 years of experience leading cross functional Risk or Data Professional certification such as CISA, CDMP, or PMP At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position.

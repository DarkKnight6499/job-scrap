The position is focused on helping clients address complex treasury challenges through strategic insights, industry expertise, and best practices guidance The role operates in close partnership with GPS Sales, Client Coverage, and Product Management teams, while maintaining a primary focus on delivering client advisory, thought leadership, and market insights to complement GPS solutions. Responsibilities: Manages client engagements and reviews, account management, complex deals, and/or emerging solution opportunities with clients to grow market share through deal support Serves as a bank representative presenting at key industry events, leading consultative discussions at multiple levels of the organization, and providing product line expertise Assists banking line partners in understanding treasury management, product risk, and ensuring applicable policies are applied through the sales cycle Manages the strategy and tactical execution of segment initiatives across all major Lines of Business, driving collaboration between product, sales, and client relationship teams Identifies and develops responses to emerging product trends, competitive strategies, and market gaps Develops and implements programs and policies in partnership with market leadership to drive Global Treasury solutions and local market strategies Acts as the product or industry expert in client call planning and strategic client reviews, identifying opportunities and influencing solutions Required Qualifications: 7–10 years of experience in corporate treasury, finance, and/or treasury advisory consulting, ideally within a multinational environment. Ability to travel up to 30% of the time. Demonstrated experience advising clients or senior stakeholders on treasury strategy and operations. Strong understanding of corporate treasury principles, operating models, and processes. Exceptional analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate complex issues into actionable insights. Advanced communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to influence senior stakeholders. Strong presentation and thought leadership capabilities.

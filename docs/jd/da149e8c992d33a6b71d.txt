What you will do The Portfolio Analysis Group (PAG) partners closely with investment teams to provide the analytics and insights that support portfolio construction, risk monitoring, performance analysis, and investment decision-making. Our PAG Analyst Program is a three-year rotational program designed to provide broad exposure across asset classes, investment strategies, and portfolio management teams. Analysts complete one-year rotations across PAG and select partner teams, developing a strong foundation in portfolio analytics while gaining firsthand experience across the investment process. Core rotations may include General Account, Investment Grade, High Yield, Emerging Markets, US/Global Rates & Hedge Fund, Structured Products, and Multi-Sector. Satellite rotations may include CLO Structuring, Structured Products Research, and Investment Risk Management. This incoming Analyst will begin the program aligned to our Structured Products / Asset-Based Finance (ABF) team , where they will work closely with Portfolio Managers and other investment partners to analyze portfolio positioning and performance, monitor risk and client objectives, and deliver actionable insights that support day-to-day portfolio management. This position follows a hybrid work model, with working 3 days in the Newark, NJ office and 2 days remote. What you can expect Analyze portfolio positioning relative to benchmarks, client objectives, risk parameters, and investment goals, identifying changes in exposures and opportunities to improve portfolio alignment. Produce portfolio analytics and performance attribution to help explain investment results and inform portfolio management decisions. Incorporate client guidelines, investment objectives, and risk tolerances into portfolio monitoring, scenario analysis, and portfolio management workflows. Partner with Portfolio Managers, Traders, Risk Managers, Quantitative teams, Credit Research, Client Advisory, technology partners, and senior PAG team members. Support timely market, portfolio, and client analyses while improving recurring reports, analytical tools, and repeatable processes. What you will bring Bachelor’s degree and 2+ years of relevant experience in investment management, financial services, portfolio or risk analytics, research, or a related analytical field. Proficiency with Excel and familiarity with investment and data analytics tools such as Bloomberg, Aladdin, SQL, and Python preferred. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to turn complex analysis into clear, actionable insights. A collaborative mindset and ability to work effectively across investment, risk, research, and technology teams. What will set you apart Strong analytical skills, intellectual curiosity, and a proactive approach to problem solving. Ability to manage multiple priorities and deliver accurate, high-quality work in a fast-paced investment environment. Exposure to coding best practices, including documentation, version control, testing, peer review, and writing maintainable code.

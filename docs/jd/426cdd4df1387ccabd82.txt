We are looking for a Senior Financial Analyst to join our Charlotte team. This is a hybrid position that requires two days a week on-site in our Ballantyne-area office. As a Senior Financial Analyst, you will report to the Accounting Manager and work closely with corporate department heads. Responsibilities include but are not limited to: supporting the month-end close process through preparation of journal entries and account reconciliations; preparing and analyzing department spending results; actively participating in the forecasting and budgeting cycles; assisting with government compliance and reporting requests.

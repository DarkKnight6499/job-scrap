Business Overview: Americas Capital Introductions is part of the Prime Finance business within the Equities division. The individual will be responsible for building and maintaining relationships with Americas based institutional investors, with a focus on each investor’s individual investment objectives and portfolio needs to service Citi’s Hedge Fund clients’ capital raising needs. Furthermore, Capital Introductions is an integral part of our Prime Services sales efforts, team members are expected to collaborate closely with the origination sales team on client needs and activity.

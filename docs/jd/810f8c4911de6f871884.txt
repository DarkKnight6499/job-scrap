TowneBank has a culture that: Has a commitment to exquisite service for our members that will lead to our ability to create a reasonable rate of return for our shareholders, a bright future for our dedicated bankers, and a leadership role for our bank in promoting the social, cultural, and economic well-being of our community. Embraces our hometown banking philosophy, where our family members, directors, and employees are active volunteers, serving on numerous boards and donating their time and talents to local organizations. LEAD (Leadership Exploration and Development) is a leadership and management development program. Scheduled to begin in July 2027, this program offers onsite training at the bank’s headquarters in Suffolk, VA. The first half of the program is spent in specific job rotations throughout our headquarters in Hampton Roads. The second half of the program is spent in specific on-the-job training in a selected Career Track (see below) within one of Towne’s regions. The LEAD Program is designed to develop the skills and knowledge to be a successful leader in a top-performing financial institution. Learning opportunities include presentation skills, advanced communication, business etiquette, ethics, financial management, and more. Participate in classroom and online learning, job rotations, special projects, group activities, and community volunteerism. Build professional networks, receive mentoring from previous program graduates and executives, participate in employee resource groups, and engage with local community organizations. The LEAD program includes a strong focus on our mission of serving others and enriching lives, from both the professional and philanthropic perspective.

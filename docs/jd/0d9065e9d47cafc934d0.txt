We are actively seeking a talented Junior Public Credit Analyst to join our Public Bond Team in Newport Beach, CA. As a Junior Public Credit Analyst, you will contribute to Pacific Life's long-term success by performing and communicating fundamental credit analysis on public fixed income investments supporting the company's General Account backing our policy holder obligations. You will fill a new role on the Public Credit Analysis team, which currently consists of 11 investment professionals. The research group’s investment analysis process includes a combination of fundamental company analysis, industry analysis, understanding protections offered in different security types, and the ability to calibrate risk between investment opportunities. How you will make an impact: Support and collaborate with Public Bond Credit Analysts in the analysis of fundamental credit research on investment grade and high yield corporate credits Support senior analysts in developing Buy/Sell/Hold Recommendations for inclusion in the company’s General Account Work closely with the entire 11-person Public Credit Analysis team to determine the best opportunities for our portfolio Develop and maintain relationships with sell-side analysts, credit rating agencies, and other information providers Gain exposure to multiple industries, portfolio management, and credit investment decision-making while working alongside experienced investment professionals The experience you bring: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Accounting, or a related field. 0-3 years of experience in credit research, investment analysis, banking, valuation, accounting, consulting, or related financial field.

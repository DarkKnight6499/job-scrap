Overview This is remote role and could be hired in NC, FL, SC. The Manager Regional Commercial Real Estate Underwriting is responsible for the oversight and management of associates and activities related to a Commercial Underwriting team. Primary responsibilities include management of people, processes, performance, and credit related projects. Responsible for identification, development, and retention of solid underwriting talent, and works with Senior Management as needed on talent management initiatives. Strategically reviews and analyzes processes to assist Senior Management to optimize production activities and ensure quality of team efforts. Recommends adjustments to processes, procedures, tolls, and potential policy changes as appropriate to achieve business goals. Monitors individual and team performance, and seeks opportunities for continuous improvement. Provides expertise to the team in underwriting, servicing, and other activities that support Commercial Lending. Assists Senior Leadership with risk identification and mitigation, and recommends appropriate enhancements to portfolio management and monitoring practices as needed.

The Impact You will have in this role : Reporting to the Director of the Offensive Cyber Operations team, you will help drive the strategic direction, vision, and operational effectiveness of security assessment and penetration testing activities. You will ensure all testing efforts align with documented requirements, reducing organizational risk while maintaining a high-quality and resilient assessment program. This role requires deep expertise in penetration testing and offensive security practices. As a key leader within the Offensive Cyber Operations (OCO) program, you will oversee and coordinate end-to-end security assessments, evaluate and enhance assessment procedures and tools, and support the implementation of testing methodologies and reporting standards. You will serve as a subject matter expert in penetration testing, leveraging current regulatory guidance, industry best practices, and awareness of emerging vendors and tool-related risks to strengthen the organization's security posture. Your Primary Responsibilities : Align testing strategy with business goals by translating drivers into technical solutions and capability recommendations. Gather requirements, assess capabilities, and provide recommendations and adoption strategies supporting security and business needs. Guide Cyber Security decisions related to network design, cloud adoption, threat modeling and new technology integration. Maintain continuous improvement practices to ensure resiliency and operational excellence across OCO testing functions. Administer, coordinate, and implement vendor and OCO testing processes, ensuring compliance with procedures and regulatory frameworks. Provide tactical and strategic recommendations supporting technology delivery that is agile, timely, cost‑effective, and risk‑appropriate. Guide the development of new processes, automation of controls, and enhancement of monitoring activities using data analytics. Lead cross‑functional OCO teams to deliver measurable reporting supporting the Cyber Security & Resiliency portfolio. Maintain relationships with partners across IT, business units, BISOs, ERMs, and other teams to ensure alignment and effective coordination Monitor industry trends, business developments, and emerging technologies to continuously mature the OCO program. Provide regulatory and policy compliance guidance to OCO teams and ensure consistent application of internal standards. Manage risk through procedural adherence, rapid issue resolution, and proper escalation/documentation of findings. **NOTE: The Primary Responsibilities of this role are not limited to the details above. ** Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Information Security, Cybersecurity, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. Minimum of seven (7) years of professional experience in one or more technical disciplines, including Penetration Testing, Networking, Firewalls, Server Administration, Encryption, Cloud Technologies, Containerization, Databases, or Software Development. At least two (2) years of hands-on penetration testing experience in enterprise environments.

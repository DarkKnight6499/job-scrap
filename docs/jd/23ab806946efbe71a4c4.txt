As a Sr. Implementation Associate II, you'll move Pacific Life, and your career, forward by leading the successful implementation of complex Pension Risk Transfer (PRT) and Defined Contribution Lifetime Income (DCLI) transactions from point of sale through onboarding and transition to Pacific Life platforms. In addition to serving as a trusted implementation leader, you will drive strategic initiatives, own critical business processes, and identify opportunities to improve operational efficiency, scalability, and client experience across the Sales Enablement & Implementation (SE&I) organization. You will fill an existing role that sits on a team of approximately 10 professionals in the Sales Enablement & Implementation (SE&I) team and will partner closely with Sales, Pricing, Operations, Compliance, and Technology teams. We are a highly collaborative team focused on delivering exceptional client outcomes while continuously improving how we work and preparing the organization for future growth How you'll help move us forward: Lead all aspects of the onboarding process for complex PRT and DCLI transactions, ensuring successful implementation and transition to Pacific Life platforms. Serve as the primary relationship manager for plan sponsors, consultants, prior recordkeepers, third-party administrators, and key internal stakeholders throughout implementation and ongoing support. Own and continuously improve key SE&I processes by establishing best practices, documentation, controls, service standards, and governance to support growth and risk mitigation. Lead cross-functional initiatives and projects that improve implementation capabilities, operational effectiveness, scalability, and client experience. Act as a subject matter expert and mentor by sharing knowledge, training team members, and influencing outcomes across Sales, Pricing, Operations, Compliance, and Technology.

All work requirements are subject to change at any time based on leader discretion and/or business need. The Opportunity This role will be based out of our New York office. This role is an integral part of the broader Ally Energy & Infrastructure team and will primarily be responsible for managing a portfolio of senior project finance loans extended to Power, Energy and Infrastructure borrowers owned primarily by private equity sponsors and developers. The portfolio will be comprised of mostly lead/agency and co-lead roles. The PM will lead the execution of amendments, waivers and loan modifications through direct interaction with clients and syndicate banks, as well as CF management, Ally Risk and Ally CF support functions, including credit approval, legal, loan administration and operations. The PM is the primary interface between the client and bank post-closing and as such is responsible for balancing the needs and expectations of the borrower with policies, procedures and risk tolerances of Ally CF. The Work Itself Know the client and the client's business, and maintain a professional and responsive relationship with appropriate contacts of the client. Assist with loan set up and closing, and act as administrative agent for certain lead deals with syndicate bank groups. Execute post-close requirements of the credit agreement. Minimize risk. Execute corporate governance responsibilities. Identify New Business Opportunities Underwritings - Prepare underwriting memorandum for loan modifications applicable to portfolio. Mentor and train less experienced team members. Assist in creating/maintaining a strong credit culture. The Qualifications We’re Looking For Strong knowledge of renewable and conventional power, data centers and related infrastructure, and project finance required. 5 - 10 years of progressive portfolio management/underwriting experience with relevant focus in the energy and project finance sector. Required BA in Business, Accounting, Finance, Economics or related field or any degree with relevant professional experience required. Master's degree preferred. Proven success in relationship management. Completion of bank/finance company credit training program preferred. Excellent communication (verbal and written), interpersonal and organizational skills. Highly analytical, detail oriented with problem solving capability. Ability to work independently with demonstrated strong time management skills. Well-developed presentation and relationship building skills. High level of accountability and the ability to effectively prioritize tasks. Technical: Advanced knowledge of MS Office: Excel, Word, PowerPoint Access and financial forecast models.

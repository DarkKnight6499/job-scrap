Job Title - Associate Business Analyst - Associate - ACS Song Management Level: Level 12 - Associate Location: Kochi Must have skills: Requirement Gathering/Documentation Good to have skills: Basic understanding of APIs Experience: 1 year – 2 years of experience is required Educational Qualification: Graduation (Accurate educational details should capture) Job Summary As a Business Analyst - you will support business analysis and requirement management activities by working closely with Analysts, Senior Analysts and project leadership. You will contribute to translating business needs into clearly documented requirements and support the delivery of digitally enabled solutions across client engagements. This role focuses on learning Accenture delivery methodologies, tools and ways of working while building foundational consulting and analysis skills. Roles and Responsibilities Support the analysis of current-state (as-is) processes and assist in documenting future-state (to-be) processes under the direction of senior team members. Assist in planning, preparing and documenting client workshops, interviews and requirement gathering sessions. Work with client and internal stakeholders to understand stated requirements and contribute to documentation using process models, basic story maps, data analysis, and document reviews. Support day-to-day communication with delivery teams by helping clarify documented requirements and assumptions. Assist with iteration planning, backlog refinement and sprint activities, including drafting user stories and acceptance criteria under guidance. Support stakeholder engagement activities and help maintain alignment between the project team, client, and project leadership. Contribute to the identification of solution options by documenting business needs, dependencies and constraints to support decision-making. Create and maintain requirement artefacts (user stories, process flows, functional documentation) based on Accenture and client-defined standards. Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders such as Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) to gather inputs required for analysis and documentation. Support alignment between requirements, solution design and implementation artefacts, escalating gaps or inconsistencies when identified. Assist in maintaining requirement traceability, accuracy and version control throughout the delivery lifecycle. Professional and Technical Skill Foundational knowledge of business analysis and requirement documentation practices using structured templates and tools. Ability to understand project scope, objectives, and inception documentation with guidance. Exposure to Agile and iterative delivery methodologies, including backlog refinement and sprint execution. Ability to proactively raise issues, risks and clarifications to Analysts, Senior Analysts or Project Managers. Basic application awareness of enterprise or commerce platforms (e.g. SAP Hybris, Salesforce or similar) is beneficial. Demonstrates structured thinking and clear communication, shows learning agility, proactively takes ownership of assigned responsibilities, and collaborates effectively within a team‑based delivery environment.

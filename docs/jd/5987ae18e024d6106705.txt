Analyst / Associate Role Join a high-quality, growing alternative investment management firm as a member of the Valuation Team. This role provides exposure to the valuation of a diverse portfolio of private market investments. The role will also contribute to the continued development of the team’s valuation processes, technology, and analytical capabilities. This is an opportunity to work closely with senior professionals across the firm and gain a broad understanding of Apogem’s investment strategies and portfolio companies. The successful candidate will have the opportunity to develop meaningful expertise in private market valuations, with a clear path for continued professional growth and advancement within the organization. Key Areas of Responsibility Valuation Analysis and Governance Perform valuation analyses of private investments across Apogem’s investment strategies. Support the review and validation of third-party valuation marks, including analysis of methodologies, assumptions, and market inputs. Assist with the preparation of quarterly valuation committee materials and supporting analyses. Maintain clear and well-documented support for valuation conclusions. Third Party Valuation Provider Oversight Assist with the oversight of third-party valuation providers, including coordinating data requests, reviewing valuation deliverables, and tracking open items. Review third-party valuations for consistency with established methodologies, policies, and procedures. Identify and escalate valuation issues or discrepancies to senior team members. Valuation Process and Technology Enhancement Leverage technology and data tools to improve the efficiency, consistency, and scalability of the valuation process. Identify opportunities to automate manual processes, improve data quality, and streamline reporting. Support the development and implementation of enhancements to valuation processes and procedures. Performance Reporting and Portfolio Monitoring Partner with investment teams to collect, validate, and maintain quarterly portfolio company financial and operating data. Monitor portfolio company performance and identify trends or changes relevant to investment valuations. Prepare recurring and ad hoc portfolio performance reporting for internal stakeholders. Financial Analysis and Modeling Perform financial analysis, valuation modeling, sensitivity analyses, and other ad hoc analyses across the firm’s investment portfolios. Analyze financial and operating information to identify trends, variances, and factors impacting investment valuations. Support senior team members on valuation-related projects and analyses. Collaboration & Professional Development Develop strong working relationships with investment teams, finance, fund accounting, operations, and other internal stakeholders. Develop a strong understanding of Apogem’s investment strategies, portfolio companies, and underlying investment structures. Build foundational expertise in private market valuation methodologies, financial modeling, and relevant accounting and valuation guidance. Stay informed on market developments and trends relevant to the valuation of private investments. Candidate Profile Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Mathematics, or another quantitative or analytical discipline. At least 2–5 years of experience in valuation, portfolio management/support, transaction advisory, or a related role. Experience with Private Credit, Private Equity, or other alternative investment strategies. Experience working with third-party valuation providers and/or external auditors. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel, including financial modeling and data analysis. Experience with portfolio monitoring and/or investment management systems, such as iLevel, Chronograph, or similar platforms. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate data into concise, accurate, and actionable insights. Demonstrated resourcefulness, initiative, and an entrepreneurial mindset, with the ability to operate effectively in a dynamic environment. Strong organizational and time-management skills, with the ability to prioritize multiple tasks and meet deadlines.

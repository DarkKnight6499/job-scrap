About this role The Quality Associate is responsible for inspecting manufactured products to ensure they meet strict quality standards and customer expectations. This hands-on role involves identifying product defects, monitoring production processes for improvements, maintaining accurate inspection records, and collaborating with the production team to resolve quality issues. Key Responsibilities Inspect all products manufactured by Carrier Charlotte Factory to ensure they meet quality standards and specifications. Identify defects and non-conformities in products and document them in the quality management system. Verify that all product labeling, packaging, and documentation are accurate and complete. Monitor production processes to identify opportunities for process improvement and implement corrective actions. Provide feedback to the production team on quality issues and collaborate with them to resolve quality problems. Work closely with the Quality Control team to ensure that all products meet customer expectations. Maintain accurate and complete records of inspection activities and results. Support 8D activities as needed, including leading and supporting containment. Measure components as required (e.g., lead length, bend radius, etc.). Support implementation of quality management systems (ISO9001). Perform other activities as required by the Quality Manager, Production Supervisors, or Trainer. Take pictures of all units being audited and upload them for traceability.

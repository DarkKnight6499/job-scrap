Asset & Wealth Management A career with Goldman Sachs Asset & Wealth Management is an opportunity to help clients across the globe realize their potential, while you discover your own. As part of one of the world's leading asset managers with over $2 trillion in assets under supervision, you can expect to participate in exciting investment opportunities while collaborating with talented colleagues from all asset classes and regions, and building meaningful relationships with your clients. Working in a culture that values integrity and transparency, you will be part of a diverse team that is passionate about our craft, our clients, and building sustainable success. Bringing together traditional and alternative investments, Goldman Sachs Asset & Wealth Management provides clients around the world with a dedicated partnership and focus on long-term performance. As a primary investment area within Goldman Sachs, we provide investment and advisory services for pension plans, sovereign wealth funds, insurance companies, endowments, foundations, financial advisors and individuals. WIS Overview The Wealth Investment Solutions (WIS) team within Goldman Sachs Asset Management manages over $375bn in assets across 80,000+ customized portfolios for individual as well as institutional investors across the wealth spectrum. WIS designs and manages personalized investment solutions for individual investment objectives, including tax-managed, factor-based and value aligned strategies across the Equity and Fixed Income asset classes. Role Overview The ETF Portfolio Management Team within WIS is responsible for the oversight and management of ETF portfolios, including portfolio rebalancing, cash management, risk management and other day-to-day portfolio-related activities. The ETF PM team works with ETF market makers to manage primary market creations/redemptions activity and delivering tax-efficient outcomes in the portfolios. The ETF PM team also works with the broader division in the research and development of new ETF products and strategies, enhancements to existing products, industry and competitive analysis, systems development, and other select projects.

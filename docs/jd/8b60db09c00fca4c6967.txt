Key responsibilities UBS Bank USA is seeking an Associate Director, Tech Risk Specialist in New York, NY Are you an innovative thinker? Develop and implement comprehensive technology risk governance routines including guidelines and procedures for effective governance of divisional technology assets and processes. • Monitor risk levels and ensure appropriate controls and measures are in place to address identified risks. Establish and maintain strong relationships with internal and external stakeholders at a senior level. • Can work Hybrid (In-office/Remote).

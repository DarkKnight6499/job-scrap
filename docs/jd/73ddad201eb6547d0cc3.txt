Job Description: At Regions, the Corporate Banking Associate is a client coverage and execution support role within Regions’ Diversified Industries group. This role partners with senior Corporate Bankers to support relationship management, credit structuring, transaction execution, and the delivery of integrated banking solutions for public, private, and sponsor-backed companies with revenues of $500 million and above, with a strategic focus on the $500 million to $2 billion segment. This role is focused on supporting ongoing client coverage, and coordinated product delivery across lending, treasury management, capital markets, and risk management. The Diversified Industries group is an industry-agnostic, geographically segmented platform that partners closely across lending, treasury management, capital markets, investment banking, and risk management to deliver comprehensive financial solutions. Associates are expected to combine strong financial analytics with client-facing execution skills and active contribution to origination and relationship development efforts.

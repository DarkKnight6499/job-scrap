The Difference You Make: The Senior Associate, VMO Reporting is responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining various reporting programs for the Vendor Management Organization (VMO). Responsible for collecting, analyzing, and presenting data from various sources to create comprehensive reports for internal and external stakeholders, often with a focus on identifying trends, insights, and key performance indicators (KPIs) to inform decision-making. Extracting data from various sources like databases, spreadsheets, and applications. Performing statistical analysis, trend analysis, and identifying patterns within data sets. Identifying opportunities to optimize data collection, analysis, and reporting process. Leverage various tools including but not limited to Power Query, Python and Tableau. Perform analysis to determine root cause of process/program deficiencies, and then drive the respective activities required to achieve process improvement.

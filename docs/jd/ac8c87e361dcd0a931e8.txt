Job Description Ready to take your career global? Make your mark at one of the biggest names in payments. We’re looking for a Lead Senior Credit Analyst to join our ever evolving Credit Risk Team and help shape the future of global commerce. In this role, you’ll serve as a trusted risk expert, helping Global Payments grow with confidence by balancing opportunity and risk across complex merchant relationships and transactions. Your decisions will directly influence portfolio quality, business growth and client success by evaluating sophisticated credit scenarios, structuring risk-based solutions and strengthening our overall risk framework. As a subject matter expert, you’ll have the opportunity to guide strategy, mentor others and help ensure Global Payments continues to deliver secure, innovative payment solutions that power businesses around the world. What you'll own Drive sound credit decisions that enable sustainable business growth while protecting the organization from financial, operational and reputational risk across complex merchant opportunities. Shape risk outcomes by identifying, quantifying and mitigating exposure, helping maintain a high-quality portfolio that supports long-term client and business success. Influence strategic business decisions through expert credit recommendations, risk insights and deal structuring that balance commercial objectives with responsible risk management. Advance credit excellence by strengthening policies, frameworks and risk practices that improve consistency, compliance and decision quality across the organization. Elevate team capability by mentoring analysts, sharing expertise and helping build a culture of continuous learning, accountability and high-performance decision-making. What you'll bring Bachelor's Degree in Finance, Accounting, Statistics, Business or a related field with strong analytical and financial acumen. Significant experience in credit analysis, risk assessment or financial underwriting, including proven success evaluating complex business and merchant relationships. Demonstrated ability to make independent, data-driven decisions that balance risk management objectives with business growth opportunities. Strong communication and influencing skills with experience explaining complex credit recommendations and negotiating risk-based business terms with diverse stakeholders. Proven ability to navigate ambiguity, exercise sound judgment and deliver high-quality outcomes on complex and high-impact decisions. It's a bonus if you have Experience conducting due diligence for portfolio acquisitions, conversions or large-scale merchant onboarding initiatives. Experience mentoring, coaching or developing less experienced analysts and serving as a subject matter expert across teams. A track record of contributing to credit policy development, risk framework enhancements or process improvements that strengthen business performance and risk management outcomes.

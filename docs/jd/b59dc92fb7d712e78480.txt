THE FINANCE TEAM

Examples of key problems the team is working on

 - Vendor/broker management. Source, evaluate, and negotiate with insurance brokers and carriers; run renewal RFPs to ensure competitive pricing and coverage terms as the portfolio grows.

 - Premium and budget management. Own the insurance budget, forecast premium spend as new sites and assets come online, and manage the renewal cycle across property, casualty, and builder's risk lines.

 - Compliance and requirements tracking. Ensure coverage meets contractual requirements from lenders, JV partners, customers, and landlords (minimum limits, additional insured status, certificates of insurance), and keep documentation audit-ready.


ROLE SCOPE

 - Own every insurance program at Fluidstack end to end, from program design and broker selection through placement, renewal, and budget, across property, construction, liability, and cargo lines.

 - Run claims from first notice to resolution, driving brokers, TPAs, carriers, and Legal to outcomes instead of waiting on them.

 - Drive loss control and property risk engineering across operating sites and active gigawatt-scale construction, turning risk engineering recommendations into completed fixes with Construction, HSE, and Data Center Operations.

 - Gather and analyze exposure data across a fleet growing by gigawatts and land complete, on-time renewal submissions to underwriters every cycle.

 - Set the insurance requirements in leases, EPC contracts, and vendor and customer agreements, and review counterparty terms so coverage gaps never surface mid-claim.




WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

 - You've managed commercial property and casualty insurance programs yourself, on the corporate risk management or brokerage side, and you know how property, construction, and liability lines actually get structured, placed, and priced.

 - You've run claims to resolution, including large or contested ones, coordinating litigation counsel, TPAs, adjusters, and carriers on strategy rather than just processing paperwork.

 - You explain insurance to engineers, lawyers, and executives clearly enough that they act on it.

 - Bonus: Insurance designations (CPCU, ARM). Capital-intensive or construction-heavy industries (data centers, energy, semiconductors). Construction insurance programs (builder's risk, OCIP/CCIP). High-value equipment in transit (cargo, stock throughput).

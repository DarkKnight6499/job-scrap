Corporate Finance Manager
Micron
Location: Boise, ID - Main Site

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all . Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever. As a Corporate Finance Manager at Micron, you will help direct our financial future. Your focus will be the long-range planning process and maintaining a detailed financial model. You will collaborate with Finance, Corporate Strategy, Business Units, Engineering, and Operations leadership. Together, you will develop assumptions for the long-range plan and evaluate scenarios that align with company goals. Responsibilities Financial Planning, Forecasting & Reporting Develop and consolidate long-term financial plans: Maintain accurate corporate financial projections and partner with business and finance leaders to refine assumptions, ensure alignment, and meet planning deadlines. Prepare executive and board reporting: Provide timely financial reports and insights for executive and board evaluation, including support for investor communications. Monitor financial performance and enable decisions: Track critical financial metrics and provide relevant financial insights to support strategic, value-creating decisions. Drive FP&A process improvement: Partner across functions to identify and implement improvements that increase efficiency and financial performance. Systems Transformation & Process Excellence Partner with Finance Systems, IT, and business collaborators to improve corporate planning systems through automation, AI adoption, advanced analytics, and reporting optimization. Operational Efficiency Drive process improvement: Identify and support opportunities to simplify processes, optimize reporting, and improve efficiency while maintaining high accuracy standards. Maintain planning rigor: Adhere to established planning calendars and meet all deadlines. Ensure reporting consistency: Maintain Finance reporting standards and consistent benchmark definitions. Minimum Qualifications 8+ years of Finance experience and a bachelor’s degree in finance, economics, accounting or a related field 5+ years of experience in corporate FP&A, financial statement analysis, and forecasting and planning Expertise in advanced financial modeling and proficiency with financial systems and modeling tools. Excellent communication, interpersonal, and negotiation capabilities, with the skill to articulate complex financial information to executives and the board and engage effectively across organizational boundaries. Strong analytical skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and tight deadlines in a fast-paced environment while maintaining a careful, methodical approach. Knowledge of AI-enabled efficiency, automation, and analytics tools. Preferred Qualifications Graduate-level qualification in Finance or Business Outstanding business competence and financial skill with a deep understanding of P&L, cash flow, and balance sheet dynamics.

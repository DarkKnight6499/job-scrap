At Nationwide®, “on your side” goes beyond just words. Our customers are at the center of everything we do and we’re looking for associates who are passionate about delivering extraordinary care. Join Nationwide Financial’s Life Valuation team as an NF Actuary and help deliver accurate, compliant actuarial valuation for life insurance products. In this role, you’ll bring expertise in Statutory, GAAP, and Tax valuation, take ownership of quarterly processes, and help the team adapt to evolving standards and modernized tools. You’ll partner across Finance and the business to turn complex actuarial analysis into clear insights and recommendations. What you’ll do Serve as a subject-matter expert in Statutory, GAAP, and Tax valuation for life insurance products. Lead analysis and implementation of evolving valuation standards, including LDTI and PBR. Own and continuously improve quarterly valuation processes, validation tools, controls, documentation, and governance. Build, maintain, and analyze actuarial, projection, and valuation models; support financial reporting, risk analysis, product calculations, and reserve setting. Collaborate with Tax, Controllership, Pricing, and partners across data, modeling, assumptions, product development, analytics, and reporting. Respond to auditor and regulator inquiries and communicate findings to technical and nontechnical audiences, including senior finance leaders. What you’ll bring FSA designation (required). Strong actuarial business-function knowledge and valuation principles. Typically seven or more years of actuarial experience. Experience with life insurance products and Statutory/GAAP valuation is preferred. Proficiency with Microsoft Office and analytical tools such as Power BI or SQL. Experience with AXIS or ALFA modeling software is preferred; VB.NET or Python programming experience is a plus. You’ll thrive in this role if you enjoy solving complex valuation problems, improving processes, working across teams, and explaining technical analysis in a clear, practical way.

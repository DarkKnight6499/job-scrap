Job Family : Management Consulting Travel Required : Up to 10% Clearance Required : Ability to Obtain Public Trust What You Will Do : Guidehouse is seeking an experienced Risk Analyst to support enterprise‑level programs by conducting research, performing qualitative and quantitative analyses, developing reports, and supporting ongoing risk‑related initiatives. This role works closely with subject matter experts and program leads to evaluate organizational risks, document findings, and support decision‑making. The position requires strong analytical skills, structured communication, and comfort working in environments that rely on formal documentation, recurring reporting cycles, and structured governance. Key Responsibilities Risk Research & Analysis Conduct qualitative and quantitative analyses to assess potential risks, evaluate likelihood and impact, and identify contributing factors. Support development, escalation, or de‑escalation of risks through structured review, data synthesis, and research. Perform root‑cause analysis and contribute to the development or refinement of associated response actions. Participate in interviews with subject matter experts to gather risk data and validate assumptions. Reporting & Documentation Prepare clear, data‑driven reports that summarize risk insights, analysis outcomes, recommendations, and trends. Support the drafting of reports for internal or external stakeholders, including semiannual or annual deliverables associated with enterprise priorities. Contribute to the development of structured frameworks such as risk registers, key risk indicators (KRIs), and performance‑related metrics. Maintain accurate and timely documentation aligned with established templates, quality standards, and review cycles. Program & Process Support Track assigned tasks, milestones, updates, and corrective actions, providing regular status summaries to program leadership. Analyze existing processes to identify opportunities for improving risk management workflow, documentation consistency, or reporting clarity. Support the development of processes used to evaluate the effectiveness of risk responses or organizational governance frameworks. Assist with integration of enterprise‑level risk information into broader planning, budgeting, or performance management activities. Collaboration & Stakeholder Engagement Provide research and analytical support to working groups and committees, preparing materials and supporting follow-up actions as needed. Engage with team members to ensure alignment between risk insights and organizational objectives. Respond to inquiries related to risk data, analysis methods, or program documentation. What You Will Need : Must be able to OBTAIN and MAINTAIN a Federal or DoD "PUBLIC TRUST"; candidates must obtain approved adjudication of their PUBLIC TRUST prior to onboarding with Guidehouse. Candidates with an ACTIVE PUBLIC TRUST or SUITABILITY are preferred. Bachelor’s degree THREE (3) or more years of working experience. Experience must include: Supporting Federal agencies or government programs TWO (2) years of experience in risk analysis, data analysis, program support, or analytical roles in structured or regulated environments. Experience conducting research, synthesizing data, and developing structured, narrative analytical products. Experience supporting cross‑functional projects with documentation requirements and recurring deliverables. What Would Be Nice To Have : Professional certifications related to risk management or internal controls (e.g., CRMA, CIA, CRM) are preferred but not required. Understanding of risk frameworks, performance measurement concepts, or structured program governance is desirable. Ability to develop clear, well‑organized reports and presentations that communicate complex findings. Ability to work collaboratively and manage multiple priorities with attention to detail.

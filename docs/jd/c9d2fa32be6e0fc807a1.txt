As Change Management Associate Director, you will join our Employee Experience and Change Management Team, helping design and deliver change management approaches for a broad range of business initiatives. You will partner with leaders across the business, lead a small team of change management professionals, and serve as a trusted resource on organizational change. What You'll Do: Lead a small team of change management professionals: help set the team's work plan, objectives, and performance measures with senior leadership, and support team members through coaching and development. Support and lead change initiatives by partnering with business stakeholders to help build the case for change, prioritize work, and turn change drivers into programs with a clear vision. Develop targeted plans (communication, sponsor roadmap, training, and resistance management) informed by change readiness assessments. Identify change-related risks across people, operations, and the organization, and escalate them to leadership with clear mitigation recommendations. Apply and help improve change management processes and tools, bringing a continuous improvement mindset to day-to-day delivery. Track KPIs and use quantitative and qualitative analysis to measure success and inform recommendations and coordinate with external consultants and vendors as needed. Be a collaborative, dependable leader in a matrixed environment, building strong working relationships across the business to deliver successful outcomes. Required Education, Experience & Skills: Bachelor’s degree and a minimum of seven years of relevant experience Experience managing people and leading cross-functional teams, programs, or initiatives In-depth project management skills Strong quantitative and qualitative analytical skills Proven ability to collaborate, influence, and drive outcomes across stakeholders Experience working effectively with senior leaders including at the Vice President level and above Strong written and verbal communication skills Effective presentation skills Preferred Qualifications: Advanced degree Relevant professional certification, such as Prosci Certified Change Practitioner or Certified Change Management Professional (CCMP) . Work Conditions: Hybrid work environment, with defined in-person presence requirements. Extended hours and travel may be required.

Essential Job Functions and Responsibilities Examines and processes new insurance policies, complex renewals, endorsements, and cancellations using various systems. Enters or loads data into appropriate systems and researches and resolves data errors. Generates and distributes policy documents, certificates of insurance, and other related materials. Organizes, prepares, and updates reports. Determines and adjusts billing status as needed. Identifies opportunities to streamline procedures, enhance efficiency, and reduce costs. Performs other duties as assigned. Job Requirements Education: High School Diploma (Associate degree preferred) Experience: Generally, a minimum of 6 years of experience in insurance operations, Property/Casualty, Excess and Primary insurance. Completion of or continuing progress toward a professional designation preferred, such as Property/Casualty Insurance license. Knowledge in Excess and Primary line of business. Scope of Job/Qualifications: Strong knowledge of local, state, and federal insurance-related policies. Advanced analytical and organizational abilities with a focus on prioritization. Advanced knowledge of insurance data processing systems. Excellent interpersonal and communication skills, analytical, organizational skills, team player and detail oriented.

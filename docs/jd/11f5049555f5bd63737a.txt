Job Summary Bring your expertise to JPMorgan Chase. As part of Risk Management and Compliance, you are at the center of keeping JPMorgan Chase strong and resilient. You help the firm grow its business in a responsible way by anticipating new and emerging risks, and using your expert judgement to solve real-world challenges that impact our company, customers and communities. Our culture in Risk Management and Compliance is all about challenging the status quo and striving to be best in class. As a Vice President in the dynamic RM&C Digital Strategy & Enablement team within the Finance & Business Management organization, you will be a key player in a fast-paced, results-oriented environment, partnering with senior leaders across Risk Management and Compliance, Financial Planning and Analysis, and Corporate Technology. You will have the opportunity to blend strategy, problem-solving, and collaboration, providing oversight and governance on technology budgets and transformation initiatives. You will monitor ongoing progress on our investments, create transparency to maximize benefits, refine governance frameworks, and enhance detailed technology portfolio and financial reporting. In this role, you will offer leadership and guidance as meaningful narratives are produced around portfolio reporting, helping us drive impactful outcomes across the organization. Job Responsibilities Oversee and enhance the technology investment governance framework, aligning with strategic business objectives and maximizing return on investment. Drive the annual technology planning and budgeting process in partnership with finance, business, and technology leaders. Partner with business leads on well-written annual investment details, developing and overseeing a process to review and approve new technology demand throughout the year, and partnering with each business lead to develop business cases during the annual budget. Manage the technology investment portfolio, including tracking, reporting, and optimizing spend across projects and products to drive transparency and accountability for investment decisions. Standardize how business benefits and return on investment are defined, handle ad-hoc reporting, lead the connectivity of technology portfolio management systems, and translate complex portfolio data into consumable information for stakeholders. Serve as a key liaison between technology, finance, and business units to clearly align on investment priority, and effective decision-making regarding technology investments. Define and implement standardized practices, frameworks, and governance for the product operating model across Risk Management & Compliance, gaining consistency in product ownership, lifecycle management, and performance measurement – based on firmwide guidance. Establish and monitor KPIs and success metrics for technology investments and product operating model effectiveness; provide regular updates and recommendations to senior leadership. Identify and implement best practices for investment governance and product management, driving continuous improvement in processes, tools, and methodologies. Demonstrate strong, clear, and concise written and verbal communication skills and an ability to edit and prepare executive level communications.

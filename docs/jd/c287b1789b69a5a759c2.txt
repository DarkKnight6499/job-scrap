The Difference You Make: The Fixed Income Middle Office Analyst is responsible for providing front-to-back operational support to Santander CIB's Fixed Income business. The role serves as a key control function between Trading, Sales, Operations, Finance, Compliance, and Technology, helping to ensure accurate trade execution, timely settlement, effective risk mitigation, and exceptional client service. The successful candidate will support both pre-trade and post-trade processes while actively contributing to operational excellence initiatives, process improvements, and regulatory compliance efforts across the Fixed Income product suite. Trade Support & Lifecycle Management Provide day-to-day support to Fixed Income Trading and Sales desks across multiple products and client segments. Perform trade validation, allocation, matching, and post-execution trade processing. Investigate and resolve trade breaks, discrepancies, and settlement exceptions in a timely manner. Monitor trade lifecycle events to ensure accurate processing through settlement. Reconciliation & Control Functions Conduct daily reconciliations across trading, settlement, and accounting platforms. Identify, escalate, and resolve discrepancies related to trades, allocations, positions, and client reporting. Monitor and manage settlement fails while partnering with internal and external stakeholders to drive timely resolution. Support control processes designed to ensure data integrity and operational accuracy. Client & Counterparty Support Respond to client inquiries related to trade activity, reconciliations, and cash differences. Assist in the investigation and resolution of client-facing operational issues. Maintain strong working relationships with clients, counterparties, custodians, and service providers. Static Data & Onboarding Support Coordinate the setup and maintenance of client accounts, counterparties, settlement instructions, and trading relationships. Ensure account and settlement data is accurately maintained across relevant platforms. Support onboarding initiatives for new clients, products, and business activities. Stakeholder Management & Governance Partner closely with Trading, Sales, Operations, Compliance, Finance, and Technology teams. Coordinate with local and global operations teams to facilitate successful trade settlement and issue resolution. Escalate material operational risks and exceptions in accordance with established governance procedures. Contribute to a strong risk and control culture across the organization. Projects & Process Improvement Participate in strategic initiatives, system implementations, and operational enhancement projects. Identify opportunities to streamline workflows and improve efficiency through automation and process optimization. Support testing, implementation, and documentation of technology and process changes. Reporting & Management Information Produce and distribute daily Profit & Loss (P&L) and management reporting. Prepare operational metrics and management information to support decision-making and oversight.

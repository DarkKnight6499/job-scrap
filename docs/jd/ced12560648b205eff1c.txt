Responsibilities This position is responsible for the management of Commercial Underwriters and the credit process in one or more locations ensuring that the processes and procedures are adhered to for sound financial underwriting of Commercial Banking transactions. This position includes decisioning and underwriting complex credit request and monitoring compliance with approved commercial loan servicing and financial covenants. The Underwriting Manager also monitors portfolio quality and ensures proper Asset Quality Rating guidelines are adhered to.

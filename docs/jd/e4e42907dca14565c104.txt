Treasury Management Consultant
Columbia Bank
Location: Denver, Colorado; Phoenix, Arizona
Link: https://columbiabank.wd108.myworkdayjobs.com/Columbia/job/Denver-Colorado/Treasury-Management-Consultant_JR103245

About the Role: Responsible for providing a consultative approach to large, more sophisticated business clients with complex treasury needs, and / or a broad and deep client base of TM customers that have standard to mid-sized treasury needs. Responsible for developing new treasury management business, expanding business relationships, including handling high touch clients. Collaborates with Relationship Managers, Bankers and Division Managers, as well as other business lines and business partners to facilitate the on boarding of new clients to the bank. Consults with clients on their entire Working Capital Management cycle, from receivables to payables, liquidity, international / FX and credit needs when appropriate. Develop sales plans to identify the expansion of existing clients in conjunction with the Relationship Managers by preparing call schedules and presentations. Responsible for presenting a consultative working cash flow presentation to prospects and existing clients. Lead client/prospect sales calls with Relationship Managers and Technical Solutions Consultant. Drive the collaboration between business lines to implement new solutions for new and existing clients. Lead consultative conversation with new and existing customers to define the solutions to meet their goals and business objectives. Lead and facilitate the implementation and structure of new and existing services. Mentors and coaches more junior TM consultants and associates, trains new TM consultants as appropriate. Responsible for monitoring industry trends and competitive practices, including the impact of technology, regulatory and legislative changes related to treasury management. Partner with Product managers in the development of new product solutions and the enhancement of existing product and services. Demonstrates compliance with all bank regulations for assigned job function and applies to designated job responsibilities – knowledge may be gained through coursework and on-the-job training. Keeps up to date on regulation changes. Follows all Bank policies and procedures, compliance regulations, and completes all required annual or job-specific training. Maintain a working knowledge of Bank's written policies and procedures regarding Bank Secrecy Act, Regulation CC, Regulation E, Bank Security and other regulations as applicable to this job description. May be asked to coach, mentor, or train others and teach coursework as subject matter expert. Actively learns, demonstrates, and fosters the Columbia corporate culture in all actions and words. Takes personal initiative and is a positive example for others to emulate. Embraces our vision to become “Business Bank of Choice” May perform other duties as assigned. About You: Bachelor's Degree in Finance, Accounting, Marketing, Business or other related fields (preferred) Master’s Degree (preferred) More than 10 years — of experience in treasury management sales, service and/or support. (Req) Extensive knowledge and understanding of bank payment systems, treasury management solutions and competitive practices. Proficient knowledge of sales principles and practices to effectively market treasury management solution to prospects and clients. Demonstrated strong ability to consistently develop and execute sales planning within assigned sales territory. Proven ability to consistently achieve established sales and customer satisfaction goals, demonstrate effective new business development strategies, and build/source new relationships. Proven ability to effectively manage complex commercial account relationships and consult client best practices and operations to identify deficiencies to lead, develop and implement effective solutions. Extensive analytical, problem solving, excellent customer service, organizational and interpersonal skills. Excellent written and verbal communication skills required to lead, develop and present effective sales proposals.

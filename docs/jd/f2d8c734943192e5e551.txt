Job Location IVORYDALE INNOVATION CENTER Job Description Research and Development (R&D) at Procter & Gamble, the largest consumer packaged goods company in the world, includes a diverse group of roles that contribute to the innovation and development of our products. It encompasses roles in product research, formulation, testing, and scientific analysis. You will find variety and excitement starting Day 1. This is an Associate Researcher role within our Researcher Track career path. Researcher Track employees are motivated by hands-on experimentation and applying foundational science through defined or standardized experimentation, data generation, detailed observations, and documentation of results. The exact day-to-day work depends on the position. The Researcher Track is part of P&G's Business, Technical and Administrative (BTA) level roles and provides a technical, non-management career path. As employees grow in the track, they deepen their technical mastery through increasing application of scientific, experimental, and procedural skills. Associate Researcher is an entry-level role for individuals who are competent in basic science and math and are inspired to apply those skills in a laboratory, pilot plant, or technical development environment. At P&G, a wide variety of R&D work is available to Associate Researchers, in the following major areas: Products Research Deeply understand our consumers and their needs to inform product design parameters for new or improved products. Product Formulation & Design Translates consumer needs into finished products that delight consumers using the best available technologies/chemicals/materials. Analytical Use lab equipment to solve tough technical problems via chemical, physical, mechanical, imaging, and other measurement capabilities. Packaging & Device Development Creates and improves packages and devices that delight consumers and retailers. Process Development & Pilot Plant Operations Translates ideas, concepts, and technologies into commercially feasible chemical and mechanical processes and scales them up from lab to pilot plant to manufacturing. The Opportunity: P&G has an opportunity for an Associate Researcher to work in our Surface Care Business at our Ivorydale Technical Innovation Center. Surface Care is an exciting place to work and a fast-growing brand Globally. Surface Care is an important contributor to the global Home Care business and represents significant growth opportunities for the segment and our share. Swiffer and Mr. Clean are unique products in Home Care portfolio where you get to work and learn about not only liquids but also substrates and devices. This opportunity includes innovative research and development to enhance the quality and effectiveness of Surface Care products. This collaborative environment fosters a multidisciplinary approach, integrating science, consumer insights, and product design to meet the evolving needs of families. Team members play a crucial role in conducting experiments, analyzing data, and translating findings into actionable improvements for P&G's Surface Care portfolio. The Surface Care team is looking for someone to join their team in an open role supporting their upstream durable product solutions.

Director, Structuring
Standard Chartered
Location: New York, US
Link: https://jobs.standardchartered.com/job/New-York-Director%2C-Structuring/1374143657/

Job Summary Grow the Structuring business in the Americas (North America and LATAM) through value added solutions (bespoke hedging, investment and financing) to corporate, BBD, and Investor clients Key Responsibilities Strategy • Work with respective Product Heads, Sales Heads and in-country Heads of Markets to help grow the structured credit business in line with SCB’s overall strategy • Drive structured business covering complex or bespoke investment/financing products across clients in Americas • Work in close collaboration with client coverage teams and various product partners to design bespoke solutions based the client’s situation and objectives • Provide inputs for the regional Americas and Global Structuring team to share product/client experience • Drive service excellence via robust product support, bespoke solutions and strategic cross-asset guidance Business • A structurer needs to deliver practical and viable solutions, within several objectives and constraints, conciliating among various stakeholders: • Clients (economic objectives, hedging policies, risk appetite, ...) • Bank’s resources (Credit limits, balance sheet returns, liquidity availability, reputational risk, ...) • Markets and products (trading limits, market liquidity, available products and payoffs, ...) • Regulatory (Legal and compliance, tax, accounting, ...) • Work with respective FM Product teams and relevant Regional/Country Heads, identify the initiatives needed to grow the Credit Structuring business on a short, medium and long-term basis • Work in partnership with stakeholders to deliver new product capabilities to enhance efficiency and expansion of the business • Drive structured transactions from idea origination to deal execution • Demonstrate excellent soft skills including deal communications, idea sharing, stakeholders liaison and strong team play • Co-operate on global initiatives and share information and ideas with sale and structuring • Deliver training and education on structured products to internal and external audiences Qualifications • Education: Bachelor’s degree • Training: 7-9 years’ experience in credit structuring, exposure to Europe and Americas regions, FM products knowledge • Licenses: Series 7 and 63 • Membership: FINRA • Certifications: DODD FRANK, SEC AP • Languages: English About Standard Chartered We're an

Job Description: Opportunity in the Product Risk Execution team, within the Global Payments Solutions (GPS) organization. The Product Risk Execution organization reflects our commitment to elevating risk management to become a strategic function and enabler of Responsible Growth. This team develops best practices, streamlines processes, and enables execution of risk activities by supporting product managers with expertise and specialization on all aspects of product risk management. Product managers remain responsible for risk in their product, and product executives retain responsibility for their governance routines. Responsibilities: • Direct report to the Global Head of GPS Product Risk Execution, matrix report to Global Liquidity Executive • Enable execution of risk management across the Global Liquidity Product team. Ensure high quality of product risk governance across Issues Management, Single Process Inventory (SPI), Audit Management, Targeted Risk Assessments, Third Party, Law Rule Reg & Clearing Rule Management, and Product Risk Assessment (PRA) processes. • Engage and influence across senior leaders of various control partners such as Business Controls, COO, Global Banking Risk, Global Financial Crimes Compliance (GFC), Global Compliance and Operational Risk (GCOR), and other key stakeholders such as Operations & Technology on GPS risk matters • Manage regular routines with Product executives & other partners to identify and review current open issues, watch items, upcoming regulations & new product initiatives pipeline. • Drive and QA development of Product Intake assessments, Product Risk Assessments (PRAs), Single Process Inventories (SPIs), Issue documentation, action plans, closure documentation and other Risk deliverables based on inputs from Product Managers and in line with the enterprise expectations • Lead SPI owners on risk & controls assessment including identifying processes, and providing advisory on creation and updates of SPIs. • Lead, when needed, on regulatory change management, timely assessments, past due items and escalations in alignment with the regulatory change management process. Escalate issues or conflicts in legal and regulatory requirements as appropriate. • Advise Product Managers, when needed, on responding to audit’s questions and requests for information, Targeted Risk Assessments (TRAs) from 2nd line (GCOR / GFC etc) or regulatory engagements (questions, consultations, inspections, and exams). • Monitor key risk indicators across GPS products, including key control

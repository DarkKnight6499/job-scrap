Department Overview The Evidence Product Management team owns the roadmap for delivering Motorola Solutions’ body cameras, vehicle cameras, and evidence management software. These products are used worldwide to keep communities and individuals safe in public safety and enterprise organizations. To help drive our rapid growth, we are looking for an enthusiastic associate product manager/product manager to join this team and take ownership of our in-car video (ICV) device. If you enjoy working in a fast-paced environment, where you will be exposed to the latest technologies, and where your hard work and dedication will be rewarded, joining our team could be your best career move. Job Description We want an enthusiastic associate product manager/product manager for the In-Car Video product line to: Work to understand the market's product requirements and enable their delivery, including competition. Based on the product’s lifecycle, create a maintenance plan to manage associated products. Work alongside the product owner and development teams to help create well-maintained, refined, and prioritized product backlogs that include consistent, measurable “definitions of done.” Define product release plans, including measuring their progress towards completion and tracking the predictability. Manage prioritization of market expansion or establishment, including coordination with marketing, sales, training, support, and partners, and identify translations and territorial approval requirements. Assist with specific large-opportunity projects. Persuade and influence others through strong communication. Develop and demonstrate a high level of subject matter expertise. Qualifications: BS in Computer Science, Telecommunications, Product Management At least 5 years of experience in in-car video and/or telematics hardware, firmware, applications, and solutions development. A good understanding of and experience in the enterprise segment of the in-car video market is a plus. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to understand and communicate the product roadmap & vision to the development team, refine product backlog items, and report progress to a broad audience, including the business, product management stakeholders, and the development teams English language skills at a level allowing efficient and effective communication Self-motivated, positive attitude, an ability and desire to motivate others.

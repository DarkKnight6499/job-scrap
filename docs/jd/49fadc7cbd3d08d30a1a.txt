Position Reporting to the Portfolio Managers, the Investment Research Analyst will be responsible for the identification, analysis, diligence and ongoing monitoring of leveraged loan and high yield bond investments across all Polen Capital Credit strategies (Opportunistic Credit, CLO, US High Yield). The Analyst will own coverage of a defined set of industries, form independent views on issuers across the capital structure, and make buy and sell recommendations that directly inform portfolio construction. Polen Capital Credit (formerly DDJ Capital Management) is proud of its 26-year history of generating performance for clients through a concentrated, high-conviction, fundamental research-driven approach to investing in high yield bonds and leveraged loans. Responsibilities Provide industry coverage across all Polen Credit strategies and portfolios, conducting in-depth fundamental research on leveraged loan and high yield bond issuers using available public and private information, and delivering buy/sell recommendations to the Portfolio Managers Evaluate primary market opportunities and secondary market ideas and assess relative value across the capital structure Build and maintain detailed financial models Thoroughly research and analyze the financial, legal and ESG aspects of every investment under coverage Monitor existing positions on an ongoing basis and proactively update the Portfolio Managers and recommendations as credit profiles change Prepare concise written investment memos and present recommendations to the investment team Participate in management meetings, lender calls and company diligence, and engage with private equity sponsors, arrangers and the sell-side Develop and maintain a far-reaching network of contacts in the industry and the broader financial community to enhance idea generation and research Collaborate with Polen’s distribution and marketing efforts to maintain and grow assets under management.

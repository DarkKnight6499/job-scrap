Blackstone is the world’s largest alternative asset manager. Blackstone seeks to deliver compelling returns for institutional and individual investors by strengthening the companies in which the firm invests. Blackstone’s over $1.3 trillion in assets under management include global investment strategies focused on real estate, private equity, credit, infrastructure, life sciences, growth equity, secondaries and hedge funds. Further information is available at www.blackstone.com . Follow @blackstone on LinkedIn , X (Twitter) , and Instagram . Job Description Business Unit: Institutional Client Solutions, Private Capital Group Job Title: Institutional Client Solutions – Analyst Job Description: Blackstone is seeking a business development professional to support the effort to proactively develop new clients and expand relationships with sophisticated family offices, RIAs and endowments and foundations across the entire Blackstone platform. The Private Capital team is looking for an analyst or associate who is organized, detail-oriented and client centric. The key function of the role will be to support business development efforts across the West Coast. Help identify and cultivate high quality relationships with family offices; this includes supporting the effort to develop goals, objectives, strategies, and tactics to create meaningful relationships with family offices, RIAs and endowments and foundations, resulting in a consistent awareness of Blackstone’s strategies and growth in assets under management Collaborate on various research projects utilizing internal and external resources to help grow the business Maintain a high level of prospecting activity to map the West Coast market Assist on marketing efforts with LP base – semi-annual think tank events, fund specific webinars, and other sales initiatives Support and encourage a team-oriented model with effective communication, transparency and trust Collaborate on various research projects utilizing internal and external resources to help grow the business Qualifications: Blackstone seeks to hire individuals who work well in a team-driven working group, are highly motivated, intelligent, have sound judgment and have demonstrated excellence in prior endeavors. The successful candidate must have: 2+ years of financial industry experience Strong communication and presentation skills, including effectively positioning the capabilities of a platform or product to investors Experience engaging clients Desire to work both independently and collaboratively in a dynamic environment, and collaborate across business units Strong organizational skills including prioritizing targets during a busy period of client meetings Self-starter, entrepreneurial and goal oriented, with a high level of accountability and drive Some travel required Bachelor’s Degree The duties and responsibilities described here are not exhaustive and additional assignments, duties, or responsibilities may be required of this position.

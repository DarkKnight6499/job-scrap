CAPITAL SOLUTIONS GROUP Goldman Sachs operates at the forefront of one of the most important structural trends in finance: the convergence of public and private markets. We combine a leading global advisory franchise, a broad origination business and an investing platform that deploys capital across liquid and alternative asset classes. The Capital Solutions Group brings together a comprehensive suite of financing, origination, structuring and risk management solutions. The depth and breadth of our relationships, combined with the strength of our deal flow, provide clients with unique insights to maximize value and unlock opportunity. Our integrated structure enables us to deliver comprehensive, tailored solutions for both issuing and investing clients. YOUR IMPACT We are seeking a highly skilled Vice President to join the Equity Capital Markets Group in New York. This is an exceptional opportunity to join a leading ECM franchise and play an important role in originating, structuring and executing equity capital markets transactions for corporate, financial sponsor and venture capital clients. The Equity Capital Markets Group works closely with public and private companies, venture capital firms, entrepreneurs and financial sponsors to originate, structure and execute equity financings, including initial public offerings, follow-on offerings, block trades, convertible securities and derivatives. The group is organized by product, including common equity and structured equity, and works closely with industry coverage teams and other areas of the firm, particularly Equity Sales and Trading.

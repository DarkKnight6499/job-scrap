Intelligently utilize internal and non-public sources of data to improve pricing quality and risk management. Work with market tick data at scale - querying and managing historical data, building backtest pipelines, and parallelizing batch jobs for reporting across many currency pairs. Perform statistical analysis of portfolio performance to drive iterative improvements.

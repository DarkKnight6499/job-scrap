Inc. (Oppenheimer) is a leading middle-market investment bank and full-service broker-dealer. With roots tracing back to 1881, the Company is engaged in a broad range of activities in the financial services industry, including retail securities brokerage, institutional sales and trading, investment banking (both corporate and public finance), equity & fixed income research, market making, trust services and investment advisory and asset management services. Job Description: Oppenheimer & Co. Inc. is seeking a Research Associate to join our Emerging Technologies and Services team in New York City. Working directly with a Senior Analyst, you will help cover a high-growth universe spanning electronic components, consumer hardware, and digital media (including video games and mobile AdTech). This is a fast-paced role for a self-starter who excels at complex financial modeling and clear, evidence-based investment writing. Responsibilities Research & Analysis: Support the Senior Analyst in developing and marketing equity research products, including company-specific reports and thematic industry analysis. Financial Modeling: Build and maintain integrated income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow models for covered companies. Data Management: Organize and maintain proprietary industry data sets and valuation "comps" to support investment theses. Project Ownership: Independently initiate and complete research projects with minimal supervision, often under tight deadlines. Communication: Express strong, well-reasoned investment opinions through clear writing and internal collaboration. Requirements Finance Expertise: Strong background in accounting and finance with the ability to model complex business scenarios. Advanced skills in integrated 3-statement modeling and valuation. The candidate must be able to build a working 3-statement model in Excel from scratch. Communication Skills: Proven ability to write clearly and concisely without hyperbole. Ability to produce high-quality research reports under time constraints. Mindset: Intellectual honesty, a high degree of curiosity, and the "nimbleness" to update views when presented with new facts. Work Ethic: A self-starter attitude with the ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities. Education/Experience: Experience in equity research, video games, consumer electronics, or semiconductors is preferred. Soft Skills: Ability to admit knowledge gaps and orient quickly when dealing with unknowns. Analytical Rigor: Strong attention to detail and high degree of intellectual curiosity.

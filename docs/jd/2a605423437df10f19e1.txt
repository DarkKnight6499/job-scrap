Vice President – Equity Research Controls and Business Management Position Summary MUFG is seeking a Vice President, Equity Research Controls and Business Management, to serve as the primary governance, controls, and business management partner for its US Equity Research organization. This highly visible role sits at the intersection of Research, Compliance, Risk, Technology, Operations, and senior management, helping to ensure the Research platform operates in a controlled, efficient, and scalable manner. The successful candidate will advise Research leadership on regulatory, supervisory, operational, and strategic matters while driving initiatives that strengthen governance, enhance operating effectiveness, and support long-term business objectives. The position combines responsibility for first-line controls oversight with broader business management activities, including strategic planning, technology and vendor management, management reporting, process improvement, and execution of key business initiatives. This is an opportunity to play a central role in the management and continued evolution of a leading Equity Research franchise within a global financial institution. Key Responsibilities Serve as the primary controls and business management partner to the US Equity Research organization, advising leadership on regulatory, supervisory, operational, and strategic matters. Maintain the Research governance, risk, and control framework, including policies, procedures, controls, risk assessments, management reporting, and oversight processes. Lead the Research Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA) process and coordinate ongoing risk monitoring, control reviews, issue management, and remediation activities. Identify, assess, and escalate regulatory, operational, supervisory, and reputational risks impacting the Research business. Develop and monitor management reporting, key risk indicators, business metrics, and dashboards to support effective decision-making and oversight. Coordinate regulatory examinations, compliance reviews, internal audits, control testing, and other assurance activities affecting the Research organization. Support regulatory inquiries and interactions by coordinating information requests, documentation, and management responses. Advise Research leadership on regulatory developments, governance matters, supervisory practices, and opportunities to enhance the control environment. Serve as a designated backup Supervisory Analyst, performing review and approval responsibilities as needed to support research publication oversight, regulatory compliance, and business continuity. Support execution of strategic initiatives, operating priorities, and business plans to enhance the effectiveness and scalability of the Research platform. Partner with Technology, Operations, Compliance, Legal, Finance, Human Resources, and Risk Management to drive process improvements, workflow enhancements, automation opportunities, and control enhancements. Manage key Research vendor and technology relationships, including platform governance, service delivery, contract oversight, and operational support. Support budget management, resource planning, expense oversight, and other business management activities for the Research organization. Prepare presentations, analyses, and materials for senior management, governance committees, regulators, and other stakeholders. Promote a strong culture of accountability, risk awareness, and effective governance across the Research organization. Qualifications Required Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Business Administration, Accounting, or a related field. 7-10 years of experience supporting an Equity Research organization in a controls, supervisory, compliance, business management, operational risk, or related governance capacity. Strong understanding of SEC and FINRA requirements applicable to Equity Research, including FINRA Rule 2241, Global Research Settlement obligations, research conflicts management requirements, information barrier controls, and research publication governance.

Company: Qualcomm Technologies, Inc. Job Area: Engineering Group, Engineering Group > ASICS Engineering General Summary: About the Team Qualcomm’s System Validation & Emulation (SVE) team is part of the central SoC validation organization responsible for ensuring end-to-end silicon quality across pre- and post-silicon phases . This role focuses on content quality and system-level validation enablement , offering deep exposure to Qualcomm SoCs through bare-metal validation content , scalable automation frameworks, and complex system debugs. Engineers in this role work across HW and SW boundaries to develop, validate, and scale system-level test content , ensuring high-quality silicon validation across a wide range of SoC domains. Excellent Opportunity to work on cutting-edge SoCs across multiple product segments , including: Premium mobile and flagship platforms Data center AI Accelerator platform Role Overview Qualcomm is building next-generation AI accelerator platforms featuring advanced memory technologies and chiplet-based architectures. We are seeking an experienced Memory System Validation lead to define and execute validation strategies for custom high-bandwidth memory subsystems integrated with large-scale AI compute platforms. In this role, you will lead pre-silicon and post-silicon validation of next-generation stacked memory solutions, driving memory bring-up, performance characterization, stress validation, reliability analysis, debug, and production readiness across the product lifecycle. The ideal candidate possesses deep expertise in DDR/HBM technologies, memory controller architectures, silicon bring-up, and system-level debug. Key Responsibilities Memory Bring-Up & Validation · Own end-to-end HBM validation strategy from emulation through production silicon. · Develop comprehensive validation plans covering: o Memory initialization o Training flows o Power-state transitions o Reliability testing o Error recovery mechanisms o Manufacturing diagnostics o Reliability & RAS Validation · Drive Pre-silicon memory bring-up · Drive first-silicon memory bring-up and root-cause analysis of failures. System-Level Validation · Validate interaction between: o HBM o AI accelerators o Compute fabrics o Cache hierarchy o NoC infrastructure · Develop stress scenarios representing real-world AI workloads. Debug & Root Cause Analysis · Lead complex silicon debug efforts. · Analyze crashes, hangs, data corruption, training failures, and performance bottlenecks. · Collaborate with: o Memory architecture teams o PHY teams o Firmware teams o Package and SI/PI teams o AI software teams Automation & Methodology · Develop scalable automation frameworks for memory validation. · Build telemetry, analytics, debug, and performance-monitoring tools. · Leverage AI-assisted validation and debug methodologies to improve productivity and coverage. Required Qualifications BS/MS/PhD in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or related field. 8+ years of experience in memory validation, silicon validation, or platform bring-up. Strong understanding of: DDR4/DDR5/LPDDR5/LPDDR6 architectures HBM technologies Memory controllers PHY architectures Cache and coherency systems Experience with: Pre-silicon validation FPGA platforms Post-silicon bring-up Strong debugging skills using: Logic analyzers Protocol analyzers Performance counters Firmware traces Programming experience in Python, C/C++. Preferred Qualifications Prior experience validating: HBM2, HBM3, HBM3E, Advanced stacked-memory technologies Experience with AI accelerator, GPU, HPC, or server-class platforms. Familiarity with: NoCs, Chiplets, Coherent interconnects Knowledge of: Signal Integrity (SI) Power Integrity (PI) Thermal characterization Package-level interactions Experience defining validation methodologies for first-generation memory technologies. Minimum Qualifications: • Bachelor's degree in Science, Engineering, or related field and 6+ years of ASIC design, verification, validation, integration, or related work experience. OR Master's degree in Science, Engineering, or related field and 5+ years of ASIC design, verification, validation, integration, or related work experience.

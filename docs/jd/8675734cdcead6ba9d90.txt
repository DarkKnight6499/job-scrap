Work you’ll do: Architect and Design: assist in the design and development of enterprise-grade application architecture applications and platforms, with a focus on scaling for production. This includes defining the technical architecture, selecting appropriate technologies, and ensuring solutions are robust, scalable, and secure. Support modernization efforts across clients’ services capabilities working with a highly matrixed collaborative environment, working as a valued member of a cohesive architecture and development team. Assist in the design and deployment of applications using Cloud Native principles on a hyperscaler platform (AWS, Azure, GCP). Leverage a wide range of hyperscaler tools and services, including containers (Docker, Kubernetes), serverless functions, and managed databases. Should have experience in leveraging various GenAI tools to accelerate software development life cycle. Assist in the management and delivery components of client engagements that identify, design, and implement technology and creative business solutions for large companies. Responsibilities include, among others, managing small teams in the identification of business requirements, functional design, process design (including scenario design, flow mapping), prototyping, testing, training, defining support procedures. Assisting clients with application architecture design, assessment and optimization including definition, documentation and implementation of new application architectures Review and evaluation of key attributes of existing application architectures Assist in the recommendation of improvements to existing Application Architectures Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in computer science or engineering, or a related technical field with a strong foundation in development (Java or .NET) with early experience as a full stack developer. 4+ years’ experience as a Solution Architect, with a strong focus on application development and scaling solutions for production environments. 4+ years of experience with on-prem to AWS Cloud migration/integration which should include migration patterns (auth, middleware, data stores, event patterns, caching, telemetry); serverless patterns; data migration 4+ years providing hands-on technical assistance to the delivery team, reviewing designs and code for architectural fit, monitoring engineers and serving as the primary technical liaison between business stakeholders and engineering. 4+ years working on large-scale engagements as a Solution Architect in industry or in consulting. Experience working as part of a large collaborative team. Hands-on experience in Java, Python, or .NET. Experience managing small on and offshore teams. 1+ years’ experience designing and delivering production-grade AI solutions such as LLM applications, RAG systems, agentic workflows, intelligent automation or ML-enabled products. Hands-on software engineering capability in Python and/or a modern full-stack capability. Experience owning the end-to-end solution architecture which includes defining integration patterns, data flows, and technical design decisions across systems. Experience driving the technical delivery on-site (not remotely), translating business requirements into detailed technical designs, sequencing work across integration and data layers, ensuring the solution is built to spec from design to deployment. Deep understanding of AI/ML concepts, including experience with LLMs and their application in enterprise settings. Familiarity with various hyperscaler tools and services. Hyperscaler Architect certification is required (e.g., AWS Certified Solutions Architect, Azure Solutions Architect Expert, or GCP Professional Cloud Architect).

Job Description Ares Management is seeking a Senior Associate to join the Real Assets Financial Planning & Analysis (“FP&A”) team, with primary responsibility for the Real Estate business. The Real Assets FP&A team is part of the firm’s larger Finance organization and partners closely with Real Assets business leaders, fund accounting, corporate accounting, business operations and global FP&A colleagues in Los Angeles, London and other regional offices. The Senior Associate will play a hands-on role in the annual budget, monthly and quarterly forecast, and long-range planning processes for Real Assets. The candidate will build and maintain fund-level models and deliver clear, accurate reporting to senior management. The ideal candidate has a working understanding of real estate funds and management fee and incentive fee mechanics, and a strong aptitude for financial modeling, data and automation. This role requires a dynamic, detail-oriented professional who can operate with increasing independence in a fast-paced, growing environment. Primary Functions and Essential Responsibilities The Senior Associate will support the broader Real Assets FP&A team in partnering across the enterprise to provide accurate projections and insights to senior management.

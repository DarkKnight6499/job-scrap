Sr. Associate, Capital Markets & Analytics This position in Capital One’s Capital Markets and Analytics team offers the candidate a unique opportunity to be part of a diverse team, interacting with all facets of Treasury and Balance Sheet Management. The analyst will perform core Balance Sheet & Treasury Planning activities, including supporting Capital Markets Funding Plan and Treasury P&L modeling and reporting and driving process improvements in the team’s models and data infrastructure. The role requires exceptional decision-making, analytical, problem solving, leadership, and communication skills, and the candidate must be able to operate in a collaborative and dynamic environment that requires strong partnership, teamwork, and the ability to handle concurrent projects in addition to the primary responsibilities of the team. A passion for introducing new technologies and process innovation into current workflows will also be a strong consideration for this role. Responsibilities: Support modeling and reporting for the Capital Markets Funding Plan and Treasury P&L, as part of the monthly Corporate Outlook process and annual stress testing exercises Drive enhancements in the team’s modeling and data infrastructure to support continued integration efforts and upcoming enterprise wide initiatives Partner with cross-functional teams and drive data, process, and analytics automation initiatives Demonstrate ability to explore and quickly grasp new technologies to progress varied initiatives and help drive automation and advanced analytics Build and maintain relationships with diverse partner teams across the company, providing guidance to teams within Finance (Treasury, Balance Sheet Management, FP&A, etc.), lines of business, and operational staff functions Assist in evaluating new regulatory changes and their impact on processes Provide support to ensure all deliverables are completed on time and prepare updates for Senior Leadership Basic Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree or military experience At least 2 years of experience in Financial Services At least 1 year of experience in Python, SQL, Tableau, or a combination Preferred Qualifications: 2+ years’ experience working with Capital Markets products Experience with risk management, compliance, audit, finance, or a combination Strong communication and presentation skills Strong critical thinking with an ability to balance changing priorities Prior experience working effectively in a collaborative environment Experience with Google Suite At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position.

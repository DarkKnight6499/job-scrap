We are currently recruiting Engineering and Capital Projects Analysts whose skills and interests align with the below description: Engineering and Capital Projects Analyst This Analyst oversees technical and financial aspects of fixed assets and infrastructure, managing projects with a focus on detail and strategy. Advises government agencies on business processes, controls, and data management, providing actionable insights through detailed reports and presentations. Collaborates with clients to assess risks, deploy analytics, and drive informed infrastructure decisions.

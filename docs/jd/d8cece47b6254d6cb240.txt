We're fully remote, with team members across the U.S. About the Role We are seeking a detail-oriented and proactive Compliance Senior Associate to join our Compliance team. This role supports the Firm's regulatory compliance program by assisting with e-communication surveillance, due diligence, risk assessments, regulatory examinations, and governance processes. The ideal candidate has a strong foundation in AML/BSA, KYC, and OFAC compliance within financial services, and is comfortable working across a broad range of compliance functions in a fast-paced environment. Responsibilities Assist with the Firm's e-communication archiving reviews to monitor and ensure adherence to firm policies. Provide input on and help complete Due Diligence Questionnaires (DDQs) and Requests for Proposals (RFPs). Complete weekly, monthly, and quarterly compliance checklists in a timely and accurate manner. Help maintain and update the Firm's compliance manual to reflect current policies and regulatory requirements. Assist in the preparation of risk assessments, annual reviews, and vendor reviews. Support the Firm's responses and documentation requests related to regulatory inquiries, third-party examinations, and internal audits. Help maintain governance around relevant committees, including scheduling, preparing materials, and documenting meeting notes. Requirements 5+ years of experience in KYC, AML, or compliance operations within financial services or fintech. Deep knowledge of AML/BSA regulatory requirements, risk-based customer due diligence, and OFAC sanctions compliance. Strong ability to analyze situations for compliance and business risk, and to implement effective solutions to address and mitigate such risk. Excellent organizational skills with strong attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple deadlines simultaneously. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to work cross-functionally and interact with regulators, auditors, and internal stakeholders. Preferred: 2+ years of prior financial services compliance experience within a Broker-Dealer (B/D) or Registered Investment Adviser (RIA).

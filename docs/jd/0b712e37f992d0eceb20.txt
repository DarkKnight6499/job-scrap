About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a CIB Lending Operations Administration Specialist to join our Loan Trade Support team as a part of our Corporate Investment Banking Lending Operations (CIBLO) division. This role will support the CRE & Syndicated Lending Operations Servicing team by processing agent notices including advances, payments, repricing, fee applications and all maintenance activities. Learn more about our career areas and lines of business at wellsfargojobs.com . In this role, you will: Ensure the accuracy, completeness, and control integrity of data and documentation across the lending lifecycle; participate in loan administration initiatives, execution of day‑to‑day operational processes, data reconciliations and adherence to established risk and control standards Participate in low‑ to moderately complex initiatives and identify process improvement opportunities within Corporate & Investment Banking Lending Operations in middle office function Independently process transactions with moderate risk analysis; prepare documentation and service execution, process efficiency, post‑transaction execution, credit data accuracy and validation, including regulatory and documentation controls policy and process learning Process and analyze routine to moderately complex loan administration related transactions; conduct site inspections and portfolio reviews, prepare and support loan approval documentation Identify and recommend procedural improvements; review legal and credit documents and validate data using protocols and internal systems Perform quarterly regulatory data reviews, manual controls, monitor documentation exceptions and follow up with stakeholders Support portfolio monitoring controls and escalate issues as needed, while executing post‑transaction processing for corporate credit facilities Present recommendations to resolve low‑to moderately complex situations Exercise independent judgment to support medium‑risk deliverables; apply growing expertise in functional policies, procedures, and compliance requirements Collaborate with functional colleagues, internal partners, and stakeholders Required Qualifications: 2+ years of credit operations, loan operations, or deal experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, or education Desired Qualifications: Operations experience with the Commercial real estate product Commercial loan documentation, preparation or review experience Familiarity with Loan IQ Residential and commercial appraisal experience Sound analytical skills and the ability to interpret and research loan origination, legal, or real estate documents Knowledge and understanding of builder, construction, or renovations lending Knowledge and understanding of the Flood Disaster Protection Act (FDPA) Ability to navigate multiple computer systems, applications and utilize search tools to find information Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced collaborative environment, while managing competing priorities Intermediate Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, and Outlook) skills Advanced verbal and written communication skills Job Expectations: Ability to work in office at posted location for a minimum of three days per week Ability to work from 8:30-5:30pm Position is not eligible for Visa sponsorship or transfer or a Visa sponsorship Primary Posting Locations: 1525 W W T Harris Blvd, Charlotte, North Carolina 28262-8522 Posting End Date: 18 Oct 2026 *Job posting may come down early due to volume of applicants.

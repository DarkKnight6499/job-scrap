Join Mizuho as a Treasury Senior Solutions Architect! The Treasury Senior Solutions Architect is responsible for defining, managing, and delivering strategic Treasury and Cash Management capabilities across the bank's Treasury ecosystem. This role acts as the primary liaison between Treasury Operations, Front Office, Finance, Risk, and Technology teams to ensure Treasury platforms support evolving business, regulatory, liquidity, funding, and cash management requirements. The ideal candidate combines deep Treasury domain expertise with strong technical acumen and product management skills. The role owns the Treasury technology roadmap, backlog prioritization, business requirements, product lifecycle management, and end-to-end delivery of Treasury initiatives including Liquidity Management, Cash Positioning, Funding, Intraday Liquidity, Nostro Management, Reconciliation, Regulatory Reporting, and Open Banking integrations. Manage vendor relationships and product evaluations. Strategy & Ownership Define and maintain the strategic roadmap for Treasury platforms and services.

JPMorgan Chase
Location: New Rochelle, NY, United States

Make a visible impact on how we protect customers and support strong branch practices. In this role, you will bring an independent, objective perspective to branch activities and help promote consistent adherence to policies and procedures. You will work closely with branch and market leaders, communicate clearly, and produce well-supported documentation that drives action. If you enjoy field work, variety, and accountability, this role offers meaningful exposure and growth. An Analyst plays a critical role in the Quality Assurance function, conducting independent testing in branches to ensure adherence to established policies and procedures. This position requires significant travel, which varies based on geographic location and branch assignments. Branch Review Analysts are expected to support multiple states and should anticipate out-of-town travel. On average, overnight travel for a Branch Review Analyst ranges from 40% to 50%, and may occasionally reach up to 60% depending on business needs. Additionally, extended working hours are required while conducting branch reviews in the field.

The Analyst, Working Capital & Trade Finance (WCTF) role is a position focused on building the analytical, execution, risk management, and client-support capabilities needed to contribute across origination, structuring, implementation, and transaction monitoring. The Analyst supports senior team members in evaluating and executing new and existing WCTF transactions while developing a strong understanding of the Bank's working capital solutions, internal processes, and control framework. The role requires accuracy, accountability, intellectual curiosity, and effective collaboration in a fast-paced environment. Analysts are expected to deliver high-quality work with appropriate guidance, manage assigned responsibilities and competing priorities, raise questions and issues promptly, and assume greater ownership as their product knowledge, judgment, and execution capabilities develop. Responsibilities: Transaction Analysis, Structuring & Execution Support the analysis, structuring, approval, implementation, and closing of new and existing WCTF transactions under the guidance of senior team members. Prepare accurate financial analyses, working capital assessments, transaction economics, ROE/RORA simulations, pricing and obligor limit analyses, deal sheets, and screening materials. Assess transaction risks, structural considerations, and key assumptions; identify inconsistencies and escalate questions with supporting analysis. Prepare term sheets, approval materials, presentations, implementation submissions, documentation inputs, and closing deliverables. Coordinate assigned workstreams, timelines, and follow-ups across internal stakeholders, promptly escalating risks, delays, or unresolved items. Origination & Client Support Support client pitches, product proposals, peer analyses, and other marketing materials in partnership with senior Originators and Structurers. Research prospective clients, industries, and market developments to evaluate the applicability of WCTF solutions and inform transaction analysis. Participate in internal and external calls, prepare meeting materials and follow-up items, and support timely responses to Relationship Manager and client inquiries. Communicate pipeline and transaction updates for assigned opportunities clearly and accurately. Develop an understanding of client objectives, constraints, and transaction requirements while operating within the Bank's risk appetite. Risk Management, Controls & Professional Accountability Perform responsibilities in accordance with applicable credit policies, regulatory requirements, KYC standards, internal controls, and the Bank's risk appetite. Support monitoring and reporting for assigned WCTF assets, including performance, utilization, obligor exposure, and transaction requirements; escalate deterioration, operational issues, or control concerns promptly. Apply disciplined quality control to all work products, ensuring information is accurate, complete, consistent, and appropriately supported. Identify and escalate potential credit, operational, compliance, information security, data integrity, or conduct issues and support timely remediation. Demonstrate accountability, reliability, sound judgment, and clear communication while managing multiple assignments and deadlines. Collaboration, Development & Process Improvement Collaborate effectively across WCTF, the broader Working Capital organization, and other functions throughout the Bank. Build product, credit, industry, market, legal documentation, systems, and execution knowledge and apply that learning to assigned responsibilities. Incorporate feedback, address knowledge gaps through training and self-study, and progressively assume greater ownership of assigned work. Contribute to process improvement, standardization, automation, artificial intelligence, and special projects that enhance efficiency, reporting quality, or risk management. Share knowledge and best practices to support team effectiveness and consistent execution. Qualification s: Bachelor's degree from a four-year accredited institution, preferably with coursework in finance, accounting, economics, business, or a related field. Foundational financial analysis and modeling skills, with proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint. Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities, with the ability to learn new products, industries, systems, and transaction structures. Clear and professional written and verbal communication skills. Excellent organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple assignments, follow through on commitments, and meet deadlines with appropriate oversight. High attention to detail, commitment to data accuracy, and awareness of risk and control responsibilities. Collaborative mindset, intellectual curiosity, and willingness to seek guidance, incorporate feedback, and support team priorities. Prior internship or experience in banking, working capital, trade finance, structured finance, credit, corporate finance, or a related field is beneficial but not required.

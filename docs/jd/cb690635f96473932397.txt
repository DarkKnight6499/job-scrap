Remote work beyond this arrangement and alternative office locations are not available. * Role Summary The Manager will be on the Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) team helping to support the Investments business unit and Product. This will include financial reporting, strategic planning, forecasting, financial modeling, and partnering with the business. The leader will be responsible for analyzing the financials of the business unit to identify and communicate areas of financial risk and/or opportunity. They will possess a mix of complex business analysis and problem-solving skills, strong verbal and written communication skills, and knowledge/interest in asset management and global trends. Responsibilities Updates/refines short-term and long-range forecasts and methodologies using multiple inputs and assumptions to reflect changes in business strategies using deep knowledge of the business unit gained through business partnerships. Supports budgeting, quarterly forecasting, and long-range planning processes. Performs monthly variance analysis (actuals vs. budget/forecast/prior periods) and clearly communicates key drivers. Supports month-end close activities, ensuring accuracy and completeness of financial results. Supports business unit decision making by leveraging advanced Excel modeling skills. Conducts trend analysis on financial data including AUM, revenue and product related costs. Qualifications Required: Bachelor's degree and 5+ years of relevant work experience Preferred: Confidence and ability to interact with senior leadership Excellent verbal and written communication skills Ability to lead cross-functional initiatives, providing guidance and support to analysts Advanced Power Point and Excel skills High proficiency in analyzing and synthesizing complex financial data into meaningful executive summary statements Responsive, team-oriented approach with excellent client-service skills for effective relations with internal clients Experience creating data visualizations, storyboards, or dashboards in Power BI a plus Experience with financial planning systems (i.e. Oracle, SmartView) Knowledge of accounting principles FINRA Requirements FINRA licenses are not required and will not be supported for this role.

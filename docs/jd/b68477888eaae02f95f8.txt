Associate, Finance - Treasury Do you want to leverage your analytical skills and be part of an exciting, fast-paced environment surrounded by smart, inclusive, and energetic individuals? Come and join our Capital One Travel Treasury team! As an Associate, Treasury, you will be the operational engine of our liquidity and cash management processes. You will execute critical daily operations, manage transactional workflows, and ensure robust risk controls are maintained through proper Segregation of Duties (SOD). This role is highly collaborative, requiring you to serve as a point of contact for cross-divisional teams while performing the essential financial analysis that drives senior leadership's decision-making. If you thrive on accuracy, problem-solving, and continuous improvement, this is the role for you. What You’ll Do (Responsibilities): Daily Cash Operations : Perform daily cash positioning, bank reconciliations, funding execution support, rewards funding calculations, bank account administration, and reporting inputs. Transactional Workflows: Handle first-level operational tasks and transactional workflows, including preparing daily cash data, supporting reconciliations, maintaining clear documentation, and actively tracking exceptions. Financial Analysis: Perform detailed financial analysis and reporting to directly support the Senior Manager’s strategic decision-making and business objectives. Risk Controls & Compliance: Execute daily operations flawlessly while ensuring proper Segregation of Duties (SOD) is maintained to support a strong internal control and risk environment. Cross-Functional Support: Serve as Point of Contact (POC) for Treasury support, partnering effectively with cross-divisional internal teams to ensure seamless communication and execution. Basic Qualifications: Bachelor’s Degree or military experience At least 1 years of experience in Financial Analysis Preferred Qualifications: Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) license or Certified Public Accountant (CPA) license 3+ years’ experience in financial analysis 3+ years’ experience with financial modeling 2+ years’ experience in strategic analysis & planning At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position.

The Global Credit Finance team oversees a portfolio of 10 different products with ~$100B of notional commitments. Its 150+ member team operates in the US, EMEA and APAC with offices in Boston, Irvine, London, Munich, Gdansk, Luxembourg, and Bangalore. Global Credit Finance’s mission is twofold: to attract and retain clients of State Street using the firm’s balance sheet; and to add income and diversification to the company’s portfolio of on and off-balance sheet investments. Our products include Private Markets – Subscription Finance; Private Credit; Real Money Funds-Liquidity and Leverage: CLOs; Municipal Finance; Commercial Real Estate, Leveraged Loans; Corporates, and Stable Value Wrap. Role Overview A Senior Associate on the Business Management team will be responsible for supporting the effective execution of Global Credit Finance (GCF) priorities by partnering with senior leadership and teams across the division on business management, organizational effectiveness, client strategy, data governance, and key initiatives. Reporting to senior GCF leadership, this role will help translate strategic priorities into execution, while supporting strong governance, communication, and engagement across the organization. The role will also contribute to client-focused initiatives and efforts to strengthen data quality, ownership, and governance practices across the business. This role blends elements of business enablement, governance coordination, client strategy, data management, and administrative oversight, with a focus on supporting executive decision-making, coordinating cross-functional initiatives, and driving day-to-day execution across the organization.

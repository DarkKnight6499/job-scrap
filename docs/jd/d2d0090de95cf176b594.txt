Responsibilities PJT Camberview, PJT Partners’ industry leading shareholder advisory business, provides investor-led advice to public company boards and management teams around the globe on shareholder engagement, strategic investor relations, activism and contested situations, ESG and sustainability and other complex corporate governance matters. PJT Camberview seeks an experienced and highly motivated Associate to join our advisory team. The Associate should have experience in buy-side or sell-side equity research and/or an advisory role providing strategic advice to public companies. This role is not sector-specific, and candidates will be expected to operate within a generalist model. The successful candidate will demonstrate an ability to develop and translate a deep understanding of current issues related to active investment management, and activism into actionable advice for clients. Responsibilities of the Associate include, but are not limited to, the following: Execution of Client Engagements Conduct analyses of fundamentals, investor challenges and investor messaging opportunities and develop creative solutions for clients that address misperceptions and other issues that may adversely impact valuation Assist in drafting a compelling investment thesis and supporting narrative to help clients retain current shareholders and appeal to prospective shareholders Build financial models and conduct detailed financial and valuation analyses Perform deep dives and benchmarking on companies and industries Support all aspects of day-to-day client engagements Prepare sophisticated and effective management/investor presentations, press releases, scripts and other deliverables relating to a range of fundamental and governance issues Demonstrate the capacity to work directly with clients and prospective clients, successfully contributing to both relationships Support Senior Leaders in Business Development Assist in identifying and pursuing new business opportunities Prepare senior leaders for pitch meetings with potential clients Assist in creation of company-specific pitch materials highlighting the most relevant services for that particular prospect Support the ongoing development of marketing materials Firm Leadership Serve as a leader and mentor to Analysts, helping to develop the next generation of Associates Assist in identifying and recruiting new team members Qualifications We seek to hire individuals who are highly motivated and intelligent. Successful candidates will possess the following: 4+ years of work experience in a buy-side or sell-side equity research role and/or an advisory role providing strategic advice to public companies Bachelor's Degree, with a strong academic record Experience performing in-depth fundamental research Strong analytical skills Ability to communicate effectively in presentations and express complex strategies in an organized and articulate manner Ability to comfortably interact with senior-level executives and clients Effective time management skills and ability to meet deadlines Collaborative and team-oriented with sound judgment and strong interpersonal skills Excellent verbal and written communication skills Demonstrated leadership skills Highly organized, detail-oriented and proactive Strong Excel and PowerPoint skills Expected annualized base salary of $200,000 to $250,000.

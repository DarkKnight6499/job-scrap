Job Description Summary Job Description Commercial Finance Analyst – HDGT, Americas Role Summary As the Commercial Finance Analyst for the HDGT (Heavy Duty Gas Turbine) segment in the Americas, you will play a critical role in driving profitable growth and financial governance across the Inquiry-to-Order (ITO) process. You will partner with Commercial, Sales, and Finance leadership to translate complex deal strategies into robust financial summaries, ensuring risk mitigation, cost accuracy, and optimal cash flow. Key Responsibilities: Strategic Deal Support: Drive commercial discipline by managing accurate cost rollups and sensitivity analyses and strict compliance with delegation of authority approval policies. Proposal Optimization: Influence bid strategy by evaluating key financial drivers—including cash forecasts, trade finance, FX strategy and tax implications—to optimize cash flow, title transfer, and revenue recognition. Process Governance and FP&A: Lead the consolidation and reporting of financial data, support operation planning and forecasting. Participate in the operating rhythm dynamics within the business. Cross-Functional Partnership: Act as a key liaison between Sales, Pricing, and Treasury teams to negotiate financial triggers and ensure effective risk management, including hedging, trade finance and payment security. Continuous Improvement: Champion a LEAN culture by identifying opportunities to streamline processes, improve data accuracy, and enhance the efficiency of financial workflows. Compliance & Reporting: Provide support for internal and external audits related to project accounting, process compliance, and business forecasting. Required Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, or an equivalent field. Demonstrated experience in Finance and/or Operations within a corporate environment. Proficiency in financial systems, data analysis tools, and ERP platforms. Desired Skills & Characteristics: Industry Expertise: Prior experience in the energy sector or with large-scale industrial companies is highly preferred. Familiarity with long-cycle project accounting or large capital equipment business models is a significant advantage. Accounting Acumen: Solid understanding of US GAAP and statutory accounting principles, with the ability to bridge accounting requirements with ERP solutions. Analytical Rigor: Ability to synthesize complex data sets into actionable insights and high-quality financial reporting. Strategic Collaboration: Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to build effective partnerships across diverse commercial and support functions.

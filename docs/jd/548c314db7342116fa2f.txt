Line of Business: TD Wealth Job Description: The Associate Institutional Relationship Manager (AIRM) is a high touch point of client contact accountable to serve the unique and complex needs of clients in the Institutional segment. The Associate Institutional Relationship Manager partners with other Institutional Relationship Managers (IRM), Institutional Investment Advisors, and Captive Specialty Practice subject matter experts (SME) to grow the Institutional business through supporting business development efforts, responding Request-For-Proposals (RFP), and interfacing with OneTD partners. The Associate Institutional Relationship Manager is also responsible for retaining and growing assigned client relationships and is accountable for exceeding client's expectation for service. The AIRM is at the center of the Institutional model providing day-to-day account management and oversight, delivering excellent client service, supporting account administration, and developing materials with Specialty Practice SME and IRMs for OneTD partners and centers of influence.

Through an investor-first approach, the firm brings to clients the best of both worlds: specialist expertise from seven specialist investment managers offering solutions across every major asset class, backed by the strength, stability, and global presence of BNY. We’re seeking a future team member for the role of Senior Specialist, Client Portfolio Manager to join the BNY Investments Distribution team. This role is located in Boston or New York City. In this role, you’ll make an impact in the following ways: Synthesize insights from investment sources - including portfolio manager discussions, investment meetings, and data platforms - to develop and deliver unified, consistent fixed income product talking points, materials and portfolio narratives that support client conversations and drive the sales process within the intermediary channel. Act as the first point of contact for fixed income product-related questions—partnering closely with sales—often joining key meetings to clarify product positioning, performance drivers, and competitive differentiators. Provide comprehensive coverage across mutual funds, ETFs, and SMAs, ensuring accurate, timely insights on product. Conduct ongoing competitor and strategic landscape analysis and be proficient in positioning our funds against peer offerings—articulating relative strengths, trade-offs, and use cases across categories, share classes, and market conditions. Liaise with internal stakeholders (e.g., marketing, compliance, legal, product management) to align messaging and create external content. Partner with portfolio managers and client portfolio managers to develop a deep understanding of investment strategies, portfolio construction, and decision-making, while maintaining regular dialogue to gather updates ( strategy changes, positioning, performance, flows, outlook) and distribute actionable summaries across the intermediary sales channel. Play a key role in planning and execution of new product or vehicle launches Develop thought leadership content, including white papers and platform publications, that communicates investment perspectives and market insights. To be successful in this role, we’re seeking the following: Bachelor’s degree required; advanced degree preferred. 2–5 years of experience in investment or related analytical roles. Strong analytical and strategic thinking skills for building investment strategies. Excellent communication skills for explaining technical concepts clearly. Proficiency with financial analysis tools and investment management software.

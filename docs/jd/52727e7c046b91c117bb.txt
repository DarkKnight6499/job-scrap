Title: Private Assets Product Manager - Sr. Associate Department: Corporate and Product Strategy Location: Charlotte, NC Primary Responsibilities The Private Assets Product Management role is the internal representative of the investment team, responsible for defining, developing, implement and maintaining investment products, through the entire lifecycle. The candidate will support Barings’ array of Private Assets, including Real Estate Equity, Real Estate Debt, Private Equity Solutions, Private Credit and Infrastructure Debt and Equity. The candidate will support the global team and gain exposure to multiple fund types and structures. Key areas of focus include: Product Launch · Project manage and socialize fund launches with all relevant stakeholders across Barings · Assist with the structuring, documentation and financial modelling of new and existing funds · Be subject matter expert of fund commercial terms and mechanics (liquidity, ESG guidelines, performance fees, etc.) · Maintenance of technical expertise and understanding of evolving regulatory framework, proactively considering efficient responses to change requirements · Shepherd product launches through Barings governance process · Build strong relationships with and work alongside firm’s Client Portfolio Managers, Legal, Compliance, Sales, Operations, Investment, Finance and Risk teams, as well as external legal counsel, fund administrators, service providers and investors to ensure effective co-ordination across wide range of stakeholders Product Maintenance · Support product level modifications and activities · Establish and implement consistent internal processes Qualifications · CPA, CAIA, CFA or other equivalent qualification preferred · Practical experience of private assets investment strategies · Highly organized, numerate with strong attention to detail and excellent project management skills · Advanced Excel skills · Clear communicator, both verbally and written for both internal and external audiences or stakeholders #LI-ME1 Requisite Skills Additional Skills Barings is an Equal Employment Opportunity employer; Minority/Female/Age/Sexual Orientation/Gender Identity/Individual with Disability/Protected Veteran.

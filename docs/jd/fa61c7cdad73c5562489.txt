Role Purpose: The Associate Senior Solutions Architect will translate the vision for reimagined corporate services, personalized workspaces, and mobile experiences into secure, scalable, integrated, and intuitive solution architectures for an AI-enabled and increasingly agentic future. The role will lead architecture from discovery through delivery, turning business and user needs into coherent designs across applications, data, integration, AI, automation, infrastructure, and networks. It will guide key technical decisions, embed enterprise standards and responsible AI principles, and strengthen architecture practices to ensure solutions are resilient, reusable, responsible, maintainable, and deliver lasting value to Corporate users across the World Bank Group. Duties and accountabilities: • Solution Architecture and Technology Evaluation: Lead end-to-end architectures for personalized digital workspaces, mobile apps, and corporate digital services, translating business, user, and product needs into secure, scalable, integrated solutions. Evaluate technology options, dependencies, risks, trade-offs, nonfunctional requirements, and build-versus-buy decisions. • Digital Experience and Mobile Solutions: Architect intuitive, responsive, accessible, and personalized experiences across web and mobile, addressing iOS native and hybrid approaches, app distribution, identity, personalization, notifications, integration, device capabilities including offline and AI, performance, and consistent cross-channel experiences. Balance user experience with reuse, maintainability, cost, and sustainability. • Responsible AI and Intelligent Automation: Architect secure and governed AI, agentic, data, and automation capabilities that enable personalized, contextual, and proactive services. Ensure appropriate data, integration, identity, security, monitoring, governance, and human oversight. • Technical Leadership and Architecture Assurance: Guide multidisciplinary teams from prototype through deployment, resolve architectural issues and dependencies, and ensure delivery aligns with approved architectures and enterprise standards. Promote reusable patterns, modern engineering practices, and effective management of exceptions and technical debt. • Stakeholder Alignment and Value Realization: Work across business, product, design, engineering, security, and technology teams to align architectural decisions and trade-offs, and ensure solutions deliver measurable improvements in user experience, service delivery, adoption, performance, risk, and cost. • Competence and Practice: Coach and develop peers and junior staff, strengthen cross-team collaboration, and contribute to high-performing global teams through knowledge-sharing, clear accountability, and continuous improvement of solution architecture practices.

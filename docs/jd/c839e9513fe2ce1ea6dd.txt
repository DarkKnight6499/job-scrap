Job Summary This role supports the growth and profitability of the Employee Benefits business by analyzing and evaluating risk for new and renewing groups. The position applies underwriting guidelines to validate rates, plan designs, and contract provisions while ensuring accurate and timely case implementation. Working closely with Underwriting, Contracts, Premium Administration, and Field partners, the role helps resolve implementation issues and deliver positive customer experience. Success in this role requires strong analytical skills, sound judgment, effective communication, and the ability to manage multiple priorities. Key Responsibilities Analyze and evaluate new business, sold cases, and renewal risks while applying underwriting guidelines and contract provisions. Support business growth and profitability by validating rates, plan designs, and insurance risk assessments. Partner with Underwriting, Contracts, Premium Administration, and Field teams to ensure accurate and timely implementation of cases. Resolve implementation and service issues through effective communication, problem-solving, and cross-functional collaboration. Manage multiple priorities, identify process improvements, and provide subject matter expertise, training support, and mentorship to team members. Skills and Background You’ll Need Education: High School Diploma or equivalent required Experience: Minimum of 1 year of insurance experience, preferably in disability benefits, with an understanding of plan design and benefit structures. Experience providing customer service in a high-volume and/or complex work environment. Proficient in Microsoft Excel, including data extraction, formatting, and basic formula functions. Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to explain information clearly and effectively. Excellent organizational and time management skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities. Ability to build and maintain strong working relationships with Underwriting, Contracts, Premium Administration, and Field partners. Demonstrated problem-solving skills with strong attention to detail and accuracy. Ability to work independently while also contributing as a collaborative and supportive team member. Key Behaviors of a Successful Candidate Customer Focus: Understands and anticipates both internal and external customer needs and develops customer-inspired solutions that exceed expectations. Winning Together: Actively engages colleagues to achieve shared outcomes by developing trust, inviting diverse perspectives and pushing to bring the best ideas forward. Improvement Mindset: Continually seeks new ways to create business/ customer value by identifying and implementing opportunities for improved efficiency, effectiveness and innovation.

Join us and transform the lives of patients while transforming your career. Associate Strategic Planning & Operations What you will do Let’s do this. Let’s change the world. The Associate Strategic Planning and Operations (SP&O) is responsible for supporting Global Development Business Operations and working closely with other SP&O team members, key stakeholders, and various levels of management. Business Process Support for the Medical Material Review Process, Development Council and other governance to ensure smooth operations, compliance and overall process health. Various on-going business support to include but are not limited to: Project management assistance which may include chartering, facilitation, presenting, and deliverable development within the project timelines. Organization, scheduling and operational excellence for governance and SPO Operations. Global Development Contracts Subject Matter Expert and Support. TA Budget management assistance. SharePoint design/maintenance. Administrative support, Calendaring, meeting support and planning. Develop and/or manage department documents (e.g. onboarding guides, etc.). What we expect of you We are all different, yet we all use our unique contributions to serve patients. The highly competent professional we seek is a go getter with these qualifications. Basic Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree OR Associate’s degree and 4 years of project/business management experience OR High school diploma / GED and 6 years of project/business management experience Preferred Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree or 4 years of strong project/business management experience. Reliable/dependable team player with the ability to work independently with minimal direction. Excellent organizational skills and superior attention to detail. Strong ability to multi-task and prioritize workload. Strong analytical skills and the ability to think strategically. Proficient knowledge of Microsoft Outlook, Excel, PowerPoint, Word and SharePoint. Experience with MS Project, Smartsheet and database design is helpful. Project a positive, ‘can do’ attitude and eagerly contribute to all initiatives as needed and requested.

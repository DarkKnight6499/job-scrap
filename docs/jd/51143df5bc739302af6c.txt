A Career that Empowers You to Build Your Future Lennar is seeking a detail oriented Financial Analyst who will be supporting the SG&A and Corp G&A department. The Financial Analyst will be tasked with ensuring the accuracy of financials and delivering reporting to various stakeholders within Lennar. This position will also support team members with financial analysis and reporting to leadership and Corporate department teams. Your Responsibilities on the Team Provide financial insights and documentation to Lennar stakeholders to support data-driven decision-making for all SG&A and Corp G&A related expenses. Support the monthly forecast process, expense reconciliation, efficiencies initiatives, and analysis. EPM / Hyperion / Essbase / E1 / Dashboard reconciliations. Preparation and compilation of the monthly executive summary reporting. Quarterly financial and operational reporting. Review financial transactions to ensure accuracy Work cross-functionally with different divisions and departments identify risk and opportunities while also ensuring forecast accuracy. Detailed analysis of department processes to identify efficiency and automation opportunities Your Toolbox Bachelor’s degree in finance/accounting, or a related field preferred. A minimum of 2+ years of relevant finance or accounting experience or education is required. Must have financial data analytical skills and experience in preparing high level reports from raw data. Experience with spreadsheet modeling, data visualization, account reconciliation, and analysis. Proficiency required in Excel (Macros, Pivot Tables, Graph & Advanced Formulas) Desired experience using AI resources for process automation Ability to read, research, analyze and interpret general accounting data.

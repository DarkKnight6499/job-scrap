GENERAL FUNCTION: Performs a wide range of duties related to the review of collateral, which includes assisting in the process before and after a loan funds. From UCC filings, amendments, and terminations to flood searches and collateral assignments of life insurance policies. Responsible and accountable for risk by openly exchanging ideas and opinions, elevating concerns, and personally following policies and procedures as defined. Accountable for always doing the right thing for customers and colleagues, and ensures that actions and behaviors drive a positive customer experience while operating within the Bank’s risk appetite. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES: · Responsible for ensuring all loans are properly collateralized in accordance with the collateral structure outlined by the credit team. · Create and execute processes to drive 100% collateral accuracy (we can't have a single funded loan without perfected collateral). · Pull and interpret Lexis Nexis results on individuals and business entities. · Run lien searches as well as title searches to ensure awareness of all existing liens and potential cross-collateralization. · Determine flood zone for commercial real estate property. · File various types of UCC liens using LienSolutions. · Assign UCC-1 lien filings over to the proper bank partner post-funding. · Monitor all filings for our existing portfolio to make sure our lien positions are never compromised. · Execute collateral assignments of life insurance policies in a timely fashion. · Draft subordination agreements from templates. · Develop outstanding communication and trust with our team of closing officers and operations specialists. · Collaborate with our product and engineering teams to build clean, lightweight collateral management functionality into our loan origination software. · Manage expected service levels related to collateral decision and condition clearing. SUPERVISORY RESPONSIBILITIES: None. MINIMUM KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS & ABILITIES REQUIRED: · High degree of attention to detail. · Demonstrated leadership skills. · Proven computer skills. · Demonstrated organizational skills. · Demonstrated teamwork and customer service skills.

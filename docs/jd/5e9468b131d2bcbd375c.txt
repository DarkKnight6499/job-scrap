Job Overview: As an Associate Analyst within our Client Compensation Processing Operations team, you will play a critical role in delivering expert solutions to middle and back-office operational inquiries and expediting advisor commission payments. You will have the opportunity to become a subject matter expert (SME) in the set-up of payout rules, products and sponsors, billing of fees and expenses, research and payout of advisor compensation, supporting financial advisors and ensuring accurate and timely payments. In this role, you will be responsible for activities related to the management and administration of financial advisors' commissions payments which will include working with internal stakeholders and fund companies to resolve complex commission problems and processing commission payments. Being flexible and adaptable to competing priorities will be key success factors for this position. Responsibilities: Develop expertise in Client Compensation Operations processing and procedures Serve as dedicated liaison, taking ownership of Client Compensation escalations Process transactions with precision and accuracy, ensuring accurate and timely payments flow to financial advisors and their firms Own and supervise firm-wide, multi-department projects to build or enhance experience workflows related to the department Proactively review audit controls to mitigate risk, remove redundant requirements, and increase transparency with audit partners Proactively assist with large-scale projects and transformation efforts Plan and champion initiatives that enhance the advisor experience and positively impact the broader LPL organization What are we looking for?

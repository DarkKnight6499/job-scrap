Position Overview: This role is within the Enterprise Operations &Technology Risk Management team, which provides comprehensive risk management support with a focus on Identity and Access Management (IAM). We work closely with all three lines of defense, and business partners on risk reduction activities. You will leverage your expertise to evaluate risks, assess adequacy of controls, provide audit support, and continually look for ways to streamline processes, including leveraging AI capabilities, within IAM and other EO&T processes as needed. What’s more, you will use your risk management experience to support leadership in achieving our top priorities of risk reduction, issue remediation, driving sustainability, and proactively identifying control/process gaps. Our Impact: · Partner with our IT Stakeholders to understand and manage risks and controls associated with IAM processes, serving as a liaison for 1LOD. · Provide transparency of risk exposures through implementing sound reporting for risk-based decision making. · Perform gap assessments, risk assessments, and advise on effective remediation, driving issues from inception to closure. Your Impact: In this role, you will be expected to: · Understand and examine documented identity and access management processes and the associated risks & controls · Provide risk insight for various IAM principles (e.g., user access lifecycle, access review and recertifications, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), Separation of Duties (SoD), Just-in-Time (JIT) access, user authentication/authorization, etc) · Ensure timely development of remediation action plans and support issue lifecycle management from inception to closure · Coordinate audits and exams, acting as a liaison between 1LOD and oversight groups · Provide risk and control guidance across other EO&T processes, as needed Qualifications: · 2-4 years of risk, control, compliance, or operational risk experience. Experience in Internal Audit, Information Technology, or Identity and Access Management is preferred · Bachelor’s degree in Management Information Systems or Business Administration, with an emphasis in Technology, Computer Science, Accounting Information Systems, related field, or equivalent overall experience · Ability to effectively collaborate with upstream and downstream risk and controls stakeholders and influence outcomes · Understanding of NIST, SDLC, COBIT, ITIL, ISO, IT General Controls is preferred · Understanding of IAM solutions using Sailpoint · Certification in, or active pursuit of, one or more of the following is preferred: CISA, CRISC, CISM, CISSP, or other relevant credentials.

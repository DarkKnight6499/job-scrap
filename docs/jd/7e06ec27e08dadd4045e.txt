Summary/Function:
Administration of shareholder voting process, voting request escalation and record retention.  Administration of shareholder votes for managed accounts. 
Qualifications:
•    High school diploma or three years related office experience
•    Proficient use of computer programs; experience with Microsoft Excel a necessity
•    Attention to detail and accuracy
•    Ability to prioritize and multi-task
•    Ability to work under pressure
•    Ability to work without supervision yet work as part of a team
•    Ability to interact with employees and outside vendors in a professional and personable manner
•    Ability and willingness to follow policies and procedures
•    Commitment to maintain client confidentiality and data security
•    Communicate in a clear and service-oriented manner; use appropriate, professional language and grammar to effectively exchange ideas and information
•    Ability to proactively work with both external and internal clients; relate with others in a professional manner to accomplish work responsibilities and objectives
•    Ability to maintain regular, reliable, in-person attendance on the job is a necessary and essential function
Duties:
•    Daily voting maintenance which includes voting, adding, and deleting new and closed accounts and keeping records of all ballots received as well as supervision of the prospectus mailings on all mutual fund purchases.
•    Provide backup for Managed Assets Data Reconciliation Clerk as needed. 
•    Perform any other duties as assigned.

Position Summary Financial Planning & Analysis is responsible for providing senior management with analysis and insight into Mizuho America's financial results. With responsibility for the region’s management reporting and analytics, FPA work closely with a variety of corporate and business functions to provide insights and inform decision making for the firm’s executive leadership. This role will focus on advancing Workforce planning capabilities, which will allow us to advance our analytics and to transform the way we plan for our workforce in forecasting and budget; this role will have the opportunity to partner closely with HR as well as our business leaders (front office and corporates) Within FP&A, this position will work closely with a variety of seasoned professionals across teams and functions to create management reporting & analysis for senior executives to aid in understanding overall performance, expense trends, resource consumption, and how these parameters inform business strategy. Key skills include attention to detail, focus on accuracy, intellectual curiosity, and experience with financial data and business writing. Experience with presentation development is preferred. Key Skills & Qualifications Minimum 3-5 years of specific roles in business planning, budgeting and/or financial analysis required.

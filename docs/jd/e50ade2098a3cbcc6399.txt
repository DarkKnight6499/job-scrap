We are looking for a Senior Financial Analyst for our Cloud Finance division. The position is required to balance strategic analysis and insight generation while managing the methods we use to close, forecast, and analyze our expenses. This role will involve partnering with teams across the organization to drive progress towards scalable planning, reporting, and analytics. This is a highly visible role that will involve regular interaction with various business partners: engineering, procurement, finance, and other supporting teams across NVIDIA. A successful candidate will have shown experience in past roles supporting decision making in constantly evolving environments. The candidate must have the ability to incorporate new approaches and methodologies while dealing with complex data.

Why Join the Technician Apprentice Program? Get paid while you learn Tuition and program costs fully covered by GlobalFoundries Pay increases up to three times per year as you progress through the program Earn approximately 23 college credits toward an Associate Degree in General Engineering Gain hands-on experience with cutting-edge semiconductor manufacturing equipment Receive mentorship and coaching from experienced technicians Build a long-term career with clear advancement opportunities Continue your education through GlobalFoundries' Tuition Reimbursement Program What You'll Do As a Technician Apprentice, you'll learn how to maintain and troubleshoot the advanced equipment used to manufacture semiconductor chips that power everything from smartphones and vehicles to medical devices and communications technology. Key Responsibilities Learn semiconductor equipment maintenance and troubleshooting Perform preventive and first-call maintenance activities Complete technical coursework through Vermont State University Work alongside skilled technicians and engineers Follow quality, safety, and operational procedures Support continuous improvement and equipment reliability initiatives Work Schedule One of the biggest benefits of working at GlobalFoundries is our compressed work schedule, which provides full-time pay and benefits while offering more days off than a traditional Monday-Friday job. Day Shift 6:45AM - 7:15 PM Employees work a compressed 12-hour schedule that provides multiple days off throughout the month. Many employees appreciate having weekdays available for appointments, family commitments, schoolwork, or simply more time to enjoy life outside of work.

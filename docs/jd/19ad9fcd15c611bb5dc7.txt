About you and this role Dow has an exciting and challenging opportunity for Associate Analytical Technician role located in Las Pinas, Philippines. This role supports manufacturing operations by conducting laboratory analyses, as needed, which provide critical data. Prepares samples, performs preventive maintenance of laboratory instrumentation and uses data systems to acquire data. Reports results to the client. Requires established basic laboratory skills to perform a range of routine lab activities using existing standard operating procedures and/or analytical/ scientific methods. Responsibilities Trains to perform routine and non-routine analyses under direction of senior team member until proficiency is demonstrated. Prepares lab reagents and standards used for testing. Monitors lab chemicals and consumables inventory. Collects samples according to the Sample Plan. Performs basic calibration and preventative maintenance of analytical systems equipment. Provides evaluation of validity of data for routine analyses and takes appropriate action. Monitors analytical systems equipment and escalates problems as appropriate. Documents, communicates and archives analytical results and data. Reviews and revises lab test procedures as needed. Follows EH&S and Operating Discipline Management System (ODMS) procedures and practices for work tasks. Suggests improvements to lab work processes and tools. Escalates non-routine requests to appropriate team member for follow-up. Qualifications Graduate of BS Chemistry, BS Chemical Engineering or equivalent disciplines Valid PRC registration/license as Chemist or Chemical Technician 1-3 years of related working experience Can work on shift Can work alone with minimal supervision Preferred Skills Communication Skills: Ability to convey information clearly and effectively. Collaborating and Leading at all levels: Working well with others and contributing to achieve common goals. Be able to lead as necessary. Problem Solving Skills: The ability to identify issues, analyze problems, develop effective solutions and escalate when necessary. Analytical Thinking: Ability to deconstruct information into smaller categories to draw conclusions Adaptability: being able to adjust to different situations, learn new skills and handle change effectively.

At Cadence, we hire and develop leaders and innovators who want to make an impact on the world of technology. About the Role Cadence is seeking a system and process improvement driven and analytically strong Staff Finance Analyst to join our Commissions Operations team. In this role, the individual will work with the Commissions and Sales Operations teams to improve processes and introduce automation to help scale, improve accuracy, and reduce risk to financial results related to our commissions system. The ideal candidate brings curiosity, desire to improve and innovate, along with solid finance fundamentals and hands-on Excel proficiency. Key Responsibilities Get involved in commissions operations details and processes. Identify opportunities to improve and automate steps and processes. Liaise with Sales Operations on input and output between both teams. Required Qualifications Hands-on experience with commissions systems; familiarity with finance data extraction and reporting workflows. Bachelor's degree in Finance or a related field. Around 5 years of progressive experience, ideally within the technology industry. Strong proficiency in Excel — comfortable with pivot tables, look-ups, data manipulation, and building structured workbooks. Solid analytical skills with high attention to detail and comfort working with large data sets. Preferred Qualifications Experience with Commissions software and exposure to Sales Compensation plans and operations Working knowledge of revenue accounting principles (ASC 606 / software revenue recognition concepts a plus) Track record of process improvement and automation projects What We Offer A collaborative environment where your contributions matter and support financial integrity and decision-making.

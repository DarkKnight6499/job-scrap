Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all . Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever. Responsibilities Lead and develop the U.S. Satellite Scenario and Capital Planning team, fostering a culture of collaboration, accountability, continuous improvement, and employee growth. Develop and evaluate capacity loading strategies, business scenarios, and long-range plans that support technology ramps, manufacturing expansion, and business objectives. Partner with planning, manufacturing, engineering, procurement, finance, and other multi-functional teams to translate business requirements into actionable capacity and capital plans. Lead scenario analyses and capacity evaluations to assess business alternatives and provide clear, data-driven recommendations to senior leadership. Analyze capital investment requirements, tooling strategies, technology transitions, and capital efficiency opportunities to support effective investment decisions. Drive alignment across site and central planning organizations on planning assumptions, capacity requirements, capital priorities, and business objectives. Establish and maintain planning processes, governance, data integrity standards and change management practices to ensure planning accuracy and timely execution. Champion planning innovation through advanced analytics, automation, dashboards, and decision-support tools that improve visibility, efficiency, and planning effectiveness. Minimum Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Industrial Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Operations Research, Supply Chain, or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience. Experience in semiconductor manufacturing, industrial engineering, capacity and capital planning, production planning, or a related analytical function. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with experience evaluating complex datasets and translating findings into actionable business recommendations. Proven ability to lead multi-functional initiatives, manage complex projects, and influence decisions across organizational boundaries. Excellent communication and presentation skills, including the ability to effectively communicate technical and business insights to leaders and collaborators. Preferred Qualifications Advanced degree in Industrial Engineering, Operations Research, Manufacturing Engineering, Supply Chain, Business Administration, or a related field. Experience leading teams, including coaching, mentoring, talent development, and performance management responsibilities. Strong understanding of semiconductor manufacturing operations, capacity modeling, technology transitions, and factory planning processes. Experience with strategic scenario planning, capital planning, cost-of-transition analysis, capital asset management, or technology node planning. Experience using advanced analytics and digital tools, including Power BI, Tableau, Python, AI-enabled planning solutions, and decision-support automation. Experience and/or interest in developing Agentic AI workflows into current or future processes.

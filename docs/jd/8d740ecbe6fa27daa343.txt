This team covers a wide range of responsibilities, including designing, trading, and execution strategies across EM Rates products — Bonds, Interest Rate Swaps (IRS), FX Swaps, and Repo on Government Bonds. You will get a chance to cover a wide range of responsibilities, including designing trading and execution strategies for EM Rates products (Bonds, IRS, FX Swaps, and Repo Government Bonds); researching micro-patterns and trends; building, maintaining, and upgrading applications and tools; and supporting day-to-day operations.

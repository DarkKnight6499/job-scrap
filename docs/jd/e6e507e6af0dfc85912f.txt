For further information about Key Risk please visit www.KeyRisk.com Responsibilities The PEO Risk Management Specialist provides occupational risk assessment, safety consulting, and risk management services in support of Key Risk's Professional Employer Organization (PEO) program. This role partners closely with PEO clients, brokers, underwriting, claims, and service teams to identify, evaluate, and mitigate workplace risks while supporting client retention, profitability, and growth. The Specialist serves as a trusted advisor to PEO clients and works collaboratively to implement effective safety and workers' compensation management strategies. Through data analysis, client consultation, training, and proactive service planning, this individual helps improve workplace safety performance, reduce claim frequency and severity, and support sustainable underwriting results. Key functions include but are not limited to the following: Conduct occupational risk assessments and evaluate workplace exposures across diverse industries and client operations. Identify safety, regulatory, and workers' compensation risk factors impacting PEO clients. Develop and implement safety improvement plans designed to reduce injuries and improve client performance. Assist clients in establishing effective safety committees, policies, procedures, and employee training programs. Monitor client progress toward safety and risk management objectives and recommend corrective actions when necessary. Support development and implementation of return-to-work and transitional duty programs. o PEO Underwriting Support Partner with underwriting during prospect evaluation, onboarding, renewal, and retention activities. Evaluate prospective clients and provide risk management recommendations that support underwriting decisions. Participate in pre-bind and renewal discussions to identify opportunities and concerns related to workplace risk. Assist in evaluating loss trends, claim drivers, and operational changes that may affect account performance. o Client Service and Account Management Serve as the primary risk management liaison for assigned PEO clients. Conduct client onboarding activities, including orientation, safety program review, and service plan development. Collaborate with PEO service teams, claims professionals, and underwriting partners to deliver coordinated client solutions. Build strong relationships with client leadership, HR teams, safety personnel, and agency partners. Maintain accurate records of client contacts, recommendations, and service activities. o Training and Education Deliver virtual and onsite training programs focused on workplace safety, injury prevention, regulatory compliance, and workers' compensation best practices. Facilitate new client orientation and ongoing educational sessions. Develop and distribute educational resources and risk management communications. o Data Analytics and Program Performance Analyze loss runs, claim trends, and operational data to identify opportunities for risk improvement. Utilize internal reporting tools and predictive insights to prioritize service activities. Measure effectiveness of safety initiatives and provide recommendations for continuous improvement. Prepare client-facing reports, presentations, and performance reviews. o Compliance and Regulatory Support Monitor and communicate changes in OSHA, state, and industry safety regulations. Support clients in understanding regulatory requirements and implementing compliant programs. Assist with compliance-related initiatives and internal reporting requirements. Escalate significant exposures and emerging risks to management as appropriate.

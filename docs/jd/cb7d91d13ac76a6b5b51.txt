Job Description: Position Summary Guggenheim is seeking an experienced, motivated Securitized Products Trader to join our Fixed Income Desk. This role is for a trader that has an existing book of business and ability to start generating revenue upon joining Guggenheim. Reporting directly to the Head of Fixed Income Trading, the candidate will be responsible for trading on behalf of customers and enhancing our presence in the marketplace. This position requires strong collaboration with our salesforce to facilitate customer requests, market the product and the sector, provide liquidity, and manage risk. The candidate will make markets in their sector, while providing superior client service.

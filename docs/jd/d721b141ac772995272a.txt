Job Description Visa's Technology Management organization is seeking an Analyst to support and evolve the Demand Management function within our Third-Party Technology Management practice. This role combines business analysis, data analytics, and process automation to improve how technology demand is planned, forecasted, tracked, and fulfilled across hardware, software, and services. The ideal candidate brings a blend of analytical thinking, process improvement expertise, and technical aptitude. They are comfortable evaluating existing workflows, identifying inefficiencies, and implementing automation solutions using data analytics, AI-powered tools, and low-code technologies. This role will partner closely with technology, finance, procurement, and vendor management teams to transform demand management processes into scalable, data-driven capabilities. The Work You'll Do: Support the development and execution of Visa's multi-year technology demand planning process across hardware, software, cloud services, and vendor-based technology solutions. Analyze demand data, consumption trends, asset utilization, and budget forecasts to identify optimization opportunities and improve planning accuracy. Partner with stakeholders to gather business requirements, document current-state processes, and design future-state solutions that improve efficiency and decision-making. Develop and maintain dashboards, reports, and analytical models that provide visibility into demand forecasts, budget utilization, portfolio health, and operational performance. Evaluate existing demand management workflows and identify opportunities to automate manual processes using AI, low-code platforms, scripting, workflow automation tools, and data-driven solutions. Design and implement process improvements leveraging technologies such as ServiceNow workflows, Power Platform, generative AI tools, and business intelligence platforms. Collaborate with technology, finance, procurement, and vendor management teams to align demand forecasts with strategic objectives, budgets, and supply planning. Support asset lifecycle planning, technology refresh initiatives, software renewals, and capacity planning activities. Create data quality controls, process documentation, and governance mechanisms to improve the accuracy and reliability of demand management data. Assist with purchase planning, vendor coordination, maintenance renewals, and demand intake processes while continuously seeking opportunities to improve the user experience and operational efficiency. Serve as a trusted partner to stakeholders by translating business challenges into actionable analytics, insights, and automation solutions.

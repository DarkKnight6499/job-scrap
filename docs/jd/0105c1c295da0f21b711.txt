The VP of Portfolio Management, Chubb Small and Lower Midmarket (“SLM”) is a dynamic leadership role with P&L accountability for a large and growing multiline P&C portfolio. The successful candidate will combine technical expertise in underwriting, portfolio management, pricing, and analytics with leadership, collaboration, and influence skills to drive profitable growth for the division. This role reports to the Chief Operating Officer of SLM with a matrixed reporting relationship to the Head of Commercial Insurance (“CI”) Portfolio Management.

What you will be doing: The Vertex R&D and DTE Strategic Sourcing team is seeking someone who has an interest in Sourcing or Procurement. The individual should be able to multi-task, be process oriented, feel comfortable and confident interacting with different stakeholders and have excellent excel/analytical skills. The Strategic Sourcing department strives to deliver value, improve financial performance and drive returns for the business in the strategic sourcing of goods and services through proactive analysis and understanding of Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), strategic engagement with our internal business partners and external suppliers and by educating the organization in Procurement and Sourcing best practices. The individual will assist with the day-to-day management of various spend categories such as biology and chemistry services, preclinical sciences, biospecimens, and global regulatory affairs, which includes administrative support, process mapping, building spend reports/ dashboards, supporting or leading RFX’s (Request for Information, Quotation, Proposal, etc.) and managing other projects as needed. Since the team is small, you will have the opportunity to contribute to a wide range of tasks. Administrative support for the strategic sourcing department including RFX (Request for Information/Proposal/ Quotation) processes, coordination and attendance of bid defense meetings Contracting database management Running and/ or developing spend reports/ dashboards (Excel & Power BI) Process mapping Support Supplier Relationship Management program Rate card and category spend analysis Planning and executing negotiations What you will need to succeed: Must have completed at least two years of undergraduate coursework with a focus in either sourcing, procurement, supply chain or commercial/ marketing, communications, business operations, project management, or a scientific discipline (biology, chemistry, or physics) with an interest in business. Students pursuing a master's degree will also be considered. Legal authorization to work in the United States, now and in the future.

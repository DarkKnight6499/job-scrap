The C redit Analyst C IB supports Corp & Institutional Banking (CIB) Lenders / Lending Units to meet various requirements including credit risk compliance, financial crime compliance, loan operations and deposit operations . You will work with the RM teams at deal origination and/or processing loan amendments and modifications, provide client research, prepare detailed credit analyses for deal screen and credit application, customer due diligence including KYC . Assist with and facilitate coordination with various internal and external stakeholders ensuring account opening, provide loan origination/closing support, assist with covenants compliance and ticklers/ Globalwave maintenance throughout the life cycle of the account. Compliance with bank policy and regulatory requirements. As assigned, with moderate direction and guidance, proactive and self-driven: Perform due diligence on new transactions and loan amendments/modifications. Prepare detailed credit analysis for new loan application and loan amendments/modifications. Oversee covenants compliance including detailed review and verify financial covenant reports and maintain the tickler and Globalwave database. Complete KYC/AML/OFAC/USA PATRIOT Act due diligence and analysis – assembly of the KYC/BSA package. Provide support to portfolio managers, underwriters, and relationship managers, including maintenance of credit documentation and files in Globalwave. Work with internal support group on deal closing, booking, and funding and on-going activities during the loan life cycle and account opening and on-going account maintenance. Maintain loan syndication site for Lead Admin agent deals. Assist with episodic projects including internal and external audits, credit policy and procedure review, etc. Alignment with Bank of Hope core values. Job Qualifications/Requirements Education/Credentials Minimum Education Level: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting or related fields. Prior Experience Minimum Job Experience: 0-2 year Skills Demonstrate role appropriate knowledge on Corporate Banking, Credit and related policies and procedures. Demonstrate role appropriate knowledge on banking regulations and state/federal regulations. Demonstrate role appropriate knowledge on regulatory and financial crime compliance. Thorough understanding of accounting principles, financial statements, and cash flow analysis. Work independently and be self-starting/proactive toward completing credit related tasks and obtaining necessary documentation, due diligence research, and completing customer onboarding and/or loan closing / account opening and maintenance tasks. Strong time management skills and ability to work under pressure within time constraints. Strong verbal communication and interpersonal skills to manage both internal and external relationship.

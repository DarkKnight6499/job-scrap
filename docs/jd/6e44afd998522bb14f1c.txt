The Role The position is responsible for leading many aspects of oversight, governance and portfolio support to work with the team to manage a growing portfolio of privately held assets. This individual will be the primary coordinator of portfolio support which entails day-to-day operational requirements for a multi-billion portfolio working in partnership with the investment team and various shared resources in legal, tax, insurance, risk, treasury, and audit . Second, the position will support oversight of individual portfolio assets in collaboration with the investment team. The role will partner with the management teams of portfolio companies via board and observer seats . And third, the position will support the development & communication of the investment strategy and performance for the natural resources platform. This role requires hands-on work, day-to-day tactical responsibilities and a flexible can-do attitude. This position will report to the Managing Director of the Natural Resources Platform. The platform consists of dozens of direct real asset investments and continues to add new exposures to its portfolio. As a result of this growth, Devonshire is adding this new position. Primary responsibilities will include the following: own day-to-day portfolio management, oversee certain portfolio assets, and support the development & communication of investment thesis, strategy and performance.

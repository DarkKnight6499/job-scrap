The Vehicle Recovery Associate will support the Loss Prevention operations in recovery of stolen vehicles. What You’ll Do: Reconciliation of daily ISO report for assigned region contacting local Law Enforcement agencies to confirm recovery of stolen vehicles Submit Impound recovery notification upon receiving confirmation of recovery vehicle Verify the status of the vehicle ensuring the stolen alarm has been cleared and vehicle is eligible for recovery to process in Case Management system, Foundry. Process recovery of Missing and Missing Alarmed vehicles placing the vehicle back into rentable status Review daily SVR submissions and when required closes contracts. Communicates with field security personnel ensuring vehicles are recovered timely. Prepares necessary documentation to send to Law Enforcement agencies confirming recovery of the stolen vehicle Continues to follow-up with tow lots and Law Enforcement to ensure timely delivery of stolen vehicles back to ABG. Maintains a close working relationship and constant interaction with Leadership and field personnel.

Company Federal Reserve Bank of Minneapolis The Federal Reserve Bank of Minneapolis (Bank) is seeking a candidate for a full-time Business Analyst position in its Legal Division. This analyst independently manages the operations of the Legal Division (Division), serves in an office manager capacity for the Division, and provides administrative and project management support to attorneys. The successful candidate must be able to manage complex projects and processes independently, must have exceptional judgement and problem-solving skills, and will thrive as a leading contributor on a collegial and collaborative team. This position requires a regular on-site presence. Responsibilities: Provide direct support for key functions of the Division, including contracts management, oversight of the Bank’s ethics program, administration of the Bank’s policies review processes, and management of legal holds. Manage the core operations of the Division independently, including project and process management; oversight of the Division records program; tracking attorney work; and management of the Division’s strategic objectives program, insurance administration program, and business continuity processes. Serve as office manager for the Division, including managing the Division calendar and agenda for Division meetings, managing invoices and expense reports, purchasing supplies, taking meeting notes, and assisting with events and visitors. Support attorneys directly, including document support (drafting, formatting, proofreading), calendar management, and assisting with onboarding and offboarding. Serve as ambassador and liaison to other Bank divisions to assist in coordinating work and joint projects. Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree required. To be considered for the Business Analyst II level , minimum of 3 years of direct work-related experience and equivalent combination of related higher education and experience. To be considered for the Business Analyst III level , minimum of 5 years of direct work-related experience and equivalent combination of related higher education and experience. Experience managing complex projects independently. Exceptional judgment, decision-making, problem-solving, and communications skills, both oral and written. Exceptional interpersonal skills and ability to work with people at all levels of the organization. Computer/technology skills including advanced proficiency with SharePoint, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. Proficiency with AI tools a plus. Salary & Benefits: Salary offer will be based on qualifications/experience of the candidate, alignment with market data, the needs of the position, our total compensation package, and internal equity.

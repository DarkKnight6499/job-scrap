Why you'll love this job: Strategy & Enablement, within the Enterprise Transformation organization, sits at the intersection of strategy and execution, leading the development of enterprise strategy and translating strategic priorities into measurable business outcomes. The function connects strategic planning, program and initiative design, strategic roadmaps, decision-making frameworks, and value realization to translate strategic objectives into disciplined execution and measurable business outcomes. Through this work, the team drives organizational alignment, strengthens accountability, and enables informed decision-making to deliver strategic outcomes and long-term enterprise value. As a Strategy & Enablement Associate, you will support the development of enterprise strategy and the translation of strategic priorities into actionable and measurable roadmaps and detailed plans. Working closely with Strategy & Enablement leadership, the Enterprise Program Management Office team, and partners across business, technology, and functional teams, you will conduct research and analysis, build executive-ready materials, and coordinate the routines that keep enterprise priorities on track. Success in this role requires intellectual curiosity, strong analytical and problem-solving skills, and a disciplined approach to execution. The ideal candidate is collaborative, detail-oriented, and eager to learn, with the ability to structure ambiguous problems, produce high-quality work products, communicate clearly with a range of stakeholders, and apply direction and feedback to progressively more complex assignments in accelerated timeframes. Your Primary Responsibilities: The Strategy & Enablement Associate supports the team through research, analysis, and executive material development, and helps drive the execution routines that translate strategic priorities into measurable business outcomes. In this role, you will: Conduct market, industry, competitive, and internal analyses to inform strategic plans and identify opportunities to improve organizational effectiveness. Gather, validate, and synthesize data into clear insights and recommendations. Develop executive-level presentations, reports, and narratives that clearly communicate priorities, progress, risks, findings, and recommendations. Connect signals across initiatives, functions, and external market developments to surface patterns, dependencies, and implications. Support the translation of enterprise strategic objectives into initiatives, roadmaps, and execution plans. Support governance frameworks, decision-making processes, and performance metrics by coordinating inputs, maintaining reporting discipline, and tracking actions and decisions. Coordinate inputs from business, technology, and functional partners to support recurring planning, reporting, and value realization cycles. Contribute to continuous improvement of Strategy & Enablement tools, templates, processes, and reporting practices.

Job Requisition ID: 95316 Location Designation: Hybrid - 3 days per week The Foundational Business Operations and Shared Services (FBOSS) Portfolio and Program Management team supports the successful execution of initiatives across our Foundational Business areas. The team partners closely with business, operations, and technology stakeholders to bring structure, coordination, and visibility to projects that support evolving business needs, system capabilities, and operational priorities. We are seeking a proactive, organized, and collaborative Senior Associate, Project Coordinator to support projects from planning through implementation. Working across both Waterfall and Agile environments , this individual will help coordinate activities across multiple workstreams, maintain project plans and documentation, track dependencies and risks, and ensure stakeholders have the information needed to keep initiatives moving forward. This is an excellent opportunity for someone with a strong foundation in project coordination who enjoys working across teams, solving problems, and bringing structure to complex initiatives. Role Overview As a Senior Associate, Project Coordinator, you will be an important member of the FBOSS Portfolio and Program Management team, supporting initiatives across multiple Foundational Business Value Streams. You will work closely with project leads and stakeholders across business, operations, and technology to coordinate project activities, maintain project discipline, and support the successful delivery of new system implementations, enhancements, and other business initiatives. The role requires someone who is highly organized, comfortable managing multiple priorities, and able to identify potential risks or dependencies before they impact delivery. You will also help create clear visibility into project health through project plans, status reporting, dashboards, meeting coordination, and risk and issue tracking. What You'll Do Support the planning, coordination, and execution of projects using Waterfall, Agile, or hybrid delivery approaches . Partner with project leads and stakeholders to help define project scope, objectives, milestones, deliverables, and timelines . Develop and maintain key project documentation, including project plans, status reports, dashboards, RAID logs, dependency trackers, meeting materials, and action-item logs . Coordinate project activities across business, operations, technology, testing, and other project teams to help keep deliverables on schedule. Facilitate project meetings and working sessions, document key decisions and action items, and ensure appropriate follow-up. Monitor project milestones, dependencies, risks, and issues, escalating concerns when appropriate and helping drive items toward resolution. Prepare concise and effective project status reports and dashboards that provide stakeholders with visibility into progress, upcoming milestones, risks, and dependencies. Coordinate with testing partners to ensure test plans, scripts, results, issues, and related documentation are appropriately captured and tracked. Help identify potential delivery risks, roadblocks, and cross-functional dependencies that could affect project objectives or timelines. Support implementation and rollout activities for new systems, enhancements, and business capabilities. Build strong working relationships across teams and help create an environment of accountability, transparency, and collaboration . What You'll Bring Required Qualifications 3–5 years of experience in project coordination, project management support, program coordination, business operations, or a related discipline. Experience supporting projects within Waterfall, Agile, or hybrid delivery environments . Strong organizational and project management skills, with the ability to coordinate multiple workstreams, priorities, deadlines, and stakeholders. Experience developing and maintaining project plans, status reports, dashboards, action-item trackers, risk and issue logs, or similar project documentation. Ability to proactively identify risks, issues, dependencies, and potential impacts to project timelines . Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to communicate clearly with stakeholders across business and technical teams. Demonstrated ability to facilitate meetings, capture decisions and action items, and drive appropriate follow-up. Experience coordinating with business and technology partners throughout the project lifecycle, including testing and implementation activities. Working knowledge of project and productivity tools such as JIRA, Microsoft Project, PowerPoint, Excel, and related collaboration tools . Strong attention to detail, follow-through, and the ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, collaborative environment. Preferred Qualifications Experience supporting projects within the insurance, financial services, or other complex regulated industries . Experience supporting technology implementations, system enhancements, operational transformation, or process improvement initiatives . Exposure to AI-enabled solutions, automation initiatives, or AI-supported delivery approaches . Familiarity with Agile ceremonies, sprint planning, backlog management, or tools such as JIRA. Experience creating executive-ready project reporting, dashboards, or communications for senior stakeholders. What Makes This Opportunity Compelling This role provides the opportunity to work across a broad range of business and technology initiatives while gaining exposure to how projects move from planning through implementation. You will collaborate with stakeholders across multiple functions, develop deeper project and program management capabilities, and contribute to initiatives that support the ongoing evolution of our Foundational Business.

Industry/Sector Health Services Specialism Operations Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As an Epic Payer Platform Senior Associate you will support healthcare payer platform work within our Advisory practice, drawing on Epic consulting knowledge to help clients modernize core systems, improve operating performance, and strengthen member-facing experiences. As a Senior Associate you will analyze complex issues, manage assigned workstreams, build meaningful client connections, and guide junior team members while using critical thinking and professional judgment to move deliverables forward. In this role at PwC, you will contribute to efforts across claims, reimbursement, care and utilization management, digital experience, and platform execution, with exposure to Oracle OHI, Epic, Facets, and Nasco in support of payer modernization goals. Responsibilities - Analyzing payer platform requirements and translating them into Epic-enabled workflow recommendations - Supporting client workstreams focused on payer modernization, claims operations, and platform execution - Gathering and interpreting business and technical inputs to inform process improvement and implementation decisions - Applying Epic software knowledge alongside electronic health record and health information system concepts to support solution delivery - Reviewing current-state operations and identifying opportunities to streamline member, provider, and administrative processes - Developing deliverables that reflect healthcare technology, digital transformation, and operational improvement priorities - Using analytical reasoning and business analysis methods to organize issues, track dependencies, and resolve workstream gaps - Contributing to discussions with client stakeholders by clarifying requirements, documenting decisions, and advancing next steps - Mentoring junior team members on task execution, work planning, and structured problem solving - Monitoring work product for consistency with project goals, delivery timelines, and firm methodologies What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 4 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for a Master's or Masters of Business Administration degree - Experience supporting payer platform modernization using Epic Software - Understanding of healthcare technology and health information systems - Experience analyzing claims, reimbursement, or care management operations - Familiarity with digital transformation in health services environments - Developing client relationships while guiding junior team members Travel Requirements Up to 80% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $77,000 - $202,000.

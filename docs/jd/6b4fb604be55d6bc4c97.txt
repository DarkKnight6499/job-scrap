Join a team that has received numerous awards for being a top place to work: TruStage awards and recognition Job Purpose: This position plays a critical role within the Third Party Risk Management function by supporting enterprise third-party risk activities, partnering with the Third Party Risk Management leader, and helping drive continuous improvement. Success in this role requires leadership, indirect influence, stakeholder management, collaboration, problem solving, personal agility, and a continuous improvement mindset. Job Responsibilities: . Lead, monitor, and execute daily Third Party Risk Management operations. Perform due diligence on complex, high-visibility third parties. Provide backup leadership support to the Third Party Risk Management Leader. Serve as a key resource to the Third Party Risk Management leader by providing thought partnership, operational support, backup leadership coverage, and support for complex issues. Build strong relationships and collaborate with business, risk and program stakeholders across the Third Party Risk Management ecosystem. Support, coach, and mentor emerging team members to build capability, consistency, and confidence in program execution. Lead complex Third-Party risk management initiatives, provide guidance and subject matter expertise to peers, and drive outcomes that support the function’s effectiveness, scalability and continued growth. Proactively identify, escalate, facilitate and track response activities that support effective management of third-party risks. Support the development of Third Party Risk Management policies, program materials, procedures, and internal resources. Evaluate Third Party Risk Management processes through the lens of effort, value, and enterprise risk to identify opportunities for simplification and improvement. Lead and support initiatives that improve processes, tools, technology, data quality, and program visibility. Work with third parties and key cross-functional stakeholders to ensure Third Party Risk Management activities are performed consistently across the portfolio and within agreed timelines. Conduct quality assurance reviews to promote accuracy, consistency, and adherence to Third Party Risk Management standards. Educate business, risk, and program stakeholders on roles, expectations, processes, and procedures. Promote strong stakeholder experience through responsive communication, effective support, and consistent execution. Leverage data, reporting, and portfolio insights to monitor Third Party Risk Management activity, identify trends, and support leadership decision-making. The above statement of duties is not intended to be all inclusive and other duties will be assigned from time to time. Job Requirements: Bachelor's degree in business, risk management, information technology, or related field, or equivalent work experience. 5+ years of progressive experience supporting Third Party Risk Management process improvement, technology enablement, data quality, reporting, or program transformation initiatives. Experience applying Third Party Risk Management practices such as risk identification, tiering, due diligence, risk assessment, monitoring, reporting, and stakeholder coordination. Ability to coach, guide, and share knowledge with less experienced team members. Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills with the ability to leverage data and portfolio insights to identify trends, monitor activity, and support decision-making. Experience building, enhancing, and using Third Party Risk Management technology platforms. Strong communication, facilitation, influencing, listening, and collaboration skills across stakeholder groups. Strong organizational and time management skills, with the ability to manage competing priorities and meet deadlines. Intellectually curious; able to research and find solutions independently. Action-oriented with a focus on execution and follow-through. Ability to apply an enterprise risk perspective when evaluating risk, options, and recommended actions.

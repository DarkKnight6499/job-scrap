Job Description: Join a collaborative team at the forefront of product innovation and financial oversight. As a Senior Associate Product Controller supporting Card & Connected Commerce and Travel business units, you will deliver end-to-end controllership across multiple legal entities and manage inter-company entries within the Chase Credit Card & Connected Commerce organization. You’ll partner with Finance, P&A, Operations, Data, Technology, and Controls to ensure the accuracy, integrity, and compliance of financial statements and regulatory reporting. Job Responsibilities: Oversee legal entity management and inter-company entry processes, ensuring timely and accurate posting across business units. Support financial close, reconciliations, and reporting for Card & Connected Commerce and Travel entities. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to migrate workstreams into JPMC infrastructure and enhance process efficiency. Maintain robust documentation and controls to support audit readiness and regulatory compliance. Provide analytical support to leadership, including variance analysis, issue escalation, and resolution. Assist in designing and implementing scalable processes that enable new product initiatives and ongoing portfolio management. Required qualifications, capabilities, and skills: Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics, or related discipline. 3+ years of relevant experience in finance, accounting, or risk reporting experience. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills; ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment. Proficiency in Microsoft Excel; familiarity with SAP, Hyperion Essbase, or similar financial systems is a plus. Excellent communication skills and ability to build strong working relationships across Finance, Operations, and Technology. Preferred qualifications, capabilities and skills: Experience with legal entity management, inter-company accounting, or supporting consumer banking products. Familiarity with US GAAP and regulatory requirements. Experience supporting audits or regulatory exams.

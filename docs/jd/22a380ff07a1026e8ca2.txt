About the Job CNA Is seeking an experienced Financial Analysis Consultant to join the Business Unit FP&A Team. The Business Unit FP&A team primarily partners with leaders in the Underwriting and Actuarial departments to deliver deeper insight into P&L results, driving improvements in top-line growth, expense management, and profitability. Additionally, this role will seek opportunities to optimize and streamline reporting, build out new analytical resources, lead special projects, and be a key player in financial forecasts and plans. This role is located in Chicago, IL and team comes into the office in a hybrid model.

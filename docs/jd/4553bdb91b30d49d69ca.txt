Excited to grow your career? BBVA is a global company with more than 160 years of history that operates in more than 25 countries where we serve more than 80 million customers. We are more than 121,000 professionals working in multidisciplinary teams with profiles as diverse as financiers, legal experts, data scientists, developers, engineers and designers. About the job: Summary of Responsibilities : The Associate will be part of the team responsible for the origination and structuring of secured real estate financing transactions for BBVA’s U.S. client base. Analyze and evaluate credit worthiness of real estate and data center loans through detailed cash flow modeling and market analysis. Required tasks include, but are not limited to review of offering memorandums, financial and operating statements, sponsorship transaction history, real estate market information, cash flow modeling, loan structuring, and credit and management memorandum preparation. Preparation and documentation management for new deal execution including information memoranda, credit agreements, and bank meeting presentations, under the direction of senior members of the team. List Major/Essential Job Duties: Assist in the new deal origination/modeling/structuring process for the real estate finance team. Evaluate new transactions to determine the quality of each opportunity. Assist in the preparation of deal screening memos, credit applications, risk ratings, and other supporting materials required during the approval process. Conduct market research on a macro and micro level for various property types, markets and submarkets. Coordinate the engagement of third party vendors (legal, valuation, engineering, etc.) Assist with customer due diligence / know your customer checks on new clients transactional due diligence, and loan closing process. Prepare detailed cash flow and real estate analysis models complete with deal, market, and industry information for the analysis of potential new business. Assessing BBVA’s internal risk rating, as well as running return models for each transaction. Portfolio management and information reporting on in process transactions and closed deals, including interaction with finance on fee recognition Loan approval, closing, and booking for transactions booked in NY using internal processes and systems, working with various teams to book transactions Maintain and update various sizing and structuring models to provide efficient and transparent analysis and feedback on new deals to originators, relationship managers, and credit risk as deals progress through the structuring and closing process. Maintain various databases to track origination and market color: pricing, structure, leverage, etc. Interact closely with the syndication team, as applicable. Participate in periodic site inspections. Level of Complexity and Risk Involved: Moderately complex responsibilities, with a critical component being the underwriting of real estate loans, due diligence, and preparation of credit memoranda. Scope of Duties : Interaction with other teams within the BBVA Group Maintenance of internal reports and their distribution to the appropriate areas Loan market updates and data management Understanding and development of financial modeling, financial statement analysis Oral and written presentations for client marketing and internal purposes Client meeting participation when practical Preparation and monitoring of the loan closing process Loan booking on the Bank’s loan servicing tool Identification and use of research and tools Participation in due diligence meetings Skills, knowledge, and abilities/Competencies : Degree in Economics, Accounting, Finance or related field Excellent communication and presentation skills Strong organizational skills to manage loan approval deadlines in a fast-paced environment Ability to work in a team environment and under tight deadlines Self-motivated, accountable, and team-oriented Financial modeling and knowledge of financial statements Proficient in ALL Microsoft Office Tools Advanced Excel a necessity and PowerPoint a must, Argus a plus All qualified applicants will receive consideration for employment without regard to race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, disability, or status as a protected veteran.

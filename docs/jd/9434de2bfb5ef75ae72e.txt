We are looking to add a Senior Associate / Vice President, Commercial Strategy & Growth to Wealth Management Managed Investments. Managed Investments is responsible for the management and/or implementation of nearly $1 trillion in assets across various advisory programs, including Chase Wealth Management and the Private Bank. As Managed Investments continues to scale and evolve, there is a growing need to strengthen how we coordinate strategic priorities, drive innovation, and deliver new capabilities that improve advisor and client outcomes. This role will sit at the center of several of the organization's most important initiatives, serving as a key partner to senior leadership in advancing the Office of Portfolio Construction (OPC), driving cross-functional execution, and shaping the future growth strategy of the business. The individual in this role will work closely with Managed Investments leadership, Product, Technology, Investments, Platform, and Distribution teams to ensure strategic initiatives are prioritized, coordinated, and delivered effectively. In addition, this individual will help establish the processes, insights, and strategic frameworks needed to drive business growth, improve transparency into performance, identify market opportunities, and accelerate innovation.

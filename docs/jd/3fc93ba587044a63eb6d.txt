Job Description Brookfield is seeking an Associate to Join the Brookfield Housing team, based in New York City, to support investment / portfolio management activities in the alternative housing space, including Manufactured Housing and Student Housing. The global housing portfolio also contains premier assets in North America, Europe, Asia, Australia, and Brazil. This is an opportunity to join an experienced team responsible for one of Brookfield’s largest real estate sector portfolios. This team seeks to maximize asset value in each asset through leasing, redevelopment of existing housing, or in some cases through the addition of mixed-use components. This team is principally responsible for overseeing the work of real estate managers and operating teams in the execution of investment business plans. Strategic decision-making – such as financing, capital investments, development / redevelopment, hold-sell, business plan decisions, etc. – are the purview of this team of portfolio managers. Collaboration with leasing, development, finance, tax, compliance, legal etc. is required to monitor investments to ensure business plan and strategy objectives are being met. This opportunity encompasses the strategic oversight of Brookfield’s real estate investments. The new Associate will support the portfolio management team on the North American Housing business, focused primarily on student housing assets, manufactured housing, and other alternative housing assets, in the role outlined below.

Job Description As a Lead Associate on the Enterprise Operational Risk team, you will provide expert risk guidance and help assess and identify potential risks that could affect Fannie Mae's reputation, safety, security, operational resilience, or financial success. You will partner with stakeholders across the enterprise to understand business objectives, evaluate risk exposure, and support informed business decisions. The Impact You Will Make Lead and guide risk assessments to identify operational risks and their potential implications for the enterprise. Partner with stakeholders across the organization to understand business objectives, processes, and emerging risk considerations. Guide analyses, assessments, and reports that identify areas of potential risk exposure and evaluate proposed risks and their impact. Monitor risk information, processes, and controls using rigorous analysis to identify trends, gaps, and opportunities to reduce risk. Document risk resolutions, control guidelines, and recommendations that support consistent risk management practices. Guide team reviews of business strategies and translate risk insights into practical recommendations as consumer behaviors and regulatory expectations evolve. Contribute to an enterprise risk culture grounded in sound judgment, accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

The Operations Support Associate is responsible for supporting day-to-day dispatch operations and administrative activities for Last Mile delivery services. This role works closely with delivery teams, Macy's customers, transportation providers, and internal stakeholders to ensure timely communication, operational compliance, and successful delivery execution. Key responsibilities include monitoring delivery routes and service performance, managing customer and driver escalations, maintaining accurate records and documentation, facilitating communication across the delivery network, and providing real-time solutions to operational challenges. The Operations Support Associate plays a critical role in ensuring service expectations are met while supporting a positive customer experience and operational efficiency. Success in this role requires strong communication skills, attention to detail, sound judgment, and the ability to multitask in a fast-paced environment. The ideal candidate is dependable, trustworthy, highly organized, and proficient with computer systems and technology. They must be able to adapt quickly to changing priorities while maintaining a high level of professionalism and customer focus.

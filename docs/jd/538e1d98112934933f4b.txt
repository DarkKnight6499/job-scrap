Regular or Temporary: Regular Language Fluency: English (Required) Work Shift: 1st shift (United States of America) Please review the following job description: Supports and executes the successful implementation and delivery of the AI Risk Oversight (AIRO) Program. Responsible for understanding and executing key second-line risk oversight activities, including risk advisory, effective challenge, and independent risk assessment responsibilities to drive impactful risk management outcomes. This role will also lead the evolution of Information Risk Oversight (IRO) reporting capabilities through the design, development, and implementation of a future-state reporting ecosystem. The ideal candidate will combine strategic vision with hands-on execution to transform risk reporting by leveraging AI-enabled capabilities, automation, advanced analytics, and innovative technologies. The individual will be responsible for developing scalable, actionable reporting solutions that provide meaningful insights to executives and stakeholders while enhancing the organization's ability to monitor and communicate risk exposures across the enterprise. To ensure reporting remains relevant and impactful, the successful candidate will maintain close engagement with AI Risk Oversight activities, governance processes, risk assessments, and emerging risk themes. This role requires the ability to translate complex risk information into clear, actionable intelligence that supports effective decision-making and risk oversight. This position reports to the Head of AI Risk Oversight and works closely with Information Risk Oversight partners, business stakeholders, technology teams, and other risk functions to deliver against strategic priorities, strengthen risk governance, and advance reporting transformation objectives. Essential Duties and Responsibilities Following is a summary of the essential functions for this job. Other duties may be performed, both major and minor, which are not mentioned below. Specific activities may change from time to time. 1. Lead the design and development of a future-state Information Risk Oversight reporting ecosystem that enhances the organization's ability to measure, monitor, and communicate risk exposures across the enterprise. 2. Develop and execute a reporting transformation strategy leveraging AI-enabled capabilities, automation, advanced analytics, and innovative technologies to establish scalable, efficient, and actionable reporting capabilities across the Information Risk Oversight function. 3. Drives successful execution of the AIRO Coverage and Operating Model including risk advisory, effective challenge and independent risk assessment responsibilities and delivery. 4. Effectively contributes to the execution and delivery of the EDRO strategic plan including the supporting tactical plans/roadmaps. 5. Ensures all applicable laws, rules, regulations, internal policies, standards, and procedures are being followed and can evidence adherence. 6. Drives effective issues management and remediation execution in alignment with the Enterprise Issue Management (EIM) Program as applicable. 7. Produce, enhance, and deliver executive reporting, metrics, dashboards, and insights that support risk governance, oversight activities, and informed decision-making across the Information Risk Oversight function. 8. Promotes and fosters a strong Truist risk culture driving proactive risk management and remediation accountability across the enterprise. 9. Performs effective challenge on covered areas for all significant AI-related processes and initiatives for validation. 10. Provides risk advisory to strengthen AI risk controls when applicable.

Northwestern Mutual Investment Management Company , located in Milwaukee, WI, is a wholly owned subsidiary of the Northwestern Mutual Life Insurance Company. Our firm manages almost $300 billion of insurance, annuity, and pension fund assets. Our goal is to achieve superior and sustainable long-term performance through a disciplined investment process and an experienced staff. At Northwestern Mutual, the Public Investments team manages approximately $135 billion in fixed income securities and derivatives for the company’s General Account and related entities. About the Job: Our Public Investment team seeks a Corporate Bond Trader for its Investment Grade (IG) Credit Trading desk. The successful candidate will bring experience, eagerness, and enthusiasm to the role, continually seeking new ways to measure and achieve best execution while helping improve our capabilities and speed to decision-making. He or she will report to the Head of IG Credit Trading.

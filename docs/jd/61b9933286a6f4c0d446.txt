Senior Market Analyst, Derivatives
London Stock Exchange Group
Location: USA-New York-28 Liberty Street

Shape the future of financial markets We're looking for a curious and analytical professional to join our Derivatives Valuation & Analytics team. In this role, you'll help deliver trusted valuation data and insights that support clients across global financial markets. You'll work alongside colleagues in product, technology, quantitative research, and client services to solve complex challenges, improve processes, and develop innovative solutions. Whether you're building analytics, enhancing models, or supporting client needs, your work will help drive the quality and reliability of our products. We welcome applicants from a variety of backgrounds who can demonstrate strong analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and a passion for financial markets. What you'll do Support the production and delivery of valuation and analytics services for a range of financial instruments. Help ensure the accuracy, quality, and timely delivery of pricing data and analytical outputs. Partner with colleagues across product, technology, and quantitative teams to improve processes and client outcomes. Gather, document, and communicate business requirements to support new products and enhancements. Provide analytical support for client requests and valuation-related enquiries. Contribute to the development, testing, and implementation of models, methodologies, and analytical tools. Use Python and other technologies to automate workflows and improve efficiency. Identify opportunities to enhance processes, controls, and the client experience. Products and Markets The team supports a broad range of financial products, including interest rate, foreign exchange, equity, credit and structured products. Experience with any of the following is beneficial but not required: Interest Rate Swaps (IRS) Swaptions Foreign Exchange (FX) Options Equity Options Credit Default Swaps (CDS) Total Return Swaps (TRS) Inflation Swaps Structured and hybrid products What you'll bring Essential Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. An interest in financial markets and valuation concepts. Experience using Python or another programming language to analyze data or improve processes. Ability to communicate clearly and build effective working relationships. A collaborative approach and willingness to learn. Preferred Experience in quantitative analysis, research, valuation, risk, or financial markets. Knowledge of derivatives or capital markets products. Advanced study in Finance, Financial Engineering, Mathematics, Economics, Physics, Engineering, Computer Science, or a related discipline. What makes this opportunity exciting Work on challenging problems that combine finance, technology, and data. Learn from experienced professionals across multiple disciplines. Contribute to products used by leading financial institutions around the world. Grow your technical, analytical, and market knowledge in a supportive environment. Be part of a collaborative team that values different perspectives and experiences.

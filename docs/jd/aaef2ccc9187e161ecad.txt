The Role In your role as the US Benefits Leader, you will focus on designing and governing the employee benefit programs in the US. You will be responsible to ensure Kyndryl's benefit programs are compliant, market aligned, providing a quality employee experience. You will be responsible for the managed spend and ensuring that program design and spend is optimized to ensure the highest value proposition. You will have overall responsibility for the benefit communication strategy to ensure our programs are engaging and that employees understand the value of the programs available. Success in this role is measured by effective governance, compliance, optimization of managed spend, while designing programs that are market aligned, contemporary with trend and reflecting an employer of choice. What you will do Set strategy for design, market position, and cost investment/ containment for all employee benefit programs in the US Establish cadence with benefits delivery to ensure alignment, coaching and support, and accurate administration of the company programs Manage core supplier partnerships - such as consultants, brokers, carriers, and vendor suppliers' others to ensure best in class service and timely execution of all deliverables that deliver an engaging experience and deliver on ROI and VOI Enhance employee experience to ensure awareness and easy of navigation for Kyndryl's benefit programs Harmonize and simply programs to enable consistency, and investment in market aligned programs Act as consultant and partner to HR and business leaders in curating solutions to human capital needs Collaborate with the HR M&A/ HRBD team for corporate transactions and employee transfers regarding their impact on benefits Your Future at Kyndryl Every position at Kyndryl offers a way forward to grow your career.

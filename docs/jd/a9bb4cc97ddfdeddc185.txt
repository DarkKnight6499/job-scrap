Job Description: Senior Manager, FP&A – Better For You Accelerator is Mars Snacking's growth engine, focused on unlocking growth across a portfolio of high-potential brands and categories. Our Better For You portfolio includes KIND®, RXBAR®, Nutri-Grain®, Special K Bars®, and Pure Organic®, combining the scale and resources of Mars with the agility, innovation, and entrepreneurial mindset of a growth business. As Senior Manager, FP&A – Better For You, you will play a critical leadership role in driving financial performance and shaping the future of a growing portfolio of brands. Partnering closely with Commercial, Supply Chain, and senior cross-functional leaders, you'll deliver the insights, planning, and strategic guidance that help accelerate growth while modernizing how the business plans, reports, and makes decisions through automation, advanced analytics, and AI-enabled solutions. Reporting to the CFO and leading a team of two, this is an opportunity to make a meaningful impact in a fast-paced, evolving environment where curiosity, agility, and innovation are highly valued. What are we looking for? Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or related field required; CPA and/or MBA preferred. 5-7+ years of progressive finance experience across FP&A, Commercial Finance, Operations Finance, or related areas. Demonstrated experience leading complex planning, forecasting, budgeting, and performance management processes. Proven ability to partner with and influence cross-functional stakeholders to drive business decisions and deliver financial results. Experience managing ambiguity and driving outcomes during periods of change, transformation, system implementations, ERP transitions, or similar business initiatives. Strong GAAP knowledge and ability to model and analyze all three financial statements. Excellent communication, presentation, and business partnering skills, with the ability to effectively challenge and influence stakeholders at all levels. Curious, analytical, and self-driven leader who thrives in fast-paced environments and is comfortable creating solutions where a playbook does not exist. Preferred Qualifications Experience supporting large-scale businesses and complex P&Ls. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, and ERP systems, with SAP experience preferred. Experience with Power BI, automation tools, AI-enabled solutions, or advanced analytics. Consumer Packaged Goods (CPG) or food manufacturing experience. What will be your key responsibilities? Lead the annual operating plan and budgeting process, from methodology through stakeholder implementation, proposal review, and financial statement integration. Own the management reporting process end-to-end, including close timelines, close meetings, and reporting deliverables, while continuously improving quality and efficiency. Analyze KPIs and develop performance commentary, delivering insight-driven recommendations and presentations for Accelerator and Better For You leadership teams. Partner cross-functionally to forecast the balance-of-year P&L, NCFO, and Latest Estimates, consolidating risks and opportunities while driving alignment on actions to deliver results. Support long-range financial planning and evaluate large-scale strategic initiatives that shape the future of the business. Drive reporting simplification, process automation, and adoption of AI and advanced analytics tools to enhance efficiency and decision-making. Lead and develop a team of two associates, fostering a culture of accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement. Support ad hoc analyses, business cases, and special projects as needed.

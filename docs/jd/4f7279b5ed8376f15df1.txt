About The Role & Team We are seeking a detail-oriented and highly motivated Treasury & Cash Management Analyst to join our finance team. This role will support the firm's daily cash management, banking operations, settlement funding, and treasury reporting activities. The analyst will work closely with treasury, finance, operations, and banking partners to help ensure the accurate movement, monitoring, and reconciliation of cash across the organization. This position provides an excellent opportunity to develop treasury and brokerage finance expertise while gaining exposure to regulatory funding requirements, cash management processes, and financial operations within a fast-paced financial services environment. In This Role, You Will Cash Management & Treasury Operations Prepare daily cash position reports across corporate, customer, and settlement accounts. Monitor daily cash balances and support funding activities across bank accounts. Assist with wire transfers, ACH transactions, and other cash movement activities. Coordinate with internal teams and banking partners to ensure timely settlement and funding activities. Reconciliation & Financial Controls Perform daily cash reconciliations between bank accounts and internal systems. Investigate and resolve cash breaks, outstanding items, and transaction discrepancies. Maintain treasury documentation and support established internal control procedures. Escalate unusual transactions or unresolved issues to treasury management. Reporting & Analysis Prepare recurring treasury reports, cash forecasts, and management reporting packages. Support variance analysis between forecasted and actual cash activity. Gather and organize data for management, audit, and regulatory reporting requests. Process Support Assist with treasury process improvements and automation initiatives. Participate in testing system enhancements and process changes. Support the maintenance of treasury procedures and process documentation. Audit & Compliance Support Assist with internal and external audit requests. Support regulatory examinations by providing documentation and reporting. Help ensure treasury activities are conducted in accordance with company policies and regulatory requirements. The Skills You Bring Bachelor’s degree in finance, Accounting, Economics, Business Administration, or a related field. 0-3 years of relevant experience in finance, accounting, treasury, banking operations, or financial services. Internship or entry-level experience within a brokerage, bank, fintech, or accounting environment is a plus. Strong Microsoft Excel skills. Experience with banking platforms, reconciliation processes, or financial systems preferred. Ability to analyze data and identify discrepancies. Strong attention to detail and organizational skills. Analytical mindset with a willingness to investigate and solve problems. Effective verbal and written communication skills. Ability to manage multiple priorities in a deadline-driven environment. Eagerness to learn and develop technical treasury and finance expertise. What Makes You Stand Out Exposure to brokerage operations, treasury operations, or banking processes. Familiarity with cash reconciliations, wire processing, or financial reporting. Experience working with Microsoft Excel, Access, Power BI, or other reporting tools.

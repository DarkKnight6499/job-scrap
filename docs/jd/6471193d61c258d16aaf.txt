About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking an Associate Executive Specialist in Corporate & Investment Banking (CIB). CIB delivers a comprehensive suite of banking, capital markets and advisory solutions, including a full complement of sales, trading and research capabilities, to corporate, government and institutional clients. We focus on our clients' overall financial needs, with consideration and respect for their total relationship with Wells Fargo. Learn more about the career areas and business divisions at wellsfargojobs.com . In this role, you will: Support a strong risk and compliance culture while building trusted customer relationships Accountable for following risk programs, policies, and regulatory requirements, including timely issue identification, escalation, and resolution Proactively monitor and manages risk, exercises sound judgment, and makes decisions aligned with the organization's risk appetite and compliance standards Provide a high level of professional support which includes a balance of experienced executive level administrative work, as well, as ongoing ad-hoc project and business initiatives such as planning meetings, tracking deliverables and pulling routine reports Perform heavy calendaring, complex travel planning, extensive document creation and editing, and preparing expense report submissions Support projects Act as information source on group and company policies Receive direction from supervisor and exercise judgement within defined parameters while developing understanding of the Administrative Support function Collaborate and consult with peers, colleagues and managers to resolve issues and achieve goals Interact with external customers Required Qualifications, US: 6+ months of Executive Administration experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education Previous Corporate & Investment Banking or Financial Services experience Desired Qualifications: 5+ years of Executive/Administrative Assistant experience supporting senior leaders in a corporate environment. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Outlook, Excel, and Concur. Demonstrated experience managing complex calendars, travel arrangements, and expense reporting. Experience supporting multiple executives and balancing competing priorities in a fast-paced environment. Strong organizational skills with exceptional attention to detail and follow-through. Proven ability to exercise sound judgment, maintain confidentiality, and work independently. Strong communication and interpersonal skills; ability to interact effectively with senior leadership, clients, and business partners. Experience preparing and managing executive business materials, including reports, presentations, agendas, and meeting documentation. Proven project coordination and administrative leadership experience, including onboarding/offboarding and cross-functional partnership.

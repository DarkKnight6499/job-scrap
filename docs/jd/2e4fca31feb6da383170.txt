PRINCIPAL OBJECTIVE OF THE POSITION Lead and develop multifunctional teams to ensure effective process technology transfer of commercial and clinical products manufactured at the Devens Single Use Facility, resolve process issues, and optimize process and facility efficiency. This role is accountable for maximizing productivity, minimizing costs, and resolving process challenges through leadership, technical expertise, and cross-functional resource coordination. MAJOR DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Leads technology transfer and new product introduction into the manufacturing facility. Accountable for providing support to manufacturing teams to achieve production targets for clinical and commercial products. Directs activities associated with product changeover, continuous improvement initiatives, project prioritization, and production and maintenance scheduling to meet site metrics. Allocates department resources to support deviations, defects, and changes associated with maintaining safe and compliant production. Champions the company's, site's, and department's mission, goals, and guidelines, taking action that advances cultural evolution, innovation, and leadership across the organization. Promotes and demonstrates safety leadership in pursuit of an injury-free operating environment, ensuring safety investigations and CAPAs are closed promptly. Collaborates with Manufacturing staff to identify trends, variances, and bottlenecks in the manufacturing process, and helps develop solutions to maximize productivity, quality, and process efficiency. Recruits and develops a high-performing team with diverse backgrounds and talents; coaches and counsels team members to build their skills and fosters an environment of continuous learning, improvement, and innovation. Maintains and communicates performance metrics to the team, setting and upholding high performance expectations. Establishes an environment that fosters continuous improvement in cost, quality, safety, and customer service. Builds and maintains strong relationships inside and outside the organization. Supports health authority inspections. KNOWLEDGE AND SKILLS B.S. in biological sciences, chemical/biochemical engineering, or a related discipline, with a minimum of 10 years of relevant experience in the biopharmaceutical industry, or equivalent experience; or M.S. in the same disciplines, with a minimum of 6 years of relevant experience in the biopharmaceutical industry, or equivalent experience; or Ph.D. in biological sciences, chemical/biochemical engineering, or related discipline. Mastery of SOPs and cGMPs, with the know-how to work and manage within a regulatory environment. Demonstrated aptitude for engineering principles and manufacturing automation systems. Demonstrated ability to shift the thinking of, or gain acceptance from, others in sensitive situations. Proven leadership ability to make difficult decisions in a logical, rational manner; demonstrated ability to work as a senior management team member and engage and influence team members in a matrixed environment. Previous managerial experience demonstrating proficiency in selecting, motivating, and retaining a high-performing team; effectively managing personnel issues; and planning and administering financial resources. Adaptable to a fast-paced, complex, and ever-changing business environment. Effective communication skills to coordinate with support groups and obtain needed cooperation and support in a timely and effective manner. Demonstrated strong track record of change management, including implementation of continuous improvement and OPEX strategies.

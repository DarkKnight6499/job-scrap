As an Agility Associate in Digital Product Portfolio Management team, you will play a crucial role in supporting teams through the product development lifecycle. Your broad knowledge of agile principles and practices will be instrumental in facilitating group discussions and decision-making processes. Ensure a collaborative and inclusive environment that fosters continuous improvement and adaptability. Your contribution will be key to achieving short-term objectives that impact both your team and the wider department. You will support Technology, Product, and Operations teams using Scrum and Kanban practices. You will have skills in Jira, Jira queries, Excel, pivot tables, analytics, reporting, release management, and identifying patterns that drive team improvement. You will be strong communicator who can translate technical details into business language, manage multiple priorities, and provide hands-on support to help teams deliver effectively.

When you join the growing BILH team, you're not just taking a job, you’re making a difference in people’s lives. Job Description: Mount Auburn Hospital Schedule 24 hours per week Three 8-hour shifts Schedule based on department coverage needs About the Role Support the Level II Nursery in a combined Unit Coordinator/Patient Care Associate role focused primarily on clerical, supply, and unit-support responsibilities. You will help keep the nursery organized, answer phones, support secure visitor access, and ensure necessary supplies are available. The position includes occasional hands-on newborn support, such as assisting with feedings under the direction of the nursing team, but it is not primarily a clinical-care role. What You’ll Do Answer the unit telephone and route calls and messages appropriately Welcome parents and approved visitors and assist with access to the secured unit Communicate professionally with families, nurses, physicians, and hospital staff Order, receive, organize, and put away unit supplies Monitor inventory and notify the appropriate team member when supplies need to be reordered Restock formula, feeding products, diapers, linens, and other newborn-care items Maintain clean and organized supply, clerical, and patient-care areas Assist with unit paperwork and other clerical responsibilities Support nurses and other members of the care team with assigned unit tasks Occasionally assist with feeding newborns under the direction of licensed staff Follow hospital confidentiality, safety, infection-prevention, and unit-security procedures Cross-train and support other areas as needed What Success Looks Like You will be successful in this role if you are: Reliable and dependable Organized and attentive to detail Comfortable balancing clerical and occasional patient-care responsibilities Willing to complete routine stocking and supply-management tasks Warm and professional with parents and families Comfortable working around newborns Able to communicate clearly by phone and in person Focused during frequent interruptions Flexible and willing to help wherever the unit needs support A collaborative team member who understands that operational tasks are essential to patient care Why Join This Team? You will join a maternal-child health team where Unit Coordinators, Patient Care Associates, nurses, and physicians work together to support newborns and their families. This position offers exposure to the Level II Nursery environment while building experience in hospital operations, family communication, unit coordination, and newborn-care support. Candidates should understand that the role is centered primarily on clerical duties, supply management, and supporting the daily operation of the nursery, with occasional direct newborn care. Minimum Qualifications High school graduate or equivalent Previous clerical experience (preferably in a healthcare setting). Medical terminology is preferred. Ability to read and write at level 8, as documented on "Invest," the hospital's Employee Learning Laboratory software. An ability to set appropriate priorities and organize workflow. An ability to concentrate and pay attention to detail during frequent interruptions. Strong interpersonal skills are necessary to interface with support and professional staff and patients. Work Environment and Other Requirements Consistently promote a culture of Wellbeing, Empathy, Collaboration, Accountability, Respect, and Equity as outlined in the organization's WE CARE values. Demonstrated commitment and actions to valuing diversity and contributing to an inclusive working and learning environment Busy, heavily trafficked patient-care environment. Normal hospital environment. Exposure to infectious diseases, chemicals, and other toxic agents. Ability to walk and stand for up to 90% of work time, and to push and pull heavy work objects (including patients), and lift 50 lbs. Fine motor control for limited periods to perform patient treatments.

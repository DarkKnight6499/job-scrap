Our benefits include: Medical coverage on date of hire 100% employer paid cash balance pension plan 401(k) with company match fully vested on date of hire Minimum of 15 days paid vacation and 12 paid holidays Paid parental leave and family caregiver leave Visit our Benefits & Perks Page for more information on benefits provided to regular full-time employees. ​ About The Position The Senior Capital Planning & Governance Specialist is responsible for supporting the capital investment lifecycle for Ameren's Digital organization. The position partners with leaders to align budgets, develop and prioritize business cases, validate spending plans, and maintain accurate capital plan data within applicable systems. Key responsibilities include: Support, collaborate, and challenge Ameren functions to ensure optimal Digital portfolio investments of ~$1B over the next five years to be presented to senior leaders on a routine basis. Proactively provide support and collaborate with Digital Leaders in matters of capital governance and analysis to include: capital planning, tracking of projects, forecasting, cost effectiveness analysis (as needed), and communication with internal and external stakeholders. Guide leaders through the emerging demand process when capital needs arise throughout the year. Prepare reports, perform ad-hoc analysis, and investigate issues brought forth by Digital and/or Business Leaders related to capital plans. Assist in the preparation of materials to get project approvals (e.g. business case development, documentation for Work Order approvals, etc.) Independently create professional presentations that clearly communicate capital plans, analyses, and key insights in partnership with the Capital Planning and Digital teams. Support rate reviews, audits, and other regulatory matters related to Digital capital including reporting and data requests. Serve as administrator for the capital planning tool (OPPM) including new user training, implementing enhancements, troubleshooting bugs, and the testing of any system upgrades. Lead continuous improvement initiatives with Digital's Project Management Office and Financials organization to increase efficiency, transparency, and accuracy utilizing internal and external (benchmarking) best practices. Qualifications - Lead Capital Planning & Governance Specialist Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Business, Finance, Economics, Engineering or related field from an accredited University required.

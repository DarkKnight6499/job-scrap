The HRBP builds positive working relationships and acts as a contact for leaders and employees within the assigned client group. Complies and maintains accurate electronic and paper personnel records utilizing workday. Providing high level administrative support for Plant Manager, Supervisors, Operations, Human Resources, Benefits, Payroll, Accounting, Safety, and other departments in Westlake Pipe & Fittings. DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES May include, but are not limited to, the following: Responds to inquiries regarding policies, procedures and programs. Develop and maintains knowledge of company systems (Workday). Recruits, interviews, tests and selects salaried and hourly employees for client group. Conducts new employee orientation to foster positive impression of business unit. Administers salary and hourly compensations programs to ensure compliance and equity within the client group. Administers vacation, sick leave, leave of absence, and other employee related reports requested by management. Prepares new hire and termination documentation, and conducts exit interviews to determine termination reason. Responsible for accurate recording and data entry of employee information such as personal data, compensation, tax data, attendance, performance reviews or evaluations, and termination date and reason into Company's HRIS. Processes employment applications and assists in other employment activities such as pre-employment background checks, drug screens, new employee orientation, etc Maintains and updates employee files and file room including electronic files to include personnel files, medical files and general files. Manages company sponsored events and programs such as community outreach and service award banquets. Coordinates client group training with client group and human resources management. Gather and maintain all safety records and ensure their accuracy. SAP verifications and reporting to include cycle counts and inventory. Receives and reviews invoices, codes and submits for approval. Backup for Vendor Invoice Management (VIM) accounting and coding Updates and maintains bulletin board postings. Comply with all Company policies and procedures. Any additional duties and tasks as required.

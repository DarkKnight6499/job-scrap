The opportunity The Insurance Actuarial Advisory Service (IAAS), Property & Casualty (PC) practice within FSO is seeking an individual that is highly motivated with experienced pricing abilities, robust knowledge of data analytics and the associated tools and programming languages, excellent operational management capabilities and communication skills. This position will report directly to the Leader of the IAAS, Property & Casualty Actuarial Advisory practice. Put simply, our actuarial advisers deliver creative and innovative thinking to high-profile property and casualty insurance companies worldwide, supporting them in making confident financial judgments in support of their business goals. Here, you will: Deliver EY’s actuarial transformation and premium acceleration service offerings Develop efficiencies throughout the practice Streamline the operational aspects of the group Your key responsibilities As a Manager within the Insurance Actuarial Advisory Services Property & Casualty practice you will be challenged to participate on engagement teams focused on several of the following areas: Reserving, actuarial opinion and regulatory services Predictive Modeling and customer analytics Actuarial Transformation® and modeling Rate setting all lines of business Financial forecasting, valuation Product development Mentor and develop junior team members Skillset and attributes for success Effectively manage and motivate client engagement teams with diverse skills and backgrounds. Demonstrate in-depth technical capabilities and professional knowledge. Demonstrate ability to quickly assimilate new knowledge. Possess good business acumen and remain current on new developments in advisory services capabilities and industry knowledge. Develop a strong personal brand and enhance the EY brand by writing articles, participating in industry groups and speaking at seminars. Cultivate a good working relationship with the senior managers, fellow managers, and staff on the team. To qualify for the role, you must have: Bachelor's degree in Actuarial Science, Mathematics, Statistics, and/or other related majors. Approximately 6+ years of experience in an actuarial-oriented position. A minimum of 2 years of project or people management responsibility. Associate of the Casualty Actuarial Society required, Fellowship preferred. At least 3 years of experience with Excel, SAS, VBA and/or C++. Strong writing, presentation, conceptual, analytical, and interpersonal skills as evidenced by preparing and presenting results and/or findings to clients or senior management. Strong organizational skills. Self-motivated. Ability to understand and persuade on complex subjects both in writing and verbally. Experience working in a team environment on project-based work. Ideally, you will also have Previous consulting experience or experience implementing new processes/procedures in a corporate environment. Experience with loss reserving software. Experience with rating engines and platforms. 2 years working with advanced analytics including knowledge of R, SQL, Python or similar data and analytics programming languages. What we look for We are most interested in intellectually curious people with a clear passion for actuarial work. You will also need to be a great communicator, as well as flexible in the face of often rapidly changing projects and engagements.

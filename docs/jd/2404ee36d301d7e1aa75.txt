Divisional Profile When clients need capital, Global Capital Markets (GCM) responds with market judgment and ingenuity. Whether executing an IPO, debt offering, securitization, or structured financing transaction, GCM integrates Morgan Stanley's expertise across Investment Banking and Sales & Trading to deliver seamless advice and sophisticated financing solutions. Securitized Products Capital Markets (SPCM) originates, structures, and executes asset-backed and structured finance transactions across a wide range of asset classes. The business works closely with corporate clients, financial sponsors, institutional investors, and banking coverage teams to develop innovative capital solutions and optimize financing strategies. Role Description Morgan Stanley is seeking a Vice President to join the Securitized Products Capital Markets team, with a focus on Data Center Finance. The team advises leading data center operators, digital infrastructure platforms, hyperscale sponsors, private equity firms, and investors on structured financing solutions to support the rapidly growing demand for digital infrastructure. This role offers the opportunity to work at the intersection of securitized products, infrastructure finance, private credit, and capital markets. The Vice President will lead transaction execution, support client coverage efforts, develop innovative financing solutions, and collaborate across Investment Banking, Fixed Income, Infrastructure, and Sponsor Coverage teams.

At Nasdaq, you'll have the chance to start your career in an environment where learning, teamwork, and innovation come first. I n this role, you'll support our Equity Analysis team at Nasdaq Dorsey Wright and gain hands-on experience with investment research, financial markets, and the intersection of regulation and policy — giving you a strong foundation for a career in finance. You don't need to know everything on day one — we'll help you build the skills, confidence, and network to grow your career. What You'll Do Support senior analysts in evaluating and monitoring investment managers and related recommendations. Assist in generating and updating research reports, quantitative analyses, and presentations. Contribute to primary research by engaging with industry contacts and building market intelligence. Monitor and analyze developments in financial markets Collaborate with teammates across functions to share insights and contribute to ongoing investment initiatives. What We're Looking For Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Finance, Business, Engineering, or a related field. Eagerness to learn, ask questions, and take on new challenges in a fast-paced environment. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to interpret complex financial and regulatory documents. Good organizational skills and attention to detail. Curiosity about global markets, investment products, and financial research. Nice to Have Internship or academic project experience related to equity research or financial analysis. Familiarity with financial data tools or platforms (e.g., Excel, Bloomberg). Involvement in finance-related student organizations or extracurricular activities. This position is located in Richmond, Virginia and offers the opportunity for a hybrid work environment at least 3 days a week in-office, subject to change, providing flexibility and accessibility for qualified candidates.

Business Insights Analyst- Branch Network Distribution
Citizens Financial Group
Location: United States
Link: https://hcgn.fa.us2.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1/job/50057

Position Overview Citizens Bank is executing a multi-year transformation of its retail branch network, shifting toward a performance-driven, growth-oriented distribution model and investing in branch reformats, refreshes, closures, de novos, staffing models, and operating-hour changes. The Business Analyst, Branch Network Distribution will support the reporting, analytics, experimentation, and impact measurement used to understand network performance and evaluate whether branch actions are delivering their intended outcomes. The analyst will work with more experienced team members to prepare data, build recurring reporting, conduct structured analyses, and translate findings into clear business insights. This is a hands-on developmental role for an early-career analyst who is eager to build technical, analytical, and business skills while supporting visible priorities across Distribution Transformation, Retail Banking, Marketing, Finance, and the Consumer Bank. Key Responsibilities Network Performance Reporting & Insights Support the development and maintenance of reporting and dashboards that measure branch network performance using leading and lagging indicators. Prepare performance views across banker, branch, market, and enterprise levels to support day-to-day management and longer-term decisions. Analyze trends, risks, and opportunities and summarize findings for review by senior team members and business partners. Branch Action Analytics & Impact Measurement Support analyses that measure the results of branch network actions, including reformats, refreshes, closures, de novos, staffing changes, and changes to operating hours. Assess outcomes across customer behavior, productivity, financial performance, and growth using established analytical approaches. Help account for factors such as market conditions, seasonality, and overlapping initiatives, with guidance from experienced analysts. Experimentation & Test-and-Learn Support Assist in developing pilots and tests with clear objectives, success measures, comparison groups, and evaluation plans. Prepare analyses that help determine whether an initiative should be expanded, refined, or discontinued. Document methods, assumptions, and results so conclusions are transparent and repeatable. Physical Network Marketing Value Analysis Support analysis of the branch network as a customer acquisition, relationship-deepening, and brand asset. Help assess how branch presence, format, and investment relate to customer behavior across channels and over time. Partner with Marketing, Finance, and Distribution colleagues to incorporate relevant data into growth and investment analyses. Data Preparation & Analytical Execution Build working knowledge of branch, banker productivity, customer, marketing, and financial data. Use SQL and tools such as Alteryx, or equivalent tools, to source, cleanse, join, and prepare analytical datasets. Complete quality checks and help maintain consistent metrics, definitions, assumptions, and documentation across analyses and reporting. Business Intelligence & Tool Support Build and maintain recurring reports, dashboards, and data visualizations using established data models and team standards. Help improve the automation, performance, usability, and documentation of reporting assets. Learn and apply business intelligence, visualization, and emerging analytical tools to improve productivity while following applicable governance requirements. Business Partnering & Communication Work with team members and business partners to clarify questions, gather requirements, and translate business needs into structured analysis. Prepare clear presentations, summaries, and supporting materials that explain findings, implications, and limitations. Respond thoughtfully to feedback and refine analyses and reporting based on stakeholder needs. Project Support & Continuous Improvement Manage assigned work, meet agreed timelines, and provide clear updates on progress, questions, and risks. Contribute to team projects by completing defined analytical workstreams with guidance and review. Identify practical opportunities to improve data quality, repeatability, speed, and clarity. Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, Economics, Analytics, Statistics, Data Science, or a related field. 0-3 years of experience in analytics, business intelligence, finance, strategy, or a related role; relevant internships, academic projects, or co-op experience may be considered. Foundational SQL skills and experience working with structured data; exposure to Alteryx or a similar data preparation tool is helpful. Experience with a business intelligence or visualization platform through work, internships, or academic projects. Working knowledge of analytical methods, including trend analysis, performance measurement, and basic test or comparison design. Ability to organize data, check work carefully, and communicate findings in a clear and concise manner. Interest in financial services, retail banking, customer analytics, or physical distribution is helpful but not required. Leadership & Soft Skills Analytically curious - asks thoughtful questions, looks for patterns, and is eager to understand what the data means Detail-oriented - produces accurate work and checks data, assumptions, and outputs carefully. Clear communicator - explains analysis in straightforward language and adapts based on feedback. Collaborative - works effectively with teammates and business partners and contributes positively to team outcomes. Organized and accountable - manages assigned work, follows through on commitments, and raises questions early. Growth-oriented - seeks feedback, develops new technical and business skills, and applies lessons to future work.

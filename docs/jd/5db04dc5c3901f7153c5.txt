Position Overview We are seeking an Electrical Maintenance Technician to support electrical and instrumentation maintenance activities. This role is responsible for ensuring stable plant operations through power distribution facility management, troubleshooting equipment malfunctions, and supporting automation systems including PLC and DCS. Due to the nature of the work, this position requires a strong commitment to safety, responsibility, and compliance with established safety procedures. Key Responsibilities Manage and maintain electrical, instrumentation, and PLC-based automation systems Troubleshoot equipment malfunctions and perform repairs and improvements Document root cause analysis and corrective actions Perform general electrical maintenance activities Maintain instrumentation devices such as PT, TT, LT, FT, control valves, and sensors Maintain PLC, DCS, and HMI systems Inspect high-voltage electrical systems, MCC panels, and electrical components Diagnose electrical issues using appropriate testing equipment Perform switchgear, panel, and circuit work in compliance with electrical safety procedures Inspect and replace aged wiring and insulation cables to prevent failures Analyze equipment operational data to improve reliability Execute preventive maintenance activities Select and manage critical spare parts Minimum Qualifications Associate degree or higher (preferred majors: Electrical, Electronics, Automation, etc.) Basic understanding of in factory automation systems (PLC, DCS, HMI) (AB PLC, Siemens DCS, LS Electric PLC, etc.) Experience handling or maintaining instrumentation devices such as PT, TT, LT, FT, and control valves

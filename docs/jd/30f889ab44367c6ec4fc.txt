The role is responsible for developing and maintaining strong relationship with SCOR carrier clients, with a particular focus on large case and concierge teams. This position provides underwriting consultation and guidance to client companies, communicates underwriting decisions, fields inquiries, and exercises sound judgement in decision-making. Providing underwriting strategies and alternative solutions for challenging risks, while working on internal projects and guideline development are required. Apply sophisticated underwriting procedures to assess complex and/or medical and large case financial risks for facultative clients. Interact with clients to secure specialized or supplementary information; develops alternative solutions when routine/usual methods do not work and provides excellent customer service through understanding and innovation. Communicate with all levels of client’s underwriters and other company management personnel, explaining the reasoning and theory which surrounds an underwriting impairment, financial challenge and/or decision. Keep up to date with current medical topics, current financial documentation and business environment as well as changing / new financial evaluation concepts and measures, data sources and their use in case underwriting. Contribute to the development of underwriting approaches for high-net-worth and business insurance cases, while ensuring underwriting consistency. Experience with financial underwriting for estate planning, business insurance, premium financing, foreign national risk and high–net-worth markets. Function effectively and professionally within a team with little supervision while striving for continuous growth. Understand and effectively implement all SCOR Global Life Americas retention and retrocession schedules. Project work is required to enhance SCOR underwriting and client knowledge, to stay on top of client requests and be proactive in client communication. Demonstrate a willingness and desire to leverage Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools and technologies in daily underwriting processes and case review, actively seeking opportunities to enhance efficiency, accuracy and decision-making through innovation and digital solutions. Work on SOLEM guidance; underwriting data review and analysis to manage complex underwriting and business challenges. Other duties as required for the position.

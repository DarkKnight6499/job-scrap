As a National Accounts Associate at Nasdaq Dorsey Wright you'll play a meaningful role in supporting and growing Nasdaq's relationships with key enterprise and home office partners. You'll contribute to business development efforts, coordinate cross-functional initiatives, and help ensure our partners receive a best-in-class experience. You'll thrive in this position if you're organized, collaborative, and client-focused — with a passion for financial services and a drive to deliver results in a fast-paced, global environment. Key Responsibilities Support national account relationships by coordinating client meetings, due diligence discussions, and follow-up activities that strengthen partner engagement. Advance sales opportunities by assisting account executives throughout the full cycle — from initial outreach through implementation. Collaborate across Sales, Marketing, Product, Operations, and Client Success teams to respond to partner inquiries and support platform initiatives. Track and report on partnership activity, advisor adoption, pipeline metrics, and platform utilization to inform strategic decisions. Support the planning and execution of industry conferences and partner events, including attendee coordination and post-event follow-up. Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in business, finance, marketing, or a related field. At least 3 years of experience in account management, sales support, or a related client-facing role. Strong organizational and communication skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a collaborative environment. Experience maintaining records and tracking activity in a CRM platform such as Salesforce. Ability to develop clear presentations and reports for both internal leadership and external client audiences. Preferred Qualifications Experience in financial services, fintech, or a platform -based business . Familiarity with enterprise sales processes or distribution partnerships. Exposure to cross-functional teams and the ability to coordinate across multiple stakeholders. This position will be located in New York or Philadelphia and offers the opportunity for a hybrid work environment at least 3 days a week in-office, subject to change, providing flexibility and accessibility for qualified candidates.

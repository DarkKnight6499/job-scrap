We are seeking an exceptiona l Revenue and Pricing Finance Analyst to join our Sales Finance Revenue Analytics team, based in our Santa Clara office. As part of the Product FP&A team, you will support critical activities and data products related to revenue, pricing, and rebates. This is a unique opportunity requiring proven experience with financial concepts, business processes, and data analysis. What you’ll be doing: Develop and maintain data models for pricing and rebate analysis, consolidating and curating data from multiple systems into a single source of truth. Collaborate with multi-functional teams to find opportunities for process optimization, automation, and standardization. Prepare revenue analysis and reporting, maintaining the highest standards of data integrity, quality, and compliance with relevant regulations. Evolve reporting processes from ad-hoc analyses and prototypes to production-ready, systematized workflows. Develop a thorough understanding of our business and processes to inform our data strategy. What we need to see: 5+ years of experience in Finance, FP&A, or a similar function (e.g. Business Operations) supporting top line revenue. Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience in Finance, Economics, Business Analytics, Data Science, or Engineering. MBA or equivalent experience is a plus. Experience and proficiency with Excel, SQL, ETL development, BI Tools (e.g. Tableau or PowerBI), and Data Platforms (e.g. Databricks or Snowflake). Advanced financial modeling and reconciliation skills, with the ability to move fluidly between detailed analysis and big-picture storytelling. Highly motivated self-starter with the ability to work independently in a fast-paced and ambiguous environment, managing multiple priorities and functional areas. Demonstrable ability to communicate effectively to audiences across a wide spectrum of seniority and technical acumen. Ways to stand out from the crowd: Industry-specific experience in computer hardware or data center components. Experience applying accounting principles to revenue reporting and analytics. Experience with quarter-end activities, external reporting, audit compliance, or SOX controls. Knowledge or experience in Salesforce, SAP COPA, SAP SD, SAP BPC.

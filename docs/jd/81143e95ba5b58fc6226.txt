The Team Upstart’s Compliance team helps ensure the company operates in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements and maintains effective risk management practices. As part of the second line of defense, the team partners with business and operations stakeholders to oversee compliance issues, strengthen remediation practices, and support regulatory readiness. As the Senior Compliance Associate, Issues Management at Upstart, you will support the end-to-end compliance issue management lifecycle, from identification and tracking through remediation validation and closure. You will help strengthen program effectiveness by identifying emerging risks, improving issue reporting, and ensuring remediation efforts are timely, well-documented, and sustainable. How you’ll make an impact Execute second-line compliance issue management activities, including issue intake, documentation, tracking, remediation monitoring, validation, and closure. Review and challenge remediation plans, milestones, and supporting evidence to ensure corrective actions address identified issues and meet established requirements. Maintain accurate issue inventories, monitor remediation progress and aging, and proactively identify delays or high-risk issues requiring escalation. Support independent validation and closure activities by evaluating remediation evidence, documenting findings, and confirming closure requirements have been met. Analyze issue trends, recurring themes, and root causes to deliver reporting and insights that inform compliance oversight and risk-based prioritization. Partner with Engineering, Operations, Legal, and Compliance stakeholders to improve remediation quality, strengthen issue management procedures, and support regulatory readiness. Minimum Qualifications Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience. 3+ years of experience in compliance, issue management, audit, or risk management within banking or financial services. Experience supporting the lifecycle of compliance, risk, audit, or regulatory issues, including issue tracking and remediation monitoring. Experience reviewing remediation plans, corrective action milestones, supporting documentation, or closure evidence. Experience maintaining issue records or inventories within a regulated risk or compliance environment. Preferred Qualifications Knowledge of second-line compliance oversight, issue management frameworks, and remediation validation practices within financial services. Experience using governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) platforms or issue management systems. Ability to analyze issue aging, remediation progress, and recurring root causes to identify emerging risks and inform reporting. Ability to collaborate across Compliance, Risk, Audit, Legal, and business teams to navigate remediation challenges and improve issue resolution. Certified Regulatory Compliance Manager (CRCM), Certified Internal Auditor (CIA), or another relevant professional certification.

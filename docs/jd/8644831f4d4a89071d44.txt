Job Description Ares Secondaries has pioneered and innovated within secondary markets across three decades and a range of alternative asset classes, including private equity, real estate, infrastructure, and credit. Over the course of our 30-year history in this space, we have invested across thousands of partnership interests, driving highly differentiated and proprietary transactions through our deeply established relationships with institutional investors, fund sponsors, and market advisors. Today, Ares Secondaries has more than 150 professionals across six offices in New York, Boston, Dallas, London, Hong Kong, and West Hartford, Connecticut. Ares Secondaries is a market leader across GP-led, LP-led, and preferred equity structured transactions. As the team continues to grow, the firm is currently looking to hire an Associate to join our Real Estate Secondaries Investment Team in our Boston office. Key Responsibilities Evaluate real estate secondary investment opportunities through detailed due diligence, market research, benchmarking, and financial modeling. Conduct quantitative and qualitative analysis of real estate limited partnership interests, GP-led transactions, continuation vehicles, and underlying real estate assets and portfolios. Analyze property-level and fund-level performance across a variety of sectors, including multifamily, industrial, office, retail, hospitality, self-storage, data centers, and other real estate asset classes. Prepare and present investment recommendations to investment committees and the broader investment team. Support the investment team in transaction execution, portfolio monitoring, asset management oversight, and ongoing valuation analysis of existing investments. Build industry relationships by engaging with real estate fund sponsors, operating partners, advisors, consultants, and attending industry conferences and sponsor meetings. Education / Experience Required 2–4 years of experience in real estate secondaries, real estate private equity, investment banking, principal investing, or other real estate-focused investment roles. Strong academic record demonstrated leadership abilities, and excellent communication and relationship-building skills. Advanced financial analysis, valuation, and modeling capabilities, including property-level cash flow modeling, DCF analysis, NAV analysis, portfolio valuations, and fund waterfall structures. Familiarity with real estate investment concepts, transaction structures, and market dynamics across private real estate funds and direct property investments.

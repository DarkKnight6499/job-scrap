COMPANY OVERVIEW KKR is a leading global investment firm that offers alternative asset management as well as capital markets and insurance solutions. KKR aims to generate attractive investment returns by following a patient and disciplined investment approach, employing world-class people, and supporting growth in its portfolio companies and communities. KKR sponsors investment funds that invest in private equity, credit and real assets and has strategic partners that manage hedge funds. KKR’s insurance subsidiaries offer retirement, life and reinsurance products under the management of Global Atlantic Financial Group. References to KKR’s investments may include the activities of its sponsored funds and insurance subsidiaries. TEAM OVERVIEW KKR’s Enterprise Risk Management (“ERM”) function is responsible for ensuring the consistent application of the risk management framework globally to help KKR achieve its strategic objectives and operational efficiency while operating within the firm’s risk appetite. ERM’s objectives: Identify and monitor risk from the firm’s verticals to understand global, horizontal risk trends and issues Assess and measure risks, control gaps or enhancements for risk relevant programs Aggregate risk information for monitoring and reporting; inform risk-based decision making With a focus on creating a proactive risk management culture, embedding risk awareness into decision making processes across the firm, as part of our global organization, you will have the opportunity to contribute to an integrated approach to risk management, helping to shape our firm’s approach to risk - making a meaningful impact on our ability to navigate an increasingly complex risk landscape. POSITION OVERVIEW KKR is seeking a high-performing Technology & Data Risk leader to join its Enterprise Risk Leadership Team within the Risk Domain Office. This role will be based in our New York office, 5 days per week. The Risk Domain Office is responsible for providing independent oversight of the firm’s most significant enterprise risk domains and developing an integrated view of risk across businesses, functions, and geographies. As a core component of KKRs Enterprise Risk Operating Model, the team plays a critical role in identifying emerging risks, synthesizing cross-risk insights, strengthening enterprise risk oversight, and supporting informed decision-making at the executive and board levels. This role is a senior leadership position within the Risk Domain Office and will play a central role in shaping the continued evolution of KKR’s risk domain oversight capabilities. Working in close partnership with Enterprise Risk leadership, the successful candidate will help define priorities, mature oversight practices, strengthen enterprise risk integration, and drive a proactive and insight-driven approach to risk management across the firm. The successful candidate will provide direct oversight of one or more enterprise risk domains, with a particular focus on Technology and Data Risk, while also maintaining an enterprise-wide perspective across the broader risk landscape. They will work closely with business leaders, technology leadership, control functions, regional risk teams, and governance forums to identify emerging risks, challenge risk management practices, and provide integrated risk perspectives that support informed decision making. This role offers broad exposure across KKR’s global businesses, including Asset Management, Insurance, Strategic Holdings, and Corporate Functions. The successful candidate will be a well-rounded risk leader with strong risk management expertise, sound judgment, and the ability to influence stakeholders across a complex organization. They will be expected to exercise independent judgment, provide effective challenge, and translate complex and interconnected risks into clear, actionable insights for senior management, risk committees, and board-level governance forums. This is a unique opportunity to play a foundational leadership role within a growing enterprise risk function, helping shape the future development of KKR’s Risk Domain Office and strengthening how the firm understands, oversees, and manages risk at scale.

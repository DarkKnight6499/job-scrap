Position Summary The hiring manager will also consider candidates for the Third Party Risk Management Analyst I (F08), Third Party Risk Management Analyst II (F11) and Senior Third Party Risk Management Analyst (F13). The level of position offered will depend on the depth and bread of the selected candidate's qualifications and experience. Under moderate supervision, assesses the risks of doing business with third parties. Works with Sourcing & Vendor Management, business areas, and other stakeholders to manage risks. Duties and Responsibilities Conducts risk assessments that identify, analyze, mitigate, and report on the risks associated with products and services provided to ERIE by third parties. Collaborates with internal and external partners to ensure risks are assessed and mitigated. Maintains knowledge and understanding of best practices to control privacy, compliance, security, operational resiliency, and other risks, and applies them when assessing third parties. Monitors ERIE’s third parties for changes in their risks and recommends actions to manage the risks. Examines potential impacts from vulnerabilities to software and hardware used by ERIE’s third parties as well as incidents ERIE’s third parties have experienced and recommends responses. Identifies improvements to Third Party Risk Management Program and processes and participates in or leads their implementation. Participates in the training and mentoring of newer third party risk analysts. The first three duties listed are the functions identified as essential to the job.

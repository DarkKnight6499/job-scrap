Overview COMPANY OVERVIEW Pattern Energy is a leading renewable energy company that develops, constructs, owns, and operates high-quality wind and solar generation, transmission, and energy storage facilities. Our mission is to transition the world to renewable energy through the sustainable development and responsible operation of facilities with respect for the environment, communities, and cultures where we have a presence. Our approach begins and ends with establishing trust, accountability, and transparency. Our company values of creative spirit, pride of ownership, follow-through, and a team-first attitude drive us to pursue our mission every day. Our culture supports our values by fostering innovative and critical thinking and a deep belief in living up to our promises. Headquartered in the United States, Pattern has a global portfolio of more than 46 power facilities and transmission assets, serving various customers that provide low-cost clean energy to millions of consumers. Responsibilities JOB PURPOSE The Pricing & Structuring Analyst supports the growth and optimization of Pattern Energy’s renewable energy portfolio through pricing, valuation, market analytics, risk analysis, and deal structuring support for power, capacity, renewable energy certificates, and power purchase agreement transactions. This role partners cross-functionally with Asset Management, Trading, Origination, Market Fundamentals, Risk, Finance, Legal, Operations, Grid Integration, and Enterprise Technology to evaluate commercial opportunities, portfolio exposures, hedge strategies, and data-driven recommendations for business stakeholders. Key Accountabilities Support pricing, valuation, and deal structuring for structured power transactions, including physical and financial hedges, PPAs, capacity products, RECs, and other environmental attributes. Analyze market risks, including energy, basis, shape, congestion, weather, optionality, volumetric exposure, liquidity, and renewable intermittency. Build and maintain risk-adjusted pricing, revenue forecasting, hedge effectiveness, scenario analysis, and portfolio optimization models aligned with approved methodologies, portfolio strategy, and risk appetite. Analyze forward markets, ISO/RTO data, nodal pricing, renewable generation shapes, weather-driven impacts, and portfolio performance to identify material risks, changes, and optimization opportunities. Support risk management and hedge strategy analysis for power, capacity, and environmental markets. Partner with Enterprise Technology and commercial stakeholders to define requirements and improve analytics tools, dashboards, reporting infrastructure, ETRM workflows, pricing automation, reporting transparency, and data quality. Translate complex market data and risk drivers into practical commercial insights and recommendations for internal stakeholders. Qualifications Experience/Qualifications/Education Required Educational and Experience Requirements Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, Energy Management, or a related field. At least two years of relevant experience in power markets, commercial analytics, trading, origination, risk management, asset management, energy structuring, or a related analytical field. Working knowledge of U.S. wholesale power markets, ISO/RTO operations, forward markets, congestion, ancillary services, and settlement mechanics. Experience supporting pricing of energy transactions, hedges, PPAs, renewable products, retail supply, or asset-backed commercial structures. Strong quantitative modeling skills, including the ability to analyze and synthesize large, complex data sets quickly and accurately. Proficiency with Excel and at least one analytics tool such as Python, SQL, R, VBA, Power Query, Tableau, Power BI, or Alteryx. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to communicate analytical results clearly and effectively. Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously, prioritize work, and support ad hoc analysis on a regular basis. Additional Requirements Skills & Ability: Preferred experience in one or more of PJM, ERCOT, CAISO, MISO, or SPP. Experience supporting renewable generation, battery storage, hybrid assets, or load-serving obligations. Familiarity with RECs, renewable portfolio standard compliance, carbon products, and other environmental attribute markets. Exposure to ETRM platforms, preferably Allegro or an equivalent system.

Your Role As an Associate Data Developer , you will support Wind River’s enterprise data warehouse and analytics platform, helping deliver data, metrics, dashboards, and actionable business insights across the organization. You will work with business and executive stakeholders to understand requirements and help design the data path from ingestion through modeling and visualization. The role combines data development, analytics, business intelligence, and automation, with a focus on creating scalable solutions and following data engineering and analytics best practices. Responsibilities Analyze complex data and deliver actionable business insights to cross-functional teams. Work with stakeholders to gather requirements and translate business needs into data and analytics solutions. Build dashboards and visualizations using Tableau or other modern BI tools. Support data flows from ingestion through transformation, modeling, and visualization. Perform data cleaning and modeling when required data is not available in the visualization layer. Develop and maintain solutions using SQL, Python, and dbt . Create tools, processes, and automation that reduce the burden on the data stack and support stakeholder requirements. Monitor new analytics initiatives, identify barriers to adoption, and support corrective actions. Promote data engineering and analytics best practices and collaborate with junior engineers. Your Background Bachelor's degree in Computer Science; emphasis in Data Engineering is a plus. 1+ years of experience with Tableau or another modern BI tool . 1+ year of SQL experience; Snowflake SQL is preferred. 1+ year of experience with dbt ; semantic layer experience is a plus. 2+ years of Python development experience. Experience with enterprise systems such as Workday, Oracle Business Suite (OBI/HRIS), Salesforce, Jira, Adaptive, or Anaplan is a plus. HTML and JavaScript experience as related to Looker is preferred. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to take ownership and initiative. Ability to work independently and collaborate effectively with cross-functional and global teams. Fluent written and verbal communication skills.

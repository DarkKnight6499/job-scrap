The Opportunity The BL Risk Testing Analyst II plays a key role in supporting the development and execution of business process testing and monitoring activities for the Insurance business line. The position is responsible for executing testing and monitoring of assigned areas. Execution of testing includes identifying and assessing process risks and controls, flowcharting business processes including identifying and evaluating the design and operational effectiveness of controls, executing tests, documenting results, assessing review findings and making initial determination of reportable issues. During the process, completes work in accordance with Ally Financial testing and monitoring methodology. The Work Itself Independently execute assigned testing activities, including test of controls and validation/transactional testing Support program planning and execution Develop, execute and document testing in accordance with policies and procedures Utilize sound analytical and technical skills to provide consultation regarding emerging control practices. Analyze business process and internal control documentation and develop written summary of findings to review with Management. Identify root causes and provide meaningful recommendations. Perform ad hoc projects as assigned to assist in analyzing Ally's risk and control environment. Work within defined plans and schedules. Maintain open line of communication with management of assigned areas The Qualifications We’re Looking For 1+ years of work experience in relevant field required, experience in testing, auditing or risk management preferred Bachelors (or higher) degree in business required or relevant experience can be used in lieu of degree (including military service) CPA or CIA preferred Familiarity with Ally Insurance products or experience with F&I and/or P&C business preferred Strong project management skills Detail-oriented, deadline driven, self-directed and organized individual Strong written and verbal communications skills Strong understanding of risk and control concepts Ability to understand a range of processes within the Insurance business line Well-developed analytical and critical thinking skills Experience in data analytics and analysis Proven ability to excel in highly interactive team environment Working knowledge of Microsoft Office applications (Word, Excel, and PowerPoint) #LI-Hybrid #DFS Minimum Qualifications 1+ years of experience High school Diploma or GED equivalent How We'll Have Your Back Ally's compensation program offers market-competitive base pay and pay-for-performance incentives (bonuses) based on achieving personal and company goals.

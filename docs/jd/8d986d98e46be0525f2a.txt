Automation Associate Automation Core Build our future together: The Automation Core is a centralized high-throughput core laboratory that provides automation services to research groups and technology centers across Research & pre-clinical Development (R&pD). We are seeking an enthusiastic and hard-working R&D Automation Associate to join the Development team of the Automation Core, where you will be responsible for implementing and optimizing automated processes to address evolving scientific challenges. The ideal candidate will be passionate about laboratory automation technologies and eager to collaborate across diverse scientific and engineering functions to achieve our organizational goals. When & where: Location: Tarrytown, NY Discover your role: Creating, modifying, and troubleshooting robotic methods on liquid handling devices (E.g., Hamilton STAR, etc.), integrated systems (E.g., HighRes, etc.), and supporting devices. Transferring and revalidating established processes to other Automation Core systems. Conducting validation testing of protocols for release into production operations. Utilizing JIRA to accurately document and manage assigned tasks and projects. Contributing to development team discussions and presenting work at department meetings. This role requires: Can work independently on defined tasks and projects with guidance from senior colleagues. Enjoy collaborating with cross-functional teams in a dynamic research environment. Have strong organizational and communication skills to escalate technical and project-related issues. Possess good problem-solving skills necessary to troubleshoot system problems. Have a positive attitude to learn and recommend improvements to existing processes. A minimum Bachelor's degree in life science, engineering, computer science, or related field with 0-3 years of relevant experience working with automation systems within a laboratory environment. Applicable internship experience or a strong understanding of molecular biology, flow cytometry, genomic, or cell-based assays are highly beneficial for entry level candidates. Preferred: Experience in programming and operating Hamilton liquid handling instruments. Preferred: Knowledge of integrated systems. Preferred: Programming experience utilizing Python or C#.

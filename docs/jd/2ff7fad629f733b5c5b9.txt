Role Overview: The Capability Strategy Lead defines and evolves the foundational business and digital capabilities required to deliver a best-in-class advisor experience – today and in the future. This role takes a holistic, enterprise view across people, process, technology, and data to ensure FX is intentionally building the capabilities needed to execute strategy, rather than delivering disconnected solutions. What You'll Do: Capability Framework & Assessment • Define core FX capabilities aligned to strategic priorities and advisor needs. • Assess current-state maturity and identify structural capability gaps. • Establish clear capability definitions and success criteria. Alignment & Integration • Ensure capability development is coordinated across initiatives and teams. • Identify dependencies and integration risks early. • Support consistent execution through alignment with program and product leaders.

Job Summary: The Sr. Manager, Solution Sales, Risk-as-a-Service is part of the North America sales team, partnering with Account Management to grow adoption & transaction margin for PayPal's RaaS solutions across new and existing accounts. These solutions apply AI-driven risk models, built on transaction data from across PayPal's global network, to help merchants protect revenue while preserving approval rates. Fraud Protection Advanced puts fraud decisioning directly in the merchant's hands with expert support, Chargeback Protection has PayPal assume that risk on eligible transactions, and Dispute Automation centralizes how merchants manage and contest disputes across payment providers. This is a consultative sales role. The Specialist lead with discovery, diagnosing each merchant's specific needs before designing the right solution & positioning differentiated value against competitors, from opportunity through activation. Success is measured by profitable volume within assigned verticals. Job Description: Essential Responsibilities: Lead complex projects of diverse scope requiring an understanding of current business trends. Participate in complex problem resolution and determine methods for new assignments. Implement initiatives that result in global process improvements. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to drive product sales strategies. Provide expertise in product knowledge and market insights to enhance sales efforts. Minimum Qualifications: 8+ years relevant experience and a Bachelor’s degree OR Any equivalent combination of education and experience. A consistent track record of exceeding quota targets. A strong analytical mind, with the ability to build ROI-based business cases and evidence-based sales narratives. Experience selling or knowledge of omni-channel and in-store payments solutions. Expertise in getting the most out of AI, using AI tools to accelerate discovery, research, solutioning, and pipeline analysis, not just familiarity with them. Familiarity with Salesforce CRM. Financial analysis experience. MBA or other advanced degree in Finance, Business, or a related field.

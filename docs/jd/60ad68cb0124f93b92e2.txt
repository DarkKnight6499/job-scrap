Summary The Risk Operations Support Manager leads the operational governance activities that support the Model Risk Management program. This role manages governance processes, related technology and data initiatives, and a team responsible for maintaining consistent, timely, and well-documented model risk practices across the organization. The manager partners with model owners and users, business and risk stakeholders, technology teams, and senior governance committees to support effective oversight and informed decision-making. Duties & Responsibilities Lead and coordinate Model Risk Management governance activities, including governance standards, procedures, committee support, inventory governance, and program reporting. Manage MRM projects and cross-functional initiatives, including project planning, requirements, milestones, risks, dependencies, stakeholder communication, and implementation follow-through. Oversee the Archer platform and related MRM workflows, data quality, user requirements, reporting, enhancements, access coordination, and issue resolution in partnership with technology and platform teams. Manage model risk issues and remediation governance, including issue management, tracking, aging, escalation, status reporting, evidence review, and preparation for closure consideration. Oversee ongoing monitoring governance to support timely completion, consistent standards, escalation of exceptions, and clear reporting of model performance and control results. Lead model and data documentation initiatives, including documentation standards, inventories, data lineage and source information, governance records, and coordination with model owners to address documentation needs. Coordinate and help score model risk assessments, including risk identification, tiering and classification support, inventory maintenance, and periodic review activities. Support governance of end-user computing tools and artificial intelligence use cases, and represent Model Risk Management on the AI Governance Advisory Review Committee. Manage and develop direct reports and prepare materials, dashboards, metrics, and presentations for the Model Risk Management Committee and other senior management or governance forums; present topics as assigned. It is the responsibility of this role to take ownership of all tasks and challenges that they encounter in the operation of their assigned position. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions.

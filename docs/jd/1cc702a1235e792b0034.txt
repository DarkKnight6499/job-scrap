This position in the Valuation Advisory & Risk Solutions (VARS) department is an integral member of a team that provides valuations as a service to a client base the includes investment managers, depository institutions, and lenders. Asset classes covered include residential whole loans, consumer whole loans, structured finance securities, and other related fixed income assets. The successful applicant to this role will assist colleagues in performing valuations, maintaining valuation models, and other operational tasks as needed, while expanding their knowledge of the fundamentals of valuation and developing competencies in diverse asset classes. This role requires in office two days per week. Essential Job Functions: Produces valuation analyses of non-agency mortgage whole loans (non-QM, RTL, HELOC, 2nds, and jumbo), residential mortgage backed securities (RMBS), asset backed securities (ABS), and unsecured consumer loans for clients as needed with a high degree of competency, integrity, and customer service Assist in the maintenance of valuation models and data sets as needed. Manages and executes certain projects independently Such other activities as may be assigned by your manager Qualifications/ Requirements: Bachelor’s Degree in related field required CFA or demonstrated progress towards CFA designation preferred Mid-level professional with 2-4 years of industry and/or relevant experience, typically at an Analyst level role or external equivalent.

You will help shape how we listen to employees and turn feedback into meaningful action. In this role, you will collaborate with partners across Human Resources and the broader organization to deliver high-quality surveys and listening programs. Your work will support our Employee Opinion Survey and other life-cycle surveys, helping leaders understand culture, management practices, and employee experience. As a Senior Associate, Employee Voice within the HR CDAO organization, you will be responsible for supporting the firmwide employee listening strategy as a survey subject matter expert (SME). This person will partner with numerous stakeholders within the firm, as well as with vendors, in the design, implementation, ongoing administration and results analysis of surveys and other related listening projects. This role will be critical in supporting efforts for the firmwide Employee Opinion Survey and other life-cycle surveys. This person will take an active role in interpreting and communicating survey results across the firm and in developing actionable insights about the firm’s culture, management practices and employee experience. This position reports to the Employee Voice Data Management Director within the HR CDAO organization. You will collaborate closely with workforce data partners to integrate employee listening data with broader employee data platforms.

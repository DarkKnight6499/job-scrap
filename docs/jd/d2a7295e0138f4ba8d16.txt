Job Description What is the opportunity? RBC Capital Markets is seeking an experienced Java Developer with strong server-side expertise and low latency systems knowledge to join our Credit eTrading technology team. In this role, you will be instrumental in developing and maintaining enterprise-grade backend systems and server-side applications for Global Spread Products. You will work with a talented team of engineers to design, develop, and support mission-critical trading systems that power our business. Full stack capabilities are beneficial, but server-side development expertise is the primary mandate for this position. What will you do? You will be part of the Global Spread Products team within Credit eTrading, a fast-paced environment where your contributions directly impact trading operations and business outcomes Build and improve the scalable data platform that powers analytics and AI tools across our trading and banking divisions, managing data pipelines, analytics products, system performance, and cost efficiency Create standardized processes and operational approaches for eTrading workflows, including model packaging, deployment standards, and reproducible training methods You will develop performance-critical trading systems where low latency optimization is essential to our competitive advantage Partner with business teams to understand their needs, manage expectations, and ensure new products are adopted and scaled across the organization Collaborate with leadership to identify technical bottlenecks, recommend platform improvements, and prioritize work to maximize long-term efficiency What do you need to succeed? Must-have Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Engineering, Mathematics, or related field, or equivalent hands-on experience Minimum of 10 years of strong, hands-on experience developing Java and J2EE/JEE technologies with emphasis on server-side development. Proven track record of translating complex business problems into robust, well-tested data products used by demanding stakeholders Multithreading experience is mandatory, as our systems rely on concurrent processing Experience with low latency performance optimization and understanding techniques to minimize latency in high-frequency trading systems Ability to work effectively with both business teams and technical partners, handle competing priorities professionally, and keep projects aligned with business goals Nice-to-have Familiarity with capital markets operations, client engagement models, or investment banking workflows Familiarity with enterprise messaging systems such as Solace is highly valued Experience with entity resolution, graph-based data structures, or event-driven data products What’s in it for you?

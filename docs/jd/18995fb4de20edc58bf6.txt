Analyst – Corporate Risk Solutions Overview of the Department As an Analyst within the Corporate Risk Solutions Group, you’ll support our North American corporate clients in managing the interest rate and currency risks associated with their financing and broader business activities through derivatives. Our product offering includes interest rate swaps, cross-currency swaps, foreign exchange derivatives, and interest rate options. Main Purpose of the Role Support the Corporate Risk Solutions sales team in delivering a high-quality client experience and achieving the desk’s objectives, with primary responsibility for accurate and timely trade booking, execution support, post-trade coordination, and maintenance of robust operational controls. Key Responsibilities • Book derivative transactions accurately and promptly in relevant systems, verifying economics against confirmations, trader recaps, and client instructions and resolving discrepancies before downstream processing • Provide day-to-day execution and post-trade support, including trade capture, lifecycle-event coordination, settlement follow-up, documentation tracking, and investigation of booking or operational breaks • Coordinate with salespeople, traders, operations, legal, compliance, and other internal partners to ensure transactions progress efficiently from pricing and execution through confirmation and settlement • Support pricing requests and trade executions by gathering complete transaction details, preparing internal records, and communicating accurate information to relevant stakeholders Bachelor’s degree required • Strong attention to detail and a consistent focus on accuracy and operational controls • Ability to manage multiple time-sensitive tasks, prioritize effectively, and take ownership of items through completion • Clear written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to work effectively across sales, trading, operations, and control functions • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, including the ability to identify, investigate, and resolve trade or booking discrepancies • Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint highly preferred • Experience with Bloomberg beneficial • Fluency in English required The typical base pay range for this role is between $110K - $115K depending on job-related knowledge, skills, experience and location.

Who we are looking for: We are looking for a detail-oriented payments analyst to support implementation and subsequent BAU operations of the payment anomaly detection capability. The role will perform hands-on business analysis, rule validation, test execution, process documentation and operational readiness, then transition after go-live into day-to-day operations ,rule performance, exception and control support. Why this role is important to us? Anomaly detection must identify unusual payments without introducing unnecessary processing delays or additional approval, audit, or client-communication activities. This role provides the detailed analysis and validation required to make the capability accurate, usable and operationally sustainable. Join us if contributing to our Payments Business Risk and Resiliency Programs is a challenge you are up for. What you will be responsible for Document business requirements, user stories, process maps, decision tables and acceptance criteria under the direction of the workstream lead. Develop and maintain detailed rule definitions, thresholds, tolerances, reason codes, expected outcomes and test cases. Analyse sample and historical payment data to validate rule logic, identify data-quality issues and assess expected alert volumes and false-positive patterns. Execute functional, integration, regression and user acceptance tests across screening, decisioning, case management, UI, reporting and audit functions. Capture defects with reproducible evidence, support prioritisation and retest corrected functionality. Validate queue prioritisation, time-to-cut-off logic, risk tiers, alert grouping, case statuses, client-contact information, templated communications and implementing Agentic AI. Prepare standard operating procedures, quick-reference guides, training materials and operational playbooks for go-live. Support production-readiness activities, access testing, maker-checker controls, audit evidence and cutover planning. After go-live, monitor alerts, rule outcomes, false positives, exceptions, service levels and data issues; prepare analysis for tuning and governance. Align anomaly detection and cyber-response processes so suspicious or incorrect payment activity is triaged effectively during resilience events. Support high-volume events and post-event reviews, ensuring decisions, approvals and evidence are captured. What we value: These skills will help you succeed in this role: Payments operations or payments-control experience with strong analytical and documentation skills. Ability to interpret payment data, process flows, business rules and test results. Awareness of SWIFT or ISO 20022 messages, payment rails, cut-offs, screening or fraud controls is beneficial. Disciplined approach to testing, evidence capture, issue management and operational procedures. Strong communication, teamwork and attention to detail. Education & Preferred Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience in business, finance, data, technology or a related field. 5+ years of relevant financial services experience.

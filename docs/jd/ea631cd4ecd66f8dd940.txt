The IT Business Analyst is responsible for the discovery, analysis, documentation of GRSC/GRC business requirements, translating business needs into user stories and viable technical solutions. The role facilitates business and technical grooming sessions, evaluates feasibility and impacts, manages defects and enhancements, testing and production issue resolution/post Go-Live support, documents business processes and technical diagrams (current state and future state), with minimal supervision. They will work closely with internal business users, vendors, developers, and other IT partners to deliver effective system capabilities. They will follow prioritization set by the business/technical product owner for this function. The individual will also be asked to review client systems or processes to identify deficiencies and propose improvements to achieve better operational efficiency where possible without sacrificing quality. Principle Duties Execute assigned business projects on behalf of clients and according to client’s requirements, while following prescribed processes to complete tasks within a defined timeframe. Collect information about the client’s business through a variety of methods – documentation, meetings, and participating in discovery. Review and analyze moderate to complex reinsurance arrangements, large data sets and processes to identify patterns and root causes to ensure data quality and compliance. Gather business requirements, develop test plans, execute testing, and document results. Formulate recommendations and solutions with attention to a client’s needs, capabilities and limitations. Present findings and suggestions to clients with ample justification and practical advice.

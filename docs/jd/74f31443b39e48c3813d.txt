(Oppenheimer) is a leading middle-market investment bank and full service broker-dealer, With roots tracing back to 1881, the Company is engaged in a broad range of activities in the financial services industry, including retail securities brokerage, institutional sales and trading, investment banking (both corporate and public finance), equity & fixed income research, market making, trust services and investment advisory and asset management services. Our Investment Banking Team is committed to providing in-depth industry knowledge and delivering creative, market-based strategies to clients in all major industries, with a specific focus on Consumer and Retail, Energy, Financial Institutions, Healthcare, Industrial Growth and Services, Industrials, as well as Technology, Media and Communications. Job Description We are looking for a candidate to fill a Sr. Analyst / Associate position in our Debt Private Placements Group based in New York. This role presents an exciting opportunity to join a new and growing team led by an industry veteran with over 20 years of experience across private credit, hedge funds and investment banking. The recently launched Debt Private Placements Group will partner with Oppenheimers Industry Coverage bankers to raise capital for the firms investment banking clients via the private credit market across all sectors and credit asset classes. The position will serve as a critical member of the team, working directly with the Head of the Debt Private Placements and other senior professionals, as well as cross-functional Managing Directors, Executive Directors, and sector banking teams of all levels on pitches and engagements. The successful candidate will be given significant responsibility in transaction execution.

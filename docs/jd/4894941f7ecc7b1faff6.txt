We offer corporate and investment banking, treasury management, as well as research and advisory services to clients around the world. #bmocapitalmarkets The Corporate Hedging Solutions group is a team within Global Markets Global Fixed Income (GFI), responsible for originating and executing hedging and financing transactions for BMO’s corporate and commercial clients. Working closely with investment & corporate banking, the team serves as product experts with a coverage universe including interest rate, foreign exchange and equity derivatives, as well as structured solutions. Seeking a highly motivated and experienced Vice President to join the Corporate Hedging Solutions team, focusing on the marketing, origination and execution of interest rate derivative solutions within the U.S. universe of corporate clients. This individual will support client engagement and revenue generation through analysis, production of marketing and execution of customized interest rate derivative solutions for corporate clients. The ideal candidate has experience in fixed income markets, a strong analytical foundation, and a drive to win business and build relationships. In this role, you will support the coverage of key corporate client relationships, including day-to-day analysis, marketing, pitching and execution across interest rate products with near-term growth opportunities into primary coverage activities. You will need to be comfortable speaking to the corporate finance rationale behind hedging strategies, as well as the technical details underlying the various products, to effectively work with mid- to large-cap corporations across multiple sectors. You will work collaboratively with Investment & Corporate Banking, trading, structuring, credit & risk management, research and back office to provide comprehensive coverage and efficiently deploy the bank’s resources. Familiarity and experience in foreign exchange products and strategies is also beneficial. The role is based in New York, New York.

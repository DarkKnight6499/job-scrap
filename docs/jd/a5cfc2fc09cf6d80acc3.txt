Job Overview: The AI Governance organization is seeking an Assistant Vice President, AI Governance to lead the day-to-day execution and administration of the firm’s AI Governance Program. This leader will manage the end-to-end governance process for artificial intelligence use cases, including intake and workflow administration, risk assessment coordination, policy and standards management, committee governance, inventory oversight, approvals, issue tracking, reporting, and program enhancement. The Assistant Vice President will ensure AI initiatives are consistently identified, documented, assessed, reviewed, approved, monitored, and governed throughout their lifecycle. The role serves as the central coordination point across Technology, Information Security, Data Governance, Privacy, Compliance, Legal, Enterprise Risk Management, Internal Audit, and business stakeholders to enable responsible AI adoption while maintaining clear accountability and effective oversight. Roles & Responsibilities: Lead day-to-day execution of the enterprise AI Governance Program and support the program strategy, operating model, priorities, and capability roadmap. Own and administer the end-to-end AI use case intake, triage, review, approval, change, monitoring, and retirement workflow. Ensure AI use cases are appropriately documented, classified, assessed, approved, inventoried, and governed in accordance with policy and established risk-based requirements. Coordinate AI risk assessments across relevant subject matter experts and control functions, ensuring required reviews are completed and decisions, conditions, and residual risks are clearly documented. Manage the enterprise AI use case inventory, including data quality, ownership, status, risk classification, approvals, exceptions, dependencies, and lifecycle information. Lead the administration of AI governance committees and working groups, including agendas, materials, meeting facilitation, minutes, decision records, approvals, action items, and escalation tracking. Manage the AI governance policy, standards, procedures, and supporting guidance, including periodic reviews, stakeholder approvals, publication, communications, and implementation tracking. Oversee policy exception and governance waiver processes, ensuring requests are supported by appropriate rationale, risk acceptance, compensating controls, approvals, and expiration requirements. Design, maintain, and continuously improve governance workflows, roles, decision rights, service-level expectations, documentation standards, and escalation protocols. Monitor intake volumes, cycle times, assessment status, approvals, policy compliance, exceptions, remediation commitments, and other program indicators to identify trends and process improvements. Develop dashboards, metrics, committee reporting, and executive-level insights regarding the AI inventory, governance activity, material risks, exceptions, issues, and program maturity. Track identified risks, conditions of approval, issues, and remediation commitments through closure, and escalate overdue or material matters through appropriate governance channels. Partner with Technology, Information Security, Data Governance, Privacy, Compliance, Legal, Enterprise Risk Management, Internal Audit, and business teams to promote coordinated and efficient governance coverage. Support regulatory examinations, Internal Audit reviews, independent assessments, and other inquiries related to AI governance processes, records, and controls. Monitor regulatory developments, industry frameworks, and emerging practices related to AI governance, and coordinate updates to policies, processes, assessment requirements, and committee oversight. Develop training, communications, procedures, and job aids that clarify AI governance requirements and support consistent adoption across the firm. Promote a culture of responsible innovation, accountability, transparency, collaboration, and continuous improvement. What are we looking for?

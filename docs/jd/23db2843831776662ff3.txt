The Impact you will have in this role: We are seeking a FinOps Practitioner to lead cloud financial optimization across AWS, Azure, and Snowflake environments within a regulated financial services organization. This role serves as a critical bridge between Finance, Engineering, Architecture, and Cloud Operations, ensuring that cloud investments are aligned with business objectives, financial discipline, and operational efficiency. In this role, you will establish and mature FinOps capabilities aligned with the industry‑standard FinOps Foundation framework (Inform, Optimize, Operate) while driving governance, transparency, and accountability for cloud spend. You will partner closely with stakeholders to track FinOps objectives, influence architectural and consumption decisions, and embed a culture of fiscal responsibility across the organization. Your Primary Responsibilities : Establish and mature key FinOps capabilities aligned with the FinOps Foundation framework (Inform, Optimize, Operate) across AWS, Azure, and Snowflake environments. Lead FinOps practices to optimize and govern cloud spend across multi‑cloud and data platform ecosystems. Collaborate with Finance, Engineering, Architecture, Product, and Cloud Operations stakeholders to define, track, and measure FinOps objectives, ensuring ongoing visibility into cost performance and business outcomes. Develop and enforce cost allocation models, tagging standards, and chargeback/showback frameworks. Establish governance standards for resource usage, pricing model adoption (e.g., Reserved Instances, Savings Plans), and Snowflake warehouse and consumption optimization. Conduct detailed analysis of cloud and data platform cost data to identify savings opportunities, waste, and efficiency improvements. Implement and recommend rightsizing, scheduling, and reserved capacity strategies while balancing performance, resilience, and cost efficiency. Partner with engineering teams to influence architectural decisions through financial impact analysis and cost modeling. Collaborate with Finance and Business Unit leaders on budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis, and financial planning for cloud and data platform expenses. Build and maintain cloud spend models that demonstrate historical trends, growth patterns, and unit‑cost metrics. Communicate cost insights and optimization outcomes to senior leadership through dashboards and executive‑ready reporting. Leverage cloud cost management and FinOps tooling (e.g., AWS Cost Explorer, Azure Cost Management, Snowflake Usage Views, CloudHealth, CloudAware, Cloudability) for continuous monitoring and reporting. Serve as a liaison across Finance, Engineering, Architecture, and Product teams to foster cost awareness, accountability, and informed decision‑making. Champion FinOps principles, standards, and continuous improvement initiatives across the organization.

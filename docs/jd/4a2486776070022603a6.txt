What You'll Be Doing The Senior Portfolio Solutions Specialist provides customized portfolio analyses, asset allocation, and other recommendations to Financial Advisors serving high net worth and institutional clients. The Senior Portfolio Specialist handles complex client situations with strong presentation and communication skills and is well versed in the fundamentals of portfolio construction and wealth management applied through a deep knowledge of Stifel's Investment Products & Services platform. What You'll Bring • Advanced knowledge of securities including but not limited to stocks, bonds, mutual funds, ETFs, SMAs, structured products, alternative investments, and options strategies.• Advanced understanding of the fundamentals of portfolio construction, including asset allocation, modern portfolio theory, and investment management principles.• Knowledge of investment advisory business including advisory program types.• Analytical Skills - Knowledge of investment performance measurement methodologies, calculations, and related statistics.• Working knowledge of portfolio accounting, investment performance measurement, trading methodologies and associated systems within Stifel.

The Spring 2027 Underwriting Undergraduate Internship provides students with an opportunity to gain practical, hands-on experience within the underwriting function while developing foundational business, analytical, and professional skills. This internship is designed to support the development of the next generation of diverse business professionals through direct exposure to underwriting operations, risk assessment, financial analysis, and strategic decision-making within the healthcare industry. Participants will work alongside underwriting professionals to gain insight into the evaluation of healthcare products, member populations, pricing strategies, financial performance, and risk management practices. Interns will learn how underwriting supports the organization's business objectives through data-driven analysis, forecasting, financial modeling, and the development of recommendations that contribute to informed business decisions. The program provides hands-on training and real-world experience in underwriting and related business areas, exposing participants to a variety of career opportunities within underwriting, finance, analytics, and healthcare operations. Interns will have the opportunity to participate in meaningful projects, support day-to-day underwriting activities, conduct research and analysis, and contribute to initiatives that impact business performance and strategic growth. Typical responsibilities include supporting the day-to-day operating objectives of the underwriting function, assisting with analysis and reporting activities, participating in projects and programs relevant to the business area, contributing to the planning and implementation of initiatives, and conducting research that supports recommendations and proposals for action.

Duties & Responsibilities Analyze Treasury Management Support, Specialty, Implementation, client training, ServiceNow, Genesys, OnRamp, Treasury Navigator, and related operational data to identify trends, root causes, recurring issues, client friction points, and opportunities to improve quality, efficiency, visibility, and client experience. Lead or support continuous improvement initiatives from intake through implementation, including documenting current state, defining desired future state, mapping workflows, evaluating risks and dependencies, recommending solutions, coordinating approvals, and tracking action items to completion. Assist with developing, maintaining, and improving operational procedures, job aids, quick reference materials, process maps, rubrics, and other resources to support consistent execution, effective training, regulatory alignment, quality assurance, and knowledge sharing across Treasury Management teams. Partner with Treasury Management leaders, specialists, product, and other stakeholders to gather requirements, validate improvement opportunities, communicate recommendations, coordinate testing, and support change management for new or enhanced workflows, systems, reporting, and tools. Support quality assurance, client experience, and performance measurement efforts by assisting with data collection, reporting, dashboard input, sampling approaches, trend analysis, and recommendations for coaching, training, procedure updates, or process redesign. Participate in system enhancement, automation, and workflow testing by preparing test scenarios, executing user acceptance testing, documenting results, communicating defects or gaps, and validating that implemented changes meet business requirements and support a positive client and teammate experience. It is the responsibility of this role to take ownership of all tasks and challenges that they encounter in the operation of their assigned position.

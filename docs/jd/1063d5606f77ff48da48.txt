Location: New York City Working Environment: Hybrid ( role not available as fully remote) Your Team's Impact The Associate Director of Digital Assets for CUSIP Global Services (CGS) will be responsible for developing and implementing CGS’ overall strategy related to cryptocurrencies and tokenized securities. You will work with other stakeholders, FactSet, the American Bankers Association, and the CUSIP Board of Trustees, to ensure that CUSIP and CGS ISINs remain fit-for-purpose and at the forefront of identification solutions What You'll Do Develop and implement CGS’ overall strategy related to cryptocurrencies and tokenized securities. Work with other stakeholders – FactSet, the American Bankers Association, and the CUSIP Board of Trustees – to ensure that CUSIP and CGS ISINs remain fit-for-purpose and the identifiers of choice in the digital asset market. Cultivate and leverage relationships with participants in the digital asset ecosystem – exchanges, wallet hosting services, and token issuers – to keep CUSIP/ISIN at the forefront of identification solutions Participate in related standards activities – ISO TC68 and ANSI X9 – to help ensure CGS’ interests are represented in the maintenance and implementation of digital asset standards What We're Looking For Bachelor's degree 12+ years of experience in financial services, including exposure to both traditional finance (TradFi) and digital assets/DeFi markets. Deep understanding of capital markets, financial instruments, securities issuance, and reference data ecosystems Expertise in financial services standards and identifiers, including CUSIP, ISIN, LEI, and related industry frameworks Knowledge of the digital asset ecosystem, such as tokenized securities, cryptocurrencies, smart contracts, wallets, blockchain networks (public and private), digital asset exchanges, and token issuers Demonstrated experience working with industry standards organizations, market infrastructure providers, regulators, or trade associations Ability to develop and execute strategic initiatives and influence stakeholders across financial institutions, industry bodies, and emerging digital asset market participants Established network within the digital asset and financial services communities Must be legally authorized to work in the United States without the need for employer sponsorship, now or at any time in the future The budgeted salary range for this position in NYC is $145,000 - $175,000.

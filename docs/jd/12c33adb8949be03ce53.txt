How Will You Make An Impact? The Culture & Engagement Associate plays a key role in bringing our employee experience to life across the Florence, KY campus. This role supports the execution of engagement and culture initiatives for a population of 4,000+ employees across multiple buildings. Working closely with the Engagement & Culture Manager, Site HR, and Operations leaders, this position focuses on delivering high-quality programs, events, and communications that strengthen engagement, recognition, and workplace culture. This is a hands-on role ideal for someone who thrives in a fast-paced environment and enjoys being highly visible and connected to employees on the floor. Location This role is based on-site at our Florence, KY campus (3 buildings). What Will You Do? Support the execution of campus-wide engagement initiatives, events, and recognition programs across all buildings Partner with Site HR and Operations to coordinate and deliver engagement activities that align with business needs Assist in gathering and organizing employee feedback (e.g., surveys, pulse checks, roundtables) and help track follow-up actions Coordinate logistics for engagement events, including scheduling, materials, communications, and on-site support Maintain and track engagement calendars, participation, and program effectiveness metrics Support post-survey action planning by helping organize data, materials, and communications for leaders Serve as a visible and approachable resource for employees, helping to promote programs and encourage participation Assist with communication efforts, including creating flyers, emails, and on-site messaging to drive awareness and engagement Help ensure consistency in engagement and recognition programs across all buildings while adapting to shift and workforce needs Partner with leadership and HR to identify opportunities to improve employee experience at the campus level Support additional HR and engagement-related initiatives as needed How Will You Get Here?

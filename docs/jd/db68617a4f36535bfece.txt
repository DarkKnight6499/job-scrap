Why Stifel Stifel’s Capital Structure Advisory group has over two decades of experience guiding companies, creditors, shareholders, and sponsors through complex financial restructurings, recapitalizations, and strategic transactions. With a track record of over 190 company representations, $450 billion in restructured debt, and $21 billion in M&A advisory, Stifel CSA is known for its intellectual rigor, creativity, and hands-on approach. Our professionals collaborate closely with Stifel’s industry bankers to provide clients access to the full spectrum of strategic alternatives. Most engagements are completed out-of-court and often remain confidential, underscoring our reputation for discretion and excellence. About Stifel At its core, the Stifel story is a journey of growth and optimism for the future.

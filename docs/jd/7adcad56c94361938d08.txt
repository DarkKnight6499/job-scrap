Job Description: Job Title: Corporate Banking Coverage (CBC), Industrials, Infrastructure & Energy Corporate Title: Associate Location: New York, NY Overview Deutsche Bank’s Corporate Banking Coverage (CBC) group manages relationships with large corporations globally to solve diverse problems for clients. The US CBC group covers US Headquartered clients and their international subsidiaries in EMEA, APAC, and Brazil. We help our clients meet objectives and challenges in financing, risk management, working capital, investments, and transaction banking. As an associate on the team, you will work directly with Managing Directors, Directors, and Vice Presidents, supporting them with all client activities (marketing, execution, and ongoing relationship management). In addition, you will assume responsibility for managing internal processes for deal executions, internal approvals, Business aspects of regulatory reporting and ongoing management of client relationships.

Build the control environment that earns trust in enterprise data. This newly created leadership role will establish LPL’s first-line Enterprise Data Governance Risk & Controls capability, connecting governance standards to practical controls, measurable risk reduction, and clear executive visibility. You will shape the operating model, build durable partnerships across Product, Technology, Risk, Compliance, Legal, Operations, and Internal Audit, and help ensure trusted data supports client service, regulatory readiness, analytics, and responsible AI adoption. Job Overview The Vice President II, Enterprise Data Governance Risk & Controls is a senior leader within the Enterprise Data Governance & Responsible AI organization, reporting to the Senior Vice President, Data Governance & Responsible AI. As the first-line leader for enterprise data governance controls, this role establishes and matures the framework, control inventory, monitoring, issue-management, evidence, and reporting practices used to identify, assess, mitigate, and communicate data-related risk across the enterprise. The leader will work closely with first-, second-, and third-line partners to align data governance controls with the Enterprise Risk Management Framework, regulatory expectations, and sustainable business operations. Responsibilities Establish and lead the Enterprise Data Governance Risk & Controls function, including its strategy, operating model, governance framework, accountability model, and multi-year maturity roadmap. Design and oversee an enterprise data governance risk and control framework spanning data quality, lineage, classification, retention, critical data, privacy, cybersecurity, AI, and third-party data risk. Define standards for control design, inventories, documentation, evidence, testing, monitoring, issue management, remediation, and continuous improvement. Partner with Product, Technology, Business, Data Governance, and Responsible AI leaders to embed effective controls throughout the data lifecycle without creating duplicative processes or testing. Align first-line controls with Enterprise Risk, Operational Risk, Information Security Risk, Privacy, Compliance, Legal, Model Risk Management, and other second-line requirements. Create enterprise metrics, key risk indicators, continuous monitoring, dashboards, and executive reporting that provide transparency into data risk posture and control effectiveness. Own the identification, escalation, remediation, and validated closure of data-related control gaps through established RCSA and GRC processes. Serve as the first-line lead for data governance audits, regulatory examinations, and risk assessments, coordinating evidence and demonstrating control design and operating effectiveness. Evaluate emerging regulatory, industry, and technology risks and translate them into pragmatic enhancements to the enterprise control environment. Build, lead, and develop a high-performing team while advising senior leaders on control maturity, risk strategy, investment priorities, and operational resilience. What are we looking for?

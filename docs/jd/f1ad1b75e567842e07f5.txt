Principal Associate, CMS Operations and Support Specialist- CDX Principal Associate, CMS Operations and Support Specialist- CDX This role is within Capital One’s Consumer & Developer Experience (CDX) organization, on the Platform Administration & Governance team. CDX is responsible for Capital One’s digital customer experiences, including its award winning mobile app, its online presence, and Enterprise-grade products and platforms used across all lines of business. Our team is responsible for administering and governing these platforms, and advising on the large-scale transformation of our content supply chain to unlock highly personalized, intelligent experiences for millions of current and prospective customers. The Platform Administration & Governance Team is seeking an experienced, detail-oriented platform administrator with experience managing content management systems to join Capital One as our CMS Operations and Support Specialist. As the CMS Operations and Support Specialist, you will be the frontline champion for our proprietary Content-as-a-Service (CaaS) platform, and its users, within our Foundry ecosystem. You will be the guardian of our platform’s operational hygiene and a critical force multiplier for Foundry’s product and engineering teams. You will own CaaS Tier 1 support, offloading critical operational tasks from our engineering team and leadership. You will manage incoming requests, keep our Jira and sprint boards immaculate, and ensure our support data is accurate and actionable. WHAT YOU’LL DO Platform Support and User Advocacy Frontline Support: Own our Foundry Support Slack channel, managing an average of 30–50 Tier 1 requests per week (answering "how-to" questions, helping clients get access to the CaaS platform via AllAccess, performing basic tasks like clearing caches, and guiding users through documentation). Intake & Escalation Management: Smoothly transition complex issues to Tier 2 Jira tickets. Guide clients through the intake form process and ensure all necessary context and data are gathered upfront so engineers can dive straight into working on tickets without back-and-forth communication. Daily Check-Ins & Triage: Lead the daily support check-ins, triage Tier 2 Jira tickets, and update the Confluence project board. You will actively represent the customer's voice during prioritization meetings. Operational Governance & Access Control Access Management: Manage and approve user access requests Onboarding Execution: Support the rollout of our redesigned onboarding process, managing intake reviews, assisting with domain provisioning, and rigorously tracking SLAs Governance Policing: Actively monitor for duplicate or "ghost" domains and inactive users Agile Ceremonies & Data Hygiene Sprint & Jira Hygiene: Own backlog grooming, board maintenance, and the opening/closing of sprints KPIs & Dashboard Upkeep: Maintain our Slack and KPI dashboards. Monitor performance metrics and proactively alert the team if SLAs are at risk of being missed. Feedback Ingestion: Monitor the in-platform feedback widget, triaging submissions and tagging key themes to feed into our Net Promoter Score (NPS) and Voice of the Customer (VoC) pipelines Agile Ceremonies & Data Hygiene Customer Communications: Draft and distribute the Foundry newsletter, release notes, and regular system status updates. Knowledge Management: Maintain and update our self-service documentation, decision-tree guides, and chatbot datasets. Training Logistics: Coordinate the scheduling and materials for team webinars and training sessions. BASIC QUALIFICATIONS Bachelor’s Degree or Military experience At least 4 years of experience managing user access controls or account permissions At least 2 years of experience administering enterprise content management systems PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS 5+ years of platform management or CMS operations experience 5+ years experience in a marketing role 5+ years of experience in financial services, or another highly regulated industry 2+ years of experience evaluating or implementing GenAI, automation, or new technology integrations within a marketing ecosystem At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position.

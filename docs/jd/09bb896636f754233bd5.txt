Job Summary The Associate Materials Specialist performs detailed duties to maintain supply and inventory levels for deployed systems and/or products. Monitors spare part availability, repair turnaround and inventory levels. Answers inquiries regarding order status, availability, required dates, order status, and the like. The Associate Materials Specialist does not have formal supervisory responsibility, no mentoring or advisory responsibilities; accountable for developing own technical contribution. May be assigned to provisioning, inventory management, spares, replenishment and/or return areas. Performs routine assignments in the entry level to a professional job progression.

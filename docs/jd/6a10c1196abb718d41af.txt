What You'll Do: The Principal in the Stress Testing Group performs a broad array of risk management activities to support OCC’s stress testing and liquidity risk management functions. They will serve as a mentor for more junior staff on the team. This role will support IT in the development of a new stress testing system while also maintaining and enhancing the current implementation. They will assist in regulatory exam-related activities and supporting the regulatory filing process. They will drive improvement of analytical capabilities within the team and produce clear and concise reporting for management. They will also have direct interaction with OCC senior management, Clearing Members, regulators and other stakeholders. Founded in 1973, OCC is dedicated to promoting stability and market integrity by delivering clearing and settlement services for options, futures and securities lending transactions. As a Systemically Important Financial Market Utility (SIFMU), OCC operates under the jurisdiction of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC), and the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System.

Private Wealth Business Associate
Neuberger Berman
Location: New York, NY

Position Overview: Neuberger's Private Wealth business is seeking a highly motivated and results-driven individual to lead initiatives focused on process improvement and operational excellence. This individual will play a critical role in enhancing the efficiency, scalability, and effectiveness of the Private Wealth business by evaluating current processes, identifying opportunities for improvement, and driving implementation efforts. The role requires a blend of strategic thinking, operational expertise, and change management capabilities. The role of Business Associate will be responsible for the ownership and execution of several strategic programs and analyses related to the growth and design of the Neuberger's Private Wealth organization. Our choice candidate will be a detail oriented strategic thinker with a passion for solving complex problems, dealing with ambiguity, organizational design, and who possesses an unrelenting customer obsession and a strong desire to make their mark on the organization. Primary Responsibilities: Process Inventory and Assessment: Conduct a comprehensive inventory of all current processes within the Private Wealth business Map out workflows, roles, and responsibilities to fully understand the existing operating model Identify manual, duplicative, or unnecessary steps in current processes Opportunity Identification and Design: Pinpoint areas for process enhancement, including automation opportunities and strategic redesign of workflows Propose innovative solutions to streamline operations while maintaining compliance and quality Recommend changes to organizational structure and roles & responsibilities to align with optimized processes Future-State Operating Model Development: Develop a forward-looking operating model that incorporates process improvements, automation, and enhanced efficiency Document future-state workflows and define measurable goals for success Implementation and Change Management: Lead the execution of process enhancements, ensuring seamless transition from current state to future state Collaborate with stakeholders to align on changes and secure buy-in across impacted teams Implement change management strategies to drive adoption of new processes and tools Provide training and support to teams to ensure successful implementation of process changes Monitoring and Continuous Improvement: Establish metrics to track the effectiveness of implemented changes Monitor ongoing performance of processes and identify additional areas for improvement Act as a champion for continuous improvement within the Private Wealth business Experience & Skills Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Finance, Operations Management, Engineering, or a related field. Advanced degree or certifications such as Lean Six Sigma, PMP, or similar are a plus 3+ years of experience in process improvement, operational excellence, or related roles Experience within the financial services industry, private wealth management highly preferred Strong knowledge of process mapping, workflow analysis, and operational efficiency tools Proficiency in automation technologies and familiarity with digital transformation initiatives Expertise in change management principles and techniques Candidate must be a self-starter with the ability to operate independently and make decisions Ability to build relationships and partner across a complex organization Ability to multi-task and manage multiple simultaneous projects Ability to influence seniors and stakeholders outside of your span of control Excellent time management, follow-through, and project delivery skills Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, able to simplify complexity Exceptional analytical skills Strategic thinker Strong attention to detail Highly organized Owner of outcomes Team player Applicants must be authorized and have the right to work in the country where the role is located without the need for current or future sponsorship.

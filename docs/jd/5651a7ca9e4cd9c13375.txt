Role Description: The Business Analyst will work directly for and in close partnership with the CIO of Equities, focused on strategic content and act as a trusted analytical and execution partner; emphasizing quantitative risk analysis, performance attribution, and data-driven decision support across the diverse, multi-strategy equity platform. The ideal candidate has a strong analytical background and is highly organized, intellectually curious, and comfortable operating with ambiguity and shifting priorities. Responsibilities: Quantitative Risk & Performance Analysis Conduct cross-desk risk, factor-exposure, and attribution analysis spanning regional, cap-size, and specialized equity strategies Build risk decomposition, scenario, and factor-attribution frameworks using FactSet Investigate specific questions from the CIO (e.g., "what is driving dispersion across our value strategies this quarter?") drawing on data from various analytical tools Apply the firm's proprietary AI tools to accelerate data synthesis and pattern identification across the platform Strategic Content & Executive Communications Prepare high-quality strategic presentations for the CIO's use with the Executive Committee, Boards, and key clients Translate complex investment and business topics into clear, compelling narratives for senior and external audiences Draft talking points, briefing packs, and pre-read materials ahead of key meetings, offsites, and reviews CIO–Support Function Liaison Drive follow-through on CIO commitments and cross-functional deliverables, tracking action items to closure Manage information flow to and from the CIO, ensuring the right issues are escalated with recommended paths forward Special Projects & Firm-Wide Initiatives Support ad hoc strategic initiatives and cross-platform analyses as directed by the CIO of Equities Provide project support for one-off strategic efforts (e.g., new capability build-outs, M&A/lift-out evaluations, operating model changes) Skills, experience, qualifications and knowledge required: 5–8 years' experience in investment management, quantitative research, or risk analytics, ideally across multi-strategy equity platforms (regional, cap-size, or specialized) Proficiency in FactSet required; working knowledge of Bloomberg expected Familiarity with Style Analytics and/or Barra a plus, not required Comfort adopting AI-driven analytical tools Strong statistical/quantitative skillset (risk modeling, attribution, factor analysis);

As a Commodities Sales professional in our Commodities business, this candidate will be instrumental in developing and expanding client relationships across the broader commodities landscape. The candidate will provide complex structured, exotic, and vanilla hedging and risk management solutions as well as physical and financing solutions to a broad range of commodities clients. The ideal candidate will source liquidity and pricing for clients through an extensive network of market participants and counterparties. They will have the ability to produce daily written market commentary highlighting market activity, opportunities, and key developments for distribution to a global audience of investment professionals. Knowledge of internal and external trading platforms, electronic execution tools, and derivatives markets will be essential in advising clients and facilitating transactions.

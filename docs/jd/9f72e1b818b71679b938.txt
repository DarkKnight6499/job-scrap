Credit Structuring Associate
Intesa Sanpaolo
Location: New York, US

Overview The Associate will be part of a strategic team aimed at strengthening the Operating Model of the Americas Debt Origination & Credit Structuring practice through the development of "Centers of Excellence & Competence" dedicated to cover all asset classes under the Credit Structuring model (including Specialized Lending, MLT Complex, LBOs, Fund Finance, etc.) with a particular focus on debt structuring, and portfolio management, to provide added value to the business area, optimize cross-sell, also with the support of the relationship units. What you'll be doing Key responsibilities include: Work closely with debt origination, the respective industry teams and other departments, to evaluate new transactions, review project documents, deal structuring, negotiations, and complete credit applications Respond to various client requests, explain transactions structure to other support functions (i.e. legal, treasury, credit administration, loan operations, and control functions etc.), and assist risk and credit teams as questions arise Prepare credit applications, outlining rationale, structure, terms and conditions, financial modelling and sensitivity analysis, return analysis, credit rating, as well as other requested or required forms or information needed (including full familiarity with all relevant Bank tools) Evaluate specialized and complex investments utilizing all available sources of information including governing documents, published research, financial reports, news, rating agency reports, and market information. Who we are looking for We would be pleased to hear from you if you have: 3-5 years of experience in in Project Financing structures Bachelor’s Degree in Finance, Economics or International Business from an accredited education institution or comparable/related work experience Financial modelling proficiency is key MS Word, Excel and PowerPoint proficiency Excellent analytical and quantitative skills to manage complex processes Excellent writing skills.

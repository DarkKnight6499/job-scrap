GLOBAL BANKING & MARKETS Global Banking & Markets provides financing, advisory services, risk distribution and hedging solutions to institutional and corporate clients through our FICC and Equities (“GBM Public”) and Investment Banking (“GBM Private”) franchises. YOUR IMPACT We are looking for an experienced professional to join the Supervisory Risk & Controls team within Global Banking & Markets and lead the expansion of the supervisory program across GBM Private, with a particular focus on the Capital Solutions Group (CSG). In this role, you will develop a deep understanding of CSG’s business activities and lead the design, implementation and oversight of a supervisory framework that enables the business to operate effectively while managing supervisory-related risk. You will work closely with senior bankers and partners across Compliance, Engineering, Legal and other stakeholder groups to establish robust controls, monitoring, data capabilities and governance. OUR IMPACT The Supervisory Risk & Controls team manages controls covering supervisory-related risks arising from business activities across Global Banking & Markets. The team partners with the businesses to implement controls, support ongoing monitoring and oversight, and develop supervisory tools that help identify, manage and escalate risk. The Capital Solutions Group brings together a comprehensive suite of financing, origination, structuring and risk management solutions. Its integrated model combines deep client relationships with broad transaction experience to deliver tailored solutions for issuing and investing clients. This role offers the opportunity to help shape the supervisory framework supporting a growing and strategically important business.

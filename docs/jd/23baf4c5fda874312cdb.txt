Job Description Area Control Leader, Global Engineering Solutions (Associate Director) The Area Control Leader is a key position in the Global Estimating Solutions, Project Business Services (PBS) Organization. This role has the mandate to provide effective and innovative cost and schedule solutions for our business partners to maximize their value and for our teams to ensure successful project execution. The leader will need to bring energy, experience and innovation to carry out the following: Ensure minimum essential planning/scheduling and controlling elements are in place for all projects in their assigned portfolio. Support deployment of innovative methods and tools to improve project/portfolio performance (Target Value Delivery, Last Planner ® Production Planning & Control, AI solutions). Monitor leading indicators and trends to provide early warning of issues to project teams, the E&C Lead and Regional Directors Collaborate with teams to solve problems before they become overruns or missed deadlines. Focus on control and continuous improvement. Responsibilities: Establish and implement Project Control execution plans. Develop, monitor and maintain overall Monthly Project Cost Reports including: cost budgeting/forecasting/reporting, earned-value analysis, progress measurement/schedule status, change management, risk management, and early warning of issues. Support implementation of Target Value Delivery. Develop and maintain overall Project Spending Plans. Interface with AE (Architect Engineer) & CM (Construction Management) Project Control personnel to ensure timely communication and support of project scheduling, control and reporting requirements. Work with internal SMEs in estimating and strategic scheduling to support transition from planning to execution. Schedule development, maintenance, analysis and reporting using the Critical Path Method (CPM) and the Last Planner® System. Development and monitoring of project staffing plans for new projects and ongoing projects. Implementation of Change Management and Risk Management processes. Support of project financial close out requirements including asset definition and valuation. Liaison with various groups outside the Engineering organization. Required Education: Bachelor of Science in Engineering or a relevant discipline. Required Qualifications: 10+ years applicable Project Control related experience. Strong leadership, interpersonal, and communication skills and willing to accept and drive change and innovation. Working knowledge of project cost and financial management aspects within SAP system (or similar) software. Working knowledge of scheduling and risk analysis software (e.g. Primavera P6, Primavera Risk Analysis, MS Project). Ability to travel across the Americas up to 25% of their time. Preferred Qualifications: Experience in Lean Project Delivery Methodologies (e.g. Target Value Delivery and Last Planner®). Pharmaceutical experience, including facility qualification and validation. Project Management, Construction Management and/or Estimating experience. Knowledge of Capital Management processes. Lean/Six Sigma experience. Understanding of MS Power Applications and AI. Required Skills: Capital Planning, Capital Projects, Engineering Principle, Engineering Standards, Environmental Safety, Maintenance Supervision, Microsoft Project, Path Analysis, People Leadership, Primavera Risk Analysis, Project Budgeting, Project Delivery, Project Estimations, Project Facilitation, Project Implementations, Project Management Methodology (PMM), Project Planning, Project Progress Reporting, Project Resource Management, Project Tracking, Sterile Manufacturing, Strategic Thinking Preferred Skills: Current Employees apply HERE Current Contingent Workers apply HERE US and Puerto Rico Residents Only: Our company is committed to inclusion, ensuring that candidates can engage in a hiring process that exhibits their true capabilities.

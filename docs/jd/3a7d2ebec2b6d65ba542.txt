Industry/Sector Banking and Capital Markets Specialism Data, Analytics & AI Management Level Director Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Financial Risk Management - Risk Data Transformation Director you will guide client engagements focused on data strategy, data governance, data quality, reporting, and transformation activities within our Advisory practice. As a Director you will set direction, shape executive client relationships, and drive new ideas that connect business needs with technology and risk priorities while mentoring future leaders and supporting growth across the PwC Network. In this role at PwC, you will help clients in banking and capital markets turn risk data into clearer insights, strengthen operating models, and build practical solutions that support informed decision-making and regulatory expectations. Responsibilities - Leading financial risk data transformation initiatives across banking and capital markets client engagements - Applying deep expertise in risk data modeling, risk data models, and risk metrics to support regulatory reporting, analytics, and business decision-making - Setting direction for data analytics solutions that support regulatory risk, compliance, and financial risk reporting - Overseeing development of data quality, data governance, and data lineage capabilities for risk data environments - Translating business and regulatory needs into data engineering approaches, data pipelines, and reporting solutions - Developing analytical frameworks that use SQL, Python, and data visualization tools to uncover risk insights - Shaping client strategies for risk modeling, data integration, and modern analytics platform adoption - Driving business development opportunities through tailored risk analytics solutions and industry insight - Guiding teams through complex delivery work and mentoring emerging leaders across data and risk transformation efforts What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 9 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for at least one of the following fields of study: Business Management, Computer Science, Data Science, Risk Management - Preference for a Master's of Business Administration - Directing BCBS 239 risk data aggregation and risk reporting programs for G-SIBs or large regional banks, including regulatory remediation of MRA and MRIA findings - Demonstrating risk data transformation leadership within financial services - Translating regulatory risk concepts into data and analytics solutions - Driving data governance, data quality, and lineage initiatives - Leveraging SQL, Python, Databricks, Snowflake, and scalable data pipelines to deliver complex risk and regulatory data solutions - Guiding executive client conversations across complex engagements - Shaping growth strategies and mentoring emerging leaders Travel Requirements Up to 60% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $155,000 -

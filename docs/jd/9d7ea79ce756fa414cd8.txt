Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever. Job Summary: Micron Technology is seeking highly motivated Reticle Manufacturing Technicians to support efficient production operations and reduce cycle time in the manufacturing environment. Technicians play a critical role in facilitating product movement, prioritizing plate runs to meet shipping deadlines, and responding to area inefficiencies such as tool errors, data issues, and lot tracking problems. You will exhibit a high level of dependability and passion, embodying our core values of People, Innovation, Tenacity, Collaboration, and Customer Focus. Responsibilities: Manage Work-In-Progress (WIP) using department-specific reports and software tools to ensure efficient workflow. Perform machine and buddy checks, product inspections, and maintain cleanliness standards through regular shift wipe downs. Monitor and resolve lot-related issues, including SWRs, recipe/tool discrepancies, high mean time at step, and hold lots. Track bottlenecks and prioritize key workstations to meet production and ship gap targets. Maintain accurate machine logs and lot tracking data; ensure proper documentation and system updates. Conduct SPC checks, monitor post-inspection results, and enter detailed comments when limits are exceeded. Support procedural updates, training initiatives, and continuous improvement activities in collaboration with engineering. Communicate effectively during passdowns, maintain safety compliance, and manage time to meet operational goals. Minimum Requirements: High school diploma or equivalent. Basic understanding of manufacturing operations and procedures. Flexibility to work 12-hour shifts (3 on/4 off and 4 on/3 off) and be willing to move to different shifts and work overtime to support business operations. Stand for long periods, walk several miles per shift, lift 25–40 lbs occasionally, and perform tasks requiring bending, crouching, or kneeling while navigating safely over uneven terrain or within confined spaces to perform job duties effectively. Preferred Requirements: AAS or AS Degree in Mechatronics, Electronics, Mechanical Engineering, Science, Physics, Mathematics, or Supply Chain Management (or equivalent trade school/military experience). 2 years of experience maintaining manufacturing equipment, automation systems, or technical military experience. 2+ year experience identifying performance gaps and implementing strategic improvements. Experience and/or interest in developing Agentic AI workflows into current or future processes.

We are now looking for a Senior Validation Architect for NVLink! NVIDIA is seeking an experienced technical leader passionate about defining advanced chip interconnects and protocols. You’ll be part of a team responsible for defining next generation communications standards and products building on our current NVLink technology. The job scope ranges from developing initial product requirements, working with research, other architects, software/firmware and product development teams to shepherd the design through all phases of the product lifecycle. What you will be doing: Working with other architects to define architectural modeling and testing requirements for new NVLink features Driving architectural testing requirements into lower level testbenches Implementing functional models and integrating those into fullchip and multichip environments for both architectural validation and firmware/software development Supporting and debugging tests in model, emulation, and post-silicon environments What we need to see: You have a MS or PhD in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science or Computer Engineering (or equivalent experience) At least 8+ years of relevant experience in some combination of architecture, design, and verification Firsthand work experience with innovative ASIC design processes C++ and scripting language (Python, Makefile) experience; SystemC modeling a plus Strong debugging skills in FW/SW environments and IP model integration Testplan development and execution experience You've completed and shipped multiple projects and are skilled at processor and/or I/O Architecture. A self-starter excited to work as part of a small dynamic team NVIDIA is widely considered to be one of the technology world’s most desirable employers.

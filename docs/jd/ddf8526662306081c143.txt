The Role: The Manager, Global Commercial FP&A serves as a finance partner supporting global planning, forecasting, and performance management across Commercial and Medical functions. This role coordinates the consolidation and analysis of budgets, forecasts, and long-range plans, helping translate financial results into clear narratives for business partners and leadership. Through strong cross-functional partnership, this individual supports standardization and transparency across financial processes and contributes to transformation initiatives that enhance analytics, improve efficiency, and elevate the impact of FP&A within the organization.

Today’s financial institutions operate in an increasingly complex environment where understanding and managing risk is critical to investment decision-making. Market volatility, evolving regulatory requirements and increasingly sophisticated portfolios have created a growing need for high-quality, transparent and scalable risk and investment analytics. Firms need consistent analytics and data across their organizations to understand portfolio exposures, assess potential risks and make informed investment decisions. Bloomberg’s Risk & Investment Analytics data solutions help clients identify, measure and manage risk across their investment processes. By delivering robust analytics and data that can be incorporated into clients’ enterprise workflows, Bloomberg helps risk managers, portfolio managers, investment teams and senior executives gain greater transparency into their portfolios and make more informed decisions. When investment and risk teams work from consistent, trusted data and analytics, the entire enterprise benefits. Risk teams will appreciate Bloomberg's ability to help identify, measure and manage uncertainty, particularly during volatile market conditions. Investment teams can leverage analytics to better understand the drivers of portfolio risk and performance, while senior executives gain greater transparency into exposures across their businesses.

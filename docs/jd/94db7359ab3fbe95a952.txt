About the Role You will drive the revenue and pricing strategy for one of our newest business lines. You will quickly become an expert about our product, operations, and merchants. You will be on the forefront of growth — partnering closely with Sales to win new deals, shaping our go-to-market approach, building pricing models, and standing up the processes that help us accelerate speed to revenue, grow the top line, and protect margin as we scale.

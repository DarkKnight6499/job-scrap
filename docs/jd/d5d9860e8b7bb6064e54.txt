Position Summary The FP&A Analyst position is a vital member of the Corporate Finance department in the CFO organization, providing direct financial oversight and strategic decision support for Texas Capital. The ideal candidate will bring experience with Excel and strong analytical and modeling skills. This role is suited for a detail-oriented and tech-savvy finance professional who thrives in a collaborative environment. This position will impact firm-wide strategy, have visibility to top Finance leadership, and provide the opportunity to learn the inner workings of entity-wide financials at the highest level. Responsibilities Forecast Consolidation: Consolidate financial forecasts across business units, ensuring accuracy, timeliness, and compliance with GAAP reporting standards Conduct scenario modeling and sensitivity analysis, and complete intercompany eliminations and validations Reporting: Develop and maintain comprehensive financial reporting packages for executive leadership and key stakeholders Conduct variance analysis and work with Finance teams to explain deviations from business unit forecasts Identify and implement process improvement opportunities to streamline routines, enhance reporting, and increase efficiency through automation and best practices Business Partnering: Collaborate with Enterprise Finance teams to provide financial guidance and support distribution of centralized targets Support product pricing, funds transfer pricing, capital allocation, and investment decisions Drive accountability through financial insights and line of business performance reviews System Administration (no programming required): Roll forward actuals into forecast Update system user access Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Business, or a related field Up to 1 year of experience in FP&A, financial systems, or related roles with a strong understanding of GAAP and financial reporting Hands-on experience with Oracle EPM (Planning, Hyperion Essbase, Smart View) preferred Ability to drive enterprise-level financial analysis and reporting through advanced proficiency with Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint, deliver clear, executive-ready analysis and presentations Understanding of banking products, balance sheet management, and regulatory environment Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet tight deadlines in a fast-paced environment High integrity and commitment to ethical standards The duties listed above are the essential functions, or fundamental duties within the job classification.

Responsibilities Support the evaluation and underwriting of risks across traditional and renewable energy, power generation, mining, petrochemicals, and related industries. Review underwriting submissions, operational information, financial data, and loss history to support risk selection , pricing, and coverage decisions. Collaborate with underwriters, risk engineers, claims professionals, and multinational specialists to develop tailored insurance solutions. Build relationships with brokers and clients while supporting new business and renewal opportunities. Monitor industry, regulatory, and emerging risk trends that may impact clients and underwriting decisions. Develop technical knowledge of energy insurance while building analytical, decision-making, and relationship-management skills .

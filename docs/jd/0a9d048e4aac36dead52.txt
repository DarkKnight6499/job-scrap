As Manager of Projects , you will be responsible for the successful development and execution of multiple sophisticated maintenance capital programs and projects at operating power generation facilities, involving collaborative solutions with plant operations, original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), contractors, and other internal and external partners. Focus areas may include gas turbines, reciprocating engines, generators, transformers, switchgear, substations and switchyards, controls, and balance-of-plant systems. Knowledge of project scheduling and forecasting, the ability to collaborate with internal and external business partners, and the desire to nurture a team environment are keys to success! In this role within Williams’ growing Power organization, you’ll lead the team that delivers our maintenance capital program: the planned investment we make in operating power generation facilities to keep them safe, reliable, and available, including equipment repairs, replacements, upgrades, and reliability improvements. You’ll own the maintenance capital plan, align the right project managers to the right projects, and partner closely with plant operations leadership so that work is prioritized and scheduled around planned outages and maintenance cycles. This is a hands-on leadership role for someone who has executed work on operating assets, understands how operations and maintenance teams think, and wants to build and develop a project team as the organization grows across multiple regions.

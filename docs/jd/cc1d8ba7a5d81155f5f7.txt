Cell Models & Delivery Biology Principal Research Associate

Job Description General Summary Vertex is seeking a highly experienced and motivated Principal Research Associate to develop and execute assays supporting research on non-viral delivery of nucleic acid therapeutics. The successful candidate will bring strong hands-on expertise in molecular biology, cell biology, gene-expression analysis, and protein-based assays. This individual will work closely with cross-functional partners to generate high-quality data that informs discovery-stage decisions and candidate progression. This laboratory-based role will design, execute, optimize, and interpret molecular and cellular assays that characterize functional delivery, intracellular activity, gene expression, and immunological and pharmacological responses. Key Duties and Responsibilities Design, execute, and optimize in vitro assays supporting non-viral delivery biology and gene replacement strategies. Develop and apply cellular and molecular biology workflows to evaluate uptake, dose response, intracellular trafficking, functional delivery, gene expression, and pharmacological activity. Perform nucleic acid extraction and downstream gene-expression analyses, including qPCR, RT-qPCR, TaqMan assays, and related molecular characterization methods. Execute protein and cellular assays, including ELISA, MSD, Western blotting/Jess, immunoprecipitation, reporter gene assays, and cell-signaling assays to assess cellular responses and delivery outcomes. Support evaluation of gene-editing and gene-replacement approaches using relevant molecular and cellular readouts. Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams and external partners to design studies, communicate findings, and advance program priorities. Prepare clear experimental and technical summaries, data reports, and presentations; proactively communicate findings. Maintain accurate, timely, and well-organized electronic laboratory notebook records and structured documentation/SOPs. Knowledge and Skills Significant hands-on laboratory experience in molecular biology, nucleic acid therapeutics, protein-based assays, and tissue culture. Experience with: RNA/DNA extractions, qPCR, construct design, gene expression analysis, reporter assays, Western Blot/Jess, MSD, immunoprecipitation, ELISA etc. Experience with one or more of the following: RNA/DNA therapeutics, Lipid nanoparticle (LNP) dosing, receptor biology and intracellular trafficking, gene expression, cell transfection/transduction experiments, or confocal imaging. Demonstrated ability to identify technical gaps, improve assay performance, and refine experimental workflows Strong collaboration skills, with the ability to work in a highly cross functional, fast-paced research environment. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to summarize complex experimental findings clearly. Experience with one or more of the following: IND-enabling studies, data visualization, report writing, electronic lab notebook keeping (e.g. Benchling), or structured data documentation. Education and Experience Master’s Degree (or equivalent degree) in biological sciences (or a related field) and 8+ years of relevant employment experience. Bachelor’s Degree (or equivalent degree) in biological sciences (or a related field) and 10+ years of relevant employment experience.

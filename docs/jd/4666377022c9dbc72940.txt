For further information on how we process your data, please see the privacy notice for applicants here. * As at 30 June 2026 Purpose of the Role The Trade Support Analyst will be responsible for the following: Assist traders and portfolio managers with day to day issues regarding trade bookings and/or trade settlement. Capture all securities trades for the US teams, assist UK team as needed. Provide support to London in the US afternoon. Liaise with various operations teams to resolve downstream issues. Specific Responsibilities Work with traders/portfolio managers to resolve all trading and/or booking issues. Research and respond to all trade/position break emails from internal teams throughout the day. Monitor and assist with the management of US timed option expiries. Liaise with Compliance around fund limits and monitor to make sure positions are correct. Liaise with departments such as Legal/Compliance/Investment Teams/IT and the Settlement Team on a regular basis. Manage the handover from London in the US afternoon and back to London at the US close. Deal with any ad-hoc operational issues that may arise. Key Competencies Excellent Client Service (Internal & External): Speaks the client language. Understands the client. Shows empathy with the client. Shows personal responsibility and is accountable. Manages the client’s expectations. Exceeds client’s expectations. Delivers Professional & Ethical Standards: Applies Man Group ethics and values. Demonstrates a positive attitude. Globally oriented. Business aware. Seeks to continuously enhance own relevant skills. Works comfortably in an entrepreneurial environment. Ensures compliance. Effective Team Working: Valued and sought after as a team member. Recognises others.

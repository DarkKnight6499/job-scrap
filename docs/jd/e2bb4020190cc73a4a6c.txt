What you’ll do: The Fraud Quality Specialist is responsible for executing and continuously improving the First Line Quality Program within Fraud Prevention & Investigations. This role performs Quality Control (QC) reviews before work is completed to identify and correct issues prior to case closure and Quality Assurance (QA) reviews after work is complete to evaluate the effectiveness, consistency, and quality of fraud operations. The Fraud Quality Specialist partners closely with Fraud Operations leadership to establish and reinforce quality standards, identify operational improvement opportunities, analyze root causes, recommend practical corrective actions, and coach team members to improve performance. The role also supports the evolution of the quality program through process improvement, quality analytics, automation, and responsible use of artificial intelligence (AI). Core Purpose: Execute and continuously improve the First Line Quality Control (QC) and Quality Assurance (QA) Program for Fraud Prevention & Investigations. Promote consistent, high-quality fraud investigations through objective quality standards, coaching, and continuous process improvement. Identify opportunities to improve quality, operational effectiveness, investigator performance, and customer outcomes through meaningful analysis and practical recommendations. Leverage data analytics, automation, and AI-enabled tools to improve the efficiency, consistency, and effectiveness of quality reviews. Key Responsibilities: Quality Control & Quality Assurance Reviews: Conduct preventive Quality Control (QC) reviews of fraud alerts, investigations, disputes, SARs, and data quality activities prior to work completion, as well as retrospective Quality Assurance (QA) reviews of completed work. Evaluate compliance with quality standards, internal procedures, regulatory requirements, and service level expectations while ensuring consistency, accuracy, completeness, and sound investigative decision-making. Quality Standards & Review Methodology: Maintain and continuously improve QC and QA review methodologies, validation criteria, sampling methodologies, quality standards, review templates, and scoring guidance. Exercise sound judgment when evaluating work quality, particularly in situations where procedures require clarification or do not fully address unique circumstances. Quality Analytics, Reporting & Trend Analysis: Analyze QC and QA results to identify recurring themes, emerging risks, operational trends, and opportunities for improvement. Develop meaningful quality metrics, dashboards, and management reporting that provide actionable insights into operational performance and quality. Root Cause Analysis & Continuous Improvement: Analyze quality findings to determine underlying causes and distinguish between training opportunities, procedural gaps, process deficiencies, system limitations, workload challenges, and individual performance issues. Develop practical corrective and preventive action recommendations that strengthen operational effectiveness and improve quality outcomes. Process Improvement: Review operational processes and quality review results to identify opportunities to improve efficiency, consistency, customer experience, and risk management. Recommend enhancements to quality review methodologies and operational workflows while partnering with business process owners to implement improvements. Procedure Review & Enhancement: Evaluate operational procedures during quality reviews and identify opportunities to improve clarity, consistency, and effectiveness. Partner with the Governance Lead by providing recommendations based on quality review findings, operational experience, and identified process gaps. Coaching & Performance Development: Provide timely, constructive feedback to investigators and fraud operations staff based on QC and QA review results. Develop targeted coaching, job aids, and training recommendations that reinforce quality standards, improve investigator performance, and reduce recurring quality issues. Innovation, Automation & AI: Identify opportunities to improve quality reviews through automation, reporting enhancements, workflow optimization, and AI-enabled tools. Leverage technology to improve review efficiency, documentation quality, analytical capabilities, and reporting while maintaining appropriate human oversight and governance. Projects & Strategic Initiatives: Participate in departmental projects, audit remediation efforts, quality improvement initiatives, and cross-functional activities. Provide quality-related insights that strengthen fraud operations, improve internal controls, and support strategic objectives. Maintain and continuously improve QC and QA review methodologies, validation criteria, sampling methodologies, quality standards, review templates, and scoring guidance.

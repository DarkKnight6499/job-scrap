The Financial Analyst, Resource-Led Planning CoE, will support enterprise workforce planning capabilities through financial analysis, forecasting, reporting, governance, and planning transformation initiatives. This role will partner across Finance, Workforce Planning, People Team, Finance Systems, Analytics, and Technology teams to support integrated headcount planning, workforce planning analytics, reporting, and process improvements that enable more informed workforce and investment decisions. This position combines traditional FP&A responsibilities with workforce planning and planning transformation activities. The successful candidate will support workforce forecasting, reporting and analytics, planning-cycle execution, data governance initiatives, and continuous improvement efforts that enhance planning accuracy, transparency, and operational efficiency. The role offers an opportunity to contribute to one of Finance's key transformation priorities while building expertise across workforce planning, analytics, reporting, governance, and enterprise planning processes. Key Responsibilities Support workforce planning, forecasting, reporting, and analytics across enterprise planning cycles. Deliver reporting, dashboards, and insights to support workforce investment and planning decisions. Analyze workforce trends, forecasts, variances, risks, and opportunities. Support governance, data quality, documentation, and continuous improvement initiatives. Partner cross-functionally to enhance planning processes, reporting capabilities, and decision support. Contribute to planning transformation, automation, and planning system enhancement initiatives. This role is a Hybrid position to be located in our Austin, TX office location. Expectations will be 3 days in office to be aligned with the candidate and hiring manager.

Drive platform modernization efforts that improve operational efficiency, developer productivity, model governance, and business process automation across front-office, risk, and finance functions. Work cross-functional projects for strategic platform initiatives. Partner across organizations to align delivery milestones, and business priorities while ensuring measurable outcomes such as workflow automation, improved data quality, reduced operational risk, and faster time-to-market. Collaborate closely with stakeholders to define target-state architectures for official End-of-Day (EOD) market data distribution. Define and govern architecture standards, platform frameworks, and control models supporting FRAME Policy, Daily Marking Policy requirements, market input consistency and enterprise market data governance. Oversee development of FRAME APIs, milestone orchestration, DAL persistence services, and strategic integrations that improve data availability, simplify onboarding, and accelerate decommissioning of legacy market-data infrastructure. Guide engineering teams in the development and adoption of AI/Agentic solutions. Drive delivery of intelligent agents and workflow automation capabilities for model review document generation, evaluation and scoring workflows, governance controls, and software delivery lifecycle optimization. Manage and mentor engineering teams responsible for cloud-native platforms, market data services, and AI-enabled applications. Establish best practices in software engineering, solution architecture, DevSecOps, platform reliability, observability, and AI adoption while fostering technical excellence and leadership across multiple strategic initiatives. Establish engineering governance, SDLC controls, and production management practices that improve delivery predictability, system resiliency, audit readiness. Lead modernization initiatives. Support organizational growth and balance capacity against strategic priorities, business transformation programs, modernization initiatives, and AI platform.

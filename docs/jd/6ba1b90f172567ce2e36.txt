About the role: The Associate, Member and Provider Programs is responsible for working cross functionally with internal and external Oscar stakeholders and representing the needs and interests of the department in company wide initiatives. The position requires project management, implementation and communication skills, detail orientation and documentation with an ability to work across disparate initiatives and stakeholders at the same time. You will report into the Senior Manager, MPSO Initiatives. Work Location: This position is based in our New York City office, requiring a hybrid work schedule with 3 days of in-office work per week. Thursdays are a required in-office day for team meetings and events, while your other two office days are flexible to suit your schedule. #LI-Hybrid Pay Transparency: The base pay for this role is: $101,844 - $133,670 per year. Responsibilities: Leads the end-to-end management of cross-functional implementations/projects for member experience and member & provider contact centers. Assessing impact of new programs on existing operations and workflows along with member/provider/colleague experience. Steering program development to create leading consumer experience. Proactively identify risks and implement mitigation strategies. Developing needed frontline training, workflows, knowledge base, and other artifacts to ensure successful launch of new initiatives. Leading monitoring and issue triage at program launch. Collaboratively works with all relevant internal and external stakeholder groups to formulate proactive implementation, communications, and program strategies for successful change management and risk mitigation initiatives. Maintaining project plans, documentation and updating stakeholders on progress and status. Apply technology to transform how work is done unlocking better experiences for frontline colleagues and members at scale.

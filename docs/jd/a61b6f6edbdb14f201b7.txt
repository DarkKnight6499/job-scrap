Overview The Senior Private Investment Valuation Analyst will play a critical role in Wellington's private markets valuation platform, providing independent fair value assessments across private equity investments, LP fund interests, evergreen vehicles, and other illiquid securities. The role combines fundamental investment analysis, valuation governance, third-party provider oversight, ASC 820 expertise, and cross-functional collaboration to support robust and scalable valuation processes across a rapidly growing private investment platform. The successful candidate will bring extensive experience evaluating private equity and late-stage growth investments, supported by a strong foundation in financial analysis, valuation theory, and ASC 820 fair value measurement principles. They will exhibit sound judgment, intellectual curiosity, and exceptional attention to detail, while thriving in both independent and team-oriented environments. Progress toward, or completion of, a CFA charter or MBA is strongly preferred.

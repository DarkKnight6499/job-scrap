Management Level: 84 Work Shift: 1ST SHIFT (United States of America) SUMMARY: The Global Trade Access Compliance (GTAC) Associate Export Documentation is a key role within the Export Trade center of excellence in the Global Business Unit. This position ensures regulatory compliance to facilitate the shipping of Tyson products to international destinations. Key Responsibilities: Analyze, prepare, and manage export documentation, including health certificates, bills of lading, certifications, COAs, pack lists, shipping instructions, and slaughter letters. Provide necessary legal documentation for international product delivery. Coordinate with veterinarians, brokers, agents, and ship-owners to track and release exports. Ensure accuracy in export documentation to comply with global regulations and meet shipment schedules. Prioritize and expedite export document sets based on established priorities. Communicate with internal and external stakeholders, including plants, OCS locations, ocean carriers, scheduling teams, export coordinators, CFS stations, and trade lane managers. Utilize the USDA-FSIS PHIS Export Module and stay updated on customs regulations and export procedures. Create and update tracking reports on shared drive folders and IT systems, providing data to resolve issues. Perform additional duties as assigned by the supervisor. Regular contact with USDA personnel, freight forwarders, cold storage facilities, and document couriers. Requirements: Strong decision-making and problem-solving skills. Ability to coordinate with logistics, customer service, inventory management, accounting, billing, credit, sales, and other cross-functional partners. Additional Duties: Sorting and distributing mail. Preparing shipments via UPS, DHL, USPS, and Federal Express. Ordering supplies and other tasks as needed. This role is essential for ensuring the smooth and compliant export of Tyson products to various international markets. Qualifications: Education: Bachelor's degree preferred or equivalent combination of education and/or experience. Experience: 3+ years of experience in a compliance-related field and/or food industry. Computer Skills: Strong computer skills including MS Office suite. Experience with SAP preferred. Communication Skills: Excellent verbal and communication skills. Professional communication skills with both internal and external partners. Travel: Some travel is required.

Barclays Capital Inc. seeks Investment Banking Associate in New York, NY (multiple positions available): Build and maintain financial models, including DCF, LBO, merger models, and comparable analyses. Conduct valuation analyses and prepare supporting schedules and outputs. Perform financial statement analysis and assess company performance and capital structure. Prepare client presentation materials, including pitchbooks, board updates, and transaction analyses. Draft and review transaction materials such as confidential information memoranda (CIMs), management presentations, and process letters. Support execution of M&A and capital raising transactions, including managing data rooms and coordinating due diligence. Conduct industry and company research to support transaction workstreams and client materials. Monitor market activity and relevant precedent transactions across equity and debt markets. Coordinate with internal teams (legal, compliance, risk, operations) to support transaction approval and execution processes. Assist senior bankers in managing client interactions, including calls, diligence sessions, and workflow tracking. Prepare internal committee materials and ensure compliance with firm procedures and regulatory requirements.

Schedule Mon- Fri Overview Must live in one of the following states: AZ, CA, CO, HI, ID, IL, IA, MI, MN, NV, NH, NJ, NM, OK, OR, PN, TN, TX, UT, VT, WA, WI, or WY The Senior Risk Analyst position plays a critical role in promoting a risk aware culture and supporting America First Credit Union’s (AFCU) efforts to identify, measure, monitor, and control risk. The Senior Risk Analyst reports to the Manager of Risk Management and works closely with a variety of business units and subject matter experts to assess, communicate, and improve risk management practices throughout the organization including Credit, Interest Rate, Liquidity, Transaction, Compliance, Strategic, and Reputational Risk areas. By supervising a team of Risk Analysts, the Senior Risk Analyst facilitates risk assessments, reporting of key risk indicators (KRIs), and maintaining a risk registry to help strengthen internal controls and provide essential insights into AFCU’s overall risk profile. Responsibilities Help train, and mentor risk analysts, co-workers, and stakeholders.

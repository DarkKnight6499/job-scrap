Regular or Temporary: Regular Language Fluency: English (Required) Work Shift: 1st shift (United States of America) Please review the following job description: As part of the Capital Markets division within Truist Securities, Inc., the Securitized Products Group (SPG) provides structured debt capital solutions to clients of Truist across ABS (consumer, commercial, and esoteric asset classes), non-agency CMBS, middle-market lending, and CLO markets. SPG (i) partners with Sales and Trading to place securitized products with institutional investors in the new issue market and (ii) partners with Credit Delivery to originate collateralized credit facilities for our clients, funded by Truist Bank and/or syndicated to other banks and/or institutional investors. SPG collaborates with internal partners including Banking, Sales and Trading, Credit Delivery, Risk, Operations, and Legal to achieve seamless execution, appropriate risk mitigation and strong client experience.

Senior Risk Manager - Global Payment Network (GPN) Controls Advisory Team We’re looking for a strategic, forward thinking Senior Manager for our Global Payment Network (GPN) Controls Advisory Team. In this role, you will use your leadership, communication, analytical and problem solving skills to partner with the GPN business to modernize its controls. This talented team will act as a critical bridge between risk and business partners, providing expert guidance on controls best practices, new control builds, and modernizing and improving existing controls. You will need a well developed risk and control mindset, strong judgment, and the ability to influence priorities to GPN Leaders and across the Lines of Defense teams to grow and maintain a strong Controls Advisory program. The ideal candidates will possess a unique combination of strong leadership qualities including strategic planning, outstanding communication and soft skills, with natural problem solving and analytical skills and collaboration abilities. This position will require strong judgment and relationship management to understand the needs and requirements of partners across the company. Scope and Responsibilities: Act as a point of contact for controls-related projects and inquiries to provide expert advice and guidance on new control builds as well as modernization of existing controls. Drive initiatives to automate and improve control processes where appropriate. Develop of a scalable controls advisory function and framework to support the GPN business. Help to ensure alignment of first line controls with relevant regulations, risks, internal policies, and industry best practices. Partner with second and third lines of defense to ensure a cohesive and effective overall control environment. Provide oversight and guidance to the GPN business on the implementation and adherence to the controls governance framework and partner to embed controls governance principles within their day-to-day operations. Build and maintain strong relationships with key stakeholders across the first, second, and third lines of defense. Here’s what we’re looking for in an ideal teammate: You are a curious, critical thinker who seeks to understand the business, its processes, risks and control environment. You create leverage, positive energy and an environment that fosters trust, collaboration, and belonging. You possess a relentless focus on quality and timeliness. You adapt to change, embrace bold ideas, and are intellectually curious. You like to ask questions, test assumptions, and challenge conventional thinking. You develop influential relationships based upon shared risk objectives. You do the right thing and lead by example. Basic Qualifications: Bachelors Degree or military experience At least 5 years of Risk Management experience, with expertise on control creation, testing, methodology and design best practices. At least 5 years of experience supporting, partnering and interacting with internal business clients Experience leveraging AI tools to efficiently mitigate risk and improve processes Preferred Qualifications: Masters Degree 5+ years of Project Management experience leading cross functional projects and programs in Risk 5+ years of Financial Services risk management experience 3+ years of experience in Data Analysis 3+ years of people management experience Certified Risk Management Assurance (CRMA) or Certified Regulatory Compliance Manager (CRCM) Lean, Agile, Six Sigma or Business Process Management PMP Certification or Masters Certificate of Project Management (CPM) At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position.

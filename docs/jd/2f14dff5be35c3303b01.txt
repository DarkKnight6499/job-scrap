Responsibilities The Liquidity Advisor collaborates with Treasury Management, Commercial and Corporate Banking, as Corporate Treasury to optimize liquidity, strengthen deposit stability, and enable strategic balance sheet decisions. Operating as a recognized subject-matter expert, this role engages others in enterprise-wide liquidity strategy, influences pricing and product design decisions, and translates complex analytics into clear executive recommendations. This role advances ONB’s priorities around organic growth, exceptional client experiences, primacy, sales excellence, and operational rigor. They take the lead on deposit relationships and are responsible for generating new deposit business and fees, according to assigned deposit goals, by initiating and developing deposit relationships with businesses, industries, internal partners and others as appropriate. They are responsible for individual portfolio management and client relationship expansion, including partner referrals. Working across teams, the Liquidity Advisor contributes to strategic initiatives by understanding and executing with pricing, product, and portfolio decisions, while advancing ONB’s priorities around organic growth, client experience, and sales effectiveness.

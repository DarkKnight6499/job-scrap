Workplace Services Business Unit (BU) Risk provides first line risk management support to the four business units in Workplace Services (WS): Designated Brokerage Services, Retirement Business Services, Retirement Plan Services, and Stock Plan Services. Reporting to the Senior BU Risk Manager, this individual contributor Manager will play a key role in supporting fraud investigations on behalf of WS. BU Risk investigates suspected incidents of fraud and pursues recovery efforts of unauthorized activity in client accounts. You will also help identify areas for process improvement to limit risk to the firm and improve client experience. Your responsibilities will include, but not limited to, supporting the following program activities: Conduct high-profile, sophisticated fraud investigations, making consistent, well-reasoned decisions on risk and fraud issues. Manage a portfolio of cases in a fast-paced, dynamic environment, balancing casework with additional responsibilities. Communicate regularly with participants, clients, consultants, and key stakeholders, providing updates at the appropriate level of detail to enable action. Partner with key business partners to lead or support BU Risk initiatives and risk governance programs for Workplace Services. Provide consultation and analytical support to identify process and control weaknesses, develop solutions, and monitor implementation to ensure flawless client service and regulatory compliance.

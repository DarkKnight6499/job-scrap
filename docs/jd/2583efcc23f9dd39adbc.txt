RFFE Pricing Staff - Financial Analyst
Qualcomm

Company: Qualcomm Technologies, Inc. Job Area: Finance & Accounting Group, Finance & Accounting Group > Finance General Summary: Exciting opportunity to join a dynamic, fast‑paced finance team within Qualcomm’s Radio Frequency Front End (RFFE) Business Unit. This role supports commercial pricing strategy and financial decision‑making for customers and products within QCT and across multiple businesses. The position partners closely with Sales, Business Development, Legal, Operations, and FP&A to evaluate pricing opportunities, support negotiations, and influence forecast and accounting. This is a high‑visibility position with regular exposure to senior finance and business leadership. Key Responsibilities Support pricing strategy and deal analysis for new design wins, renewals, incentives/rebates and customer‑specific agreements Build and maintain financial models to evaluate pricing, margin, and ROI; perform scenario and sensitivity analysis to support pricing decisions and negotiations Support quarterly rolling financial forecast processes, along with SOX controls and average unit cost variance analysis Partner cross‑functionally to ensure pricing and product execution align with business objectives and internal policy Minimum Qualifications: • Bachelor's degree. • 4+ years of Finance, Accounting, or related work experience. *Completed advanced degrees in a relevant field may be substituted for up to two years (Master’s = one year, Doctorate = two years) of work experience. Preferred Qualifications: MBA or advanced degree Experience in technology or semiconductor industries Exposure to pricing strategy, deal support, or commercial finance Experience with Tableau, Salesforce, AI tools, or similar tools 6-10+ years of experience in finance, pricing, or FP&A Strong analytical and financial modeling skills Advanced proficiency in Excel and PowerPoint Qualcomm is an equal opportunity employer.

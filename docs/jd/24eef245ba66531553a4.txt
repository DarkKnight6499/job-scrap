Impact You'll Make Stout is seeking an experienced Associate to join our Investment Banking group, specifically within the Capital Markets team. Associates support the day-to-day execution of middle-market capital raising transactions. The Capital Markets team advises both sponsor-backed and non-sponsor middle market clients on private placements from investors such as private credit lenders, commercial banks, and other non-control investors. This role offers exposure to high-profile, primarily non-investment grade transactions across multiple industries, with opportunities to strengthen credit, execution, and client-facing skills. The position will focus on capital raising and placement across the full capital structure, with an emphasis on secured and unsecured debt. Qualified candidates will have a successful private placement track record and the ability to explain both debt- and structured equity-oriented transactions. Associates at Stout gain meaningful client exposure and continue to build technical skills while working closely with experienced Stout professionals. What You'll Do Structure and place debt (asset-based loans, revolving credit facilities, term loans, and structured debt) and structured equity using strong credit expertise Lead credit analysis and due diligence Prepare and deliver lender presentations and associated materials and three-statement cash flow modeling with complex debt schedules Work with practice leaders across the firm and sector leaders within investment banking to win and execute capital raising transactions Attend and facilitate client and lender meetings Assist in negotiation of terms and credit documentation Play a leadership role across all aspects of the transaction process Leverage AI-enabled tools and emerging technologies to enhance research, streamline deliverable preparation, and improve overall efficiency and insight What You Bring Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Accounting, Business, or a related field. 3+ years of relevant professional experience in leveraged finance, capital markets investment banking, commercial or corporate credit, or another capital raising role Experience working across the credit lifecycle and transactions Prior leveraged finance or high yield experience preferred, but not required Advanced proficiency in Excel, Microsoft Office, Word, and PowerPoint Beginner or intermediate knowledge of SAS, SQL, Python, R, or Tableau Demonstrated ability to produce clear, accurate, and well-structured client materials. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to interact effectively with clients, senior professionals, and transaction counterparties. Sound judgment and the ability to anticipate issues, establish priorities, and manage multiple workstreams in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment. High attention to detail and a strong sense of ownership over work product and deadlines. Familiarity with or interest in using AI tools and technology to enhance productivity, research, and analysis Must be authorized to work in the United States without current or future employer sponsorship.

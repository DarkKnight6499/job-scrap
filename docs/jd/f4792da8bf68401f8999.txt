Job Description What is the Opportunity? Global Manager Research (GMR) is a team of analysts dedicated to providing investment manager research and due diligence in support of RBC’s global businesses and distribution platforms. The broader team consists of investment professionals located in Minneapolis, Toronto, Asia and London and supports an asset base in excess of $650 billion USD. The team conducts initial and on-going research, analysis and due diligence of investment management firms offering mutual funds, separately managed accounts, exchange-traded funds, and sub-advisory relationships. The primary responsibility of the Senior Quantitative Research Analyst is to oversee all aspects of the quantitative tools and methods used to monitor, score, and source investment managers. This includes maintaining and improving existing tools, as well as creating and designing innovative tools that improve the efficiency, consistency and effectiveness of the research and due diligence process. What will you do?

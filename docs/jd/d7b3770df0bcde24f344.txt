HarbourVest Partners (HVP) is seeking a Vice President, Evergreen Valuation to lead the build and delivery of the valuation technology, data, and control environment supporting HVP’s Evergreen offerings. This role will establish a scalable and resilient infrastructure for the valuation process, develop governed gold-copy data, automate key workflows and controls, and deliver analytics that improve valuation attribution, back-testing, oversight, and decision-making. Working closely with Fund Accounting, Investment Management, Product Development, IT, Information, Risk Management, auditors, third-party providers, and external valuation specialists falls under the Vice President’s role. We're seeking an experienced private-markets valuation expert who has optimally modernized data, technology, controls, and operating processes. The candidate will combine strong valuation judgment with product ownership, program execution, and a practical understanding of data governance, control design, supervised fund operations, and technology-enabled transformation. The ideal candidate is someone who is: A valuation specialist who translates business and control requirements into a clear technology and data strategy. Experienced in building scalable systems, supervised data structures, developed automated procedures, and balanced control frameworks. Deep expertise in private-market investments, approaches to valuation, fund configurations, NAV processes, and audit and regulatory expectations. Able to apply analytics and AI to strengthen controls and decision-making while maintaining appropriate validation, explainable, observation, and human oversight. An effective cross-functional leader who can align valuation, accounting, investment, product, technology, data, risk, and external partners. Highly analytical and able to turn sophisticated data into clear insights for valuation committees, investment committees, executive leaders, auditors, and control functions. Accountable, pragmatic, and passionate about results, with the ability to lead multi-year initiatives from strategy and development through implementation and ongoing ownership. What you will do: We are looking for the Vice President to lead all aspects of the strategy, roadmap, delivery, and ongoing performance of the technology, data, analytics, and control capabilities supporting Evergreen valuation. Technology, Data & Control Infrastructure: Build and operate a resilient, scalable, and well-controlled ecosystem supporting daily, monthly, and quarterly Evergreen valuation activities—from source-data ingestion and calculation through review, approval, reporting, and audit support. Define and implement the multi-year architecture and product roadmap related to Evergreen valuation, including core platforms and upstream and downstream integrations. Establish authoritative gold-copy datasets for portfolio, fund, market, accounting, and valuation information, with clear ownership, lineage, quality standards, and change governance. Automate data ingestion, validation, reconciliation, calculation, workflow, approval, and evidence retention to improve speed, consistency, and control. Design AI-enabled controls and exception monitoring, supported by detailed use cases, validation, performance monitoring, and human review. Implement dashboards and management reporting for data quality, workflow status, valuation exceptions, control performance, and service levels. Embed information security, access management, business continuity, auditability, records retention, and regulatory requirements into the design and operation of the environment. Analytics, Attribution & Operating Model: Develop analytics that explain valuation movements and attribute changes to market, company-specific, methodology, foreign exchange, cash-flow, and other relevant drivers. Build the data and analytical framework for systematic back-testing of prior valuation assumptions and conclusions against subsequent transactions, realizations, and other observable outcomes. Partner with valuation teams to standardize data requirements, methodologies, workflow stages, review protocols, and control evidence across Evergreen products. Serve as product owner for Evergreen valuation technology by setting priorities, defining requirements, coordinating delivery and testing, and driving adoption. Lead relevant vendors, data providers, and implementation partners, including service levels, issue resolution, control assurance, and roadmap alignment. Establish metrics for cycle time, data quality, exception rates, control effectiveness, adoption, and realized automation benefits, and use them to drive continuous improvement. Provide clear reporting and insights to the Valuation Committee, fund boards, senior management, auditors, and control partners.

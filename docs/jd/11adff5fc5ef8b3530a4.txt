The Associate Engineer in Telecom Engineering is responsible for the Engineering and Design of Fiber Optic Networks and associated Communication Systems that provide the Enterprise and Operational communication services within Eversource. The Associate Engineer will develop detailed engineering design documentation and specifications for the Eversource communications infrastructure. Primarily this will include being the assigned telecom engineer for Substation and or Fiber/Line projects. The Associate Engineer will work with other disciplines to develop scope, specifications, design, and schedule necessary to deliver reliable and complaint solutions. This is a hybrid role. The first three months will be full time in the office. Qualifications: Education: A Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Electrical Engineering Technology, Telecommunications/Network Engineering, or an associated discipline from an accredited engineering school is preferred. Requires a Bachelor’s degree in engineering. Experience: 0-3 years of related experience is required. Base knowledge and interest in communications multiplexers/systems, network equipment and associated circuits is preferred.

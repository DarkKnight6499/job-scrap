Dedicated to innovation, our Surgical business unit is focused on identifying and solving critical, unmet needs in cardiac surgery. In understanding the challenges of open-heart surgery, we partner with surgical teams to deliver modern technologies intended to enhance patients’ quality of life and expand their potential treatment opportunities. It’s our driving force to help patients live longer and healthier lives. Join us and be part of our inspiring journey. This is a key role operating with exposure to leadership and key stakeholders across Public Affairs. The individual serves as a trusted partner driving invoicing and contracting needs with a focus on Healthcare Providers (HCP) and doing so with professionalism, sound judgment, and leadership presence. How you'll make an impact : Success in this role requires exceptional communication, attention to detail, and relationship management skills, with the ability to engage confidently and directly in challenging conversations while maintaining credibility and respect. The individual must be comfortable navigating ambiguity, managing competing priorities, and influencing without direct authority. The role demands a high degree of trust, discretion, and confidentiality. The ideal candidate demonstrates executive presence, professionalism, and a polished approach while consistently fostering collaboration, accountability, and organizational effectiveness. Generate and coordinate Healthcare Provider (HCP) and vendor contracting and amendments through completion with functional contract advisor leads, including corporate counsel, procurement/sourcing, compliance, risk management, information security, and other stakeholders; manage assigned follow-ups for sections of work to meet established timelines (20%) Ad-hoc assist with the initiation, review, redlining, coordination, and negotiation of routine transactional HCP and vendor contracts in accordance with approved playbooks, company policies, standard practices, and guidance from Legal and business stakeholders (10%) Manage internal payment and contracting systems (e.g. HCP engage, Coupa, Coupa go, ECLM) to ensure timely execution and payment of contracts (15%) Interface with cross-functional and external stakeholders to help resolve routine pending contract approvals, workflow issues, and contracting questions within established policies and procedures, including external stakeholders to complete information needed and/or obtain clarification on information needed to finalize contracts and invoices (15%) Review contract intake materials for completeness and accuracy, identify missing routine information needed for stakeholder review, and work with requestors to resolve basic intake questions or route issues for guidance.

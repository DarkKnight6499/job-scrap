The Impact you will have in this role: The Client Interfaces Portfolio Delivery Manager is responsible for driving delivery excellence, governance, planning, and operational transparency across the Client Interfaces portfolio. This role partners closely with Product Managers, Product Owners, Engineering Leads, Tribe Leadership, EPMO, Finance, and key stakeholders to ensure initiatives are effectively planned, governed, tracked, and delivered. The successful candidate combines strong portfolio management expertise with working knowledge of machine-to-machine client interaction technologies, including APIs, messaging, streaming, file transfer, connectivity, onboarding, and client integration platforms. The individual serves as a trusted advisor to leadership, ensuring portfolio investments translate into measurable outcomes while maintaining compliance with enterprise governance and delivery standards. The role also serves as the primary liaison between the EPMO lead and Client Interfaces Platform, coordinates platform-wide planning and leadership reporting, and provides the Tribe Lead and Product Management team with actionable portfolio insights and recommendations.

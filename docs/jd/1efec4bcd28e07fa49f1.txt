Risk & Underwriting Leadership Development Program Analyst (RULDP): This position will start on Monday June 7, 2027 Location: is Hybrid in Denver, CO Your Future in Underwriting Starts Here. Imagine a career where your ideas, analysis, and judgment help drive smarter decisions and stronger outcomes. In RULDP Evolve at The Cigna Group, you won’t just learn underwriting; you’ll live it. Through immersive training, mentorship, and real responsibility, you’ll gain the confidence and expertise to evaluate risk, influence strategy, and build a career with purpose and impact. Designed to support your transition from academia to industry, this program is ideal for recent graduates and early‑career professionals who are curious, motivated, and eager to apply their academic knowledge in a real-world setting. Key Responsibilities: Assess risk characteristics and recommend pricing and terms that balance competitiveness with financial discipline. Apply quantitative analysis and underwriting judgment to evaluate profitability drivers and articulate trade-offs. After training, help manage a book of business by monitoring performance, identifying trends, and taking proactive action. Collaborate with Sales and Account Management to design solutions that meet customer needs and support retention. Negotiate with clients and brokers using clear rationale, professional presence, and strong follow-through. Share insights and best practices that improve underwriting consistency, efficiency, and outcomes. Leadership Development: Coaching, workshops, mentoring, and hands-on business initiatives to accelerate your growth. Mentorship & Networking: Connect with early-career talent and senior leaders dedicated to your professional development. Required Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree is required.

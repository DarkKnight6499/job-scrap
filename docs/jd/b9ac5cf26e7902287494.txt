Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. The Bioanalytical Sciences (BAS) Automation group at Regeneron is seeking a Laboratory Automation Associate. This position will contribute to the development, implementation, and maintenance of laboratory automation solutions within BAS. In this role, you will work with automation technologies used to streamline bioanalytical laboratory workflows, support automated assay development, and help ensure reliable and efficient laboratory operations. When & where: Location: Tarrytown, NY (On-site) Discover your role: Working with integrated laboratory automation systems, including liquid handlers and automated laboratory instrumentation in a regulated environment Collaborating with automation engineers and laboratory scientists to develop, implement, and troubleshoot automated workflows for laboratory processes Developing and updating programs for automation systems, including debugging scripts, performing test runs, and supporting process optimization activities Performing manual and automated experiments to support assay development, troubleshooting, and automated process investigations Providing technical support and guidance to end users on the operation and maintenance of automation systems and laboratory instrumentation This role requires: A Bachelor's degree in a scientific or engineering field 0–3 years of relevant industry experience in a laboratory environment Basic programming knowledge or experience Basic knowledge or experience with laboratory instrumentation Enjoyment of troubleshooting technical challenges and identifying practical solutions Strong problem-solving skills and a detail-oriented mindset The ability to effectively communicate and collaborate with cross-functional teams An interest in laboratory automation and learning new technologies Experience operating liquid handling instruments (e.g., Tecan, Hamilton, Lynx, or similar platforms) and knowledge of integrated systems is preferred Does this sound like you?

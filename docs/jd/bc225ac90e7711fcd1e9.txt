Role Summary: As Financial Analyst – Food & Beverage and Marketing, you will connect two views of the guest: how they respond to our marketing and what they buy at the concession stand. Reporting to the Finance Manager – Marketing and working closely with the Finance Manager – Food & Beverage, you will spend most of your time on Marketing finance, including loyalty and subscription economics, promotion ROI, digital channels and the marketing budget. Your F&B work centers on where the two meet: F&B promotions, mobile ordering and digital menu boards. Responsbilities: Loyalty, Subscription and Promotion Analysis Maintain loyalty and subscription reporting on membership, retention and member F&B spend Measure the ROI of value-day and discount programs using pre/post analysis Analyze ticket and F&B combo promotions for impact on attendance, incidence and per cap Build promotion post-mortems summarizing results, payback and lessons learned Digital Channels Maintain app, web and mobile ordering dashboards, tracking conversion and F&B attach rates Analyze digital menu board results with the Finance Manager – Food & Beverage Marketing Budget and Partnerships Track media, agency and campaign spend against budget and forecast, explaining variances Prepare marketing accruals with Accounting and maintain forecast inputs Model the impact of brand and studio partnerships, sponsorships and co-promotions Reporting and Data Build monthly reporting packages that connect Marketing and F&B results Validate marketing, digital and point-of-sale data before results are reported Requirements: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics or a related field 0–2 years of experience; new grads with finance or accounting internships are welcome Strong Excel skills, including building and maintaining models Comfort working with large data sets and turning them into clear findings Strong attention to detail and a commitment to accurate, well-documented work Clear communication, including explaining results to marketing and operations partners Curiosity, initiative and the ability to manage multiple deadlines Preferred Experience with Power BI or SQL Exposure to an enterprise planning tool such as Anaplan Exposure to marketing analytics, promotion measurement or loyalty programs Internship or work experience in retail, restaurant, entertainment or consumer marketing Work Arrangement This is a hybrid role based at our corporate headquarters in Plano, Texas, with three days per week in the office.

The Associate Director, Global Policy Strategy Execution will help shape and execute BMS’ global policy agenda by developing clear policy strategies, evidence-based positions, advocacy tools, and thought leadership content that support business priorities and strengthen BMS’ credibility with external stakeholders.The role will operate across Global Policy and Government Affairs and the wider cross-functional matrix, working closely with regional and market teams, functional experts, research colleagues, communications, legal, and senior leaders to ensure policy priorities are well defined, well supported, and effectively implemented. Key responsibilities: Global policy strategy and execution Lead the development, coordination and execution of global policy strategies that advance BMS priorities, including policies that reward innovation, support patient access, and strengthen pro-growth investment environments. Translate complex policy, business, and external environment issues into clear priorities, recommendations, and advocacy approaches for use at global, regional, and market levels. Work across Global Policy and Government Affairs and the cross-functional matrix to ensure alignment on policy priorities, advocacy resources, and execution plans. Policy development, evidence, and advocacy tools Develop policy proposals, positions, narratives, and toolkits, in close collaboration with functional experts, that equip BMS teams to engage effectively with policymakers, other external stakeholders, and policy influencers. Partner within Global Policy and Government Affairs to define evidence generation priorities, identify research needs, and determine the most effective approaches for credible policy research. Build partnerships with academic institutions, think tanks, and other external experts to support independent research, generate new policy ideas, and strengthen BMS’ external thought leadership. Thought leadership and online policy platform Define the editorial and policy strategy for BMS’ online policy platform, ensuring it presents credible, timely, and policy-relevant content that advances BMS’ policy priorities and external reputation. Lead the development of content that showcases BMS’ policy ideas, evidence, patient- and innovation-focused perspectives, and practical advocacy tools. Coordinate internal review, governance, and content planning processes so the platform remains high quality, current, and aligned with BMS standards and priorities. Facilitation of external policy executive advice for BMS leaders Support the work of the Global Policy Strategy & Impact team to help BMS senior leaders access strategic insight and external advice on the policy and business environment. Provide strategic input into the framing of executive advisory meetings, develop high-quality briefing materials, and guide follow-up to ensure insights are translated into meaningful action. External engagement and partnership building Engage external stakeholders in relevant global forums to advance BMS’ global policy agenda, complementing regional and market-level advocacy. Support the Senior Director, Global Policy Strategy Execution, Trade & IP Policy Lead in developing and maintaining relationships with selected multilateral organizations, trade associations, think tanks, academic experts, and policy forums where engagement can advance BMS priorities. Represent BMS policy priorities with clarity, judgement, and credibility, while ensuring external engagement is coordinated with relevant internal stakeholders. Skills and experience required Approximately eight years of relevant experience in public policy, government affairs, health policy, external affairs, or a related field, preferably with exposure to the biopharmaceutical sector. Advanced degree in political science, law, economics, business, public policy, health policy, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience. Bachelor's degree required. Strong understanding of the policy issues affecting the biopharmaceutical industry, including pricing and reimbursement, market access, innovation policy, investment incentives, tax, and the broader external environment. Understanding of the U.S. healthcare policy environment and at least one other country or system. Proven ability to implement policy strategies, through advocacy materials, policy narratives, and senior-level briefing documents that are clear, persuasive, and grounded in evidence. Ability to influence across the matrix, build trusted relationships, and work effectively across functions, geographies, and cultures. Experience working with government stakeholders, multilateral organizations, trade associations, think tanks, academic institutions, or other external policy-relevant organizations. Strategic and analytical mindset, with the ability to develop practical solutions to complex policy and business challenges. Highly organized, self-directed, and able to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced and evolving environment. Strong attention to detail and a proven ability to deliver high-quality work within deadlines. Excellent drafting, communication, and presentation skills. Fluent in English; proficiency in additional languages would be an asset.

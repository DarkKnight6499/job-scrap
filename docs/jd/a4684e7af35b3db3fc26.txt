Global Banking & Markets – Capital Solutions Group - Private Equity Capital Call (PECC) Financing, Analyst - New York Role Overview: The Global Fund Finance business within the Capital Solutions Group in Global Banking and Markets deploys the firm’s balance sheet across a full suite of structured financing solutions across the private market ecosystems.

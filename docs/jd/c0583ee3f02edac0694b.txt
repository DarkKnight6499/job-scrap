If Braze sounds like a place where you can thrive, we can’t wait to meet you. WHAT YOU'LL DO The Finance team is seeking a Financial Analyst to join our growing Financial Planning & Analysis team. You will be the primary FP&A business partner to the CIO organization, responsible for tracking, forecasting and reporting on key technology metrics and investments across Corporate IT, Business Systems, and Data Analytics functions. You will report to the Manager of FP&A and work closely with CIO leadership and group heads to improve technology financial planning and monthly reporting standards. This role requires someone who is highly analytical and strategic, is able to communicate clearly with both finance and technology leaders, and thrives in a fast-paced environment. Main responsibilities: Partner with the CIO organization and business leaders across Corporate IT, Business Systems, and Data Analytics to generate technology operations budgets, forecasts and long-range plans including monthly, quarterly, and annual cycles Track, forecast and report on key technology financial metrics including AI tokenization spend, infrastructure spend, cloud consumption, licensing costs, vendor contracts, and IT operational KPIs Support monthly close process through close partnership with accounting and operations teams, ensuring accurate tracking of technology/AI investments and spend Provide insights and commentary on key technology trends, infrastructure investments, cloud migration initiatives, and technology modernization to finance leadership and business stakeholders Report on actual technology financial results against targets, provide variance analysis, assist in guidance on future trends, and present findings to Finance and CIO leadership Evaluate and develop financial models and scenario analyses to support strategic technology decisions, including AI investments, cloud migration, infrastructure investments, and operational efficiency improvements Challenge assumptions and provide independent financial perspective on technology strategies, infrastructure investments, and digital transformation initiatives Support LRP and budget planning by translating business requirements and growth targets into technology investment needs and roadmaps Support ad hoc projects and strategic initiatives including contract analysis and cost optimization opportunities WHO YOU ARE 3+years of progressive experience in Finance and FP&A, preferably in Tech Undergrad degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics, or

Job Family : Technology Consulting Travel Required : None Clearance Required : Ability to Obtain Public Trust What You Will Do: Support a federal Chief Information Officer (CIO) in enhancing office operations and project delivery across a wide range of initiatives and systems. Lead the development of strategic plans, roadmaps, and project plans that support complex technology integration efforts. Provide insights to clients to help establish project priorities, resources, and timelines, building trusted relationships with stakeholders along the way. Initiate, execute, and close out projects of varying scope, size, and complexity while serving as a senior business analyst. Sourcing and evaluating customer requirements across multiple systems and stakeholder groups. Track the delivery of key initiatives and enable success through strong analysis and coordination. Use technical expertise to identify and escalate project risks and dependencies. Collaborate with agency leaders, vendors, and contracting teams to support successful delivery. Manage and monitor data calls provided by internal and external stakeholders. Create and report briefings to client leadership. Own and execute additional key Department initiatives as required by the CIO. What You Will Need: U.S. citizenship is contractually required for this role. Must be able to OBTAIN and MAINTAIN a Federal or DoD "PUBLIC TRUST"; candidates must obtain approved adjudication of their PUBLIC TRUST prior to onboarding with Guidehouse. Minimum of EIGHT (8) or more years of experience supporting technology projects. PMP Certification is highly preferred, but ability to obtain is required. Demonstrated success delivering multiple technology initiatives and building deep trust with customers and stakeholders. Experience managing or supporting technical projects, escalating risks, and/or managing schedules. Experience with enterprise systems and deployments (e.g. IT Service Management, Change Requests, and/or Business Analysis, etc.). Ability to understand complex system and project needs and translate them into actionable business analysis. What Would Be Nice To Have: Bachelor's degree is highly preferred An ACTIVE and MAINTAINED TOP SECRET Federal or DoD security clearance. Excellent verbal and written communication skills. Consulting experience; Federal government consulting a plus. The annual salary range for this position is $113,000.00-$188,000.00.

About the Group Moelis & Company’s Capital Markets Group structures and executes customized financing solutions and advises clients on all aspects of public and private debt and equity transactions. The Group provides independent, objective financing advice across a broad range of situations, including buyouts, growth capital, platform formation, refinancings, restructurings, recapitalizations, project financing, public company financings, and IPOs. Across public and private transactions, the Group advises clients on preparation, positioning, valuation, investor messaging, and end-to-end execution of the financing process. Our independence and objectivity, coupled with direct and long-standing institutional buy-side relationships, inform our market views and strengthen the quality of our execution, leading to successful transactions for our clients. Representative product areas include: Debt products: ABL, public and private term loans / unitranche, DIP / exit financing, investment grade debt, high yield bonds, mezzanine debt, and other bespoke credit solutions Equity and equity-linked products: IPOs, convertible debt, structured equity, preferred equity, PIPEs, corporate and portfolio company recapitalizations, follow-on and public company financings, and Board of Directors advisory The Group maintains direct dialogue with a wide range of institutional investors, including private equity sponsors, venture capital firms, hedge funds, alternative asset managers, bank and non-bank lenders, sovereign wealth and pension funds, family offices, and other capital providers, to ensure the appropriate capital pool can be accessed for our clients. For more information, please refer to: https://www.moelis.com/what-we-do/capital-markets/ Role Overview Vice Presidents are expected to assume significant levels of responsibility requiring intellectual curiosity, sound judgment, leadership, analytical rigor, and strong communication skills. The day-to-day responsibilities of a Vice President include performing financial analysis and modeling, developing and evaluating financing structures, preparing client and investor presentations, managing Associates & Analysts, and coordinating across clients, senior bankers, investors, lenders, and other transaction counterparties to help execute transactions from mandate through closing.

How you’ll help move us forward: • Support quoting activities for institutional Defined Benefit solutions. • Complete liability cash-flow projections and related analyses. • Maintain accurate pricing updates reflecting economic, competitive, investment, risk-management, and experience assumptions. • Support post-sale case activities, including repricing analyses, profitability monitoring, and coordination of pricing deliverables used by implementation and operations teams. • Support testing and implementation of process or tool enhancements, including development testing, beta testing, UAT, and production rollout. • Provide timely and accurate information for decision-making.

Morgan Stanley Investment Management (MSIM) is one of the largest global asset management organizations of any full-service securities firm, with more than 40 years of history, a presence in 26 countries, and a total of $2.0 trillion in assets under management as of June 30, 2026. MSIM strives to provide outstanding long-term investment performance, service and a comprehensive suite of investment management solutions to a diverse client base, which includes governments, institutions, corporations and individuals worldwide. Morgan Stanley's Tactical Value team is a part of the ~$62bn Private Credit & Equity platform within MSIM. The group manages ~$4bn and makes private equity, growth equity, and structured credit investments across industries. The strategy capitalizes on both connectivity with Morgan Stanley IBD and GCM as well as relationships outside of the firm for sourcing, diligence, and value creation after the investment. The team is a generalist group that invests in both large cap and middle market deals with check sizes of $50-$750MM+. Recent industry focus includes investments in tech/cybersecurity, consumer, professional/industrial services, media and sports. This is a unique opportunity to join a growing and entrepreneurial team with significant upward mobility. Responsibilities: MSIM Tactical Value is looking for a Pre-MBA Associate to work directly with senior investors and decision-makers and serve as a key contributor throughout the lifecycle of investing, including the evaluation of investment opportunities, conducting in-depth sector analysis, developing and analyzing financial projections and valuations, and monitoring portfolio companies post investment.

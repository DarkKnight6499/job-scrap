Lead partnership with Product leadership and collaborate with Treasury, Risk, Finance, and business stakeholders to deliver actionable insights and balance sheet optimization. Set the global operating rhythm, strengthen governance and controls, and drive strategic execution to optimize profitability, pricing, resilience, and capital/funding efficiency. Lead digital transformation initiatives to automate and elevate portfolio financial processes, enhancing oversight, transparency, and decision-making across the lending portfolio. You will set and continually elevate the global operating rhythm , strengthening governance and controls while driving disciplined strategic execution to optimize profitability, pricing excellence, resilience, and capital/funding efficiency . Champion digital transformation to automate portfolio reporting and analytics, enhancing oversight, control effectiveness, and management information quality across the franchise.

Job summary: We are seeking a highly skilled FP&A Senior Manager to join our growing FP&A department at Integrity Marketing Group. Let’s talk job responsibilities: Manage and train a team of FP&A Analysts supporting the Health Insurance Business Units, while fostering high performance. Own revenue forecasting as a core focus of this role for the Health division’s business units, including building and maintaining revenue models, monitoring key revenue drivers and trends, and partnering with business unit leaders to ensure forecast accuracy. Assist the FP&A Senior Director in leading core financial processes related to Forecasting, Budgeting, Long Range Planning, Financial Reporting and Analysis. Own the implementation of the planning process, milestones and consolidation of data. · Produce KPI reporting and root cause trends in KPIs. Maintain, enhance, and contribute to continuous improvement of the FP&A reporting & modelling tool, OneStream. Provide financial and strategic support to senior management and business unit leadership, including preparing financial presentations and other projects. Support executive reporting and the related period-end discussions with business leaders · Work cross-functionally with the: Accounting team on variance analysis and monthly reporting. Business Intelligence & Analytics team on revenue modelling, KPIs, metrics and other business drivers. People and Culture teams on headcount metrics, compensation & benefits analysis, and workforce planning. Department leaders on annual planning and forecasting Develop business cases, scenario analyses, and financial models, as needed Contribute to initiatives to define and improve planning processes, drive automation, and streamline deliverables. Compare actual results with forecasts/annual operating plans to analyze and identify risks and opportunities. Perform ad-hoc business analysis for leadership team to further inform business decisions.

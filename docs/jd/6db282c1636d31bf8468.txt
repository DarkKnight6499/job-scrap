This individual will play a key role in driving the measurement, tracking, and communication of IAM governance activities, ensuring leadership has clear visibility into risk posture, control effectiveness, and operational performance. The role will support the development, enhancement, and maintenance of governance reporting, dashboards, and key performance indicators (KPIs) aligned with organizational expectations. The IAM Governance Sr. Associate will collaborate closely with cross-functional stakeholders to gather data, validate metrics, and ensure consistency in reporting across governance processes such as access certifications, policy adherence, and remediation tracking. This individual will also contribute to the continuous improvement of governance reporting frameworks, helping to standardize outputs and improve data quality. Additionally, the role will support the IAM Governance Manager in coordinating governance activities, preparing materials for senior leadership, and maintaining documentation related to governance processes, controls, and reporting methodologies.

Position Summary Grant Thornton is seeking a Senior Associate to join our People & Culture Business Impact team, supporting large-scale P&C transformation initiatives across our evolving platform. This role works closely with the Associate Director, P&C Business Impact, and other key stakeholders, providing day-to-day management of these efforts, managing project timelines, and driving progress on initiatives. The ideal candidate brings project management expertise within the People function, a proven track record of relationship management and cross-functional partnership, and strong organizational and documentation skills. Key Responsibilities Serve as the primary day-to-day point of contact for assigned entities Support the Associate Director, Business Impact on strategic initiatives and coordination across stakeholders. Develop and maintain project plans, timelines, milestones, and trackers for P&C initiatives across workstreams. Create and manage project documentation including meeting minutes, action logs, RAID logs, and status reports. Conduct data analysis and prepare reports to inform decision-making and monitor progress. Collaborate with cross-functional teams and adapt to diverse cultural contexts across the platform Support change management planning and execution activities, partnering with the Change Management team on communications, training rollout, and readiness tracking. Proactively identify, assess, and mitigate risks and issues, escalating to the Associate Director as needed. Support requirements gathering and process mapping activities with business and HR stakeholders. Identify opportunities for process improvement and implement scalable solutions. Qualifications Bachelor's degree required 3 to 6 years of experience supporting PMO or project delivery functions within People & Culture/HR, organizational development, consulting, transformation, change management, technology-enabled, or enterprise programs. Strong project management, organizational, documentation, and communication skills , with close attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously. Proficiency in data analysis and reporting tools (e.g., Excel, Power BI, Smartsheet). Familiarity with project management methodologies; PRINCE2 or PMP certification is a plus. Demonstrated ability to leverage productivity tools (e.g., Microsoft 365 Copilot or other AI tools) appropriately to accelerate work, improve quality and support analysis and communications Ability to collaborate effectively with stakeholders across organizational levels and geographic regions while managing multiple priorities in a fast-paced, matrixed environment. Experience supporting HRIS or HR system implementations is advantageous. Prior experience supporting mergers, acquisitions, or divestitures within an HR or people function is a plus.

The Junior Analyst, Commercial Operations Analytics supports data-driven decision-making within Global Commercial Sales Operations by assisting with compensation related reporting, analytics, forecasting support, and operational insights. This role focuses on executing standardized analytics, maintaining dashboards, and supporting cross-functional teams with accurate and timely data. The Junior Analyst partners closely with Senior Analysts and the Senior Manager to support compensation reporting, KPI tracking, and commercial performance analysis across the Tissue Technologies (TT) and Codman Specialty Surgical (CSS) Global Product Divisions. This role provides foundational analytics support while developing skills in sales analytics, forecasting, and commercial operations within a global environment. SUPERVISION RECEIVED Sr Manager, Commercial Operations (Sales Analytics and Opportunity Development) SUPERVISION EXERCISED Individual Contributor KEY RESPONSIBILITIES COMPENSATION ANALYTICS AND INCENTIVE SUPPORT Conduct basic modeling and scenario analyses to evaluate proposed compensation plan changes. Assist with compensation accrual reporting and collaborate with Finance on payout reconciliation activities. Support annual and periodic quota-setting processes through data compilation, performance analysis, and forecasting. Prepare recurring reports and dashboards related to sales compensation, territory performance, and quota achievement. Validate compensation-related data from Salesforce, ERP, and reporting systems to ensure accurate incentive calculations. Assist in analyzing sales compensation performance, payout trends, quota attainment, and incentive effectiveness. Support the administration, tracking, and validation of sales incentive compensation plans and commission calculations.

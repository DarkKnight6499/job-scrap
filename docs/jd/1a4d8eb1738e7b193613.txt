Overview The BSA High Risk Team Lead is responsible for overseeing the Bank's enhanced due diligence (EDD) program and day-to-day monitoring of higher-risk customer relationships. This role leads a team of High Risk Analysts responsible for conducting transaction analysis, investigating unusual activity, and preparing comprehensive EDD reviews utilizing the Bank's AML monitoring systems. The Team Lead ensures accurate identification, investigation, escalation, and documentation of suspicious activity, including the review and filing of Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) when appropriate. The BSA High Risk Team Lead may also assist with oversight of Customer Identification Program (CIP) and Customer Due Diligence (CDD) exception monitoring processes. This includes reviewing CIP and CDD exceptions for completeness and accuracy, ensuring timely remediation of deficiencies, monitoring trends, escalating unresolved issues, and partnering with business lines to maintain compliance with regulatory requirements and the Bank's Customer Due Diligence Program. In addition, the BSA High Risk Team Lead may serve as the primary BSA liaison for the Bank's Wealth Management program. Responsibilities include coordinating and scheduling customer reviews, conducting risk assessments, approving customer relationships, and ensuring adherence to the Bank's risk-based customer due diligence requirements. The Team Lead collaborates closely with the BSA High Risk Officer and business-line partners to address issues identified during customer reviews, support regulatory examinations and internal audits, and promote the consistent application of BSA/AML policies and procedures. This position plays a key role in mitigating financial crime risk while ensuring compliance with applicable regulatory requirements and industry best practices. Lead and supervise a team of BSA High Risk Analysts, providing guidance, coaching, and technical support to ensure quality and timely completion of assigned work. Monitor team workflow, productivity, and case inventories to ensure compliance with established service level standards and regulatory requirements Review and assist with alerts, investigations, and cases requiring escalation or subject matter expertise. Coordinate and oversee EDD reviews, ensuring completion according to established schedules, procedural and regulatory requirements, along with providing recommendations for account retention or closure Review and analyze OFAC sanction alerts, CDD/EDD alerts, treasury management customer reviews, and other high risk activity Assess transaction patterns, identify red flags, and prepare risk-based analysis reports for escalation or decisioning. Ensure thorough and accurate customer due diligence (CDD) and enhanced due diligence (EDD) for elevated-risk customers Perform monitoring and analysis of transaction activity to identify unusual patterns (structuring, rapid balance shifts, abnormal cash volumes, wires, ACH, new accounts, etc.) Oversee and/or conduct SAR investigations, determining whether the activity meets filing thresholds and ensuring SARs are filed accurately and timely.

Role Summary We are seeking an experienced technology leader to head the Data, Reporting, and Analytics function within the Working Capital Solutions (WCS) domain, which includes Supply Chain Finance, Trade Finance, and Commercial Cards. In this role, you will define and execute the reporting and analytics strategy, design and deliver scalable data solutions, and select appropriate data platforms, reporting tools, and analytics technologies. You will support a wide range of business, regulatory, and operational use cases while ensuring data integrity, consistency, and governance across the organization.

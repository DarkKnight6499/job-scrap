The Global Fund Banking Business Analyst III is responsible for supporting the Global Fund Banking Portfolio Management team through a combination of portfolio operations, reporting, process automation, and business analysis activities. This role serves as a key resource for enhancing operational efficiency, developing business intelligence solutions, and providing direct portfolio management support during peak workload periods. Responsibilities & Qualifications Duties & Responsibilities: Core responsibilities include designing, developing, and maintaining reporting solutions, dashboards, and automated workflows utilizing Microsoft Power Platform tools, including Power BI and Power Automate. The analyst will partner with Portfolio Management, Credit, Operations, and Technology teams to identify opportunities for process improvements, streamline manual activities, improve data quality, and deliver actionable insights through reporting and analytics. This role will also perform Portfolio Management Analyst responsibilities as business needs dictate, including supporting month-end and quarter-end activities, executing risk management controls, ensuring borrower compliance requirements are met, tracking and reviewing borrower financial reporting, processing loan maintenance activities, coordinating approvals for loan advances, and interacting directly with clients regarding portfolio servicing needs. Additional responsibilities include analyzing business processes, documenting requirements, managing reporting inventories, supporting system implementations and enhancements, conducting data validation and quality assurance, maintaining procedural documentation, assisting with ad hoc projects, participating in queue management activities, and serving as a subject matter expert for portfolio management reporting and automation solutions. This position requires regular interaction with multiple Global Fund Banking teams, business partners throughout the bank, clients, and senior leadership. Qualifications Bachelor's degree with 2 years of professional experience in business analysis, operations, reporting, or process automation within banking or financial services; or High School diploma/GED with 6 years of related experience.

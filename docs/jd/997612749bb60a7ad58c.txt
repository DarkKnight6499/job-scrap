A Day in the Life: Monster Energy Company is seeking a proactive and detail-oriented Treasury Accountant to join its accounting/reporting team. This is a unique opportunity to shape and centralize treasury-related accounting activities across our APAC/OCEANIA/LATAM operations. The successful candidate will play a key role in managing intercompany agreements and settlements, supporting regional treasury accounting, and collaborating with finance teams. The Impact You'll Make: Intercompany & Global Operations Manage and reconcile intercompany agreements and settlements across Monster Energy’s global entities. Ensure timely and accurate processing of intercompany transactions in line with group policies. Liaise with international finance teams to resolve discrepancies and improve processes. Support the accounting and settlement of global management services agreements, ensuring compliance with transfer pricing policies and local regulations. Account for intercompany funding structures, including equity injections, loans, and capital repatriations. Ensure proper withholding calculations and payments of intercompany/foreign payments. Track and record withholding certificates across APAC/OCEANIA/LATAM. Treasury Accounting Support Assist the treasury team with all treasury-related accounting across OCEANIA, LATAM, and APAC regions. Prepare and monitor treasury-related journal entries including FX, interest, and cash movements. Support month-end and year-end close processes, including reconciliations and reporting packs. Contribute to treasury-related statutory and management reporting. Maintain accurate records of investment activities, including short-term deposits, money market instruments, and other financial assets. Track and report on investment performance and compliance with internal investment policies. Support the accounting of letters of credit, bank guarantees, and other local credit facilities, particularly in less liquid or emerging markets. Ensure proper documentation and accounting treatment of trade finance instruments. Function Setup & Transition Collaborate with AP and financial accounting teams. Identify and implement improvements to streamline workflows and enhance accuracy. Assist in the development and documentation of treasury accounting policies and procedures. Who You Are: Qualified or part-qualified accountant (CPA, CMA, CIA, or Enrolled Agent). Experience in treasury accounting, intercompany transactions, and financial control. Strong understanding of FX, cash management, financial instruments, and investment accounting. Familiarity with letters of credit, guarantees, and local banking practices in emerging markets. Excellent Excel skills; experience with ERP systems (e.g., SAP) is a plus. Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.

Position Summary The Senior Manager, Pain Portfolio is responsible for executing global medical initiatives that support the Companion Animal (CA) Pain portfolio and contribute to category growth, differentiation and customer value creation. Working in close partnership with the Director- Pain-Global Portfolio Support, Global Marketing partners, and in-market medical teams, this role ensures the effective implementation of the global medical strategy through excellence in scientific engagement, medical communications, education, KOL collaboration and field execution. The role serves as a critical link between global portfolio strategy and local market implementation, ensuring that medical activities are scientifically robust, customer-focused and aligned with business objectives. Through close collaboration with regional and country medical teams, the incumbent will help deliver a consistent and compelling scientific value proposition that strengthens Zoetis' position as the preferred partner in pain management. The Senior Manager, Pain Portfolio will champion medical excellence across global markets, enabling veterinarians to make confident clinical decisions through trusted scientific expertise, practical guidance and evidence-based solutions. Lead the execution of short-, medium- and long-term medical plans for the CA Pain portfolio, ensuring alignment with broader brand objectives. Support implementation of global medical priorities across markets by collaborating closely with regional and country medical teams. Monitor execution and impact of medical initiatives, identifying opportunities for continuous improvement and optimization. Cross-Functional Partnership Serve as a core member of the Pain Brand Team, partnering closely with Global Marketing colleagues to ensure alignment between medical and commercial activities. Collaborate with Center of Excellence functions and above-market teams to convert veterinary insights into customer-focused tools, programs and marketing assets. Contribute medical expertise to integrated brand planning and execution. Support development of omnichannel and digital customer engagement initiatives from a medical perspective. Medical Communication & Trainin g Contribute to the development of scientifically accurate and customer-relevant medical content and technical communication materials. Lead the development and delivery of medical training programs to support medical excellence across global markets. Ensure all materials and activities comply with applicable scientific, regulatory and ethical standards. Promote best practices and consistent scientific messaging across regions.

Summary Supports the Director of Risk Strategy in developing and executing enterprise risk strategy, including support for AI risk governance, scenario analysis, emerging-risk assessment, risk taxonomy and control-framework enhancements, risk appetite analysis, and other strategic risk initiatives. The role supports coordination across risk disciplines and business functions, contributes to decision-ready analysis, and supports effective challenge and reporting to management and governance committees. Duties & Responsibilities Support the maintenance and ongoing enhancement of the Bank’s AI governance framework, including policies, standards, and controls for responsible and expanding AI use, consistent with evolving market and risk practices. Collaborate with business leaders, risk partners, and relevant governance bodies associated with AI on the development of tools and processes that support responsible governance. Work collaboratively with key stakeholders to support the maintenance and evolution of a comprehensive AI Risk Governance framework, adapting and evolving as new technology and use cases are introduced. Partner with other risk areas to support the assessment and monitoring of risks related to AI use cases. Assist in identifying program gaps and tracking resolution by the relevant owners. Support the development of an emerging risk framework, including an enterprise-wide structured scenario analysis. Assist in the development of risk strategies for the enterprise that align with corporate risk appetite and strategic objectives. Assist in the development and maintenance of a testing approach for non-model AI assets, leveraging tooling. Support the ongoing monitoring of testing results. It is the responsibility of this role to take ownership of all tasks and challenges that they encounter in the operation of their assigned position.

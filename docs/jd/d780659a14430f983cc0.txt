About This Role As Associate Director, Integrated Digital Supply Chain Planning - Solution Architect, you will be accountable for the end‑to‑end solution architecture, design, and delivery of enterprise Connected Planning capabilities, with a strong focus on Kinaxis Maestro. This role serves as the architect and technical authority for Connected Planning within the Digital Factory Center of Excellence, leading the definition, deployment, and sustainment of scalable, high‑performance planning solutions across global business units. Your work will directly enable smarter decision‑making, improved supply chain performance, and enterprise‑wide digital transformation. Job Description As an Associate Director, Integrated Digital Supply Chain Planning - Solution Architect, you will be responsible for: Leading end‑to‑end solution architecture and design for Kinaxis Maestro, including data models, integration patterns, and configuration frameworks. Translating complex business requirements into scalable, high‑performance Connected Planning solutions. Defining solution blueprints, module configurations, and best‑practice modeling approaches across planning domains. Ensuring alignment with enterprise architecture standards, data governance, and IT security requirements. Overseeing and guiding global implementations across Demand Planning, Supply Planning, S&OP / IBP, Inventory Optimization, and Capacity Planning. Providing hands‑on leadership in Maestro configuration, scenario modeling, and workbook development. Leading design workshops, solution walkthroughs, and deep‑dive sessions with business and IT stakeholders. Acting as the technical escalation point and mentor for architecture decisions, design standards, and best practices. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering, Supply Chain, Information Systems 10+ years of experience in Supply Chain Planning or Digital Supply Chain transformation roles 8+ years of experience in a global industrial company support multiple BU’s 8+ years of experience in leading or supporting digital transformation for industrial manufacturing and supply chain operations 5+ years of hands‑on experience with Kinaxis Maestro or similar platforms (o9, SAP IBP, Blue Yonder, OMP) across multiple planning domains Preferred Qualifications MBA Experience managing external vendors and strategic partners. Experience managing project financials, resource planning, and benefits realization for enterprise digital products. Proven experience leading global implementations and cross‑functional teams. Strong understanding of integrated planning domains including Demand, Supply, Production, S&OE, and Inventory.

About the role We’re seeking a Manager to join the Corporate Finance team at Chime! In this role, you’ll play a central part in one of the company’s most important priorities: preparing Chime’s finance organization to integrate a recent acquisition. You’ll serve as Corporate Finance’s lead on integration readiness, partnering cross-functionally with FP&A, Accounting, Data, and Systems teams to align processes, reporting, planning models, and forecasts ahead of close, then own pro forma views and combined company KPI tracking post-close. You’ll also be a hands-on partner across a lean Corporate Finance team, supporting public company workstreams and using AI tools to build automation and dashboards that make our work faster and more scalable. We’re looking for a highly organized, analytical, and collaborative finance leader who can bring structure to ambiguity, drive cross-functional workstreams to completion, and translate complex financial detail into clear insights for senior leadership. In this role, you can expect to Lead Corporate Finance’s workstream for the integration of a recent acquisition, building and tracking the Day 1 readiness plan (close calendar, chart of accounts, reporting cutover), milestones, and dependencies through close and into post-close Partner cross-functionally with FP&A, Accounting, Data, and Systems teams to align on account mapping, reporting hierarchies, data flows, and planning models (Pigment) for the combined company Build pro forma and combined-company 3 statement financial views and combined KPI tracking, and incorporate the acquired business into the monthly forecast, annual operating plan, and long-range model Identify integration risks, gaps, and open decisions early; drive issues to resolution and provide clear, concise status updates to Finance leadership Central point of contact on finance-wide communications and updates related to integration Identify and track forecasted cost and organizational synergies that have been previously identified and sized Report out on measures of success related to integration progress and develop dashboards to track milestones Serve as a flexible partner across the Corporate Finance team, supporting ad hoc analyses and strategic projects Leverage AI tools to automate manual processes, build dashboards, and increase efficiency across Corporate Finance To thrive in this role, you have Minimum 5-7 years of experience in FP&A, corporate or strategic finance, investment banking, corporate development, or consulting; public company experience and/or M&A integration experience strongly preferred Bachelor's Degree, or equivalent degree in Business, Finance, Accounting or relevant field; MBA or CPA a plus Strong understanding of the three financial statements and public company reporting, including the quarterly earnings cycle Proven ability to lead complex, cross-functional projects with many stakeholders and firm deadlines Expertise in Excel / Google Sheets - you can build scalable, efficient, and intuitive models with ease;

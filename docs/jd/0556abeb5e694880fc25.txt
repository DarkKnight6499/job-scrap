Institutional Portfolio Group is the client sector within Institutional Client Group responsible for all aspects of the Group's relationships with developing or foreign headquartered institutional clients. The Institutional Portfolio Group (IPG) Associate will support coverage bankers in managing and growing relationships across a portfolio of Banks and Public Sector clients, with a particular focus on non-US/international clients and public sector institutions in the Americas/Latin America. The role blends relationship management, business development support, transaction execution, and strong ownership of credit and financial crime risk obligations. The Associate will coordinate the delivery of HSBC’s global product platform, support high-quality client engagement, and ensure the portfolio is managed in line with HSBC’s internal standards and external regulatory expectations.

Is the opportunity to join a culture where “We Do the Right Thing,” and “We Courageously Shape Our Future Together” important to you? If so, Guardian is seeking a motivated individual to join our team as a Financial Analyst. Reporting to the Head of Planning & Expense Consolidation, this role plays a central position within Group FP&A, supporting consolidated expense planning, reporting, and strategic analytics across the Group Benefits organization. The Financial Analyst partners closely with Expense Management, Product, Pricing, and senior leadership to deliver actionable insights, enhance expense transparency, and support business decision-making. This role combines executive reporting, operational rigor, and forward-looking analytics to influence financial outcomes.

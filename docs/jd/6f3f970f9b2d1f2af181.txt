The Difference You Make: This role supports business clients by delivering responsive service and maintenance for standard and complex Treasury Management products. You will coordinate documentation, approvals, operational requests, and issue resolution while maintaining accurate records and a strong client experience. Provide day-to-day service and maintenance support for standard and complex Treasury Management products used by business clients. Support products including Treasury Portal, Remote Deposit Capture, Wire Origination, ACH Origination, Fraud Services, File Services, Virtual Account Manager, Lockbox, Bank Check Express, Payment Link, and related services. Leverage call-center technology to respond to client needs, research requests, and support timely resolution. Facilitate the full maintenance process, including preparing and executing documentation, obtaining client and internal approvals, submitting requests to Operations, and validating configuration. Manage a pipeline of service and support cases in Salesforce, with continuous monitoring, follow-up, documentation, and resolution tracking. Create and maintain accurate client files, upload correspondence and executed documents to Salesforce and approved shared drives, and follow record-retention requirements. Communicate and collaborate with clients, relationship teams, Operations, and internal partners to manage the client experience and resolve issues. Understand and follow Bank Compliance requirements and Treasury-specific policies and procedures.

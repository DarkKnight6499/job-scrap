The SP group is part of Citizens’ Capital Markets & Advisory (CM&A) division. The VP will report to the Head of ABS and the role will be based in Stamford, CT. Familiarity with Digital Infrastructure sector a plus For ABSL transactions, prepare and present concise credit underwriting memos, coordinate legal doc review with counsel, organize cash flow modelling and rating analysis, and communicate with internal approval constituents.

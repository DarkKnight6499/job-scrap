Job Summary: The selected candidate would be responsible for various activities under company’s independent review function including findings management, reviewing and challenging aspects related to the company’s liquidity risk exposure on a day-to-day and long-term basis for various financial products with a deep understanding and focus on Broker-Dealer activities. Major Responsibilities: Lead Independent Review Function findings management Maintain key policy & procedure documents for risk management and key stakeholders. Manage Cash Flow Forecast, ILST, Intraday, RRP, Limits and EWIs recalibration cycles and off-cycle adjustments Oversee development of balance sheet construction and stress testing methodology Monitor developments in regulatory guidelines, assess requirements and implement compliant solutions Prepare and present reports/committee packages and supplemental information to senior management Continuous development and enhancement of existing framework Ad-hoc analysis to support various requests. Requirements: 5-10 years of bank liquidity risk experience Working knowledge of MS Excel, PowerPoint, VBA and Copilot Strong analytical, technical, communications, and interpersonal skills Solid understanding of EPS (Reg YY) and 2052a Regulatory Requirements Education: Bachelor’s degree in business administration, finance or related field.

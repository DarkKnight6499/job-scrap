Role Description: An excellent opportunity has arisen to join the Core Equity team as a Portfolio Manager/Senior Equity Analyst covering Healthcare. The Core Equity team is an 8-person dedicated team in Philadelphia, managing US Small Cap Core, SMID Cap Core, and Large Cap ESG Strategies in mutual funds and separate account vehicles. In this role, you will: Work closely with the team's CIO to build a robust Healthcare portfolio designed to produce excellent risk-adjusted returns across multiple products. Collaborate on investment recommendations and support decision-making and client service efforts. Conduct fundamental analysis of equities including researching industries and interviewing company management teams in an effort to track overall economic and corporate trends. Build financial models to analyze company revenue, cash flows, and earnings and create value comparison and price targets. Skills, experience, qualifications and knowledge required: Bachelor 's Degree in Business, Finance, or related field with a minimum of 10 years' experience in relevant Healthcare research. Extensive experience in analyzing companies within the Healthcare sector. Exceptional skills in Microsoft Excel and proficiency in FactSet. Strong interpersonal, communication, and presentation skills with ability to work in a collaborative, team-oriented environment. High level of personal and professional integrity with a passion for markets and investing. CFA preferred This is an in-person role based in Nomura’s Philadelphia office.

Job Summary The Commercial Lines Underwriting Manager is responsible for leading a team of commercial lines’ underwriters handling medium-touch / high-touch new and renewal business that requires underwriting expertise and individual risk decision making aligned to the Commercial Lines appetite. The role is responsible for productivity, prioritization, balancing of workloads, and sound underwriting decisions of their team of underwriters. The role is also responsible for handling underwriting referrals exceeding the authority limits of team members. The role ensures alignment and collaboration with the Underwriting Operations Team and Territory Managers/Leaders, driving a culture of virtual teaming to ensure achievement of territory plans and targeted financial results. The role is responsible for coaching and mentoring team members along with continuously growing the overall underwriting acumen on the team and its members, while also focusing on driving the business development practices and acumen that will ensure the achievement of territory objectives. Job Responsibilities Leads a team of Commercial Line underwriters handling medium-touch / high-touch new and renewal business that requires underwriting expertise and individual risk decision making aligned to the Commercial Lines appetite. Manages productivity, prioritization, balancing of workloads, and sound underwriting decisions of their team of underwriters. Handles underwriting referrals exceeding the authority limits of their team members. Exercises underwriting authority to achieve profit through quality risk selection and exposure control. Ensures alignment and collaboration with the Underwriting Operations Team and Territory Managers/Leaders, driving business development best practices in the underwriting environment, aligned to field operations. Partners with Territory Leader/Managers ensuring alignment to territory strategy & vision, creating a shared accountability and team environment for underwriting, underwriting support, and territory partners. Ensures efficient, consistent, quality underwriting decisions and ensures adherence to compliance of all underwriting rules and policies. Utilizes knowledge, experience, technical underwriting and subject matter expertise to coach and mentor team members. Performs talent management responsibilities including employee selection, performance management, coaching in technical and career development, employee-related issues, employee morale and work environment. Partners with the QA underwriting function, utilizing findings to help develop underwriting acumen and decision making of the team. Works to increase the overall business and underwriting acumen of the team and its members through one-on-one interactions (e.g., reviewing underwriting decisions, answering questions, listening to phone calls). Ensures individual authority grants for underwriters are established following a well-calibrated, objective, data-driven process. Participates in professional industry groups and stays abreast of industry changes, advancements, and jurisdictional issues to communicate, develop, and incorporate best practices in Commercial Lines. Job Qualifications 6+ years of Commercial Lines Underwriting experience. Bachelor’s degree in related field and/or commensurate work experience.

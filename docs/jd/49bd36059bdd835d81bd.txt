Summary This position executes specific essential functions within the second line Risk Management Division to support SouthState’s Risk Operations team, which is designed to reinforce sound risk management principles across the enterprise. Duties & Responsibilities Manages stakeholder development initiatives in the Archer GRC platform. Develops strong partnerships with second- and third-line business units and stakeholders. Serves as Archer GRC system consultant to address end user questions. Develops Archer GRC reporting and determines dashboard creation needs from system users. Drives the system change management process for existing and future solutions for incorporation into the GRC tool. This includes leading requirements gathering, development, user acceptance testing, and documenting sign-off efforts. Designs test scripts and reviews user acceptance testing results for proposed changes in system lower environments. Supports creation of Archer Governance Committee materials as well as monitoring and presentation of system development progress. Facilitates documentation of Committee meeting minutes with executive stakeholders. Manages assigned operational functions of the Risk Management Division, including execution and planning of Enterprise Risk Management Program Roadmap initiatives and Archer Development Roadmap initiatives as assigned. Develops and maintains departmental procedures and program-related reporting as assigned. Performs administration and project management duties as needed. May develop, facilitate and/or support training for business lines on the Archer end user experience, operational risk topics, current industry practices, and/or risk and control standards. It is the responsibility of this role to take ownership of all tasks and challenges that they encounter in the operation of their assigned position. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions.

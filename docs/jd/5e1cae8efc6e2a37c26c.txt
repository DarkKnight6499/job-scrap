Job Description As a valued colleague on our operational accounting team, you will assist in analyzing business transactions and financial information to determine and maintain records of assets, liabilities, profit and loss, tax liability, and other financial activities. THE IMPACT YOU WILL MAKE The Finance - Accounting - Associate role will offer you the flexibility to make each day your own, while working alongside people who care so that you can deliver on the following responsibilities: Gather, document, and maintain financial information from relevant finance and accounting sources. Prepare and review journal entries and supporting documentation in accordance with accounting policies and procedures. Analyze business and financial information to determine appropriate accounting treatment and record results with support from team and manager. Analyze financial results to ensure accuracy and support business decisions. Support month-end and year-end close processes, including reporting and account certifications. Assist with internal and external audits by providing documentation and responding to inquiries. Develop reports to document assets, liabilities, profit and loss, tax liability, and/or other financial results. E xecute accounting and financial operations processes and related controls. Ensure compliance with internal controls, accounting standards, and regulatory requirements. Identify opportunities to enhance operational efficiency, data quality, and process effectiveness.

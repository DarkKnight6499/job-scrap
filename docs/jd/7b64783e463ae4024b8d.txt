This role supports the full product lifecycle—from idea generation and feasibility analysis to strategic alignment, stakeholder review, and implementation readiness (in partnership with regional product implementation platforms). Track regulatory developments and industry trends to inform product positioning and ensure compliance readiness. Apply structured project management methodologies to coordinate cross-functional execution and ensure timely delivery. Communicate effectively across stakeholder groups, with a strong emphasis on presentation skills and executive-level reporting. Manage multiple workstreams in a dynamic environment, demonstrating agility and sound decision-making under ambiguity. Build and maintain strong relationships across a matrixed organization, including Investment Teams, Legal, Compliance, and Distribution.

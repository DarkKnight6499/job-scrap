What you will do: Clarios is seeking a highly analytical and commercially minded Financial Risk Manager to support the company's global financial risk management and treasury activities. This role will help identify, measure, monitor, and manage exposures related to foreign exchange, commodities, interest rates, liquidity, and other market-driven risks. The Financial Risk Manager will partner closely with Treasury, Corporate Finance, Accounting, Business Units, and external banking partners to develop data-driven risk managemen strategies that support the company's financial objectives while maintaining an appropriate risk profile. The ideal candidate combines strong treasury and capital markets knowledge with quantitative analytical skills, intellectual curiosity, and a continuous-improvement mindset. How you will do it: Financial Risk Management • Analyze and quantify global exposures related to foreign exchange, commodities, interest rates, and balance sheet risk. • Support the design, execution, and monitoring of hedging strategies using derivatives and natural offsets. • Perform scenario analysis, sensitivity analysis, stress testing, and value-at-risk (VaR) assessments to support risk management decisions. • Develop risk dashboards, KPIs, and reporting packages for Treasury leadership and senior management. • Monitor market developments and provide actionable insights regarding currency, commodity, interest rate, and macroeconomic trends. • Evaluate hedge performance and recommend improvements to risk management strategies and policy frameworks. Treasury & Capital Market • Support debt management, refinancing activities, bank relationship management, and capital structure initiatives. • Assist with execution and administration of derivatives, including FX forwards, commodity hedges, interest rate derivatives, and other treasury instruments. • Coordinate with banking partners on market transactions, documentation, pricing analyses, and execution support. • Participate in treasury projects involving funding strategies, liquidity optimization, and financial risk mitigation. Quantitative Analytics & Technology • Develop and maintain analytical models that improve measurement of market risk and hedge effectiveness. • Leverage data analytics, automation, and emerging technologies to improve treasury processes and decision support capabilities. • Build tools and reporting solutions that increase transparency and reduce manual effort across treasury activities. • Support ongoing evolution of treasury technology platforms, market data processes, and risk reporting infrastructure. Accounting, Controls & Governance • Support compliance with treasury policies, internal controls, and applicable accounting requirements. • Collaborate with Accounting to facilitate hedge accounting documentation, testing, and reporting. • Assist with audits, regulatory requests, and internal control reviews. • Maintain high standards of documentation, governance, and operational discipline across treasury activities. Cross-Functional Business Partnership • Work closely with Finance, Procurement, Commercial teams, and Operations to understand evolving business exposures. • Translate complex market risks into practical business recommendations. What we look for : Required: • Bachelor's degree in finance, Economics, Accounting, Mathematics, Engineering, Statistics, or a related field. • 5+ years of experience in Treasury, Financial Risk Management, Capital Markets, Corporate Finance, Banking, or a related discipline. • Strong understanding of: o Foreign exchange markets o Commodity markets o Interest rate risk o Derivative instruments o Corporate treasury concepts Preferred: • Experience supporting multinational corporate treasury operations. • Knowledge of hedge accounting concepts. • Experience with treasury management systems and market data platforms. • Programming or analytics experience (Python, Power BI, SQL, VBA, or similar). • Professional certifications such as: CFA, FRM and CTP #LI-TD1 #LI-Hybrid What you get: Medical, dental and vision care coverage and a 401(k) savings plan with company matching – all starting on date of hire Tuition reimbursement, perks, and discounts Parental and caregiver leave programs All the usual benefits such as paid time off, flexible spending, short-and long-term disability, basic life insurance, business travel insurance, and Employee Assistance Program Global market strength and worldwide market share leadership HQ location earns LEED certification for sustainability plus a full-service cafeteria and workout facility Clarios has been recognized as one of 2026's Most Ethical Companies by Ethisphere.

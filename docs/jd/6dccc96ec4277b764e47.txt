Skills and Competencies Broad knowledge of economics, including familiarity with microeconomics, macroeconomics, and econometrics, with the ability to interpret economic data and analytical findings across regional and global markets Strong quantitative and analytical skills, with demonstrated experience working with large structured datasets and applying statistical or economic analysis methods Clear and effective written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to respond to client inquiries, support the development of presentations, and explain analytical results to both technical and non-technical audiences Ability to manage recurring data validation and forecasting workflows alongside longer-term research projects within a structured monthly cycle, working independently and collaboratively Experience in consumer credit, household balance-sheet analysis, or auto-credit markets is preferred; experience on the credit side of the auto sector is a strong advantage, though sector-specific knowledge is not required as the team provides training for motivated candidates Experience with programming languages such as Python, SAS, C, or a comparable language, with the ability to critically assess whether AI-generated code is reasonable and accurate rather than accepting outputs without verification; preferred but not required Familiarity with cloud computing environments and data engineering tools, including AWS services such as Glue ETL, S3, and Athena, is preferred and considered a differentiating strength Basic understanding of artificial intelligence concepts, with curiosity and enthusiasm for learning how AI tools can be used to improve processes and drive efficiency. Interest in exploring AI systems and a willingness to develop awareness of responsible AI practices, including risk management and ethical use Education Bachelor's degree in economics, finance combined with significant economics training, or mathematics paired with applied economics knowledge is required; a master's degree is preferred A bachelor's degree with three or more years of relevant experience in economic forecasting and consulting will also be considered Responsibilities Support consumer credit and auto-sector forecasting, data validation, economic research, and client communication within a structured monthly analytical workflow. Validate and quality-check incoming consumer credit and auto-sector datasets on a recurring monthly basis, ensuring accuracy, completeness, and consistency Produce consumer credit and auto forecasts while analyzing regional, global city, and country economic data to support timely and accurate deliverables Develop research papers and analytical projects focused on consumer credit trends, household balance sheets, and the auto credit industry; may serve as primary author on topical analysis Act as a direct resource to external and internal clients for states, countries, and metro areas, responding to inquiries and managing customized client deliveries with increasing independence Contribute to client presentations and, with experience, assume increasing responsibility for presenting at virtual, in-person, and internal meetings, including product demonstrations May specify simple forecast equations and support model building with supervision; function as a peer editor for junior colleagues after gaining sufficient experience Collaborate with senior economists and cross-functional team members to maintain high-quality, timely deliverables and support ongoing workflow improvements About the Team Our Consumer Credit Economics team at Moody's Analytics delivers rigorous economic research, forecasting, and data analysis focused on consumer credit markets, household balance sheets, and the auto sector. We partner with banks, financial institutions, and a broad range of clients to support informed decision-making through timely forecasts, high-quality datasets, and actionable analytical insights. By joining our team, you will have the opportunity to develop deep expertise in consumer and auto credit economics while contributing to meaningful research and client-facing work. Our team embraces the responsible use of emerging technologies, including AI-powered tools, to drive efficiency and innovation in how we deliver trusted economic intelligence to our clients.

Job Location WHBC - WINTON HILL BUS CENTER Job Description Research and Development (R&D) at Procter & Gamble, the largest consumer packaged goods company in the world, includes a diverse group of roles that contribute to the innovation and development of our products. It encompasses roles in product research, formulation, testing, and scientific analysis. You will find variety and excitement starting Day 1. Associate Researcher is an entry level role for individuals who are competent in basic science and math and are inspired to apply those skills towards hands-on experiments. At P&G, a wide variety of R&D work is available to Associate Researchers, in the following major areas: -Products Research: Deeply understand our consumers and their needs to inform product design parameters for new or improved products. -Product Formulation & Design: Translates consumer needs into finished products that delight consumers using the best available technologies/chemicals/materials. -Analytical: Use lab equipment to solve tough technical problems via chemical, physical, mechanical, imaging, and other measurement capabilities. -Packaging & Device Development: Creates and improves packages and devices that delight consumers and retailers. -Process Development & Pilot Plant Operations: Translates ideas, concepts, and technologies into commercially feasible chemical and mechanical processes and scales them up from lab to pilot plant to manufacturing. The Opportunity: Join the P&G Feminine Care team and help create Always Feminine Care breakthrough product innovations shape the future of our category. This role in product design and product research discipline involves researching new product ideas, developing MVP prototypes, and conducting technical and consumer tests to ensure products meet design intent with superior performance and quality standards. You will collaborate with multiple functions across technical community including Analytical, process & formulation, Material Science and Design, and have opportunity to work with R&D partners globally across North America, Greater China and Europe. We have a very vibrant and diverse team and are looking for an individual who is inspired by hands-on experimentation and willing to learn and grow new skills. Key Responsibilities: This role will include day-to-day applied research including: Technical Product Assessment : Conduct hands-on lab and sensory experiments to evaluate product performance on benefit, potential risk and build C2T2P model, with guidance from your manager or coach. Consumer Research : Plan and execute qualitative and quantitative studies, including in-use wear observations, returned product analysis, show tests, and product diary studies. Product Prototyping : Build creative product prototypes for consumer show tests and in-use studies, partner with PD and Design experts. Technical Documentation & Insights : Document, analyze, and clearly summarize technical and consumer data to support decisions. Method Development : Develop test protocols and methods which are relevant to consumer perception on product benefits. Collaborate and network effectively within immediate team or work domain as required to maximize job impact and personal learning Job Qualifications Required Qualifications: Education Requirement: Associates degree in Science, Engineering or related field or a High School diploma with relevant experience and interests. Available to work 8am – 4pm at P&G Winton Hill Business Office 5 days per week.

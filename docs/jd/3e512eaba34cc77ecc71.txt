Support key strategic initiatives, including reserving methodology changes, scenario generator updates, captive reinsurance structures, and other high-priority actuarial projects. Partner with actuarial teams across NF to align modeling approaches and assumptions impacting the forecast. Identify and implement process improvements that enhance the efficiency, accuracy, and transparency of capital reporting and forecasting. Present technical actuarial findings to senior leaders and translate complex analysis into clear recommendations. Collaborate closely with cross-functional partners, both within and outside of actuarial, to support enterprise priorities.

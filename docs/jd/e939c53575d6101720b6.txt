What you’ll do in the role: Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC is seeking an Associate, Banking in New York, New York to execute corporate finance transactions including mergers and acquisitions, equity and debt capital raising, and other strategic advisory engagements for financial institutions. Perform complex financial modeling, valuation analysis, and scenario analysis, and conduct detailed industry and company research to support transaction execution and strategic decision-making. Analyze financial statements, capital structures, and operating performance of clients within the financial services sector including banks, insurance companies, asset managers, and fintech firms. Prepare and deliver client presentations, pitch materials, confidential information memoranda, and other transaction-related documentation. Participate in client meetings, communicate analytical findings, and support development and maintenance of client relationships with senior executives. What you’ll bring to the role: -Requires a Master’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Business Administration, or a related field of study -Requires three (3) years of experience in the position offered or three (3) years as a Financial Analyst, Financial Consultant, or a closely related occupation -Requires three (3) years of experience with the following skills: Corporate finance activities and strategic financial planning; Financial modeling and scenario analysis; Financial statement analysis, financial reporting, forecasting and budgeting, and business performance analysis; Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP) accounting; Project management; Financial operations of financial institutions including Insurance and Insurtech companies, and industry and company research; and Financial transformation, risk assessment, and business case development. -Requires any amount of experience with the following skills: Transaction execution, Valuation analysis, and Cash flow analysis; Initial Public Offering (IPO) readiness, Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) reporting support, and audit preparation; and Financial advisory and client relationship management.

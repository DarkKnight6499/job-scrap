Design Your Future at PVH Sourcing Associate, Nike Mens & THUM POSITION SUMMARY The Sourcing Associate will be responsible for supporting category management in conjunction with the supply chain & business strategies. This includes sourcing, product development, pre-production and production responsibilities, meeting organizational goals & metrics. PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES: · Calendar management (adherence & assist in problem solving) · Support manager with projects for management business review / meetings and other projects assigned · Collaborate with category manager and PVH offshore offices to ensure deliverables are being met and systems are updated · Create cost charts & analyze costing for all new developments, collaborating with vendors on cost options to meet AUC targets · Develop and maintain tracking reports for research & developments · Responsible for assisting in monitoring seasonal status of samples and offering mitigation plans where needed at all stages in development · Participate in proto / sms reviews & line finalization meetings · Daily follow up/email communications with cross functional team on/offshore · Collaborate with Sourcing Manager and Design on seasonal R&D, along with new sourcing opportunities · Manage Supply Team Projects as needed – i.e. Seasonal Audit, Sample tracking · Handle other responsibilities and projects as assigned · Maintain updated information via systems (Entefy/PLM) – including product allocation, costing, proto tracking, testing & risk assessment · Work with hub teams and vendors to resolve development and production issues · Monitor calendar deliverables with vendors and cross-functional partners, escalating any issues or late deliverables to Manager · Receive and distribute submits as needed (samples, trim cards, etc) · Send parcels as needed QUALIFICATIONS: A minimum of 3 years’ experience in Product Development, Sourcing & Manufacturing Bachelor’s Degree is required · Must be analytical; understand the supply goals & metrics. · Good judgment & decision-making skills (with an ability to get to root cause analysis). · Excellent communication & presentation skills (to include good listening & negotiation skills) · Must be detail oriented, resourceful, have excellent follow-up skills; learning and using all appropriate contacts to gather information & drive solutions. · Must be flexible and able to function in a fast-paced environment, with competing priorities and deadlines. · Ability to collaborate and clearly communicate, transcending different cultures, geographies & time zones. · Computer skills - Microsoft office, specifically Excel is a must (V-look up/X look up and Pivot Table experience necessary) · PDM/PLM/Centric/Entefy are a plus DECISION MAKING: Minimal decision making will be required initially but as there is increased familiarity with the role, decision making will be required with regards to style allocation and development opportunities. RESOURCEFULNESS/CREATIVITY: Must be detail-oriented & able to problem solve and offer solutions to improve efficiencies. Must be flexible to adapt to changing trends of business and/or ad hoc projects. ENVIRONMENT: Must be able to function in a fast-paced, continually evolving environment.

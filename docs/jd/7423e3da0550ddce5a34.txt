The Opportunity Abbott Rapid Diagnostics is seeking a highly motivated Research Associate II –Chemistry/Biochemistry to join our Research & Development team in San Diego, California. In this hands-on laboratory role, you will support the synthesis, purification, characterization, and evaluation of oligonucleotides used in diagnostic applications. The successful candidate will work closely with multidisciplinary teams to deliver high-quality oligonucleotide reagents that enable the development of innovative diagnostic technologies What You'll Do Perform synthesis of DNA and RNA oligonucleotides using automated oligonucleotide synthesizers. Conduct oligonucleotide cleavage, deprotection, purification, and characterization using established laboratory methods. Purify oligonucleotides using reverse-phase HPLC, ion-exchange HPLC, or cartridge-based purification systems. Perform analytical testing using HPLC, UV-Vis spectroscopy, LC-MS, and related analytical techniques. Maintain accurate laboratory documentation, including experimental procedures, synthesis records, yields, and quality-control results. Support assay development and product development teams by providing high-quality oligonucleotide and technical feedback. Execute biochemical experiments and sample preparation procedures under supervision. Maintain laboratory equipment and perform routine preventative maintenance and calibration Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Chemistry, Biochemistry, Analytical Chemistry, Molecular Biology, or a related scientific discipline. 2-5 years of laboratory experience in chemistry, biochemistry, molecular biology, or related fields. Hands-on experience with oligonucleotide synthesis, cleavage, and deprotection techniques. Familiarity with analytical characterization methods such as HPLC, UV-Vis spectroscopy, and mass spectrometry. Strong organizational skills with the ability to maintain accurate laboratory records and documentation. Demonstrated attention to detail and commitment to producing high-quality results. Excellent communication, teamwork, analytical, and problem-solving skills Preferred Qualifications Experience operating and maintaining automated oligonucleotide synthesizers (e.g., Dr. Oligo, MerMade, or equivalent systems). Hands-on experience with post-synthetic oligonucleotide modification and conjugation chemistry involving fluorophores, quenchers, or other ligands. Strong understanding of solid-phase oligonucleotide synthesis processes and common oligonucleotide modifications, including fluorescent labels. Experience with oligonucleotide purification using reverse-phase and ion-exchange chromatography. Proficiency with analytical platforms including Agilent, and/or ÄKTA chromatography systems. Experience with LC-MS analysis for oligonucleotide characterization. Knowledge of solid-phase organic synthesis principles and their application to oligonucleotide chemistry. Experience supporting diagnostic assay development or molecular diagnostics projects The base pay for this position is $73,900.00 – $116,000.00 In specific locations, the pay range may vary from the range posted.

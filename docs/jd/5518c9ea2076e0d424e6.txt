About Our Team Micron Technology's Boise Front-End operations serve as an essential element within the company's U.S. manufacturing foundation and growth. The finance group in Boise Operations collaborates with site operations and global finance teams to build scalable finance processes, boost cost insight, and support business decisions as the site progresses through construction, startup, manufacturing ramp, and ongoing operations. Position Overview The Senior Financial Analyst I reports to the Director, Boise Front-End Controller and is part of the Boise Operations Finance group. This position supports Boise front-end manufacturing operations by managing budgeting and oversight tasks related to construction, startup, manufacturing ramp, and ongoing operations. In this position, you will prepare budgets, forecasts, product-cost inputs, financial analyses, and management reports. You will explain financial and operating results against budgets and offer information and recommendations to guide business decisions. You will collaborate closely with the Boise office and international teams, including Financial Planning and Analysis, Global Cost Consolidations, and Global Central Finance. In this role, you will gain manufacturing-finance proficiency through practical involvement in planning, cost analysis, financial reporting, and partnering with business units. You will join expert site and global finance professionals and are anticipated to expand your independence and range of duties over time. Responsibilities Financial Forecasting and Evaluation Prepare and maintain financial models, forecasts, and budget inputs for designated manufacturing activities. Prepare budget-to-actual analyses, explain significant cost and operational drivers, and identify items requiring follow-up. Track assigned operational and financial performance measures and prepare recurring reports for operations and finance collaborators. Conduct scenario analysis based on assumptions supplied by site and Global Front-End Finance teams. Convert financial and operational data into clear observations and recommendations that support business decisions. Manufacturing Finance and Accounting Support Partner with operations to develop quarterly product-cost and operating-expense inputs, including raw materials, spare parts, and repairs and maintenance. Supply financial and operational data to global Central Finance groups for use in financial models, forecasts, and reporting. Coordinate and maintain cost-center updates in finance systems to support accurate reporting and approval workflows. Prepare and post recurring monthly journal entries and maintain appropriate supporting documentation. Support month-end and quarter-end close activities for assigned areas. Business Partnership and Process Improvement Collaborate with Boise operations and global finance teams to analyze financial results, recognize risks and opportunities, and assist in business decisions. Identify cost-reduction and productivity opportunities and support cross-functional improvement initiatives. Support the implementation and consistent use of standardized finance processes, systems, and procedures. Find opportunities to simplify recurring work, improve data accuracy, and automate reporting or analysis. Boost efficiency, data quality, and financial decision support through the adoption of AI-enabled solutions. Minimum Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Business, or a related area. More than 5 years of pertinent experience in financial analysis, FP&A, accounting, cost finance, or a similar area. Experience working with financial systems, spreadsheets, and data presentation solutions to analyze data and prepare financial information. Experience developing financial models and applying fiscal and business data to guide decisions. Ability to communicate financial information clearly to finance and non-finance audiences. Knowledge of U.S. GAAP and financial-control principles. Ability to work independently on assigned responsibilities, manage multiple priorities, and seek guidance on unfamiliar or complex matters. Preferred Qualifications Experience in semiconductor, electronics manufacturing, advanced manufacturing, or another high-technology industry. Experience with manufacturing cost accounting, product costing, budgeting, forecasting, or variance analysis. Proficiency in Excel, including formulas, data analysis, and financial modeling. Experience with SAP, SAP Analytics Cloud, Tableau, Power BI, or similar ERP, planning, and reporting tools. Curious and motivated to learn, with an interest in improving finance processes and tools. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with attention to accuracy and data quality. Ability to build effective working relationships across finance and operations. Experience using automation, analytics, or AI-enabled tools to improve recurring finance work.

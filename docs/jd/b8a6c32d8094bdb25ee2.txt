Financial Analyst - CPST
ALM First Financial Advisors
Location: Dallas, Texas
Link: https://almfirst.bamboohr.com/careers/99

About Us: ALM First Financial Advisors is a leading, trusted strategic partner for depository institutions, offering an array of financial advisory services. Since 1995, our expertise in asset liability management, fixed income portfolio management and hedging, has allowed us to deliver deeper insights into financial institutions’ balance sheets, strengthening their financial performance and building efficiencies. With more than $82 billion of investments under management. ALM First is an SEC-registered investment advisor, acting as an unbiased third party, offering commission-free, fee-based services to over 450 financial institutions nationwide. Job Summary: This position will work within the ALM Strategy Group at ALM First and will report to the Director of Capital Planning and Stress Testing (CPST) and Liquidity Stress Testing (LST). The candidate will assist in producing and maintaining Capital Planning and Stress Testing (CPST) and Liquidity Stress Testing (LST) for institutional depository clients. Responsibilities: Load and maintain client balance sheet, deposits, funding, and liquid assets while ensuring data integrity at both the level of the instrument and the balance sheet. Communicate effectively and proactively with financial analysts and financial advisors to ensure stress scenarios are appropriately specified, documented, and modeled to meet the needs of each client. Identify and document data, system, and modeling issues, communicating findings clearly and consolidating technical requirements into actionable development items Execute and process analytics — including liquidity stress testing, contingency funding plan support, and monitoring of liquidity coverage and reliance metrics Maintain high standard of efficiency regarding workflow performance as reporting volume varies over the quarter Qualifications: 0-4 years of relevant work experience, preferably in asset management, banking, or related field Understanding of bank and credit union balance sheet, funding, and liquidity concepts and macroeconomic scenario drivers. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with experience translating business requirements into technical language Knowledge of ALM software such as ZM by Moodys Analytics, QRM, or Empyrean is a plus Working knowledge of Bloomberg and other relevant analytics platforms Minimum Education Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree and strong academic record with a concentration in finance, economics, mathematics, or other related quantitative/analytical discipline required. Advanced degree and/or professional designation such as CFA/FRM is a plus. Language Skills: Ability to read, analyze, and interpret general business periodicals, professional journals, technical procedures, or governmental regulations. Ability to write reports, business correspondence, and procedure manuals. Ability to effectively present information and respond to questions from groups of managers, clients, and team members.

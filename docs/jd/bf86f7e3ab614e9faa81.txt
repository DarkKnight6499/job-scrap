Senior Manager, North America FP&A Consolidation & Analysis The Senior Manager partners closely with cross-functional stakeholders to evaluate business performance, identify risks and opportunities, support key initiatives, and drive profitable growth. This position plays a critical role in developing and enhancing financial planning processes, leading complex analyses, and ensuring accurate and timely reporting. The role also provides leadership within the Finance organization by mentoring team members, promoting continuous improvement, and supporting enterprise-wide Finance and CFO initiatives. Role Responsibilities Lead North America financial planning, forecasting, consolidation, and analysis processes. Develop and maintain annual operating plans, periodic forecasts, long-range plans, and scenario analyses. Provide detailed financial analysis and reporting to support business operations and strategic initiatives. Serve as a day-to-day finance business partner to operational and functional leaders, helping establish priorities and providing financial guidance. Analyze business performance, profitability, operating expenses, investments, and key financial metrics. Identify business risks and opportunities and develop recommendations to support informed decision-making. Design, develop, and refine financial models and analytical tools to support a dynamic business environment. Lead preparation and delivery of management reporting packages and business reviews. Drive process improvements to increase efficiency, enhance forecasting accuracy, and improve reporting capabilities. Collaborate with Finance, Accounting, Operations, Sales, and other business functions on strategic initiatives and enterprise projects. Lead or contribute to CFO organization projects and cross-functional business initiatives. Provide leadership, coaching, and development support to team members, including assigning work, reviewing deliverables, and fostering professional growth. Ensure compliance with company policies, financial controls, and reporting requirements Basic Requirements Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, Economics, Mathematics, or a related field. 7+ years of progressive experience in Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A), Corporate Finance, Accounting, or a related financial discipline; or an equivalent combination of education, training, and experience. Experience leading financial planning, forecasting, budgeting, reporting, and analytical processes. Experience developing financial models and business performance analyses. Experience partnering with business leaders to support operational and strategic decision-making. Preferred Master's degree in Business Administration (MBA), Finance, Accounting, or a related field. Experience supporting large, complex, multi-functional organizations. Experience with enterprise planning and reporting systems such as Essbase, Hyperion, SAP, Oracle, Power BI, Tableau, or similar platforms. Experience supporting commercial, retail, sales, operations, or customer-facing business functions. Knowledge of advanced analytics, automation, and AI tools.

Responsibilities: Support maintenance of Integrated Master Schedules (IMS) Support installation planning products including draft master plans, layout studies, and facility requirement validations to support informed planning / investment decisions Support draft NEPA (National Environmental Policy Act) documentation / conduct environmental planning analyses that identify compliance considerations and support government environmental determinations Support multidisciplinary engineering reviews to assess design submissions for compliance with UFC (Unified Facilities Criteria) standards, code requirements, and technical criteria Support draft Statements of Work (SOWs), Performance Work Statements (PWSs), and technical RFP components that clearly articulate requirements for government review Support market research and compile historical cost data to support the development of Independent Government Cost Estimate (IGCE) inputs Analyze contractor O&M performance data / prepare advisory compliance evaluations that highlight risks, deficiencies, and opportunities for improvement Participate in technical / planning discussions, offering informed interpretation of engineering, planning, environmental, and programmatic issues Qualifications: A minimum three years of experience with Integrated Master Schedules, data analytics, or facilities planning support; U.S. Federal government consulting experience preferred Bachelor's degree from an accredited college/university Experience maintaining Integrated Master Schedules, program reports, or similar PM artifacts Experience supporting facilities planning, master planning, environmental planning, NEPA documentation, or multidisciplinary design review in a federal or regulated environment Knowledge of UFC standards, building codes, NEPA processes, and cost estimation principles Experience synthesizing large volumes of technical / programmatic data into clear and actionable reports Proficiency with MS Project, Primavera, or other scheduling tools;

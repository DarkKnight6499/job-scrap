Quantitative Trader

Job Description: Job Title: Quantitative Trader Location: New York, NY Overview Deutsche Bank New York Branch seeks a Quantitative Trader in New York, NY to develop innovations in quantitative trading, including opportunities for automation. Requires a Master’s degree in Finance, Financial Engineering, or related field or equivalent, and two (2) years of experience managing and running electronic trading strategies across asset classes, including pricing, execution, liquidity management, inventory management, position management, and real-time risk management in fast-moving market environments; developing, designing, validating, back-testing, and implementing electronic trading strategies using database languages including KDB/SQL, Python for systematic trading, and trading analytics workflows; performing advanced statistical analysis and modelling including performance attribution, market impact analysis, transaction cost analysis (TCA), financial modelling, and quantitative risk management; applying advanced mathematical and quantitative methods, including probability theory, and optimization techniques, to analyze, develop, and validate pricing models across asset classes; collaborating cross-functionally with traders, quantitative researchers, sales, and technology teams to design and enhance electronic trading infrastructure, including automation, execution optimization, liquidity distribution, and data-driven trading decision frameworks across electronic trading platforms. Salary range: $150,000.00 – $170,000.00/year. This position is eligible for Deutsche Bank's Employee Referral Incentive Program.

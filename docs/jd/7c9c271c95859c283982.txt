Work Flexibility: Hybrid What you will do: As our Market Insights Associate, you will support the growth and expansion of the Market Insights function within Stryker Sustainability Solutions. You will contribute to market modeling, competitive intelligence, dashboard development, and primary and secondary market research efforts that help the business better understand market opportunities and competitive dynamics. Working closely with Marketing, Finance, Strategy, Commercial Operations, and R&D, you will help gather, organize, and interpret market, customer, and competitive information to support business planning and decision-making. The role offers broad exposure to the medical device industry and the opportunity to contribute to strategic initiatives. You will need to be curious, analytical, and eager to learn about market dynamics, customer needs, and competitive trends. You will also need to enjoy solving problems, working with data, and communicating findings through clear analyses and visualizations that help stakeholders better understand the market and make informed decisions. This is a hybrid role and as such is defined as going into the office every Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday with the ability to work from home Monday and Friday.

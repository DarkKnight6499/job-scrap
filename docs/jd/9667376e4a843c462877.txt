Responsibilities & Qualifications Duties & Responsibilities: Portfolio Strategy - Develops and implement TMT investment policies. Analyzes data, research, and market trends to meet client investment needs. Ensures compliance with investment and fiduciary guidelines within proprietary investment products. May assist senior leadership with initiatives that support wider strategic objectives. Client Servicing - Services the largest, most complex client accounts through ongoing reporting, monitoring, and handling of client portfolios. Develops extensive reports and presentations that provide clarity on investment options, performance, and outlook. Provides support to new acquisition and sales efforts with high-net-worth clients. Portfolio Management - Oversees selection and trading of securities and effective execution of intended investments. Team Lead - Assigns portfolios to selected associates. Makes appropriate changes to investment policies, procedures, and efficiencies in order to meet objectives. Manages the performance, training, and evaluation of assigned portfolio staff. Maximizes portfolio achievements by providing professional development. Qualifications: Required: Bachelor's Degree and 6 years of experience in Credit Analysis and Credit Underwriting of Portfolio Management OR High School Diploma or GED and 10 years of experience in Credit Analysis and Credit Underwriting of Portfolio Management.

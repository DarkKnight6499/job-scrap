Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. Regeneron is currently looking for a Associate Director, MES Operations, to direct all aspects of the operation and utilization of the Regeneron Manufacturing Execution System (MES). This role is responsible for ensuring that MES operations fully meet the needs of the business by aligning with Clinical and Commercial Manufacturing leadership and ensuring that associated policies and processes are effectively implemented and maintained. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. When & where: Location: Rensselaer, NY Work model: Onsite Role Discover your role: Provides leadership, vision and management to the MES operations team Directs the needs of functional groups and establishes the priorities for MES operations Develops strong business relationships with Clinical and Commercial Manufacturing, Quality Assurance and Material Management groups to attain a solid understanding of their goals and objectives to ensure alignment with MES operations Develops a strong business relationship with Information Technology to ensure that MES operations goals and objectives are met Aligns MES operations team's business practices with Clinical and Commercial Manufacturing objectives to pro-actively anticipate future needs and create solutions to address them Responsible for the strategic vision of MES Operations and manages the delivery of objectives Conducts requirement and design sessions with users as needed for continuous process improvement Reviews company documentation including SOPs, requirements and Manufacturing Batch Records to assess MES impact Ensures that MES Recipe authoring, workflows, Electronic Batch Records, etc. are developed and maintained to enable MES operations Provides MES operations support for User Acceptance Testing (UAT) for upgrades and other changes to MES Provides performance and usage metrics as required Collaborates with appropriate training groups to develop and schedule training sessions that best serve the business needs Provides MES operations team members with guidance and support to ensure that they have the skills and tools required to consistently support business needs and individual professional goals Reviews and approves MES Change Controls Ensures GxP compliance Responds to audit inquiries Provides guidance and coaching to staff to develop technical and management skills for career advancement This role requires: BA/BS in engineering, biological or physical science, computer science or information technology and 10+ years of experience working with MES or manufacturing systems within a life science manufacturing environment, including previous management experience, or

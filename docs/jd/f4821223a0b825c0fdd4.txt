Director, T&O Operational Risk Officer - Capital Markets & Commercial Banking
BMO Financial Group
Location: Chicago, IL, USA
Link: https://bmo.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/External/job/Chicago-IL-USA/Director--T-O-Operational-Risk-Officer---Capital-Markets---Commercial-Banking_R260029787

Application Deadline: 11/12/2026 Address: 320 S Canal Street Job Family Group: Audit, Risk & Compliance Director, T&O Operational Risk Officer - Capital Markets & Commercial Banking, will establish and lead a new business unit-centered operational risk oversight capability. This role is responsible for developing and executing end-to-end operational risk reviews across Capital Markets and Commercial Banking, integrating insights from operational, technology, cyber, data, AI, fraud, physical security, and resilience risk domains to create a holistic view of business unit risk. The Director will lead a team of risk professionals and partner closely with business leaders, technology leaders, Business OROs, and Risk Specialty Area (RSA) teams to identify key risk drivers, systemic issues, emerging risks, and opportunities to strengthen the control environment. The role serves as a trusted advisor to senior leadership, enhancing executive reporting through actionable risk insights, meaningful risk narratives, and a clear understanding of residual risk exposure. Success will be demonstrated through: Establishment of a new business unit-centered operational risk oversight capability. Delivery of integrated operational risk reviews that provide a holistic view of risks, controls, dependencies, and operational outcomes. Development of comprehensive business unit risk profiles. Identification of thematic concerns, systemic issues, and emerging risks. Enhanced executive reporting that clearly articulates residual risk, key risk drivers, business impacts, and remediation priorities. Improved management understanding of cumulative operational risk exposure and opportunities for risk reduction. Key Responsibilities: Business Unit Operational Risk Oversight Establish and lead the business unit operational risk oversight model. Lead end-to-end Integrated Operational Risk Reviews across critical business units and business processes. Assess process design, technology dependencies, control effectiveness, operational outcomes, and residual risk. Develop business unit risk profiles and assess cumulative risk exposure. Integrate insights across operational, technology, cyber, data, AI, fraud, physical security, and resilience risk domains. Identify key risk drivers, emerging risks, thematic concerns, and systemic issues. Deliver actionable risk insights and recommendations to management. Thematic Risk & Incident Analysis Analyze significant incidents, loss events, issues, audit findings, and control assessments. Assess the impact of negative outcomes on the business unit risk profile. Identify recurring themes, root causes, and systemic concerns. Develop proactive recommendations to reduce risk and improve operational resilience. Executive Reporting & Stakeholder Engagement Enhance executive reporting through clear risk narratives and business-focused insights. Translate complex operational and technology risks into meaningful business impacts. Present risk themes, emerging risks, and review outcomes to senior management and governance forums. Partner with Business OROs, RSA teams, technology leaders, and business stakeholders to drive risk-informed decision-making. People Leadership Lead and develop a geographically distributed team, including direct oversight of team members supporting operational risk activities across North America, Europe, and Asia, while ensuring consistent methodologies, execution standards, and stakeholder engagement. Establish consistent review methodologies, reporting standards, and effective challenge practices. Build organizational capability in integrated risk reviews, thematic analysis, and systemic risk identification. Qualifications & Experience Required : Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, Risk Management, Information Technology, Engineering, Computer Science, or a related discipline. 10+ years of experience in Operational Risk, Technology Risk, Enterprise Risk, Internal Audit, Risk Consulting, or related disciplines within a large financial institution. Professional certifications such as CRISC, FRM, PRM, CISA, CIA, or equivalent risk, audit, cybersecurity, technology, or governance certifications. Strong understanding of Capital Markets, Commercial Banking, and supporting banking operations. Significant experience conducting end-to-end risk assessments, operational risk reviews, thematic reviews, or enterprise-wide risk assessments. Experience assessing business processes, technology environments, control frameworks, and operational risks. Broad understanding of technology, cyber, data, AI, fraud, operational resilience, and related risk domains. Demonstrated ability to identify thematic concerns, systemic risks, interdependencies, and cumulative risk exposure. Proven people leadership and stakeholder management experience. Strong policy, standards, and methodology development experience. Exceptional communication, presentation, and executive storytelling skills.

A day in the life As the Insurance Associate on the East zone team, you’ll collaborate with the Client Advisory teams to provide critical support in the preparation for prospect and client meetings. You’ll assist in the client review process by ordering designated summaries ahead of the review target date and supporting the team in the preparation of stewardship reviews. You’ll work on updating summaries, creating spreadsheets and other documents as requested, binding materials, obtaining quotes, and remarketing when applicable. Additionally, the role involves assisting in the new business process by establishing Applied (a system we use) files, creating binders, identification cards, cancellation forms, and coordinating the delivery of new business policies. While working alongside a Client Advisor Manager or Client Advisor, you will quote coverage via carrier websites, locate carrier-specific information, and obtain policy declarations. You will also support Client Advisor Managers and Client Advisors by scheduling client, carrier, and guest visits, including managing security clearances, reserving meeting spaces, and providing proper greetings. Lastly, you’ll process endorsements, handle mortgage/lienholder inquiries, respond to client identification requests, and address billing inquiries. Our future colleague.

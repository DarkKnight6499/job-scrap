Join Barclays as Fundamental Credit Research Analyst, where you will work closely with the senior analyst covering the investment grade Technology, Media, and Cable/Telecom sectors ("TMT"). The multi-sector nature, coupled with the speed at which AI is impacting the industries and credit markets, makes this a very dynamic role. You will support all aspects of research coverage including following and tracking industry developments and data, modelling companies, and publishing actionable, trade-oriented research. To be successful as a Fundamental Credit Research Analyst, you should have: Experience supporting research responsibilities including financial modeling, qualitative analysis, and writing Experience leveraging AI tools appropriately to drive efficiency and insights Knowledge of current events and how they affect financial markets in your assigned sector Some other highly valued skills may include: Proficiency in Excel Prior experience building and maintaining financial models Prior experience with trade-oriented research reports You may be assessed on the key critical skills relevant for success in role, such as risk and controls, change and transformation, business acumen, strategic thinking, digital and technology, as well as job-specific technical skills.

Position Overview We are seeking a highly capable and driven Strategy Associate to join the Chief Development Officer’s team (“CDO”), a group focused on advancing the firm’s new businesses, strategic initiatives, joint ventures, and investment opportunities. The Strategy Associate will play a role in shaping, structuring, and executing the firm’s growth agenda — from early-stage opportunity assessment through deal execution and transition into the firm’s functional and operating teams. The work spans financial modeling, investment analysis, strategic planning, partnership structuring, and cross-functional coordination. This is an ideal role for someone with a strong analytical and transactional foundation who is looking for a broader, more entrepreneurial mandate — one that combines rigorous analytical work with strategic judgment and the opportunity to help build new businesses from the ground up. Responsibilities Construct detailed financial models for existing and new strategies and businesses as well as investment opportunities — including scenario analysis, sensitivity testing, and valuation frameworks. Prepare presentations detailing objectives, rationale, and recommendations for contemplated initiatives, partnership structures, and investment opportunities for senior leadership. Conduct research and analysis to rapidly develop fluency in new industries, asset classes, counterparties, and opportunity spaces as the mandate evolves. Assist in the coordination and implementation of strategic initiatives and new business efforts across various groups within the firm, serving as a connective thread between parties. Support the structuring and negotiation of joint ventures, external partnerships, and new business arrangements — including reviewing and distilling legal and structuring documentation such as JV agreements, partnership terms, and fund documents. Prepare materials for various firm committee presentations. Develop transition documentation and handoff packages as new businesses move from the CDO function into the operating structure. Track and communicate workstreams, milestones, and deliverables across active initiatives, ensuring nothing falls through the cracks during execution. Serve as a cross-functional resource, engaging with teams across the firm to understand the operational, regulatory, and structural requirements of new business builds. Qualifications 4-5 years of experience in investment banking, corporate development, private equity or a related transactional role with a strong foundation in financial modeling, valuation, and transaction execution. Experience in M&A, restructuring, capital markets, or financial sponsors coverage a plus. Exceptional financial modeling skills with the ability to build and maintain complex, dynamic models from scratch. Advanced PowerPoint skills with the ability to produce clear, polished, and executive-ready presentations. Strong written and verbal communication skills — able to synthesize complex information into concise, well-structured memos, summaries, and recommendations. Comfort with legal and structuring documentation — ability to read, interpret, and distill JV agreements, term sheets, partnership terms, and fund documents without requiring legal training. Strong research and synthesis capabilities — able to quickly develop informed views on unfamiliar industries, markets, and counterparties. Proficiency in Excel and financial data platforms. Personal and Behavioral Competencies High level of intelligence, internal drive, intellectual inquisitiveness, eagerness, and analytical ability – with a genuine interest in understanding how the firm’s investment, operational, legal/compliance, and business development functions interconnect. Quantitative, strategic, and tactical mindset with the ability to move fluidly between big-picture thinking and detailed execution. Comfort with ambiguity and shifting priorities — new mandates arrive on short notice and the ability to reprioritize without losing quality is essential. Low ego, high ownership — willingness to do whatever the workstream requires, from building a model to coordinating a call to formatting a deck. Proactive rather than reactive — anticipates what’s needed, identifies gaps, and mobilizes to fill them. Judgment and discretion — the work involves sensitive strategic and commercial matters, and the role requires mature handling of confidential information. Optimistic attitude with willingness to contribute however and wherever possible or required. Seeks out an environment with the potential to rapidly assume additional challenges and responsibilities. Ability to operate independently while collaborating across disciplinary teams and contributing to a cooperative and entrepreneurial environment. Demonstrated ability to deal with high-pressure situations, seek out solutions, and work with others to resolve problems in a timely and effective manner.

We are seeking a highly motivated finance leader to join our team and support FP&A for Snowflakes Partnerships organization. In this role, you will be responsible for partnering across the Snowflake Partnerships leadership, Sales & Partner Operations and Corporate & Sales Finance teams to provide financial leadership for Global Alliances.

This is a strategic finance leadership role that will drive impact by providing day-to-day financial oversight of the Partnerships business, monthly financial close management, financial & business health insights, spend management, budgeting, forecasting & planning, and financial compliance.

As the Partner Programs & Investments Sr. Finance Manager, You Will:

 - You will be the finance FP&A lead supporting the Partnerships organization and partnering across the Partnerships Finance, Corporate Finance and Sales Finance teams

 - Responsible for leading and providing financial support in all Partnerships financial rhythm of business that includes monthly close, accrual management, BVA, forecast and monthly and quarterly financial & business insights

 - Responsible for leading the Partnerships financial planning in collaboration with business leadership and finance peers within Partnerships & Investment Finance and across Corporate and Sales finance. 

 - Effectively scale and manage budgets and forecast HC and discretionary business investments 

 - Identify opportunities to evolve and scale Partnerships financial & business health metrics and lead the alignment, development, automation, of reporting to support managing financial & business performance

 - Coordinate and represent the Partnerships organization on all financial deliverables to corporate functions.

 - Be a compliance champion and ambassador to ensure compliance with company policies and provide though leadership on appropriate governance and controls for partner and alliances

 - Create simplification and deliver efficiencies across financial forecasting, processes and reporting using Snowflake’s reporting & AI capabilities

Required Skills/Expertise:

 - 8+ years of related experience in FP&A with GTM Finance, Sales Finance and/or Channel/Partner Sales/ Ecosystem Finance experience

 - Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration or related field. Advanced degree or professional certification (CFA, CPA) is a plus

 - Experience leading financial rhythm of business (month end close management, budgeting and planning, forecasting) in a technology organization

 - Experience defining, creating business alignment, automating, and reporting out on financial and business key performance indicators (or OKRs) 

 - Proficiency in MS office Suite and G-Suite   

 - Experience with BI and Data Visualization tools is a plus

 - Exceptional financial, analytical, and problem-solving skills with previous experience in managing Salesforce, Adaptive, Looker, or similar tools

 - Ability to break down complex ideas and issues and communicate in simple terms to various levels of the organization

 - Excellent communication and presentation skills (written and verbal)

 - Strong customer service orientation and a desire to help others succeed

 - Prior experience in working and supporting large Channel/Alliances/Partner organizations

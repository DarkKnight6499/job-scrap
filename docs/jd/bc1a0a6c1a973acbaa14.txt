Investment Banking Vice President – Building Products and Distribution within Industrials Group With a global presence and extensive expertise, Santander CIB provides industry-leading support for the full range of Global Banking activities. We provide global support for corporate and institutional clients, delivering tailored services, products and solutions including Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A), Equity Capital Markets (ECM), Acquisition & Leveraged Finance (ALF) and Debt Capital Markets (DCM). The Difference You Make: We are seeking a Sr Associate / Vice President in Investment Banking supporting our Building Products and Distribution industry coverage within our Industrials Group. You will play a key leadership role in the origination and execution of complex transactions while developing relationships with key clients and driving new business opportunities. Support the origination and lead execution of M&A Advisory transactions. Manage day-to-day deal processes, ensuring milestones and timelines are met. Oversee the development and quality control of financial models and client materials. Serve as the primary point of contact for clients on transaction execution matters. Identify and cultivate new business opportunities in collaboration with senior bankers. Provide mentorship and guidance to Analysts and Associates. Travel as requested by senior team members and / or clients. What You Bring: The ideal candidate brings Investment Banking coverage experience in the Industrials sector for a bulge bracket or leading boutique advisory firm.

SUMMARY The Portfolio Risk Analyst is responsible for monitoring portfolio risk across First Interstate Bank's loan portfolio. This position conducting analysis and underwriting activities, including developing and publishing risk-related reports on borrower and portfolio trends within the parameters established by management. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Performs enhanced analysis of portfolio trends, including but not limited to policy exceptions, borrowing base monitoring, asset quality, loan loss history, key financial performance metrics, and general loan-level data inputs. Assists in the ongoing development of credit risk analytics capabilities, insight generation, data management and reporting, and forward-looking risk assessment capabilities. Oversees monthly department performance monitoring and data input tracking. Tests, monitors, and generates industry and portfolio segment reports using data stored in the Loan Origination System. Assists with regular portfolio reviews to strengthen the analytical foundation for assessing and reporting on credit performance outlook, portfolio analysis, risk profiling, and risk appetite monitoring. Oversees and manages the PLSR process to ensure all problem loans comply with policy requirements. Produces analytical materials to support discussions with cross-functional teams. Communicates analytical findings to supervisors, risk team members, line staff, and Finance. Generates monthly, annual, and ad hoc reports on the Bank’s loan portfolio. Develops advanced knowledge of existing and new loan systems (e.g., Fiserv, CreditTrack, Primatics). Assists in the development of credit policies, procedures, and guidelines that support the objectives of the Credit Risk Management department. Completes essential functions associated with departmental SOX controls. Performs other job-related duties and special projects as assigned.

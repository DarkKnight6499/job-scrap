Public accounting experience is preferred but not required. You have foundational knowledge of mutual funds, investment vehicles, post-trade operations, fund accounting, and the importance of accurate and timely NAV production. You have a solid and growing understanding of fund accounting responsibilities, including daily NAV calculation and dissemination, income distribution activities, expense processing and oversight, trade accounting, and third-party provider oversight. You have experience preparing, reviewing, or supporting operational outputs in a deadline-driven environment, with a focus on accuracy, timeliness, quality review, and risk mitigation.

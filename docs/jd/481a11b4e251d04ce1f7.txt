The Impact You will have in this role : The Development family is responsible for creating, designing, deploying, and supporting applications, programs, and software solutions. May include research, new development, prototyping, modification, reuse, re-engineering, maintenance, or any other activities related to software products used internally or externally on product platforms supported by the firm. The Application Support Engineering role specializes in maintaining and providing technical support for all applications that are beyond the development stage and are running in the daily operations of the firm. Works closely with development teams, infrastructure partners, and internal / external clients to escalate and resolve technical support incidents. Your Primary Responsibilities : Verify analyses performed by team members and implement corrective actions required to prevent recurrence of incidents and production issues. Resolve critical application alerts and production incidents in a timely manner, including root cause analysis, business impact assessments, and coordination with development and infrastructure teams. Review, maintain, and enhance knowledge articles, operational procedures, support documentation, and runbooks to ensure accuracy and operational readiness. Collaborate with business, development, infrastructure, security, and vendor teams to troubleshoot application issues and escalate as required. Validate and provide responses to requests for information associated with audits, compliance reviews, and regulatory requirements. Execute Disaster Recovery (DR) procedures and validation testing during planned exercises and unplanned outages, providing Business Continuity Management (BCM) evidence as required. Identify, develop, and implement automation opportunities to reduce manual effort and improve monitoring, alerting, and operational efficiency. Partner with development teams throughout the software development lifecycle to provide operational support input, production readiness reviews, and reliability best practices. Execute and support pre-production and production application deployments, vendor application upgrades, and change management activities in accordance with established procedures. Provide Enterprise Application Support (EAS) for business-critical applications, ensuring high availability, reliability, performance, and compliance with established service level objectives. Utilize Git and Bitbucket for source code management activities, including reviewing repositories, managing branches, analyzing code changes, and supporting deployment processes. Leverage Jenkins and CI/CD pipelines to support automated application deployments, release management, environment validation, and operational troubleshooting. Monitor application health, investigate system performance issues, analyze logs, and coordinate resolution activities across infrastructure, middleware, database, and development teams.

Summary OCC's AI Research & Engineering (AIRE) team is building the company's first generation of agentic AI capabilities on Anthropic's Claude models and AWS infrastructure. The Associate Principal, AI Engineering owns the design and delivery of agentic AI systems end-to-end, mentors junior engineers, and contributes technical judgment on AI application and/or AI infrastructure work in a largely greenfield environment. Primary Duties and Responsibilities: To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each primary duty satisfactorily. Own the design and delivery of agentic AI systems end-to-end, from architecture through production deployment Architect agent orchestration patterns, tool and context integration, and evaluation pipelines for reliability in a regulated environment Own AI infrastructure supporting AIRE's agents and applications on AWS, and contribute to the team's growing infrastructure self-sufficiency Implement technical safeguards against AI-specific risks, including hallucination detection, prompt injection defense, and output validation, in collaboration with OCC's AI Governance function Build audit logging and human-in-the-loop checkpoints into agentic workflows so agent actions remain traceable and reviewable in a regulated environment Mentor Associate and Senior Associate engineers through code and architecture review Contribute technical point of view to the team's evolving engineering standards and practices, in partnership with the Executive Director Supervisory Responsibilities: None (no direct reports; mentors junior engineers without formal supervisory authority) Qualifications: The requirements listed are representative of the knowledge, skill, and/or ability required. Technical Skills: Required: Strong Python; proficient SQL; solid distributed systems and API design fundamentals Required: Hands-on experience building LLM-powered applications or agentic systems Required: Working knowledge of AI-specific risk areas (prompt injection, output validation, hallucination mitigation, data privacy), or demonstrated ability to build this expertise quickly Required: Cloud infrastructure experience (AWS preferred); comfort with Docker, CI/CD, and containerization fundamentals Preferred: Direct experience with Claude, the Anthropic API, or Claude.ai/Console Preferred: Experience designing agent orchestration or Model Context Protocol (MCP)-based integrations Preferred: Production experience operating LLM-powered applications or agentic systems at scale Preferred: Kubernetes and infrastructure-as-code (Terraform) Preferred: Background in financial services, derivatives, or other regulated industries Preferred: Exposure to AI security practices such as red-teaming or adversarial testing of model inputs and outputs Preferred: Data engineering experience (pipelines, data modeling), particularly for candidates interested in AI infrastructure work Education and/or Experience: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field; Master's preferred 4-7 years of software engineering experience, with production AI/LLM systems experience preferred Certificates or Licenses: None required About Us The Options Clearing Corporation (OCC) is the world's largest equity derivatives clearing organization. Founded in 1973, OCC is dedicated to promoting stability and market integrity by delivering clearing and settlement services for options, futures and securities lending transactions. As a Systemically Important Financial Market Utility (SIFMU), OCC operates under the jurisdiction of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC), and the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System. OCC has more than 100 clearing members and provides central counterparty (CCP) clearing and settlement services to 19 exchanges and trading platforms.

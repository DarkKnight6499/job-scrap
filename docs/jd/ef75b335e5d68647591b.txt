Become a part of our caring community Associate Director, Technology Solutions Provider Solution Engineering Become a part of our caring community The Associate Director, Technology Solutions is a technology leader within Humana’s Provider Solution Engineering domain, responsible for delivering modern, scalable technology solutions that advance Provider business capabilities and enterprise objectives. This role provides leadership across platform engineering, solution delivery, AI-enabled transformation, cloud modernization, and engineering practices. The Associate Director partners across Provider business and technology teams to translate strategic needs into sustainable technology solutions while improving speed, quality, scalability, and operational effectiveness. The successful candidate will bring a demonstrated track record of leading successful technology transformations, modernizing complex platforms, and applying AI and emerging technologies to materially improve engineering and business outcomes. Key Responsibilities Collaborate on the engineering strategy, modernization, and evolution of Provider technology platforms, creating scalable and reusable capabilities that support multiple products and business functions. Drive and advance modern platform engineering practices that improve developer productivity, automation, reliability, observability, scalability, and speed to market. Promote and accelerate the adoption of AI, Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), intelligent automation, and AI-assisted engineering to improve software delivery and operational excellence. Drive AI initiatives from experimentation through production-scale deployment, ensuring measurable business value and organizational adoption. Partner closely with architecture, product, data, compliance, security, and business stakeholders to align technology investments with strategic priorities. Influence platform and solution design decisions that reduce complexity, improve maintainability, and minimize technical debt. Advance engineering excellence across architecture, software development, testing, DevOps, CI/CD, observability, operational readiness, and production support. Collaborate across engineering teams and technology leaders to deliver complex, strategic, and high-impact initiatives. Foster and model a culture of innovation, accountability, continuous improvement, collaboration, and measurable results. Serve as a thought leader, identifying opportunities to apply emerging technologies that create competitive advantages. Use your skills to make an impact Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. 10+ years of progressive technology experience delivering large-scale technology, digital transformation, platform engineering, or modernization initiatives. Experience delivering complex, cross-functional programs involving multiple engineering teams and business stakeholders. Technical Expertise Deep experience delivering enterprise technology transformation initiatives involving cloud, platform modernization, applications, integrations, and operating model evolution. Demonstrated hands-on experience implementing AI/Generative AI solutions, enterprise AI platforms, intelligent automation, AI-assisted software engineering, or LLM-based capabilities. Strong understanding of modern software engineering practices, including: Cloud-native architecture APIs and microservices CI/CD pipelines DevOps and DevSecOps Automation and observability Platform reliability and resiliency engineering Ability to translate business strategy into technology roadmaps, architecture strategies, and executable outcomes. Preferred Qualifications Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Technology, Business, or a related field. Experience in healthcare technology, particularly within provider, payer, or healthcare administration organizations.

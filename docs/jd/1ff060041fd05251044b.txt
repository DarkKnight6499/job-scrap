Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism SAP Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a SAP Security and Access Controls Sr Associate you will provide consulting services that help clients confirm compliance and strengthen security within SAP applications, analyze client requirements, implement security measures, and guide compliance with regulatory standards within our Cyber, Data & Tech Risk practice. As a Senior Associate you will analyze complex problems, manage assigned workstreams, build meaningful client connections, and mentor junior team members while using critical thinking, data insights, and a broad range of tools and methods to support delivery. In this role at PwC, you will help clients mitigate risk, protect sensitive data, and maintain access control processes that support business application performance. You will also contribute to delivery across SAP compliance and security efforts, anticipate client needs, and develop your understanding of the business context as it changes. Responsibilities - Analyzing SAP security and access control requirements for client environments and translating them into role design and authorization solutions - Implementing SAP Governance, Risk and Compliance controls to support access provisioning, segregation of duties, and compliance review activities - Reviewing user access, sensitive transaction activity, and control exceptions to identify risk areas and recommend remediation actions - Developing and maintaining security documentation, audit support materials, and access control records for client engagements - Supporting compliance assessments by evaluating SAP configurations against regulatory guidelines and internal control requirements - Collaborating with engagement teams to interpret findings, address control gaps, and track remediation progress - Guiding junior team members through security testing tasks, documentation standards, and issue resolution approaches - Interpreting technical and business requirements to help clients strengthen SAP security processes and protect sensitive data What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 3 years of experience of experience delivering SAP compliance, security, and governance solutions, including auditing, advisory, and implementation engagements What Sets You Apart - Preference for a Master's or Masters of Business Administration degree in at least one of the following fields of study: Accounting, Analytics/Data Science, Business Administration/Management, Computer Science/Information Systems, Cybersecurity, Economics, Engineering, Finance, Mathematics/Statistics, Operations/Supply Chain, Project/Technology Management, Risk Management/Insurance - Demonstrating SAP security and access controls consulting experience for client engagements - Showing experience with SAP GRC, compliance reviews, and access remediation - Interpreting business process risks and control design across SAP environments -

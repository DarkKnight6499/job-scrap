At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. Regeneron is currently seeking an Associate Manufacturing MES Specialist to join our Manufacturing MES team. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. When & where: Location: Raheen, Limerick What you’ll do: Leading deployment and support of the Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES) manufacturing Batch Records (MBR’s), Generic manufacturing Batch Records (GMBR’s) and Equipment data Chairing review and approval sessions of MES project deliverables against the overall project plan MES Design Spec review using the MR/BS and completed design spec/process flow map templates Leading Pre-PE executions (Static and Dynamic) and testing of MES records Reviewing data for build of OPM recipes to support the MES build Coordinating with the team on plant testing (Shakedowns) of MES documents, testing protocol and ensuring GDP and compliance Coordinating workflows for documentation approval, MES Deployment Support – documentation readiness and change control support Coordinating with area owners to ensure Process Area ready for MES Deployment (hardware, barcoding etc.) Coordinating sustaining deployed MES records to ensure timely close-out of executed MES records Leading MFG element of go live activities in the lead up to MES records live on the production floor and ensure hypercare activities Supervising KPI data to aid wider MES/MFG team in driving consistency and timeliness The environment and physical rhythms of this role: Knowledge and experience of MES preferably Werum PAS/X Logical troubleshooting and problem-solving skills Excellent analytical, written and oral communication skills Let’s find out if we’re a fit: BS/BA/BEng in a Technical Discipline required Associate Specialist: 0-2 years experience Experience working within a Life Sciences manufacturing environment advantageous Thrive today.

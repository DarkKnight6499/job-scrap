What’s in it for you: Trane Technologies is hiring an Associate Category Manager in Davidson North Carolina! The Associate Category Manager will manage a segment of the Diversified Components category within the North America region. This is a fantastic opportunity for career development into Category Management. What you will do: Collaboration : Identify, engage and align with internal stakeholders (engineering, operations, quality or other business partners) to capture category requirements and ensure supplier qualification and quality; Partner with business unit Procurement teams to efficiently transition suppliers as needed; Support region/sector New Product Development (NPD) Procurement teams through preferred supplier programs. Negotiations : Support negotiations and supplier selection; accountable for contract lifecycle management and compliance. Cost Management: Responsible for delivering category productivity through price, value analysis (VA) or continuous improvement projects. Supplier Management: Support supplier segmentation, Supplier Relationship Management (SRM) and quarterly supplier reviews for all strategic suppliers within category; Ensure creation of supplier profiles for all SRM suppliers. Risk Management : Actively monitor and mitigate supplier risk including financial, capacity, quality, and compliance. What you will bring: Bachelor’s Degree in Business, Supply Chain, Operations, Engineering or related field. 2+ years of procurement / sourcing experience, ex: consulting, purchasing / buyer, and/or analyst experience. Ability to assess vendors and demonstrated negotiating skills. Demonstrated experience facilitating discussions and strong conflict resolution skills. Professional communication; both verbal and written are required.

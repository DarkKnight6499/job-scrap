The Director of Business Banking Credit & Risk is responsible for leading credit risk management, underwriting oversight, and servicing for the Business Banking and SBA lending portfolios. This role partners closely with Business Banking Sales, SBA Sales, Operations, Credit Administration, and Commercial Credit leadership to ensure sound credit decisions, regulatory compliance, and effective risk management practices. The Director provides strategic leadership to credit underwriting and servicing teams while supporting the growth of the Bank's Business Banking and SBA portfolios. Job Responsibilities: Partner with the Director of SBA Sales, Director of Business Banking Sales, Director of Business Banking Operations, Commercial Credit leadership, and associated teams to evaluate new Small Business, Business Banking, and SBA lending opportunities. Provide oversight and leadership of underwriting teams responsible for evaluating, structuring, and recommending credit facilities. Collaborate with Commercial Credit Officers and business line partners to assess credit risk and support sound lending decisions. Monitor portfolio performance and identify trends, emerging risks, and opportunities within Business Banking and SBA portfolios. Manage loan-level, portfolio, policy, compliance, operational, and audit risk across all Business Banking segments. Partner with the First Line of Defense and peer leaders to identify, mitigate, and manage risk exposure to the Bank. Lead teams responsible for credit underwriting, servicing, and credit policy adherence. Ensure loan originations are underwritten, approved, documented, and monitored in accordance with WSFS loan policy, Business Banking guidelines, and SBA Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs). Maintain compliance with applicable banking regulations, internal policies, and industry best practices. Conduct periodic portfolio reviews to assess asset quality and identify potential areas of risk. Prepare and review credit memoranda to ensure appropriate loan structure, eligibility, risk assessment, and adherence to policy requirements. Review Business Banking and SBA loan packages developed by Relationship Managers and Loan Officers for compliance with lending objectives, policies, and procedures. Support strategic initiatives to improve credit quality, efficiency, risk management processes, and portfolio performance. Minimum Qualifications : Bachelor's degree in Business, Finance, Accounting, or a related field preferred. Minimum of 10-15 years of experience in commercial credit, Business Banking, SBA lending, or a related credit risk management environment. Progressive leadership experience managing underwriting, credit administration, or risk management teams. Relevant banking, credit, or risk management certifications are a plus.

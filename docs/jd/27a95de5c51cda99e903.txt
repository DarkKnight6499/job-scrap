Job Description: As Director of Enterprise Risk Management, you will lead the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta’s [i.e., the District’s] enterprise risk management (ERM) program and its internal control over financial reporting program. This role partners with business leaders to identify, assess, and mitigate current and emerging risks that could affect the achievement of strategic objectives, and provides independent risk insight to senior leadership and the Board of Directors. The Director also leads the framework for financial reporting controls, working with business areas and auditors to meet applicable public-company auditing standards and Sarbanes-Oxley Act requirements. Location: You must live within commuting distance of Atlanta or an approved Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta office. Key Responsibilities: Enterprise Risk Management Own and continuously improve the District's ERM framework, including risk taxonomy, assessment methodology, and reporting standards. Lead enterprise risk assessments and facilitate risk and control self-assessment sessions with business and functional areas. Identify, monitor, and report on risk events, trends, and emerging risks across legal, financial, operational, strategic, and technology and data categories. Partner with the Strategy team to align risk activities with strategic planning and objectives. Deliver clear, concise risk analysis and recommendations to the Executive Leadership Committee and the Board of Directors. Internal Controls Lead the District's financial reporting controls program, including design, testing, and monitoring of controls. Advise business areas on control ownership, documentation, and remediation of deficiencies. Oversee the primary liaison with internal and external auditors on control matters. Collaboration and Influence Build strong relationships across the District's core functions (bank supervision, research, financial services, and corporate support) to understand processes and risk exposures. Represent the District on System-level work groups addressing risk assessment methodology and policy. Lead risk training and awareness efforts to promote an active risk management culture. Program Operations and Leadership Oversee the automation, data analytics, and reporting tools that support the ERM program. Manage project plans, cross-functional work groups, and proposals for program enhancements. Lead, develop, and evaluate a team of risk professionals, setting objectives, monitoring performance, and supporting career growth. Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in business, finance, accounting, or related field; advanced degree or certification (CPA, CIA, CRISC, FRM, CERA) preferred. 10+ years of progressive experience in ERM, internal audit, or internal controls. Knowledge and Technical Skills Working knowledge of COSO frameworks, Sarbanes-Oxley Act, and risk assessment methods. Demonstrated ability to facilitate senior-level discussions and communicate effectively with senior executives and boards. Experience managing teams and analytics or Governance Risk and Compliance (GRC) applications.

As an Individual Contributor, this role serves as a trusted advisor to relationship teams by providing advanced underwriting support, portfolio oversight, credit expertise, risk management guidance, and client monitoring for complex commercial and corporate banking relationships. The position helps balance growth objectives with prudent portfolio management practices. • Complex Credit Advisory: Evaluates complex credit requests, renewals, modifications, annual reviews, and covenant structures while developing informed recommendations and underwriting guidance. • Portfolio Risk Oversight: Reviews portfolio performance, borrower financial condition, industry trends, and risk indicators to identify concerns and recommend appropriate actions. • Client and Relationship Support: Participates in client discussions and relationship planning activities while helping identify opportunities to strengthen relationships and expand banking solutions. • Transaction Structuring Support: Assists relationship teams with evaluating transaction structures, credit terms, covenant frameworks, and risk mitigation strategies. • Mentoring and Best Practices: Shares expertise, provides guidance to less experienced portfolio professionals, and promotes consistency in underwriting and portfolio management practices. • Performs other responsibilities and duties as assigned.

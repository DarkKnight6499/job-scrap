Analyzes data and drivers behind the results while articulating succinct summaries of performance including full P&L, Sales & SGM, comprehensive category and brand reporting. Provides concise information & key management indicators to the team. Partner with brand teams across the globe to coordinate new product launches, commercial execution, investment strategies & market trends implications to build New Product Development forecasts for the Tools & Outdoor organization. Strong cross functional collaboration to ensure alignment and execution of NPD results. Support executive presentations such as budget reviews, quarterly reviews, strategic planning, and others. Self driven analyst, with strong attention to detail to consume many data points and develop a cohesive executive reporting. Highly proficient in Excel, PowerPoint, knowledge of Hyperion HFM and SAP or their equivalent. Power BI and/or Alteryx experience a plus. Experience with process improvements and optimization a plus.

Job Description: The Group Strategic Advisers, LLC (SAI) is a registered investment advisor and Fidelity Investments Company that provides investment management services to clients through Fidelity’s retail and institutional distribution channels. For more than 30 years, SAI has specialized in the investment management of managed accounts and other custom solutions to help investors reach their financial goals. With more than $1.4 trillion in assets under management in a blend of Fidelity and third-party investment vehicles, Fidelity’s SAI is a leading provider of managed solutions for retail, workplace, and institutional clients. The Role We seek a proactive, curious, innovative quantitative analyst to join the SAI Quantitative Research team, focusing on asset allocation research and portfolio construction. SAI is looking to advance its research capability and expand quantitative research input in support of our Asset Allocation decision-makers. The selected candidate will help drive and execute a vision for asset allocation and macroeconomic investment research, bringing strong technical skills and expertise in economics, finance, mathematical modeling, and quantitative analysis. This role will support a team of SAI asset allocation portfolio managers in developing strategic and tactical allocation across several managed account products with a large asset base. This role will involve both ad hoc support of portfolio managers and individual contributions to help improve decision-making, streamline workflows, and unlock new sources of insight. This individual will collaborate with a wide range of stakeholders across SAI, help build the function within SAI, and foster a culture of experimentation in SAI’s complex yet collaborative environment. The ideal candidate will be capable of driving innovation and have a strong understanding of SAI’s investment, research, business, and trading processes. Responsibilities The successful candidate will operate as an embedded quantitative analyst with the SAI Asset Allocation team and, over time, will be expected to expand their investment impact and thought-leadership, including the design and implementation of an expanded asset allocation and macroeconomic research function for SAI.

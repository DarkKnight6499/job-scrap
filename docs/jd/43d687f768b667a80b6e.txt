As a Workflow Solutions Associate, you’ll be part of the team that keeps things moving behind the scenes, making sure requests are handled quickly, accurately, and with care. Every interaction is an opportunity to deliver clarity, confidence, and forward momentum. You’ll receive comprehensive on-the-job training to assess and advise on client materials, such as money movements, account updates, and identity verification. You’ll learn how to navigate complex scenarios and apply sound judgment in a structured environment. The ability to adapt, think critically, and maintain a sharp eye for detail will be key to your success. Above all, timeliness and accuracy are paramount. Your work directly impacts our advisors’ ability to serve their clients—and we take that responsibility seriously.

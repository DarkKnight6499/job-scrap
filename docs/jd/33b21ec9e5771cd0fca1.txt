Your opportunity Develop and execute category strategies across professional services, corporate services, marketing and client services, and HR and employee services spend, aligned to business priorities, budget planning, risk requirements, and enterprise procurement objectives. Lead sourcing events, including RFIs, RFPs, RFQs, supplier evaluations, bid analysis, commercial negotiations, and recommendation papers for stakeholder decision-making. Own renewal planning for assigned categories, including early identification of upcoming renewals, consolidation opportunities, and negotiation strategy; mitigation of commercial risk and pricing increases. Partner with business stakeholders to define requirements, challenge scope where appropriate, and ensure sourcing activity supports value for money, appropriate service levels, and practical business outcomes. Support legal services and professional services spend management through panel strategy, rate benchmarking, alternative fee arrangements, scope discipline, and cost containment. Manage assigned spend categories, including Professional Services: consulting, legal, audit, tax, recruitment, and contingent labor; Corporate Services: facilities, travel, workplace, real estate, fleet, print, and office services; Marketing and Client Services: creative agencies, events, conferences, media, PR, sponsorships, and digital marketing; and HR and Employee Services: benefits, wellbeing, learning, relocation, payroll, and background screening. Develop and implement a contingent workforce strategy, including supplier rationalization, rate-card governance, worker classification controls, statement-of-work discipline, tenure management, onboarding processes, and reporting to improve cost, compliance, and operational outcomes. Analyze spend, contract, supplier, and market data to identify savings opportunities, supplier consolidation opportunities, demand management levers, and unmanaged or fragmented spend. Partner with Legal on contract negotiation strategy, fallback positions, and escalation points, while ensuring procurement leads the commercial strategy and supplier negotiation plan. Collaborate with Information Security, Data Privacy, Business Continuity, and other business teams to support appropriate due diligence, onboarding, monitoring, and exit planning. Track and report sourcing pipeline, savings delivery, avoided cost, contract risk improvements, renewal status, cycle times, and stakeholder outcomes. Build strong relationships with key suppliers and internal stakeholders, acting as a trusted commercial advisor and escalation point for assigned categories. Contribute to procurement playbooks, templates, governance standards, sourcing workflows, and continuous improvement initiatives that support a scalable global strategic sourcing model.

You’ll get to: Create and follow up the Internal Audit program. Act as the Site’s Document Control Coordinator. Act as the Site’s Corrective Actions Coordinator. Work with the Staff to create and maintain the training matrix. Administer training programs created by the department owners based on operations needs. Coach Department owners in creating certification programs for key positions. Schedule trainings and create a culture of mindfulness of safety practices. Track, maintain and archive all training documentation and databases to keep a real-time status. Provide support on EHS related activities as needed. Drive and promote safety culture and employee engagement throughout the business. Drive the 5S program throughout the facility. Define schedules and generate monthly reports. Other duties assigned to meet the business needs. The Person: You love to learn and grow and be acknowledged for your valuable contributions. You’re not intimidated by innovation. Associate degree in any Manufacturing related certification a plus. 2 to 5 years of experience in Manufacturing with an emphasis in training, health and safety systems or equivalent experience in a technical environment requiring adherence to training on strong health and safety practices. Knowledge of Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) regulations. OSHA 10-hour course completion (prefer OSHA 30-hour course completion). Experience as a trainer/teacher of manufacturing, EHS subjects. Knowledgeable in performing audits. Minimum OSHA courses completed (Recordkeeping; Hazard Identification; Risk Assessment). Computer software animation or AI knowledgeable. TCEQ, & EPA knowledgeable (preferred). Bilingual (English/Spanish) Ability to communicate verbally and/or in written form effectively. Experience with Lean Manufacturing and 5S concept a plus.

About Alternative Investment Platform (AIP) The Alternative Investment Platform is a global team comprised of 100+ alternative investment professionals across 13 offices, including New York, San Francisco, Miami, Dallas, Salt Lake City, Los Angeles, Albany, Zurich, London, Dubai, Hong Kong, Bengaluru, Shanghai, and Singapore. The team is responsible for ~$200B in AUS, serving institutions, foundations, family offices, high net worth and ultra-high net worth individuals for Goldman Sachs Wealth Management. AIP, one of the leading allocators globally, includes the Alternative Capital Markets business, focused on curating a best-in-class alternatives platform and client experience across Goldman Sachs managed and externally managed strategies. The team curates investment opportunities for clients across the alternatives landscape, advises on building alternatives portfolios, and manages discretionary portfolios through our Alternatives Portfolio Services (APS) platform. Additionally, AIP includes our Private Company Investments (PCI) team and Private Secondaries Group (PSG). The Private Secondaries Group is a leading private markets secondary solutions team that advises clients on liquidity, portfolio optimization, and secondary investment opportunities. The team executes transactions across private fund interests, direct company exposures, structured liquidity solutions, and Goldman Sachs-sponsored investment vehicles. Leveraging deep market expertise and a broad investor network, PSG helps clients actively manage private market portfolios while providing access to differentiated secondary market opportunities in one of the fastest-growing segments of alternative investing. Role Overview This Associate role offers the opportunity to play a key role in the continued growth and evolution of the Private Secondaries Group. The individual will support the origination, underwriting, and execution of complex secondary transactions, delivering analytical insight, transaction support, and strategic advice to clients pursuing liquidity solutions. As a core member of the deal team, the Associate will be responsible for financial modeling, portfolio valuation, preparation of client-facing materials, and coordination of transaction processes. The role includes managing due diligence workflows, evaluating bids, and supporting the execution of sophisticated secondary transactions across a range of asset classes and structures. Beyond transaction execution, the Associate will contribute to the ongoing development and scalability of the business, helping enhance processes, deepen client relationships, and support the expansion of the firm's secondary platform. HOW YOU WILL FULFILL YOUR POTENTIAL Underwriting & Valuation Transaction Pricing & Analytics: Perform valuation, performance, and pricing analyses to support underwriting recommendations, assess transaction dynamics, and inform client decision-making. Market Intelligence: Conduct research on industry trends, fund managers, and secondary market activity to support transaction execution and strategic client advice. Financial Analysis & Valuation: Develop and maintain financial models to assess private equity, venture capital, private credit, real estate, and other private market portfolios and assets. Transaction Execution & Client Deliverables Transaction Execution: Support the execution of complex secondary transactions, coordinating key workstreams across the transaction lifecycle from initial underwriting through closing. Marketing & Advisory Materials: Prepare high-quality client deliverables, including transaction marketing materials, analytical presentations, teasers, confidential information memorandums, and strategic advisory content. Due Diligence & Bid Evaluation: Manage due diligence processes, data room administration, and transaction workflows, while analyzing buyer indications and bids to develop actionable recommendations for clients. Business Development & Client Engagement Market Coverage & Investor Engagement: Support buyer and seller outreach efforts by identifying prospective counterparties, developing market intelligence, and helping maintain relationships across the secondary ecosystem. Stakeholder Coordination: Coordinate with internal and external stakeholders, including legal, compliance, fund managers, and investors, to facilitate transaction execution, documentation, and successful closings. Client Advisory Support: Contribute to client communications, transaction updates, and strategic discussions, helping deliver thoughtful liquidity solutions and a high-quality client experience.

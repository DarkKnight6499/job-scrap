Start-up Ventures: Associate, M&A Advisory at Wisable
D. E. Shaw
Location: New York
Link: https://www.deshaw.com/careers/start-up-ventures-associate-m-a-advisory-at-wisable-5803

Wisable, a tech-enabled business brokerage spun out of D. E. Shaw’s venture studio, seeks a transaction advisor to guide clients through the business sale process, from go-to-market through closing. This advisor will work directly with small business owners who want to exit, helping them identify the right buyer for their business and navigate each stage of the transaction. This position is with Wisable, not the D. E. Shaw group. As a portfolio company of the D. E. Shaw group’s venture studio, DESCOvery, Wisable benefits from the firm’s industry expertise and network. Please note that Wisable will be reviewing all applications and making hiring decisions independently of the D. E. Shaw group. Manage sell-side M&A advisory engagements with small business owners, ensuring a smooth and successful transaction process. Build trust as the client’s primary advisor, guiding them through a complex transition with empathy and confidence. Leverage Wisable’s AI-powered tools to conduct financial analysis, curate marketing materials, and manage deal workflows. Educate sellers on the M&A process, prepare them for client meetings, and facilitate productive seller-buyer interactions and information exchanges. Assist the seller in evaluating offers and manage the due diligence and closing processes. Cultivate and maintain strategic relationships with key transaction stakeholders, including lenders, legal counsel, and other involved parties. A bachelor’s degree or higher, along with an impressive record of academic and professional achievement. At least five years of experience, with a minimum of two years of experience in investment banking, private equity, or related fields. Experience in SMB M&A is preferred. Excellent emotional intelligence and interpersonal skills, with a demonstrated ability to build trust over time, especially with non-technical clients. A highly organized, proactive, and self-directed working style. The expected annual base salary for this position is $175,000 to $225,000. There will be variable compensation in the form of a year-end bonus.

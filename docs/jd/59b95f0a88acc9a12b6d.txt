Description Summary: Manage a team of fixed income analysts and traders that provide internally vetted fixed instruments to purchase in client accounts. Manage and oversee a series of Fixed Income Strategies. Participate in the Asset Allocation process of the firm. Member of Investment Policy Committee. Duties and Responsibilities: CFA Charter Holder preferred. Lead the development and implementation of the fixed income strategy. Oversee fixed income research and guide investment portfolio managers. Manage the fixed income buy-list and hold-list. Support risk management framework and collaborate with the investment strategy team. Represent Huntington in internal/external presentations and support sales efforts for Wealth Management. Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and monitor market trends/conditions. Develop actionable investment insights and communicate fixed income strategy to stakeholders. Support cross-sell initiatives and provide training on fixed income concepts. Review enhanced Leveraged Cash Flow due diligence when appropriate. Assist Payments Advisors/Relationship Managers in structuring transactions and developing risk mitigation strategies. Manage the approval process for requested limits, amendments, waivers, and consents. Performs other duties as assigned. Basic Qualifications: At least 15 years of related experience.

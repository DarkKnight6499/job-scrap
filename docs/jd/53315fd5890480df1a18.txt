You will report to the Compliance Director, and reside in or near one of our collaboration hubs: California: Costa Mesa Texas : Allen Illinois: Schaumburg Join Experian's Financial Services and Data (FSD) division as an Expert Compliance Risk Advisor and help guide our business through a complex regulatory landscape. You will collaborate with Legal, Information Security, and business teams to assess compliance risks, support regulatory exams, and lead practical solutions that enhance control effectiveness. You'll have the opportunity to: Provide support to internal partners on regulatory compliance requirements and issues related to consumer credit services. Perform gap assessments of BU/functional area procedural documents against Global Policies to ensure adherence and, if applicable, work with the business line on action plans designed to address control breakdowns and track remediation of regulatory issues and process improvements. Work with a variety of teams to ensure compliance with laws, regulations, and policies, including Legal, Information Security, and Governmental Affairs. Maintain understanding of business line operating processes, strategies, products, and services. Participate in exams as the Subject Matter Expert by working with the appropriate BU in collecting and analyzing exam data, documents, and other requested documentation. Work with the Regulatory Exam Manager in the delivery of ad hoc regulatory requests within specified service level agreements (SLAs). Participate where needed in the preparation and presentation of materials for regulatory meetings (kick-off deck, quarterly update). Work in a team environment, address conflict, and build productive relationships with customers, partners, and team members to achieve targeted results. Your background: 3+ years of experience in regulatory risk and compliance/audit processes. Knowledge of federal and state privacy laws and regulations, including FCRA, FACTA, and GLBA, preferably from a consumer reporting agency (CRA) perspective. Understand complex business issues and develop practical, resolving strategies. Comfortable working both independently and in team settings. Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience.

Job Description: Job Title Fixed Income Trader Corporate Title Vice President Location New York, NY Overview Wealth Management business supports clients of Deutsche Bank in the areas of wealth advisory, discretionary, trust planning, custody, and brokerage as well as the deposit business with combined $75bn AUM. Fixed Income Portfolio Management is part of Wealth Management’s Discretionary Portfolio Management team and provides investment management services for clients of the bank. You will be an integral part of the team working to deliver Portfolio Management expertise to these clients.

Electronic Trading Risk Management (ETRM) is a front-office team within Morgan Stanley’s Institutional Equity Division (IED) responsible for managing the risks associated with electronic trading of equities and listed derivatives in the Americas. ETRM is a cross-functional and highly technical/analytical team, uniquely positioned to identify, understand, solve, and monitor a variety of challenges related to electronic and algorithmic trading. ETRM partners closely with a diverse set of stakeholders including Product Management, Traders, Client Coverage, Legal and Compliance, Technology, Operations, Desk Strategists, Quantitative Research, Clients and External Regulators. This role requires a balance of technical/analytical skills, the ability to partner with multiple functional areas of the firm and an interest in trading and market structure. Core responsibilities of this role include: Implementation and ownership of electronic trading risk controls (both pre-trade and post-trade), designed to enable business development in a risk-controlled manner Monitoring electronic trading activity and controls in real-time and taking action, when necessary, to address issues Performing analysis on and curating large complex datasets and system logs to diagnose issues and develop fact-based recommendations and solutions to problems and inquiries Owning and enhancing a variety of critical T+1 order and execution reporting controls focused on managing operational and regulatory reporting risks Facilitating meetings and engaging directly with stakeholders at all levels and across the firm Providing electronic trading subject matter expertise and risk oversight as it relates to: Trading algorithm development New electronic trading product development Implementation of new sources of electronic liquidity, both internal and external to Morgan Stanley Project management of cross-IED electronic trading technology change initiatives Providing subject matter expertise, analysis, data curation respect to electronic trading regulatory inquiries, firm litigation matters, and internal audit exams Managing and developing junior team members The successful ETRM candidate has: 5-8+ years of relevant professional experience in Financial Services or Technology or related industry Bachelor’s degree with strong academic performance Preferred Major or Minor in Computer Science or Information Systems or similar technical field Direct professional experience in a securities trading business in some functional capacity, ideally focused on algorithmic/electronic trading A desire to work in a fast-paced, dynamic, and

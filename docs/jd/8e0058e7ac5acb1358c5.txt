About Our Team Micron Technology's Boise Front-End operations serve as an essential element within the company's U.S. manufacturing foundation and growth. The Operations Finance team collaborates with site operations and global finance teams to build scalable finance processes, boost cost insight, and support business decisions as the site progresses through construction, startup, manufacturing ramp, and ongoing operations. Position Overview The Financial Analyst II is an early-career member of the Boise Operations Finance team. This role is designed for a recent college graduate who is interested in developing expertise in manufacturing finance, financial planning, cost analysis, accounting support, and business partnership. In this role, you will support budgets, forecasts, product-cost inputs, financial analyses, management reporting, and month-end activities for Boise front-end manufacturing operations. You will analyze financial and operating results, investigate budget variances, and work with finance and operations partners to provide clear and accurate information that supports business decisions. You will receive guidance from experienced site and global finance team members while building knowledge of Micron's manufacturing operations, finance processes, systems, and controls. Responsibilities and ownership are expected to increase as you develop technical knowledge, business understanding, professional judgment, and independence. Responsibilities Build and maintain financial models, forecasts, budgets, and scenario analyses to support manufacturing operations. Analyze budget-to-actual results, track key performance measurements, and provide reporting and insights to finance and operations collaborators. Support product costing, operating expense planning, journal entries, financial reporting, and month-end/quarter-end close activities. Partner with operations and global finance teams to assess financial performance, risks, opportunities, and business drivers. Maintain cost center data and ensure accurate financial information within finance systems and reporting processes. Translate financial and operational data into actionable recommendations and decision-support insights. Identify and support cost reduction, enhanced efficiency, and improvement in execution initiatives across the organization. Drive efficiency through process standardization, automation, analytics, AI-enabled solutions, and improved data quality. Minimum Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Business, Economics, Data Analytics, or a related field, completed before the position start date. Proficient in Excel or similar spreadsheet tools for organizing, analyzing, and presenting financial/operational data. Experience with accounting, budgeting, forecasting, financial modeling, and data analysis through coursework, projects, or internships. Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to explain financial information to diverse stakeholders. Knowledge of accounting principles and financial controls, with strong attention to detail, accountability, and a willingness to learn. Preferred Qualifications Finance, accounting, or business internship experience with exposure to budgeting, forecasting, cost accounting, and variance analysis. Advanced Excel skills, including formulas, pivot tables, lookup functions, charts, and data analysis. Familiarity with SAP, SAC-P, Tableau, Power BI, or similar finance and reporting tools. Interest in manufacturing, technology industries, operational improvement, automation, analytics, and finance solutions powered by advanced technology. Strong collaboration and relationship-building skills, with the ability to contribute effectively in team environments.

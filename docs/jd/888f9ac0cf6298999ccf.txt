As a Senior Product Associate in Card Transactions You will help shape the future of our authorization and transaction-processing platforms by combining product thinking, analytics, customer insight, and strategic planning. You will contribute to initiatives focused on platform modernization, resiliency, delivery excellence, regulatory readiness, and customer enablement across the Card Transactions organization. The successful candidate will be comfortable working across multiple stakeholder groups, analyzing data, developing business recommendations, and supporting product strategy through all phases of the product lifecycle. The Card Transactions mission emphasizes modern, resilient, and adaptable platforms that enable customer growth, payment innovation, and operational excellence.

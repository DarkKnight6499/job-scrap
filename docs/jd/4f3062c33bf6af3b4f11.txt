Job Description: Job Title Corporate Trust Analyst Corporate Title Associate Location New York, NY Overview As a Corporate Trust Analyst, you will join the Trust and Securities Services Escrow Operations team that provides escrow solutions on insurance trust, margin loans, merger & acquisition, commercial real estate and subscription facilities products. You will administer daily cash and securities transactions on our client accounts. With guidance from your Account Manager(s), you will independently and proactively resolve client issues to stakeholders’ satisfaction. You will also share constructive feedback and best practices to strengthen team performance and support the Bank’s evolving business needs. In addition, you will work with Account Managers, Administrators, and other product partners including Product Management, Sales, Transaction Management Group, Business, Operations, and Technology to support the product as required under internal policies and applicable legal and regulatory frameworks.

WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH The Apparel Product Risk Engineering (PRE) team is part of the Global Development & Materials, Global Product Standards organization. The quality and performance of Nike products are pivotal to Nike’s competitive advantage, and the PRE team ensures athlete* satisfaction through a focus on Standards, Risk Assessment, Testing, and Manufacturing Preparation. PRE works directly with Design, Development, and other product creation stakeholders to prevent design and manufacturing risks prior to buy ready. While assessing and mitigating risk, PRE also helps to define and update product standards that inform the performance of Nike’s apparel. PRE is also seen as a subject matter expert as product moves from concept to bulk production, providing insights and requirements to ensure quality at scale. PRE is part of the Global Development & Materials – Apparel function at Nike and is uniquely positioned to collaborate and influence Stakeholders across the supply chain including Design, Development, Merchandising, Partner Management, and Manufacturing Partners. WHO WE ARE LOOKING FOR We’re looking for a Senior Product Risk Engineer to work on the Apparel PRE team. This role uses fundamental engineering skills and knowledge of engineering tools to enable data-driven decision making and draws on an understanding of manufacturing and product creation environments, including staying abreast of innovation and advancements related to manufacturing. A successful candidate is metrics-oriented with strong analytical skills and the ability to leverage models and synthesize data to assess and manage risk. The candidate needs to have the ability to influence stakeholders through clear and straightforward communication, and has experience in successfully initiating, planning, implementing, and closing a project/process within scope, budget, and time constraints. We are looking for someone who is optimistic, self-aware, embraces change, holds their decisions and actions accountable, will actively seek feedback to grow and develop, values and respects the opinions of their teammates and embraces the diversity of people, ideas and cultures.

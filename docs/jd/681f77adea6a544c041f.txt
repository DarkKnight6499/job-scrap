Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are hiring multiple Quality Assurance (QA) Validation Specialists to join our team at our new highly automated drug substance manufacturing facility in Saratoga Springs. This position validates and qualifies laboratory and analytical equipment in accordance with regulatory requirements, with secondary support for manufacturing equipment and utility validation in a highly automated, digitally integrated biologics environment.

Job Description Capgemini is seeking an experienced Account Leader to drive strategic growth, executive client relationships, and Data, Analytics, AI, and GenAI engagements within our Banking & Capital Markets portfolio. This is a client-facing leadership role focused on account growth, business development, executive relationship management, and expansion of Data & AI services across key Financial Services clients in the New York Metro area. Key Responsibilities • Lead executive client relationships across Banking & Capital Markets accounts. • Serve as a trusted advisor to client stakeholders on Data, Analytics, AI, GenAI, and Digital Transformation strategies. • Identify and develop new business opportunities within existing and prospective accounts. • Drive account growth through pipeline development, account planning, and expansion of Capgemini's Data & AI capabilities. • Lead proposals, RFP responses, solution development, and executive presentations. • Partner with delivery leaders and cross-functional teams to drive successful client outcomes. • Establish governance models, monitor account performance, and ensure strong client satisfaction. Required Qualifications • 15+ years of experience in Data, Analytics, AI, Cloud, Digital Transformation, Technology Consulting, or IT Services. • Proven experience managing and growing strategic client accounts. • Demonstrated success owning and expanding multi-million-dollar account portfolios. • Strong executive presence with experience building relationships at the CIO, CTO, CDO, COO, and business executive levels. • Experience leading large pursuits, proposals, RFPs, and complex deal cycles. • Strong understanding of Data, Analytics, AI/GenAI, and modern cloud data platforms. • Deep Banking & Capital Markets industry experience. • Experience working with global delivery organizations. • Strong communication, leadership, negotiation, and stakeholder management skills. Additional Details: Preferred Qualifications • Experience selling and delivering Data & AI transformation programs within Banking & Capital Markets organizations. • Experience with Azure, Databricks, Snowflake, Microsoft Fabric, AWS, or similar data and AI platforms. • Prior experience in consulting or IT services organizations. Location & Travel • Candidates should be located in the New York Metro area or willing to relocate. • Regular client interaction within the New York Metro area is expected. • Travel to client locations will be required based on business needs. Work Authorization • Candidates must be authorized to work in the United States without current or future employer sponsorship. • Capgemini is unable to provide visa sponsorship or visa transfer support for this position. Ideal Backgrounds We are particularly interested in candidates with experience from organizations such as consulting firms, global systems integrators, technology services providers, data and AI firms, or Banking & Capital Markets organizations where they have been responsible for account growth, executive client relationships, business development, and technology transformation initiatives. If you have a strong track record of growing strategic Financial Services accounts and helping clients leverage Data, Analytics, AI, and Cloud technologies to achieve business outcomes, we'd like to hear from you.

Start your Voyage – Apply Now Get to Know the Opportunity Voya Investment Management is seeking a highly motivated self-starter Analyst within the Investment Data & Analytics Team. The individual will report to the VP, Investment Analytics. Success in this role requires a strong analytical mindset and a high attention to detail. This role will be responsible for overseeing daily analytics reports to ensure accuracy. This role also plays a crucial role as part of a team supporting data and analytics as part of the investment process. The Contributions You Will Make Investment Data Concepts: Partake in conversations applying knowledge of investment data concepts including aspects of Discounted Cash Flows (DCF), Duration, Yield, and overall ability to tie the structure of the asset to the analytics that are produced within an analytics model. Analytics Data Oversight: Accountable for oversight of investment analytics data for both public and private fixed income instruments. Ensure analytics related deliverables are completed timely and accurately. Ensure root cause analysis approach is performed on data quality issues affecting investment data to ensure end-to-end resolution; coordinate resolution with other groups as required. Stakeholder Engagement: Liaise with various groups, including Risk, Investment Teams, Compliance, etc. on daily production and key initiatives. Risk Model Evaluation: Partake in conversations to evaluate and understand investment data models. Key Projects: Partake in conversations related to team projects and initiatives. Investment Strategy Knowledge: Work with the senior members of the team to develop knowledge in the various investment strategies and respective nuances. Data Requests: Ensure prompt responses to investment data requests from key customers, including traders, portfolio managers, sales, and client service personnel. Reporting: Responsible for producing recurring and ad hoc fixed income analytics reports to internal and external stakeholders, applying knowledge of fixed income to assess accuracy. Vendor Engagement: Liaise with key vendors to establish recurring touchpoints to raise outstanding items and key topics. Drive the coordination of enhancements within the investment platform with the vendor. Benchmark Rebalance Processes: Work alongside the investment teams to perform analysis on benchmark rebalances to ensure the data is comprehensive and accurate. Focus on Continuous Improvement: Bring a fresh perspective on existing issues, processes, and controls to develop and recommend new and improved controls and procedural enhancements. Minimum Knowledge & Experience Bachelor's degree in Economics, Finance, Accounting, or related field. Masters in a similar degree, preferred. 3-5 years’ experience in a similar or related role supporting fixed income analytics. Highly effective communicator with excellent interpersonal and presentation skills. Excellent ability to problem solve, execute process improvements resulting in robust processes. Demonstrated knowledge and/or ability to learn investment products. Ability to collaborate across teams to devise comprehensive solutions. Ability to multi-task, set direction and thrive in a fast paced, deadline-driven environment. Highly motivated self-starter with a strong work ethic that is eager to develop and learn. An analytical mindset, strong project management, organizational, and problem-solving skills. Advanced Excel & SQL skills required; Snowflake & python, a plus. Demonstrated use of AI required with an open mindset to utilization of AI.

Job Summary This senior level individual-contributor role leads complex, cross-functional initiatives spanning Land, Division Orders, Accounting, IT, Data & Analytics and other business partners. The position serves as the primary Land business lead for SAP and other major system, data and process initiatives, translating operating requirements into coordinated project plans, decisions, business requirements and implementation outcomes. The role is accountable for driving work across functional boundaries, identifying dependencies and risks, coordinating subject-matter experts, and ensuring solutions support integrated Land and Accounting processes.

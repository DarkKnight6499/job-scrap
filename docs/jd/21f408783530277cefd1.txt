Position Title Business Banking - Portfolio Manager I Location Nationwide, MI 48098 Job Summary Job Profile Summary The Business Banking - Portfolio Manager I is responsible for managing a portfolio of Small Business and/or Business Banking loans to ensure credit quality, compliance, and profitable growth. This role strengthens client relationships; monitors risk and supports the Bank’s lending strategy by delivering sound credit oversight and portfolio management that balance opportunities with prudent risk management. This role manages an assigned portfolio of Small Business and Business Banking loans, ensuring portfolio quality, compliance with credit policy, and alignment with the bank’s growth objectives. Partners with SBC’s, Relationship Managers, Credit Officers, and business leaders to structure credit solutions, monitor borrower performance, and identify emerging risks. By delivering accurate, timely portfolio reviews and recommendations, the Business Banking-Portfolio Manager I supports sound credit decisions that balance client needs with prudent risk management. Ultimately, this role contributes to sustaining strong client relationships, protecting the bank’s assets, and driving long-term profitability. Job Responsibilities: JOB RESPONSIBILITIES Credit & Portfolio Management Manage an assigned portfolio of Small Business and/or Business Banking loans, ensuring credit structures remain appropriate and in compliance with Bank Credit Policy. Conduct periodic reviews of borrower performance, analyzing repayment capacity, leverage, and key risk factors to provide well-documented recommendations. Proactively identify early warning signals and escalate issues, recommending modifications, renewals, or restructuring as appropriate. Partner with SBC’s and Relationship Managers to deepen client relationships by providing credit expertise and supporting business development goals working with companies up to $10MM in revenue. Engage with borrowers, attorneys, accountants, and other external advisors to validate financial performance and address portfolio issues. Collaborate with Credit Officers and senior leaders to support credit decisions that balance client needs with prudent risk management. Portfolio Monitoring & Compliance Track the receipt of financial statements, tax returns, borrowing base certificates, guarantor information, and covenant compliance documentation. Monitor adherence to financial covenants and loan terms, preparing detailed reports on portfolio risk trends and exceptions. Ensure portfolio management activities adhere to regulatory and compliance requirements, mitigating risk exposure to the bank. Uses independent judgement and discretion to make decisions. Analyzes and resolves problems. ADDITIONAL ACCOUNTABILITIES Performs special projects, and additional duties and responsibilities as required. Consistently adheres to regulatory and compliance policies and standards linked to the job as listed and complete required compliance trainings. Accountable to maintain compliance with applicable federal, state and local laws and regulations.

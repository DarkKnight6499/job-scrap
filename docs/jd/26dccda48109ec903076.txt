Job Summary With Safety and Compliance in the forefront, responsible for minute-to-minute optimization of AEP’s Regulated generation fleet and load obligations, on an assigned desk (e.g. PJM, ERCOT/SPP, or Hydro). This includes adherence to all FERC / NERC / RTO and Corps of Engineers requirements during normal and emergency conditions. It also requires a continuous improvement mindset to drive change and provide input on potential market changes. This position has the authority and is expected to perform all necessary tasks to maintain the reliability of of the grid in both normal and emergency conditions, without consulting upper management, and in support of and under the direction of the respective Transmission Operator, Balancing Authority, and the Reliability Coordinator. Alternatively, this individual may be assigned to dayshift to assist in the ongoing support and coordination of all RTMO rotating teams and interfaces with other groups within Com Ops, the broader Generation organization, Regulatory, IT and other groups within and outside AEP. This also includes being able to fill in on the RTMO rotating teams as needed. Job Description What You’ll Do: Demonstrates competency in execution and monitoring of the activities of one 24-hour RTMO desk to ensure: Compliance with all requirements / regulations associated with NERC, RTO, and FERC hydro licensing and safe operation of AEP’s assets in the markets. This includes following all reliability entity directives and instructions to support maintaining the reliability of the electric grid. Timely and accurate communication and information/data exchange of all reliability and market information with RTO / TOP groups. Risk balanced economic real time optimization of AEP’s generation and demand response resources. Real time communication within RTMO and other Commercial Operations groups (e.g. Day Ahead Market Ops), power plants, RTOs, Transmission Ops, generation co-owners, and counterparties. Execution of all RTMO policies, procedures, and business processes to support overall goals and objectives. This includes timely and accurate completion of all reporting and training requirements. Active participation in and support of AEP’s culture objectives, include an innovation / continuous improvement mindset. Detailed understanding of real time market rules / processes for at least one RTO (or Hydro desk FERC / RTO rules / processes), general understanding of real time market rules / processes for other RTOS, and basic understanding / awareness of Day Ahead and Settlement RTO Market rules and processes. Note: This position is posted at multiple grade levels and offers a range of opportunities for advancement. The minimum qualifications outlined below apply to the Real-Time Market Operations Associate level, with increased educational, skill or experience requirements for higher level Real-Time Market Operations. What We’re Looking For: Education requirements are listed below: Entry-level candidates should have a two-year technical degree or two years of military service in a related field; OR High School diploma/GED with 2 years of relevant, industrial work experience. Experience requirements are listed below: No additional experience required Two (2) years of relevant work experience that demonstrates an ability to operate a power system desired. Power industry and/or power plant experience is a plus. Licenses/Certifications: Must obtain PJM Certification within 18 months of hiring. Other: Twelve (12)-hour shift work is required, including nights, weekends, and holidays. Appropriate pre-employment test required.

Location: Des Moines, IA What this job involves - This role will support brokerage (leasing/sales) activities by strongly leveraging artificial intelligence (AI) to assist with market analysis, business development initiatives, client deliverables, and special projects that support strategic decision-making for clients and the brokerage team. The ideal candidate will execute the below job functions with a high level of quality, accuracy, and attention to detail such as: Maximize the use of AI to prepare offering materials, presentations, and pitch materials for new and existing assignments and business pursuits utilizing effective work planning capabilities. With AI create sophisticated analytical tools, including financial models, dashboards, and data visualizations to support brokerage strategy, client reporting, and market analysis. Collect, validate, analyze, and maintain commercial real estate, economic, demographic, and market data, including information related to properties, leases, tenants, availabilities, developments, and sales activity. Leverage AI-enabled tools and emerging technologies to improve research efficiency, data analysis, reporting accuracy, presentation development, and workflow optimization. Assist with market research initiatives by identifying trends, interpreting market drivers, and translating quantitative and qualitative information into actionable insights for internal teams and clients. Read and review real estate documents (i.e. leases, operating expense statements, environmental and engineering reports, appraisals, etc.) and prepare abstract reports for clients and prospective clients. Review and analyze client portfolio data, legal documents, and due diligence materials to support transaction execution and strategic recommendations. Review and analyze client’s quantitative portfolio data, legal documents, and other material as part of project due diligence process. Develop compelling visualizations and client-ready materials that communicate market conditions, trends, and opportunities in a clear and impactful manner Attend JLL University (“JLLU”) which is a 13-week training, development, and networking program specifically for our new Associate Brokers.

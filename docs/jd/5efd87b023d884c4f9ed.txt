About The Role & Team We are seeking a detail-oriented and motivated Treasury Associate to join our finance team. This role supports the firm's daily cash management, liquidity monitoring, banking operations, and settlement funding activities. The successful candidate will work closely with treasury, finance, operations, and banking partners to help ensure accurate cash positioning, timely movement of funds, and effective management of the firm's liquidity needs. This position offers an excellent opportunity to gain broad exposure to brokerage finance, treasury operations, regulatory funding requirements, and cash management processes within a fast-paced financial services environment. In This Role, You Will Cash Positioning & Liquidity Monitoring Prepare and monitor daily cash position reports across corporate, customer, and settlement accounts. Support daily liquidity management activities to ensure sufficient funding for operational and settlement obligations. Assist in identifying and escalating potential cash shortages or funding requirements. Treasury Operations Initiate and process wire transfers, ACH transactions, and other banking activities in accordance with established controls and approval procedures. Coordinate with banking partners and internal stakeholders to ensure timely movement of funds. Maintain banking records, account documentation, and treasury-related reporting. Reconciliation & Controls Perform daily reconciliation of cash activity between bank accounts and internal systems. Research and resolve reconciliation breaks, transaction exceptions, and banking inquiries. Support the execution of treasury controls and assist in maintaining process documentation. Reporting & Analysis Prepare routine cash forecasts, liquidity reports, and treasury metrics. Analyze variances between projected and actual cash activity. Assist in gathering data and preparing reports for management, auditors, and regulatory examinations. Process Improvement Identify opportunities to enhance treasury workflows and improve operational efficiency. Participate in automation initiatives and system enhancements related to cash management and banking operations. Support testing and implementation of treasury-related process improvements. Audit & Compliance Support Assist with internal and external audit requests. Support regulatory examinations by gathering documentation and responding to information requests. Help ensure treasury activities comply with internal policies and regulatory requirements. The Skills You Bring Bachelor’s degree in finance, Accounting, Economics, Business Administration, or a related field. 2-5 years of experience in treasury, finance, banking operations, cash management, brokerage operations, or a related financial services function. Exposure to cash positioning, reconciliations, banking transactions, or settlement processes preferred. Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel. Experience working with banking platforms, treasury management systems, or financial applications preferred. Ability to analyze financial data and identify discrepancies. Strong organizational and analytical skills. Excellent attention to detail and accuracy. Effective written and verbal communication skills. Ability to prioritize tasks and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment. Team-oriented mindset with a willingness to learn and develop professionally. What Makes You Stand Out Experience within a retail brokerage, clearing firm, fintech, bank, or financial services organization. Exposure to regulatory reserve, settlement funding, treasury, or cash management functions. Progress toward CPA, CTP, or other relevant professional certifications.

Join us and transform the lives of patients while transforming your career. Sr. Associate IS Business System Analyst What you will do Let’s do this. Let’s change the world! In this vital role, you will partner with manufacturing and technology teams to deliver reliable, compliant, and innovative data solutions that support critical operations. You will help drive digital transformation, operational excellence, and data-driven decision-making through the effective use of PI Data Historian and related technologies. Administer, configure, and maintain PI Data Historian platforms supporting Drug Substance and Drug Product operations, including equipment integration across manufacturing areas. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure effective integration between PI Data Historian and manufacturing systems, including MES, PCS, DCS, PLC, SCADA, and related technologies. Coordinate with vendors and external support teams to troubleshoot and resolve application-related issues, ensuring system reliability and operational efficiency. Lead and support technology project implementations and ongoing operational activities, including server deployment coordination, validation support, work order execution, and cybersecurity patch management. Manage multiple medium-to-large cross-functional initiatives simultaneously, ensuring timely execution of milestones and delivery of business value. Ensure compliance with cybersecurity, privacy, data integrity, and regulatory requirements through routine patching, security assessments, and adherence to established standards. Serve as a Technology Subject Matter Expert (SME) in support of internal and external audits, inspections, and regulatory assessments. Support the development and adoption of data analytics, data science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence capabilities to enhance business performance and decision-making. Provide guidance and technical expertise on Informatics and Technology solutions, supporting business priorities and continuous improvement initiatives. Collaborate with business, Technology, and Enterprise Architecture teams to support project portfolios, evaluate solution alternatives, and contribute to business case development. What we expect of you We are all different, yet we all use our unique contributions to serve patients. The Sr. Associate IS Bus Sys Analyst professional we seek is an individual contributor with these qualifications. Basic Qualifications: Master’s degree OR Bachelor’s degree and 2 years of Information Systems experience OR Associate’s degree and 6 years of Information Systems experience OR High school diploma / GED and 8 years of Information Systems experience Preferred Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Engineering and/or Computer Science. Experience administering and supporting Data Historian Systems, preferably OSI PI, including integration with manufacturing and process control systems such as PCS, DCS, PLC, SCADA, and related technologies. Hands-on experience with PI System components, including PI Asset Framework (AF), PI Vision, Performance Equations, RT Reports, and other reporting and visualization tools. Experience working with databases and data platforms, including SQL, Oracle, Databricks, and other relational database technologies. Knowledge of data analytics, data science, statistical analysis, business intelligence, machine learning, and data visualization tools such as Tableau, Spotfire, Python, and R. Demonstrated ability to collaborate effectively with business partners and cross-functional Technology teams to deliver solutions and drive projects from concept through implementation. Experience working in Agile environments and applying Agile methodologies to project delivery and continuous improvement initiatives. Understanding of cybersecurity principles and practices, including access management, vulnerability remediation, patch management, and system compliance. Experience supporting the full application lifecycle, including administration, maintenance, enhancement identification, implementation, and ongoing operational support. Strong verbal and written communication, presentation, and stakeholder management skills, with fluency in both English and Spanish.

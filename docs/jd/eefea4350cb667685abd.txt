The Associated Broker is responsible for preparing and submitting business to our carrier partners, following up for quotes, and providing analytics for key accounts. In addition to assisting with placement activity, responsibilities will include coordinating deadlines for internal /external colleagues and clients. This individual will utilize their expertise to help enhance results for the team. If you’re looking to join a growing organization with unlimited growth potential, this may be the perfect opportunity for you! The Role Gather information and analytics to assess and evaluate client risks to support the placement of client risks into the market. With the support of more experienced brokers, develop client and prospect presentations to secure new, and build on existing, relationships. Research market solutions to learn how to identify and develop optimum risk solutions for clients. Ensure that quality and compliance procedures and processes are adhered to. Execute straightforward new business placement, renewals, and simple alterations of existing cover, under supervision. Record and maintain all information accurately on the appropriate systems. Work closely with associates from other departments that support the broking process (e.g., analytics). Build technical skills by satisfactorily completing basic and intermediate training courses as required. Prepare draft submission documents and finalize submissions for delivery. Review quotes against specifications and develop side-by-side comparisons. Prepare program proposals. Review program binders for accuracy against quotes and complete file reviews. The Requirements High School Diploma required; bachelor’s degree preferred 1-3 years’ experience Property and Casualty license required within 3 months Solid presentation skills (written and oral); demonstrates active listening and note-taking Relationship management and customer focus; ability to interface with customers and collaborate internally with teammates to serve customers Negotiation skills (carrier and client); ability to maintain as-is renewal terms and handle simple business placements Business Acumen: demonstrates basic knowledge/understanding of the insurance business. Project Management: ability to execute assigned tasks in a timely fashion. Adaptable and receptive to feedback; will seek guidance when needed End-to-end process knowledge: fluent in the insurance process and can drive results by influencing other key players in the process. Leadership skills: ability to make decisions and support them; ability to take control of one's own work; ability to seek guidance and coordinate with others when needed. Insurance Technical skills: demonstrates knowledge of core insurance principles. Understands coverage forms and principles. Strong technology acumen – ability to utilize technology-based record management systems; advanced MS Office skills and familiarity with other relevant online tools.

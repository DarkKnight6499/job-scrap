JOB FUNCTION / SUMMARY: The Senior Associate Relations Partner serves as a subject matter expert and advisor on complex associate relations matters. This role manages sensitive investigations, advises leaders on high-risk associate issues, and partners closely with Legal, Compliance, Risk Management, and HR leadership to mitigate organizational risk. The Senior Associate Relations Partner drives consistency in employment practices, supports leadership development. ESSENTIAL DUTIES & RESPONSIBILITIES: Lead complex and high-risk investigations involving associate complaints, misconduct, ethics concerns, Integrity in Action reports, retaliation allegations, and potential regulatory or policy violations. Provide expert consultation to leaders and HR partners regarding performance management, terminations, and conflict resolution. Serve as the primary HR partner for investigations related to ethics, compliance, and SOX-related concerns involving associates. Develop investigation strategies, conduct interviews, prepare findings, and recommend corrective actions. Partner with Legal, Compliance, Risk Management, Internal Audit, and business leaders to address sensitive employment matters and reduce organizational risk. Analyze associate relations metrics and trends, recommending proactive solutions to improve workplace culture and associate experience. Guide leaders in creating development-focused action plans and performance improvement strategies for associates. Deliver training on workplace conduct, investigations, compliance requirements, and effective people management practices. Mentor Associate Relations Partners and provide expertise on complex case management. Support the development and continuous improvement of associate relations policies, procedures, and processes. SUPERVISORY RESPONSIBILITIES: None MINIMUM REQUIRED EDUCATION, EXPERIENCE & KNOWLEDGE: Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or related field required; or an equivalent combination of education and experience. SHRM-SCP, SPHR, SHRM-CP, or PHR certification preferred. 5 years of progressive Human Resources experience with significant associate relations responsibility conducting complex workplace investigations. Demonstrated expertise in employment law, risk mitigation, investigations, and workplace compliance. Strong verbal and written communications, conflict resolution, and problem-solving skills. Strong analytical, influencing, and leadership skills. Banking, financial services, or other highly regulated industry experience preferred.

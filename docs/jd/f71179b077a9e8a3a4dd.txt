Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Financial Due Diligence Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Deals Financial Due Diligence Senior Associate you will support transaction advisory work that helps clients evaluate financial performance, identify deal issues, and assess earnings quality, cash flows, and assets in mergers, acquisitions, and divestitures. As a Senior Associate you will analyze complex situations, build meaningful client connections, and contribute to workstreams while developing your personal brand, technical skills, and judgment in an environment that values critical thinking and thoughtful review. In this role at PwC, you will focus on financial due diligence within our Deals practice, reviewing financial information, interpreting data, and helping deliver insights that support client decisions across deal activity. Responsibilities - Analyzing financial statements, earnings quality, cash flow, and working capital to identify transaction risks and value drivers - Reviewing historical and projected performance data to support buy-side and sell-side diligence activities - Developing analyses of key deal issues, accounting adjustments, and business performance metrics for client decision making - Building client relationships by responding to questions, interpreting feedback, and communicating findings clearly - Managing assigned workstreams and coordinating deliverables across transaction phases and deadlines - Using financial modeling, valuation, and data analysis tools to evaluate deal assumptions and transaction outcomes - Mentoring junior team members by reviewing work products, sharing technical guidance, and helping prioritize tasks - Applying critical thinking and professional standards when assessing complex transactions and evolving business conditions What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 3 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for at least one of the following fields of study: Accounting, Actuarial Science, Analytics/Data Science, Business Administration/Management, Computer Science/Information Systems, Economics, Finance, Health Administration/Public Health, Mathematics/Statistics, Risk Management/Insurance - Demonstrating Financial Due Diligence experience across buy-side transactions - Interpreting earnings quality, cash flow, and working capital drivers - Building client relationships while navigating complex transaction issues - Applying critical thinking to financial analysis and deal insights - Mentoring junior team members while managing assigned workstreams Travel Requirements Up to 60% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $77,000 - $202,000.

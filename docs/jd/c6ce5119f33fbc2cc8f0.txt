The Difference You Make: The Analyst, Portfolio Management is responsible for assisting with all aspects of the portfolio management process to ensure timely and accurate implementation of investment strategies across various portfolios. The incumbent will collaborate with investment teams and all internal cross-functional teams where necessary. Communicates effectively with borrowers and serve as one of the primary points of contact for loan closing and post-closing loan management. Reviews and processes funding disbursement requests, ensuring proper documentation and compliance with loan conditions. Collects quarterly financial statements and reporting documentation and assist in determining financial condition, credit-worthiness and project compliance for all borrowers. Maintains working knowledge of loan policies and procedures. Works closely with the Finance department to ensure correct accounting for all loans. Tracks, analyzes, communicates, and makes recommendations to address loan delinquencies. Rebalances portfolios due to cash flows, guideline changes or general market movements. Manages and maintains appropriate cash levels and equitizing wherever necessary. Monitors dispersion across portfolios within similar strategies and coordinating actions needed with portfolio teams. Ensures accounts are being managed consistently with client guidelines. Partners with all internal and external teams in support of funding new accounts, transfers-in-kind, client transitions and general account set up. Tracks trading trends of Santander's products. Builds strong working relationships with Santander portfolio teams. Mitigates Companywide operational risk within daily processing environment. Maintains loan portfolio management system and related databases. What You Bring: To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily.

Role Description The Investment Operations Analyst is responsible for supporting and overseeing the daily operations of the investment book of record, ensuring the accuracy, integrity, and timely delivery of critical portfolio, transaction, and cash flow data to global portfolio managers and risk teams. This role serves as a subject matter expert for investment operations, driving process improvements, strengthening operational controls, resolving complex reconciliation issues, and enhancing data quality across a broad range of investment products. The ideal candidate combines strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and a continuous improvement mindset to support the efficient management of investment operations. Core Responsibilities Serves as subject matter and technical expert, identifies and resolves complex operational issues. Maintains the global daily operations for the investment book of record. Provides daily records to the global portfolio managers and risk analysts. Analyzes cash flow, transaction, and portfolio holdings data. Leads process improvements efforts, completes comprehensive analysis, and recommends solutions. Documents procedures across all investment product types. Leverages fund and operational knowledge to aid in the production of gold copy data and enhance output. Maintains communication with internal and external partners to resolve process and reconciliation issues. Oversees the research and implement complex process improvements. Oversees daily operation, manages controls and verifies completion of processes. Identifies opportunities for process improvements and proactively proposes solutions. Participates in special projects and performs other duties as assigned. Qualifications Minimum of two years related work experience. Undergraduate degree or equivalent combination of training and experience. Accounting or Finance degree preferred.

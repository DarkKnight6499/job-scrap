JOB SUMMARY Under general supervision, provides leadership in the technical support to the manufacturing organization to ensure adherence to assembly procedures, on-going quality and efficiency, success of new product introductions and prototype production. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES · Provides primary liaison support between engineering and manufacturing for the transfer of customer documents to manufacturing process. · Provides technical leadership to manufacturing personnel. · Interprets assembly drawings. Works with production assemblers to create assembly work instructions. · Aids engineering in determining tools and processes required for assembly. · Establishes routing requirements, standards and data entry into QM Module. · Implements correct status entry into SAP. · Assists in the transition of new products and prototypes to the manufacturing floor for specific equipment lines. · Review labor standards for re-costing/re-pricing. Assists in providing labor quotes. · Assists in up-front planning for creation of assembly work instructions. · Assists in implementation of ECO, ECR, BOM and Problem Feedback into manufacturing. . JOB QUALIFICATIONS KNOWLEDGE REQUIREMENTS · Ability to effectively present information and respond to questions from groups of managers, clients, customers, and the general public. · Ability to define problems, collect data, establish facts, and draw valid conclusions. · Ability to operate a personal computer including using a Windows based operating system and related software. EDUCATION & EXPERIENCE REQUIREMENTS · High School Diploma · Technical degree in a related area. · Minimum of 3 years experience. · Or an equivalent combination of education, training or experience.

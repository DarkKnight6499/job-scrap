The Senior Finance Analyst will play an integral role on the Engineering Finance team, which oversees the consolidation and analysis of financial information (EBITDA, Capital, Headcount, Allocations) across multiple lines of business in critical and emerging technologies. This position offers unique exposure to operational and strategic planning that has an enterprise-wide impact. This role will report to the Manager of Engineering Finance within the Engineering Finance organization. Here you can: Support the annual budgeting, quarterly forecasting, and monthly financial outlook processes for assigned areas. Analyze key financial metrics, variances, and trends, and prepare concise summaries for executive-level presentations. Perform monthly close activities, including allocations, accruals, and company-wide cross charges, ensuring timely and accurate completion. Ensure the accuracy and completeness of financial data, including headcount information, and provide reporting on financial performance. Build effective working relationships across the Operations and Technology Finance organization. Serve as a point of contact for Engineering teams, supporting their day-to-day financial needs. Partner with operational teams to support purchase order management, reporting, and analysis. Maintain clear financial process documentation. Perform ad hoc financial analyses to support business decisions. Identify and implement opportunities to improve financial processes and reporting. Collaborate with cross-functional teams across the United States, Europe, and Asia. Basic Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, or a related field. At least two years of finance experience with progressively increasing responsibilities. Solid financial and analytical skills, including the ability to develop financial models, analyze large data sets, and communicate results clearly. Proven attention to detail and proficiency working with data in Excel, and other financial systems. Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel and PowerPoint, with experience using Copilot or similar productivity tools. Ability to collaborate and communicate effectively with colleagues across Finance, Operations, and other cross-functional teams. Exceptional organizational and time-management skills, with the ability to prioritize multiple assignments, meet tight deadlines, and make sound decisions under pressure. Ability to adapt and perform effectively in a fast-paced, results-oriented, and rapidly changing environment.

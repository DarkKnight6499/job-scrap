The Director, Portfolio Management is primarily responsible for managing an assigned portfolio of sponsor-backed middle-market leveraged loans and also plays a significant role in underwriting, structuring, executing, and syndicating new transactions. The Director maintains comprehensive oversight of assigned credits, including financial performance and trend analysis, covenant compliance, reporting requirements, risk ratings, and emerging risks. The role interfaces with Ally CF executive management, Ally Risk, referral sources, sponsor groups, bank syndicate members, management teams, and Ally CF support functions, including legal, loan administration, and operations. The Director provides day-to-day guidance, reviews work product, and supports the training, development, and mentoring of Analyst and Associate team members. The ideal candidate possesses strong credit skills, sound commercial judgment, and the ability to maintain detailed oversight of loan portfolio activity. The Work Itself Maintain comprehensive oversight of an assigned portfolio of commercial loans and proactively identify emerging risks. Complete credit reviews, risk-rating assessments, and ongoing portfolio monitoring, and present portfolio updates and risk assessments to senior management and credit committees. Manage portfolio administration, including financial-performance monitoring, covenant compliance, exception reporting, facility renewals and amendments, and coordination and management of the bank group. Monitor sponsor activity, industry dynamics, and macroeconomic developments impacting portfolio companies. Identify emerging risks and develop mitigation strategies. Review and negotiate credit documentation in consultation with legal counsel. Develop and maintain relationships with private equity sponsors, management teams, investment bankers, and lending partners to support portfolio oversight and identify future business opportunities. Review and analyze borrower operating performance, liquidity, leverage trends, covenant compliance, and cash flow generation. Evaluate amendment, waiver, acquisition, add-on investment, and refinancing requests. Lead portfolio management activities for criticized and challenged credits, including development of risk-mitigation strategies, amendment and restructuring negotiations, liquidity monitoring, and coordination with internal workout and risk teams. Participate in underwriting new transactions, including business due diligence, financial analysis, leverage and cash-flow assessment, enterprise valuation, sponsor evaluation, and transaction structuring. Evaluate transaction risks and recommend appropriate covenant, collateral, pricing, and structural protections. Prepare and present underwriting recommendations to credit committees and senior management. Support syndication and lender presentation activities. Assist with training and / or mentoring of Analyst / Associate team members. The Qualifications We’re Looking For 7+ years of relevant leveraged finance, underwriting, or portfolio management experience. Demonstrated experience managing middle-market leveraged loan portfolios. Experience managing borrower amendments, waivers, restructurings, and refinancing activities. Experience in banking, commercial lending, private credit, underwriting, financial analysis, credit analysis, auditing, or collateral management. Experience with borrower and sponsor relationship management. Completion of a formal credit training program preferred. Advanced understanding of leveraged lending structures, cash flow underwriting, enterprise valuation methodologies, debt capacity analysis, covenant structures, sponsor equity dynamics, and leveraged buyout transaction modeling. Demonstrated experience underwriting and/or managing private equity sponsor-backed middle-market companies. Experience presenting credit recommendations, portfolio reviews, and risk assessments to senior management and credit approval committees. Excellent communication (verbal and written), interpersonal and organizational skills. Highly analytical and detail-oriented, with strong problem-solving capabilities. Ability to work independently with demonstrated strong time management skills. Well-developed presentation and relationship-building skills. High level of accountability and the ability to effectively prioritize tasks. Technical: Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel, Word, and PowerPoint, including financial forecasting and modeling.

Company : Highmark Health Job Description : JOB SUMMARY The Finance Technology Product & Portfolio Lead is responsible for establishing, leading, and continuously evolving the centralized Finance Technology Demand and Portfolio Management function. This role serves as the primary bridge between Finance stakeholders and Technology teams, ensuring business initiatives are appropriately evaluated, prioritized, funded, governed, and delivered. The incumbent will own the end-to-end demand lifecycle from intake through value realization, enabling a transparent, data-driven approach to investment decisions, resource allocation, portfolio governance, and strategic execution. This role will also drive product strategy and roadmap development for assigned Finance technology capabilities while ensuring alignment to Finance transformation objectives, operational priorities, regulatory requirements, and enterprise strategy. ESSENTIAL RESPONSIBILITIES Develop and manage the overall Finance technology product roadmap and portfolio, ensuring alignment with strategic business objectives and priorities. Lead the demand management process for Finance technology, including intake, prioritization, and resource allocation for new initiatives and enhancements. Act as a primary liaison between Finance business stakeholders and technology teams, translating business needs into technical requirements and vice versa. Monitor portfolio performance, project progress, and budget adherence, implementing corrective actions as needed to ensure successful delivery. Drive continuous improvement in Finance technology processes, tools, and practices to enhance efficiency and effectiveness. Other duties as assigned or requested.

RTX Corporate is looking for a talented Global Trade Digital Tools Business Analyst to join our team! What You Will Do: The Global Trade Digital Tools Business Analyst plays a critical role in supporting the development, enhancement, and optimization of digital solutions that enable global trade compliance and operational efficiency. This position collaborates with global trade compliance teams across RTX and its Business Units to translate regulatory and business requirements into functional system capabilities. The analyst ensures digital tools meet compliance standards, deliver value, and align with enterprise digital strategy. Key responsibilities include: Partner with stakeholders to understand business processes, regulatory obligations, and operational pain points raised by BU Global Trade and Digital Technology partners. Translate business needs into detailed requirements, user stories, workflow diagrams, and acceptance criteria. Provide input into the design and configuration of global trade digital tools and integrations. Collaborate with technical leads, developers, and solution architects to enable delivery of system enhancements and new capabilities. Manage and prioritize feature backlogs, ensuring clear communication across business and technical teams. Support functional User Acceptance Testing (UAT) for features delivered by the Digital Technologies Team, ensuring all acceptance criteria are met. Assist with deployment planning, including rollout communication, training materials, and change management activities in partnership with BU Global Trade partners. Provide input for user-focused documentation, training content, and knowledge articles. Qualifications You Must Have: Typically requires a Bachelor’s degree in business, information systems, or a related field and minimum of 10 years of experience, or minimum of 7 years of experience with an advanced degree. Experience in global trade, compliance operations, or business analysis. Hands-on experience with digital tools or ERP modules related to trade compliance, logistics, or data management. Experience in translating business requirements into functional system capabilities. Qualifications We Prefer: Experience with internal and external trade compliance platforms (e.g., RGEMS, SAP GTS, OCR). Basic familiarity with global trade regulations such as authorization management, jurisdiction and classification, restricted party screening, and related topics. Experience working in agile environments or with product development teams.

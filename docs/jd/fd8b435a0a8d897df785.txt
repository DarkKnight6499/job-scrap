The Production Support Associate helps prepare and ship prescription orders in a fast‑paced pharmacy fulfillment center. This role is hands‑on and detail‑driven, supporting patient safety and accuracy while working with automated systems. It’s a great fit for candidates interested in pharmacy technician support, healthcare operations, or fulfillment work . Our Cheektowaga location is located just outside of Buffalo, New York. Pay Rate: $18/hr + $1 Shift Differential Shift: Sunday - Thurs (2pm - 10:30pm) What You Will Do Fill prescription vials with tablets and capsules using system instructions Pick and verify medications by name, strength, and quantity Replenish automated medication cells with bulk product Use handheld scanners to track inventory and orders Perform basic counting and calculations for prescriptions Pack and label orders for shipment Follow SOPs, HIPAA guidelines, and pharmacy safety standards Keep the work area clean, organized, and compliant Identify issues and escalate concerns when needed Basic Requirements Minimum Education & Typical Experience or Equivalent Combination: Typically requires 1+ years of related experience Basic math, reading, and writing skills Visual ability to read screens, labels, and scanners Preferred Skills / Experience Experience in pharmacy, healthcare, warehouse, or fulfillment roles Exposure to pharmacy technician or medication handling work Comfort using computers, scanners, or inventory systems Strong attention to detail and quality Ability to work well in a fast‑paced environment Work Environment On‑site, climate‑controlled pharmacy fulfillment center Fast‑moving, production‑driven setting Standing, walking, and repetitive motion throughout the shift No travel required We are proud to offer a competitive compensation package at McKesson as part of our Total Rewards.

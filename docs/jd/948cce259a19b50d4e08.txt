Job Purpose: Our dedicated Fund Opinions (FO) group works with financial sponsors across a range of transactions including Continuation Vehicles, cross-fund transactions and portfolio company mergers. The team reviews the client’s transaction, analyses the applicable investment(s) and delivers fairness opinions and related valuation advisory services to assist its client’s transaction approval process and investor communications. The group’s clients include leading private equity, credit, venture capital, infrastructure and hedge funds. The open position is for an Associate in the New York or Chicago office who will be dedicated to executing client engagements as well as growing Houlihan Lokey’s Fund Opinions practice. As an Associate, you will join a team that focuses on complex analytics, financial models and valuation across a wide range of industries and asset classes. The group frequently partners with other internal teams, including industry coverage groups and asset class specialists, to deliver fairness and valuation opinions as well as other advisory services to our clients.

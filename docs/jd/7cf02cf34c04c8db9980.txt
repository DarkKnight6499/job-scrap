As a Zurich Specialty Marine Underwriter , you’ll have the opportunity to combine your technical underwriting expertise, market-facing skills, and relationship-building strengths to help grow and manage a profitable Ocean Marine portfolio. You will analyze and underwrite new and renewal marine liability business while building strong partnerships with brokers, agents, and customers. Working with limited direction and within delegated authority levels, you’ll apply The Zurich Way of Underwriting Framework to assignments of moderate technical complexity. This role offers the opportunity to make sound underwriting decisions, identify new business opportunities, strengthen key distribution relationships, and deliver a high level of service to customers. If you enjoy balancing technical underwriting with relationship management and business development, this role provides an opportunity to make a meaningful impact in the Marine market. Some of the key accountabilities of this role include: Administer and monitor underwriting rules and guidelines, rating manual rules, and insurance laws and regulations. Develop, maintain, and collaborate with Line of Business representatives to support business-related needs, as appropriate. Develop and maintain strong relationships with distributors (agents & brokers) in support of business retention and acquisition. Partner with customers and distributors to understand their insurance-related needs and provide solutions. Support the organization’s sales culture by serving as a Help Point for agents, brokers, and customers. Proactively seek renewal and new account opportunities. Complete detailed opportunity assessments with key distributors to identify growth opportunities. Qualify accounts to align with the organization’s appetite while balancing sound business opportunities. Conduct broker/customer and on-site calls with a planned and well-prepared purpose. Cross-sell other lines of business to increase the organization’s product density within the account. This role will be filled at either the Executive Underwriter or AVP Underwriting Director level. The hiring manager will determine the appropriate level based upon the selected applicant’s experience and skill set relative to the qualifications listed for this position. Executive Underwriter Required Qualifications: High School Diploma or Equivalent and 5 or more years of experience in the Underwriting or Market Facing area. OR High School Diploma or Equivalent and 10 or more years of experience in the Claims or Underwriting Support area OR Zurich Certified Insurance Apprentice including an Associate Degree and 3 or more years of experience in the Underwriting or Market Facing area OR Zurich Certified Insurance Apprentice including an Associate Degree and 8 or more years of experience in the Claims or Underwriting Support are AND Knowledge of Microsoft Office Experience working on time restraints for quotes on new and renewal business Experience working in a team environment OR AVP, Underwriting Director Required Qualifications: High School Diploma or Equivalent and 7 or more years of experience in the Underwriting or Market Facing area OR High School Diploma or Equivalent and 14 or more years of experience in the Claims or Underwriting Support area OR Zurich Certified Insurance Apprentice including an Associate Degree and 5 or more years of experience in the Underwriting or Market Facing area OR Zurich Certified Insurance Apprentice including an Associate Degree and 12 or more years of experience in the Claims or Underwriting Support area Preferred Qualifications: Bachelor’s Degree Knowledge of Marine underwriting Strong negotiation skills Strong verbal and written communication skills Strong relationship-building, active listening, needs analysis, and win-win negotiation skills Demonstrated presentation skills with a strong solutions and service orientation Knowledge of underwriting philosophy, techniques, and national/local filing regulations and guidelines Your pay at Zurich is based on your role, location, skills, and experience.

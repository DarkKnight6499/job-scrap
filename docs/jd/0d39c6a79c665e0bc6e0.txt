Iron Mountain is seeking a vigilant Operator to join our Global Safety, Risk, and Security team through our SkillBridge Program. You must be an active duty member of the US military and be qualified to participate in the Department of War (DOW) SkillBridge Program. In this role, you will serve as a frontline guardian within our North America Security Operations Center (NA-SOC), directly overseeing threat monitoring and incident response across our regional facilities. Operating from our Boyers, PA location, you will leverage advanced security technology and clear communication to protect our people, property, and critical processes. Here, your sharp analytical skills and swift decision-making will actively maintain the safe, resilient environment our teams and clients rely on every day. What You’ll Do (Responsibilities) In this role, you will: Monitor security systems: Continuously oversee CCTV, access control, alarms, and open-source intelligence platforms to detect potential threats and maintain real-time situational awareness. Manage incident response: Evaluate incoming security alarms, dispatch personnel or emergency services according to protocol, and de-escalate emerging situations. Analyze threats: Assess physical vulnerabilities, evaluate potential operational impacts, and recommend clear mitigation strategies to safeguard company assets. Coordinate communication: Liaise effectively between internal stakeholders and external emergency services during both daily operations and critical events. Maintain system integrity: Log all incident reports with precision and verify that all NA-SOC security equipment and monitoring software remain fully operational. What You’ll Bring (Skills & Qualifications) The ideal candidate will have: You must be an active duty member of the US military and be qualified to participate in the Department of War (DOW) SkillBridge Program. 2 years of experience in the private security sector, security operations center (SOC), or law enforcement. Strong knowledge of physical security applications, including CCTV, intrusion detection, and access control systems (such as ProWatch, Milestone, Avigilon, or Crisis24). Associate degree in Criminal Justice, Business Administration, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience). Military Occupational Specialty (MOS) preferred in military police, global embassy security, or related security fields. U.S. Citizenship, a valid state-issued driver's license, and the ability to obtain government security clearances as required.

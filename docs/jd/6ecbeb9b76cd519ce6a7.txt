Wells Fargo is seeking a Business Relationship Support Associate within Global Payments & Liquidity (GPL). This role supports the WellsOne Commercial Card business and serves as an operational partner to internal teams managing a large portfolio of commercial card clients. Team members are responsible for researching and resolving email-based servicing requests, validating customer authorizations, navigating established procedures, and ensuring timely, accurate resolution of client inquiries. This is a primarily internal-facing role that requires strong commercial card knowledge, attention to detail, sound judgment, and the ability to effectively manage multiple priorities in a high-volume environment. In this role, you will: Manage and support a portfolio of commercial card accounts and servicing requests Research and resolve customer inquiries in accordance with established policies, procedures, and controls Review account-related requests, validate required documentation, and ensure compliance with authorization requirements Prioritize and manage a queue of requests while meeting service level expectations Identify process improvement opportunities and support operational efficiency initiatives Partner with internal stakeholders to resolve issues and meet client needs Develop expertise in WellsOne Commercial Card products, servicing processes, and compliance requirements Exercise independent judgment when researching issues and recommending solutions Required Qualifications 6+ months of Business Relationship Support experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education Desired Qualifications Commercial Card servicing experience, Treasury Management Dedicated Client Services experience, or related payment product support experience Experience with TSYS, CCAT, ServiceView, WellsOne Expense Manager or other commercial card servicing platforms Strong understanding of commercial card products, account maintenance, servicing processes, and customer authorization requirements Experience researching and resolving complex servicing inquiries while managing multiple requests and service level commitments Experience supporting customers through email-based servicing or case management environments Intermediate Microsoft Office skills, particularly Outlook, Excel, and PowerPoint Job Expectations Schedule: Monday - Friday, hybrid work schedule. Expectation of working hours/ scheduling needs may vary between teams, but generally need to be available to work during core business hour (7 am to 6 pm MT) Ability to travel up to 10% of the time Specific compliance policies may apply regarding outside activities and/or personal investing; affected employees will be expected to provide information to the Wells Fargo Personal Account Dealing Team and abide by applicable policy requirements if hired.

As the Associate Document Management Technician, you’ll have the chance to thoroughly review incoming provider, payor, and patient correspondence to determine the appropriate document naming conventions, routing, and electronic filing within OnBase and EPIC. Within this role, the DMT handles all incoming correspondence as well as a variety of general and specific administrative duties, including mail service tasks, filing, printing, scanning, and end-user requests. All processes will adhere to state and federal regulations and HIPAA guidelines. What You’ll Work On • Sort, deliver and prepare/process correspondence received electronically and via mail • Review and act on indexed correspondence exceptions (all ingress methods) • Participate in document collection to satisfy time sensitive payor audits. • Provide any supporting documentation through the Release of information module with EPIC to payors and government representatives following HIPAA regulations. Required Qualifications • High School Diploma or General Education Degree (GED). • Experience with Electronic Health Record (EHR) operating systems and work involving electronic records. • Proficient in computer systems, keyboarding skills and Microsoft Office Suite or related software. • Knowledge of medical terminology and/or health insurance terms. Preferred Qualifications • 2 years Revenue Cycle billing or Health Information Management (HIM) experience. • Experience with OnBase, Epic or other EHR application.

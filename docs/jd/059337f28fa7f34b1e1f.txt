Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Cybersecurity & Privacy Management Level Senior Manager Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Security Risk & Engineering - Enterprise & Cloud Security - Senior Manager you will lead client-facing cloud security work across IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS environments, helping shape security design, implementation, risk assessment, vulnerability testing, and ongoing threat monitoring within our cybersecurity practice. As a Senior Manager you will bring advanced judgment, influence, and technical depth to guide complex client conversations, direct teams through ambiguity, and develop clear approaches that connect security priorities to business outcomes. In this role at PwC, you will draw on your cloud security knowledge, experience with Cloud Service Providers, and ability to communicate actionable recommendations to support secure transformation and help strengthen the security posture of client environments. Responsibilities - Leading enterprise and cloud security engagements that design, implement, and elevate protections across IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS environments - Advising clients on cloud security architecture, control design, and risk reduction approaches across multiple cloud service providers - Directing risk assessments, vulnerability testing, and security reviews to identify gaps across cloud-based systems and identity environments - Translating technical findings into clear recommendations that align security requirements with business and technology priorities - Coordinating security engineering activities for network, endpoint, and cloud infrastructure safeguards - Monitoring emerging threats, control weaknesses, and cloud security trends to inform remediation strategies and future-state designs - Guiding teams through complex security challenges while coaching team members to strengthen delivery quality and technical capability - Applying sound judgment to prioritize issues, escalate critical risks, and support informed client decisions across large initiatives What You Must Have -

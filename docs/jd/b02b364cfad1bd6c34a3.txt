Global Capital Risk Analyst
Guardian Life
Location: New York
Link: https://guardianlife.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/Guardian-Life-Careers/job/New-York/Global-Capital-Risk-Analyst_R000110112

Is the opportunity to join a culture where “We Do the Right Thing,” and “We Courageously Shape Our Future Together” important to you? If so, Guardian is seeking a motivated individual to join our team as a Global Capital Risk Analyst. Reporting to the Head of Operations, Service, Claims & Transformation Finance, this role plays a central position within Group Benefits Finance, supporting financial analytics, performance insights, and strategic decision support across all operational areas. This role will help shape Guardian’s enterprise capital planning across its U.S. and Bermuda-domiciled entities. This role offers a rare opportunity to work at the intersection of capital strategy, regulatory frameworks, investment decisions, and business planning, with exposure to senior cross-functional partners and analysis that directly informs capital forecasting and enterprise decision-making. You will partner closely with Investment Management, Corporate Actuarial, Asset/Liability Management, Enterprise Risk Management, and Enterprise/Line of Business Financial Planning and Analysis, giving the analyst a broad view into how critical financial, investment, and strategic business considerations tie together. This position reports to the Head of Global Capital Risk Management who, in turn, reports to the Head of Market Risk. You will Manage a continuous forecast of regulatory capital (RBC and BSCR), while driving standardization and synergies across RBC and BSCR models to modernize and strengthen enterprise-wide capital planning. Analyze and monitor actual financial results to identify implications to Guardian’s capital position and build a deeper understanding of key risks and emerging opportunities across the enterprise. Develop a thorough understanding of Guardian’s asset allocation strategy and how investment decisions influence capital outcomes. Proactively drives opportunities to support capital optimization across the balance sheet, gaining exposure to high-impact initiatives that shape Guardian’s financial strength. Support data and technology initiatives around building capital-related databases and interactive capital dashboards that enhance transparency, analysis, and decision support for senior leadership. You are A self-motivated professional who is energized by ownership, continuous learning, and the opportunity to shape important work independently. Adept at translating complex topics into clear, concise messages for senior stakeholders and cross-functional partners. Able to use data to tell a compelling story, frame thoughtful recommendations, and drive practical solutions. Eager to build trusted relationships across levels and functions, with an interest in learning how different areas of the business connect to capital planning and strategy. Comfortable operating in a fast-paced environment, balancing multiple deliverables. Organized with a strong attention to detail. You have 5+ years of experience in the life insurance industry; familiarity with Bermuda’s capital framework highly desirable. Degree in finance, accounting, economics, or other quantitative discipline preferred. FSA, CPA, or CFA charterholder/candidate preferred. Skilled in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint; Claude and Python a plus. Proven communication and relationship building skills. Location: Hybrid role - 3 days in a Guardian office located in Hudson Yards, NYC. 2 days WFH.

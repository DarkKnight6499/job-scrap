Position Purpose: The Sr. Asset Protection Operations Analyst leads enterprise projects and programs that mitigate operational shrink, eliminate systemic process defects, and strengthen Asset Protection execution. As the first-level project owner for major shrink initiatives, this role drives program design, rollout, and sustainability, and is accountable for delivering measurable results. The Sr. Analyst partners with Asset Protection field leadership, Store Operations, AP Analytics, Finance, and Inventory Accounting to resolve complex operational shrink concerns and escalations. Key responsibilities include end-to-end project leadership; root cause analysis of operational shrink drivers; managing escalation pathways for shrink concerns submitted by the field; leveraging AI and automation to streamline workflows and surface insights; enhancing existing shrink programs and reporting; driving process improvement; and leading enablement for field partners. The Sr. Analyst is expected to influence without authority and represent Asset Protection in cross-functional forums. Prior store operations experience, a strong understanding of how shrink occurs in stores, demonstrated project management capability, and the ability to communicate effectively with all levels of store, field, and SSC leadership are highly preferred. Key Responsibilities: Perform data gathering tasks and analytics - identify trends, develop solutions, project/strategy planning and data utilization Produce documents for teams and leadership to review and utilize. Deliver presentations to teams and executive leaders and sponsors. Synthesize findings and make recommendations to upper management.

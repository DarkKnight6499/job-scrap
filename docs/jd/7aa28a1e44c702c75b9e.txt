Are you looking to join an organization that is growing and dynamic? What about a high-energy, collaborative environment that rewards hard work? J.S. Held is a global consulting firm that combines technical, scientific, financial, and strategic expertise to advise clients seeking to realize value and mitigate risk. Our professionals serve as trusted advisors to organizations facing high stakes matters demanding urgent attention, staunch integrity, proven experience, clear-cut analysis, and an understanding of both tangible and intangible assets. The firm provides a comprehensive suite of services, products, and data that enable clients to navigate complex, contentious, and often catastrophic situations. We are actively seeking a Associate Director to join the Construction Advisory & Disputes Team. We are seeking applicants that have a specialization in construction claims and dispute resolution. This position is for a professional who has knowledge or experience with contract and/or change order management, critical path scheduling analysis, preparation of change orders or claims, cost analysis and quantification of damages, review of drawings and specifications, and other engineering and construction-related documents. The Candidate will primarily be involved in construction dispute resolution, claims preparation and analysis, delay/ disruption analysis, and providing programming support on live projects. Associate Director candidates will primarily be the lead assistant to the Delay Expert(s). This individual will provide forensic delay analysis and contribute to the preparation of the expert report. An individual is required who can show initiative, perform advanced data research, manage client relations and deliver to tight deadlines. This role will involve frequent client interaction and will require the successful candidate to prepare and make presentations to communicate results. It is preferred that the individual has previous experience in providing consultancy services on substantial and/or complex disputes in delay-related matters. They should be able to demonstrate experience in writing reports to a high level and have strong industry experience in large construction and engineering projects. We anticipate that the successful candidate will have a good academic record and be able to demonstrate how they have distinguished themselves within their chosen field. They will be expected to work collaboratively with the team and deliver commissions to the client’s satisfaction. Assisting in promoting the construction advisory services in the industry. Required Qualifications Associate Director candidates must have more than 10+ years of experience in forensic delay analysis in the construction and engineering industry. A recognized undergraduate degree in construction management, engineering quantity surveying, or similar from a leading university. Proficient in programming software (i.e., Primavera 6 and Microsoft Project). Chartered professional status, or equivalent. Minimum 5 years on-site construction experience with engineering or construction firms, or a construction claim/dispute firm. Excellent oral and verbal communications skills. Ability to work in multidisciplinary teams. Professionalism and client facing ability, able to build trust. A strong technical underpinning in project planning. Relationship building and strong interpersonal skills. A Flexible approach to their work. An understanding of the demands of project-based work, able to work under pressure and an ability to meet deadlines. A sense of personal responsibility for the quality of their work. Prefered Qualifications A post graduate qualification in construction law (or similar) is desirable but not essential. Chartered professional status, or equivalent. Fluent in English. Command of a second language is desirable. Physical & Mental Qualifications Prolonged periods sitting at a desk and working on a computer. Must be able to travel for various assignments.

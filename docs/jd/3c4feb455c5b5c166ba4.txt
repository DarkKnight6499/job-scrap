Company: Marsh Description: We are seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Business Support Associate to join our North America Broking Executive team, part of our reinsurance business at Marsh. This role will be based in New York, NY. The is a hybrid role that requires at least four days a week in the office. This role will provide day-to-day administrative support to senior executives across presentation development, reporting, coordination, calendar management, travel planning, and expense management. This is an excellent opportunity for a recent graduate or early-career professional to gain meaningful exposure to the business, build professional skills, and contribute to important team priorities. Unlike a traditional administrative role, this position is closely embedded in the executives’ work and supports a range of business activities, including PowerPoint and Excel development, tracker maintenance, meeting preparation, and coordination across stakeholders. We will count on you to: Build, edit, and format PowerPoint decks and other client-facing and internal materials Support reporting, trackers, and analysis in Excel Coordinate inputs across the team and keep workstreams organized and on track Prepare background materials, meeting notes, presentation content, and ad hoc research as needed Assist with follow-up actions, deadlines, and deliverables to ensure strong execution Manage complex executive calendars, including scheduling meetings and resolving conflicts Coordinate domestic and international travel, executive expenses, office activities, and meeting logistics Support client events, conference coordination, and other business-related tasks as priorities evolve What you need to have: Recent graduate with a bachelor’s degree in business administration or a related field 0–2 years of experience in an office, professional, or internship environment Strong foundational skills in PowerPoint and Excel, with the ability to learn quickly and apply new tools and processes Highly organized with strong attention to detail, follow-through, and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a changing environment Professional communication skills, a collaborative mindset, and strong service orientation What makes you stand out: Interest in business operations, team coordination, or analyst-leaning support work Ability to learn new tools and processes quickly, with a proactive and resourceful approach Polished, professional communication and the ability to work with minimal supervision once trained Why join our team: We help you be your best through professional development opportunities, interesting work and supportive leaders.

Job Title: VP, Senior Credit Analyst Job Description: Summary: Responsible for underwriting all commercial loan transactions secured by real estate, accounts receivable, inventory or fixed assets and unsecured loan transactions at all levels of complexity. Underwriting will include financial analysis with a concentration on cash flow analysis, collateral evaluation and an overall review of the request that results in a solid recommendation. Other responsibilities include reviewing financial information on a periodic basis and analyzing borrowing base certificates along with accounts receivable and inventory reports. Essential Duties and Responsibilities: Underwrite commercial loan transactions, which includes CRE, C&I, leveraged finance, and tax credits Clearly communicate with internal and external customers Work with others to produce needed outcomes and meet deadlines Complete follow-up analysis on financial statements and borrowing base history Maintain up to date knowledge of financial industry, economy, market conditions, and competition Ensure credit related follow-up items (primarily financial statements, Borrowing Base Certificates and Covenant compliance certificates) are reflected on the follow-up systems Provide support to lending officers in structuring and analyzing potential transactions Partner with lending units to maintain oversight of future loan maturities and current pipeline to ensure deadlines are met and maintain a high level of customer service Be an active participant in weekly loan committee meetings as a representative of the Credit Department Assist the Credit Manager in providing coaching and mentoring to junior credit associates At times, accompany Relationship Managers on customer calls and participate in client meetings Qualifications: Ability to underwrite a transaction in a clear and concise manner Ability to analyze financial statements in terms of cash flow, debt service, projection analysis and trend analysis Highly developed analytical skills Excellent written and verbal communication skills Must possess strong decision-making skills Detail oriented with the ability to analyze cash flow, leverage and other financial metrics Ability to think critically, solve problems, make decisions, and build trust across the organization Ability to manage multiple complex tasks simultaneously and prioritize appropriately Present professional image Demonstrated ability to maintain a high degree of focus on customer service Supervisory Responsibilities: None Education and/or Experience: Bachelor’s degree in finance, business, or a related field A minimum of 10 years’ experience in commercial credit involving complex transactions Experience in asset based lending required both in underwriting and monitoring Computer and Software Skills: Knowledge of Microsoft Word, Excel and Power Point Bankway Salesforce Baker Hill-Statement Analyzer Buker’s Tax Analysis Gmail Certificates, Licenses and Registrations: None Equal Opportunity

Be part of a team where your expertise in finance and technology drives accurate valuations for millions of securities daily. This role will play a crucial role in providing accurate and insightful valuations, which are essential for our clients' investment decisions and risk management strategies. This includes market data collection and analysis, price generation, quality control, and client interaction. Continuously improve product and service quality through daily market surveillance, communication with market makers, perform data and financial analysis, enhancing process controls, and ensuring data quality. Collaborate with trading desks, market participants, and research teams to enhance market knowledge and understanding of market pricing. Address all client concerns daily with an emphasis on providing first-class service and quality, whether via emails, calls, or in-person meetings. Perform in-depth analysis on an individual security or portfolio of securities and explain findings to clients. Develop and maintain financial modeling tools by utilizing advanced quantitative techniques such as Python. Explore new areas of product growth with a focus on autonomously developing innovative ideas and solutions. Required Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills: Bachelor's degree or higher in Financial Engineering, Mathematics, Computer Science, Finance, Economics or similar. Firm understanding of the principles of finance, business operations, derivative products, option pricing and statistical modeling. Strong software development skills in Python and Excel VBA with high aptitude for learning new technologies. Ability to collect and analyze large amounts of market information, extract its essence, and incorporate the findings into valuation models and processes. Clear, logical thinker with strong quantitative abilities. Ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment of real-time market pressures, remaining focused on client needs. Excellent communication skills to interact with clients, portfolio managers, traders, research, and sales.

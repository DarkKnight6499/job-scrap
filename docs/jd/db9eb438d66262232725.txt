Job Description Ares Management is seeking a highly motivated and detail-oriented Senior Associate to join the Credit Financial Planning & Analysis team. The Credit Financial Planning & Analysis team currently consists of five members in Boston, New York, Los Angeles and Mumbai and works in a very team-oriented environment as a part of the firm’s larger Finance organization. The team is responsible for various projection models, fund forecasting and other corporate finance functions, and works closely with numerous groups across the organization, including Credit investment professionals and senior management as well as investor relations, fund accounting, business operations, portfolio management and technology teams. The ideal candidate would have a fundamental understanding of alternative investing and/or private credit and would demonstrate an aptitude in financial modeling and forecasting solutions. The candidate would also partner closely with our technology team to support automation, AI integration and data analytics initiatives and would display capability and passion for automation and technological data. The role requires a dynamic, adaptive, experienced hands-on professional to ensure best practices in a fast-paced, rapidly changing environment. Primary functions and essential responsibilities The Senior Associate will support a VP and the broader FP&A team in partnering across our enterprise to provide accurate projections for senior management. Responsibilities include: Building and maintaining fund projection models for public and non-private regulated funds such as Business Development Companies and Interval Funds Preparing and assisting with various scenario and/or sensitivity analyses and ad hoc business requests before presentation of this data to senior management to assist in corporate strategy Managing various automation initiatives of forecast projection models with a focus on Anaplan or similar enterprise modeling software while incorporating best practices around AI and data technology Supporting the implementation of new systems and processes, including data collection, testing, analysis, presentation and supporting the roll out Coordinating with other teams such as fund accounting, investor relations, Credit investment professionals, portfolio management and/or business operations to gather and update key inputs for fund models on a timely basis Organizing presentations and other data requests for lenders, investors and rating agencies while assisting with fund capital formation and balance sheet management Assisting in reporting to internal stakeholders by creating presentations or data-driven dashboards that provide thoughtful analysis and identify required action items The Senior Associate will also provide a fresh mindset and an ability to lead, manage, motivate, and inspire the FP&A team to effectively embrace and drive objectives, visions, and goals.

Key responsibilities Are you a strategic leader with a deep understanding of investment banking and a passion for driving business performance? UBS is seeking an Investment Banking Coverage & Advisory Management Analyst or Associate Director to help lead and optimize our Coverage & Advisory platform. In this role, you will work closely with senior bankers and product partners to shape business strategy, enhance execution across coverage teams, and drive operational excellence. You will serve as a key partner to senior leadership, helping to manage and scale the business while maintaining a strong focus on client delivery and revenue growth. Your responsibilities: - Partner with senior Coverage & Advisory leadership to define and execute strategic priorities across industry teams - Parttake in business management activities, including pipeline monitoring, revenue tracking, budgeting, and resource allocation - Drive coordination across coverage and product partners (M&A, ECM, DCM, LevFin) to ensure efficient transaction execution - Support and influence origination efforts by identifying trends, client opportunities, and sector-level insights - Lead key initiatives to improve processes, enhance productivity, and optimize team performance - Manage stakeholder relationships across the Investment Bank, including Legal, Compliance, Risk, and Finance - Provide leadership and oversight to junior team members, supporting talent development and workflow management - Contribute to client materials and high-priority transactions as needed, ensuring quality and alignment with UBS standards - Support regulatory and governance requirements associated with the Coverage & Advisory business Your Career Comeback We are open to applications from career returners. The team You’ll be part of UBS’s Investment Bank within the Coverage & Advisory organization, working alongside senior bankers across sectors and regions.

Job Description: The Global Client Group (GCG) is Dimensional’s distribution arm, comprising sales channels that target a diverse client set. By putting the client first, we strive to transform the way the world invests, and our solutions are a testament to the value provided to our clients. GCG is responsible for developing new business relationships as well as managing current relationships with our global clients. Our Broker-Dealer Advisor channel is responsible for working with wirehouse and full-service broker dealer financial advisors across the country. This Senior Associate role will support Regional Directors in business development and client services activities for our Broker Dealer clients across the east coast.

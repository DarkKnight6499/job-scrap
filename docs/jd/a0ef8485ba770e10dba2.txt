The Global Technology (GT) organization at JPMorganChase is a world-class technology team of 65,000+ technologists across 20+ technology hubs worldwide with $20B+ in annual technology investment. GT develops high-quality technology products and solutions for JPMC’s millions of clients and customers, and 320,000+ employees—everything from employee and infrastructure platforms to big data and mobile solutions, innovations in agentic / generative AI, electronic payments, cybersecurity, machine learning, cloud development, and more. The Global Technology Strategy (GTS) team is an internal management consulting team that works closely with GT Leadership—the Firm’s CIOs, CISO, CTO, and other senior technology leaders—to drive technology strategy, enable innovation, and solve the biggest technology problems across the firm. We work on several types of strategy projects, including problem sizing/solutioning, process reimagination, operating model and organizational design, firmwide transformation, and more, to help JPMC define how technology should be utilized to enable the firm’s overall business strategy, including in areas such as AI/agentic, data strategy, technology infrastructure, line of business technology, product development lifecycle etc. As an Associate on the Global Technology Strategy team, you play a crucial role in defining and deconstructing technology problems for the organization. When someone joins the Global Technology Strategy team, they accelerate their career by working on high-impact and complex problems, learning and applying industry-leading technology practices, and becoming part of a highly collaborative team that prioritizes professional development and ownership.

The Role The Asset Management function within Blue Owl’s Alternative Credit platform is responsible for the ongoing monitoring, analysis, and oversight of portfolio investments. The team plays a critical role throughout the lifecycle of an investment, from post-close surveillance to active engagement in restructurings, workouts, and other complex situations. Through close collaboration with investment teams, borrowers, and external partners, the Asset Management team helps protect capital, drive performance, and support strategic decision-making across the platform. Responsibilities: Asset Management Assist senior members of Asset Management team with overall monitoring, surveillance, and management of existing portfolio positions. Review performance of financial asset transactions versus covenants and analyze trends within a transaction. Understand loan mechanics to perform ongoing borrowing base reviews, collateral verifications, financial analysis and liquidity assessments of corporate level risk for each investment. Design and maintain borrowing base and servicer report templates through the translation of legal documentation for lending as well as borrowing (leverage facilities) transactions. Participate in borrower update calls and provide support during negotiations with borrowers for modifications and other requests from management teams. Produce quarterly cash flow models for individual transactions. Ensure timely receipt of reporting from borrowers and management teams. Fulfill recurring reporting requirements for leverage facilities – borrowing bases, servicer reports, financials, covenants, and other transaction specific deliverables. Participate in monthly Asset Management meetings with senior Blue Owl management Assist with documentation of amendments and other legal matters. Engage in field exam related activities - examiner engagement, scope design, report review, and issue remediation. Workout Support Support senior members of Blue Owl’s Asset Management team on workouts, restructurings, liquidations, and other special situations. Participate in watchlist meetings and provide updates to senior Blue Owl staff regarding developments on problematic credits. Assist senior staff with complex litigation involving current or exited Blue Owl positions.

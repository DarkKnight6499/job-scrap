A role in Financial Planning & Analysis means belonging to a great team of genuine, diverse, courageous and versatile people who put integrity and ethics at the heart of everything they do. Together, you’ll drive outstanding financial results by enabling and accelerating Target’s strategic execution. You’ll nurture influential partnerships with leaders who value your credibility, thanks to the quality and reliability of your insights. To teams across Target, you’ll bring your skills for analytical rigor, creative problem-solving and sound decision-making. You’ll be an active contributor to cross-functional teams. As a Sr. Manager Financial Planning & Analysis, Stores Finance , you’ll oversee the end-to-end financial performance for a region of 500+ stores and will be responsible for the ongoing support to the Regional Senior Vice President and multiple Vice President level business partners, helping to guide stores through an evolving and dynamic retail environment. You will learn how Target’s strategies, from merchandising to digital fulfillment and everything in between, come to life through our stores and leverage your operational knowledge, financial acumen, and internal cross-functional relationships to help deliver on financial goals while ensuring our stores are supported to fuel the business and their teams. Key responsibilities include: Create and own monthly sales forecasts and hourly payroll plans for the region, ensuring operational, financial and team objectives are strategically balanced. Build strong relationships with senior stores leaders to understand and assess operational wins and opportunities and share and drive key financial outcomes. Identify areas for productivity and process improvement through analysis and modeling, leading with your point of view and bringing to life through partnerships with stores leaders and cross functional partners. Support designing, testing and implementing optimal regional strategies that can be scaled chain-wide Function as a stakeholder on cross-functional project teams to deliver key strategic initiatives. Form strong partnerships within the Stores Finance team, Labor Engineering, Store Operations, and HR. Core responsibilities of this job are described within this job description.

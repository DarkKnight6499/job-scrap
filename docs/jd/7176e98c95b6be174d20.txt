Salary Range $150,100.00 - $247,700.00 Target Openings 1 What Is the Opportunity? Construction provides a full suite of insurance solutions suited to construction contractors of all sizes, including General Liability, Property, Marine, Automobile, Workers' Compensation, Umbrella, and more. As an Underwriting Officer, Construction, you are an underwriting subject matter expert and integral member of a field leadership team. By leveraging your expertise to educate underwriters, lead Collaborative Underwriting Review Evaluations (CUREs), and drive the development and successful execution of underwriting strategies, you will influence the overall profitability, growth, and retention of business for an assigned region. What Will You Do? As an underwriting subject matter expert, assess, design, implement, and/or lead regional underwriting strategies and practices in support of profit and growth objectives. Oversee and influence underwriting execution through individual risk and/or portfolio CUREs as well as through the evaluation of underwriter proficiency and underwriting quality. Drive the development of key underwriting capabilities and best practices through coaching and trainings while educating underwriters on strategy and guidelines to enhance regional efficiency and effectiveness. Monitor trends, emerging issues, and underwriting results for a regional book of business and partner with underwriting leadership to take action, as needed. Perform other duties as assigned. What Will Our Ideal Candidate Have? Bachelor’s degree. Eight or more years of commercial underwriting experience with experience in guaranteed cost and loss sensitive construction risk. Expert level knowledge of risk appetite and risk selection, construction risk products, as well as the regulatory environment and local insurance marketplace. Strong critical thinking skills with the ability to proactively identify underwriting and financial challenges and analyze available information to make timely decisions in alignment with risk appetite. Strong interpersonal and communication skills with the ability to lead and influence others. What is a Must Have? Six years of commercial underwriting experience. What Is in It for You?

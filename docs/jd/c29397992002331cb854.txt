Senior Business Analyst
Popular
Location: San Juan, PR
Link: https://jobs.popular.com/job/San-Juan-Senior-Business-Analyst/1446419300/

General Description Provides analytics enablement and customer insights that support data-driven Retail and Commercial segment strategies. The role owns and enhances trusted data foundations, standardized metrics, dashboards, and reusable analytical assets; translates business priorities into frameworks for opportunity sizing, customer prioritization, financial impact, and performance measurement; and partners across functions to deliver scalable, actionable insights. This position advances customer-centric growth, personalization, primacy, acquisition, deposit growth, and relationship-deepening initiatives while ensuring data quality, consistency, and business continuity. Essential Duties and Responsibilities • Lead analytics enablement for Retail and Commercial initiatives, including customer-centric growth, personalization, primacy, trigger-based acquisition, deposit growth, and segment activation. • Own the generation, maintenance, enhancement, quality, and documentation of foundational datasets, field definitions, standardized metrics, segmentation logic, model inputs, and reusable analytical layers that support scalable execution and business continuity. • Resolve data-integrity issues and automate or improve workflows to increase accuracy, efficiency, usability, and alignment with evolving business needs. • Develop and enhance segment dashboards and analytical frameworks for opportunity sizing, prioritization, profitability, revenue and deposit growth, ROI, engagement, and initiative performance, delivering timely and actionable insights. • Analyze customer behavior, product relationships, transaction patterns, channel usage, triggers, and unmet needs to identify acquisition, retention, cross-sell, and relationship-deepening opportunities and strengthen engagement and propensity models. • Collaborate with Finance, Product, Segment, Technology, Data, Salesforce, and business teams to validate assumptions, align methodologies, modernize self-service data products, and conduct all work ethically and in compliance with laws, regulations, corporate policies, required training, and financial-crime controls. Education: Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Statistics, Economics, Business Administration, Finance, Data Analytics, or related field. Advanced degree preferred. Experience Minimum of 5 years of relevant experience in analytics, customer insights, financial analysis, business intelligence, or data-driven strategy, preferably in banking or financial services. Experience must include complex datasets, dashboards, metric development, business cases, financial impact measurement, executive-ready insights, and cross-functional decision support. Region Locations San Juan, Puerto Rico Additional Requirements Advanced analytics knowledge, including segmentation, customer profiling, propensity modeling, trigger-based analytics, trend analysis, performance measurement, customer engagement, and prioritization modeling. Strong understanding of Retail and Commercial banking products, financial drivers, profitability, deposits, lending, customer lifetime value, relationship depth, primacy, and cross-sell opportunities. Ability to define methodologies and build business cases, including revenue uplift, deposit growth, cost-benefit analysis, ROI, profitability impact, and initiative-level measurement. Proficiency with SAS, SQL, Excel, Power BI, Snowflake, Python, Microsoft 365, CRM/Salesforce concepts, dashboards, reporting, and internal data environments. Ability to develop trusted data assets, standardized metrics, documentation, executive-ready insights, reusable analytical layers, and self-service data products. Strong communication, stakeholder management, consultative problem-solving, independent prioritization, and ability to create new analytical solutions in ambiguous environments. Values 1.

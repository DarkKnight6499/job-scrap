As a Manager, you will lead client engagement workstreams, oversee high-performing teams, and independently solve complex business and operational challenges to deliver high-quality outcomes. You will be responsible for managing client relationships, mentoring and coaching team members, and identifying opportunities to improve processes and drive innovation. This role requires collaboration across business, finance, and technology stakeholders to help clients enhance performance management capabilities and enable more strategic, data-driven finance organizations.

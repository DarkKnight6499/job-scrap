Job Description For over 130 years, we have pioneered groundbreaking science. Today, we are driven by continuous innovation to develop breakthrough medicines, vaccines and technology. Rooted in direct experience on the farm and in the clinic, we work hand in hand with our customers every step of the way. Our ability to excel depends on the integrity, knowledge, imagination, skill, diversity, and teamwork of our people. We strive to create an environment of mutual respect, encouragement, and collaboration. As part of our global team, colleagues in this role work cross-functionally with talented and dedicated partners while developing and expanding their skills and careers. This role ensures timely, compliant handling of technical service inquiries, product complaints, and adverse events for the companion animal portfolio. Responsibilities include triaging incoming inquiries, managing technician level complaint processing end-to-end, supporting pharmacovigilance veterinarians, and serving as a liaison for product quality investigations and customer follow-up. The role supports accurate case documentation, quality assurance coordination, and clear communication with veterinarians, pet owners, sales representatives, and distributors. Technical Support, Triage, and Complaint Handling (some combination of the below) Serve as a first point of contact via phone, email, fax, written correspondence, and web inquiries (Technical Services 800-line). Triage technical service inquiries, determining when to escalate to veterinarians and handling those that do not require veterinarian attention. Address questions related to product usage and competitor product comparisons. Processing and documenting complaints (may include but not limited to, call handling, data entry and/or releasing). Identify trends and initiate product testing procedures; track testing through Global Quality and follow up with customers. Retrieve, process, and follow up on after-hours product complaints. Support PV veterinarians with: Customer correspondence and follow-up. Uploading correspondence/medical records into the complaint database. Reviewing medical records for accuracy and forwarding to PV veterinarians. Reviewing compensation requests and processing reimbursements. Keeping the complaint database updated in real time. Quality Assurance for Product Complaints: Serve as primary contact for product quality issues, testing procedures, and warranty questions. Initiate product sample returns from the field; ensure timely receipt, coordinate with Global Quality, and manage destruction when appropriate. Upload QA testing results into the complaint database and close the loop with complainants . Cross-Functional and Commercial Support: Share relevant information and trends in the veterinary marketplace with internal stakeholders. Support special projects (e.g., diagnostic testing programs). Assist with sales training and provide administrative/training support at meetings or national sales meetings. Provide pharmacovigilance data and reports upon request. Support the Equine PV team as needed by triaging incoming calls; addressing customer concerns and questions; handling product quality complaints (PQCs), product replacements, and reimbursement requests; and performing case entry when required. Required Education and Experience (one of the following): BA/BS with at least 1 year of experience in the veterinary field; or Associate’s degree (biological sciences preferred) with 3 years of veterinary-related experience; or RVT/CVT/LVT with 2 years of related experience; or 5 years of pharmacovigilance and/or animal science and/or companion animal customer care experience. Core Requirements: Minimum 1 year of pharmacovigilance experience and/or experience in animal science/companion animal customer care. Strong computer skills: Word, Excel, Outlook; comfort with databases and data entry accuracy. Excellent verbal and written communication skills; able to interact effectively with internal stakeholders and external customers. Nice-to-Have Qualifications: Experience with product knowledge management, database fundamentals, and internal/external compliance regulations. Knowledge of parasiticide products in the animal health industry. Prior call center or customer service experience. Skills and Competencies: Customer focus: Empathetic, responsive, and clear in communications with veterinarians, pet owners, and partners. Detail orientation: Accurate, timely case entry and meticulous document handling. Compliance mindset: Adheres to timelines and regulatory requirements for adverse events and quality complaints. Collaboration: Partners effectively with PV veterinarians, Quality, Sales, and other internal teams. Time management: Manages multiple cases and follow-ups within established timelines. Data literacy: Proficient with complaint databases; able to upload and manage records with accuracy. Professionalism: Maintains confidentiality and demonstrates diplomacy in sensitive situations.

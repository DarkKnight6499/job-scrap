Our nimble, decentralized culture can provide you with a wealth of opportunities to learn about our business and quickly gain experience to accelerate your career. ***This is an Onsite working arrangement at our Oakland, CA office.*** About the role: Colliers is seeking a Financial Analyst to provide dedicated financial and analytical support to our Institutional Industrial Advisors team, working closely with Nick Mascheroni and supporting industrial investment sales assignments and business development efforts. The position will be based in Oakland and will play an integral role in underwriting, valuation, market research, and transaction execution. The ideal candidate combines strong financial and analytical skills with a solid understanding of commercial real estate. This role will provide exposure to all stages of the investment sales process, from initial valuation and pitch preparation through marketing, due diligence, and closing. In this role, you will: Develop financial models and underwriting analyses for industrial properties and portfolios using Microsoft Excel and Argus Enterprise. Review and analyze rent rolls, leases, operating statements, budgets, and property-level financial information to identify key assumptions, risks, and opportunities. Perform valuation analysis and research industrial leasing and investment sales comparables, tenant activity, vacancy, absorption, and new supply. Prepare financial analysis and supporting materials for broker opinions of value, pitches, offering memoranda, and investment sales assignments. Analyze property characteristics and market factors affecting tenant demand, investment positioning, and value. Assist with due diligence and other transaction-related analysis throughout the investment sales process. Support transaction execution, including property tours, buyer inquiries, offer tracking, due diligence materials, and closing requirements. Maintain property, investor, and transaction data used for marketing, business development, valuation, and client presentations. Work closely with senior brokers to evaluate pricing, offers, buyer feedback, and market conditions. Participate in team discussions regarding active assignments, prospective business, transaction status, and market activity. What you bring: Bachelor’s degree in Real Estate, Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a related discipline, or an equivalent combination of education and experience. 2–6 years of experience in commercial real estate underwriting, financial analysis, investment sales, or a related field; industrial real estate experience preferred. Advanced proficiency with Microsoft Excel and experience with Argus Enterprise. Strong understanding of commercial real estate valuation, lease structures, cash flow analysis, and investment return metrics. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with exceptional attention to detail. Ability to manage multiple assignments and deadlines in a fast-paced, transaction-oriented environment. Strong written, verbal, and interpersonal communication skills. Ability to work independently while collaborating closely with brokers and other team members.

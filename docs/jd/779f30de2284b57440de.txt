The Associate Director, MRO Site Leader provides overall leadership for service management and overhaul operations at Landing Systems’ Miami Gardens facility in Florida. The site provides landing gear repair and overhaul services supporting commercial and military aircraft platforms. This leader will have responsibility for a complex manufacturing organization of approximately 200 employees, including a significant direct-labor workforce, and will establish the strategy and operating plans necessary to achieve customer, operational, financial, and organizational objectives. The Site Leader will drive performance across EH&S, quality, delivery, cost, inventory, productivity, people, and continuous improvement, while leading the organization through significant growth and operational transformation. The successful candidate will be a highly visible operations leader who can translate business strategy into execution, develop organizational capability, and build the operating discipline necessary to deliver sustainable performance.

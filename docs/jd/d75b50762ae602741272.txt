Job Location MASON BUS AND INNOVATION CTR Job Description Research and Development (R&D) at Procter & Gamble, the largest consumer packaged goods company in the world, includes a diverse group of roles that contribute to the innovation and development of our products. It encompasses roles in product research, formulation, testing, and scientific analysis. You will find variety and excitement starting Day 1. Associate Researcher is an entry level role for individuals who are competent in basic science and math and are inspired to apply those skills towards hands-on experiments. At P&G, a wide variety of R&D work is available to Associate Researchers, in the following major areas: -Products Research: Deeply understand our consumers and their needs to inform product design parameters for new or improved products. -Product Formulation & Design: Translates consumer needs into finished products that delight consumers using the best available technologies/chemicals/materials. -Analytical: Use lab equipment to solve tough technical problems via chemical, physical, mechanical, imaging, and other measurement capabilities. -Packaging & Device Development: Creates and improves packages and devices that delight consumers and retailers. -Process Development & Pilot Plant Operations: Translates ideas, concepts, and technologies into commercially feasible chemical and mechanical processes and scales them up from lab to pilot plant to manufacturing. The Opportunity: Are you a passionate and detail-oriented scientist with a strong aptitude for hands-on laboratory work and a desire to make a tangible difference in consumer health? We are seeking a highly motivated Associate Researcher to join our Oral Care Measurement Science Innovation team. In this pivotal role, you will be instrumental in demonstrating the pre-clinical and consumer-relevant performance benefits of our innovative products and technologies. You'll work in in-vitro testing laboratory, bringing scientific rigor and technical mastery to every project. This isn't just a lab-based role; it's a chance to be at the forefront of oral care science, directly impacting product commercialization and driving global business growth. If you thrive in a collaborative atmosphere, enjoy solving complex technical challenges, and are dedicated to precision and excellence, we want to hear from you! Key Responsibilities & Impact Areas: As an Associate Researcher, your contributions will directly support our mission to deliver superior oral care solutions. Your primary responsibilities and impact will include: Execute in-vitro models maintaining precision, robustness and integrity Proactively identify and resolve equipment and methodological issues, Deliver timely and insightful technical reports Cultivate strong partnerships with project teams Act as the dedicated owner for several laboratory instruments. Job Qualifications Required Qualifications: Education Requirement: Associate degree in science (chemistry, biology, biotechnology) or related field with three years or more of practical hands on laboratory experience or a High School diploma with five or more years of practical hands on laboratory experience. Candidates 4-year degrees in non-engineering disciplines are welcome to apply. Proficiency in using Microsoft Office Suite. Available to work full time Monday-Friday at Mason Business Center.

Work Schedule Standard (Mon-Fri) Environmental Conditions Adherence to all Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) Safety Standards Job Description Join Us as a QC Specialist - Make an Impact at the Forefront of Innovation This position will be responsible for the overall strategy and authoring and review/adjudication of validation and transfer protocols and reports. This role will partner closely with Quality Assurance (QA), Analytical Development, and various QC functions to support clinical and commercial biologics QC readiness. The successful candidate will preferably have strong experience in GMP documentation, biologics analytical methods, and method validation and transfer. What You'll Do: • Lead the validation, qualification, verification, and lifecycle management of analytical methods supporting biologic drug substance and drug product testing. • Serve as the technical lead for analytical method transfers to internal laboratories, contract testing laboratories (CTLs), and regulatory affiliates, ensuring successful demonstration of method equivalency and readiness for routine testing. • Author and review method transfer plans, protocols, gap assessments, risk assessments, statistical evaluations, and final transfer reports. • Provide technical expertise, training, troubleshooting, and hands-on support during method validation and transfer activities to ensure successful implementation at receiving laboratories. • Collaborate with Analytical Development, Quality Assurance, Regulatory Affairs, and external partners to support product development, commercialization, and post-approval lifecycle activities. • Ensure analytical methods, specifications, reagents, standards, and testing procedures are compliant with applicable GMP, health authority, and regional regulatory requirements. • Investigate method transfer failures, atypical results, deviations, OOS/OOT events, and analytical discrepancies, implementing effective corrective and preventive actions as needed. Education & Experience Requirements: • Bachelor's degree or equivalent in Biochemistry, Biology, Biotechnology, Microbiology, Analytical Chemistry, Pharmaceutical Sciences, or a related scientific discipline. • Previous experience that provides the knowledge, skills, and abilities to perform the job (comparable to 5+ years’) or equivalent combination of education, training, & experience. In some cases an equivalency, consisting of a combination of appropriate education, training and/or directly related experience, will be considered sufficient for an individual to meet the requirements of the role. Qualifications: • Demonstrated experience leading analytical method validation, qualification, verification, and transfer activities for biologic drug substance and drug product programs. • Experience authoring and reviewing validation and transfer protocols, reports, technical assessments, and GMP documentation. • Experience supporting global testing networks, including contract testing laboratories (CTLs), CDMOs, and in-country testing laboratories. Preferred Qualifications: • Strong understanding of cGMP, ICH, USP, EP, JP, and applicable global regulatory requirements governing analytical testing and method validation. • Hands-on experience with biologics analytical techniques such as HPLC/UPLC, CE-SDS, icIEF, ELISA, and cell-based potency assays. • Strong knowledge of analytical method validation principles, statistical analysis, comparability assessments, and method transfer strategies. Working Conditions and Environment: • Work is performed in an office environment with exposure to electrical office equipment. • Occasional drives to site locations with occasional travel both domestic and international. • Long, varied hours required occasionally • Interaction with clients/associates on rare occasions • Rotating shifts required on rare occasions where applicable • Exposure to toxic materials on rare occasions.

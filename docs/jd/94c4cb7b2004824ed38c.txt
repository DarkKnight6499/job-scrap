Enterprise and Operational Risk Management is part of Corporate Risk Management and helps strengthen enterprise and operational risk practices through independent oversight, consultation, and effective challenge. The Operational Risk Liaison Team provides risk intelligence that supports informed decisions and a strong risk environment. As the primary Operational Risk Liaison for the Finance and Legal enterprises and Capital Stress Testing activities, you will provide enterprise-wide risk oversight, consultation, and effective challenge across first- and second-line teams that contribute to CST, working closely with Financial Risk Management, Model Risk Oversight, and the Treasury Capital Stress Testing team.. You will apply Risk Management, Risk Assessment, Analytical Thinking, and Decision Making to identify exposures, assess the CST control environment, and drive meaningful outcomes. Success requires strong attention to detail, Stakeholder Management and Communication skills to influence without direct ownership, present well-supported assessments to leadership, and build alignment across Finance, Treasury, Financial Risk Management, Model Risk Oversight, and other partners. Your Collaboration and Adaptability will help the organization navigate evolving risks, competing priorities, and seasonal demands while strengthening risk-based decision-making. Provide independent oversight, consultation, and effective challenge of business-led initiatives, including the evaluation of new instruments used in Finance ensuring operational risk considerations are appropriately identified, assessed, and addressed throughout implementation. Provide effective challenge to CST process execution, governance documentation, controls, and cross-functional handoffs to identify and address operational risk exposures. Assess CST-related issues, remediation activities, and emerging risk themes across Finance, Treasury, Financial Risk Management, Model Risk Oversight, and other contributing business teams. Provide support and mentorship to business partners to ensure adherence to the Operational Risk framework elements and policy requirements. Analyze operational risk data to support risk identification, assessment, monitoring, and reporting activities.

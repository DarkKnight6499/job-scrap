Regular or Temporary: Regular Language Fluency: English (Required) Work Shift: 1st shift (United States of America) Please review the following job description: Implements and optimizes technology operations and systems‑level solutions while contributing to the planning, governance, and improvement of operational processes. Utilizes technical and analytical expertise to maintain production stability, ensure asset accuracy, evaluate operational risks, and improve processes that support business operations. Works independently to analyze issues and recommend solutions while collaborating with cross‑functional teams and/or vendors. Will be onsite (5 days per week) at either of the posted locations. Job is not available for sponsorship. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Following is a summary of the essential functions for this job. Other duties may be performed, both major and minor, which are not mentioned below. Specific activities may change from time to time. 1. Executes processes to supports the delivery, stability, and continuous improvement of technology services by monitoring operational performance, identifying issues, and recommending enhancements to processes, tools, and workflows. 2. Demonstrates initiative by analyzing data, reports, and system outputs to assess the health, effectiveness, and lifecycle status of assigned technology components, escalating issues when necessary. 3. Conduct root-cause analysis, evaluate workflow dependencies, and suggest enhancements to reduce risk, improve efficiency, or strengthen the reliability of technology services. 4. Identifies recurring operational issues and performs root-cause analysis, develops solution recommendations, and proposes design‑level improvements to tools, runbooks, and workflows. 5. Evaluates operational trends, risks, and performance data and provides recommendations that influence business operations, resource planning, and technology strategy. 6. Continuously identifies opportunities for automation, tooling improvements, and process refinement that drive scalability, reduce manual effort, strengthen compliance, and enhance overall service reliability. 7. Assesses lifecycle status, utilization patterns, and operational impacts of assigned technology assets or services, recommending optimization strategies, control improvements, or adjustments to ensure accurate management and cost‑effective operations. 8. Supports interactions with vendors, partners, and adjacent teams, validating changes, and confirming that proposed solutions align with established operational and architectural expectations for the assigned area. 9. Follows and helps refine incident management, disaster recovery, and business continuity procedures for the assigned domain, participating in technical investigation and resolution activities and capturing learnings for future improvements.

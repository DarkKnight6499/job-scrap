Use your analytical skills to help shape the responsible adoption and governance of artificial intelligence at a leading global financial services firm. Manage projects with a strong project management mindset by clarifying ownership, stakeholders, milestones, dependencies, and deliverables. Identify, propose and implement improvements to further automate reporting, governance processes and tools. Extract and wrangle data from various sources using Excel, Alteryx, and/or SQL. Stay current on the latest AI advancements, emerging governance practices, and integration technologies.

The Financial Analyst will be a member of the Drivetrain and Morse Systems accounting and finance team. This position will work as a business partner to the Plant Controller with responsibilities for key finance dept roles with the goal to learn plant operations, build relationships, collaborate with internal customers, and operate an efficient and effective location. The position has visibility to local management and has a high impact to plant operations. This position is based at the Livonia, Michigan plant. Financial Analyst Key Accountabilities · Manage the Livonia accounts payable function including invoice matching, entry into SAP, vendor setup, management and ad hoc issue resolution. · Manage the Livonia billing function including occasional billing, RMA issuance, and weekly shipping document reconciliation. · Perform banking functions such as daily cash ledger and positive pay review, and monthly bank reconciliations · Communicate and schedule regular reviews of key plant spending performance vs. forecast.

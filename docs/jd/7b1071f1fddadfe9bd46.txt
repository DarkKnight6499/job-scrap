We are looking for an Analyst or Associate to join the Corporate Derivatives Group in New York, a team that brings together corporate finance, derivatives and global markets to solve complex risk-management challenges for our clients. Sitting within Global Capital Markets (GCM), the team advises corporate clients on managing the interest rate, foreign exchange and other market risks that arise from capital raising, M&A and other strategic activity. We work closely with colleagues across Investment Banking and Sales & Trading to develop and execute tailored hedging and derivative solutions. The role provides an unusual opportunity to combine real-time financial markets exposure with strategic corporate advisory work . Team members engage across the full lifecycle of a transaction, from understanding a client's underlying exposure and evaluating alternative strategies, through structuring, pricing and execution. What you'll do Advise corporate clients on interest rate, foreign exchange and other market risks Analyze exposures and develop tailored hedging and derivatives strategies Structure, price and execute transactions in partnership with Sales & Trading Work alongside Investment Banking and capital markets teams on financings, M&A and other strategic transactions Prepare and deliver clear, thoughtful analysis for client discussions Develop a deep understanding of the interaction between financial markets, corporate finance and risk management What we're looking for Bachelor’s degree 2–4 years of relevant experience in investment banking, capital markets, derivatives, markets or a related field Strong analytical and quantitative capabilities Intellectual curiosity and a genuine interest in financial markets Strong communication skills and the ability to explain complex ideas simply Sound judgment, attention to detail and the ability to perform in a fast-moving environment MBA, CA and/or CFA designation is beneficial but not required Relevant securities licenses, or willingness and ability to obtain them WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT FROM MORGAN STANLEY: At Morgan Stanley, we raise, manage and allocate capital for our clients – helping them reach their goals.

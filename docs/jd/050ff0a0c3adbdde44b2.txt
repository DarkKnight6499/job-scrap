The Opportunity This is not a remote opportunity Must be able to travel up towards 5% The Associate Customs Trade Compliance Analyst supports Abbott's import, export, and Foreign Trade Zone (FTZ) compliance programs by ensuring adherence to U.S. Customs regulations, FDA requirements, export controls, and corporate policies. The role analyzes trade and operational data, conducts compliance reviews and audits, maintains classification and country-of-origin records, and supports customs inquiries and corrective actions. Reviewing import, export, and FTZ transactions for regulatory compliance. Conducting audits, identifying compliance risks, and recommending corrective actions. Maintaining tariff classifications, country-of-origin determinations, valuation data, and trade records. Supporting customs activities such as RFIs, post-entry amendments, and regulatory inquiries. Partnering with logistics, supply chain, procurement, operations, and freight forwarders to ensure compliant trade activities. Preparing reports, analyzing data, and driving process improvements and cost-saving opportunities. Escalating compliance issues and supporting implementation of corrective actions. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills. Advanced Excel proficiency. Ability to determine HTS classifications and assess Section 232 tariff applicability. Ability to review commercial invoices, packing lists, and country-of-origin documentation for compliance and reject non-compliant supplier documentation when necessary. Experience with compliance reviews, auditing, data analysis, and working across cross-functional teams. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree or an equivalent combination of education and work experience Intermediate Microsoft Office Skills with proficiency in Excel Ability to work across teams and time zones possessing organization and planning skills to simultaneously handle a variety of complex matters under the pressures of a deadline Strong financial skills and understanding of how to read and use government regulations Proficient with statistics and analysis Preferred Qualification Related experience in trade compliance, supply chain, logistics, finance, or related field.

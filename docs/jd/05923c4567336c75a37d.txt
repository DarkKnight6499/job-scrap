Technical Business Analyst The Opportunity: You know that the most impactful public health solutions are made at the intersection of mission and technology, and as an IT business analyst, you’re highly skilled in both. Here, you’ll have the chance to work with engineering teams as they develop digital products to support your clients’ most pressing missions. We’re looking for someone like you to translate mission needs into AI use cases, sof tware requirements, and user-friendly client experience s. As an IT business analyst, you’ll develop leading-edge applications. Partnering with your multi-disciplinary technical team, you’ll identify clients’ business needs, design user journeys, define scenarios and edge cases, and document requirements. You’ll understand the overall project direction and nuanced user needs clearly, and you’ll lead your team as they fulfill these needs by creating deployable features. You will also develop project artifacts for a variety of audiences, from end-users to executive leaders. Together, you’ll deliver impact to our public health customers. Join us. The world can’t wait.

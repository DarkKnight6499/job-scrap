In this dynamic role, you will be a Senior Associate in the manufacturing organization at Amgen North Carolina (ANC). The initial schedule will be Monday-Friday, 8-hour days, and may include work from home. The schedule will transition to a 12-hour day or night shift as business needs dictate. With general direction, the Senior Associate is responsible for executing on-the-floor operations within manufacturing in accordance with cGMP practices.

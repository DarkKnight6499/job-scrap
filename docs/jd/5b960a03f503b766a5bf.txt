Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Transfer Pricing Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Transfer Pricing Senior Associate you will support clients on transfer pricing matters, helping them develop and maintain pricing approaches for intercompany transactions while navigating tax rules and managing compliance-driven work. As a Senior Associate you will build client connections, guide junior team members, and handle more complex workstreams within our Tax Services practice, drawing on critical thinking, data interpretation, and a growing understanding of business context. In this role at PwC, you will contribute to transfer pricing analyses, prepare client-ready deliverables, and help translate technical findings into practical recommendations that support client service engagements. Responsibilities - Analyzing transfer pricing arrangements and applying tax regulations to support compliance across intercompany transactions - Preparing transfer pricing documentation, economic analyses, and technical memoranda for client engagements - Interpreting financial data, tax filings, and business context to develop pricing recommendations for related party dealings - Supporting client service teams in planning and executing transfer pricing projects across multiple jurisdictions - Reviewing functional interviews, industry information, and legal entity structures to inform pricing methods and benchmarking selections - Developing financial models and conducting comparable company searches to support arm's length analyses - Tracking evolving tax rules, guidance, and dispute trends to inform client deliverables and issue spot potential exposure - Mentoring junior team members on transfer pricing work products, review feedback, and method selection What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 2 years of experience - Before starting full-time at PwC, meet the educational requirements to be eligible to sit for the CPA exam in your intended state of employment (Member of the Bar or other tax, technology, or finance-specific credentials may qualify for this opportunity). What Sets You Apart - At least one of the following: Certified Public Accountant, Member of the Bar or other tax, technology, or finance-specific credentials may qualify for this opportunity - Demonstrating transfer pricing knowledge across client engagements - Interpreting economic data and financial modeling findings - Applying tax documentation and tax research skills - Navigating complex client situations with critical thinking - Building client relationships while mentoring junior team members Travel Requirements Up to 20% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $77,000 - $214,000.

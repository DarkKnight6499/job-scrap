The Technology, Product and Data Compensation team is seeking a Compensation Associate. This position supports compensation strategy and execution across Firmwide functions through market analysis, compensation advisory, regulatory support and project delivery. As a Compensation Associate within the Technology, Product and Data Compensation team, you will work closely with HR Business Advisors and Compensation colleagues to provide analytical insights, support compensation decision-making and contribute to the successful delivery of key compensation initiatives. This role offers exposure to a wide range of compensation activities and requires strong analytical capabilities, stakeholder management skills and attention to detail. Job Responsibilities: Support compensation activities across the employee lifecycle, including new hire offers, promotions, transfers, retention reviews, forecasting and compensation budgeting. Conduct quantitative and qualitative analyses to identify trends, evaluate compensation-related challenges and support data-driven decision-making. Prepare compensation models, presentations and recommendations for HR and business stakeholders. Partner with HR Business Advisors to provide guidance on compensation practices, policies and programs. Support the review and enhancement of compensation policies, processes and governance frameworks. Monitor external market trends and compensation benchmarks to support competitive and effective compensation programs. Support annual compensation planning and year-end compensation processes. Contribute to compensation-related projects, including process improvements, policy updates, reporting enhancements and technology initiatives. Identify opportunities to increase operational efficiency through process simplification, reporting improvements and the use of emerging technologies. Required Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills: 3 years of experience in Human Resources, Compensation, Finance, Consulting or a related analytical discipline. Strong analytical, quantitative and data management skills. Advanced Microsoft Excel skills and proficiency in Microsoft Office applications. Strong verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to explain complex concepts clearly to diverse audiences. Ability to build effective partnerships and manage stakeholders across multiple levels of the organization. Strong organizational and project management skills, with the ability to manage competing priorities and deadlines. Experience preparing business presentations, reports and recommendations for senior stakeholders. High level of accuracy, attention to detail and commitment to confidentiality and controls. Ability to work independently in a fast-paced and dynamic environment. Preferred Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills: Previous experience in compensation, reward, workforce planning or broader Human Resources functions. Experience working with compensation surveys, market data and benchmarking methodologies. Familiarity with compensation-related regulatory requirements and governance frameworks. Experience supporting annual compensation planning or year-end compensation processes. Experience using Business Objects, Tableau, Alteryx, Power BI or similar reporting and analytics tools. Financial services industry experience is beneficial but not required. To be eligible for this role, you must be authorized to work in the United States.

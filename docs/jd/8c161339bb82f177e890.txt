New York Life Ventures is hiring a Senior Associate to join the Investments team. As a key member of the investment team, the successful candidate will make an immediate impact by employing a data and network driven approach to identifying investment opportunities in areas of thematic relevance to New York Life. The candidate will own and drive workstreams across the end-to-end investment process (evaluation, research, due diligence, transaction execution, portfolio management and operations) across both direct and fund investments. Importantly, and given New York Life Ventures’ strategic investment mandate, the successful candidate will actively qualify and evaluate opportunities on both their investment and strategic merits while contributing to the refinement and evolution of our investment themes.

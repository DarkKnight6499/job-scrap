Job Description: Job Title Change Enablement Partner, TDI Regulatory, Risk & Control (RRC) Corporate Title Assistant Vice President Location New York, NY Overview As a Change Enablement Partner, you will deliver day-to-day internal and executive communications and support ongoing staff engagement for Deutsche Bank’s Technology, Data and Innovation Regulatory, Risk and Control subdivision. Working under the oversight of the senior Change Enablement Lead, you will help execute the integrated Change and Communications Roadmap globally for the Risk subdivision. You will align colleagues with central messaging, manage divisional digital channels, and encourage global participation in Technology, Data and Innovation signature initiatives. You will also support the broader Americas communications team with regional signature events when required. What We Offer You A diverse and inclusive environment that embraces change, innovation, and collaboration A hybrid working model, allowing for in-office / work from home flexibility, generous wellbeing benefits, retirement savings plans, parental leave, and family building benefits Educational resources, matching gift and volunteer programs What You’ll Do Adapt and deliver the integrated Change and Communications Roadmap for the Regulatory, Risk and Control subdivision, aligning annual priorities with central Technology, Data and Innovation messaging and people-engagement objective Develop and deliver a subdivision communications strategy with Regulatory, Risk and Control leadership, and provide targeted communications advice to senior leaders supporting global organizational goals Prepare announcements, briefings, town halls, and staff-engagement materials for senior leadership, while maintaining quality control for wider leadership communications Drive global participation in regional and Technology, Data and Innovation-wide signature events and town halls, helping colleagues stay informed and connected Maintain the Regulatory, Risk and Control SharePoint presence, moderate Viva Engage channels, coordinate external LinkedIn content with Group Communications, and apply the Technology, Data and Innovation visual identity across communications Coordinate agendas and technical hosting for global town halls and executive roundtables, and provide hands-on support for Americas signature events, including Bank on Tech and Hackathon activities, when required Skills You’ll Need Proven experience in corporate communications, event facilitation, or change management, preferably supporting Risk or Technology functions within a global financial institution Proficiency with Microsoft Office, SharePoint, and Viva Engage, with experience managing digital content channels and communications pipelines Proven ability to provide end-to-end support

Position Summary Position Overview Guggenheim Investments (“GI” or “Firm”) is seeking a Senior Associate to join its Policies, Procedures, and Controls team. The team, which is part of the Office of the President, is the core driver of the Firm’s governance framework for policies, procedures and related documentation, with direct responsibility for developing, managing, and enhancing the policies and procedures that support GI’s global investment management and operational activities. This Senior Associate will work in a fast-paced, growing environment and will collaborate closely with team members, subject matter experts and senior stakeholders across the Firm, including Investment Management, Portfolio Management, Operations, Client Services, Marketing, Compliance, Legal, and Risk Management. The role requires being highly proactive, execution-focused, and able to multitask and adhere to deadlines, and will support how the Firm documents, governs, and communicates its processes and controls. The position is based in our New York City office full-time. Responsibilities Key Responsibilities: Assist in developing, drafting, and updating policies, procedures, charters, process flows, checklists, and related documentation to accurately reflect Firm practices, products, processes, and regulatory requirements. Support end-to-end lifecycle for assigned documentation, including working with team members and subject matter experts to understand processes and controls, drafting and refining content, helping to navigate internal governance and approvals, and coordinating formal issuance in line with Firm standards. Proactively identify opportunities to enhance team efficiencies. Maintain centralized inventories and repositories to ensure documentation is current, organized, and easily retrievable. Work on various projects and initiatives, by helping to define and track milestones and deliverables, and ensuring timely completion in a high-demand environment. Help to address ad hoc projects and initiatives related to policies, procedures, governance, and process documentation in response to evolving business needs, regulatory/Internal Audit expectations, and strategic priorities. Prepare and contribute to materials and reports for senior management and governance forums. Develop and maintain high-quality PowerPoint, Word, Excel, and Visio materials, ensuring a professional, consistent, and accurate presentation aligned with Firm standards. Monitor Internal Audits, including reviewing/challenging Internal Audit findings, and assisting with drafting remediation Action Plans, where applicable. Develop, enhance, and maintain team administrative documents (e.g., trackers, templates) Perform other duties and responsibilities, as assigned, including, but not limited to, matters relating to policies, procedures, charters, controls, governance, and associated processes and documentation. Qualifications Basic Qualifications: Minimum of three to five years of experience in a financial services organization or equivalent experience at a top consulting firm or top law firm. Demonstrated ability to work effectively on multiple projects and initiatives simultaneously, under tight deadlines and in a high-pressure environment, while maintaining high quality and accuracy. High level of attention to detail and a strong focus on accuracy. Preferred Qualifications An advanced degree (such as JD, MBA, or a relevant graduate degree) is desirable. Collaborative working style, and an ability to build and maintain relationships with stakeholders across functions, including investment, operations, control, and support teams.

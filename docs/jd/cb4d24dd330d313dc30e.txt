Position Summary The Credit Risk Analytics Manager is responsible for developing, maintaining, and enhancing the Bank’s credit risk models. This role partners with Lending, Technology, and Finance teams to deliver custom-tailored credit risk and projection models. The position plays a key role in credit risk modeling and governance, risk analytics, and data management initiatives. Essential Duties and Responsibilities Develop, implement, and maintain credit risk models using tools such as Python, SQL, Excel, and Moody's Analytics solutions (including RiskCalc, CMM, PortfolioStudio and related Moody's platforms). Maintain comprehensive model governance documentation in accordance with SR 26-02 model risk management guidance, including model development documentation, methodology papers, conceptual soundness assessments, implementation testing, performance monitoring reports, user procedures, and inventory documentation. Prepare analytical support and documentation for independent model validation, internal audit reviews, regulatory examinations, and management oversight committees. Respond to validation findings, tracking issues to resolution and ensuring corrective actions are appropriately documented and implemented. Ensure all model inputs, calculations, assumptions, and reporting outputs are supported by transparent, reproducible, and auditable processes. Promote strong model governance practices that facilitate effective challenge, independent review, and examiner readiness. Prepares periodic reports and/or dashboards utilizing PowerBI to monitor model performance, anomalies, inconsistencies, or unexpected results. Performs other duties and responsibilities as assigned.

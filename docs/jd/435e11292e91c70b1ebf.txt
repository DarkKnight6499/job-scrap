Key responsibilities Do you thrive on intellectual challenges and take pride in building robust, well-designed quantitative solutions? Are you interested in applying advanced quantitative methods to complex financial products for a firm with a truly global footprint? We're looking for an outstanding Quantitative Analyst who can: Research, develop and enhance the valuation and risk management models and tools supporting Cross Asset Financing businesses, including Structured Financing and Fund Financing. Apply quantitative techniques to model complex fund structures in bespoke Fund Financing facilities. Apply advanced statistical techniques to develop prepayment and default models for securitized products. Partner with other Quantitative Analytics (QA) teams to develop automated and scalable cross-asset solutions used by Trading, Structuring, and Sales. Collaborate closely with Trading, Structuring, Risk, and Control functions to perform statistical analysis, develop valuation and risk methodologies, and support business decision-making. Your Career Comeback We are open to applications from career returners. Find out more about our program on ubs.com/careercomeback . The team The Credit Quantitative Analytics (QA) team is the Front Office group responsible for developing quantitative models, methodologies, and technology that support pricing and risk management for Credit businesses across the firm, including Credit Solutions, Flow Credit, Cross Asset Financing, Global Banking. The team’s models and infrastructure are used for quoting clients, risk managing trading positions, marking the firm’s books and records. The team works closely with Trading, Structuring, IT and control functions, as well as other QA teams such as Equities, FX, Rates and the central infrastructure team. Your skills and experience Strong academic background, preferably at the Master’s or PhD level, in a quantitative discipline such as Mathematics, Physics, Computational Finance, Engineering, or a related field. 5+ years of relevant work experience Proficiency in one of the object-oriented programming languages such as C , C#, Java or Python, with the ability to develop robust and scalable quantitative solutions. A solid foundation in quantitative finance and derivatives pricing, with knowledge of modern numerical and/or machine learning techniques. Knowledge of securitized products, fixed income analytics, and complex fund structures is a plus. Ability to identify and drive opportunities to apply AI and emerging technologies responsibly to enhance business outcomes and operational efficiency. Strong stakeholder management and influencing skills with the ability to communicate complex ideas in a fluent and articulate manner. You’re curious to explore how AI can improve how we build, deliver and optimize workflows. You do this with sound judgment – validating outputs and aligning with policies, risk standard and ethical use.

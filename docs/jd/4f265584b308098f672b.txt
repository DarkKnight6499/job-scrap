Barclays is seeking an Investment Banking Equity Capital Markets Healthcare Analyst. In this role, you will support the origination and execution of equity capital market transactions, with a focus on Investment Grade clients across the Healthcare sector. This position offers the opportunity to apply ample corporate finance and analytical skills, combined with a solid understanding of global markets, to help clients raise capital effectively. As an Analyst, you will be part of a client‑facing team, where good communication skills are essential. The group is focused on originating new transactions and helping companies optimize their capital structures. You will work closely with Investment Banking Coverage and teams across Global Finance to deliver a full suite of financing and risk‑management solutions. To be successful as an Investment Banking Equity Capital Markets Healthcare Analyst, you should have: A background in Investment Banking, ideally with direct experience in Equity Capital Markets, and a considerable understanding of equity markets, interest rates, macroeconomics, and corporate finance. Quantitative and assessing skills, with the ability to interpret market and corporate data to develop insightful client solutions. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to convey complex information clearly and effectively. Relevant licensing, including SIE, Series 79, and Series 63. Highly organized, with ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously, work well contending priorities, and meet tight deadlines. Some other highly valued skills may include: Robust industry knowledge and experience that complements product and market expertise. Exposure to additional Global Finance products, including derivatives, equity‑linked financing solutions, and other complementary instruments. A collaborative leadership mindset, with the ability to guide while fostering a good teamwork culture. You may be assessed on the key critical skills relevant for success in this role, such as risk and controls, change and transformation, business acumen, strategic thinking, digital and technology, as well as job-specific technical skills. This role is located in New York, NY. This role is regulated by FINRA.

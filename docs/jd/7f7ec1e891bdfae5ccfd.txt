New York Life Investment Management (NYLIM) is seeking to add an Investment Analyst to its Global Fixed Income Group - High Yield Corporate Credit Team. Current HY/LL assets under management in the Group’s portfolios is approximately $25 billion and growing. Portfolio strategies include total return and CLO portfolios investing in HY/LL corporate debt across institutional and retail funds. What You’ll Do: Perform fundamental credit research, investment analysis, and ongoing portfolio surveillance for specific industry sectors in the high yield and leveraged loan credit markets. Present investment recommendations to the high yield investment committee for approval. Partner with portfolio managers and traders to assess relative value, portfolio fit, and recommended position sizing for purchase and sale decisions. Meet with issuer management teams to identify opportunities and conduct due diligence. Develop and maintain relationships with research analysts at peers, broker-dealers and the major rating agencies. Prepare industry, issuer and portfolio review reports for management as needed. Actively monitor holdings in covered names/industries to help drive strong alpha-generating returns, providing timely recommendations with regard to portfolio positioning. What You’ll Bring: 5-10 years of relevant industry experience with a strong background in credit research with a thorough understanding of leveraged finance. BA required; MBA or CFA is a plus. Meaningful investment experience with high yield bonds and leveraged loans, preferably in both total return and CLO strategies. Well-developed quantitative, analytical, and problem-solving skills. Knowledge and experience working with AI. Excellent written & oral communication skills. Strong team player with the ability to manage and prioritize numerous tasks.

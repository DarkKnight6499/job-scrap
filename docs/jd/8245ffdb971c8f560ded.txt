Location: 4900 Tiedeman Road, Brooklyn Ohio Job Summary: The Treasury Operations Department processes transactions and creates reports related to investment portfolio, collateral management, funding (including liabilities), and safekeeping functions. Treasury Ops is primarily responsible for supporting various Lines of Business at Key and its affiliates. Portfolio functions include fixed income securities (i.e. bonds) trade settlement and related incoming principal and income processing. Funding functions include Key debt issuances, principal and interest payments, and monitoring of overall cash position at the FED. Collateral functions include pledging of securities for the benefit of various Key contracts, products and public deposits. The Treasury Ops team uses a third-party software database system to help accomplish the above daily tasks. The department regularly prepares managerial, financial and regulatory reports. The Senior Analyst works in partnership with leadership and possesses functional expertise to drive and analyze operational support and processes. The role also resolves highly complex problems, re-allocates resources when necessary, eases workloads during periods of peak volume, and prepares department regularly managerial, and financial reports. Works with Department Manager on developing and implementing strategic direction. Facilitate training and guidance to operations staff and assists in addressing human resource functions as needed. Continuous improvement champion, responsible for refining and improving operations processes and procedures. Critical involvement in any special projects or tasks.

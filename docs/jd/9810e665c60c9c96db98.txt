Job Description Summary The Associate-Production role is expected to be in individual capable of reading electrical and mechanical drawings and translating that into a finished product with limited supervision to the highest level of quality. Job Description Essential Responsibilities Assemble components onto panels and into enclosures following mechanical layout and assembly drawings with zero deficiencies. Wire components per electrical schematics following predefined guidelines and work standards. Capable of completing minor modifications to metal panels and enclosures including cutting, drilling, and tapping. Qualifications/Requirements Associates Degree in Electrical Engineering or related discipline or minimum 2 years of related industry experience. Knowledgeable with panel assembly and wiring. Knowledge of UL508A standards. Willingness to learn new product. Desired Characteristics 2+ years manufacturing/assembly/wiring experience. Panel assembly and wiring experience. Strong attention to detail. Familiar with assembly and wiring a range of drive product including AC & DC drives, variable speed drives, motor exciters, remote I/O stations, and operator stations.

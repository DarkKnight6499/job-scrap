About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Business Credit Analyst to support Home Lending Portfolio Credit Management. This role will develop and maintain credit risk reporting and dashboards, monitor Home Lending originations, analyze emerging trends and risks, and provide analytical support for credit risk initiatives. The position requires strong analytical and technical skills, attention to detail, and the ability to communicate findings and collaborate effectively with business partners. In this role, you will: Develop, maintain, and enhance Home Lending credit risk reports and dashboards used to monitor origination activity, credit characteristics, credit risk metrics, and key risk indicators. Analyze Home Lending origination data to identify trends, concentrations, emerging risks, opportunities, and performance drivers; research material changes, data discrepancies, and potential business implications. Support low to moderately complex Business Credit initiatives, including quantitative analyses of credit strategy, policy, and guideline changes by evaluating historical performance and assessing potential impacts. Develop recommendations to address credit risk matters and reporting findings, exercising independent judgment within established policies, procedures, and compliance requirements. Identify and implement opportunities to improve reporting, monitoring, and analytical processes, including enhancements to data quality, efficiency, and usability. Communicate analytical findings and recommendations to managers, peers, internal customers, and business partners, and collaborate across teams to resolve issues and achieve business goals. Required Qualifications: 2+ years of Business Credit experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education Experience using Excel and PowerPoint to aggregate and analyze data to present findings Desired Qualifications: Experience developing, maintaining, and enhancing credit risk reports, dashboards, or portfolio monitoring tools, including reporting on credit characteristics, risk metrics, trends, and key risk indicators. 1+ years of experience using SAS, SQL, Python, or similar analytical/programming tools to query, manipulate, analyze, or validate data. Experience working with large and complex data sets, including researching data discrepancies, performing data validation, and ensuring accuracy and completeness of reporting. Experience analyzing loan, credit, or portfolio data to identify trends, concentrations, changes in credit characteristics, emerging risks, and potential business impacts. Experience using Power BI, Tableau, or comparable visualization tools to develop dashboards and clearly communicate analytical findings. Experience in consumer credit risk, mortgage credit risk, portfolio risk management, risk analytics, lending analytics, or financial analysis. Knowledge of residential mortgage lending, Home Lending originations, underwriting, credit policy, or portfolio performance. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and critical-thinking skills with demonstrated attention to data accuracy, reconciliation, controls, and data quality. Ability to work independently, exercise sound judgment, manage multiple analytical priorities, and collaborate effectively with risk, business, technology, data, and reporting partners. Experience identifying and implementing reporting or process improvements, including automation, improved data quality, reduced manual effort, or enhanced usability.

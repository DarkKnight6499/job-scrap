Your Impact The primary purpose of this role is to manage the team responsible for conducting Associate Relations investigations and reviewing/approving corrective action reports. The role will provide direction to AR Advisors, review and assign incoming cases, and conduct manager reviews upon the completion of the investigations. This role will support education and training on workplace investigation processes across the enterprise and serve as point of contact for escalated AR concerns. What You Will Do Lead and/or oversee workplace investigations with different levels of complexity and risk, with support from AR Directors and/or Managers. Lead a team responsible for conducting workplace investigations into associate concerns and review of corrective action reports. With oversight from AR Directors and/or Managers, execute Associate Relations strategy that aligns to the company's culture while optimizing employee engagement and fostering positive associate relations. Provide guidance on investigation management and policy interpretation that aligns to local, state and federal laws and statutes and company policies and practices. Partner with field HR and business leaders to build strong partnerships and develop proactive positive associate relations strategies that drive business objectives. Cultivate and maintain trusted and productive relationships with human resources, legal, business operations leaders, etc., across the country. Provide coaching, guidance and expertise to HR Business Partners to address employee conduct and performance related concerns. Ensure corrective/disciplinary actions consistently, fairly and legally across the enterprise. Inspire, motivate, develop and influence individuals and teams. Support and guide AR Advisors on case escalations. Review and assign all incoming workplace investigation cases and conduct manager reviews upon the completion of the investigations. Communicate feedback findings and recommendations on case management processes concisely to all levels of associates and leadership both verbally and in written form. Partner with various HR and business leaders to build strong partnerships and develop proactive positive associate relations strategies that drive business objectives.

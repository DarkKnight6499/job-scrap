North America Portfolio Analyst, Officer
State Street

Who We Are Looking For We are seeking a highly motivated, client-focused professional with a strong interest in financial markets, investment management, and relationship management. As a Relationship Management Analyst within the North America Asset Manager Portfolio team, you will partner with senior Relationship Managers in supporting a diverse portfolio of asset management clients across North America. This role offers an excellent opportunity to gain exposure to client relationship management, strategic account planning, business development initiatives, and the broad range of solutions State Street provides for investment managers. The successful candidate will play a key role in coordinating client activities, supporting relationship strategies, and ensuring a positive client experience. You will regularly collaborate with teams across Relationship Management, Sales, Product, Operations, Client Service, Legal, and Finance, while developing a strong understanding of State Street's business and the evolving needs of institutional asset managers. Why This Role Is Important To Us The North America Asset Manager Portfolio team is responsible for supporting and growing relationships with a broad range of asset management clients. Our objective is to serve as a trusted partner by delivering exceptional service, identifying opportunities for growth, and connecting clients to the full capabilities of State Street. As a Relationship Management Analyst, you will be an important member of the client coverage team, helping to execute relationship strategies, coordinate client engagement activities, and facilitate effective collaboration across the organization. Your contributions will directly support client satisfaction, relationship growth, and the successful execution of strategic initiatives. This role provides valuable exposure to senior leaders, client-facing relationship management, and many facets of the investment servicing industry, creating a strong foundation for future career opportunities within Relationship Management, Sales, Client Management, and other areas across State Street. What You Will Be Responsible For As a Relationship Management Analyst, you will: Support Relationship Managers in executing client relationship strategies and day-to-day account management activities. Coordinate client requests and inquiries by partnering with internal service, product, operations, and support teams to drive timely resolution. Assist in the preparation of client presentations, executive briefings, relationship reviews, account plans, and business updates. Maintain and update client information within Salesforce and other relationship management systems. Support opportunity tracking, revenue reporting, pipeline management, and other management reporting activities. Assist with client billing administration, documentation, and collection activities, as needed. Coordinate contract management activities, including contract amendments, new service additions, and document management within the Enterprise Contract Management System (ECMS). Organize client meetings, due diligence reviews, governance sessions, and other client engagement activities. Support responses to client due diligence requests, RFIs, and RFPs. Conduct research and analysis on client organizations, industry developments, market trends, and competitive activity. Track action items, client commitments, and project deliverables to help ensure successful execution of client initiatives. Partner with stakeholders across business units and regions to support client outcomes and strategic objectives. What We Value These skills and attributes will help you succeed in this role: Strong client focus and commitment to delivering exceptional service. Intellectual curiosity and a desire to understand the investment management and financial services industries. Excellent organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines. Strong written, verbal, and presentation communication skills. Ability to build effective working relationships across teams and levels of the organization. Analytical mindset with strong problem-solving and critical-thinking abilities. Proactive approach to ownership, accountability, and execution. Strong attention to detail and commitment to accuracy. Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment. Collaborative team player with a global perspective. Education & Preferred Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, or a related field preferred. 2-5 years of experience in relationship management support, client service, asset servicing, custody, fund administration, sales support, consulting, or related financial services roles. Knowledge of institutional asset management, custody, middle office, fund administration, transfer agency, or related financial services products preferred. Experience supporting client meetings, executive presentations, and business initiatives. Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, Word, and other Microsoft Office applications. Salesforce or CRM experience preferred. Additional Requirements Strong interpersonal and relationship-building skills. Ability to communicate effectively with clients and senior internal stakeholders. Demonstrated project management and organizational capabilities. Ability to work independently while managing competing priorities. High level of professionalism, judgment, and attention to detail. Willingness to learn, take initiative, and contribute to a high-performing team environment. What You Can Expect This role provides a unique opportunity to develop a broad understanding of State Street's asset servicing and investment management solutions while working alongside experienced Relationship Managers. You will gain exposure to client strategy, relationship management, business development, and executive engagement activities while supporting a portfolio of leading asset management firms. Successful candidates will develop the commercial, analytical, and client-facing skills necessary to build a long-term career within State Street and the broader financial services industry.

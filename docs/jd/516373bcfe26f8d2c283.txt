Position Overview HarbourVest is seeking an experienced IT Business Analyst to support the data, reporting, and visualization capabilities of our Client Portal. This role will partner with business interested parties, product owners, data engineering teams, and external partners to ensure the reliable delivery and support of client-facing reports, dashboards, and investment data. The ideal candidate brings strong expertise in Power BI, investment data, Snowflake integrations, data mapping, and production support, with the ability to translate business needs into clear technical requirements and high-quality solutions. Key Responsibilities Data, Reporting & Visualization Gather and document requirements for client-facing reports, dashboards, metrics, and visualizations. Support the development and enhancement of Power BI solutions and validate data for accuracy, completeness, and consistency. Maintain business rules, data definitions, reporting requirements, and operational documentation. Investment Data & Snowflake Integration Support investment data across client, fund, portfolio, transaction, and performance domains. Develop and maintain source-to-target mappings, transformation rules, and integration requirements. Partner with Data Engineering on Snowflake solutions and analyze data flows across source systems, Snowflake, Power BI, and the Client Portal. Perform data reconciliation, impact analysis, and root-cause analysis to address data quality issues. Production Support & Operational Excellence Provide functional support for Client Portal data, reporting, and visualization capabilities. Participate in incident triage, issue investigation, root-cause analysis, and resolution. Support production releases through testing, validation, post-deployment monitoring, and maintenance of runbooks and support procedures. Find opportunities to improve data quality, reporting processes, and increase support efficiency. Offshore Support Partner Coordination Work closely with, and at times provide functional management of, the offshore support partner in India. Coordinate day-to-day priorities, work intake, assignments, project progress, support coverage, and issue resolution across time zones. Provide business context and requirement clarification, review the work, and ensure alignment with quality and service expectations. Support knowledge transfer, operational readiness, open status reporting, and accountability across the distributed team. Business Analysis & Partner Management Facilitate requirements sessions and translate business needs into user stories, process flows, functional requirements, and acceptance criteria. Support backlog prioritization, solution design, user acceptance testing, and release planning. Act as a liaison among business users, product teams, data engineers, developers, support teams, and third-party partners. Qualifications Required 5+ years of experience in a Business Analyst, Data Analyst, or related role. Demonstrated experience with Power BI, Snowflake or a similar cloud data platform, data mapping, data integration, and reporting requirements. Working knowledge of SQL and Python experience analyzing, validating, and reconciling data. Experience supporting production applications and resolving data-related issues. Excellent analytical, communication, documentation, and partner management skills. Ability to translate complex technical concepts into clear, business-friendly language. Preferred Experience in private equity, asset management, financial services, or investment operations. Familiarity with client, investor, fund, or investment performance reporting. Experience supporting a client-facing portal or digital platform. Experience coordinating or functionally managing offshore support teams, preferably in India. Experience with Agile delivery practices and tools such as Jira and Confluence.

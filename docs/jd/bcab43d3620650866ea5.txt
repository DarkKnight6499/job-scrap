Job Title Portfolio Strategy Lead Job Description Summary Job Description The purpose of this position is to lead the achievement of strategic goals and deliverables for multiple clients within a large, complex, and geographically diverse real estate portfolios. Essential Duties and Responsibilities • Proactively support the account team in client engagements to ensure excellence and consistency. • Assists in solving complex problems; takes a broad perspective to identify innovative solutions • Provide service excellence by understanding key client business issues, supplementing standard techniques and tools with innovative approaches, evaluating, and validating analysis and developing/delivering recommendations • Support the delivery team and their work across multiple workstreams, and own the day-to-day relationships necessary for delivery excellence • Technical presenter - Conceptualizes the framework for collecting data, analyzing the data, and translating it into a story • Innovative-inquisitive, challenges status quo, thinks outside the box; researcher motivated to become thought leader • Designs and delivers frameworks to proactively facilitate senior client leadership workshops for strategic development and other decisioning • Subject matter expertise - designs integrated workplace/human experience/portfolio solutions in support of business objectives • Financial acumen - designs the framework for financial modelling and translates the data analyzed into a story Qualifications • Must have experience in areas such as: Portfolio Optimization, Cost Optimization, Portfolio Strategy related to Mergers & Acquisitions, Workplace Strategy, etc • Extensive experience and expertise using structured consulting methodology related to scoping, framing, and delivering consulting engagements that delight FORTUNE1000 clients • Candidates with Big 4-type Consulting background are strongly encouraged to apply • Experience influencing and relationship management at the senior executive level • Possess strong analytical skills and the ability to develop conclusions and recommendations • Ability to multi-task and work both in a team and independently in a high-pressure deadline driven environment • Demonstrated ability to manage multiple and complex matters daily • Capacity to deal with ambiguity as well as address and solve complex problems • Exceptional communication skills - both written and verbal • Possess excellent customer service and interpersonal relationship skills Education and Experience • Bachelor's degree (BA/BS) from four-year college or university. • 8+ years of experience successfully leading and delivering consulting engagements for occupier clients related to Commercial Real Estate Strategy Certificates and/or Licenses • Real estate license (where required by state law) Professional certification, professional designations (e.g. BCCR, CCIM, CPM, MAI) and/or graduate level training in commercial real estate is desirable.

A role in Enterprise Risk Governance means creating effective and scalable governance processes, monitoring frameworks, and assurance routines that enable business and compliance program owners to identify, monitor, test, and mitigate risks. The team drives enterprise visibility, strengthens control effectiveness, and supports executive and Board-level oversight through structured governance and data-driven reporting. You will build valuable relationships and make a meaningful impact as you coordinate critical resources across teams in a complex and fast-paced retail environment. As a Lead Consultant, Risk Insights & Monitoring , you will: Lead cross-functional development of enterprise risk and control monitoring standards that strengthen governance and control effectiveness. Partner with risk owners to convert documented controls into monitorable controls and practical KPIs/KRIs that demonstrate control performance. Develop and implement monitoring procedures and routines to evaluate the effectiveness and sustainability of internal controls across multiple business areas and compliance domains. Identify monitoring gaps, data limitations, and unmonitorable controls; establish compensating testing or validation strategies to ensure coverage of key risks. Enhance traceability between risks, controls, monitoring metrics, testing results, and findings within Risk Cloud to improve transparency and reporting accuracy. Develop a strong understanding of regulatory expectations, industry trends, and emerging risks to inform monitoring strategies and governance priorities. Influence compliance program leaders and key stakeholders regarding monitoring solutions, implementation strategies, and maturity expectations. Lead complex analysis of monitoring data, control performance trends, and risk indicators to identify emerging issues and drive risk-based mitigation strategies. Establish trusted relationships with risk owners to support adoption of monitoring standards and continuous improvement actions. Collaborate with Technology, Data, Internal Audit, and stakeholders to support dashboard development, data quality improvements, and integrated assurance visibility. Support risk governance by defining risk driver metrics, escalation triggers, and executive-ready reporting insights.

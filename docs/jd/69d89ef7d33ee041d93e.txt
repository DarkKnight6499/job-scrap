Responsibilities Lead Human Risk Management projects and workstreams, including planning, execution, risk management, and stakeholder coordination. Design, manage, and continuously improve social engineering programs, including phishing, vishing, smishing, and other behavioral security initiatives. Develop and deliver awareness campaigns, learning experiences, and communications using adult learning, behavioral science, and marketing techniques to encourage secure decision-making. Analyze employee behavior, simulation outcomes, learning effectiveness, security culture data, and other program metrics to identify trends, measure impact, and recommend improvements. Develop dashboards, scorecards, reports, and presentations that translate human risk data into actionable insights for stakeholders and senior leaders. Partner across business, technology, risk, compliance, legal, communications, and learning teams to align Human Risk Management programs with enterprise priorities. Communicate program performance, risks, recommendations, and strategic insights to technical teams, business partners, and leadership. Serve as a trusted advisor on Human Risk Management, social engineering, security culture, and awareness best practices. Build cross-functional relationships and influence decisions to advance shared security and business outcomes. Apply artificial intelligence and emerging technologies to improve program effectiveness, operational efficiency, learning experiences, and Human Risk Management outcomes. Experience managing projects or programs and coordinating initiatives across cross-functional teams. Experience analyzing data and translating findings into recommendations, decisions, and measurable business outcomes.

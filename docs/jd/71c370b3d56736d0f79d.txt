Title and Summary Manager, Corporate Treasury Overview Mastercard is looking for a Manager, FX Trading & Strategy to join the New York City office and oversee daily FX trading, risk management and support the implementation of new processes and procedures. The responsibilities for this role include: • Develop new foreign exchange capabilities to support business initiatives involving cross-border payments • Define FX desk workflows, policies, procedures, and implement a high level of automation • Execute the company’s foreign exchange risk management program and strategies • Monitor FX trade executions and open positions • Measure FX risk according to company policy and report exposures to senior management • Analyze approaches to optimize risk management • Onboard trading partners • Identify requirements to trade exotic currencies • Support the testing of FX risk management, quoting, and trading systems • Communicate relevant news, market events, and FX system behavior to team members • Analyze market trends (liquidity, spreads, volatility, etc.) for the business • Evaluate business cases for incremental revenue opportunities related to foreign exchange transactions and support the business with project-based FX analysis • Contribute to all aspects of the trading desk activities including position reconciliation, risk management tasks and post trade analysis Experience / All about You: Ideal candidate will be proficient in or possess the following: • Experience in developing new business or service offerings • Experience in and/or willingness to start a new trading operation including defining front, middle and back office responsibilities and workflows • FX experience in algorithmic and/or traditional trading in spot, forward, and NDFs • Experience in designing, programming, and/or managing pricing and risk management algorithms or processes is a plus • Strong understanding of FX markets in G-7 and G-20 currencies; some experience with exotic currencies is a plus • Strong knowledge of corporate finance and capital markets • Excellent analytical skills • Strong MS Excel and PowerPoint skills • Proven track record of prioritizing work and managing multiple projects, in addition to daily workload • Ability to work independently and manage ambiguity • Strong oral and written communication skills • Excellent leadership qualities, including getting work done through others, being organizationally savvy, thought leadership and influencing skills • Finance, accounting, economics or similar degree;

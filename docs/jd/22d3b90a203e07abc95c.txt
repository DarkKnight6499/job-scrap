General Summary: Under general supervision, Associate performs quality inspections, general service repairs, brake repairs, engine repairs, heating and air condition repairs, 4-wheel alignments, repairs to steering and suspension, safety inspections, and diagnostics resulting from quality opportunities. Principle Duties And Responsibilities: General repairs, brake repair, engine repairs, heading and air condition repairs, steering and suspension repairs including 4-wheel alignments, and safety inspections and diagnostics resulting from quality opportunities Ensure that all vehicles meet CarMax Mechanical Quality Standards. Drive vehicles to and from various locations as requested by Manager Provide outstanding customer service at all times. Maintain, repair, and clean CarMax provided tools, equipment and spaces. Job Specifications: Read, interpret and transcribe data in order to maintain accurate records. Complete all training as required by position including Kronos Training for Hourly Associates Successfully work with and through associates of other departments within the Store. Perform multiple duties in a high-energy, fast-paced working environment Required to have great attention to detail and have manual dexterity in both arms and hands Speak clearly and listen effectively in dealing with customers/associates, both in person and over the phone. Lift objects that weigh as much as 50 lbs. Monitor shop compliance with CarMax, O.S.H.A., and EPA policies Where required by State/Local law, maintain ASE Certification in Engine Repair (A1), Steering and Suspension (A4), Brakes (A5), Heating Air Conditioning (A7) Working Conditions: Combination of both indoor and outdoor environment; May include working at times in noisy and/or inclement weather conditions. May require walking or standing for an extended time. Flexible work hours with shifts that may include nights, weekends, and holidays. Must wear CarMax clothing (acquired through the company) at all times while working in the store.

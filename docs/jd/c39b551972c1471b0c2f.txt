Overview This is a hybrid role (3 days per week in office) with the expectation that time working will regularly take place inside and outside of a company office. This position is within the Treasury Operations area, specifically within the Financial Network Solutions Group. This position will support the technical and strategic business initiatives within the Financial Network Solutions Group in establishing and maintaining a global centralized banking network with emphasis on utilization of our own bank whist driving efficiency with our banking partners. The candidate will proactively partner with internal parties, including but not limited to Treasury, Finance, Accounting, Tax and Legal to support the different functional goals and assist in financial reporting and analysis for FINS. Will provide daily support for assigned systems, products, or business units. Identifies potential improvements to current procedures, plans, and controls to achieve financial objectives.

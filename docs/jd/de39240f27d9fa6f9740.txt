Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Advisory - Other Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Finance Function Strategy Consulting Senior Associate you will help clients shape finance operating models, organizational design, governance, and operating rhythms that align with broader business goals. As a Senior Associate you will analyze complex challenges, build meaningful client connections, manage assigned workstreams, and develop deliverables while mentoring junior team members and deepening your understanding of the client’s business context. In this role at PwC, you will use finance consulting skills to assess current-state processes, identify gaps and inefficiencies, support finance transformation and change management efforts, and translate findings into clear recommendations for leaders within our Finance Consulting practice. Responsibilities - Analyzing finance operating models, organizational structures, and current-state processes to identify gaps, inefficiencies, and redesign opportunities - Developing target operating model recommendations for finance functions, including governance, roles, decision rights, and service delivery approaches - Preparing client-ready deliverables that translate complex findings into clear insights, options, and implementation roadmaps - Interpreting financial and operational data to support recommendations for finance transformation, performance improvement, and business case development - Facilitating discovery sessions with finance and business leaders to gather requirements, validate assumptions, and shape solution designs - Applying tools and methodologies to assess finance processes, reporting needs, capabilities, and workforce implications - Supporting change management activities by assessing impacts, documenting dependencies, and helping teams prepare for new ways of working - Contributing to workstream planning, task tracking, and issue resolution while managing assigned deadlines and deliverables - Reviewing analysis and deliverables prepared by junior team members and providing mentoring, feedback, and technical guidance - Building client relationships by anticipating needs, responding to feedback, and adapting work to shifting priorities and ambiguity What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 3 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for at least one of the following fields of study: Accounting, Analytics/Data Science, Business Administration/Management, Computer Science/Information Systems, Economics, Finance - Demonstrating finance transformation, operating model, or organizational design experience - Interpreting data to inform recommendations for finance leadership - Applying critical thinking to complex client challenges and ambiguity -

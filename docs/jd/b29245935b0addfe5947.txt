Regular or Temporary: Regular Language Fluency: English (Required) Work Shift: 1st shift (United States of America) Please review the following job description: We are seeking an Application Security Sr. Associate Analyst to join our Application Security team and help developers build secure software across the enterprise. This is a developer-enablement role and an entry point into a specialized application security career. A high-level degree or technical degree, along with a demonstrated understanding of application security concepts through education, internships, or relevant project work. A willingness to learn and build deeper expertise in supporting the Application Security team is essential. This role will develop a strategic approach to support the Application Security team’s book of work, helping coordinate day-to-day priorities, track key milestones and deadlines, and provide visibility into delivery progress. The role will proactively identify risks, dependencies, and potential delays to ensure appropriate escalations are made in advance and commitments remain on track. The role will work across the full range of AppSec functions, including static and composition analysis (SAST, SCA), cloud and container scanning, security tool pipeline integration, OSINT, and DevSecOps automation. With guidance from senior analysts and stack owners, and will build knowledge across these areas to effectively support the team, document processes, assist developers, and help develop training and automation that scale the team’s impact. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Following is a summary of the essential functions for this job. Other duties may be performed, both major and minor, which are not mentioned below. Specific activities may change from time to time. Learn how the team supports developers across AppSec disciplines (such as SAST, SCA, cloud and container scanning, OSINT, and DevSecOps automation) and help address routine questions under guidance. Help create and maintain developer training, secure-coding guidance, and self-service enablement content. Assist with managing team processes and workflows to streamline operations and improve efficiency including measuring Key Performance Indicators and Key Risk Indicators (KPI/KRI) Assist with security tool and pipeline integration workflows and help communicate results to stakeholders. Help build and maintain metrics, dashboards, and reporting on vulnerability posture and remediation progress. Develop automation solutions to streamline evidence collection, data gathering, and repetitive tasks, with guidance from senior team members.. Gather and organize data from multiple tools and sources to support insights and reporting. Apply AI and automation tools to make triage, reporting, and enablement more efficient. Technical Skills Develop an understanding of the Application Security team’s key functions and help support day-to-day priorities and requests. Assist with managing the team’s book of work, including tracking priorities, deadlines, milestones, and deliverables. Help identify potential risks, delays, or concerns and ensure they are communicated and escalated in advance. Create and maintain tracking and training materials, guidance, and other resources to support team members and business partners. Document processes, procedures, and workflows to make information easy to understand, share, and reuse. Support coordination of team activities and help ensure work progresses according to established timelines. Assist with developing and maintaining metrics, dashboards, and status reporting to provide visibility into progress and key priorities. Gather, organize, and review information from multiple sources to support reporting, decision-making, and team needs. Identify opportunities to improve or streamline processes and reduce repetitive work. Use AI and automation tools to improve efficiency, reporting, communication, and overall team support.

Citizens is seeking early career professionals, with 1-2 years of experience, to join the execution team within its Capital Markets & Sponsor Finance Group (“Capital Markets”). As an Analyst II within Capital Markets , you will work closely with the team’s senior professionals in originating, structuring and executing leverage finance transactions, primarily within the U.S. middle market. Analysts’ responsibilities will require significant and regular transaction involvement, from origination through closing, as well as the ability to work on multiple projects and transactions concurrently. You will play a critical role on deal teams and interact directly with Managing Directors and other senior management members, other groups within Citizens, client senior management teams, private equity investment teams, and legal counsel. Analysts will work directly with senior professionals across several different industries and perform analyses and support on all aspects of investment products. In this role at Citizens, you’re made ready for a fulfilling career with exciting new challenges and opportunities to stretch yourself! Primary responsibilities include Assists in the evaluation and execution of assigned investment transactions, including M&A, securities issuance, capital restructuring, etc. Provides financial analysis reports for investment transactions. Prepares marketing documentation such as pitch materials and board and management presentations. Drafts memorandums and road show presentations for client companies. Qualifications, Education, Certifications and/or Other Professional Credential Required: We seek multi-faceted, results-driven, creative individuals ideally with 1-2+ years experience in Investment Banking or in the Big 4 Deals Advisory practice, who can generate ideas and execute them successfully as part of a dynamic team. Bachelors Degree in Finance or related field Preferred: You will need to demonstrate superior problem solving and analytical capabilities accompanied by evidence of academic excellence and a strong work ethic.

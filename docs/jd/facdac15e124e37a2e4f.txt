The Opportunity Join Currency and Commodity Operations in a role that helps keep Precious Metals and domestic Money Market activity running smoothly and safely across a highly controlled environment. You’ll support pre and post-settlement services including confirmations and fails management for Money Markets and globally traded Precious Metals. This role sits alongside a business that provides 24-hour global coverage across precious metals products and operates one of the largest commercial metals vaulting facilities in the US. You’ll oversee daily processing, support change and technology delivery and help maintain strong client and stakeholder relationships. Success means accurate and timely processing, clear escalation of risks and issues and continuous improvements to controls and efficiency.

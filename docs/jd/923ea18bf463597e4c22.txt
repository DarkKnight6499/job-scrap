A day in the life. MMA Rx Solutions is the national pharmacy center of excellence for Marsh McLennan Agency. In Collaboration with its leadership The Associate Strategic Client Executive Rx Solutions will support the sales process and serve as the main point person for the MMA field offices on post-sale implementation and ongoing client support as it relates to pharmacy management. The position will also work with PBMs and our strategic partners to ensure their deliverables related to both the vision of this approach and that the needs of the MMA field offices are met.

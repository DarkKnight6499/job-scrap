Your Team Responsibilities As a PCS Consultant in our Coverage team, you’ll be at the forefront of strengthening strategic partnerships and delivering exceptional client experiences across the private markets landscape. You’ll play a pivotal role in ensuring our clients realize the full value of MSCI’s private markets solutions through trusted, long-term collaboration. If you are relentlessly client-focused, excel at uncovering client needs, and are ready to seize opportunities, this is your opportunity to make a difference.

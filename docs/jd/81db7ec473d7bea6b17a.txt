Brief Overview of Position As we aim to provide exceptional customer service, we are looking for a qualified, customer-focused individual to join us as a Treasury Implementations Associate. As a Treasury Implementations Associate, you carry the responsibility of engaging with our internal partners and clients to provide insight, knowledge, and excitement about our Treasury products and services across all channels. This role manages the complete lifecycle of Treasury client relationships — from initial product implementation and client education to ongoing servicing and support. You will be instrumental in onboarding new Treasury products for existing clients, managing new client implementations, ensuring seamless transitions between operational phases, and supporting clients throughout their entire journey with Texas Capital Bank. Your work ensures that clients receive consistent, high-quality service whether they're launching a new Treasury solution or optimizing existing services.

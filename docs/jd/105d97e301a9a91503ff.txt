Financial Institutions have drastically changed over the last decade, driven by increased regulatory constraints, diverse competition inside and beyond traditional banking organizations, and emerging technologies reshaping long-standing ecosystems. Sia’s Financial Services Business Unit provides a comprehensive suite of core capabilities designed to address the diverse and evolving needs of our clients, enabling them to navigate complex challenges, seize new opportunities, and achieve their strategic objectives in an increasingly competitive and dynamic business environment. We are seeking highly motivated professionals at the Consultant and Senior Consultant levels to join our Financial Services Consulting practice as a Forward Deployed Business Analyst (FDBA). Whether your expertise lies in wealth and asset management, capital markets, corporate banking, risk and compliance or other areas in financial services, we want to hear from you. This is not a traditional Business Analyst role. As a FDBA, you will be embedded with client teams to own business outcomes, not just document requirements. You will leverage data, analytics and AI as a core part of your workflow to move faster, think deeper and deliver smarter solutions and meaningful transformation across financial services organizations. You will act as a strategic advisor, AI operator and execution partner, using AI to eliminate administrative work and focus your time on enhanced decision making, problem-solving, stakeholder alignment, operational efficiency and solution design. Responsibilities: AI-Enabled Consulting Delivery: Apply traditional consulting tools and emerging AI-enabled, no-code, low-code, automation, and analytics platforms to accelerate research, analysis, documentation, prototyping, process mapping, stakeholder alignment, and delivery execution while including defining agent responsibilities, decision boundaries, escalation paths, and human-in-the-loop controls to ensure safe, reliable, and compliant operation within financial services environments. Client Engagement & Delivery: Work directly with client business, operations, product, data, and technology teams to understand strategic objectives, operational challenges, technical constraints, stakeholder priorities, and desired business outcomes. Strategic Problem Solving: Assess current-state processes, systems, data flows, risks, dependencies, and pain points to identify root causes, evaluate business impacts, and help define the design and implementation of strategic initiatives that incorporate both traditional and AI-enabled strategic solutions (e.g., operating model transformation, system implementations, process optimization) Technical Business Analysis: Translate business needs into clear functional requirements, user stories, process flows, data requirements, business rules, acceptance criteria, and solution documentation that bridge business stakeholders and technical delivery teams with the support of internal and client-approved AI solutions. Solution Design & Implementation Support: Partner with product owners, engineers, architects, data teams, and client stakeholders to support solution design system implementation, workflow optimization, integration planning, UAT, validation, and implementation readiness across front, middle, and back-office platforms including .targeted use cases of AI- or agent-enabled solutions. Workstream Ownership & Execution: Own assigned tasks and deliverables by managing timelines, tracking risks, issues, decisions, and dependencies, proactively communicating progress, and helping drive initiatives from analysis through execution. Client-Ready Communication & Deliverables: Prepare structured, accurate, and polished materials, including presentations, executive summaries, business cases, impact assessments, process documentation, status reports, meeting outputs, and stakeholder communications for internal teams, client stakeholders, and senior leaders. Internal Firm Support: Support proposals and business development initiatives, including client presentations and thought leadership, contributing perspectives on digital, data, and AI trends within financial services.

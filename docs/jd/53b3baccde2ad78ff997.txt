Job Summary: We are seeking a highly motivated Front Office Support Analyst to support our Equity and Fixed Income trading businesses. This role is responsible for providing production support for front-office trading platforms, regulatory reporting systems, and related market connectivity infrastructure. The ideal candidate will thrive in a fast-paced trading environment and possess strong technical, analytical, and problem-solving skills. Key Responsibilities: Provide day-to-day production support for Equity and Fixed Income trading systems. Support front-office users, including traders, sales teams, and business stakeholders. Troubleshoot and resolve application, infrastructure, and connectivity issues. Monitor trading and regulatory reporting platforms to ensure operational stability. Partner with technology, operations, compliance, and business teams to support critical initiatives and projects. Analyze system issues and implement sustainable solutions and process improvements. Perform scripting and automation tasks to improve operational efficiency. Participate in system upgrades, releases, testing, and change management activities. Assist with regulatory reporting support and controls. Required Qualifications Experience supporting front-office trading environments. Knowledge of Equity and Fixed Income trading workflows. Strong troubleshooting and analytical skills. Hands-on experience with Windows and Unix/Linux operating systems. Experience with scripting languages such as Shell (sh, ksh, bash) and/or batch scripting. Programming experience in one or more of the following: Python, Java, C#, or C++. Ability to work independently in a fast-paced, high-pressure environment. Excellent verbal and written communication skills. Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field, or equivalent combination of education and experience. Preferred Qualifications Knowledge of Equity regulatory reporting including CAT, TRF/ORF, and Rule 606. Knowledge of Fixed Income regulatory reporting, including TRACE. Experience with FIX Protocol (4.2 - 5.0) and routing platforms. Experience supporting Bloomberg TOMS, Ullink Bridge, FlexTrade, or similar OMS platforms. Experience with Oracle, Sybase, MongoDB, or other relational and NoSQL databases. Knowledge of JavaScript and modern web technologies. Advanced Microsoft Excel skills, including macros. Understanding of Equity and Fixed Income securities and trade lifecycle.

Credit Risk Management (CRM), as the second line of defense, is a key function in protecting and making sure that we take prudent risk for Nomura. The group evaluates transactions and approves, declines, or modifies them depending on the credit quality of each counterparty and structure. The department also assigns internal credit ratings, and establishes and manages credit risk limits in accordance with the risk tolerance. The Lending & Asset Finance team within Credit Risk is looking for a dynamic individual at the Vice President to support coverage of Nomura’s for structured and leveraged credit businesses including Lender Finance and Private Credit, Asset-Backed Lending and Trade Finance, and Leveraged Loans. Key Responsibilities: Analyze credit risk across a broad range of private credit and opportunistic credit transactions including direct lending, bespoke asset-backed financing, lender finance, trade finance, and leveraged finance, among other structured credit products. Scope of work includes, but is not limited to, fundamental financial analysis, evaluation of complex and bespoke deal structures, review of legal terms & conditions, and coordinating credit approval processes including senior-level briefings. Take ownership of analyzing and assigning internal credit ratings, evaluating key risks and mitigants across novel private credit transactions, and preparing internal memorandums to document decision rationale. Engage directly with front office deal teams as an independent credit authority on deal structuring, transaction design, and commercial terms across a diverse and complex credit portfolio. Propose and negotiate structural changes for new and existing transactions to proactively manage credit risk, including in higher risk or deteriorating credit situations. Participate in and lead due diligence discussions via calls and on-site visits with clients’ or borrowers’ management teams. Provide feedback on structuring of key commercial / legal terms in conjunction with other control areas including Legal / Finance. Own and manage a portfolio of Investment Grade & Sub-Investment Grade credit exposures, including ensuring timely credit reviews, participation in periodic lender calls, and holistic portfolio reviews. Track developments in regulatory & industry-specific operating environments and monitor counterparty credit profile or changes in external ratings. Ensure pro-active socialization and / or escalation of such developments as needed. Participate in various firm-wide projects within Risk and Business colleagues across regions, including regular and ad-hoc portfolio / limit reviews. Ability to mentor and support development of junior members of the team.

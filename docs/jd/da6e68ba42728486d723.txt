For more information on ITS, see this video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?reload=9 v=VTFGffa1Y7w Unit Context: The ITSIG (Data, Information and AI Governance) Unit is the strategic driver of trust, agility, and responsible innovation in the organization’s data, information, and AI landscape. The unit develops and oversees governance frameworks, policies, and stewardship models that enable the Group, and the public, to maximize the value of its data and information. By embedding governance into product and service delivery, platform design, and business engagement, the unit positions governance as a catalyst for AI transformation and business impact. The unit also fosters partnerships and thought leadership, ensuring the Group remains at the forefront of global best practices in data, information, and AI governance. Duties and accountabilities: Role Purpose The Associate Senior Information Management Officer will lead the design and implementation of the WBG data, information and AI governance framework integrating data, information and AI governance into a coherent enterprise approach. The framework will strengthen quality, accountability, regulatory compliance, audit readiness and responsible AI use. As a subject-matter expert, the incumbent will lead complex, multidisciplinary initiatives and translate strategy into sustainable governance practices, controls and measurable results. • Shape enterprise data, information and AI governance policies, standards and guidance, translating business, technology, risk and governance needs into clear, actionable requirements. • Ensure alignment responsibilities, decision rights, workflows, guidance, and monitoring approaches for defined workstreams, and other implementation measures. • Enable effective data, information and AI governance bodies and working groups by preparing decision-ready materials, facilitating stakeholder alignment, documenting decisions and driving follow through on agreed actions. • Build trusted relationships

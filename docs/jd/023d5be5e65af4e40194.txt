Job Description Summary The Strategic Partnerships Associate will play a key role in supporting all facets of Partnership Management. This position spans both client support and analytical responsibilities, including developing client reporting, supporting investor meetings, managing the internal pipeline, and responding to ad hoc client requests. Sitting at the intersection of client engagement and investment insight, the role requires strong analytical thinking alongside the ability to interpret portfolio performance, positioning, and investor outcomes. This individual will work closely with the Strategic Partnerships team to deliver high‑quality, data‑driven insights and materials. The role involves managing and servicing institutional investors that are strategically valuable to Ares and requires a high level of attention to detail, responsiveness, and judgment. The position provides broad visibility across the entire Ares platform and will be based in New York. The Strategic Partnerships team focuses on developing and deepening relationships with select Ares LPs by delivering tailored solutions. The team works closely with senior stakeholders across Ares’ four business lines as well as non‑investment teams (including Relationship Management, Finance & Accounting, Legal, Compliance, Investor Operations & Services, Tax, and Quantitative Research). Ares is a flat, flexible, and dynamic organization that cultivates a culture of collaboration, entrepreneurialism, integrity, and partnership.

Job Description We are seeking a Sales and Relationship Analyst (Officer level) to join our growing SSIM Middle East & Africa (MEA) Client Coverage team, based out of Cape Town, South Africa. You will be part of an ambitious and entrepreneurial business development team covering a range of client segments with a focus on Intermediary and Institutional Clients. Product coverage will span the full range of State Street Investment Management capabilities including passive equities, global fixed income, cash, ETF’s, quantitative and fundamental equities, multi-asset, Private Markets and OCIO solutions. The individual will gain exposure to region, and will provide critical support to the sales and relationship management teams across RFP support, pipeline management, roadshows and business development activities. We want you to develop a deep understanding of the SSIM franchise in the Middle East and Africa MEA region to advance our pipeline and actively contribute to the region’s annual gross and net new revenue targets. This is the ideal role for someone looking to build a career in asset management distribution, with a focus on the South Africa market and other selected Sub-Saharan Africa countries. The position offers strong exposure to institutional and intermediary clients, as well as SSIM’s full product suite including ETFs, mutual funds, fixed income, equities, cash and multi-asset solutions.

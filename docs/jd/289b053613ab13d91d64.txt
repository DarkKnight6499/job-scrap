Build and understand complex financial models. Perform complex financial analysis with minimal oversight. MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Bachelor’s degree in Finance , Accounting, Economics, or a related field. 3–5 years of experience in Banking, Investment Banking, or Capital Markets (strong Analyst performance required for internal promotion). MBA typical but not required based on prior experience. Proven track record of contributing to the origination and closing of complex financial transactions as part of a team. Securities Industry Essentials (SIE), Series 79, and Series 63 licenses (or ability to obtain within company policy). Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint.

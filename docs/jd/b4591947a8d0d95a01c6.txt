Learn more . Back to search results Position Summary Build a career rooted in purpose, innovation and impact by helping organizations modernize finance and planning capabilities. As a Senior Consultant on our FP&A Advise team you will help clients improve financial planning, forecasting, reporting and analytics so leaders can make more informed business decisions. This role offers the opportunity to work across finance transformation initiatives that combine process design, data, technology, and business insight. If you enjoy solving complex finance challenges and delivering practical, scalable solutions, this role offers meaningful growth and client impact. Recruiting for this role ends on 12/1/2026. Work you'll do As a Senior Consultant in Finance Transformation, you will be responsible for… Advising clients on financial planning and analysis processes, including planning, budgeting, forecasting, and management reporting Supporting the design and implementation of finance transformation solutions that improve accuracy, efficiency, and decision support Gathering business requirements, documenting future-state processes, and translating business needs into functional designs Developing analyses, financial models, and executive-ready materials to support client recommendations and project delivery Collaborating with cross-functional teams to support workstream execution, stakeholder alignment, and implementation milestones A successful candidate would possess these skills: Ability to work independently and collaborate as part of a team Effective written and verbal communication skills Meticulous attention to detail and quality of work product Ability to build and sustain professional relationships Ability to lead projects or workstreams Ability to manage and prioritize multiple tasks in a fast-paced and dynamic environment Strong interpersonal skills and professional demeanor Ability to meet deadlines Ability to provide clear guidance to others The team Deloitte’s Finance Transformation offering helps finance and global business services leaders navigate an increasingly challenging environment marked by disruptive tech like AI, market uncertainty, and regulatory change, and recognize the opportunity to better serve the enterprise, shareholders, customers, and regulators. Within that offering, our Business Finance team helps drive strategic value through financial planning, forecasting, and analytics capabilities. By leveraging next-gen processes, scalable data platforms, and AI, our team delivers accurate, timely, and actionable insights. Qualifications Required: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, Information Systems, Data Analytics, Engineering, or Computer Science 4+ years of experience in financial planning and analysis, finance transformation, enterprise performance management, or business finance 2+ years of experience gathering business requirements and translating requirements into finance process, data, reporting, or technology designs 2+ years of experience with at least one planning, reporting, or analytics platform such as Anaplan, Oracle Enterprise Performance Management, OneStream, SAP Analytics Cloud, Workday Adaptive Planning, or IBM Planning Analytics 2+ years of experience creating budgets, forecasts, financial models, variance analyses, or management reports using Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint Ability to travel 50%, on average, based on the work you do and the clients and industries/sectors you serve. Limited immigration sponsorship may be available.

class="job_description"> Zions Bancorporation’s Enterprise Technology and Operations (ETO) team is transforming what it means to work for a financial institution. With a commitment to technology and innovation, we have been providing our community, clients and colleagues the best experience possible for over 150 years. Help us transform our workforce of the future, today We are seeking a Technology Risk Relationship Manager to support technology and cybersecurity risk management activities within the Enterprise Technology & Operations (ETO) Division. In this role, you will serve as a trusted advisor to technology and cybersecurity leaders, helping them identify, assess, monitor, and manage risks and controls across their areas of responsibility. As a Technology Risk Relationship Manager of ETO's first line risk management team, you will partner closely with stakeholders to strengthen risk management practices, improve control effectiveness, and ensure alignment with internal requirements, regulatory expectations, and industry best practices. This Technology Risk Relationship Manager is ideal for someone with a strong background in technology risk management, cybersecurity, governance, controls, audit, or related technology disciplines who enjoys solving complex problems, building relationships, and driving meaningful risk management outcomes. Responsibilities: Serve as the primary risk advisor for assigned technology and cybersecurity domains. Partner with business and technology leaders to identify, assess, and manage technology and cybersecurity risks. Facilitate risk management activities, including risk and control self-assessments (RCSAs), control identification and maintenance, issue management, and risk treatment planning. Evaluate processes, risks, controls, issues, and metrics to ensure risks are appropriately managed and mitigated. Develop strong relationships with stakeholders and foster collaboration across teams. Assist with the development and monitoring of key risk indicators (KRIs) and other risk monitoring activities. Provide guidance on aligning controls and risk management practices with industry frameworks and standards, including NIST, COBIT, and FFIEC. Lead or support cross-functional risk initiatives to enhance risk management capabilities and overall control effectiveness. Develop and deliver risk management training and awareness activities. Prepare risk reporting and presentations for leadership and risk governance committees. Qualifications: 6+ years of experience in technology risk management, cybersecurity, IT audit, IT controls, governance, compliance, or a related field. Experience assessing technology processes, risks, controls, issues, and metrics. Demonstrated ability to lead complex initiatives and influence stakeholders across multiple teams. Strong relationship management, communication, and collaboration skills. Working knowledge of technology risk management frameworks and standards, including NIST, COBIT, and FFIEC. Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Information Security, Computer Science, Business, Accounting, or a related field. A combination of education and experience may meet qualifications. Preferred Qualifications: Professional certifications such as CISSP, CISA, CRISC, or similar credentials. Experience with RSA Archer or other governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) platforms. Experience with ServiceNow. Technology risk management experience within the banking, financial services, or other highly regulated industries.

Physical Requirements Must be capable of working at elevated heights. Must be able to lift 50 lbs repetitively. Must not be unusually sensitive to chemicals (contact lenses not recommended). Vision correctable to 20/20. Must be able to stand on feet for long periods. Standing on a concrete floor Wearing proper personal protective equipment (hair net, bump cap, ear plugs, safety shoes, and uniform) Bending, stooping, twisting, turning, standing for extended time periods, reaching, and climbing stairs. Must be able to adapt to temperature and environmental extremes such as hot to cold, dust, dirt, and chemicals. Forklift assignments require OSHA certification and getting on and off frequently. Able to work with minimal supervision. What will be your key responsibilities?

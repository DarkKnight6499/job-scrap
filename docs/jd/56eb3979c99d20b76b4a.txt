About The Role The Corebridge Financial Corporate Treasury team is searching for a Treasury Analyst who has a strong Finance and Treasury background, preferably with prior work experience in the Insurance and Financial Services Industries. Successful candidates for this role are self-starters with balanced backgrounds of analytical skills, and strong soft skills in both written and verbal communications. In this role, the new hire will be responsible for performing all day-to-day activities such as forecasting and cash management. Responsibilities This role will be responsible for a number of areas within the Global Bank Administration team, including opening and closing operating, custody and virtual accounts, managing signatories on accounts, setting up and amending services on accounts, dealing with ad-hoc requests from various AIG businesses and managing/supporting projects as required. A key aspect of the role is ensuring compliance by the business units with Corbridge’s bank account policies and procedures. The role will involve a combination of teamwork and close collaboration with a small team. Skills and Qualifications Undergraduate degree required. Finance or business major preferred Experience in Finance, Treasury, and Risk Management in a Financial firm with demonstrated knowledge in corporate finance, investments, and accounting Self-starter, quick learner, and flexible team player Excellent problem solving, and analytical skills with high level of intellectual curiosity ability Ability to multitask and work well under pressure and tight deadlines while retaining strong attention to detail Demonstrate professionalism in handling confidential and sensitive information MS Office Suite (particularly MS Excel and MS PowerPoint.)

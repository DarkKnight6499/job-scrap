What You'll Do

Public Cloud Revenue Forecasting

 - Own the Public Cloud revenue forecast across on-demand, reserved, and committed-use offerings

 - Build and maintain driver-based models linking capacity, utilization, and pricing to revenue

 - Forecast new customer acquisition, expansion, contraction, and churn by product line

 - Deliver monthly revenue forecasts along with variance analysis and commentary

 - Partner with Accounting on billing, consumption reconciliation, and revenue recognition

Headcount & OpEx Management (BvA)

 - Own OpEx and headcount planning, tracking, and reporting for the Public Cloud organization

 - Maintain hiring plans, compensation assumptions, and organizational change tracking against approved headcount

 - Prepare monthly and quarterly budget vs. actual analysis, identify key drivers, and partner with business owners to explain performance

 - Support accrual and reclassification inputs with Accounting through the monthly close

GTM Finance Partnership & Strategy

 - Serve as the finance partner to Public Cloud GTM leadership on go-to-market planning and resource allocation

 - Model sales capacity, quota coverage, and rep productivity; evaluate territory and quota scenarios

 - Analyze pipeline conversion, bookings-to-revenue linkage, and funnel efficiency

 - Build the business case for GTM investments and measure realized return

Customer Segment Analysis

 - Develop segment-level views of revenue, margin, retention, and lifetime value

 - Build cohort and consumption-behavior analyses to identify expansion opportunity and churn risk

 - Size segment opportunity and inform prioritization across self-serve, mid-market, and enterprise

 - Surface customer concentration risk and account-level margin outliers to leadership

Pricing Support

 - Support pricing strategy with margin, elasticity, and competitive analysis across instance types and regions

 - Model P&L impact of list price changes, discount structures, and committed-use constructs

 - Support deal review on non-standard pricing, approval thresholds, and margin guardrails

Internal Usage & Allocation

 - Track and report internal, non-revenue consumption of Public Cloud capacity across Engineering, Research, and demo or benchmark use

 - Build and maintain allocation and chargeback methodology

 - Quantify the opportunity cost of internal usage relative to external monetization

 - Partner with Infrastructure Finance on capacity availability and utilization reporting

Executive Reporting & Communication

 - Deliver Public Cloud insights for monthly business reviews, executive reviews, and board materials

 - Translate consumption and operational data into a clear financial narrative

 - Provide actionable recommendations to drive revenue growth, margin, and capital efficiency

 - Have direct experience forecasting revenue in a consumption-based or usage-based business model

 - Possess extensive experience in financial modeling and analysis, with a deep expertise in constructing complex financial models and interpreting financial statements to drive strategic decision-making

 - Have excellent analytical, strategic thinking, and decision-making skills

 - Possess strong Excel skills and experience with financial software systems

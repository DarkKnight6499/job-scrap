The principal function of this role is to support the Global Private Wealth Strategic Accounts team in enabling and managing HarbourVest’s private wealth intermediary relationships. As HarbourVest develops relationships with distribution partners in the wealth channel, each platform requires the coordination of a complex set of processes across onboarding, legal and compliance, operations, client service, and ongoing due diligence. The Strategic Accounts Senior Associate will take ownership of this work, including onboarding new partners, maintaining platform infrastructure, leading RFP/DDQ responses, developing presentation materials, and coordinating distribution partner activities. The role serves as a trusted point of contact for platform partners and internal stakeholders, ensures that internal teams are aligned and requirements are met, and contributes to process improvements that make platform enablement more scalable and efficient.

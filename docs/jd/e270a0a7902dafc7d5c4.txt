Our Mission is to enable our customers to make the world healthier, cleaner and safer. Whether our customers are accelerating life sciences research, solving sophisticated analytical challenges, improving efficiency in their laboratories, improving patient health through diagnostics or the development and manufacture of life-changing therapies, we are here to support them. The Sr. Financial Analyst, Global Functions Finance will support finance activities across Global Functions with a focus on standardizing, simplifying and automating recurring financial processes . This role will partner across functional Finance teams to develop common approaches to reporting, forecasting, business reviews, data management and financial planning. A key part of the role will be helping Global Functions Finance improve the quality, consistency and structure of financial data used across the organization. While the individual may not own source-system data directly, they will provide guidance, oversight and recommendations to improve data definitions, governance, integration and usability. The role will also serve as a strong advocate for the use of technology, automation and AI within Finance . The successful candidate should be energized by emerging technologies, actively identify practical AI use cases, challenge manual ways of working and help accelerate adoption across the Global Functions Finance organization. Responsibilities Partner across Global Functions Finance teams to standardize recurring financial processes , including reporting, forecasting, business reviews, templates and planning calendars. Identify opportunities to simplify, centralize and automate repeatable finance activities , reducing manual effort and improving consistency across functions. Serve as a champion for AI, automation and emerging technology within Finance , proactively identifying use cases and helping teams adopt new ways of working. Evaluate Finance processes and recommend opportunities to use AI and automation for reporting, commentary, analysis, workflow management, data preparation and other recurring activities. Support Global Functions financial consolidation, forecasting, AOP, STRAP and performance reporting . Develop and maintain standardized forecasting models, reporting tools and analytical templates that can be leveraged across multiple functions. Help establish consistent data definitions, structures and governance practices across Global Functions Finance. Partner with Finance, systems and data teams to identify data-quality issues and recommend improvements to data sourcing, mapping, validation, reconciliation and integration. Provide oversight and guidance to improve the accuracy, consistency, traceability and usability of financial data across reporting and planning processes. Develop dashboards and reporting solutions that improve transparency, accessibility and consistency of financial information. Partner with functional Finance teams to understand existing processes, identify common requirements and implement scalable solutions while preserving appropriate business context and judgment. Establish process documentation, controls and cross-training to improve sustainability, governance and knowledge sharing. Track process improvements and help quantify improvements in cycle time, quality, automation and capacity. Provide financial analysis and decision support for cross-functional Global Functions initiatives as needed. Location Primary office location is Waltham, MA (Thermo Fisher Scientific Headquarters).

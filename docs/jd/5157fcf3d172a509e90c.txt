Position Overview: Our client is the Securitized Products group within the investment bank of a premier European-based global financial services institution located in New York City. Their ABS group is consistently ranked among the top-3 in U.S., and they consistently look to grow their platform and expand into new products. The role is on the Asset Backed Securities Financing Execution team. About Securitized Products Our clients’ Securitized Products team delivers integrated & innovative strategies to help meet the unique capital and investment needs of our clients across the securitization spectrum including financial institutions, sponsors, state-owned institutions, insurers, asset managers, funds, and corporates. Securitized Products is a client focused market making business, dealing in Asset Backed Securities (ABS), Commercial Mortgage-Backed Securities (CMBS), Agency and Non-Agency Residential Mortgage-Backed Securities (RMBS), Commercial Mortgage Back Securities Index (CMBX) and Collateralized Loan Obligations (CLO) Secondary Trading. Securitized Products also has a significant financing footprint, with desks which is focused on providing clients with longer term financing solutions across a range of assets and jurisdictions (including loan portfolios and securitizations, bridge loans, warehouse loans, and repo facilities). Overall purpose of role Role will primarily initially support our consumer ABS (autos/credit cards/specialty finance/equipment finance/BNPL) and non-traditional ABS (whole business securitization/digital infrastructure/royalty streams/IP monetization) asset classes. Key Accountabilities The key Roles and Responsibilities will include the following: Perform data analysis of collateral portfolios, historical performance statistics, transaction, and collateral cash flow projections, and expected case losses for consumer and non-traditional ABS asset classes Prepare comprehensive detailed credit memorandums containing transaction and structural analysis, credit analysis, cash flow model results, and a review of risks and mitigants. Coordinate review and approval of new transactions, as well as amendments/waivers for existing transactions with members of Group Risk Assist with conducting client, seller, and servicer due diligence in coordination with new and existing transactions Assist with structuring warehouse securitization transactions (initially in a primarily analytical capacity with a longer-term goal of developing legal and structural expertise in ABS asset classes) Assist senior members of the team with negotiating transaction legal documentation with clients, external counsel, and internal legal team members Run transaction returns using the capital models and help prepare capital committee approval documents Conduct active portfolio and transaction monitoring tasks using the company’s internally developed portfolio monitoring systems and reviewing servicer/settlement/manager reports on an ongoing basis Coordinate transaction closing mechanics with middle office, conduit funding desk, operations, legal, compliance and other internal stakeholders to close and fund new transactions, as well as support ongoing operational needs for clients (act as the primary client interface for post-close deal support) Skills & Experience 3-6 years of relevant experience preferred (securitization/ABS exposure preferred but not required) Degree in finance, economics, or a quantitative / STEM background preferred but not required (also open to structured finance legal backgrounds) Solid understanding and interest in credit markets, financial accounting, statistics, and corporate and investment banking products Strong interpersonal and communication skills, both oral and written High proficiency in Word, PowerPoint, Excel Strong quantitative and analytical skills Ability to multi-task across time sensitive deal-flow in a fast-paced environment Note: This job description is not intended to be all inclusive or exclusive.

Job Summary This role supports the global AGS service business by consolidating and analyzing P&L performance, driving forecast cycle coordination, and delivering clear financial insights to business partners. The position plays a key role in management reporting, variance analysis, and process simplification, while helping improve the efficiency and effectiveness of financial reporting. Key Responsibilities Management Reporting: Consolidate the global AGS service business P&L and monitor key metrics throughout the quarter. Provide regular reporting and insights to business partners to support decision-making. Forecast Cycle Management: Drive communication with regions on forecast deliverables, timelines, and key milestones. Coordinate meetings with regional teams to review business updates, assumptions, and risks. P&L Analysis: Take ownership of variance analysis, identify key business drivers, and develop clear messaging for leadership and stakeholders. Support ad-hoc analysis as needed. Business Partnering: Communicate financial results, trends, and risks in a clear and actionable way. Influence business partners on key decisions through fact-based analysis and recommendations. Process Improvement and Simplification: Identify opportunities to simplify financial processes and reporting. Lead or support initiatives that enhance efficiency, standardization, and effectiveness across the finance workflow. Continuous Learning and Improvement: Proactively build business knowledge, strengthen analytical capability, and adopt best practices that improve both individual and team performance.

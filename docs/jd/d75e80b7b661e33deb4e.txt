Job Description: The Job/What You'll Do: This is a unique opportunity to help build AssetMark's Private Wealth business from the ground up. As a Client Portfolio Manager, you'll work directly with high-net-worth advisors and clients while partnering closely with the Chief Investment Officer, Portfolio Management, Research, Wealth Planning, and Private Wealth Consulting teams. This is a highly visible role where you'll help shape portfolio strategy, develop investment thought leadership, and influence the future of AssetMark's Private Wealth offering. If you're passionate about investing, enjoy building relationships, and want to make a meaningful impact from day one, we'd love to meet you. The Client Portfolio Manager (CPM) delivers an exceptional investment experience for AssetMark's Private Wealth clients through comprehensive portfolio reviews, personalized investment guidance, and ongoing client engagement. Working closely with Private Wealth Consultants, the Chief Investment Officer, Portfolio Management, Research, Wealth Planning, Product, Marketing, and Financial Advisors, the CPM helps ensure portfolios remain aligned with clients' evolving objectives, market conditions, and long-term financial goals. The CPM also develops investment commentary, portfolio review content, and advisor-facing collateral that support AssetMark's investment strategies, portfolio enhancements, and new product launches. This is a full-time position in our Charlotte, NC office with a hybrid work schedule.

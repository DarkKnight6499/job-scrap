The Impact You will have in this role : As an Application Security Senior Associate, you will help strengthen the organization’s application security posture by executing security testing, assessing vulnerabilities, and partnering with application and engineering teams to drive effective remediation. You will contribute to the ongoing maturity of Application Security practices while supporting secure software development, risk management, and control requirements. Your Primary Responsibilities : Execute Software Composition Analysis (SCA), Static Application Security Testing (SAST), and Dynamic Application Security Testing (DAST) activities to provide effective application security coverage. Review application security findings, validate risk and severity, and support prioritization, remediation, retesting, and closure . Partner with application development and engineering teams to explain security findings, recommend practical remediation approaches, and track vulnerabilities through resolution. Support Application Security platforms and services through security test execution, issue analysis, operational monitoring, troubleshooting, and adherence to established processes. Apply established security standards, controls, and governance requirements across secure development and application security testing activities. Support Free and Open Source Software (FOSS) and vulnerability exception processes by reviewing supporting evidence, documenting decisions, and tracking time-bound remediation commitments. Contribute to AppSec metrics, reporting, automation, and continuous improvement initiatives to strengthen security testing coverage and overall delivery. Maintain complete and accurate documentation and evidence to support risk, control, audit, and regulatory requirements . **NOTE: The Primary Responsibilities of this role are not limited to the details above. ** Qualifications: Minimum of 6 years of related experience. Bachelor’s degree preferred or equivalent experience. Relevant experience in Application Security, secure software development, vulnerability management, or a related cybersecurity discipline. Working knowledge of SCA, SAST, and DAST concepts and the application vulnerability management lifecycle. Ability to analyze technical security findings, assess risk, and communicate remediation requirements clearly to application and engineering teams. Experience working with Application Security tools, development pipelines, ticketing or workflow systems, and structured security evidence. Knowledge of secure development practices and common application security risks, such as the OWASP Top 10, is preferred. Talents Needed for Success: Communicates technical security findings clearly and collaborates effectively with application development and engineering teams. Demonstrates sound judgment and analytical thinking when evaluating vulnerabilities, risk, and appropriate remediation actions. Works with accuracy and attention to detail, follows through on commitments, and promptly escalates risks, issues, or blockers. Demonstrates strong organizational and prioritization skills when managing multiple security findings, remediation activities, and deadlines. Approaches technical issues with curiosity and a practical, risk-based mindset focused on improving application security. Seeks feedback, shares knowledge, and contributes to the continuous improvement of Application Security processes and practices. Supports an environment where honesty, transparency, teamwork, accountability, and respect are expected.

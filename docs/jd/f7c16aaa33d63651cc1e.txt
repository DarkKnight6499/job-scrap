You will support client leads or serve as a lead of your own based on your experience, knowledge, and skillset. This is a dynamic opportunity to develop deep industry knowledge while contributing to impactful client outcomes! We will count on you to: Work with client staff, investment committees, and boards to understand needs, objectives and investment constraints. Examine and monitor developments within the economy, global capital markets, and the asset management community to communicate relevant updates to clients Thrive and be motivated by an environment that embraces AI and technology-enabled tools Work collaboratively with client team members to manage projects by defining objectives, making recommendations, and ensuring client deliverables are timely and represent best practices. Liaise with investment managers, custodians, and Mercer experts to gather and clarify client-specific data supporting service delivery.

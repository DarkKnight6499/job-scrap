Job Description: The Role As a Vice President in the Asset Management Treasurer’s Office, you will work with senior members of Asset Management, specifically collaborating with Fidelity’s alternatives center of excellence in evaluating new alternative products and designing, executing, and maintaining oversight programs for significant business processes, including accounting policy, financial reporting, valuation, expenses, distribution and governance. This position will work across the Treasurer’s Office and with various Fidelity teams such as the back-office functions, legal, risk, compliance, and investment divisions in considering product structure, unique aspects of operations and financial, regulatory and tax reporting. You will be part of the team that assesses oversight practices and operational readiness of these products, following the products through to launch and continuing oversight through the on-going operations. This role requires strong influencing skills as well as the ability to maintain and improve business partner relationships. The Expertise and skills you bring Bachelor’s degree in accounting or finance preferable Minimum 15 plus years industry or equivalent experience Certified public accounting certification (CPA) preferred Experience with various alternative asset classes, with a primary focus on private equity and private equity fund-of-fund structures Technical and business knowledge of various legal entity types (e.g., hedge funds, mutual funds, master/feeder, multi-class, open and closed end, etc). Knowledge of operational risk management and internal controls, governance and oversight processes Understands the key elements of a Sarbanes-Oxley compliance program for an alternative’s asset manager Experience evaluating opportunities for improvements in process through technology solutions Advanced technical skills in mutual fund and private fund accounting including financial reporting Advanced knowledge of investment types, fund products and investment company operations Ability to manage multiple points of view and influence others Ability to make and effectively communicate decisions Strong collaboration skills and an ability to work optimally across organizations Excellent written and verbal communication skills; Solid organizational and interpersonal skills The Team The Asset Management Treasurers Office provides fiduciary oversight of Fidelity’s funds, shareholder activity, and service providers. We use industry knowledge, technical experience, and our responsibility as fiduciaries to set and monitor adherence to fund policies, and we support the Funds’ Boards of Trustees in fulfilling their responsibilities to the Fidelity Mutual Funds. In addition to overseeing Fidelity’s existing products, we also actively support Asset Management’s efforts to expand investment capabilities by ensuring appropriate oversight is in place for new products prior to implementation.

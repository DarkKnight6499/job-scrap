Position Summary: Analyst / Associate role to join our Analytics & Data organization in the NNA Analytics and Insights team function which is responsible for Net New Assets (NNA) reporting, forecasting and insights on the key drivers of NNA - the most important and visible metric for the WM business. The candidate will be responsible for the ongoing distribution of multiple various reports to senior management and stakeholders, engaging teams across the organization to help with consolidating and streamlining NNA reporting. Additionally, you will help with determining insights on NNA forecasting for WM Business with the focus on Private Markets opportunities and analyzing competitor flows by investigating additional analytical or predictive use-cases from existing data. Key Responsibilities: Key responsibilities will include but will not be limited to the following: Assist in supporting A&D NNA reporting across multiple projects and stakeholders, including the Private Markets Analytics Collaborate with team in the US, Mumbai, and Budapest to distribute a suite of senior management reports to be produced on daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly basis. Problem solving skills – ability to identify key issues, gather data to investigate those issues and develop actionable recommendations. Analytical skills – Analyze data to identify key trends, proficient in data reconciliation, comparison and benchmarking. Data validation skills – Be able to leverage different data sources and methodologies to verify accuracy of output. Teamwork skills – The candidate must be flexible in their work style and be able to work collaboratively with others on the team. Provide reporting support as needed. Experience: Bachelor’s degree in science/engineering/mathematics; MBA or other advanced degree preferred Analyst: Experience of 2-2.5 years in data programming & SQL, preferably in the financial services industry. Required Skills: Ability to work independently and possess a strong sense of accountability/ownership. Strong written, verbal, presentation, and interpersonal skills Proficient in MS Excel, PowerPoint & Outlook Experience or knowledge in data analytics toolsets such as: SQL, Excel, Tableau, Python, Dataiku (nice to have) Ability to work in a team environment and partner with multiple individuals across various groups.

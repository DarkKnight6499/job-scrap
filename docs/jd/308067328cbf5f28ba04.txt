Requisition #: 76820 The Role at a Glance We are excited to be adding a Business Analyst to join our Group Protection Operations & Partner Data Enablement team. In this role, you will be working across Group Protection (GP), leading complex analysis and delivery activities within the Operations & Partner Data Enablement Portfolio. The work will apply deep knowledge of digital domains, products, customer journeys, business processes, and data to support product delivery, enable informed decision-making, and drive measurable business improvements. In this role, you will collaborate with Product Owners, development teams, QA/QE, business stakeholders, and subject matter experts to define, refine, validate and delivery solutions aligned with business needs and strategic objectives. This opportunity will provide the ability to grow within the group insurance industry while gaining new skills and building strong working relationships. What you'll be doing Elicit and document of GP Operations & Partner Data Enablement requirements: planning, prioritization, scope, effort estimations and traceability to downstream and upstream teams/applications. Employ business analysis best practices in the areas of elicitation, facilitation, and documentation (such as business need definition, root cause analysis, and process flow documentation) to document complex issues, presenting findings to management/leadership. Partner with the product owner throughout delivery to decompose features, develop and refine user stories and acceptance criteria, and maintain the backlog based on stakeholder feedback and priorities. Assess, select, and recommend solutions and/or alternative approaches that will achieve business goals, obtaining buy-in from business partners. Champion scope of work across development teams to ensure solutions are complete and meet business requirements. Validate that the solution meets the business need by developing acceptance criteria and evaluation plans, coordinating testing with business users. Learn the product and industry through interaction with our customers and internal subject matter experts. Provide leadership and guidance to less experienced members of the team to help them be the best they can be. Champion and enhance organizational initiatives by positively influencing and supporting change management and/or departmental/enterprise initiatives. In addition, you will identify, recommend, and champion process improvements and organizational initiatives to positively influence the team and quality. Identifies and supports opportunities to improve products, customer journeys, business processes, and ways of working through analysis, design, implementation, and change support. Adapts to changing project and team needs while strengthening collaboration through mentoring, knowledge sharing, and engagement across business areas.

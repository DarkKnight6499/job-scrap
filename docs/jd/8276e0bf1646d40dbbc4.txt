Business Overview New York Life Real Estate Investors (NYL REI) is the real estate investment platform within New York Life Investment Management. With over $73.2 billion in assets under management as of December 31, 2025, NYL REI invests across real estate equity, debt and securities strategies, serving institutional investors and the New York Life General Account. Leveraging more than a century of real estate investment experience, New York Life Real Estate Investors (“REI”) believes that investment principles which incorporate sustainability best practices promote favorable relative returns for our clients over the long term. What You'll Do: The candidate will lead investment performance analysis, portfolio risk analytics, and strategic reporting for the General Account commercial mortgage loan portfolio, with additional responsibilities supporting third-party investment portfolios as needed. This individual will serve as a key analytical partner to senior leadership, providing insights into portfolio performance, risk exposures, investment trends, and opportunities to optimize portfolio positioning. This role will also lead the development of analytical tools and reporting capabilities to enhance investment decision-making, improve operational efficiency, and support the evolving needs of the commercial mortgage investment platform. Key Responsibilities Portfolio Analytics: Lead portfolio performance measurement, benchmarking, and attribution analysis to evaluate investment results and identify key drivers of returns and risk. Analyze portfolio trends, commercial real estate market conditions, and macroeconomic factors to provide actionable insights and recommendations to senior investment leadership. Develop and maintain analytical models and tools to evaluate historical performance, forecast portfolio outcomes, and support investment decision-making. Assess portfolio risks including; concentrations by balance, maturity, property type and geographic concentrations, and credit metrics. Conduct scenario analysis and stress testing to assess the impact of changes in interest rates, property valuations, credit conditions, and other market factors on risk-based capital and portfolio risk and performance. Surveillance and Reporting: Forecast and analyze portfolio cash flows, investment returns, maturities, and refinancing exposure to support portfolio positioning and investment strategy. Develop and maintain portfolio KPIs, dashboards, and reporting tools to support ongoing portfolio monitoring and senior management presentations. Oversee the accuracy, consistency, and integrity of portfolio data, performance metrics, and management reporting. Partner with internal teams to enhance analytical capabilities, automate processes, and improve data infrastructure. Support portfolio performance commentary, strategic presentations, and reporting for the General Account and third-party investment portfolios, as needed. What You'll Bring: Bachelor’s degree required, preferably in real estate, finance, economics, accounting, or a related quantitative discipline with an investment focus. CFA charterholder or advanced degree (MBA or equivalent) or additional professional designations preferred. Minimum of 8 years of relevant experience in commercial real estate debt, commercial mortgage portfolio management, institutional investment management, or investment analytics. Strong understanding of commercial mortgage loan investments, real estate credit fundamentals, portfolio performance measurement, and risk analytics. Demonstrated experience in financial modeling, portfolio performance analysis, benchmarking, and evaluation of investment risk and returns. Advanced proficiency in Excel and experience with portfolio analytics, reporting, and data visualization tools; knowledge of SQL, Python, or Power BI preferred. Ability to work independently, exercise sound investment judgment, and serve as a thought partner to senior leadership. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to synthesize complex analytical findings and effectively engage stakeholders at all levels. Strong analytical, organizational, and project management skills, with exceptional attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a collaborative, fast-paced environment.

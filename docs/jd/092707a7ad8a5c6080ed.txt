Investment Analyst, Fundamental Equities
D. E. Shaw
Location: New York
Link: https://www.deshaw.com/careers/investment-analyst-fundamental-equities-6058

Join our D. E. Shaw group in New York The D. E. Shaw group seeks talented analyst individuals to join its Fundamental Equities group as integral members of one of the sector-focused teams. Fundamental Equities offers a collegial work environment and the chance to collaborate with an innovative and collaborative team. Support fundamental investment strategies. Formulate and apply mathematical modeling and other optimizing methods to develop and interpret information to make trade decisions. Collect and analyze data and develop algorithms, forecasting models, and trading tools for the analysis of companies. Build financial models that pertain to current and prospective investments in public equities. Analyze business models and strategic imperatives for current and potential investments and update current and future valuation expectations. Utilize statistical and machine-learning techniques to generate trading ideas and manage risk. Maintain relationships with management teams, research analysts, and industry experts. A bachelor’s degree in economics, finance, international business, or a related field of study. One year of experience with formulating and applying mathematical modeling and optimizing methods to develop and interpret information to make investment decisions; collecting and analyzing data and developing forecasting models and investment tools for the analysis of companies; building financial models that pertain to current and prospective investments; analyzing business models and strategic imperatives for current and potential investments and updating current and future valuation expectations; and utilizing statistical techniques to generate investment ideas and manage risk. As is customary for the employer, applicants who pass initial screening will be required to complete and pass a standard pre-hire assessment test. The annual base salary for this position is $250,000 - $300,000. Our compensation and benefits package includes variable compensation in the form of a year-end bonus, guaranteed in the first year of hire, and benefits including medical and prescription drug coverage, 401(k) contribution matching, wellness reimbursement, family building benefits, and a charitable gift match program.

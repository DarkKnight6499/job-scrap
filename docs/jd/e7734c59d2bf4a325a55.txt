About Investment Data and Analytics Our organization’s mission is to build technology that lets the firm systematize and scale its understanding of private markets and management of our funds. Our team works side-by-side with investors and quantitative researchers to innovate technology-driven investment processes; solve broad industry problems ranging from AI driven digitization of unstructured data to systematic monitoring of private equity portfolios. The Associate role combines hands-on engineering execution with the opportunity to build deep expertise in private markets data. Working under the guidance of senior engineers, you will develop, test, and operate the pipelines and datasets our investment and research teams rely on — taking on progressively more complex ownership as your technical and domain judgment grows. What you will do: Data engineering — build, test, and deploy pipelines and transformations across financial/economic datasets using modern data stacks. Infrastructure/platform engineering — run and monitor production data workflows, troubleshoot failures, and implement alerting, logging, and automated testing within CI/CD. Data Quality — develop validation and reconciliation checks, monitor pipeline health through KPIs, and own root-cause analysis for data issues. Collaboration — partner with data engineering, DevOps, application, and investment teams to clarify requirements and deliver well-documented solutions. And ... Problem solving — perform deep data analysis to support investment model research and identify manual processes worth automating. What you bring: Demonstrated ability in data engineering and analysis, including data-centric testing and deployment of FinTech applications. Proficiency in SQL (MSSQL or equivalent RDBMS) and Python, with experience building ETL pipelines for large datasets. Experience with DBT and Azure Data Factory and Pipelines; exposure to Snowflake, MS Synapse, or Power BI a plus. Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills with outstanding attention to detail, a genuine interest in financial markets, and comfort working in a scrum/agile SDLC environment.

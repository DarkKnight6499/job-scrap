Follow us on LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, and our YouTube channel – Cleveland Fed Conducts advanced analysis and policy directed research that contributes to internal publications, external publications, policy advising, and outreach. As part of a multidisciplinary and collaborative team of researchers and professionals, you will work in all areas of policy advising and support directly for the Bank President and senior Bank principles. We are part of the Executive function. The Senior Policy analyst will analyze and research on issues that affect financial markets and monetary policy across a broad range of topic areas, expanding the bank’s capabilities to monitor and assess the impacts of market developments and functioning on policy. You will use database knowledge to compile statistical data, create data exploration tools, conduct initial data exploration on your own and work with senior staff members on their research. This is an outstanding opportunity for someone who is highly motivated to learn about monetary policy and financial markets, while contributing to impactful work directly connected to the key policy making responsibilities of the Federal Reserve. Essential Accountabilities Support team deliverables to advise Reserve Bank President, other senior Bank leaders, and the public on matters related to financial markets and monetary policy through published reports and presentations. Develop and maintain materials and tools to educate internal and external stakeholders on key issues affecting financial markets, market functioning, and monetary policy. Support in the writing and editing of speeches for the Reserve Bank President and other principles. Develop and maintain strong relationships with financial industry leaders, as well as key stakeholders across the Federal Reserve System to advance Bank-level objectives Conducts advanced directed research and analysis for internal and external publications and presentations. Leads the recruitment and training of new team members. Attends department seminars and training sessions to enhance economic knowledge and develop technical skills. Performs other duties as assigned or requested. Education and Experience Bachelor's Degree in finance, economics, mathematics, statistics, computer science, or related field. 3-5 years of as an analyst in financial industry, regulatory agency, or academic institution. Knowledge Areas – Advanced: Knowledge of finance, monetary policy, macroeconomics, or other related fields.

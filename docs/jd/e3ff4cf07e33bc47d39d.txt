Secondary Team The HarbourVest Secondary Investment Team has been a pioneer and leader in the secondary market since making its first investment in 1986. The team recently closed one of the largest secondaries funds ever raised with $15.1B of commitments (at the hard cap) for their oversubscribed Dover Street XI fund combined with a further $3.4B for secondary co-investment opportunities. When paired with other pools of secondary capital the firm manages, HarbourVest is investing over $11B of equity per year into the secondary market globally across 20-25+ transactions. While HarbourVest invests across both the GP-led and LP-led market, the firm is widely considered a leader in the rapidly expanding GP-led Continuation Fund market where the team is actively leading and structuring some of the largest transactions in the market today. Currently, there are over 70 investment professionals on the team today spread across North America, Europe, and Asia Pacific.

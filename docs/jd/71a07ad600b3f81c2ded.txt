The Impact you will have in this role: This role is responsible for supporting the execution of the company’s IT Sourcing strategy, including hardware, software, telecom/networking, private cloud, public cloud, and IT professional services. The Senior Associate will execute sourcing initiatives across IT categories and partner closely with IT stakeholders to drive value, mitigate risk, and support alignment with DTCC’s financial and operational objectives. The Senior Associate will collaborate across the organization with Finance, Legal, Third-Party Risk, Procurement Operations, and IT stakeholders to execute sourcing initiatives and support the delivery of strategic sourcing outcomes. The ideal candidate will be able to quickly ramp into active sourcing initiatives, manage multiple workstreams, and operate effectively with guidance from IT Strategic Sourcing leadership. Job Description: The Senior Associate, IT Strategic Sourcing will have cross-functional responsibilities including the following: Partner with IT stakeholders to support the execution of sourcing strategies aligned to business needs and control objectives. Manage sourcing initiatives across IT categories, including RFx development, supplier evaluation, commercial analysis, and contract negotiations. Serve as a hands-on sourcing professional responsible for developing RFx materials, conducting commercial and contractual analysis, and supporting sourcing initiatives through completion. Support supplier relationship management activities to promote effective performance, value delivery, and risk mitigation. Execute sourcing processes, including contracting and downstream procurement activities, in alignment with policies and procedures. Proactively manage assigned renewals and sourcing pipelines to drive savings and minimize business disruption. Support vendor QBRs and performance discussions for key suppliers. Analyze spend and contract data to identify savings opportunities and support sourcing decision-making. Execute sourcing initiatives against defined financial, operational, and risk-based objectives. Coordinate with internal stakeholders to ensure sourcing timelines, requirements, and deliverables are effectively managed. Support and participate in supplier negotiations to achieve competitive pricing and contractual terms. Contribute to the development and enhancement of sourcing processes, tools, and reporting. Partner with IT Strategic Sourcing Directors and Associate Directors to execute category strategies, sourcing priorities, and performance targets. Collaborate with other members of the Strategic Sourcing team and share sourcing knowledge and best practices.

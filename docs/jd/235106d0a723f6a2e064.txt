Supervisor, Treasury
AmTrust Financial Services
Location: Jersey City, NJ, US
Link: https://careers-amtrustgroup.icims.com/jobs/20944/supervisor%2c-treasury/job

Overview Oversee the organization’s cash and investment portfolio utilizing TMS (Treasury Management System) to ensure appropriate cash flow levels are maintained and ensure assets are safe guarded with investment returns commensurate with the risk profile outlined by the Board of Directors. Maintains a solid understanding of AmTrust’s mission, vision, and values. Upholds the standards of the AmTrust organization. Responsibilities Manages a team with a diverse array of talents and responsibilities. Implements and manages changes and interventions to ensure project goals are achieved. Manage cash balances to ensure that the corporation and its subsidiaries have adequate liquidity to meet operating cash needs. Identify and implement best practice process and procedures to centralize and integrate cash management for maximum efficiency. Oversee day-to-day cash, cash investments, liquidity management activities and ensure the safeguard of assets utilizing the TMS. Coordinate and ensure business units compile rolling weekly cashflow forecasts and manage seasonal liquidity to ensure adequate funding for large payments. Support the finance team in identifying and analyzing investment opportunities and make recommendations to management for execution. Provide reporting to accounting, management and board of directors. Review and approve weekly payment proposals and ensure payments are processed timely. Monitor and optimize bank fees, lockboxes and card services in support of established guidelines. Facilitate the selection, placement of new employees, and guide/coach employees’ work behavior and development to result in a motivated and productive workforce, who have the skills, knowledge and abilities to function effectively to achieve/exceed quality and productivity goals. Participate in continual improvement/quality initiatives, policies and processes in order to achieve quality and profitability goals. Assist and participate in special project and strategic planning activities as needed. Keeps current with market trends and demands. Performs other functionally related duties as assigned. Qualifications Required: Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Business OR Economics. Minimum 5 years related experience in a Treasury/Finance/Accounting team. Experience using Treasury Management Systems AND/OR Banking Software. Advanced proficiency in Excel (ability to utilize a wide array of formulas and PivotTables) and PowerPoint. Ability to proactively solve problems and escalate issues through the proper channels and strong, polished presence with quantitative intuition. Strong quantitative AND/OR risk analysis skills. Strong analytical AND/OR financial modeling skills. Effective written and verbal communication skills Demonstrated supervisory skills. Proactive, detailed-oriented, flexible, good time management skills, reliable. Ability to quickly learn new technology systems and software Excellent organizational and analytical skills with superior problem-solving ability. Able to work in a fast-paced environment with little supervision. Must be a self-starter.

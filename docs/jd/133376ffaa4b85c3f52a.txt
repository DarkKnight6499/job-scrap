Create client marketing materials and participate in deal pitches and diligence meetings. Conduct in-depth collateral analysis to synthesize information from data tapes, run cash flow models and optimize capital structures. Prepare presentations and data analysis for rating agencies and coordinate the structured finance rating process for both warehouse lending and capital markets transactions. Create investor presentations and participate in roadshow activities, including investor meetings and calls. Review and comment on legal documentation for warehouse lending and capital markets facilities. Experience creating marketing materials. Strong data analysis skills, including analyzing collateral tapes and running cash flow models. Experience reviewing legal documentation for commercial and nontraditional securitization transactions. Knowledge of transaction structural features of commercial and nontraditional securitizations.

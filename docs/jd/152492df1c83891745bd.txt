The Difference You Make: Supporting the Business Credit Center within the Operations & Administration the Analyst, Portfolio Administration is responsible for driving activities relating to the management of commercial loans within the Business Credit Center portfolio. Delivering an industry-leading customer experience for our business clients and manage the administrative functions related to general customer service requests, loan maturities and documentation, credit quality maintenance, risk mitigation and management, and collateral tracking. This position will require significant customer contact and will liaison with Relationship Managers to co-manage existing client relationships. The ability to work in a fast-paced environment, multi-task, and prioritize workflow is critical. As an Analyst, the individual will handle time-sensitive customer service requests and will be directly engaged with clients and Bank Operations staff to promptly resolve issues. Manage a portfolio of Small Business loans and alert senior management to issues which affect portfolio asset quality. Deliver superior customer service to business relationships and ensure consistent client experiences throughout the Bank's footprint. Drive the renewal and modification process for loans, lines and letters of credit by coordinating the underwriting and closing processes. Perform routine, thorough reviews of relationships in assigned loan portfolio to ensure accurate risk grades and data integrity at all times. Identify revenue generation and cross-sell opportunities by mining existing customer base and working closely with sales partners. Support the Bank's loan payment collection efforts to remain within targets for past dues and charge-offs. Identify and support the transfer of problem loans to the Commercial workout unit.

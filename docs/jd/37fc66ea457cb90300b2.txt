Job description Role Overview Evercore is seeking an Associate to join its Real Estate Strategy & Operations team in New York. Working closely with the Head of Real Estate & Construction, this individual will support the firm's global real estate portfolio, including space planning, lease management, real estate transactions, project coordination and portfolio analysis. The ideal candidate has a background in architecture or design, strong AutoCAD skills, and an interest in expanding into the broader business and strategic aspects of corporate real estate. Key Responsibilities Global Portfolio Strategy & Analytics Assist with collecting and organizing information for potential office expansions, consolidations, relocations and reconfigurations. Space Planning Use AutoCAD to review, interpret and, as required, modify floor plans, test fits, seating plans and other space-planning documentation. Assist in reviewing office layouts and space requirements to understand capacity, suitability and potential configurations. Help prepare preliminary test fits and space-planning scenarios under the guidance of senior team members. Maintain accurate floor plans and related space and occupancy documentation across the portfolio. Assist in documenting business and workplace requirements for external architects and designers. Transactions & Lease Management Develop an understanding of the firm's lease portfolio, including key lease terms, critical dates, renewal requirements and other portfolio considerations. Assist with tracking key lease terms, critical dates and transaction milestones, while maintaining accurate lease and portfolio records. Support the evaluation of lease renewals, acquisitions, expansions, contractions, relocations and other real estate transactions. Prepare side-by-side comparisons of transaction options using information and criteria provided by the team with senior guidance. Provide administrative and analytical support during transaction reviews, approvals and execution. Data, Reporting & Executive Decision Support Assist in preparing analyses, briefing materials and presentations for internal and senior stakeholders. Organize real estate, financial and operational information into clear summaries, tables and presentation materials. Update and maintain portfolio dashboards, project status reports, option comparisons and other recurring reporting. Help improve the quality, consistency and accessibility of portfolio, occupancy and real estate data. Maintain organized and accurate project, portfolio, lease and drawing documentation. Build collaborative working relationships with Real Estate, Facilities, Finance, HCG (HR), IT, Legal and business teams, as well as external partners. Project Coordination/Administration Assist with project coordination across planning, design, construction, occupancy and close-out activities. Help document project requirements, meeting notes, scope items and next steps with input from the team. Help coordinate meetings, information requests and follow-ups with architects, engineers, project managers and other consultants. Participate in the review of design, program, schedule, budget and other project considerations. Take on additional tasks and defined workstreams as skills and experience develop. Assist with project close-out documentation and lessons-learned summaries. Qualifications 1–3 years of experience in architecture, workplace design, space planning or a related field; relevant internships considered. Bachelor's degree in Architecture, Design or a related discipline preferred. Intermediate to advanced AutoCAD proficiency and ability to read, interpret and modify architectural floor plans. Proficiency in Excel and PowerPoint; strong analytical and organizational skills. Detail-oriented, proactive and comfortable managing multiple priorities. Strong communication skills and ability to work with internal stakeholders and external partners. Interest in developing knowledge of corporate real estate strategy, transactions and operations.

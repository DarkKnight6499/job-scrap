We are rated a Top Workplace by the Chicago Tribune (past 12 years) and Employee Recommended award by the Globe & Mail (past 6 years) Competitive pay and annual discretionary bonus eligible Comprehensive benefit package including medical, dental, vision, life, a 401k plan with a generous company match and tuition reimbursement to name a few Family-friendly work hours Promote from within culture The Syndications Associate supports the Bank's syndicated lending platform, working closely with Senior Syndicators in the execution, administration, and ongoing management of C&I, Franchise Finance, and Sponsor Finance syndicated credit facilities. The role participates throughout the transaction lifecycle, including the preparation of marketing materials, underwriting support, syndication activities, loan closings, lender communications, portfolio reporting, amendments, waivers, settlements, portfolio monitoring, and other Administrative Agent responsibilities. In addition, the Associate serves as the primary analyst and underwriter for the Bank's Small Business Investment Company (SBIC) portfolio. Responsibilities include fund underwriting, investment due diligence, portfolio monitoring, valuation and performance reporting, and support of CRA-related initiatives. The role is responsible for management reporting related to the SBIC portfolio activity and cross-sell activity within the SBIC portfolio. Over time, the Associate is expected to assume increasing ownership of the SBIC program and become an important contributor to the Sponsor Finance team's strategic growth initiatives. What You’ll Do: Support the Bank's syndicated lending platform across C&I, Franchise Finance, and Sponsor Finance credit facilities. Prepare marketing materials, lender presentations, confidential information memoranda, term sheets, commitment letters, and syndication packages; supporting syndication and closing activities; administering lender communications, notices, and reporting; coordinating amendments, waivers, assignments, and settlements; assisting with loan booking, operational issue resolution, and documentation management; and supporting portfolio monitoring, covenant compliance reviews, and annual credit review processes. Serve as the primary analyst supporting the Bank's SBIC program. Investment underwriting, due diligence, portfolio monitoring, valuation analysis, performance reporting, and support of CRA-related initiatives. Manage the SBIC Investment Committee process, including meeting schedules, agendas, presentation materials, minutes, and follow-up tracking. Coordinate with internal stakeholders, fund managers, and Sponsor Finance personnel to ensure investment, reporting, and portfolio management deliverables are completed on a timely basis. Prepare management reporting to monitor sponsor relationships and cross-sell activity arising from SBIC investments, supporting measurement of the Bank's success in leveraging SBIC investments into broader banking relationships Support process improvement initiatives, AI and automation projects, vendor evaluations, and enhancements to marketing materials and presentations. Participate in annual sales meetings and broader Capital Markets initiatives while developing knowledge of FX, derivatives, treasury management, and other Bank products. Maintain strong working relationships across business lines, support the development of junior team members, respond promptly to internal requests, and demonstrate a professional and collaborative approach in all interactions.

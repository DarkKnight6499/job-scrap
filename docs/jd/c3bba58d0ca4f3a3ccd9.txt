Essential Functions Design and develop agent components and templates for the organization. Build and orchestrate agentic AI workflows that coordinate multiple tools, models, and data sources. Lead small-scale implementation projects, coordinating with stakeholders and team members. Create and maintain standardized documentation and "recipe cards" for AI solutions. Conduct quality assurance testing and debugging of agent components. Evaluate, configure, and manage Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers and integrations.

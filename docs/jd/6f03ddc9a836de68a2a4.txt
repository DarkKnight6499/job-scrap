Our New York office is looking for an Analyst / Associate candidate to join the Strategic Investments team. Strategic Investments makes and manages investments on behalf of Morgan Stanley across market infrastructure, trading and wealth management platforms, and innovative technology companies that are complementary to the Firm’s overall strategic agenda. Team members manage the full investment lifecycle from sourcing, selection, evaluation, due diligence, negotiation and closing to post-investment monitoring / strategic involvement and exit. Responsibilities include the preparation of detailed valuation analyses and presentations to the Investment Committee, as well as research and monitoring of industry developments relevant to the Strategic Investments portfolio and Morgan Stanley’s businesses. The individual will work closely with the rest of the team as well as colleagues across products and functions firmwide to identify and assess investment opportunities and manage the portfolio. Team members who succeed in this group bring both creative and structured analytical thinking, a strong passion for investing, and a commitment to building deep, enduring partnerships with colleagues, investors, and technology companies.

Position Overview As an Analyst II, Credit Risk, you will play a critical role in shaping CarMax Auto Finance’s credit strategies and driving operational excellence. In this dynamic position, you’ll leverage advanced data analytics, statistical modeling, and database tools to analyze, test, and implement credit product strategies that enhance both business performance and customer experience. Your insights will help optimize credit processes, improve originations, and deliver actionable recommendations to senior leadership. You’ll also design and maintain reporting dashboards for key metrics such as originations, bookings, and sales trends, ensuring timely and accurate updates for decision-making. This role demands a strong commitment to compliance and operational risk controls, aligning with CarMax’s reputation for integrity and transparency. Why CarMax?

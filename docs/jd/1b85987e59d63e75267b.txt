Job Description Join a fast-paced operations team where accuracy, responsiveness, and collaboration make a meaningful impact. As a Custodial Records Associate, you will process important file-release requests for banks, servicers, issuers, investors, and other business partners. You will review incoming requests, verify required information and authorized signers, accurately enter information into internal systems, and initiate requests for physical files. Your work helps ensure files move efficiently through the Vault, release verification, and shipping teams. What You’ll Do Review release requests received through a shared mailbox and confirm that all required forms and information have been provided. Validate authorized signers, loan information, release details, destinations, and shipping information. Accurately enter information into internal systems and initiate file-pull requests. Process payoff, litigation, foreclosure, court-related, servicing, and other file-release requests. Route inquiries and requests to the appropriate department when another team’s support is required. Coordinate with the Vault, release verification, shipping, Client Service Management, Structured Finance, and other front-office and back-office teams. Manage changing priorities and time-sensitive requests while maintaining accuracy. Communicate professionally with customers and business partners, including during challenging or urgent situations. Use Microsoft Excel to sort information and locate details within spreadsheets. Support work across multiple DCS locations based on team workload and business needs What Will Help You Succeed You will be successful in this role if you are detail-oriented, adaptable, and comfortable working independently in a computer-based processing environment. The team handles approximately 30 to 40 emails each day, and priorities can change quickly when urgent requests are received Strong candidates will bring: Strong customer service and professional communication skills A high level of attention to detail and accuracy The ability to manage deadlines and changing priorities General Microsoft Excel experience, including sorting and locating information Flexibility and adaptability in a fast-paced environment The ability to collaborate effectively with several partner teams Sound judgment when handling sensitive or high-risk requests Basic Qualifications Associate's degree or vocational training, or equivalent work experience Three or more years of related experience Physical requirements (with or without reasonable accommodation) Ability to push, pull, and/or lift 20 lbs or more; standing for long periods; able to bend or squat; willingness to climb stepladders up to 10 feet high.

Micron Technology is a global leader in memory and storage innovation, accelerating the transformation of information into intelligence. The High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) Heterogeneous Integration Group (HIG) develops advanced memory solutions that power next-generation Machine Learning (ML), Artificial Intelligence (AI), and high-performance computing applications. Our team brings together experts across design, validation, manufacturing, test, and quality to deliver industry-leading products through a highly collaborative and innovative environment. As a Design Validation Product Engineer, you will play a critical role in the development and validation of next-generation HBM products from design through qualification and high-volume manufacturing. This position provides the opportunity to work at the intersection of semiconductor design, silicon validation, hardware and software debugging, and data analysis while collaborating with multi-functional teams around the world. The level of responsibility will be aligned with a combination of education, experience, and demonstrated technical capabilities. Responsibilities Develop expertise in JEDEC HBM specifications, internal architecture documentation, and HBM design schematics and layouts. Perform silicon validation and characterization of HBM products using laboratory equipment, circuit simulations, debugging techniques, and data analysis. Collaborate closely with Product Engineering, Design, Manufacturing, Test, Quality, and other multi-functional teams to identify and resolve technical issues throughout the product lifecycle. Analyze validation data, identify trends, and implement solutions that improve product performance, quality, reliability, and manufacturability. Apply AI-Assisted and AI-Enabled tools, including data analysis and debugging solutions, to improve validation efficiency, accelerate root-cause analysis, and support engineering decision-making. Minimum Qualifications Bachelor's degree or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field. Experience with one or more of the following: schematic analysis, circuit simulation, data analysis, laboratory debugging, or pattern manipulation. Demonstrated problem-solving and analytical skills through academic projects, internships, research, or professional experience. Ability to manage multiple priorities effectively and deliver high-quality work in a fast-paced environment. Proven ability to collaborate and communicate effectively within a team-based engineering organization. Preferred Qualifications Coursework or experience in VLSI design, semiconductor processes, Python, and C/C++ programming. Experience with Verilog, VHDL, or SystemVerilog. Knowledge of memory architectures, semiconductor validation methodologies, or high-speed interface technologies. Experience Leveraging AI or Artificial Intelligence tools to support engineering analysis, automation, debugging, or productivity improvements. Demonstrated initiative, innovation, and strong written and verbal communication skills, with a passion for continuous learning and process improvement.

Overview of the Position The Retail Banking Risk Specialist is instrumental in maintaining Retail Banking’s risk and compliance posture, performing daily risk-related monitoring across all financial center operations while identifying, assessing, and mitigating operational and compliance risks proactively. The Retail Banking Risk Specialist oversees business continuity program implementation and continuous oversight, ensuring readiness and resilience across the organization. This individual applies rigorous analytical thinking to diagnose business performance, identify critical organizational levers, and translate data-driven insights into decisive action—particularly in areas of risk assessment, issue remediation, and process optimization. Exercising strong independent judgment and decision-making skills, managing competing priorities with excellent attention to detail and accuracy while supporting team members and providing guidance that adheres to bank guidelines, policies, and applicable laws. Work experience : 7+ years of experience in retail banking operations, reconciliation, or a related support function.

Finance & Strategy Investment Analyst The Opportunity: The right mixture of great ideas and funding can create change. In a complex organization, allocating funding to where it can be most effective can be challenging. That’s why we need a financial analyst like you who can navigate the requirements, policies, and regulations that govern funding, to make sure that the Government’s resources can make the most impact. As a financial analyst on our team, you will help to drive national and economic security by leading due diligence and financial modeling for a government-backed investment initiative targeting frontier innovations. You will assess scalable investment opportunities across all stages, from emerging startups to established public market innovators. You will collaborate with top-tier investment professionals to formulate investment strategies, structure deal terms, and develop data-backed financial narratives across multiple industry verticals. You will also translate expert-level technical and market insights into actionable intelligence, strengthening multi-disciplinary due diligence and strategic investment outcomes. Join us. The world can’t wait. You Have: Experience with financial market dynamics Experience translating complex investment data and delivering polished, executive-ready briefings Experience with Excel modeling, including functions and formulas Experience synthesizing a large volume of information into an investment-style writing sample such as investment theses, equity research reports, investment memos, market outlooks, or whitepapers Experience formulating investment strategies, identifying core risk vectors, and optimizing return mechanics across a variety of industries and investment stages Experience conducting financial due diligence using financial statements, KPIs, and market data Experience developing macro and micro market landscapes and establishing investment narratives around this information Ability to be a strategic and highly adaptable professional with a passion for next-generation technology, apply rapid knowledge integration and execution in fast-moving, high-pressure environments, and pay strict attention to detail Ability to obtain and maintain a Public Trust or Suitability/Fitness determination based on client requirements Bachelor's degree in Business Administration or Finance and 2+ years of experience with investment banking, early-stage startup, strategy advising, sell-side analytics, investment research, or venture capital environments, or Master's degree in Business Administration and 1+ years of experience with investment banking, early-stage startup, strategy advising, sell-side analytics, investment research, or venture capital environments Nice If You Have: Experience building dynamic financial frameworks and investment tracking tools CFA Level I, II, or III Candidate or CPA license Certification Vetting: Applicants selected will be subject to a government investigation and may need to meet eligibility requirements of the U.S. government client.

Human Capital Client Leader
Aon
Location: Irvine, California; Los Angeles, California
Link: https://jobs.aon.com/jobs/106369

The Strategic Client Leader position is a senior level role that serves as the primary business liaison for HR Solutions between Aon and its clients. While the role is multifaceted, in short, the Strategic Client Leader has overall accountability and responsibility for Aon’s relationship with the client. At a strategic level, the role is encouraged to develop an in-depth understanding of their client’s business. They should also understand the strategies senior management will deploy to drive desired business outcomes. That understanding is typically derived from meaningful discussions with C-suite contacts and their direct reports. Equipped with a clear strategic business purpose, the Client Leader works closely with the client and Aon subject matter experts. They explore and offer Aon’s thought leadership, innovation, and services to improve the client’s human capital strategy. This supports the client’s overall business strategy. At a functional level, this role serves as an integrator ensuring that the various Aon team members are client focused, providing consulting and operational insights based on the client situation and delivering a high-quality product and/or service consistent with the Aon Client Promise and continuous improvement principles. Aon is in the business of better decisions At Aon, we shape decisions for the better to protect and enrich the lives of people around the world. As an organization, we are united through trust as one inclusive team and we are passionate about helping our colleagues and clients succeed.

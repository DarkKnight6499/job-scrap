Description Summary: The Commercial Bank Business Risk Officer, Sr. will have responsibility for driving and overseeing key risk activities for the assigned Commercial Business Unit(s) (i.e., Corporate, Specialty & Government Banking; Commercial Real Estate; Middle Market Banking; Commercial Administration Services; Asset Finance; Capital Markets, etc.). Key risk activities include identifying, monitoring, measuring, mitigating, and reporting on the Business Unit’s risk and control environment. Initially, this position will cover Middle Market Banking and core credit processes and systems for the Commercial Bank. This leader will play a lead role in establishing the risk management strategy, execution model, and culture for the organization. Additionally, this position will support and manage a team of Segment Risk colleagues through direct or indirect reporting lines.

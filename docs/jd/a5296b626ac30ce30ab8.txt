Start your Voyage – Apply Now Profile Summary: As a key leader within Voya Investment Management’s Investment Pricing & Valuation function, the position within Private Debt Valuation will play a significant role in the end-to-end valuation framework for private debt investments. The role will partner closely with investment teams, finance, risk, compliance, auditors, and external valuation providers to help ensure valuations are well governed, appropriately supported, and aligned with relevant accounting and regulatory requirements. Profile Description: Valuation Oversight • Apply deep valuation expertise to private debt, commercial mortgage loans, infrastructure debt, asset-based finance, and other complex or illiquid credit instruments, including evaluation of contractual cash flows, capital structures, credit spreads, liquidity, default risk, recoveries, and collateral. • Review and challenge valuation methodologies, assumptions, inputs, and conclusions to support well-reasoned fair value determinations. • Perform oversight of valuation exceptions, price challenges, back-testing, calibration, and other valuation review activities. • Maintain clear and defensible documentation supporting valuation conclusions and governance decisions. Methodology and Provider Oversight • Serve as Voya’s senior technical counterpart to external valuation providers covering illiquid assets, critically evaluating methodologies, models, market evidence, and valuation conclusions and leading the resolution of significant valuation differences. • Conduct provider due diligence, methodology reviews, performance assessments, and ongoing monitoring activities. • Evaluate valuation approaches and models utilized by external providers, including discounted cash flow, market, transaction, and yield-based methodologies. • Provide effective challenge when valuation outcomes, assumptions, or methodologies appear inconsistent with market conditions or available evidence. Governance and Risk Management • Develop and evolve valuation policies, procedures, governance forums, and escalation processes. • Identify valuation risks and control enhancement opportunities and help implement sustainable solutions. • Prepare materials and present valuation matters to valuation committees, senior management, auditors, and other stakeholders. • Provide valuation subject-matter oversight for the use of private debt fair values across accounting, investment book of record, NAV, and client-reporting processes. • Respond to audit, regulatory, client, and due diligence inquiries related to private debt valuation. Leadership and Business Partnership • Lead, develop, and mentor valuation professionals while promoting high standards of quality, accountability, and execution. • Partner with investment teams, accounting, operations, technology, legal, compliance, and risk functions to address valuation matters and support business initiatives. • Serve as the primary liaison for private debt valuation matters with auditors and clients. Operating Model and Continuous Improvement • Support the evolution of Voya IM's private debt valuation operating model. • Drive process improvements, automation and AI initiatives, and enhancements to valuation reporting and controls. • Monitor and keep pace with changing industry practices, valuation methodologies, and regulatory developments that may impact private debt valuation. Knowledge & Experience: • Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Mathematics, or a related field. • 8+ years of experience in valuation, valuation advisory, credit analysis, audit, risk management, or related fields, with deep knowledge of financial metrics, capital structures, and financial statements. • Strong knowledge of ASC 820 Fair Value Measurement, SEC Rule 2a-5 and valuation practices for illiquid credit investments. • Experience applying valuation methodologies such as discounted cash flow (DCF), comparable company analysis, and precedent transaction analysis. • Demonstrated ability to review, understand, and challenge complex valuation models and methodologies. • Strong project management skills and understanding of internal business controls. • Sound independent judgment in complex valuation scenarios requiring significant professional judgment. • Prior experience leading teams and developing investment or valuation professionals. • Experience with private credit, commercial mortgage loans, infrastructure debt, asset-based finance, or other specialty credit sectors. • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including experience presenting valuation matters to senior management, valuation committees, auditors, and clients. • Experience working with auditors and third-party service providers, including pricing vendors and administrators. • Highly organized and detail-oriented, with a strong control mindset and focus on accuracy and risk management. • High level of ethics, values, and integrity. #LI-MG1 Compensation Pay Disclosure: Voya is committed to pay that’s fair and equitable, which means comparable pay for comparable roles and responsibilities.

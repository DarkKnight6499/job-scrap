The Role
Nitro is an AI-native data platform that democratizes life sciences data for non-technical commercial teams, turning fragmented HCP/HCO, prescription, and industry data into decisions the field can act on. We're looking for a QA Engineer to drive quality for the platform and help modernize how we test it. Here, testing isn't a gate bolted onto the end of shipping, it's part of the same function as building and deploying. You'll own your share of that full chain. This is a junior role for someone who wants to build a real career in QA and automation. You'll start hands-on with day-to-day testing and grow into broader ownership of our test automation as you develop. We're investing in making our testing environment more AI-forward, and you'll have real room to shape and extend that work, not just maintain what exists. The function itself is evolving, too: our Operations org is moving from traditional QA toward evaluating AI-native systems, and this role is your entry point into where that's headed. 
 What You'll Do  Own day-to-day QA across releases: new data connectors, schema changes, customer rollouts, platform upgrades, ensuring changes are solid before they reach customers Help cut down false-failure noise in the test suite and improve how efficiently it runs, so the team can trust what it's telling them Get close enough to the business logic behind our data pipelines to know not just whether a test passes, but whether it's testing the right thing Build new automations as new features roll out Requirements  Genuine interest in building a career in QA and automation. Entry-level to ~1-2 years of QA or testing experience Comfortable moving between technical detail and business context. You care about why a test matters, not just whether it passes Familiarity with coding with Claude or similar Strong foundations in Java and SQL Proficiency with version control systems (Git) Nice to Have  Familiarity with API/UI test automation frameworks and tools like Cucumber, Selenium Experience with AWS (EC2, S3, IAM) Knowledge of CI/CD tools like Jenkins Interviewing with Veeva We value your time and believe in a transparent hiring process.

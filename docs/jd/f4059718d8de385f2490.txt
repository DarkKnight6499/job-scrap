Credit Analyst - Convertible Arbitrage - New York - Two Sigma Careers Credit Analyst - Convertible Arbitrage Location NY New York United States Business Investment Management Function Quantitative Research Experience Level Experienced Apply Share this job Email LinkedIn Position Summary Two Sigma is a leading quantitative investment management and trading firm. The company applies a scientific approach to investing, combining cutting-edge technology, artificial intelligence, data science, and quantitative research with rigorous human inquiry to capitalize on market opportunities and deliver alpha for investors. Our team of engineers, quantitative researchers and data scientists looks beyond the traditional to test hypotheses and develop creative solutions to some of the world’s most complex economic problems. We are looking for a credit analyst to join the Convertible Arbitrage team within Derivatives and Relative Value (DRV), a modeling team that aims to expand the spectrum of asset classes and strategies used for systematic investment management at Two Sigma. Our convertible arbitrage strategy identifies mispriced convertible securities and captures the difference between market price and fair value by trading the convertible and hedging its exposures with equities, equity options, Treasury futures, corporate bonds and credit default swaps. Our goal is to apply Two Sigma's data science and systematic trading expertise to convertible arbitrage, and this role will bring fundamental credit expertise to that effort. You will take on the following responsibilities: Conduct fundamental credit analysis on convertible issuers, including capital structure, liquidity, covenants, recovery and refinancing risk Assess credit risk on positions under stress, and work with portfolio managers on sizing, hedging and exit decisions Provide analytical support for primary market opportunities, including new issuance, buybacks and other capital markets transactions Develop issuer-level views that support engagement with issuers and counterparties Monitor credit exposures across the portfolio and flag emerging risks proactively Help build data-driven tools and processes, including LLM-based and agentic workflows, that make credit analysis faster and more scalable You should possess the following qualifications: 5–10 years of experience in fundamental credit analysis, on the buy side (credit, distressed or convertible funds) or sell side (leveraged finance, high-yield or convertible research) A deep understanding of corporate capital structures, credit documentation and recovery analysis Familiarity with convertible bonds, or with how equity and credit interact across the capital structure; hands-on convertible experience is a strong plus Proficiency using AI and agentic workflows for research and analysis (e.g., using LLM tools to process filings, transcripts and credit documents) Comfort working with and analyzing data programmatically; Python or similar experience is a plus Strong analytical and communication skills, and the ability to present concise, well-reasoned recommendations to portfolio managers You will enjoy the following benefits: Core Benefits: Fully paid medical and dental insurance premiums for employees and dependents, competitive 401k match, employer-paid life & disability insurance Perks: Onsite gyms with laundry service, wellness activities, casual dress, snacks, game rooms Learning: Tuition reimbursement, conference and training sponsorship Time Off: Generous vacation and unlimited sick days, competitive paid caregiver leaves Hybrid Work Policy: Flexible in-office days with budget for home office setup The base pay for this role will be between $165,000 and $250,000.

Key responsibilities Are you passionate about leadership and driving growth? Does being a connector across various roles and teams excite you? We are looking for someone like you to: • partner with Market Executives and Market Directors to execute strategic priorities, drive business growth, and achieve market performance objectives • lead initiatives focused on revenue growth, net new asset acquisition, advisor productivity, client acquisition, and market profitability • serve as the primary advisor on Financial Advisor compensation matters, partnering with advisors, market leadership, and business stakeholders • coach and support Financial Advisors in business planning, practice management, prospecting strategies, pricing decisions, and complex client situations • identify opportunities to increase assets under management, deepen client relationships, expand market share, and improve advisor effectiveness • support recruitment, onboarding, succession planning, and leadership development initiatives for Financial Advisors and Field leaders • oversee market business activities, referral programs, international and cross-border initiatives including Country Coverage framework and ensure compliance with applicable regulatory requirements • partner with operations, administrative, and client service teams to drive process improvements, organizational effectiveness, client retention, and an enhanced advisor and client experience Your Career Comeback We are open to applications from career returners. The team You will work as part of a collaborative market leadership team alongside Market Directors, Supervisory Officers, Operations, and Administrative leaders. Together, the team is responsible for delivering a best-in-class advisor experience, driving market performance, fostering a strong culture, and ensuring consistent execution of firm priorities across the Pacific International Market. The Associate Market Executive serves as a strategic leadership partner to the Market Executive and Market Directors, helping lead the execution of business objectives across the International Market. The role is responsible for driving business growth, supporting Financial Advisor success, overseeing operational and administrative effectiveness, strengthening governance and risk management practices, and advancing recruiting and talent development initiatives. The position acts as a key connector across leadership, advisors, operations, client service, risk, compliance, and corporate partners to deliver a strong client experience and sustainable market growth. Your skills and experience • SIE, Series 7, Series 66, Series 9 and Series 10 licenses required • fluency in Mandarin and/or Cantonese is required • proven ability to lead through influence and collaboration • demonstrated success managing complex initiatives and multiple stakeholders • exhibits business acumen with a strong understanding of wealth management, advisor practice management, and business development • knowledge of cross-border business practices, international client relationships, and country-specific consideration, with awareness of international regulatory frameworks and client service models • strong understanding of compliance, supervisory, and operational risk requirements • experience managing complex and sensitive business situations • exceptional communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to build credibility with senior leaders, advisors, and business partners • you’re curious to explore how AI can improve how we build, deliver and optimize workflows. You do this with sound judgment - validating outputs and aligning with policies, risk standards and ethical use.

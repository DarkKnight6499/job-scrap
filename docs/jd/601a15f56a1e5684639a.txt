Job Description: The Role As a member of the team, the Principal Business Analyst leads initiatives with diverse scope and complexity, acting as the primary interface between end users, product management, and the systems organization. Our Analysts collaborate with business partners to identify and assess complex business requirements . We work with business practitioners such as Portfolio Managers, Program Managers, Senior Management, and Attribution Analysts in defining analytical approaches to solving business requests. Finally, we support our delivery team’s dedication to quality by crafting and implementing tests that address business needs and ensure the delivery of solutions to our end users. The Expertise and Skills You Bring In this role, you will apply your experience in business analysis to support the delivery of data-driven insights and solutions within a dynamic and evolving environment. You will work closely with business and technology partners to understand needs, translate requirements into clear and actionable outputs, and help drive initiatives forward. This includes analyzing data, developing use cases, supporting user acceptance testing, and contributing to product documentation to ensure solutions meet business objectives. You will bring a strong understanding of how to interpret and leverage data, using it to inform decisions, define key performance indicators, and identify opportunities for improvement. Operating in a collaborative environment, you will partner across teams to deliver outcomes while also demonstrating the ability to work independently and take initiative. Success in this role requires curiosity, adaptability, and a thoughtful approach to problem-solving, particularly when navigating ambiguity or shifting priorities. You will also contribute to improving processes and outcomes by challenging existing approaches and recommending enhancements that mitigate risk and drive positive change. Strong communication skills are essential, as you will be expected to convey complex ideas clearly and influence stakeholders through concise and impactful messaging. A solid understanding of technology and system architecture will further enable you to bridge business and technical perspectives effectively. The ideal candidate is a motivated learner with a growth mindset, strong organizational skills, and the ability to manage multiple priorities. You are comfortable working with data, collaborating across teams, and contributing to a culture of continuous improvement.

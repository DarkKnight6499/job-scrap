Citibank, N.A. seeks a Treasury Risk Senior Analyst for its Tampa, Florida location. Duties: Establish risk and control standards for the first line of defense. Actively challenge the first line in the execution of its risk management responsibilities. Independently identify, measure, monitor, control, and report aggregate market risks to ensure aggregate risk oversight. Ensure Stress Testing Oversight by reviewing the output of periodic stress testing exercises and assessing the appropriateness of exposure levels and limits. A telecommuting/hybrid work schedule may be permitted within a commutable distance from the worksite, in accordance with Citi policies and protocols. Requirements: Requires a Bachelor’s degree or foreign equivalent in Economics, Mathematics, Statistics, Finance, Financial Mathematics, or related quantitative field and 2 years of progressively responsible, post-baccalaureate experience as a Financial Analyst, Treasury Risk Senior Analyst, Model/Analysis/Validation Analyst or related position involving market, credit and operational risks for a global bank. 2 years of experience must include: Risk Management; Financial Data Analysis; Statistical Analysis; Python, MS Excel, VBA, R; and CCAR, CECL, QMMF.

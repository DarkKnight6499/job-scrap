Job Description General Summary: At Vertex, we’re relentless in our pursuit of scientific innovation to create transformative medicines for people with serious diseases. We are currently seeking a Gene Therapy Account Manager (GTAM) to support our Sickle Cell Disease (SCD) and Transfusion-dependent β-thalassemia (TDT) Heme Business Unit. This role is responsible for the Southeast territory which includes FL, GA and AL As part of a national team, the Gene Therapy Account Manager, Associate Director (GTAM) will approach each day with a passion for improving the lives of people living with Sickle Cell Disease and Transfusion-dependent β-thalassemia. This role is focused on Key Account Management (65%) and Access/Reimbursement (35%) as a customer centric, results-driven individual contributor that supports operational readiness and access for the safe and effective use of Casgevy to medical providers per assigned coverage plans. The GTAM serves as a single point of contact for covering Activated Treatment Centers (ATC) and manages those accounts. This role serves as a team player and problem solver. The GTAM proactively identifies opportunities for improvement, takes ownership of driving toward solutions, progress and positive change. This role must effectively collaborate with internal and external stakeholders and possess mental resilience. Key Responsibilities: Serve as primary point of contact (POC) between authorized treatment centers (ATC) and internal cross-functional partners to support patient access, reimbursement and operational readiness. Act as the primary interface for customer needs throughout the patient journey, ensuring timely triaging (as needed) to the appropriate subject matter experts and internal stakeholders to remove barriers and facilitate seamless care delivery. Support ATCs and affiliated referral networks; including ongoing training and education throughout and after the onboarding and activation process, ensuring order readiness across patient services, reimbursement pathways, and site operations both during and after activation. Coordinate Vertex tasks related to ATC goals/demands with cross-functional partners to ensure seamless coordination within the site. Compliantly educate on the Casgevy profile and patient journey, in accordance with the product label and company training and policies. Build and maintain strong relationships with key stakeholders (e.g., HCPs, C-Suite level personnel, administrators, pharmacists, and care coordinators involved in Gene Therapy delivery key decision makers) at sites of care and referring sites, with the goal of building awareness for Casgevy. Develop and execute comprehensive account and HCP engagement strategy for ATCs; track and report progress towards KPIs and patient journey milestones. Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to ensure HCPs and other account-based decision-makers have timely access to critical solutions and necessary support to facilitate a wide breath of conversations from manufacturing to patient support. Own account planning and lead field team coordination to internally drive future account success; facilitates and provides appropriate and timely feedback to Vertex cross functional partners, management and key stakeholders regarding account level business trends, changes in the therapeutic landscape, business issues and opportunities. Maintains strong and deep clinical expertise in Hematology, including Sickle Cell Disease (SCD), Transfusion-dependent B-thalassemia (TDT) and Hematopoietic Stem Cell Transplantation (HSCT). Acts as a bridge between the company and Key Thought Leaders with consistent conference attendance to foster strong relationships, share insights, and inform strategic initiatives. Communicate access and reimbursement principles, payer coverage (including CMMI), hospital coding, SP/SD differences, and pharmacy operations for a complex new technology and escalates complex access and reimbursement topics to Access and Reimbursement (ARL) team. Conversant in billing & coding guide and understands the claims submission and appeals process and appropriately initiates the escalation process. Ability to think broadly, strategically, analytically, and thrive in a fast-changing environment.

About this role We are seeking an exceptional engineering leader to join Aladdin Engineering as Director, Investment Operations & Portfolio Services Engineering . This leader will be responsible for defining and executing the technology strategy, modernization, and operational excellence of a portfolio of mission-critical platforms that power portfolio services and investment operations across Aladdin. These capabilities provide the trusted portfolio data, processing, and workflows that connect investment decisions with trading, accounting, risk, performance, client reporting, and operational servicing functions. The successful candidate will lead globally distributed engineering teams, drive platform modernization, accelerate AI-enabled capabilities, and partner closely with Product, Operations, Architecture, and Engineering leadership to deliver scalable and resilient solutions supporting trillions of dollars in assets. Key Responsibilities Technology & Platform Leadership Define and execute the technology strategy for Portfolio Services and Investment Operations platforms. Drive modernization through cloud-native architectures, distributed systems, automation, and AI-enabled solutions. Establish architectural direction focused on scalability, resiliency, performance, observability, and operational excellence. Partner across Product, Operations, Risk, Accounting, Trading, and Engineering to align technology investments with business outcomes. Engineering Leadership Lead and develop engineering managers, architects, and senior engineers across globally distributed teams. Foster a culture of accountability, innovation, engineering excellence, and continuous improvement. Build organizational depth and strengthen leadership succession. Business & Operational Excellence Deliver a portfolio of strategic initiatives while balancing long-term modernization with business priorities. Improve platform reliability, operational efficiency, automation, and client experience. Own platform health, resiliency, risk management, and production excellence. Champion engineering practices that prioritize stability, transparency, and operational discipline. AI & Transformation Accelerate adoption of AI-enabled engineering and operational capabilities. Drive intelligent automation, exception management, workflow optimization, and developer productivity improvements. Simplify legacy platforms and create scalable foundations for future growth. Required Experience 15+ years of software engineering experience with significant leadership responsibilities. Proven success leading large-scale engineering organizations and mission-critical platforms. Experience managing engineering managers and globally distributed teams. Track record delivering modernization and transformation programs in complex environments. Leadership Capabilities Enterprise-first mindset with strong influence across organizational boundaries. Exceptional people leader with a passion for developing future leaders. Strong executive communication and stakeholder management skills. Demonstrated ability to lead through complexity, ambiguity, and change. Technical & Domain Expertise Deep expertise in distributed systems, scalability, resiliency, observability, and cloud-native architectures. Strong software engineering and platform modernization experience. Experience with AI-enabled engineering, automation, and operational platforms. Knowledge of portfolio management, investment operations, accounting, cash, reconciliations, trading, risk, or related financial technology domains. Success in the Role Delivery of strategic business and client outcomes. Modernization and simplification of Portfolio Services and Investment Operations platforms. Reliability, scalability, resilience, and operational performance of critical systems. Strength of the engineering leadership bench and talent pipeline. Adoption of AI-driven capabilities and automation. Effective partnership across Engineering, Product, Operations, and Investment teams.

Key Responsibilities Training Program- Design & Delivery Enhance training programs across operations, prioritizing service functions in the deposit, treasury, credit, & wealth spaces. Align training content with current procedures, controls, and system changes so materials remain accurate. Provide guidance and mentorship to employees across operations. Assist leadership with onboarding, training delivery, and quality management activities. Support workforce coordination and operational coverage planning. Promote operational discipline, accountability, and continuous improvement culture. Operational Readiness Define the trigger point(s) that ensure Operations is engaged early in change initiatives. Assess operational impact, UAT needs, documentation requirements, communication plans, and training needs for each change. Partner with the production team to define standards for Business Requirements, UAT testing scope, and required governance and control updates (RCSA, procedures, processes, workflows). Serve as the operations point of contact for go-live readiness, confirming impacted teams are prepared before implementation. Governance Documentation Oversight Oversee the governance document management lifecycle, including creation, review, approval, and periodic refresh. Identifying automation opportunities throughout. Develop and maintain standardized templates that reduce manual keying and refresh effort. Track document ownership, review cycles, and version control to keep governance content current. Stakeholder & Client Support Serve as a key liaison between operations and business partners. Support relationship managers, sales, product teams, and client service teams. Participate in operational governance and working group meetings. Assist with escalated client servicing matters and operational inquiries. Coordinate across operations, technology, risk, and compliance teams during incidents or process changes.

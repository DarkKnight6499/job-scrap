Job Description The PCS Sourcing & Procurement Intern will gain a hands-on experience in the end-to-end supply chain, allowing the intern to contribute to strategic value creation while mastering operational purchasing workflows. Phase 1: Procurement Operations (Foundational - Weeks 1-4) Focus: Learning the "mechanics" of the business and ensuring service continuity. Onboarding: Introduction to ERP systems, procurement policies, and compliance standards. Execution: Managing the lifecycle of a Purchase Order (PO)—from requisition entry to delivery tracking. Issue Resolution: Assisting the team in solving invoice discrepancies and managing urgent delivery expediting. Reporting: Developing weekly reports on PO status and spend activity to support stakeholder communication. Phase 2: Strategic Sourcing (Advanced - Weeks 5-10) Focus: Applying analytical skills to drive long-term value. Market Analysis: Conducting research on specific commodity markets to identify potential cost-saving opportunities or supply risks. Supplier Evaluation: Supporting the RFP (Request for Proposal) process by creating evaluation scorecards for incoming bids. Negotiation Prep: Assisting in the preparation of negotiation scripts and benchmarking data for upcoming contract renewals. Risk Assessment: Updating the supplier risk register based on current market trends or performance data. Phase 3: Capstone Project (The "Value-Add" - Weeks 11-12) Focus: Demonstrating impact. Deliverable: The intern will select one category or process to analyze. They will identify a "pain point" (e.g., an inefficient approval process or a lack of supplier diversity in a category), conduct an analysis, and present a proposed strategy for improvement to leadership. Required Qualifications Rising Junior pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain Management, Business Administration, Finance, Economics, Industrial Engineering, or Data Analytics. Strong data literacy; comfortable using VLOOKUPs, Pivot Tables, and data visualization. Comfortable navigating software interfaces such as SAP, Oracle, or Microsoft Dynamics. Proficient in using online tools to gather market intelligence, company financials and commodity pricing trends. Must have the ability to work in the US for an unlimited amount of time without sponsorship. Desired Characteristics Analytical thinking skills to determine the best cost of ownership. Professional when interacting with suppliers and clear when updating internal project managers and stakeholders. This candidate has strong attention to detail and a problem solving ability.

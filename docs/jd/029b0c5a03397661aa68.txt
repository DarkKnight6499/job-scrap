For more information visit: Bain Capital KEY RESPONSIBILITIES Bain Capital’s global Research & Market Data Services (RDS) team is looking for a Research Associate with a healthcare focus to provide global industry, economic, and demographic research and information services to deal teams and investment professionals across the Bain Capital platform. The Research Associate needs to be a flexible, creative thinker who is comfortable with building AI skills and solutions to support and manage multiple research projects at once in a fast-paced setting. Research projects may be loosely defined or necessitate bespoke solutions, requiring the Research Associate to filter, synthesize, and conceptualize data to deliver innovative outcomes. There is a strong emphasis on sourcing research and data from third-party providers to support due diligence on potential investments. Research Services: Provide comprehensive, synthesized research support to global deal staff and investment professionals in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment. Strong research expertise required. Experience in supporting the healthcare sector is preferred. Collaborate proactively with investment professionals and RDS team members to anticipate ongoing information needs and identify research collaboration opportunities. Stay current on relevant industry drivers and trends. Identify, evaluate, and develop partnerships with key third-party sources to support research for potential and existing investments. Represent the RDS team in initiatives aimed at enhancing information services and team core competencies. GENERAL QUALIFICATIONS Bachelor’s degree and a minimum of 2-5 years of experience in a financial services, consulting, or other fast-paced corporate research environment. Experience in the healthcare sector. Demonstrate competence, flexibility, and creativity in conducting complex research inquiries. Familiar with secondary research tools (e.g., Factiva, AlphaSense, S&P Capital IQ, Pitchbook, IQVIA, and other healthcare-specific services). Possess strong analytical, problem-solving, and verbal and written communication skills. Self-motivated with a proven ability to work effectively in a diverse team environment. Demonstrate a strong service orientation with the ability to problem-solve and improve processes both independently and collaboratively. Capable of multitasking, prioritizing, and following through in a fast-paced work environment. Able to work independently and collaboratively with internal business partners. Experience working with AI in research process across platforms including Claude or ChatGPT required. Proficiency in an additional language is a plus. Eager to learn.

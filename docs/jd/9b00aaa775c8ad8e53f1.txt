Job Description The Senior Accountant, Property will be an integral part of our Property Accounting Team and responsible for the day-to-day property accounting functions in addition to overseeing and mentoring junior team members. The ideal candidate will be enthusiastic, detail-oriented, comfortable with a team-oriented, high volume environment, able to multi-task, and able to think critically to ensure that key processes are effectively and efficiently completed. Essential Duties: · Maintain and oversee the accounting books and records of a property portfolio in accordance with policies and procedures. · Manage and accurately record tenant billing and collections, accounts payable and the monthly closing process. · Review and prepare yearly CAM reconciliations. · Prepare monthly and quarterly portfolio wide analytics. · Analyze each property’s respective lease agreements for appropriate billing and compliance. · Oversee and manage one or more Property Accountants. Support and mentor Property Accountants to master role and responsibilities. · Review monthly reports for each property. Includes analyzing monthly fluctuations and budget-to-actual variances and reconciling account balances to underlying supporting documentation and business activity. · Communicate monthly results to Asset Management and Finance Departments. · Prepare and review annual operating budgets for property portfolio. Includes coordination with Asset Management and Finance Departments and each property’s respective property management team for approval of operating budgets. · Assist external reporting team with the preparation and review of reports filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission and with the Internal Revenue Service. Requires coordination with auditors and tax. · Analyze loan agreements and coordinate with Finance Department to manage compliance with loan agreements. · Assist with construction management which can include, monitoring jobs for completion, approving invoices and job set-up, job close-out and working with the development team on draw requests. Required Knowledge, Skills & Abilities: · This position requires a bachelor’s degree in Accounting or Finance demonstrating a strong academic record during undergraduate studies. · Knowledge of accounting to record accounting entries, analyze and interpret information, and make deductions from varying facts and circumstances required. · 3+ years of relevant experience in a high-volume environment preferred. · Experience with Yardi and Avid preferred. · A motivated, detail-oriented individual with strong analytical and communication skills is required. · The ability to multi-task in a highly dynamic environment and meet challenging deadlines is critical. · Requires the ability to self-manage, independently exercise professional and technical judgement, problem solve and take initiative. · An aptitude for Microsoft Office applications, including strong Excel skills required.

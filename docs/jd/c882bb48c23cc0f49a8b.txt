OVERVIEW OF THE COMPANY Fox Corporation Under the FOX banner, we produce and distribute content through some of the world’s leading and most valued brands, including: FOX News Media, FOX Sports, FOX Entertainment, FOX Television Stations and Tubi Media Group. We empower a diverse range of creators to imagine and develop culturally significant content, while building an organization that thrives on creative ideas, operational expertise and strategic thinking. JOB DESCRIPTION FOX News Media is seeking a creative, organized, and highly motivated Manager, Commerce to support the development and execution of commerce content, brand partnerships, and product integrations across FOX Nes platforms. This role will work closely with the Managing Editor, Commerce, serving as a key creative and operational partner in bringing commerce ideas to life. The Manager will help develop segment concepts, identify and communicate with relevant brands, coordinate products and samples, track deliverables, and ensure projects move smoothly from initial idea through execution. The ideal candidate understands the intersection of editorial content, commerce, brand partnerships, and broadcast production. They should be comfortable working in a fast-paced, highly collaborative environment and managing multiple projects at once. A SNAPSHOT OF YOUR RESPONSIBILITES: Support the development of commerce segments, seasonal content, product features, and integrated brand opportunities. Research and identify relevant products, brands, retailers, and emerging trends for potential editorial and partnership opportunities. Conduct brand outreach and maintain ongoing communication with brand representatives, agencies, and public relations contacts. Help coordinate product samples, product information, pricing, links, assets, approvals, and other materials needed for segments and commerce content. Manage project timelines, requests, feedback, deliverables, and follow-up across multiple initiatives. Coordinate with editorial, production, digital, social, sales, legal, marketing, and affiliate teams to support seamless execution. Assist with the development of pitches, presentations, segment briefs, talking points, and integration ideas. Support on-air, digital, social, streaming, and affiliate commerce opportunities. Help prepare products and materials for on-air appearances, shoots, demos, and live or taped segments. Track brand participation, campaign details, content needs, deadlines, and post-segment follow-up. Monitor commerce trends, consumer interests, seasonal moments, and timely opportunities for FOX audiences. Contribute creative ideas for new commerce franchises, recurring segments, sponsorship opportunities, and brand integrations. Attend meetings and represent the Commerce team with professionalism, organization, and strong follow-through. WHAT YOU WILL NEED: 3+ years of experience in commerce, brand partnerships, media, public relations, production, marketing, editorial, or a related field. Strong understanding of brands, products, consumer trends, and content development. Excellent communication, writing, organization, and project-management skills. Ability to build and maintain strong relationships with brands, agencies, internal teams, and external partners. Comfortable managing multiple priorities in a deadline-driven environment. Strong attention to detail and ability to follow projects through from concept to completion. Comfortable working with product samples, digital assets, spreadsheets, presentations, and project-tracking tools. Ability to think creatively while also managing the operational details required to execute a successful segment or partnership. Experience with broadcast, digital media, affiliate content, social media, or on-air integrations is a plus.

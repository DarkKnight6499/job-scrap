Global Treasury & Payments Advisor I
First Citizens Bank
Location: New York, New York; Charlotte, North Carolina
Link: https://jobs.firstcitizens.com/jobs/34903

Overview This is a hybrid role with the expectation that time working will regularly take place inside and outside of a company office. We are seeking a dynamic, highly motivated Treasury Management Sales Associate to support Treasury Management Sales Advisors across both the Corporate Banking and Middle Market Banking segments. This role combines strategic sales support, client engagement, and independent sales execution to drive portfolio growth and deliver exceptional client experiences. The Associate will partner closely with Sales Advisors to support strategic client portfolios ranging from Middle Market companies to large, complex corporate relationships. The ideal candidate will possess strong sales acumen, treasury management knowledge, analytical capabilities, and the ability to manage internal sales processes while independently identifying, managing, and closing opportunities for foundational treasury solutions. Responsibilities & Qualifications Duties & Responsibilities: Client Support and Communication - Assist in responding to client inquiries and providing basic insights on treasury and payment solutions. Support the creation of educational materials for clients on treasury products and services. Participate in pre-bid conferences, fact-gathering analysis, and follow-up calls to identify client needs. Product Knowledge and Support - Educate clients and associates on Treasury Services concepts, products, features, and benefits. Assist in the setup of sales closings and serve as a secondary relationship contact for clients. Support the team in maintaining operational efficiency and best practices. Market Analysis and Research - Conduct analysis of treasury and payment market trends. Prepare reports on high value sales leads for sales advisors. Maintain clean sales pipeline for advisors, ensuring follow-up on any dormant sales opportunities. Business Development Support - Assist in identifying appropriate clients to call and conduct sales efforts. Support the preparation of formal written proposals for corporate, municipal, and nonprofit bank relationships. Position Specific Skills: Treasury product knowledge Client relationship management Financial analysis Presentation skills Basic understanding of treasury operations and payment systems. Familiarity with financial analysis techniques and regulatory requirements. Understanding of client relationship management in financial services. Qualifications: Bachelor's Degree and 1 years of experience in OR High School Diploma or GED and 5 years of experience in Treasury Management, Cash Management, Commercial Banking, Payments, Treasury Sales Support, or related financial services experience. Preferred Experience Banking or financial services experience supporting middle market and/or large corporate clients and complex treasury relationships. Core Competencies Sales Execution & Coordination; Consultative Communication & Relationship Building; Operational Excellence; Strategic Analysis; Treasury & Solution Knowledge; Client Relationship Management; Presentation Skills; Financial Analysis; Project Management.

Join Barclays as a Planning and Stress Testing Model Execution AVP, where you'll have the chance to drive the execution and evolution of critical financial planning and stress testing models that support strategic decision-making across the bank. In this role, you will lead the onboarding of existing and emerging quantitative modeling methodologies, enhance model infrastructure, and coordinate model redevelopment efforts in response to performance monitoring, validation feedback, and changing business requirements. You'll collaborate with stakeholders across Finance, Risk, and Quantitative teams to ensure robust model governance and regulatory compliance while contributing to the validation lifecycle. This position offers broad exposure to financial banking operations and provides a unique opportunity to partner with multiple business areas, influencing the bank's forecasting, capital planning, and stress testing capabilities. To be successful as a Planning and Stress Testing Model Execution AVP, you should have: Advanced quantitative modeling and analytics experience supporting financial planning, forecasting, capital management, and stress testing frameworks. Strong understanding of banking, financial markets, and regulatory stress testing requirements, with the ability to apply analytical insights to business and risk management objectives. Proven ability to execute, monitor, and enhance financial models while partnering with Finance, Risk, Quantitative Modeling, and Validation teams in a highly regulated environment. Other highly valued skills and experience include: Strong business acumen with the ability to assess complex financial and stress testing outcomes and their impact on strategic decision-making. Background in accounting, consulting, finance, or a related discipline, with experience applying analytical insights in a banking or corporate environment. Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to convey complex modeling and financial concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences. You may be assessed on the key critical skills relevant for success in role, such as risk and controls, change and transformation, business acumen strategic thinking and digital and technology, as well as job-specific technical skills.

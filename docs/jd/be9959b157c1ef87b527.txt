Job Purpose & Scope Responsible for providing essential operational and administrative support to the Credit Risk Review (CRR) team by coordinating workflows, organizing documentation, and ensuring timely execution of review activities as outlined in the CRR review plan. Essential Job Functions Assists in managing and maintaining the CRR review pipeline to ensure reviews progress efficiently and on schedule. Sends reminders and communications related to due dates, missing items, and review timelines. Tracks open issues, exceptions, and required follow-ups from reviewers and the line of business. Compiles data for metrics such as review coverage, findings, accuracy scores, override frequencies, and issue tracking. Maintains dashboards used for monitoring review progress, coverage goals, and review results. Supports preparation of quarterly and annual reports for CRR, CRMC, and Board committees. Coordinates periodic updates to CRR policy, procedures, templates, and SharePoint/Teams sites. Assists in staffing and resource planning by tracking reviewer workloads and upcoming review calendars. Assists reviewers by collecting credit files, loan documents, financial statements, collateral reports, appraisals, and supporting data prior to kickoff. Prepares agendas, take meeting minutes, and distribute follow-up action items. Coordinates with the third-party vendor on requests such as system issues, access needs, monthly data-tape confirmations, and more. Enters CRR valuation requests into RIMS accurately and in a timely manner. Ensures satisfactory ongoing monitoring and coordination of appraisal vendor status. Collects and archives vendor compliance documentation. Tracks, communicates, and escalates process risks and issues. Generates MyLR reports. Completes special projects and performs other responsibilities as assigned. Knowledge, Skills & Abilities Knowledge of Credit Risk Management systems and platforms such as MyLR, RIMS, and High attention to detail. Ability to work effectively and demonstrate flexibility in a continually changing environment. Ability to demonstrate effective organization, critical thinking, analytical and problem-solving skills. Proficient in computer applications, including Microsoft Office (Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Visio), Nitro PDF Pro, and more.

Job Description Job Summary The FP&A Analyst supports financial planning, forecasting, budgeting, reporting, and analysis activities across the organization. The role develops financial models, analyzes business performance, prepares management reports, and provides insights that support operational and strategic decision-making. The analyst identifies trends, risks, and opportunities, conducts variance analyses, and recommends improvements to financial planning and reporting processes. Work is performed independently under general supervision. Essential Duties and Responsibilities Essential duties and responsibilities may include the following. Other duties may be assigned. Prepare and analyze current and historical financial data and performance. Prepare and maintain financial reports, forecasts, and projections based on analytical findings. Evaluate capital expenditures and depreciation. Analyze financial performance trends and recommend improvements to business results. Develop and maintain financial models, forecasts, and performance metrics. Support the development and implementation of best practices that improve financial processes, reporting accuracy, and operational efficiency. Partner with business leaders and cross-functional teams to support financial and operational decision-making. Maintain daily availability, including occasional evenings and weekends, as necessary to meet business needs and deadlines. Supervisory Responsibilities This position has no supervisory responsibilities.

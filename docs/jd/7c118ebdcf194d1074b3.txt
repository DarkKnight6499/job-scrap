Overview We’re hiring a Treasury Operations Manager to join our team! You’ll be at the center of how money moves through our business. From owning merchant settlements, managing liquidity across our banking and custody network, and making sure payouts happen accurately and on time across every currency and entity we operate in. This is a hands-on role with real ownership and real-time decision-making. You’ll position cash ahead of market opens, make critical funding decisions, execute wires and on-chain transfers under dual control, and jump in to investigate when a settlement doesn’t land where it should. And you won’t just run the operation, you’ll help build what comes next. You’ll take workflows that rely on spreadsheets and tribal knowledge and turn them into scalable, repeatable processes that can keep pace with a growing payments business. If you’ve worked in treasury or payment operations within payments, banking, fintech, or crypto and love moving fast, solving problems, and building better systems as you go, you’ll feel right at home here.

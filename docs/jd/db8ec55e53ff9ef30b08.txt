Deloitte’s Digital Assets practice combines the competencies of risk management, regulation, technology, and cryptography to help organizations address challenges in an integrated manner. Our Deloitte Regulatory, Risk & Forensic team helps client leaders translate multifaceted risk and an evolving regulatory environment into defensible actions that strengthen, protect, and transform their organizations. Join our team and use advanced data, AI, and emerging technologies with industry insights to help clients bring clarity from complexity and accelerate their path to value creation. Work you’ll do As a Manager in Digital Assets Strategy & Operating Model Design, you’ll take ownership of client relationships and lead small teams or workstreams within larger programs. You’ll oversee the delivery of strategic digital asset initiatives, ensuring solutions meet client needs and exceed expectations. Your role will blend hands-on leadership, project management, and subject matter expertise as you coach junior resources, facilitate workshops, and drive consensus among stakeholders. You’ll have the opportunity to shape operating models, validate business cases, and make a tangible impact on the future of finance and technology. As a Manager, you will have opportunities to: Lead client engagements and manage teams or workstreams focused on developing and refining enterprise-wide digital asset strategies, including value propositions, use cases, roadmap prioritization, and investment requirements. Oversee the design and implementation of target-state operating models for digital asset initiatives, encompassing organization structure, governance, processes, roles, skills, and performance measures. Guide the development and validation of business cases for digital asset projects, including financial modeling, risk assessment, and scenario analysis. Manage client relationships, ensuring high-quality delivery and alignment with client objectives. Coach and mentor junior team members, fostering professional growth and knowledge sharing. Facilitate workshops and drive consensus among diverse stakeholders. Serve as a subject matter resource for business and operational models considerations as well as risk assessments across compliance, regulatory, BSA/AML, cyber, operational, blockchain, and financial domains. Identify strategic risks and opportunities in the digital asset ecosystem using industry insights and collaboration with colleagues. Educate clients and internal teams on digital assets, operating model leading practices, and emerging technologies. Prepare technical documentation and diagrams for digital asset solutions Provide risk and regulatory advisory support in defining and reviewing business models. Deploy business and finance process changes and risk-mitigating controls to enable digital-asset-specific capabilities. Utilize technology-based tools and methodologies to analyze public blockchain data.

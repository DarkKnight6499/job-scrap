Job Description: The Director, TDS U.S. Operational Risk Management & Regulatory Change is responsible for leading regulatory change initiatives, strategic risk management programs, and operational risk transformation activities across TD Securities U.S. This is achieved through oversight of regulatory-driven enhancements, coordination of remediation initiatives, implementation of risk management capabilities, partnership with enterprise and U.S. stakeholders, and integration of specialist non-financial risk perspectives to strengthen operational risk management practices, governance, and regulatory readiness. Department Overview TDS U.S. Operational Risk Management (ORM) provides independent oversight and challenge of operational and non-financial risks across TD Securities U.S. As part of the second line of defense, the team supports the effective implementation of TD's Enterprise Risk Framework and associated risk management programs while promoting strong governance, risk transparency, and regulatory compliance. The team works closely with business, risk, compliance, audit, and corporate function partners across TD Securities, CUSO, TDGUS, and Enterprise Risk Management to support the ongoing enhancement of risk management capabilities, governance frameworks, and regulatory readiness. Job Description The Director, TDS U.S. Operational Risk Management, Regulatory Change & Transformation plays a critical leadership role within TDS U.S. Operational Risk Management, reporting to the Head of TDS U.S. Operational Risk Management. The position is responsible for leading large-scale regulatory, governance, and operational risk management initiatives impacting TD Securities U.S. The role serves as a key liaison across businesses, risk functions, enterprise partners, and specialist non-financial risk teams to support implementation of regulatory expectations, remediation commitments, operational risk transformation activities, and enterprise risk integration initiatives. The successful candidate will lead complex cross-functional programs, provide strategic risk management leadership, coordinate engagement with enterprise and CUSO risk stakeholders, and support the ongoing maturation of operational and non-financial risk management capabilities across TDS U.S. This position will appeal to an individual who thrives in a dynamic environment, enjoys leading large-scale initiatives, and seeks broad exposure to senior leadership, regulatory matters, enterprise risk programs, and strategic risk management activities.

About DoorDash Labs DoorDash Labs is an independent robotics and automation team within DoorDash, focused on transforming last-mile logistics at scale. Our work reaches millions of people — and we're building the operational foundation to make that possible safely. About The Role As a Senior Operations Associate on the Operations Safety team, you will be a hands-on contributor to the safety systems, incident response, and SOP processes that support DoorDash Labs' autonomous products. You will execute day-to-day safety operations, analyze incident and operational data, and work closely with engineering, product, and field operations teams to help ensure safety is built into every phase of development and deployment. This is an individual contributor role for someone who thrives at the intersection of operational rigor and cutting-edge autonomy. What You'll Do Support incident response — triage and help resolve safety incidents; document findings and contribute to root cause analysis and corrective action follow-through Maintain and improve SOPs — draft, update, and maintain standard operating procedures; help ensure compliance across Labs programs and flag gaps to the team Track safety analytics & reporting — build and maintain dashboards and reports on operational safety metrics; surface trends, risks, and recommendations to the team Execute on-site and remote safety operations — monitor operations, run audits and safety checks, and escalate issues promptly across remote and on-site functions Support cross-functional safety work — partner with engineering, product, legal, and field operations to help embed safety into product design, launches, and scale Develop training materials — create and update training content and job aids for operators and field teams; help onboard and coach new team members What We're Looking For Required 4+ years in operations, safety, or a related field — autonomy, robotics, aerospace, or defense experience a plus Experience supporting safety or operational systems in a regulated or safety-critical environment Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain risk data and findings clearly to cross-functional partners Strong analytical mindset; comfortable with ambiguity and fast-changing priorities Participate in on-call rotation and promptly respond to on-call events with urgency and rigor on nights and weekends. Preferred Familiarity with safety management systems (SMS), ISO 45001, or equivalent frameworks Experience developing training programs or instructional materials.

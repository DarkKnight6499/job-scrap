Associate Product Owner (New York)
Fitch Ratings
Location: New York, NY, US
Link: https://careers.fitch.group/job/New-York-Associate-Product-Owner-%28New-York%29-NY-10001/1450864833/

The Fitch Solutions Product organization is currently seeking a Product Owner based in our NYC office to join the Scoring & Risk Analytics Tribe. Fitch Solutions is a trusted, independent provider of financial information services, drawing on more than a century of leadership, transparency and reliability. With teams operating in over 30 countries, our analysts, lawyers, journalists and economists combine global perspective with deep local expertise. We pair human intelligence with innovative technology to deliver high-quality credit research, data and proprietary analytics that help global markets manage risk and identify opportunity. Our success is powered by an inclusive, collaborative culture in which every expert voice contributes to exceptional client value. Fitch Solutions is a part of Fitch Group, wholly owned by the Hearst Corporation, which is comprised of three main businesses: Fitch Ratings | Fitch Solutions | Fitch Learning. About the Team The Fitch Solutions Product organization is organized into five product tribes, each with a single lead, roadmap and defined scope. The Product Owner will sit within the Scoring & Risk Analytics Tribe , which is responsible for the model-based outputs across Fitch Solutions’ portfolio and in the development and governance of its various credit risk assessment tools and capabilities. This role involves working closely with the Product Manager and cross-functional teams to drive the product lifecycle from ideation to launch. The ideal candidate will have a solid understanding of product management principles, excellent communication skills, and a passion for creating exceptional user experiences. How You’ll Make an Impact: Product Development: Assist in the development and execution of the product roadmap, ensuring alignment with business goals and customer needs. Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with engineering, design, marketing, and sales teams to ensure successful product delivery. Backlog Management: Maintain and prioritize the product backlog, ensuring that it is up-to-date and reflects the current business priorities. User Stories: Develop detailed user stories and establish acceptance criteria to ensure stories are ready for development. Agile Ceremonies: Run agile ceremonies, including backlog grooming, sprint planning, sprint reviews, and retrospectives. Feature Validation: Conduct User Acceptance Testing (UAT) to validate that stories meet the definition of done and acceptance criteria. Customer Interaction: Discuss features with customers, focusing on usability, time to value, feature completeness, and the overall user journey. Prototyping and Testing: Collaborate with engineering and design to prototype features, conduct A/B testing, and validate designs with end users. Issue Resolution: Handle issues with current features and provide solutions based on customer feedback. Performance Monitoring: Track and analyze product performance metrics e.g. team productivity, product adoption, feature performance. This role would focus on collection, analysis, and reporting as well as implementing improvements at the squad level. You May be a Good Fit if: Education: Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field. Experience: 2+ years of experience in product management, product development, or a related role. Technical Skills: Familiarity with Agile methodologies, experience with product management tools (e.g.,Jira, Trello), and basic understanding of software development. Experience with AI technologies is a plus. Analytical Skills: Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to make data-driven decisions e.g. familiarity with performance metrics like DORA. Communication Skills: Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to articulate complex ideas clearly. Team Player: Ability to work effectively in a collaborative, fast-paced environment. Customer-Centric: Passion for understanding and addressing customer needs. Preferred Experience: Experience in financial information services industry, familiarity with UX/UI principles and design thinking, and experience working with remote teams. What Would Make You Stand Out: Preferred Experience: Experience in financial information services industry, familiarity with UX/UI principles and design thinking, and experience working with remote teams. MBA is a plus.

Job Description Ares Management Corporation is seeking an experienced Senior Associate to join our Corporate Accounting team focused on operating expenses, including accruals, monthly expense analyses and management reporting. The Ares Corporate Accounting Department oversees and manages the financial statement close process and internal and external financial reporting for the public management company. Under the leadership of the Chief Accounting Officer, the Corporate Accounting Department is comprised of functional teams that operate collaboratively to oversee operating expenses, revenues, consolidation and SEC reporting. The Corporate Accounting team has close working relationships with external service providers and internal stakeholders to ensure the reporting of accurate results both internally and externally. This role will require a dynamic, adaptive and agile hands-on professional who will be capable of ensuring optimal processes in a fast-paced rapidly growing environment.

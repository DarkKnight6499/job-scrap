Job Description The Corporate Finance team is seeking a Senior Associate to participate in a strategic initiative to develop, implement, and manage an operating plan for the Ares Employee Co-Investment Program (the “Employee Co-Invest Program”). The Employee Co-Invest Program facilitates the investment by Ares employees in Ares managed funds. The individual will have day-to-day operational ownership for numerous funds including, but not limited to, investor onboarding and relationship management, operations, reporting and servicing, and fund accounting responsibilities. The role requires an individual who is a strategic thinker and able to collaborate with various cross-functional teams to understand the challenges and needs of stakeholders. This role will also be responsible for managing and continuously improving the data, reporting/insights, technology, and processes needed to deliver a seamless investor experience to our internal investors. Ideally, the candidate will have experience with investment accounting and/or investor portal implementations. The candidate must be resourceful, innovative, forward thinking and committed. This is an entrepreneurial opportunity that will afford the candidate significant responsibility and the opportunity to help build and grow a successful team within a diversified platform. The candidate must be focused on continuous improvement in accuracy and timeliness of ongoing management financial and operational reporting.

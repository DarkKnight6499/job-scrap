Location Designation: Hybrid - 3 days per week Join New York Life, the largest mutual life insurance company in the United States and one of the largest insurers in the world, for an opportunity to help us fulfill our mission to make an impact on the lives of others. Who we are: New York Life Group Benefit Solutions (NYLGBS) works with businesses and their employees to help provide financial security, peace of mind, and market-leading outcomes. Our Distribution and Sales teams cultivate relationships with insurance brokers and clients, delivering solutions that support growing organizations and their employees. What you’ll do: Phase 1: Formal Training Program You will begin your journey with a comprehensive training experience that includes: Classroom and virtual instruction on group insurance products, systems, and sales processes Hands-on exercises focused on quoting, proposal development, and sales operations Exposure to internal partners including underwriting, pricing, product, marketing, and service and claim operations Company-sponsored licensing to enable you to sell group insurance products Mentorship, coaching, and structured learning milestones Phase 2: Sales Desk Placement & Development Upon successful completion of training, you will: Support quoting and proposal generation for employer groups in the select market (50-250 lives) Own the end-to-end RFP process from intake to proposal delivery Track quote activity and maintain systems Assist with implementation coordination and onboarding Execute all phases of the renewal cycle for select accounts including plan design changes, underwriting review, and renewal delivery Partner with Sales Desk Lead and sales representatives within assigned region to drive growth within the select market Phase 3: Progression into Sales Roles As you build experience, you will transition into a Sales Representative or Account Executive role where you will: Manage broker and client relationships within assigned book of business Promote NYLGBS products and solutions through active market engagement Drive new business opportunities through broker partnerships Act as a trusted partner to internal and external stakeholders What you’ll bring: Strong communication, organization, and attention to detail Customer-focused mindset Ability to manage multiple priorities Willingness to learn and grow Bachelor’s degree preferred Proficiency with Microsoft Office; Salesforce a plus Pay Transparency Salary Range: $65,000-$92,500 Overtime eligible: Exempt Discretionary bonus eligible: Yes Sales bonus eligible: No Actual base salary will be determined based on several factors but not limited to individual’s experience, skills, qualifications, and job location.

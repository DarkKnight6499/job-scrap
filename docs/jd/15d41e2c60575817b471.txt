Job Description: Job Title: Quantitative Strategist - Credit Corporate Title: Vice President – Director Location: New York, NY [ALL ROLES TO BE CONSIDERED] Overview We are seeking an experienced Quantitative Strategist to join Deutsche Bank's Credit Strats team in New York. As part of our global Strategic Analytics organization, you will partner closely with Trading, Sales, Structuring, and Technology teams to develop innovative pricing, risk, and analytics solutions that support the credit business. Working directly on the trading floor, you will combine quantitative expertise, software engineering skills, and market knowledge to deliver scalable front-office analytics, tools and strategic platform enhancements. You will transform business needs and requirements into solutions that drive business decision-making and performance.

What We Need: The Senior Financial Analyst, Corporate FP&A supports the company's enterprise-wide financial planning process including monthly actuals, budget, forecast, and Long-Range Forecast. This role partners closely with segment finance teams to consolidate results, analyze variances and margin trends, and prepare accurate, well-supported reporting for senior finance leadership and executive management. What You'll Do: Support the preparation and maintenance of the consolidated Income Statement, Cash Flow, and related schedules across Actual, Budget, Forecast, and Long Range Forecast (LRF) scenarios. Partner with segment FP&A teams across the Multiplatform Group, Digital Audio Group, and Audio & Media Services to consolidate monthly and quarterly results and prepare Budget-vs-Forecast-vs-Actual variance analysis. Build and maintain detailed revenue and expense models, including revenue trends, seasonal patterns, and other key business drivers. Analyze consolidated and segment-level Adjusted EBITDA margins; investigate and clearly explain movement in cost of sales and operating expense trends. Support preparation of materials for lenders, rating agencies, and the Board of Directors, including debt schedules and leverage ratio analysis. Perform detail-level audits of financial models to confirm formula integrity and consistency before models are used for internal or external reporting. Assist with the annual budget and periodic reforecast cycles, including templates, timelines, and consolidation across business units. Prepare ad hoc analyses, board decks, and presentations for senior finance and executive leadership. Identify and help implement opportunities to streamline and automate recurring reporting processes. What You'll Need: Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a related field. 3+ years of experience in FP&A, corporate finance, investment banking, transaction advisory, or public accounting. Advanced Microsoft Excel skills (complex formulas, financial modeling, pivot tables) and strong PowerPoint proficiency. Solid understanding of financial statements, GAAP, and core corporate finance principles. Excellent analytical and organizational skills with a high degree of accuracy and attention to detail. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to present financial information clearly to non-finance audiences. Ability to manage multiple deadlines and shifting priorities in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment. Master's degree in relevant field or CPA designation. Experience with Workday ERP and/or SQL or Power BI is preferred. Experience in media, advertising, broadcasting, or another advertising-revenue-driven industry is preferred.

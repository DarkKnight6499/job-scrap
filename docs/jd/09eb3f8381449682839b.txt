Analyst/Associate - Multinational Coverage Global Banking and Markets Global Banking and Markets (GBM) is a leading Capital Markets and Investment Banking platform with a competitive presence across the Americas, operating globally over 100 years. Scotiabank’s strong US presence provides our clients an important bridge to this key global market for trade and investment flows across the Americas and the world. Global Banking & Markets provides a full range of investment banking, credit and risk management products and services relevant to the financing and strategic development needs of our clients. Our products include debt and equity financing, mergers & acquisitions, corporate banking, institutional equity sales, trading and research, fixed income products, derivatives, energy, foreign exchange and precious metals. We also cross-sell the full range of wholesale products and services offered by the Scotiabank Group including transaction banking and trade finance solutions. Be part of an innovative Global Capital Markets and Investment Banking business with a unique geographic footprint that puts capital to work for our clients across industries! We work together to drive ambition to every future! Overview Multinational Banking is a key growth pillar of Scotiabank’s refreshed strategy, which entails the delivery of a differentiated and consistent client experience across our footprint, placing the client at the center of our service model, regardless of where they choose to do business. In partnership with the Parent Account Managers (PAM) and Operations Account Managers (OAM), the Associate will support the US Subsidiary Account Manager (SAM) in managing the relationship with local subsidiaries of Multinational Corporate and Commercial clients (MNC), while supporting the connectivity with PAMs and product partners across Scotiabank’s global footprint. What You’ll Do Work collaboratively with the US SAM, country teams, and key internal stakeholders to deliver the enhanced Multinationals value proposition to Scotiabank’s Multinational clients. Support PAMS and OAMs globally and US SAM in developing and managing holistic global account plans and executing on local and global multi-product and service plans to grow the global relationship of our MNCs and to support the MNCs’ financial needs. Champion a client centric culture and leverage Scotiabank’s global and in-country footprint and systems, and product knowledge to grow client revenue and primacy. Build strong relationships and partner effectively with key stakeholders across Scotiabank’s various lines of business and around the globe. Maintain good knowledge of Scotiabank's local and global platform, product capabilities, operational processes, risk appetite and risk culture. Assisting in portfolio management including: preparing pitch books, credit write ups (for non-lending and lending products), and deal execution oversight. Assist with the framework set up for the client portfolio, create templates for analysis, perform due diligence and streamline operations across MNC accounts. Maintain reporting profitability metrics, and perform analysis on client engagement and share of wallet. Provide mentorship to Analyst and support their development.

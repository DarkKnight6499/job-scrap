Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Valuation Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Deal Valuation Senior Associate you will provide analysis and valuation services for mergers, acquisitions, divestitures, restructuring, and other financial transactions, helping clients evaluate deal value and potential risks. As a Senior Associate you will analyze complex problems, manage assigned workstreams, build meaningful client relationships, and develop deliverables while mentoring junior team members and navigating ambiguity. In this role at PwC, you will support transaction-focused client work within our Deals practice, using valuation methods, data analysis, and business context to inform insights and recommendations across the deal lifecycle. Responsibilities - Conducting valuation analyses for mergers, acquisitions, divestitures, and restructuring activities - Building financial models and reviewing assumptions used in business, asset, and intangible asset valuations - Interpreting market data, financial statements, and deal terms to support pricing, diligence, and value creation decisions - Applying valuation methods for financial reporting, tax, fairness, and regulatory reporting needs - Analyzing transaction structures, synergy estimates, and value drivers to inform client decision making - Preparing clear deliverables that summarize findings, methodologies, and key valuation considerations - Managing assigned workstreams, tracking milestones, and adjusting to shifting client priorities - Reviewing the work of junior team members and providing guidance on technical approaches and deliverable development - Building client relationships by responding to questions, anticipating needs, and supporting discussions across the deal lifecycle What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 3 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Building meaningful client connections in valuation engagements - Managing client workstreams across complex deal situations - Interpreting valuation data into clear recommendations - Using critical thinking to break down complex concepts - Anticipating team and client needs within changing priorities - Upholding professional standards and independence requirements Travel Requirements Up to 40% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $77,000 - $202,000.

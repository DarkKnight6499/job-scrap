Job Overview: The Associate Analyst will play a key role within the Client Reporting (Statements Communications) team, part of LPL’s Operations. This team is responsible for managing regulatory mailings including Trade Confirmations, Prospectuses, Monthly/Quarterly Statements, and Client Letters. Additional responsibilities include handling Return Mail and DBA (Doing Business As) Statement Logos. The role involves collaboration across departments to ensure accurate and timely delivery of client communications. Responsibilities: · Execute daily, weekly, and monthly operational tasks such as file transmissions to vendors, data validation, and reporting. · Perform test scripts and support User Acceptance Testing (UAT) for statement-related systems and enhancements. · Analyze exception reports related to statement, confirmation, letter, and prospectus delivery. · Support internal and external audit requests with accurate documentation and data. · Review and improve departmental processes to enhance risk oversight, operational efficiency, and consistency in client communications. What are we looking for? We’re looking for strong collaborators who deliver exceptional client experiences and thrive in fast-paced, team-oriented environments. Our ideal candidates pursue greatness , act with integrity , and are driven to help our clients succeed . We value those who embrace creativity, continuous improvement, and contribute to a culture where we win together and create and share joy in our work. Requirements: · 2+ years of professional experience in business operations or financial services. · 2+ years of intermediate skills in Microsoft Excel, including pivot tables and complex formulas. Core Competencies: · Strong problem-solving and analytical skills. · Ability to manage multiple priorities effectively. · Excellent written and verbal communication. · Self-motivated and capable of working independently. Preferences: · Experience with SQL or similar scripting languages. · Experience working with large data sets and performing quality control oversight. · Experience cross collaborating with multiple internal business units and offshore team. · Experience with Lean Six Sigma, Design Thinking, or Agile-Scrum methodologies. · Bachelor’s degree in Finance or related field. · FINRA Securities Industry Essentials (SIE) and/or Series 99 certification.

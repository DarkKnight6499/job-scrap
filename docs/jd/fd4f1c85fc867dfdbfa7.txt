Job Description: The Role The Senior Financial Analyst will be responsible for coordinating the group with the Daily Reserve Computation under SEA Rule 15c3‑3 and the Monthly Net Capital Reporting under SEA Rule 15c3‑1. The role will also steer key daily responsibilities, including monitoring critical balance movements, performing reconciliations, and ensuring data completeness and accuracy across regulatory reporting workflows. The Senior Financial Analyst will work closely with internal teams to research and resolve exceptions, maintain process documentation, and contribute to ongoing enhancements that strengthen reporting controls and efficiency. The Team Fidelity’s Regulatory Reporting team is a critical function that is responsible for the firm’s SEC and FINRA reporting obligations as they pertain to its regulated broker dealers, including National Financial Services, LLC. This group performs daily Reserve computations as required under SEA Rule 15c3-3 for the protection of Customer assets, monitors Net Capital requirements as required under SEA Rule 15c3-1, and is responsible for the reconciliation of all cash banking activities for the firm. In close partnership with Controllers, Compliance, Operations and other functional groups, this team acts as a key control function for the firm’s financial reporting obligations.

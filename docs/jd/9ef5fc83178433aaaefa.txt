The Associate Director will serve as a strategic finance partner to leaders across the Kite organization. The role will lead forecasting and budgeting, decision support and analysis, management reporting, and process improvement initiatives. Success requires the ability to interact with and present effectively to senior leaders across Kite. As an Associate Director, Kite Corporate FP&A, you will... Essential Responsibilities: Lead planning and forecasting: Own the long-range plan (LRP), Annual Budget, periodic forecasts, and scenario analyses for Kite. Translate business strategies and operating assumptions into well-supported financial plans and provide clear recommendations on resource allocation, trade-offs, and investment priorities. Oversee consolidation and reporting: Lead the end-to-end consolidation of budgets, forecasts and actuals. Deliver accurate, timely, and decision-ready management reporting packages that meet functional and corporate requirements. Serve as a strategic finance partner: Build strong partnerships with senior leaders across the supported functions, Accounting, and Gilead Global FP&A Consolidations, providing objective guidance, constructive challenges, and practical solutions. Drive performance management: Analyze results and forecasts, identify key drivers, risks, and opportunities, and partner with leaders on actions to meet financial goals. Develop executive insights: Distill complex financial and operational data into clear presentations highlighting implications, recommendations, and decisions. Build financial models: Evaluate strategic initiatives, scenarios, investments, and business needs through modeling and sensitivity analysis. Support close activities: Partner with Accounting and the business to review accruals, validate results, investigate variances, and communicate performance. Improve processes and systems: Lead initiatives that enhance planning, reporting, data quality, and efficiency, including Future of Finance initiatives. Strengthen governance and controls: Ensure compliance with policies and controls, address gaps, and protect financial reporting integrity. Manage priorities and ad hoc requests: Deliver high-priority analyses and projects while balancing recurring work, emerging needs, accuracy, and responsiveness.

JFS Wealth Advisors is looking for a Portfolio Management Specialist to provide operational support to the trading team, Investment Committee, and Chief Investment Officer. This individual will be responsible for rebalancing client accounts within the firm’s portfolio management system, Tamarac Advisor Rebalancing, executing and reconciling trades, and supporting the firm’s investment services and asset management teams. This position is hybrid for the first 90 days, followed by a schedule of three days in the office and two days remote. Primary Responsibilities Maintain Tamarac Advisor Rebalancing Maintain client accounts, including model assignments, restrictions, and cash reserves. Prepare and input trade orders according to portfolio guidelines. Regularly review security settings and restrictions. Run and monitor saved searches within Tamarac. Execute and reconcile trades across multiple custodians Accurately process daily trade ledgers to custodians. Review trades, identify discrepancies or items requiring additional review, and coordinate with advisors and client service teams to resolve questions. Support the Investment Committee and Chief Investment Officer Provide operational and administrative support as needed. Research and gather investment-related data, prepare presentations, and generate reports. Collaborate with internal teams Work closely with trading, asset management, financial planning, and investment teams to ensure efficient workflows and resolve operational issues. Maintain knowledge of the firm’s compliance and operational policies and procedures. Assist with process improvements and special projects as assigned. Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, or a related field, or equivalent education. 3+ years of relevant experience within financial services, preferably in the RIA industry. Recent college graduates may also be considered if their education and experience align with the position. Working knowledge of asset allocation and portfolio construction. Experience with portfolio rebalancing software and trading platforms preferred. Experience with Tamarac Advisor Rebalancing, Tamarac Advisor View, and Practifi CRM preferred. Familiarity with Schwab and Fidelity custodial platforms preferred. Experience with Morningstar Advisor Suite and/or vCharts preferred. Industry designations or licenses are a plus. Proficiency in Microsoft Office and ability to learn new investment-related software. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, including the ability to identify tax and investment-related issues within portfolios. Excellent communication, organizational, and time management skills, with strong attention to detail. Ability to prioritize tasks, meet daily deadlines, and work effectively in a team-oriented environment. Commitment to professionalism, client service, and maintaining confidentiality of sensitive financial information. Ability to work occasional overtime and travel to client locations as needed. Physical Requirements Ability to sit at a desk and work on a computer for prolonged periods. Ability to stand, walk, and perform routine office tasks. Ability to lift up to 10 pounds as needed.

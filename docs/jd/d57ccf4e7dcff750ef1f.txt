Learn more . Back to search results Position Summary Insurance finance leaders today face disruption from artificial intelligence, market volatility, and regulatory change, such as IFRS 17 and LDTI. As an FP&A Senior Consultant focused on the insurance industry, you will help insurance carriers strengthen financial planning, forecasting, and analytics capabilities so they can make more informed business decisions. This role supports finance transformation efforts for life and annuity (L&A), property and casualty (P&C), specialty and reinsurance organizations by combining process improvement, data enhancement, and technology innovation to deliver timely, actionable insights across the enterprise. Recruiting for this role ends on 12/24/26 Work you'll do As an FP&A Senior Consultant within the Insurance Finance Transformation team, you will be responsible for: • Leading financial planning, forecasting, budgeting, and reporting transformation projects for insurance carriers' finance organizations • Driving finance transformation initiatives across planning, analytics, management reporting, and decision support processes for L&A, P&C, specialty and reinsurance carriers • Developing financial models, scenario analyses, and performance insights (including loss ratio, combined ratio, expense ratio, and reserve/premium trend analysis against external and macroeconomic impacts) to support strategic and operational decisions • Collaborating with actuarial, investments, underwriting, claims, and other client stakeholders to assess current-state finance processes and design scalable future-state solutions • Supporting the implementation and adoption of data, automation, and artificial intelligence capabilities within FP&A processes • Supporting alignment between statutory (NAIC), US GAAP, IFRS and capital (e.g., RBC, BSCR, etc.) reporting requirements within FP&A planning and forecasting processes • Designing and facilitating client workshops (from preparation through execution) to drive design decisions and advance stakeholder thinking on future-state processes • Managing work plans and timelines across multiple concurrent workstreams to drive deliverables to closure A successful candidate would possess these skills: • Ability to work independently and collaborate as part of a team • Effective written and verbal communication skills • Present to key internal and client stakeholders, including meeting facilitation activities • Meticulous attention to detail and quality of work product • Deep understanding of an insurance carrier income statement, balance sheet, liquidity and capital financial statements and analytics • Deep understanding of insurance-specific financial concepts, including loss reserves, reinsurance ceding, premium recognition, and combined ratio • Familiarity with regulatory reporting requirements of an insurance company, including NAIC statutory reporting and IFRS 17/LDTI • Familiarity with actuarial concepts and terminology, and ability to collaborate effectively with actuarial teams • SQL, Python, or Alteryx based data prep/automation • Ability to build and sustain professional relationships • Ability to lead projects or workstreams including project management activities (e.g., status tracking, RAID documentation, key design decisioning) • Ability to manage and prioritize multiple tasks in a fast-paced and dynamic environment • Strong interpersonal skills and professional demeanor • Ability to meet deadlines • Ability to mentor and provide clear guidance to others The team Deloitte's Finance Transformation offering helps insurance finance and global business services leaders navigate an increasingly challenging environment marked by disruptive tech like AI, market uncertainty, and regulatory change, including IFRS 17, LDTI, and evolving statutory requirements, and recognize the opportunity to better serve the enterprise, shareholders, customers, and regulators. Within that offering, our Insurance Business Finance team helps insurance carriers drive strategic value through financial planning, forecasting, and analytics capabilities, with a focus on earnings improvement, expense management, and capital and reserve planning. By leveraging next-gen processes, scalable data platforms, and AI, our team delivers accurate, timely, and actionable insights to life, annuity, P&C, and reinsurance clients.

Job Description Summary We are seeking an experienced Quality Assurance leader to oversee centralized quality and control activities across projects during both Inquiry to Order (ITO) and Order to Remittance (OTR) phases. In this high-impact role, you will develop project quality plans, manage non-conformances and customer complaints, drive root cause analysis (RCA), and ensure strict compliance across high-voltage substations or transmission and distribution projects. You will be a key technical resource, guiding team members, partnering with stakeholders, and conducting supplier and site audits to guarantee operational excellence. Job Description Roles and Responsibilities Lead centralized quality assurance and control activities across the project life cycle (ITO to OTR), ensuring seamless interface coordination between all stakeholders. Develop, implement, and monitor Project Quality Plans, Inspection & Test Plans (ITPs), and Project Key Performance Indicators, deploying corrective action plans to address deviations. Manage the end-to-end non-conformance (NCR) and customer complaint lifecycle, coordinating with suppliers, subcontractors, and internal partners to drive continuous improvement. Execute rigorous Root Cause Analyses (RCA) utilizing 8D, DMAIC, 5 Whys, Fishbone, and Pareto methods to resolve complex quality issues and prevent recurrence. Support auditing, surveillance, and validation of subcontractor and supplier activities, conducting onsite audits at project and manufacturing sites. Participate in project gate reviews (Engineering, Procurement) and risk mitigation discussions while developing and delivering quality-related training as a Subject Matter Expert. Required Qualifications Engineering Degree: Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering (preferred), Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, or equivalent technical discipline. Mandatory Industry Experience: Must have 5 to 10+ years of quality engineering experience specifically within high-voltage substations or transmission and distribution (T&D) projects , featuring robust, hands-on exposure to FAT/SAT and field construction quality. (Candidates without this specific industry experience cannot be considered). Leadership & Standards: 5+ years of people management experience combined with a strong working knowledge of industry standards (IEEE, ANSI, NEC, NEMA) and blueprint/schematic reading. Certifications & Travel: Recognized professional certification (ASQ, PMI, PMP, CPSQ, CQE, or CMQ/OE), expertise in NCR/CAPA and RCA tools, and the willingness/ability to travel up to 75% with a valid driver's license. Desired Characteristics Direct experience leading quality activities in EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) projects and deep knowledge of ISO 9001 standards and auditing. Strong data analysis capabilities utilizing Paretos, dashboards, and IT systems (Microsoft Office, Power BI, document control, SAP). Familiarity with working around large, energized equipment while adhering to rigorous safety protocols, including wearing required PPE (e.g., fire-retardant clothing). Physical capability to work in all weather conditions, climb ladders, stand, walk, lift, bend, kneel, stoop, and reach as required on job sites.

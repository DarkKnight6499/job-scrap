Role Overview In this role, your primary responsibility will be onboarding new brokers and assisting with surplus lines tax filings. Other tasks may include assistance with inputting specific event details into our aggregation management system as well as other compliance related projects and administrative duties. Key Responsibilities Within the first 60 days , you will gain an understanding of the Specialty Group’s various underwriting segments. During this time, you will learn about the different lines of business through direct interaction with the underwriting teams, compliance team and independent research Within the first 90 days , you will gain an understanding of our software programs. You will learn how to utilize our aggregation management system to be able to coordinate the input and monitoring of Event Cancellation risks and assist with aggregation reports to be delivered to our reinsurers and insurance carriers. You will also learn to utilize various systems used to onboard third-party insurance brokers. Within 6-12 months, you will develop a level of comfort with the surplus lines processes and procedures. You will be able to assist in completing surplus lines forms and affidavits and independently administer special compliance projects. At the 12-month mark, you should have a robust understanding of how compliance fits within the Specialty Group.

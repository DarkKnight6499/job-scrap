Duties: Analytics (15%): Execute complex strategic analytics and business intelligence projects that provide growth opportunities and data driven insights to support content leadership decisions. Data Analysis (15%): Analyze platform usage patterns and customer segmentation data to recommend strategies for optimizing feature access and usage-based provisioning models. Work with Product, RevOps, and Finance teams to improve cost efficiency by aligning feature availability and infrastructure utilization with customer usage patterns and revenue contribution. Royalty Reporting (10%): Collaborate with Finance and Business intelligence teams to build a robust royalty reporting framework, ensuring consistent, compliant and true reporting and payouts to vendor partners. Democratize Data (15%): Partner with Product Management, Engineering, Revenue Operations, and Finance teams to define analytics requirements and ensure alignment between business objectives and product development priorities. Collaborate cross-functionally to monitor the provisioning and rollout of platform features and data products, ensuring efficient resource allocation and alignment with customer demand. Dashboarding (15%): Develop and maintain advanced analytical models, executive-level dashboards, and reporting frameworks to monitor key business metrics and present actionable insights and strategic recommendations to senior leadership to inform business planning, resource allocation, and performance optimization.

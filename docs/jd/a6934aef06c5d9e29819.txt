About The Role We are seeking a Research Analyst who will be based in either Washington D.C. or New York City office within the Corporate Positioning & Insights practice, serving on the primary research team. Research Analysts support the execution and analysis of primary research engagements, working closely with more senior team members across quantitative and qualitative projects. Responsibilities include supporting survey and qualitative research logistics, developing initial drafts of research materials and deliverables, conducting data analysis, and helping ensure projects are delivered accurately and efficiently. What You’ll Do Support the development of primary research materials, including first drafts of questionnaires, discussion guides, and other research instruments. Assist in preparing survey specifications, including sample quotas, banner plans, table stubs, and analysis requirements, and review outputs to ensure they are accurate and complete. Support the day-to-day execution of research projects, including survey fielding, survey link testing, vendor coordination, focus group logistics, timeline tracking, and quality control. Develop first drafts of project materials and deliverables, including PowerPoint presentations, toplines, data tables, and written summaries of findings. Review survey data, tabulations, and research outputs for accuracy and consistency. Support focus groups and in-depth interviews, including preparation, note taking, analysis, and development of findings. Assist senior team members with project management across larger and more complex research engagements. Contribute to the continuous improvement of research processes, tools, and methodologies – including AI-powered methodologies.

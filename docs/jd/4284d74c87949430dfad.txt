Job Description Ready to take your career Global? The Director of Portfolio Management Enablement activities that support the execution of SMB portfolio priorities across Customer Success and key business partner organizations. Serves as the primary liaison between Customer Success, Pricing, Revenue Operations, Product, Finance, Business Intelligence, GTM, VAS, Billing, and Operations teams to drive cross-functional initiatives, operationalize business requirements, improve portfolio visibility, and ensure successful execution of revenue growth, pricing, product adoption, and portfolio management initiatives. What you'll own: Serves as the primary liaison between Customer Success and partner organizations including Pricing, Revenue Operations, Product, GTM, VAS, Finance, Business Intelligence, Billing, and Operations to support portfolio priorities and strategic initiatives. Translates Customer Success and portfolio management needs into clear business requirements, process improvements, operational workflows, and system enhancements. Leads cross-functional initiatives focused on portfolio growth, pricing actions, revenue optimization, customer retention, product adoption, and customer experience improvements. Partners with Business Intelligence, Data Architecture, and reporting teams to improve portfolio visibility through enhanced reporting, data governance, customer segmentation, business definitions, and performance measurement capabilities. Establishes governance, prioritization, and accountability processes to ensure portfolio initiatives are effectively executed across business functions. Oversees Partner Stop Payment and Buyout programs, including operational governance, process optimization, business requirements, automation opportunities, and stakeholder alignment. Identifies and addresses revenue leakage, billing process gaps, operational inefficiencies, and system limitations that impact portfolio performance and customer outcomes. Partners with Pricing, Finance, Revenue Operations, and Billing teams to support pricing and billing enhancements that improve profitability, protect margins, and strengthen portfolio economics. Ensures back-office operational, process, and system changes are effectively communicated, documented, tested, and ready for frontline execution. Develops business cases, implementation plans, and recommendations that support portfolio priorities, operational improvements, and revenue-enhancing initiatives. Provides leadership, coaching, and development to team members while fostering a culture of collaboration, accountability, continuous improvement, and operational excellence. Not an exhaustive list; other duties as assigned.

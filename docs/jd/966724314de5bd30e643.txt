Accounting Associate II - Corporate Subsidiaries Corporate Subsidiaries Controllership Fulltime/Boston, MA or Springfield, MA The Opportunity This Accounting Consultant role is a key role to our Corporate Accounting and Reporting team. The position reports to the Director of Corporate Subsidiaries Controllership. The role is responsible for the accounting, reporting, and control activities supporting MassMutual’s non-insurance subsidiaries, with a particular focus on investment activity and related financial results. The incumbent will partner with Investment Management and other stakeholders to analyze transactions, resolve accounting issues, support close and audit requirements, and improve the processes used to deliver accurate and timely reporting. This is an opportunity for an individual who thrives in a highly dynamic and agile accounting environment to collaborate across teams focused on solid execution and improving outcomes. We are seeking an accounting professional who wants to join a highly motivated team of professionals focused on proactively driving the development and execution of process improvements, meaningful analysis and improved coordination with the business partners. The Team The Corporate Subsidiaries Controllership team provides controllership and governance oversight for MassMutual’s non-insurance subsidiaries. The team partners across Finance and the business to promote accurate and timely financial reporting; oversee legal entity governance, capital activity, non-routine transactions, and external audits; strengthen internal controls; and drive consistency in the review and oversight of subsidiary results. The incumbent will help shape the team’s operating model, develop talent, and advance improvements that strengthen execution across the subsidiary portfolio.

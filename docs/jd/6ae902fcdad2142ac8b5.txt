We are seeking a highly motivated and experienced Senior Organizational Change Management (OCM) Consultant to join our growing team. In this role, you will play a critical part in helping our clients successfully navigate complex organizational transformations, particularly within the technology sector. You will be responsible for leading and managing high-impact OCM initiatives, from developing effective adoption strategies and communication plans to identifying and mitigating resistance to change. This includes collaborating with business leaders, mentoring team members, contributing to business development efforts, and ensuring the timely delivery of high-quality OCM solutions. The ideal candidate possesses a strong consulting background, excellent communication skills, and a proven track record of delivering successful OCM outcomes.

About the Galaxy Summer Internship Program The Galaxy NYC Summer Internship Program is an immersive, nine-week experience taking place on-site in our New York HQ office. During the nine weeks, you will gain hands-on, practical work experience on our Sales and Trading team(s), working on real products and problems alongside the people building them. In addition to learning on the job, Galaxy interns participate in professional development programming, social events, and networking opportunities with senior leaders. This year, we’re adding a rotational element to our Sales and Trading internship, giving interns exposure across more than one area of the business. When you apply, you’ll be asked to rank your top three desk preferences. If we think you’re a great fit, we’ll be in touch and will let you know which seat you’re being considered for.

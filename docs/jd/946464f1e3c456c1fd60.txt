Analyst, Listings, Capital Markets & ETFs
Intercontinental Exchange
Location: New York, New York
Link: https://careers.ice.com/jobs/13988

Job Purpose In this high-visibility position, the Analyst will support NYSE's Global Listings, Capital Markets and Exchange Traded Funds (ETF) teams and NYSE management in obtaining new business and strengthening existing relationships. The Analyst will conduct data analysis, prepare presentation materials, oversee the new listing process for both operating companies and exchange traded products, manage databases, and work cross-functionally within the organization and with external stakeholders. The Analyst will liaise with C-level executives, investor relations professionals, ETF issuers and product teams, underwriters, outside counsel, members of the trading and market-making community, sales, events, marketing and regulation. Responsibilities Support the Capital Markets, Listings and ETF business lines. Understand and communicate the NYSE and NYSE Arca value proposition to corporate and ETF issuers. Customize presentations for IPO prospects, ETF issuers, client meetings and other transactions, including capital markets updates, industry comparisons, deal tables, peer trading comparisons and visibility proposals. Lead companies and ETF issuers through the listing process, from pre-launch through listing day, serving as point of contact and coordinating with counsel, regulation and operations teams. Execute Designated Market Maker (DMM) interviews with corporate issuers and support the selection process. Maintain databases and report to management on market landscape, KPIs and the corporate and ETF pipelines. Partner with senior team members on strategic projects across the NYSE, conducting research and analysis, preparing presentation materials, and supporting execution.

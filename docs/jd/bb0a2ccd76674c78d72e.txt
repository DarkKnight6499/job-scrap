Job Description What is the opportunity? The Fixed Income Purchases & Sales group within RBC Capital Market’s Global Operations division, is looking for an Analyst to perform daily processing, clearance, settlement and/or Purchase & Sales functions associated Federal and Non-Federal Fixed Income securities. This group is focused on supporting fixed income products across North America. This is an exciting opportunity to become a part of a dynamic team providing critical support for the firm's day-to-day business activities, while contributing towards the division's target operating model. What will you do? Effectively work with teams across multiple business entities in a fast-paced environment to ensure the settlement of US Fixed Income trades Take ownership of inquiries, deliver on commitments, and ask for support as needed Trade Confirmations (verbal/written) Fail trade reporting and resolution Cash, position break and claim resolution Handle all trade exceptions on a timely basis and troubleshoot trade issues prior to settlement Ensure compliance of all department procedures and policies Work on Canadian statutory holidays is required (Time in lieu will be provided) Collect and analyze information What you need to succeed? Must haves New Graduate or 1 - 2 years of work experience Excellent communication skills, both written and oral Ability to work in a fast past environment Detail oriented, organized and results driven Strong analytical skills with problem solving abilities Strong interpersonal skills and ability to work in a team environment and support others as required Proficient computer skills in Windows based software Nice-to-haves Basic understanding of Fixed Income Products Recognized financial industry qualifications (ie.

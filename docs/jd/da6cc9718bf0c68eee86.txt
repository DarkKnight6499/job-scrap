Responsibilities The Real Estate Portfolio Lead is responsible for managing and optimizing the Bank's real estate portfolio through asset management, transaction execution, financial analysis, and strategic planning. This role leads lease, acquisition, disposition, and portfolio initiatives while providing oversight of lease administration, lease accounting, and third-party service providers. The position partners closely with Facilities, Design & Construction, Legal, Finance, and business leaders to maximize portfolio performance, manage risk, and support the Bank's long-term real estate strategy. Salary Range The salary range for this position is $81,700/yr - $165,100/yr plus bonus. The base salary indicated for this position reflects the compensation range applicable to all levels of the role across the United States. Actual salary offers within this range may vary based on a number of factors, including the specific responsibilities of the position, the candidate’s relevant skills and professional experience, educational qualifications, and geographic location. Duties and Responsibilities: Portfolio & Asset Management Manage the Bank's leased and owned real estate portfolio. Monitor portfolio performance, lease obligations, operating costs, occupancy metrics, and market conditions. Track critical lease, property, and ownership dates and develop recommendations to support portfolio optimization. Manage relationships with landlords, tenants, brokers, governmental agencies, and external stakeholders. Oversee lease administration and lease accounting functions to ensure data accuracy, compliance, audit readiness, and effective reporting. Portfolio space management activities, including occupancy planning, utilization analysis, and workplace optimization initiatives. Manage property tax assessments and appeals in partnership with third-party consultants and internal stakeholders. Support management and disposition of OREO and other held-for-sale assets. Oversee property-related matters, including easements, encroachments, access agreements, deed restrictions, and other real estate rights and obligations. Transactions, Acquisitions & Negotiations Lead lease, renewal, relocation, acquisition, disposition, and development transactions across the portfolio. Develop transaction strategies and buy-versus-lease analyses to support business and financial objectives. Coordinate due diligence, internal approvals, entitlements, and transaction documentation. Manage brokers, attorneys, appraisers, consultants, and other external service providers throughout transaction execution. Represent the Bank in negotiations with landlords, sellers, buyers, developers, governmental entities, and community stakeholders. Lead real estate acquisitions and dispositions, including financial evaluation, contract negotiation, due diligence, closing activities, and post-transaction integration. Manage municipal requests and approvals, including zoning matters, variances, permits, right-of-way issues, and other local government requirements that impact the portfolio. Strategy & Leadership Support development and execution of corporate real estate, occupancy, and workplace strategies. Provide data-driven recommendations and insights to senior leadership regarding portfolio performance, risk management, and optimization opportunities. Assist with merger and acquisition real estate due diligence, integration planning, and portfolio rationalization efforts. Mentor team members and foster collaboration across Corporate Real Estate and partner functions. Provide oversight and strategic direction to lease administration, transaction management, tax appeal consultants, and other third-party service providers to ensure high-quality service delivery and alignment with organizational objectives. Additional Success Measures Effective management of tax appeals, municipal approvals, and property-related obligations resulting in cost savings and risk mitigation. Accurate space management and occupancy reporting that supports business planning and workplace strategy. Successful execution of acquisitions, dispositions, easements, and other complex real estate transactions. Strong governance and administration of lease obligations, critical dates, and compliance requirements across the portfolio. Knowledge & Technical Skills Strong understanding of commercial real estate principles, market dynamics, lease structures, and transaction processes. Ability to analyze financial statements, real estate valuations, occupancy costs, investment returns, and buy-versus-lease scenarios. Strong analytical, strategic thinking, and problem-solving capabilities. Demonstrated ability to lead cross-functional teams and build collaborative partnerships across business units. Excellent written and verbal communication skills with strong attention to detail.

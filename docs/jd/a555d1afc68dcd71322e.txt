The Opportunity This role is on a hybrid schedule in our Detroit, MI office, in a few days per week with some remote work. Responsible for executing quantitative analysis, reporting, and portfolio surveillance to identify, measure, monitor, and communicate auto credit risk. This role will leverage business, technical, and analytical skills to evaluate the evolving nature of auto credit risk, support Risk Appetite and KRI monitoring, and create transparency into expected and actual portfolio performance. Through analysis, independent reporting, and in-depth research, this role will contribute to Independent Risk Management’s responsibility to provide objective oversight and effective challenge. This role will help assess portfolio trends, validate key metrics, support breach commentary and escalation routines, and evaluate whether Ally remains aligned with its risk appetite, risk limits, and emerging credit-risk priorities. The Work Itself Job Responsibilities Execute recurring portfolio monitoring and reporting processes across the consumer auto lifecycle, including originations, vintage performance, delinquencies, losses, proforma performance, and key risk concentrations Support monthly Risk Appetite and KRI reporting by aggregating data, reviewing threshold performance, identifying breaches, validating results, and preparing concise root-cause and action-oriented commentary Maintain, improve, and automate repeatable reporting processes and dashboards that provide clear visibility into portfolio performance, emerging risks, and business line strategy impacts Work with large, complex datasets using tools such as SQL, Power BI, Excel, and related platforms to produce accurate, well-controlled analysis and reporting Review business line reporting and analysis through an independent risk lens, helping identify trends, anomalies, data-quality issues, and areas requiring further review or escalation Prepare clear, concise reporting and analysis for internal leadership routines, Credit Dashboards, Credit Flash updates, Risk Appetite materials, and ERMC/RC support as needed Conduct ad-hoc analysis and special projects focused on emerging credit risks, underwriting or pricing strategy impacts, affordability, fraud, vehicle values, delinquency formation, and loss-performance trends Collaborate with IRM and business line partners to understand drivers of performance, support effective challenge, and communicate risks in a practical, decision-useful manner Qualifications 1+ years of experience in analytics, reporting, risk management, finance, or a related quantitative field; experience in financial services or consumer automotive lending preferred Strong technical and data skills, including experience with SQL and Power BI; Python, R, Snowflake, SAS, or other data/analytics tools a plus Advanced proficiency with Microsoft Office products, including Excel and PowerPoint, with the ability to translate analysis into clean, accurate, executive-ready materials Strong analytical, problem-solving, and critical-thinking skills, with attention to detail and the ability to identify trends, anomalies, and potential data-quality concerns Ability to manage recurring deliverables and ad-hoc requests while maintaining accuracy, organization, and timely execution Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to summarize complex credit-risk topics clearly and concisely for management audiences Ability to work collaboratively with teammates, business partners, and stakeholders while developing independent analysis and judgment Curiosity and willingness to learn about auto finance, risk appetite, portfolio performance, credit policy, forecasting, and emerging consumer credit risks Education level: Bachelor's Degree preferably in Statistics, Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, Computer Science, Data Analytics, Mathematics, or a related quantitative curriculum;

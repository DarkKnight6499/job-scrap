American University is a student-centered research institution located in Washington, DC, with highly-ranked schools and colleges, internationally-renowned faculty, and a reputation for creating meaningful change in the world. Learn more about American University Department: School of Communication Time Type: Full time FLSA Status: Exempt Job Description: The School of Communication at American University (AU) in Washington, D.C., invites applications for a full-time, faculty position at the rank of associate or full professor, with an emphasis on interactive storytelling, worldbuilding, and/or playful design in the public interest. Depending on qualifications, the appointee may be recommended for tenure at the time of hiring. Candidates should hold a terminal degree as appropriate to their focus, such as an MFA or Ph.D. The salary range is from $105,000-$124,000. The School of Communication (SOC) at American University engages the most pressing issues of our time through teaching, scholarship, and professional experiences in communication and media. In the Film and Media Arts division, our faculty and students make media that matters in and beyond one of the most dynamic and influential cities in the world. The candidate is expected to work closely with or lead American University’s Game & Interactive Media Center, which is a world-class hub for experiential education, interactive storytelling, and computational media with a public purpose. While the Center is increasingly focused on undergraduate learning, its graduate programs were ranked #24 in the world by the Princeton Review in 2026, including the MA in Game Design and the MFA in Games and Interactive Media. The Center recently launched a new minor in Game Design, with one track for media studies students and another for those with an interest in computer science and game development. The ideal candidate will be an outstanding media maker, educator, and curricular strategist. They should ideally be comfortable with many principles of good game design. The candidate’s practice in interactive media should connect to one or more domains in the public interest: the environment, health, urban and spatial planning, education, or social issue storytelling, and should interface with science communication and journalism, museums, nonprofits, etc. As more AI tools are integrated into production pipelines, the candidate should have some knowledge of where such tools are useful and how they can be guided, and be able to engage thoughtfully with students who are both AI resistant and AI enthusiasts. Job responsibilities may include teaching courses; directing undergraduate and graduate game programs; mentoring students; securing new partnerships and funding for original media projects and high-impact collaborations; and participating in School of Communication and University governance. The position, which will begin in August of 2027, comes with powerful opportunities for collaboration with a tremendous mix of organizations in Washington, DC, from arts funders to policy think tanks to global nonprofits. Collaborating across the university with our world-class faculty is particularly well-suited to the Game & Interactive Media Center and is encouraged. Consideration of applications will commence on November 1, 2026. Candidate interviews will begin in late 2026/early 2027 and continue until the position is filled. To apply, submit a letter of application, curriculum vitae, one letter of recommendation (two more letters will be requested for those who reach the interview stage), a teaching portfolio, optional creative portfolio, and optional excerpt of exemplary publications; finalist candidates will be asked for two letters of recommendation (likely to coincide with interviews). Please submit applications via Workday. Please contact the SOC Faculty Affairs Coordinator at 202-885-2133 or email facultyaffairs_SOC@american.edu if you have any questions.

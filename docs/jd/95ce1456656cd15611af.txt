Fitch Learning is a leader in financial services training. With unrivaled breadth and depth, we deliver learning solutions for apprentices, graduates, and experienced professionals across the financial services industry. Our offerings include expert faculty, e-learning, coaching, and blended assessments that help improve individual and organizational performance. Working at Fitch Learning offers the opportunity to be part of a global leader in financial education, committed to delivering innovative training solutions. You’ll collaborate with a team of experts dedicated to empowering professionals with the skills and knowledge needed to succeed in the finance industry. By joining us, you’ll help create impactful learning experiences that drive career advancement and business performance. Fitch Learning is seeking an Associate Director, Sales & Business Development based in our Chicago office. About the Team We are looking for a dynamic, commercially driven sales professional to help grow Fitch Learning’s presence across the financial services market. This role is focused on sales by developing strategic client relationships, generating new business, and leading consultative sales conversations for our premium training and learning solutions. You will work closely with senior stakeholders across client organizations, identifying opportunities to sell and deliver tailored solutions that address business needs and create long-term value. This is an exciting sales opportunity to expand our client base, strengthen existing partnerships, and drive revenue growth in a highly visible commercial sales role.

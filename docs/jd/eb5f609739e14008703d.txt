The Senior Associate, Portfolio Management & ALM works alongside senior portfolio managers to monitor and report on performance and risk, producing analysis that guides tactical decisions to enhance returns while maintaining liability alignment. What You Will Do: Support daily management of existing asset portfolios, which includes monitoring risk positions against cedant guidelines and internal risk thresholds. Engage in recurring discussions with asset managers on market opportunities, portfolio performance, compliance with guidelines, and tactical shifts across public and private fixed-income markets. Analyze and report on key drivers of portfolio performance against target and suggest ways to improve portfolio performance. Collaborate with asset managers to develop investment recommendations based on market conditions and ALM positioning. Evaluate prospective investments and their relative value versus competing alternatives. Assist with onboarding of new investment mandates. Work closely with Actuarial, Risk, Capital, Finance, and Treasury teams to support pricing, due-diligence efforts, and company-wide initiatives. Build strong working relationships across the firm. What You Will Have: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Mathematics, or a related discipline; advanced graduate degree or progress towards CFA and/or comparable actuarial/risk credentials viewed favorably. Minimum of 3+ years of experience in insurance asset management, fixed-income portfolio management, or roles focused on liability-driven investment mandates. Familiarity with fixed-income markets across public and private sectors, ALM principles, and portfolio performance measurement is viewed favorably. Strong technical background in analysis and reporting using MS Office; familiarity with Python or SQL is a plus.

Colstrip Business Analyst In this role, you will be responsible for analytical, technical, financial and business process solutions for the Colstrip Power Plant which will directly support the Plant. You will assist with Owner relations, presentations of relevant budget materials, and plant operations materials. The business analyst is someone who is naturally curious and focused on continuously improving their knowledge of the business, customer focus, technical abilities and understanding of concepts and the development and maintenance of relationships with our stakeholders at all levels of the organization. How You’ll Power the Future At the Colstrip Power Plant, your work fuels progress. In this role, you’ll do more than contribute you’ll drive meaningful change by: Responsible for running, maintaining and improving financial models and understanding said model’s output to produce high-quality business solutions. Creates comprehensive reports including data analysis and other relevant information to improve business functions and maintain strong relationships with business stakeholders. Prepares solutions to moderately complex stakeholder questions and/or requests on the financial and business aspects of Colstrip Power Plant. Works with internal and external groups to accurately translate business requirements into strategic financial and technical solutions. Build knowledge of the business processes to support the development/improvement of ongoing operations. Develops a high-level knowledge of Plant Operations, Maintenance and Systems, with a broad understanding of how the Power Plant operates. Interprets financial, project based, plant process, budget and operational data for discussion with stakeholders to assure the most accurate and efficient financial and business practices are in place. Ensures data consistency across enterprise resource planning, databases, customer relationship management and accounting. Assisting with reporting requests, team documentation updates and research projects for leadership. Refreshing recurring team metrics, updating active tracking sheets and building drafts of team data reports. What You Have Minimum Requirements Bachelor’s degree; or associate degree and 4 to 6 years related work experience; or 5 to 7 years related work experience. Proficiency in Microsoft Office products, especially Excel and PowerPoint. Solves complex problems independently through learning and persistence. Ability to work collaboratively with all levels/groups within the organization by development of strong trust relationships. Excellent presentation skills. A strong customer-driven focus. Excellent communication and interpersonal skills with the ability to present ideas in a concise and focused manner. Preferred Qualifications Dual Degree in Business Management/Finance/Accounting or closely related and 2+ years’ of related experience., Demonstrated ability to work in a team environment.

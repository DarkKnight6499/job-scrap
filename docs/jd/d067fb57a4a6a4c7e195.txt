Associate/VP: Equity Research - Semiconductors Job Description: This job is responsible for assisting with the recommendation process related to the buying, selling, or holding of financial securities and/or studying the role of government in the economy. Key responsibilities include performing financial and statistical analyses, conducting research, preparing reports, helping to originate, structuring and modeling transactions, and assessing market trends. The Semiconductors team covers a wide range of companies involved in the design and manufacturing of chips that serve the cloud computing, AI, automotive, networking and industrial markets. This is an exciting role that offers an opportunity to learn about cutting edge technology, and to collaborate with a wide range of BofA global teams. The ideal candidate will have an interest/passion for working in the technology industry, modelling skills and desire to work in a collegial and collaborative team environment.

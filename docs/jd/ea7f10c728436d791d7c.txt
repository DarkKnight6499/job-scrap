What you will bring: Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering (Mechanical, Chemical preferred), Physics, or related degree is required 0-3 years’ experience in new product development, contract engineering, consulting engineering, MEP design, or building energy modeling preferred Key Competencies (Preferred): Strong computer skills using MS Office Suite and other Windows programs required Excellent communication skills, both written and oral, are required Customer-centric mindset Familiarity with CAD and BIM tools (i.e. Revit) Familiarity with building energy modeling tools Knowledge of HVAC products and systems Experience in a customer-facing technical role Understanding of the software development process and Agile practices Knowledge of commercial building construction industry is a plus.

SS&C is a leading provider of mission-critical, AI-powered technology and services empowering financial services and healthcare organizations to work smarter, faster, and securely. Founded in 1986, SS&C is headquartered in Windsor, Connecticut, and has offices worldwide. More than 23,000 financial services and healthcare organizations, from the world's largest companies to small and mid-market firms, rely on SS&C for expertise, scale, and technology. Job Description Job Title: Associate Manager, Operations Locations: Los Angeles, CA (hybrid) About the Role The Associate Manager, Operations will support daily operational activities and contribute to the efficient functioning of critical business processes within SS&C Technologies. This role involves assisting with team supervision, process improvement initiatives, and ensuring high-quality service delivery. Why Join SS&C SS&C combines proprietary technology with deep industry expertise to support complex financial and health care operations. Our teams design, implement, and operate solutions that help clients manage data, automate processes, and scale their businesses with confidence. You will work with industry experts, modern platforms, and evolving technologies, gaining exposure to real-world operational challenges and large-scale enterprise environments. How You Will Make an Impact Assist in the day-to-day management and oversight of operational workflows and processes. Support the Operations Manager in leading and developing a team of operational specialists, including training and performance feedback. Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and operational metrics to identify areas for improvement and efficiency gains. Contribute to the development and implementation of new operational procedures and policies. Ensure adherence to regulatory requirements, company policies, and service level agreements (SLAs). Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including client services, technology, and compliance, to resolve operational issues. Participate in project initiatives aimed at enhancing operational effectiveness and client satisfaction. Prepare and present operational reports and analyses to management. Act as an escalation point for complex operational inquiries and issues. Required Experience Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Finance, Operations Management, or a related field. 3-5 years of experience in an operations role, preferably within the financial services or technology industry. Proven experience assisting with team leadership or supervision. Strong understanding of operational processes, workflows, and best practices. Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills with a keen eye for detail. Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite, particularly Excel. Strong communication (written and verbal) and interpersonal skills. Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, dynamic environment and manage multiple priorities. What Sets You Apart Familiarity with SS&C products or similar financial technology platforms is a plus. Join SS&C, where innovation meets global opportunities.

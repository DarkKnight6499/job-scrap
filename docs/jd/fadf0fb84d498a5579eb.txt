Key responsibilities Are you skilled at turning market and portfolio data into clear, actionable recommendations? Do you enjoy executing trades, improving portfolio positioning, and ensuring every client meeting is backed by clean, accurate analytics? We’re looking for a Portfolio Associate to: • prepare client reports and meeting materials, refreshing day-of as needed • recommend trades and asset allocation updates in advance of client meetings and implement trades once reviewed • execute investment and trading activity across portfolios, including support for alternative investments and structured products and required transaction paperwork (as applicable) • analyze and interpret portfolio performance and provide recommendations for change in partnership with the Financial Advisor and Investment Committee • perform business analytics and due diligence support to sustain the growth of the business The team You’ll be working as part of Parham | Deidiker Family Wealth Advisors, based in Nashville, TN, within Wealth Management Americas. Our team supports ultra-high net worth individuals and families through comprehensive planning and disciplined portfolio management. As a Portfolio Associate, you’ll play a key role in meeting preparation, investment execution and trading, and ongoing portfolio and risk management support for the team. A Portfolio Associate supports the team by preparing investment analysis, executing portfolio and trading activity, and partnering with the Investment Committee and Financial Advisors to implement disciplined, client-aligned portfolio recommendations.

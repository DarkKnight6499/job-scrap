PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT ANALYST SENIOR - WEALTH SALES PERFORMANCE AND INCENTIVES WHAT IS THE OPPORTUNITY? The Wealth Management division is seeking a Senior Analyst (Business Intelligence) to support the Sales Performance Analytics and Compensation Incentives Manager. This role offers an excellent opportunity to develop deep expertise in financial analytics, sales performance measurement, and incentive compensation plan administration. As a key member of the analytics team, you will work closely with the Manager, assisting with day-to-day operations and gradually expanding responsibilities to support the division during peak periods and manager absences. This role involves both analytical and operational responsibilities: analyzing sales performance across business units, supporting the design and delivery of performance reporting, and directly assisting with incentive compensation plan administration. You will work with the Wealth Management executive leadership team, supporting their decision-making with timely, accurate, and insightful reporting. WHAT WILL YOU DO? Analytics & Reporting Develop and maintain reports and dashboards to measure sales performance, productivity, and profitability across business units and client-facing roles Integrate and validate data from multiple systems (CRM, financial platforms, etc.) to create coherent datasets for analysis Produce routine performance reports, scorecards, and ad hoc analyses for division leadership, regional managers, and individual contributors Analyze trends, identify discrepancies, and research root causes; recommend solutions to management Assist in creating and maintaining performance management reporting at division and regional levels Support the Manager in designing new reports and refining existing ones based on stakeholder feedback Incentive Compensation Support Assist with the design and administration of incentive compensation plans across business units Support the creation of manager and individual colleague scorecards and performance tracking reports Help manage and execute the incentive reporting process, including reconciliations and audit support Participate in issues resolution related to compensation calculations and reporting Support the annual budget development, goal-setting process, and year-end compensation activities Assist with internal and external audits (OCC, etc.) by preparing reports and documentation Technical & Operational Duties Manage data integration workflows, including writing and optimizing SQL queries and leveraging Snowflake for data extraction and transformation Create advanced Excel tools including complex formulas, macros, and PivotTables for data analysis and reporting Build and maintain visualizations and dashboards in Tableau or similar BI tools Support vendor and application management related to analytics responsibilities Ensure compliance with all Bank Operational and Credit policies, regulatory requirements, and governance standards Serve as deputy to the Manager, stepping in to manage ongoing operations during absences or peak periods Collaboration & Development Partner with the Manager and analytics team to understand stakeholder needs and deliver requested analyses Collaborate with regional managers, finance partners, and HR Compensation to support incentive plan execution Develop technical and analytical capabilities while building knowledge of the Wealth business Contribute to team projects and initiatives that enhance analytics capabilities WHAT DO YOU NEED TO SUCCEED? Must-Have Requirements Minimum of 5+ years of financial analysis experience and/or sales analysis experience, with demonstrated success in a professional analytical role Minimum of 2+ years of sales performance reporting and/or compensation analytics experience (preferred 3+ years) Minimum of 3+ years of advanced Excel experience, including demonstrated proficiency with complex formulas, macros, PivotTables, and other advanced data manipulation techniques Minimum of 1+ years working with SQL or other advanced database query tools (SQL, Snowflake, Python, or Alteryx preferred) Experience running or supporting incentive compensation plans or sales compensation programs is a strong plus Banking or financial services industry experience preferred Skills & Competencies Strong analytical and mathematical skills; comfort working with large datasets and complex analyses Hands-on proficiency with Tableau, Salesforce CRM Analytics, or similar business intelligence tools Advanced Microsoft Office skills (Excel, Access, Word, PowerPoint) and related financial systems Excellent verbal and written communication skills; ability to translate complex analyses into clear insights for various audiences Demonstrated ability to identify root causes, research issues, and engage partners in problem-solving Strong attention to detail and ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously Ability to work independently and as part of a team Proven project planning and coordination skills Personal Attributes Growth mindset with strong desire to develop expertise in compensation analytics and sales performance measurement Detail-oriented with strong follow-through Collaborative and service-oriented Comfortable working in a fast-paced, data-driven environment Ability to manage sensitive compensation and performance data with confidentiality and professionalism WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU?

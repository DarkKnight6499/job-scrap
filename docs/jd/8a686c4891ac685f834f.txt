Senior Emerging Markets Research Associate
Dodge & Cox
Location: San Francisco Office

Role Overview Dodge & Cox is seeking an experienced, highly skilled Research Associate to join our team. The Senior Emerging Markets Research Associate will work with senior analysts and members of the Dodge & Cox investment team in identifying, researching and advocating for emerging market companies. Dodge & Cox's well-established reputation provides us with unparalleled access to corporate management teams. We regularly host Fortune Global 500 executives at our offices in San Francisco. This is a two-to-three-year position, after which most individuals enroll in business school or pursue a career in investing. Key Responsibilities Researching emerging market companies that may be attractive investments Monitoring portfolio investments, competitors, and industry developments Building and maintaining financial and valuation models Engaging with company management teams, industry experts, and sell-side analysts as requested Creating investment-related presentation materials Ideal Candidate Profile High levels of academic and professional achievement and leadership ability Attention to detail with the ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously Excellent written and verbal communication skills Intellectually curious with a genuine appetite for understanding businesses and industries in depth Comfortable working across time zones and cultures, and able to build productive relationships with external research partners Detail-oriented and conscientious, producing analysis of exceptional quality Able to work independently and exercise sound judgment with limited supervision Qualifications 2-4 years of combined experience working for an investment manager, investment bank, or a substantially similar experience. At least 2 years of experience doing fundamental company level research for an investment manager. Four-year college degree (B.A. or B.S.) with an excellent academic record that includes quantitative coursework Strong ability to collect and synthesize quantitative and qualitative data Knowledge of global equity markets, especially in developing economies Strong quantitative, analytical, and computer skills Experience applying AI tools (e.g., Claude, Copilot) to research workflows The salary range for this position is $175,000k - $200,000k.

Job Summary The Financial Analyst Sr Staff will lead the preparation of dynamic financial forecasts and ad hoc analysis of strategic opportunities. Additional duties of this Senior role include analyzing/making recommendations on general business strategy, and developing/managing staff. Job Description What You'll Do: Initiate, formulate and develop assumptions for financial forecasts, in conjunction with operating company/business segment personnel, using corporate financial models including the identification of significant strategic issues and alternative and/or contingency plans. Review monthly/quarterly variance reporting and analysis of budget vs. actual results and actual vs. prior year results on a consolidated or segment level performed by others for the primary purpose of improving future forecasts. This analysis will also aid operating companies in understanding their financial results and assist Financial Reporting in SEC reporting. Develop/manage staff where applicable and serve as an example/advisor/mentor to departmental analysts. Prepare and make reports to executive management and Board of Directors as assigned or required. Develop special strategic evaluations of corporate business opportunities including but not limited to evaluating acquisition and divestiture opportunities and make recommendations based upon the results of the evaluations. Identify and implement opportunities for process improvement, cost savings and revenue enhancement. Communicate clearly and confidently with senior level management on matters of strategic opportunities and/or regulatory strategy where applicable. Provide leadership to a team of energetic employees that collaborate well with others and are interested in providing best-in-class support to the operations of our energy assets. What We're Looking For: Education requirements are listed below: Bachelor's Degree in Business, Accounting, Finance or Engineering required. Work Experience requirement listed below: 10 or more years of work experience in the disciplines mentioned in the education requirement including managerial experience. Other: This position requires extensive coordination with corporate finance, financial planning and analysis, accounting/tax, commercial operations, generation and transmission, regulatory, requiring the incumbent to work across functional boundaries and operating companies. A successful candidate will exhibit the following qualities/skills: Effective Leadership Skills, Presentation Skills, Proficient in Microsoft Office, UI Financial Model Programming, PeopleSoft, and Business Object; Ability to communicate clearly, concisely and comfortably with all levels of the organization, Strategic Thinking, Relationship building; Teamwork, Adaptability/Flexibility, Customer Focus, Planning/Organizing Skills. MBA, CFA, CPA, CMA, CIA, PE, CFP or other professional certification preferred. Familiarity with AEP accounting desired.

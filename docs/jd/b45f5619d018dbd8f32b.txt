Get To Know The Team: Based in Windsor, Connecticut, the Associate Desktop Systems Technician supports centralized IT hardware lifecycle and asset management operations for SS&C employees across the organization. The primary focus of this hands-on position is processing returned and incoming technology equipment, maintaining accurate asset records, and coordinating equipment for redeployment, repair, or approved disposal. The role also provides first-level help desk and remote technical support to employees across SS&C locations. The anticipated work mix is approximately 65% device lifecycle and asset operations and 35% end-user support, based on operational needs. This position requires an onsite presence in Windsor 5 days per week to manage the physical receiving, inspection, processing, and tracking of returned IT equipment.

Our associates serve with humility and a deep respect for their responsibility in helping our customers achieve their goals and realize their dreams. Together, we’re on a quest to change banking for good. We’re currently seeking a Product Design Director to lead our Anti-Money Laundering and Compliance & Ethics teams. These teams sit within our larger Risk and Audit Design portfolio, which is part of Capital One’s Experience Design organization. These teams are leading, and are responsible for, our innovative experiences that power our AML and Compliance platforms, keeping Capital One and our customers safeguarded in a well managed and effective manner. As a People Leader, you will lead the AIML and Compliance & Ethics portfolios, which consists in total of 10 - 12 product designers. Your ability to navigate complexity, delegate, and empower your teams will be vital to success in this role. Familiarity with AI tools and application of them in improving workflows and efficiency is expected. The Experience Design team at Capital One is at the forefront of enriching our customers’ digital and physical experiences and we are passionate about creating memorable, meaningful product experiences that build the Capital One brand with humanity and drive business advantage and innovation. We champion a thriving environment of collaboration, authenticity and healthy critique, in which we honor diversity of thought, create a culture of belonging, and elevate one another. If you’re a creative innovator who embraces an environment where you can experiment, learn, and change banking for good, we’d love to hear from you . Role Expectations As a Director of Anti-Money Laundering and Compliance & Ethics teams, you’ll lead a talented, cross-disciplinary team of designers and collaborate deeply with our research teams and Product and Tech leadership. You’ll be responsible for leading high-impact, highly complex projects and defining and driving design strategy that informs the higher-level vision of the organization and aligns to business objectives. If you're energized by design problems where precision, trust, and clarity aren't nice-to-haves but the whole point — where your work directly shapes how investigators catch financial crime and how the company upholds its ethical commitments — this is a rare seat at that table. Some of the qualities we are looking for to help meet role expectations are: Regulatory/compliance design literacy — experience designing within highly regulated environments (financial services, healthcare, or similar), where every flow has audit trail, disclosure, or policy implications. Complex workflow simplification — ability to take dense, rule-heavy processes (investigation queues, case management, escalation paths) and design them into clear, low-error user experiences Cross-functional fluency with risk/legal/compliance stakeholders — comfortable translating between design and non-design partners like investigators, compliance officers, or auditors who think in terms of controls and risk, not UX heuristics Data-dense interface design — experience designing for users who need to scan, triage, and act on large volumes of structured data quickly (alerts, transaction review, case files) Here are some of the expected competencies for this role: Product Design Expertise: Leverage established expertise and leadership in product design to elevate the quality and maturity of the design practice at Capital One. Leveraging a variety of skills across interaction, visual and service design; in collaboration with cross-functional partners, help to oversee and optimize the use of appropriate tools, platforms, frameworks and design systems and enable your team to develop artifacts that may include vision stories, journey maps, blueprints, high-fidelity designs and prototypes Leadership: Create conditions for a positive and inclusive team environment and set a vision for an inclusive, collaborative culture that makes your team feel valued and heard Human-Centered: Champion voices of both customers and partners, modeling the mindset that business and customer outcomes are not distinct but complementary, ultimately resulting in experiences that exceed baseline customer needs and expectations Business-Focused: Demonstrate expert knowledge of products, processes, our customers, competitors and industry market trends, applying relevant insights to shape how Design should engage and solve business challenges Problem-Solving: Share insights around technical advances and data capabilities that could influence outcomes, while helping larger teams navigate technical limitations and boundaries in service of bold technological solutions, data destinations and efficiency Collaboration: Connect the dots across teams, portfolios, platforms and LOBs, bringing together partners and processes in ways that drive more coherent experiences, accelerate delivery and elevate the value of design Communication: Present work across levels and job functions, modeling a strategic human-centered approach that integrates design frameworks, data and research insights to make the complex accessible, influence senior stakeholders and achieve impactful results The ideal candidate has a strong portfolio demonstrating your leadership, process, results and impact.

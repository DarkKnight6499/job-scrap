Application Deadline: 11/29/2026 Address: 300 E John Carpenter Freeway Job Family Group: Procurement Seeking a senior procurement leader with deep expertise in direct procurement, strategic sourcing, and vendor contract management within a standalone operating environment. The successful candidate will lead the transformation and establishment of a procurement function that is currently supported through transition service arrangements, ultimately building and scaling a self-sufficient sourcing and procurement organization. This leader will be responsible for conducting a comprehensive review of existing third-party contracts, assessing vendor performance, spend, and risk, and determining the optimal sourcing strategy, including renegotiating agreements with incumbent vendors or identifying and onboarding new providers. As the functional head of Procurement and Sourcing, the individual will will partner closely with business leaders to establish procurement frameworks, identify external support requirements previously provided through BMO, and build a sustainable procurement operating model that supports the organization's long-term growth and independence.

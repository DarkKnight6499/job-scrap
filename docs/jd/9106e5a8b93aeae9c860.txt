What skills and experience you’ll bring on this journey: You’ll have a minimum of a high school diploma or equivalent with 2+ years of Underwriting or business-related experience. • You’ll possess an intermediate working knowledge of Microsoft Office programs (Word, Excel, and Outlook). • You’ll possess strong written and verbal communication skills. • You’ll demonstrate the ability to effectively multi-task and problem solve.

Job Summary (Basic Function) The Associate Director, Technology Product Management for Medi-Span has two connected areas of accountability: owning product strategy for the North America Commercial market and leading strategy and roadmap direction for assigned product areas. These areas currently include solutions that support drug pricing and market access, supply chain and regulatory information, dispensing and pharmacy workflows, and patient-facing medication information. This leader is the primary Medi-Span Product partner for Commercial Sales opportunities and turns customer, market, competitive, regulatory, and product-performance evidence into clear priorities and investment decisions. The role balances growth with customer commitments, security, modernization, regulatory needs, and ongoing product health, while ensuring priority work moves from discovery through launch, adoption, and measurable results. The role leads Product professionals and works closely with Engineering, Design, Content, Sales, Marketing, Finance, Customer Success, Customer Support, Systems Implementation, and other Product leaders. Success requires sound judgment, strong commercial healthcare knowledge, and the ability to align teams around customer problems and sustainable business value. This leader also leads adoption of AI-enabled ways of working and the Agentic Product Development Life Cycle across their product areas. Wolters Kluwer Health is an established, fast-growing, and innovation-driven healthcare information technology company that delivers trusted clinical technology and evidence-based solutions to support decision-making and improve outcomes across the healthcare ecosystem.

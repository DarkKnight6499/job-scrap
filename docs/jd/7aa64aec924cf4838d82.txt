Job Description: Senior Business Analyst The Role The Senior Business Analyst is responsible for identifying, analyzing, and documenting business requirements to support complex, large-scale brokerage trading systems within the Fidelity Center for Applied Technology (FCAT). This role involves close collaboration with both business and technology stakeholders to ensure solutions align with strategic objectives. Strong communication and collaboration skills are critical for success in this position. The ideal candidate shows a focus on achieving goals, with a proactive approach to driving outcomes through clear, consistent communication with leadership and cross-functional teams. The candidate must be able to effectively convey information to diverse audiences, translating between technical and business perspectives as needed. Equally important is the ability to work collaboratively across organizational boundaries—both within FCAT and across partner organizations—by building and sustaining strong, productive relationships. The Expertise and Skills You Bring Proficient in conducting business analysis, writing requirements, designing, maintaining dynamic solutions and translating strategic objectives into action. Collaborates with database and solution architects to develop new solutions, creating relevant artifacts to deliver a functional specification, while conducting data mapping and data modeling exercise of new requirements Possesses transferable expertise in post trade lifecycle for Equity, Derivatives, digital asset products. Holds strong understanding of equity trading, options, bookkeeping, tax systems and designs systematic flows of a trading lifecycle. Possesses knowledge of basic accounting principles (GAAP). Proficient in defining user stories to successfully complete define-build-test cycles. Participates in system testing, user acceptance testing, and validation of implemented solutions. Proficient using Confluence and JIRA Demonstrates ability to translate business requirements, prioritize delivery, think strategically, and accept technical direction. Maintains strong technical interest in financial services and exhibits skill in researching and learning new technologies. Has experience working with geographically distributed teams. Demonstrates strong written and verbal communication skills, as well as strong interpersonal skills. Is motivated, self-directed, and results oriented. Bachelor’s degree required, preferably in Finance or Computer Science/Engineering. The Team The team is part of an exciting incubator within Fidelity, focused on rapidly transforming ideas from concept to scalable solutions that bring pioneering innovations to market. As part of the Fidelity Center for Applied Technology (FCAT), this group operates at the intersection of creativity, technology, and business strategy. FCAT serves as the firm’s innovation engine, employing emerging technologies and human-centered design to develop forward-thinking products and services that enhance people’s financial lives. The team not only builds and incubates potential new lines of business but also partners across Fidelity to share expertise on innovative tools, methodologies, and techniques. For a Senior Business Analyst with a passion for creating impactful products and driving innovation, this opportunity offers the chance to work in a fast-paced, collaborative environment where ideas are quickly brought to life and scaled for real-world impact.

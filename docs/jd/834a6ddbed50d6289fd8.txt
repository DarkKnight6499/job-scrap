What You Will Do: Want a front-row seat to the excitement of a major television newsroom? One day you could be helping producers prepare election coverage, the next you might be out in the field with a reporter covering breaking news, sitting in on editorial meetings, editing video, or helping the assignment desk track developing stories. You'll gain firsthand experience in how stories move from an idea to a broadcast and how they're shared across television, streaming, digital, and social media platforms. You'll also work closely with our assignment desk, fielding phone calls and emails from viewers, public information officers, community organizations, and newsmakers. This role requires strong interpersonal skills and the ability to communicate professionally and confidently with a wide variety of people. On any given day you might be: Working alongside producers, executive producers, reporters, photojournalists, digital producers, and assignment editors. Attending editorial meetings where story decisions are made. Assisting with newsgathering, breaking news coverage, and special projects. Learning video editing and newsroom production workflows. Supporting election coverage and major planned events. Seeing how content is created and distributed across broadcast, streaming, digital, and social media platforms.

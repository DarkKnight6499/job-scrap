Derivatives Trader, AVP - State Street Investment Management

The State Street Investment Management Trading team is a multi-asset trading desk that has been developed to meet the varied requirements that many of our client's demand. This role supports a broad range of investment strategies including index, quantitative, active, liability-driven, overlay, and asset allocation mandates. The successful candidate will gain exposure to exchange-traded and OTC derivatives utilized across global investment portfolios. Secondary/supporting roles relate to equities and foreign exchange. If you have 2+ years of previous buy side or sell side derivative trading experience, a deep interest in markets, ability to manage trading costs and be able to deliver a high level of service in a fast-paced environment, then this is the job for you. You will be working in a team environment with other traders located in Boston, London, and Hong Kong and you will report directly to the Head of Trading Americas in Boston. This is a fantastic opportunity to trade for a wide range of investment strategies and teams at State Street Investment Management. This job is located in Boston, Massachusetts. Specific Duties and Qualities sought: Provide support and assistance to the derivative trading team. Provide coverage to the global Portfolio Management teams with primary focus on derivatives. Responsible for ensuring quality, accuracy, and timely execution of client orders. Engage and communicate effectively with executing brokers to deepen these relationships across execution channels, gather market color, explore execution strategies and resolve exceptions. Ensure close cooperation and relationship building with support and oversight functions such as Portfolio Management, IT, Risk, Operations, Legal, Sales, Marketing and Compliance. General understanding of derivatives markets, equities and foreign exchange. Proficient in Bloomberg and execution management systems Knowledge of clearing, margining, collateral management, and derivative lifecycle processes. Demonstrated interest in capital markets, macroeconomics, and investing. Technical and Education Requirements: Four-year education degree or higher preferred Preferred experience with some or all of Excel, Python, Java, VBA, SQL. Ability to utilize data and analytics to evaluate execution performance and identify trading opportunities. Contribute to trading technology enhancements, workflow automation, and process optimization initiatives. Partner with technology teams to improve trading tools, reporting, and operational resiliency. Tableau data-visualization experience a plus. Required Competencies: Maintain the highest standards of conduct and integrity within the business and ensure compliance with industry practice, company policies, statute and regulatory requirements in all matters. Demonstrate a strong sense of ownership, accountability, and partnership while approaching markets from an investor-centric perspective. Possess the ability to operate on a team in a fast-paced environment; having flexibility and the skill to manage competing priorities. Possess strong written and verbal communication skills Salary Range: $90,000 - $157,500 Annual The range quoted above applies to the role in the primary location specified. If the candidate would ultimately work outside of the primary location above, the applicable range could differ.

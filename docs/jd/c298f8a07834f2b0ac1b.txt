Your Team, Your Impact The Associate Workplace Specialist ensures operational excellence, safety, and service delivery for our Santa Clara headquarters. This role acts as a key liaison between employees, site leadership, and Global Workplace Services (GWS), supporting daily building operations, vendor management, capital programs, budgeting, and health and safety compliance. The ideal candidate is a proactive, detail-oriented professional who thrives in a fast- paced, dynamic corporate environment and is committed to delivering a world-class workplace experience. support emergency preparedness drills and protocols Support ISO audit walks, document findings, and help drive corrective actions to closure Order and manage inventory of GWS supplies and materials Draft, edit, and distribute internal communications, announcements, and site-wide updates Maintain up-to-date signage, wayfinding, and site information for employees and visitors Budgeting & Financial Management Support development and management of operating budgets;

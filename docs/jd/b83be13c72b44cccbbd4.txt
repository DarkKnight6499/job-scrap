Position Summary The Manager, Model Risk is a senior individual contributor responsible for independently leading model validation activities and providing effective challenge across the model lifecycle. The role operates within the second line of defense and provides independent effective challenge evaluating the conceptual soundness, data, methodology, implementation, performance, limitations, and governance of models used across the enterprise and supports compliance with the organization's Model Risk Management framework and applicable regulatory expectations. Key Responsibilities Independently plan and execute risk-based validations of new and existing models, including assessments of conceptual soundness, data quality, methodology, assumptions, implementation, performance, limitations, and documentation. Lead validation engagements from scoping through final reporting, including the development of testing approaches, documentation of findings, and communication of conclusions. Provide independent effective challenge to model developers, owners, and users and assess whether identified model risks are appropriately understood, documented, and mitigated. Evaluate model performance monitoring, change management, ongoing review, and governance practices for alignment with the Model Risk Management framework and applicable regulatory expectations. Perform quantitative analysis and testing using relevant statistical techniques and programming tools such as Python or R. Prepare clear, well-supported validation reports and present conclusions, limitations, and findings to business partners, senior management, governance committees, and regulators, as appropriate. Partner with model development, risk, finance, technology, and business teams while maintaining independent second-line oversight. Contribute to enhancements of model validation practices, procedures, templates, and risk assessment methodologies. Qualifications Bachelor's degree in mathematics, statistics, economics, finance, data science, engineering, or a related quantitative field. 5+ years of direct experience performing model validation, independent model review, or closely related model risk oversight within financial services. Demonstrated experience evaluating model conceptual soundness, data, assumptions, methodology, implementation, performance, limitations, and ongoing monitoring. Proficiency in Python, R, SAS, SQL, or comparable analytical tools used to independently test and assess models. Strong knowledge of quantitative analysis, statistical techniques, model governance, and model risk management principles. Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to document defensible conclusions, communicate technical issues clearly, and influence stakeholders through effective challenge. Preferred Skills Experience validating credit risk, allowance, forecasting, stress testing, decisioning, or other financial risk models. Working knowledge of applicable model risk management guidance and industry practices, including SR 11-7 or comparable regulatory expectations. Experience validating and governing risk, financial, or forecasting models within the financial services industry, including familiarity with CECL framework models and related regulatory, accounting, and model risk management expectations. Value Proposition Join a high-impact second line of defense function that plays a critical role in shaping the organization’s risk posture and decision-making. As Manager, Model Risk, you will operate at the intersection of quantitative analytics, governance, and regulatory engagement, providing independent oversight that directly influences strategic outcomes across credit, finance, and enterprise risk. Own complex validation engagements from initial scoping through final reporting and issue follow-up. Apply independent judgment and effective challenge to models that support important credit, finance, and enterprise risk decisions. Influence enhancements to model risk practices and governance without direct people-management responsibility. Expand technical, regulatory, and executive communication capabilities across a diverse model portfolio. This role is suited for an experienced model validation professional seeking meaningful individual-contributor responsibility, broad model exposure, and the opportunity to influence model risk practices across the enterprise.

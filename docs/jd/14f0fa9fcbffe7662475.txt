About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a talented person who will put our customers at the center of everything we do. We are seeking a candidate who embraces a workplace where everyone feels valued and inspired. Help us build a better Wells Fargo. It all begins with outstanding talent. It all begins with you. In this role, you will: Onboard Treasury Services for large non-bank financial institutions. Help manage client onboarding and product implementations from the sales support perspective, serving as a key partner among downstream and upstream onboarding teams. Partner closely with Treasury Management Consultants, helping them achieve speed to revenue. Proactively communicate with customers, answering questions, providing updates on ongoing initiatives, and generally serving as the extension of the consultant. Get to know your clients and understand their unique needs and requirements. Identify opportunities for process improvements within scope of responsibilities or functional area. Review and research basic or tactical operational assignments that require evaluation and selection of alternatives, related to low to medium risk deliverables. Present recommendations for resolving more complex situations and exercise some independent judgement while developing understanding of function, policies, procedures, and compliance requirements. Have opportunity to develop and practice sales skills, with the goal of eventually becoming a Treasury Management Consultant.

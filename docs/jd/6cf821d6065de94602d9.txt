The IT Business Lead Analyst is a senior-level position responsible for directing and delivering key initiatives within our four-pillar intelligent servicing automation program. In this role, you will drive the end-to-end transformation of back-end servicing lifecycles, integrating predictive processing, risk-based governance, and generative artificial intelligence to eliminate manual operational touchpoints. You will partner with solutions architects, engineering leads, and business stakeholders to design scalable systems leveraging predictive processing, risk-based governance, and generative artificial intelligence across global operations. Responsibilities: Lead end-to-end discovery phases, feasibility studies, and business process mapping for strategic intelligent servicing and automation initiatives Translate high-level business objectives into structured epics, detailed user stories, unambiguous acceptance criteria, and comprehensive workflow diagrams. Collaborate with solutions architects and engineering leads to ensure technical designs satisfy business requirements, operational standards, and control mandates Automate back-end servicing lifecycles by eliminating manual touchpoints across transaction processing, validation, exception management, and reconciliation. Perform data analysis using SQL and data profiling techniques to uncover root causes, validate business logic, and evaluate automation performance. Drive backlog refinement sessions, sprint planning participation, and sprint demonstrations alongside cross-functional engineering teams. Deploy predictive processing models that analyze historical client behavior and response patterns to drive straight-through transaction execution. Establish a tiered risk-based automation framework that aligns controls and oversight to transaction risk profiles with enhanced governance for high-risk activities. Implement generative artificial intelligence solutions to automate control execution, real-time monitoring, compliance validation, and proactive exception identification.

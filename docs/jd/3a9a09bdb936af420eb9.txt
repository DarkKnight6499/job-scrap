The Opportunity As a dedicated Investment Analyst (Mid-Level), you will provide analytical and operational support across the investment management process. You will conduct portfolio research and statistical analysis, including asset allocation modeling and benchmarking, to support enterprise investment portfolios and forecasting, planning, and reporting activities. You will also support Second and Third Line of Defense requests, including audits, Risk Control Self-Assessments (RCSAs), risk and control inventories, control testing, and key risk and performance indicator reporting. This role ensures investment analysis and reporting are accurate, timely, and aligned with stakeholder needs. We offer a flexible work environment that requires an individual to be in the office 4 days per week. This position can be based in one of the following locations: San Antonio, TX, or Charlotte, NC. Relocation assistance is not available for this position. What you'll do: Applies proficient knowledge to conduct research and statistical analysis of USAA investment portfolios. Completes ad hoc and recurring analysis, research market trends and compiles data. Discusses concerns and makes recommendations on possible investment modifications. Prepares reports and presentations for investment portfolios that include portfolio performance, risk, and attribution. Prepares other ad hoc reporting as required by internal or external stakeholders and reconcile results consistent with fund/or strategy objectives. Participates in compliance initiatives to update new requirements on USAA technical systems and manage compliance templates to align with policy and regulatory constraints. Develops an understanding of the fixed income, alternatives and equity asset classes and shares knowledge with operations teams. Stays informed of industry initiatives and trends that may impact portfolio analytics. Applies proficient knowledge to assist team members with the onboarding of new asset managers, to include coordinating efforts with stakeholders. Collects data and provides support to team members in the preparation of investment presentations. Ensures risks associated with business activities are effectively identified, measured, monitored, and controlled in accordance with risk and compliance policies and procedures. What you have: Bachelor's degree; OR 4 years of relevant education and/or experience. 4 years of experience in financial analysis within the investment industry or related financial services industry. Knowledge of fixed income, equity and/or alternative asset classes. Knowledge of applicable regulatory and compliance laws and regulations. Proficient knowledge of financial asset classes and investment management or investment operations processes. Proficient knowledge and use of Microsoft Products. What sets you apart: US military experience gained through military service or gained as a military spouse / domestic partner. Familiarity with investment platforms (e.g., Aladdin, PAM, Bloomberg) Hands-on experience partnering with custodians, vendors, and external service providers, including issue resolution and performance oversight. Proficiency in data analysis, leveraging relational databases, to streamline processes and drive operational efficiency.

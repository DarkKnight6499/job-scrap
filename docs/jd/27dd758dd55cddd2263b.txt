COMPANY OVERVIEW KKR is a leading global investment firm that offers alternative asset management as well as capital markets and insurance solutions. KKR aims to generate attractive investment returns by following a patient and disciplined investment approach, employing world-class people, and supporting growth in its portfolio companies and communities. KKR sponsors investment funds that invest in private equity, credit and real assets and has strategic partners that manage hedge funds. KKR’s insurance subsidiaries offer retirement, life and reinsurance products under the management of Global Atlantic Financial Group. References to KKR’s investments may include the activities of its sponsored funds and insurance subsidiaries. POSITION OVERVIEW The Reinsurance Management position will act as the key contact for internal and external queries related to reinsurance on both Life and Annuity products. Additionally, this role will be the primary point of contact for reinsurers and reinsurance administrator TAI. This position will coordinate and interact internally including product development, corporate actuarial, legal and IT. External interactions include our third party reinsurance partners, external reinsurers and key stakeholders. Position is being considered to join our Des Moines office. RESPONSIBILITIES: Analyze financial impact of changes in reinsurance coverage and retention Oversight of work effort performed by the reinsurance TPA and internal modeling team Organize and present to internal committees and senior management ongoing program performance and potential strategic management decisions Strong people skills, including external relationship management Provide product and system expertise related to reinsurance for all areas of the company and our reinsurance third party administrator (TPA) Respond to escalated issues relating to reinsurance pricing, implementation or queries QUALIFICATIONS: Exposure to reinsurance and/or actuarial background required.

Role Overview Citi Ventures is the strategic investing and innovation arm of Citi, focused on investing in emerging technologies and supporting innovation across the firm through three core verticals: Strategic Investing: Sources and executes minority investments in innovative, early-stage companies to support and accelerate innovation across Citi. Partnerships & Venture Development: Helps commercialize Citi products and intellectual property through partnerships, and other strategic initiatives. Business Development: Drives cross-enterprise engagement across Citi’s franchise, connecting capabilities, partnerships, and opportunities across Wealth, CCB, and other businesses. The portfolio coverage team supports these core Citi Ventures verticals by providing operational, analytical, and program management support. The team helps drive consistency across Venture Investing activities, enhance operational effectiveness, and support key strategic initiatives and process improvements. This role offers a unique opportunity to gain exposure to venture investing, portfolio management, commercialization, and business management activities while working closely with a broad range of stakeholders across Citi Ventures and partner functions including Finance, Controllers, Legal, Compliance, Risk, and Operations. The successful candidate will support efforts to improve coordination, transparency, and consistency across Citi Ventures. Position Scope & Responsibilities Portfolio Management Support monitoring of the venture portfolio for existing companies within our enterprise and fintech coverage. Assist with Quarterly Portfolio Review (QPR) and LP reporting processes, including gathering company updates, reviewing financial performance, and preparing supporting materials. Assist in maintaining portfolio company profiles, capitalization tables, valuation analyses, and investment tracking materials. Contribute to portfolio company onboarding activities and ongoing portfolio management processes. Help develop and maintain portfolio dashboards, reporting tools, and CRM data. Support commercialization initiatives and assist with preparing materials for portfolio companies and business partners. Build and maintain effective working relationships with internal stakeholders and innovation-focused teams across Citi. Investor Operations Assist in preparing senior management updates, business reviews, committee materials, and leadership presentations. Provide analytical support to the Venture Investing team, including pipeline tracking, portfolio monitoring, and performance reporting. Support execution of business initiatives, process enhancements, and strategic projects across the team. Help develop dashboards and reporting tools used to track team objectives, portfolio metrics, and operational performance. Assist with coordinating deliverables, project milestones, and key governance activities. Support strategic planning exercises and team initiatives aligned with Citi Ventures' priorities. Provide ad hoc analytical and operational support for management requests and business needs. Assist with operational processes related to new venture investments, including documentation and funding activities. Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Business, Mathematics, Statistics, Engineering, or a related quantitative field. Interest in venture capital, startup ecosystems, innovation, and financial services. 2-5 years of relevant experience in financial services, investment banking, consulting, corporate strategy, venture capital, private equity, business analytics, or related fields. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and critical-thinking skills. Excellent attention to detail and commitment to producing high-quality work. Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to develop clear and effective presentations. Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint. Experience working with data, financial analysis, reporting, and presentation development. Ability to manage multiple workstreams and priorities in a fast-paced, team-oriented environment. Strong interpersonal skills and ability to collaborate effectively with colleagues across functions and levels. Self-motivated, proactive, and eager to learn. Comfortable working independently while seeking guidance when appropriate. Demonstrated ability to support projects from inception through execution. Positive attitude, intellectual curiosity, and willingness to challenge assumptions constructively.

Position Summary: Admilparant (an LPA1 antagonist) is a foundational asset for Bristol Myers Squibb in pulmonology, representing a potential first-in-class treatment option for patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) and progressive pulmonary fibrosis (PPF). The Associate Director, HCP Medical Education & Congress Strategy supports the execution of Admilparant’s national KOL engagement, congress, and medical education programs across IPF and PPF, in collaboration with the Director, while independently leading the brand’s regional insight generation strategy and regional congress calendar. This role designs the regional programs, sessions, and interactions that surface the insights driving brand strategy, builds direct relationships with KOLs and HCPs, supports preparation and execution at Admilparant’s priority national congresses, and administers the speaker bureau that supports Admilparant’s peer-to-peer education. The Associate Director must be able to operate in a fast-paced, launch-stage organization, with high expectations and accountability on quality of execution, and bring a bias for action, strong attention to detail, and a compliance-first mindset to all programs. The ability to build strong working relationships with MSLs and Home Office Medical, Market Access, Commercial colleagues, the Congress Excellence team, and external agencies is essential. This role supports an investigational compound and indications currently under development and not yet approved by the FDA. Key Responsibilities: KOL Engagement Planning & Execution Support planning and execution of commercial KOL programs, including advisory boards, promotional speaker development, and peer-to-peer educational events, managing logistics, content development, and legal/compliance review, in alignment with the Director.

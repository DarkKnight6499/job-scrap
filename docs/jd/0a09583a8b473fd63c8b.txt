What You’ll Do FTI Investment Administration & Reporting (IA&R) provides outsourced accounting, Treasury, and financial reporting services to public and private real estate companies, including investment managers, equity and mortgage REITs, mortgage finance companies, developers, and institutional investors. The Senior Consultant, Treasury will be a hands-on subject matter expert and team lead within the IA&R Treasury function, responsible for the accurate, timely, and well-controlled execution of both Treasury and Accounts Payable (AP) activities, including client payments, invoice processing, cash management, and bank administration. The role works directly with clients, vendors, and Fund Administration and Property Accounting personnel, manages and develops a team in the US and/or offshore, and drives technology-enabled process improvements that create efficiencies and standardize operations. Success requires strong organizational management, the ability to multi-task across multiple clients and payment cutoffs, and consistent delivery against tight deadlines. The ideal candidate is an assertive self-starter, collaborative, detail-oriented, and a proactive problem solver.

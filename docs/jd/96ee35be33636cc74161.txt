About This Job The Senior Analyst, Commercial Credit and Risk (MCC Secretary) leads the day-to-day administration of the Credit and Lending governance framework by serving as the Secretary for the MCC and Supporting the DCC and HCDCC, and related credit governance forums. This position is responsible for administering committee governance processes, supporting policy development and maintenance, coordinating executive reporting, performing portfolio analytics, tracking governance actions, and partnering with stakeholders across the Commercial and Consumer Banks, Risk, Compliance, Legal, Audit, and Credit Administration. The role serves as the primary governance lead for credit governance and is expected to operate independently with minimal supervision, exercise judgment regarding governance readiness and documentation quality, and escalate material issues to management.

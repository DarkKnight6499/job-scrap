To learn more, visit www.blueowl.com Blue Owl is seeking a highly motivated Senior Associate to join its SEC & Financial Reporting Team. Based in Short Hills, NJ, this role will report to the Head of SEC & Financial Reporting and play a key role in the firm's external reporting, SEC compliance, financial analytics, and reporting process improvement initiatives. The ideal candidate is detail-oriented, analytical, and thrives in a fast-paced, collaborative environment. Key Responsibilities Assist in the preparation and review of external financial reporting, including quarterly and annual filings such as Forms 10-Q, 10-K, 8-K, proxy statements, and other SEC filings, ensuring accuracy, quality, and timely submission. Support the preparation of quarterly earnings materials and management reporting packages, including Audit Committee and Board presentations. Prepare and analyze firmwide AUM reporting, including key business drivers such as fundraising, deployment activity, and performance metrics, while coordinating with stakeholders across the firm to validate data and ensure consistency. Assist with disclosure checklists, disclosure committee materials, and statutory financial statements under both U.S. GAAP and applicable international reporting standards. Partner with cross-functional teams to support an efficient financial close and reporting process. Build strong working relationships across Finance, Fund Accounting, Legal, Compliance, Corporate Development, Investor Relations, Marketing, Investor Operations, and other business functions. Provide guidance, training, and review support to offshore team members to promote professional development and ensure high-quality deliverables. Collaborate with Finance and Technology teams on enhancements to reporting systems, data pipelines, automation initiatives, and reporting tools. Identify opportunities to strengthen reporting processes, enhance internal controls, improve efficiencies, and document procedures. Support compliance with Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) requirements, including maintaining policies, procedures, and related documentation. Monitor and evaluate emerging accounting standards, SEC regulations, and reporting requirements. Assist with quarterly reviews and annual audits, including responding to auditor requests. Maintain and enhance process documentation and reporting procedures. Participate in cross-functional projects and strategic initiatives as needed. Qualifications Bachelor's or Master's degree in Accounting, Finance, Business Analytics, Data Science, or a related field. 5+ years of relevant professional experience. CPA designation preferred. Public accounting experience (Big Four preferred) and/or experience within financial services, alternative asset management, or a public company environment preferred. Strong understanding of U.S. GAAP, SEC reporting requirements, and public company reporting practices. Advanced Microsoft Excel skills; experience with programming languages, databases, or data analytics tools is a plus. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and critical-thinking skills. Demonstrated ability to manage multiple priorities and deliver results in a dynamic, fast-paced environment. Exceptional attention to detail and organizational skills. Strong work ethic, sense of ownership, and willingness to roll up sleeves to get things done. Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to effectively collaborate across teams and levels of the organization. Ability to work both independently and collaboratively, with a strong intellectual curiosity and commitment to continuous learning. Preferred Qualifications Experience with XBRL reporting. Experience using Workiva. Experience with Oracle or other general ledger systems, including journal entry processing. Advanced Microsoft PowerPoint skills. Experience leveraging modern AI tools, including prompt-based assistants and workflow automation technologies, to improve efficiency and enhance output quality.

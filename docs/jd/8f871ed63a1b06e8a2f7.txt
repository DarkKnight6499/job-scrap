Purpose We are seeking an experienced and technically accomplished Manager to join our Margin & Collateral Management Operations team supporting the U.S. Prime Services business. This role requires a strong operational risk and control mindset, with a focus on supporting daily margin and collateral processes, following established procedures, and escalating issues appropriately. The incumbent will assist with margin call preparation, exposure monitoring, settlements, reconciliations, and exception management while developing knowledge of margin regulations. The role will work closely with Operations, Risk, Compliance, Technology, and business partners to help ensure timely, accurate, and well-controlled support for client financing activity. What You'll Do Prime Brokerage Margin • Monitor, calculate, and issue daily margin calls across the Prime Brokerage business, ensuring all margin requirements are met accurately and on time each business day. • Ensure client margin accounts comply with applicable regulatory requirements and internal risk and credit guidelines. • Support the broader Prime Brokerage business through client onboarding, margin documentation reviews, and the ongoing management of client-specific margin terms and financing arrangements. • Identify and promptly escalate margin deficiencies, account breaches, and atypical trading practices, including wire transfers and day-trading patterns, to senior management. • Investigate and resolve breaks, discrepancies, and exceptions in a timely manner. • Prepare and update daily reports, dashboards, and operational metrics. • Monitor workflow queues to ensure deadlines and service-level expectations are met. • Support testing, system enhancements, and process documentation updates. • Collaborate with internal teams and counterparties to resolve operational issues and enhance processes. • Ensure compliance with internal controls, policies, and regulatory requirements. What You'll Bring • Three to five years of Prime Brokerage operations experience. • Hands-on experience with collateral workflows, margining, and exposure reconciliation, including bilateral and tri-party processes. • Understanding of Prime Brokerage operations, including margining, reconciliation, and settlement practices. • Good understanding of FINRA Rule 4210, portfolio margin, and Regulation T requirements. • Strong attention to detail, analytical thinking, and problem-solving skills. • Effective communication skills and the ability to work collaboratively in a fast-paced environment, while managing multiple priorities. • Good understanding of Prime Services, margin, collateral, settlements, or the trade lifecycle. • Strong analytical skills, with the ability to review data, identify issues, and support timely resolution. • High attention to detail and accuracy when working with reports, reconciliations, and operational information. • Strong communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with internal teams and escalate issues when required. Required Experience • Two to five or more years of experience in Prime Brokerage, Margin and Collateral Operations within a global financial institution, broker-dealer, or bank. • Working knowledge of U.S. broker-dealer and bank margin regulations, including Regulation T, Regulation U, FINRA Rule 4210, SEC Rule 15c3-3, and SEC Rule 15c3-1, with the ability to calculate, interpret, and apply applicable margin requirements. • Working knowledge of OTC, Repo, and Prime Brokerage products, Asset Transfers, Portfolio Margin Regulatory Reporting, and Day Trading Practices. • Familiarity with cross-product margining across equities, fixed income, derivatives, and securities financing within a unified operational framework is an asset. • Familiarity with Prime Brokerage documentation and legal, regulatory, and trading agreements. • Experience with Global One, Loan One, EquiLend, Colline, AcadiaSoft, Anvil, and Broadridge BPS is an asset. • FINRA Series 99, Operations Professional registration, is required. • Undergraduate degree in Finance or Business.

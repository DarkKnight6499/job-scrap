Job Description: Position Summary Guggenheim Securities (“GS”) is seeking experienced Vice Presidents and Associates to join its Consumer, Retail & Restaurant Investment Banking Group in New York. The Consumer, Retail, and Restaurant Group at Guggenheim Securities is one of the leading practices on Wall Street with significant opportunities to continue growing the group. Our team has a proven track record advising clients on M&A, equity and debt capital raising and liability management. The team focuses on several sub-sectors including fitness / healthy living, restaurants, direct to consumer, apparel / specialty retail, large box retail, specialty retail, food retail / food distribution and several other categories. Guggenheim has one of the most active M&A advisory practices in the sector as well as a top debt financing practice for franchisors and multi-unit companies. Our Associates and Vice Presidents play an integral role in evolving and enhancing our client relationships and are active participants throughout the life of a transaction and beyond. Our Associates and Vice Presidents benefit tremendously from the experience and partnership they have with our Senior Managing Directors and Managing Directors. GS offers an unparalleled opportunity to work as part of small client teams on major strategic and financing assignments, providing meaningful exposure to senior bankers and client representatives. Candidates should be top performers who are willing to work hard in a dynamic environment where initiative, creativity, maturity, and enthusiasm for learning are highly valued.

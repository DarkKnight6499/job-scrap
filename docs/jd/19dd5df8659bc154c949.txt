Job description What is the Position? Evercore seeks to hire an experienced Associate for its Healthcare Advisory Team located in Menlo Park, CA. Primary Responsibilities: Evercore’s differentiated approach to advisory provides Associates with the ability to work closely with senior bankers on industry-transforming, multi-billion dollar transactions around the world. The small transaction team structure creates a strong platform for Associates to learn investment banking and assume high levels of responsibility at early stages of their careers. As part of the team, the Associate will have the opportunity to work on a variety of strategic advisory assignments including: sellside and buyside M&A engagements; activism and raid defense; divestitures and spin-off engagements; equity and equity-linked capital-raising; special committee engagements; debt and restructuring advisory and, more broadly, evaluation of strategic alternatives. Specific Qualifications: Candidates must have a strong interest in investment banking with solid quantitative and analytical skills, as well the ability to think broadly about a range of strategic issues confronting the firm’s clients. Knowledge of accounting, valuation, finance, and business strategy is a must. Responsibilities for an Associate include managing analysts, overseeing financial and valuation analysis, resolving complex financial and accounting issues, industry and competitive analysis, due diligence, drafting client presentation materials, management and execution of live deal situations, and assistance with new business development. Evercore offers highly qualified Associates the chance to advance more rapidly and take on more responsibility than in traditional bulge-bracket firms, exposure to blue-chip clients, and the chance to be part of rapidly growing, highly regarded firm and team. Graduated from a top MBA school or Analyst to Associate promote; class of 2023 strongly preferred 2+ years of relevant investment banking experience Superior analytical skills and demonstrated excellence in academic coursework Strong interpersonal skills and ability to articulate ideas Strong communications skills, both written and oral Strong work ethic Enthusiastic, outgoing and achievement-oriented character Background in Life Sciences or strong academic foundation in the sciences (biology, chemistry, or other adjacent major to Life Sciences) preferred but not necessary Expected Base Salary Range: $185,000 - $250,000 In addition to a competitive base salary, employees may be eligible to receive a discretionary bonus delivered in the form of cash and/or deferred equity.

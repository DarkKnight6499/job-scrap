What You'll Be Doing The Commercial Real Estate Credit Analyst I supports the underwriting, monitoring, and administration of commercial real estate lending relationships. This role analyzes borrower, guarantor, and property financial information; assists with the preparation of credit analyses and approval memoranda; and supports ongoing portfolio management activities. Working under the guidance of senior credit professionals, the position develops foundational knowledge of commercial real estate lending, credit risk assessment, financial analysis, and bank credit policies while contributing to sound credit decisions and portfolio quality. What We're Looking For Underwrite opportunities, analyze financial statements, and develop cash flow models to be utilized in support of commercial loans and commercial real estate loans. Prepares ongoing credit evaluations, quarterly reporting, and financial statement analysis, risk rating, regulatory reviews, and annual reviews. Analyze and monitor of a commercial loan portfolio. Ensure loan documentation, financial statement requirements, and other compliance requirements are up-to-date. Develop a basic knowledge of banking, the commercial real estate industry, corporate and commercial real estate borrowers, and collateral types. Assist in preparation of required internal reporting. What You'll Bring Understand and comply with all regulations. Knowledge of economic and accounting principles and practices, financial markets, banking, and the analysis and reporting of financial data. Effectively communicate (written and oral) Ability to work collaboratively and cross functionally. Education & Experience Minimum Required: Bachelor's Degree in finance, accounting, another business related field or equivalent experience Minimum Required: 1+ years of banking or related experience Licenses & Credentials Minimum Required: None #LI-JK1 Systems & Technology Proficient in Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, Outlook, and advance knowledge of Microsoft Excel.

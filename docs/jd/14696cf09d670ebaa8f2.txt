The Great American Risk Solutions IT team is seeking a Senior Technical Business Analyst. This individual will combine advanced business analysis with hands-on technical expertise to translate complex business needs into reliable, scalable IT and data solutions. They will partner with business stakeholders and technical teams throughout the requirements and software development life cycles, with responsibilities spanning technical specifications, application configuration, SQL and data analysis, automation, testing, deployment, and production support. Essential Job Functions and Responsibilities Analyzes complex business issues, gathers and defines business and technical requirements, and translates them into detailed specifications for IT and data solutions aligned with organizational goals. Collaborates with business stakeholders, program and project managers, and cross-functional technical teams to model system requirements, evaluate capabilities, and develop workflow charts, diagrams, and solution specifications. Leads configuration and maintenance of enterprise applications, tunes systems for efficiency, and implements enhancements to improve performance, reliability, functionality, and scalability. Responds to complex application service requests, troubleshoots system and data issues, and documents findings and resolutions clearly for business and technical audiences. Develops technical specifications for system enhancements, contributes to coding and advanced bug fixes, and develops and implements automated system protocols and scripts to reduce manual processes. Leads testing, debugging, and deployment of application updates, verifying alignment with business and technical requirements while ensuring quality and minimal disruption to business operations. Uses SQL and analytical tools to gather and analyze data, investigate issues, and support solution decisions. May develop and maintain complex reports and dashboards to support business needs and decision-making. Manages application-level security, including user access and compliance with data protection standards. May have responsibility for performance and coaching of staff and may have a participatory role in decisions regarding talent selection, development, and performance management for direct reports. Performs other duties as assigned. Job Requirements Education: Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems, Information Technology, Computer Science, Business, or a related field. Experience: Generally, a minimum of 10 years of related experience spanning business and systems analysis and hands-on application support, configuration, systems administration, or application development. Experience includes defining requirements and designing, coding, testing, debugging, documenting, maintaining, or configuring enterprise applications in a business setting. Completion of or continuing progress toward a professional designation preferred, such as Azure Administrator Associate or equivalent cloud certification, ITIL Foundation Certification, Certified Business Intelligence Professional (CBIP), or Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP). Scope of Job/Qualifications : Advanced knowledge of business and systems analysis, ITIL practices, and the software development lifecycle (SDLC). Advanced proficiency in enterprise platforms, SQL, reporting tools (e.g., Power BI, Tableau), and scripting languages, with the ability to translate business needs into actionable technical requirements and practical system solutions. Provides requirements guidance and participates in all phases of the requirements life cycle for complex programs and projects. Recommends best practices and introduces effective analysis techniques, tools, and approaches. Requires a high level of understanding of the organization’s business and technology operations, advanced analytical skills, and excellent communication, stakeholder engagement, and business relationship skills. Communicates complex topics clearly, aligns solutions with business needs, and anticipates downstream impacts. Viewed as a senior resource on the team; provides technical advice and support to lower-level positions. May serve as a Scrum Master, facilitating and coaching an agile team and helping others understand how to interact with the team, or as an agile development team member creating and testing the product increment.

The Chase Home Lending Product Strategy team creates a Home Lending product ecosystem that consistently converts market trends, customer needs and innovative solutions into an intentionally focused portfolio of work for our product teams that achieves the highest value priorities and transforms the business through speed, scale, and automation. As the Senior Product Strategy Associate in Chase Home Lending, you will have the opportunity to bring your mortgage industry knowledge and curiosity to a team focused on shaping the future of home lending products and programs. You will work across multiple areas of the business in Consumer and Correspondent Originations and Servicing to focus on building strategies covering the full breadth of Home Lending products as well as other strategic initiatives. You will partner with experienced product managers and business leaders to evaluate opportunities, solve complex problems, and support the development of customer and employee focused solutions. If you enjoy learning new subject areas, building relationships, and turning analysis into action, this role offers broad exposure and meaningful impact. Job responsibilities Support the evaluation of mortgage product, policy, and process enhancement opportunities through research, analysis, and stakeholder engagement. Support the assessment of investor, agency, regulatory, and market changes to evaluate impacts to existing products, programs and strategic priorities. Assess competitive, industry and market information to identify emerging trends and opportunities to inform future product strategy recommendations. Analyze business performance, customer experience, operational processes, and strategic priorities to develop recommendations. Collaborate across the Product Strategy team and with and functional partners to develop business cases, product strategy recommendations, governance materials and supporting materials for leadership review and decision-making. Coordinate discovery activities and gather input from Operations, Risk, Legal, Compliance, Finance, Capital Markets, Technology, and other key stakeholders. Contribute subject matter expertise and business support throughout the implementation lifecycle of approved initiatives. Working knowledge of mortgage products, processes, and the home lending lifecycle; Experience conducting research, analysis, and problem solving to support business decisions. Ability to translate complex information from diverse sources into clear, actionable business insights by preparing presentations, reports, communications, and recommendations for business stakeholders.

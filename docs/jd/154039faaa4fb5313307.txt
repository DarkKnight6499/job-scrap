First Citizens Bank
Location: Boston, Massachusetts; New York, New York

Overview This is a hybrid role with the expectation that time working will regularly take place inside and outside of a company office. The Special Assets Group plays a critical role in protecting the bank's portfolio and supporting clients through periods of financial stress, operational challenges, and strategic transition. The team manages higher-risk and adversely graded credit relationships across the Technology, Life Science and Healthcare Innovation Bank portfolio and works closely with company management teams, boards, venture capital investors, private equity sponsors, and internal business partners. The team seeks to maximize recovery, minimize losses, and preserve franchise value while maintaining constructive relationships with borrowers, sponsors, management teams, and investors. Through thoughtful problem solving and a deep understanding of the innovation ecosystem, the Special Assets Group helps navigate complex situations and deliver balanced outcomes for both clients and the bank. This role supports the portfolio management of a variety of problem loans. Primary responsibilities will include underwriting / executing debt restructuring and portfolio management. The ideal candidate will have the ability to work in a dynamic / fast paced environment, managing a variety of deal stages and types, with a strong attention to detail. Responsibilities & Qualifications Duties & Responsibilities: Underwriting: Process Management - Assists in the review of the assigned portfolio for credit quality and credit risk rating accuracy and notes any policy exceptions. Requires underwriting skills on a variety of technology/life sciences verticals as well as credit products. Prepares quarterly credit review packages as well as credit committee minutes. Reporting/Portfolio Management - Maintains credit files, monitors past dues, prepares underwriting packages, interfaces and collaborates with operations, and contacts clients to obtain financial information. Assists in the preparation of quarterly Individual Assessments. Knowledge of Credit Policy, Risk Rating Matrices, and Bank systems - Must understand and have the ability to navigate a number of Bank systems. Knowledge of Lending Products and Restructuring - May manage a small number of less complex accounts and/or charge-offs, including recommending and implementing strategies to mitigate loss and interacting with clients directly. Requires basic negotiation skills, basic understanding of workout strategies, and strong credit acumen.

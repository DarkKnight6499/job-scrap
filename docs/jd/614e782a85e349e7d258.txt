About the Role We’re hiring a VP, Commissioning & Validation to own commissioning as a global discipline across our data center campuses in the Americas, EMEA and APAC. You’ll set the standard that decides when our critical infrastructure is ready for service, and you’ll put your name to it. You’ll build and lead a small central team that writes, owns and validates the commissioning scripts every campus runs. The team authors and validates the standard but doesn’t do the field work. Execution is spread across four groups: engineering provides the design basis, construction and vendor crews carry out start-up, local site commissioning teams run the acceptance gates, and operations accepts the facility. You’ll keep all four working to one standard, and you’ll work closely with our controls engineering team so equipment and its automation are always tested together as one system. The core of the role is proving equipment where it’s built, not finding problems in the field. You’ll run the same validated script at the vendor’s factory and again on site, with the same acceptance criteria, and keep one traceable record for each serial number from factory test to final site acceptance. The commissioning certificates your office issues carry contractual Service Commencement consequences, so this role has real authority over readiness, acceptance and schedule. What you’ll be doing Own the global commissioning standard Publish and version-control one global commissioning script library (L1–L5 and integrated systems testing), with controlled UL/NEC and IEC regional variants so no region forks the standard Define Factory Acceptance Test (FAT) protocols, pass/fail criteria and witness requirements for every critical equipment class Co-own controls testing with the controls engineering team, building sequence-of-operation, BMS/EPMS point-to-point and alarm tests into every master script Feed every field finding back into the library within one release cycle, and own the deferred and seasonal test registers Build and run the FAT program Run a global FAT calendar with every major OEM, with script releases timed to procurement milestones and witness assignments for each test Require witnessed FAT for the most critical equipment (UPS, MV switchgear, chillers, CDU skids, electrical power modules and cooling-plant modules), and apply sample-based witnessing with full data submission to the rest Hold green-tag authority, so no critical equipment ships without passing its approved script Work with Supply Chain to write FAT requirements, witness rights, script authority and data deliverables into every purchase order and owner-furnished equipment contract Reconcile OEM start-up procedures to Nscale scripts before mobilization Hold commissioning and acceptance authority Issue the commissioning certificate required before any phase, building or leased space is accepted, including third-party and colocation capacity, where your standards become the due-diligence checklist Decide script content, pass/fail criteria, witness requirements and certificate issuance.

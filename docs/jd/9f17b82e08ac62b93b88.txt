Job Description Purpose of the role: The role involves performing risk analysis of proposed asset securitization facilities requiring Barclays commitment for internal books or for underwriting for distribution in the financial markets. Review proposals, liaise with the deal teams, take part in client engagement due diligence reviews, identify and analyse key risks in the proposals, recommend compensating controls, review transaction structures and related documentation, review models and model assumptions to ensure that the key risks are captured and the repayment profile is consistent with the assigned risk ratings, and recommend approval or rejection, as appropriate, including escalation if required. Lead the credit analysis of related exposures to a client in cases where the primary exposure is the ABS facilities. These include direct corporate exposures such as revolving credit facilities or term loans, credit derivatives, or equity-backed loan commitments. Accountabilities: Ownership of the quality, accuracy, robustness, and timeliness of the credit risk analysis paper, including getting required approvals Credit sanctioning and approval of US ABS warehouse facilities and securitised products across consumer and non-consumer asset classes including auto loans, personal loans, student loans, trade receivables, equipment leases, and other esoteric assets. Performing transaction due diligence, including management meetings, sponsor/platform assessments, third-party audit reviews (AUPs), and collateral verification processes. Ownership of risk ratings, counterparty tiering, escalation decisions, and sanctioning authority recommendations under the SLF escalation framework. Ongoing surveillance of the US ABS portfolio, including utilisation, concentration risk, collateral performance, trigger breaches, and covenant compliance. Portfolio stress testing, risk appetite monitoring and reporting to governance forums including WCRMC, IBRC and US regulatory reviews. Production and challenge of portfolio reviews covering credit quality, diversification, utilisation, ratings accuracy, and emerging risk themes, and contributing to portfolio reviews and internal risk discussions across teams. Regulatory engagement with bodies such as the FRBNY, PRA and internal audit, including remediation of findings and enhancement of control frameworks. Responsibility for the timely assignment of risk limits within internal systems, and monitoring of exposure Collaborating with both team members across teams on various risk projects to share knowledge or train junior analysts. Role Requirements: The candidate must have expertise in credit risk analysis of asset backed transactions in a senior decision-making capacity, either with a rating agency, other large banks, frequent ABS issuers or large institutional ABS investors. Competence in analysis of transaction structures, cash flow modelling, and experience reading transaction and legal documents. Demonstrated leadership in risk management at a large institution Risk decision making background within a large organization, such as a rating agency, other large banks or an institutional ABS issuer or investor.

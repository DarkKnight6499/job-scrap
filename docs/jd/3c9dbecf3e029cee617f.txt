Position Summary We seek to hire an Associate focused on real estate credit. The Associate will be responsible for helping to analyze and execute on a wide variety of investment opportunities across the credit capital stack. The Associate will be fully involved in all aspects of the investment process, particularly the financial modeling, market research, responding to investor inquiries, monitoring existing investments and preparation of investment committee memorandums. Responsibilities The 2-Year Associate will be a key member of the Real Estate Credit team and will work closely with the senior investment professionals.

Job Requisition ID: 95071 Location Designation: Hybrid - 3 days per week Business Overview: New York Life's Cross FX Enablement team within the Field Experience Value Stream is transforming User Acceptance Testing into a strategic Experience Assurance capability that validates end-to-end advisor, agent, and client experiences across products, systems, and business processes. Role Overview: The UAT Senior Associate plays a critical role in ensuring releases are experience-ready, not just test-complete, by focusing on business readiness, cross-product workflow validation, and release confidence. Working closely with Product Managers, Product Owners, Business Analysts, Quality Assurance, Engineering, Operations, and field stakeholders, this role helps define risk-based validation strategies, identify critical user journeys, assess release readiness, and provide fact-based recommendations throughout the delivery lifecycle. UAT participates early through a shift-left engagement model, influencing requirements quality, acceptance criteria, testability, and validation strategy before development is complete. This role focuses on validating real-world business outcomes, advisor experiences, and cross-product workflows while leveraging automation, production insights, field feedback, and continuous improvement practices to reduce production risk and release confidence across the Field Experience Value Stream What You’ll Do: 1) Experience Assurance Strategy & Tiered Engagement •Assess initiatives and determine the appropriate UAT engagement model (Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3) based on business risk, cross-product impact, integration complexity, regulatory exposure, and advisor experience impact. •Define risk-based validation strategies focused on end-to-end business workflows and critical advisor journeys. •Align UAT activities with PI planning, release planning, roadmap priorities, and value stream objectives. 2) Shift-Left Quality & Requirements Readiness •Participate early in discovery, refinement, and planning activities to improve requirements quality and readiness. •Review and influence user stories, acceptance criteria, business scenarios, and dependency identification to ensure they are testable and aligned to business outcomes. •Provide consultative guidance to Product Managers, Product Owners, and Business Analysts on business validation and experience assurance strategies. 3) End-to-End Journey Validation •Define and validate critical advisor, agent, and client journeys across multiple products, systems, and business processes. •Identify cross-product dependencies, workflow handoffs, and integration points that may impact business readiness and user experience. •Validate end-to-end business outcomes rather than isolated product features. 4) Test Design, Automation & Coverage Management •Develop journey-based validation scenarios, test strategies, and business readiness assessments. •Map critical user journeys to existing regression and automation coverage to identify gaps and improvement opportunities. •Partner with QA and engineering teams to support scalable automation strategies, AI-assisted testing opportunities, and coverage expansion. 5) Release Readiness & Risk Assessment •Execute end-to-end validation activities for prioritized initiatives based on engagement tier. •Evaluate production readiness, unresolved defects, dependency risks, mitigation plans, and potential business impacts. •Provide release-readiness assessments and go/no-go recommendations to delivery and business leadership. 6) Production Insights & Continuous Improvement •Analyze production incidents, support tickets, advisor feedback, and recurring operational issues to identify validation coverage gaps. •Convert production learnings into future testing scenarios, regression suites, journey validations, and process improvements. •Contribute to UAT governance, standards, templates, metrics, frameworks, and operating model improvements across the value stream. 7) Cross-Functional Leadership & Business Partnership •Act as a consultative partner enabling quality and business readiness across the delivery lifecycle. •Facilitate collaboration across Product, BA, QA, Engineering, Operations, and Business teams to improve release confidence and customer outcomes. •Represent advisor and field-user perspectives in readiness discussions, defect triage, and release decisions.

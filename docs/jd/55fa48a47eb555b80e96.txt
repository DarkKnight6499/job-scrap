At Bloomberg Finance we are committed to providing world-class support to our business partners by providing insight into business performance, financial oversight and thought leadership. You will join the Bloomberg Media Financial Planning & Analysis team, supporting senior leadership through consolidated financial reporting and analysis, forecasting and planning, and strategic decision support. You’ll play a key role in developing a holistic view of Bloomberg Media’s revenue performance and outlook, while partnering across the business on financial and strategic analyses that inform senior management decisions. We’ll trust you to • Develop a strong understanding of Bloomberg Media’s businesses, revenue models, products, strategic priorities, and key financial and operating drivers. • Lead consolidated revenue reporting and analysis, partnering with business Finance teams to understand performance and outlook and identify key drivers, trends, risks and opportunities. • Produce concise, executive-ready reporting, analysis and visualizations that synthesize performance, outlook and key takeaways for senior leadership. • Support periodic forecasts and annual planning by consolidating business inputs, reviewing and challenging assumptions, and developing an independent perspective on the revenue outlook. • Apply critical thinking and rigorous analysis to internal and external data to evaluate business performance, identify trends, and answer complex financial and strategic questions. • Partner closely with Media Strategy on strategic analyses and cross-functional projects, including market and opportunity sizing, business case development, benchmarking, and other analyses supporting senior management decisions. • Develop and maintain high-quality Excel-based models and analyses to evaluate financial performance, key KPIs, business opportunities, and strategic scenarios. • Develop recurring competitive and market analysis, monitoring industry trends and competitor financial performance to identify relevant insights and implications for Bloomberg Media. • Proactively identify opportunities to improve and automate reporting, planning and analytical processes to increase efficiency, consistency and accessibility. You’ll need to have: • Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a related field of study. • Minimum 5 years of experience in FP&A, business analytics, or corporate or strategic finance. • Strong analytical and critical thinking skills, with the ability to structure complex problems, analyze financial and operational data, and draw actionable conclusions. • Ability to synthesize complex information into concise insights and effectively communicate findings to senior stakeholders. • Ability to work independently, manage multiple priorities, and deliver high-quality work within tight deadlines. • Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint, with experience using enterprise financial systems such as SAP, Oracle, or similar platforms. We’d love to see: • Proficiency in SQL, Power BI, Alteryx, or similar data and analytics tools, a plus. • Experience in Media or a business with multiple commercial models, a plus.

Assistant Portfolio Manager, Core and Multi-Sector Strategies
Vanguard
Location: Malvern, PA
Link: https://vanguard.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/vanguard_external/job/Malvern-PA/Assistant-Portfolio-Manager--Core-and-Multi-Sector-strategies_182376-1

Join Vanguard’s active fixed income team in a unique portfolio management development opportunity supporting two lead portfolio managers across a growing active fixed income franchise. This role plays a critical part in portfolio construction, risk management, trading, and implementation across CORE and CORE+ fixed income strategies. The successful candidate will partner closely with sector teams, capital markets, and investment professionals to optimize portfolio outcomes while developing broader portfolio management and investment decision-making responsibilities over time. Internal collaboration, institutional knowledge, and the ability to build strong cross-functional relationships are key to success in this role. This is an in-office role based in Malvern, PA HQ. Responsibilities: Partner with lead portfolio managers to oversee portfolio construction, risk positioning, liquidity management, and cash allocation across Vanguard’s CORE and CORE+ active fixed income strategies. Maintain consistency across mandates by interpreting investment strategy objectives and ensuring alignment with portfolio guidelines and risk parameters. Collaborate with sector teams to manage portfolio positioning within target ranges while optimizing the use of cash, derivatives, and other investment tools. Execute fixed income transactions directly and through trading desks to support portfolio objectives and implementation needs. Generate and evaluate macroeconomic and cross-asset investment ideas that contribute to portfolio performance and risk-adjusted returns. Manage assigned overlay strategies across interest rates, currencies, and credit derivatives, as delegated by lead portfolio managers. Partner with capital markets and sector teams to support ETF create/redeem activity and portfolio implementation for active fixed income ETFs. Monitor portfolio risks and identify opportunities to enhance processes, analytics, models, and portfolio management practices. Develop and deliver investment reviews, portfolio updates, and presentations for internal stakeholders and external clients. Represent the portfolio management team in client meetings and other forums as needed. Contribute to strategic initiatives, special projects, and continuous improvement efforts across the investment team. Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related analytical discipline, or an equivalent combination of training and experience. Eight or more years of experience in active fixed income investing. Strong understanding of credit risk, interest rate risk, and currency risk management. Experience utilizing derivatives markets and instruments within portfolio management strategies. Demonstrated knowledge across multiple fixed income asset classes. Experience supporting portfolio construction, risk management, and trading activities within institutional investment environments. Experience working within the ETF ecosystem is preferred. Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) designation or progress toward the designation is preferred. Strong analytical, communication, and relationship-management skills with the ability to influence across investment teams.

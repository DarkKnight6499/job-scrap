The team is responsible for American Express Third Party security monitoring, outreach, and escalations as part of the Security Overwatch and Third-Party Security (TPS) programs. The team partners and collaborates with Business units, TPS teams, TLM and Third Parties to review security risks and vulnerabilities, drive closure of gaps that are linked to American Express standards, requirements, and best practices, which ultimately supports the reduction of third-party risk and/or exposure to American Express. In this position the Senior Associate-CyberOps & Assurance - Third Party Security will primarily be responsible for day-to-day third-party continuous monitoring, investigations, third-party outreach and escalations of risks and vulnerabilities impacting our Sentinel classified Third Parties, leveraging threat intelligence data, attack surface management and network monitoring solutions. Additional responsibilities include: Rotational Duty - Research open-source Intel feeds for Cyber threats, risks and vulnerabilities that could have potential impact on our Third Parties and document key Intel in a Daily Threat Report Partner & collaborate with the Enterprise Incident Response Program (EIRP) team on any identified third party security incidents or breaches potentially impacting Amex or Amex data. Support project activities that drive continuous process/program improvements and enhancements to reduce third party risk to Amex. Support annual SOP documentation reviews, updates, additions. Understanding of the Amex Third Party environment and experience with the TPSO (Third Party Security Overwatch) program and reporting Experience with Cyber Attack Surface Management solutions, specifically BitSight and Black Kite. Familiarity working with SOAR ticket management solutions such as ServiceNow. Familiarity with Threat Intelligence platforms and handling reports based on the Traffic Light Protocol (TLP). Working knowledge of Third-Party Lifecycle Management and Process Unity Knowledge & understanding of common security controls, detection capabilities, best practices, and solutions for securing digital environments. Knowledge & understanding of cyber security threats, risks, and vulnerabilities. Understanding of what information or assets are of value to threat actors and how organizations are breached. Must have strong verbal and written communication skills, interpersonal collaborative skills, and the ability to communicate security and risk-related concepts to technical and non-technical audiences. Proven problem solver with ability to provide in-depth analysis of complex problems, manage risk and make quick decisions.

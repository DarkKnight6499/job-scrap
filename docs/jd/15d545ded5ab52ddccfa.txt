Position Overview Star Mountain Capital is seeking a highly analytical, detail-oriented Portfolio Reporting Analyst to support the firm's portfolio monitoring, financial reporting , and investment data management activities. This individual will play an important role in ensuring the accuracy, consistency, and timeliness of portfolio -level reporting across the firm's investment strategies. The ideal candidate will possess strong financial and analytical skills, advanced Excel capabilities, and the ability to work with large datasets while maintaining a high degree of accuracy. This position requires collaboration across Portfolio Management, Investments, Finance, Operations, and Investor Relations to support internal decision-making, investor reporting , and ongoing portfolio oversight. Key Responsibilities Portfolio Reporting & Analysis Prepare and maintain recurring monthly, quarterly, and annual portfolio reporting packages. Track portfolio performance, investment valuations, and relevant portfolio -level KPIs. Support the preparation of portfolio dashboards, performance summaries, and management presentations. Identify reporting discrepancies, unusual trends, and potential data integrity issues, escalating findings as appropriate. Assist with ad hoc reporting and analytical requests from senior management and investment professionals. Data Management & Reporting Systems Ensure consistency of portfolio information across reporting systems and deliverables. Support ongoing improvements to data collection, reporting processes, and system integrations. Identify opportunities to automate recurring reports and improve reporting efficiency. Cross-Functional Collaboration Support Investor Relations with accurate portfolio -level information for investor presentations, reporting , and due diligence requests. Assist in preparing materials for internal portfolio reviews, investment committee meetings, and senior leadership discussions. Respond to internal reporting requests while maintaining data accuracy and confidentiality. Process Improvement & Reporting Efficiency Identify opportunities to streamline reporting workflows and reduce manual processes. Develop and enhance reporting templates, dashboards, and analytical tools. Assist in implementing standardized reporting procedures and data quality controls. Leverage technology, automation, and analytical tools to improve the scalability and effectiveness of portfolio reporting . Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business Analytics, or a related field. 1–3 years of relevant experience in financial reporting , portfolio analytics, investment operations, accounting, or financial analysis. Advanced Microsoft Excel skills, including pivot tables, complex formulas, lookups, and financial data analysis. Experience leveraging AI tools, including Claude or similar platforms, to automate workflows, streamline financial reporting, enhance data analysis, and improve operational efficiency. Strong understanding of financial statements, financial metrics, and accounting fundamentals. Exceptional attention to detail and ability to identify inconsistencies across financial datasets. Strong organizational and time-management skills, with the ability to manage multiple reporting deadlines. Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively across multiple departments. Preferred Qualifications Experience within private credit, private equity, asset management, investment banking, or financial services. Understanding of private investment performance metrics, including IRR, MOIC, NAV, and investment-level returns. Experience with Power BI, Tableau, SQL, or other data visualization and analytical tools. Exposure to debt investments, credit agreements, and portfolio company financial reporting . Experience improving or automating financial reporting processes.

About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Lead Business Execution Consultant to support the availability and effective management of card plastics and customer-facing collateral used in card issuance, reissue, replacement, and fulfillment. You will apply advanced inventory planning, forecasting, and operational expertise to lead complex, cross-functional initiatives that balance customer demand, supplier capacity, production lead times, cost, and risk. You will also provide strategic consultation, strengthen governance and controls, and drive improvements that support reliable card production and customer fulfillment.

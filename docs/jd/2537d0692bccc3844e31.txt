Vice President, US Equity Research
Deutsche Bank
Location: New York, 1 Columbus Circle

Job Description: Job Title: Vice President, US Equity Research Location: New York, NY Overview Deutsche Bank Securities, Inc. seeks a Vice President, US Equity Research in New York, NY to determine and defend investment theses, stock ratings (Buy/Sell/Hold), and price targets for covered companies. Requires a Master's degree in Finance, or a related field or equivalent, and three (3) years of experience (or alternatively, a Bachelor's degree in Finance, or a related field or equivalent, and five (5) years of progressively responsible experience) performing individual company and industry research in the U.S. equities financial sector; performing financial modeling using Excel, Bloomberg, and other data sources to build and maintain Earnings Models, Comparable Company Analysis models, and valuation models including Price-To-Earnings and Sum-of-the-Parts; translating quantitative results into formal initiation of coverage reports, investment recommendation changes, and quarterly earnings notes for institutional clients to inform investment decisions; developing data capturing capabilities using Bloomberg, Morningstar Direct, Dune Analytics, and The Block; maintaining a wide range of databases by gathering, synthesizing, and quality-checking data from company filings, third-party data providers, and blockchain analytics platforms; performing equity securities valuation to assist institutional clients within investment process by developing and defending price targets and investment ratings (Buy/Sell/Hold) on covered securities; performing extensive industry and sector research on both traditional and alternative asset managers, traditional exchanges, new digital asset companies and online brokers; building financial models, including earnings models and valuation frameworks; and applying sound valuation methodology such as Price-To-Earnings and Sum-of-the-Parts valuation to support stock initiation and recommendation processes. Salary range: $200,000.00 – $250,000.00/year. This position is eligible for Deutsche Bank's Employee Referral Incentive Program.

Learn more about Bank OZK benefits . Job Purpose & Scope Supports all Central Fulfillment Lending Groups with all aspects of client servicing. Essential Job Functions Accepts in-bound calls from call-center or customers directly regarding various questions on the servicing of their mortgage. Works with customers to update insurance policies through Escrow Dept Requests. Works to explain late-charges or requests approval to waive fee per policy. Uses the Bank’s origination system to develop and maintain production reporting. Maintains a daily record of transactions in process, closing calendar development, funded closing report. Communicates with members of Central Loan Processing, Title Companies, and Consumer Finance Group staff regarding status of loan missing documentation needed from the Loan Booking Dept. post-closing. Reconciles monthly reports to lenders’ monthly application/funding results. Provides a high level of customer support to ensure the Bank is meeting the customers’ needs and to maximize the customer/Bank relationship. Provides administrative support to Manager. Maintains good punctuality and attendance to work. Follows Bank policy, procedures, and guidelines. Knowledge, Skills & Abilities Ability to communicate effective both verbally and in writing. Ability to demonstrate initiative to accomplish work objectives. Ability to work effectively and demonstrate flexibilityin a continually changing environment. Ability to demonstrate effective organization, critical thinking, analytical and problem-solving skills. Ability to manage multiple tasks with exacting deadlines in a fast-paced environment. Ability to work without close supervision. Ability to maintain attention to detail. Ability to work effectively in a team environment. Ability to work extended hours. Skill in using computer and Microsoft Office, including Outlook, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. Basic Qualifications High school diploma or equivalent required Minimum of one (1) year of work experience with Microsoft Outlook, Word and Excel software Minimum of one (1) year administrative/clerical experience NMLS, or successful completion of NMLS process within 30 days of hire, required.

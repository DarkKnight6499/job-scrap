You will work with global sales leadership, client service, operations, legal and compliance, and central risk teams. We are seeking a Nashville-based Risk Officer to join our Institutional Investment Management Operations and Risk Team (IIM Ops/Risk). This role will play an integral part in mitigating risks within the Client Group and managing risk-related projects to ensure positive outcomes for our clients around the world. To be successful, the risk officer must collaborate with teams across the firm – Operations, Investments, and Compliance, to address new risks facing the industry, and advocate on behalf of our clients. Champion and steer enterprise risk management initiatives on behalf of the Client Group Produce risk and control reporting and track risk items.

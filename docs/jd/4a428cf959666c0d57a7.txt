We are looking for a Product Engineer-Associate to join our Summerville, SC location. Your Responsibilities: The Curtiss-Wright Electro-Mechanical Systems (EMS) division is seeking a Product Engineer to join our Steam & Air Solutions team. You will be directing or performing the design and development of steam turbine products and components of existing and new designs within the guidelines for product design that will meet industry and contractual requirements at the most efficient cost. Your Challenge: Conduct studies and investigations to determine feasibility of designs associated with the application of turbines Define product design requirements in accordance with contract requirements, including computation of performance Analyze test results to evaluate and determine performance, design qualities, and operating deficiencies for the product Develop standard performance curves and calculation procedures to assist marketing in quoting products Coordinate with other areas of engineering to ensure contractual requirements are met. Conduct product improvement programs toward minimizing costs, improving performance and promoting standardization Investigate field problems and provide corrective action in terms of component redesign, revised operating procedures or maintenance practices Develop computer programs required to support and analyze design concepts with respect to performance and mechanical design. Investigate manufacturing deviations and provide corrective action. May supervise and direct subordinates, product design engineers and/or technicians in the design and modification of components for steam turbines Independently perform assignments that require thorough knowledge of thermodynamics, machine design, metallurgy, installation, operation and application of steam turbines Travel to manufacturing suppliers to support, instruct, and manage the machining of critical components This position will require 5% - 15% travel per year dependent upon projects assigned. What you Bring: Due to DoD requirements, candidates must be US citizens Must be able to obtain and maintain DoD Security clearances and approvals BS in Mechanical Engineering, BS Mechanical Engineering Technology, or Aero Engineering discipline and 3-5 years of experience in the steam turbine or related industry required Ability to create UG drawings, 3-D models Experience in managing projects and working in a team environment Knowledge of Oracle manufacturing systems/software Steam turbines manufacturing/design is an asset Who we are: Our Values Environmental, Social and Governance With a long track record of reliable, technologically superior designs and global resources, Steam & Air Solutions (SAS) is the logical choice to meet the Navy’s current and future turbine-drive and shipboard compression requirements. SAS’s equipment is used on hundreds of U.S. Navy ships to provide power and compression for propulsion, power generation, pump drives, ship service air, oxygen / nitrogen (O2N2) feed air, generator drives, and deballasting.

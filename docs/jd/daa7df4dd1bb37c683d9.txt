About the job As US DUPIXENT Associate VIE within our DUPIXENT Rhinology team, you’ll gain hands-on experience in pharmaceutical digital marketing while supporting our mission to improve the lives of patients suffering from Chronic Rhinosinusitis with Nasal Polyps (CRSwNP) and Allergic Fungal Rhinosinusitis (AFRS). In this role, you will be instrumental in executing and optimizing digital marketing strategies to engage healthcare professionals (HCPs) and raise awareness about innovative treatment options in the US market. You will work closely with cross-functional teams to deliver impactful digital campaigns and content that educate and inform the medical community. Join the team transforming care for people with immune challenges, rare diseases, cancers, and neurological conditions. In Specialty Care, you’ll help deliver breakthrough treatments that bring hope to patients with some of the highest unmet needs. Ready to get started? About Sanofi We’re an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people’s lives and delivering compelling growth. Our deep understanding of the immune system – and innovative pipeline – enables us to invent medicines and vaccines that treat and protect millions of people around the world. Together, we chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives. Main responsibilities: Partner with alliance Rhinology HCP marketing and Non-Personal Promotion to develop and execute a digital creative plan: Define and align promo material scope (Website, Emails, Banner Ads, Social Posts, 3rd party content, Interactive Visual Aid). Partner with agencies and internal teams to develop compelling, compliant content. Manage content approval workflows through Alliance Medical, Legal, and Regulatory (JRC). In partnership with HCP marketing, monitor Performance & Optimize: Establish KPIs and tracking mechanisms for all digital initiatives. Support Strategic Initiatives: Stay current with digital marketing trends, competitive landscape, and best practices in pharmaceutical marketing. Assist with budget tracking and vendor management as needed. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure alignment of digital strategies with overall brand objectives.

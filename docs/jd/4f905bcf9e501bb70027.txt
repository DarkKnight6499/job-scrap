Rockland Trust is seeking an experienced CBUD Portfolio Manager to join the Business Banking Portfolio Management team. Using a combination of quantitative tools and traditional credit analysis, the Portfolio Manager employs their commercial credit experience, sound judgement, and pragmatism to administer and manage risk of a large portfolio of C&I and investment real estate relationships with total credit exposures up to $3,000,000. Description: Timely renewal of all loans in assigned portfolio through effective and efficient underwriting, and diligent follow-through Succinctly analyze and underwrite renewals, modifications, restructures, etc., balancing the need to mitigate risk and customer satisfaction Leverage data (e.g. line utilization, deposit trends, credit scores, etc.) to identify and mitigate potential/emerging risk Accurately identify and apply credit policies, judiciously recommending approval of reasonable exceptions where the overall credit profile fits within the bank’s risk tolerance, assign risk rates timely and accurately Collaborate with customers and/or relationship management team to obtain required financial statements and other information Manage higher risk relationships, facilitate transfers to workout, as necessary Negotiate with customers to restructure debt or take other actions, to mitigate higher risk credits Work in partnership with Credit Operations and colleagues to ensure completion of all pre-closing activities Work with Loan Operations, as necessary, to ensure data integrity, resolve issues Assist borrowers with various servicing requests, (release collateral, guarantors, etc.) Review and address key data integrity processes and reports Represent the PM team in various technology, process improvement, or other initiatives to improve the speed, efficiency, and operational integrity of the team Respond to audits, regulatory exams, and other third party reviews.

Current Need We are seeking a Field VP, Portfolio Partnerships to lead the expansion of enterprise level relationships throughout our western region. The ideal candidate will live within the territory. Relocation will not be provided for this position. Position Description Responsible for developing, advancing, and expanding enterprise-level relationships with Private Equity (PE) firms and Management Services Organizations (MSOs) within the Ambulatory Care market. This role focuses on securing platform- or membership-wide alignment that positions McKesson Medical-Surgical as a preferred partner across affiliated portfolio companies (PortCos) and member organizations. The Field VP operates at the enterprise level, leading executive engagement, shaping portfolio-wide commercial strategy, and structuring agreements that enable coordinated field execution. Once enterprise alignment is established, the role partners closely with Account Executives (AEs, field reps) to drive compliance across the broader network. This position requires strong executive presence, enterprise sales experience, and the ability to navigate complex, multi-entity stakeholder environments while aligning platform-level strategy with disciplined field execution. Key Responsibilities Enterprise Platform Development Identify, prioritize, and pursue Private Equity (PE) firms and Management Services Organizations (MSOs) that represent high revenue potential and strategic value to MMS Lead enterprise-level engagement with PE and MSO executives to establish platform- or membership-wide alignment across affiliated portfolio companies (PortCos) and member organizations Develop clear enterprise pursuit plans outlining executive stakeholders, network structure, commercial strategy, and competitive landscape Build and maintain senior executive relationships (C-suite, operating partners, procurement leadership) to create enterprise sponsorship and platform access Platform Governance & Expansion Structure and advance enterprise agreements that enable coordinated growth across PortCos and member organizations Partner with Account Executives (AEs) teams to drive pricing consistency, product alignment, and contract compliance across the network Maintain visibility into platform performance, adoption, and expansion opportunities, and drive coordinated action across distributed entities Serve as the executive point of alignment to ensure continuity between enterprise commitments and field execution Minimum Requirements Bachelor’s Degree or equivalent and typically requires 10 + years of relevant experience.

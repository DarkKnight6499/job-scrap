Job Description The Asset Liability Analyst role will assist with analyzing Origin’s interest rate, capital, and liquidity risk position through modeling, analyzing data, and making recommendations to help ensure risk remains within acceptable limits to the bank. This role will prepare information and reports for ALCO in support of governance process. It will also analyze data and models in order to make recommendations regarding the net interest margin, the bank's key measure of profitability. Duties and Responsibilities include the following. Performs complex analysis and measurements in the areas of interest rate risk, capital risk, liquidity risk, asset liability forecasting and other cash flow risk measurements. Analyze model inputs and data feeds (outputs) for accuracy. Review user reporting, providing appropriate analysis and alerting management to anomalous results. Utilize data to create trend analyses for monitoring assumptions and market risk activities. Responsible for processing and accuracy of the company’s Funds Transfer Pricing (FTP). Basic understanding of FTP and other internal profitability metrics. Utilizes models and knowledge base for annual internal stress testing. Ensures accuracy of models through back-testing process and review of key results. Recommend model improvements and updates as needed. Develop / enhance documentation to align with internal Model Validation standards. Supervisory Responsibilities This position does not have supervisory duties.

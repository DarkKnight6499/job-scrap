Key responsibilities UBS SECURITIES LLC is seeking an Associate Director, Global Banking in New York, NY. Are you an innovative thinker? Are you focused on the details, even when under pressure? Do you enjoy delivering enhanced change capabilities across a range of business functions? We’re looking for an Associate Director, Global Banking to: • Assess deal impact, evaluate risk, and support valuation and pricing decisions. Advise clients on buy-side and sell-side transactions, including due diligence, valuation analysis, and transaction structuring. Support the execution of equity offerings including IPOs, follow-on offerings, and debt issuances. Utilize financial databases for retrieving market data, financial metrics, peer benchmarking, transaction comps, and industry research. • Build and maintain financial models, including discounted cash flow (DCF), leveraged buyout (LBO), merger models, initial public offering model, sum-of-the parts model, and comparable company and precedent transaction analyses. Apply corporate finance theory and economic principles such as time value of money, cost of capital, enterprise valuation, capital structure optimization, Risk and Return, and market efficiency to perform financial analysis. Build and maintain detailed financial models, including DCF, LBO, precedent transaction, and comparable company analysis. • Create marketing materials, strategic pitch books, and client presentations for internal and external meetings. Conduct in-depth research and analysis on relevant sectors, market trends, and competitive dynamics to support client engagements and identify opportunities. • Coordinate due diligence efforts, managing data rooms, working with legal and accounting advisors, and supporting the deal process from start to finish. Provide key analytical support in idea origination efforts and transaction execution, including mergers and acquisitions, corporate restructurings, leveraged buyouts, and execution of equity and debt capital markets transactions. • Create financial models and tool sets for valuation analysis. Research key industry and business trends. Be responsible for transaction execution and ongoing interactions with clients. Cover a broad portfolio of industrial segments, including capital goods, transportation, industrial services, aerospace, and defense, and building products.

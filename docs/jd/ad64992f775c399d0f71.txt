Macquarie Asset Management is seeking a Securitized Research Analyst to join our Global Fixed Income team, covering a broad and dynamic universe across the securitized credit landscape — from agency and non-agency RMBS to CMBS, ABS, and beyond. Your analysis will drive positioning across multiple strategies and investment styles, in a market environment shaped by shifting rate expectations, evolving credit performance, and growing investor demand for structured solutions. We are a global financial services group operating in 30 markets with 57 years of unbroken profitability. At Macquarie, you’re empowered to shape a career that is fulfilling and creates value. You will bring your insights and expertise to the task at hand and feel supported as you make your own kind of impact for a better future. What role will you play? Press space or enter keys to toggle section visibility You will conduct deep fundamental and structural research across securitized credit markets, developing differentiated fundamental and relative value views that feed directly into investment decisions. Working alongside experienced portfolio managers and a globally connected research team, you will be a genuine contributor from day one. This role demands analytical rigor across the full capital structure: from senior tranches to mezzanine and subordinated positions, across deal vintages, collateral types, and credit regimes. You will analyze collateral performance, deal structures, cash flow waterfalls, and macro sensitivities, build actionable investment views, and work with portfolio management teams to implement these positions across a variety of strategy styles. This role requires 4-5 days onsite in our Philadelphia office. What you offer Press space or enter keys to toggle section visibility 3–7+ years of experience in securitized credit, gained at a fund manager, sell-side desk, rating agency, bank or similar role. Demonstrated experience in securitized credit is required, and experience across multiple securitized credit asset classes - including some or all of ABS, CMBS, RMBS, Agency MBS, or other structured products - is preferred. A self-starter mindset – you will work independently and with the portfolio management team, take ownership of your coverage, and bring genuine passion for structured markets and fixed income. A bachelor’s degree is required, and CFA candidacy or a strong desire to pursue the designation is valued. Proven ability to analyze transactions across the capital structure, with comfort evaluating collateral quality, structural protections, and relative value at different attachment points. Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to synthesize complex structured transactions into clear, actionable investment views and manage a broad coverage universe simultaneously. Strong Microsoft Office and Bloomberg skills, with proficiency in cash flow modelling and securitization analytics tools such as Intex. Exposure to AI tools is viewed as an asset and a genuine growth opportunity as we continue to integrate these capabilities into the research process.

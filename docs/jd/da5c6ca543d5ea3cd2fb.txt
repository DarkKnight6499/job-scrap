THE ROLE Serve as the lead Enterprise Risk Business Partner supporting Operations at MFS, providing independent risk advice, effective challenge, and oversight across operational processes, controls, transformation initiatives, and emerging risks. Partner with Operations leadership and process owners to identify, assess, monitor, and communicate risk, strengthen the control environment, and incorporate risk considerations into business decisions and change initiatives. WHAT YOU WILL DO Serve as the lead Enterprise Risk Business Partner for Operations, developing a detailed understanding of its business activities, critical processes, risk profile, strategic priorities, and control environment. Provide independent risk advice and effective challenge to Operations leadership, process owners, project teams, and governance forums on significant operational risks, control matters, business decisions, and transformation initiatives. Lead and facilitate Risk and Control Self-Assessments and targeted risk assessments across Operations, helping stakeholders identify inherent risks, evaluate control design and effectiveness, assess residual risk, and develop appropriate risk responses. Support the identification, assessment, escalation, and ongoing monitoring of operational risk issues, including processing errors, control deficiencies, incidents, losses, near misses, service disruptions, and recurring risk themes. Provide risk oversight for significant Operations change initiatives, including process redesign, automation, system implementation, operating-model changes, outsourcing, vendor arrangements, and other technology-enabled transformation. Assess the design and effectiveness of key operational controls, working with business owners to identify practical opportunities to strengthen preventative, detective, and corrective controls. Develop and monitor risk indicators, metrics, and reporting that provide Operations leadership, Enterprise Risk, and governance committees with timely insight into risk exposure, control performance, incidents, emerging risks, and remediation progress. Identify recurring themes, concentration risks, interconnected risks, and opportunities for broader control or process improvements. Partner with Operations and relevant control functions on issue management, including root-cause analysis, corrective-action planning, risk acceptance, escalation, and validation of sustainable remediation. Promote a strong risk-aware culture within Operations through training, workshops, risk discussions, practical guidance, and constructive challenge that positions Enterprise Risk as a business partner and enabler of sound decision-making. Contribute to the continued enhancement of MFS’s Enterprise Risk framework, including risk taxonomy, risk appetite, assessment methodologies, operational risk standards, and reporting practices. Represent Enterprise Risk in Operations governance forums and significant initiatives, communicating risk considerations clearly and escalating material matters when appropriate. WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR Bachelor's degree or equivalent work experience. Must have at least 6 to 8 years of experience in enterprise risk, operational risk, investment operations, internal controls, audit, compliance, or a related role within financial services. Strong understanding of operational risk management principles, including risk identification, RCSA, control assessment, issue management, incident analysis, risk acceptance, and ongoing monitoring. Knowledge of investment-management or financial-services operational processes and the risks associated with transaction processing, data quality, reconciliations, asset servicing, valuation, reporting, technology dependencies, and third-party services. Demonstrated ability to evaluate the design and effectiveness of preventative, detective, and corrective controls and to recommend practical, risk-based improvements. Experience assessing the risks associated with significant business, process, technology, vendor, and organizational change. Ability to provide independent, constructive challenge while developing credible and collaborative relationships with senior leaders, process owners, subject-matter experts, and control partners. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, including the ability to analyze incidents, identify root causes and recurring themes, and translate complex information into clear risk insights. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including experience preparing executive-ready risk assessments, reports, presentations, and governance materials. Ability to work independently, manage multiple priorities, and exercise sound judgment in determining when matters require escalation. Strong project-management and organizational skills, with a demonstrated ability to deliver high-quality work within established timelines. Some periodic travel, domestic and international, may be required. PREFERRED SKILLS, QUALIFICATIONS & EXPERIENCE Understanding of internal control concepts and experience in conducting risk assessments preferred. Experience conducting or facilitating risk assessments, control evaluations, issue management, and management reporting. Experience with Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) tools. Experience with risk analytics, data visualization, and reporting tools such as Tableau or Power BI. Experience within investment management, asset management, fund administration, or fund treasury organizations. Experience in investment operations, asset management, investment management, fund administration, custody, asset servicing, or another complex financial-services operating environment. Understanding of the investment transaction life cycle and related operational dependencies, including data, systems, vendors, reconciliations, and handoffs between business functions.

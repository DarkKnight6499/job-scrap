The liquidity, account, and escrow landscape is rapidly evolving as new technologies and fintech entrants emerge, cash flows become increasingly 24x7, and regulatory, legal, tax, and market complexity continues to grow—making continued platform modernization and innovation critical to delivering client value and sustaining growth.

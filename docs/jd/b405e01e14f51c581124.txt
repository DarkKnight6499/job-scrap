Assess current campaign processes to identify inefficiencies, gaps, and opportunities for standardization and simplification. Define and implement enhanced tools and digital capabilities to improve data transparency, workflow performance, and decision-making across campaign activities. Establish and refine the Campaign Cross-Functional Team (CFT) structure to ensure clear roles, accountability, and streamlined communication across partner organizations. Evaluate and integrate AI-enabled solutions to automate manual processes, enhance analytics, and increase operational efficiency. Collaborate with HR and functional leaders to recommend talent retention strategies and incentive structures to sustain specialized campaign expertise. Develop and maintain a transformation roadmap, performance metrics, and reporting cadence to measure success and ensure alignment with organizational objectives. Serve as the central point of contact for campaign process governance, ensuring consistency, compliance, and effective communication across functional teams.

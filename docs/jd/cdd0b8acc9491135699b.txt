Responsible for Corporate FP&A activities and serves as the primary finance partner for budget management for Micron’s People Organization and Legal Organization. Partners with leadership to set budgets, review performance against plan, track intraquarter budget results, and perform ad hoc financial analyses to assess project financial viability. Provide financial support to the various global organizations. Collaborate with cross-functional teams in Finance, People, and more in order to manage the worldwide quarterly budget expense plan. Perform as an excellent business partner and share responsibility for assignee cost and allocations cross charges. Use innovative problem-solving skills to maintain efficient and accurate processes to meet the business needs. Build and maintain custom and dynamic financial reports that help drive better decision making and provide actionable insights. Communicate effectively with team members and leaders in diverse geographies. Employer will accept a Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business Administration, or related field and 3 years of experience in the job offered or related occupation.

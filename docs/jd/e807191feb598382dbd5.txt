The Role:
Creditflux, an ION Analytics company and the leading information source worldwide for the rapidly evolving market for credit funds and CLOs, is seeking a reporter to cover North American structured credit. Our North American product provides sophisticated investors and advisors with deep-dive coverage of credit funds and structured credit, with an emphasis on the CLO market, private credit fundraising and credit fund strategies.
The Reporter role is focused on breaking news on the CLO market as part of team of journalists and credit and legal analysts based in New York and around our global offices, as well as contributing a variety of longer-form articles to our monthly magazine, published both in print and online.
 
 Roles and Responsibilities: · Reporting, writing, and filing copy on a daily basis · Contributing to special projects such as conferences, databases, webinars and podcasts · Assisting with developing coverage outside of assigned beats · Cultivating and meeting with sources · In-region travel as necessary Required Skills, Experience, and Qualifications: · Previous experience covering fixed-income markets a plus · Strong writing skills and an investigative mindset · Time management and communication skills · Networking and contact-building skills · A self-starter attitude with a cooperative approach Estimated Salary Range and Benefits: 
 
The estimated salary range is $100,000 - $130,000. Salary is negotiable depending on experience and skills.

Our total rewards program is designed to support employee well-being and professional growth.  We offer a competitive benefits package including a discretionary bonus, paid holidays, a 401(k), and comprehensive medical, dental, and vision coverage. Additional benefits include life insurance, short and long-term disability, Commuter FSA, Family Building Benefits, and more. All benefits are subject to eligibility requirements.

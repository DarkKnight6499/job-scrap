POSITION DESCRIPTION This role is a VP position within the Americas Research Risk Management team. This team is one of the teams that comprise the Global Research First Line Risk Management organization. The role will help Morgan Stanley Research deliver on its mission by strengthening governance structures, increasing efficiencies, and enhancing operational, conduct and reputational risk frameworks in a globally consistent manner. This team supports the execution of day-to-day risk functions and works with global colleagues to manage the successful resolution of risk issues.

Position Description:
GMO’s Emerging Debt Team is a focused team that manages $9.5 billion in dedicated EMD AUM for a variety of clients (pension funds, endowments, sub advisory, etc).  It has a first-decile, 30-plus-year track record in managing hard currency strategies (EMBIG-D benchmark), and a first-decile 17-year track record in managing local currency strategy (GBI-EMGD benchmark).  It also manages a select set of bespoke mandates. 
 
The Emerging Debt Team is seeking to add a Corporate Credit Analyst to partner with and support a six-member team of portfolio managers and analysts. The team’s corporate credit research platform covers constituents of J.P. Morgan’s EMBI and CEMBI index suites, as well as select off-benchmark opportunities, with a particular focus on companies that benefit from varying degrees of government support, with a resulting coverage universe of 300+ names. This position is based in Boston.
 
 Responsibilities: GMO’s Emerging Debt Team has a differentiated investment philosophy and process. Research analysts leverage quantitative methods to organize and inform fundamental research. The successful candidate will be expected to quickly absorb and apply the investment process, producing consistent, high-quality outputs aligned with the team’s approach. Be comfortable balancing broad research coverage with targeted deep dives. The role requires coverage of a diverse universe of corporate credits across 80+ EM countries (IG and HY), 10+ sectors spanning corporates and financials, and all levels of the capital structure, while also conducting in-depth analysis of specific credit situations when warranted. Perform rigorous analytical work, including corporate credit analysis, assessment of sovereign linkages, and evaluation of structural and contractual features of individual instruments across public and private debt markets. Maintain and regularly update proprietary financial and credit models. Conduct robust due diligence through a variety of information sources, including meetings with company management teams, discussions with sell-side and independent industry analysts, and ongoing review of industry publications and relevant market data. Communicate analysis clearly and concisely through credit opinion write-ups, investment committee presentations, and other formats. Demonstrate the ability to present effectively to both internal and external audiences, tailoring content and delivery to the context and level of expertise of the audience. Effectively collaborate in a small collegial team environment. The successful candidate must be a proactive self-starter with the initiative and ability to work independently, while also partnering effectively with teammates and contributing to a collaborative research culture.

The opportunity EY has curated a dynamic ecosystem of Alliance and Ecosystem relationships which creates new forms of customer value to help our clients rapidly and digitally transform. Our ecosystem consists of more than 100 relationships which allow us to provide a wide range of collaborative opportunities and solutions to our clients. We help them tap into the latest technologies to achieve transformational outcomes and be more agile, innovative, resilient and better equipped to respond to disruptive change. Alliances are a critical aspect of the go-to market strategy for the firm. To achieve our revenue targets, we have identified the priority go to market focus areas for each of the alliance. The priority areas are the most strategic programs for the EY Strategic Alliances and are intended to drive revenue at scale. For SAP, the Global Alliance Campaign Associate Director will focus on EY’s SAP Public Cloud strategy, working with EY global and regional leaders to define our SAP Public Cloud strategy enable execution of firm-wide sales campaigns and on-going program activation and collaboration with SAP sales and engineering teams to drive the joint go to market. Activities may include coaching on SAP Public Cloud solutions, identification of target markets and addressable market, sales messaging, cadencing key EY and SAP leaders, escalating issues as appropriate and conducting business reviews with analytics/insights. Your key responsibilities Your role will sit within the Alliance and Ecosystem Relationship team that provides alliance management oversight for the top EY alliances. Specifically, the SAP Campaign Associate Director will work closely with EY’s Alliances leadership, counterparts within the Alliance home practice and EY account and business development executives to coordinate and deliver upon the objectives of the campaign. Your primary responsibilities will include: Design SAP Public Cloud GTM campaign strategy and campaign program elements. Collaborate with EY SAP leadership and the Alliance team on alliance components of the campaign (relationship map, cadence, governance, contracts, resale, etc). Identify target account strategy in conjunction with Global and Region Go-To-Market Leaders, Alliance Relationship Directors, Sector and Solution Leader(s). Develop messaging and value proposition for campaign including securing alignment and buy-in from the Alliance. Work with Brand, Marketing and Communication to develop sales collateral, marketing strategy, and other enablement materials as well as coordinate events as appropriate. Coordinate with Internal technology team, the Alliance Practice or Sector and/or the Alliance itself to track development of technical elements (demos and other tech assets). Activate each region with internal and external awareness sessions. Engage with Region SAP Go to Market Leaders to secure region support and identify region leads from EY and SAP to own the sales motion and drive in-market sales efforts. Incubate target accounts and land sales motion with Alliance Client Executives to expand. Provide early-stage big deal pre-sales support. Drive cadence with EY and SAP executives; drive visibility and preference to EY across regions, escalating issues as appropriate. Oversee campaign governance and sales / pipeline discipline – stand-up of campaign through campaign execution (awareness, financial tracking, account cadence, relationship alignment). This role is based in the US or Europe, offering a mix of virtual work and regular coordination with team members globally. Some travel may be required. Skills and attributes for success To qualify for the role you must have: Able to juggle many activities and provide enablement services in the areas of: alliance partner management, business development, marketing, contract management, financial management, content management, governance oversight, operational support, strong relationship building skills and is highly organized Experience on SAP Public Cloud solution and products, understanding on its business benefits. Experience in business development and/or alliance management for technology or professional services company Strong networking and influence management skills resulting in driving actions to outcomes and delivering results actions across organizational boundaries Highly credible communicator with excellent organization and project management skills and proven track record delivering alliance sales and revenues Ideally, you’ll also have Experience and evidence of success in building and nurturing win-win alliance relationships What we look for: We’re looking for a seasoned go to market or alliance professional who can build strong relationships with internal and external stakeholders and bring creativity, and consistent quality to this critical part of the business for EY.

all first line ORM activities About the Team The Moody’s Ratings Risk Management team was established in 2020 as the second line of defense risk function across Moody’s Ratings. The team sets risk policies and provides guidance, oversight, and challenge to ensure consistent implementation and ongoing adherence to risk standards. Operating as a global center of excellence, the team partners closely with the business to strengthen risk management practices across the organization.

Work You’ll Do As Technical Leader at Deloitte, you will be responsible for leading complex Salesforce architecture and delivery efforts that align business goals, technology requirements, and implementation outcomes. Advise clients on Salesforce architecture, platform capabilities, integration approaches, and implementation strategies for Sales Cloud and Service Cloud programs. Lead solution design decisions across Salesforce data models, customization approaches, enterprise integrations, and application architecture to support business and technology requirements. Oversee delivery teams through planning, design, build, test, and deployment activities for large-scale Salesforce implementations, while monitoring scope, quality, risks, and status. Support business development activities by contributing to proposals, solutioning discussions, and client presentations that demonstrate Deloitte’s experience and approach. Mentor practitioners, support recruiting and staffing activities, and contribute to practice growth through internal initiatives, thought leadership, and market-facing content.

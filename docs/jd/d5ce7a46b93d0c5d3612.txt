About The Opportunity As a Procurement Associate for Wonder you will work collaboratively & cross functionally with the merchant team, forecasting team, warehouse operations & accounts payable. You will ensure that all raw materials arrive to the Wonder production & distribution facilities in time and in specification. The Impact You Will Make Develop & maintain positive relationships with supplier partners via e-mail, virtual meetings & direct calls. Sustain proper inventory levels by evaluating/submitting purchase orders & ensuring they are delivered on time. Address & rectify any quality concerns of raw materials presented by culinary & operational teams to supplier partners. Communicate forecasting changes to supplier partners. Work cross-functionally to meet launch dates of new raw materials.

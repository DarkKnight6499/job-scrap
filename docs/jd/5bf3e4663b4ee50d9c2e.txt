The risk landscape is continuously changing due to market volatility, geopolitical events, regulatory reform, cyber threats, and the rapid adoption of AI. At the same time, organizations rely more than ever on an expanding ecosystem of third parties to operate and grow. That reliance brings real value, but also exposure: disruptions, data and cyber incidents, regulatory scrutiny, and concentration risk that often sit outside an organization’s direct control. Clients are looking to move beyond point-in-time, questionnaire-driven third-party programs toward approaches that are connected, more continuous, and able to support better decisions across the third-party lifecycle – from sourcing and onboarding through ongoing monitoring and offboarding. Achieving that requires both a strong understanding of how third-party risk should be managed and the ability to enable it through technology, including platforms like ServiceNow. As a member of our Risk Consulting practice, you will help clients assess, design, and transform their third-party risk management programs and the technology that powers them. You will bring a rare combination of TPRM domain knowledge and risk technology experience, allowing you to contribute across program design, process and operating model work, and platform implementation, and to help clients connect third-party risk with related disciplines such as procurement, cyber, operational resilience, enterprise risk, and compliance. The opportunity This is an opportunity to grow both personally and professionally at a global, multi-disciplinary consulting firm. You will build and apply expertise in both third-party risk management and the technologies that enable it, helping clients use risk platforms, AI, and data across the third-party management lifecycle. You won’t only work within risk competencies. You’ll bring the breadth of the firm, working alongside procurement, legal, contracting, and many other teams to help clients set up end-to-end processes with third-party risk built in throughout the lifecycle. You will also have the opportunity to help clients shift to managed services models based on their needs and continue to optimize those capabilities. That breadth will speed up your development and position you as a trusted advisor who can connect what a program needs to achieve with how to make it happen. Your key responsibilities As our Risk Consulting capabilities continue to evolve and grow, we are seeking highly motivated Senior consultants who can support clients from design through implementation of their third-party risk programs. You will work with multi-disciplinary teams across the firm, both onshore and offshore, to help clients assess, design, and transform their programs end to end. That includes orchestrating third-party risk processes, putting technology to work, and moving from traditional point-in-time models to data-driven, real-time risk sensing.

Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Fraud, Investigations & Regulatory Enforcement (FIRE) Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Risk & Regulatory - Government Contract Consulting - Senior Associate you will help clients navigate regulatory requirements and government contract matters by providing advice on compliance strategies, controls, and risk management approaches. As a Senior Associate you will analyze complex problems, manage assigned workstreams, build meaningful client connections, and mentor junior team members while using critical thinking to interpret data and develop practical recommendations within our Risk and Compliance practice. In this role at PwC, you will contribute to client service engagements that call for sound judgment, attention to detail, and comfort navigating ambiguity as you support work across regulatory risk compliance and government contract consulting. Responsibilities - Analyzing government contract compliance requirements and identifying regulatory risks across client engagements - Developing compliance strategies and practical recommendations that address contracting, reporting, and control requirements - Reviewing policies, procedures, and supporting documentation to identify gaps in regulatory adherence and internal controls - Interpreting data, contract terms, and program records to inform findings, insights, and proposed actions - Supporting investigation and remediation activities tied to fraud, misconduct, and regulatory matters - Preparing clear client deliverables that summarize issues, observations, and next steps for compliance improvement - Coordinating workstreams with team members while monitoring progress, dependencies, and changing client priorities - Guiding junior staff through task execution, review comments, and issue resolution on assigned work - Building client relationships by anticipating needs, responding to questions, and adapting to shifting project conditions - Applying regulatory, governance, and compliance frameworks to assess controls, reporting, and monitoring activities What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 3 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Building meaningful client connections in government contract consulting - Anticipating team and client needs in complex situations - Using critical thinking to break down complex concepts - Interpreting data to inform insights and recommendations - Developing understanding of changing business context - Upholding professional standards, code of conduct, and independence Travel Requirements Up to 60% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $77,000 - $202,000.

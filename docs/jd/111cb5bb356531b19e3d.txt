Summary: The Risk Analyst I (“Analyst”) will be assisting in the build out and execution of Osaic's risk management program. The Analyst will complete assigned projects, with direction and guidance from the direct supervisor, that enhance documentation of key risks and accompanying controls, risk frameworks and protocols, and deriving risk information and insights from data. Analyst will engage with subject matter experts to create workflow maps, identify risks and accompanying controls, and provide guidance in identifying or developing risk metrics and reporting to senior leadership. The ability to meet with business leaders, learn new processes, document those processes accurately, and identify key risks, controls, data patterns, and gaps in controls is critical to the role. The Analyst will report directly to the VP of Enterprise Risk. Education Requirements: Bachelor’s Degree from accredited university in Business Administration, Finance, Accounting, Risk Management, or related field required. High School Diploma or equivalent (GED) is required. Certifications: SQL, Power BI, or other similar certifications in the area of analytics is preferred. Responsibilities: Responsible for learning business processes of business units under review and be able to provide guidance on identified risks and design of controls. Provide guidance regarding "Three Lines of Defense" risk control framework. Ability to produce process flow maps (in Vizio) showing key decision points, ownership of specific steps within the process, and relevant risks and controls. Ability to meet the business managers, ask questions, listen, and analyze discussions to produce meaningful insights and accurate documentation. Consult on assessment and enhancement of risk management framework throughout the company, including identification of risks and accompanying controls and provide accurate assessment of control effectiveness. Assessment of data to determine control effectiveness, trends, and "red flag" events. Ability to create monthly metric reporting for specific risk programs and be able to "tell the story" to managers and senior leaders. Review of applicable regulations and written regulatory guidance applicable to business unit (or risk program). Ability to assess if written supervisory procedures and firm policies adequately address regulatory requirements and business risks. All other duties as assigned. Basic Requirements: Entry level (0 to 1 year) of previous work experience Attention to what other people are saying, taking time to understand the points being made, asking questions as appropriate, and maintaining focus and striving for comprehension at all times. Logic and reasoning to identify the strengths and weaknesses of alternative solutions, conclusions or approaches to problems. Communication to others to convey information effectively as well as communicating effectively in writing as appropriate for the needs of the audience. Knowledge of financial services industry trends, directions, major issues, regulatory considerations and trendsetters; ability to provide specific financial knowledge and experience to help organizational development. Ability to come up with unusual or clever ideas about a given topic or situation, or to develop creative ways to solve a problem. Ability developing constructive and cooperative working relationships with others and maintaining them over time. Job requires being pleasant with others on the job and displaying a good-natured, cooperative attitude. Administration of own time and the time of others and the ability to shift back and forth between two or more activities or sources of information. Knowledge of functions, features and applications of Microsoft Office; ability to use the cross-functional features of its applications to enhance overall organizational efficiency. Knowledge of data and data science techniques to identify trends and create predictions Preferred Requirements: Master’s in Business Administration, Finance, Accounting, Risk Management, or related field.

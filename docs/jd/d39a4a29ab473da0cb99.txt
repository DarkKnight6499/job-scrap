Job Description The Default Management Foreclosure Associate will be assigned a list of loans to review and ensure the account is eligible for foreclosure. They will perform review to initiate foreclosure referrals on defaulted mortgages, gather required documents, prepare referral packages and send them to the attorney. They will assist in multiple projects throughout foreclosure including distressed properties, charge offs, non-monetary breached loans and quality control.

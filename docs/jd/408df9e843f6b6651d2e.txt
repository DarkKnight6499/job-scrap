We're looking for an FP&A Manager to lead financial reporting and performance analysis across our rental business segments, and to serve as the primary link between Sales and Finance — connecting commercial performance to financial results. The person in this role owns the strategic development of our profit allocation tool. Because the tool's evolution depends on inputs and collaboration from IT, Risk, and other Finance functions, this role requires someone who can drive cross-functional alignment as much as they can build a model.

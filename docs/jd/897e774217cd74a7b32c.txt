Identify threats, weaknesses and opportunities in the risk profile, and raise concerns promptly. Conduct scenario analysis, stress testing and regular or ad hoc reviews of key risk themes. Develop risk management strategies that support the growth of systematic trading and investor products. Prepare and present risk reports, insights and recommendations to senior management. Partner with Trading, Finance and Operations to assess and manage risks. Improve risk management tools and systems, and advance the use of artificial intelligence in analytics and automation.

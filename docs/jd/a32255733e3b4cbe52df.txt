Build the financial insights behind critical business decisions. We’re hiring Finance Business Partners - FP&A professionals across Consultant, Associate Manager and Manager levels . About the Role: The Finance Business Partner provides financial analysis, planning, reporting, and commercial insight to help business and functional stakeholders improve performance and make informed decisions across FP&A, operational and commercial finance, investment, and cost management. What you’ll do: Your responsibilities may include: Analyze and communicate financial and operational performance, highlighting key drivers, trends, risks, opportunities, and actions. Improve dashboards, report frameworks, metrics, and processes to strengthen quality, efficiency, and decision support. Support budgeting, forecasting, and long-range planning through financial modelling and analysis. Perform variance and scenario analysis, develop business cases, and recommend actions to improve outcomes. Partner with stakeholders to provide practical financial insight on priorities including resource allocation, pricing, investment, productivity, and cost optimization. Build strong cross-functional relationships and, at senior levels, support coaching and knowledge sharing. Support finance policies, controls, governance, and data quality. Simplify and automate processes and contribute to ERP, planning, reporting, analytics, and AI-enabled finance improvements. What we’re looking for: You’ll bring experience in one or more of the following areas: Experience in Finance Business Partnering, FP&A, management accounting, commercial finance, or operational finance within a complex organization. Proven ability to deliver financial insight and decision support across areas such as supply chain, manufacturing, e-commerce, CapEx, OpEx, or corporate functions. Retail and/or FMCG experience is preferred; required experience varies by career level. Strong financial modelling, analytical, problem-solving, and data interpretation skills, with sound knowledge of budgeting, forecasting, reporting, and performance management. Advanced Excel skills and experience with ERP, planning, and BI platforms such as SAP, Oracle, Anaplan, OneStream, IBM Planning Analytics, Power BI, or Tableau. Strong communication and presentation skills, with the ability to explain financial concepts clearly and build collaborative relationships across the organization. Commercial mindset and ability to turn analysis into practical recommendations and influence decisions through constructive challenge.

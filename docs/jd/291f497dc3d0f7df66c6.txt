About this role Your team The Alternatives Client Platform (ACP) team supports the coordination and administration of investor communications and collateral across BlackRock’s private markets platform. The team is responsible for delivering high-quality client and fund reporting, marketing materials, data management, analytics, and operational support that help provide a best-in-class experience for alternative investors. By combining process excellence, data-driven analysis, and technology-enabled solutions, ACP drives scale, innovation, and operational efficiency across private markets business lines. Your role and impact As an Associate within the Alternatives Client Platform team, you will play a key role in supporting investor communications, client reporting, fundraising activities, and operational improvement initiatives across private markets strategies. You will partner with a broad range of stakeholders, including portfolio managers, asset managers, fund administration teams, product specialists, and client-facing teams to deliver accurate, timely, and insightful information to clients and prospects. This role provides the opportunity to deepen your understanding of private markets while contributing to a high-performing and collaborative team environment. Your responsibilities In every role at BlackRock, you'll be expected to apply sound judgement and critical thinking to solve complex problems, adapt as the business evolves, and combine the curiosity to explore new approaches and technologies with the rigor to challenge the results. The scope of this role also includes the following responsibilities: Produce and coordinate client reporting packages by gathering, validating, and analyzing fund, portfolio, investment, and market data across private markets strategies. Partner with portfolio managers, asset managers, fund administrators, and other stakeholders to support recurring reporting cycles, custom client requests, and regulatory reporting requirements. Analyze and maintain performance, exposure, assets under management (AUM), flow, and track record data to support investor communications and business-line reporting needs. Coordinate the preparation, approval, and distribution of client-facing materials, including webinars, presentations, marketing collateral, and communication platform content. Support fundraising initiatives through request for proposal (RFP) and due diligence questionnaire (DDQ) responses, partnering with internal teams to gather, validate, and communicate relevant information. Develop ad hoc client materials, respond to information requests, and support large-scale data management activities across funds, strategies, and supported business lines. Drive operational excellence through process enhancement, technology adoption, workflow standardization, and effective information management practices. Your experience Bachelor's degree required. 3+ years of experience in financial services or asset management; experience supporting alternative investments, client reporting, portfolio reporting, or product strategy activities preferred. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to communicate effectively across stakeholder groups and collaborate across functions. Strong analytical, quantitative, and problem-solving skills with meticulous attention to detail and the ability to synthesize complex information. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel, Word, and PowerPoint, with strong data management and reporting capabilities; experience with VBA or Python is a plus. Proven ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment while demonstrating initiative, accountability, sound judgment, and a continuous improvement mindset.

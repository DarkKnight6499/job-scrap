Role Description: The Tech Lead, Investment Quant Tools will lead the technology capabilities that enable portfolio risk management and quantitative analytics. This key role will work directly with the business leadership to plan the vision and execution of the roadmap. The role is being created as part of a broader transformation led by the CTO to better align NAMI's technology with business priorities, enable scale and adopt Nomura's global technology standards. Key responsibilities for this role include: Platform Strategy & Architecture Own the technology roadmap and architecture for Investment Risk across equity, fixed income and multi asset. Lead joint evaluation processes for major platform or vendor decisions, including requirements definition, RFPs, and assessment of functionality, architecture, data integration impact, and total cost of ownership Drive rationalization and integration across the client technology platform portfolio Ensure architectural alignment with enterprise standards set by Data and Platforms Enablement Coordinate with Production Services Engineering on release and deployment planning Skills, experience, qualifications and knowledge required: Bachelor’s degree required; advanced degree in business, technology, or related discipline preferred 10+ years of progressive technology leadership experience Experience leading a technology team that directly supports the investment process Experience leading large-scale transformation initiatives Direct experience with platforms such as Snowflake, Airflow, AWS, and market data vendors (Bloomberg, Aladdin, FactSet, Barra) Critical leadership skills including driving results, collaborating with the business, and influencing stakeholders required Nomura Leadership Behaviours Explore Insights & Vision: Identify the underlying causes of problems faced by you or your team and define a clear vision and direction for the future.

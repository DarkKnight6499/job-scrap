Job Description: Joining Russell Investments’ Customized Portfolio Solutions (CPS) team means being part of an investment organization focused on delivering customized solutions that help institutional clients manage risk, improve portfolio implementation, and achieve their investment objectives. The Senior Portfolio Manager, Overlay Solutions is a senior investment professional responsible for managing complex client portfolios and contributing more broadly to the investment leadership, evolution, and growth of Russell Investments’ Overlay Solutions capability. The team manages more than $135 billion in client assets and implements customized currency and derivative-based strategies across a diverse range of institutional client mandates. This role has broader responsibility across Overlay Solutions than a traditional Portfolio Manager position, with an expectation to develop expertise across multiple strategies and client applications rather than operating within a narrowly defined area of coverage. The Senior Portfolio Manager will serve as a senior resource for complex portfolio decisions, client engagements, new mandate implementation, investment innovation, risk oversight, and cross-functional initiatives. The Senior Portfolio Manager will help elevate the capabilities of the broader team through coaching and mentorship, create connectivity across investment disciplines, lead through influence, and increasingly translate investment expertise into broader team and business impact. Exercise independent investment judgment within established client objectives, investment guidelines, and governance frameworks. Partner closely with Trading, FX, quantitative research, transition management, and other investment teams to develop and implement solutions for complex or atypical client needs.

Senior/Lead Financial Analyst
Liberty Mutual
Location: Boston, Massachusetts, United States
Link: https://searchjobs.libertymutualgroup.com/careers/job/618520435619

Description As a Finance & Accounting Analyst at Liberty Mutual, you will be a key strategic partner to our innovative and growing Energy , Public Entity and Inland Marine underwriting teams. This role is central to driving business performance through robust financial reporting, planning, and analytics. You will be responsible for the full scope of financial and operational reporting, planning, and forecasting. In this position, you will manage complex financial analysis reports and projects, leveraging strong data stewardship and analytics to provide strategic recommendations to the leadership of these market-facing teams. Responsibilities : Prepare and enhance critical financial and operational reports, analyses, plans, and forecasts in accordance with prescribed procedures. Act as a data steward for the teams, obtaining required data for analyses from appropriate internal (e.g., general ledger, loss warehouse) or external sources in a timely, accurate, and efficient manner. Perform in-depth variance analyses (e.g., plan vs. actual, prior year, multi-year trends) and identify significant items requiring further investigation to support our underwriting partners. Ensure the timeliness, accuracy, and completeness of all reports and analyses by maintaining full documentation of recurring job duties, data sources, and assumptions. Partner with market-facing Energy, Public Entity and Inland Marine underwriting teams, providing financial insights that directly support their strategy and goals. Identify opportunities for efficiencies and improvements to processes, controls, and technology systems related to the functions being performed. Perform follow-up investigations on financial or operational variances and suggest resolutions. This position can be filled as either a Senior Financial Analyst or a Lead Financial Analyst, depending on experience. The posted description and salary range are for the Lead Financial Analyst. The selected candidate should live within 50 miles of the Boston office and be able to come into the office two times/week. This policy is subject to change. Qualifications Bachelor`s degree (Finance or Accounting) required. CPA or advanced degree in Accounting, Finance, or Business preferred. Minimum of 5 years of finance experience. Must demonstrate attention to detail in a fast-paced work environment and the ability to manage and prioritize multiple projects simultaneously to drive results. Communicates well with own team and across organizational boundaries to ensure the successful completion of shared goals. Comfortable leading meetings and able to communicate issues and trends to senior management and other groups at the appropriate level of detail. Solid understanding of Finance Tools and Analytical Methods.

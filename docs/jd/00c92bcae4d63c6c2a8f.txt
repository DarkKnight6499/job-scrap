ABOUT THE ROLE We are hiring a Senior Product Manager for our Liquidity team, which builds the products that help members get through the gap between paychecks. This is a hands-on role: you will own discovery and execution for your area end to end, write your own specs, and work closely with leaders who are involved in key decisions. Liquidity products: early access to earned pay and fee-free overdraft, from eligibility and limits to settlement and the member experience around them. These products carry real complexity under the hood (eligibility rules, risk models, bank partners, compliance) and the member experience has to stay simple and clear. Build the roadmap for your area, bring your own point of view on what should be in it, and drive it from discovery through launch.

Job Description Overview: Visa Consulting and Analytics (VCA) partners with Visa’s clients to solve their most important strategic challenges and drive meaningful business results. We bring together strategy consulting, analytics, payments expertise, and deep industry insight to help financial institutions and merchants grow, compete, and innovate in a rapidly evolving payments landscape. The Risk Practice within Visa Consulting & Analytics (VCA) partners with North American issuers, merchants, acquirers, issuer processors, fintechs, and payment enablers to solve some of their most critical business challenges across disputes, fraud, authorization, and broader payment risk. We help clients balance growth, customer experience, operational efficiency, regulatory obligations, and financial performance in an increasingly complex payments landscape. Combining strategy consulting, advanced analytics, deep payments expertise, and transformation execution, our work helps clients turn insights into action and deliver measurable business outcomes. Visa is seeking a Senior Manager to join our Risk Practice and support the commercialization of Visa Disputes Optimization (VDO). This role will focus on dispute strategy, operating model transformation, and dispute optimization across the payment ecosystem. This role is ideal for a commercially minded consultant with a proven ability to build trusted client relationships, identify and shape new business opportunities, and deliver high-impact engagements. Build ROI-driven business cases and sales narratives that appeal to all players in the payment ecosystem.

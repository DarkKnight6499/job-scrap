THE DIFFERENCE YOU MAKE Santander Corporate & Investment Banking is looking for a Structured Finance Associate within The European Structured Finance Energy team based out of Madrid (Luca de Tena office) The European Structured Finance Energy team is the front-office origination and execution platform covering Power and Renewables financings across Europe. The team operates on a predominantly pan-European basis, with potential involvement in selected global initiatives. Santander is consistently ranked #1 in Europe and top 3 globally in renewables structured finance. The platform covers both advisory and lending mandates, across a broad range of financing structures. We are seeking an Associate with a strong desire to contribute to the continued growth of our financing and debt advisory activities within the Energy transition space. We’re shaping the way we work through innovation, cutting-edge technology, collaboration and the freedom to explore new ideas. To succeed in this role, you will be responsible for: Support both the lending and debt advisory segments of the business, including strategic planning, marketing, client engagement, and coordination with external advisors and partners. To help senior members of the team to originate and execute financing opportunities across the Power and Renewable sectors. Assist with business origination activities and preparation of top-quality marketing and internal materials, both from a content and visual perspective. Ability for building from scratch complex financial models is highly desirable, ensuring that accurately reflect transaction terms and due diligence inputs, running sensitivity analysis, and making adjustments as deals evolve. To design new value-added solutions, as the team progresses providing more complex structures. Responsible for mentoring and developing more junior members of staff, primarily on financial modelling workstreams, providing constructive feedback on a formal and informal basis. Support the execution of new and existing mandates across lending and advisory: Advisory: Assist in structuring and placing debt financings, including financial modelling, coordination of due diligence providers and legal advisors, preparation of marketing materials (teasers, information memoranda), and overall process management. Lending: Review due diligence materials, financial models and documentation, participate in negotiations with clients or their advisors, and support presentations to internal credit and pricing committees.

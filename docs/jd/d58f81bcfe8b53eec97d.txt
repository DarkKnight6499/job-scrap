Primary Purpose of Position Responsible for performing entry-level technical tasks, under direct supervision, in support of artificial intelligence-enabled solutions, automation, data-informed process improvements, and digital tools that support Constellation business objectives and operations. This role supports and helps accelerate the continued adoption of Artificial Intelligence (AI) within Constellation Generation by identifying practical opportunities to apply AI, simplify workflows, reduce manual effort, and improve worker efficiency, productivity, and consistency. The role also supports business stakeholders, engineering teams, and technology partners by assisting with requirements gathering, solution design, testing, deployment, documentation, adoption support, and ongoing improvement of AI-enabled tools in accordance with applicable safety, security, governance, regulatory, and responsible AI expectations. Perform assigned technical activities including requirements gathering, process mapping, prompt development, solution configuration, data analysis, testing, troubleshooting, documentation, and Power BI reporting and dashboard development. Support the development, deployment, adoption, monitoring, and continuous improvement of AI enabled solutions, Copilot agents, workflow automations, and other digital tools that enhance business processes, support engineering functions, and advance the adoption of artificial intelligence within Constellation. Collaborate with business stakeholders, engineering personnel, Information Technology, cybersecurity, data governance, and compliance teams to deliver solutions that are practical, maintainable, secure, and aligned with Constellation standards.

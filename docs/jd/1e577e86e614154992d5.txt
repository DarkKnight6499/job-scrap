The opportunity Our actuarial advisers deliver creative and innovative thinking to high-profile property and casualty insurance companies worldwide, helping them make confident financial decisions aligned with their business goals. Here, you will be an actuary right at the heart of that mission, leading engagement teams, working directly with clients, mentoring and developing team members and building your personal brand as a leader in your field. Your key responsibilities As a Senior within the Insurance Actuarial Advisory Services Property and Casualty team, you will be challenged to lead engagement teams and contribute to projects such as: Reserving, actuarial opinion and regulatory services Ratemaking analytics and services Actuarial process redesign and software integration Predictive analytics Data visualization Market strategy advice Reinsurance analysis and advice Ad hoc financial reporting advice Skills and attributes for success Build strong relationships with client personnel and consistently deliver high-quality services. Monitor progress, manage risk and ensure key stakeholders are kept informed about outcomes. Stay abreast of current business, regulatory and industry trends relevant to the client's business. Demonstrate in-depth technical actuarial expertise and professional knowledge. Exhibit strong business acumen and a commitment to continuous learning. Commit to developing a strong personal brand by staying informed regarding economic/market developments and their impact on the firm's clients. Progress towards professional credentials from the Casualty Actuarial Society or other industry organizations. To qualify for the role, you must have: Bachelor's degree in Actuarial Science, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field. Approximately 3+ years of experience in an actuarial position. Associate of the Casualty Actuarial Society, (ACAS)or actively pursuing Associateship within the Casualty Actuarial Society with a minimum of 5 actuarial exams passed. 2 years of experience with Excel, SAS, VBA and /or C++. Strong writing, presentation, conceptual, analytical, and interpersonal skills as evidenced by preparing and presenting results and/or findings to clients or senior management. Ability to understand and persuade on complex subjects both in writing and verbally Experience working in a team environment on project-based work. Ideally, you will also have: 2 years of experience with R, Python, or other relevant programming languages. Previous consulting experience or experience implementing new processes/procedures in a corporate environment. What we look for: We are most interested in intellectually curious people with a clear passion for actuarial work. You will also need to be a great communicator, as well as flexible in the face of often rapidly changing projects.

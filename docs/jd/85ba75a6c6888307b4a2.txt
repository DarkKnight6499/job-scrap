Underwriting Director - UQ06ED We’re determined to make a difference and are proud to be an insurance company that goes well beyond coverages and policies. Working here means having every opportunity to achieve your goals – and to help others accomplish theirs, too. Join our team as we help shape the future. As an Underwriting Director in the Navigators Wholesale Property business you will be responsible for underwriting, servicing and growing a profitable book of E&S Property business through a wide variety of wholesale brokers with a focus on all segments of E&S property on full limits, primary, excess, and quota share basis. Expertise should include underwriting of named perils, all risks, and single catastrophe related risks across multiple classes including habitational, light manufacturing, mixed real estate and other E&S related classes. Underwriting Execution Achieves assigned departmental goals and objectives Underwrites new and renewal business, may or may not have the highest levels of Underwriting Authority among underwriting staff Analyzes regional/assigned book of business on a quarter-by-quarter basis; Builds appropriate action plan with manager Expertly applies all applicable underwriting guidelines when making account decisions Capable of underwriting complex business with limited involvement from manager or product/referral teams Coaches and supports junior underwriters on team Proposes individual account insurance program to reflect risk quality, identified exposures and controls, loss experience, and achievement of bottom line profit results for the company with occasional need for input from manager, product or referral teams. Sales /Agency Management Independently build territory sales plans to achieve financial operating plan Create, drive and execute strategies for new and/or renewal accounts with individual brokerages Serve as local lead in creation of sales plans and checkpoints with brokers where appropriate Establishes and maintains a pipeline of new business prospects Responsible for communicating individual account strategy to broker, educates others on strategy, and involves manager as appropriate Trusted advisor to broker – viewed as a solution provider due to technical knowledge, underwriting acumen and service Coaches junior staff on agency management Thorough knowledge of value proposition and capabilities of other segments of The Hartford to further a One Hartford approach to agency and account management Conducts cost/benefit analysis before proposing changes Product Knowledge and Policy Command Presents to junior underwriters on coverage and policy language Thorough knowledge of federal, state and local laws that impact our business Contributes to the creation of/maintenance of underwriting guidelines through collaboration with product team Strong working knowledge of profitability trends of the departmental and individual book of business, proactively seeks opportunities to improve and enhance results Qualifications 10+ years of E&S property underwriting experience is required; experience working with wholesale insurance brokers preferred Demonstrated success in developing and maintaining solid relationships with all internal and external business partners Excellent communication, interpersonal and presentation skills An ability to think analytically about business problems, make recommendations and propose solutions. High energy self-starter, who is resilient and has an entrepreneurial spirit Demonstration of solid time, organizational, and desk management skills Goal-oriented and delivers outcomes Ability to challenge the status quo and compete to win Superior technical knowledge and sound decision-making and analytical skills This role can have a Hybrid work schedule.

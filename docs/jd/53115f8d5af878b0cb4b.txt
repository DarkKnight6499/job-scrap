Enterprise Identity is the foundation of American Express's digital customer experience, enabling secure, seamless, and reliable authentication and authorization services. The Enterprise Identity Application Support (AS) team partners with Product, Engineering, SRE, and Program teams to ensure the availability, performance, and resilience of mission-critical Identity and Access Management (IAM) platforms. These capabilities include American Express Logon, OAuth-based authorization for Open Banking, customer identity systems such as Product Registry and Profile, and authentication services including One Auth, AuthN+, and Verification Services. Supporting high-availability objectives of up to 99.999%, the team operates 24x7x365 through a global follow-the-sun model spanning the United States and India.

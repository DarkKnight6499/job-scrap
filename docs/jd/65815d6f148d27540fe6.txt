Senior Risk Manager-Global Payments Network We are seeking a visionary Senior Risk Manager to join the International Regulatory Risk Management team for the Global Payment Network (GPN). In this critical leadership role on the Network Risk Operations team, you will be responsible for managing the evolution of our international regulatory operations, building a highly governed, scalable engine that informs our business trajectory and fuels global expansion. You will manage the creation of enabling environments and turn adaptability in the face of global regulations into commercial differentiators. By centralizing our regulatory engagement models, you will proactively shape markets and position GPN as a preferred partner in the global payments ecosystem. Key Responsibilities Manage international regulatory reporting and complex global exams for the Global Payment Network, including major international audits. Drive post-exam insights into continuous, systemic control enhancements. Lead and orchestrate international change management frameworks to comply with shifting international laws, ensuring the network remains agile and fully compliant across borders. Facilitate country entrance and exits, in line with business strategy. Design and deploy a unified regulatory engagement model for the Global Payment Network, establishing clear operational guardrails and centralized oversight for all international regulatory interactions. Seamlessly coordinate and drive alignment across a globally distributed network of teams. Drive scalability and optimize resource allocation by aligning regulatory strategies with market commercial priorities, jurisdictional risk profiles, and enterprise risk appetite. Partner with Business, Product, and Technology teams to build "enabling environments" globally. Translate complex, international regulatory trends into technical specifications and market-leading offerings, turning compliance capabilities into a competitive advantage. Basic Qualifications: Bachelor's degree or military experience At least 3 years of experience in financial services risk management At least 5 years of experience in project management At least 7 years of experience supporting, partnering with, and interacting with internal business clients Preferred Qualifications: At least 8 years of experience in financial services risk management At least 7 years of experience leading cross-functional projects and programs within a risk management organization Project Management Professional (PMP) Certification or Master's Certificate in Project Management Experience leading cross-functional teams, establishing strategic direction, and managing stakeholder expectations At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position .

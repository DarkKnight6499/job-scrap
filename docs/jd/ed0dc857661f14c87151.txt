Start your Voyage – Apply Now Get to Know the Opportunity The Associate Consultant Relations Advisor’s (ACRA) primary objective is to collaborate with US Consultant Relations (“CR”) to drive commercial success with Investment Consultants and other Professional Buyers. ACRAs need to effectively partner with Investment Teams, Marketing & Product, Client Service and Business Development to enable US Distribution to achieve its sales targets. The Contributions You Will Make Provide support to the Consultant Relations team to cover marketing and distribution of Fixed Income, Alternatives, Equities and Solution-driven strategies. Organize and prepare for consultant meetings, calls, on-sites, and Voya sponsored events. Coordinate with internal partners (e.g., RFP, CPM, Marketing, Product teams) to complete consultant requests and track appropriately. Identify and implement best practices on the team for utilization across all team members and regions. Maintain consultant strategy for each national and global firm. Collaborate with Regional Client Advisory teams. ACRA’s will also partner with Business Management to maintain in Salesforce all activities/call reports, pipeline updates, and organizational information including key contacts. ACRAs are responsible for timely and accurate data to allow proper reporting to be reviewed during monthly regional CA/CR meetings and one-on-one meetings with the leadership of Distribution. Minimum Knowledge and Experience Undergraduate degree with 2-3 years of work experience, preferably within the institutional investment management industry or in an investment banking analyst program; product knowledge across Fixed Income, Equities and Alternatives is preferred. Demonstrated ability to learn complex investment/product concepts, with strong analytical and creative problem-solving skills. Excellent interpersonal, teamwork, written/verbal communication, and presentation skills. Strong project management, organization, and planning skills, with a demonstrated ability to meet deadlines without compromising accuracy or quality; strong ownership of projects and tasks. FINRA Series 7 and 63 licenses are required within 90 days of filling the position. Candidates who have not begun the CFA program will be expected to pursue the CFA Charter beginning in their first year.

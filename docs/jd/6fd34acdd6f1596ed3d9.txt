Job Description: At Regions, the Regions Business Capital Underwriting Analyst supports the relationship management and origination efforts of Regions Business Capital. Primary Responsibilities Models financial projections and sensitizations Spreads and analyzes financial statements Evaluates risk ratings and calculates RAROC's Performs research on specific companies and industries Analyzes collateral data Writes analysis memos and assists in the preparation of credit approvals and preparing other documents Performs other duties necessary to support the goals and objectives of the department and overall business unit This position is exempt from timekeeping requirements under the Fair Labor Standards Act and is not eligible for overtime pay. This position is incentive eligible. Requirements Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance or a related field At least 2 accounting classes Skills and Competencies Ability to produce an accurate work product Ability to work long hours and have a flexible schedule to meet tight deadlines Accounting and finance skills/abilities, with prior financial analysis experience Detail-oriented with a demonstrated ability to analyze complex data and facts Proficient modeling and Microsoft Excel skills Proficient verbal and written communication skills Self motivated, quick learner, with the ability to handle and prioritize multiple tasks Regions will not sponsor applicants for work visas for this position.

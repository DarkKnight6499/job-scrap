Position Title: ABS Commercial / Esoteric – Analyst / Senior Analyst (NY / Chicago) Entity: Kroll Bond Rating Agency, LLC Employment Type: Full-time Location: New York, NY or Chicago, Illinois, United States Summary/Overview : KBRA (Kroll Bond Rating Agency) is seeking a credit analyst to join our Commercial Asset-Backed Securities ( ABS ) team. The position is open to candidates in our New York City Office or Chicago Office. The ideal candidate should have a strong interest in ABS. Knowledge in commercial ABS asset classes (including, for example, data centers, communications infrastructure, equipment, or aviation) is preferred, though not required. An Analyst within the ABS group will be a part of the transaction rating team, working with other members of the team through all aspects of the rating process from collateral analysis to report publication. About the Job: Conduct and assist in transaction analysis including originator/servicer evaluation, collateral performance analysis, modeling and analysis of bond structure, and review of legal structure and documentation. Assist in the preparation of credit memoranda and presentation of transactions to rating committees. Prepare and contribute to individual transaction reports for publication. Assist in the publication of market commentaries related to topical events or research. Engage in ongoing surveillance of rated transactions and updating ratings as warranted. Attend and participate in seller/servicer due diligence. Willingness to travel as needed. You will be successful in this role if you have: Bachelor's degree required Analyst level: Zero (0) to two (2) years of experience in performing credit analysis within the ABS sector at a Rating Agency, Buy-Side Investor, Credit Research, or similar environment. Senior Analyst level: Minimum of two (2) or more years of experience in performing credit analysis within the ABS sector at a Rating Agency, Buy-Side Investor, Credit Research, or similar environment. Exceptional attention to detail. Strong oral and written communications skills (writing sample on request). Strong Excel skills (excel test may be administered as part of the interview process). Ability to take initiative, work independently, and work closely with partners in a collaborative environment. Proficient with relevant financial modeling software (e.g. Intex) is a plus. Familiarity with Generative AI tools such as ChatGPT for research, data insights, and general productivity is a plus. Salary Range: The anticipated annual base salary range for this full-time position is $80,000 to $110,000. Offer amounts are determined by factors such as experience, skills, geography, and other job-related factors.

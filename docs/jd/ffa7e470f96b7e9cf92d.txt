At Broadridge, we've built a culture where the highest goal is to empower others to accomplish more. If you’re passionate about developing your career, while helping others along the way, come join the Broadridge team. Broadridge is hiring! We are looking for a Technology Financial Operations (FinOps) Analyst to join our team. This is a superb opportunity for a strong Analyst that enjoys working in a fast-paced environment using new technology and solutions! Candidate must push through hurdles and ensure that the various teams stay on track and meet the needs of our clients. Responsibilities: Lead the Public Cloud Finance Operations (FinOps) process, becoming the expert in AWS and Azure hosting costs, drivers and reporting. Collate data from multiple sources and tools to research cost anomalies and provide monthly cost and usage reporting to the business. Collaborate with our Private Cloud Engineering team as a Hardware Asset Manager. Assist the engineering group to manage hardware data, invoice management from our vendors, maintenance, and hardware refresh projects. Develop, maintain, and expand consumption models related to the Public and Private clouds and present the data to the business teams as part of monthly consumption meetings Cross functional collaboration with Tech, Finance and Engineering teams, educating each group with the infrastructure and financial implications – specifically with AWS and hardware costs Identify bottlenecks and opportunities for improvement and make recommendations to management. Manage all your assigned projects assessing risk to timeline, budget, and drive final product to completion Analyze FinOps business cases across headcount, consulting, private and public cloud, and software investments to optimize ROI and business outcomes for each Broadridge business, tracking required approvals through the established prioritization workflow. Support the development and ongoing refinement of the GCIS consumption model across private cloud, software, labor, telecom, and mainframe services to improve cost transparency, enable accurate cost allocation, and inform consumption and investment decisions across Broadridge businesses. Qualifications You'll have a minimum of 5+ years of experience in finOps or Finance. Proficient in developing Power BI models and dashboards Experience with public cloud vendors including but not limited to AWS, Azure, and GCP General understanding of Data Center Hardware and use of lease or capital financial implications Finance experience with planning, forecasting, and consumption models is a plus Proficient in MS-Excel and Power Point Consistent track record for delivering results Good interpersonal skills and experience interacting with clients/users at all levels, singly and in groups. Able to work on multiple tasks at any one time while maintaining high quality standards Bachelor's Degree or equivalent experience Compensation Range: The salary range for this position is between $110,000 to $130,000 USD.

The Role Kyndryl Consult is the fastest growing business within Kyndryl, serving as a trusted advisory partner to enterprises navigating mission-critical transformations. Within Kyndryl Consult, our Value Realization Office practice partners with global organizations to turn complex technology, digital modernization, and artificial intelligence investments into measurable, sustained business outcomes. As enterprises rapidly advance their digital agendas, our team provides the discipline, transparency, and strategic oversight needed to ensure every technology investment delivers verifiable business value. As the Value Realization Lead, you will play a pivotal leadership role building and scaling our Value Realization Office capability while shaping and directing high-impact client engagements. You will focus intensely on artificial intelligence value realization, portfolio management, governance, and agentic AI deployments. In this role, you will guide executive leaders across industries to define what value means for their organizations, prioritize high-potential initiatives, establish accountable governance, and detect value leakage early to make decisive scale, stop, or redirect decisions. You will operate directly across the full engagement lifecycle, working fluidly in both pre-deal advisory and post-deal delivery environments. On a daily basis, you will engage senior client executives—including Chief Information Officers, Chief Digital Officers, and Chief Financial Officers—leading discovery workshops, shaping commercial proposals, and steering multi-disciplinary teams of AI architects, data engineers, and management consultants to implement practical, outcome-driven value governance. At Kyndryl, we value collaboration and teamwork. As a Business Process Consultant, you will work closely with all stakeholders, including customers, project managers, and technical teams, to ensure that our solutions meet the highest standards of quality, efficiency, and effectiveness. Your Future at Kyndryl Key Responsibilities; Client Advisory and Opportunity Shaping You will identify and shape Value Realization Office opportunities across AI, agentic AI, cloud, data, ERP, application modernization, managed services, and enterprise transformation programs. You will lead executive discovery sessions, author strategic proposals, structure commercial agreements, and deliver high-impact presentations to client decision-makers. You will guide clients through value-definition workshops to establish strategic outcomes, value trees, robust business cases, OKRs, KPIs, baselines, targets, and realization timelines. AI Portfolio Governance and Operating Models You will design comprehensive AI portfolio management frameworks covering use case intake, qualification, prioritization, funding, stage gates, dependency management, risk, cost, and pilot-to-scale decisions. You will establish clear Value Realization Office operating models that define decision rights, governance cadences, value-owner accountability, finance and PMO integration, escalation thresholds, and transition into business-as-usual ownership. You will define and monitor outcome and performance metrics for AI use cases, tracking productivity, cost, revenue, cycle time, quality, experience, risk, resilience, token economics, and sustained operational impact. Value Intelligence and Targeted Interventions You will connect financial, delivery, operational, adoption, workforce, risk, and technology data to uncover value leakage and deliver decision-ready portfolio insights rather than routine status reporting. You will lead decisive interventions when value is at risk, recommending reprioritizations, rescoping, adoption actions, funding adjustments, remediation, or the discontinuation of low-value initiatives. You will guide organizations through stage gates and retirement criteria to ensure capital and operational budgets stay focused on high-impact solutions. Capability Building and Collaborative Delivery You will build scalable Value Realization Office capabilities through repeatable methods, maturity models, prioritization frameworks, value taxonomies, templates, dashboards, micro-apps, case studies, delivery standards, and practitioner enablement. You will partner closely with AI architects, data engineers, product leaders, finance, risk, change, workforce, and delivery teams to translate governance requirements into deployable controls, data flows, dashboards, and decision processes. You will mentor consulting practitioners and embed value management habits into client organizations for sustained long-term impact.

Role Responsibilities We are looking for an accomplished Senior Site Reliability Engineer who has built and operated high-volume, always-on platforms where disruption, silent data loss, or incomplete processing can have significant customer, financial, and regulatory consequences. You will bring modern site reliability engineering, distributed-systems, observability, automation, and resilience practices to applications supporting financial-crime prevention and regulatory compliance. Your work will help ensure that critical services remain available in an environment where every single event and data element make a difference. This role combines the scale and complexity of global fintech with the rigor required for high-consequence, regulated systems. Design, implement, and continuously improve the reliability, scalability, performance, and resiliency of Risk and Compliance platforms. Establish and manage service level indicators, service level objectives, error budgets, recovery time objectives, and recovery point objectives for critical services. Build observability solutions that provide visibility into both technical system health and business-processing health , validating that critical transactions, compliance controls, and downstream workflows are received, processed completely, and delivered successfully across the platform ecosystem. Develop monitoring, reconciliation, exception management, replay, recovery, and validation capabilities that help identify incomplete processing, data loss, and workflow failures while strengthening processing integrity and recoverability across distributed systems. Design telemetry, dashboards, logging, distributed tracing, and alerting that accelerate identification of infrastructure, application, dependency, data, and business-process failures. Create alerting strategies aligned to customer, transaction, control, and regulatory impacts rather than relying solely on infrastructure metrics. Automate operational processes and build self-service capabilities that improve diagnosis, recovery, and engineering productivity while reducing operational risk. Lead and support incident response activities, root cause analysis, corrective action planning, and validation that critical business processing has been fully restored. Experience with chaos engineering or controlled failure testing using AWS Fault Injection Service, LitmusChaos, Gremlin, or related approaches. Partner with software engineers, architects, infrastructure teams, cybersecurity teams, data engineers, and business stakeholders to embed reliability, observability, and recoverability into solution design. Execute resiliency testing, capacity testing, and disaster recovery exercises while leveraging AI-assisted engineering capabilities to improve operational effectiveness, diagnosis, and response outcomes. Role Requirements Basic Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience. 7+ years of experience building, operating, or supporting distributed production systems. 5+ years of experience applying Site Reliability Engineering, Production Engineering, Platform Engineering, or similar practices. Experience developing software or automation solutions using Java, Python, or related programming languages. Experience with AWS, Kubernetes, Docker, Helm, and modern containerized platforms. Experience with observability technologies such as Dynatrace, OpenTelemetry, distributed tracing, logging, monitoring, and alerting. Experience supporting distributed systems, event-driven architectures, cloud-native applications, complex service dependencies, and business-critical processing workflows.

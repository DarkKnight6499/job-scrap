The Product Management Associate will play a key role in supporting the growth and management of HarbourVest's global Direct Co-Investment platform. This highly visible position sits at the intersection of investments, fundraising, client engagement, and business strategy, offering broad exposure to product development, portfolio analysis, investor communications, and strategic initiatives. Working closely with Investment, Investor Relations, Solutions, Finance, Legal, Compliance, and Tax teams, you will help drive successful fundraising and deliver best-in-class client servicing across HarbourVest's Direct Co-Investment funds and customized client solutions. Please note that this role is not focused on evaluating and executing Direct Co-Investments. The ideal candidate is someone who: Is an analytical, organized, and creative problem-solver with exceptional attention to detail. Takes ownership of deliverables and consistently produces high-quality work in a fast-paced environment. Can effectively manage multiple projects and competing deadlines with limited supervision. Is a strong communicator who can distill complex fund, portfolio, and investment information into clear and concise materials for a variety of audiences. Enjoys collaborating across teams and building strong relationships with stakeholders globally. Has a data-driven mindset and is motivated to leverage technology, analytics, and process improvements to enhance business operations. Demonstrates sound judgment, professionalism, and discretion when handling sensitive information. Is intellectually curious and interested in developing deeper expertise in private markets and direct co-investments. What you will do: Serve as a key liaison between the Direct Co-Investment investment team and Investor Relations professionals, supporting fundraising, client servicing, and strategic growth initiatives. Support the full lifecycle of Direct Co-Investment products and client relationships, from product development and fundraising through portfolio monitoring, reporting, and ongoing management. Develop and maintain investor-facing materials, including presentations, fund updates, PPMs, DDQs, RFP responses, data rooms, and marketing collateral. Coordinate responses to investor inquiries and due diligence requests, including both quantitative and qualitative analyses. Conduct market, competitor, and portfolio research to support product positioning, fundraising efforts, client engagement, and strategic decision-making. Prepare fund, portfolio, and investment analyses, as well as recurring client, advisory board, and annual meeting materials. Support strategic client and separately managed account initiatives, including prospect research, onboarding, implementation planning, and customized solutions. Monitor fund and portfolio performance, capital activity, liquidity, and portfolio construction metrics to support business management and investor reporting. Drive process improvements and technology-enabled solutions that enhance reporting, scalability, operational efficiency, and data integrity. Contribute to strategic initiatives and business planning efforts that support the continued growth and management of the Direct Co-Investment platform.

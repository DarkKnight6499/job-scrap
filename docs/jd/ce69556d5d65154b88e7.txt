Position Summary: MUFG is seeking a Front Office Sales and Trading Support Specialist to join its Front Office Solutions team in New York. This role is ideal for a motivated technology professional who enjoys working directly with front-office users in a fast-paced trading environment. The successful candidate will provide day-to-day support for electronic trading platforms, assist with regulatory reporting processes, troubleshoot production issues, and collaborate with internal technology teams and external vendors to deliver high-quality solutions to the business. This position provides excellent exposure to Fixed Income and Equity trading workflows, electronic trading systems, and regulatory reporting within a leading global financial institution. Key Responsibilities Provide day-to-day production support for Bloomberg TOMS and related front-office trading applications. Deliver first-line support for Sales & Trading users and Alternative Trading Systems (ATS). Troubleshoot and resolve issues related to platform availability, trade processing, pricing, market data, and position feeds. Partner with technology teams, infrastructure groups, and third-party vendors to investigate and resolve production incidents. Support Equity and Fixed Income regulatory reporting processes. Assist with testing, implementation, and deployment of new platforms, enhancements, and system upgrades. Support integration efforts between vendor platforms and proprietary MUFG systems. Monitor system health, identify process improvements, and contribute to operational excellence initiatives. Create and maintain workflow documentation, support procedures, and technical documentation using Visio, Word, and internal Wiki platforms. Participate in incident management and escalation procedures to ensure timely issue resolution. Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related technical discipline, or equivalent work experience. Strong verbal and written communication skills with the ability to interact effectively with business and technology stakeholders. Experience supporting users in a fast-paced, client-facing, or front-office environment. Understanding of Fixed Income and/or Equity products and associated trade lifecycles. Ability to work independently, prioritize multiple tasks, and learn new technologies quickly. Hands-on experience with Windows and Unix/Linux operating systems. Experience with scripting and automation tools such as Shell (sh), Korn Shell (ksh), Bash, or Batch scripting. Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving abilities. Preferred Qualifications Experience supporting Bloomberg TOMS or similar Order Management Systems (OMS). Knowledge of Equity regulatory reporting requirements, including: CAT (Consolidated Audit Trail) TRF/ORF reporting SEC Rule 606 Knowledge of Fixed Income regulatory reporting, including TRACE. Familiarity with FIX Protocol (FIX 4.2 – 5.0) and electronic trade-routing platforms. Advanced Excel skills, including formulas, reporting, and macros. Experience with Bloomberg products and market data services. Knowledge of Murex (MURE) or similar capital markets platforms. Experience supporting electronic trading, order management, or execution management systems within a financial services environment. .

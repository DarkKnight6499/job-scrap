The Difference You Make: The Associate, Product Management leads and executes strategic product initiatives, delivering high-quality digital products that provide measurable customer and business value. This role partners with stakeholders and Agile delivery teams to define, prioritize, and execute product roadmaps while ensuring solutions align with customer needs, organizational objectives, and technical capabilities. The incumbent owns backlog prioritization, requirements definition, release readiness, and lifecycle management to drive customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, and business growth. Acts as a subject matter expert (SME) on customer needs, industry trends, digital experiences, and product capabilities. Owns and prioritizes the product backlog, ensuring teams focus on delivering the highest-value features and enhancements. Partners with Engineering, Architecture, Operations, Risk, Compliance, Marketing, and Client Services to define and deliver product solutions. Translates customer and business needs into epics, features, user stories, and acceptance criteria. Facilitates Agile ceremonies including backlog refinement, sprint planning, sprint reviews, and release planning. Collaborates with technical teams on API integrations, backend services, platform modernization, and system enhancements. Supports product launches and organizational readiness efforts, ensuring successful adoption across clients and internal teams. Utilizes customer feedback, analytics, and market research to drive prioritization and continuous product improvement. Creates compelling product demonstrations and presentations for leadership, stakeholders, and customers. Build partnerships across business and technology teams to enable successful product delivery and long-term growth. Monitors product performance using KPIs, Agile metrics, and customer insights to guide future roadmap decisions.

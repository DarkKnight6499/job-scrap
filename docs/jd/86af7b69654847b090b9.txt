About The Department Project Sandbox sits within Franklin Templeton's AI Platform and AI Investment Group. The team turns high-value investment ideas into governed, end-to-end prototypes that combine quantitative analytics, agentic AI, deterministic optimization, and live monitoring. The team works closely with investment professionals, product, data, engineering, Legal, Risk and Compliance, Global Investment Solutions, and AI governance stakeholders to ensure that prototypes are useful, explainable, auditable, and aligned with enterprise controls. The initial team is a focused, hands-on pod of software engineers, data scientists and LLM specialists, and a technical lead. The culture is evidence-driven and collaborative: team members challenge assumptions, document limitations, verify AI outputs against source data, and advance work only when the evidence and controls support the next stage. Job Overview This role is the principal technical leader for Project Sandbox, accountable for architecture and evidence standards across quantitative research, agentic AI, portfolio construction, and risk engineering. The Lead - AI Quant Engineer will lead the end-to-end design of multi-agent investment workflows and guide platform and framework choices while remaining hands-on in prototyping, critical implementation, and review. Success means Sandbox can demonstrate technically sound, economically defensible, and governance-ready prototypes with verified data, reproducible research, auditable decisions, explicit limitations, and clear readiness gates. How you will bring value Set the technical architecture, engineering roadmap, and design principles for Project Sandbox across quantitative research, agentic AI, portfolio construction, and risk. Lead multi-agent investment workflows spanning research agents, portfolio decision support, independent risk challenge, deterministic optimization, and live monitoring. Keep risk arithmetic, limits, exposures, and other verifiable quantities deterministic and authoritative; require automated reconciliation, audit trails, and human review for agent decisions. Define research and evaluation standards covering point-in-time data, walk-forward and out-of-sample testing, robustness, transaction costs, leakage, overfitting, LLM groundedness, and failure analysis. Provide technical leadership for portfolio and risk engineering, including factor exposures, beta and hedging, liquidity, VaR, constraints, optimization, attribution, and scenario analysis. Make and document architecture, model, framework, build-versus-buy, integration, cost, latency, reliability, security, and operating-model decisions. Partner across investment, product, data, engineering, architecture, Legal, Risk and Compliance, Global Investment Solutions, and AI governance; present evidence, risks, limitations, and readiness recommendations to senior leaders. Mentor engineers and data scientists, resolve technical impasses, lead code, design, and research reviews, and establish reusable standards and best practices.

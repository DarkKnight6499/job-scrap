Industry/Sector Insurance Specialism Guidewire Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Guidewire Configuration & Integration Consultant, Senior Associate you will develop and customize applications using the Guidewire platform, supporting policy administration, claims management, and billing solutions for insurance clients. As a Senior Associate you will analyze complex problems, manage assigned workstreams, build meaningful client connections, and guide junior team members while deepening your technical skills and understanding of the broader business context within our Advisory practice. In this role at PwC, you will design, code, test, and support software solutions that align with client needs, help integrate business applications, and contribute to work that supports operational efficiency and service delivery. Responsibilities - Designing and configuring Guidewire solutions for policy, billing, and claims workflows - Developing integration components and data flows across insurance application environments - Testing application changes, troubleshooting defects, and validating system behavior across releases - Translating client requirements into technical specifications and workable solution designs - Supporting cloud transformation, core platform modernization, and data and analytics initiatives - Reviewing deliverables from junior team members and providing feedback on technical tasks - Building client relationships and interpreting business context to shape implementation decisions - Applying software development methods and Guidewire tools to solve complex configuration and integration challenges What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 3 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for a Master's or Masters of Business Administration degree in at least one of the following fields of study: Actuarial Science, Business Administration/Management, Business Analytics, Commerce, Computer and Information Science, Computer Applications, Computer Engineering, Computer Engineering & Accounting, Data Processing/Analytics/Science, Economics, Electrical Engineering, Engineering, Engineering and Business, Finance, Information Technology, Management Information Systems, Mathematics, Mechanical Engineering, Operations Management/Research, Risk Management, Software App, Statistics - Demonstrating Guidewire BillingCenter, ClaimCenter, or PolicyCenter configuration skills - Demonstrating Guidewire integration experience across policy, billing, and claims - Applying software development methodology within insurance technology projects - Interpreting data to inform client recommendations and delivery decisions - Navigating ambiguity while anticipating client and team needs - Building client relationships and mentoring junior team members Travel Requirements Up to 60% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $77,000 -

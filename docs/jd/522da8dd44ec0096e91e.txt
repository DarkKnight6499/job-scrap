advanced tools a plus) Draft professional, client‑ready deliverables that clearly tie facts, economics, and tax positions together Assist in the development and implementation of transfer pricing policies and intercompany pricing models for large multinational enterprises Review intercompany legal agreements to ensure consistency with transfer pricing policies and delineation of economic risks between related parties Contribute to AI-enabled process improvements and automation of routine transfer pricing documentation Work directly with senior leaders and clients on complex engagements Mentor junior staff and contribute to team culture and knowledge sharing Essential traits: 3+ years of experience in transfer pricing, international tax, or related field (e.g., Economics, Valuation, Financial Analysis) Strong analytical skills Ability to formulate defensible, economically sound transfer pricing positions around a given set of facts for a business' value chain and global operating structure Clear written and verbal communication skills Interest in developing advisory and client‑facing skills Strong command of Microsoft Excel / modelling Demonstrated experience developing and mentoring staff Certification (e.g., CPA, CFA) or Master’s degree in a relevant field (e.g., Economics, Business, Finance, or Accounting) would be a plus Your recruiter will be happy to walk you through your U.S.-specific benefits, which include: Healthcare Coverage: Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision plans.

Position Summary The Associate Stakeholder Engagement Manager supports strategic public outreach for Florida Delivery and Transmission projects, including new infrastructure and upgrades to existing infrastructure. The role requires advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Teams, SharePoint, and Power BI, along with experience coordinating events and public meetings. Key Responsibilities The Associate Stakeholder Engagement Manager helps execute strategic project outreach and supports Stakeholder Engagement teams and leaders. Responsibilities include preparing and distributing project notifications; developing collateral and job aids; responding to hotline calls and emails; and maintaining project records. The role may also provide direct project support, requiring familiarity with project details, milestones, and key messages. Coordinates public meeting logistics—including venues, materials, and resources—with internal partners such as Government and Community Relations, Corporate Communications, Project Management, and Engineering. The role maintains databases and shared drives to track project activities, events, contacts, and records. The Associate Public Engagement Manager also attends and contributes to departmental and project team meetings, other required meetings, and field reviews as needed.

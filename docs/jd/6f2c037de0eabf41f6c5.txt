About the Role The Mortgage Trader will play a critical role in fulfilling Loomis Sayles’ commitment to deliver superior risk-adjusted returns for clients by identifying and capitalizing on relative value opportunities within the US RMBS universe. This is an exciting opportunity to join a growing product platform with over $27 billion in AUM. About the Team Disciplined Alpha is a 15-member investment team based in Orinda, CA that manages over $27 billion in fixed income assets for institutional clients. The team’s investment process combines deep fundamental research, an intense focus on risk-adjusted relative value, active portfolio rotation, and integrated risk management to deliver consistent alpha to clients. The DA team manages eight different fixed income strategies. Core Disciplined Alpha is the flagship product with over $17 bn in assets and a 16-year track record. Responsibilities Partner with the Portfolio Manager in trading and managing Agency MBS specified pools, TBAs, and CMOs, while contributing to broader portfolio management and investment decision-making activities. Evaluate the Agency MBS universe, prepayment dynamics, and market drivers to identify relative value investment opportunities and generate investment ideas across the MBS market. Assess and communicate market sentiment, liquidity conditions, trading flows, and volatility trends that influence portfolio positioning and risk-taking decisions. Engage and collaborate with Loomis Sayles Mortgage & Structured Finance group. Cultivate and maintain relationships with broker-dealers to source market intelligence, trading opportunities, and research insights, translating market developments into actionable investment recommendations. Drive relative value analysis and risk assessment efforts as a member of the Disciplined Alpha Team, contributing directly to investment selection, portfolio construction, and risk management decisions. Represent the team through regular travel to Boston, meetings with broker-dealers in New York, and participation in industry conferences to enhance market insight and expand professional networks.

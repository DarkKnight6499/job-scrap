Industry/Sector EUR X-Sector Specialism Operations Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Commodities Trading Systems Consultant Senior Associate you will support client efforts to improve sourcing and procurement processes within our Operations Consulting practice, bringing together business insight, systems knowledge, and advisory skills to help shape practical solutions. As a Senior Associate you will analyze complex problems, manage assigned workstreams, build meaningful client connections, mentor junior team members, and navigate ambiguity while deepening your technical skills and understanding of the broader business context. In this role at PwC, you will help clients evaluate commodities trading systems such as Allegro, RightAngle, and Endur/Openlink, identify process improvement opportunities, and develop approaches that support operational efficiency, supplier collaboration, and supply chain resilience. Responsibilities - Analyzing client sourcing and procurement processes to identify opportunities for cost reduction, supplier optimization, and workflow improvement - Supporting sourcing strategy development and source-to-pay transformation efforts across commodities trading systems engagements - Configuring and maintaining commodities trading platforms such as Allegro, RightAngle, and Endur or Openlink to support client operating needs - Translating business requirements into system functionality, process designs, and implementation tasks for procurement technology solutions - Reviewing vendor selection, contract negotiation, and contractual compliance monitoring activities to support procurement governance - Applying e-procurement tools, digital tooling, and procurement software to streamline transaction processing and reporting - Interpreting demand management data, financial metrics, and supplier relationship information to inform recommendations and action plans - Coordinating project resource management activities and tracking deliverables across assigned workstreams -

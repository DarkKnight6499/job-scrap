Essential Job Functions and Responsibilities Develops and leads the implementation of technical training programs tailored to the needs of the underwriting team, with a focus on product-specific knowledge. Support the development, implementation, and ongoing refinement of underwriting strategy, guidelines, and product frameworks to ensure profitable growth and consistent risk selection. Leads and facilitates engaging and effective training sessions, ensuring that all underwriters understand and can apply product-specific underwriting guidelines. Updates training materials to reflect changes in underwriting guidelines, regulatory requirements, industry best practices, and product features. Develop and enhance Alternative Markets insurance products through the design and maintenance of proprietary forms, rates, rules, endorsements and coverage solutions that support divisional objectives, profitable growth, and competitive differentiation. Design and implement innovative insurance solutions tailored to specialty programs and niche markets aligned with underwriting objectives, market demands, and the Alternative Markets risk appetite. Provides regular feedback and coaching to underwriters based on assessment results, leading to measurable improvements in performance.

Roku is looking for a Senior Financial Analyst to join the Devices Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) team. The Devices FP&A team provides broad exposure to senior leadership and key decision makers across the company. This role will play a critical role in supporting Roku's International growth, helping shape the financial strategy behind Roku's global Players business. What is the role? This Senior Financial Analyst, International Players & Devices role will serve as a strategic finance partner to international business leaders, country leads, Sales Operations, Accounting, Retail Strategy, and cross-functional teams. This role will own the financial planning and performance management cadence for International Players across regions, translating operational inputs into clear insights, risks, opportunities, and recommendations for leadership. The successful candidate will connect regional units, pricing, promotions, marketing, product economics, operating expenses, and P&L outcomes into a coherent view of the business and help leaders make better decisions with that view. This position requires strong analytical acumen, the ability to manage multiple workstreams across geographies, and a collaborative approach to working with diverse stakeholders. The ideal candidate thrives in fast-paced environments and brings a track record of delivering high-quality financial analysis and reporting that drives strategic outcomes.

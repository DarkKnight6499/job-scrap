Industry/Sector Banking and Capital Markets Specialism Data, Analytics & AI Management Level Senior Manager Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Financial Risk Management - Risk Data Transformation Senior Manager you will lead data and analytics efforts that help clients improve risk reporting, data quality, and decision support across financial risk functions. As a Senior Manager you will apply sound judgment, guide complex work, and shape outcomes through clear communication, coaching, and strategic thinking while developing teams and supporting client conversations. In this role at PwC, you will focus on transforming risk data into usable insights, bringing structure to complex data environments, and helping teams turn business needs into practical analytics solutions within our Advisory practice. Responsibilities - Leading risk data transformation initiatives across banking and capital markets client engagements - Advising clients on data, analytics, and technology approaches that modernize financial risk management processes - Managing complex workstreams involving capital, liquidity, credit risk, and asset-liability management data - Developing analytics and reporting solutions that support regulatory change, risk monitoring, and decision-making - Applying systems thinking to identify data quality gaps, process inefficiencies, and transformation opportunities - Guiding teams in building scalable data models, dashboards, and automated reporting capabilities - Validating outputs with clients and adjusting deliverables based on feedback and changing business needs - Coaching team members through ambiguous situations while driving progress across multiple priorities - Communicating clear, compelling findings that connect technical analysis to business impact - Modeling professional and technical standards while contributing to diverse, inclusive team performance What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 7 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for at least one of the following fields of study: Accounting, Analytics/Data Science, Biology/Biomedical Sciences, Business Administration/Management, Computer Science/Information Systems, Economics, Engineering, Finance, Financial Mathematics/Quantitative Finance, Health Administration/Public Health, Mathematics/Statistics, Operations/Supply Chain, Project/Technology Management, Risk Management/Insurance, Science - Demonstrating financial risk data transformation experience within banking - Applying data analytics and visualization skills to risk reporting - Translating complex risk and regulatory data into clear stories -

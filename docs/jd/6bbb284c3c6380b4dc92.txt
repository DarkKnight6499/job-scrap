Private Equity Group J.P. The team manages diversified commingled funds, evergreen products and customized separate accounts investing in all stages and types of private equity including buyout and venture capital across partnerships, secondaries, and co-investments. Our global client base includes pension plans, insurance companies, endowments & foundations, as well as family offices, trusts, and high-net worth individuals. Job Summary The Private Equity Group is seeking a Full-Time Associate to join its Information Management team. The need for an associate is a function of growth in the business and the successful candidate will perform similar functions to existing members in the same position. The associate will require strong quantitative skills and/or a desire to learn advanced Excel techniques for both maintaining and improving portfolio and performance analytics across a range of data and information sources. The associate will be responsible for servicing client needs and supporting the portfolio managers responsible for investing the clients’ assets, which will include evaluating information contained in correspondence from underlying investments, coordinating investment activity, performing reconciliations and overseeing the clients/portfolios service providers (for example, the custodian), and preparing communications, including financial reports, for the clients’ use. The position requires a strong understanding of financial/accounting/tax practices and will also include support in building and creating presentation materials, reviewing investment valuations and financial analysis. The associate is required to have strong PowerPoint, Excel, financial reporting and writing skills, as well as strong attention to detail.

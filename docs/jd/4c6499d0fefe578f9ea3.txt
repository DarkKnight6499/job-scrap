About The Opportunity We are seeking a Sr. Associate, Procurement Systems to own the configuration, administration, and continuous improvement of our procurement technology environment. This role will serve as the functional owner of procurement system workflows, configurations, controls, and integrations, partnering closely with Procurement, Finance, Legal, IT, Security, and business stakeholders. The ideal candidate combines strong procurement process knowledge with systems expertise and a control-oriented mindset. You will translate business and compliance requirements into scalable system configurations, ensure appropriate procurement controls are embedded in workflows, and continuously optimize the user experience and operational efficiency of the procurement process.

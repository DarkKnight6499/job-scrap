Position Summary Supports Duke Energy’s corporate financing activities through the analysis, execution, and administration of financing initiatives, regulatory proceedings, and select capital markets activities. The role prepares and leads financial analyses and cross-functional work related to securitizations, tax-exempt financings, governmental authority loan programs, Treasury rate case witness support, and other strategic financing opportunities, and prepares related presentations for leadership review. Works cross-functionally across the broader Treasury organization and with Regulatory, Accounting, Tax, Legal, business units, and external stakeholders to support sound financial decision-making, maintain financial flexibility, and optimize and diversify the Company’s funding sources. The role also provides analytical bench strength and transaction support for debt and equity capital markets activities, working horizontally across Treasury with the Credit and Capital Markets team. Level Differentiation Senior Analyst Scope and autonomy: Supports and independently manages assigned analyses, schedules, and workstreams, coordinating with stakeholders as needed. Regulatory Work: Supports assigned rate case activities, including: filing schedules and discovery responses. Financing work: Supports financial analysis and execution of strategic financing initiatives. Judgment and leadership: Applies sound analytical judgment, escalates issues, and contributes to team objectives. Lead Analyst Scope and autonomy: Independently leads complex analyses and cross-functional initiatives, identifies issues, develops recommendations, and drives deliverables to completion. Regulatory Work: Serves as the Corporate Finance lead for assigned rate case activities, including testimony development. Independent coordination of Treasury deliverables and cross functional responses. Applies best practices across jurisdictions. Leads financial analysis, evaluates financial and customer impacts, develops financing alternatives, and supports execution. Operates effectively in ambiguous environments, validates model outputs, coordinates senior stakeholders, and influences decisions without direct authority. Key Responsibilities Regulatory Finance and Rate Case Support Support or lead assigned rate case activities, including development of minimum filing requirement schedules, intervenor discovery responses, Treasury deliverables, and cross-functional responses. Draft and support testimony related to financing, capital structure, cost of capital, and other corporate finance matters. Coordinate responses across Treasury and other Finance organizations to promote consistency and accuracy. Provide hearing preparation and on-call support during active regulatory proceedings. Strategic Financing and Special Projects Support or lead financial analysis and execution of strategic financing initiatives, including securitizations, tax-exempt financings, and alternative financing structures. Evaluate financial and customer impacts associated with emerging opportunities, capital investment strategies, and special projects. Monitor markets and develop financing alternatives and recommendations regarding evolving capital markets trends. Support analysis of financing for large infrastructure investments, special-purpose vehicles, and potential customer-sponsored financing arrangements. Securitization Financing Support analysis and execution of storm recovery and other securitization transactions. Develop financing analyses and financial models; assess whether model outputs are reasonable. Coordinate with Regulatory, Legal, Financial Accounting and Analysis, Accounting, Tax, investment banks, and consultants to support transaction execution. Leadership and Project Management Coordinate cross-functional initiatives involving Treasury, Regulatory, Tax, Financial Planning and Analysis, Accounting, Legal, and business unit personnel. At the Lead level, independently lead complex initiatives, coordinate stakeholders, identify issues, and drive deliverables to completion. Tax-Exempt Financing Programs Support planning and execution of multiple tax-exempt financing transactions and related inducement resolutions. Identify and evaluate new tax-exempt financing opportunities and preserve financing options. Governmental Authority Loan Programs Serve as a Corporate Finance liaison supporting efforts related to governmental authority financing programs. Coordinate financing draw requests, liquidity planning, financing analyses, and administration requirements. Develop forecasts for draw requests and related planning. Project Finance and Transaction and Risk Committee Support Develop financial analyses requested by Transaction and Risk Committee Corporate Review Team points of contact. Develop and maintain financial models used to evaluate project economics and regulatory lag. Capital Markets Support Provide ad hoc analytical and transaction support for debt capital markets, equity capital markets, and interest rate hedging activities in collaboration with the Director, Credit and Capital Markets, and the Assistant Treasurer. Prepare periodic Treasury position, SEC, and Investor Relations reporting requirements. Draft financing authorization applications filed with utility regulatory commissions. Assist with external relationships, including banking partners, investors, credit rating agencies, and outside counsel. Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications.

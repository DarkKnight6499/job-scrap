Corporate Development Associate
D. E. Shaw
Location: New York
Link: https://www.deshaw.com/careers/corporate-development-associate-6065

The D. E. Shaw group seeks a talented associate to join its Corporate Development team. This team is responsible for proposing, developing, and executing high-priority initiatives that drive the firm's growth, such as new investment strategies, businesses, products, and other commercial activities. This role is focused on developing creative and rigorous proposals for growing the firm, translating strategic ideas into structured plans, and delivering high-quality outcomes, all within a collaborative and intellectually rigorous environment. Propose, research, and execute new business ideas. Coordinate and manage critical projects across the firm. Work with various teams at the firm to generate and develop new products. Contribute to corporate strategy and regularly present recommendations to senior management. A bachelor's or graduate degree, an impressive record of academic or professional achievement, and up to three years of work experience. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to distill complexity into clear, actionable recommendations and updates for senior management. Previous finance knowledge or experience is not required—past Corporate Development associates have come from fields including law, engineering, basic science research, philosophy, computer science, and government. The expected annual base salary for this position is $135,000 to $160,000. Our compensation and benefits package includes variable compensation in the form of a year-end bonus, guaranteed in the first year of hire, and benefits including medical and prescription drug coverage, 401(k) contribution matching, wellness reimbursement, family building benefits, and a charitable gift match program.

We believe the greatest impact comes from combining technological capabilities with human expertise, judgment, and accountability Group Details TARA is Tradeweb's AI product for market data and trading analysis, expanding across asset classes globally and into additional trading workflows. We are seeking an AI Product Operations Associate to manage the day-to-day client service and operational processes that support TARA’s growth. You will help clients get access, resolve routine issues, coordinate internal follow-up, and keep users informed. Working closely with TARA Product, Sales, Support, Technology, and the Technical Project Managers, you will take operational requests through to completion and maintain clear records of outstanding issues and next steps. This role offers direct client exposure and the opportunity to take ownership of new operational processes as TARA expands. Job Responsibilities Serve as the first point of contact for TARA user questions, access problems, and application issues. Capture relevant details, resolve routine requests using documented procedures, and escalate issues with clear reproduction steps. Coordinate client onboarding, including approval requests, ServiceNow tickets, permissions checks, and confirmation that users can launch TARA. Track open requests and incidents across internal teams, follow up on blockers, and keep clients and Sales informed through resolution. Coordinate standard risk and governance questionnaires, approved responses, and legal acknowledgements with Product, Legal, and Cyber Risk. Maintain onboarding guides, support procedures, FAQs, and operational records. Help coordinate release communications and user readiness checks. Prepare demo access and meeting logistics, distribute approved materials, and follow up on routine client questions and outstanding requests. Review existing usage and operational dashboards, identify missing reports or alerts, and route issues to the appropriate technical owner. Summarize client activity for Sales and Product. Record client feedback and recurring issues for Product review and improve operational processes as TARA grows. Qualifications Bachelor’s or master’s degree in Business Administration, Finance, Economics, Information Systems, or a related field required. 1–3 years of experience in client service, onboarding, product operations, application support, or a similar role, ideally within financial services or trading technology. Strong organization, attention to detail, and consistent follow-through across multiple requests and internal teams. Clear written and verbal communication, sound judgment on escalation, and confidence working directly with clients. Comfort learning applications, ticketing systems, dashboards, and access workflows. Familiarity with fixed income or electronic trading is helpful. Interest in AI products and willingness to learn TARA's capabilities and limitations. Coding experience is helpful but is not required.

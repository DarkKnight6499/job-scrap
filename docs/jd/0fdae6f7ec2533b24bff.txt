Responsible for driving faster credit decisions, consistent underwriting outcomes, portfolio quality, client experience, operational efficiency, and organizational performance across a centralized portfolio management structure. Oversees a team of Portfolio Managers, Team Leads, and specialized underwriting resources while ensuring alignment with Commercial Banking, Risk Management, and Transformation objectives. Key Accountabilities Strategic Leadership Lead execution and ongoing enhancement of the Express Channel operating model for Commercial Banking relationships below $10MM TCR. Drive achievement of strategic Project Freestyle objectives, including simplification, standardization, optimization, and automation of credit delivery processes. Develop and execute workforce, organizational design, and capacity management strategies to support growth and portfolio transitions. Partner with Commercial Banking, Risk, Operations, Product, and Technology leaders to advance future-state credit delivery capabilities. Lead change management activities supporting portfolio migration, organizational transition, and operating model adoption. Portfolio Management & Credit Leadership Maintain overall accountability for portfolio health, credit quality, risk management, and regulatory compliance across the Express portfolio. Ensure consistent application of Express credit box standards, underwriting requirements, covenant structures, and documentation practices. Monitor portfolio trends, concentration risks, covenant performance, and asset quality indicators. Escalate emerging credit issues and implement corrective action strategies as required. Exercise lending authority and provide oversight of delegated credit decisions consistent with established credit qualifications. Ensure disciplined execution of annual reviews, renewals, monitoring activities, and portfolio surveillance processes. Underwriting & Client Delivery Deliver a scalable underwriting model designed to support a three-business-day target for qualifying new-money transactions. Oversee dedicated Express underwriting resources and client acquisition portfolio managers supporting new originations. Drive service excellence through efficient credit decisioning and a simplified client experience. Ensure consistency in deal structures, covenant frameworks, documentation standards, and approval processes. Partner with Relationship Managers to facilitate growth, improve client satisfaction, and deepen client relationships. Transformation & Process Excellence Champion automation initiatives including AI-supported underwriting, Deal Eval, AURA, automated data population, and streamlined monitoring processes. Lead implementation of simplified annual review practices and standardized underwriting methodologies. Establish performance metrics, SLAs, quality measures, and productivity benchmarks across the organization. Identify and implement continuous improvement opportunities to reduce process variation, manual effort, and operational complexity. Support enterprise transformation programs impacting commercial credit delivery. Talent Leadership Lead, coach, and develop Team Leads, Portfolio Managers, and specialized underwriting professionals. Build a high-performing, client-focused culture emphasizing accountability, risk discipline, innovation, and continuous improvement. Support talent planning, succession management, and development of future credit leaders. Partner with CADP and Talent Development teams to build a sustainable talent pipeline.

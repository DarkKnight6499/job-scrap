Work you’ll do: We are seeking experienced SAP Treasury leaders to help drive market growth, lead complex client engagements, and bring deep subject matter expertise across treasury strategy, process transformation, and SAP Treasury solution delivery. As a Senior Manager – SAP Treasury, you will focus on developing business opportunities, strengthening client relationships, and leading advisory services, solution design, and delivery for SAP treasury transformation and implementation initiatives. Responsibilities include: Serve as a trusted subject matter expert in SAP Treasury, including SAP Cash Management, SAP In-House Cash, SAP TRM, and banking/connectivity. Lead client pursuits, shape solution strategy, and support proposal development and client presentations. Own and deliver complex SAP Treasury engagements from assessment and design through build, testing, cutover, go-live, and stabilization. Lead, coach, and develop teams across multiple workstreams, fostering accountability and execution excellence. Advise clients on treasury operating model design, process improvement, and transformation opportunities to enhance their technology deployment and user experience. Serve as a senior advisor across multiple concurrent engagements, driving quality, consistency, and successful delivery outcomes. Contribute to eminence, marketplace activation, and practice development efforts. Develop tools, assets, and accelerators that strengthen the practice and enhance delivery consistency. Apply strong project economics skills, including staffing, forecasting, delivery governance, and risk management. The Team: Our Controllership and Treasury Transformation offering modernizes the finance function to align with the evolving business objectives of organizations. We collaborate with CFOs, CAOs, controllers, and treasurers to develop an efficient, high-quality, data-driven, and tech-enabled controllership and treasury function. This empowers our clients to deliver greater value to their business partners and investors.

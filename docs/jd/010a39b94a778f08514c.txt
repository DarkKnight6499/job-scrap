Summary: The Associate Director, US Access Strategy - Psychiatry is responsible for the design and implementation of market access tactics that deliver on the Cobenfy access Strategy as defined by the Neuroscience Access Strategy Lead. You will partner closely with Cobenfy brand & indication leads to design and implement strategy and monitor metrics with matrix partners (Brand Marketing, Trade/Dispensing, Field Sales, Field Access, HEOR and Reimbursement teams, Commercialization Analytics). This individual will lead development and execution of plans and resources and will play an integral role for Cobenfy Access Strategy team, leading matrix collaboration and managing agency partners to successfully execute access strategy and tactics. This role is aligned to Cobenfy as a core brand team member, however, reports to US MAx directly as part of the Neuroscience Access Strategy organization. Responsibilities: Lead and foster collaboration with cross-matrix partners (marketing, HEOR, sales, analytics, access/reimbursement, medical affairs, etc.) to execute a comprehensive, best-in-class Access Strategy in our psychiatry indications. Coordinate efforts with Sales and Brand Team, AEs, BI&A, and Pricing & Contracting to develop pull through targeting, messages and tools to support brand launch/growth: Identifying critical design elements of planned tactics to accelerate desired behavioral changes Coordination of operational planning for payer marketing plan and sales meetings in accordance with overall US Cobenfy strategy Innovative design of tactics informed by Market Research, Commercialization Analytics and customer facing access and brand teams Development/ownership of tactics accomplished in partnership with MAx & brand teams, PReP team and external partners Roll-out of tactics in partnership with field-based teams (eg. Sales, PALs, AEs) directly or via POA/launch meetings Manage agency and other vendors in development of promotional materials and tactics Contribute to the development and implementation of the annual brand planning and functional planning processes Review of all payer-related communications including serving as brand primary point person for Account Executives and Sales Manage Op Ex budget to ensure effective execution of tactics are aligned with brand plan Collaborate with training team(s) to develop training and support MAx team on training, BMS tools and resources, market intel, policy landscape, etc., to ensure team is equipped to engage key customers on access topics. Qualifications: BA/BS degree or equivalent. MBA and/or PharmD preferred 4 to 7 years of relevant marketing / brand management experience, customer-facing role in Market Access or relevant market access functional experience (eg. Pricing & Contracting, Reimbursement, Policy) Experience working in psychiatry or other serious mental illness categories a plus Working knowledge of US government payor space required Exceptional interpersonal and communication skills to effectively and efficiently drive performance within a matrix organization, and form strong, productive partnerships Demonstrated ability to work across boundaries and partner with various stakeholders to build consensus and achieve results Experience and knowledge of promotional regulatory processes.

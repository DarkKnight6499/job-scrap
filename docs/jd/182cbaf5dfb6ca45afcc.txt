Associate, Accounting - Commercial Controllers This Accounting Associate role will support Capital One’s Commercial Broker Dealers registered with the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA) and the Securities Exchange Commission (SEC). Candidates should have the ability to manage priorities and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment. Candidates must be able to develop strong relationships with key business partners. Additionally, candidates should possess the ability to identify process improvement and technology options and assist in implementation of automation solutions. Responsibilities: Prepare journal entries and monthly reconciliations Perform month end closing and financial reporting processes timely and accurately Manage accounts payable for business and interface with Enterprise Supplier Management Prepare and present monthly, quarterly and other ad hoc analytics for line of business, Accounting, and Finance leadership Support quarterly SEC and Regulatory Reporting as well as other ad hoc reporting requests Interact directly with Finance and key business partners, associates throughout the controller’s group, tax, and external and internal auditors Participate in challenging project initiatives with the team. Support automation and data transformation efforts Identify and drive process efficiencies. Ability to obtain Series 99 license within one year of joining the team Basic Qualifications Bachelor's degree At least 1 year of experience in accounting, finance, securities or a combination Preferred Qualifications: Master’s degree in Finance or Accounting 2+ years of accounting or securities industry experience or a combination Financial Industry Regulatory Authority Series License 7, or 10, or 24, or 27, or 99 or any combination At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position.

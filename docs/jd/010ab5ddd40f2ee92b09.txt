The Opportunity The Associate Analyst, Analytics primary responsibilities will include extensive data-based analysis to monitor portfolio credit risk across channels. The ideal candidate must be passionate about delivering results, must be an innovative and strategic thinker, willing to challenge status quo, can learn quickly, is self-motivated, detail oriented, and can sift through voluminous amounts of information to pinpoint which items are relevant to understanding Business results. They will be able to apply knowledge of business and balanced judgment in interpretation of data (analytics). The Work Itself As a part of the Governance and Early Delinquency Portfolio Monitoring team, your responsibilities will include: Support extensive data-based analysis to identify and recommend opportunities targeted towards portfolio monitoring and managing credit risk across channels. To provide timely and accurate analysis that drives business objectives and goals. Ability to Perform analysis to drive change within the business Identify, quantify, and propose expansion/curtailment recommendations Support analytics projects which require technical skills in SQL/Python and the ability to navigate data ambiguity Perform ad hoc analysis and quickly provide results and recommendations Engage in a culture of Diversity and Inclusion The Qualifications We’re Looking For Preferred Qualifications : Bachelor degree preferred Knowledge of consumer financing industry (e.g., auto, card, mortgage etc.). Demonstrate a solid understanding of data mining and analytical techniques. Candidate should possess outstanding analytical and problem-solving skills. Practical experience working with large datasets with SAS and/or SQL or appropriate coursework in lieu of this experience. Quick learner and has demonstrated ability to initiate, develop and execute small to medium scale projects. Work well within a team of analysts and collaborate with individuals with technical and non-technical backgrounds. Candidate must have strong attention to detail. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to effectively present material.

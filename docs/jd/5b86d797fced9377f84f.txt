As a Principal Financial Analyst, you will serve as a strategic finance partner to the Cardiac Diagnostics & Services Marketing organization. You will provide financial planning, forecasting, reporting, and analytical support to business leaders while delivering insights that support commercial strategies, investment decisions, and operational execution. Primary Responsibilities Partner with Marketing, Strategy, and Innovation leadership to provide financial analysis and decision support. Lead financial planning, forecasting, annual operating plan (AOP), strategic planning, and integrated business planning activities. Execute month-end and quarter-end close activities, including reporting, variance analysis, and performance reviews. Develop financial models to evaluate business performance, market trends, gross margin initiatives, and strategic investments. Analyze profit and loss (P&L) results, identify risks and opportunities, and provide recommendations to improve financial performance. Support business reviews and leadership presentations through consolidated reporting, analytics, and financial insights. Evaluate investment opportunities, project costs, and business initiatives while providing recommendations to support resource allocation decisions. Drive process improvements that enhance forecasting accuracy, reporting efficiency, and financial decision support. Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree and 7+ years of finance or accounting experience OR advanced degree and 5+ years of finance or accounting experience. Experience in financial planning, forecasting, budgeting, financial reporting, and variance analysis. Experience developing financial models and translating financial data into business insights. Experience partnering with business leaders to support strategic and operational decision-making. Preferred Qualifications Master of Business Administration (MBA) or Certified Public Accountant (CPA) designation. Experience supporting commercial, marketing, strategy, or business development organizations. Experience with SAP, OneStream, Power BI, or similar financial reporting and planning systems. Knowledge of United States Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP), capital policies, or project accounting practices. Experience working within a global or highly matrixed organization. For Baccalaureate degrees earned outside of the United States, a degree that satisfies the requirements of 8 C.F.R. § 214.2(h)(4)(iii)(A) is required.

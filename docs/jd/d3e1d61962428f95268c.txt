Regular or Temporary: Regular Language Fluency: English (Required) Work Shift: 1st shift (United States of America) Please review the following job description: Partner with Relationship Managers to support a portfolio of middle-market, specialty, and/or commercial clients. The role is responsible for financial analysis, client relationship development, and coordination of treasury, lending, and capital solutions. The Associate I supports prospecting, developing pitch materials, and servicing; owns basic tasks and client follow-ups within defined processes and review. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Following is a summary of the essential functions for this job. Other duties may be performed, both major and minor, which are not mentioned below. Specific activities may change from time to time. 1. Conduct industry and company research to source prospect names and refine lists in coordination with the coverage team and partners 2. Develop standard pitch materials and compile industry/economic data following defined templates. 3. Evaluate client financial performance by performing preliminary review of financial statements, cash flow, and key performance metrics to support banker recommendations, identify growth opportunities, and deliver tailored banking solutions. 4. Prepare talking points and contribute to internal calls (e.g., org calls, credit screens); may present assigned sections with coaching. 5. Compile industry-specific content for internal and external purposes. 6. Take ownership of basic client follow-up activities within defined SLAs and with supervisor review as needed. 7. Track calling and pipeline activity; reinforce proper use of CRM with the coverage team; produce hygiene reports as requested. 8. Develop enhanced understanding of the market, credit, and products; build relationship management skills with guidance. 9. Demonstrate increasing knowledge of assigned industry; proactively contribute ideas and grow comfort with client interaction. 10. Effectively work with partners and teammates with guidance; take ownership of discrete tasks within a defined workplan. 11. Contribute perspectives around industry initiatives that support business growth; escalate risks or documentation gaps to RM/Specialist.

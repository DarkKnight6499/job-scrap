Associate Product Manager, Data Governance
Toast
Location: Remote, US
Link: https://careers.toasttab.com/jobs?gh_jid=8305972

Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy. The Enterprise Data Solutions (EDS) team at Toast is on a mission to deliver a trusted, scalable, and governed data foundation that empowers every team to make decisions with speed and confidence. We are seeking an Associate Product Manager to join our Enterprise Data Solutions team. In this role, you will be a key contributor to the operational execution and strategic evolution of our Enterprise Data Governance Program. You will play a critical role in making Toast's data trustworthy, compliant, and discoverable as the company scales its AI and data capabilities. A day in the life (Responsibilities) Measurement: Stand up the dashboards to monitor that data requirements are being adhered to across the organization. Design & Strategy: Support the facilitation of the Data Governance Council working sessions and act as a Subject Matter Expert for the enterprise Business Glossary. Automation & Scalability: Partner with the data catalog team to streamline metadata standards, automating data discovery across business platforms and analytics tools. Governance & Documentation: Develop comprehensive governance documentation, stewardship training materials, and SOPs to ensure domains scale with consistent practices. Continuous Improvement: Research emerging data management frameworks and AI governance best practices to proactively enhance enterprise data policies. Platform Health: Take ownership of governance dashboards, proactive compliance monitoring, and data quality tracking to maintain overall data foundation reliability. Stakeholder Collaboration: Work closely with cross-functional partners (e.g., Engineering, Analytics, Legal) to translate business definitions into technical metadata specifications. What you'll need to thrive (Requirements) Years of Experience: 2+ years of professional experience in data governance, data management, product management, or analytics. Technical Mastery: Proven experience with modern data platforms (Snowflake, dbt) and data catalogs (DataHub, Alation, Atlan), specifically focused on enterprise metadata and stewardship deployments. Implementation: Demonstrated ability to implement data governance frameworks, business glossaries, and data domain models for core enterprise applications. Problem Solving: Solid understanding of industry standards and best practices within data management, metrics standardization, and metadata curation. Tools: Experience with SQL for governance reporting, analytics, and data quality analysis. Communication: Excellent analytical and verbal skills to communicate complex governance policies and lead cross-functional workshops across technical and non-technical audiences. What will help you stand out (Nonessential/Nice to Haves) Certifications: Certified Data Management Professional (CDMP) or active coursework toward certification. Domain Knowledge: Familiarity with compliance frameworks (e.g., SOC 2, GDPR, CCPA) or data management frameworks like DAMA-DMBOK. Breadth: Experience with adjacent tools and frameworks such as data observability platforms (Monte Carlo, Great Expectations) or AI governance models.

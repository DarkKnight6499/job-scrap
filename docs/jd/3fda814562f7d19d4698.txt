What You’ll Do: The Fund Finance Associate supports the Fund and Lender Finance team and provides ongoing portfolio management support in connection with the team’s lending activities. The role works closely with senior investment professionals across the full transaction lifecycle, from opportunity evaluation and due diligence through structuring, credit approval, execution, and ongoing portfolio management. The successful candidate will have skills in credit and market analysis including cash flow analysis and modeling, capitalization, industry research, credit policy and legal documentation, among others, in analyzing and summarizing the creditworthiness of clients and prospects. Responsibilities will also include preparing credit approval memos; performing annual loan reviews; and providing support to the team’s loan syndication efforts. Collaborate with internal partners including Senior Relationship Managers / Client Coverage; Credit; Loan Operations; Treasury Services; AML/KYC teams. This position is intended to be a developmental role for early career individuals seeking a career in Portfolio Management; but may also be constructive for a colleague seeking a path to Relationship Management, Credit, or many other Commercial roles within the Bank. Responsibilities Gather and analyze financial and business information to determine creditworthiness for Fund Finance credit products. Complete financial spreads, risk rating, compliance testing, and other portfolio monitoring & maintenance activities. Provide underwriting support to Bankers / RMs when required. This may include financial statement analysis, collateral analysis, financial modeling, industry and business risk evaluation and management assessment. Conduct credit underwriting and due diligence on prospective transactions, including meetings with portfolio company management teams and private equity sponsors. Build operating and LBO-style models to test borrower performance under a range of operating and capital structure scenarios. Contribute to credit approval memos describing the borrower’s business, industry dynamics, and financial results, and present recommendations to senior professionals and credit committees. Support ongoing monitoring of existing exposures, including quarterly financial reviews, covenant compliance, amendment requests, liquidity assessments, risk rating recommendations, and valuations. Performs other duties as assigned. What Do You Need? 3-5 years of banking experience in private credit. High level of attention to detail Bachelor’s Degree required (Finance, Accounting, or related field) Prior Lender Finance or Fund Finance experience Background in investment banking, transaction advisory, or commercial banking is also valued; a formal commercial credit training program is a plus Strong analytical skills and sound judgment Ability to manage several transactions at once in a fast-paced environment Builds effective working relationships with colleagues across the Bank Must haves Detail oriented Financial modeling skills Familiar w/lender finance deals, fund finance deals is a plus Strong credit skills Strong communication skills This role can potentially advance to a PM role Technology Skills: Ability to work with the Microsoft Suite and learn/work with other Customers Bank’s applications. 2 or more years demonstrated proficiency in Microsoft Office suite (advanced Excel skills for financial modeling in particular) Familiarity with AI and automation tools that support productivity, workflow efficiency, and client engagement.

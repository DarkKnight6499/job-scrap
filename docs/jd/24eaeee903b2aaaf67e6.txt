When clients seek to raise capital in the private markets to support their next phase of growth, Alternative Capital Solutions provides innovative structuring, market insight, and execution expertise. Sitting within Global Capital Markets, the team focuses on privately placed equity and equity-linked capital raises , including growth capital, structured equity transactions, and PIPE financings , working closely with industry bankers, equity sales & trading, private wealth management, and other product partners to deliver integrated solutions for the world’s leading private and public companies. Alternative Capital Solutions advises clients on complex capital structure and financing ambitions, combining market judgment and transaction execution to meet strategic objectives across market cycles. Our New York City office is looking for an Analyst or Associate to join the Alternative Capital Solutions team.

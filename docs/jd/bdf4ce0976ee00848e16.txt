The Global Liquidity Product Team in GPS is responsible for managing over $600B of corporate and commercial deposits across ~40 different currencies. The team covers deposit strategy, deposit pricing, deposit appetite, product development and product management - including sweeps, zero balance accounts, physical cash concentration and multi-currency notional pooling. The team also supports large/complex liquidity deals, drives liquidity thought leadership, analyzes the impact of monetary policy on wholesale banking and leads the GPS response to regulatory changes/inquiries. The North America Liquidity Product Management team is responsible for delivering innovative liquidity solutions that help clients optimize cash positioning, improve operational efficiency, and manage working capital across complex domestic and international structures. This role serves as a Product Leader within North America Liquidity Product Management and owns the Global Liquidity Platform, the primary liquidity platform supporting the global team. The role is accountable for the platform strategy and roadmap, the relationship with Technology, and identifying opportunities to integrate capabilities across the global liquidity product suite where doing so creates client or business value.

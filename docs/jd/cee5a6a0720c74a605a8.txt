Your Role At Baxter The Documentation Associate I provides support for document and change control activities to help ensure accurate and timely product and system releases. This role assists with reviewing, processing, maintaining, and filing controlled documents according to established procedures. The Documentation Associate I supports daily documentation operations, responds to routine requests and questions, assists with audits and change control activities, and participates in project and process improvement teams as needed. This position is responsible for managing Iron Mountain/Records 365 archival and assisting other Document personnel as necessary. What You'll Be Doing Document Configuration & Product Data Management Review documentation for accuracy and compliance with procedures; release or reject as appropriate. Ensure all document and system elements are correctly identified, accurate, and assembled for review and approval. Author/Own internal documents as related to the Document Control Department. Day-to-Day Support & SME Responsibilities Serve as a focal point for document control, change control processes/procedures, issues, or inquiries. Provide coaching for new Change Control Management Owners and Approvers. Communicate change status to originators and provide guidance to alternate documentation personnel. Provide backup support for other document personnel. Maintain Facility Employee Signature Files. Serve as the Iron Mountain/Records 365 Administrator/SME. Manage/maintain Redbooks. Document Number Assignment. Generate Inspection Checklist (ICL) for Manufacturing. Maintain Document Filing and Archiving. Office Supply Purchase Requests. Provide administrative and other support such as document requests and copier maintenance. Provide guidance and backup to the Documentation Clerk. Preparation, Scanning, Verification and Archival of Device History Records (DHRs). Perform other duties as assigned. Change Control Governance Board (CCGB) Backup Support Prepare documentation, agendas, and minutes for CCGB meetings. Provide guidance to Change Owners on completing the CCGB initial meeting template and non-routine Change Control system/process functionalities (e.g., Change Amendments, Change Plan Amendments, Holds). Audit Support Support internal and external audits, including ISO, CFR, Health Canada, MDSAP, and corporate audits. Assist with Change Control and Document Control requests during audits, including assembling Document Control/Change Control requests and presenting information to auditors. Prepare ad hoc reports (as requested by management) for planned internal/external audits. What You'll Bring High school diploma or equivalent required. Two (2) or more years’ experience with Product Data Management / documentation systems or equivalent change control systems. Undefined Proficient with computers and in the use of Microsoft Office Suite. Experienced in document, data, and product configuration management, including change control. Strong verbal / written communication and problem-solving skills. Strong attention to detail with the ability to independently organize workload, perform under strict deadlines and changing priorities with minimal supervision. Ability to handle multiple tasks concurrently. Ability to communicate with all levels within the organization. Able to demonstrate responsiveness to customer needs, within job scope. Knowledge of Food & Drug Administration (FDA) Regulations and application of Good Manufacturing Practices.

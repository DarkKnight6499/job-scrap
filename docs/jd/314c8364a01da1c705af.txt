About Evergreen Portfolio Management The Evergreen Portfolio Management team manages HarbourVest's product suite of private markets evergreen portfolios. The team actively manages fund liquidity, supports new investment activity, and advises on the launch of new product portfolios. We are looking to expand our team amid strong momentum and growing demand for our evergreen products. Within our team, we value individuals who bring an entrepreneurial approach and are eager to make a meaningful impact at HarbourVest. In this role as an Evergreen Portfolio Management Investment Data Associate, you will handle various responsibilities while building and maintaining our investment data universe. You will work on a nimble team developing connections across the HarbourVest platform including the dedicated Portfolio Managers, capital markets, investment teams, investment operations, legal, as well as senior leadership. Evergreen Portfolio Management Investment Data Associates think like portfolio managers, researchers and problem solvers. This requires strong data skills, analytical capabilities, familiarity with the investment process (preferably private equity), a deep understanding of investment concepts, and an entrepreneurial attitude. What You’ll Do Serve as the primary liaison between Evergreen Portfolio Management and HarbourVest’s investment data lake, ensuring seamless data access and alignment. Generate analytical insights that advise liquidity planning, portfolio construction, and performance optimization across evergreen strategies. Leverage the investment data lake to proactively develop data‑driven recommendations that improve Evergreen Portfolio Management decision making. Maintain regular communication with Evergreen Portfolio Managers and senior investment professionals to translate insights into strategic actions. Build strong cross‑functional partnerships with the Quantitative Investment Sciences team to deepen analytical rigor and expand modeling capabilities. Create and maintain dashboards that transform complex investment data into clear, actionable views for portfolio management. Produce high‑quality internal and external materials drawing from HarbourVest’s investment data repository to support fundraising, client engagement, and firmwide inquiries. Innovate new applications of investment data across evergreen strategies (e.g., private equity, private credit, infrastructure) to scale the platform and strengthen decision‑making frameworks.

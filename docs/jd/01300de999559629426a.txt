Tenaska is one of the largest privately held companies in the United States, an organization that’s adept in natural gas and power marketing, power management, development and acquisition of generation assets, operation of power plants and more. Job Summary: This position will involve the preparation, review and analysis of detailed financial performance models related to development, acquisitions and federal income tax-oriented investments of energy projects, preparation of detailed tax capital account models, business valuation models, reports, and review of financial information to assist the Tenaska Capital Deployment & Finance group in the analysis of individual investments up to complex multi-asset portfolios. Location: Onsite in Omaha, NE Essential Job Functions: Create detailed economic and investment valuation models that require forecasting, valuation, sensitivity analysis by utilizing Microsoft Excel, Bloomberg and other tools. Prepare and review of financial information for completeness and accuracy for use in analyzing complex business opportunities. Work with internal and external professionals, such as engineering, operations, asset management, finance and accounting, to construct and analyze financial and operating performance aspects of complex projects in an economic reporting model. Understand and participate in the negotiation and implementation of financing documents, project documents, tax law and regulations, purchase and sale agreements, and partnership documents. Create and maintain relationships with consultants, lenders, investment bankers, project sponsors and equity partners. Prepare and review analysis, reports, and research for distribution/presentation to senior management. Communicate with senior management on the results of economic analysis.

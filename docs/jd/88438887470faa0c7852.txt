We’re looking for an Authorized Officer, Research Analyst - Partnership Solutions to: • Evaluate, select, and monitor fixed income investment managers and strategies. Design and apply Python or Excel based analytical tools to automate data processing and conduct performance, risk, attribution, and scenario analysis across various market environments. • Apply knowledge of fixed income securities, portfolio construction, and risk management to assess duration, convexity, liquidity, and diversification within multi-manager portfolios. Conduct comprehensive quantitative and qualitative investment manager due diligence, evaluate investment processes and controls, and synthesize findings into formal investment recommendations. • Integrate data from multiple financial systems to produce standardized research outputs and reports. Prepare written materials and deliver presentations to investment committees and client-facing teams. Communicate complex technical analyses clearly and professionally. • Contribute to the expected growth of assets for Partnership Solutions and UBS AM. • Can work hybrid (In-office/remote). Your skills and experience Education & Experience Requirement • This position requires a Master’s degree or foreign equivalent in Economics, Mathematics, Finance, or related field of study. Position requires experience with the following: • (1) Fixed income markets and securities, including bond pricing, yield measures, duration, convexity, and yield curve analysis; (2) using financial databases and analytical platforms, such as Bloomberg; (3) obtaining and analyzing financial markets, performance, and holdings data for investment research; (4) advanced Excel, including building and maintaining financial models and using formulas and functions such as lookup functions, conditional logic, and pivot tables for investment analysis; (5) Python for financial data analysis; (6) data manipulation and quantitative investment analysis; (7) performing statistical analysis and data validation, including summary statistics, correlation analysis, time-series analysis, and identification of outliers or data anomalies in financial datasets; (8) scenario analysis, stress testing, or sensitivity analysis to evaluate the impact of changes in key economic, financial, or model assumptions on analytical results or portfolio outcomes; (9) preparing written investment research materials, including analytical reports and investment memos, for academic or professional purposes; (10) creating and delivering formal presentations of investment analysis for academic or professional purposes.

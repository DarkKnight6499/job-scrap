The Global Financial Planning and Analysis (FP&A) organization is seeking a Senior Analyst to contribute to the Current Quarter Revenue Assurance (CQRA) team. The role strengthens visibility, confidence, and decision-making across current-quarter forecasting activities by connecting financial analysis, operational inputs, and business insights. Success in this position contributes to informed forecasting outcomes and cross-functional alignment across global operations. How you will make an impact: Perform CQRA analysis covering orders, revenue, and margin updates Consolidate and reconcile monthly bookings results with regional teams Manage historical metrics repositories and analyze current and historical trends to inform future activity Conduct bridge analysis across forecast, results, and budget comparisons Identify and communicate forecast risk, upside opportunities, cover requirements, and forecast movements across operational factors Drive process and tool improvements that increase efficiency and forecast accuracy Leverage AI tools, including Co-Pilot and related solutions, to generate KPI reporting, trend analytics, and forecasting insights The must haves: Education: University degree in Finance (FP&A), Supply Chain Operations, or a related field 6+ years of relevant professional experience Ability to manage multiple priorities and competing demands within time-sensitive environments Application of business acumen, strategic thinking, and cross-functional collaboration Utilization of advanced Excel and PowerPoint to create analyses, executive summaries, and decision-support materials Communication of complex analysis to senior stakeholders and business partners Flexibility to accommodate global time zones as needed Nice to haves: Experience with Anaplan modeling and its broader environment Experience with Power BI reporting and dashboard creation Working knowledge of Oracle, CPODS, Salesforce Experience working in a global, matrixed environment Pay Range: Pay range information is not available for this role.

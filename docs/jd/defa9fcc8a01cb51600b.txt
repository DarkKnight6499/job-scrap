Principal Responsibility Corporate Trust AVP in Issuer Services forms part of MSS Operations. Issuer Services provides trustee and agency services across seven core products to enable clients to pursue international capital market activities. We expedite movements of cash and securities, protect the rights of interested parties and we assist our colleagues in CIB with the identification of solutions to meet client requirements. The role holder will primarily be responsible for the effective daily processes and controls in relation to the administration of a portfolio of deals covering corporate bonds, escrows, money market, structured, insurance trusts, and/or project finance transactions. The analyst is responsible for the delivery of services to the client by meeting our contractual obligations. The activities include cash and trade settlement, physical custodian responsibilities and vault management. Additionally, oversight and support to the wider Corporate Trust Operations teams in India and Malaysia. The holder will work closely with US and Global Service Center Corporate Trust management to ensure end-to-end delivery and management of operational risk. The candidate will have opportunities to shape the structure, drive system development and automation opportunities and drive a disciplined and sustainable control environment. Strong stakeholder management and operational risk and control mindset is essential.

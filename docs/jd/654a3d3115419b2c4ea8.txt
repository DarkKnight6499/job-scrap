Associate, Accounting Does the idea of working with professional, highly trained accountants inspire you? Are you an accounting professional with a demonstrated ability to work well in teams and develop new ideas? Would you like to work in a collaborative environment where your contributions are recognized and rewarded, all while having a work-life balance? If so, opportunities for professional and personal fulfillment in a dynamic, progressive organization await you at Capital One! An Accounting Associate will perform multiple types of reconciliations, settlement processes, and general ledger work to support the dynamic lines of business at Capital one. You will work with a superior team to learn the accounting processes and procedures at Capital One, and have the opportunity to grow your responsibilities in analysis and project work with your team. Responsibilities: Prepare and review journal entries and reconciliations for review with management Prepare monthly and quarterly reports for review with management Research and resolve data issues as they arise Identify and drive process improvements Participate in challenging project initiatives with the team Provide business requirements for data collection and sourcing, and provide compliance efforts to outside auditors Perform ad hoc requests Basic Qualifications: Bachelor’s Degree, or military experience At least 1 year of experience in public or corporate accounting Preferred Qualifications: Bachelor’s Degree in Accounting Certified Public Accountant (CPA) 2+ years of experience in public, corporate accounting, audit or finance or a combination 1+ year of experience with Google Office or Microsoft Office products, or a combination At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position.

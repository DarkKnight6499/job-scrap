Use Your Power for Purpose At Pfizer, our purpose is to deliver breakthroughs that change patients’ lives. Your work will directly support the discovery sciences, ensuring drug safety and efficacy, and aiding clinical trials. By leveraging cutting-edge design and process development capabilities, you will help accelerate the delivery of top-tier medicines to patients globally, making a significant impact on improving patients' lives. We are seeking an Associate Technician in the Expert Singleton Purification (ESP) group in Groton, CT. within Medicine Design. ESP is responsible for the analytical screening and purification of mid to large scale reactions. Experimentalists in ESP engage in many facets of analytical chemistry development from early-stage compound synthesis and route scouting to late-stage deliveries for toxicology studies and beyond. The successful candidate will generate experimental data in the laboratory with the team, perform various tasks necessary for the development and evaluation of analytical methods. As part of technical operations, you will be a team member who is relied upon to have a good understanding of procedures, techniques, tools, materials, and equipment. Your decision-making will help you prioritize workflows based on the available resources. You will follow standard procedures to complete tasks, some of which may vary in scope, sequence, complexity, and timing. It is your hard work and focus that will help in making Pfizer ready to achieve new milestones and help patients across the globe. How You Will Achieve It Proper handling of chemicals commonly used in analytical chemistry labs (acids, bases, organic solvents, non-reactive gases). Manage own time, professional development, meets established timelines for deliverables and takes accountable for own results. Execute experiment designs provided by scientists semi-independently. Operate, maintain, and clean equipment designed for analysis (Waters/Agilent /Shimadzu UPLC-MS, SFC-MS) Actively maintain inventory levels of laboratory supplies, chemicals, and solvents to ensure continuous process workflows. Document conditions and amounts used during experiment executions within an electronic lab notebook (Signals). Troubleshoot various issues that arise when using common lab equipment. Maintain general understanding of lab safety associated with handling of peroxide forming chemicals and other hazardous chemical compounds. Software knowledge associated with operating laboratory equipment. Ensures work areas are kept stocked, cleaned, and orderly. Here Is What You Need (Minimum Requirements) H.S. diploma or equivalent with either: Additional field related education (see preferred qualifications) with 2+ years’ experience in chemistry laboratory roles or API (active pharmaceutical ingredients) Manufacturing operations with some experience in a laboratory setting. Demonstrated oral and written communication skills, including the ability to maintain an accurate scientific notebook. Familiarity with modern analytical tools and techniques. Bonus Points If You Have (Preferred Requirements) Process Technology or Biotechnology Certificate, or an A.S. in Chemistry or related scientific field Proficiency in using various computer applications including Word, Excel, Chem Draw. Ability to work independently and as part of a team Strong analytical and critical thinking skills Familiarity and basic understanding of common AI tools and a curiosity for learning how they can be used to enhance productivity PHYSICAL/MENTAL REQUIREMENTS The position requires an ability to work in a laboratory environment performing experiments in a laboratory fume hood, and to set up samples for data analysis. Able to lift packs of 20L solvent containers.

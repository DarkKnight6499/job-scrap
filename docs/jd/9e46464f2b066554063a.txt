About this role Wells Fargo is seeking a Healthcare Treasury Implementation Manager to lead a team responsible for delivering Treasury Management implementations for clients across Healthcare, Higher Education and Not-for-Profit, and For-Profit Healthcare segments . This leader will manage a team of Implementation Consultants who oversee client implementations from initial handoff through completion, partnering closely with Sales, Treasury Management, and other key stakeholders. The role requires strong people leadership, healthcare industry knowledge, Treasury Management and/or product implementation experience, client delivery, and operational execution . The successful candidate will bring an understanding of the healthcare environment and its unique client needs while developing team capabilities, overseeing complex implementations, addressing barriers to execution, and improving the overall client experience. In this role, you will: Lead, coach, and develop a team of Treasury Management Implementation Consultants responsible for managing client implementations Oversee a portfolio of implementations,

SUMMARY The HMDA Risk Specialist I is responsible for supporting the Bank’s Home Mortgage Disclosure Act (HMDA) compliance program. This position ensures accurate collection, analysis, and submission of HMDA data. The specialist works closely with other associates across compliance, lending operations, and business units to support the bank’s risk management objectives and deliver exceptional service to our clients. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Conduct reviews of loan files to ensure accurate and timely reporting of HMDA data fields. Assist with validation, and correction of HMDA-related information in accordance with regulatory requirements. Assist with the preparation of regulatory reports and assist in the timely and accurate submission of the HMDA Loan Application Register (LAR). Collaborate with associates in the Compliance, Risk, and Lending Departments to resolve discrepancies and provide guidance on HMDA data collection. Support audits and exams by retrieving documentation, preparing reports, and addressing examiner or auditor inquiries. Participate in ongoing HMDA and fair lending compliance training. Contribute to risk assessments, internal controls testing, and remediation efforts as instructed by management. Performs other related duties as required SUPERVISORY RESPONSIBILITIES No supervisory responsibilities QUALIFICATIONS To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily. Additionally, the qualifications listed below are representative of the knowledge, skills, and/or abilities required in this position: Bachelor (4-year college) degree 0+ years of Banking experience or equivalent combination of education and experience Strong attention to detail and organizational skills. Knowledge of HMDA regulations and compliance principles preferred; willingness to train and develop in the field required. Proficiency with common PC software (Excel, Word, compliance systems/databases). Effective communication skills to interact with associates at all levels and provide excellent client service. Ability to handle confidential information with discretion.

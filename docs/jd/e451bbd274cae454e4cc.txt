The opportunity Our IPO and SPAC Advisory practice provides IPO and private capital issuers and SPAC targets with transaction perspectives, insights and advice as they evaluate and execute capital markets transactions. As a transaction advisory Senior or Manager, you will be a vital member of the team advising our clients on navigating transformational capital markets transactions. Our work starts early in the transaction cycle as we help our clients prepare to transition to the public markets and for their interactions with Wall Street and other key service providers around a prospective transaction. Our responsibilities include: developing and aligning key stakeholders around the company’s equity story / investor messaging and company and transaction models, underwriting syndicate strategy and financial advisor selection support, transaction and fee precedent analysis, perspectives on transaction structuring, valuation, marketing and pricing strategies, and general capital markets transaction management alongside our dedicated project management practice. Your key responsibilities Advising clients on strategic financing opportunities and transaction management Preparing and delivering compelling presentations and analyses Researching, reviewing and analyzing precedent transactions Being a flexible and energetic part of a dynamic team helping companies achieve transformative capital markets transactions Skills and attributes for success Advise companies regarding strategic transaction alternatives. Provide expert, tailored advice to C-suite executives seeking independent views ahead of negotiations or key decisions Supervise, coach and mentor other team members in preparing compelling presentations and related materials for clients and business development. Contribute to business development, client solution and team development initiatives. To qualify for the role you must have A bachelor’s degree 1-4 years of recent experience in the Equity Capital Markets group at a major investment bank Existing Series 7 and/or 79 plus Series 63 securities licenses (in good standing). Strong experience evaluating and executing financing transactions. Established networking skills, including excellent interpersonal and relationship-building skills with the ability to develop and maintain relationships at all levels of an organization. The ability and willingness to travel and work in excess of standard hours and locations as needed. What we look for We’re interested in talented professionals with the ability to visualize our clients’ goals and think creatively to facilitate them. If you have an entrepreneurial spirit, and a genuine passion for taking on new challenges, this role is for you.

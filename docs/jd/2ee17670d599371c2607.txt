The Underwriting Analyst (UA) supports Underwriters for both Regional Accounts (1,000-4,999 lives) and National Accounts (5,000+ lives) business. The functions the UA supports are: pre-sales, renewals, plan changes, and customer experience reporting. Key responsibilities include: Pre-sale: • Reviews RFP submissions and census for completeness and accuracy. Requests missing information from the Underwriter. • Interprets group life, long-term disability, short-term disability, accidental death & dismemberment, accident & critical illness contracts/booklets to create ratings based on the in-force plan design, to develop manual rates. Highlights any discrepancies while utilizing independent thinking skills to propose alternate plan designs as necessary to closely match plan designs. Renewals/Plan Changes/Reporting: • Initiates consultative huddle discussion with the Underwriter to gain clarity on any outstanding questions and review proposed plan designs. • Consistently maintains 97% target and minimum 93% accuracy for delivering ratings by assigned due date. Renewal • Locates current census and checks for completeness and applies data integrity elements; escalating when viable census is not received. • Compares renewal census with current premium and experience reporting to identify any discrepancies and notes any concerns. • Reviews current plan, researches and applies any plan changes from prior renewal. • Reviews experience information and inputs required data into pricing tools to develop formula rate. • Identifies and troubleshoots discrepancies as needed. • Initiates consultative huddle discussion with the Underwriter to gain clarity on any outstanding questions, summarizes the renewal information, highlights areas of concern, and makes recommendation to Underwriter • Populates Renewal Notes Template QUALIFICATIONS: • 3+ years’ work experience, experience in Employee Benefits a plus • Solid MS Office Knowledge including Microsoft Excel formulas, sorting, v-look-ups, pivot tables; PowerPoint knowledge preferred • Strong verbal and written communication skills • Demonstration of solid time, organizational, and desk management skills with ability to multi-task in a fast-paced environment. • Strong commitment to continuous learning, with curiosity and initiative to expand technical expertise, strengthen consultative capabilities, and adopt new tools. WORK ARRANGEMENT: This role will have a Hybrid work schedule, with the expectation of working in an office (Hartford, CT; Alpharetta, GA; or Lake Mary, FL) 3 days a week (Tuesday through Thursday).

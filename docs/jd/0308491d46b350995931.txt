Job Title: Operations Associate Job Description: The Role: Our site in Hillsboro, OR is seeking an Operations Associate to join the production team. This role will serve as a front-line Operations Associate and aid in the production processing work across the factory’s 24/7 compressed shifts. In this role, you will set up production equipment, prepare raw materials for production, and perform verifications of materials to complete the production batches successfully. Skills needed for a successful Operations Associate are critical thinking and problem solving, attention to detail and quality focused mindset. Below are the following shift opportunities: Sunday – Tuesday with every other Wednesday alternating 5:30 AM – 6PM Sunday – Tuesday with every other Saturday alternating 5:30 PM – 6AM Thursday – Friday with every other Wednesday alternating 5:30 AM – 6PM Wednesday – Friday with every other Saturday alternating 5:30 PM – 6AM What You’ll Do: Set up machinery by positioning parts, loading materials, and entering specifications in accordance with engineering parameters. Ensure adherence to department policies, procedures, regulatory compliance, and environmental health and safety requirements. Monitor one or more stages of the manufacturing process while equipment is running. Assess the quality of products and identify any nonconformities. Regularly inspect, clean, and perform routine maintenance on machinery to keep it in good working order. Complete productivity reports, detailing the number of parts or products made during each shift. Provide input into process procedures as required. Work with internal resources to resolve problems. What we are looking for: Required: Have proficiency in windows-based computer applications. Ability to read and understand work instructions. Ability to read specs and follow work instructions. Good communication and teamwork skills. Positive attitude and strong work ethic. High School Diploma/GED. Ability to work a 12-hour shift, on your feet, and lift up to 40 pounds. Preferred Experience 1-3 years of manufacturing or relevant work experience. Demonstrated ability to communicate effectively both verbally and in writing. Detail oriented. Forward thinking (drives for continuous improvement). Ability to align to organizational values. Outstanding Candidates Will Have Demonstrated knowledge of regulatory and compliance guidelines and practices. Demonstrated strong problem-solving skills. Demonstrated knowledge of safety practices.

THE WORK: As a Sales and Delivery Lead for Mid-Market T alent ( ADP, UKG ) , you will oversee delivery execution across multiple programs/engagements , ensuring quality outcomes, client satisfaction, and strong team performance . You will also partner with Sales and Solution teams to standardize and accelerate delivery by building reusable solution assets and templatized solution offers that improve speed to value . Additionally, you will contribute to proposal development and support/lead client Orals for complex pursuits . Key Responsibilities : A) Delivery Leadership Across Multiple Programs Lead end-to-end delivery execution across multiple programs or engagements ( MM T&O portfolio), ensuring delivery excellence and measurable outcomes. Manage planning, delivery governance, escalations, and delivery risk across programs. Ensure consistent delivery standards, operational discipline, and continuous improvement. Develop and grow talent by coaching teams, reinforcing delivery best practices, and partnering with HR/leadership on capability building. Partner with client stakeholders and internal functions to drive adoption of change and stabilize delivery performance. ​ B) Drive Innovation, Standardization & Speed-to-Value Identify repeatable delivery patterns and convert them into solution assets , including: Delivery accelerators, reusable components, playbooks, reference architectures, and tooling approaches. Build and maintain templatized T alent (ADP, ADP Lyric, UKG) solution offers (where applicable), enabling faster proposal responses and reduced time-to-start for new engagements. Promote standardization across delivery methods to improve predictability, cost, and quality. Support innovation initiatives that improve delivery throughput while maintaining risk controls. ​ C) Solutioning & Sales Collaboration (Proposals) Collaborate with Sales, Solution Architecture, and other bid teams to shape delivery strategy during pursuits. Support proposal development by contributing: Delivery approach, governance model, staffing logic, and implementation plan. Differentiators tied to reusable assets/accelerators and known delivery methods Create or review proposal content to ensure it aligns with feasible delivery execution, commercial expectations, and client requirements. D) Support and Lead Orals Participate in bid Orals and executive discussions to present delivery strategy and execution confidence. Lead oral sessions where required—aligning storytelling across delivery, solution, and commercial commitments. Support Q&A responses by coordinating with SMEs and ensuring consistent messaging across stakeholders.

We’re seeking a future team member for the role of Vice President, Market/Client Risk Management to join our Conventional Trust team. This role is located New York, New York, Pittsburgh, PA or Houston, TX. The Vice President, Market/Client Risk Management plays a pivotal role within the Business Development function, focusing on identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks associated with market and client activities. This position supports BNY's strategic pillars by ensuring a robust risk management framework that aligns with the organization's principles of integrity and client focus. The role requires a strong understanding of market dynamics and client interactions to effectively manage and minimize potential risks, thereby contributing to the overall stability and growth of the organization. In this role, you’ll make an impact in the following ways: Conduct comprehensive risk assessments by leveraging expertise in market and client dynamics to identify potential threats and opportunities. Develop and implement risk management strategies that align with BNY's principles, ensuring a proactive approach to mitigating risks. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to enhance risk awareness and integrate risk management practices into business development processes. Monitor and report on risk exposures, providing insights and recommendations to senior management to support informed decision-making. Foster a culture of risk awareness by providing training and guidance to stakeholders, emphasizing the importance of risk management in achieving business objectives. Stay abreast of industry trends and regulatory changes to ensure compliance and adapt risk management strategies accordingly. To be successful in this role, we’re seeking the following: Bachelor's degree in Finance, Business Administration, or a related field. Advanced degree or professional certification (e.g., CFA, FRM) is preferred. 5-10 years of experience. Strong analytical skills to assess and interpret complex market and client data. Excellent communication and collaboration skills to work effectively with diverse teams. Proficient in developing and implementing risk management strategies within a business development context.

Join Gilead and help create possible, together. Job Description We are seeking a Director, Portfolio Strategy & Analytics. This role serves as a strategic partner to assigned Gilead Asset Teams (GATs), Development & Evidence Teams (DETs), and other cross-functional stakeholders, providing strategic analyses, portfolio insights, and decision support that enable effective asset and portfolio planning. They support the development and execution of portfolio processes, methodologies, and analytical approaches used in asset- and portfolio-level decision-making. Working with Global Project Management, Therapeutic Area leadership, Finance, the G.Ecosystem Office, and other partners, the Director helps deliver consistent, high-quality planning, options evaluation, and strategic decision support. This position represents PS&A on cross-functional teams and selected governance and planning forums, contributes to enterprise portfolio initiatives, and supports capability building, process improvement, and the continued evolution of portfolio analytics, tools, and ways of working. **Position is located in Foster City, California** Responsibilities Serve as the PS&A lead or representative for assigned assets, therapeutic areas, or portfolio initiatives, with guidance as appropriate for the scope and complexity of the assignment. Partner with GATs, DETs, and cross-functional leaders to frame strategic questions, evaluate options, assess risks and trade-offs, and support key decision points. Develop and deliver high-quality analyses, insights, forecasts, and recommendations that inform asset-level and portfolio-level decisions. Provide strategic and analytical support for governance discussions, portfolio reviews, investment decisions, and lifecycle planning activities. Contribute to the development and implementation of PS&A processes, methodologies, and best practices, including lifecycle planning and options development and evaluation. Coordinate the timely delivery of recurring and ad hoc analyses, presentations, and insights for leadership teams and governance forums. Assess asset-level opportunities within the broader context of portfolio strategy, priorities, constraints, and resource allocation, and highlight implications for decision-makers. Act as a strategic thought partner to functional and program leaders by providing objective analyses and well-supported recommendations for complex decisions. Identify and recommend opportunities to improve portfolio processes, analytical approaches, systems, tools, and reporting capabilities; lead defined improvement efforts as assigned. Support change management and implementation activities associated with new portfolio processes, tools, and planning methodologies. Contribute to knowledge sharing, capability building, and continuous improvement across PS&A and partner organizations, including coaching colleagues and sharing effective practices. Provide matrix leadership for complex cross-functional initiatives and project teams, building alignment, clarifying deliverables, and supporting execution without direct management authority. Minimum Required Education and Years of Experience Bachelor's Degree and Twelve Years’ Experience OR Masters' Degree and Ten Years’ Experience OR PhD and Eight Years’ Relevant Experience in Life Sciences. Requirements Minimum of 6 years of experience in strategic portfolio analysis, planning, decision support, or a related area in life sciences. Significant experience working on and influencing complex cross-functional drug development programs and portfolio initiatives. Demonstrated ability to develop strategic analyses and insights that inform asset-level and portfolio-level decisions. Experience contributing to portfolio processes, governance discussions, lifecycle planning, resource prioritization, or related activities. Strong knowledge of biopharma portfolio strategy, drug development, commercialization, and relevant disease-area considerations. Demonstrated ability to influence stakeholders, resolve cross-functional issues, and build alignment across functions, teams, and levels of leadership. Experience contributing to process improvement, change management, and implementation of new ways of working. Strong strategic thinking, analytical problem-solving, project management, and communication skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities. Ability to distill complex information into clear business recommendations and concise communications for senior leaders and governance forums. Demonstrated success working in highly matrixed environments and leading through influence. Ability to effectively represent assigned work with senior leaders and governance forums and respond to questions with credibility and sound judgment.

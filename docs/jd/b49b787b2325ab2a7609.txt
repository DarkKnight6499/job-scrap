We are currently looking for a Director to lead Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) for our Film business. In this role, you will partner closely with the Netflix Film leadership team to oversee short- and long-term financial planning for our live-action and animated film slates. The ideal candidate is a strategic and analytical finance leader with deep experience in entertainment budgeting and forecasting. You have a proven track record of building and coaching high-performing teams, designing scalable processes, and implementing financial and reporting systems. You bring exceptional financial modeling and communication skills, thrive in ambiguity, and excel at managing complex, cross-functional workstreams. As a key thought partner to Film leadership and Strategy teams, you will help shape the financial roadmap to scale our global film strategy across all formats and distribution windows. You possess a global mindset, strong business acumen, and the ability to influence senior leaders and drive alignment in a highly cross-functional environment. Your intellectual curiosity, passion for continuous improvement, and ability to translate data into actionable insights will be critical to our success. This position reports to the Vice President of Content, Marketing, Consumer Products & Experiences FP&A, who is based in Los Angeles. To learn more about our unique culture, please visit our culture memo and long-term view. Responsibilities: Lead, coach, and develop a high-performing Film FP&A team. Oversee the forecast and long-range plan for the Film organization, proactively identifying risks and opportunities. Partner with Film Leadership and Strategy teams to ensure financial plans are aligned with business strategy. Design and implement scalable forecasting and reporting workflows to ensure efficiency in our planning processes and consistency across Content teams Work closely with FinTech and Data & Insights teams to develop automated financial systems, forecasting tools, and management reporting solutions. Develop financial KPIs, metrics, and dashboards to measure and communicate the ongoing impact of the Film business to leadership and equip the business with actionable information. Build and maintain strong relationships with business partners, senior leaders, and cross-functional teams to drive effective decision-making and deliver on targets. Conduct ad hoc financial analyses, scenario planning, and opportunity sizing to support strategic initiatives. Qualifications: 15+ years of progressive finance experience in a fast-paced, highly analytical environment. Prior experience in FP&A, management consulting, or strategic finance in the tech, media, or entertainment spaces is required. MBA a plus.

BOIS leverages Blue Owl’s global platform, origination capabilities, and investment expertise to help insurers achieve their return, capital, and liability management objectives. BOIS serves nearly 100 insurance clients and manages more than $30 billion of insurance-related assets. Blue Owl seeks an Associate-level professional in its New York office to join the Strategic Investment team. This role represents a unique opportunity to work across multiple strategic, transaction driven, elements of the insurance ecosystem focusing both on equity style investing and asset origination & structuring.

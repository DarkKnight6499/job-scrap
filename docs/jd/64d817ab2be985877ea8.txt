Meet the Team You will play a key role in providing detailed financial insights and analysis to drive business decisions, while also supporting regular forecasting and reporting deliverables. This role supports a distributed team, requiring collaboration across multiple time zones and the flexibility to work around a global clock to meet deliverables and business needs. Your Impact You have teamwork and interpersonal skills. You are committed to continuous improvement; you take initiative to improve processes and give to the overall success of the team. Play a key role in supporting mid-year and new-year readiness across multiple businesses. Produce actionable insights and analysis around the Financial Plan. Understand and consolidate key items from product roadmaps to help understand upcoming headwinds and tailwinds for Sales. Assist in modeling out-quarter and next year forecast under different scenarios. Provide support across the team for regular reporting and forecasting deliverables. Leverage AI to increase automation and efficiency within workstreams. Support Ad hoc requests. Minimum Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, or a related field. Minimum 2 years of experience in a financial analyst, FP&A, Sales Finance, or related role. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to analyze financial data, identify key drivers and trends, and translate findings into actionable insights. Highly detail oriented and organized, with the capability to prioritize multiple deadlines, results, and priorities while maintaining a high degree of accuracy and quality. Strong verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to optimally communicate financial results, trends, risks, and opportunities to cross-functional and global partners. Proven ability to identify and drive operational improvements and efficiencies within processes. Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint Experience using AI tools to improve and automate business processes Preferred Qualifications Experience supporting a global organization and working with stakeholders across multiple geographies and time zones.

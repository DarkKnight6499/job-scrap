Director of Carrier Managed Service Portfolio and Offer Development – Americas, EMEA Location: This role can be located anywhere in the United States Ciena is seeking a Director of Carrier Managed Service (CMS) Portfolio and Offer Development to lead the evolution of managed services for telcos, service providers, carriers, and network operators globally. This role drives portfolio strategy, partnership growth, and go-to-market execution while creating value for strategic partners across a rapidly evolving telecommunications market. Success in this position combines business strategy, telecom technology, and relationship leadership to accelerate growth and service innovation. How you will make an impact: • Lead the development, evolution, and execution of the managed services portfolio strategy for telcos, service providers, carriers, and network operators globally. • Refresh the managed services catalog and introduce new service offerings aligned with partner and provider outcomes. • Coordinate cross-functional engagement across Account Management, Product Line Management, Partner Programs, Regional Leaders, and CMS Business Development Consultants. • Cultivate relationships with service providers and operators to align business development activities with growth strategies. • Direct sales enablement and go-to-market initiatives within the Ciena Partner Network ecosystem ( CPNe ). • Influence stakeholders through thought leadership and business-focused communication of complex technology concepts. • Coach and mentor global teams while actively contributing to consulting, business development, and sales enablement activities. The must haves: • Experience: 10+ years in telecom sales, business development, product management, or channel management. • Leadership in business development, consultative sales, partner management, and growth of service provider relationships. • Application of managed services portfolio strategy, service catalog development, offering standardization, and lifecycle governance. • Experience working within global, cross-functional matrix environments and collaborating across multiple business functions. • Knowledge of telecommunications managed services, market opportunities, and telecom technologies including optical transport, Carrier Ethernet, IP, and wave services. • Commercial and financial acumen applied to growth strategy, service launches, pipeline development, and partner performance. • Exceptional project management, communication, presentation, storytelling, and executive engagement skills. Nice to haves : • Experience with direct and indirect sales strategy, channel programs, and partner ecosystems. • Familiarity with the telecommunications and channel ecosystem, including Global Carriers, Tier 1 and Tier 2 Operators, Regional Service Providers, MSOs, Submarine Operators, Indirect Channels, Hyperscale Data Center Operators, Public Cloud Providers, and Neo-scalers. • Knowledge of global business practices, cultural nuances, and market dynamics across regions. • Demonstrated ability to synthesize partner success stories into business-focused narratives.

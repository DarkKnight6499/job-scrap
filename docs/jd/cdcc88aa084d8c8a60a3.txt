Scopely is hiring a Corporate Finance Manager to join our Finance team in Culver City on a hybrid basis. Corporate Finance oversees company-wide financial planning and reporting, and you will have broad visibility into Scopely’s P&L and business operations. You will own Scopely’s corporate close and reporting cadence, a core part of the role while automation matures. Success means progressively replacing manual processes with AI-native automation until the cadence runs itself. As automation frees capacity, your focus will shift to higher-impact work: partnering across the company to evaluate returns and facilitate growth of Scopely-wide initiatives. Why This Role Stands Out Lead the Automation: Be part of an AI-first finance team and drive Corporate Finance process improvement and automation projects from concept to launch See the Big Picture: Consolidate Scopely-wide financials across all our titles including leading games like MONOPOLY GO!, Pokémon GO, MARVEL Strike Force, and Star Trek Fleet Command Guide Leadership: Partner directly with the CFO to evaluate the company’s trajectory, surfacing risk early, and shaping mitigation plans. Prepare and present reporting deliverables to the CFO and finance leadership Size the Return: Model returns on competing initiatives with cross-functional partners to inform investment decisions. Grow Your Craft: Report to the Corporate Finance Senior Director who will invest in your development with hands-on mentorship What We're Looking For Experience: 5+ years in corporate finance, FP&A, investment banking, consulting, or other strong analytical roles. Experience with month-end close, consolidation, and financial reporting. Process Improvement: Demonstrated ability to develop scalable financial processes and introduce automation that reduces repetitive manual work Financial Modeling & Analytics: Advanced Excel/Google Sheets skills with a demonstrated ability to build models that size the financial impact of company-wide initiatives Stakeholder Management: Proven ability to partner with senior cross-functional stakeholders, build consensus, and influence decisions without direct authority.Communication: Excellent written and verbal communication skills with the ability to synthesize complex analysis into clear, concise narratives and recommendations Nice to Have Experience with financial systems (Oracle, Workday Adaptive Planning) and data platforms (BigQuery).

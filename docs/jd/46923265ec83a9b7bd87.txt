Role Overview : The Senior Associate, Integrated Absence Product Manager is responsible for bringing strong expertise to advance the strategy, design, and execution of New York Life's Group Insurance integrated absence and accommodations solutions. This role partners across Operations, Digital, Technology, Clinical, Compliance, Distribution, and Implementation teams to deliver market-leading leave, disability, and ADA experiences for employers and employees. This individual will recommend future-state product capabilities, identify operational efficiencies, translate business needs into product requirements, and support the development of scalable solutions that improve customer experience, operational effectiveness, and market competitiveness. Exercise judgment in analyzing market trends evaluating product enhancement opportunities, recommending investment priorities, and making product design and implementation decisions that impact multiple business functions. Serve as a subject matter expert in integrated absence management, applying advanced knowledge of leave of absence, disability, paid family & medical leave, ADA accommodations, and related business processes to develop innovative solutions to complex product and operational challenges. Lead cross-functional product initiatives involving Operations, Technology, Digital, Clinical, Compliance, and Distribution, resolving competing priorities, influencing stakeholders, and driving alignment on product design and implementation decisions, while informing and driving the development of business case and recommendation for strategic investments.

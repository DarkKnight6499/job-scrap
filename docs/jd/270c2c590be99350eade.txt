Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism CMAAS (Capital Markets and Accounting Advisory Services) Management Level Manager Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Capital Markets Accounting Advisory Services Manager you will advise clients on technical accounting, financial reporting, transaction structures, and capital markets topics across deals and transformational business events. As a Manager you will lead teams, coach team members, manage client accounts, and drive workstreams through independent problem solving, planning, and review of deliverables. In this role at PwC, you will support clients navigating acquisitions, divestitures, exits, capital raising, IPOs, and other events that can affect value, while helping deliver clear, timely, and well-structured advisory work within our Deals practice. Responsibilities - Leading capital markets accounting advisory workstreams across mergers, acquisitions, divestitures, capital raising, and other transformational events - Advising clients on technical accounting, financial reporting, and transaction structure considerations for public and private deals - Managing project planning, budgeting, timelines, and deliverables for multiple client engagements - Guiding teams in the preparation of accounting analyses, financial statements, and reporting deliverables under GAAP and IFRS - Reviewing accounting positions, modeling outputs, and transaction documents to validate alignment with applicable guidance - Coaching team members on complex accounting issues, client service delivery, and practical application of methodologies and tools - Identifying accounting, reporting, and operational issues that can affect deal execution and transaction readiness - Building trusted client relationships and communicating clear recommendations on capital market and accounting matters What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 5 years of experience - At least one of the following: an active CPA License What Sets You Apart - Preference for at least one of the following fields of study: Accounting, Economics, Finance - Demonstrating capital markets accounting advisory or transaction support experience - Applying technical accounting knowledge during deals and reporting change - Managing project timelines, budgets, and deliverables across client workstreams - Coaching junior team members and reviewing work for accuracy - Navigating capital raising, IPO, or carve-out readiness activities - Communicating accounting issues clearly to clients and stakeholders Travel Requirements Up to 60% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $99,000 - $232,000.

ROLE DESCRIPTION

Form Energy is hiring a highly motivated FP&A Staff Analyst to support financial operations for the engineering and R&D organization.This role reports into our Director of Corporate FP&A. This role is ideal for a finance professional who enjoys partnering closely with technical organizations and influencing decision-making through data-driven insights. You will operate with significant autonomy while partnering directly with G&A teams and leadership. This is an exciting opportunity to help shape and be part of the growth of a fast-moving company with breakthrough technology and an incredible mission!



This is a hybrid role, which will require working onsite from one of our offices 3+ days per week. 



Relocation assistance is available. 




WHAT YOU'LL DO:

 - Develop department budgets (expense & capital investments), monitor, and analyze actual spending against budget; report on actual performance and key performance indicators to support business decisions

 - Gather, review, analyze and report spending data

 - Drive the budget development process, particularly with the quantification of the program plan. Lead budget review meetings with business partners and engineering leadership

 - Drive reporting, process, and discipline for finance routines to shape and influence decision-making (e.g. annual budget, quarterly forecast, monthly reviews, long-range plans)

 - Support internal controls (examples: purchase request approvals, monthly accruals, budget versus actual reviews, regular risk assessment)

 - Partner with cross-functional teams, such as Finance Systems and Finance Business Intelligence to develop reporting dashboards

 - Drive continuous process improvement, standardization, simplification, and reporting enhancements

 - Maintain high standards of financial accuracy, documentation, and operational rigor




WHAT YOU'LL BRING:

 - Minimum of 8 years of experience in Corporate Finance

 - Bachelor’s degree in accounting or finance

 - Deep understanding of expense accounting principles including accruals, amortization, and capitalization

 - Ability to influence stakeholders, challenge existing processes, and drive continuous improvement

 - Excellent written/verbal communication skills

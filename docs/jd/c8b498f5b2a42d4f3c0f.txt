Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Risk Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Regulatory Risk & Compliance - Health Industries - Care Coordination Senior Associate you will help clients navigate regulatory requirements and manage compliance risks across care coordination activities within our Advisory practice. As a Senior Associate you will analyze complex issues, build meaningful client connections, and support assigned workstreams by applying critical thinking, professional judgment, and a deeper understanding of the health industries context. In this role at PwC, you will contribute to compliance strategies, interpret data to support insights, and uphold professional and technical standards while mentoring junior team members and adapting to changing client needs.

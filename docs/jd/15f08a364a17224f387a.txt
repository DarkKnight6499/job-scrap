Pharmacy Underwriting Development Program Senior Analyst: Start Date: January 25th, 2027 Your Future in Underwriting Starts Here. Have you imagined a career where finance, healthcare, data, and strategy come together? As a Risk & Underwriting Senior Analyst in The Cigna Group’s Pharmacy Underwriting Development Program, you will help support pricing and underwriting strategies for pharmacy benefits solutions. Your work will contribute to competitive financial offers, thoughtful risk management, and business decisions that support our clients, customers, and enterprise growth. Through structured training, hands-on projects, and meaningful team collaboration, you will develop knowledge of Pharmacy Benefits Management, build technical and professional skills, and gain exposure to business-critical work in a fast-paced, supportive environment. Designed to help you transition from college to career, this program is ideal for recent graduates and early-career professionals who are curious, motivated, and eager to apply their academic knowledge to real-world business challenges. Key Responsibilities: Support pricing strategies for new and renewing business, including RFPs, direct offers, and market checks. Analyze financial data to assess business impacts, including formulary, network, and specialty drug changes. Contribute to client proposals and recommendations that balance competitiveness, client needs, and financial risk. Assist with contracting and implementation activities to support pricing accuracy and consistency. Collaborate with cross-functional partners to develop compliant, business-aligned financial solutions. Support forecasting, budgeting, and strategic planning through financial trend analysis. Build PBM industry knowledge while strengthening analytical, communication, and business skills. What You’ll Get: Structured Curriculum: Comprehensive training modules in which you will develop knowledge of Pharmacy Benefits Management, build technical and professional skills Mentorship & Development: Coaching, mentoring, and hands-on business initiatives to accelerate your growth. Required Qualifications A bachelor’s degree is required. Preference for the following major and/or minors are preferred: Risk Management, Economics, Statistics, Mathematics, or Finance Relevant internship in related field preferred (Risk Management, Economics, or Finance or similar) Comfort working with Excel for analysis, reporting, modeling, or data-driven decision-making. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Attention to detail and sound decision-making abilities. Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced, collaborative environment. Preferred Qualifications: GPA of 3.0 or higher preferred Demonstrated leadership, curiosity, innovative thinking, and ability to build strong working relationships. Eagerness to learn, grow, and contribute to a team-oriented culture. Customer-focused mindset with interest in healthcare, finance, risk, or underwriting.

Join us and transform the lives of patients while transforming your career. Associate Manufacturing (Pilot Plant) Let’s do this. Let’s change the world. In this vital role you will support the execution of cell culture, harvest, and purification process studies at the 500L scale, to include operation of single-use bioreactors, centrifugation equipment, chromatography skids and filtration systems. The Amgen Rhode Island AR6 Pilot Plant provides critical technical support for team supply, commercial process development, technology transfer, process characterization, and scale-up/scale-down activities. This is a day shift position that includes off shift and weekend coverage. Role Responsibilities: Aseptically maintain mammalian cell culture through vial thaw, flask passaging, bioreactor operations to harvest, which may include, but not limited to; centrifugation, multistage clarification and/or ATF (Alternating Tangential Flow) filtration Protein purification via multi-stage chromatography and filtration systems to include, but not limited to; Ion-Exchange Chromatography (IEX), Affinity Chromatography and Tangential Flow Filtration Set-up and operation of bioreactors and vessels, centrifugation and filtration harvest equipment and downstream equipment to include, but not limited to; chromatography and Ultra Filtration Diafiltration (UF/DF) skids Perform bench-work studies such as titration and filtration Perform in-process sampling and monitoring Analyze in-process samples and perform routine analytical equipment maintenance Perform batch and filtration of media and buffers Perform new product introductions (NPI), technology transfer and participate in equipment or experiment design Perform documentation of experimental data in electronic lab notebooks in accordance with Good Laboratory Practices (GLP) Perform general lab organization and cleaning Management of inventory, operational excellence (OE) initiatives and preventative maintenance for systems and equipment Inform-role for operational decision making, troubleshooting and investigative work. Basic Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree OR Associate’s degree + 6 months of biotechnology in manufacturing, engineering, or process development work experience OR High school diploma / GED + 2 years of biotechnology in manufacturing, engineering, or process development work experience Preferred Qualifications: An established record of encouraging safety in a lab or manufacturing setting Experienced in performing bioprocess unit operations in cell culture and/or purification Knowledge in troubleshooting process equipment issues including automation control strategies and utilities such as clean in place (CIP) and clean steam Experience in the operation of control systems (Delta V) and method development on Cytiva purification systems using Unicorn Experience with data analysis and interpretation Experience collaborating with and across functional areas and sites Strong written and verbal communication skills; strong computer skills Experience in leading Operational Excellence (OE) efforts to drive efficiency and safety What you can expect of us As we work to develop treatments that take care of others, we also work to care for your professional and personal growth and well-being.

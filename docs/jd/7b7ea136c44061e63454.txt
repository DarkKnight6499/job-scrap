Job Posting Title: Disney Consumer Products Retail Creative Graphic Design Intern, Spring 2027 Req ID: 10160400 Job Description: About the Role & Program Disney Consumer Products (DCP) brings the magic of Disney’s beloved stories, franchises, and characters to families and fans around the globe. From toys to apparel, books to games, our global marketing organization has a profound impact on The Walt Disney Company and the global markets in which we operate! With a portfolio of world-class franchises including Disney, Pixar, Marvel, 20th Century Studios, National Geographic, and Star Wars, we are a worldwide industry and category leader delivering innovative products that entertain and inspire! Are you passionate about graphic design, brand storytelling, and seeing your creative work come to life in immersive retail environments? Then, this opportunity is for you! As a Retail Creative Graphic Design Intern, you’ll collaborate with a dynamic, creative team to design and implement impactful graphic packages across iconic Disney destinations, supporting hundreds of retail locations. This internship is a full-time, six-month commitment in Orlando, FL, from January 2027 to June 2027. What You Will Do You will help shape immersive, storewide graphic experiences across Disney destinations—from Walt Disney World and Disneyland to Disney Cruise Line and more—supporting over 300 retail locations where craftsmanship, collaboration, and creativity meet. On any given day, you might: Support the Retail Creative Team in the design, production, and implementation of storewide graphic packages to support over 300 retail locations as part of Disney Experiences Build environmental graphics to support new film and product strategies while adhering to established brand standards Support the team in implementing site-specific and global graphic packages Coordinate fabrication of 2D or layered graphics with vendors on time while adhering to specifications and maintaining a high standard of quality Support the Global Retail Sign Program with updates as needed Responsible for preparing mechanical files, printing, and fabrication of designs Coordinate and support the production and installation of Retail Creative park initiatives Please Apply If You Have/Are Demonstrated knowledge in composition, hierarchy, and color theory, as well as a clear understanding of typography and layout Proficiency in Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop Must be comfortable in a hands-on production environment with close attention to detail Proven organizational and time management skills, and proven ability to work independently and collaboratively Ability to prioritize multiple projects with short deadlines Ability to articulate conceptual and design decisions, and the ability to give and receive feedback regularly Strong verbal and written communication skills It Would Also Be Great If You Are Experience working in a graphic production environment Familiarity with project management software Previous knowledge of and desire to learn new software as needed to further support the needs of the organization (i.e., motion graphics, 3D modeling, etc.) Education Junior or Senior year preferred Major or previous coursework in Graphic Design, Art Design, or a related major preferred Required Materials A portfolio review is required for this position.

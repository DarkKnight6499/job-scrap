About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Lead Market Risk Officer within Corporate & Investment Bank (CIB) Portfolio Risk. This role will support the Aggregate Market and Counterparty Risk Oversight team by proactively assessing risk across the company, individual businesses, and legal entities to provide a consolidated view of market and counterparty risk. The role will identify material exposures, concentrations, trends, and changes in risk profile and provide clear analysis for senior management, governance committees, and regulators. The position will also support the Statement of Risk Appetite process, with a significant focus on establishing, calibrating, and maintaining risk appetite boundaries in coordination with relevant business, oversight, and control teams. In this role, you will: Provide aggregate oversight of market and counterparty risk across the company, businesses, and legal entities Identify material exposures, concentrations, trends, and emerging risks Analyze changes in risk profile and communicate clear conclusions to senior audiences Support the design, calibration, and maintenance of Statement of Risk Appetite boundaries Perform analysis and benchmarking to assess proposed boundary changes Coordinate boundary development across relevant business, oversight, and control teams. Develop presentations of proposed changes for governance and risk committees and senior leaders for review and approval Assess alignment of boundaries with strategy, financial resources, risk

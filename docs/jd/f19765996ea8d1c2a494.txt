Job description Group Description: Equity Capital Markets (ECM) originates, advises, structures, and executes equity and equity-linked offerings for corporates and financial sponsors. ECM sits at the intersection of Evercore’s investment banking advisory business and the Evercore ISI research / sales & trading business. The team services clients providing underwriting capabilities across IPOs, follow-ons, spin-offs and convertible/derivative strategies. What's the Position? Evercore seeks to hire an Analyst for its team located in New York City, preferably with ECM-specific experience. Primary Responsibilities: Analysts in ECM participate in the origination and execution of all deal activity. Specific responsibilities will include creating materials for pitches and other meetings; analyzing companies, sectors, deal activity, corporate filings, and shareholder data; building relationships with internal and external deal constituents; constructing and managing internal deal and investor-related databases. The role will involve communicating and coordinating with members of the broader Evercore team in corporate advisory (coverage, restructuring, shareholder activism and defense) and at Evercore ISI (research, sales & trading). Assist in identifying and targeting new business opportunities Develop/define equity offering strategies and appropriately communicate and advise corporate clients Shape positioning and valuation strategies for companies as they market themselves to public investors Support senior bankers on equity transaction execution and new business origination Develop / maintain institutional investor relationships that can be leveraged to market transactions and provide client feedback in deal and non-deal settings Maintain real-time capital market knowledge and macro and microeconomic themes that drive equity markets and investor sentiment Develop and maintain equity marketing materials Development of pitch books and related client materials Participate in other projects as requested by management Specific Qualifications: Analysts in ECM play a vital role in the building of Evercore’s underwriting business. Analysts are involved in supporting the team in generating new ideas and business opportunities, as well as executing live opportunities. We are looking for excited, creative, organized, and resourceful multi-taskers with high intellectual curiosity and the long-term orientation to build the premier independent ECM franchise. Evercore’s ECM business is growing – the candidate should be entrepreneurial and embrace a rapidly changing environment.

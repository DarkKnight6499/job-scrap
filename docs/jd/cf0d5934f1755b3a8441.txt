Principal Associate, Associate Experience/Events - Global Enterprise Affairs Global Enterprise Affairs (GEA) works to change banking for good through the collective efforts of seven teams and key partnerships across the enterprise. We work with external and internal stakeholders to envision and create a world of greater socioeconomic opportunity by advocating for an inclusive society, building thriving communities, and creating financial tools that enrich lives. Our teams include Corporate Communications, Community Impact and Investment, Community Finance, CRA Strategy and Program Management, Global Government and Policy Affairs, Regulatory Affairs, and our Command Center. We pride ourselves on disrupting the status quo, and GEA teams have unparalleled access to a diverse group of motivated changemakers similarly focused on the ingredients for a better future. The Command Center is looking for an exceptionally bright, multitasking, and self-motivated Principal Associate to join the newly established centralized Events team in GEA, which aims to drive greater efficiency and impact for GEA internal and external events. This role will support the execution and delivery of planned events in 2027, and will help establish a new Center of Excellence on the team to help drive greater standardization around event strategy and execution. This position requires strong project management skills, strong written and verbal communication skills, demonstrated experience and expertise in managing cross-functional teams, and a solid understanding of the latest trends in event production, sponsorships, and reputation building.

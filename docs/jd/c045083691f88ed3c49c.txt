Manager, Enterprise Risk Management Capital One, a Fortune 500 company and one of the nation’s top 10 banks, offers a broad spectrum of financial products and services to consumers, small businesses and commercial clients. Our goal is to create one of the nation’s great banks, and we have the necessary ingredients: a strong balance sheet, resilient businesses, a massive customer franchise, strong analytical capabilities, and great people. We nurture a work environment where people with a variety of thoughts, ideas and backgrounds, guided by our shared values, come together to make Capital One a great company – and a great place to work. Capital One’s Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) organization has responsibility for helping the overall organization identify, manage, and mitigate key risks that may keep the company from achieving its strategic objectives. We are currently seeking a Manager to join the Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) organization to support the execution, maintenance, and enhancement of the Company’s Risk Appetite Framework, including process execution, risk metrics strategy, and technology enablement. The Manager will have the opportunity to apply risk management and analytical skills to some of our highest profile Risk Management projects, including implementing solutions to ensure Capital One’s continued stability and success. This includes bringing unique perspectives and experiences to help us be rigorous, curious, and creative, as well as enabling our organizations to maintain a sustainable and holistic risk profile in order to deliver superior products while also protecting our customers. Additionally, this role is expected to provide oversight and effective challenge across all risk categories (e.g. financial and non-financial risk) and assist in developing key governance analysis and reporting for Senior Management and the executive risk committees / Board of Directors. This manager must possess strong problem solving and analytical skills, attention to detail, process or project management capability, and results driven competencies are essential to succeeding in this role. This individual should feel comfortable working with quantitative and qualitative information and can move from the “gray” area to grounded recommendation in an organized and transparent manner. Specific responsibilities include: Support the execution and day-to-day operations of the enterprise Risk Appetite Framework and risk metric programs Drive the project management and milestone execution for the risk metrics system of record initiative and other strategic team projects Execute and enhance Risk Appetite and risk metric strategies, methodologies, governance, and reporting elements Cultivate data sources to feed metrics/reporting; clearly define requirements for creation and ongoing enhancements of an enterprise risk metric system of record Perform and support detailed validation data analysis to include trending, problem identification, and development/completion of remediation plans Consult on analysis and reporting outcomes to observe trends and provide insights on changes to the Company’s risk environments Collaborate effectively with stakeholders and leaders across multiple organizations to achieve objectives Demonstrate problem-solving and analysis skills with attention to detail Demonstrate strong communication, collaboration, problem solving and influencing skills across various levels of the organization Actively support internal control testing, audits, and regulatory exams Stay abreast of the changing regulatory environment and understand the impacts to Capital One Balance multiple priorities to help drive business value and support team objectives, while managing tasks and activities related to risk management initiatives in a well-managed capacity Basic Qualifications: Bachelor’s Degree or military experience At least 4 years of experience in Project or Process Management At least 4 years of Financial Services or Risk Management experience At least 4 years of experience in metric analysis or governance reporting Preferred Qualifications: Master’s Degree 5+ years of experience in project, process, or framework management 5+ years of Financial Services risk management experience 2+ years of internal strategy or management consulting experience At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position.

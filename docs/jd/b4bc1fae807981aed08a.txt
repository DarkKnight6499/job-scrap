Your Role at Baxter The Baxter Associate Director, Baxter GPS Operational Excellence leads complex, enterprise-level improvement projects across business units and corporate functions. This role exemplifies Baxter GPS leadership and serves as a key enabler of embedding GPS into how Baxter operates. It applies Kaizen methodology, Lean principles, and Six Sigma tools to simplify end-to-end value-stream processes, eliminate waste and non-value-added complexity, solve high-impact business problems, accelerate growth, strengthen customer outcomes, and deliver measurable operational and financial results. The Black Belt partners with business-unit and functional leaders to align stakeholders, implement solutions rapidly, and sustain improvements across organizational boundaries. This role can be based in Deerfield, IL; St. Paul, MN; Bellevue, WA; Skaneateles, NY; or Raleigh, NC and follows a hybrid work model, with regular in-office presence expected at the selected Baxter location. Candidates should expect approximately 50% travel to Baxter sites and business locations to lead Kaizen events, facilitate improvement initiatives, coach leaders, and support operational excellence priorities. What You'll Be Doing Lead high-impact projects and Kaizen events across business units and functions from problem definition through rapid implementation and sustained performance, using Kaizen, DMAIC, and other fit-for-purpose improvement methods. Map, simplify, and improve end-to-end value streams that span Manufacturing, Supply Chain, Finance, Commercial, Quality, Engineering, and other enabling functions by removing handoffs, duplication, delays, rework, and non-value-added steps. Analyze operational, financial, customer, and commercial data to identify root causes, variation, waste, growth opportunities, and performance gaps. Plan and facilitate Kaizen events, value-stream mapping, process-simplification workshops, and structured problem-solving sessions with senior leaders, functional experts, and frontline teams across organizational boundaries. Develop business cases, project charters, implementation plans, performance measures, and control plans; validate operational and financial results with Finance and process owners. Align sponsors and stakeholders across business units and functions, resolve competing priorities, and drive decisions required to advance enterprise initiatives. Train and coach business leaders, Lean leaders, project teams in Kaizen, Lean Six Sigma, change leadership, and practical problem-solving; mentor them in applying continuous process improvement methods, leading effective improvement events, and sustaining results. Exemplify Baxter GPS leadership and partner with business-unit and functional leaders to embed GPS principles, behaviors, improvement methods, management routines, and standard work into how Baxter operates. Communicate project progress, interdependencies, risks, decisions, and results to executive sponsors and senior leaders. Ensure changes support applicable quality, safety, financial-control, privacy, and regulatory requirements. What You’ll Bring ~15+ years of relevant experience. Current Lean Six Sigma Black Belt certification preferred. Bachelor’s degree in engineering, operations, business, supply chain, or a related field. Demonstrated experience leading Kaizen events and end-to-end value-stream simplification projects with measurable outcomes. Strong proficiency in statistical analysis, root cause analysis, process mapping, and change management. Ability to influence stakeholders and lead teams without direct authority. Clear written and verbal communication skills. Flexibility to travel based on business needs, potentially up to 50%. What Will Help You Stand Out Experience leading enterprise or multi-business-unit transformation initiatives in a complex, matrixed organization. Broad business experience across several functions, such as Manufacturing, Supply Chain, Finance, Commercial, Quality, Engineering, or shared services. Experience in medical devices, pharmaceuticals, or another regulated industry. Experience coaching Green Belts and building continuous improvement capability. Familiarity with operational, financial, commercial, and customer performance measures, including quality, yield, cycle time, productivity, inventory, cost, revenue, and service. What Success Looks Like Success in this role will include delivering sustained improvements across business units and functions, simplifying end-to-end value streams, shortening lead times, reducing handoffs and non-value-added steps, and delivering verified financial and operational benefits. You’ll also contribute to stronger customer and commercial outcomes, effective adoption of new ways of working, and increased Kaizen and problem-solving capability across the organization.

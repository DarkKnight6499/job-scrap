The Global Liquidity Product Team in GPS is responsible for managing over $600B of corporate and commercial deposits across ~40 different currencies. The team covers deposit strategy, deposit pricing, deposit appetite, product development and product management - including sweeps, zero balance accounts, physical cash concentration and multi-currency notional pooling. The team also supports large/complex liquidity deals, drives liquidity thought leadership, analyzes the impact of monetary policy on wholesale banking and leads the GPS response to regulatory changes/inquiries. The North America Liquidity Product Management team is responsible for delivering innovative liquidity solutions that help clients optimize cash positioning, improve operational efficiency, and manage working capital across complex domestic and international structures. This role serves as the Product Manager for North America Cash Concentration capabilities while helping shape the strategic direction of the broader Global Liquidity Platform. The successful candidate will be responsible for balancing the management of a mature and highly scaled product portfolio with the development of future capabilities that support evolving client needs, industry trends, regulatory expectations, and enterprise strategic priorities.

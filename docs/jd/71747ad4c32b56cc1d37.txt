Become a part of our caring community The Financial Analytics Professional 2 manages data to inform decisions about daily operations, strategic planning, and business performance. Assignments vary and often require interpretation, independent judgment, and selection of the appropriate course of action. Within the Medicare Risk Adjustment Finance Department, the Financial Analytics Professional 2 develops reports for bids and budgets, assesses changes in Medicare regulations, and supports Stars financial reporting. This role produces, updates, and improves financial models while building strong relationships with business partners. Success requires technical proficiency, problem-solving ability, and a willingness to learn. Collaborates with colleagues in Stars Analytics and Medicare Risk Adjustment Translates complex financial and nonfinancial data into concise, presentation-ready insights that support management decisions Extracts raw data from multiple databases and summarizes it efficiently Develops and maintains process documentation Uses SAS, SQL, and Excel regularly Key Traits Supportive, team-oriented, and eager to teach and learn Analytical, research-focused, and attentive to data and detail Passionate about driving forward big, creative ideas and continuous improvement Curious and open-minded Use your skills to make an impact Required Qualifications Bachelor's Degree 3+ years technical experience Programming skills in languages such as SQL and Python Experience in compiling, modeling, interpreting and analyzing data in order to identify, explain, influence variances and trends Explain variances and trends and enhance modeling techniques Experience in managing data to support and influence decisions on day-to-day operations, strategic planning and specific business performance issues Must be passionate about contributing to an organization focused on continuously improving consumer experiences Preferred Qualifications Experience with Power BI and Databricks Financial or actuarial background Medicare Advantage and/or Stars experience Additional Information Work Style: This is a remote position.

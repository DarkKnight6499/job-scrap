What You Will Do The Logistics Team member will be responsible for ensuring all product inbound, stocked and shipped is as accurate as far as case dimensions, case quantity, inner pack quantity, selling units, I2 of 5, UPC, description and packaging. The Planning/Logistics Team Member will audit and inspect product on a daily basis to determine vendor compliance as well as recommend new packaging/handling requirements to improve safety and damage control. The Planning/Logistics Team Member will ensure all stock and nonstock returns are completed accurately and timely as well as the responsibility for communicating all logistic issues to the General Office. In addition, this position may require working in other departments as needed. Any other duties deemed necessary by the Manager on duty or the Planning Coach. Travel Requirements: This role does not require regular travel.

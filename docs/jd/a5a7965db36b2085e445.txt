As an Engineer, Field Applications (New College Graduate), you will be placed in a twelve-to-twenty-four-month developmental rotation program that offers real-world experience learning about ADI’s technologies, customer support, and opportunity generation – providing an excellent foundation for technical expertise and soft skills growth. Participants will gain exposure to the broad portfolio of ADI technologies before eventually focusing primarily on power management and automotive battery management systems solutions while building customer engagement skills, and business acumen. During the program, you will go through a progressive curriculum where you will receive professional development opportunities and mentorship before integrating into the worldwide sales team and deploying to your target market.

An integral part of the Production & Operations Finance team, the Senior Financial Analyst helps manage the Rockefeller Center Operations (RCO) budgeting, forecasting, and reporting for Capital and Space Allocations as well as ad-hoc financial analysis. Here you can: Consolidate and report actuals, quarterly estimates, strategic and operating plans (LRP and Budget), and related variances vs budget. Coordinate monthly closes for the Finance Manager, including helping to process recurring journal entries and helping to manage the actuals vs. budget reconciliation. Perform standard and special analyses that provide insightful views into the operations and financial performance for RCO, as well as a point of contact for resolution of financial questions with operating and FP&A partners. Ownership and maintenance of Facilities space allocations Create ad-hoc financial reports and models to support evolving business needs. Working closely with RCO Project Management team to build capital budget plans Creating/Updating/Maintaining files to assist with budgeting & tracking of expenses Looking for opportunities to build/enhance current models Answering ad hoc requests as necessary Maintaining up to date finance process documentation Posting monthly/quarterly allocations, cross charges, & intercompany transfers Here you'll need: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting or related field 2+ years of experience in Finance Must be proficient with MS Office Suite products – particularly advanced Excel and PowerPoint skills Knowledge of SAP or similar application preferred What we'll look for: Exceptional financial modeling and analysis skills, with experience in forecasting, budgeting, and variance analysis Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to communicate summarized and concise financial information to key stakeholders including operations, controllership, FP&A, and senior leadership Team Player with the ability to function in a cross functional environment and willingness to jump into projects to meet department goals Ability to quickly grasp new concepts and problem solve independently Excellent organization and time management skills; ability to deliver on multiple project assignments, meet tight deadlines and be effective under pressure Outstanding interpersonal skills Continuous improvement attitude, ability to seek out and implement best practices Ability to work in a fast-paced, dynamic environment Experience in the Media or Facilities industry is a plus Hybrid : This position currently has a hybrid schedule, which requires contributing from the office a minimum of four days per week.

We are seeking a talented individual to join our Investment Fixed Income Manager Research Team at Mercer. This role will be based in Chicago, IL & Minneapolis, MN. This is a hybrid role that has a requirement of working at least three days a week in the office. This is an opportunity to participate and undertake detailed due diligence of fixed income strategies, ultimately proposing strategies for Mercer's recommended list by synthesizing Mercer's view through a forward looking research report that articulates why Mercer believes a manager's strategy will outperform its respective benchmark over a full market cycle. In this role, you will have access to a wide variety of asset managers via participation in meetings.

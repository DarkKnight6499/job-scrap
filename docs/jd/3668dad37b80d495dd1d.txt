Industry/Sector Pharma and Life Sciences Specialism Assurance Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Minneapolis Assurance Senior Associate - Health Industries you will focus on building meaningful client connections and developing your financial statement audit skills within our Audit and Assurance practice. As a Senior Associate you will analyze complex issues, manage assigned workstreams, and mentor junior team members while using critical thinking, professional skepticism, and a clear understanding of the broader business context. In this role at PwC, you will contribute to audits of financial statements, internal controls, and governance processes, while supporting work that evaluates compliance with regulations and helps strengthen the credibility of financial information. Responsibilities - Analyzing complex financial statement audit areas within healthcare and life sciences client engagements - Executing assigned workstreams for internal controls, financial reporting, and regulatory compliance testing - Reviewing supporting documentation, journal entries, and account activity to identify misstatements or control gaps - Interpreting accounting standards, auditing methodologies, and firm guidance to support audit procedures - Applying data analysis, digital tools, and visualization methods to develop audit insights and document findings - Coordinating status updates and client requests while building meaningful relationships across engagement teams - Mentoring junior team members on audit tasks, documentation, and issue tracking - Communicating observations, risks, and recommendations clearly to senior team members and clients What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 2 years of experience - Before starting full-time at PwC, meet the educational requirements to be eligible to sit for the CPA exam in your intended state of employment. What Sets You Apart - Preference for a Bachelor's degree in at least one of the following fields of study: Accounting - At least one of the following: Current pursuit of or an active CPA license - Demonstrating experience in audit services for health industries clients - Applying accounting and financial reporting standards within client engagements - Interpreting financial data to inform insights and recommendations - Using auditing methodologies to analyze complex concepts and workstreams - Building client relationships while guiding junior team members Travel Requirements Up to 40% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $72,000 - $184,440.

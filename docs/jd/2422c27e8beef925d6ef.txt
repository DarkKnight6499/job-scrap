Your Opportunity At Schwab, you’re empowered to make an impact on your career. Here, innovative thought meets creative problem solving, helping us transform the finance industry together. As an Associate Regional Bond Specialist , you’ll support a team of Fixed Income specialists serving High Net Worth and Ultra High Net Worth clients by helping uncover growth opportunities, develop investment strategies, and deliver exceptional client experiences. You'll partner closely with branch teams, support client prospecting efforts, and contribute to Fixed Income events and business development initiatives. Using your Fixed Income expertise, you'll help build and execute bond strategies, including ladders and barbells, while supporting client analysis and portfolio discussions. Through formal training and hands-on experience, you'll deepen your knowledge of Fixed Income strategy construction, client prospecting, portfolio analysis, event planning, and business development. At Schwab, we value continuous learning, growth mindset, skill-building, and career development, providing opportunities to strengthen your expertise while making a meaningful impact. We believe in the importance of in-office collaboration and fully intend for the selected candidate for this role to work on site in the specified locations.

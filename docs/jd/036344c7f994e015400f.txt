General Summary: Supports management in the following areas: identifying and acquiring ground-up development opportunities, evaluating projects, supporting ongoing development initiatives and supporting ongoing regional operations and business needs. Summary of Key Duties and Responsibilities: Acquisitions & Investments: Conducts market research and analyzes trends across each of the region’s core product types and markets, and presents findings to senior team members to identify new opportunities Performs ROI analysis, including cash flows, IRRs, waterfalls, etc., for potential acquisitions of new properties for development, redevelopment or expansion of current properties. Additionally, supports senior-level team members in evaluating exit opportunities once a project has achieved the forecasted business plan Spearheads the collection of due diligence materials to evaluate site feasibility. Assists in the review of purchase & sale agreements, legal documents and other materials Assists with drafting investment committee materials and business plans for new acquisitions and ongoi ng dev elopment projects Development Execution: Assists in the analysis of prospective tenant leases, project financing, joint ventures, property management and other special projects/transactions Supports preparation of debt and equity offering memoranda for projects Drafts, updates and evaluates operating expense, cash flow and income projections against established budgets and operating proformas Observes ongoing construction meetings with contractors, architects, and project engineers Coordinates with Development Accounting team to manage the loan draw process Ongoing Regional Operations: Coordinates with internal departments including Design and Construction, Accounting, Business Analysis, Treasury, Legal and Tax to collect market, economic, and financial data. Works closely with these departments to prepare budgets, project financials and determine financing options Assists the Development Accounting team in monitoring development cost performance Assists in the development of proposed annual and long-range operating and capital expenditure budgets and plans for RGDC and RGDC projects Works with real estate and construction colleagues regionally and nationally to develop and evaluate strategic opportunities for Rockefeller Group's development portfolio Develops, drafts and prepares reports and presentations to support RGDC's corporate asset strategy and real estate initiatives, strategic studies and lease transactions Maintains a regional market database for product types applicable to the region including office, multifamily and industrial. Reviews and makes recommendations based on changes in market fundamentals, supply pipelines and shifts in micro and macro economic conditions Analyzes variances of forecasted and actual results to determine the accuracy of projections and the financial status of projects Anticipates, recognizes, and informs management of business issues and opportunities that may affect project facilitation Prepares weekly and quarterly internal and external project reporting Performs other ad hoc duties and special projects as assigned Education Requirements: Bachelor’s Degree required, with preferred field of education in business, real estate, economics, architecture, engineering and/or finance. Experience / Knowledge / Skills Requirements: 0-2 years of experience in real estate development, finance, capital markets, brokerage, or related field, including internships. If entry level, prior internship in related field strongly preferred.

Our globally distributed team of people managers and individual contributors monitors, prepares, reviews, files, and escalates regulatory reports across regions and reporting regimes. A key focus is the global shareholder disclosure program, covering substantial shareholder, beneficial ownership, short-position, and takeover obligations, alongside Form PF and NFA filings. We work closely with Legal, Compliance, Technology, Operations, Data, Risk, Audit, and external vendors. Together we strengthen controls and deliver accurate, timely, technology-enabled reporting. The Rol e The Director, Global Regulatory Reporting is a strategic leadership role accountable for overseeing, delivering, and continuously improving Invesco's global regulatory reporting program. You will lead multiple related teams, including people managers. You will set priorities, allocate resources, and develop the plans and policies that drive team performance and results. You will resolve complex, multi-dimensional regulatory, data, and operational issues. You will also turn evolving requirements into scalable operating models and technology-enabled solutions. You will influence senior executives, vendors, and regulators to support the global Compliance program and the firm's strategic priorities. Key responsibilities: Leading and developing a global team of people managers and individual contributors. This includes setting direction, priorities, resourcing, backup coverage, and succession plans, and holding teams accountable for quality and timeliness. Owning the global shareholder disclosure program (substantial shareholder, beneficial ownership, short-position, and takeover filings). You will ensure filings are complete, accurate, timely, and well evidenced. Overseeing the preparation, review, and submission of Form PF and NFA filings, including CPO-PQR and CTA-PR. Providing senior guidance on regulatory interpretation and implementing new requirements with Legal and regional Compliance. You will also lead regulatory engagement on incidents, examinations, and remediation. Maintaining a risk-based control framework covering quality assurance, testing, and issue management. You will promptly escalate material exposures, control failures, and recurring issues. Partnering with Data Management, Technology, and external vendors to define requirements, resolve data issues, and oversee regulatory monitoring systems. Driving regulatory-change, remediation, automation, and responsibly governed AI-enabled initiatives with disciplined project governance. Influencing senior stakeholders, representing GRR in governance forums and firm-wide strategic initiatives, and fostering an inclusive, accountable, high-performing culture. Experience required: 10+ years of experience in investment management, financial services, regulatory reporting, Compliance, Legal, Operations, or a related control function. This includes substantial experience leading global or remote teams and people managers. Deep working knowledge of global shareholder disclosure regimes, including substantial shareholder, beneficial ownership, short-position, takeover, aggregation, attribution, and threshold-monitoring requirements across major jurisdictions. Proven experience overseeing recurring filings, complex reviews, control design, issue escalation, regulator or audit responses, and remediation in a deadline-driven environment. Strong understanding of asset-management processes, funds and accounts, position and transaction data, and regulatory reporting systems, data flows, and lineage. Experience partnering with technology teams and external vendors. This includes translating regulatory needs into clear business, data, control, and testing requirements, and implementing automation while keeping appropriate human oversight. Demonstrated ability to influence senior executives, vendors, and regulators in a matrix organization without relying solely on direct authority. Excellent analytical, problem-solving, judgment, written communication, and executive presentation skills, with the ability to resolve complex, multi-dimensional issues. Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience required. An advanced degree or relevant certification is preferred, as is experience with Form PF and NFA filings (CPO-PQR, CTA-PR) and shareholder disclosure monitoring systems.

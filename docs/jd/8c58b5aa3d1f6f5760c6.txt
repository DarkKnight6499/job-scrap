The following position is to join our RTX Enterprise Services team: The ITSM ServiceNow Business Analyst will be responsible for the capture and implementation of requirements for the ITSM product area, with a primary focus on Incident, Problem, and Change Management. This role involves working closely with Stakeholders and IT teams to understand business requirements, translating them into acceptance criteria for development, and ensuring the effective implementation of those requirements into the ServiceNow platform. Why This Role Matters ServiceNow is essential to how our organization delivers and supports technology services. You will join a collaborative ServiceNow team that includes architects, developers, technical product managers, and business analysts, and contribute to the ongoing evolution of enterprise-scale implementation. Your work will directly influence: ITSM process efficiency and automation The quality and consistency of Incident, Problem, and Change workflows User experience for thousands of internal customers Platform stability, scalability, and operational excellence Modernization initiatives including workflow optimization, improved automation, and enhanced reporting What You Will Do Partner with stakeholders to gather and define requirements aligned with ITSM objectives Create detailed user stories and acceptance criteria reflecting business needs and technical considerations Ask probing questions to ensure requirements are complete, feasible, and aligned with platform capabilities Develop test scripts, validate story completion, and support defect analysis and reporting Collaborate closely with architects and developers to ensure alignment with ServiceNow design standards and best practices Maintain a holistic understanding of business workflows and ServiceNow ITSM module behavior Create knowledge articles and documentation for both technical and business audiences Support ongoing platform enhancements, continuous improvement efforts, and other duties as required Qualifications You Must Have Typically requires a degree in Science,Technology, Engineering or Mathematics (STEM) and a minimum of 5 years of prior relevant experience unless prohibited by local laws/regulations. 4+ years of experience as a Business Analyst, including at least 2 years of direct experience supporting or working with the ServiceNow platform. Strong understanding of ServiceNow ITSM processes, platform best practices, and hands-on experience with Incident, Problem, and Change Management modules. Proven ability to collaborate with SMEs, business stakeholders, developers, and internal customers to gather requirements, define functional specifications, and translate business needs into technical solutions. Excellent analytical, critical-thinking, and problem-solving abilities, with proficiency in Microsoft Excel and Visio for data analysis, documentation, and process mapping.

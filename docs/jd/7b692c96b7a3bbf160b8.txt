We are looking for an experienced Product Manager to join a team expanding Zelle beyond P2P payments—a groundbreaking pay-by-bank payment method that will enable merchants and banks to offer customers instant, secure account transfers, bill payments and checkout powered by the Zelle network and real-time payment rails. This is a 0-to-1 product build that will bring Zelle to new environments, challenging traditional card and payment networks with a superior, bank-direct alternative. The ideal candidate brings deep expertise in commerce payments—including e-commerce, point-of-sale, and merchant payment acceptance—and thrives at the intersection of product strategy, network design, and customer experience. You will help define the data, features, and user experiences unique to commerce use cases. If you want to shape the future of how America pays and moves money, this is the role. As a Product Manager in Chase Banking Payments on the Zelle Expansion team you will define and deliver new payment experiences that expand Zelle beyond person-to-person payments. You will partner across product, engineering, design, operations, risk, and external stakeholders to create a bank-direct payment method that supports account transfers, e-commerce, point-of-sale (POS), and bill payment experiences. You will translate strategy into clear roadmaps, customer journeys, and measurable outcomes.

Role Summary The Senior Finance Manager provides strategic financial planning and analysis leadership for the Consumer Lending business line, partnering closely with senior leaders to support financial performance, resource allocation, and business strategy. This role leads forecasting, budgeting, financial reporting, and financial modeling efforts while overseeing a team of financial analysts. The position offers the opportunity to influence key business decisions, drive process improvements, and strengthen financial planning capabilities across a high-impact area of the organization. Key Accountabilities Lead financial planning and analysis activities for the Consumer Lending business, including forecasting, budgeting, long-range planning, and financial reporting. Partner with business leaders to evaluate financial performance, strategic initiatives, investments, and business opportunities. Oversee the development and enhancement of complex financial models to support decision-making and performance management. Manage and develop a team of financial analysts, fostering a high-performing and collaborative environment. Present financial insights, recommendations, and executive-level reporting materials to senior leadership. Drive continuous improvement of financial planning processes, forecasting methodologies, and analytical capabilities. Serve as a key liaison between finance and Consumer Lending leadership to align financial strategies with business objectives. Establish and reinforce consistent financial planning standards, best practices, and reporting accuracy across assigned areas.

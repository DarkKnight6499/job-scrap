As Senior Financial Analyst, you will be responsible for the division wide reporting and analysis processes with a focus on cashflow, capital investment, and balance sheet. This role supports creation/management of annual budgets with periodic forecast updates. You will provide leadership, oversight, coordination, and planning, to execute the financial planning processes and ensure timeliness, accuracy, and integrity of financial information. This role has high levels of visibility to executive management. The main functions of this position include (1) the coordination of ADC’s cashflow and balance sheet financial planning activities including the compilation, analysis, and distribution of management review packages, (2) preparation and analysis of capital projects, (3) communication between ADC’s Division Headquarters, various ADC financial teams around the world, and Corporate Finance. The Senior Financial Analyst is responsible for providing analytics, explanations of key performance drivers, management presentations, and business support to enable informed decision making by executive management at the ADC, Medical Device and Corporate levels. This is an onsite opportunity in Alameda, CA. What You'll Do Support Finance Management on key projects and analyses. Integral player in development of annual plans and periodic updates as needed.

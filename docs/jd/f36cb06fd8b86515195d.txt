The Opportunity The Compliance Risk Manager Senior – Broker/Dealer provides independent second-line oversight and guidance to help ensure business products, services, and processes comply with regulatory requirements. This role partners closely with the business and key stakeholders to identify and manage compliance risk while promoting a strong culture of compliance, integrity, and sound professional judgment. The ideal candidate will bring Series 7 and 24 licenses, experience working with regulators, knowledge of FINRA rules and requirements, and financial services testing or audit experience. We offer a flexible work environment that requires an individual to be in the office 4 days per week. This position can be based in one of the following locations: San Antonio, TX, Plano, TX, Phoenix, AZ, Colorado Springs, CO, Charlotte, NC, or Tampa, FL Relocation assistance is not available for this position. What you'll do: Coordinate market conduct examinations and data calls. Provides guidance to the business to ensure documents, projects, processes or product initiatives align with regulatory and legal requirements and enterprise policy. Synthesizes analyses, identifies root cause(s) of potential compliance risk, provides recommendations that influence business solutions and validates resolution. Reviews implementation planning for new laws and regulations and ensures appropriate parties have been engaged. Conveys information and position clearly, concisely, logically and in an organized manner. Implements compliance risk management activities in accordance with enterprise compliance policies and procedures. Collaborates with various department in the areas of Underwriting, Claims, State Management, Enterprise Licensing and Legal. What you have: Bachelor's degree; OR 4 years of relevant education and/or experience. Experiences that will support your success: 6 or more years relevant experience in compliance, risk, legal or audit within the financial services or insurance industry or specialized technical fields directly related to the role. Demonstrated experience working in a highly regulated environment. Advanced knowledge in a specific area of compliance laws, regulations, and regulatory expectations. Demonstrated ability to apply advanced knowledge of regulatory compliance to consult and provide insight and guidance. Advanced knowledge of Microsoft Office tools to include Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. Experience providing guidance and best practices to team members. What sets you apart: Securities license 7 and 24. Experience working and communicating with regulators Familiarity with FINRA rules and requirements. Testing or Audit experience in financial services US military experience through military service or a military spouse/domestic partner Compensation range: The salary range for this position is: $103,450 - $197,730 .

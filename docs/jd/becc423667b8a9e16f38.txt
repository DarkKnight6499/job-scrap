Position Summary This role will support the Aetna Business Operations consolidated reporting team. You will be responsible for consolidating our financials, creating the management presentations and/or talking points for month close, forecast and budget as well as our multi-year strategic plan. You will also be responsible for driving standardization across the overall organization in the way work is done. This role will work closely with the Aetna Business Operations finance teams to ensure that our reporting is both accurate and timely and that we are getting to the why behind the key drivers. The successful candidate should exhibit strong communication skills, and the ability to consolidate/report results to business partners and management. This individual will be expected to be able to multi-task and prioritize multiple activities and responsibilities in a fast-paced environment, with the ability to work independently within a guidance framework defined by management. Fundamental components: Assist with coordination of planning and analysis of projects of varying nature and complexity which may include financial metrics, process improvement initiatives. Proficiency with Anaplan, Hyperion and S4 Hana.

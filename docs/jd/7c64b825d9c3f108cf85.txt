Manager FP&A Retail Strategic The Manager, FP&A Retail Strategic, is responsible for supporting the financial planning, forecasting, reporting, and analysis activities for the Strategic Retail business. This role serves as a key finance business partner to commercial and retail leaders, providing actionable insights that support business performance, profitability, customer growth, and strategic decision-making. Role Responsibilities Lead forecasting, budgeting, and operating plan processes supporting the Strategic Retail business. Perform detailed financial, profitability, customer, and performance analyses to support business objectives. Serve as a day-to-day finance business partner to Retail and Sales leaders, providing insights and recommendations to drive business performance. Develop and maintain financial models, forecasts, scenario analyses, and management reporting tools. Monitor business performance and identify key risks, opportunities, and emerging trends. Analyze revenue, margin, expenses, and investment performance and provide recommendations to improve business results. Support customer profitability analysis and strategic account planning initiatives. Prepare periodic financial reports, business reviews, forecasts, and presentations for leadership teams. Collaborate with Sales, Retail, Operations, Accounting, and other cross-functional stakeholders to support strategic initiatives. Identify and implement process improvement opportunities that enhance efficiency, reporting accuracy, and business support capabilities. Participate in Finance and CFO organization projects and special business initiatives. Provide guidance, coaching, and oversight to team members as applicable. Ensure compliance with company financial policies, internal controls, and reporting requirements. Basic Requirements Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, Economics, Mathematics, or a related field. 5+ years of experience in Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A), Corporate Finance, Accounting, Business Analysis, or a related field; or an equivalent combination of education, training, and experience. Experience supporting budgeting, forecasting, financial reporting, and performance analysis processes. Experience developing and maintaining financial models and business analyses. Knowledge of advanced analytics, automation, and AI tools. Preferred MBA, CPA, CFA, or other relevant advanced credential. Experience supporting retail, commercial, sales, consumer products, or customer-facing organizations. Experience supporting strategic retail accounts or large customer portfolios. Experience with Essbase, Hyperion, SAP, Oracle, Power BI, Tableau, or similar planning and reporting systems.

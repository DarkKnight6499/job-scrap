Job Description: Technology Delivery Leader, Workplace Investing Portfolio Management The Role As the Technology Delivery Leader, Workplace Investing Portfolio Management , you will lead portfolio management, delivery reporting, governance, and executive visibility across the Workplace Investing technology portfolio. You are passionate about transforming portfolio data into meaningful insights that help leaders make informed decisions and effectively manage strategic priorities. You bring a strong blend of portfolio leadership, technology delivery experience, and analytical capability, with the ability to connect data from multiple sources and deliver clear, actionable reporting to senior leaders. You are motivated by improving transparency, reducing manual processes, and building scalable reporting solutions that provide real-time visibility into portfolio performance, financial results, risks, dependencies, and strategic outcomes. You will partner closely with Technology and Executive Leadership to ensure delivery progress, financial performance, risks, dependencies, and business outcomes are clearly communicated and well understood. This role will lead portfolio governance activities, executive reporting, Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs), Quarterly Technology Reviews (QTRs), and strategic communications, while driving the adoption of dashboards, automation, and modern reporting capabilities, including AI-enabled tools where appropriate. Technical Expertise Build and maintain portfolio reporting solutions utilizing TrackIT data, Portfolio Central, financial systems, and other enterprise or Workplace Investing data sources. Design and deliver executive dashboards that provide leadership with timely visibility into portfolio performance, delivery health, financial metrics, accomplishments, risks, and key milestones. Leverage automation, reporting tools, and analytics platforms to improve reporting efficiency, data quality, and decision-making. Partner with technology teams to identify, acquire, integrate, and govern portfolio data required to support strategic reporting and executive insights. Drive the transition from manual PowerPoint-based reporting to scalable dashboard and reporting solutions. Utilize analytics, automation, and AI-assisted capabilities to identify trends, risks, dependencies, and opportunities across the portfolio. Planning and Execution Accountable for the orchestration, preparation, and execution of portfolio-level governance and reporting activities, including QBRs, QTRs, and executive delivery reviews. Lead the development and continuous improvement of a portfolio management framework that provides a single source of truth across Workplace Investing technology initiatives. Establish and enhance reporting processes that deliver timely, accurate, and actionable portfolio insights for executive stakeholders. Drive accountability, transparency, and effective execution across the Workplace Investing technology portfolio. Identify opportunities to improve portfolio processes through automation, analytics, streamlined workflows, and data-driven decision-making. Support large, cross-functional programs by coordinating delivery updates, identifying dependencies, escalating risks, and ensuring alignment on strategic priorities. Communication and Collaboration Provide concise, executive-ready updates on portfolio status, accomplishments, risks, financial performance, and upcoming milestones. Develop strategic portfolio communications and narratives that translate complex delivery information into meaningful business insights. Create and maintain executive dashboards and reporting packages that support leadership decision-making. Partner with program and delivery leaders to ensure accurate and timely reporting of portfolio performance. Communicate portfolio priorities, delivery plans, roadmap adjustments, and strategic outcomes to leaders across the organization. Facilitate alignment across multiple workstreams and stakeholder groups to drive successful delivery outcomes. Support senior leadership by delivering forward-looking portfolio summaries, strategic recommendations, executive briefings, and reporting materials.

Blackstone is the world’s largest alternative asset manager. Blackstone seeks to deliver compelling returns for institutional and individual investors by strengthening the companies in which the firm invests. Further information is available at www.blackstone.com . Follow @blackstone on LinkedIn , X (Twitter) , and Instagram . Blackstone Credit & Insurance (“BXCI”) is one of the world’s leading credit investors. Our investments span the credit markets, including private investment grade, asset based lending, public investment grade and high yield, sustainable resources, infrastructure debt, broadly syndicated loans, collateralized loan obligations, direct lending and opportunistic credit. We seek to generate attractive risk-adjusted returns for institutional and individual investors by offering companies capital needed to strengthen and grow their businesses. BXCI is also a leading provider of investment management services for insurers, helping those companies better deliver for policyholders through our world-class capabilities in investment grade private credit. The Liquid Credit Strategies Team (“LCS”) manages $123B in AUM across diversified portfolios of fixed income investments, primarily consisting of senior secured floating rate bank loans, high yield and investment grade corporate bonds, and debt and equity tranches of Collateralized Loan Obligations ("CLOs"). The group manages and sub-advises a wide array of funds and accounts in the US and Europe, including CLOs, separately managed accounts, commingled funds, and registered funds. Job Description & Responsibilities Blackstone Credit & Insurance is seeking a Senior Associate to support the Restructuring team within the Liquid Credit business. This team is responsible for leading liability management and balance sheet restructuring initiatives across Blackstone’s Liquid Credit platform. Responsibilities will include: Analyzing liability management and balance sheet alternatives Underwriting and structuring new-money investments related to restructuring transactions Interfacing with restructuring advisors and other lenders Conducting financial analysis, due diligence, industry research, and competitive analysis Analyzing legal documentation and drafting memoranda for internal and external use Qualifications Blackstone seeks to hire individuals who are highly motivated, have sound judgement and have demonstrated excellence in prior endeavors. In addition to these skills, the successful candidate should meet the following qualifications: 2+ years of relevant work experience in restructuring, distressed investing, or special situations Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Business Administration, Economics, or a related field Exceptional financial modeling and analytical skills Deep understanding of credit markets, particularly restructuring and leveraged finance Strong initiative and motivation, with a demonstrated ability to develop creative solutions to complex problems Proven track record of collaborating effectively in fast-paced investment environments, with excellent communication, teamwork, and problem-solving skills The duties and responsibilities described here are not exhaustive and additional assignments, duties, or responsibilities may be required of this position.

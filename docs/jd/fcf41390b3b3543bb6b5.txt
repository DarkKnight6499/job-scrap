JOB OPENING #: 9828 POSITION TITLE: Investment Analyst DEPARTMENT: Investment Management - Equity - POSITION SPECIFICATIONS: Bachelor's degree required; advanced degree (Ph.D., M.D., Pharm.D., M.S., or equivalent) in Biology, Biotechnology, Biochemistry, Chemistry, Pharmacy, Biomedical Sciences, or a related scientific discipline is highly valued. 3-7 years of experience in biotechnology or pharmaceutical equity research, healthcare investing, investment banking, consulting, venture capital, or a scientific research environment with demonstrated investment interest. Strong understanding of biotechnology, drug development, clinical trial design, regulatory pathways, and pharmaceutical markets. Experience building and maintaining financial models and conducting valuation analysis. Advanced proficiency in Excel and financial modeling. Demonstrated passion for investing and capital markets. Exceptional analytical, research, communication, and presentation skills. Ability to synthesize complex scientific information into actionable investment conclusions. MAJOR DUTIES: Conduct fundamental equity research on biotechnology and pharmaceutical companies, with a focus on identifying attractive growth investment opportunities. Evaluate clinical trial data, scientific literature, medical research, regulatory developments, and intellectual property to assess company prospects and investment risks. Develop and maintain detailed financial models, earnings forecasts, valuation analyses, and industry frameworks. Engage with management teams, physicians, scientists, key opinion leaders, and other industry participants to deepen investment insights. Attend biotechnology, pharmaceutical, medical, and scientific conferences and maintain awareness of emerging technologies, therapeutic areas, and industry trends. Analyze company financial statements, competitive positioning, and strategic developments. Monitor industry data, clinical milestones, and regulatory events relevant to portfolio holdings and investment candidates. Prepare investment recommendations and presentations for portfolio management discussions. HOURS/LOCATION: 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. (Overtime as required) New York City (Hybrid) EXPLANATORY COMMENTS: Self-motivated, intellectually curious, and capable of managing multiple research priorities simultaneously. Willingness/ability to travel to industry conferences, scientific meetings, and company visits. The annual base salary range for this position is $125,000 to $225,000.

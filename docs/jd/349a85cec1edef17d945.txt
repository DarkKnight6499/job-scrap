Job Summary Assist with the support of the O&M and/or Capital budgeting process, reprojections, variance/other standard/ad-hoc reporting and/or the improvement requisition process for a business unit(s), corporate group(s), operating company(ies) and/or for AEP Consolidated. Includes providing assistance with related planning, budgeting and reporting applications and the billing/allocation processes. Job Description Support the O&M and Capital budgeting process, reprojections, variance/other standard/ad-hoc reporting and the improvement requisition process for a business unit(s), corporate group(s), operating company(ies) and/or for AEP Consolidated. Includes providing support related to planning, budgeting and reporting applications and the billing/allocation processes. What you'll do: Support the planning and budgeting activities for the O&M and Capital resource allocation process; including long-range forecasts, detail budgets, re-projections and contingency plans. Prepare monthly and quarterly variance reports Prepare ad-hoc reports as needed to support analysis. Support decisions and analysis affecting operations and maintenance activities, capital investment, customer service, and profitability objections. Assist in routing and approval process for Improvement Requisition; ensuring that Authorization Policy and IR Procedures are followed Engage in developing and improving standardized processes surrounding budgeting, forecasting, reporting, and improvement requisitions. Perform special studies, analysis, investigations, and assignments as may be required. Provide assistance in responding to regulatory and other data requests. Assist in Financial Forecasting process alongside OPCO / Business Units within I&M it relates to Capital and O&M expenditures to resolve any potential issues.

Position Summary *This is a fully remote position open to any state on time zone in the US* The FP&A Analyst on the Medicaid Finance Team has a supporting role in budgeting, forecasting, and analysis to drive corporate decisions. The position is responsible for financial analysis of SG&A Expenses, providing transparency to Business Partners across Health Plans, Shared Services, and Medicaid/Finance Leadership. The Analyst supports in reporting results by translating financial data into visualizations and presentation materials. The position reports directly to the FP&A Senior Manager within Medicaid Finance. Successful candidates are curious, take initiative, exhibit attention to detail, collaborate well with team members and work effectively toward deadlines. Key responsibilities for the Analyst include but are not limited to: Supports in financial planning, budgeting, and forecasting of SG&A Expenses. Provides transparency on FTE and Vendor spend, and savings initiatives. Create variance analysis reports and financial models. Presents detailed monthly financial reports to managers. Includes measures to ensure accuracy and efficiency. Escalates financial issues to management. Supports with day-to-day SG&A deliverables and ad-hoc requests Documents processes with detailed procedures.

Job Summary: The Lead Finance Specialist is a senior member of the FP&A team and plays a critical role in delivering high‑quality financial analysis, supporting Bank-wide planning and budgeting, and strengthening the organization’s ability to use data effectively in decision‑making. The role combines strong foundational financial management skills with modern analytical and automation capabilities. This position works independently, partners with senior leaders, and may lead cross‑functional initiatives. The Lead Finance Specialist is responsible for conducting complex financial analysis, interpreting results, and developing insights that guide strategic and operational decisions with clarity and certainty. While financial management remains the core of the role, the position also incorporates responsibilities related to improving how the organization collects, analyzes, and uses data. Success in this role requires strong finance and accounting knowledge, the ability to communicate clearly, and comfort using modern analytical tools that enhance FP&A processes. The individual will collaborate across business and technology teams and help bring greater consistency, efficiency, and insight to financial reporting and forecasting. Principal Accountabilities Financial Management and FP&A (foundational responsibilities) Conducts and oversees complex financial analysis of operating expenses, capital spending, workforce planning, and forecasts. Leads the development, review, and consolidation of Bank-wide budgets and multi‑year financial plans. Reviews and interprets business plans and budgets, summarizes key issues, and works with business leaders in support of their operational execution. Advises business areas on financial management practices, policy interpretation, and the implementation of new programs. Ensures strong financial controls, accurate documentation, and integrity of financial data throughout FP&A processes. Data-Enabled Financial Insights & Process Improvement Uses effective AI prompting techniques to gather information, refine analyses, and generate clear, higher‑quality financial insights using AI‑enabled tools. Analyzes data from ERP and financial systems to support budgeting, forecasting, and strategic decision‑making. Uses data visualization to clearly communicate trends and risks. Applies modern analytical techniques—such as trend analysis, scenario modeling, and pattern identification—to enhance financial insights. Uses emerging tools, including AI‑supported capabilities, to improve forecasting, reporting, and insight generation. Automates recurring reports and processes to improve efficiency and reliability. Promotes strong data stewardship, including accuracy, consistency, and responsible use of financial and operational data. Strong Collaboration and Leadership Prepares executive summaries, presentations, and communications that translate complex analysis into clear and simple messages for senior leaders. Serves as a subject‑matter resource on financial analysis, data interpretation, and effective business partnership practices. Provides guidance to less experienced analysts to strengthen FP&A capabilities across the organization. Risk & Controls Identify and mitigate financial planning and budget risks across the organization. Maintain documentation, version control of relevant procedures. Knowledge and Experience Required Financial Competencies Bachelor’s degree in accounting, finance, economics, or a related field. Master’s degree preferred. Strong understanding of cost and expense management, financial statements, accounting, and budgeting/forecasting methods. At least 8 years of experience in financial analysis, forecasting, and the use of financial systems (e.g., Workday, Adaptive Insights). Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and project management capabilities. Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities. Data and Analytical Skills Understanding of how to apply modern analytics features—including AI‑enhanced tools—to improve and deliver proactive business insights. Ability to develop effective AI prompts to improve the quality, clarity, and relevance of outputs from AI‑enabled analytical tools. Experience with data modeling, data analysis, databases and working with structured datasets. Proficiency with visualization and reporting tools such as Tableau or Power BI. Familiarity with tools that support process automation and basic scripting or analytics (e.g., SQL, Python, agent building or similar), with emphasis on practical application rather than deep technical development. Strong understanding of data governance principles, including data quality and responsible data use. Degree or certification in any of these skill sets preferred.

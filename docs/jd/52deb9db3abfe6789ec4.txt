Company Name: tastytrade Role: Active Trader Consultant Location: Chicago, IL Position Summary Active Trader Consultants (ATCs) are results-driven sales professionals responsible for acquiring and onboarding new retail brokerage accounts from active, self-directed traders. This role focuses on identifying, engaging, and converting high-potential prospects, such as day traders, options traders, futures traders, margin/portfolio margin traders, and frequent equity/ETF investors, who are currently managing accounts at competitor firms or trading through multiple platforms. This is a high-energy sales position, involving outbound outreach, referrals from service, platform demonstrations, and competitive positioning. ATCs are trusted advisors on advanced trading tools, robust platforms, margin/portfolio margin, asset classes supported, and service capabilities. By delivering exceptional consultative experiences, they will drive new account growth, increase trading assets under custody, and boost trading volume and revenue. Successful ATCs have deep market knowledge, strong prospecting skills, and the ability to build long-term relationships that maximize client loyalty and firm revenue.

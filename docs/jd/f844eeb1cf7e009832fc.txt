Drive Construction Innovation as our Next Technical Services Business Analyst - Spectrum Are you ready to transform how leading construction organizations operate by engineering high-impact, custom software solutions? At Trimble, you will collaborate directly with enterprise clients to turn complex business challenges into streamlined workflows, leveraging cutting-edge technology across the entire building life cycle . About Us: Trimble is empowering stakeholders through planning, design, construction and the entire asset lifecycle . The company’s innovative and connected ecosystem of solutions improves coordination and collaboration between teams, phases and processes. Automating work and transforming workflows, Trimble is enabling teams to deliver with confidence at every turn. For more information, visit: construction.trimble.com. What Makes This Role Great : In this role, you will act as a trusted consultant at the intersection of business strategy and technical software design, directly architecting tailored solutions on the Viewpoint Spectrum platform that empower construction companies to optimize their financial and operational efficiency. Key Exciting Responsibilities Completes the case intake process for Spectrum and related product software customization projects, focusing on the analysis of the customer’s business goals, objectives and needs. Primary responsibility for the development of a solution and creation of related Rough Order of Magnitude (ROM) estimates and Work Orders. Viewed by customers as a consultant. Defines needs, determines a strategy and develops a plan/proposal for delivery of the project. May involve the recommendation to shift future performance to a third party. Provides approved project scope details to assigned Technical Services Specialists and assists with customer handover activities, as appropriate. Drives internal feedback cycles to refine future scoping efforts and maintains related documentation. Performs other miscellaneous duties, as assigned. Well-organized, self-directed team player. Prioritizes and plans work activities, uses time efficiently via conscientious multitasking and develops realistic action plans. Ability to quickly develop and maintain a high level of knowledge of software products and remain expert on current product knowledge both from an internal and from a user perspective. Employs a systematic approach to analyze and solve problems. Acts proactively to anticipate risks and seeks to mitigate them before they occur. Visualizes the big picture, identifies key issues and designs harmonious and effective solutions. Monitors own work to ensure quality solution delivery. Demonstrates excellent written and verbal communication skills. Listens effectively, transmits information accurately and understandably, and actively seeks feedback. Effectively presents and explains information to various group sizes and levels of knowledge. Demonstrates professionalism, discretion, and good judgment in all interactions with coworkers, customers, vendors and others. Adapts to changes in the work environment, manages competing demands and is able to deal with frequent interruptions, delays and unexpected events. Essential Skills & Experience Bachelor’s degree in business, training, or technology, a minimum of three years of software consulting experience or two years of custom solution delivery experience, or an equivalent combination of education and experience. Demonstrated expertise within the Viewpoint Spectrum Software platform across all modules/disciplines (Accounting, Operations, Service Management, etc.). Demonstrate ability to analyze SQL data. Accomplished relationship-building skills, requirements gathering experience, and negotiating skills. Demonstrated ability to successfully drive multiple projects in parallel. Subject matter expertise in the following areas: Accounting principles and practices (e.g. payroll, accounts payable, accounts receivable, inventory, purchase orders, general ledger) Construction project management principles and practices (e.g. project set-up via estimate, project buy-out, project documents, project change orders, job costing, job billing). Bonus Points For Prior professional services or software consulting experience managing billable projects within tools like NetSuite, FinancialForce, or Kimble. Practical familiarity with Viewpoint Field Management, Human Resources Management, or Financial Controls solutions. Background knowledge or direct operational experience within the construction industry.

The Regional Control Associate works closely with the Regional Risk and Business Service Officer (RRBSO) and Associate Regional Risk and Business Service Officer (ARRBSO) to support business and service initiatives across the Region. The Regional Control Associate is responsible for the coordination, oversight and tracking of Market and/or Branch audit practices related to service functions, in addition to assisting in day-to-day approvals that flow through the Region. The role includes management of business and service projects, as well as training and development initiatives. DUTIES and RESPONSIBILITIES • Primary responsibility is to manage business and service initiatives to support the Regional Risk and Service team as an extension of the RRBSO and ARRBSO(s) • Provide support to Market Management during Branch Examinations to ensure the exam report clearly demonstrates supervisory function and leverage exam findings to identify training opportunities for entire Region • Conduct office visits to execute periodic control testing and review market self-assessment testing related to audit readiness program • Act as a liaison between the Market Risk and Service Teams and the ARRBSO/RRBSO to assist in escalations • Review and action various regional approvals requiring attention (e.g., Pricing exceptions (lending/advisory), Entitlements Provisioning and Reporting, 4th Party Checks, Credit Adjustments, Entertainment and Conferences) • Oversee and execute service controls in partnership with the Market Risk and Business Service Officers (MRBSOs) to help ensure timeliness and completeness of reviews • Manage remediation efforts which are inclusive of data analytics and reporting in order to drive results • Standardize operating procedures, leveraging national best practices and guidance in support of coaching and training the service leadership team (Business Service Officers and Service Managers) • Administer other duties delegated by the Regional Management Team including but not limited to managing guardianship adjustments, large error reporting, fraud escalations, state registration waivers, AFG, and executing on strategic firm and regional priorities EDUCATION, EXPERIENCE, KNOWLEDGE, and SKILLS Education and/or Experience • Four-year college degree or professional certification preferred • Previous industry management

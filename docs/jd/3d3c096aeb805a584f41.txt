Position Information Hiring Manager: Associate Director Department: Global Product Group Department Overview The Global Product Group (“GPG”) is a component of the Investor Partners Group (“IPG”) that is responsible for the investor product lifecycle, spanning product ideation and development, product marketing and execution, product management and reporting. The team oversees, manages and seeks to optimize the Firm’s existing suite of investor products. GPG collaborates with Firm leadership and business unit leaders to plan and execute high impact projects such as new fund launches, product extensions, customized separate account vehicles and new multi-product strategic partnerships. The team also serves as the product expert for the Firm’s investor product offerings. Position Responsibilities This individual will help scale the quality, consistency and efficiency of investor-facing content across the Global Product Group. This individual will work with Investor Relations, Product, Marketing, Legal & Compliance and Technology Teams. The successful candidate will be an excellent writer and editor with the ability to manage cross-functional projects and deliver accurate, audience-appropriate responses under tight deadlines. Primary duties and responsibilities include, but are not limited to: Content Management (~ 50% of time spent) Owning and continuously improving the centralized library of approved content including standard responses, supporting data and templates Establishing and maintaining taxonomy, metadata, naming conventions, version control, ownership and review cadences Coordinating content refreshes with subject-matter experts and managing reviews with Legal & Compliance and other control functions Identifying content gaps, duplication and inconsistent language and driving remediation through completion Developing guidance, templates and training to support effective use of approved content Projects & Process Improvement (~ 30% of time spent) Acting as a business lead for technology initiatives supporting content management and investor diligence including content repositories, request intake, workflow tools, dashboards and AI-enabled capabilities Partnering with Technology and Change Management and external vendors through solution design, testing, implementation and post-launch adoption Using workflow and content analytics to identify bottlenecks and opportunities for automation and improved self-service Preparing quarterly and ad hoc reporting on content usage and user activity Investor Diligence (~ 20% of time spent) Managing and contributing to investor and consultant diligence requests including DDQs, RFPs, RFIs, data requests and bespoke meeting materials Triaging requests based on complexity, deadline, audience and required approvals; These behaviors and competencies drive our ability to win together. Leadership: Role models in this area consistently focus on the right goals and priorities and continually develop themselves and others. Achieving Results: Role models in this area are high achievers who develop careful plans and deliver consistently and effectively. Personal Effectiveness: Role models in this area build strong relationships, treat others with respect and communicate effectively. Thinking Critically: Role models in this area understand our business, rely on analytical reasoning and seek diverse perspectives to solve problems.

PagerDuty is looking for a Senior FP&A Manager to own a full view of expense management across G&A, R&D, and GTM. You'll serve as a key finance business partner to leaders across the organization, owning analysis, forecasts, and executive-level reporting needed to manage expense well and operate at public company standards. This role will require to be in office 2 day per week. Key Responsibilities Own a full view of expense management across G&A, R&D, and GTM, serving as a trusted finance partner to senior leaders in each of those groups and a point of coordination Drive AI and automation transformation to improve efficiency across expense management, reporting, and forecasting Own the Cost of Revenue forecast and monthly close cycle for all departments, partnering across the FP&A team and accounting. Identify material risks, opportunities, and drivers behind performance versus plan. Build financial models and driver-based scenario analysis to support investment decisions, resource allocation, and strategic planning across the business. Develop executive and board-level reports and presentations. Manage and provide insights on vendor spend and relationships. Basic Qualifications 10+ years of experience in corporate finance, accounting, or expense operations, including SaaS company experience. 2+ years of senior level experience responsible for finance or expense operations. Hands-on experience with finance systems (e.g., NetSuite, Coupa, Zuora, Anaplan). A track record of building or scaling processes and controls in a growing organization. Strong analytical skills and comfort working with cross-functional stakeholders at all levels. Experience with SOX compliance and internal audit requirements. Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, or a related field. Preferred Qualifications CPA or MBA a plus. Experience deploying AI in finance operations and workflows.

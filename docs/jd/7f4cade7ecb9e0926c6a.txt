Job description OVERVIEW Evercore is seeking a highly motivated Associate Corporate Controller to join the Finance team, reporting to the Lead Controller – Corporate Functions. This role will serve as an integral role in supporting the Firm’s infrastructure (the “Corporate Group”) on financial reporting needs through comprehensive financial reporting and analysis. The successful candidate will contribute to the groups’ budgeting, forecasting, and monthly/quarterly close processes, and will support financial decision-making and strategic planning across the Global Corporate Group. This role would also be tasked with preparing and maintaining supporting schedules that contribute to the Firm’s quarterly and annual public reporting. PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES Financial Reporting and Analysis: Support the Lead Controller – Corporate Functions to prepare and maintain non-compensation monthly, quarterly, and FY budgets and forecasts across the US, EMEA, and Asia Synthesize complex financial analysis into easily digestible and actionable communications to support strategic decision-making, including budgeting, forecasting, and performance evaluation across the corporate functions Reconcile third party vendor usage to verify accuracy of invoicing process Assist in preparation of management presentation materials for monthly and quarterly review Collaborate cross-functionally with global corporate groups – specifically RESO, Human Capital Group, and Communications & Event Marketing – to support key business initiatives through financial reporting, analysis, and presentation Support the preparation and coordination of the annual non-compensation budget process Assist in consolidating the monthly corporate group accrual process Prepare the consolidated monthly non-compensation analysis package, partnering with peers to investigate and resolve key variances Accounting Operations: Support month-end, quarter-end, and year-end close processes, ensuring they are completed on time and accurately Support the company’s ASC 842 lease accounting process, including lease classification, right-of-use assets and liabilities, journal entries, reconciliations, lease schedules, and financial disclosures, ensuring accuracy, compliance, and timely reporting Collaborate closely with the Accounts Payable team to ensure accurate invoice processing, including reviewing entries during month-end close, and initiating reclassifications or updates as needed Coordinate with the SEC/Accounting Policy team to provide support for filings, as needed Collaborate with Finance teams in US and Europe to meet operational targets and deliver ad-hoc projects, as necessary SPECIFIC QUALIFICATIONS The ideal candidate will have a strong team-based attitude and work ethic. They will be ambitious and resourceful, as well as intellectually curious and eager to learn. Additional qualifications include: Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field 3–5 years of progressive experience in accounting, finance, or a related role within financial services Experience with financial systems and ERP platforms Exceptional analytical and problem-solving skills with a high level of attention to detail Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite, particularly Excel (advanced functions, financial modeling) Excellent communication and interpersonal skills for collaboration with senior leadership and external stakeholders Strong organizational and time-management skills with the ability to prioritize and manage multiple projects Integrity and sound ethical judgment Strategic, adaptable mindset with a commitment to continuous improvement Expected Base Salary Range: $100,000 - $110,000 This position requires valid United States work authorization and is not eligible for visa sponsorship.

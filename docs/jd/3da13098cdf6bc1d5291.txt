Job Location IVORYDALE INNOVATION CENTER Job Description The Capital Purchases Manager supports the sourcing and commercial management of capital investments across Fabric, Home, and Habitat Care. Job Responsibilities Working closely with engineering, project teams, business leaders, and strategic suppliers, the role helps deliver capital projects by supporting supplier selection, commercial negotiations, purchase order execution, risk management, and overall purchasing stewardship. This role provides broad exposure to manufacturing technologies, supplier markets, and large-scale capital investments while building foundational purchasing and leadership capabilities. Job Qualifications Required Mininum of 2 years of purchasing experience Bachelors Degree in Supply Chain Management, Business, Economics, Data and Analytics or relevant degree Strong business acumen with the ability to evaluate commercial trade-offs and support value-based sourcing decisions. Effective communication and collaboration across multifunctional teams, suppliers, and project stakeholders. Analytical problem-solving skills with the ability to assess data, identify opportunities, and manage risk. Ownership mindset with strong project management, organization, and execution discipline. Negotiation and influencing skills, coupled with a commitment to purchasing policies, compliance, and stewardship. Learning agility and curiosity to develop expertise in capital equipment, construction services, and manufacturing technologies.

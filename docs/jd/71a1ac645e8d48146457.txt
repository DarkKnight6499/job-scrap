The Impact You’ll Make in this Role As IT Director, Global Order Execution & Fulfillment IT Portfolio / Value Stream Operations Leader, you will lead the global technology strategy and execution for the end-to-end Market-to-Order-to-Cash value stream. In this enterprise leadership role, you will partner across businesses, ESC/GBS functions and IT to modernize critical application portfolios, strengthen process governance, and accelerate digital, ERP, automation, and AI-enabled transformation at scale. In this role, you will make an impact by: This enterprise leadership role will own the strategy, governance, transformation, and performance of global application portfolios across the Market-to-Order-to-Cash value stream. The role will drive a cohesive end-to-end ownership model that enables operational excellence, digital transformation, AI adoption, and scalable business outcomes. Set the enterprise vision and roadmap for Order Execution and Fulfillment applications, aligning business priorities, IT strategy, investment plans, and execution outcomes. Lead global application strategy, design, development, and support across Order Processing, Management & Fulfillment, Price Execution, Billing, Credit Risk Management & Collections, Customer Issue Resolution, Dispute, Returns & Short Pays, Cash Application, Service & Repair, Variant Configuration and Customer Lifecycle Processes leveraging platforms including SAP ECC/S/4, B2B portals, BPCS, EDI, digital, ERP, and AI-enabled technologies. Drive transformation across process, operational excellence, technology, automation, data, and AI , ensuring clear governance, role alignment, prioritization, and measurable value delivery. Establish structured process governance to improve standardization, process ownership, stakeholder alignment, execution discipline, and continuous improvement across the portfolio. Lead portfolio management discipline , including project prioritization, execution tracking, performance reporting, benefits realization, and risk management. Partner with senior business, GBS/ESC functions and IT stakeholders to define scope, cost, priorities, strategic plans, and investment decisions across Order Execution, Fulfillment, and related end-to-end portfolios. Support enterprise transformation and M&A activities , including divestitures, spin-offs, closures, acquisitions, and related application separation or integration needs. Build and scale a high-performing global IT organization , developing talent across distributed geographies, cultures, and business environments.

Role Overview We are seeking a Field Experience Application Support Lead responsible for the delivery of reliable, high-quality application support services across the Field Experience portfolio within the Foundational Business Organization. This role serves as the primary point of operational accountability for an assigned portfolio of Field Experience applications, ensuring production stability, service continuity, and a consistent end-user experience for Field Experience IT and business managers. The ideal candidate brings deep hands-on experience in application production support, observability tooling, incident management, and cloud operations within a complex, multi-platform environment. What You'll Do Promote a clear understanding of application support services within Field Experience by gaining in-depth knowledge of the business and the applications they rely on. Enhance relationships and communication processes across technology and business support teams to encourage collaboration, improve operational efficiency, and address support gaps as needed. Maintain a consistent, aligned approach in the execution and oversight of all Service Management functions, including Incident Management, Problem Management, Service Request, and Change Management, ensuring consistent service quality and reliability across all Field Experience applications. Manage contracted support personnel and third-party vendors responsible for the execution of support services, ensuring 99.5% availability across all critical applications and associated business processes. Lead recurring operational review forums with Field Experience IT and business managers to review application service performance and delivery quality against SLAs and Service Level Objectives (SLOs), assess end-user satisfaction and feedback from the Field Experience business community, align on corrective actions, ownership, and near-term support priorities, and maintain transparency and accountability across all application support activities. Develop and maintain operational dashboards and reports that provide transparency into application health, incident trends, and service delivery quality. Lead major incident response end-to-end — from detection and triage through resolution and post-incident review — ensuring timely, accurate communication to Field Experience IT and business managers throughout. Conduct rigorous root-cause analysis (RCA) and drive permanent corrective actions to prevent recurrence; maintain and execute problem management activities, track known errors, and drive long-term fixes aligned with ITIL best practices. Proactively monitor application and platform health using observability tools such as New Relic, Sumo Logic, Dynatrace, CloudWatch, Splunk, and PagerDuty, identifying and resolving issues before business impact occurs. Drive consistent utilization of observability and monitoring toolsets while influencing the continued maturity of ITIL processes and metric reporting. Oversee the ideation and execution of automation opportunities that reduce manual, repeatable tasks, reduce run cost, and drive efficiency within Field Experience support operations. What You'll Bring Required Skills Leadership and Coordination: Proven ability to lead and coordinate cross-functional teams in high-pressure production support situations, with excellent organizational and time management skills. Communication Skills: Clear and effective verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to translate complex technical issues into business-relevant updates for Field Experience IT and business managers. Problem-Solving: Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to think critically and make quick decisions under pressure in complex, multi-platform technology landscapes. Hands-on troubleshooting mentality, with a willingness to dive deep into complex application issues and solve problems with a clear understanding of business impact and business acumen. Incident and ITSM Experience: Hands-on experience with incident management, problem management, change management, and service request processes using ServiceNow or equivalent ITSM platforms. Vendor and Support Management: Strong experience managing vendor staffing, offshore teams, and managed service providers, with the ability to identify resource gaps, manage contention issues, and hold support actors accountable. AI Literacy, Tool Adoption, and Education: Familiarity with AI-assisted operations tools and a demonstrated ability to incorporate them into daily support workflows, including using AI for log analysis, alert triage, anomaly detection, and incident summarization. Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field — or equivalent professional experience. Preferred Skills 5+ years in application production support or technology operations within a large enterprise or financial services environment; AWS, GCP, cloud, and Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) experience; and ITIL certification or equivalent. Financial services experience preferred. Proficiency with observability tools such as New Relic, Sumo Logic, Dynatrace, PagerDuty, Splunk, or CloudWatch, including experience with New Relic AI capabilities such as AI-assisted alert correlation, anomaly detection, and applied intelligence for proactive incident identification and noise reduction. Experience supporting multi-platform environments including Microsoft, Java, Oracle, SaaS, PaaS, and MuleSoft; mainframe experience a plus. Experience with PagerDuty AIOps and automation features, including Event Intelligence, the SRE Agent, and ML-driven alert grouping and triage to reduce mean time to detect and resolve. Hands-on automation experience using scripting languages such as Python, Shell, or PowerShell, or workflow automation tools such as ServiceNow Flow Designer, AWS Lambda, or similar, to eliminate manual, repeatable support tasks, streamline incident response, and improve operational efficiency.

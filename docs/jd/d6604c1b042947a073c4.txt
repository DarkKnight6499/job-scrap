Citizens Bank is seeking an Associate candidate to join the Asset-Backed Securitization (ABS) business line within the Asset Backed Finance (ABF) group. The ABF group is part of Citizens’ Capital Markets division. The Associate will work with ABS team members in support of non-mortgage securitization origination, with a focus on execution of asset-backed securitization loan facilities. The Associate will report to the Head of ABS and the role will be based in Stamford, CT. Digital infrastructure asset class experience a plus. Prepare and present concise credit underwriting memos for ABSL transactions, including organizing client and asset class diligence, coordinate legal doc review with counsel, organize cash flow modelling and rating analysis, and organize communication with internal approval constituents. Review and comment on legal documents for ABSL transactions backed by various traditional and new asset classes Perform research and analysis on Term ABS transactions and assist with execution of underwriting roles on Term ABS deals. Digital infrastructure asset class experience a plus. Contribute to the development of pitch materials and structuring / execution advice to clients for Term ABS and ABSL transactions Manage process required for execution of material ABSL transaction amendments and participate in ABSL portfolio reviews and credit meetings. Present internal business and credit partners a summary of client’s business, the transaction structure, key risks and cash flow stress scenarios Build and maintain strong working relationships with credit and portfolio management partners as well as related sector focused investment banking colleagues.

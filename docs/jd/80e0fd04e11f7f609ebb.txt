The Opportunity Reporting to the Sr. Supervisor, Accounting Operations, the Associate Accounting Specialist is a key member of the Insurance Accounting Operations team and performs a wide variety of accounting functions and analyses. This role is a hybrid position with 3 days in our Schaumburg, Illinois office and 2 days work from home The Work Itself Perform billing activities. Process incoming cash receipts and apply funds to appropriate receivables. Submit purchase order requisitions in Coupa. Perform accounts payable functions including processing invoices, check requests, check printing and electronic payments including required correspondence. Prepare month end close journal entries, reconciliations and analyses. Research reconciliation variances and propose adjustments. Respond to inquiries from internal and external customers and stakeholders. Participate in accounting and technology projects by testing enhancements. Support the department's compliance with internal controls, governance, and vendor management. Create and maintain desktop procedures. Complete projects as assigned. The Qualifications We're Looking For Associate or bachelor’s degree in accounting or related discipline preferred. Experience preparing accounting entries, reconciliations, and payments Experience with accounting systems (e.g. SAP, Workday, Coupa) · Intermediate knowledge of Microsoft applications Word, PowerPoint, and Excel (vlookups, pivot tables) Interpersonal, analytical and organizational skills. Good communication skills, both written and verbal. Strong attention to detail with critical thinking, analytical and organization skills. Comfortable in a fast-paced environment.

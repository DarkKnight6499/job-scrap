Position Summary The Funding Management Analyst is responsible for the management of front store merchandising vendor funding activities and analysis, as well as analytical support for the category P&L. The role will also be responsible for adhoc analytical support to generate and interpret financial and business specific analysis as well as creating forecasts and communicating variances to business owners and managers. The analyst will use analysis of supplier allowance funding trends and forecasts to identify risks and opportunities which may impact category profitability. Responsibilities Support Category Management by providing and interpreting various financial and business specific analysis and forecasts, with a specific focus on Supplier Allowances and identifying opportunities to improve and optimize category profitability. Develop analytical materials and prepare material and presentations in a way that can be easily understood by others who may not have the same background or expertise. Articulate conclusions and recommendations to the leadership team and category management. Process all vendor funding for assigned categories, including value calculations, billing execution, ongoing vendor support and performance reporting. Assist in the development of annual forecasts and budgets for vendor funding, working with the leadership team and category management. Ensure funding collection is performed in compliance with all required regulations and support audits requests from internal and external auditors. Enhance and enforce documentation, archiving, and audit processes for agreements with vendors. Engage cross-functionally across the organization to drive results and resolve issues encountered by the team including the ability to assess and course-correct as necessary. Partner closely with business partners to break down questions, design analysis and determine required actions to solve problems.

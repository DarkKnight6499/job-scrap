Responsibilities Within the PCU Portfolio Analysis unit, the Risk Analytics team is seeking a Data Analyst. Responsibilities include developing data-driven approach to problem solving and opportunity identification, in collaboration with business partners, by understanding business needs and marrying those with awareness of our modeling strategies. Our team is highly collaborative working closely with business partners primarily across Underwriting Solutions, P&C Modeling, P&C Actuarial, and Financial Operations as we monitor risk of our business. This role will provide an opportunity to demonstrate deep technical knowledge, own and support data processes and strategy to support new business and reunderwriting model threshold work, solve complex problems. Specifically for this role we are looking for the following: Query and aggregate data sets from different sources/platforms to support business objectives. Conduct data analysis and field ad hoc data requests from business partners and leadership. Manage data quality to ensure accuracy in reporting, projection, and analytic efforts. Monitor and track key performance indicators through dashboarding and reporting, often in collaboration with other areas. Collaborate on underwriting strategy within P&C Underwriting and enterprise business partners. Support the creation of visualizations and maps using tools such as Python, PowerBI, and GIS. Help develop and maintain automated reports and notebooks in cloud environments such as AWS SageMaker. Steward data methodologies and calculations to enhance consistency. Communicate findings clearly, with support, to both technical and non-technical audiences. Work Arrangement This is a hybrid position which allows you to spend some of your time working from home as well as some time working collaboratively in-office. During the job training period you may have a higher percentage of time spent in-office to support your learning experience. This role is 2 days per week in the office, 3 days per week working from home. Specific days in-office vary by location and may be adjusted at any time based on business need. Candidates must be located within a reasonable daily commuting distance to any of our major hub locations, Bloomington IL, Dunwoody GA, Richardson TX or Tempe AZ. Hours of operation are 8:00 AM - 4:15 PM M-F, with flexible start times. May occasionally require irregular hours based on workload. Qualifications Identify and clarify data quality pain points and opportunities for improvements. Experience navigating different generations of data structures. Work independently with minimal supervision. Coach peers on data processes and strategies. Experience working with or adjacent to data models. Ability to effectively utilize business intelligence data systems, models, financial/statistical resources, and analytical software applications in the creation of reports, charts, and visuals. Ability to summarize metrics for reporting and identify corrective actions. Proficient in BI Tooling (i.e., Power BI, Qlik, QuickSight). Advanced skills within the Microsoft suite, especially Excel, Word, PowerPoint. May apply specialized knowledge of research/statistical techniques. Establishes positive relationships and coordinates work across teams and departments. Ability to communicate complex information to both technical and non-technical audiences. Proficiency in SQL and Python strongly preferred. Familiarity with AWS Suite and cloud-based infrastructure. Experience working in GitLab or other versioning software.

The role will focus on Medical Devices and Life Sciences Tools & Diagnostics, combining thematic research, company diligence, private-market intelligence, and direct executive engagement to develop differentiated, investment-relevant insights Your skills and experience • ideally 2-6 years of relevant experience in investment banking, buy-side or sell-side equity research, management consulting, private equity, venture capital, or a related healthcare investment or strategy role. • Understanding of the healthcare ecosystem, with experience in Medical Devices and/or Life Sciences Tools & Diagnostics strongly preferred. • Ability to engage with technical, scientific, clinical, regulatory, commercial, and financial content and translate it into clear investment implications. • Working knowledge of the private-market investment process and the diligence questions typically evaluated by institutional investors. • Strong research, financial modeling, market-sizing, written communication, and presentation skills.

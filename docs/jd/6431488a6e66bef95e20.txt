The Global Currencies and Emerging Markets (GCEM) Strats team works closely with Trading to develop quantitative research, models and technology to solve complex markets and risk-management problems. As a quantitative researcher within the GCEM Strats team, you will focus on Systematic Option Strategies and partner with the trading desk to research, test and implement systematic solutions to Market-Making, signal generation and enhanced risk management across global macro markets. Your Impact As a quantitative researcher, you will help shape the research agenda for Systematic Option Strategies. You will take market observations and trading hypotheses through rigorous data analysis, model development, validation, strategy implementation and live deployment. Your research will directly influence how the desk conceptualizes, prices and hedges option risk. You will develop forecasting and prediction models, signals, and systematic hedging strategies, with careful attention to transaction costs, liquidity, tail risk, capacity and robustness across market regimes. The primary focus is systematic FX options and volatility research; relevant experience may come from FX, equities, rates, commodities or another liquid macro asset class. You will work in a collaborative environment involving close interaction between Researchers and Traders while utilizing public and proprietary firm data. This is a highly visible, markets and trading-facing role with a clear path from original research to measurable commercial impact. Responsibilities As a quantitative researcher within GCEM Strats, you will drive and contribute to applied research for Systematic Option Strategies, translating market intuition and observations into robust, scalable systematic risk management and market making strategies. This includes, but is not limited to, a mandate to: Originate and test systematic option market making approaches, incorporating a diverse array of techniques and signals. Develop forecasting models for volatility markets, capturing skew, smile, term structure, carry effects, cross-asset relationships and market regimes. Design and implement systematic hedging strategies for vega and related risks subject to real market constraints, balancing expected P&L, transaction costs, liquidity, basis and tail risks. Own the end-to-end research process: idea generation, data processing, hypothesis design, feature engineering, backtesting/validation, portfolio construction and performance attribution. Apply robust research standards, including bias controls, regime and stress analysis, sensitivity testing, realistic implementation assumptions and clear documentation. Assess production performance of successfully deployed research and refine models as markets evolve. Partner with Traders to gain insights into and leverage institutional knowledge of market structure and function that can inform research. Build reusable analytical tools and contribute to shared research infrastructure, data standards and best practices. Communicate methods, assumptions, risks and results clearly to Trading and senior stakeholders.

About the Role: Responsible for the development and execution of strategic information technology GRC plans. This includes providing technical and regulatory expertise in specified areas: technology governance, risk, and compliance (GRC), technology risk assessments and change management. What You Will Do: Establish and maintain the Technology governance framework, decision rights, forums, escalation paths, risk and exception methodologies, evidence standards and accountability model. Direct Technology risk identification, assessment, treatment, acceptance routing, monitoring and escalation, including material and emerging risks. Own issue, remediation and exception governance, including action-plan quality, due dates, overdue escalation, recurring-issue analysis and closure evidence. Provide consolidated executive reporting on Technology risk, change performance, control issues, exceptions, remediation status and trends.

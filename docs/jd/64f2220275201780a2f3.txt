Vice President Few underwriting opportunities offer this combination: the scale of a leading global bank and the energy of a growing platform. Key Responsibilities Lead underwriting and credit analysis for conduit loan transactions across office, multifamily, industrial, retail, hospitality, and mixed-use properties. Analyze property cash flows, rent rolls, financial statements, market fundamentals, and sponsorship. Prepare credit memoranda and present recommendations to investment and credit committees. Structure loans in accordance with the company’s credit standards and CMBS requirements. Coordinate third-party due diligence, including appraisals, environmental reports, and legal reviews. Work directly with borrowers, brokers, and internal stakeholders throughout the transaction process. Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Finance, Real Estate, Economics, or related field. 5+ years of commercial real estate underwriting experience, with significant CMBS/conduit lending exposure. Strong understanding of CMBS structures, securitization, and commercial real estate finance. Advanced financial modeling and cash flow analysis skills. Ability to work with existing rating agency models. Proficiency in Excel.

Growth Operations AUDIT SOLUTION DELIVERY Role purpose Growth Operations is the strategic operating partner to the National Managing Partner, Audit & Assurance Growth and the Audit Sales Leader. Reporting to the Head of Operations, Integration and Transformation within Audit Solution Delivery, the role serves as the connection point between Audit & Assurance growth strategy and execution, ensuring growth priorities are translated into actionable plans, adopted across the practice and measured against intended outcomes. The role drives execution discipline across the Audit & Assurance growth agenda by providing visibility into strategic pursuits, pipeline performance, market opportunities, campaign effectiveness and growth commitments. It helps leaders identify opportunities, remove barriers to execution and maintain accountability for growth priorities. The role is strategic in perspective and operational in execution. The role does not own client relationships, market strategy, pursuit decisions, revenue targets, proposal development, pricing decisions, account management or business development activities. It owns the information, governance, adoption, accountability and business processes that support growth execution. Role mandate Translate Audit & Assurance growth priorities into actionable plans, measurable outcomes and ongoing execution discipline. Facilitate annual growth strategy development and execution against evolving market conditions and business priorities. Design, maintain and continuously improve the operating model that supports growth leadership, governance and decision making. Drive adoption and accountability for growth programs, campaigns and business development initiatives across the practice. Maintain visibility into strategic pursuits, pipeline performance, growth commitments and market opportunities. Facilitate growth governance processes and pursuit reviews that support proactive decision making and successful execution. Deliver reporting, analysis and business insight that enable leaders to evaluate performance, prioritize investments and remove barriers to growth. Coordinate targeting programs, market development initiatives and cross-functional growth activities in partnership with marketing, sales and business leaders. Identify execution risks, adoption challenges and operational inefficiencies and recommend actions that improve growth performance. How success will be measured Growth priorities remain visible, actively managed and on track throughout the year. Strategic pursuits, pipeline performance and growth commitments are consistently reviewed through established leadership forums. Growth programs and campaigns achieve defined adoption, activation and performance objectives. Leadership has timely access to the information, analysis and recommendations needed to make proactive growth decisions. Pipeline creation, pipeline conversion, win rates and market development activities are monitored against defined targets and trends. Growth commitments made in leadership forums are tracked, assigned and advanced to completion. Reporting and growth insights are delivered on a consistent cadence without leadership needing to request them.

At Freddie Mac, our mission of Making Home Possible is what motivates us, and it’s at the core of everything we do. Since our charter in 1970, we have made home possible for more than 90 million families across the country. Join an organization where your work contributes to a greater purpose. Position Overview Freddie Mac’s Single-Family Modeling team is seeking a Quantitative Analytics Professional to develop and enhance collateral models using state-of-the-art statistical methods, machine learning, and data technologies. Your work will inform daily decisions about mortgage collateral and support effective risk management across Freddie Mac’s portfolio. You will collaborate with modeling teams and business partners to research, prototype, develop, and validate models using large-scale structured and unstructured data. You will apply distributed computing, advanced machine learning, and emerging AI—including GenAI where appropriate—to improve collateral models and modernize modeling workflows, with a focus on business value, reproducibility, and strong governance. Our Impact Freddie Mac is leading the U.S. housing market forward by making homeownership and rental housing more accessible and affordable. Our team develops robust, scalable models that support the Single-Family business by enabling critical, data-driven risk decisions. Your Impact Collateral Modeling and Research: Develop and validate analytical methods and models to assess collateral risk for new and existing financial and mortgage products. Research enhancements to existing models, conduct experiments, develop prototypes, and apply industry best practices to address business needs. Advanced Technology Application: Evaluate and apply emerging statistical, machine learning, and AI technologies—including GenAI where appropriate—to improve collateral models and modeling workflows, consistent with business objectives, regulatory requirements, and enterprise risk management frameworks. Model Development Practices: Apply effective tools and practices for model development, version control, and reproducibility. Improve consistency, transparency, and quality across modeling teams. Data and Computing: Use distributed computing and data-processing technologies to analyze large-scale structured and unstructured datasets and modernize end-to-end model pipelines. Independent Problem-Solving and Business Support: Independently develop practical solutions to complex modeling problems under limited direction. Provide analytical support and day-to-day technical expertise to business and product partners. Collaboration and Communication: Collaborate across teams to translate business needs into analytical solutions. Document and communicate research findings, model results, and recommendations to technical and business audiences. Qualifications Master's degree in quantitative finance, statistics or a related quantitative field. Coursework and / or work experience applying predictive modeling techniques from finance, statistics, mathematics, data science, and computer programming to large data sets. Qualifying coursework may include—but is not limited to—statistics, mathematical programming, optimization, machine learning, computational methods, design and analysis of algorithms, Bayesian methods, derivatives, and Monte Carlo methods/modeling. Coursework or experience applying statistical, machine learning, or econometric methods to large datasets. Proficiency in Python, SQL, and Spark for predictive modeling and processing large-scale structured and unstructured data; experience with relational or NoSQL databases. Familiarity with LLMs and GenAI applications, including prompt design, evaluation, or agentic workflows, is a plus. Experience with end-to-end model development; financial services or regulated-industry experience is a plus. Strong problem-solving skills and ability to manage competing priorities in a fast-paced environment. Excellent written and verbal communication skills for technical and business audiences. Keys to Success in this Role Technical Contribution: Develop and enhance collateral models and analytical solutions that address business needs and deliver measurable value. Quantitative Research and Data Skills: Apply strong quantitative, empirical analysis, and research skills, supported by hands-on experience processing large volumes of structured and unstructured data. Innovation Mindset: Demonstrate curiosity, initiative, and independent thinking. Learn and apply emerging statistical, machine learning, AI, and data technologies to improve models and development workflows. Cross-Functional Collaboration: Build effective working relationships across teams and contribute constructively to technical discussions and decisions. Clear Communication: Explain complex concepts concisely, communicate research findings effectively, and present a clear project narrative for technical and business audiences.

As a member of the Private Consumer Credit Operations team, you will support the day-to-day operational activities associated with private consumer credit investments and portfolios. Why Join SS&C SS&C combines proprietary technology with deep industry expertise to support complex financial and health care operations. How You Will Make an Impact Provide operational support for private consumer credit portfolios and investment activities. Support the processing, settlement, and lifecycle management of private credit transactions. Review and reconcile cash, positions, transactions, and other portfolio activity. Research and resolve cash, position, transaction, and market value discrepancies and exceptions. Monitor operational workflows and ensure transactions are processed accurately and within established deadlines. Coordinate with internal teams, including Fund Accounting, Investor Services, Technology, and other Operations functions. Work with external administrators, custodians, counterparties, lenders, and other service providers to resolve operational issues. Review and validate transaction and portfolio data to ensure accuracy and completeness. Support cash activity, including payment processing, funding activity, and cash reporting. Assist with the onboarding of new investments, accounts, and portfolio activity. Maintain appropriate documentation and operational records. Identify opportunities to improve processes, strengthen controls, and increase operational efficiency. Participate in projects, system implementations, and process enhancements related to Private Consumer Credit Operations. Build strong working relationships with internal and external stakeholders and serve as a point of contact for operational matters. For Associate Manager-level responsibilities, provide additional oversight of workflows, assist with issue escalation and resolution, and support junior team members as needed.

The Impact you will have in this role: Position Summary: The Operational Risk Analyst supports operational risk governance and reporting activities, with focus on incident reporting, monthly ORR reporting, Golden Source production, Archer data quality, Power BI support, MRC materials, and risk acceptance monitoring. The role validates operational risk data, identifies reporting-impacting issues, coordinates resolution with stakeholders, prepares management-ready commentary, and maintains evidence to support audit and governance review. Principal Responsibilities: • Produce and validate the monthly incident Golden Source for Operational Risk and Resilience (ORR), MRC, committee, and management reporting. • Review incident records for key reporting fields, including severity, risk family, root cause, business mapping, legal entity, pending deletion status, and assigned themes. • Support weekly Incident QA Review routines, including status tracking, Archer updates, and stakeholder follow-up. • Refresh and validate Power BI views, source files, and supporting materials used in monthly ORR reporting. • Prepare incident and risk acceptance commentary for monthly reporting, executive summaries, and committee materials. • Support risk acceptance reporting, renewal tracking, overdue review, evidence requests, and management follow-up. • Maintain process documentation, job aids, QA notes, and evidence packages. • Identify process, data, and system enhancements in Archer and Power BI that improve reporting quality, traceability, and efficiency. • Additional activities as needed. Recurring deliverables include: • Weekly Incident QA Review support, including preparation, status tracking, and Archer follow-up • Preliminary and final Flash reporting support, including file refreshes and validation checks • Monthly Golden Source production, validation, distribution support, and evidence retention • Monthly ORR reporting support, including trend analysis, Power BI refreshes, risk acceptance commentary, and MRC-ready materials Experience: • 1-2 years of experience in operational risk, enterprise risk, incident management, risk reporting, audit, governance, or a related financial services function. • Experience preparing management or committee reporting, including trend analysis, executive commentary, data validation, and evidence support. • Experience with Archer, Power BI, Excel, SharePoint, ServiceNow, or similar risk and reporting tools preferred. Preferred Knowledge and Skills: • Strong analytical skills, with ability to identify data quality issues and reporting impacts • Strong written communication skills, including clear commentary for management reporting • Advanced Excel and reporting skills, with experience refreshing, validating, reconciling, and explaining data outputs • Knowledge of operational risk incident management, risk acceptance, risk taxonomies, root cause classifications, and reporting governance • Ability to work across Operational Risk, Aligned Risk, Technology, business areas, reporting teams, and management. • Ability to manage recurring deadlines in a high-volume reporting environment. • Ability to separate source-supported conclusions from assumptions and items requiring stakeholder confirmation. • Ability to maintain documentation and evidence packages for audit and governance review. • Strong planning, prioritization, and follow-up skills. • Experience improving reporting workflows, data checks, or system requirements preferred.

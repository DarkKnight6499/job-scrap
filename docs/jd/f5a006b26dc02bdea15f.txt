Job Description This role is within the Corporate FP&A Revenue team, which is responsible for the reporting, planning and analysis of revenue and revenue drivers. The Senior Financial Analyst will help deliver financial insights and recommendations to Visa's leadership team, support the coordination of global forecast and budget cycles, and analyze performance against these plans. This role will also be responsible for supporting the production of quarterly Visa Inc. Board of Directors and Earnings materials, and of monthly business reporting. This is a hybrid position to be based in our San Francisco, CA office location. Expectation of days in office will be confirmed by your Hiring Manager.

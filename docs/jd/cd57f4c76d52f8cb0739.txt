The individual will build the team's methodology, establish model standards, oversee quality assurance processes, and ensure all approved models are accurate, traceable, and compliant with operational and risk requirements. The role will work closely with Relationship Managers, Transaction Administration teams, Product, Legal, and Risk partners. This position combines deep Public Finance expertise, advanced financial modeling capabilities, operational leadership, and control discipline.

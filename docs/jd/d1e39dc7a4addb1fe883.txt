Position Overview: Neuberger Wealth is seeking a highly motivated and results-driven individual to lead initiatives focused on process improvement and operational excellence. This individual will play a critical role in enhancing the efficiency, scalability, and effectiveness of the Neuberger Wealth business by evaluating current processes, identifying opportunities for improvement, and driving implementation efforts. The role requires a blend of strategic thinking, operational expertise, and change management capabilities. As a senior member of the Wealth Platforms & Experience organization, this role will be responsible for the ownership and execution of several strategic programs and analyses related to our Client and Advisor experiences. This includes activities related to the entirety of the client/account lifecycle, including everything from onboarding, maintenance, reporting, and experience, as well as their impact on Advisor tools and workflows. Working closely with our Wealth Advisor Teams, as well as stakeholders from across the firm, this leader will advocate for a re-imagined platform that can deliver our future state capabilities for both Client and Advisor experiences. Our choice candidate will be a detail oriented strategic thinker with a passion for solving complex problems, dealing with ambiguity, organizational design, and who possesses an unrelenting customer obsession and a strong desire to make their mark on the organization. The candidate will bring an experienced lens to analyze our current state and identify what could be integrated or improved further; while owning the implementation plan. We are looking for 12+ years of direct wealth management home office experience in roles across Operations or Business Architecture. This role is pivotal to the ongoing Private Wealth transformation, ensuring best-in-class experiences, digital enablement, and operational excellence.

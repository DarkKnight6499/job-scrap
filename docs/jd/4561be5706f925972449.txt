Senior Underwriting Assistant
AmTrust Financial Services
Location: Overland Park | US-IL-Chicago | US-AZ-Scottsdale, KS, US
Link: https://careers-amtrustgroup.icims.com/jobs/22221/senior-underwriting-assistant/job

Overview Overview AmTrust is seeking a Senior Underwriting Assistant to support our Nonprofit underwriting team. This role serves as a key partner to underwriters by assisting with renewal preparation, policy transactions, broker service requests, and account management activities that support portfolio growth, retention, profitability, and service objectives. This position offers an excellent opportunity for insurance professionals looking to expand their underwriting knowledge and develop toward future underwriting responsibilities. Successful candidates will gain exposure to commercial underwriting operations, risk evaluation, agency management, and portfolio management while working alongside experienced underwriters within our growing nonprofit practice. The ideal candidate is organized, customer-focused, and eager to learn while taking on increasing responsibility over time. This is a hybrid position requiring 4 days per week in the office and 1 day remote. Preferred location is Kansas City, MO, though qualified candidates may also be based in Chicago, IL or Scottsdale, AZ. Responsibilities · Support the renewal process by preparing renewal submissions, gathering underwriting information, and coordinating account activity to ensure timely completion. · Process endorsements, policy changes, cancellations, and other policy transactions assigned to the underwriting department. · Provide support to underwriters and agency partners by responding to inquiries regarding renewals, endorsements, policy documentation, billing questions, and account servicing needs. · Fulfill broker requests for loss runs, policy documents, endorsements, invoices, and other account-related information. · Monitor renewal pipelines and follow up on outstanding items, including inspections, loss control recommendations, subjectivities, and agency requests. · Complete requotes, manual rating, and other underwriting support activities within established authority and guidelines. · Maintain accurate underwriting documentation and ensure compliance with company procedures and quality standards. · Support underwriting team objectives by helping drive retention, profitable growth, and exceptional agency service. Qualifications Qualifications · 2+ years of commercial lines insurance experience, preferably supporting underwriting operations. · Experience with one or more of the following coverages: Commercial Property, General Liability, Professional Liability, Commercial Auto, or Umbrella. · Strong understanding of commercial lines policy transactions, endorsements, renewals, and policy documentation. · Excellent organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment. · Strong written and verbal communication skills and a commitment to superior customer service. · Ability to build effective relationships with agents, brokers, underwriters, and internal business partners. · Demonstrated interest in developing a long-term career in commercial underwriting. · Strong analytical, problem-solving, and time management skills. Preferred Qualifications · Experience supporting nonprofit, human services, healthcare, educational, or religious organization accounts. · Prior underwriting assistant, underwriting technician, account management, or renewal specialist experience. · Previous delegated underwriting authority. · Professional insurance designations such as AINS, AU, API, CISR, or CPCU. Career Development Opportunity This role is designed for individuals seeking to build a career in commercial insurance underwriting. Team members will have opportunities to: · Develop expertise in nonprofit and human services risks. · Gain exposure to risk analysis, underwriting decisions, and portfolio management. · Strengthen agency and broker relationship management skills. · Build technical knowledge across multiple commercial lines coverages. · Progress toward increased underwriting authority and future underwriting opportunities.

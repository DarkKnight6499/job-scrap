Chase Auto is a leading provider of auto financing with a portfolio of more than $80 billion in assets and relationships with 75% of U.S. franchised automotive dealers. The business is part of the JPMorgan Chase franchise and serves auto dealers, consumers, and vehicle manufacturers. It offers a range of products and services to meet the financial needs of its clients from retail lending, captive financing, and floorplan products to cash management, private banking, and payment processing. As a Tesla Brand Associate in Chase Auto, you will work closely with the Brand Manager and Brand Director to support the team's analysis, program implementation, and executive communications. This includes preparing recurring reports and ad hoc analysis, coordinating program phases from development through implementation with internal and OEM partners, and drafting presentations and materials for internal and senior audiences. The Brand Associate serves as a partner to the Brand Manager, and occasionally to the Brand Director, providing seamless coverage across reporting, implementation, and communication responsibilities. This requires a solid understanding of the team's programs, priorities, and objectives. Job responsibilities Prepare and distribute recurring reports and documents to the client and internal employees, and support the team with ad hoc analysis to track program and portfolio performance. Coordinate with product groups, tech teams, stakeholders, and lines of business across all program phases, from initial development through implementation, reporting out on progress regularly, both internally and with the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) partner. Prepare internal and external presentations, including materials for senior audiences, and support the Brand Manager with executive communications as needed. Work with internal departments to resolve day-to-day operational issues and provide support to Sales and Credit teams. Serve as a backup to the Brand Manager on occasion, ensuring continuity across reporting, implementation, and communication responsibilities. Required qualifications, capabilities and skills Strong Excel and PowerPoint skills, for building reports, analysis, and presentations. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to turn data into clear, accurate reporting. Clear written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to prepare materials for internal and external, including senior, audiences. Exceptional attention to detail and a commitment to quality. Strong organizational skills and the ability to coordinate across multiple stakeholders and program phases. Preferred qualifications, capabilities and skills Experience in the automotive business or industry knowledge. Solid understanding of Chase Auto products, policies, and pricing. Credit lending, audit, or risk experience. Chase Auto internal operations knowledge. Private Label/OEM experience.

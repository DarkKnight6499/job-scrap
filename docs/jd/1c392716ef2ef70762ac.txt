Turner & Townsend is currently seeking a Financial Analyst to join the team in our corporate office in Seattle, WA. The Financial Analyst is responsible for all accounting relating to project cost and project revenue control. This is accomplished by monitoring and maintaining project cost, billing, and revenue information in the financial database, Microsoft Dynamics AX 2012 (DAX). The Financial Analyst works closely with Area, Regional, and Project Mangers. Responsibilities: Issue new project numbers. Approve or reject Project Setup Notices and Budget Revisions based on their compliance with the client, contract, and CBRE Heery's policies regarding contracts and revenue recognition. Process approved Project Setup Notices and Budget Revisions. Advise Project Managers on how to set up projects, track costs, and analyze projects. Ensure that internal controls are being met in regard to contracts & signing procedures. Transfer time and expenses as requested by Management in DAX. Run DAX processes on an as needed basis to recalculate project costs, revenue and billings. Possessing a clear understanding of how these processes move and impact data from DAX Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, Project Accounting, and General Ledger. Review CBRE Heery, Inc. financial statements, including FLASH Blues, Grays, and Greens while understanding the impact of project changes to CBRE Heery Inc.'s Profit & Loss Statement. Routinely conduct comprehensive reviews of all assigned projects using DAX & DAX Reports. Additional project analysis and special assignments as needed. Provides informal assistance such as technical guidance, and/or training to co-workers. May coordinate and assign tasks to co-workers within a work unit and/or project. SOX control responsibilities may be part of this role, which are to be adhered to where applicable.

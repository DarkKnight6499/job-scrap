Real Assets, Treasury Officer
State Street
Location: Atlanta, Georgia; Clifton, New Jersey

Who we are looking for An individual who is willing to become a leader within the Real Assets Fund Services business unit of the State Street Alternative Investment Services Group. The team is comprised of dedicated treasury professionals who are passionate about delivering treasury solutions to premier Real Assets fund clients. As a Treasury Officer you will partner with both the client and internal personnel to establish treasury process workflows and meet client deadlines. We are looking for talent who can join this growing team in Atlanta, GA and are passionate about building innovative solutions to revolutionize how State Street services our customers. What you will be responsible for As Treasury Officer you will manage functions for multiple clients/funds including, but not limited to: Processing daily wire transactions Managing internal workflow to ensure that client deadlines are met Cash reporting and forecasting Credit facility, bank account and database administration Administrating capital calls and distributions Leading special projects for the treasury department Preparing various client correspondences Maintaining working relationship with all client contacts, auditors, tax, lawyers and banking personnel What we value These skills will help you succeed in this role Strong critical thinking, problem solving, and decision making skills Attention to detail An ability to be a leader within their team, as well as be a leader amongst your peers Strong communication, interpersonal, and organizational skills, which includes excellent time management and prioritization skills Candidates must demonstrate initiative, be able to perform well under pressure, and simultaneously handle multiple assignments in order to meet client deliverables Education & Preferred Qualifications A college degree (finance, business, mathematics, accounting or economics degree preferred) At least 6+ years of previous experience Advanced Excel skills (advanced formulas, pivot tables, vlookup) CTP is favourable but not required Experience with treasury workstation Experience leading verbal and written communications at senior levels with internal and external clients Experience in effectively leading teams and driving operational efficiencies.

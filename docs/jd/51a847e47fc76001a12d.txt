Location Designation: Hybrid - 3 days per week This position is a hybrid role - Tuesday - Thursday in the NY Office, Monday & Friday work from home. Senior Associate (PF3) - Separate Accounts (FP&A) What You'll Do: • Review monthly and quarterly account reconciliations, journal entries, and trend analysis for investment and insurance activities related to the NYLIAC and NYL Separate Accounts, with a focus on accuracy, reasonableness, and key business drivers. • Perform analytical review of monthly and quarterly investment and insurance activity, identifying trends, variances, and items requiring follow-up. • Support the preparation and review of the annual Separate Account Statutory statements and other regulatory filings. • Review Statutory accounting disclosures and footnotes, including supporting analysis and documentation. • Review monthly and quarterly internal management reports and provide value-add analysis to support financial reporting and management decision-making. • Participate in special projects and ad hoc requests, including process improvement, automation, reporting enhancements, and other value-add initiatives. • Review work completed by offshore accounting staff and provide timely feedback to support accuracy, consistency, development, and continued process improvement. What You'll Bring: • Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field with 3–5+ years of experience in investment accounting, financial reporting, accounting operations, or related finance functions. • Knowledge of life insurance industry accounting, Statutory accounting principles, and/or insurance Separate Accounts is a plus. • Understanding of financial controls, SOX/MARS requirements, and best practice review processes. • Strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret financial results, identify trends and variances, and communicate key business drivers and insights. • Proficiency in Microsoft 365 applications, including Excel, Word, and PowerPoint. • Experience leveraging technology, automation, and AI-enabled tools to improve efficiency, enhance analysis, strengthen documentation, and support decision-making. • Demonstrated ability to identify opportunities to streamline processes, reduce manual effort, improve controls, and enhance reporting effectiveness. • Self-motivated professional with strong interpersonal skills, sound judgment, professionalism, and integrity. • Adaptable, curious, and open to learning new tools, technologies, processes, and ways of working. • Excellent organizational, multitasking, and prioritization skills. • Knowledge of Analysis for Office, Wings, SAP, or similar financial reporting and accounting systems is a plus. #LI-VL1 #LI-HYBRID Pay Transparency Salary Range: $80,000-$105,000 Overtime eligible: Exempt Discretionary bonus eligible: Yes Sales bonus eligible: No Actual base salary will be determined based on several factors but not limited to individual’s experience, skills, qualifications, and job location.

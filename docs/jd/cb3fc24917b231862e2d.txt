Summary of Role: This position is for a senior engineering leader who will be leading a team of bench characterization engineers supporting new product development. This role requires a candidate with people leadership skills coupled with hands-on technical skillset. Candidate will have equal amount of focus spent between people leadership skills and hands-on technical contribution. This role requires close co-ordination and collaboration with design teams, SW/FW teams, Product/Test engineering teams, PM teams, and also may require engagement with external customer teams, to deliver product characterization results that support project goals. Essential Responsibilities: Lead silicon bring-up, validation, and characterization activities for complex SoCs. Manage and develop a team of validation engineers and lab technicians. Define validation plans and drive execution across functionality, performance, power, and reliability. Lead debug and root-cause analysis of silicon issues in collaboration with Design, Product Engineering, DFT, and Test Engineering teams. Oversee characterization of analog, mixed-signal, and RF subsystems and IPs across digital, analog, and RF product lines. Code bench test plans bench characterization board/socket hardware design, including compliance to parasitics requirements, stack-up, grounding, shielding, power integrity, and component placement. Develop and deploy automated validation and data analysis solutions using Python, MATLAB, or equivalent data-analytics tools. Own lab infrastructure, equipment planning, calibration, safety, and operational excellence. Drive correlation between simulation, bench results, and ATE production test data. Review characterization data, correlate findings to system-level parameters, and ensure reports are ready for stakeholder review. Support qualification, customer engagements, and production release activities. Other Responsibilities: Communicate team progress, risks, and priorities to engineering leadership. Required Qualifications: BS/MS in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related discipline with 10yrs+ of work experience in semiconductor characterization, bench measurements, or related engineering work. Experience leading, mentoring, or managing engineers, or demonstrated readiness to grow into people leadership. Experience using benchtop equipment such as power supplies, multimeters, signal generators, AWGs, SMUs, pattern generators, VNAs, oscilloscopes, spectrum analyzers, etc. Programming experience in developing test code and automation using Python, MATLAB, LabVIEW, or equivalent. Strong debugging, problem-solving, and cross-functional leadership skills. Strong cross-functional communication skills and the ability to represent the team to senior stakeholders. Preferred Qualifications: Experience with RF/mmWave, radar, wireless, networking, or high-speed communication products. Project management skills - i.e., the ability to innovate and execute on solutions that matter; the ability to navigate ambiguity. Strong written and verbal communication skills.

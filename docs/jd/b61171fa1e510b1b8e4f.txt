Portfolio Strategy Analyst The Portfolio Strategy Analyst role is exists at the intersection of investments, client engagement and thought leadership. This individual will become an expert on portfolio analytics and client service while growing towards a client-facing Portfolio Strategist role. Key Responsibilities and Duties Generate portfolio analytics reports and synthesize portfolio strengths, potential risks, and ideas for improvement inline with the client’s stated goals and constraints. Manage the ongoing development of Nuveen’s portfolio analytics suite, working alongside key stakeholders and our 3rd party technology partner, to ensure maintenance and enhancement requests are implemented in a timely manner and without interruption of our portfolio consulting services. Help train Nuveen’s new hires on the team’s portfolio consulting offering and best practices when it comes to leveraging technology to deliver investment advice to clients. Become an expert in portfolio construction concepts and connect these concepts into the service the team provides to clients. Lead projects that support the continued evolution of the team’s services. Educational Requirements University (Degree) Preferred Work Experience No Experience Required Career Level 5IC PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS: Desire to work towards CFA Strong technical skills to work across portfolios/platforms and the ability to partner and collaborate across teams Related Skills Analytical Skills, Asset/Investment Modeling, Asset Allocation, Asset Management, Communication, Economic Analysis, Financial Risk Management, Forecasting, Investment Research/Analysis, Investments, Investment Strategy Acumen, Programming Anticipated Posting End Date: 2026-11-02 Base Pay Range: $89,200/yr - $106,100/yr Actual base salary may vary based upon, but not limited to, relevant experience, time in role, base salary of internal peers, prior performance, business sector, and geographic location.

Party Reference Data is a firmwide function supporting all lines of business, managing party data throughout its lifecycle from onboarding to disposition. Parties include clients, issuers, obligors, guarantors, counterparties, exchanges, and more, and this reference data sits at the heart of every transaction executed by the firm. We are building a scalable, flexible platform to meet evolving business and regulatory demands, with the goals of improving the client experience, simplifying operations, and enhancing analytical capabilities. As a Party Reference Governance Associate in Party reference data governance, you will support strategic and business data analysis, data access/entitlement analysis, and strategic data initiatives. You will help enable consistent party data lifecycle management—from onboarding through disposition—by partnering with reference data strategy and operations stakeholders. You will contribute to scalable, flexible practices that improve usability, operational efficiency, and analytical capabilities.

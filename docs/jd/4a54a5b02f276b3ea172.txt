This position schedule is Monday-Friday business hours, with occasional weekends and holidays as needed for coverage About This Role The Quality Control Materials Associate II plays a critical role in supporting the release of raw materials and utility systems used in Biogen manufacturing operations. In this position, you will perform raw material sampling, inspections, testing, utility monitoring, and validation activities while ensuring compliance with GMP and GLP requirements. Working closely with Quality Control leadership, Manufacturing, Quality, and cross-functional partners, you will help maintain the integrity and accuracy of laboratory data that directly impacts material release decisions. Success in this role requires strong scientific fundamentals, attention to detail, technical problem-solving skills, and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced GMP environment. What You'll Do Perform raw material sampling, inspections, release testing, cleaning validation, and utility monitoring activities Ensure compliance with GMP, GLP, and site quality requirements Author investigation protocols and reports, change control requests, and validation documentation Review laboratory data and documentation to ensure accuracy, completeness, and compliance Support investigations, deviation management, and corrective action implementation Partner with Quality, Manufacturing, and other cross-functional teams to support business and production schedules Maintain laboratory systems that ensure data integrity and inspection readiness Participate in projects that improve operational effectiveness, compliance, and quality performance Required Skills High school diploma with 2-4 years of Quality Control GMP experience OR Associate's Degree with 1 year of experience OR Bachelor's Degree in a scientific discipline Understanding of fundamental laboratory techniques Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet timelines in a fast-paced environment Strong verbal and written communication skills Ability to collaborate effectively across teams and departments Ability to adhere to safety requirements Ability to meet the physical mobility demands as outlined below Physical Mobility Demands The incumbent could stand for four (4) hours, walk for three (3) hours, sit for three (3) hours, kneel or bend for one (1) hour, is required to perform upper body twisting motions for one half (½) hour and climb steps for one half (½) hour during the normal course of performing the essential duties of the position. The incumbent will also be required to lift and move up to 25 pounds and occasionally lift and move up to 50 pounds with assistance for up to 100 feet, occasionally push/pull more than 100 pounds The incumbent manipulates small objects and performs activities or operates equipment that requires flexion, extension, or movement of the wrist; grasping; manipulation using hands and/or fingers; etc. The incumbent is required to wear personal protective equipment, such as, safety shoes, safety glasses and/or hearing protection, as well as full gowning in a lab environment for up to 75% of every shift Preferred Skills Experience working in a GMP environment Familiarity with raw materials Experience referring to and complying with SDS material sheets Job Level: Entry Level Additional Information The base compensation range for this role is: $57,000.00-$74,250.00 Base salary offered is determined through an analytical approach utilizing a combination of factors including, but not limited to, relevant skills & experience, job location, and internal equity.

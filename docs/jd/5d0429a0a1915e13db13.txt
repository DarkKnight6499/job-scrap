Responsibilities The Treasury Management Service Specialist Senior will have primary responsibility for supporting the Service Specialists within TM Client Services. This role will aid in the development of team members’ knowledge as well as escalation of situations where standard support paths are not viable. Team members in this role will also monitor daily workload and elevate instances where service level adherence is at risk. Additionally, this role will compile management reports that will show relevant support trends as well as complete quality audits of work completed by TM Client Services Specialists.

Job Description Summary Under general supervision, and with a high level of autonomy, uses knowledge and skills obtained through education, training and / or prior experience to assist with both the underwriting of new Real Estate Investment Trust (“REIT”) and Commercial Real Estate (“CRE”) transactions, as well as monitoring an active portfolio of existing REIT & CRE loans. Specifically, the position will be located at Raymond James Bank’s Home Office in St. Petersburg, FL, where you’ll work alongside a seasoned team of professionals, including Banking Officers and other Credit Associates. This position combines corporate-style REIT analysis and hands-on underwriting of secured CRE loans, providing exposure to large syndicated financings and direct sponsor relationships. We are looking for individuals who are passionate about modeling corporate and property-level cash flows and assessing risk across a range of institutional commercial real estate asset types - retail, industrial, multifamily, hospitality, senior housing, student housing, data centers, and self-storage assets, and more! Above all we value individuals who are enthusiastic, hardworking, eager to learn / help others learn - and can strive in a team-oriented environment. Knowledge of: · Advanced concepts, practices and procedures of REIT and CRE credit analysis. · Finance and/or accounting principles, practices and procedures. · Federal and State laws.

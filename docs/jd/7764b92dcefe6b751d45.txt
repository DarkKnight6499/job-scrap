Job Description Position Summary The Senior Associate will support the oversight and execution of the firm’s real estate investment and fund activities through financial analysis, thoughtful evaluation of asset and fund performance, and hands‑on involvement in transactions and reporting. This role plays a key part in maintaining models and projections, enhancing data and analytics infrastructure, and partnering across teams to deliver clear insights to internal stakeholders and investors. The ideal candidate brings strong analytical skills, technical proficiency, and the ability to operate effectively in a fast‑paced, collaborative environment. Responsibilities Provide support in managing fund financial model, forecasting equity deployment, investment volume, fund and asset-level returns, and portfolio structure. Manage property cash flow models for leasing activities, capital expenditures, market leasing assumptions and capital markets assumptions. Lead the quarterly valuation process for the fund's properties utilizing Argus and excel. Support detailed buy/sell/hold analyses at the property, submarket, and market levels. Collaborate with acquisitions and development teams to review proposed new investments for the fund. Support budgeting process including capital projections for existing assets, equity raise forecasts, and deployment projections. Ownership of ongoing internal and external reporting deliverables. Assist Investor Relations team with due diligence and ad hoc requests from investors. Acquire detailed market knowledge including understanding location, product, supply and demand within each market. Work with finance, technology and investor relations teams to build and maintain data systems and dashboards. Required Knowledge, Skills & Abilities 5-7 years of applicable work experience, preferably in commercial real estate (investment, asset management, valuation), investment banking, or data science. Bachelor’s degree in finance, accounting, engineering, data science, or related field or equivalent experience. Appraisal or valuation experience desired. Experience with Microsoft Power Platform (Power Query, Power BI, SQL), CoPilot, LLMs, Python, Jira, RealPage AIM, and database management highly desired. Strong Excel model building skills, PowerPoint presentation skills, and proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite. Experience with Yardi IP/IM and Argus Enterprise preferred. Proficiency with accounting fundamentals and financial analysis. Understanding of DCF, IRR, and investment valuation concepts. Ability to define problems, collect data, establish facts, and draw valid conclusions. Ability to build and maintain effective, collaborative relationships with internal and external partners. Organized and detail oriented with the ability to adapt to a changing and fast-paced environment. Strong interpersonal skills, communication, and collaboration in a team environment. Self-starter with superior analytical skills; a problem solver with an astute attention to detail. Ability to communicate (verbal and written) in an articulate and effective manner.

Regular or Temporary: Regular Language Fluency: English (Required) Work Shift: 1st shift (United States of America) Please review the following job description: The ABL Credit Delivery team, comprised of ABL Structuring and ABL Asset Management, underwrites asset-based loans (“ABL”) to new and existing clients and actively manages the existing portfolio to meet client needs while also ensuring appropriate risk/return strategies. ABL Structuring and ABL Asset Management adhere to operational/regulatory compliance in their structuring, underwriting, and managing ABL loans for overall credit quality, collateral monitoring, financial analysis, legal diligence and risk mitigation in the execution of transactions, which may include original approvals, amendments, refinancings and ancillary credit product approvals. ABL Credit Delivery Associates support ABL Structuring and Asset Management in underwriting and portfolio management responsibilities, may be assigned to support senior Credit Delivery Teammates but require increasingly less oversight, be responsible for their assigned opportunities and exhibit confidence in all facets of independent credit evaluation. ABL Credit Delivery Associates take ownership of projects in coordination with Credit Delivery leadership and mentor junior teammates.

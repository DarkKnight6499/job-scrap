GLOBAL BANKING & MARKETS Global Banking & Markets provides financing, advisory services, risk distribution and hedging for institutional and corporate clients through our FICC and Equities (“GBM Public”) and Investment Banking (“GBM Private”) franchises. YOUR IMPACT We are looking for an experienced professional to join the Supervisory Risk & Controls team within Global Banking & Markets, supporting the expansion of the supervisory program across GBM Private with a focus on the Capital Solutions Group (CSG). In this role, you will help develop and implement a practical supervisory framework for CSG activities, strengthen monitoring and governance, and partner with business and control stakeholders to identify, assess and manage supervisory risk. You will combine an understanding of banking activities with sound judgment, disciplined execution and clear communication. OUR IMPACT The Supervisory Risk & Controls team manages controls addressing supervisory risks arising from business activities across Global Banking & Markets. The team partners with the businesses to establish clear supervisory expectations, implement controls, support ongoing monitoring and oversight, and develop the tools and governance needed to manage risk effectively. CSG brings together financing, origination, structuring and risk management capabilities to provide tailored solutions for issuing and investing clients. Supporting this business requires close coordination across GBM Private and GBM Public and a supervisory approach that is consistent, proportionate and responsive to evolving activities and risks. HOW YOU WILL FULFILL YOUR POTENTIAL Role Responsibilities Develop a strong understanding of CSG business activities, products, transaction lifecycles and associated supervisory risks Support the design and implementation of the supervisory framework for CSG, including desk controls, monitoring, escalation protocols and supporting tools Translate supervisory expectations into clear, practical controls that are appropriately calibrated to the business and its risks Assess the effectiveness of controls, identify gaps or emerging issues, and help drive timely and sustainable remediation Support governance processes and management reporting that provide clear oversight of covered risks, controls, issues and trends Build effective relationships with bankers and stakeholders across Compliance, Legal, Engineering and other relevant functions to implement and maintain the program Partner with GBM Public supervisory teams to promote consistency across the divisional framework while reflecting the specific activities and risks of GBM Private Coordinate multiple workstreams, maintain clear documentation and communicate progress, decisions and escalations to relevant stakeholders Contribute to the continued development of the supervisory program as CSG activities, products and regulatory expectations evolve SKILLS & EXPERIENCE WE’RE LOOKING FOR Basic Qualifications Bachelor’s degree 4-5 years of relevant professional experience in risk and controls, compliance, business management, investment banking or a related field Knowledge of banking activities, including financing, origination, structuring and underwriting; direct experience with these activities is preferred Familiarity with securities markets, derivative products and transaction processes Demonstrated ability to design, implement or assess controls and to translate risk concepts into practical business requirements Strong analytical, problem-solving and organizational skills, with close attention to detail Ability to manage multiple priorities and deliver high-quality work in a fast-paced environment Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to present facts clearly, exercise influence and escalate issues appropriately Sound judgment and the ability to build trusted relationships with business and control stakeholders across functions and regions Strong team orientation and a willingness to take ownership, collaborate and adapt as the program evolves Salary Range The expected base salary for this New York, New York, United States-based position is $100000-$160000.

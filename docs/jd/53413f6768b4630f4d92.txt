Position: Associate Director, Non-Bank Financial Institutions, Treasury Management Officer – Global Transaction Banking Sales Purpose As Associate Director, Non-Bank Financial Institutions (‘NBFI’), Treasury Management Officer (TMO), you will oversee a portfolio of global NBFIs (Insurers, Asset Managers, Broker/Dealers, Exchanges, among others), creating and executing on client-focused strategic plans to deliver against the priorities of Global Transaction Banking (‘GTB’). The Associate Director will partner closely with their leadership, Corporate & Investment Banking (CIB) global relationship managers, product managers, strategic pricing, risk and other key internal stakeholders to support this plan execution. You’ll serve as a trusted advisor and single point of contact across all markets, offering dedicated GTB coverage for all products including deposits, cash management and trade finance. TMOs are organized by industry sector and coordinate the overall GTB relationship with in-country subsidiary coverage to provide a consistent and simplified client experience. By leveraging your industry leading expertise in global liquidity management, payables and receivables optimization, alternative and emerging payments, foreign exchange, working capital solutions, and digital transformation, you’ll strengthen existing connections, prospect new ones and introduce clients to strategic partners as you deliver Scotiabank’s global value proposition and local transaction banking platform.

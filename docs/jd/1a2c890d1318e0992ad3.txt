About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Mortgage Structuring & Analytics Associate to join the Mortgage Finance Group (MFG). MFG provides financing and capital markets solutions to entities that own, originate, or service residential real estate loans and related assets. The Structuring & Analytics team within MFG is responsible for the front-office analytical aspects of the business, including structuring transactions, underwriting collateral credit, advising clients on asset disposition strategies, valuing mortgage assets, tracking market conditions, managing portfolio risk, and presenting performance metrics to management. The Associate will report to an Executive Director on a team of four professionals and will work directly on live transactions from day one.

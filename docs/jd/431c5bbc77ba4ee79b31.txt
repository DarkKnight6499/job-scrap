Job Posting Title: Retail Store Design Intern, Spring 2027 Req ID: 10160408 Job Description: Job Description About the Role & Program Disney Consumer Products (DCP) brings the magic of Disney’s beloved stories, franchises, and characters to families and fans around the globe! From toys to apparel, books to games, our global marketing organization has a profound impact on The Walt Disney Company and the global markets in which we operate. With a portfolio of world-class franchises including Disney, Pixar, Marvel, 20th Century Studios, National Geographic, and Star Wars, we are a worldwide industry and category leader delivering innovative products that entertain and inspire! The Retail Store Design Intern will support fixture design, development, and implementation at Walt Disney Parks and Resorts. We design and develop retail stores within the parks in North America. Our team is highly motivated and creative. This role supports Store Design and Fixture development to ensure Disney’s expectations for quality and excellence are met. In addition, this internship will demonstrate how the store design process works and how it integrates with the broader Store Design/Visual team. This exciting internship is a full-time, six-month commitment in the Orlando, FL area from January 2027 to June 2027. What You Will Do Coordinate with key internal and external business partners to confirm/verify fixture requirements and requests Craft and develop fixture presentation drawings for review, approval, and implementation Lead and manage the fixture shop drawing submittal and review process, including design intent, constructability, etc. Have a strong understanding of retail standards/principles Please Apply If You Have/Are Proficiency and experience in AutoCAD, Revit, 3D rendering software, SketchUp, and/or Rhino Ability to read architectural plans and shop drawings, and present conceptual designs Excellent written and interpersonal skills Collaborative and displays attention to detail Ability to multitask, prioritize workload, and handle deadlines Ability to analyze detailed information and take appropriate action Digital literacy in Excel, Word, and PowerPoint It Would Also Be Great If You Are Familiarity with financial planning software or retail analytics tools Education Junior or Senior year preferred Major or previous coursework in Architecture, Interior Design, Industrial Design, or a closely related field Required Materials A portfolio review is required for this position.

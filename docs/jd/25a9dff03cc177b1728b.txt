Make your mark in Underwriting AIG’s underwriting teams help businesses navigate complex and evolving risks by delivering innovative insurance and risk management solutions across a broad range of industries and exposures. Our underwriters work closely with brokers, clients, and internal partners to evaluate risk, analyze financial and exposure information, and develop tailored solutions that meet clients’ unique needs. As an Underwriter, you will build expertise in risk assessment, financial analysis, and relationship management while gaining exposure to the commercial insurance industry. You will collaborate with experienced underwriting professionals and cross-functional partners, including claims, risk engineering, actuarial, and analytics teams, to make informed, data-driven decisions and help solve complex business challenges. Opportunities are available across several underwriting businesses, including Financial Lines, Retail Property, Retail Casualty, Lexington Property, Lexington Casualty, Alternative Channels, Captive Solutions and Warranty . Each area provides the opportunity to develop specialized underwriting knowledge while building a strong foundation for a career in commercial insurance . Responsibilities Supporting the evaluation and analysis of risks to help determine appropriate coverage , terms, and pricing. Reviewing underwriting submissions, financial information, loss history, exposure data, and other relevant materials. Building relationships and communicating with brokers, clients, and internal business partners. Assisting with the preparation of quotes, proposals, renewals, and other underwriting documentation. Learning to apply underwriting guidelines, appetite, and risk selection strategies. Partnering with experienced underwriters and cross-functional teams such as Claims, Actuarial, Risk Engineering, and Analytics. Using data and analytical tools to support underwriting decisions. Developing knowledge of AIG’s products, industry trends, market conditions, and the specific business area in which they’re placed. Supporting portfolio management and helping identify opportunities for profitable growth.

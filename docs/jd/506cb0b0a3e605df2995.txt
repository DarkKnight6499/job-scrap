Key responsibilities Do you understand the critical importance of safeguarding sensitive data across diverse channels and environments? Are you passionate about identifying risks, strengthening controls, and driving effective data protection practices across a global organization? We're looking for someone like this who can: • execute against run the bank activities such as data breach investigation and complaint review • respond to field inquiries regarding data privacy and data loss prevention. • analyze data leakage incidents in Data Loss Prevention (DLP) tool to identify improvements to rule sets, shepherd rule changes through program governance and work with technology on implementation • work with stakeholders in Global Wealth Management US (GWM US) Divisional Information Security Office (DISO), Central Information Security Office, Technical Information Security Office, Data Protection Legal, Data Protection Office, Cyber Security Operations, vendors and business to manage potential data breaches front to back ensuring compliance with Reg S/P timelines. • act as SME on Data Loss Prevention (DLP) system policy and operation • act as SME on Reg S/P and identity theft prevention program regulation • education of field / clients of recommended steps safeguards in response to reports of suspected identity theft Your Career Comeback We are open to applications from career returners. Find out more about our program on ubs.com/careercomeback . The team You will work as part of the Divisional Information Security Office at Wealth Management Americas. We support the Division in matters of data protection and privacy. Our mission is to implement, interpret and enforce policy related to data privacy and protection. Your skills and experience • ideally, 2+ years in Information Security for Financial Services organization • university degree in a business or technology related field • strong leadership skills to coordinate actions across multiple stakeholders • understanding of regulatory environment, risk frameworks and industry best practices regarding data protection • knowledge of Data Loss Prevention systems and methods • high degree of diligence - attention to detail and ability to follow through / follow-up is critical; • self-starter who is also a team player. Exceptional ability to collaborate and openly communicate; • strong communication skills (verbal, written, and presentation); • knowledge of internet access governance, data breach handling and data classification tools and practices • experience in project management • proficient with MS Office suite and common reporting tools such as PowerBI • you’re curious to explore how AI can improve how we build, deliver, and optimize workflows.

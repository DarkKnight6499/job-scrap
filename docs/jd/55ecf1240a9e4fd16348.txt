Job Description The Performance Operations Support Associate 2 provides administrative and operational support for the Lien Release function. When a borrower’s first or second mortgage is paid off, the team prepares the appropriate release documents and sends them to the applicable county for recording. This process includes obtaining mortgage documents from custodians, reviewing documentation, entering required information, and completing quality reviews. This position will focus primarily on research, exception processing, and specialty work. When documentation cannot be obtained through the standard process, the employee will research alternative options, communicate with appropriate stakeholders, and follow the item through completion. The position may also support transactions involving attorneys and loan modifications by requesting custodial files, providing documentation, tracking payoff activity, and ensuring each item is properly documented and closed. The successful candidate will demonstrate strong attention to detail, personal accountability, independent follow-up, and the ability to complete work within established productivity and turnaround expectations. Primary Responsibilities Research mortgage-document exceptions and identify appropriate solutions when documents cannot be obtained through standard processes. Obtain and review mortgage and custodial documents needed to complete lien-release activities. Enter, maintain, track, and compile information accurately. Process exceptions and maintain complete and accurate records. Communicate professionally with external parties, including title companies and attorneys, primarily through email and occasionally by outbound telephone. Provide requested custodial documentation to attorneys and other applicable third parties. Track transactions through payoff and ensure assigned items are properly documented and closed. Take ownership of assigned files, independently follow up on outstanding items, and communicate issues to stakeholders. Complete quality reviews and help ensure work complies with internal and regulatory standards, guidelines, and procedures. Prepare reports and support research, data maintenance, and recordkeeping activities.

We’re looking for an Associate Director, Research - Quantitative Strategy - Associate to: • Assist in the construction, accuracy and maintenance of detailed quantitative models within the research platform. Conduct primary quantitative equity research into new data sets. • Analyze stock level, macro and industry data in the development of quantitative models and tools. Deliver high quality analysis to clients and write and publish results. • Develop new alpha models that support trading and investment decisions and research activities. • Create and back test tradeable investment strategies to explore inefficiencies in the financial market and identify trading signals. Utilize mathematical finance including equity pricing theory, portfolio construction and risk management. • Can work hybrid (In-office/remote).

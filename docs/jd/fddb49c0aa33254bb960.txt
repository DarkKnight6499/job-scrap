Who we are looking for The Platform Business Analyst within the Execution, Financing and Clearing division plays a pivotal role in advancing technology and analytics solutions that enhance transparency, intelligence, and execution for both clients and internal teams. This position sits at the intersection of finance and technology and centers on leveraging technology and data to create impactful products for State Street’s financing and execution platform. The ideal candidate will combine deep analytical skills with strong communication skills and business acumen. Ability to apply quantitative methods to business challenges and explain them to a diverse audience is a must. Success in this role requires developing relevant business insights through data, collaborating cross-functionally, and keen attention to detail. Why this role is important to us The team you will be joining is a part of State Street Markets (SSM). When owners and managers of institutional assets need research, trading, securities lending, and innovative portfolio strategies, they turn to SSM business units. As our investment research and trading arm, SSM’s number one goal is to enhance and preserve our clients’ portfolio values by applying technology, optimizing trading, and linking asset classes and markets across the world. Join us if making your mark in the capital markets industry from day one is a challenge you are up for. What you will be responsible for Prototyping and iterating on innovative solutions, working with internal stakeholders and clients to deliver tailored products efficiently. Engaging directly with clients and prospects, conducting product demonstrations, managing proofs of concepts, and serving as a subject matter expert. Collaborating with internal technology teams, external vendors, and partners to ensure successful product delivery from ideation to deployment. Managing roadmaps, identifying timeline risks and proactively preparing and communicating audience-appropriate project status Preparing end user documentation and facilitating end user training Maintaining high standards of governance and risk management throughout the solution development process.

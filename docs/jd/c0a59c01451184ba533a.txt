At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are seeking a Sr. QA Validation Specialist to join our Quality Assurance team. As the Senior QA Validation Specialist, you will validate/qualify equipment, systems, and processes in accordance with regulatory requirements and support, follow and implement company manufacturing standards policies and procedures. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide.

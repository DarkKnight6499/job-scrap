The Senior Financial Analyst is responsible for delivering accurate financial analysis, forecasting, reporting, and business insights to support strategic and operational decision-making. In this role, you will partner with finance leadership and cross-functional stakeholders to evaluate financial performance, identify trends, improve planning processes, and provide recommendations that strengthen profitability, cost management, and overall business performance. What you’ll do Financial Planning and Analysis : Lead budgeting, forecasting, and long-range planning activities, ensuring assumptions are well supported and aligned with business objectives . Management Reporting: Prepare accurate and timely financial reports, dashboards, and presentations for finance leadership and business partners. Variance Analysis : Analyze actual results against budget, forecast, and prior periods to identify key drivers, risks, and opportunities. Financial Modeling : Develop and maintain financial models to support scenario planning, profitability analysis, pricing decisions, capital investments, and strategic initiatives. Business Partnership : Collaborate with operational leaders and cross-functional teams to provide financial guidance, challenge assumptions, and support informed decision-making. Cost and Margin Analysis : Evaluate cost structures, margin trends, productivity opportunities, and financial performance by product, customer, business unit, or function. Month-End Support : Partner with accounting teams during month-end close to validate financial results, explain variances, and ensure reporting accuracy. Process Improvement : Identify and implement improvements to reporting, forecasting, data quality, and analytical tools to increase efficiency and insight. Compliance and Controls : Support adherence to company policies, internal controls, and financial reporting standards. Special Projects : Support ad hoc analyses, business cases, financial reviews, and strategic projects as required . What You Need to Be Successful Education: A bachelor’s degree in finance, accounting, business, economics, or a related field is required . CPA, CMA, CFA, or MBA designation is considered an asset. Experience : Typically requires 5 or more years of progressive finance or accounting experience, including financial analysis, budgeting, forecasting, and management reporting. Technical Skills : Advanced Microsoft Excel skills are required ; experience with ERP, consolidation, reporting, planning, or business intelligence tools is strongly preferred. Financial Acumen : Strong understanding of financial statements, variance analysis, forecasting methodologies, cost accounting, and business performance metrics. Analytical Skills : Excellent analytical and problem-solving abilities, with the ability to translate complex data into clear insights and actionable recommendations. Communication Skills : Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to present financial information clearly to both finance and non-finance stakeholders. Business Partnership : Demonstrated ability to build relationships, influence stakeholders, and work collaboratively across functions. Professional Standards : High level of integrity, accuracy, accountability, and discretion when handling confidential financial information.

Technical Support Engineer, Associate
Qualcomm
Location: San Diego, California, United States of America
Link: https://careers.qualcomm.com/careers/job/446721831795

Company: Qualcomm Technologies, Inc. Job Area: Operations Group, Operations Group > Test Equipment Coordination/Planning General Summary: Provide technical support and maintain all electronic measurement, test platforms and test equipment (M&TE), for engineering and manufacturing areas. Support M&TE software and operating systems integrations by diagnosing and troubleshooting common problems. Minimum Qualifications: • Bachelor's degree and 1+ year of relevant operations experience. OR Associate's degree and 3+ years of relevant operations experience. OR High School Diploma or equivalent and 5+ years of relevant operations experience. *Completed advanced degree in a relevant field may be substituted for up to one year (Master’s = one year) of work experience. Key Responsibilities: Equipment Lifecycle Management • Install, configure, deploy, and maintain measurement and test equipment (M&TE) across engineering and laboratory environments. • Support equipment onboarding, relocation, retirement, and asset disposition activities. • Ensure all M&TE assets are accurately tracked within inventory management systems and enterprise databases. • Maintain equipment documentation, configurations, warranty records, and technical specifications. Calibration & Compliance Support • Coordinate calibration recalls and preventive maintenance activities to maintain compliance requirements. • Track calibration schedules and ensure equipment availability while minimizing operational disruption. • Assist with audit readiness activities and support compliance initiatives related to equipment management. • Monitor equipment status and escalation of overdue calibration or maintenance requirements. Technical Troubleshooting & Support • Provide first-level and intermediate technical support for laboratory instrumentation, test systems, and associated software. • Diagnose and resolve hardware, software, operating system, and connectivity issues affecting M&TE. • Investigate system performance, reliability issues, and user-reported incidents. • Escalate complex technical issues to IT organizations, engineering teams, manufacturers, or external service providers as necessary. • Support root cause investigations and corrective actions to prevent recurring failures. Laboratory & Engineering Support • Support engineering project activities requiring installation, configuration, and validation of test equipment. • Assist engineers with laboratory setup, instrumentation connectivity, and test environment readiness. • Support network-attached instruments including configuration, access, security, and connectivity troubleshooting. • Collaborate with engineering teams to optimize equipment utilization and operational efficiency. Systems & Software Administration • Install, configure, and maintain operating systems, drivers, firmware, and software applications supporting M&TE. • Support Windows, Linux, and macOS environments used within laboratory and engineering operations. • Assist with software licensing, updates, patches, and version control activities. • Monitor system health and maintain operational availability of supported equipment and applications. Customer Support & Documentation • Serve as the primary point of contact for M&TE-related support requests. • Respond to customer inquiries in a timely and professional manner. • Document incidents, resolutions, preventive actions, and support activities within ticketing systems. • Develop and maintain technical procedures, user guides, and troubleshooting documentation. • Assist in creating and delivering training materials for equipment users and support personnel. Continuous Improvement • Identify opportunities to improve equipment reliability, inventory accuracy, calibration compliance, and service delivery. • Participate in process improvement initiatives involving automation, digital asset management, and data integrity. • Support implementation of new tools, technologies, and operational best practices.

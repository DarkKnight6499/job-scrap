Join Barclays as an Agency Collateralized Mortgage Obligations (CMO) Trading Analyst . At Barclays, our vision is clear to redefine the future of banking and help craft innovative solutions. In this role, you will execute trades and guide risk across a defined portfolio of Agency Mortgage-Backed Securities and related financial instruments, ensuring alignment with market conditions and firm strategy. You will support daily market-making and hedging activities while maintaining ample oversight of risk metrics, P&L reporting, and trade accuracy. You will collaborate closely with structurers, customer service teams, research, and technology partners to enhance analytics, trading infrastructure, and deal execution. To be successful as an Agency CMO Trading Analyst, you should have: Experience in Agency MBS products, including agency pass-throughs, specified pools, and CMO structures An understanding of market-making, hedging strategies, and risk portfolio Proficiency in daily P&L preparation, reconciliation, and risk reporting Some other highly valued skills may include: Problem-solving capabilities with attention to detail Effective communication skills for interfacing with various teams Ability to coordinate multiple priorities and execute tasks under time-sensitive conditions Collaborative mindset with a willingness to partner across functions to drive outcomes You may be assessed on the key critical skills relevant for success in this role, such as risk and controls, change and transformation, business acumen strategic thinking and digital and technology, as well as job-specific technical skills. This role is located in our New York, NY office. This role is regulated by FINRA.

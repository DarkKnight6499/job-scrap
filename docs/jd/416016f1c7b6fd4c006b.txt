Job Description: What Are We Looking For? The GTM Excellence, Salty Portfolio & Brands Manager plays a critical role in ensuring our portfolio teams operate effectively as strategic cross-functional partners to regional teams. This individual serves as a key influencer and connector, translating market initiatives into actionable best practices and shaping the Go-To-Market (GTM) playbook for some of our most iconic billion-dollar brands. A key focus of this role is establishing and embedding best practices during a period of significant organizational transformation. Success will require seamless collaboration across a global, multifunctional team and close partnership with regional GTM organizations to drive consistency, capability building, and commercial excellence. Qualifications Bachelor's degree required; MBA preferred. A concentration in Marketing, Business, or a related field is a plus. 8+ years of experience in shopper marketing, customer strategy, category management, sales, or related commercial functions. At least one rotation or direct experience within Sales. Global or international business experience preferred. Consumer Packaged Goods (CPG) industry experience required. Proven ability to collaborate, influence, and communicate effectively across all levels of an organization. Strong strategic thinking, analytical, and stakeholder management capabilities.

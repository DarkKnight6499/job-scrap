What You’ll Do (Responsibilities) In this role, you will: Maintain facility integrity: Monitor internal and external CCTV, security, and fire alarm systems to proactively identify and respond to safety or security incidents. Enforce access control: Utilize security technology and electronic provisioning systems to verify authorized entry for employees, visitors, and vendors while preventing unauthorized access. Perform environmental patrols: Conduct daily roving foot and vehicle inspections of key internal and external assets in all weather conditions to verify site security. Manage operational reporting: Document daily activities accurately and report any significant incidents to ensure comprehensive audit and risk records. De-escalate critical situations: Rapidly evaluate emerging threats or policy violations to safely diffuse potential risks and ensure facility compliance.

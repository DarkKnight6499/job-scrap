Job Overview: The Trading Analyst reviews activity in the trading inventory accounts and works closely with management, senior analysts, trading compliance and other departments to provide regulatory and procedural oversight to the fixed income trading desk. Assignments are complex and require sophisticated analysis combined with seasoned judgment. The Analyst will operate with wide latitude for making independent decisions, determinations, and recommendations, which typically have significant financial, operational, legal, or reputational impact. Responsibilities: Responsible for entering trades that can’t be entered by traders Resolving trade fails Identifying and repairing trade rejects and other trade-related issues in a high volume, fast paced environment. Reconciling Fixed Income inventory accounts Working with Senior Analysts to ensure timely reporting of fixed income transactions Review, prepare and present regulatory report cards to management Reviewing processes for efficiencies and providing recommendations to management What are we looking for?

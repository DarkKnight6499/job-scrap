Job Title Manager, Capital Markets Financial Analysis Hub Job Description Summary Position Summary The Capital Markets Financial Analysis (“CMFA”) Hub Manager is a CMFA subject matter expert responsible for the day-to-day management of the CMFA Hub’s delivery of financial analysis on behalf of Cushman & Wakefield’s Investment Sales brokerage services including industrial, retail, and office property types. In this role, the CMFA Hub Manager provides oversight on the execution of financial analysis requests by providing direction, conducting quality control reviews, and lending subject matter expertise. The CMFA Hub Manager provides oversight on team member development and performance, ensuring alignment on service delivery expectations. The CMFA Hub Manager serves as a key contributor to the CMFA Hub’s maturity with involvement in training and onboarding activities, implementing standardized workflow processes, implementing best practices, and identifying continuous improvement opportunities to ensure sustained delivery of high-quality, efficient services to Cushman & Wakefield's brokers. The ideal candidate will be based in Chicago, New York, or Washington D.C. and available to work on-site in a local Cushman & Wakefield office. Team members may be expected to work various time zones, depending on the product type and markets they service.

Brand Creative Copywriter - Senior Associate
JPMorgan Chase
Location: NY, United States
Link: https://jpmc.fa.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/210806596

As a Senior Associate Copywriter in Brand Creative, you will write for JPMorganChase’s owned channels and brand platforms. Day to day, you will concept, write and refine copy across digital, social, email, collateral and experiential/environmental touchpoints, partnering closely with designers, art directors, and cross-functional teams from early concept through final execution. This work matters because it defines and protects the voice of our brand—ensuring every message is on-brand, intentional, and culturally resonant. Job responsibilities Write clear, compelling copy across owned channels including web/app experiences, digital content, social, email, print collateral and out of home. Concept social creative, attend social shoots and oversee social edits. Shape brand voice and messaging frameworks to ensure consistency and resonance across touchpoints. Collaborate with designers, art directors, and cross-functional partners to evolve ideas from concept through production and delivery. Ensure work is on-brief—aligned to strategy, objectives, and audience insight; help clarify and strengthen briefs when required. Edit and elevate copy from stakeholders and partner teams to improve clarity, tone, and brand consistency while preserving intent. Present copy and rationale with clarity and confidence in internal reviews and stakeholder discussions. Apply strong cultural awareness to craft storytelling that feels authentic, current, and audience-aware. Integrate generative AI tools and emerging technologies into your workflow to support ideation, iteration, and production. Manage multiple projects simultaneously, balancing speed, quality, and deadlines in a fast-paced creative environment, with extreme attention to detail. Required qualifications, capabilities and skills 5+ years of experience as a copywriter, creative writer, or brand writer in an agency or in-house creative team. 3+ years of experience writing for large, recognized brands across multiple channels. A portfolio demonstrating exceptional writing craft, editorial judgment, and range across brand voice, content, and experience writing. Bachelor’s degree in English, Communications, Journalism, Advertising, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience). Demonstrated experience developing concepts in close partnership with design and art direction. Strong conceptual thinking and the ability to translate strategy into clear, original, audience-relevant storytelling. Strong presentation, written, and verbal communication skills; able to articulate writing choices and rationale with clarity. Experience writing within brand guidelines, content standards, and approval workflows; able to incorporate feedback and iterate quickly.

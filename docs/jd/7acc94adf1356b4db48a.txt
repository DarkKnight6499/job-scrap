THE ROLE The Cash Management Associate processes capital stock reporting and cash forecasting for investment personnel, prepares reporting for institutional clients, and provides operational support to clients, custodians, and MFS investment personnel. The associate ensures investment and client account activity is processed and recorded accurately, follows established procedures, and completes assigned daily cash management activities in a timely manner. WHAT YOU WILL DO Supports daily cash management activities by monitoring cash positions, assisting with liquidity forecasts, and processing timely movements of funds between accounts. Participates in departmental and cross-functional initiatives to improve processes, enhance client service, and reduce operational risk. Follows established cash management policies and procedures and provides input to the manager on potential updates based on day-to-day processing experience. Identifies and communicates opportunities to use technology or process improvements to reduce manual work and increase efficiency. Researches operational and administrative issues, escalates complex matters as appropriate, and assists with implementing approved resolutions. Prepares reports and reviews information for accuracy and completeness before distribution to internal and external audiences. Coordinates with offshore hub team members on assigned daily activities, responds to routine questions, and escalates exceptions when needed. Shares process knowledge and supports onboarding or cross-training activities as requested. WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR Bachelor's degree or equivalent 0-2+ years of experience in financial services and/or investment and trading operations preferred. Proven ability to perform job responsibilities effectively in a demanding environment. Attention to detail and accuracy required. Strong oral and written communication skills required. Ability to multi-task and prioritize competing demands. Strong organizational, communication, and interpersonal skills. Proficient in Microsoft Excel.

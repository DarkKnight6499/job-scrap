Job Summary: A Treasury Management Analyst should demonstrate commitment to delivering distinctive service. This position will be responsible for servicing the ongoing needs of our treasury management clients. The role will assist clients with the onboarding and implementation process, ensuring they understand how to use treasury management services such as Online Banking, Remote Deposit, ACH, Wire Transfers, Positive Pay, ACH Block & Filter, etc. The Treasury Management Analyst will partner with Treasury Management Advisors and Financial Advisors to ensure the overall success of the treasury management program. Job Duties and Responsibilities: Provide distinctive service to clients and prospects, in person and over the telephone. Respond to client inquiries and satisfactorily resolve client issues. Support firm-wide achievement of the three-ring standard (all phones answered by a live person within three rings). Implement treasury management products and services in accordance with policies and procedures. Ensure pricing is accurate as services are added, altered, or removed. Partner with Operations during the implementation of new treasury management services and as needed when servicing treasury management clients. Assist clients in using specific features of systems and services. Provide training to clients as needed. Handle limit increases in accordance with policies and procedures. Research and resolve issues for clients. Work to uncover root cause for issues and determine how to prevent repeat occurrences. Partner with peers, operations and IT as needed. Test new services and system features as enhancements and upgrades to our systems are implemented. Assist team members and peers as needed to ensure delivery of distinctive service, including but not limited to online enrollments, support calls, responding to client messages, training, and implementations. Engage in constructive feedback with a positive attitude. Build strong partnerships with TM advisors and support all sales and servicing activities. Proactively look for opportunities that will impact the overall success of the TM team. Meet all the client's financial needs, both business and personal, and refer clients to other specialty areas such as Trust, Investments, Insurance, Treasury Management, etc. as appropriate.

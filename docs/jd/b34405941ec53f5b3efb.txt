Job Description What is the opportunity? RBC Capital Markets is a global investment bank providing services in banking, finance, and capital markets to corporations, institutional investors, asset managers, and governments globally. Our Equity-Linked Capital Markets Origination team structures and executes innovative equity-linked solutions, including convertible bonds, equity derivatives, and other hybrid securities, to meet clients' complex financing and strategic needs. We are seeking a highly motivated Investment Banking Analyst to join our Equity-Linked Capital Markets Origination team. This role offers unparalleled exposure to structuring and executing equity-linked transactions, collaborating with cross-functional teams, and engaging directly with clients. The candidate will develop expertise in financial modeling, transaction execution, and market analysis while contributing to RBC’s leadership in the equity-linked space.

Purpose of the Job To effectively lend client securities in the Americas to optimize revenues globally, ensuring that a high level of customer service is maintained within the agreed client mandate and risk framework Environment of the Job HSBC Agency Lending sits within the Securities Services (“SSv”) asset class as part of the wider Markets and Securities Services (MSS) business SSv provides a range of securities services to a client base which includes sovereign wealth funds, major insurance companies, asset managers, investment banks, government agencies, investment trusts, collective investment schemes, corporates and pension funds Securities Services is globally located with major centers in locations including London, Hong Kong, New York and Dubai. Total staff numbers exceed 3,000 The service range includes global custody, UK sub custody, trustee and depository services, investment administration, performance consultancy, securities lending, treasury and FX Assets under custody/administration total c$5trn The business is regulated by the FSA Guidance and Authority The jobholder is subject to compliance with FCA and Fed rules, general Bank guidelines and normal budgetary disciplines The jobholder makes decisions on individual loans for non-discretionary contracts (within the terms of that contract) and discretionary contracts where full authority is held by the individual to trade at competitive and profitable rates The jobholder must comply with all US regulatory and licensing requirements Ensure that SSv develops and consistently maintains a premier quality reputation, internally and externally, for the Securities Lending aspects of its business. Support internal functions in the delivery of business priorities External stakeholder management – Clients, Counterparts and 3rd party vendors Internal stakeholder management – Client Management, Product Management, Treasury Operations and Business Development Build trust across regions and locations Influencing and negotiating to gain a win-win outcome Use input from client and market contacts to contribute to the development of new and enhanced SSv lending products Improve client retention and satisfaction though effective delivery and enhanced revenue from the lending products Risk and Conduct High standards of ethics and market conduct Trade within the HSBC Governance framework including but not limited to: HSS Trading Limits HSS Credit Risk Exposures Best execution and fair distribution Risk awareness and judgement of ad-hoc events which could threaten day to day business and clients. Escalation protocols and procedures in accordance with policy. Awareness and application of tax, legal and regulatory requirements. Qualifications - External Prior experience in securities lending or a related financing / markets role, with exposure to equity finance, fixed income financing, repo, collateral, or inventory-driven trading activity Understanding of securities lending transactions, pricing, and market dynamics, with the ability to balance revenue opportunities with client constraints, liquidity needs, and operational considerations Strong understanding of supply / demand dynamics, specials, utilization, collateral structures, and counterparty behavior Understanding of client profiles and investment strategies, with the ability to optimize risk and return outcomes within client guidelines and portfolio constraints Knowledge of counterparty profiles and market activities, including prime brokers, broker-dealers, repo desks, and funding desks Good understanding of the securities lending trade lifecycle and wider custody functions, including income, settlements, corporate actions, recalls, returns, and exception management Experience managing inventory allocation and identifying revenue opportunities across a global borrower base Experience partnering with internal support teams, including Technology, Operations, Middle Office, and Product, to develop solutions, improve workflows, and enhance efficiency and controls Strong analytical skills, commercial judgment, and attention to detail Advanced Excel skills required; Bloomberg and relevant trading, financing, or custody platform knowledge preferred.

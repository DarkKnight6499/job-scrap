Associate Product Manager – TLS Data & Personalization As an Associate Product Manager within Travel & Lifestyle Services (TLS), you will help drive data and personalization capabilities across digital and agent servicing channels to deliver seamless, relevant, and connected Card Member experiences throughout the travel journey. You will partner closely with Engineering, Product Management, Analytics, and cross-functional business teams to define product requirements, prioritize capabilities, and deliver scalable, data-driven personalization solutions. This role provides an opportunity to shape how customer insights, data, and technology come together to enable more contextual, proactive, and personalized travel experiences while supporting business objectives and operational efficiency. Key Responsibilities Product Strategy & Roadmap: Support the development and execution of the data and personalization product roadmap, aligning priorities with TLS business objectives and Card Member needs. Data & Personalization Capabilities: Help define and deliver capabilities that leverage Card Member data, preferences, behavioral insights, and travel context to enable relevant experiences across digital and agent servicing channels. Product Requirements & Delivery: Partner with Engineering and cross-functional product teams to translate business needs into detailed product requirements, user stories, and acceptance criteria. Cross-Channel Experience: Collaborate with digital and servicing teams to enable consistent, connected, and personalized experiences across Card Member touchpoints. Prioritization & Execution: Manage product backlogs, evaluate feature priorities, identify dependencies, and support delivery through Agile development processes. Data-Driven Decision Making: Leverage product analytics, customer insights, and performance metrics to identify opportunities, evaluate effectiveness, and inform product enhancements. Stakeholder Collaboration: Build strong relationships with Engineering, Analytics, Data, Design, Risk, Compliance, and business stakeholders to align on requirements, timelines, and expected outcomes. Product Performance & Optimization: Define and track KPIs related to personalization effectiveness, customer engagement, adoption, and servicing efficiency to support continuous improvement. Risk & Data Governance: Partner with appropriate teams to ensure personalization capabilities align with applicable data privacy, security, governance, and regulatory requirements.

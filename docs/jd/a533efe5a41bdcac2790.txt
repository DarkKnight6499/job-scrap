Senior Business Analyst
First Citizens Bank
Location: Raleigh, North Carolina; North Carolina, United States
Link: https://jobs.firstcitizens.com/jobs/36100

Overview This is a remote role that may be hired in several markets across the United States. Assesses a wide scope of factors affecting business profitability, growth, and efficiency, and aids strategic initiatives to address identified issues. Evaluates current practices, collects information and industry trends, and creates specifications to support business initiatives. Develops and implements process improvements. Participates in special analytical projects and advanced technical reporting. Provides expert support for business operations by responding to inquiries, identifying issues, and working to continuously improve business practices. Handles escalated matters that arise during daily operations, providing guidance to lower-level business analysts and leading workflow. Develops and implements modifications and enhancements. Serves as an analytics resource to wider organization or business units. Determines business needs through highly technical analysis and reporting. Develops strategies to achieve company and department goals, and communicates these through the use of specifications, project plans, or other forms of documentation. Produces reports based on data analysis, industry trends, and process capabilities. Conveys patterns, problems, and areas of improvement. Develops process enhancements based on reporting. Sources, compiles, and interprets data. Performs data analysis on an advanced level, effectively communicating and directing analysis output. Responsibilities & Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Information Systems, Computer Science, Finance, Economics, or related field plus six (6) years of experience in the job offered or as a Data Scientist, Data Analyst, or related occupation. Alternatively, employer will accept a Master’s degree in Business Administration, Information Systems, Computer Science, Finance, Economics, or related field plus four (4) years of experience in job offered or as a Data Scientist, Data Analyst, or related occupation. Requires at least three (3) years of banking experience. Requires experience with Database management. Requires experience with Tableau or Power BI to visualize data trends and analyze data. Requires experience with SAS, R, and Python to perform enhanced data mining and leverage programming to analyze complex data. Requires experience with ETL and SAS or SQL from extraction to complex data transformations within a relational database environment.

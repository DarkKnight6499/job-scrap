Location: 4910 Tiedeman Road, Brooklyn Ohio Job Summary Lead the oversight and management of Regulatory Exam activities for the Regulatory Relations Team. Work under the leadership of the Regulatory Liaison to ensure elements of the Regulatory Exam Management Program are implemented and executed across the enterprise. Essential Functions Actively oversees and executes all aspects of regulatory exams for Key including: providing consultation and guidance to leadership and LOBs in preparation for regulatory exams; interfacing with external regulatory examiners; educating LOB personnel on established protocols for exam processes and ensuring those protocols and processes are followed. Interacts with regulatory examiners from various governmental agencies with some level of influence. Manages physical and information security aspects for regulators. Manages information requests from various governmental agencies including: determining strategy for response, compiling and challenging content, determining format, and ensuring timely delivery following established protocols. Performs a variety of quality assurance functions over processes and documentation related to regulatory meeting set up and materials, regulatory report distribution and regulatory exams. Manages regulatory distribution of information, including risk governance materials. Provides direction to committee chairs, secretaries and report owners on content, format and protocols for distribution. Perform RCSA control testing for Regulatory Relations Team Identifies process improvement opportunities. Maintains strict deadlines and ensures compliance with these deadlines within the department and across Key. REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS · College degree · Microsoft Office, including Excel, PowerPoint, Word, Teams, Outlook · 8+ years of financial services experience · Demonstrated ability to prepare and deliver reporting and presentations for senior management PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS · Regulatory experience · Risk Management or audit experience · MBA Core Competencies All KeyBank employees are expected to demonstrate Key’s Values and sustain proficiency in identified Leadership Competencies.

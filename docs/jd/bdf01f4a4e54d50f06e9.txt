This role is well-suited for a candidate who brings strong quantitative aptitude, technical curiosity, and an interest in applying analytical tools to complex business challenges in the natural gas industry. The Quantitative Analyst will work as part of a collaborative team alongside experienced quantitative, technical, and business professionals to understand business needs, support data integrity, and follow best practices related to security and compliance. This role blends technical problem-solving with close collaboration across the business, providing an opportunity to build practical analytical solutions while developing deeper knowledge of natural gas operations over time.

Grant Thornton is seeking an Associate Director, Procurement Integration Lead to join the team. Approved office locations can be found below. Grant Thornton’s Global Sourcing, Procurement and Vendor Management team is looking for an Associate Director to own and lead Procurement integration activities and savings identification & delivery across all Grant Thornton entities, globally. This role will be directly reporting to the Global Head of Procurement and will be accountable for integration and onboarding outcomes – translating the target operating model design into an entity-by-entity execution plan, resolving on-the-ground adoption issues, acting as the escalation point for urgent or business critical requests and reporting integration progress and change impact to the firm’s leadership. The role also owns the roadmap for the transition to a future ERP-enabled environment, bringing best practice across the end-to-end procurement process. The Associate Director will directly lead a team of Category Managers & Buyers/Analysts, bringing hands-on category management expertise to support negotiations and savings delivery, enabling synergies and incremental value from acquired third-party spend.

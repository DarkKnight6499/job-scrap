For further information about Nomura, visit www.nomura.com Aon’s Benefit Index®, Nomura’s benefits rank #1 amongst our competitors Department Overview: Nomura's newly established Global Trading Controls team operates within the Global Middle Office function, a core component of Global Operations. With a strategic presence across key financial centers in Asia, EMEA, India, Japan & the United States, Global Operations encompasses Global Middle Office, Client Services, Collateral Management, Shared Services, Regulatory Reporting and Wealth Management, delivering comprehensive and integrated operational support across the firm. The Global Trading Controls team is responsible for the design, implementation and ongoing maintenance of critical regulatory and legal entity control frameworks for the business divisions. The team provides both strategic guidance and operational support for the firm’s global portfolio of control initiatives, ensuring robust governance and compliance frameworks are embedded throughout the organization. Role Description: This position reports directly to the Global Head of Trading Controls, based in London. The role holder will assume full responsibility and oversight for the US portfolio, with a primary focus on US regulatory controls and booking structure frameworks. The position will provide strategic business partnership and functional support to stakeholders across the region, ensuring alignment with global standards and engagement of appropriate stakeholders firm wide.

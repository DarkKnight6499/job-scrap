Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Functional & Industry Technologies Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a GRC/IRM ServiceNow Technology Implementation Solutions Senior Associate you will help organizations implement and optimize technology platforms that support governance, risk management, and regulatory compliance.

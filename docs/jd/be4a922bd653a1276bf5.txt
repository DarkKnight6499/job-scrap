Job Description: Summary Fidelity Investments established a new Chicago-based lending team in 2021 to pursue private credit investing. Our leveraged finance platform targets middle-market private equity backed businesses seeking financing for LBO’s, acquisitions, dividends and other investments. We are looking for investor-minded, entrepreneurial, and accomplished candidates looking to join a growing team, gain significant experience and responsibility quickly, and help leverage the Fidelity name in this growing market. The Role Based in Chicago, this is an opportunity to join a team for the newly formed middle market direct lending strategy of Fidelity’s High Income & Alternatives group. This is a new important strategy for Fidelity where you will be a key member of the investment team. This individual will be responsible for supporting senior debt origination, underwriting and portfolio management capabilities within the broader direct lending group. This includes support of the firm’s effort to construct and lead high quality, diversified senior loan portfolios that exceed market risk-adjusted returns, and evaluation and execution of financing opportunities in a manner that balances strong credit disciplines with a positive customer relationship approach. Success in this role will require excellent work ethic, a self-starter mentality, and the ability to work independently, while also collaborating within a growing team. Successful Vice Presidents/Associate Directors will be primary candidates for promotions as the team continues to grow. Responsibilities The Vice President/Associate Director will lead and actively contribute to all aspects of origination, due diligence, and relationship/portfolio management. These responsibilities include, but are not limited to: Leading underwriting processes, which includes analyzing and summarizing all due diligence materials related to the credit quality of businesses, specifically the analysis of the company’s historical and present financial condition, products and/or services, sales and marketing strategies, customer and supplier base, industry dynamics, competitive position, management team, and financial projections Actively communicate with investment bankers, company management and private equity sponsors Working with internal and external legal counsel regarding investment documentation Becoming a subject matter expert on key private credit strategies and an advocate for the firm Monitoring trends in the market and proposing solutions in response Qualifications We are seeking candidates who have the following characteristics: Bachelor’s Degree in a relevant field required, advanced degree a plus 6-10 years of experience in leverage finance, preferably with a top tier alternative lending platform Advanced financial modeling skills and comfort dealing with a variety of capital structures Leadership skills and ability to think strategically and creatively Ability to create compelling, thoughtful, and creative presentation materials Excellent written and oral communication skills Collaborative and proactive personality The Group Fidelity’s High Income & Alternatives division brings deep investment expertise across non-investment-grade corporate credit, emerging markets debt, and real estate debt. Our platform spans the full capital structure, offering investment solutions in high-yield bonds, leveraged loans, convertible securities, and equities, with capabilities across both public and private markets. In addition to our core offerings, we provide a range of specialized strategies, including tactical asset allocation, structured credit, and liquid alternatives arbitrage strategies. Our division is distinguished by a fully integrated, highly experienced team that leverages Fidelity’s robust research platform and advanced risk management capabilities.

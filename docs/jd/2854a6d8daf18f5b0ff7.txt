A ssociate, Transaction Management We are seeking a n Associate, Transaction Management to join JLL and a high-performing team responsible for executing a large volume and variety of real estate transactions that create enterprise value for one of JLL’s largest global technology clients — a Fortune 100 technology company — in partnership with the client’s internal real estate team. Location: This role will be in San Francisco, CA or New York, NY; option for remote based on experience. What this job involves: You will join a high performing, multi-disciplinary team to deliver Transaction Management solutions to one of JLL’s largest global technology clients. This client is known for pairing enormous scale with a fast-moving, founder-style culture — an ownership mentality, a healthy disregard for the impossible, and an unrelenting focus on the people who use its products. JLL’s team is expected to operate with the same energy, transparency, and bias for action. Critical responsibilities include (but not limited to): Establish and maintain a strong, trusted client relationship; support account leaders in executing business and real estate strategies Assist the client with cost reduction, financial and occupancy optimization, monetization, and other portfolio goals Build and analyze financial models (Net Effective Rent, termination penalty, scenario cash flow/P&L) to support transaction and portfolio-level decisions Prepare transaction analysis and materials — RFPs, comparison packages, LOIs, broker opinions of value, spend approval documentation, and executive business cases (P&L impact, cash flow, strategic alignment) Coordinate with stakeholders across functions to drive spend and transaction approvals; support document execution for all transaction types Manage lease administration — track terminations, renewals, rent commencements, and portfolio-wide lease/property data; provide monthly reporting Maintain and improve financial templates/tools and KPI data used across the broader transaction team, supporting onboarding and identifying process effic iencies Be extremely proactive and responsive “Raising the Bar” Leverage, invent and simplify best practices, including the continuous improvement of process, tools and templates “See around corners” - providing actionable advice on mitigation of anticipated risks/taking advantage of opportunities Proactively identify process inefficiencies, formulate and execute on implementation plans, followed by tracking and demonstrating results Every day is different, and in all these activities, we’d encourage you to show your ingenuity — with deep respect for the client’s partners and colleagues at every turn, and an unwavering focus on the end user of the client’s products in every decision you make.

Job Description Leads the technology risk discipline, driving the execution and continuous enhancement of the technology risk management framework across the Technology organization. Oversees identification, assessment, mitigation, and monitoring of technology-related risks—including cybersecurity, data privacy, IT operations, and emerging technologies—ensuring alignment with business priorities, risk appetite, and regulatory expectations. Implements and operationalizes the technology risk strategy and governance structures, ensuring consistent adoption of policies, standards, and practices across technology functions while establishing clear accountability and effective oversight. Directs complex risk identification and assessment efforts, with a forward-looking focus on emerging technologies, strategic initiatives, and cross-functional risk exposures. Drives the design and adoption of mitigation strategies and control improvements. Acts as a senior advisor and thought leader to technology and business leadership, shaping technology risk posture, influencing strategic decision-making, and driving the timely escalation, prioritization, and resolution of material risks across the organization. Leads risk reporting, metrics, and analytics, delivering clear, actionable insights to senior leadership while fostering a strong risk-aware culture and strengthening organizational capability in managing technology risk. Key Responsibilities Technology Risk Governance and Oversight : Ensuring adherence to PNC's Technology Risk strategy, Enterprise Risk Framework, policies, and regulatory requirements. Candidate must have excellent written communication and verbal presentation skills. Risk Reporting and Executive Communications : Developing and delivering risk reporting, key risk metrics, Technology Committee materials, and executive-level governance reporting. Improving the timeliness, quality, transparency, and effectiveness of executive risk reporting. Controls and Compliance Management : Overseeing compliance with technology risk requirements, monitoring risk metrics, and ensuring effective execution of controls and oversight activities. Leadership of a Technology Risk Team : Managing a team that includes Technology Risk Managers, Technology Risk Advisors, a Technology Risk Supervisor, and analytics resources. Strategic Planning and Cross-Functional Coordination : Translating technology risk strategy into actionable plans, coordinating with business and technology stakeholders, and driving enterprise-wide risk and governance initiatives. Talent Development and Organizational Leadership : Leading talent discussions, development planning, succession activities, and employee growth initiatives within the Technology Risk organization.

Job description Group Description: Equity Capital Markets (ECM) originates, advises, structures, and executes equity and equity-linked offerings for corporates and financial sponsors. ECM sits at the intersection of Evercore’s investment banking advisory business and the Evercore ISI research / sales & trading business. ECM’s activities include IPOs, follow-ons, convertible/derivative strategies, as well as other equity-adjacent advisory work around capital structure analysis and M&A. What's the Position? We are seeking an Analyst or Associate to join our Equity Capital Markets Syndicate team. The Syndicate team sits at the intersection of Equity Capital Markets, Investment Banking and the public equity markets and plays a central role in the marketing, execution and distribution of equity and equity-linked transactions. The Analyst/Associate will work closely with senior members of the ECM team on the execution of IPOs, follow-on offerings, convertible securities and other equity capital markets transactions. The role provides significant exposure to live transactions, institutional investors, Equity Sales & Trading, corporate clients and financial sponsors. This is a highly execution- and markets-oriented role requiring strong analytical skills, attention to detail, sound judgment and the ability to operate effectively in a fast-paced environment. The ideal candidate will have 1–3 years of experience in investment banking or capital markets and a strong interest in public equity markets. Primary Responsibilities: Analysts and Associates in ECM Syndicate participate in the execution of deal activity and work closely with senior Syndicate professionals throughout the transaction process. Specific responsibilities include: Supporting the execution of equity and equity-linked transactions, including IPOs, follow-on offerings, and convertible securities Working closely with senior Syndicate professionals throughout the transaction process, including positioning, launch, bookbuilding, pricing, allocation, and closing Monitoring and analyzing order books and investor demand, including order quality, concentration, price sensitivity, momentum, and investor participation Coordinating with Equity Sales to gather and synthesize investor feedback on live and prospective transactions Analyzing transaction sizing, pricing, allocations, trading dynamics, and aftermarket performance to help inform execution strategy Monitoring equity market conditions, new issuance activity, investor sentiment, comparable companies, and precedent transactions Preparing market updates, transaction materials, investor feedback summaries, pricing analyses, and other materials for internal and client discussions Analyzing companies, sectors, corporate filings, shareholder data, and broader ECM market trends Building relationships and coordinating with members of the broader Evercore team across Investment Banking and Evercore ISI Research, Sales & Trading Maintaining and analyzing internal deal, investor, and ECM issuance databases and supporting broader ECM business development initiatives Qualifications: Analysts and Associates in ECM play a vital role in building Evercore’s underwriting business. We are looking for excited, creative, organized, and resourceful multi-taskers with high intellectual curiosity and the long-term orientation to help build the premier independent ECM franchise.

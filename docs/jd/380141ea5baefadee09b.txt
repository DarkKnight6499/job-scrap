Management Level: 86 Work Shift: 1ST SHIFT (United States of America) Summary The Associate Customer Development Manager supports the development and execution of customer strategies for key Retail Poultry customers. This role partners with Sales and cross-functional teams to coordinate customer planning, develop compelling selling stories, and ensure teams are prepared for key customer engagements. Essential Duties & Responsibilities Supports customer strategy development and execution in partnership with Sales and Sales Strategy & Execution. Coordinates key customer initiatives, including Top-to-Tops, Joint Growth Plans, and Quarterly Business Reviews. Manages planning, content development, timelines, and cross-functional coordination for key customer meetings. Develops customer-facing presentations, selling stories, and sales tools to support customer engagement and growth. Partners with Category Management, Marketing, Analytics, Innovation, and Poultry Business Units to incorporate relevant insights and business priorities into customer materials. Prepares leadership and Sales teams for key customer meetings through coordinated research, content, and meeting materials. Supports retail conferences and other strategic customer engagements. Collaborates across businesses and functions to deliver cohesive OneTyson customer stories. Knowledge, Skills & Abilities Strong project management and organizational skills. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Ability to develop clear, compelling customer-facing presentations. Strong cross-functional collaboration and relationship-building skills. Ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines. Proficiency in PowerPoint and Excel; experience with Power BI and Canva preferred. Requirements: Education: BS or BA degree in business-related program or equivalent combination of education and business experience. Experience: 3 plus years professional business experience strong analytical and financial acumen with the ability to articulate business intelligence. Retail industry experience is a plus. Person must be detail-oriented and a highly-skilled project manager able to manage several projects at one time. Computer Skills: Intermediate skills with computer especially with PowerPoint and Excel; Knowledge of the Tyson Business Computer Systems is preferred. Communication Skills: Excellent verbal and written communication skills required.

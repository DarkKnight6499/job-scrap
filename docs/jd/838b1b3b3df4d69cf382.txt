BAIN CAPITAL ASSET MANAGEMENT The Bain Capital Insurance investment team is comprised of 30 U.S. team members and 4 U.K. team members, who spend their time reviewing investment opportunities, conducting due diligence, preparing investment materials and monitoring existing portfolio investments. In addition, we are continuing to build out Bain Capital Asset Management (BCAM), a purpose-built asset management group with an investment mandate centered around providing insurance-focused asset management capabilities to both internal and external partners. BCAM focuses on managing assets backing liabilities across life insurance, annuities, P&C, and Long-Term Care products, with a focus on both capital preservation and generating strong loss-adjusted returns. Additionally, BCAM will leverage the Bain Capital platform and captive asset origination platforms to focus on investments across a wide spectrum of asset classes, from traditional fixed income to esoterics and alternatives. While prior experience in the insurance industry would be beneficial, it is not required. The successful candidate will, however, have relevant finance experience within the asset management industry.

Senior Market Analyst, Derivatives
London Stock Exchange Group
Location: USA-New York-28 Liberty Street
Link: https://lseg.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/USA-New-York-28-Liberty-Street/Senior-Market-Analyst--Derivatives_R0122615

Support the pricing, production, and delivery of derivatives valuation and analytics products across the Americas region. Work closely with clients, product teams, and technology partners to ensure the quality, accuracy, and timely delivery of derivatives pricing data and analytics. Key Responsibilities Lead the end-to-end production cycle for derivatives products, including quality checks, production, and client deliveries. Support pricing and valuation activities across a range of derivatives products. Capture and document requirements for both vanilla and sophisticated derivatives products. Handle client pricing workflows and provide analytical support throughout the valuation cycle. Serve as a key contact for client communications related to derivatives pricing and analytics. Support the development, testing, and implementation of quantitative models and methodologies. Use Python and other programming languages to automate processes and enhance analytics. Products Covered Vanilla Products: IRS, Swaptions, FX Options, Equity Options, CDS, TRS, Inflation Swaps Complex Products:: Dispersion Swaps, Volatility Swaps, Hybrid Products, Exotic Structured Notes Qualifications & Experience Master's degree in Financial Engineering, Quantitative Finance, Mathematical Finance or equivalent. Experience in Quantitative Analytics, Quantitative Research, Derivatives Analytics, is preferred but not a must. Understanding of derivatives pricing and valuation concepts. Experience with Python or other programming languages. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and interpersonal skills. Join us and make a difference in global financial markets!

Corporate Finance Underwriting Analyst – Oil & Gas (ADP)
First Citizens Bank
Location: Dallas, Texas
Link: https://jobs.firstcitizens.com/jobs/35652

Overview This is a hybrid role, with the expectation that time working will regularly take place inside and outside of a company office. First Citizens Bank’s Oil and Gas Finance Group lends money to the Exploration & Production, Midstream and Compression segments of the Energy Value Chain. About the Analyst Development Program : As an Analyst you will participate in the Commercial Bank Analyst Development Program which is designed to build a broad and deep technical skillset through formal classes, online coursework, and on the job training. The program starts on July 7, 2027, on-site at our New York City Office with an 8-week intensive curriculum where participants learn about: Underwriting fundamentals Financial Analysis and Excel Modeling First Citizens Bank Core Values Application-specific training on systems Business unit overviews with team leaders Strategic priorities of the Bank Credit Policies and Procedures Following the 8 weeks, Analysts join their teams and stay connected with colleagues through training and professional development sessions. We provide a superior foundation and launchpad for continued success for our Analysts as they begin their careers. Responsibilities & Qualifications Upon completion of the ADP program, candidates will join the Energy, Oil & Gas team and work within a team construct to assist in the credit underwriting and portfolio management of diverse debt solutions for companies. Analysts will perform: Financial modeling in excel of E&P, midstream, and compression companies. Conducting thorough due diligence on projects, including company and industry analyses, as well as reviewing engineering reports. Preparation of internal credit memorandums. Preparation of marketing materials for potential clients. Work as part of a Deal Team on live transactions, engaged in many aspects of credit underwriting and portfolio management, financial analysis, credit memo preparation. Participate in internal credit approval meetings and client/customer calls, as appropriate. Attend professional development and informational sessions, networking and community service events. POSITION SPECIFIC SKILLS Analytical Reasoning Communication (Written and Verbal) Credit Analysis Critical Thinking Skills Financial Analysis Proficiency in Excel BASIC QUALIFICATIONS Minimum Required Education and Experience: Bachelor's Degree OR Associate's Degree/Equivalent and 2 years' experience Coursework or internship in commercial lending or credit Analysis, financial statement analysis, and finance fundamentals PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS Bachelor's degree completed upon start of the program (by July 7, 2027) Major or concentration in a quantitative field including engineering, finance, accounting or economics Strong verbal and written communication skills Preferred minimum undergraduate GPA 3.0. Previous internship experience in an analytical role within an energy focused institution.

Join us and transform the lives of patients while transforming your career. Transactional Finance, Senior Associate What you will do Let’s do this. Let’s change the world. In this vital role you will be responsible for executing transactional finance activities across the procure-to-pay (P2P) lifecycle. This role ensures accurate, timely, and compliant processing of financial transactions while supporting operational workflows, resolving issues, and maintaining data integrity. Working with general guidance, the role applies established processes and procedures to deliver consistent results and support effective service delivery within GBTC. Responsibilities: · Execute P2P transactions, including requisition support, purchase order creation, invoice validation, payment processing, and expense tracking · Ensure accuracy, completeness, and timeliness of transactions in alignment with SLAs and established processes · Support financial execution for R&D activities, including lab consumables, capital equipment, and OPEX · ·Process transactions supporting Healthcare Community (HCP-related) and Non-HCP engagements · Resolve transactional issues of moderate complexity, including discrepancies related to invoices, payments, and vendor data · Investigate and escalate more complex issues as needed · Communicate with stakeholders to resolve questions and ensure continuity of operations · Utilize enterprise systems (e.g., SAP, Ariba, Anaplan, C3) to execute and track transactions · Maintain accurate and complete data within systems, including C3 · Support system issue identification and resolution in partnership with technical teams · Generate and review reports to support operational tracking and accuracy · Partner with HCC, sourcing, AP, and finance teams to support transaction processing · Support execution of HCP and Non-HCP engagement workflows · Provide guidance to stakeholders on transactional processes and requirements · Ensure adherence to established SOPs, controls, and compliance requirements · Maintain accurate documentation and system records · Support audit and compliance activities as required · Identify and escalate risks or issues · Identify opportunities to improve efficiency and accuracy within day-to-day work · Support implementation of process improvements and automation initiatives · Contribute to maintaining consistency in processes across teams · Provide guidance and support to less experienced team members · Assist with onboarding and documentation of processes · Share knowledge and best practices within the team · Working knowledge of P2P processes and transactional finance operations · Experience with enterprise systems (e.g., SAP, Ariba, Anaplan, C3) · Understanding of SOPs, controls, and compliance requirements What we expect of you We are all

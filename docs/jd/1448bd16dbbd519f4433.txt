What you will be doing: The Vertex Internship Program provides students with a hands-on opportunity to gain valuable insights into the pharmaceutical industry. Interns will work closely with some of the brightest minds in the field, experiencing an innovative, team-oriented, and collaborative environment where fearless pursuit of excellence is a core value. Participants will be challenged by their project plans, connect with peers, and engage with organizational leaders through Professional Development Workshops. In addition to their specific work assignments, interns will contribute to meaningful projects and feel valued as part of the Vertex community. The selected intern will join the Global General Ledger (‘GL’) Accounting team, which operates under Vertex’s Record to Report umbrella. This team manages and maintains the company’s global financial records, ensuring accuracy, consistency, and compliance for financial reporting. The intern will assist with key financial activities, processes, and ad-hoc requests, gaining exposure to GL operations in the biotech industry. This role offers a unique opportunity to develop communication and presentation skills while supporting accounting and senior management, as well as contributing to process optimization initiatives to streamline the financial close process. Key Responsibilities: GL Close Support - Gain hands-on experience with the monthly GL close by supporting cash management and other general accounting activities. Assist with post-close governance analysis to build an understanding of key close activities, dependencies, and opportunities to improve overall close efficiency. Project Assistance and Coordination - Provide support to the GL team on various projects and initiatives as opportunities arise, which may include project testing, system implementations, integration activities, and process improvement efforts. Support may involve project coordination, action item tracking, documentation, testing, and other activities based on project needs.

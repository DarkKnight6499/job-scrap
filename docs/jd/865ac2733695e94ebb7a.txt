Investment Associate Fiduciary Trust International is hiring an Investment Associate to join our office located in Atlanta, GA. The Investment Associate serves as a key partner to Portfolio Managers in all aspects of the investment process, including the design, execution, and ongoing management of client portfolios in alignment with the firm’s investment policies and client-specific objectives. The role is expected to serve as training for junior investment professionals who are interested in developing a career in wealth management. How You Will Add Value Core Responsibilities You will construct customized portfolios aligned with client objectives, constraints, and firm market views. You will analyze incoming portfolios and recommend tax-aware transitions to firm investment strategies. You will execute equity, fixed income, and fund trades with accurate allocations. You will support tax-loss harvesting, gain/loss analysis, tax-lot management, and concentrated-position strategies. You will monitor account activity, income, distributions, capital flows, and portfolio risks. You will apply developing judgment to cash management, trade allocations, and portfolio implementation. Strategic Responsibilities You will review portfolios and recommend rebalancing or tactical adjustments within established guidelines. You will prepare portfolio analytics, performance reports, cash-flow analyses, and long-term wealth projections. You will interpret markets, economic developments, research, and firm views for portfolio implications. You will independently manage smaller client accounts as experience and proficiency increase. Teams & Collaboration You will partner with Portfolio Managers on portfolio decisions, performance insights, and client discussions. You will prepare customized analyses, proposals, allocations, recommendations, and client presentation materials. You will progressively present performance, positioning, market perspectives, and recommendations in client meetings. You will coordinate portfolio setup, benchmarks, and composites with performance and client service teams. What Will Help You Be Successful 1-5 years of relevant financial services experience Foundational knowledge of markets, portfolio construction, asset allocation, and major asset classes Strong Excel proficiency and familiarity with portfolio, trading, performance, accounting, and customer relationship management (CRM) systems Experience with Charles River, Aladdin, or comparable platforms preferred Strong analytical judgment and ability to translate portfolio, risk, performance, and market data Client-focused mindset with strong attention to detail and risk awareness. Clear written and verbal communication with colleagues, clients, and prospects. Professional maturity, accountability, collaboration, independence, and intellectual curiosity. Interest in long-term wealth management growth and increasing portfolio and client responsibility.

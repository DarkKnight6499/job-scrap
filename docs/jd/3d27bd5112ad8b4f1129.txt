About the Legal & Regulatory Group The Legal & Regulatory Group is responsible for partnering with Bridgewater businesses to protect the legal and regulatory interests of the firm and its clients and managing legal and regulatory risk to ensure that employees act in accordance with the highest legal and ethical standards. Our group maintains in-house expertise in many areas, including corporate, investment management, private funds, intellectual property, employment and commercial law and compliance. The team works closely with a broad range of departments within the firm and manages relationships with external counsel and regulatory consultants. About Your Role The Senior Compliance Officer ("Senior Compliance Officer") will be expected to be a leader within Bridgewater's compliance function on day one. Together with other members of the team, the Senior Compliance Officer will be responsible for ensuring compliance with Bridgewater's regulatory obligations. While expected to contribute across all areas of the compliance program, the Senior Compliance Officer's primary responsibility will be owning Bridgewater's regulatory reporting and change management processes from start to finish. This role is about doing as much as it is about thinking and advising. The Senior Compliance Officer will be personally accountable for making sure the right filings are made accurately, excellently, and on time. Candidates should expect to spend meaningful time in the details of the filings themselves, not solely advising others on how to prepare them. Responsibilities Include Preparing, reviewing, and submitting Bridgewater's regulatory filings and reports, and personally driving each one through to completion, including gathering and validating underlying data and resolving issues as they arise Building and running the processes, controls, and methodology used to produce accurate filings and manage regulatory interactions Scanning the regulatory horizon for forthcoming changes and implementing them in how filings and interactions are actually executed Owning quality assurance so that every filing and interaction is accurate, complete, and timely Advising stakeholders on their regulatory obligations and the processes used to meet them The Senior Compliance Officer will also be expected to work with the Chief Compliance Officer ("CCO"), Deputy CCO, and other senior leaders on strategic initiatives and essential workstreams. For a high achiever, this strategic work is expected to grow over time, and ultimately the scope of the role will be limited only by the abilities and dedication of the person in it.

Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Operations Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Procurement Strategy and Strategic Sourcing - Value Capture Senior Associate you will analyze sourcing and procurement opportunities, develop value capture strategies, and help clients improve cost performance, supplier collaboration, and procurement effectiveness within our Operations Consulting practice. As a Senior Associate you will build meaningful client connections, manage assigned workstreams, use critical thinking to break down complex concepts, and develop your technical skills while learning how to guide and inspire junior team members. In this role at PwC, you will assess sourcing strategies, identify opportunities to reduce spend, support supplier relationship management, and contribute to digital procurement initiatives that help clients improve operational performance. Responsibilities - Analyzing client sourcing and procurement needs to identify value capture opportunities and cost reduction levers - Developing strategic sourcing approaches that improve supplier performance, procurement efficiency, and supply chain resilience - Managing workstreams for procurement strategy, contract negotiation, and supplier selection activities - Interpreting spend, supplier, and market data to shape actionable recommendations and sourcing decisions - Supporting contract management and contractual compliance monitoring across sourcing initiatives - Applying procurement software and e-procurement tools to streamline processes and improve visibility - Assessing supplier capabilities through evaluation, negotiation, and relationship management activities - Coordinating planning, budgeting, and forecasting inputs for sourcing and procurement initiatives - Guiding junior team members on task execution, issue resolution, and deliverable preparation - Building client relationships by anticipating needs, adapting to changing priorities, and presenting clear insights What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 3 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for at least one of the following fields of study: Accounting, Business Administration/Management, Computer Science/Information Systems, Design/Multimedia, Economics, Engineering, Finance, Operations/Supply Chain, Organizational Management/Behavior, Project/Technology Management, Science - At least one of the following: Certified Supply Chain Management Professional (CSCMP), American Production and Inventory Control Society (APICS), Institute for Supply Management (ISM) - Demonstrating procurement strategy skills in value capture engagements -

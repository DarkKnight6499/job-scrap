Job title: Associate Director Field Effectiveness and Communications Location: Cambridge, MA / Morristown, NJ Our Team The Customer Facing Capabilities (CFC) organization is designed to drive excellence in field operations and commercial execution across Sanofi's North American business. This team supports field teams through capability building, process optimization, and technology enablement — delivering impact across Field Learning & Development, Field Deployment & Incentive Compensation, CRM, Field Reporting & Engagements, Field Sales Analytics, and Change Management. The objective of the Customer Facing Capabilities team is to: Drive standardization and continuous improvement across field operations processes Remove barriers between field-facing functions and accelerate best practice sharing Free up field teams to focus on customer engagement and commercial execution Clarify accountabilities for day-to-day field activities and long-term capability improvement Realize efficiencies and economies of scale across North America field operations Accelerate adoption of next-generation tools and ways of working About the Job The Associate Director, Field Effectiveness & Communications serves as the primary communications lead for Sanofi's Customer Facing Capabilities (CFC) organization, supporting North America field operations. This role will partner with cross-functional teams to develop and execute internal communication strategies that keep the field organization informed, engaged, and equipped during periods of workflow enhancement, system updates, and broader business transformation. This role plays a critical part in translating complex, technical changes into clear, actionable messaging while building the trust and alignment needed to drive adoption. In addition, this individual will support key stakeholder engagement programs — including Field Sales Leadership feedback loops, and CFC Champion governance — ensuring that the voice of the field is heard, acted upon, and continuously integrated into CFC's ways of working.

The Operations Risk and Control AVP will be responsible for assisting with the duties of the BURM (Business Unit Risk Manager) for Operations. The associate will be responsible for key aspects related to issue management, risk assessments, control testing, risk metric reporting, documenting loss events, and assisting operations business lines with internal audits and/or RCSA’s.

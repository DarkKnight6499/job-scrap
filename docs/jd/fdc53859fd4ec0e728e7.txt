The Role As Associate Director, Customer Innovation & Design Leadership, you will lead customer innovation engagements end to end—from challenge framing and executive workshops through prototype direction, evidence, and the decisions that follow. Serving as the customer's strategic contact and a trusted advisor to senior leadership, you will bridge the gap between complex business problems, research insights, and tangible concepts people can test. This is a strategic design-leadership and consulting role focused on human-AI systems. Composed in ambiguity, you will create just enough structure to move fast while directing multidisciplinary teams of creative technologists, designers, researchers, and engineers. You bring practical AI fluency and a clear point of view on how AI reshapes customer experiences, driving outcomes that lead directly to funded sprints, pilots, and enterprise transformation. What You Will Do Own the customer journey, shaping brief arcs, success measures, immersion, co-design workshops, builds, and post-Lab follow-through. Lead CIOs, CTOs, COOs, and senior leadership teams through ambiguity, constructive challenge, and strategic decisions. Translate enterprise priorities, user needs, and operational realities into clear value hypotheses and actionable design challenges. Define human-AI system interactions—designing handoffs, exceptions, control, and accountability between people and agents. Direct multidisciplinary teams through focused prototype builds, setting fidelity and defining evidence using realistic scenarios and synthetic personas. Connect engagement findings and validation evidence to definitive executive recommendations and owned next steps. Turn validated work into reusable methods, patterns, prompts, agent states, policies, and reference builds. Build practice capability, coach multidisciplinary talent, and dedicate capacity to moonshot initiatives in human-AI collaboration. Executive presence and facilitation skills, with a track record of guiding C-suite stakeholders through complex co-design and decisions. Hands-on familiarity with digital, spatial, or physical prototyping, design tools (Figma, Miro), and enterprise AI governance.

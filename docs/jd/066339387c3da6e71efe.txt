Location: 4910 Tiedeman Road, Brooklyn Ohio Compliance Associate, Regulatory Intelligence About the Job This position is in the Regulatory Intelligence Compliance Team within Compliance Risk Management. The position is responsible for identifying, summarizing, and consulting on current and emerging legal and regulatory compliance requirements as they relate to all products, services, business processes, and lines of business across Key. The position may also be responsible for performing regulatory research and analysis on applicable topics potentially impacting the organization. The qualified candidate must be able to work both independently and as part of a team, adhere to schedules, meet deadlines within a limited timeframe, multi-task, and use sound judgment taking into consideration compliance risk implications for KeyCorp. Essential Functions Identify new or revised rules, regulations, laws and guidance applicable to the financial services industry. Review Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Generative AI outputs for their soundness, clarity and accuracy in summarizing regulatory changes, revising where necessary. Collaborate with Subject Matter Expert(s) in providing research and analysis of new or modified regulatory requirements. Identify the individual, unique business areas to which regulatory changes may apply. Identify the risk disciplines to which regulatory requirements apply. Track regulatory changes and analysis in the Governance, Risk and Compliance (“GRC”) system of record. Engage with risk partners to ensure required remediation plans are appropriately implemented in a timely manner, and that the status of these plans is accurate. Provide sound judgment and decision-making in analyzing and escalating concerns. Assist with maintaining internal policies and procedures including updating and drafting training materials or revising procedures as needed and supporting and leading ad hoc projects. Effectively partner and establish trusted relationships with risk, legal and business liaisons, as well as senior management and others regarding corporate-wide compliance issues. Commitment to in-office presence at one of Key’s primary offices consistent with corporate expectations (currently, 3 days/week). Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in business, finance, legal or related field. Juris Doctorate and/or Certified Regulatory Compliance Manager (CRCM) designation. 2+ years of professional experience in the area of researching and analyzing statutory and regulatory requirements. Strong research, analytical, and problem-solving skills. Excellent organization and time management abilities. Strong attention to detail and accuracy. Proven relationship building skills. Proficient in comprehension of compliance laws and regulations. High ethical standards and ability to maintain confidentiality. Ability to navigate complex organizational structures, communication channels, and networks. Preferred Qualifications 1+ years’ experience as either a financial services attorney, compliance professional, bank regulator or other comparable legal experience. Experience identifying and summarizing regulatory changes with respect to the financial services industry.

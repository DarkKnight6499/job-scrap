Your opportunity Reporting to the Head of Business Architecture, the Associate Business Architect will support efforts to establish and maintain a consistent reference model of our business with stakeholders across the firm such that we can clearly map our operating model (business process, people/organization, technology, data, services and governance). The role will be to support the Head of Business Architecture with tasks relating to the management of data relating to the firm’s business architecture framework, and the way in which business architecture is applied on change initiatives across the firm. Ongoing activities might include: Maintaining current state assessments using the business architecture framework; Performing a supporting role in projects and other change initiatives where the application of business architecture forms a necessary part of the scope; Developing innovative dashboards and decision support tools for senior executives and leadership teams; Supporting key change initiatives, including merger and acquisition (M&A) integration activities; Performing analysis to support the identification of organization improvement using business architecture frameworks, methodologies and techniques; Canvassing stakeholder views/opinions from a broad range of internal teams, including operations, sales, client account management, investments, financial crime compliance and legal; Identifying trends and insights from data relating to the firm's business architecture; Providing support to the rest of the team and firm in designing target operating models and associated implementation roadmaps, ensuring they are aligned to strategy and key business objectives; Performing analysis to support the identification of common cross-organisational needs and capture synergies Creating and amendment of team management artefacts, including team procedures; controls; internal reporting templates and processes; terms of reference; toolsets; record management; Performing document management - maintaining operating model documentation in a well-structured channel in architecture tools and document stores as required; Carrying out other duties as assigned.

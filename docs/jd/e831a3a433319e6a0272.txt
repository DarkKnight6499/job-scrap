The Principal Fraud & Risk Analytics Professional is responsible for identifying, analyzing, and mitigating fraud risk through data-driven insights, strategic recommendations, and fraud prevention solutions. This role also independently drives fraud initiatives from concept through implementation, balancing fraud risk, business objectives, operational impacts, and customer experience. Sub-Family Definition The Fraud & Risk Analytics group is responsible for leveraging advanced analytics, data insights, and industry intelligence to detect emerging fraud risks, evaluate fraud performance, and develop strategies that reduce fraud losses while maintaining an appropriate customer experience. Activities may include partnering across business, technology, operations, and external stakeholders to design, implement, and optimize fraud controls, monitoring capabilities, and risk mitigation initiatives; and using data-driven analysis to identify trends, recommend actions, and influence strategic decisions related to fraud risk management. Career Specialization Definition The Fraud & Risk Analytics work focuses on: Developing and maintaining financial crime risk reporting, dashboards, scorecards, and metrics to support business and regulatory requirements Analyzing customer, transaction, and risk data to identify trends, emerging threats, anomalies, and control effectiveness Leading Know Your Customer (KYC), Customer Due Diligence (CDD), Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD), and customer identification program initiatives Overseeing transaction monitoring, alert management, investigations, and escalation of suspicious or high-risk activity Evaluating and enhancing financial crime detection methodologies, monitoring systems, and reporting processes Managing regulatory examinations, audits, and information requests related to financial crimes programs Partnering with Risk, Compliance, Technology, Operations, and business leaders to improve data quality, reporting accuracy, and operational effectiveness Recommending and implementing process improvements, automation opportunities, and risk mitigation strategies based on analytical findings Reporting key risk indicators, trends, and program performance to senior management and governance committees Other duties as assigned Career Level Definition Recognized subject matter expert in a job area typically obtained through advanced education and work experience.

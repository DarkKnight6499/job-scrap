Consults with and supports business clients in order to provide the right treasury management solutions for their unique needs. Manages a typical portfolio of treasury management clients with annual fee income of $1.0 million or greater. Generates year-over-year growth in treasury management fee income and deposit balances, and meets annual goals established by management.

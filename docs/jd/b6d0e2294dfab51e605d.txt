Associate Credit Research Analyst - Investment Grade Healthcare Sector
Lord Abbett
Location: Jersey City
Link: https://careers-lordabbett.icims.com/jobs/2339/associate-credit-research-analyst---investment-grade-healthcare-sector/job

The Role We are looking to expand our Investment Grade credit research team by adding an Associate Research Analyst to cover Investment Grade corporate credits within the Healthcare Sector. This individual will be responsible for assisting with generating investment ideas within our $55 billion Investment Grade corporates franchise and will interact with investment professionals across our taxable fixed income mandates. We’ll trust you to: Conduct detailed industry and company fundamental analysis within the Healthcare Sector Identify primary and secondary investment opportunities and make investment recommendations to investment grade portfolio managers Build and maintain industry and company financial models Construct company and industry research notes, providing background information, graphs and data tables Develop and refine research knowledge and skills under the guidance of the Co-Head of Investment Grade Corporate Research Communicate and develop relationships with issuer management teams, sell-side professionals and industry experts You'll need to have: 5-7 years of credit analysis experience covering fundamental industry trends and companies in the Healthcare Sector Excellent quantitative, communication, and investment management skills BA/BS degree required Ability to articulate investment ideas in a clear and concise manner with internal and external constituents An attitude and practice of proactive idea generation Ability to work quickly and accurately in a fast-paced, dynamic environment Some buy-side experience working in a team-oriented environment is preferred Why Lord Abbett?

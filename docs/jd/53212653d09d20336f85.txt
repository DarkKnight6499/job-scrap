SUMMARY The Production Analyst supports the Colliers Institutional Equity Advisory team, a group within Colliers Debt & Structured Finance that raises joint venture, preferred, and recapitalization equity for commercial real estate sponsors nationwide. The team advises developers and owners, primarily in multifamily, on structuring and placing LP capital with institutional investors, including pension funds and their advisors, insurance companies, private equity real estate funds, sovereign and foreign capital, and family offices. The Analyst provides cash flow modeling, underwriting, marketing, and execution support across the full life of an equity raise, from the initial sponsor pitch and investor targeting through term sheet negotiation and closing. The role is well suited to a candidate with investment banking, consulting, or principal investing training who wants direct, hands-on exposure to institutional real estate capital formation. ABOUT THE TEAM Institutional equity advisory. Raise joint venture, programmatic JV, co-GP, preferred equity, and recapitalization capital on behalf of commercial real estate sponsors, with a focus on multifamily development, value-add, and stabilized investments National coverage. Execute assignments in major and growth markets across the United States, working alongside Colliers debt, investment sales, and capital markets professionals in local offices Deep investor relationships. Maintain active dialogue with a broad universe of institutional LP capital and track evolving mandates, return requirements, and structuring preferences Sponsor-side advice. Help sponsors position their business plans, structure JV economics and governance, and run competitive processes that deliver well-aligned, long-term capital partners Lean, deal-driven culture. A small team running multiple concurrent raises, where precision in models and materials directly affects investor confidence, and where analysts take on real responsibility early ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Build and maintain institutional-quality cash flow models for development, value-add, and stabilized assets, including rent roll and operating statement analysis, development budgets, sources and uses, debt assumptions, returns analysis (IRR, equity multiple, yield on cost, cash-on-cash), and sensitivity and scenario analyses Model joint venture structures, including multi-tier distribution waterfalls, promotes, preferred returns, fees, and co-investment, as well as preferred equity and recapitalization terms; compare investor proposals on a like-for-like basis Prepare investor marketing materials, including offering memoranda, teasers and executive summaries, investor presentations, sponsor pitch books, market overviews, and case studies Support equity raise processes by building target investor lists by mandate, managing NDAs and data rooms, tracking investor outreach and feedback, maintaining process timelines, and summarizing term sheets and LOIs for sponsor decision-making Research and summarize submarket fundamentals, rent and sale comparables, supply pipelines, investor mandates, return thresholds, and JV pricing trends to help position opportunities and advise sponsors Support business development by evaluating new opportunities, reviewing sponsor track records, and preparing pitch materials and engagement proposals Coordinate with sponsors, investors, Colliers professionals across debt, investment sales, and local offices, and third-party consultants to gather information, resolve questions, and keep transactions moving from engagement through closing Review and organize due diligence materials, including sponsor track records and financials, appraisals, environmental and property condition reports, market studies, development budgets and schedules, entity documents, and JV agreement term summaries Maintain the investor database (CRM), pipeline reports, deal files, a database of comparable JV terms and pricing, and the team’s closed-transaction library with accuracy and timely updates Participate in pipeline reviews, sponsor and investor calls, and internal strategy discussions;

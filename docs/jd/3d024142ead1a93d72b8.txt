Skills and Competencies 1–3 years of professional experience in consulting, corporate development, project management, business operations, or another project-based role Strong project management and problem-solving capabilities, with the ability to structure and execute against high-priority project objectives Demonstrated experience independently managing multiple concurrent deliverables while collaborating effectively with peers to align priorities, resolve issues, and meet established deadlines Proven ability to use advanced Excel functions to analyze and synthesize complex data and PowerPoint to create clear, executive-ready presentations for senior stakeholders; PMO project tools knowledge is a plus Highly organized, self-motivated, and adaptable, with strong attention to detail and the ability to work effectively with quick turnaround times and deadlines Basic understanding of artificial intelligence concepts, with curiosity and enthusiasm for learning how AI tools can be used to improve processes and drive efficiency. Interest in exploring AI systems and a willingness to develop awareness of responsible AI practices, including risk management and ethical use. Education Bachelor's degree is required Responsibilities The Associate will support the execution of integration and divestiture initiatives and high-priority, ELT-level special projects across Moody's. Own and drive execution of discrete integration or divestiture milestones, including high-value employee-facing milestones such as application and vendor rationalization and employee onboarding and change management Support high-priority, ELT-level strategic projects, including One Moody's initiatives and other enterprise priorities, through research and analysis, cross-team planning, stakeholder alignment, and execution Partner with functional teams to map cross-functional interdependencies and ensure delivery against integration or divestiture timelines Support end-to-end project execution, from planning and sequencing through implementation, issue resolution, and close-out Identify risks, track issues, and proactively drive resolution across stakeholders, escalating where needed to maintain momentum Develop and maintain reporting and dashboards to monitor progress, track milestones, and provide transparency to senior management and business stakeholders Contribute to integration and divestiture playbooks, frameworks, and best practices while coordinating workstream meeting cadences, leading meetings, and documenting and following up on next steps and action items Leverage AI-enabled tools and digital technologies to improve productivity, accelerate analysis, enhance project execution, and support the development of presentations, reporting, and project deliverables About the Team Our Integration Management Office sits within Corporate Development and is responsible for executing M&A integrations and divestitures, as well as high-priority, ELT-level special projects. We bring structure, urgency, transparency, and accountability to complex cross-functional work — helping align stakeholders, improve the onboarding experience for newly acquired employees, de-risk investments, and accelerate time to value. By joining our team, you will contribute to some of Moody's most strategically significant initiatives, gain broad exposure across the full deal lifecycle, and play an active role in shaping how the enterprise grows and evolves through M&A. You will also have the opportunity to leverage AI-enabled tools and innovative digital technologies to drive smarter, faster execution across every workstream you support.

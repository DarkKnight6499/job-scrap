Specific responsibilities will include: Leading and assisting in business development efforts through opportunity identification, capture planning, proposal development, SOW review, and pricing strategy. Serving as an account leader responsible for expanding Guidehouse's presence within client accounts, driving sales, overseeing deliverables, ensuring contract compliance, managing engagement financials, and maintaining corporate compliance standards. Acting as a trusted advisor to client executives and senior stakeholders, helping shape and execute business strategies related to growth, customer experience, operating model transformation, and technology-enabled change. Becoming a recognized insurance industry leader within Guidehouse and among clients by bringing insights, best practices, and innovative solutions that strengthen Guidehouse's market reputation and expand our footprint. Maintaining a relentless focus on delivering measurable client outcomes and growing Guidehouse's contribution to client success while operating within the firm's strategic priorities and operating framework. Modeling courage, accountability, and sound judgment while navigating complex business, organizational, and client challenges. Driving transformational change by challenging teams to optimize both short-term execution and long-term business outcomes for clients and Guidehouse. Leading strategic initiatives that span business transformation, operations, customer experience, technology modernization, and organizational effectiveness. Engaging, mentoring, coaching, and inspiring consulting teams to achieve continuous improvement and deliver exceptional client service. Reviewing and analyzing contractual revenue, engagement profitability, forecasting, and budgeting while partnering with practice leadership on account planning activities. Guiding and mentoring team members and supporting performance management, career development, and talent growth. Providing input on staffing plans, resource deployment, talent acquisition, and employee retention strategies. Facilitating executive steering committees, strategic workshops, business reviews, and cross-functional stakeholder meetings. Driving execution of complex programs by coordinating cross-functional workstreams, managing risks and dependencies, and ensuring timely delivery of high-quality outcomes. Developing executive-level presentations, business cases, transformation roadmaps, operating model recommendations, and strategic assessments for senior client leadership.

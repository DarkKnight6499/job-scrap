Market Data Financial Analyst
Marsh McLennan
Location: 99 High Street, Boston, MA 02110, United States of America

We are seeking a talented individual to join our Investments & Retirement team, part of our People & Investments business at Marsh . This role will be based in Boston and offers a hybrid work arrangement, requiring a minimum of three days per week in the office. The Market Data Analyst will be part of the Market Data Services team that manages market data vendor services, ensuring investment professionals have seamless access to essential market data and research tools. You will play a critical role in overseeing budget, forecast, and invoice management processes to optimize costs and maximize value. This position requires strong financial analysis skills and the ability to build and maintain effective relationships with vendors and internal stakeholders. We will count on you to: Support the construction of the Market Data budget, continuously monitoring expenses and updating forecasts throughout the fiscal year to ensure alignment with financial goals. Review and validate invoices against contractual terms and budget targets; update pricing and contract details in the Optimize Spend system as needed. Lead the month-end close process by providing accurate accrual data to Finance and delivering comprehensive monthly and quarterly budget and forecast reports to senior management. Maintain dashboard reporting for executive leadership team Collaborate closely with business units to gather and define market data requirements; communicate these to vendors and negotiate terms that minimize financial impact while ensuring service quality. Manage purchase order creation and tracking for the Market Data Services team. What you need to have: Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Business, Finance, or a related field preferred. 2+ years of relevant experience in budget, forecast, and invoice management, preferably within market data or financial services environments. Exceptional oral and written communication skills with strong attention to detail. Advanced proficiency in Excel, including use of complex formulas, data validation, conditional formatting, pivot tables, dashboards, VBA/macros, and Power Query. Experience with data visualization tools and dashboard reporting Solid understanding of accounting principles and strong financial and mathematical acumen. Knowledge of market data usage by investment professionals. What makes you stand out? Experience with TRG Screen Optimize Spend or similar vendor management systems is highly desirable.

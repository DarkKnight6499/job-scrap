Job Description Summary GE Vernova is proud to offer engineering and leadership opportunities to former military veterans through our Junior Officer Leadership Program (JOLP). This program is designed to bridge the gap between professional military service and a career in the private sector. Candidates must reside in or be willing to relocate to Schenectady, NY, as this role reports ONSITE. Job Description At GE Vernova, we have the Energy to Change the World. Building on over 130 years of experience, we are leading a new era of energy - electrifying the world while simultaneously working to decarbonize it. The Junior Officer Leadership Program (JOLP) is a unique opportunity for former US military officers to work in three high-impact roles within GE Vernova’s Wind business. Each candidate will complete three 8-month-long rotations and, for the latter two, will have the autonomy to choose the direction of his or her professional passion. We seek humble yet driven leaders that embrace GE’s core values, have a passion for customers, technology and innovation, and a curiosity to continually learn and seek opportunities to improve what is around them. The program enables participants to build business skills and expand their network to ensure they are best positioned to lead in the commercial space. This program is intended for high-performing officers with a strong track record in their military experience. We challenge each JOLP to constantly ask questions, strategically challenge the status quo, and raise up their fellow teammates. Roles and Responsibilities Integrate into and contribute to various teams across multiple rotational assignments. Develop and broaden business acumen, technical skills, leadership skills, and network through formal curriculum and personal initiative. Contribute as an active participant in the direction and management of the JOLP. Required Qualifications Applicants must have served in the United States Armed Forces as a Commissioned Officer for a minimum of 4 years. Targeting the 4–10 year Commissioned Officer. Bachelor’s degree from an accredited university or college Must be able to report onsite daily to GEV, Schenectady, NY location. Expect 20% travel in the first 8-12 months (other GE locations, project sites, suppliers, etc.) Desired Characteristics Strong oral and written communication skills. Strong interpersonal and leadership skills. Ability to operate autonomously and demonstrate effective initiative in abstract and undefined environments and situations. Ability to influence others and lead small teams. Ability to coordinate several projects simultaneously.

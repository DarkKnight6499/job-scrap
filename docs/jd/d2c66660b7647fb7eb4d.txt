About Us At ANZ, we're shaping a world where people and communities thrive, driven by a common goal: to improve the financial wellbeing and sustainability of our customers. Our Institutional bank helps our largest customers move trade and capital around the region, providing our people with great opportunities to build their technical expertise and their careers. About the Role We have an exciting opportunity to join the PCM Contract Management team in Adelaide to support the SA Government Transaction Banking, Merchant Services and Commercial Cards contracts. As an Associate, PCM Contract Management, you will ensure that we meet our contractual, governance and reporting obligations. The role is client-facing and includes working with a number of internal and external stakeholders to ensure we are managing, mitigating and minimising risk associated with the SA Government Transaction Banking, Merchants and Commercial Cards contracts, ensuring that we have established processes in place to manage contract compliance. Role Location: Level 18, 83 Pirie Street, Adelaide SA 5000 Role Type: Permanent, Full-time What will your day look like? As an Associate, his role will be accountable for the following: Overseeing the SA Government contracts x2 for Transaction Banking (TB) Working closely with the TB Legal, Product, Sales & Account Management, Implementation and Service teams to ensure contract compliance Manage monthly / quarterly / bi-annual reporting requirements for internal ANZ executives and externally with the DTF Contract Manager Lead Contract Compliance meetings with the DTF Contract Manager Adhering to the highest risk management standards through an awareness of, and adherence to, all personal and organisational compliance obligations Ensuring awareness and consistent execution in alignment with latest processes, policies, and documentation requirements What will you bring? To be successful in this role, you will have the following knowledge, experiences and skills: Tertiary qualified, strong experience in Transaction Banking and Project Management Strong experience in dealing with and managing government agencies / client interactions Ability to build and maintain relationships with internal and external stakeholders and influence/negotiate change Ability to manage priorities and conflicting issues in a professional / risk-based manner Strong attention to detail and an ability to solve complex and ambiguous situations Ability to work autonomously across multiple competing priorities You’re not expected to have 100% of these skills.

Capital Markets Business Management Associate
Citizens Financial Group
Location: United States
Link: https://hcgn.fa.us2.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1/job/50049

The Capital Markets Business Management Associate will serve as a strategic partner to the Capital Markets & Advisory – Markets COO, supporting business management, strategic initiatives, governance processes, organizational effectiveness, cross-functional projects, and junior talent development across all debt products (Debt Capital Markets, Leveraged Finance, and Structured Products). This individual will operate at the intersection of business leadership, product expertise, technology, and development; acting as a key liaison among the COO organization, debt products leadership, analysts and associates, and development partners. The role requires a strong understanding of debt products, banker workflows, and talent development best practices. This role is ideal for a highly organized, analytically driven professional who thrives in a fast-paced environment and enjoys solving problems, managing multiple priorities, and partnering with senior leaders. The successful candidate will function as a trusted advisor and execution partner, ensuring business priorities are organized, communicated, and delivered effectively. Key Responsibilities Strategic Business Management Act as a liaison between the COO function, debt products leadership, and development teams to coordinate product-specific strategic initiatives and business priorities. Support execution of debt-specific business management activities, including planning, governance, policy, communication, and project coordination. Drive special projects focused on organizational effectiveness, process improvement, technology adoption, and business transformation. Maintain strong awareness of market developments, product trends, policy changes, and competitive dynamics impacting the DCM business. Executive Reporting & Analytics Develop and maintain dashboards and management reporting that provide meaningful insights into business performance, talent metrics, pipeline activity, productivity, and strategic initiatives. Partner with technology and data teams to improve reporting capabilities and data governance. Support preparation of recurring business reviews, management materials, as well as presentations and briefing materials for executive leadership, risk committees, and the Board of Directors. Translate complex business, financial, and organizational data into concise, executive-level messaging. Support creation of strategic narratives, business updates, and operating reviews. Ensure consistent, high-quality presentation standards across all leadership communications. Process Management & Operational Excellence Identify opportunities to streamline workflows and improve organizational effectiveness. Develop reporting templates, process documentation, and operating playbooks. Support implementation of technology initiatives and automation opportunities. Partner with finance, operations, technology, legal, compliance, and risk teams on business improvement efforts. Governance & Risk Coordination Coordinate policies, procedures, approvals, audits, attestations, and governance documentation. Track action items arising from risk, compliance, credit, legal, and control forums. Assist with regulatory and audit requests. Support committee administration and preparation of governance materials. Monitor deadlines and ensure completion of required control activities. Talent Development & Culture Serve as a primary coordinator and advocate for analyst and associate development across Debt Capital Markets and Leveraged Finance. Design, implement, and enhance onboarding programs, training curricula, mentorship initiatives, and development experiences. Establish metrics and reporting to track development progress, training participation, and talent outcomes. Partner with staffers, managers, business leaders, and development partners to identify skill gaps, opportunities for skill enhancement, and career progression. Monitor employee engagement and culture initiatives, helping to foster a collaborative, high-performance environment. Stakeholder Management Build strong relationships with bankers, product specialists, development resources, Human Resources partners, and senior management. Serve as a trusted advisor and point of coordination for development, reporting, and strategic business initiatives. Promote alignment across functions to ensure successful execution of business objectives. Qualifications Required Bachelor's degree in Finance, Business, Economics, or related field. 3+ years of experience within Capital Markets, Investment Banking, Corporate Banking, or a related financial services business. Demonstrated knowledge of Debt Capital Markets products and processes. Strong analytical, project management, and organizational skills. Advanced proficiency in PowerPoint and Excel, including dashboard development and executive reporting. Exceptional written and verbal communication skills with the ability to interact effectively across all levels of the organization. Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.

Since 2012, we've grown to become one of the leading single-family rental companies and homebuilders in the country, recently recognized as a top employer by Fortune and Great Place To Work®. At AMH, our goal is to simplify the experience of leasing a home through professional management and maintenance support, so our residents can focus on what really matters to them, wherever they are in life. The Senior Financial Analyst is an integral part of companywide financial analysis, planning, and forecasting. Responsibilities may include partnering with various business stakeholders to prepare short-term and long-term financial planning and/or financial analysis of business operations and property level performance based upon focus area. Prepares, collects, tracks, validates, and interprets financial information; creates and maintains budgets, reports, forecasts and conducts financial and operational analyses. Supports program and project implementation and makes significant contributions to departmental goals and planning efforts. Responsibilities: Responsible for examining, analyzing, and interpreting internal operational data from various data sources to create and provide monthly, quarterly, and annual operational metrics reports. Prepares supporting materials and analyses for the company’s public reporting and investor communications, including SEC filings, investor presentations, earnings materials, and other supplemental disclosures. Performs ad hoc financial and business analyses to support strategic decision-making, including trend analysis, forecasting, scenario modeling and other special projects as assigned. Builds, maintains, and enhances financial and operational reporting dashboards using business intelligence platforms (e.g., Tableau, Power BI, etc.). Extracts, manipulates, and analyzes large datasets using data tools (e.g., excel, SQL, etc.) to identify trends, provide actionable insights, and support strategic decision-making.

What is Global Credit Finance Global Credit Finance (GCF) is State Street’s lending business, helping clients access financing solutions while supporting the growth of the firm. GCF manages more than $100 billion in lending commitments across a variety of products and serves clients around the world. GCF is made up of more than 150 employees across the U.S., Europe, and Asia, with offices in Boston, Irvine, London, Munich, Gdansk, Luxembourg, and Bangalore. GCF works with a diverse range of financing solutions, including private markets lending, private credit, liquidity and leverage facilities for investment funds, CLOs, municipal finance, commercial real estate, corporate lending, and other specialized financing products. Our mission is to build strong client relationships by providing capital and financing solutions while generating income and diversification for State Street. Who we are looking for We are seeking a highly motivated, intellectually curious, and proactive Senior Associate to join the Global Credit Finance (GCF) Business Management team. The ideal candidate combines strong analytical and organizational skills with a strategic mindset, exceptional attention to detail, and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment. This highly visible role sits at the intersection of business management, client strategy, and operational execution, providing broad exposure across GCF's lending platforms, including Private Credit, CLOs, Capital Call Facilities, Investment Advisor Facilities, and other credit products. The successful candidate will partner closely with senior leaders and stakeholders across the division to support strategic initiatives, business planning, client engagement efforts, financial management, reporting, governance, and operational processes that enable the business to scale and operate effectively.

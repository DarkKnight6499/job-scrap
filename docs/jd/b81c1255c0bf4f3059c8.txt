Institutional Investment Solutions (“IIS”) is a highly specialized global team of seasoned investment professionals within J.P. Morgan Wealth Management Solutions, managing over $50 billion in customized multi-asset solutions for Endowments, Foundations and Institutional Families. The investment solutions we provide range from bespoke multi-asset discretionary portfolios to tailored single asset class mandates. IIS designs, implements, and delivers these solutions by leveraging the full breadth of J.P. Morgan Asset & Wealth Management’s investment capabilities—drawing on both in-house expertise and leading external managers. We understand institutional investors have distinct goals, risk profiles, and liquidity needs. Our platform is designed to address these priorities and adapt to a changing investment landscape, offering flexible engagement as a strategic partner to in-house investment teams. Our senior investment team averages over 20 years of experience, with backgrounds in Family Office CIO teams and institutional portfolio management at leading Asset Managers. Our expertise spans long-term investing, including strategic asset allocation, portfolio construction, and comprehensive cross-asset class analysis. In addition, IIS has deep experience advising institutional investors on building robust investment governance frameworks, establishing investment policy, and institutionalizing wealth for future generations. This includes identifying opportunities to provide advice and introduce J.P. Morgan’s full suite of investment solutions. Holistic Asset Allocation and Portfolio Construction: Advise clients on establishing comprehensive asset allocation policies and constructing robust, diversified portfolios. Provide guidance on best practices for investment policy and governance, ensuring clients have the frameworks needed for effective long-term wealth management. Deliver Investment Insights: Act as a thought leader by sharing J.P. Morgan’s latest investment insights with clients and advisors. This includes organizing and participating in roundtables, speaking at client events, and contributing to educational forums to ensure clients are informed and engaged with current market perspectives and opportunities. Deepen our engagement with Institutional Clients within JPM US Wealth Management Drive new business and regional growth by engaging advisors, leading client events and campaigns, collaborating on new opportunities, and participating in new business opportunities. Experience partnering with RIA or UNHW wealth advisor channels is preferred.

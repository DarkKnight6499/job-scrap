CORPORATE PLANNING & MANAGEMENT Corporate Planning & Management (CPM) is a central strategic function at Goldman Sachs, responsible for driving firmwide operational excellence, business planning, and the infrastructure that enables the firm to operate with strength, resiliency, and agility. CPM partners across every division to deliver scalable solutions, optimize business processes, and position the firm for sustainable growth. Our teams combine deep institutional knowledge with forward-looking innovation to ensure Goldman Sachs remains at the forefront of operational efficiency and strategic execution. The CPM Division unifies Finance & Planning, Spend Management, Operational Risk and Resilience, and partner with Engineering teams to deliver business planning and analytics, expense management, third-party risk management, and governance strategies across the firm. Within Spend Management, the Source to Pay function encompasses Strategic Sourcing, Procure to Pay, Integrated Travel and Expense, Infrastructure and Transformation, and Sustainable Operations — managing all aspects of the firm's spend with third parties, advising on commercial agreements, and driving operating efficiency. CONTRACT LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT (CLM) PRODUCT MANAGEMENT Within CPM, the Source to Pay function oversees the end-to-end lifecycle of procurement and payables — from sourcing and vendor management through purchasing, invoicing, and payment. We are committed to transforming this critical business capability through modern platforms, intelligent automation, and AI-driven innovation, ensuring our internal stakeholders have seamless, transparent, and efficient tools to manage the firm's spend. The Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM) team is specifically focused on optimizing the end-to-end contracting process—including contract intake, drafting, negotiation, approvals, execution, repository management, obligation tracking, and renewal/termination lifecycles—across the firm using our proprietary, in-house built CLM platform.

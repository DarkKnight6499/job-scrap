Our Impact: The Enterprise Business Risk & Control Office is a new team at Freddie Mac responsible for facilitating efficient, effective, and consistent management of non-financial risks across Freddie Mac’s first line divisions. We are building a highly motivated team that enjoys driving change and identifying improvement opportunities and solutions. The Centralized Testing group within this team is accountable for executing operational control and compliance testing for all businesses and functions across the firm. Note: this role will be largely focused on activities at Freddie Mac. Your Impact: In this role, you will be expected to: Perform control test of design and test of operating effectiveness, producing thorough, high quality work papers with appropriate evidence to support conclusions Review test work paper results including but not limited to control design, attributes and associated test procedures and sampling methodologies, identify gaps and/or recommendations Provide guidance to testers to help them develop robust and efficient test procedures Perform reviews timely and execute testing in adherence with procedures and documentation requirements Manage control test related data in GRC Tool for accuracy and completeness Challenge status quo. Identify opportunities to improve and streamline processes, including testing automation Drive efficiency and timeliness by actively managing team workload Support enterprise self-testing initiatives for consistent reporting, efficiencies, timeliness and process improvements Qualifications: 8+ years of overall experience with a minimum of 5 years of control testing and/or audit experience Bachelor’s Degree or equivalent experience Preferred relevant professional certifications (CIA, CPA, CISA etc.) Previous big four experience is a plus Keys to Success in this Role: Self-motivated and results-oriented mindset Analytical thinker with intellectual curiosity in investigating open-ended questions Detail oriented with the ability to think holistically and creatively Ability to work in a dynamic, rapidly changing business environment Produce high-quality work products Passionate about learning Team player with excellent work ethics and a positive, can-do attitude!

Risk Manager Risk Managers at Capital One are the front line of defense to ensure our Company remains stable and profitable. Risk Managers at Capital One are highly motivated Risk Management professionals with excellent organizational and communication skills. They have a high level of exposure across lines of business and have the opportunity to work with Executives to create and implement innovative solutions to identify and mitigate potential risks to the Company. As part of Enterprise Risk Management (ERM), you will work with talented associates to ensure our businesses effectively adhere to Capital One’s Risk Management Framework. This role is at the heart of our modernization efforts as we transform our processes to become more nimble and effective. You will focus on strategic improvements to the foundational elements of our Risk Identification Framework. We are currently seeking a strategic, forward-leaning, and results driven professional with outstanding problem solving skills to join the ERM team. This individual should feel comfortable navigating through the “grey” area and formulating problems and recommendations. Strong communication and storytelling capabilities are essential for this role. Responsibilities: Optimize and enhance foundational elements of the Risk Identification Framework, aligning capabilities with broader strategic objectives and transformation efforts Lead cross-functional initiatives to simplify and standardize Risk Identification processes Leverage data analysis and operational insights to identify core problems and formulate effective solutions Collaborate with 1st Line and 2nd Line stakeholders to align strategies and ensure consistent execution across business lines Partner with technology, product, and data teams to implement required system and process updates Design and implement risk metrics to measure framework effectiveness and process health Lead training across lines of defense to ensure consistent adoption and execution Establish and manage guidance to ensure consistent execution across business lines Deliver reporting and executive-level presentations that provide leadership with real-time risk visibility Fulfill audit and regulatory exam requests, and other ad-hoc deliverables Support general governance activities, including controls, and issues Basic Qualifications: Bachelors Degree or military experience At least 5 years of experience in Risk Management, Audit, Project Management, or Process Management At least 5 years of experience supporting, partnering and interacting with internal business partners Preferred Qualifications: 6+ years of Risk Management experience 3+ years of Financial Services Risk Management experience Ability to set direction, manage expectations and lead cross-functional projects At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position.

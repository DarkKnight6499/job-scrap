Overview The Senior Business Analyst is responsible for supporting the Loan and/or Trade Operations business units in data analytics tasks, Operational Excellence initiatives, system enhancements/implementation, and design & implementation of medium- to large-scale projects. This position will handle large amounts of data and design feasible solutions, and act as a liaison between the solution end users and the business partners (such as IT, Enterprise Project Management, etc.) and/or third party vendors. This position will also be responsible for the management of Loan and/or Trade Operations projects, development of system business requirements and perform User Acceptance Testing. This position also documents procedures, delivers user training and provides training to junior business analysts.

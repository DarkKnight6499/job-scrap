About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Securities Operations Associate to support the Security Master Pricing function within Wealth & Investment Management Operations as part of Wholesale Operations . The Securities Operations Associate will work across all Security Master initiatives; primary responsibilities will include validating and updating pricing securities on the BETA platform. The Security Master Pricing department is accountable for security pricing on second largest file held at the firm – the Security Master file. The team is responsible for securities not supported by a vendor. The team responds to requests sent from the field and from various internal departments that include inquiries and validation. Additionally, the team works daily, weekly, and monthly reports, completing manual updates to securities that are not updated by automated vendor feeds.

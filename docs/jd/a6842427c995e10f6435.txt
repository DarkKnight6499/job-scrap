Morgan Health is a mission-driven healthcare organization within JPMorgan Chase focused on improving the quality, affordability, and experience of employer-sponsored healthcare. We operate with the focus and agility of a smaller, specialized team, backed by the scale, resources, and reach of one of the world’s largest companies. Our team brings together healthcare, data, research, investment, and policy expertise to tackle some of the most complex challenges facing employers and their employees. If you are someone who wants their technical work to have a tangible impact on healthcare, this is a unique opportunity to build within a highly collaborative team to further Morgan Health’s mission. As a Senior Analytics Engineer within Morgan Health, you’ll play a key role in building the data foundation behind that work. You’ll have meaningful ownership of Morgan Health’s analytics environment, partnering closely with data scientists as well as other internal and external stakeholders to turn complex healthcare data into trusted, production-ready data marts that support research, insights, and decision-making. In this role, you will have the opportunity to shape how analytics engineering is done within Morgan Health — not simply inherit an established process. You will own and evolve core elements of the analytics life cycle, from data modeling and testing to CI/CD, monitoring and quality standards. Because we’re a focused team, you will work closely with the people using the data and have visibility into how your work contributes to healthcare research, insights, and ultimately better outcomes. This role is in-office five days per week, based in either New York, NY or Washington, DC.

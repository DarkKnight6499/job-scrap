Our team is growing: The account coordinator role collaborates with internal and external stakeholders to oversee and manage installer accounts within Dividend Finance’s network to meet certain goals and deliverables. This role will work with cross-functional teams to mitigate account-level risk, minimize exposure, investigate problems, track progress, and produce reports. Responsible and accountable for risk by openly exchanging ideas and opinions, elevating concerns, and personally following policies and procedures as defined.

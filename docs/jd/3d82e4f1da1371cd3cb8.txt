Help us make a measurable difference for our customers through the advancement healthcare equity programs and initiatives. As a Program Management Advisor within Cigna Healthcare Clinical, you will lead the Enterprise Health Equity and Language Accessibility Program (HE/LAP). This role blends strategy, execution, and collaboration to improve patient outcomes, strengthen regulatory readiness, and scale initiatives that reduce health disparities. You will plan, execute, and deliver initiatives that improve access, quality, and experience for customers while partnering across clinical, analytics, technology, operations, and compliance teams. Responsibilities Own end-to-end delivery of health equity initiatives, managing scope, schedule, dependencies, risks, and outcomes from intake through closeout. Develop and maintain integrated project plans, roadmaps, and delivery artifacts to ensure clear accountability and on-time execution. Select and apply appropriate delivery methodologies (Agile, hybrid, or waterfall) to meet initiative and stakeholder needs. Lead governance and delivery forums, including project kickoffs, status reviews, risk reviews, and retrospectives. Proactively manage risks, issues, and dependencies; drive mitigation strategies and timely resolution. Partner with stakeholders to manage scope changes and assess impacts to timelines, resources, and outcomes. Prepare concise, executive-ready reporting that communicates progress, risks, decisions required, and performance metrics. Support adoption and sustainability of delivered solutions through effective change management and continuous improvement. Required Qualifications Minimum 3 years of project or program management experience delivering complex, cross-functional initiatives. Experience in healthcare, health equity, or population health with a deep understanding of equity-centered design principles. Demonstrated proficiency in core project management disciplines including planning, scheduling, risk management, dependency tracking, and change control. Strong stakeholder management and communication skills, including experience presenting to senior leaders. Experience managing multiple concurrent projects in a matrixed environment. Proficiency with project and collaboration tools such as Wrike, SharePoint, Microsoft Office, and/or Microsoft Project. Ability to operate effectively in ambiguity and drive execution with minimal direction. Preferred Qualifications Bachelor’s degree preferred; Master’s degree a plus. Project Management Professional (PMP), Agile certification (CSM, SAFe), or equivalent experience. Experience leading enterprise initiatives with executive visibility and measurable delivery outcomes.

Job Description We are seeking an experienced credit professional to join our investment team at Ares Insurance Solutions. The successful candidate will support the sourcing, underwriting, execution, and ongoing monitoring of fixed income and private credit investments across corporate, leveraged finance, direct lending, and structured credit opportunities. This role will play an integral part in the investment process, working closely with senior investment professionals to evaluate opportunities, prepare investment recommendations, and monitor portfolio performance. In addition, the individual will support the firm's strategic investments in credit origination platforms and lending partnerships, helping assess platform economics, underwriting capabilities, portfolio performance, and long-term growth opportunities.

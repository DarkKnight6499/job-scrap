Work you'll do The professional will work with the India colleagues and senior leaders across Global Consulting Services practice. They will need to develop a knowledge of the firm’s Consulting Services business, its strategic priorities to enable sharing of insights, trends, and escalation of issues/variances as appropriate. The professional will own sensing of new opportunities, analyse quantitative and qualitative data, lead and coach team of managers, and proactively communicate with stakeholders. They will be required to demonstrate solid project management skills and constantly partner with senior leaders to drive global alignment around strategic priorities. The teams’ projects are typically high-impact and high-visibility aligned to the Global firm and Consulting Services leadership’s strategic priorities. Projects often involve collaboration across workstreams to ensure coordination and alignment. Key responsibilities Strategic Alignment - Co-shape and deliver asset priorities to align with OP’s refreshed strategy, driving targeted portfolio and investment choices that match MF market demands. Implementing Governance model – Implement the newly defined governance model to monitor the progress on assets, conduct due diligence on new assets and manage global support to the assets Agentic AI – Create an agentic viewpoint and identify GenAI/Agent initiatives in line with FY26 Global RRF priorities. Investments - Prepare investment requests, drive due diligence ahead of and assist on investment committee pitches, emphasizing alignment with OP objectives and validated market demand. Budget Utilization Tracking - Regularly monitor and report budget utilization for all approved investments. ROI Monitoring – Collaborate with MF product teams to establish KPIs and track ROI for each asset, ensuring alignment with strategic and financial objectives. Deal Integration - Contribute to embedding assets in Global RRF top deals, supporting a "build once, sell and use many" commercialization approach. Ascend Integration - lead scaling of Ascend-adjacent Tech Components globally. Scalability Planning - Develop and execute plans for scaling successful assets across MF and the broader network. OP Asset Program Management - Lead asset program including portfolio management, investment management, scaling, and adoption within OP and related reporting activities. The team Deloitte’s Global Consulting Services professionals support our global businesses to drive impact and create enduring value for clients in Strategy, Risk & Transactions (SR&T) and Technology & Transformation (T&T). Familiarity with Deloitte organization and proven ability to navigate and coordinate across networks, especially across geographies, industries, and/or offerings. Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and teaming skills with the ability to build collaborative relationships and influence superiors and peers in a large and complex global organization Advanced project management skills and demonstrated experience successfully managing a team Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to translate quantitative and qualitative analyses into clear messages for the audience Experience in financial analysis and data analytics (MS Excel), with the ability to identify innovative tools and approaches to enhance reporting and insight generation for the business. High-level understanding of asset business models and asset enabled business lifecycle Previous market/client-facing experience and client pursuits/selling a plus Technology literacy is required.

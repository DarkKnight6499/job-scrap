Work you'll do Summary: The Global Offering Portfolio Asset Business Leader (ABL) will work directly with Global OP Leader and broader global OP leadership team advising and shaping an OP specific Asset Strategy that reflects the Global Offering Portfolio Leader’s broader vision for that Offering Portfolio and that resonates across the global network. The ABL will advise the Global Offering Portfolio Leader on choices related to Industry plays, Alliance plays, GenAI/Agents and Delivery Transformation (i.e., the Global Ascend Program) – overall resulting into more asset-enabled sales across the global Offering Portfolio network. The ABL will take the lead in realizing the set OP specific Asset Strategy – capturing more market across the global network via asset-enabled sales for that Offering Portfolio. Responsibilities: Strategic Alignment: Co-shape and deliver asset priorities in alignment with OP’s refreshed strategy to drive targeted portfolio and investment choices that align with MF market demands Establish a global OP Asset Leadership community: Establish a Leadership community to monitor the progress on assets, conduct due diligence on new assets and manage global support to the assets Investments: Assess and prepare investment requests and assist during investment committee pitches, showcasing alignment with OP objectives / proven market demand Budget Utilization Tracking & ROI Monitoring: Regularly monitor and report budget utilization for all approved investments. Collaborate with MF asset teams to track ROI for an asset, ensuring alignment with strategic and financial objectives. Scalability Planning Develop and execute plans for scaling successful assets across MF and the broader network. Deal Integration: Contribute to embedding assets in OP top deals, reinforcing the "build once, sell and use many" approach Ascend Integration: Lead the scaling of Ascend related Tech Components across the global network Agentic AI: Coordinate GenAI/Agents efforts specific to the set OP priorities OP Asset Program management: Lead the OP specific Asset Program including portfolio mgmt., investment mgmt., scaling & adoption efforts, Go-To-Market efforts, etc. The team Deloitte’s Global Consulting Services professionals support our global businesses to drive impact and create enduring value for clients in Strategy, Risk & Transactions (SR&T) and Technology & Transformation (T&T). We deliver purpose-driven services and solutions and provide a consistent, best-in-class "One Deloitte" experience around the world.

Update procedures due to changes in process or accounting requirements with input from Operational Accountant III, IV or Manager. Participate in responsibilities associated with new business initiatives under Manager’s direction. Mentors and trains less experienced staff on routine task and questions Adhere to established policies, procedures and limits focusing on observable breaches in Corporate Risk policies. Adhere to applicable compliance/operational risk controls in accordance with Company or regulatory standards and policies. Promote an environment that supports belonging and reflects the M&T Bank brand. Maintain M&T internal control standards, including timely implementation of internal and external audit points together with any issues raised by external regulators as applicable. Complete other related duties as assigned.

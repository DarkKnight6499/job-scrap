Description Are you interested in a career that combines finance, relationship management, and strategic business decision-making? Do you enjoy analyzing financial information, solving complex business challenges, and building strong professional relationships? If so, Liberty Mutual's Surety Underwriting Development Program (SUDP) may be the perfect place to launch your career. As an Associate Surety Underwriter , you'll participate in a 12-month development program designed to build the technical, analytical, and business skills needed for a successful underwriting career. Working alongside experienced underwriters, you'll learn how surety bonds support economic growth, commercial development, and infrastructure projects while helping businesses pursue new opportunities and fulfill contractual obligations. The Details Surety underwriting blends financial analysis, risk assessment, and relationship management. Throughout the program, you'll learn how to evaluate the financial strength, creditworthiness, and operational capabilities of businesses, while developing the judgment needed to make informed underwriting decisions. You'll gain hands-on experience analyzing financial statements, assessing credit and project risk, evaluating contracts, and supporting real underwriting decisions. At the same time, you'll build relationships with agents, brokers, customers, and industry partners while strengthening your communication, business development, and negotiation skills. Training & Development The Surety Underwriting Development Program combines formal training, mentorship, self-study, job rotations, and on-the-job learning. Through a collaborative learning environment, you'll gain exposure to various products, teams, and leaders across the organization while developing a strong foundation in contract and commercial surety underwriting. As part of the program, you'll pursue the Associate in Fidelity and Surety Bonding (AFSB) designation while applying underwriting concepts through case studies, financial analysis, contract evaluation, and project risk assessment. You'll learn alongside a cohort of peers, building professional connections that will support your growth throughout your career. 2027 Start Dates: June and July This position may have in-office requirements depending on candidate location. Qualifications What you’ve got You hold a bachelor’s Degree in Finance, Economics, Business, or comparable with a minimum 3.0 cumulative GPA. You have experience with finance and accounting coursework, and possess an understanding of how to read financial statements. You also demonstrate technical competence in fundamental financial analysis. You have 0-2 years of professional experience. You think globally and are able to adapt easily to different cultures and communication styles. You possess excellent analytical, organizational, negotiation, and decision-making skills. You demonstrate outstanding interpersonal and customer service skills with an emphasis on fostering relationships. Prior internship experience is a plus, especially in a finance-related environment. You are willing to relocate upon completion of the program, based on location.

Special Assets Associate I
First Citizens Bank
Location: New York, New York
Link: https://jobs.firstcitizens.com/jobs/36143

Overview This is a hybrid role with the expectation that time working will regularly take place inside and outside of a company office. Supports in the portfolio management of a variety of problem loans. Primary responsibilities will include underwriting / executing debt restructuring and portfolio management. The ideal candidate will have the ability to work in a dynamic / fast paced environment, managing a variety of deal stages and types, with a strong attention to detail. Responsibilities & Qualifications Duties & Responsibilities: Underwriting: Process Management - Assists in the review of the assigned portfolio for credit quality and credit risk rating accuracy and notes any policy exceptions. Requires underwriting skills on a variety of technology/life sciences verticals as well as credit products. Prepares quarterly CLS/CLR packages as well as CLR minutes. Reporting/Portfolio Management - Maintains credit files, monitors past dues, prepares underwriting packages, interfaces and collaborates with operations, and contacts clients to obtain financial information. Assists in the preparation of quarterly Individual Assessments. Knowledge of Credit Policy, Risk Rating Matrices, and Bank systems - Must understand and have the ability to navigate a number of Bank systems. Knowledge of Lending Products and Restructuring - May manage a small number of less complex accounts and/or charge-offs, including recommending and implementing strategies to mitigate loss and interacting with clients directly. Requires basic negotiation skills, basic understanding of workout strategies, and strong credit acumen. Position Specific Skills: Ability to review and analyze credit and financial information Knowledge of financial principles and concepts Required Qualifications: Minimum Required Education and Experience: Bachelor's Degree OR High School Diploma or GED/Equivalent and 3 years' experience in Credit Training or Financial Analysis Preferred Qualifications: Bachelor's Degree, majoring in ajor in Accounting, Finance, Business or Economics Prior internship experience at First Citizens Bank Additional Information If hired in New York, the base pay for this position is generally between $66,600 and $98,000.

Job Summary: We are seeking an experienced Senior Business Analyst to join our Transaction Banking Technology organization. This senior individual contributor will work directly with Product Owners to assess, rationalize, clarify, and prioritize business requirements, then partner with technical system owners to translate them into feasible, scalable, and actionable technology solutions. This role spans payments, cash and liquidity management, digital channels, reconciliation, regulatory compliance, and related commercial banking capabilities. The Senior Business Analyst will also leverage AI-enabled tools to create mockups, process flows, and visual representations that enhance stakeholder understanding, alignment, and decision-making throughout the delivery lifecycle.

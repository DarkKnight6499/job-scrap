The Opportunity This position is located out of our Alameda, CA location in the Lingo Division. Meet Lingo, a new biosensing technology that provides users with a window into their body. Lingo tracks key biomarkers – such as glucose, ketones, and lactate – to help people make better decisions about their health and nutrition. As Senior Financial Analyst , you will provide support and partnership for Commercial & Marketing teams with particular focus on actuals reporting and forecasting for Plan, rLBE and Confirm processes. The role requires strong analytical capabilities, business acumen, and the ability to collaborate effectively across finance and commercial teams. This is an onsite opportunity in Alameda, CA What You’ll Work On Assist in month-end close activities for the business, including journal entries, actual variance analysis and management commentary. Support availability of timely and well-presented sales results, high-quality P&L and balance sheet, & projections vs. appropriate benchmarks as well as, trended information. Lead financial planning activities partnering closely with commercial teams to develop sales revenue, product units, gross margin, as well as OMC and SG&A expenses. Planning responsibilities include the LBE and confirm submissions, as well as current month predictors, and other forecasting processes Identify meaningful analytics including trend analyses, which can be gleaned from rLBE submissions; presentation of these findings to Commercial senior leadership to aid strategic decision making and agree upon storyline of the submission Build and maintain financial models to support new product launch, including account onboarding, sales volume ramp, pricing dynamics, etc. Support preparation of LBE deck, working with stakeholders to prepare presentation to senior management. Focus on simplifying submissions to make slides concise & executive-ready Develop metrics and reports to identify activity level for monthly review with management to address performance as well as cost and spending drivers. Provide business support as required, including meaningful, value-added profitability analyses for new product concepts, new product launches, promotional programs, instrument placements, major customer, distribution channels, and/or product lines to influence and counsel management in developing business and pricing strategies. Develop flexible, comprehensive tools to support commercial program proposals focused on ROI and fully burdened margin profitability to support various business needs, including training to improve the financial understanding of the key nonfinancial commercial management & staff. Maintain and develop system solutions and process improvements that meet the needs of the financial planning & reporting requirements. Responsible for adherence to Abbott accounting policies and procedures (CFMs) and Sarbanes Oxley requirements. Maintain controls to ensure the integrity of assets and compliance with GAAP Conduct ad hoc financial and business analyses to support management decisions.

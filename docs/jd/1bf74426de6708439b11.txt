THE OPPORTUNITY Nearwater Capital’s Middle Office team supports the firm’s three business lines: Liquid Markets, Risk Retention, and Capital Markets. We are looking for an Associate who will work across all three, rotating through products rather than sitting with one. You will own the full trade lifecycle for commercial paper, repo, fixed income, and swap transactions, working directly with the front office, counterparties, and custodians. This is a hands-on seat on a small team.

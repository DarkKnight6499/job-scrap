Job Description Summary Serve as a key member of Transamerica’s Capital Financial Planning and Analysis team, with responsibility for providing analysis and insight into capital generation results. Job Description Responsibilities: Serve as a subject matter expert in capital reporting, providing technical expertise and insight into capital generation results. Contribute to the development of management reporting and detailed financial analyses. Apply subject matter expertise to large cross-functional projects. Resolve complex financial questions where requirements, data, or processes are not fully defined by clarifying the problem, evaluating options, and developing practical solutions. Communicate complex financial information clearly to strategic and operational audiences. Extract financial data from accounting and information systems to support analysis. Identify, analyze, and interpret relevant financial data to assess past performance and project future results. Provide leadership and direction to junior-level staff, including reviewing their work for accuracy, completeness, and sound analytical conclusions. Take end-to-end ownership of assigned processes, from planning and coordinating inputs through analysis, review, and timely delivery; proactively resolve issues and follow through to completion. Apply a thorough understanding of business and financial analysis concepts. Recommend process improvements to increase efficiency. Collaborate across departments and divisions to resolve business issues and support company objectives. Adhere to the company’s internal control framework. Qualifications : Bachelor’s degree in Accounting or Finance and/or equivalent work experience. Minimum of 8 years experience, with degree. Advanced computer skills. Preferred Qualifications: One year of supervisory experience. Solid knowledge of accounting or finance in areas such as investments, insurance products, or premiums. Strong verbal and written communication skills. Strong organizational and prioritization skills, with the ability to manage multiple projects and a willingness to take on new assignments. A customer service mindset. Strong analytical skills and sound judgment, with the ability to navigate ambiguity and develop solutions without an established process. Keen attention to detail, including the ability to identify errors and inconsistencies when reviewing others’ work. Ability to work effectively independently and as part of a team. Ability to address employee issues in a timely manner. Working Conditions: Hybrid (In Office: Tuesdays, Wednesdays, & Thursdays) Fast-paced, deadline-driven office environment.

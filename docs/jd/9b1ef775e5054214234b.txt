Role Description The BIC program Associate must follow the guidelines given by FRBNY to determine whether loans are eligible or ineligible as collateral. The guideline list certain criteria and/or loan attributes that makes a loan ineligible to be pledged under the BIC program. A BIC checklist template put together by CTDAD containing questions incorporating these criteria must be completed for each loan to aid in the determination of each loan’s eligibility to be pledged. Principally, Associate reviews loan documents including collateral and security documents to prepare the BIC checklist for each loan to determine if the loan is eligible or not to be pledged under the program. For the Standby Letter of Credit (SBLC) Document Management responsibility, Associate reviews and edits proposed terms and conditions of SBLC prior to being issued by the Trade Credit Services Team. Responsibilities Review deal's documentation for potential eligibility to the FRBNY BIC Program each month. Determine the type of deal and facility can be pledged. Documentation review consists of Legal documents such as Security Documents, Credit and Loan Agreements, Guaranties and other documents. Determine the state of jurisdiction and country for obligors and of future obligors -if allowed. Determine the location of Deal's security and future security -if secured. Make distinction of syndicated deals from sole borrowers to understand if any assignment/sale restrictions apply. Review Federal pledge in accordance with Loan Syndication and Trading Association guidelines (LSTA conforming) language in Credit/Loan Agreements. Review SBLC terms and conditions as delegated by the Head of SBLC Docs Management, prior to being issued by the TCS Team (Operations). Risk & Control Identify and escalate loans & SBLC discrepancies, operational risks, and potential compliance concerns. Ensure adherence to internal control procedures and operational standards. Support audit, regulatory, compliance, and risk management reviews. Maintain appropriate documentation and evidentiary records for all transactions. Assist in the investigation and resolution of incidents and exceptions. Client & Stakeholder Management Liaise with Front Office, Relationship Managers, Loan Operations, Legal, Compliance, and clients regarding documentary requirements and discrepancies. Communicate findings clearly and professionally to internal and external stakeholders. Operational Excellence Contribute to process improvement and automation initiatives. Assist in developing procedures, training materials, and best practices. Participate in testing and implementation activities for new systems, controls, and regulatory requirements. Support departmental projects and strategic initiatives as required.

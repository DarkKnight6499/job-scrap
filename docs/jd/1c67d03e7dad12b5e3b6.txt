Description Summary: The Treasury Management Strategy & Execution Manager supports execution of Treasury Management strategic initiatives, specifically with initiative rollouts for the Sales team supporting CRB (Consumer and Regional Banking). Partners with Sales Managers to define tools, reporting and processes to help colleagues meet their sales goals and align on strategic plans. Drives continuous improvement opportunities to strengthen sales discipline and processes. Leverages market information and internal data to identify transformation opportunities and sales improvement areas. Prepares materials to demonstrate Sales performance and progress. Supports multiple initiatives and acts as a SME on certain engagements. Has oversight of sales process, sales discipline, delivery model and sales performance scorecards. Responsible for content and continued development of TM sales training. Collaborates with business partners in Product, Technology, Operations, Risk, customer segments and other areas. Guides and directs efforts to achieve sales organization effectiveness and efficiency. Duties and Responsibilities: Collaborate with finance, risk management, and other departments to deliver enhancements and reporting. Ensure compliance with internal controls and regulatory requirements. Provide regular updates to senior leadership on Sales progress and performance. Participate in the evaluation of new technologies and sales tools for Treasury Management. Assist in the development and delivery of training programs for the CRB Treasury Management sales team. Monitor and report key Treasury Management sales metrics and performance indicators for the CRB Sales team. Develop management presentations and materials on a regular basis. Perform other duties as assigned. Basic Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree 10+ years of Banking experience 7+ years of management experience within Treasury Management.

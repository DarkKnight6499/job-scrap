Your Opportunity At Schwab, you’re empowered to make an impact on your career. Here, innovative thought meets creative problem solving, helping us “challenge the status quo” and transform the finance industry together. The First Line Risk Management Office partners with business units to strengthen risk monitoring and management practices. Within this organization, the First Line Risk Management Solutions team helps the business work smarter by transforming data into actionable insights, simplifying processes, and delivering scalable automation, reporting, analytics, and digital solutions. As Sr. Manager, Risk Management Business Process and Insights, you will turn complex operational and risk management needs into practical solutions that reduce manual effort, improve decision-making, and create more intuitive stakeholder experiences. You will work across business, risk, data, and technology teams to shape well-governed automation, reporting, workflow, and AI-enabled capabilities. This is an opportunity to lead meaningful, visible work while collaborating with colleagues who value curiosity, integrity, thoughtful innovation, and shared success. What you’ll do Partner with business stakeholders to uncover operational pain points, map current-state processes, define business and functional requirements, and translate needs into scalable automation, reporting, workflow, and AI-enabled solutions. Lead process improvement initiatives that simplify workflows, strengthen handoffs, reduce manual effort, and expand the thoughtful use of automation, Power Platform tools, data transformation, and intelligent automation. Develop and maintain data transformation processes, dashboards, reporting tools, and insight solutions that turn complex risk management data into clear, actionable information. Develop and optimize data models, SQL queries, and transformation processes to improve data quality, accessibility, and usability. Identify and shape practical AI-enabled use cases, defining expected outcomes, success measures, control considerations, governance expectations, and adoption needs. Connect business, risk, data, and technology teams to ensure solutions are practical, scalable, secure, compliant, well-governed, and aligned with business objectives. Guide testing, user acceptance, implementation readiness, training, change management, and stakeholder adoption for new tools, dashboards, workflows, and digital solutions. Champion a collaborative, learning-focused environment where sound judgment, constructive challenge, and continuous improvement lead to better outcomes. *This is a high-level summary. Duties and responsibilities may change as needed. Demonstrated success improving business processes through automation, workflow redesign, reporting, data transformation, or digital tools.

Posting Date 07/01/2026 2000 16th Street, Denver, Colorado, 80202, United States of America The Clinical Transformation Business Strategy Associate will support the development of DaVita’s integrated care strategy and prepare the organization for a shift to value-based care. In an integrated care environment, we aim to provide the comprehensive and preventative care each patient needs to be healthier in the new value-based healthcare world. This teammate will be an innovator and experimenter in developing this model and will collaborate with several teams across the Village on this executive-driven transformation. This position blends innovative strategic thinking with operational execution. Core activities include: support in the development of DaVita’s specialty care roles and advancement of health equity to deliver integrated kidney care management to support the unique needs of DaVita’s CKD and ESKD patients, creating analyses to generate insights and opportunity areas within our care model, developing clinical and operational process flows to best care for our patients, and creating new field facing tools, reports, and workflows. The Business Strategy Associate, will work on a team of highly-motivated individuals with consulting, process engineering, change management, and/or clinical experience. This highly visible and cross-functional role will work directly with Kidney Care and IKC (Integrated Kidney Care) leadership. Lead project management efforts to train on best practice.

In this role, you’ll partner with business leaders to identify and advance high-impact AI opportunities that improve efficiency, quality, and customer experience. You will guide teams through AI use case development, support solution selection and execution, and connect stakeholders to the right tools, resources, and delivery paths. This role also promotes scalable, well-governed AI practices by supporting standardization, tracking outcomes, and ensuring responsible and secure use aligned to enterprise standards. What We Ask of You AI Business Partnership & Solution Delivery Serve as a trusted AI advisor to business leaders, helping identify, refine, and prioritize AI opportunities aligned to strategic business outcomes. Guide business stakeholders through AI use case development, problem definition, benefit realization, and implementation planning. Facilitate intake, assessment, and prioritization of AI opportunities, directing work to the appropriate business-led, technology-led, or vendor-supported delivery path. Partner with business and technology teams to evaluate solution options, including buy-versus-build decisions and platform selection. Connect teams with AI resources, subject matter experts, solution architects, and delivery partners to accelerate execution. Help establish repeatable patterns, reusable assets, prompt libraries, and use case inventories that support scaled adoption and reduce duplication of effort. Share success stories and emerging opportunities to demonstrate the art of the possible and inspire broader adoption across the organization. Workforce Enablement & Adoption Support the development and execution of AI workforce enablement programs that build practical AI capability across the organization. Help build and sustain AI communities of practice, superuser networks, and champion programs to encourage knowledge sharing and peer learning. Contribute to the design and execution of an enterprise AI adoption program, including on-demand learning resources, use case libraries, and clear guidance aligned to approved tools, platforms, and policies. Measure adoption, engagement, and capability-building outcomes to support continuous improvement of AI programs. Deliver hands-on, project-based learning, leadership coaching, and communities of practice that help teams apply AI to real work examples, use AI responsibly, and scale adoption through common patterns.

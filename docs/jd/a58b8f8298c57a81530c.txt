As a Director, Global Structuring at Citi, you will lead the design and execution of sophisticated financial solutions that help issuers and investors navigate global capital markets. This is an opportunity to shape Citi's global structuring strategy, own complex transaction structuring from concept through execution, and build lasting relationships with clients and partners around the world. You will sit at the center of a high-impact team driving innovation in depositary receipt (DR) programs. Responsibilities Lead the global structuring framework by reviewing, shaping, and approving proposals for complex transactions and managing the full lifecycle of depositary receipt programs . Oversee the implementation of corporate actions and lifecycle events for the depositary receipts business, including structural safekeeping accounts and sponsored depositary receipt programs. Partner with business and functional teams across the organization to implement Citi's global DR structuring strategy. Drive new product development by designing innovative structuring solutions, including digital innovation initiatives within depositary receipt programs. Negotiate and finalize deal contracts with clients and counterparty institutions, managing DR program transfers and milestone offering transactions. Advise clients and prospective clients during pitches, deal discussions, and account reviews, guiding issuers through complex corporate actions and proxy events across markets including Asia, Latin America, and Europe. Coordinate with sales, trading, legal, compliance, risk, and credit teams, as well as external parties such as transfer agents, stock exchanges, and depositaries, to ensure seamless transaction execution. Identify and mitigate financial, legal, and operational risks tied to structured transactions. Mentor junior members of the structuring team and foster a culture of collaboration and continuous learning. Research global capital markets trends, including IPO activity, cross-border investing, liquidity, and valuation, to inform business strategy and strengthen market share. Support Sales and Account Management teams by developing pitch books, client proposals, and award submissions that promote structured products. Oversee preparation of deal documentation, including communicating implementation terms and corporate action deadlines to stakeholders. Collaborate with technology teams and external partners to advance global structuring DR product capabilities. Focus on commercializing high-growth products within the global DR structuring business. Required Qualifications & Skills 10 to 12 years of progressive experience in financial structuring, product development, or a related role within an investment bank, securities settlement, financial institution, or specialized advisory firm. Demonstrated experience leading cross-functional teams to achieve high-value business goals. Strong track record structuring and executing complex financial transactions. Skilled in negotiating deal contracts and managing commercial discussions with clients and counterparty institutions. Experience advising clients on corporate actions and proxy events in international markets. Ability to identify and manage financial, legal, and operational risk within structured transactions. Working knowledge of financial systems used to manage structured products and depositary receipt programs. Beneficial Skills & Qualifications Previous experience within a depositary bank or similar institution. Familiarity with sponsored depositary receipt programs and related digital innovation initiatives. Experience developing pitch books, client proposals, and industry award submissions. What We Offer Join a global DR structuring team where your expertise directly shapes how issuers and investors access capital markets. We provide the platform, resources, and global reach to help you grow your influence and impact at a senior level. Shape global structuring DR strategy with direct influence on high-value transactions and client outcomes. Work in a hybrid arrangement with 3 days in the office and 2 days working remotely each week. Collaborate with colleagues and partners across global markets, gaining exposure to international capital markets activity. Access ongoing learning and development opportunities to deepen your expertise in DR structuring and capital markets. Benefit from wellbeing and financial wellness programs designed to support long-term career and personal goals. Build your leadership profile by mentoring the next generation of structuring professionals.

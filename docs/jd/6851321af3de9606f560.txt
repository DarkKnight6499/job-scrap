Spring (January) 2027 Kickstart Your Engineering Career The Engineering/Operational Excellence Co-op will support continuous improvement initiatives focused on optimizing processes, reducing waste, and enhancing operational efficiency. This role provides hands-on experience with Lean, Six Sigma, and improvement methodologies while collaborating with cross-functional teams. What You’ll Do Support the identification, analysis, and improvement of operational processes. Participate in continuous improvement projects using Lean methodologies (5S, Kaizen, Value Stream Mapping). Collect and analyze data to identify efficiency opportunities. Assist in process documentation (SOPs, workflows, process maps). Collaborate on cost reduction and quality improvement initiatives. Track and report operational KPIs (OEE, yield, scrap, etc.). Support the preparation of reports and presentations on results. Participate in Kaizen events and improvement workshops. Ensure compliance with safety, quality, and regulatory standards. What We’re Looking For Excellent track record on operational KPI’s under their responsibility. Must have demonstrated excellent leadership skills. Excellent interpersonal, planning and communication skills. Bilingual (English and Spanish). Result Driven and strong continuous improvement advocate. Demonstrated commitment for talent development. Strong knowledge of GMP and OSHA requirements. Who Should Apply Candidates must be currently pursuing a bachelor's degree in industrial engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related discipline and be in their second or third year of study at an accredited university. A cumulative GPA of 3.0 or above is required. Why Baxter? You’ll be part of a team that’s passionate about improving healthcare worldwide.

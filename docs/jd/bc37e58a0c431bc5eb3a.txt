Job Description: The Associate Sales & Trading is responsible for serving as support for the US ETF Business of TD Securities (USA) LLC. This position will support the US ETF desk in Chicago/New York by inputting trades (post execution), researching and resolving trade discrepancies, amend trades (per trader approval), support the gathering of documentation for new accounts group, act as a liaison between front, back and middle offices, amongst a variety of other responsibilities.

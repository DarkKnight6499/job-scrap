Impact You’ll Make Stout’s Valuation Advisory professionals help clients gain confidence by bringing clarity to the most complex valuation matters. As one of the largest independent valuation practices in the country, we provide specialized experience across a broad spectrum of industries. You will work closely with senior team members to develop foundational valuation skills while contributing to well-supported analyses for engagements involving personal property, complex property, and special income generating tangible assets. What You’ll Do Perform comprehensive financial, industry and technical analyses to support embedded intangible studies across a wide range of industries for tax purposes. Perform field inspections when required as part of a valuation project. Participate in client or management meetings providing substantive input on valuation methodologies, assumptions and conclusions. Prepare comprehensive deliverables and supporting analysis including presentations, analysis packets, and written narrative reports that are submitted for review by our senior level professionals and delivery to our clients. Leverage AI-enabled tools and emerging technologies to enhance research, streamline deliverable preparation, and improve overall efficiency and insight. What You Bring 2-5 years of relevant internship or professional experience. 4-year degree in Finance, Economics, Engineering, or other quantitative discipline. Ability to travel. Foundational understanding of financial theory, economics, accounting principles, and financial statement analysis. Strong analytical and quantitative skills with a general knowledge or interest in machinery and equipment. Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, Word, and PowerPoint; ability to build organized analyses and clearly document workpapers. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to summarize findings clearly and professionally. High attention to detail, strong organizational abilities, and a willingness to ask thoughtful questions. Ability to manage multiple priorities, meet deadlines, and respond effectively to feedback. Self-motivated with a strong work ethic, intellectual curiosity, and accountability for work quality. Professional presence and ability to build productive relationships with clients, colleagues and leadership. Work toward ASA accreditation. Familiarity with or interest in using AI tools and technology to enhance productivity, research, and analysis. Must be authorized to work in the United States without current or future employer sponsorship. How You’ll Thrive Build strong professional relationships with clients, colleagues, and leadership. Ask thoughtful questions, seek to understand the assets and businesses being analyzed, and apply feedback to improve your work. Take responsibility for assigned analyses and deliverables, communicate progress and issues promptly, and meet established deadlines. Apply critical thinking and sound judgment when evaluating data, research, assumptions, and valuation results. Display accountability and the ability to multi-task to achieve professional growth and long-term career goals.

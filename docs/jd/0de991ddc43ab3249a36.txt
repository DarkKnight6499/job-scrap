Washington Trust has an opportunity for a highly motivated Wealth Associate to join our Wealth Management Administration team. The Wealth Associate will work closely with wealth management professionals and administrative staff to provide operational, client service, and account administration support. The Associate is responsible for coordinating administrative processes, maintaining account documentation, supporting client onboarding and servicing activities, and ensuring the timely and accurate execution of operational workflows. This is an excellent opportunity for someone with a minimum of 2 years of experience in wealth management, financial services operations, or client administration. The ideal candidate will be looking to broaden their knowledge of wealth management practices and client service delivery. This position is part of a career progression track and offers opportunities to develop expertise in fiduciary services, wealth planning, client relationship management, and other areas of wealth management.

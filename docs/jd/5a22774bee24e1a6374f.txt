The Impact You will have in this role : The Enterprise Data & Corporate Technology (EDCT) IT Business Operations Associate supports the operating discipline of the EDCT Business Management Office. The role helps coordinate governance forums and recurring management routines, maintains reporting and decision records, and tracks priorities, actions, dependencies, risks, and milestones through closure. Working with IT governance, Talent Acquisition, Enterprise Procurement/ESM, Human Resources, and other stakeholders, the individual organizes business information into clear updates, follows up on open items, supports issue resolution, and helps improve the processes and artifacts used to manage execution. This is an individual-contributor position at the Associate level requiring attention to detail, analytical thinking, clear communication, organization, and dependable follow-through. Success is reflected in timely and accurate deliverables, well-maintained records, improved visibility, and reliable stakeholder support.

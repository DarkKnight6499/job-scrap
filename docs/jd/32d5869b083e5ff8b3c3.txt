Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Customer Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As an Integrated Experience Delivery Senior Associate you will help shape client experience strategies that improve customer and employee journeys, using analysis of market trends, customer insights, and organizational capabilities to define goals and prioritize experience initiatives within our Experience Consulting practice. As a Senior Associate you will analyze complex problems, build meaningful client connections, anticipate team and client needs, and guide junior team members while navigating ambiguity and deepening your personal brand, technical skills, and understanding of business context. In this role at PwC, you will contribute to experience roadmaps, support the design and implementation of experience strategies, and deliver work that aligns with business objectives and the broader goals of front office consulting. Responsibilities - Analyzing client needs, market trends, and organizational capabilities to shape experience strategy recommendations - Developing experience roadmaps and prioritization plans that align with business goals and project scope - Translating customer and employee insights into journey concepts, engagement strategies, and design direction - Using digital analytics, customer analysis, and user journey mapping to inform recommendations and delivery choices - Supporting end-to-end delivery of experience initiatives across discovery, design, implementation, and refinement phases - Applying agile methods, human-centered design, and creative problem solving to address complex experience challenges - Building client relationships through thoughtful communication, active listening, and responsive issue resolution - Reviewing work from junior team members and guiding task execution across assigned workstreams - Interpreting feedback and data to refine deliverables, identify risks, and adapt plans as priorities change - Contributing to discussions on emerging technologies, AI tools, and digital tooling that enhance customer and employee experiences What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 3 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for at least one of the following fields of study: Accounting, Analytics/Data Science, Business Administration/Management, Computer Science/Information Systems, Economics, Engineering, Finance, Financial Mathematics/Quantitative Finance, Health Administration/Public Health, Mathematics/Statistics, Operations/Supply Chain - Demonstrating experience in customer experience design or strategy projects - Applying human-centered design methods across client service workstreams - Interpreting data and insights to guide recommendations and decisions -

Discovery Program: Equity + Macro Research (On-site)
Susquehanna International Group
Location: New York, New York

Overview Are you interested in exploring career paths in investment finance? Join us at Susquehanna to learn more about what a day in the life looks like for our equity and macro analysts. Through panel discussions, meet and greets with employees, and interactive gaming sessions, you’ll experience our unique culture and learn how these analysts collaborate with traders. You’ll also explore how equity and macro analysts use quantitative and fundamental analysis to generate actionable trade ideas based on market news and events. Discovery Program events will also be inclusive of other roles at Susquehanna related to quantitative trading, offering a broader perspective on the teams and opportunities that support our trading efforts. This opportunity is open to students who are planning to graduate in the winter of 2028 or the spring of 2029. Applications will close November 16, 2026. We will be in touch in October regarding next steps for attendance. What we're looking for We are looking for students with a background and demonstrated interest in any of the following fields/majors: Finance Economics Math/Applied Math Computer Science Statistics Engineering Please submit your resume in order to be considered for the program. About Susquehanna Susquehanna is a global quantitative trading firm powered by scientific rigor, curiosity, and innovation. Our culture is intellectually driven and highly collaborative, bringing together researchers, engineers, and traders to design and deploy impactful strategies in our systematic trading environment. To meet the unique challenges of global markets, Susquehanna applies machine learning and advanced quantitative research to vast datasets in order to uncover actionable insights and build effective strategies. By uniting deep market expertise with cutting-edge technology, we excel in solving complex problems and pushing boundaries together.

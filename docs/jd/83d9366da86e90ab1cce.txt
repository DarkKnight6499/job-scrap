Senior Frontend Engineer, Portfolio The Portfolio team within the Consumer & Business group owns how millions of Coinbase users understand what they hold and how it's performing. We build the balances, holdings, and profit-and-loss experiences at the core of the Coinbase app and website, the surfaces users open first, check most often, and trust most. As a Senior Frontend Engineer on Portfolio, you'll turn complex, real-time financial data into clarity for every kind of Coinbase user, from first-time buyers to sophisticated traders. What you'll do: Own the design and delivery of core Portfolio experiences including balances, holdings, and PnL across web (React/TypeScript) and native mobile (iOS and Android) Build performant, accessible, pixel-accurate UI that renders real-time financial data at Coinbase scale Partner with backend, design, and product to take ambiguous problems from scoping through production rollout Drive frontend architecture and quality including component reuse, state management, testing, and performance instrumentation Strengthen the team's engineering bar through rigorous code reviews, technical mentorship, and well-documented architecture decisions Required Skills and Experience: 5+ years of frontend engineering experience building consumer-facing web applications with high-quality, well-tested production code Deep expertise in React and TypeScript, with strong command of modern frontend fundamentals (state management, rendering performance, accessibility) Track record shipping and owning complex features end-to-end in production, ideally in financial, trading, or other data-intensive applications where accuracy and latency matter Demonstrated experience building real-time or high-throughput UIs and integrating with backend APIs (REST/GraphQL), with consistent collaboration alongside design and product partners to shape product outcomes Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.

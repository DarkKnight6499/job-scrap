About This Job The Business Analyst II partners with cross‑functional teams to improve business processes, enhance data accuracy, and support data‑driven decision‑making. This role translates business needs into actionable insights by gathering requirements, performing analysis, and delivering reporting that drives operational effectiveness. The position will focus heavily on data operations and analytics supporting the Deposits, Lending, and Services business units. Responsibilities include developing and executing SQL-based reporting, performing ad hoc analysis to address business needs, supporting operational and production reporting, and maintaining and enhancing Tableau dashboards. The Analyst will partner with stakeholders across the bank to deliver insights, improve processes, and support data-driven decision-making. Responsibilities: Collaborate with stakeholders to elicit, analyze, and document business requirements for new initiatives, enhancements, and system integrations Develop and maintain data models, dashboards, and reports to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and track project progress Collaborate with project managers, IT professionals, and other stakeholders to define project scope, objectives, and deliverables Document business processes, system requirements, and user manuals to ensure clear understanding and support ongoing maintenance Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in a quantitative field (e.g., Economics, Finance, Statistics, Mathematics) or 5+ years of data analytics experience 2+ years of experience building and maintaining Tableau reports Strong T‑SQL skills with experience supporting ad hoc and recurring analysis Proven ability to partner with stakeholders and clearly communicate data‑driven insights Experience supporting marketing data operations, including email marketing and servicing list pulls Working knowledge of U.S. consumer financial regulations (e.g., Privacy Act, Affiliate Opt‑Outs, CAN‑SPAM) Familiarity with Azure DevOps, web analytics/debugging tools (e.g., GA4, Chrome DevTools), and paid affiliate marketing platforms is a plus Axos Employee Benefits May Include: Medical, Dental, Vision, and Life Insurance Paid Sick Leave, 3 weeks’ Vacation, and Holidays (about 11 a year) HSA or FSA account and other voluntary benefits 401(k) Retirement Saving Plan with Employer Match Program and 529 Savings Plan Employee Mortgage Loan Program and free access to an Axos Bank Account with Self-Directed Trading About Axos Born digital-first, Axos delivers financial tools and services that allow individuals, small businesses, and companies to access and manage their money how, when, and where they want.

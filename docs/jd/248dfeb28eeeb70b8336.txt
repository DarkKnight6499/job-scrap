CNA’s Healthcare Underwriting Team is seeking a strategic and collaborative leader to help guide the Aging Services Retail portfolio. This individual will have leadership responsibility for a portion of the Aging Services Retail East Team and will work in close partnership with the Aging Services AVP to co-lead the team. Together, they will drive business results, team development, and strategic growth through retail agency and broker distribution partners. JOB DESCRIPTION: Performs a combination of duties in accordance with departmental guidelines: Shares leadership responsibility for the Aging Services Retail East Team, with direct oversight of a portion of the team’s underwriters. Collaborates with the Aging Services AVP to co-lead the team, ensuring alignment with CNA’s strategic direction and business goals. Coaches, mentors, and develops underwriters and contributes to the design and delivery of underwriting training and development programs. Drives profitable growth through retail agency and broker distribution partners and key relationship management across the East region. Participates in the development and execution of underwriting policy, marketing initiatives, and business strategy. Develops and implements operating plans to enhance business performance and meet strategic objectives. Executes portfolio management strategies to achieve growth and profitability targets. Represents the Aging Services Retail team at industry conferences and events and remains current on regulatory, market, and competitive trends affecting the East region. Collaborates with senior management on complex underwriting risks, applying advanced analysis to assess risk acceptability. Determines appropriate pricing for complex risks based on financial and competitive analysis and in compliance with regulatory requirements. Acts as a change agent for the Aging Services Retail team and the CNA enterprise, supporting the adoption of new technologies and strategic initiatives. Exemplifies excellence in underwriting, leadership, distribution, documentation, and team development. May perform additional duties as assigned. Reporting Relationship AVP, Underwriting or above Skills, Knowledge & Abilities Ability to effectively lead, coach, and develop an underwriting group. Excellent communication, negotiation, and presentation skills, with the ability to interact effectively with all levels of CNA's internal and external business partners. Senior-level technical and business management acumen, underwriting skill, and knowledge of insurance and underwriting principles, practices, and procedures. Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to manage multiple projects and teams. Ability to navigate ambiguous situations and issues. Creativity in resolving unique and challenging business problems. Ability to achieve results by taking a proactive, long-term view of business goals and objectives. Knowledge of Microsoft Office Suite and other business-related software. Education & Experience MBA or its equivalent in experience. Professional designations preferred.

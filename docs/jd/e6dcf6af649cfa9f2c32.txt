Join the Card Acquisition Costing team for an exciting opportunity to provide financial and strategic analysis, oversight, and coordination of budgeting and forecasting. Our Financial Planning and Analysis (FP&A) teams are responsible for a wide range of activities including financial control, forecasting and budgeting, analysis of financial/business metrics, delivery of regular management reporting, development of new reporting capabilities, and advising the line of business CFOs on how to increase profitability and efficiencies. As a Financial Analyst within the Marketing Expense Finance Team, you will analyze, prepare, and reconcile accounting and technical data as well as prepare reports, while always thinking of and devising ways to streamline and minimize cycle times through automated applications and process improvement. You will also help develop recommendations affecting business procedures and operations and maintain financial and reporting systems. In addition, you will assist the department and other team members with special projects or components of other projects as needed. Job responsibilities Build strong working relationships with business partners and provide card acquisition expense metrics through monthly/quarterly reporting, project support, and ad hoc analyses that deliver insight into card product acquisition expense performance. Responsible for IRF/budget card acquisition expense forecasting; perform variance and analyses of actuals versus forecast, identifying risks and opportunities. Develop and track key acquisition expense metrics and provide financial analysis for senior management to assist with strategic decision-making. Partner with business CFO teams to update forecasts/budgets then performing variance analysis to understand the key drivers in marketing and presenting commentary to senior managers, explaining changes from prior forecasts/budgets. Report on a monthly and quarterly basis to stakeholders to align on the marketing expense story. Help design new reports and dashboards to efficiently deliver the financial results to senior management Enhance controls and streamline processes, introducing automation where possible.

Position Description We are seeking an Associate to join our Alternatives Business Management team. This role sits at the center of PIMCO’s private alternatives platform, partnering directly with Portfolio Managers (PMs) to drive the execution and oversight of investment activity from pipeline through close. The Associate will play a central role in supporting deal execution and transaction management across the private alternatives platform. This role is critical in ensuring transactions move efficiently from pipeline through close, with full alignment across Trading, Legal, Operations, Finance, Tax, Risk, and Compliance. This position offers exposure across the full investment lifecycle—from pipeline management and deal setup through execution and post-close processes. The Associate acts as a key integrator across teams, ensuring that all operational, compliance, and risk requirements are met without slowing deal velocity. Beyond coordination, this role provides a unique opportunity to develop a holistic understanding of how deals are structured, executed, and managed on the buy side, while working directly with investment teams and senior stakeholders. Responsibilities Key responsibilities include, but are not limited to: Liaise with PM and all relevant support groups (Legal/Ops/Finance/Tax/Risk/Compliance/Trading) to ensure all internal policies and processes have been completed prior to close Work with Trade Assistants and Trade Compliance to ensure smooth trade flow process Coordinate with all groups on the NST checklist process and completion Assist PMs with Desk’s deal pipeline management – including deal setup in DealCloud, monitoring and updating settlement information, coordinating with Alts Operations and Alts Finance on timing for liquidity purposes, as well as verifying pipeline info is properly flowing through to downstream reporting and work-streams Deal Tagging updates – coordinate with Trade Assistants and Alts Finance on appropriate tagging in the Fund Custodian and Risk systems Assist Desk PMs in review of quarterly Origination and Asset Management reporting Respond to ad-hoc requests from Portfolio Management and other groups, as necessary Qualifications 1-3 years of experience; strong, relevant internship experience will be considered Exposure to a buy-side or sell-side trading environment preferred Working knowledge of Fixed Income. Exposure to residential loan or consumer asset class (unsecured consumer, credit card, student loan, auto, BNPL, solar) desirable Compliance related experience and/ or legal background a plus Strong relationship skills with the ability to work closely with PMs and cross-functional teams Excellent communications skills; able to clearly articulate ideas and strategies clearly, both verbally and in writing Strong analytical and problem-solving skills Ability to operate effectively in a fast-paced, high-pressure environment Enjoy working collaboratively across the organization to resolve issues. Advanced Excel skills; familiarity with dv01, DealCloud, Power BI, and iLevel a plus Bachelor’s degree and strong academic record with concentration in math, science, finance, economics or other related quantitative/analytical field from a leading, accredited college or university.

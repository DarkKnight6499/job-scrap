The Fixed Income Division is comprised of Commodities, Interest Rate and Currency Products, Credit Products and Distribution. Professionals in the Division assess and actively manages risk, trade securities, and structure as well as execute innovative transactions in the fast-paced and constantly changing global markets. The Commodities Division, with 200+ front office employees, is a market leader in energy, metals, and agricultural product trading worldwide whose professionals trade in both physical and derivative commodity risk. Within the Commodities Division, North American Power and Gas desk (“NAPG”) trades physical and financial power and gas. NAPG structures and manages transactions in duration of one to twenty plus years across a large and growing client base. NAPG provides load-following services, manages resources including tolls for power generation assets and provides various risk management needs for municipals, commercial and industrial load, load aggregators, independent power producers and renewable generation developers. The Global Commodities Structuring Group works closely with Commodities Origination and Trading teams to model transactions, negotiate client confirmations, book trades and coordinate with second line functions (strats, operations, credit, valuation control, legal, finance). Structuring also plays key role to ideate and implement initiatives around new client solutions, trading risk management strategies, pricing and capital frameworks and execution process. This role is for a Structurer in the Global Commodities Structuring Group focused on NAPG, based in Houston (preferred) or New York. Responsibilities: A successful candidate would be able to develop a deep understanding of market structure, fundamentals, and client solutions within NAPG markets. The candidate would work closely with the power and gas origination and trading teams including managing risk, executing complex trades, and coming up with ideas to mitigate risks and create PnL opportunities.

Full-Time Associate, Finance Leadership Development Program (FLDP) - MBA Program 2027 Location: 1166 Avenue of the Americas, New York, NY 10036-2708, United States of America Are you capable of delivering high impact results for C-level leaders across a global enterprise? Do you have experience building lines of communication and leading projects? Are you motivated by engaging in cross-functional corporate finance projects and accelerating your post-MBA career? Marsh (NYSE: MRSH) is a global leader in risk, reinsurance and capital, people and investments, and management consulting, advising clients in 130 countries. With annual revenue of over $27 billion and more than 95,000 colleagues, Marsh helps build the confidence to thrive through the power of perspective. For more information, visit corporate.marsh.com , or follow us on LinkedIn and X . The Full-Time FLDP is a three-year program designed to cultivate future leaders for Global Finance. FLDP Associates have unique access to our key decision makers — Marsh Corporate’s CFO and the Finance Leadership Team are committed to the program and its participants. Marsh strives to attract and retain high-caliber, motivated MBAs who have the potential to become the next generation of leadership at the company. During the first two years, the program is comprised of three eight-month rotations that enable Associates to gain a well-rounded view of the company. Rotations will include working with senior leaders to analyze strategic and operational issues, gather market intelligence, and advance business growth. Your third year, you will transition to a full-time position within the Finance Organization. WHAT A TYPICAL DAY LOOKS LIKE: Support Global Finance through the assigned placement team Assist in managing and executing team projects and strategic initiatives Depending on placement, responsibilities may include, but are not necessarily limited to: Competitor and industry analyses; Due diligence and valuation modeling; Budgeting, forecasting and financial analysis; Cash flow and liquidity analysis; Corporate accounting and public company filings; Presenting detailed findings from data set analysis; Review and evaluate controls to improve operating efficiency Engage in learning, networking, community service and events WHAT WE VALUE: Ability to meet deadlines and produce high quality work on a consistent basis A strong work ethic An excellent academic track record Interest and familiarity with the industry and general corporate finance functions A self-starter who can work in a team environment and across functional areas A genuine curiosity for continuous learning and development Strong written and verbal communication skills Analytical aptitude and interest Leadership potential by cultivating, managing and strengthening interpersonal relationships Strong project management skills ABOUT YOU: You have completed an MBA program on or before Spring 2027 Ideally you will have some pre-MBA work experience You are an agile thinker who is ready to make an impact and solve complex problems You must have legal authorization to work in the U.S. on a permanent basis without the need for sponsorship now or in the future undefinedThe applicable salary for this role is $145,000.

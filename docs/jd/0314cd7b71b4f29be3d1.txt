What We’re Looking For: The Finance team at 2U provides strategic financial leadership and analytical support to stakeholders across the organization. We support decision-making and operational effectiveness through financial planning, analysis, and cross-functional collaboration, all in service of 2U’s mission to expand access to high-quality education. As a Senior Manager, FP&A, you will play a lead role in supporting high-impact, cross-functional initiatives across the business. This includes evaluating new initiatives, supporting business case development, driving financial modeling, and surfacing insights that inform company-wide decisions. You’ll work closely with Finance leadership and cross-functional teams to drive clarity around priorities, risks, and opportunities. This is an ideal role for a highly analytical and proactive individual with strong business acumen and a desire to work in a mission-driven and fast-paced environment. Responsibilities Include, But Are Not Limited To: Financial Planning, Forecasting & Strategic Analysis Help lead the annual operating plan, monthly forecasting, and long-range planning processes across the organization. Own the consolidation of enterprise financial forecasts, budgets, and long-range plans across all business units, ensuring completeness, accuracy, and consistency of financial assumptions. Develop and maintain integrated three-statement financial models (income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow) to support planning, liquidity management, and executive decision-making. Build scenario analyses, business cases, and financial models to evaluate strategic initiatives, investments, and operational decisions. Partner with executive leadership to identify risks, opportunities, and actionable recommendations that support company objectives. Business Partnership & Performance Management Partner with business leaders to evaluate financial performance, operational trends, and resource allocation. Analyze monthly financial results, identify key performance drivers, and communicate actionable insights to leadership. Develop scalable reporting, dashboards, and KPIs to monitor business performance and improve decision-making. Support cross-functional initiatives by providing financial analysis and decision support. FP&A Systems, Reporting & Process Improvement Lead continuous improvements to FP&A processes, reporting, and planning capabilities through automation and standardization. Maintain and enhance financial planning models and reporting within Adaptive Planning, ensuring data integrity, forecasting accuracy, and efficient planning processes. Improve financial reporting infrastructure and documentation to increase transparency and scalability. Prepare executive, Board, and management reporting materials that clearly communicate financial performance and outlook. Strategic Initiatives & Partner Finance Support Lead or support enterprise strategic initiatives requiring cross-functional financial evaluation and coordination. Support Degree partner cash collection initiatives through financial analysis, reporting, forecasting, and collaboration with business partners to improve working capital and cash flow. Provide ad hoc financial analyses to support evolving business priorities during the company's transformation. Leadership & Team Development Foster a high-performance culture grounded in accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement. Elevate analytical capabilities, financial acumen, and storytelling skills across the team. Optimize team structure and workflows to scale with company growth. Things That Should Be In Your Background: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or related field (MBA preferred) 10+ years of progressive experience in FP&A, corporate finance, or related field, with 5+ years in a leadership role Demonstrated experience managing and developing high-performing teams Strong executive presence with the ability to communicate financial insights Advanced proficiency with Adaptive Planning (or comparable enterprise planning systems) including model development, forecasting, reporting, and administration Advanced financial modeling and analytical skills Demonstrated competency in three-statement financial modeling Proven ability to translate complex financial analyses into clear, compelling narratives for Board and executive-level presentations Working Conditions: 5% of travel via plane, car, and train may be required Flexibility to work more than 40 hours per week when business needs warrant Physical Requirements Mobility within the office including movement from floor to floor Access information using a computer Benefits & Culture Our global employee base is a diverse collection of innovators, dreamers, and doers working together to transform lives through higher education.

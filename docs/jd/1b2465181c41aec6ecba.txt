Role Description A career within the Nomura Healthcare Group provides an exceptional learning experience led by experienced senior bankers and an opportunity to develop the investment banking skillset while acquiring strong sector knowledge and thorough understanding of industry trends. The associate will take an active role in developing, structuring and executing a broad range of advisory assignments and financing transactions for our clients.

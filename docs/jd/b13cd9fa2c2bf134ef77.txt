The Impact you will have in this role: The Channel Product Owner is responsible for translating the product vision and roadmap into prioritized squad deliverables and release plans. This role manages product scope and the squad backlog in partnership with the Product Manager, Engineering Leads, Scrum Master, squad members, clients, and key stakeholders. The Product Owner will support one or more Client Interfaces channel capabilities, including FIX, SWIFT, MQ, Kafka, and SFTP. The successful candidate will combine strong product ownership and delivery experience with knowledge of messaging, streaming, and file-transfer technologies. The individual will represent client and stakeholder needs within the squad, maintain delivery readiness, and help ensure that channel capabilities are secure, resilient, scalable, and easier to adopt. Your Primary Responsibilities: Product Ownership & Backlog Management Translate the Channel vision and roadmap into squad deliverables and release plans. Own and prioritize the product backlog based on client value, strategic alignment, delivery readiness, risk, and technical dependencies. Partner with the squad to create and refine backlog items with clear requirements, acceptance criteria, and business outcomes. Maintain sufficient backlog readiness to support effective sprint and release planning. Accept or reject completed work based on agreed acceptance criteria and the definition of done. Balance new capabilities, modernization, operational improvements, risk reduction, and technical health within the backlog. Channel Delivery & Modernization Support delivery and lifecycle management for one or more channel capabilities, including FIX, SWIFT, MQ, Kafka, and SFTP. Partner with Product Management and Technology to advance standardized, reusable channel services across business lines. Support initiatives that modernize legacy or hardware-bound capabilities through distributed, cloud, container, and automated delivery patterns. Help improve onboarding through standard workflows, reusable patterns, automation, and clearer service requirements. Evaluate implementation alternatives by considering client value, cost, schedule, risk, resiliency, and operational support. Stakeholder & Client Engagement Bring the voice of clients and stakeholders into the squad and communicate squad considerations back to the broader organization. Seek feedback from clients, business lines, operations, engineering, and other partners, and incorporate relevant feedback into the backlog. Build effective relationships across Product Management, Technology, Architecture, Client Operations, Risk, Security, and supporting teams. Facilitate walkthroughs, demonstrations, and feedback sessions for new or enhanced capabilities. Communicate delivery progress, dependencies, risks, and decisions clearly to stakeholders. Articulate the business value of releases and support adoption of shared channel capabilities. Delivery Readiness, Risk & Controls Coordinate with engineering and delivery partners to identify and resolve cross-team risks, issues, and dependencies. Support operational readiness, including documentation, support requirements, controls, monitoring, and release planning. Incorporate resiliency, security, compliance, and other nonfunctional requirements into backlog prioritization and acceptance criteria. Ensure risk and control requirements are reflected in day-to-day product ownership activities and escalate concerns appropriately. Participate actively in squad and platform planning, delivery, review, and continuous-improvement events.

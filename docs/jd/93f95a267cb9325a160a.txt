As a Fundamental Research Group Associate, you will work closely with Investment Professionals to drive deep research on long term fundamental issues in the biotech industry to generate returns for our clients. You will partner with Investment Professionals on their coverage to help generate investment ideas and work as a key contributor to the investment process. This includes monitoring holdings by researching and analyzing companies and related industries, financial modeling, drafting reports, facilitating meetings with management teams, sell-side firms and industry experts. Key Requirements: You have a minimum of 2 years of investment research experience in buy-side research or sell-side research. You hold an MD or PhD in a relevant scientific discipline. For candidates with an MD, prior laboratory or research experience is required. You have a consistent track record of top accomplishments at every step of your educational and professional career. You have an internal drive to always do more, and better than before. You have experience in successfully statistically analyzing clinical trials, evaluating mechanisms of action, and translating scientific and clinical findings with positive predictive value. You have experience accurately assessing scientific, clinical, regulatory, and commercial developments affecting biotech companies. You demonstrate the ability to leverage AI tools to drive higher quality and more comprehensive research. You have extensive experience with detailed financial modeling and fundamental analysis, working with sell-side firms and industry consultants, utilizing research tools, trade publications, online sources, regulatory filings, clinical data, and other resources to generate differentiated investment insights. You are passionate about a career in equity research with a strong interest in long-only investing and posing insightful questions to experts is routine for you. You possess intellectual curiosity, strong analytical capabilities, and an interest in understanding emerging trends across healthcare, biotechnology, and capital markets. You’ve successfully built trusted relationships both inside and outside the organization, and view feedback as essential to learning and continuous improvement.

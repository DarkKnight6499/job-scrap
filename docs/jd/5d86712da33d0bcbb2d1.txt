Produce quarterly reports for management and JPMorgan Funds Board of Trustees. Required qualifications, capabilities and skills: 2+ years experience working within an Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) business. Excellent communication skills (written, verbal, and presentation). Self-starter with critical thinking and problem solving skills, proactive, creative, and flexible with a strong attention to detail. High energy and a strong work ethic, highly organized and strong follow-up skills. Team player with the ability to quickly get up to speed and build relationships. Independently drive product initiatives and projects and effectively manage competing deliverables concurrently. Ability to multi-task and meet tight deadlines in a high pressure environment. Risk management and a Control orientated mindset is essential.

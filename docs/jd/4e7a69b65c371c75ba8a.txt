Our office is located at 299 Park Ave. Duties & Responsibilities: The Research Analyst position within the Capital Advisory Group provides an excellent opportunity to support a new initiative focused on expanding our business development efforts for our restructuring and creditor rights business. The Research Analyst will serve as the anchor for this initiative with regards to research, analytics, and overall organization. The Research Analyst may have the opportunity to evolve / transition into a traditional Transaction Analyst in the Capital Advisory Group over time as well. Responsibilities at Lincoln International are often more entrepreneurial than at other firms providing self-motivated individuals with many opportunities to contribute to a dynamic and growing platform. Successful Analysts are assertive, motivated self-starters with the desire and potential to succeed in a fast-paced, entrepreneurial environment. Key responsibilities include: Identifying and maintaining a database of potential target businesses / clients Maintaining an external watch list along with related credit profiles Preparing and completing “idea books” to pitch to target businesses / clients Preparing marketing materials for meetings with potential clients Interacting with clients and preparing meeting deliverables and follow ups Tracking the capital markets and analyzing any changes within the markets Supporting the senior members of the Capital Advisory Group (specifically within the creditor business marketing initiative) Qualifications: The Analyst position at Lincoln International is extremely challenging, offers an excellent learning experience and provides a solid foundation upon which an individual can build a future business career. Interested candidates should meet the following requirements: Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics or a related field from top tier institution; strong academic record 1-2 years of professional experience in corporate finance, equity research (or similar), accounting, or related field preferred. Analytic foundation with understanding of financial statements and proficient Microsoft Excel skills Strong writing skills and experience with PowerPoint and Microsoft Word Effective organizational skills and the aptitude to manage people and work products Self-motivated and experienced in taking initiative on thoughtful research and related analysis Strong communication skills and experience interacting with teammates and clients Additional Information: Lincoln International is not currently hiring individuals for this position who now or in the future require sponsorship for employment visa status.

Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Cybersecurity & Privacy Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Privileged Access Management Sr Associate you will focus on securing and managing access to systems and data through identity and access management work within our Cybersecurity practice. As a Senior Associate you will analyze complex access challenges, build meaningful client connections, guide junior team members, and use critical thinking to develop solutions that support broader project objectives and evolving business needs. In this role at PwC, you will design and implement privileged access controls, interpret client requirements, and apply tools and methods that help confirm secure and efficient access across environments. Responsibilities - Managing privileged access administration activities across client environments, including user provisioning, role changes, and removal of elevated permissions - Configuring and maintaining privileged access management tools, identity platforms, and access control models to support secure system access - Reviewing access requests, entitlements, and segregation of duties issues to identify risk and recommend corrective actions - Analyzing access logs and authorization data to identify unusual activity, access gaps, and process improvements - Developing technical documentation, control evidence, and operating procedures for identity and access management processes - Supporting implementation of privileged access controls across Microsoft Active Directory, Azure Active Directory, CyberArk, and related platforms - Interpreting client requirements and translating them into access governance, identity federation, and role based access control solutions - Guiding junior team members through assigned tasks, quality checks, and issue resolution during delivery cycles - Building client relationships through clear status updates, issue tracking, and responsive support for access management workstreams - Coordinating testing, troubleshooting, and remediation efforts for access management solutions in complex environments What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 3 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for at least one of the following fields of study: Business Administration/Management, Computer and Information Science, Computer Applications, Computer Engineering, Information CyberSecurity, Information Technology, Management Information Systems - Demonstrating Privileged Access Management experience in client support settings - Utilizing CyberArk, Azure Active Directory, or Microsoft Active Directory - Interpreting access controls, authorization models, and identity governance concepts - Applying critical thinking to complex access management challenges - Anticipating client needs while navigating ambiguity and change Travel Requirements Up to 40% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $77,000 -

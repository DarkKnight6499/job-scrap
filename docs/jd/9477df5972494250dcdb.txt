Job Summary Experienced individual contributor responsible for independently accomplishing customer-focused system validation for enterprise and data-center SSD products. Owns end-to-end validation for assigned platforms, contributes to validation planning and test development, applies advanced technical judgment, and provides technical guidance without formal management duties. Responsibilities: • Independently plan and complete customer-specific SSD/system validation cycles and qualification runs. • Set up, configure, and ensure the ongoing validation of environments including systems, firmware, OS, and platform settings. • Analyze validation results, identify failures, and determine next steps including debug and risk assessment. • Develop, extend, and maintain validation test cases, scripts, and automation. • Serve as technical owner for assigned projects, providing mentorship and sharing guidelines across teams. • Applying Artificial Intelligence to improve day-to-day working efficiency Minimum Qualifications: • Bachelor’s degree in Computer science, Electrical/Computer Engineering, or a related field with 6 years of experience in customer, system, or product validation roles. • Strong knowledge and experience with NVMe and PCIe protocols and debugging skills across system, firmware, and hardware domains. • Hands-on experience accomplishing system-level validation in complex hardware/software environments. • Multi-functional experience working with firmware, hardware, system, and product teams. • Proficiency in Python, C/C++, or similar languages and strong understanding of validation methodologies. Preferred Qualifications: • Experience supporting OEM, hyperscale, or cloud customer validation programs. • Ownership of customer-facing validation execution for platforms or programs. • Experience mentoring peers or providing technical guidance within validation teams. • Familiarity with industry certifications, compatibility testing, and joint qualification processes. • Strong communication skills for technical discussions with multi-functional partners.

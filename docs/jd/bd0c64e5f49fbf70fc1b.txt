Senior Associate, TPO Vendor Manager - Chase Travel
JPMorgan Chase
Location: Plano, TX, United States
Link: https://jpmc.fa.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/210803615

Join a team where you can build strong supplier partnerships while helping manage risk, resilience, and regulatory readiness. You’ll work across functions to keep third-party engagements running smoothly and governed throughout their lifecycle. As a Senior Associate, TPO Vendor Manager in Chase Travel, you will ensure relationships are established and maintained in line with Third Party Oversight (TPO) standards, regulatory obligations, operational resilience expectations, and internal risk management frameworks—enabling the business to onboard, manage, and exit suppliers safely and effectively. You’ll partner closely with stakeholders to identify risks, drive remediation, and support audit and exam readiness. Job responsibilities Support a portfolio of third-party supplier engagements from onboarding through termination, ensuring supplier engagements meet internal TPO standards, policies, and governance requirements. Maintain accurate supplier inventory records and complete portfolio documentation, monitoring portfolio health, emerging risks, and remediation progress. Coordinate onboarding activities, including required risk assessments and due diligence packages. Drive cross-functional reviews and document inputs and approvals across key partner teams Support and facilitate engagement and inputs from FVA, Information Security, Privacy, Compliance, Architecture, Legal, and other review teams as needed. Support contract lifecycle activities including negotiations, renewals, amendments, and terminations. Confirm contractual obligations align with applicable policy and regulatory requirements. Track renewal timelines and proactively manage remediation and exit activities. Identify and manage third-party operational, compliance, cybersecurity, concentration, and resilience risks, driving remediation and supporting audit, exam, and ongoing regulatory readiness compliance. Serve as the primary point of contact for business owners and supplier stakeholders, building strong internal and external partnerships while influencing cross-functional decisions and presenting risks, recommendations, and portfolio updates to senior leadership. Partner with Finance (the function responsible for ongoing sub-processor relationship management) to support the recurring process that ensures contractual commitments are met, including compliance monitoring, record updates, and coordination with internal teams.

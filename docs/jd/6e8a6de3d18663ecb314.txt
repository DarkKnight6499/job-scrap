A day in the life. As a Senior Associate on the Corporate Development team, you’ll provide direction, coordination, oversight and execution of all phases of acquisition analysis and due diligence on behalf of MMA to ensure consistency with company strategy, commitments and goals. The Senior Associate will support the firm’s Corporate Development function through research, due diligence, financial analysis and development of presentations for initiatives across MMA. The successful candidate for this position will be poised for long-term career growth and advancement opportunities across the firm. In this role, you will support the executives in strategic and financial analysis for acquisitions and other key business initiatives. In conjunction with senior management, you will research and assess transaction opportunities and build valuation and financial models. You will assist in communicating the business case for proposed acquisitions, particularly the strategic alignment, market context, and business analysis. This Senior Associate will manage the due diligence review of target acquisitions, in addition to identifying risk issue that impact transaction value and engage the right resources to address. You will meet with stakeholders and provide status updates through providing clear communication issues, potential risks, and mitigation plans, while setting and continually managing expectations. The Senior Associate will serve as a liaison with target acquisitions and internal subject matter experts to clarify due diligence requirements and ensure thorough follow through. This role will consolidate due diligence reports from subject matter experts; Distill and summarize relevant information for Corporate Development and Senior Executive team. Our future colleague.

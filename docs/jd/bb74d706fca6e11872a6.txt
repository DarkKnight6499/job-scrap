Position Summary The Senior Model Risk Analyst will play a critical role in implementing and enhancing the Bank’s Model Risk Management framework, policies, procedures, and governance practices. As part of the second line of defense, the Senior Analyst will provide independent review, effective challenge, and oversight of models, modeling processes, and designated calculations used across the Bank. The Senior Model Risk Analyst will independently lead reviews of complex financial, risk management, treasury, interest rate, liquidity, credit, valuation, accounting, mortgage, stress testing, and forecasting models used throughout the Bank, and will support the Bank’s model risk management approach to emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence and machine learning, as applicable. Responsibilities include assessing model conceptual soundness, assumptions, limitations, data, implementation, controls, documentation, performance, and compliance with regulatory expectations. The Senior Model Risk Analyst will routinely interact with model owners, senior management, external validators, auditors, regulators, vendors, and peers across the Federal Home Loan Bank System. The position requires substantial technical expertise, independent judgment, and strong communication skills. The ideal candidate will demonstrate a deep understanding of quantitative analysis, financial modeling, model governance, and regulatory expectations related to model risk management. In this role, you will help strengthen the Bank’s ability to identify, measure, monitor, and control model risk. You will partner with model owners and stakeholders while maintaining independence, support clear and actionable validation outcomes, and contribute to enhancements in model governance, validation planning, performance monitoring, issue remediation, and risk reporting. This role will have a hybrid work schedule in our Boston office in accordance with the Bank’s Hybrid Work Program. More time will be expected in the office to support onboarding initially.

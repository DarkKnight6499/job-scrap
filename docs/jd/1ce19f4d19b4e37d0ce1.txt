Are you looking to join an organization that is growing and dynamic? What about a high-energy, collaborative environment that rewards hard work? J.S. Held is a global consulting firm that combines technical, scientific, financial, and strategic expertise to advise clients seeking to realize value and mitigate risk. Our professionals serve as trusted advisors to organizations facing high stakes matters demanding urgent attention, staunch integrity, proven experience, clear-cut analysis, and an understanding of both tangible and intangible assets. The firm provides a comprehensive suite of services, products, and data that enable clients to navigate complex, contentious, and often catastrophic situations. This person would be responsible for simultaneously supervising and performing multiple forensic accounting / business valuation engagements, including constructing sophisticated analyses, understanding statutes and case law, interacting with clients, and independently developing work product for litigation and business valuation engagements. Job Responsibilities Supervises subordinates in conducting their duties & responsibilities. Analyzes opposing expert reports and summarize differences with our reports. Possesses superior knowledge of Microsoft Excel and related office software. Creates & modifies Excel models and templates. Prepares Demands, Subpoenas, Special Interrogatories, and Requests for Admission. Prepares Motions to Compel based on missing document grids. Writes Declaration Drafts. Performs Economic, industry and financial research and analysis of BV engagements. Determines cost of capital in business valuation engagements. Drafts business valuation reports. Researches and understands relevant case law and statutory law. Completes complex and sophisticated forensic accounting assignments. Compiles completed forensic accounting work for declarations, testimony and settlement. Compiles reports to be issued by the firm. Completes Xspouse™ calculations for divorce engagements. Teaches complex internal training seminars; assists in external events. Assumes designated campus responsibility for recruiting and retention activities. Organizes and attends morale and employee retention events. Prepare employee reviews. Performs other related duties as assigned by Managers and others. Supervisory Responsibilities Responsible for supervising all Associates and other direct reports Required Qualifications Bachelor's Degree (BA) from four‐year college or university, or one to two years of related experience and/or training, or equivalent combination of education and experience. CPA, CPA candidate, or other relevant experience. Generally, minimum 2 years of related professional work experience Physical and Mental Job Qualifications Prolonged periods sitting at a desk and working on a computer. Ability to travel as needed.

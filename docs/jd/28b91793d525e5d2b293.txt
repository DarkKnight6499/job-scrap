Key responsibilities At UBS, we reimagine the way we work, connect, and deliver value to our colleagues, clients, and partners. Working in an agile way helps us stay responsive, adaptable, and innovative. We're looking for a Risk Manager to: • review financial risk frameworks across the Group, Business Divisions, and Legal Entities • support the design and enhancement of financial risk frameworks, promoting firm wide alignment and incorporating industry best practices • monitor industry and regulatory developments that may impact financial risk frameworks • work closely with risk subject matter experts and stakeholders to gather information and support the timely delivery of assessments • prepare, summarize, and present framework enhancements to senior management and governance forums • support project management activities, regulatory inquiries, and ad hoc initiatives to meet evolving business needs • maintain documentation, trackers, and tools, including Power Apps The team You'll join the Risk Frameworks team within Policies, Standards and Frameworks, part of the group wide Enterprise Risk Management function. We act as trusted partners to stakeholders on policy, framework, and governance matters. Our team plays a key role in ensuring risk frameworks are clear, consistent, and fit for purpose. You'll be supported through mentoring, knowledge sharing, and opportunities to contribute to team initiatives. Your skills and experience • a university degree in economics, business administration, law, or a related field • ideally 10 years of experience in control functions or governance roles • experience in risk, compliance, governance, audit, regulatory, legal, or operational roles within financial services or a similar environment • an interest in risk management, risk frameworks, and regulatory topics • strong stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence, challenge, and drive positive outcomes • organized, with strong attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple priorities • comfortable using Microsoft Office applications; experience with Power Apps is an advantage • you’re curious to explore how AI can improve how we build, deliver, and optimize workflows. You do this with sound judgment – validating outputs and aligning with policies, risk standards, and ethical use.

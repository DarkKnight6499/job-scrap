ROLE DESCRIPTION

Form Energy is seeking a skilled and experienced Associate Manager, Facilities Maintenance to join our team. The ideal candidate will be responsible for installing, maintaining, and repairing electrical systems in commercial, and industrial settings. The Sr Facilities Coordinator will work under the supervision of the Sr Manager Site Operations and will play a key role in ensuring that scheduling of both preventative and corrective maintenance of both hard and soft services happen in a timely manner to keep operations online. These include HVAC, MEP systems, Janitorial, Security, Life-Safety & Site Services. Participation in EHS initiatives is expected as well as being an ambassador of safety throughout the facility.



This is an exciting opportunity to help shape and be part of the growth of a fast-moving company with breakthrough technology and an incredible mission! 



Relocation assistance is available. 




WHAT YOU'LL DO:

 - Evaluation of service contracts as well as schedule against F&E calibration and PM schedules.

 - Coordinate with external contractors for specialized maintenance and repair tasks.

 - Facilitate inspections of facility systems to identify any repairs or upgrades needed.

 - Maintain accurate records of maintenance activities, repairs, and inspections in CMMS

 - Generate historical reports and develop KPI's for monthly, quarterly, annual dashboards as well as predictive maintenance scheduling purposes.

 - Maintain & participate in CAPA's and RCA's following failure modes around the facility

 - Assist with property management upgrades and contractors as well as supporting external agency audits and inspections for compliance and permitting needs

 - Support Office services varying needs and requirements

 - Take lead in managing Capital Projects coordination of vendors, budget and any delays that can arise in a timely manner

 - Familiarity with BMS and troubleshooting required

 - Oversee the daily operations, maintenance, safety, and physical resources of facilities buildings, grounds, and utility systems

 - Ensure the facility adheres to local, state, and federal building, fire, and health codes (e.g., OSHA, ADA, local ordinances).

 - Coordinate with EHS on safety audits, emergency response planning, and incident resolution

 - Support space utilization planning, office layouts, and other soft services around the facility

 - Assist in budget planning & tracking as well as projecting out TI costs based on Capital projects

 - Possess the flexibility to perform miscellaneous job-related duties as assigned, supporting the broader goals and needs of the facilities group




WHAT YOU'LL BRING:

 - Certification: High school diploma or equivalent; technical certifications or an associate degree in a related field nice to have.

 - Experience: Minimum of 8  years of experience in facilities management or equivalent with a strong background in facilities maintenance, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, mechanical systems.

 - Technical Skills: Strong planning ability, excellent  judgment, and clear communication of directions required.

 - Problem-Solving: Excellent problem-solving skills with a proactive approach to maintenance issues. Ability to read and interpret technical documents, schematics, and manuals.

 - Communication: Strong verbal and written communication skills with the ability to interact professionally with clients and team members. Ability to lead and direct outside vendors with professionalism; experience in working with outside contractors.

 - Physical Fitness: Ability to work in various environments, including confined spaces, at heights, and in adverse weather conditions. Capable of lifting and carrying heavy equipment as needed.

A Day in the Life The External Reporting team is responsible for supporting Medtronic’s public company reporting and compliance obligations. The team partners closely with Global Controllership, Global Finance, Investor Relations, Legal, Treasury, Tax, Compensation & Benefits, and business finance teams to prepare external filings, financial disclosures, and audit-related deliverables while ensuring compliance with applicable accounting and reporting standards. At Medtronic, we bring bold ideas forward with speed and decisiveness to put patients first in everything we do. In-person exchanges are invaluable to our work. We’re working a minimum of 4 days a week onsite as part of our commitment to fostering a culture of professional growth and cross-functional collaboration as we work together to engineer the extraordinary. As a Senior Financial Analyst, External Reporting, you will support the preparation and review of external reporting and regulatory filings while partnering across the organization to ensure accurate, timely, and compliant financial disclosures. You will contribute to key reporting processes, financial analyses, audit support activities, and continuous improvement initiatives that enhance reporting quality and efficiency. Primary Responsibilities Prepare and support external reporting activities, including annual and quarterly SEC filings and benefit plan financial reporting. Develop and review financial statements, footnote disclosures, and supporting documentation to ensure compliance with reporting requirements. Support quarterly earnings and other investor materials. Partner with Global Controllership, Global Finance, Investor Relations, Legal, Treasury, Tax, Compensation & Benefits, and business finance teams to gather, validate, and analyze financial information. Support quarterly and annual audit and review activities, serving as a liaison with external auditors and internal stakeholders. Research and evaluate accounting, disclosure, and reporting requirements and assess their impact on financial reporting processes. Prepare analyses and presentations for internal and executive leadership reviews. Lead or support special projects involving benchmarking, complex financial analysis, and implementation of new reporting requirements or tools. Drive continuous improvement initiatives to strengthen reporting processes, controls, policy management, standardization, and overall reporting quality. Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree and 4+ years of accounting or finance experience OR advanced degree and 2+ years of accounting or finance experience. Knowledge of U.S. Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP) and financial controls. Proficiency with Microsoft Excel, Word, and PowerPoint. Preferred Qualifications Public accounting, corporate, or industry accounting experience. Experience preparing, supporting, or reviewing financial reporting and disclosure. Strong written communication skills, attention to detail, and the ability to manage multiple deadlines. Certified Public Accountant (CPA) designation. Experience with Workiva, OneStream, or other financial reporting or consolidation systems. For Baccalaureate degrees earned outside of the United States, a degree that satisfies the requirements of 8 C.F.R. § 214.2(h)(4)(iii)(A) is required.

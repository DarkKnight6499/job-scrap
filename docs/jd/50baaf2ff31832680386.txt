Location Designation: Hybrid - 3 days per week Senior Associate – Controllers Optimization & Reinvention Engine (CORE) Program Coordination (Hybrid) Location Designation: Hybrid - 3 days per week Job Location: New York, New York, United States What You'll Do: · Coordinate day-to-day execution across CORE's process automation and reporting workstreams, partnering with the technical lead to keep builds on track and exceptions resolved. · Own timeline tracking, stakeholder communication, and status reporting from workflow-level detail up through CORE leadership and executive updates. · Maintain a running log of risks, open items, and decisions across workstreams, ensuring dependencies and ownership stay visible. · Consolidate and prepare testing evidence, documentation, and change-management artifacts ahead of go-live milestones. · Maintain documentation and onboarding materials to support continuity across the program. · Own recurring meeting cadence across CORE workstreams, including agendas, follow-ups, and action item tracking. What You'll Bring: Education · Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, Business, Information Systems, or a related discipline required. · CPA, PMP, or other relevant professional certifications are a plus. Experience · 2-4 years of experience in program or project coordination within a finance, accounting operations, or transformation function. · Demonstrated success supporting process automation, technology rollout, or transformation initiatives; coordinating timelines, stakeholders, and deliverables rather than building the technical solution directly. · Exposure to journal entry, account reconciliation, audit readiness, change management documentation or managerial reporting processes is a plus. · Candidates should have experience operating within a mature corporate finance function supporting cross-functional stakeholders and multiple concurrent workstreams. Required Technical Skills · Strong proficiency with Microsoft 365, including Excel, SharePoint, Power Platform, Word, and PowerPoint. · Familiarity with workflow or automation tooling sufficient to coordinate with technical builders, not required to build solutions independently. · Comfort tracking and interpreting data and reporting outputs to support status reporting. #LI-VL1 #LI-HYBRID Pay Transparency Salary Range: $81,000-$110,000 Overtime eligible: Exempt Discretionary bonus eligible: Yes Sales bonus eligible: No Actual base salary will be determined based on several factors but not limited to individual’s experience, skills, qualifications, and job location.

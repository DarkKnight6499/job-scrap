An opportunity awaits to join a motivated and energetic team providing HIMCO management and key stakeholders throughout The Hartford with detailed portfolio analysis, forecasting, and reporting support. The Senior Financial Analyst will report directly to the Director of Investment Finance and play a key role in supporting net investment income and capital forecasting, while contributing to a variety of HIMCO and Finance strategic initiatives. This position offers significant interaction with portfolio managers, FP&A partners, and colleagues across the Finance organization. This role will have a Hybrid work arrangement, with the expectation of working in the office (Hartford, CT) 3 days a week (Tuesday through Thursday). Primary responsibilities include the following: Support The Hartford's net investment income (NII) outlook process, including forecasting invested assets, investment income, and portfolio yields across the investment portfolio Partner with Treasury, Enterprise Risk Management, and other business areas to support regulatory and rating agency capital reporting, forecasting, and scenario analysis Provide detailed analysis and monitoring of NII & Capital results and develop improved variance analysis Provide valuable financial insights and management reporting, including the creation of presentations supporting Executive Leadership Team, quarterly business review, and other internal meetings Assist in the production of monthly key performance indicators utilized by HIMCO ELT Ability to react and respond to various ad hoc analyses related to the investment portfolio Drive continuous improvement through automation initiatives and enhanced analytical capabilities leveraging AI and other internal applications Requirements: The ideal candidate is a motivated and analytical professional with strong technical capabilities, intellectual curiosity, and a desire to deepen their understanding of investments, capital markets, and financial forecasting. Success in this role requires strong communication skills, attention to detail, and the ability to thrive in a collaborative environment.

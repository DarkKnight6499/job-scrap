The Credit Review Portfolio Risk Analyst I primarily administers the Abrigo DiCOM platform to support portfolio risk evaluation, risk-based loan sampling, borrower line sheet reviews, reporting, trend analysis, and the identification of emerging risks. The role also leverages approved AI tools to enhance efficiency, consistency, and quality of lending segment examinations. The position performs credit reviews across lending segments and contributes to the achievement of departmental objectives. This role develops credit analysis expertise through exposure to a broad range of borrower financial and business profiles. The Credit Review Portfolio Risk Analyst I demonstrates developing proficiency in assessing a borrower’s repayment capacity, evaluating the accuracy of assigned risk ratings, and reviewing the effectiveness of the Bank’s ongoing monitoring activities. The position collaborates with Credit Review Officers to perform detailed analysis and examination of basic to moderate credit relationships, with guidance from the Senior Credit Review Manager as needed and communicates results to management.

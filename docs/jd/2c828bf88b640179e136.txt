Job Description The Associate Director, Digital Process & Data Analysis (DPDA) provides strategic leadership for the organization's digital reporting, analytics, and process optimization capabilities. This role partners across business and technical teams to define priorities, lead the end-to-end delivery of reporting solutions, and drive the integration of purpose-built applications, analytics, and AI-enabled tools. The Associate Director champions data-driven decision-making, establishes governance and best practices, and fosters user adoption through stakeholder engagement, communications, and capability-building initiatives that maximize business value and operational effectiveness. Responsibilities: Solution delivery and product / data support: Drive clinical/operational reporting plans across products, roles, and geographies; implement standards and adoption tactics for purpose-built apps, self-service analytics, and other products in partnership with stakeholders. Engage with business stakeholders to understand and document their needs and help translate them into clear requirements and technical deliverables Lead delivery governance for key solutions—own requirements definition, data readiness coordination, development oversight, testing, release, and maintenance—to ensure scalable outcomes and strong user adoption. Produce executive-ready insights by consolidating trends, risks, and opportunities across portfolios; deliver clear narratives and visualizations that inform strategic decisions and prioritization. Run intake and prioritization process for ad hoc analytics; ensure timely, accurate responses and convert recurring requests into reusable assets or self-service capabilities. Coordinate participation in industry benchmarking; translate findings into practical performance targets and recommendations for process or investment adjustments. Align refresh schedules and data source management to business priorities; monitor usage and reliability Key Performance Indicators (KPIs); communicate changes and incidents broadly, coordinate cross-team resolution, and drive preventive improvements. Process, compliance, & capability building: Strengthen team capability and consistency: establish and enforce documentation and development standards, mentor specialists/senior specialists, and model effective stakeholder engagement. Review quality trends across teams; assign ownership for systemic issues with remediation timelines; ensure validation practices are embedded in delivery processes. Champion continuous improvement: evaluate emerging technologies and reporting methods, pilot high potential approaches, and institutionalize practices that reduce time-to-insight and increase impact. Oversee process documentation & completeness associated with archiving & decommissioning processes Own documentation governance for the domain, ensuring a single source of truth linked to release processes; audit adherence, resolve inconsistencies, and mandate updates after changes Oversee access policies, workspace structures, and permission models in partnership with technical owners. Technical skills: Business intelligence (BI) reporting and visualization tools: Experience in setting domain-wide standards for dashboard usability, consistency, and adoption across platforms (e.g., Qlik Sense, Power BI, ThoughtSpot, Tableau). Data literacy: Deep understanding of data literacy best practices, including defining storytelling guidelines, executive readout formats, and review cadences. Data preparation and quality checks: Understanding requirements to establish a governance model for light wrangling and validation including reconciliation patterns, tolerance thresholds, User Acceptance Testing (UAT) signoff criteria, and escalation paths. Spreadsheet and tabular proficiency: Understanding best practices related to standardization of templates, checklists, and controls for spreadsheet-based analysis and QA to reduce variability and error rates. Strong working knowledge of Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC) Advanced knowledge of Good Clinical Practice (GCP) and clinical trial operations Strong understanding of database concepts, data quality, lineage, and governance Understanding of best practices related to standing up technical enablement and change management programs.

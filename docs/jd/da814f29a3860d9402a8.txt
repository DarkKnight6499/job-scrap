The opportunity Our global network of Restructuring professionals helps clients develop financial and operational strategies to improve liquidity, credit availability and shareholder return. Our Restructuring team is fully integrated both within our wider EY-Parthenon practice and our firm as a whole, and our clients reap the benefits of this broad range of knowledge, skills and experience. We work on the basis of creating long-term relationships with our clients and our teams work closely with them to understand their short and long-term goals - being there at every stage to identify problems and recommend and implement solutions that deliver measurable results. Your key responsibilities As a Senior Associate/Consultant with EY-Parthenon's Strategy and Execution Restructuring practice, you will help clients evaluate capital options, capture greater transaction value and achieve strategic objectives - whether they are buying or selling a distressed asset, restructuring a business, dealing with underperformance, or looking to improve cash management. You'll work to prepare comprehensive financial reviews of company's operations, attend legal proceedings, engage in judicial management, support liquidation processes as well as advise clients on corporate risk management issues. Skills and attributes for success Project Management - lead project teams comprising colleagues from across the EY-Parthenon sub service lines. Business and Commercially Driven - working in a fast-paced, exciting environment with strong business acumen to drive value to our clients. Business Development - drive client pursuits, including presentations and client meetings. Contribute to our practice development initiatives, supporting the continued focus on our team as a great place to work. Learning – learn and develop technical and personal skills to support achievement of career goals, through a blend of structured learning, coaching and experiences. Building Relationships - strong working relationships with senior clients, including influence, advice and support to key decision makers. To qualify for the role, you must have A bachelor's degree and 2 years of related work experience; or a graduate degree and approximately 18 months of related work experience. Experience identifying data to prepare detailed complex financial analyses, including 13-week cash flow models, to understand the client’s liquidity positions, cash flow needs and financial viability. Experience in developing financial and operational strategies to affect a financial and/or operational turnaround to improve liquidity, credit availability and shareholder return. Strong financial modeling skills including the ability to create robust and flexible financial models based on client’s financial and operational information to drive key decisions and strategies. Able to work under high pressure and adapt to a dynamic working environment. Excellent problem solving, facilitation and interpersonal skills. Excellent command of spoken and written English language. The expectation for this role is in person teaming, at client site or in an EY office, a minimum of 60% of the time or on average 3 days a week over the course of an engagement, project or performance year. The ability and willingness to travel and work in excess of standard hours when necessary. Ideally, you'll also have Auditing/ corporate restructuring experience. Client facing experience from a professional services background. Knowledge of how to leverage AI tools in a business setting, including Microsoft Copilot. What we look for We're interested in talented professionals with the ability to visualize our clients' goals and think creatively to facilitate them - often in politically charged environments. If you have an entrepreneurial spirit, and a genuine passion for taking on new challenges, this role is for you.

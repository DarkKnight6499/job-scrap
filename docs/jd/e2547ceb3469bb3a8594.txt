Company Description Etsy is the global marketplace for unique and creative goods. We build, power, and evolve the tools and technologies that connect millions of entrepreneurs with millions of buyers around the world. As an Etsy employee, you will tackle unique, meaningful, and large-scale problems alongside passionate coworkers, all the while making a rewarding impact and Keeping Commerce Human. We believe exceptional companies are built by exceptional teams, and we're intentional about it. That means hiring great people, setting them up for success from day one, and giving them real reasons to grow their careers here. We invest in development that goes beyond promotions, and we foster the trust and relationships that help people do their best work together. What’s the role? We are looking for a Senior Market Researcher who can influence marketing decision-making by translating rigorous brand and performance marketing research into clear insights that sharpen channel execution and audience strategy. You’ll work closely with marketing teams and alongside other researchers and data scientists to deliver recommendations that will craft the future of human commerce on Etsy. This is a full-time position reporting to the Senior Manager, Market Research. What’s this team like at Etsy? The Market Research team helps Etsy understand customers, sellers, and the marketplace through rigorous qualitative and quantitative research We use research to inform the development of marketing strategy & tactics and to help evaluate marketing performance (in close partnership with Marketing Analytics) We’re a mixed-methods team with a deep bench of quantitative and qualitative methods, but our team leans towards quantitative skill sets We conduct research with global consumers, small business owners, as well as Etsy buyers & sellers We actively foster an inclusive team culture that promotes diversity, collaboration, and professional development What does the day-to-day look like? Partner with Brand Marketing and Performance Marketing teams to guide marketing strategy and execution. Independently develop and execute research for widely-scoped problem spaces, including writing briefs, developing survey instruments, analyzing data, generating insights and recommendations, writing reports, and socializing results. Own key aspects of our holistic brand measurement program and channel & audience strategy, including partnering with Analytics teams to design and maintain robust brand measurement frameworks. Own stakeholder relationships to share findings and prioritized recommendations to drive Etsy’s marketing and business goals Source and synthesize secondary research, including competitive intelligence. Contribute to market research operations, including project management and overseeing the process of working with third-party research vendors (e.g., research agencies, moderators). Of course, this is just a sample of the kinds of work this role will require! You should assume that your role will encompass other tasks, too, and that your job duties and responsibilities may change from time to time at Etsy's discretion, or otherwise applicable with local law. Qualities that will help you thrive in this role are: Approximately 5+ years experience conducting primary market research, ideally in a multi-channel marketing or ecommerce environment Expertise designing and executing quantitative and mixed-methods research, including brand tracking, audience strategy, channel measurement Strong quantitative research skills, including sampling, questionnaire design, and data analysis, with the ability to develop measurement frameworks, interpret complex data from primary research and third-party sources, identify meaningful trends, and translate findings into clear business implications Experience connecting brand health, channel performance, audience understanding, and business outcomes to development holistic measurement approaches and inform marketing strategy Comfort leveraging AI tools to accelerate research workflows and experience independently exploring new use cases– while maintaining human oversight and judgement of outputs Excellent data visualization skills, including experience with Google Slides, with the ability to translate complex data into clear narratives Strong stakeholder management and communication skills, A collaborative, curious, and pragmatic mindset and the ability to balance methodological rigor with speed and proactively identify opportunities for research and measurement to improve marketing decisions Compensation & Benefits In addition to salary, you'll be eligible for an equity package, an annual performance bonus, and our competitive benefits that support you and your family.

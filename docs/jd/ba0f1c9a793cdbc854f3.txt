Provide oversight into the development of executive level BI dashboards. Accountabilities New Product / Business Second Line Oversight • Provide oversight to the new initiatives across GBM, including new products, services, business activities, and material changes to existing offerings. • Facilitate cross-functional review and challenge from holistic second line of defense perspective working with business lines, other risk teams, compliance, legal, finance, operations, and other control functions to assess risk, control readiness, and implementation considerations. • Prepare clear, concise materials and executive reporting to support senior management review, governance committee discussions, approvals, and escalation of key risks or issues. • Track initiative status, approval milestones, conditions, post-implementation review requirements, and remediation actions to promote timely execution and adherence to governance standards. • Maintain alignment with enterprise policies, risk appetite, regulatory expectations, and internal control requirements throughout the new initiative lifecycle. BI Intelligence • Oversee development of executive-level dashboards and report rationalization efforts across GBM by partnering with senior leadership to enhance reporting frameworks, improve data quality, and establish meaningful metrics that support effective oversight and informed decision-making. • Identify opportunities to streamline governance and reporting processes through automation, data analytics, and continuous improvement initiatives. • Synthesize complex risk and business information into clear, actionable reporting for senior executives, risk committees, and board-level stakeholders. • Prepare presentations, talking points, and strategic insights for executive forums, highlighting key trends, emerging risks, and decision items requiring management attention. General • Lead and drive a customer focused culture to deepen client relationships and leverage broader Bank relationships, capabilities, systems, and knowledge. • Understand how the Bank’s risk appetite and risk culture should be considered in day-to-day activities and decisions. • Create an environment in which their team pursues effective and efficient operations of his/her respective areas in accordance with Scotiabank’s Values, its Code of Conduct, and the Global Sales Principles, while ensuring the adequacy, adherence to and effectiveness of day-to-day business controls to meet obligations with respect to operational, compliance, AML/ATF/sanctions, and conduct risk. • Build a high-performance environment and implement a people strategy that attracts, retains, develops, and motivates their team by fostering an inclusive work environment and using a coaching mindset and behaviors; communicating vison/values/business strategy; and managing succession and development planning for the team Dimensions • Lead and implement change initiatives that strengthen risk management, improve cross-functional collaboration, and enhance internal processes. • Develop forward-looking strategies to address emerging challenges and drive efficiency across the organization.

Key responsibilities UBS Business Solutions US LLC is seeking an Associate Director, Capital Management Specialist in Morrisville, NC. Are you an innovative thinker? Are you focused on the details, even when under pressure? Do you enjoy delivering enhanced change capabilities across a range of business functions? We’re looking for an Associate Director, Capital Management Specialist to: • Address, analyze and assess the capital impact of new business initiatives, complex trade approvals, and changes to existing businesses. Perform quantitative and qualitative analysis of key capital rules and guidelines. • Assist in the monitoring of limits, early warning indicators and targets, working with the businesses to assess underlying trends. Prepare the annual Capital Plan and report capital metrics. • Assist with episodic issues to ensure systems and processes accurately calculate capital metrics. Provide project analytics and process design and support successful completion of capital management projects. • Contribute to initiatives in partnership with company divisions to optimize regulatory capital by the business and legal entity. • Can work hybrid (In-office/remote). Your skills and experience Education & Experience Requirement: • This position requires a Bachelor’s degree or foreign equivalent in Finance, Business Administration, Accounting, or a related field of study plus 6 years of experience in the job offered or as a Financial Investment Analyst, Risk Advisor, Regulatory Reporting Specialist, or a related occupation. Alternate Education & Experience Requirement (if applicable): • The employer will alternatively accept a Master’s degree or foreign equivalent in Finance, Business Administration, Accounting, or a related field of study plus 4 years of experience in the job offered or as a Financial Investment Analyst, Risk Advisor, Regulatory Reporting Specialist, or a related occupation in lieu of a Bachelor’s degree or foreign equivalent plus 6 years of experience. Position requires experience with the following: • (1) Capital planning and management in the banking industry; (2) Basel III capital requirements; (3) Capital adequacy assessment processes including Basel III, Pillar 2, CCAR, and ICAAP; (4) US GAAP financial reporting; (5) Quantitative data analysis; (6) Regulatory reporting; (8) Stress testing; and (9) Credit risk analysis.

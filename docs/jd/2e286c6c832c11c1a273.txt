PIMCO is seeking a Product Associate to join the Alternative Credit & Private Strategies team. This position will play a key role in helping to build and support PIMCO’s $145bn+ suite of Alternative Credit & Private Strategies products, including multi-sector private lending, asset-based finance and commercial real estate by partnering with teams across the firm including Portfolio Management, Client Management, Legal & Compliance and other areas. The position will be based in Newport Beach.

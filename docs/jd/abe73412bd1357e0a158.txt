Role Description: Product Control is a key function within Nomura's Finance department. The team collaborates closely with trading desks, risk, and global colleagues to manage the balance sheet and P&L reporting for the firm. Product Control guides strategic decisions on financial resources by analyzing daily trading activity and explaining revenue and costs. Controllers develop strong working knowledge of the products they oversee and serve as a central point of contact to provide critical guidance that supports informed business decision-making across the organization. The department provides exposure to diverse financial products and trading strategies while working alongside experienced finance professionals. The team follows a hybrid work arrangement.

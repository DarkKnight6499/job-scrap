Marsh Risk Early Career Program, Full-Time 2027 – Brokerage – East Market We are seeking talented individuals to join our Marsh Early Career Program , part of our Risk business at Marsh. These roles will be based in Atlanta and Boston with an anticipated start in July 2027. These are hybrid roles that have a requirement of working at least three days a week in the office. Join Marsh's Early Career Program as an Associate Broker, a two-year career development opportunity for recent graduates where you will gain top industry training and access to dedicated development resources while working in one of our Marsh brokerage operations across the US and Canada. As part of our program's benefits, you will enjoy a structured cohort onboarding, learning and development opportunities, a vibrant community, mentorship programs, exposure to senior leaders, and valuable networking opportunities. Take the first step towards a successful career at Marsh. Within Marsh Risk, the Brokerage Practice is integral in helping clients manage and reduce risks in real-world scenarios like medical pandemics, music festival liabilities, and financial scandals. You'll collaborate with clients to assess risks, build relationships with insurance companies, and secure competitive insurance quotes using your negotiation skills, making a significant impact in helping clients minimize their overall risk costs in today's world. Aviation – Atlanta The Aviation team provides innovative insurance and risk management solutions customized to meet the needs and address the unique challenges of their clients in the ever-evolving risk landscape of the Aviation industry. Casualty – Atlanta The Casualty team provides support to clients by evaluating their potential liability for damages or losses incurred by other parties, assisting in identifying cost saving measures, and securing appropriate insurance coverage to mitigate these risks. Energy & Power – Boston The Energy & Power team offers innovative solutions to manage the risks of natural resource energy, renewable energy, power, and utility companies.

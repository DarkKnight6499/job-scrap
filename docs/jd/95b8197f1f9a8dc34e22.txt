Senior Financial Analyst – Enterprise Data Finance - 46092 - Bloomberg Senior Financial Analyst – Enterprise Data Finance Location New York Business Area Accounting and Finance Ref # 10054981 Description & Requirements About the Role We are seeking a highly motivated and experienced finance professional to join the Enterprise Data Finance team. This role will serve as a key finance resource supporting the Enterprise Data business, providing financial analysis, reporting, forecasting, and insights to support business performance and decision-making. The role requires strong financial acumen, analytical rigor, and the ability to independently manage complex work. The successful candidate will develop deep expertise in the Enterprise Data business and its financial and commercial drivers, partnering with Finance, Sales, and business stakeholders to translate complex data into meaningful insights. Key Responsibilities • Develop, maintain, and enhance financial, sales, and pipeline models, forecasts, and related analytical frameworks for the Enterprise Data business. • Perform complex financial and commercial analysis to identify key performance trends, drivers, risks, and opportunities. • Support quarterly financial updates, annual operating plans (AOP), forecasts, and other planning activities for Enterprise Data. • Develop quantitative financial models and scenario analyses to evaluate business performance and support decision-making. • Produce high-quality financial reporting, dashboards, and presentation materials that provide clear visibility into Enterprise Data performance. • Translate complex financial and business data into meaningful insights and recommendations for Finance and business stakeholders. • Establish, track, and analyze key metrics and KPIs to measure performance and identify emerging trends. • Partner with Enterprise Data Sales and other cross-functional stakeholders to understand business activity and assess its financial implications. • Develop deep subject-matter expertise across Enterprise Data products, financial performance, and commercial drivers, serving as a key finance resource for the business. • Identify and implement opportunities to enhance reporting processes, analytics capabilities, and forecasting models. • Independently manage complex assignments and ad hoc analyses, exercising judgment and accountability for the quality and accuracy of deliverables. Qualifications & Experience • Strong finance experience, preferably with a focus on sales finance, forecasting, financial planning, or business / data analytics. • Strong analytical skills with demonstrated ability to interpret complex financial and business data and develop actionable insights. • Minimum of 5+ years experience in FP&A with demonstrated hands-on experience in FP&A • Experience carrying out complex work with autonomy and exercising judgment within the scope of responsibilities. • Demonstrated experience in financial modeling, forecasting, variance analysis, and performance measurement. • Experience creating and tracking metrics, KPIs, and data analytics / dashboards. • Strong attention to detail with a commitment to accuracy and financial discipline. • Strong communication skills with the ability to translate detailed financial analysis into clear business insights. • Experience developing high-quality reporting and presentation materials for senior stakeholders. • Ability to build effective working relationships across Finance, Sales, and other cross-functional teams. Key Attributes • Highly analytical with a hands-on, execution-focused mindset. • Comfortable navigating ambiguity and developing structured approaches to complex problems. • Strong sense of ownership and accountability with the ability to operate independently. • Collaborative, with the ability to influence and partner effectively across functions. • Curious and motivated to develop deep expertise in the Enterprise Data business. • Passionate about leveraging financial analysis and data to improve business decision-making.

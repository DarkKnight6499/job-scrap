What you’ll do in the role: Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC is seeking an Associate, Banking in New York, New York to Provide financial advisory services related to mergers and acquisitions to various clients. Create transaction structures that address key concerns faced by the client. Coordinate various parties to ensure due diligence process utilizing proprietary Firm processes. Perform various financial and valuation analysis, including discounted cash flow (DCF), comparable companies, precedent transaction analysis, leveraged buyout (LBO) analysis, and merger modeling. Prepare marketing materials including Teaser, Confidential Investment Memorandum, and Management Presentation. Support senior stakeholders with investor outreach. Leverage experience with cross-border transactions. What you’ll bring to the role: -Requires a Bachelor’s in Economics, Finance, Business Administration, or a related field of study. -Requires two (2) years of experience in the position offered or two (2) years as an Analyst, or a closely related occupation. Requires two (2) years of experience with the following skills: Applying accounting principles to analyze income statements, balance sheets, cash flow statements, working capital, revenue trends, profitability, and leverage; Analyzing capital structure, cost of capital, financing alternatives, shareholder value creation, and transaction rationale; Performing valuation analyses using discounted cash flow, discounted equity value, public company trading comparables, precedent transaction, leveraged buyout, and sum-of-the-parts methodologies; Preparing merger models to assess pro forma ownership, accretion and dilution, purchase accounting adjustments, transaction synergies, financing assumptions, and impact on earnings, leverage, and credit metrics; Drafting confidential information memorandums, management presentations, and teasers; Using Excel formulas, data tables, sensitivity analysis, scenario analysis, financial model auditing, and data organization techniques; Preparing financial analysis, valuation summaries, transaction overviews, board materials, and presentations with PowerPoint; Using virtual data room platforms including Intralinks and Datasite; S&P Capital IQ, Bloomberg, Mergermarket, and Deal Point Data.

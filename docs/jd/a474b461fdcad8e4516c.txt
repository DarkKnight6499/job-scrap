The Difference You Make: The Manager, Third Party Risk - Vendor Management is responsible for the development, enhancement, and maintenance of ongoing relationships with third parties that the Company partners with. They oversee the resources responsible for the planning, scheduling, and communications of meetings, audits, information, and change requests received from partners or identified in existing agreements. Additionally, the manager role is responsible for overseeing the day-to-day workflow and resource allocation of the Vendor Management team. Provides input into the vision and direction of the department that will support the directives set by senior management. Maintains cohesion, training, and development of the Vendor Management program. Provides oversight for the Vendor Management team to ensure information is gathered and delivered accurately and timely. Ensures audits and meetings are organized and facilitated in accordance with the partnership agreement. Prepares regular status reports and presents reports to the partners. Develops and manages Vendor Management direct reports in line with corporate objectives. Maintains a comprehensive working knowledge of all tools that are available to each associate, continually coaching and training associates. Serves as a point of contact for Business Partners. Monitors and reports SLA’s defined in the partnerships. Coordinates with customers and vendors as needed.

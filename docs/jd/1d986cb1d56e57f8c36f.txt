THE ROLE MFS is seeking an experienced Equity Research Analyst to join our investment team with a focus on Real Estate Investment Trusts. This role is responsible for conducting deep fundamental research, developing differentiated investment insights, and making stock recommendations for MFS portfolios. The ideal candidate will bring strong experience on the buy side or sell side, a passion for real estate investing, with a strong understanding of real estate fundamentals and public equity valuation. WHAT YOU WILL DO Conduct in-depth fundamental research on publicly traded REITs. Develop and maintain detailed financial models, valuation frameworks, and investment theses. Analyze company financials, property portfolios, capital structures, management teams, competitive positioning, and sector trends. Meet regularly with company management teams, industry experts, analysts, and other market participants. Generate actionable stock recommendations for MFS portfolios, including clear buy, hold, and sell recommendations. Communicate investment views effectively to portfolio managers and broader investment teams through written research, presentations, and discussions. Monitor existing holdings and relevant industry developments, including earnings, transactions, macroeconomic trends, interest rates, and real estate market fundamentals. Collaborate with other analysts and portfolio managers to assess cross-sector implications and broader market opportunities. WHAT ARE WE LOOKING FOR 5+ years of equity research experience on the buy side or sell side, preferably with coverage of REITs, real estate, financials, or related sectors. Demonstrated passion for real estate investing and public equity markets. Strong financial modeling, valuation, accounting, and analytical skills. Ability to develop differentiated investment insights supported by rigorous research and data analysis. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to clearly explain complex investment ideas. Strong judgment, intellectual curiosity, and a disciplined approach to evaluating risk and reward. Ability to work collaboratively with portfolio managers and investment colleagues in a team-oriented environment. Undergraduate degree required; advanced degree in finance, business, real estate, economics, or a related field is a plus.

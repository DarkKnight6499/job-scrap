POSITION SUMMARY The Core Financial Analyst is an opportunity to move beyond recurring journal entries and reconciliations into work that reaches the most senior levels of the Firm. This unique project-based role combines accounting policy initiatives with the development of industry expertise in insurance and risk management, real estate and lease accounting, pension and 401(k) accounting and audits, and ad-hoc financial analysis. Rather than owning a single narrow process, you will develop a broad understanding of how a global firm's finances operate — including the general ledger and accounts payable functions — and present your work directly to the CFO and the Firm's Audit Committee. The role reports into the Controllers function, to the Vice President leading the Projects Team. You will work with colleagues within the department along with other business lines throughout the firm. We are looking for a candidate with a strong technical accounting foundation, sound analytical judgment, and the ambition to increase their expertise and responsibilities over time. WHY THIS ROLE STANDS OUT Early ownership: Manage external audits, prepare financial statements, and help shape accounting policy Exceptional breadth: Four distinct disciplines: benefit plans, real estate, insurance, and special projects, within a single role Senior-level visibility: Present directly to the CFO and the Firm's Audit Committee A platform for growth: Designed for high performers ready to build on their public accounting foundation PRINCIPAL RESPONSIBILITIES Employee Benefit Plan Audits Prepare pension and 401(k) plan financial statements Monitor (monthly) plan investment statements Manage the day-to-day relationship with the Firm's external auditors Assist in Company accounting for such plans from the plan sponsor perspective Maintain policies and procedures related to benefit plans Occupancy / Real Estate Accounting Support Occupancy Controllers in financial analysis and reporting Support Accounting Policy Manager on lease accounting models and updates Insurance Assist in managing the Firm’s Corporate Insurance Portfolio Prepare insurance submission materials, manage invoice submission for payments, respond to client insurance inquiries Participate in meetings with Insurance Brokers and Underwriters Assist in managing Insurance Strategy meeting and annual update with CFO Projects Lead ad-hoc financial analysis projects and prepare the accompanying presentation materials Assist Occupancy Controller on monthly financial statement preparation Assist in preparation of the semi-annual Accounting Firm Fee Presentation for Audit Committee Assessing accounting best practices for new and existing activities and transaction flows QUALIFICATIONS Experience and credentials CPA license strongly preferred, or currently sitting for the examinations with the hours required for licensure in your state 3 -

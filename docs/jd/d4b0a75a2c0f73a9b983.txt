Job Description Summary This role will support the selection, onboarding, and oversight of third-party asset managers, with a focus on structured assets and real estate, including RMBS, CMBS, CLOs, Public ABS, Private ABF, residential and commercial real estate whole loans, and other related strategies. In addition, the role will support the due diligence of individual assets and investment strategies, portfolio construction, quantitative asset modeling, and day-to-day portfolio management. Job Description Responsibilities Support the selection, diligence, onboarding, and ongoing oversight of third-party asset managers Support the due diligence of individual investment opportunities and new investment strategies, including underwriting, documentation review, and committee presentations Support the day-to-day management of the structured asset and real estate portfolios Support the development and implementation of investment and risk management strategies, portfolio optimizations, and portfolio construction Monitor market developments, conduct sector and collateral research, and assist in formulating investment views to support the management of the portfolio Assist in the development of quantitative modeling frameworks, portfolio management tools, and the onboarding and Bachelor’s degree in a quantitative discipline 1-2 years of relevant experience in fixed income investment analytics, insurance investments, or institutional asset management Experience in a

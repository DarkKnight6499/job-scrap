Description The Vice President – Securitized Products, Esoteric ABS is responsible for supporting the origination and execution of capital markets securitization transactions (Public, 144a and 4(a)(2)). Positioned between senior coverage officers and junior team members, this role plays a critical part in driving transaction success and supporting strategic initiatives across the desk. This role will work closely with senior leaders and junior talent to manage the full lifecycle of transactions—from pitch development and investor engagement to execution and closing. The role requires strong technical expertise, market awareness, and leadership capabilities, with the ability to operate independently while contributing to team-wide goals. Duties & Responsibilities: Lead and support execution of active Bookrunner roles, ensuring timelines and deliverables are met. Prepare marketing materials, coordinate investor meetings, and manage rating agency processes. Assist in structuring and closing capital markets securitization transactions (Public, 144a (Rule 144A) and 4(a)(2) (Section 4(a)(2))). Participate in client and investor meetings, presenting materials and contributing to strategic discussions. Support origination efforts through idea generation, market reads, and pitch development. Build and maintain relationships with institutional investors and corporate clients. Manage and mentor junior colleagues throughout the transaction process. Serve as a key liaison between senior bankers and junior staff, ensuring seamless execution and communication. Assist in recruiting and onboarding new team members. Monitor industry trends, market dynamics, and investor sentiment. Assist in preparing management reports on business performance and market developments. Maintain CRM (Customer Relationship Management) systems and databases to track deal pipelines and client interactions. Gain hands-on experience across a broad range of industries and capital needs. Develop corporate finance and valuation skills relevant to capital raising and market execution. Demonstrate ownership of work products and a commitment to continuous improvement. Performs other duties as assigned.

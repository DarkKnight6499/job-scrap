About This Job The main purpose of this job is to provide administrative and operational assistance to the trust administrator in managing trust accounts. Performs various operational functions within the trust department, with a focus on account set-up/transfer and asset reconciliations, transactional processing, record keeping, and tax and portfolio accounting functions. Processes daily transactions. Enhances the client experience by providing excellent operational support. Essential Functions Provide daily trust administration and investment support in a growth-oriented and team-focused and transformational environment Use trust account/custodian systems to process daily transactions, such as ACM sweeps, post cash receipts and disbursements, print checks and execute tax cost adjustments and transaction reversals Pay bills for clients and monitor accounts to avoid overdrafts Support the discretionary distribution process and process client gifts Assist in the timely and accurate set up of new accounts; input data and monitor the new account process, including but not limited to, processing and tracking free receipts of assets with accurate tax costs; scan documents into the imaging system Implement and monitor the account closing process to ensure timely account distribution.

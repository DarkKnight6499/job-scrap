Location: New York/Chicago, IL/Atlanta, GA Job Description Key Responsibilities Gather and document business requirements, workflows, business rules, and decision logic. Translate insurance processes into AI prompts, agent instructions, decision trees, FAQs, and knowledge assets . Partner with SMEs, Product Owners, AI Engineers, Data Scientists, and Architects to define AI use cases. Support AI solutions across Claims, Policy Administration, Enrollment, Billing, Customer Service, and Underwriting . Define user stories, acceptance criteria, and validate AI outputs against business requirements. Identify opportunities for process improvement and AI-enabled automation. Required Skills 7+ years of Business Analysis experience. 3+ years in Supplemental Insurance, Group Benefits, or Voluntary Benefits. Strong knowledge of Supplemental Health, Accident, Critical Illness, Disability, Hospital Indemnity, Claims, and Policy Administration . Experience with requirements gathering, process modeling, workflows, and business rules. Exposure to Generative AI, Agentic AI, Knowledge Management, or Intelligent Automation . Understanding of RAG, LLMs, Prompt Engineering, Knowledge Graphs, and AI Governance preferred. Experience with Majesco, Mainframe, or AS400 is a plus. Agile/SAFe experience preferred.

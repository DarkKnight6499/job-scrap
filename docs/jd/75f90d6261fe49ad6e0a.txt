Asset Liability Management Manager Department: Treasury Role Type: Manager Why This Role Matters The ALM Manager serves as a critical partner in managing the credit union's financial performance, balance sheet strategy, and risk profile. This role provides the analysis, forecasting, and insights that help leadership make informed decisions regarding interest rate risk, liquidity, investments, funding, and profitability. By ensuring the organization remains financially strong and well-positioned through changing economic environments, the ALM Manager directly supports our ability to serve members today and in the future. What You’ll Do Lead the administration and optimization of the Asset Liability Management (ALM) program and financial modeling platform. Perform interest rate risk analysis, net interest income forecasting, liquidity stress testing, and balance sheet sensitivity analysis. Prepare reports, forecasts, and financial analyses for ALCO, the Finance Committee, executive leadership, and regulatory reporting requirements. Manage the Funds Transfer Pricing (FTP) process, including rate curve development and profitability reporting. Monitor and administer investment, liquidity, borrowing, brokered CD, and loan participation portfolios. Oversee collateral management, government-guaranteed education loan portfolio reporting, and related compliance activities. Support derivative activities, collateral posting requirements, and treasury-related reporting. Coordinate audit, examination, and regulatory reporting activities. Provide leadership, process improvement, and operational support for Treasury functions. Why You’ll Love This Role Influence strategic decisions that shape the credit union's financial future. Work directly with senior leaders and decision-makers across the organization. Engage in sophisticated financial modeling, balance sheet management, and risk analytics. Enjoy a highly visible role with opportunities to drive meaningful business outcomes. Collaborate across multiple departments while developing broad treasury and finance expertise. Balance analytical work with strategic planning and organizational impact. Experience: Preferred Experience in treasury, finance, or balance sheet management within a financial institution. Asset/Liability Management (ALM) experience. Experience leading projects, processes, or team members. Background in liquidity management, interest rate risk analysis, investments, or financial forecasting. Skills & Knowledge: Required Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities. Financial modeling and quantitative analysis skills. Knowledge of interest rate risk, liquidity risk, and balance sheet management concepts. Ability to interpret data and translate findings into actionable business recommendations. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Proficiency with spreadsheets, databases, and financial systems. Strong attention to detail, organization, and project management skills. Ability to work independently while building effective cross-functional partnerships. Education: Required Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Mathematics, Accounting, Business, or a related field. Preferred Master's degree in Finance, Economics, Business Administration, Mathematics, or a related discipline.

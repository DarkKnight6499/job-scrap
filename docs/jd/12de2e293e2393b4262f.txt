Have a passion for helping your clients reduce risk and increase value? Deloitte’s Digital Assets practice combines the competencies of risk management, regulation, technology, and cryptography to help organizations address challenges in an integrated manner. Our Deloitte Regulatory, Risk & Forensic team helps client leaders translate multifaceted risk and an evolving regulatory environment into defensible actions that strengthen, protect, and transform their organizations. Join our team and use advanced data, AI, and emerging technologies with industry insights to help clients bring clarity from complexity and accelerate their path to value creation. Work you’ll do As a Senior Consultant in Digital Assets Strategy & Operating Model Design, you’ll be at the forefront of helping clients navigate the fast-evolving world of digital assets. You’ll collaborate with leading organizations to develop and refine enterprise-wide digital asset strategies, design innovative operating models, and build compelling business cases for new initiatives. Your work will blend risk management, technology, and strategic thinking to deliver integrated solutions that drive value and reduce risk. You’ll have the opportunity to work with cross-functional teams, tackle complex challenges, and make a tangible impact in shaping the future of finance and technology. This is a high visibility role and will drive cross functional collaboration. As a Senior Consultant, you will have opportunities to: Work with clients and engagement teams to develop and refine enterprise-wide digital asset strategies, including defining value propositions, identifying use cases, prioritizing roadmaps, and outlining investment requirements. Assess new and emerging digital asset technologies, and design mitigation strategies for related emerging risks. Stand up new business and finance capabilities to engage with the digital asset ecosystem, including designing target-state operating models that encompass organization structure, governance, processes, roles, skills, and performance measures. Build and validate business cases for digital asset initiatives, including financial modeling, risk assessment, and scenario analysis. Research, collaborate, and report on risks and mitigating factors associated with various digital asset ecosystems. Serve as a subject matter resource for supporting client engagement teams in business and operational model considerations as well as assessing risk of digital asset ecosystems, covering compliance, regulatory, BSA/AML, cyber, operational, blockchain, and financial risks. Identify strategic risks and opportunities in the digital asset ecosystem based on industry insights, latest academic research, and exchange with colleagues. Educate clients and internal teams on digital assets and operating model leading practices. Prepare technical documentation and diagrams for digital assets, contributing to guidance on private key management, smart contract platforms, multi-signature/MPC, privacy coins, protocol governance, proof-of-work, and proof-of-stake consensus models. Provide risk and regulatory advisory support in defining and reviewing business models. Design and deploy business and finance process changes and risk-mitigating controls to enable digital-asset-specific capabilities. Facilitate use of technology-based tools or methodologies to analyze public blockchain data. Demonstrate deep knowledge of client’s business environments, risk management approaches, market trends, competitor activities, and Deloitte’s products and service lines.

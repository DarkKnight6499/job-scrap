About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking an Associate Executive Specialist to provide administrative support to the Southwest Division Executive in Commercial Banking by coordinating activities, preparing business materials, and supporting administrative deliverables. You will organize information and follow-up activities, handle confidential information, and work with others to support leader priorities and team commitments. In this role, you will: Support executives with calendar management, meeting coordination, travel arrangements, calls, correspondence, expenses, and related administrative activities Prepare presentations, reports, meeting materials, and communications by gathering and organizing information from multiple sources Coordinate administrative deliverables and follow-up activities that support leader priorities and team commitments Handle confidential information in accordance with applicable policies, procedures, and compliance requirements Work within manager guidance while developing knowledge of the supported function and its priorities Collaborate with team members, peers, and internal or external contacts to resolve routine administrative issues and support business goals Required Qualifications: 6+ months of executive administration experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education Desired Qualifications: Experience supporting calendars, meetings, records, correspondence, supplies, travel, or other routine administrative activities Skill in preparing reports, correspondence, meeting materials, or similar business documents Experience following established policies, procedures, risk requirements, or controls Ability to organize and interpret information to support business activities and decisions Experience coordinating projects or activities and working with stakeholders to support business needs Experience as a ticket administrator in Ticket Manager System and manage travel in Concur Experience with Commercial Banking MESM budgets is preferred Ability to track deliverables, manage follow-up activities, and support successful execution of business priorities and projects. Strong organizational skills with exceptional attention to detail and follow-through Proven ability to exercise sound judgment, maintain confidentiality, and work independently. Strong organizational and time-management skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment. Experience coordinating leadership meetings, organizational events, offsites, and stakeholder engagement activities. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Outlook, Excel and PowerPoint; experience using AI automation tools such as Copilot Job Expectations: This position offers a hybrid work schedule from our 2030 Main Street Irvine, Ca location.

Job Description: Job Title Global Portfolio Manager Collaborate with Innovative 3Mers Around the World Choosing where to start and grow your career has a major impact on your professional and personal life, so it’s equally important you know that the company that you choose to work at, and its leaders, will support and guide you. With a wide variety of people, global locations, technologies and products, 3M is a place where you can collaborate with other curious, creative 3Mers. This position provides an opportunity to transition from other private, public, government or military experience to a 3M career. The Impact You’ll Make in this Role As a Global Portfolio Manager for the Home Environment Markets portfolio, you will have the opportunity to tap into your curiosity and collaborate with some of the most innovative people around the world. The person hired for this position will serve as a Global Product Marketer and Strategist for the Home Environment Markets business, including responsibility across segments of our category leading Filtrete™ brand and more. This position will provide global portfolio direction for both existing and new products. Here, you will make an impact by: Collaborating with cross-functional and Area teams to achieve global sales and gross margin operating plan targets for assigned product lines Leading portfolio and brand initiatives to drive the Filtrete(TM) brand consumer KPIs (growth, mix, household penetration, share, buy rate), including cross-functional team leadership to implement impactful programs. Drive New Product Introduction pipeline and select projects, partnering with Insights, R&D, Finance, and Marketing Communications team members to develop all deliverables needed for the Gate process and define a strong business case for the programs. Contributing to global strategic and marketing planning processes Researching, interpreting, and reviewing consumer insights and market information for trends and opportunities to influence portfolio plans and programs Overseeing product line pricing strategy and portfolio lifecycle management Your Skills and Expertise To set you up for success in this role from day one, 3M requires (at a minimum) the following qualifications: Bachelor’s degree or higher (completed and verified prior to start) Nine (9) combined years of experience in Marketing, New Product Commercialization, Sales, and/or Business in a private, public, government, or military environment Nine (9) years’ experience working with cross-functional team in a private, public, government, or military environment. Additional qualifications that could help you succeed even further in this role include: MBA or graduate degree from an accredited university, with focus on marketing, strategy, business development, and/or innovation Previous experience working in the consumer-packaged goods (CPG) industry Previous experience working in the HVAC, air filtration, air care, or home environment product industries Previous experience leading innovation projects from insight to launch. Knowledge or experience in mass retail, online, and/or other consumer channels Knowledge and interest in consumer behaviors and emerging consumer trends Demonstrated success leading projects and cross functional teams to create winning solutions Strong analytical skills and ability to interpret various data and drive to decisions Excellent presentation and communication skills Knowledge and experience with the theories and practices of one or more disciplines such as: market research, channel marketing, business/market development, branding, new product development, marketing communications or sales.

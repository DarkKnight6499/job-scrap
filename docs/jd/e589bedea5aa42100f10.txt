The Impact you will have in this role: As a core member of the Data Management Team within Enterprise Data Solutions, a Senior Associate will be responsible for driving projects forward, managing key deliverables, and helping to drive alignment with key stakeholders. Data Management is a high-impact, fast paced team focused on strategic data solutions, supporting data-driven transformation and modernization efforts, and establishing, maturing, and driving the implementation of industry-leading data management practices for the enterprise. The ideal candidate for this position is a self-starter with strong analytical skills and a demonstrated ability to prioritize, own and drive projects, and quickly learn new concepts and frameworks. Success in this role demands the ability to work in a fast-paced environment and adapt to shifting priorities. You will provide critical support for the management of Data Domains aligned to the team, including support of enterprise-wide Data Management initiatives. You will need to work effectively within an agile framework as part of a cross functional team that includes Product Managers, Product Owners, Business Architects, Operations and Information Technology Subject Matter Experts, Data Architects, Data Modelers, Enterprise Test Engineers, Data Governance and Data Quality Leads, IT Business System Analysts, and other Data Management Analysts. You should also be able to work independently with the appropriate initiative-taking and problem-solving, even in ambiguous situations. This role requires the ability to access, profile and analyze data, understand and document business requirements, and communicate clearly and concisely with a broad range of technical and business stakeholders. Your primary responsibilities: Project & Delivery Ownership Own and manage multiple data initiatives across the full lifecycle from engagement and requirements discovery through execution and close‑out ensuring outcomes remain aligned with business objectives. Support project planning activities including scope definition, effort estimation, dependency management, and delivery tracking. Define, track, and report KPIs and delivery metrics aligned to business outcomes and use cases. Identify, track, and manage data‑related issues and risks, ensuring timely escalation, remediation planning, and clear stakeholder communication. Business Analysis & Stakeholder Collaboration Partner closely with Business, Operations, Product Owners, and Technology/IT stakeholders to elicit, analyze, and document data requirements for business processes, including downstream consuming use cases. Data Management & Stewardship Perform preliminary data analysis to assess data availability within DTCC, identify Authoritative Data Sources (ADS), and support onboarding approaches for new or enhanced data. Proficient in using Excel and SQL to conduct targeted data analysis, profiling, validation, lineage review, and issue investigation to support requirements definition, impact analysis, and informed decision‑making.

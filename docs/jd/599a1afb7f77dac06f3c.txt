COMPANY OVERVIEW KKR is a leading global investment firm that offers alternative asset management as well as capital markets and insurance solutions. KKR aims to generate attractive investment returns by following a patient and disciplined investment approach, employing world-class people, and supporting growth in its portfolio companies and communities. KKR sponsors investment funds that invest in private equity, credit and real assets and has strategic partners that manage hedge funds. KKR’s insurance subsidiaries offer retirement, life and reinsurance products under the management of Global Atlantic Financial Group. References to KKR’s investments may include the activities of its sponsored funds and insurance subsidiaries. K-STAR INVESTMENT SERVICES OVERVIEW K-Star Asset Management LLC ("K-Star") was formed in 2022 and is a wholly owned subsidiary of KKR. K- Star Investment Services supports the risk management of its Asset-Based Finance investments, asset management, securities investing, and client experience for its Real Estate Credit franchise as both verticals continue to scale. The K-Star team includes industry specialists to oversee residential mortgage investments, consumer finance investments, special servicing, stabilized and transitional loan asset management across many KKR investment portfolios. POSITION SUMMARY K-Star is seeking an Associate to join its ABF Investment Management Services Team focusing on Asset-Based Finance investments. This is an excellent opportunity for someone who wishes to build a career in investment services at a reputable buy-side firm. The role is based in Dallas and collaborates regularly with the deal team at KKR. The ideal candidate will have 3-5 years of relevant experience at a financial institution with exposure to mortgage and/or consumer data. The position will support the Asset-Based Finance desk and will work directly with the investment team to assist with data analysis, transaction settlements, and management related to its existing $30bn portfolio. The role requires constant communication with the deal team and various stakeholders and business groups, so strong communication and relationship-building skills are essential. We are passionate about operational excellence and providing our investment and business operations colleagues the ability to seamlessly execute liquid and private credit investments around the globe. We are committed to continuous improvement, both in the operations we perform and in the environment in which we work. We work in collaborative teams within the context and support of a large, global investment firm. We can offer challenging and rewarding work and opportunities for career advancement and learning in both the business and operational spheres.

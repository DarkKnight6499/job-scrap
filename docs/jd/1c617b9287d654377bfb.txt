Brief Description The position of Senior Associate performs value-add audits under the leadership of the Americas internal audit team. The Senior Associate is a key part of the Global Assurance & Advisory Services team to ensure we maintain a leading-practice program and continue to embed sustainable processes and controls within the Company’s culture. The ideal candidate will have 4+ years of audit and internal controls experience, the ability to work with a team of 5-8 people to deliver value-add audit and consulting services, effective presentation, communication, and problem-solving skills, and will be comfortable in dealing with ambiguity in a fast-paced and changing environment. Principal Responsibilities Execute a broad range of audit and compliance initiatives with guidance from Americas management team. Serve as the auditor-in-charge to lead audit projects. Execute key planning, fieldwork, and reporting of stages of engagements while overseeing the work of assigned staff Perform thorough reviews to analyze the adequacy of internal controls, compliance with corporate standards and other policies, regulatory provisions, etc. Leverage data analytics and collaborate with other functions of Assurance & Advisory Services including IT Audit, SOX Compliance, and the global teams during audit projects. Prepare well-organized, concise, and accurate working papers. Analyze evidence, reach conclusions, and suggest recommendations to mitigate identified risks. Validate audit findings with business and functional personnel for inclusion in the final audit report. Draft clear, concise, and well-supported audit reports in a timely manner. Develop and maintain strong technical skills in accounting, audit, information technology and other areas. Support ad-hoc activities and projects as needed. Professional Skills Maintain and exhibit the highest standards of ethics, professionalism, integrity, and discipline.Experience in fundamentals of internal audit and internal controls.Collaboration and interpersonal skills required to work effectively across regions, business units, and functions and within the audit teamCommitment to work effectively with business stakeholders and continually improve client satisfaction.High attention to detail.Exhibit a continuous improvement mindset.Strong written and verbal communication skills, including documentation of findings and recommendations.Effective listening, communication, and time management skills.Ability to handle highly confidential information in a strictly professional manner.Critical thinking, analytical, and financial analysis skills. Required Skills Bachelor’s degree in accounting or finance, or equivalent, required. Advanced skills in MS Excel and Word.Knowledge and experience working with Oracle R12, preferred. Demonstrated ability to build a positive rapport with team members.

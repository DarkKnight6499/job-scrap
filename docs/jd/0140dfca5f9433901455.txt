Job Description: TD has embarked on a multi-year digital transformation and expansion of GTB’s product suite to position the transaction bank as a leading North American cash management provider, enabling significant business growth across the franchise. The centralized Product Management organization will serve a critical role in TD’s ability to develop leading products and capabilities by enabling greater scale, capacity, quality and efficiency for our Transaction Banking businesses across Canada and US. It will deliver product strategy, product development, and go-to-market approaches for the entire Transaction Banking business. Product-related decisions will be made once, implemented once, and leveraged by all three businesses. Role Summary The Vice President, Liquidity Product Management, is a critical role responsible for defining and executing the strategic roadmap for the Product Management’s liquidity product suite, including virtual accounts, cash concentration, notional pooling, and investment services. The Vice President drives product strategy, market positioning, commercial growth, and governance while leading cross-functional initiatives and influencing senior stakeholders. Key Responsibilities Strategic Leadership & Product Vision Own the strategic roadmap for the Product Management's liquidity product suite, balancing client needs, regulatory demands, operational capabilities, and revenue objectives. Drive multi-year product strategy across markets and our three in-scope business segments (Canadian and US Commercial Banking and TD Securities), with a focus on competitive differentiation and scalable innovation. Champion strategic initiatives at senior forums, influencing executive stakeholders and steering cross-functional decision-making. End-to-End Product Ownership Lead the full product lifecycle across ideation, design, development prioritization, commercialization, and ongoing optimization. Oversee investment planning, business cases, and budget alignment for product enhancements and new builds. Ensure the delivery of complex product initiatives, partnering across technology, operations globally. Market Research & Opportunity Development Conduct and direct advanced market research to identify new growth opportunities and emerging trends in liquidity and treasury management. Evaluate strategic partnerships, industry shifts, and regulatory changes that impact the product landscape. Commercialization & Performance Management Drive commercialization strategy in partnership with sales and relationship teams to accelerate adoption and deepen wallet share. Establish and track product-level KPIs, financial performance, and client satisfaction metrics; lead remediation or enhancement programs when necessary. Own pricing strategy and profitability management for the liquidity product portfolio. Subject Matter Expertise & Governance Serve as a senior SME across liquidity solutions including virtual accounts, notional pooling, cash concentration, sweep structures, and intraday liquidity. Ensure rigorous product governance, regulatory adherence, and risk management across all offerings. Lead audit and regulatory engagement relating to the liquidity product suite. Leadership & Enablement Mentor and guide junior product managers, promoting best practices in product discipline, analysis, and stakeholder management. Lead internal and external product education efforts, including executive-level presentations and thought leadership.

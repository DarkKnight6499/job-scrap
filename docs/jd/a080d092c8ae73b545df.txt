Job Summary This Lead Financial Analyst position is responsible for providing financial support for regulated jurisdictional forecasting in the Carolinas. This position will have specific job responsibilities assigned and will receive direction from the Director of Carolinas Forecasting & Planning. Key areas of focus will include, but not be limited to, forecasting capital, wholesale, and fuel along with insightful analytics and management reporting. Key interface groups will include Rates & Regulatory Planning, Wholesale Power Sales, Accounting, FP&A, and Resource Planning. Responsibilities Modeling portions of the 5-year financial forecast for the Carolinas based on inputs provided by various stakeholder groups. Primary areas of responsibility include, but are not limited to, wholesale, fuel, and capital. Performing analytics to validate modeling results, compiling Senior Management and Board presentation materials, and ad hoc reporting/data requests. Managing capital inputs including AFUDC, depreciation expense, recoverability and cash flow impacts. Requires partnering with FP&A functional support teams to understand capital needs to align with operational plans. Partnering with Rates & Regulatory Strategy department to develop and model strategic regulatory assumptions Analyzing and communicating key business drivers of financial statements including impacts on return on equity, capitalization structure, credit metrics, etc. Providing monthly updates on current year projected earnings to FP&A leadership and preparing presentation materials for State Presidents Supporting accounting department on monthly actual versus budget analyses Assisting in strategic planning scenarios to evaluate earnings per share and cash flow impacts Working collaboratively with business partners; coordinating with numerous groups across finance and the business to build assumptions in creating the jurisdictional financial and operational plan Required/Basic Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Accounting/Finance/Economics or similar discipline with prior work experience in a Finance organization Must have 5+ years (8+ desired) of increasingly diverse or complex experience in related field in addition to degree.

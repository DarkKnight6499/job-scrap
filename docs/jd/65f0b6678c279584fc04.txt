Invesco employees get more out of life through our comprehensive compensation and benefit offerings including: Flexible paid time off Hybrid work schedule 401(K) matching of 100% up to the first 6% with a discretionary supplemental contribution Health & wellbeing benefits Parental Leave benefits Employee stock purchase plan Job Description About the Role: As a Sr. Business Analyst within the Intelligent Automation team, you will support the analysis, documentation, and delivery of business requirements for automation initiatives across the organization. This role will partner with business stakeholders, process owners, and technology teams to understand current-state processes, identify improvement opportunities, and translate business needs into clear, actionable requirements. The Sr. Business Analyst will contribute to the full requirements lifecycle, including elicitation, documentation, validation, prioritization, and change support. This role requires strong analytical, communication, and process improvement skills, with a working understanding of intelligent automation concepts and technologies. The position will help ensure automation solutions are aligned with business objectives, operational needs, and governance standards while supporting the successful adoption of automation capabilities. Responsibilities of the Role: Gather, analyze, and document business requirements for intelligent automation initiatives. Collaborate with business stakeholders, process owners, and technical teams to understand automation opportunities and define clear requirements. Support the requirements life cycle, including requirements gathering, validation, prioritization, traceability, and change management. Analyze and map current-state business processes and assist in defining future-state processes optimized for automation. Facilitate working sessions, interviews, and requirements discussions with business and technology stakeholders. Partner with solution architects, developers, automation engineers, and QA teams to ensure business requirements are accurately understood and translated into solution designs. Develop process flows, user stories, acceptance criteria, business rules, and functional documentation to support automation delivery. Assist in preparing business cases, impact assessments, and cost-benefit inputs to support automation project prioritization. Support testing activities by helping define test scenarios, validate outcomes, and coordinate business user feedback. Help ensure automation initiatives are aligned with organizational goals, compliance requirements, and governance frameworks. Monitor implemented automation solutions and gather stakeholder feedback to support continuous improvement. Stay informed on business analysis practices, process improvement methods, and intelligent automation technologies. Support change management activities, including communication materials, training documentation, and user readiness content.

Job Description: Opportunity in the Product Risk Execution team, within the Global Payments Solutions (GPS) organization. The Product Risk Execution organization reflects our commitment to elevating risk management to become a strategic function and enabler of Responsible Growth. This team develops best practices, streamlines processes, and enables execution of risk activities by supporting product managers with expertise and specialization on all aspects of product risk management. Product managers remain responsible for risk in their product, and product executives retain responsibility for their governance routines. Responsible for driving risk deliverables across the GPS Product organization, with a focus on Merchant Services, Global Receivables, and Global Cash Products. This role executes risk activities with GPS Product Managers and key stakeholders to translate enterprise risk requirements into actionable Product deliverables and drive execution through completion. The individual will proactively manage assigned deliverables, identify gaps and dependencies, coordinate across stakeholders, and escalate execution risks and barriers as appropriate. Key Responsibilitie s • Execute Product Risk requirements within established timelines. • Manage/lead assigned risk activities, including those related to third parties, applications, and data, identifying Product requirements, dependencies, and actions needed for completion. • Engage and influence across Product, Technology, Business Controls, Risk, and other stakeholders to resolve dependencies and execution barriers. • Translate enterprise risk requirements into clear, actionable Product deliverables aligned with GPS products and business processes. • Proactively track deliverables, identify gaps, coordinate resolution, and escalate risks when appropriate. • Identify recurring execution challenges and implement repeatable processes, tools, and reporting to improve consistency, transparency, and execution across GPS Product teams.

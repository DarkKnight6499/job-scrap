Cash Portfolio Manager Investment Management Boston, MA The Opportunity MassMutual is seeking a highly motivated, solution-oriented investment professional to join MassMutual Investment Management’s Fixed Income Portfolio Management team. In this newly created role, you will optimize cash in portfolios for MassMutual’s $250 billion General Investment Account (GIA) by partnering with internal investment teams and outside investment managers to fund investments, reduce cash drag, minimize overdrafts and keep portfolio cash percentages within predefined targets. The new function will operate in the Aladdin platform. The role will have front and middle office functions as part of the daily responsibilities. The Team The Fixed Income Portfolio Management (FIPM) team is responsible for asset allocation, fundamental credit underwriting and monitoring, ESG investment strategy, and macroeconomic research. FIPM is a small, highly collaborative team that works with partners across Investment Management to manage the product segment portfolios within the GIA. The team combines fundamental investment analysis and asset allocation skills with quantitative and asset/liability management expertise. This high-profile group works across Investment Management and with internal stakeholders in product, actuarial, treasury, accounting, risk, legal, compliance, and other areas.

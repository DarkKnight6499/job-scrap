ABOUT THE ROLE The Associate Production Director serves as the primary technical lead and "Director" and technical lead for our studio productions. This is a high-ownership leadership role, designed for a broadcast professional who commands the control room environment. Beyond technical execution, they are responsible for the creative integrity and visual / audio presentation of the livestream, technical execution, the professional development of the crew, and the seamless integration of technology and talent. The role shifts focus from equipment operation to the total orchestration of the production environment, ensuring a world-class experience for both the talent and the members.

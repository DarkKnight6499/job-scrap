The following position is to join our Enterprise Services Team: The Director, Corporate Real Estate Portfolio is responsible for the strategic oversight, planning, and performance management of RTX's global real estate portfolio, encompassing 1.4 million square feet across multiple countries and states, and supporting over 5,000 employees. The position involves driving portfolio strategy, aligning real estate initiatives with business needs, overseeing project delivery, and supporting enterprise-wide capital and space planning efforts. This is an exciting opportunity for a strong strategist with the ability to simplify complexity, influence senior leadership, and ensure the real estate portfolio effectively supports operational, financial, and workforce objectives. What You Will Do Lead global portfolio planning and ensure alignment with business growth, workforce needs, and financial targets. Monitor utilization, capacity, lease events, cost exposure, and key risks. Provide strategic recommendations to senior leadership based on data and business forecasts. Oversee project delivery for corporate portfolio initiatives, ensuring high-quality execution. Develop project programs including schedules, budgets, and charters to support approvals. Guide new project engagements through initiation, planning/funding, and execution using the Variable Project Management (VPM) model. Ensure compliance with corporate governance policies across all project activities. Oversee accurate and timely reporting for business units and Facilities Project Delivery. Identify project risks, lead constructability reviews, and develop mitigation plans. Lead portfolio reporting, forecasting, and scenario analysis. Manage KPIs including utilization, cost performance, and long-range planning metrics. Ensure data accuracy across systems and organizations. Present clear portfolio updates, insights, and business cases to senior leadership. Drive improvements in portfolio efficiency, cost performance, and project delivery. Standardize portfolio governance and project delivery practices. Leverage data, technology, and automation to modernize real estate operations. Develop and manage capital plans and operating budgets for the corporate portfolio. Assess financial impacts of portfolio decisions using modeling and benchmarking.

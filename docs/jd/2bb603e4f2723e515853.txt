Hybrid environment 3 days in office, 2 days remote Purpose Working closely with business leaders, technology partners, and risk management teams, the Business Analyst supports the development, maintenance, and continuous improvement of the organization’s Business Continuity (BC), Crisis Management (CM), and Disaster Recovery (DR) programs, as well as the coordination of related activities in partnership with the Enterprise Risk Management program. As an effective communicator, this role partners with stakeholders across the organization to translate risk and resilience requirements into actionable plans, processes, and reporting. The position is responsible for coordinating program activities, facilitating assessments and exercises, maintaining governance documentation, and supporting organizational preparedness and response capabilities. This role exercises independent judgment in managing assigned work, identifying opportunities for improvement, and contributing to the ongoing maturity of enterprise resilience programs. Primary Duties & Responsibilities The following statements describe the general nature of work performed by people assigned to this job, may not be performed by all individuals in this job, and are not a complete list of all job duties required. Program Coordination & Reporting Coordinates program governance activities, including schedules, milestones, documentation repositories, issue tracking, and reporting deliverables. Develops and maintains reports, metrics, dashboards, and management summaries related to program readiness, plan completion, testing results, and remediation activities. Supports regulatory, audit, customer, and internal review requests by gathering documentation, preparing responses, and tracking follow-up actions. Identifies opportunities to improve BC/CM/DR processes, templates, reporting, and supporting tools. Analyzes program data and trends to assist management in assessing program effectiveness and readiness. Business Continuity & Crisis Management Coordinates the development, maintenance, and periodic review of Business Continuity Plans (BCPs) for critical business processes. Partners with business areas to identify critical activities, recovery time objectives (RTOs), recovery point objectives (RPOs), recovery requirements, and key dependencies while providing guidance on continuity planning standards and documentation requirements. Reviews continuity plans with stakeholders, asking clarifying questions to gain an understanding of business processes, recovery requirements, and critical dependencies, and to identify gaps or opportunities for improvement. Facilitates Business Impact Analysis (BIA) activities and coordinates updates to continuity documentation on a defined schedule. Coordinates and participates in crisis management exercises, tabletop scenarios and related preparedness activities. Tracks findings, action items, risks, and remediation efforts resulting from exercises, incidents, audits, and assessments. Monitors changes in organizational structure, business processes, policies, and industry practices to ensure continuity documentation remains current and effective. Disaster Recovery Coordinates with Technology / IS teams to align business continuity requirements with technology disaster recovery strategies and recovery plans. Participates in disaster recovery testing activities, validates results, and assists with post-test reviews and improvement planning. Serves as a liaison between business and technology teams to ensure recovery requirements, priorities, and dependencies are appropriately documented and understood. Ensures disaster recovery documentation remains current and supports alignment between disaster recovery plans, business continuity plans, and crisis management procedures. Assists in tracking technology recovery capabilities and recovery objectives to support ongoing resiliency planning. Communication & Learning Builds effective working relationships with stakeholders across business units, technology teams, and risk management functions. Facilitates meetings, working sessions, and planning discussions to support program objectives and deliverables. Communicates program requirements, expectations, findings, and recommendations clearly and professionally. Contributes to employee awareness, training, and preparedness initiatives related to business continuity, crisis management, and disaster recovery.

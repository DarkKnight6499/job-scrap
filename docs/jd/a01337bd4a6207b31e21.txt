Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all . Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever. As a Financial Analyst supporting the Innovation, Technology, and Products (ITP) organization at Micron Technology, Inc., you will support financial planning, reporting, and cost allocation activities for the engineering organization. Through strong analytical skills and attention to detail, you will help ensure accurate financial reporting and provide analysis that supports informed business decisions. A significant portion of this role will focus on executing and maintaining the Engineering Cost Model (ECM), which allocates Micron's engineering expenses across the technologies and programs that engineering supports. The model is a key input to investment analysis and resource planning, helping leaders understand where engineering resources are being invested and how investments are evolving over time. This role offers an excellent opportunity to develop expertise in financial modeling, business analytics, cost allocations, and automation while gaining exposure to Micron's engineering organization, technology roadmap, and innovation investments.

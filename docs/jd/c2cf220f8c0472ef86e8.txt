Job Description: Winchester Hospital is a 5x Magnet designated, Top 10 Massachusetts recognized 223-bed regional hospital that is the leading provider of comprehensive health care services in the northwest suburban Boston area. As part of the Patient Care Services team, this position performs the following tasks under the direction of a RN: Watches over patients that have been designated as a risk-to-self. Assists patients with walking. Effectively communicates with all members of the patient care team throughout the shift and completes a thorough and comprehensive handoff with the other Patient Safety Associates and RNs. Assists in the patient’s meal and nourishment, by setting up trays, feeding the patient and/or assisting the patient in eating. Engages patients and family members in conversation and provides basic emotional support. Assists patients in the set up of personal and oral hygiene needs and dressing. Keeps the room clean and tidy to improve the patient’s and family member’s experience. Education: Preferred: High School Diploma or equivalent Other Skills/Knowledge: Required: Knowledge of basic medical terminology. Strong interpersonal skills. Preferred: For safety and quality reasons, must be able to read, write and communicate effectively in English with patients, visitors and fellow members of the hospital team.

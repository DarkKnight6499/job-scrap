The Opportunity The Analyst II will work in Ally's Model Risk Management function, under the direction of the Model Validation Director responsible for Analytical Tool and Qualitative Methodology Validation. The analyst is responsible for delivering accurate, timely and relevant validation reports and analysis for tools that support regulatory reporting, stress testing, forecasting, pricing and valuation efforts in the Ally Business Lines. The Work Itself Review spreadsheets, code, dashboards, and data for computational efficacy, conceptual soundness, and control environment effectiveness. Produce high-quality validation reports that are clear, concise, factually accurate, and interpretable, providing actionable insights that support informed business decisions and broader organizational objectives. Build repeatable and auditable processes that support the generation of reports and analysis. Answer ad hoc validation analysis requests from Model Governance and other internal 2nd LOD partners. Work collaboratively with Line of Business, Risk and other matrix teams to identify opportunities for improving Model Risk Management analysis, processes, reporting and monitoring. Support the Model Validation Director in strategic initiatives and ad hoc efforts. Consistently push for reporting efficiency gains through use of technology driven automation and well-designed and documented processes. The Qualifications We’re Looking For 2+ years' experience in banking or financial services preferred. Bachelor's degree required; Master's degree preferred. Experience in a risk or line of business function preferred. Advanced knowledge of Microsoft Office Excel, Word and SQL required. Knowledge of Python or R preferred. Excellent analytical and reasoning skills with the ability to develop and execute tests based in mathematical concepts. Excellent written and verbal communication skills required. Excellent technical writing skills required. Ability to think thoroughly within end to end processes to see downstream and upstream impacts. Excellent interpersonal skills to work effectively with diverse teams located in various areas.

The Senior Manager, Risk Intelligence & Decision Support leads the analytics and decision support elements of the Risk Automation & Intelligence portfolio, including the Key Risk Indicators and the process instrumentation and internal performance indicators that are core to GSK's Risk Operations Centre. This is a delivery leadership role combining hands-on work with team and stakeholder management. This role is critical in enabling leaders at all levels and functions of GSK to rapidly identify, evaluate and make decisions on risk, and in providing assurance that compliance requirements are met. About the team. Risk Intelligence and Decision Support turns GSK's cyber risk data into decisions, and is a core part of GSK's Risk Operations Centre. The team builds the data platform, analytics and reporting that tell CSO leadership and the business where risk actually sits, what to do about it, and how effectively and efficiently the risk and compliance programme itself is operating. Key responsibilities: This role will provide you the opportunity to lead key activities to progress your career. These responsibilities include some of the following: Lead a defined area of the capability, including its people, delivery and outcomes. Manage delivery against the capability roadmap and escalate blockers early. Bring a product management and Agile delivery mindset to the roadmap for risk intelligence and decision support tooling, working with business and business-facing teams to capture voice of customer and prioritise features. Develop team capability through coaching, review and structured feedback. Represent the area in CSO governance and cross-functional forums. Own the Key Risk Indicators (KRIs) used across GSK, ensuring they are meaningful, well-defined and consistently reported and aligned to GSK enterprise risk management priorities. Define and instrument the internal performance indicators that measure the effectiveness and efficiency of the Risk and Compliance programme itself, as a core input to the Risk Operations Centre. Build and maintain the data platform underpinning cyber risk intelligence and reporting. Work with partner teams to engineer data pipelines that bring risk, control, threat and asset data into a single model. Partner with security product teams to identify and leverage the data and telemetry produced by their tools and technologies to identify, evaluate and disposition risk at scale. Develop analytics and models that quantify cyber risk and support prioritisation decisions. Build dashboards and reporting for CSO leadership, business units and governance committees. Define the metrics that matter and retire the ones that do not. Ensure data quality, lineage and definitions are consistent across risk reporting. Lead your team in using approved AI tools to improve productivity and ways of working, role-modelling responsible use and follow GSK’s Responsible AI, privacy and security requirements. Why you?

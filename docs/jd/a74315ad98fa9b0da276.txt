We are seeking a talented professional to join Marsh’s US Marine Industries team within our Marine, Cargo & Logistics (MCL) practice. We are excited to welcome a Marine Client Advisor to our US East Zone team. If you have the experience and qualifications we are seeking, we encourage you to apply and look forward to learning more about you. We will count on you to: Independently oversee and manage large or complex accounts and bring the entire team together to formulate procedures and go-to-market strategies. Work to maintain a near-complete understanding of clients’ strategic business priorities, risk strategies, and risk management needs in order to provide appropriate advice and successfully serve clients. Utilize expert risk knowledge, along with an understanding of industry and insurance marketplace conditions, to review clients’ exposures and loss experience and develop solutions for complex client needs, including recommending additional products and services. Develop, present, and oversee the execution of renewal and placement strategies in conjunction with Placement, Advisory Leaders, and Client Executives across all zones where product, industry, or account criteria dictate. Present to prospective clients, identify opportunities for new products, and develop or recommend innovative solutions for clients and client team members to secure new clients and projects. Build and maintain relationships with clients and ensure that their complex risk management needs are appropriately met. Oversee the production of client deliverables and the delivery of final placements to clients, ensuring the quality of all materials provided. Engage in independent, senior-level client interactions and demonstrate expertise in problem-solving, coverage areas, and client relationship management. Serve as a thought leader within the Company and maintain in-depth knowledge of changing risk, insurance, and protection and indemnity (P&I) market conditions.

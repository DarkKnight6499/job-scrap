Department: Information Technology Job Description: Lead the enterprise AI Systems Governance Program, with core accountability for governing AI Systems across The Mutual Group and its member insurance carriers. This role operates under delegated authority from the CIO to execute the AIS Program and leads the AIS / Security Governance Team, which assumes responsibility for AI governance activities defined in the AIS Program, while the CIO retains final approval authority. The role is responsible for ensuring that AI Systems used across multiple member organizations (insurance carriers) are governed consistently, responsibly, and in alignment with regulatory expectations. This includes working closely with member teams to assess, approve, monitor, and continuously improve AI usage in support of their business objectives. In addition to AI Systems Governance, the role provides oversight across Cyber Security Governance, Data Privacy, and IT Controls to ensure a cohesive Technology Risk framework. Work Arrangement: Employees who live within 30 miles of the TMG home office are expected to follow a hybrid or in-office schedule. The initial training period may require additional in‑office days.

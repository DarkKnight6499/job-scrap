We work together to drive ambition to every future! Function Overview Scotiabank’s Corporate Finance Advisory team sits at the intersection of banking and capital markets. Our team advises corporate clients on capital structure, financing strategy, and credit positioning, as well as strategic decisions across valuation, M&A, shareholder dynamics, and thematic market and sector trends. We partner across the full GBM platform to deliver analytically driven recommendations and differentiated insights that create value for our clients. Core areas of focus include: capital structure optimization, credit analysis, valuation, capital allocation, risk management, and shareholder positioning. Position Highlights We are looking for a quantitative associate to lead the delivery of analytical, data-driven content and strategic financial advisory across a diverse range of clients and transactions. This role involves developing financial models and presentations to evaluate market opportunities, weigh capital structure alternatives, assess credit and liquidity needs, and analyze valuation and capital allocation decisions. Working closely with senior bankers, this analyst will translate detailed financial analysis into clear, actionable insights to inform client decisions across capital raising and deployment, M&A, and shareholder strategy. In addition, this role will leverage data science to analyze thematic trends and generate forward-looking perspectives that support client dialogue and origination opportunities. What You’ll Do Evaluate the strategic and financial needs of corporate clients and develop tailored solutions to address them Synthesize company, industry, and market data to generate differentiated ideas and identify new business opportunities Apply both equity and credit perspectives to provide thoughtful, balanced recommendations and inform due diligence Work closely with an analyst team to deliver consistent, high-quality work with strong attention to detail Build long-term relationships with clients and collaborate across stakeholders throughout the banking and capital markets platform Support deal sourcing and execution through rigorous fundamental analysis, capital markets insights, scenario modeling, and client-ready presentation materials What You'll Bring: A positive, proactive, and intellectually curious mindset required MBA, Masters, or PhD degree in a quantitative field preferred Bachelor degree also considered if substantial prior experience in investment banking, capital markets, rating agencies, consulting, or equity / credit research Professional designations such as CFA, CPA, etc. are a plus Proven record of outstanding academic and extracurricular achievement Work Arrangement: This position is an on-site role, with the expectation that you will work 5 days a week in the office.

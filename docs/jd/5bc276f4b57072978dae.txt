Job Summary Responsible to the assigned supervisor for performing entry level engineering work activities and projects requiring the application of engineering knowledge in a safe, effective, efficient and economic accomplishment of assigned objectives. Job Description What You'll Do: Technical Expertise: Apply foundational engineering principles to solve routine technical problems and support more complex assignments. Develop knowledge of applicable standards, codes, regulations, equipment, materials, systems, and business processes. Leadership & Collaboration: Work effectively with team members and higher-level engineers, coordinate routine activities as needed, and demonstrate a willingness to take on increased responsibility and leadership opportunities. Planning & Organization: Support project planning, scheduling, cost estimating, and status monitoring. Use established engineering and scheduling methods to complete assignments according to project plans and timelines. Problem Solving: Gather and analyze information to address routine technical issues. Apply sound engineering principles and identify opportunities for new or improved approaches. Communication: Communicate clearly and effectively, both verbally and in writing. Assist with technical reports and documentation, follow instructions, and seek clarification when needed. Interpersonal Skills: Build effective working relationships, collaborate across teams, share information, and contribute to a positive team environment. Decision Making: Make routine decisions within established guidelines and contribute to team decisions with guidance from more experienced employees. Business Awareness: Develop an understanding of department responsibilities and customer needs. Consider cost, reliability, and environmental impacts when supporting engineering solutions. What We Are Looking For: This is an entry level position for new graduates with a Bachelor's degree in engineering in a program accredited by ABET*; Passing the Fundamentals of Engineering examination in preparation for future licensing as a Professional Engineer (PE) is encouraged and preferred. Individuals with a Bachelor’s degree in Engineering Technology can satisfy this degree requirement, if they obtain a certified Professional Engineering (PE) license. *VP or above may waive the requirement for a degree from a university with an Non ABET accredited program What You'll Get: In addition to a competitive compensation, AEP offers a unique comprehensive benefits package that aims to support and enhance the overall well-being of our employees.

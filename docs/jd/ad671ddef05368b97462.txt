What You'll Do Provide analytical support on collateral performance, cash flow analysis, structural evaluation, and legal documentation review, applying established approaches and guidance from senior team members. Support ad hoc portfolio performance, return, and market reporting. In addition, assist in the creation of term sheets as well as internal and external portfolio and marketing presentations. Support coverage across sectors including Consumer Finance, Student Loans, Residential Mortgages, Solar, Aviation, Rail and other transportation-related assets, Equipment Finance, Fund Finance, Digital Infrastructure, Whole Business, and other specialty finance sectors. Work with senior members of the team and interact with Investment Banks, Private Equity funds, Issuers, rating agencies, and in-house partners to support transaction ratings, accounting, and regulatory capital treatment. What You'll Bring Bachelor’s Degree preferred; MBA or CFA a plus. Some Structured Finance analysis experience, with exposure to Private and 144A ABS preferred. Experience or demonstrated knowledge in credit research and analysis. Strong written and presentation skills. Experience with cash flow modeling, scenario and sensitivity analysis for asset-backed transactions is a plus. Motivated self-starter with strong collaboration skills, sound judgment, and a positive attitude.

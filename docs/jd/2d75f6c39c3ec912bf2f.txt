Third-Party Risk Consultant ETX Governance, Risk & Compliance Team Full-Time, Springfield/Boston The Opportunity As a Third-Party Risk Consultant, you will play a crucial role in implementing the third-party technology risk framework while collaborating with U.S., Romania, and India-based Digital Trust, Governance & Technology Assurance teams. This position is responsible for executing third-party risk assessments and due diligence activities across MassMutual’s third-party ecosystem, including evaluating services, data flows, system integrations, controls, and evidence to identify and document risk exposure. Additionally, you will support the adoption of risk management practices across the ETX organization. As a key member of the ETX Third-Party Technology Assurance team, you will help drive change, collaborate effectively across global time zones, and enhance operational efficiency throughout the organization. The Team The Digital Trust, Governance & Technology Assurance Team is comprised of governance and risk professionals responsible for implementing governance processes and risk management practices for the ETX (Information Technology) organization. This role will operate as part of a globally distributed team and will work closely with U.S., Romania, and India-based business, technology, procurement, compliance, legal, enterprise risk, and audit partners to design practical and effective technology governance and risk management solutions that increase operational efficiency, improve transparency, and support informed decision-making.

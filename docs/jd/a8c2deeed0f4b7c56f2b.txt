Position Summary The Credit Risk and Portfolio Management Analyst – 2nd Line of Defense will have the primary duty of providing accurate, timely, and succinct reports to the First Busey Chairman, Busey Bank President, Chief Credit Officer and Vice Chairman(s) of Credit to support the company’s portfolio and risk management efforts. Duties & Responsibilities Develop, review and publish daily, weekly, monthly and quarterly reports for the Vice Chairman of Credit to disseminate pertinent information to the Chief Credit Officer and Busey Bank President to use in their loan portfolio management efforts Develop working knowledge of required systems such as nCino, Jack Henry, Cognos, Power BI and CoStar Establish and maintain the commercial reporting page on The Vault to provide all necessary reporting Assist all Model Risk Management initiative owned by Credit Administration Create and monitor metrics at regional and corporate level to measure portfolio management performance Collect economic data for Busey regions/markets and oversee preparation of quarterly economic report Assist with developing and maintaining the processes used for the foregoing in accordance with loan policy/guidelines Provide necessary credit information and reporting to other departments such as risk management and finance Assist in other special projects as assigned Education & Experience Knowledge of: Strong oral and written communication skills Strong data collection and analysis skills Strong organizational and project management skills Ability to: Multi-task and work independently Analyze and solve problems Take independent action within established guidelines and develop new procedures and approaches when needed Perform duties under frequent time pressures Education and Training: Requires Bachelor’s degree with a concentration in Business, Finance or related field Requires 5 or more years of banking experience; preferably commercial lending or credit analysis Requires proficient knowledge of Microsoft Office Suite Requires experience in report writing and data analytics Knowledge of nCino and Axiom systems preferred Every day, our associates live Busey’s core values, doing better business, building stronger relationships and creating thriving communities, while delivering meaningful value to shareholders.

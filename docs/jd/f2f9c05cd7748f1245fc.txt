Summary: Contributes to the development of GHEOR strategy and tactics for one or more indications within the neuroscience portfolio, with input from key stakeholders across Clinical, Medical, Marketing, Market Access, Biostatistics, and Regulatory Responsibilities: Support the development of an aligned strategy for GHEOR to demonstrate the value of neuroscience assets with a focus on Alzheimer’s Disease. Develop and execute innovative GHEOR plans, including real-world evidence generation, economic modeling, and clinical outcome assessment (COA) Effectively lead scientifically rigorous HEOR studies and manage projects from concept through execution, including dissemination of evidence through scientific congress presentations, peer-reviewed publications, and field-related materials Maintain cross-functional collaborative relationships with internal stakeholders including, but not limited to, Access Strategy, Clinical, Medical Affairs, and Commercial Provide input into clinical trial design and endpoint selection aligned with the value demonstration strategy Qualifications: A PhD in a related discipline such as Epidemiology, Health Economics, or Health Services Research is preferred. A minimum of a Master's degree in a related discipline is required At least 3 years of experience conducting outcomes research, health economics research, health services research, health technology assessment or conducting epidemiologic studies Experience executing research in a matrix organization, preferably within a pharmaceutical company setting Very strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to distill complex issues into clear and actionable insights for stakeholders Experience in neuroscience is preferred #LI-Hybrid We hire for skills and capabilities, not just credentials – if this role excites you, but doesn’t perfectly match your resume, we encourage you to apply anyway.

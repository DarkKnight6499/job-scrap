At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. Regeneron is currently seeking an Associate to join our BuiLD (MBA) rotational development program, an intensive, interdisciplinary program for graduating MBA candidates. Participants receive mentorship from Regeneron leaders and lead projects in a variety of roles across the company. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide.

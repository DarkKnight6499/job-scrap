Job Description: The Role As a Business Analyst supporting the onboarding of Private Markets in Fidelity Fund and Investment Operations (FFIO), this individual will serve as a vital conduit between the product delivery teams and stakeholders to ensure we meet the differentiated needs of Private Markets structures and requirements. They will work with various internal teams to allow for the efficient functioning of this key line of business in our firm and interact daily with internal stakeholders, legal/compliance, and others. This Analyst will develop a significant internal voice on behalf of the Private Markets product and the broader FFIO organization, influencing systems and architecture solution design based on complex requirements. As a member of the team, the Business Analyst leads initiatives with diverse scope and complexity, acting as the primary interface between end users, product management, and the systems organization. Our Analysts collaborate with business partners to assess complex business requirements and translate them into effective solutions. Finally, we support our product team’s dedication to quality by crafting and implementing tests that address business needs and ensure the delivery of solutions to our end users.

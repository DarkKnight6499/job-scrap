FHN Financial Portfolio Accounting is the leading provider of fee-based investment management services for depository institutions to banks and credit unions nationwide. We are seeking a Financial Analyst to support our investment management platform through asset and liability modeling, balance sheet analytics, and strategic financial analysis. This position plays a key role in supporting institutional clients and driving informed decisions. This position requires a high level of customer service including phone and email communication to support customers on the portfolio accounting system of FHN Financial. Analysts assist customers with monthly accounting reconciliation issues, updating monthly trade activity and processing re-runs to update and/or correct the initial reports rendered. Analysts train customers to navigate the website, perform online maintenance, access monthly management reports and run analytical reports. Analysts create additional reports and files for the sales representatives and portfolio accounting clients. This position is also responsible for maintaining updates within FHN Financial databases. An extensive knowledge of accounting, fixed income products, and Microsoft Office along with an aptitude for learning the various systems used by FHN Financial is required. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Finance/Accounting or Related Fields Prior experience with bond accounting, Yieldbook, Bloomberg, and Excel spreadsheets is preferred. The position also requires experience that indicates an understanding of different types of fixed income investments Excellent customer service and written/verbal communication skills. Excellent computer skills with knowledge of email functions and Microsoft Office. The ability to maintain organization while multi-tasking. Ability to work independently without direct supervision while under pressure to meet deadlines.

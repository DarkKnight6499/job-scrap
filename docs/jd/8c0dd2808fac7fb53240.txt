Job Description Ready to take your career global? Make your mark at one of the biggest names in payments. We’re looking for a Risk Portfolio Data Analyst Senior to join our ever evolving Commercial Risk Team and help shape the future of global commerce. As a Senior Risk Analyst, you’ll work on the cutting edge with the largest FinTech company in the world. While using your advanced technical skills to protect Global Payments from threats, you will partner with business leaders to identify risks, evaluate the effectiveness of controls, and promote continuous improvement. You will have responsibility for risk reporting & portfolio analytics across the Credit organization, leveraging internal and external data to make informed credit decisions. The successful candidate will be able to mine this data for relevant patterns and trends and use the data to continuously improve our portfolio monitoring process. A key part of the role is creating robust reporting packages for executive leadership leveraging internal systems, Tableau, Microsoft PowerBi, Excel, and PowerPoint. What you will own: Ensure the strategic objectives of risk management program are met including execution of risk assessment activities, coordination of risk response and program testing and validation. Review significant events and advises business owners of action steps required to prevent future recurrence. Leverage internal as well as external data and AI to develop an industry leading Credit portfolio monitoring program Document risk analysis and controls and evaluates control design and continuous control improvement. Provide ad hoc analysis to determine how various events may impact our portfolio, including running stress testing and scenario analysis Execute risk assessment activities and coordinating risk response. Review significant events and advise business owners, to prevent future recurrence.

About the Role As a Product Development Engineer, you will support the development, evaluation, qualification, and release of innovative semiconductor products. Working across multiple stages of the product lifecycle, you will collaborate with cross-functional teams to analyze product performance, resolve technical challenges, improve manufacturability, and ensure products meet quality, reliability, and customer requirements. Key Responsibilities Support the development, evaluation, and qualification of new semiconductor products and technologies. Analyze product, test, and manufacturing data to identify trends, improve performance, and resolve technical challenges. Participate in product characterization, validation, reliability assessment, and production release activities. Collaborate with Design, Test, Manufacturing, Quality, and Applications Engineering teams throughout the product lifecycle. Assist in root cause analysis, failure investigation, and corrective action development. Contribute to process improvements that enhance product quality, yield, reliability, and operational efficiency. Develop and maintain technical documentation, test reports, product specifications, and engineering records. Support new product introduction (NPI), product transfers, and sustaining engineering activities. Must Have Skills Understanding of semiconductor devices, electronics, manufacturing processes, or product development fundamentals. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to interpret technical data. Familiarity with laboratory testing, experimentation, or engineering evaluation methods through coursework, projects, internships, or work experience. Effective written and verbal communication skills. Ability to work collaboratively in a cross-functional engineering environment. Preferred Education and Experience Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Computer Engineering, Materials Science, Physics, or a related technical field. 0-2 years of relevant experience in product development, product engineering, reliability engineering, quality engineering, manufacturing engineering, or related technical disciplines. Experience through coursework, internships, research, or professional work involving product development, product engineering, semiconductor testing, reliability engineering, manufacturing, or quality engineering. Familiarity with data analysis tools, statistical methods, or characterization techniques. Exposure to semiconductor manufacturing, qualification testing, product validation, or failure analysis. Knowledge of programming, scripting, automation, or engineering analysis tools. Experience working with laboratory equipment and measurement systems.

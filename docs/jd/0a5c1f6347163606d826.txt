The Opportunity Reporting to the Director or Manager, the Sr Treasury Analyst is responsible for supporting Treasury and associated business partners throughout the organization. The Treasury Analyst II is responsible for routine activities, within aligned function, using existing procedures and policy. The Analyst performs day to day structured work assignments requiring only moderate instruction and guidance from more senior level roles. The Work Itself Build and maintain knowledge in governance and controls. Work with business partners, including Business Line (BL) Risk, Independent Risk Management (IRM) and Compliance, to understand governance routines and requirements to support needs across Treasury Lead vital governance routines for Treasury by maintaining and improving Treasury’s governance repository Support routine activities spanning across Treasury, including standard reporting, Asset Liability Committee (ALCO) materials preparation, consolidation of Senior Management and Board level materials, and other adhoc requests Support internal and external audits Assist with supervisory activities across the Treasury group, including but not limited to, targeted and horizontal exams, Routine Continuous Monitoring meetings, ad-hoc requests and remediation efforts Analyze and improve processes Encourage a culture of collaboration, partnership and inclusion within the team Apply relevant regulations, policies and procedures to business issues and day to day responsibilities The Qualifications We’re Looking For 3+ years of experience in treasury, finance, risk, or related. Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, or Economics, or related. Experience supporting governance frameworks, control environments, and regulatory compliance activities within Treasury, Finance, or Risk functions. Proficient in all Microsoft Office applications and skilled user of Excel and PowerPoint. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Takes initiative and is a strategic thinker. Ability to grasp and apply complex concepts. Ability to work across teams to achieve a common goal. Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Attention to detail, balanced with knowing the right altitude vital to deliver on requirements.

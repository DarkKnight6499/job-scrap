Job Description: Job Title Technology Associate Corporate Title Associate Location New York, NY Overview Deutsche Bank's Global Corporate Coverage (GCC) team manages relationships with many of the world's largest corporations, delivering strategic financing, liquidity, risk management, working capital and transaction banking solutions. The US Technology Coverage team focuses on leading software, cloud, AI, semiconductor, hardware, digital infrastructure and data center ecosystem companies headquartered in North America. Working alongside senior bankers and product specialists, Associates play a critical role in supporting client coverage, identifying opportunities and helping deliver the full capabilities of Deutsche Bank to clients globally.

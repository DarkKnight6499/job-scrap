The Opportunity: Hamilton Lane is looking to hire an Associate to join our Infrastructure and Real Assets Investment Team, with the position to be based in Conshohocken, PA or Portland, Oregon. This role provides diverse exposure across private equity infrastructure and natural resource related opportunities at a leading private markets solutions provider. The investment professional will assist the deal leaders in sourcing, performing due diligence, and monitoring infrastructure and natural resource investment opportunities across minority directs, co-investments, secondaries and primaries. You will also be involved in other special projects, as necessary, under the direction of senior investment professionals. Responsibilities: Assist in all aspects of the investment process including sourcing, due diligence, structuring, negotiations, and closing on minority directs, co-investments, secondaries and primary fund investments. Guide the work of Analysts in the due diligence process, while also assisting the senior members of the Infrastructure and Real Assets team with team resource allocation, managing day-to-day aspects of investment analysis, providing oversight of the functional team, and assisting in communication with investment sponsors and clients. Manage day-to-day aspects of investment underwriting, providing oversight of the functional team, and assisting in communication with investment counterparties and clients. Complete special projects, including client projects, industry analysis and geographical studies, as necessary. Create and manage complex transaction models and distribution waterfalls. The position will provide exposure to the top private equity infrastructure firms in the industry and diversified exposure across transaction types and sectors.

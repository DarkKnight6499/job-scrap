We are seeking an individual who is passionate about digital accessibility to join the team as a Senior Digital Accessibility Specialist. This individual will report into the Director, Digital Accessibility Center of Excellence, in Technology Controls, and will be responsible for guiding internal partners in digital accessibility development, testing, and remediation, as well as verifying compliance to WCAG and other accessibility standards in alignment with Citi’s ADA policy. This individual will be responsible for educating others on how to implement and test web pages, mobile applications, emails, PDFs, and other commonly used digital file-types for WCAG compliance. Additionally, the Senior Digital Accessibility Specialist will assist with periodic monitoring and reporting of WCAG compliance, as well as designing test processes and procedures. Across all of these efforts will be work to change the culture of the organization, and update “the way we work” to include accessibility considerations. The Senior Digital Accessibility Specialist is accountable for succeeding against identified metrics. This individual will regularly assume informal/formal leadership roles within teams and must have strong technical expertise and a desire to learn and teach. They must have the courage to always do what’s right, the humility to learn from mistakes, and the ability to make decisions in alignment with the risk appetite of the organization to promote sound outcomes for Citi and its stakeholders. Responsibilities Act as the subject matter expert (SME) for WCAG guidelines and stay informed of new guidelines as they evolve Understand how a global matrixed organization operates and functions, and thrive in this type of environment Focus on highest priority demands while maintaining progress on other important work activities; understand how initiatives support organizational success; eliminate activities that distract from the critical few priorities. Establish bold performance goals; strive to achieve more than what seems possible and put in persistent extra effort when needed; hold self accountable for exceeding expectations. Work across the organization to help drive the adherence and implementation of key compliance guidelines including identifying opportunities to increase effectiveness Provide expert accessibility feedback in the areas of design (UX/UI), development, and testing to teams across Citi on new features and functionality Assist accountable parties (technology, journey owners) to interpret accessibility audit reports and identify root causes of defect creation and monitor remediation of those root causes Partner with groups across Citi to share best practices for digital accessibility Assume the intentions of others are based on common goals and shared purpose, and proactively help colleagues achieve their goals.

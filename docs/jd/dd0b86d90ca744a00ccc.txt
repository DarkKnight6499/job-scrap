US Corporates - Credit Analyst, Director - Middle Market/Private Debt Leveraged Finance Fitch US Corporates Group is currently seeking a Director within the Middle Market/Private Debt team based out of our New York/Chicago/Toronto office. What We Offer: • A fast-paced, team-oriented work environment… Excellent communication skills are essential, as is a high productivity work-ethic and results oriented mindset. • An opportunity to contribute to Fitch’s increasing presence in the rapidly growing Middle Market/Private Debt sector. • A role and platform that helps lenders to Middle Market issuers make better informed decisions via timely, insightful and forward-looking rating actions and research. We’ll Count on You To: • Further build out Fitch’s existing suite of research on Middle Market/Private Debt topics which targets Fitch’s external audience of institutional investors, private equity sponsors, private debt funds, CLO managers and credit hedge funds; • Perform data analysis of key quantitative and qualitative factors influencing the Middle Market and credit profiles of borrowers in support of new research; • Manage junior staff on a project-by-project basis to efficiently grow the research portfolio; • Communicate with investors and sell-side counterparties to further explain research findings, grow Fitch’s brand awareness and generate ideas for new research topics in Middle Market/Private Credit/Direct Lending. What You Need to Have: • 5-10 years of relevant experience; • Bachelor's degree required; • Expertise in Microsoft Excel; • Strong analytical, quantitative, and organizational skills; • Excellent written and verbal communication skills; • Ability to shift fluidly between multiple projects as priorities change; • Ability to excel in a team-oriented environment; • Employee management experience a plus. • CFA and/or MBA a plus; What Would Make You Stand Out: • An objective-driven self-starter who is looking for a role with substantial entrepreneurial potential; • A deep understanding of and a keen interest in Middle Market Lending and/or Private Credit; • A background in capital markets and/or credit analysis and well-developed corporate finance skills; • Experience executing on research projects – from idea generation through writing and presenting at industry events and in media; • Outstanding interpersonal skills displayed by a strong pattern of leadership and the ability to proactively and successfully interact with various stakeholders, both within and outside of the organization.

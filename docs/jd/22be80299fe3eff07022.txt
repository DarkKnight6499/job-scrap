The VP, Engagement Lead applies advanced industry knowledge to develop complex research and analysis strategies and leads teams in data collection and documentation to deliver best-in-class guidance to clients. This role independently drives the development of large-scale project proposals to support business growth and expand client relationships. The VP, Engagement Lead reviews and refines complex presentations, slide decks, and client communications to ensure quality and readiness for delivery. This role also partners with clients to shape and evolve project approaches, methodologies, and tools that support successful risk programs and strategic initiatives. In addition, the VP, Engagement Lead develops meeting strategies for internal and client discussions, facilitates key conversations, and leads complex or unique client engagements to deliver exceptional results.

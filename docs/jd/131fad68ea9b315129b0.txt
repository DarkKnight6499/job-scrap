Job Description Summary: The Opportunity The Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) Financial Analyst – Shared Services is responsible for monthly and quarterly financial reporting, forecast, annual budget and ad hoc analysis for business partners across the firm. This is a hands-on role with an emphasis on performing financial analysis that provides senior management with insights needed to make strategic decisions around expenses. A strong communicator and team player, this role will work closely with peers in Finance & Accounting and across the business to build strong and collaborative partnerships across Accounting & Finance, Legal, Human Capital and other corporate functions.

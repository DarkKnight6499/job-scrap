Excited to grow your career? BBVA is a global company with more than 160 years of history that operates in more than 25 countries where we serve more than 80 million customers. We are more than 121,000 professionals working in multidisciplinary teams with profiles as diverse as financiers, legal experts, data scientists, developers, engineers and designers. About the job: Description Participate in the origination and end-to-end execution of M&A and Project Finance Advisory (PFA) deals Develop detailed financial models to perform valuation exercises (DCF, FCFE, DDM, transaction multiple analysis, etc.), Fairness Opinions, and capital structure analyses (Acquisition Finance, maximum debt capacity, debt structure alternatives etc.) Assist in the preparation of pitching materials and transaction documentation (including teaser, information memorandum, management presentation, process letter, internal presentations, and market debt sounding materials, among others) Coordinate due diligence efforts by liaising with internal and external advisors, managing data rooms and facilitating information exchange Interact with clients both during the origination and execution of M&A and PFA deals Conduct comprehensive industry and market research to help originating new deals, identify potential targets and understand industry trends Responsible for drafting marketing materials (pitches) and deal materials (IMs, Teasers, Board presentations, capital structure presentations, etc.) Collaborate with other CIB teams to promote cross-selling of products and ensure smooth deal execution Actively participate in the training of analysts Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Finance, Economics, Law or Engineering. MBA qualifications will be valued, but not a must to have A minimum of 4 - 6 years of M&A experience in an investment bank or boutique Strong financial modeling, valuation, communication and presentation (verbal and written) skills Bilingual: English and Spanish required Cultural skills: customer first, we think big and we are one team Leadership skills: Empowerment, entrepreneurship, commitment to responsibilities Cross-cutting skills: Continuous learning, effective communication, data-driven decision making, knowing how to give and receive feedback SIE, Series 79, and Series 63 are required All qualified applicants will receive consideration for employment without regard to race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, disability, or status as a protected veteran.

The Information Risk function serves as the Bank’s Second Line of Defense (2LOD), independently assessing and monitoring information risks across the organization. This role supports the Information Risk Team by translating complex data into clear, actionable reports that inform senior management and decision-making committees on the Bank’s risk posture and strategy. Responsibilities include conducting, creditable review & Challenge, and validating risk assessments; analyzing the potential impact of identified risks; recommending mitigation strategies; and developing governance documentation. The position also prepares detailed reports for senior, executive, and board-level committees while contributing to broader risk activities such as business impact assessments, key risk metrics, scenario analysis, material risk identification, and issue management. Responsibilities Served as a key member of the Information Risk function, providing independent oversight and challenge of information security, technology, and cyber risks across the enterprise. Led risk assessments, control reviews, and risk validation activities to identify emerging threats, assess business impact, and recommend effective mitigation strategies. Analyzed enterprise risk data, specifically vulnerability management results, security assessments, and operational metrics to identify trends and strengthen the organization's risk posture. Developed and maintained Information Risk governance frameworks, including policies, standards, procedures, risk methodologies, and supporting documentation. Produced executive-level reporting and dashboards for senior leadership, risk committees, and Board committees, translating complex technical risks into concise business-focused insights. Partnered with business units, technology teams, and control functions to support risk management, regulatory compliance, and strategic initiatives. Oversaw key risk management activities including Risk and Control Self-Assessments (RCSAs), Key Risk Indicators (KRIs), scenario analysis, issue management, and material risk identification. Supported regulatory examinations and audits by ensuring risk documentation, evidence, and reporting met regulatory and governance expectations. Evaluated cybersecurity and technology risks related to cloud environments, third-party vendors, software development, vulnerability management, and operational resilience. Developed actionable recommendations that improved risk governance, control effectiveness, and decision-making across the organization. Created executive dashboards, risk heatmaps, and trend analyses to provide timely visibility into risk exposures and mitigation efforts. Collaborated with regulators, auditors, and stakeholders to ensure alignment with industry standards and regulatory requirements.

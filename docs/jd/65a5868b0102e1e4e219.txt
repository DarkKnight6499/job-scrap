Wells Fargo is seeking a Lead Strategic Investment Specialist (Vice President level) to join the Principal Technology Investments (PTI) team based in Palo Alto, CA. The Lead Strategic Investment Specialist is responsible for sourcing, evaluating, and executing strategic venture capital and growth equity investment opportunities across enterprise technology sectors, with particular focus on the key domains of fintech, cyber security, artificial intelligence, infrastructure software, and other technology sectors relevant to the future of financial services. The role combines venture investment expertise, industry knowledge, and relationship management across the technology ecosystem to identify emerging technologies relevant to Wells Fargo's businesses and drive both strategic and financial value creation. The position plays a key role throughout the investment lifecycle, including sourcing investment opportunities, conducting due diligence, developing investment thesis, executing transactions, and helping manage portfolio companies post-investment. This position offers a unique opportunity to combine venture investing, strategic technology evaluation, and business development within a major financial institution In this role, you will: Source

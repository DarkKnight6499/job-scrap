The Financial and Regulatory Reporting team, within the Corporate Controller’s organization, is responsible for delivering accurate, complete, and timely financial and risk reports for the Company and its subsidiaries. The team supports filings provided to federal regulators and the investing public and maintains the governance, controls, and documentation that underpin these critical reporting processes. We are seeking a Manager to lead key regulatory reporting activities and help advance the team’s reporting, data, and control framework. This role will oversee recurring deliverables, support the implementation of new products and regulatory requirements, and identify opportunities to improve efficiency, transparency, and data quality. The Manager will collaborate closely with partners across Accounting, Finance, Risk, Treasury, Product, Technology, and Data Governance. What you’ll do: Manage the preparation, review, and timely submission of assigned financial and regulatory reports. Provide leadership, coaching, and subject matter guidance to team members while promoting accountability and continuous development. Interpret reporting instructions and accounting guidance, assess impacts, and coordinate implementation of new or revised requirements. Maintain effective controls, reconciliations, documentation, and evidence to support accurate reporting and audit readiness. Analyze reporting data, investigate variances and data-quality issues, and partner with upstream owners to resolve root causes. Lead process improvement and automation initiatives that strengthen accuracy, efficiency, scalability, and transparency. Support the integration of new products, systems, and data sources into the regulatory reporting framework. Build strong partnerships across Accounting, Finance, Risk, Treasury, Product, Technology, and Data Governance. Communicate complex reporting matters clearly to business partners, leadership, auditors, and other stakeholders. Contribute to special projects, examinations, and ad hoc analyses as business and regulatory priorities evolve.

Job Description: Capital Markets Overview: TD Securities is a market leader in capital markets advisory and underwriting. Our Global Capital Markets business is comprised of Debt Capital Markets (DCM), Equity Capital Markets (ECM) and Leveraged Finance and Private Credit (LFPC). We advise clients on a broad range of strategic and tactical goals, including capital structure optimization, equity and debt market positioning and issuance, and investor communication strategies. Our client-first, product-agnostic, approach drives high quality transaction outcomes and long-lasting and productive relationships. LFPC products include leveraged loans, high yield bonds, acquisition and growth financing, corporate debt finance, fund finance, and private credit solutions. What makes Capital Markets right for you? Capital markets is a hybrid job that intersects the transaction-oriented nature of banking and the market-facing aspects of sales and trading. These roles combine the technical, client-focused and project-based work found in banking, with the fast-paced, market-driven environment of a sales and trading desk. We sit at the heart of the organization, between Investment Banking coverage, Corporate Banking, Syndicate, Sales & Trading, and High Yield Credit Strategy/Research. The role is dynamic, with each day different than the day before depending on news flow, market conditions, and macro events. Our flat organization and highly collegial structure has analysts & associates sitting on trading-style desks adjacent senior bankers to foster learning, client interaction, and relationship building. Lean deal teams allow each member to do real work and gain meaningful client exposure from origination through execution. You will play an integral role in the deal cycle from beginning to end including ownership of pitches, origination, deal and legal documentation, sales memos, marketing, due diligence, execution, and pricing. TD Securities Global Capital Markets business provides a strong and immersive mentorship program that will deliver all the tools necessary for you to succeed as a full-time associate.

Technology Risk & Control Role Summary The Portfolio Governance Lead provides centralized governance and execution oversight across a portfolio of Market applications , ensuring technology risk, control remediation, regulatory commitments, and strategic remediation initiatives are effectively managed and reported. The role maintains the integrated portfolio view, drives governance cadence, manages RAID and escalation processes, and provides senior leadership with clear visibility into material risk exposure, remediation progress, emerging concerns, dependencies, and decisions required . Key Responsibilities Portfolio Governance Maintain the integrated Risk & Control portfolio across 500+ applications. Establish consistent governance standards, reporting requirements, milestones, and escalation criteria. Coordinate activities across Risk Remediation Project Managers, application owners, cybersecurity, infrastructure, IAM, third-party risk, audit, and control teams. Ensure priority risks have a clear accountable owner, path to green, target date, milestones, dependencies, and closure criteria . These fields align with the portfolio operating model already established in Application_Risk_and_Vulnerability_Operating_Model.docx. RAID & Dependency Management Maintain the portfolio-level Risks, Actions, Issues and Dependencies (RAID) framework. Identify systemic or cross-application risks that cannot be resolved within individual workstreams. Track dependencies involving shared infrastructure, cybersecurity, IAM, vendors, funding, resources, and technology platforms. Drive accountability for overdue actions and unresolved blockers. Escalation Management Establish clear escalation thresholds for material exposure, missed commitments, regulatory deadlines, inadequate remediation plans, and unresolved dependencies. Escalate issues through the appropriate risk and technology governance forums. Ensure escalations clearly state business/risk impact, accountable owner, corrective action, target date, blocker, and decision required . Governance Calendar Own and coordinate the Risk & Control governance cadence, including: Daily: Critical/P1 exception management Weekly: Portfolio remediation and execution review Biweekly: High-risk and control-domain reviews Monthly: Risk & Control Remediation Council Quarterly: Executive portfolio and operating-model review This cadence is consistent with the governance structure established in Application_Risk_Vulnerability_Executive_Operating_Model.pptx. Monitor delivery against agreed paths to green. Coordinate with control owners to ensure remediation evidence supports closure. Ensure items are not reported as closed until required evidence and validation are complete.

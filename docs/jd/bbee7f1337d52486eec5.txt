The Role Who we are At Kyndryl, we design, build, manage and modernize the mission-critical technology systems that the world depends on every day. When a bank clears payments, an airline boards a flight or a health service opens its doors , there is a good chance our systems are underneath it. We work with thousands of enterprise customers in more than 60 countries, and in fiscal 2026 we did $15.1 billion of business doing it. Kyndryl Consult is the fastest-growing part of that business — $3.5 billion in revenue in fiscal 2026, up 18% year on year, on $4 billion of signings. What makes it different from a traditional consultancy is simple: we don't just advise. We build and we run. The recommendation and the accountability for making it work sit in the same place. The team Consult Propositions & GTM is the team that builds what Kyndryl Consult takes to market. We define the packaged consulting offers — we call them propositions — that our consultants sell, we set how they are priced, we own the story that goes with them, and we make sure our global community of client-facing consulting partners is equipped to sell them. Roughly everything a Kyndryl consultant puts in front of a client starts here. We are rebuilding the team in FY27 around five capabilities, and this is one of them. You would be joining at the design stage, not the maintenance stage. The role Our strongest consulting offers are the ones that speak a client's own language — a bank's problem, not a generic technology problem. This role exists to make that true across our industry portfolio. You'll take our propositions and build the industry versions of them: the sector-specific problem framing, the proof, the regulatory and operational context, and the commercial shape that fits how that industry buys. You'll work through our Industry Guilds — the communities of practitioners who hold our deepest sector knowledge — turning what they know into something sellable and repeatable. It's a bridging role. You sit in the portfolio strategy team, you work closely with our Advisory organization who own the Industry portfolio, and you draw on the Guilds. If you like being the person who connects groups that would otherwise not quite meet , this is that job. What you'll do Build and run industry-specific propositions through the full lifecycle — intake, build, gate reviews, launch and refresh Translate cross-industry offers into credible sector-specific versions with real proof and real context Work through the Industry Guilds to capture practitioner knowledge and turn it into repeatable assets Maintain the working interlock with Advisory on industry portfolio direction Build industry toolkits — client narrative, qualification guidance, proof points, competitive positioning and commercial inputs Watch industry trends, regulation and competitor moves and bring implications back to the portfolio How success is measured Industry propositions launched on schedule and to standard, toolkit completeness at gate, adoption across markets, and — shared with the team — the pipeline and signings attributed to industry offers.

What You'll Be Doing At Stifel, the Private Capital Markets (PCM) Vice President is responsible for leading private capital transactions and advising corporate and financial sponsor clients on tailored financing solutions, including growth equity, common and preferred equity, structured minority investments and recapitalizations. The Vice President manages transaction execution, develops client and investor relationships, supports origination alongside senior bankers and industry coverage teams, and oversees financial analysis and junior team development. What We're Looking For Lead private capital transaction execution from investor positioning and process design through marketing, due diligence, negotiation and closing. Manage client relationships and serve as a trusted advisors on financialing alternatives, transaction structures and investor selection. develop new business opportunities with senior bankers, industry coverage and financial sponsor coverage teams; build and maintain relationships with private capital investors. review and guide Associate and Analyst work, including financial models, capitalization tables, dilution analyses, investor return scenarios and client deliverables; mentor junior team members. Develop sector and private capital investor expertise to position clients, identify appropraite capital partners and assess financing alternatives. Develop recruiting relationships and help build the PCM team's talent pipeline, training resources and execution capabilities. Teamwork and community service (recruiting, reviews, mentoring, firm committees). New Business: Demonstrated ability to identify, source and execute private capital opportunities; differentiate Stifel's capabilities from competitors; execise good judgement in assessing risks, financing alternatives and investor fit. Performs other related duties as assigned. About Stifel At its core, the Stifel story is a journey of growth and optimism for the future. In our more than 125-year history, Stifel has evolved from a St. Louis-based brokerage house and is now a full-service investment bank, offering securities brokerage, trading, research, underwriting and corporate advisory services globally. Our expertise spans sectors and products in both public and private markets. We are driven by relationships, not transactions, and are committed to earning long-term trust from our clients. Our investment banking division provides a broad range of industry-leading services to its clients, including merger and acquisition advisory services, debt and equity financings, balance sheet restructuring, and mutual thrift & insurance company conversions. The combination of a full-service product offering and more than 600 bankers’ deep domain and product expertise allows us to provide solutions that meet the evolving needs of our clients.

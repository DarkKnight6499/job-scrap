Head of Treasury Capital Markets Position Summary Do you want to be part of a collaborative Treasury team? Do you have corporate Treasury experience, are detail oriented and possess good interpersonal skills to effectively interface between Treasury and Business and Investment teams? Do you think big and grow, accelerate impact, and lead change? If so, Guardian is seeking a Head of Treasury Capital Markets to lead Guardian’s treasury team to manage Guardian’s financial transactions. You are A collaborative, self-starter who will work across the Enterprise to manage the complex financial transactions supporting Guardian’s capital management and growth strategies, including the Funding Agreement Backed Notes business. You are focused on driving Guardian’s strategic priorities of growth and capital management. You are comfortable working with tight deadlines to initiate and complete financial transactions. You have Required: Bachelor’s degree in Finance or a related discipline 10-15 years of experience Strong analytical, interpersonal and leadership skills Knowledge of Treasury Management Systems, with experience with Kyriba a plus. Preferred: Experience with FHLB borrowing process and collateral posting requirements including Commercial & Residential Mortgage Loans Knowledge of FHLB 1Link Systems a plus The ability to accelerate impact and lead change undefined You will Manage Guardian borrowing programs, including external credit facilities, surplus notes, internal loans, and internal lines of credit. Review debt agreements and monitor key financial and operational requirements. Lead the capital markets activities of Guardian’s Treasury team, which includes managing the FHLB program for borrowing, repayments, interest, stock requirements, optimizing collateral posted to the FHLB, and all required reporting to internal parties, FHLB, and DFS. Collaborate with Investments executives to establish and fund warehouses for FABN program (including FHLB or other funding mechanisms), manage issuances, payments and maturities Strategically manage Guardian’s debt activity to provide visibility into program utilization and upcoming interest payments, renewals and maturities. Manage financial reporting aspects of these programs. Assist Treasury leadership with capital structure, financing and liquidity analysis. Collaborate with the Investments and Risk senior leaders to support capital management and forecasting, scenario analysis and stress-testing activities. Be the subject matter expert for senior leaders across Guardian on these programs. undefined Reporting Relationships As our Head of Treasury Capital Markets, you will report to Guardian’s Assistant Treasurer, who reports to the Treasurer .

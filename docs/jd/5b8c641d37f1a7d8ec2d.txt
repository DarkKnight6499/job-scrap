Job Location AUBURN PLANT Job Description Role Description: Assist the site capital systems manager (CSM) and capital cost controller (CCC) in carrying out and executing capital and fixed asset management (FAM) systems at the site. This role also applies technical mastery in advising site resources on MAGICS and SAFE guidelines to ensure compliance to applicable stewardship policies. Responsibilities include: · Execute various SAP reports to determine status of capital projects for the site. · Maintain and build various spreadsheets to collate capital project information so to aid the CSM in the capital cash forecasting process · Support depreciation forecasting, FADO accounting, and GMC forecasting as needed · Enter STEAM approval requests, build WBS structures for newly funded projects, and complete closeout activities for capital AE projects in SAP and eTrending · Process eROCs, eFADOs, and coordinate the asset tagging process for all capital work · Request AMRC’s to update and maintain the site Fixed Asset Register · Perform periodic asset audits throughout the site for MAGICS toolkit testing to ensure adherence to SAFE guidelines · Participate in monthly Capital reviews with the CSM and CCC to gather forecast information and discuss FAM compliance needs · Monitor adherence of capital requisitions to site policy requirements · Interact with BSS experts and Coupa resources to help with issue resolution, as needed · Participate in the Capital KEA and any MAGICS/GIA audits · Participate in the development and implementation of continuous improvement for Auburn capital systems and FAM processes and procedures · Be the backup to the site P2P personnel · Collaborate and network effectively within immediate team or work domain as required to maximize job impact and personal learning. · Responsible for the day-to-day work systems and tasks to support the business. Should be well-versed in technical office tools and software applications, calendar management, and coordinating business meetings.

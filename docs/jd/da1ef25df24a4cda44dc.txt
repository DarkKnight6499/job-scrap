Director, Structured Finance Business & Relationship Management – US CMBS Fitch Ratings is currently seeking a Director of Structured Finance Business & Relationship Management – US CM BS based out of our New York office. As one of the world’s top three credit ratings agencies, Fitch Ratings plays a critical role in global capital markets by providing supplementary credit analysis, ratings, research, and commentary to financial market participants. For over 100 years, Fitch Ratings has been creating value for global markets through its rigorous analysis and deep expertise, which have resulted in a variety of market-leading tools, methodologies, indices, research, and analytical products. Fitch Ratings is part of Fitch Group, a global leader in financial information services with operations in more than 30 countries, which also includes Fitch Solutions. With dual headquarters in London and New York, Fitch Group is owned by Hearst. Explore a career at Fitch Ratings and you’ll join a collaborative network of curious minds. Here, our differences are our strengths - in life as well as our work. Together, we work with integrity and objectivity to provide clarity to the world’s financial markets. We pride ourselves on our ability to harness the ideas, expertise, and professionalism of our global workforce, which is integral to our continued success. The accessibility and depth of experience of our industry-leading analysts, combined with our broad sector credentials, allow us to help market participants make important credit decisions with confidence. About the Team The US CMBS Business Relationship Management team focuses on building, developing, and maintaining relationships with issuers and bankers to expand our CMBS/CRE business and support growth in our ratings business. How You'll Make an Impact Business Development & Opportunity Generation Identify and qualify new business opportunities by leveraging relationships, market intelligence, and coverage gap analysis. Support efforts to grow ratings mandates across the CMBS market. Assist with engagement agreements, fee discussions, and business-development initiatives. Contribute to external presentations, marketing efforts, and outreach campaigns designed to strengthen Fitch's market position. External Relationship Management Develop and maintain relationships with existing and prospective issuers, bankers, and other market participants. Engage regularly with market contacts to strengthen relationships, support client retention, and identify new business opportunities. Respond to client questions and concerns in a timely and professional manner. Collaborate with senior colleagues and analytical teams to deliver a high-quality client experience. Market & Pipeline Analysis Analyze market activity, pipeline reporting, and market share trends to support business-development priorities. Monitor developments in the CMBS market and broader structured finance sector. Maintain accurate forecasts, pipeline reporting, and CRM records. Provide insights and recommendations to support team planning and business objectives. Marketing, Events & Collaboration Assist in developing marketing materials, presentations, and other external-facing content. Partner with Marketing and Events teams on conferences, client events, and industry engagement activities. Build strong internal relationships across Fitch to leverage expertise and support business-development efforts. You May Be a Good Fit If Bachelor's degree in business, finance, economics, or a related field. Approximately 5-8 years of relevant experience within CMBS, structured finance, banking, capital markets, commercial real estate finance, or a related field. Demonstrated experience in relationship management, business development, account management, or opportunity generation. Strong understanding of financial markets and the factors affecting structured finance issuers and investors. An energetic, proactive approach and willingness to travel occasionally for client meetings and industry conferences. Knowledge of Salesforce or similar CRM systems is a plus. What Would Make You Stand Out Ability to build trust and credibility with external market participants. Strong listening, communication, and presentation skills. Ability to develop persuasive value propositions and communicate Fitch's analytical strengths. Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously. Curiosity about market developments and a desire to continuously deepen industry knowledge. Why Choose Fitch? Hybrid Work Environment: 3 days a week in office required.

Job Title Manager, Capital Markets Financial Analysis Hub (EDSF) Job Description Summary Cushman & Wakefield’s Equity, Debt, and Structured Finance group (“C&W EDSF”) is a premier capital markets team providing solutions for complex transactions on behalf of institutional, corporate, and private investment clients offering a range of debt advisory services including construction, bridge and permanent financing throughout the capital stack including first mortgages, B-notes, mezzanine financings and preferred equity. The role reports into the Director of the Financial Analysis Hub, an Advisory shared services platform supporting Capital Markets brokerage within the Americas. The Financial Analysis Hub specializes in valuation modeling and advanced commercial real estate analytics, providing best-in-class financial analysis support across various product types for the benefit of our clients and key stakeholders. Position Summary The Capital Markets Financial Analysis (“CMFA”) Hub Manager is a CMFA subject matter expert responsible for the day-to-day management of the Financial Analysis Hub’s delivery of financial analysis on behalf of Cushman & Wakefield’s Equity, Debt, and Structured Finance (“EDSF”) brokerage services. In this role, the CMFA Hub Manager provides oversight on the execution of EDSF financial analysis and marketing deliverable requests by providing direction, conducting quality control reviews, and lending subject matter expertise to a team of financial analysts. The CMFA Hub Manager provides oversight on team member development and performance and partners with Americas EDSF brokerage teams to identify support needs while ensuring alignment on service delivery expectations. The role serves as a key contributor to the Financial Analysis Hub’s continued maturity with involvement in training and onboarding activities, implementing standardized workflow processes, sharing industry best practices, and identifying growth and continuous improvement opportunities to ensure sustained delivery of high-quality and scalable support services for the benefit of C&W EDSF brokers. The ideal candidate will be based in one of the following markets: Los Angeles, New York, or Washington, D.C. and available to work on-site in a local Cushman & Wakefield office. Team members may be expected to work various time zones, depending on the product type and markets they service.

Title: Risk Management Opportunities The Programme and Project Partners (PPP) model was mobilised in 2019 with the purpose of transforming major project delivery at the Sellafield nuclear site. The partnership is delivering a 20-year pipeline of major infrastructure projects to support the decommissioning of Sellafield and to create a clean and safe environment for future generations. In delivering its pipeline of large-scale infrastructure projects, PPP is creating opportunities for its people, supply chain, economy and communities. KBR’s rapidly growing nuclear team of teams is working at the forefront of the UK’s nuclear space on some of the most exciting new-build, defence and decommissioning programmes. KBR was recently named a “Great Place to Work-Certified” company, an honour that underscores the company’s commitment to being a UK employer of choice for people who want to do work that matters. Due to the nature of our work and security requirements, KBR does not offer sponsorship for this role. We can only consider applicants with the right to live and work in the United Kingdom Now is your chance to be part of this vital work, a once-in-a-career opportunity. There are a number of opportunities we will be recruiting for as part of this programme delivery throughout the duration of the contract, particularly supporting the business operations in Risk Management . As a member of the PPP Risk Management team, you will play a pivotal part in the PPP’s ongoing success story. OVERVIEW OF RISK MANAGEMENT OPPORTUNITIES The Programme and Project Partners (PPP) are looking for talented individuals with experience in Risk to join their Risk Management team. Roles will include Graduate Risk Practitioner, Risk Practitioner & Risk Manager . There will be the opportunity for ongoing personal development, and process and systems training. All roles will involve providing support to the PPP Head of Risk in: Delivering a best-in-class risk management service to a range of nuclear construction projects and supporting functions Establishing high standard risk management plans Facilitation of risk reviews and risk workshops to identify risks (threats and opportunities) including Project, Programme and Enterprise level risk information Development and maintenance of risk registers Establishing and recording risk assessments and treatment strategies The ongoing monitoring and reporting of risk as required Risk Analysis using PPP risk management software, processes, procedures, and techniques Carrying out Quantitative Risk Analysis (cost and schedule) in line with the PPP Processes and procedures where full training will be provided Supporting a range of projects and functions at various points in their lifecycle If you would like to be considered for a role supporting Sellafield PPP, please submit your CV and profile by clicking 'apply' against this requisition.

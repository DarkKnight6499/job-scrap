We are hiring for a Software Engineer - TRACE here at Trane Technologies. This role will work as part of the Customer Driven Solutions (C.D.S.™) team focusing on a flagship proprietary software that Trane offers. This is an exciting opportunity to join a team that is in the process of revamping a long-standing successful technology and the person in this role will help us shape the direction of future offerings—including exploring opportunities to leverage machine learning and artificial intelligence for enhanced product capabilities. This position will require someone that thrives in a team environment, analysis, design, and creates logic for customer driven business needs. We are looking for a highly motivated, diligent, quality-centric, customer-focused, organized team-player with industry best-practice awareness, strong coordination, communication skills, and Software Quality Assurance to help improve our software tools. In this role, you will be responsible for Developing and Implementing Logic for the applications. Core Job Responsibilities (others may be added): • Design, Create, and Maintain Logic. • Identify bottlenecks, and bugs, and devise solutions to address and mitigate these issues. • Ensure the best possible performance, quality, and responsiveness of applications. • Developing routine tests and coordinating the testing effort. • Develop Software Quality Assurance processes based on industry best-practices. • Collaboration with product owners, applications engineers, and customer support. • Participate in code reviews and contribute to team knowledge sharing. • Stay up-to-date with industry trends and technologies to ensure our solutions remain cutting-edge • Communicating with business users within all levels of the organization. Minimum Qualifications: • Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering, Information Technology, Computer Science, MIS (or related IT degree) and at least 3 years of work experience preferred. Key Skills or Competencies: • Understanding of the software development process. • Strong background in productivity software & collaboration tools. • Microsoft .NET Framework (C#) experience. • Familiarity with SQL and No SQL Databases. • Web browser-based development experience (Blazor, JavaScript, CSS, HTML, XML/XSLT). • Proficient in both front-end and back-end development. • Project management, time management, planning, organizational and analytical skills required. • Self-motivation, willingness to take initiative, ability to function effectively independently and in team-based situations. • Basic design ability: Including knowledge of UI/UX and basic prototype design. • Strong written and oral communication skills required with good interpersonal skills, ability to express concepts and ideas with clarity. • Sound Knowledge of Test and Defect Management tools like ALM and Jira • Experience with Agile practices is desired. • Knowledge of HVAC systems and equipment is a plus.

Your Team The Senior Associate will join Invesco's Private Markets Product – Real Estate team, supporting the development, positioning, and ongoing management of private real estate investment strategies across the U.S. market. The role will contribute to fundraising initiatives, investor and consultant engagement, product development, product positioning and messaging, due diligence efforts, and competitive intelligence activities that support strategic decision-making across the private real estate platform, with a primary focus on the institutional client channel. Your Role While the role sits within the broader Invesco Private Markets platform, the core responsibilities are focused on helping bring private real estate equity and credit strategies to market, as well as the on-going support of those already in market. This includes partnering closely with Client Portfolio Managers, Investment Teams, Sales, Marketing, Legal, Compliance, and Operations to develop high-quality materials that support fundraising, client education, investor due diligence and product updates/maintenance. You Will Be Responsible For: • Support product positioning, messaging, and go-to-market initiatives for private real estate equity and credit strategies across institutional distribution channels. • Assist in the day-to-day management of in-market real estate products, including maintaining offering documents, due diligence materials, marketing collateral, and product data, while partnering closely with Operations, Investor Relations, Marketing, Compliance, Legal, and Portfolio Management. • Build strong internal and external relationships across investment, distribution, consultant, and investor communities to support fundraising objectives. • Conduct market research, competitive analysis, and industry monitoring to identify product enhancements, investor trends, and strategic growth opportunities. • Develop and coordinate due diligence materials, including DDQs, RFP responses, data room content, product presentations, and responses to investor and consultant inquiries. • Contribute to product development and enhancement projects, including new product launches, vehicle structuring initiatives, channel expansion opportunities, and cross-functional implementation efforts. • Support investor and consultant engagement efforts by coordinating responses to strategy-specific inquiries and communicating product developments, portfolio updates, and investment outcomes. • Ability to explain and simplify complex information to others, and lead the coordination of information to ensure ongoing process or execution. The Experience You Bring: 3–5 years of experience in financial services, preferably within private markets, real estate, product management, investor relations, client portfolio management, fundraising, strategy, or related disciplines. Understanding of private real estate equity and/or credit investments, private fund structures, portfolio management, fundraising processes, and institutional investor due diligence requirements. Knowledge of institutional investor channels, including consultants, pensions, insurance companies, endowments, foundations, and OCIOs, is preferred. Ability to communicate complex investment strategies, portfolio positioning, and performance concepts to a broad range of internal and external audiences. Understanding of how private real estate strategies fit within broader private markets and institutional portfolio allocations. Familiarity with fundraising processes, competitive landscape analysis, and product lifecycle management. The salary range for this position in New York City is 113,000 Base - Max 130,000 Base/year.

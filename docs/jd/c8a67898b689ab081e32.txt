Resolve issues related to specialized complex operation functions. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Following is a summary of the essential functions for this job. Other duties may be performed, both major and minor, which are not mentioned below. Specific activities may change from time to time. 1. Collaborate with all Portfolio Managers to support activities associated with the complete portfolio management process including lettering, tickler management, research, trigger activity and delinquency monitoring 2. Monitor and process Adverse Action Letters and/or Disclosures for Centralized Lending within established regulatory guidelines 3. Monitor and clear Equifax Fraud alerts 4. Monitor and process Permissible Purpose reports for all Centralized Lending Hubs within established regulatory guidelines. Ensure proper documentation was received prior to pulling client credit reports 5. Create and maintain databases and management reports, interpreting data, and identifying trends QUALIFICATIONS Required Qualifications: The requirements listed below are representative of the knowledge, skill and/or ability required.

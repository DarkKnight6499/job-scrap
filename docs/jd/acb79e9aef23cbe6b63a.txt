Job Area: Finance & Accounting Group, Finance & Accounting Group > Finance General Summary: This is an exciting and unique opportunity to be a join the CBN Financial Planning & Analysis team. Primary responsibilities for this position will be business unit support and P&L management for the Connectivity, Broadband and Networking business line within QCT. Responsibilities The responsibilities will include ownership of the P&L for the RFF/Budget/Strategic Planning process for the business lines within CBN, monthly demand reporting to management, monthly close reporting and analysis, product council ROI analysis and providing support to key business partners. Minimum Qualifications: • Bachelor's degree. • 4+ years of Finance, Accounting, or related work experience. *Completed advanced degrees in a relevant field may be substituted for up to two years (Master’s = one year, Doctorate = two years) of work experience. Preferred Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Data Analytics, or related field. 7+ years of Finance, Accounting, or related work experience. 2+ years of work experience in a role requiring interaction with senior leadership (e.g., Director level and above). 2+ years of experience working in a large matrixed organization. Proficient in Microsoft Office tools (Excel and PowerPoint) and prefer experience in AI, Tableau, AWS & Databricks. Position requires a driven self-starter with excellent verbal and written communication skills, and outstanding problem-solving abilities.

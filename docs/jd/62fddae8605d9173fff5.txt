About the Role: Grade Level (for internal use): 12 The Team: Our Corporate Technology team serves as the centralized platform engineering function, establishing enterprise-wide DevSecOps standards and architectural patterns that enable secure, scalable software delivery across all business units. We are the strategic technology backbone that empowers global development teams through standardized platforms, governance frameworks, and innovative open-source solutions. The team values technical excellence, cross-functional collaboration, and driving organizational impact through thoughtful architecture decisions that balance security, compliance, and developer productivity. The Role: The DevSecOps Architect provides enterprise-level technical leadership for secure software delivery, cloud-native platforms, and developer enablement. This role defines DevSecOps strategy, reference architectures, engineering standards, and reusable platform capabilities that integrate security throughout the software development lifecycle. The architect partners with engineering, cybersecurity, infrastructure, risk, and business leaders to improve delivery speed, resilience, compliance, and developer experience. Key Responsibilities Define and lead the enterprise DevSecOps architecture, roadmap, and adoption strategy — designing secure, scalable CI/CD patterns and paved-road developer capabilities across cloud, hybrid, container, and orchestration environments, with governance models that drive consistent adoption across engineering value streams. Embed automated security controls across the full software development lifecycle — including static and dynamic code analysis (SAST/DAST), software composition analysis (SCA), secrets detection, artifact integrity verification, container scanning, infrastructure-as-code policy scanning, and runtime protection — translating cybersecurity and compliance control requirements into automated, auditable guardrails. Establish and govern architecture standards for source control, build automation, artifact management, release orchestration, observability, vulnerability management, and software supply-chain security — providing authoritative technical direction across developer tooling platforms and self-service capabilities. Lead architecture reviews, threat modeling sessions, design decisions, and proofs of concept — authoring reference architectures, engineering standards, architecture decision records (ADRs), and roadmaps that enable engineering and senior leadership to make defensible, evidence-based platform decisions. Drive platform reliability, resilience, and operational readiness through measurable engineering practices — defining and monitoring DORA metrics including deployment frequency, lead time, change failure rate, mean time to recovery, security findings density, and developer productivity benchmarks. Mentor architects and engineers and build communities of practice that accelerate enterprise-wide DevSecOps adoption — influencing senior stakeholders without direct authority and advancing developer experience so that secure delivery is the default, not an additional step. What We're Looking For: Required Qualifications Experience: 7+ years of experience in DevSecOps, cloud architecture, platform engineering, or cybersecurity engineering — including demonstrated leadership of enterprise-scale DevSecOps transformation initiatives or large-scale platform architecture programs CI/CD & Platform Engineering: Hands-on production experience designing and operating enterprise CI/CD pipelines using Git-based source control platforms, artifact management repositories, container orchestration technologies such as Kubernetes, and infrastructure-as-code tooling such as Terraform — with version control discipline and automated policy validation at scale Security Engineering across the SDLC: Demonstrated production experience embedding automated security controls across the software development lifecycle — including SAST/DAST code analysis, software composition analysis (SCA), secrets detection, container image scanning, and policy-as-code enforcement technologies such as OPA or Kyverno Cloud Platform: Hands-on production experience with at least one major cloud platform (AWS, Azure, or GCP) — including cloud-native IAM, secrets management platforms such as HashiCorp Vault or AWS Secrets Manager, network security, and vulnerability management in regulated or enterprise-scale environments Bachelor’s degree in computer science, engineering, cybersecurity, information systems, or equivalent practical experience. Preferred Qualifications Experience working in a regulated or security-sensitive enterprise environment, such as financial services or fintech Experience with managed container orchestration services such as Amazon EKS in production enterprise environments Experience with zero-trust architecture principles, SRE practices, and developer self-service platform design Relevant certifications in cloud security, container orchestration, DevOps, or cybersecurity (e.g., AWS Security Specialty, CKS, CISSP, or equivalent) Experience with software supply-chain security practices and observability or SIEM platform integration Experience evaluating or implementing AI-assisted development tooling or automated security operations within DevSecOps pipelines Compensation/Benefits Information (This section is only applicable to US candidates) S&P Global states that the anticipated base salary range for this position is $150,000 to $205,000.

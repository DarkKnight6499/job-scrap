At Aquatic, we are actively recruiting for a Financial Analyst to join our Finance team in Chicago. The Financial Analyst will assist in all aspects of accounting for our management company and general partner entities and the Funds we manage, including tax, investor reporting, and budgeting/forecasting responsibilities. You will collaborate closely with Finance, as well as our fund administrator, auditors, and tax advisors. The work is hands-on, detail-oriented, and visible: accurate books and clear analysis give leadership the information it needs to run the firm as it grows. We are building a modern Finance function and expect our team to use AI to work faster and smarter. Role Details Month-end close for the management companies. Build and maintain the management company's annual budget and rolling forecasts, and prepare budget-to-actual and variance analyses. Review fund administrator books and investor statements, and assist with investor reporting for the funds. Invoice, credit card, and expense reimbursement processing through Ramp. Assist in regulatory reporting, the year-end fund audit, financial statements, K-1s and K-3s, 1099s, and tax filings for the funds and management company. Use AI tools to streamline recurring workflows, analysis, and reporting, applying sound judgment in validating AI output. Help implement new tools across Finance, including our transition to an AI-native accounting platform. Collaborate with Finance and Operations on ad hoc requests and projects. Perform other related duties as necessary or assigned. Technical Experience Bachelor's degree in Accounting required. Certified Public Accountant (CPA) designation preferred. 1-3 years of experience working within finance or accounting for a financial services or audit firm. Experience with budgeting, forecasting, financial modeling, and financial planning and analysis (FP&A) processes is a plus. Proficiency with AI tools to improve analysis, automate recurring work, and increase productivity is preferred. Knowledge of management company accounting, management company taxes, fund accounting, and fund taxes is a plus. Exposure to US and international tax or international offices is a plus. Experience with investor reporting is a plus. Experience auditing hedge funds, doing tax compliance for hedge funds, and/or working directly in a hedge fund is preferred. Experience with cloud-based accounting or ERP systems is a plus. Proficiency with Microsoft Office, Ramp, and Northern Trust Hedge Fund Services (Administrator) tools is a plus.

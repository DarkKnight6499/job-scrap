Business Unit Overview: Blackstone Multi-Asset Investing (BXMA) manages $101 billion across a diversified set of businesses. We strive to generate attractive risk-adjusted returns for our clients across market cycles. Our strategies include Absolute Return, Multi-Strategy, Total Portfolio Management, and Public Real Assets. BXMA’s Total Portfolio Management business manages total portfolios on behalf of select clients (families and institutions). The portfolios are customized to client-specific objectives and aim to outperform traditional portfolios by embracing a long-term time horizon, flexible asset allocation, and robust portfolio construction. Total Portfolio Management’s open architecture approach combines external investments, private market strategies, direct investing and the Blackstone ecosystem. Job Description: The incumbent will be a core investment professional within BXMA’s Total Portfolio Management (TPM) platform, responsible for diligence and execution across fund investments, co‑investments, and select direct investments. The role requires strong independent judgment, full ownership of diligence workstreams, and consistent delivery of investment‑committee‑ready analysis in line with Blackstone standards. The incumbent will work closely with senior investment professionals and is expected to operate with high accountability, intellectual rigor, and attention to detail, while managing multiple parallel processes in a dynamic, institutional environment.

About the Role: Develop reports and analysis on various loan portfolios for processes such as concentration analysis, portfolio performance, etc. Provide support to routine and ad-hoc credit administration deliverables and analytical insights. What You Will Do: Provide support in all aspects of the credit analytics, reporting, data mining, methodologies; ensuring proper support for all reserving requirements (quantitative and qualitative) associated with decisions Analyzes and reports on credit memo and write-off activity and monitors and communicates credit concerns to management Provide support to ensure documentation is completed for internal and external stakeholders, and that effective change management is in place Identify and execute opportunities for process improvement and standardization across business lines for the organization Drive standard definitions around key data elements and identify and support projects related to achieving data consistency, availability and quality Provide support and assist in driving the identification and implementation of technology solutions to reduce manual processes, enhance controls, improve cycle times, and support consistency where practical Collaborate with leadership to enable an efficient data capture, reporting, and forecasting process Conduct special studies on targeted portfolio risks; analyze and recommend changes to data improvement, portfolio setting and other key parameter settings such as correlation structure, probability of default/loss given default correlation, loan prepayment, etc. Other duties as assigned. What You Will Need: Bachelor's degree or equivalent education and work experience. 3+ years experience. Experience in quantitative methods/techniques, statistics, predictive modeling, data science or other analytical disciplines, required. Experience with lending software, SSRS, Crystal Reports, Oracle BI, Tableau, Microsoft Access, or similar applications, preferred. Credit Risk Management work experience, inclusive of loss forecasting within a regional financial institution is preferred Written and verbal communication Attention to detail Driven and self-directed yet collaborative in approach Strong business and technical acumen and ability Solid understanding of lending practices and processes The responsibilities listed above are not all inclusive and may be changed at any time.

Are you ready to sit at the intersection of natural catastrophe risk, capital markets, and institutional investment management? Swiss Re ILS is looking for a curious, analytically driven professional to join our Portfolio Management team in the heart of New York City. This is a rare opportunity to build deep expertise in one of the most dynamic and specialized asset classes in the market — insurance-linked securities (ILS). About the Role As a Portfolio Management Analyst, you will play a meaningful role across the full investment management process — from evaluating and underwriting individual ILS transactions to contributing to portfolio construction, quantitative research, and investor support. You'll work alongside experienced portfolio managers, catastrophe modelling specialists, and reinsurance experts, leveraging Swiss Re's proprietary risk models and unparalleled market knowledge to deliver attractive risk-adjusted returns for institutional investors. If you thrive on complex problems, love working with data, and want to grow your career in a fast-paced, entrepreneurial environment, this role is for you. About You You are an intellectually curious, analytically minded professional with a genuine passion for the intersection of natural catastrophe risk, insurance, and financial markets. You enjoy tackling complex problems, diving deep into data, and translating quantitative analysis into clear, actionable investment insights. You're a motivated self-starter who thrives in a collaborative, entrepreneurial environment and is excited by the opportunity to grow across underwriting, investment analysis, portfolio management, and trading within a leading institutional investment management business.

What You'll Be Doing At Stifel, the Private Capital Markets (PCM) Analyst is responsible for supporting deal teams on private capital raises and capital solutions for corporate and financial sponsor clients, including growth equity, common and preferred equity, structured minority investments and recapitalizations. The Analyst supports origination, investor targeting, structuring, financial modeling and execution, helping clients evaluate financing alternatives and achieve their financial and strategic objectives. What We're Looking For Support deal teams on all parts of private capital transaction execution, including financial analysis, investor outreach support and due diligence coordination. Assist senior bankers in preparation for client engagement, including pitches, financing alternatives presentations and investor marketing materials. Work closely with industry coverage, financial sponsor coverage, other capital markets teams and legal to coordinate client management, new business origination and deal execution. Build financial models and valuation analyses, including capitalization tables, ownership and dilution analyses, investor returns and comparisons of financing alternatives. Draft presentation materials and internal committee memorandums for private financings; research prospective investors and maintain investor targeting lists and transaction process trackers. Analyze private capital market trends, comparable financings and investor investment criteria; support the comparison of investor proposals and key economic and governance terms. Some travel by car and/or air in conjunction with local, regional and/or national travel, up to 10%.

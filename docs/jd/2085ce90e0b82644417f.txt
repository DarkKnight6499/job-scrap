CORPORATE AND WORKPLACE SOLUTIONS Goldman Sachs is a leading global investment banking, securities and investment management firm that provides a wide range of services worldwide to a substantial and diversified client base that includes corporations, financial institutions, governments and high net-worth individuals. Corporate and Workplace Solutions Division Our mission is to deliver solutions that maximize the efficiency and productivity of our people. CWS includes the following pillars: Corporate and Workplace Experience, the Office of Global Security and Regional Office Leadership. The Corporate and Workplace Experience pillar includes our Onsite Solutions, Corporate Events and Production and Real Estate teams. Onsite Solutions and Corporate Events and Production deliver experiences to both our clients and employees globally and include teams such as Hospitality, Transportation and Event Management Real Estate plans, develops, builds and operates Goldman Sachs facilities in over 60 cities in 23 countries These businesses are supported by the Corporate Planning and Management’s Finance and Engineering teams, which includes the disciplines of financial accounting and reporting, operations finance, procurement and contracts management and business planning and technology solutions; and the CWS Administration and Communications team, which is responsible for organizational development and internal and client communications. Reporting to the Americas Head of Events the candidate will partner with the business to develop and lead an event marketing strategy and oversee events and conferences with a keen focus on best practices, commercial excellence, and consistency in delivery. Goldman Sachs events are critical to maintaining the image of the firm and assisting in the development of key client relationships. The ideal candidate should have a minimum of 3-4 years’ experience in an event management role with strong project management and team leadership skills and the ability to operate effectively in a fast-paced and dynamic organization.

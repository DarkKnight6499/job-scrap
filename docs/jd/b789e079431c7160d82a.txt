About This Role: As a Senior Associate II in Quality Audits, you will play a pivotal role within the Corporate Quality team, focusing primarily on audits in the Regional Americas. You will independently conduct risk-based audits to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements, international standards, and company quality expectations, primarily under ISO 13485 and applicable GxP regulatory frameworks including FDA and EMA guidelines. Your role will enhance our audit program by supporting inspection readiness and supplier qualification, while contributing to the continuous improvement of our Quality Management System (QMS). With a degree of autonomy and professional judgment, you will collaborate closely with internal and external stakeholders to maintain our high standards of quality compliance, making a significant contribution to the organization's overall business integrity and operational success. This is a hybrid role to be based at our offices in Research Triangle Park, NC. What You’ll Do: Support the planning and conducting of internal audits of sites, ensuring adherence to GxP requirements and ISO 13485 standards. Plan and execute risk-based audits for external suppliers, contract manufacturers, and service providers, if needed. Document and evaluate audit observations, preparing inspection-ready audit reports. Manage concurrent audits effectively, ensuring timely completion and thorough reporting. Support tracking and review of audit responses and CAPAs, driving alignment across stakeholders. Engage with site and affiliate Quality and Operations teams to evaluate compliance. Communicate findings and expectations, ensuring timelines for corrective actions. Collaborate with stakeholders to drive remediation and continuous improvement initiatives. Interface with supplier Quality and technical teams during external audits. Contribute to inspection readiness activities and health authority audit responses. Who You Are: You are detail-oriented and possess the ability to work autonomously while collaborating effectively with diverse teams. Your analytical mindset allows you to evaluate complex situations with precision, and your dedication to quality ensures compliance with rigorous standards. You excel in managing multiple audits concurrently, demonstrating efficiency and thoroughness in your work. Your strong communication skills facilitate clear interactions with stakeholders, fostering alignment and understanding of audit expectations and corrective actions. Required Skills: Bachelor of Science in Life Science, Engineering, or related technical discipline. Minimum of 3 years’ experience in a regulated pharmaceutical, biotechnology, or medical device environment. At least 2 years auditing experience. Demonstrated experience with ISO 13485 and global GxP regulatory frameworks (FDA, EMA). Fluent in English. Preferred Skills: Advanced degree. ASQ Certification.

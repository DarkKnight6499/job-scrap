FP&A GROUP The Financial Planning and Analysis group is a strategic partner to the firm's business units and its senior executives. We build the budgets, forecasts, and multi-year plans that shape how the firm invests and operates, and we maintain the management basis P&L that leadership relies on. We're a collaborative team that holds itself to a high standard: our work is accurate, insightful, and ready for senior audiences. POSITION DESCRIPTION We are a Senior Associates to help support the growing complexity, size, and volume of Bain Capital. This roles will report into a FP&A Manager and will support our private markets businesses. You will be a trusted partner to business unit leaders and a go-to resource for teammates. What you'll do · Own the analysis behind the annual budget process and the monitoring and reforecasting throughout the year for your assigned businesses. · Contribute to the firm's long-term strategic plan, translating real-time and planned business changes into clear financial projections. · Deliver periodic reporting to business unit COOs and CFOs, with thorough trend analysis that surfaces the "so what," not just the numbers. · Partner daily with the compensation team, corporate finance/accounting, and department leaders to keep plans and actuals aligned. · Prepare journal entries to bridge the tax basis P&L to the management basis. · Take on ad hoc projects from business unit and senior leadership, often under tight timelines, giving you broad exposure to the firm's businesses. · Review your own work rigorously before it leaves your desk, and proactively flag issues, risks, and opportunities rather than waiting to be asked. Who you are · A self-starter who takes ownership, sees problems through to resolution, and takes real pride in the quality of your output. · A team player who rolls up your sleeves and steps in to help colleagues, regardless of whose project it is. · Committed to meeting deadlines. You're willing to put in additional time during peak periods such as budget, forecast, and plan cycles, and you help the team get across the finish line. · Comfortable operating independently in a fast-paced environment while knowing when to escalate or ask for input. · A clear communicator who builds relationships with stakeholders at all levels, from analysts to senior executives. Qualifications · 3-6 years of experience in FP&A or accounting (public or private) · Exceptional attention to detail and the ability to prioritize multiple concurrent projects. · Sound judgment and the willingness to take responsibility for decisions. · Advanced Excel and strong financial systems skills; Anaplan experience a plus.

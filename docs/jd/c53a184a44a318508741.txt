Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Salesforce Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Salesforce CPQ/Revenue Cloud Senior Associate you will support client work in business application consulting, helping organizations analyze needs, implement Salesforce solutions, and provide training that supports day-to-day use and adoption of core platforms. As a Senior Associate you will build meaningful client connections, manage assigned workstreams, guide junior team members, and navigate complex situations while deepening your Salesforce skills and broad business awareness within our Salesforce practice. In this role at PwC, you will apply consulting skills across Salesforce applications, contribute to Revenue Cloud and CPQ delivery, interpret feedback, and help translate client priorities into practical solution work that supports operational efficiency and business goals.

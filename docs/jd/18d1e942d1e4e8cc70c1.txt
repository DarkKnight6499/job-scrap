Debt Asset Management Associate, Commercial Real Estate New York, NY Firm Overview Benefit Street Partners (BSP) is an alternative credit pioneer that seeks to deliver attractive returns through its relationships, specialist expertise and global reach. We focus exclusively on credit, building deep expertise across direct lending, structured credit, liquid credit, special situations, infrastructure debt and real estate. BSP's real estate strategy focuses on originating and managing commercial mortgage loans across the United States and in all segments of the market. BSP is a flexible capital provider, originating both floating-rate and fixed-rate loans across the capital structure, including first mortgage loans, mezzanine loans and preferred equity. BSP's real estate equity capabilities focus on multifamily, triple-net lease assets, and CMBS B-pieces. BSP's dedicated real estate team today consists of 70 professionals with origination, underwriting and asset management capabilities that enable it to generate proprietary deal flow. BSP's CRE investment team is located in New York, NY. Position Overview BSP seeks to hire an Associate with at least 2 years of relevant experience for the Asset Management group located in New York City. The position will report to the Head of Asset Management and work with a strong, seasoned team of asset management, loan origination, underwriting and securitization professionals. Through on-the-job training, the role will evolve as the candidate learns and is ready to take on additional responsibility. Responsibilities Responsibilities include but are not limited to: Monitor and analyze the financial performance of the properties and portfolios including balance-sheet bridge loans, CMBS loans and other credit investments Provide support in the review of property budgets, business plans & quarterly reforecasts Develop a working knowledge of leases, loan documents & other legal agreements Help develop financial models including discounted cash flows, IRR analysis, NPV analysis, etc. utilizing Microsoft Excel and Argus Assist in preparation of monthly, quarterly, and annual investor reports and presentations Assist in the administration of on-going loan proceeds disbursements to include construction draws, leasing costs, and release of loan reserves Responsible for the aggregation, accuracy and reporting of data to be used for reporting and decision making by credit committee. Liaise with servicers and borrower to provide optimized client service. Interface daily with members of origination, legal, finance, securitization and underwriting teams. Qualifications The successful candidate will ideally possess: 2+ years of full-time experience with a real estate background Bachelor's Degree in business, finance, economics, real estate, or related field Understanding of real estate fundamentals, commercial office leases, financing principles, and debt capital structures Familiarity with loan due diligence to include property appraisals, environmental reports, engineering reports, and insurance reviews Loan servicing or construction loan administration experience a plus Commercial real estate valuation, underwriting and/or accounting experience a plus Outstanding quantitative and analytical skills, attention to detail, ability to multi-task effectively Highly proficient in Excel Effective oral and written communication skills Ability to work both independently and as a team member Ability to work in a fast-paced, demanding environment Compensation Range Along with base compensation, other compensation is offered such as a discretionary bonus, 401k plan, health insurance, and other perks.

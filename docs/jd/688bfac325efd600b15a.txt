About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Senior Lead Business Execution Consultant to serve as the Data Domain Expert for Deposits and Liquidity within AI & Data, Global Payments & Liquidity (GPL). This role sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, data, payments, liquidity management, and digital currency innovation. As the authoritative source for Deposits, Liquidity, and Pricing & Rates data, you will ensure critical business data is trusted, accessible, governed, and AI-ready. Success in this role requires deep expertise in banking data domains and a passion for transforming complex data into a strategic asset that powers AI-driven solutions. You will partner closely with AI Domain Leads, engineers, product teams, and governance partners to enable use cases such as cash forecasting, funding management, account visibility, and pricing optimization. This is a highly visible opportunity for a hands-on data practitioner who thrives in a fast-moving environment and is excited to help shape how AI and intelligent agents leverage data across the organization. In this role, you will: Serve as the subject matter expert for Deposits, Liquidity, and Pricing & Rates data, including sources of record, business definitions, lineage, and downstream consumption. Act as a trusted advisor to leadership, driving strategic initiatives and influencing data-related decisions across the organization. Ensure data is complete, discoverable, governed, and AI-ready for analytics, automation, and agentic use cases. Define, monitor, and improve data quality standards while leading resolution of complex data issues. Partner with AI Domain Leads and engineering teams to enable high-value use cases such as cash forecasting, funding management, account visibility, and pricing optimization. Support integration of banking data with ERP, treasury management systems, and the Managed Data & AI Services platform. Collaborate with Data Governance teams to strengthen metadata management, cataloging, lineage, controls, and compliance with enterprise policies. Translate complex technical data concepts into business-relevant insights and actionable recommendations. Lead cross-functional discussions, working groups, and steering committees to facilitate decision-making and drive execution. Influence and collaborate with leaders, product teams, technology partners, and enterprise data organizations to deliver scalable solutions. Required Qualifications: 7+ years of Business Execution, Implementation, or Strategic Planning experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education Desired Qualifications: Strong expertise with deposits, liquidity, balance sheet, or pricing and rates data in a banking environment. Experience with data quality, metadata, lineage, and data governance practices. Strong SQL skills and experience with modern data platforms such as Snowflake, Databricks, or similar technologies. Experience supporting AI, advanced analytics, or data-driven business solutions. Ability to translate complex data concepts into business insights and recommendations. Proven partnership skills with business, technology, data, and governance stakeholders. Ability to work in a fast-paced environment while balancing innovation with risk and control requirements. Intellectual curiosity and interest in emerging trends across AI, payments, liquidity, and digital currencies. Pay Range Reflected is the base pay range offered for this position.

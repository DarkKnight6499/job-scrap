As the Portfolio Manager – Syndications , you will assist with the efforts and growth of our Syndications platform. This position will report to the Manager of Loan Syndications and will work closely with the Transaction Manager - Loan Syndications and internal and external partners to assist with syndication activity. Internally, this is a key function of the organization as our Syndications platform continues to grow. How you'll spend your time: Prepare marketing materials for syndicated deals (requires credit analysis). Assist with structuring syndicated transactions. Manage portfolio of existing syndicated deals, including running point on waiver, consent and amendment activity for existing UMB-led deals. Help manage deal-related inquiries from external bank partners. Review and advise on loan documentation for syndicated transactions. Coordinate with Loan Administration team for syndicated loan closings and ongoing draws for syndicated deals. Create a database of all UMB-Agented deals with ticklers for maturities and reporting items. Process and resolve inquiries and requests from external bank partners on existing syndicated deals. Maintain contact management on third-party data site Debticate for syndicated deals and assist with ongoing calling efforts. Manage collection of reporting items for syndicated deals from internal deal teams and posting to Debticate. Engage with UMB Relationship Managers and other internal parties as it relates to syndicated transactions. Assist with internal reporting, internal education and market updates. Performs other duties as assigned. We're excited to talk to you if: You have a five years of Commercial credit analysis experience. You have a solid working knowledge of Microsoft Suite. You have persuasive and concise verbal and writing skills to write clear and precise analytical documents and to adapt communication style to different audiences. You have demonstrated teamwork ability to contribute to multidisciplinary project teams. You have ability to build and maintain positive relationships within and outside of UMB. You have ability to act independently and resourcefully. This position was posted on 10/23/2026. The application deadline for this position is 45 days from the posting date unless filled earlier.

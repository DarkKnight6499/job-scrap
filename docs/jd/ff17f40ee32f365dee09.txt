Associate, Global Client Group (Traditional Institutional Group)
Dimensional Fund Advisors
Location: Charlotte; Austin

Job Description: The Global Client Group (GCG) is Dimensional’s distribution arm, comprising sales channels that target a diverse client set. By putting the client first, we strive to transform the way the world invests, and our solutions are a testament to the value provided to our clients. GCG is responsible for developing new business relationships as well as managing current relationships with our global clients. We take the time to understand clients’ evolving needs, identifying where we can add value and collaborating to provide a better investment experience. Our Traditional Institutional Group is responsible for working with large institutional clients, institutional consultants, and insurance clients across the country, including defined benefit and defined contribution plans, foundations, endowments, family offices, and corporations. Our Associates provide relationship management and analytical support to the Sales & Client Service teams in business development and retention activities for our clients. They will gain an understanding of clients’ evolving needs, identify where we can add value and collaborate to provide a better investment experience, especially as it relates to growing our ESG, fixed income, ETF, and/or SMA offerings. With proven success, Associates will have the opportunity to work directly with clients, prospects and intermediaries to develop and expand personal and professional relationships. Responsibilities: Support the day-to-day activities of the team’s Regional Directors and Senior Associates. Prepare presentation materials to be used in meetings with institutional clients and prospects. Examples of deliverables include introductory presentations, portfolio reviews and follow-up materials. Compile performance data, portfolio commentary and analytical material in preparation for meetings or in response to client inquiries. Provide quality control for the data included in client reports and requests. Facilitate the completion of due diligence requests and client questionnaires. Help maintain CRM database. Build marketing lists for institutional luncheons, investment forums and conferences. Review and edit RFPs. Communicate daily with internal groups, including operations, portfolio management, research, legal, compliance and marketing.

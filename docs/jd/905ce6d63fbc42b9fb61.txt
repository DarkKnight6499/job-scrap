Principal Associate, Financial Planning & Analysis - Financial Services As a Principal Associate, Financial Analysis in the Financial Services division, you will work for a forward leaning organization that is actively disrupting the Auto finance industry through innovation and a customer-centric approach. If you thrive at the intersection of complex data, executive storytelling, and customer-centric strategy, this role will give you the platform to make a definitive impact. You will have the opportunity to partner with business customers on driving strategic business direction that leads to sustainable performance and you will be asked to challenge our current well-managed processes to be more proactive, effective and efficient. Financial Analysts utilize a wide array of tools and data to perform analysis to identify drivers and trends that impact the near and long term trajectory of our division. Associates have the resources and space to drive significant change across the team and organization. The ideal candidate will provide financial leadership and strong influence, communication, and problem solving competencies to effectively: Design Robust Analytical Frameworks : Go beyond the surface of the data. Build clear methodologies and conduct deep-dive portfolio diagnostics to uncover customer, channel trends and P&L impacts, translating raw metrics into the actionable insights that inform leadership's action plans. Drive Executive Storytelling : Transform complex financial models into compelling, "camera-ready" narratives. You will present data-driven insights to senior audiences, providing the analytical foundation required for real-time strategic debates. Act as a Strategic Partner : Collaborate seamlessly across the business to quantify the financial implications of ongoing initiatives. You will propose and utilize advanced analytical methods to help leaders optimize overall business performance. Navigate Ambiguity with Precision : Thrive in a dynamic environment by transforming open-ended questions and ad-hoc requests into structured methodologies and clear project plans under tight deadlines. Elevate the Team : Serve as an analytical sounding board and mentor to junior analysts, raising the technical bar and fostering a culture of continuous learning. General responsibilities include : Strategic Finance Partner : Establish trusted business partner relationships as the finance partner for the Head of Auto Servicing Customer Care & Auto Servicing CTO Model Strategic Sensitivities : Act as the analytical engine for our business partners. Leverage our modeling infrastructure to run complex P&L sensitivities, translating potential business decisions into actionable financial intelligence for senior leadership. Manage Complex Deliverables : Independently prioritize and drive large bodies of analytical work to completion, seamlessly managing stakeholder expectations and complex relationships within strict regulatory and business timelines. Basic Qualifications : Bachelor’s Degree or Military experience At least 3 years of experience in financial analysis, financial modeling or a combination Preferred Qualifications : Master’s Degree in Business Administration (MBA) or Master’s Degree in Finance Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) designation or Certified Public Accountant (CPA) license 6+ years of experience in financial analysis, financial modeling or a combination Proficiency in SQL and Python, alongside experience with modern data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, QuickSight) and familiarity with emerging AI tooling to drive analytical rigor and clear business insights At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position.

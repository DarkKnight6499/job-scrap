What You'll Contribute The Manager, Structured Finance will work closely with the VP of Structured Finance and other cross-functional teams to source, structure, and execute structured finance transactions. The successful candidate will have a strong track record in structured finance and the ability to collaborate effectively with others. What You'll Do Source and execute structured finance transactions in line with the company's overall business objectives Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including legal, risk management, and operations, to ensure the successful execution of structured finance transactions Perform due diligence on potential transactions, including financial analysis, cash flow modeling, and risk assessment Assist with structuring and negotiating transactions to meet the needs of clients and partners Develop and maintain relationships with clients, partners, and investors to support structured finance transactions Monitor market trends and competitive landscape to identify new opportunities and mitigate risk Ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations governing structured finance transactions Prepare reports and presentations for senior management and other stakeholders The above information is intended to describe the general nature and level of work performed by employees assigned to this job; it is not designed to contain or be interpreted as a comprehensive inventory of all duties, responsibilities and qualifications required of employees in this role.

Work Schedule Standard (Mon-Fri) Environmental Conditions Office Job Description Location/Division Specific Information Materials & Structural Analysis Division, Brno A day in the Life: Build and maintain specified financial deliverables and activities including OPEX Forecast model, AOP and other ad-hoc initiatives. Continuously analyze and monitor the Actual Spending position and actively participate in business decisions leading towards target. Take responsibility over closing tasks and activities, such as accruals creation, various journal entries and reconciliations. Create ad-hoc analyses at the request of the Direct Manager or Business Leaders Understand and Execute Internal controls in financial reporting, compliance and operations. Keys to Success: Demonstrate above average level of intensity and business acumen. Navigate through a multi-stakeholder environment. Demonstrate structured data-driven mindset and advocate for emerging technologies. Question status quo and looks for opportunities for improvement. Education High school diploma or equivalent required. Experience Experience of Capital equipment business is preferred. Recent history of working in a fast paced, growth-based business. Previous responsibility for forecasting also preferred but not crucial. Knowledge, Skills, Abilities Ability to work in dynamic environment is crucial.

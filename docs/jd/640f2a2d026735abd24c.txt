Job Description Key Responsibilities Lead the annual budget, long-range planning, and periodic forecasting processes, including timelines, assumptions, templates, and management reviews. Prepare monthly and quarterly management reporting, including variance analysis, key performance indicators, trend analysis, and actionable commentary. Partner with business leaders to evaluate performance, clarify financial implications, and support resource allocation and investment decisions. Develop and maintain integrated financial models, scenario analyses, and business cases for strategic initiatives. Identify financial risks and opportunities, quantify potential impacts, and recommend practical actions. Improve forecast accuracy, reporting efficiency, data quality, and planning processes through standardization and automation. Support executive and board-level materials with concise, decision-oriented financial insights. Collaborate with Accounting to ensure actual results, accruals, and management reporting are complete, accurate, and aligned. Establish consistent performance-management practices and maintain clear documentation of assumptions and methodologies. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, or a related field. Seven or more years of progressive finance experience, including significant experience in financial planning and analysis. Demonstrated ability to lead budgeting, forecasting, management reporting, and financial modeling activities. Advanced proficiency with Excel and experience working with enterprise planning, ERP, and business-intelligence tools. Strong understanding of financial statements, management accounting, and key business drivers. Excellent analytical, problem-solving, presentation, and written communication skills. Ability to manage competing priorities, meet deadlines, and influence stakeholders across functions and levels. Preferred Qualifications Experience improving planning processes or implementing financial systems and reporting solutions. Experience in a complex, multi-entity, or international organization. Core Competencies Strategic thinking and commercial judgment Financial analysis and business acumen Executive-ready communication and storytelling Cross-functional partnership and influence Process improvement and systems orientation Ownership, integrity, and attention to detail Business Need and Success Measures Business need: Strengthen financial planning, increase visibility into business performance, and provide leaders with timely, reliable insights to guide decisions. Success in the first 12 months: Deliver an effective budget and forecast cycle; improve forecast accuracy and variance explanations; establish consistent management reporting; strengthen partnerships with business leaders; and implement measurable improvements in planning efficiency and data quality.

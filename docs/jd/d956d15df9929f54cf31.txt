Proactively identifies unique problems and seeks help to resolve them , if necessary. Responsible for adherence to the company's framework of internal controls. Conducts business process and data analysis to align high-level requirements to business initiatives. Prepares “as is” and “current state” documentation in written and graphical form (flows). Facilitates the elicitation of business and systems requirements. Develop new or revised processes or procedures in lieu of system development. Analyze business unit requests and document business requirements, work with various technical staff members to determine possible solutions and communicate business and system impacts management . Analysis business data and transformation rules , document requirements, and prepare documentation as needed for t echnical teams . Serve as a liaison to business unit s when gathering requirements .

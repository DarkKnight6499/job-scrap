We’re looking for a Staff Quantitative UX Researcher to help define and raise the quality of experiences across Etsy. You will lead a quantitative research and measurement practice — grounded in rigorous survey design, statistical analysis, and experimental methods — to help build buyer and seller experiences that are usable, distinctive, and polished. You will establish a shared, quantitatively grounded definition of experience quality and build the measurement systems and statistical models needed to assess it consistently. You’ll benchmark core Etsy buyer and seller flows using validated metrics and longitudinal data, identify the most consequential usability gaps, and help teams understand — with statistical confidence — whether modernization efforts are significantly improving the customer experience. This is a highly collaborative and influential individual contributor role. You will work across Etsy with Product, Design, Engineering, Analytics, and Research, translating quantitative evidence into clear priorities, standards, and recommendations. You will own a cross-functional measurement program, not simply a series of individual studies. This is a full-time position reporting to a Director of Research. What’s this team like at Etsy? The UX Foundations team helps Etsy build usable, distinctive, and polished experiences across our marketplace. The team develops the systems, patterns, guidance, and shared standards that enable product teams to deliver high-quality experiences at scale. This role will work across Buyer and Seller experiences rather than supporting a single product area. We are focused on questions such as: What defines a high-quality Etsy experience across different customer journeys, and how do we measure it quantitatively? Where are the most consequential usability and consistency gaps across Etsy, and how do we size their impact statistically? How do Etsy’s core buyer and seller flows compare with ecommerce experience standards, based on benchmarked, quantitative data? How can we measure — with statistically valid methods — whether modernization makes experiences more usable, distinctive, and polished? What repeatable, quantitative benchmarks and guidance will help teams make better product decisions? What does the day-to-day look like? Define experience quality and set the quantitative research and measurement strategy for UX modernization across Etsy. Design and lead a repeatable benchmarking program for core Etsy buyer and seller flows, using structured surveys, validated scales, and statistical models to measure experiences over time and against ecommerce experience standards. Establish important baselines and success measures spanning usability, task completion, efficiency, customer perception, and other relevant experience-quality signals, using appropriate statistical methods (e.g., regression, ANOVA, factor analysis) to validate and interpret them. Lead quantitative research — including large-scale surveys, experiments, and statistical modeling — complemented by qualitative methods, to identify and prioritize experience gaps and evaluate modernized product experiences. Integrate survey data, behavioral/log data, customer-experience tracking, competitive intelligence, and existing Etsy research into unified statistical analyses. Translate complex quantitative evidence into clear recommendations that influence product roadmaps, design standards, and investment decisions across teams. Create scalable statistical methods, measurement tools, reporting practices, and guidance that enable teams to assess experience quality consistently and with rigor. Partner with and mentor researchers across Etsy, strengthening quantitative and mixed-methods practice and elevating the organization’s approach to usability measurement. Of course, this is just a sample of the kinds of work this role will require. You should assume that your role will encompass other tasks, too, and that your job duties and responsibilities may change from time to time at Etsy’s discretion, or otherwise applicable with local law. Qualities that will help you thrive in this role are: 7+ years conducting product research, with a strong emphasis on quantitative methods. Deep expertise in quantitative research: survey design and psychometrics, sampling methodology, statistical analysis (e.g., regression, ANOVA/MANOVA, factor analysis), and experimental design (e.g., A/B testing), including interpreting comparative or longitudinal data. Hands-on experience building and validating measurement frameworks and benchmarking programs, including defining KPIs and success metrics grounded in statistical evidence. Proficiency with statistical and survey tooling (e.g., R, Python, SQL, Qualtrics or similar) and the judgment to choose the right quantitative — and, where useful, qualitative — approach to sophisticated questions. Deep knowledge of usability research and the ability to translate an abstract concept like experience quality into clear, statistically measurable dimensions. A track record of developing durable, quantitatively grounded research programs, not just individual study plans, across complex and ambiguous problem spaces. Ability to connect customer evidence, product data, and external signals into a clear point of view and actionable, data-backed recommendations. Strong communication and influence skills, including aligning senior partners and communicating statistical findings in terms of decisions, priorities, and tradeoffs. Experience with design systems, usability measurement, benchmarking, and large-scale UX transformation is helpful.

Job Description: As the Technology Delivery Leader, you have a deep Scaled Agile understanding with a skill for coordinating large and sophisticated workstreams across an organization. You are deeply committed to the quality and value delivered to our customers by ensuring proven planning practices are in place, execution of the work is well-coordinated, and owning the delivery all the way to production. You have an Agile mindset while modeling the Agile principles, Scrum values and ensuring work is happening within the Scrum and Scaled Agile Framework. You will communicate and collaborate closely with Squad Leaders/Product Owners, Product Area Leaders/Product Managers, Scrum Masters, Chapter Leaders/People Managers, Architects, and other Stakeholders to ensure dependencies are managed, impediments are removed or escalated and there is visibility into the work being done and how the program is performing. You will ensure technical and quality best practices are followed as well as help coordinate large end-to-end testing efforts and release management for your workstream. You are dedicated to ensuring we are working in the most effective and efficient ways, continuously looking for ways to improve the way we work while also coaching, teaching, and mentoring others on Agile and Scaled standard methodologies. Technical Expertise Leads release management practices within the initiative to support the larger solution. Planning and Execution Accountable for orchestration of planning preparation and execution of Quarterly Planning activities for the initiatives.

ABOUT THE TEAM The Institutional Distribution Support team sits within the broader Institutional Sales & Service team at Parametric and consists of associates and coordinators who act as the internal liaisons between external facing directors and all other teams at Parametric to ensure that requests from clients, consultants, and prospects are handled quickly, efficiently, and with a high level of white-glove service. The team is also responsible for management of the CRM (Salesforce), data and analytics, and continued process improvements for the distribution team. ABOUT THE ROLE The Client and Consultant Relations Associate (CRA) supports Parametric's efforts to build and maintain business with investment consultants to effectively execute our business strategy and achieve new business goals. The CRA coordinates closely with senior members of the team and other internal groups to provide detailed firm and investment strategy information to consultants. In addition, the CRA will collaborate extensively with Sales and Relationship Management to create and convert opportunities via investment consultants, and provide excellent day-to-day support for this important distribution channel.

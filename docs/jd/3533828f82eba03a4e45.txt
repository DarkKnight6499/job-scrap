Job Description: At Regions, the Credit Risk Review Examiner continuously monitors and examines activities, assessing and reporting the status and direction of credit risk within assigned portfolios. This role identifies key credit risk trends and evaluates origination quality, portfolio performance, and credit risk management practices through analysis of credit metrics, transaction testing, and ongoing engagement with first- and second-line business and risk partners. This position identifies issues requiring management attention and communicates review findings through formal reporting on risk rating accuracy, policy compliance, and sound credit risk management practices. Primary Responsibilities Maintains a working knowledge of sound lending practices, lending regulations, and the bank’s loan policy and underwriting guidelines Reviews credit exposures to provide assurance of compliance with Regions' credit policies, and safe and sound banking practices Participates and/or takes a leadership role in credit risk review examinations, which may include horizontal reviews across lines of business and geographies Develops and maintains positive working relationships with line of business management, credit risk management, audit staff, and other departments Analyzes trends in key quality and performance metrics, reviews transactions, and assesses processes associated with the origination and servicing of assigned loan portfolios Identifies and monitors the evolution of key risk direction drivers for assigned portfolios Performs ad hoc analysis of credit risk issues independently, frequently requiring aggregation of transaction data through the use of spreadsheet or database software Maintains specialized knowledge, as required for assigned lines of business, which may include expertise required to assess periodic validation of credit scoring models, understanding portfolio valuation techniques, etc. Proposes appropriate ratings and risk direction assessments and participates in developing findings and conclusions, presenting results to senior managers, and completing written reports within departmental guidelines Maintains documentation to support review process and findings Maintains a thorough knowledge of credit risk review procedures, sound lending practices, credit policies, and regulatory requirements Offers advice and guidance to junior examiners, assisting in the development of necessary skills and the promotion of continuous improvement initiatives Collaborates with senior management in the development of team goals and objectives, ensuring consistent achievement May serve as the lead examiner for assigned line(s) of business for continuous monitoring of credit risk conducted between examinations This position is exempt from timekeeping requirements under the Fair Labor Standards Act and is not eligible for overtime pay. This position is incentive eligible.

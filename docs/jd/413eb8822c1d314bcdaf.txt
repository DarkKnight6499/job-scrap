What You'll Be Doing Join a team that supports Financial Advisors and branch associates with margin and brokerage account needs across the firm. As a Margin Operations Associate, you’ll learn to review accounts, research margin requirements, resolve account issues, and provide accurate and timely support. You’ll gain hands-on experience with both margin and cash accounts, buying power, trading activity, and account requirements while working alongside experienced team members. Success in this role comes from attention to detail, strong problem-solving skills, teamwork, and a willingness to learn. This is a great opportunity to build a strong foundation in financial services while developing specialized knowledge in brokerage operations, trading, and margin. What We're Looking For Notify branch offices of action items and regulatory deadlines. Assist in enforcing firm policies and industry regulations. Research and review outstanding debit balances and assist in resolving trade exceptions. Document trading violations and apply account restrictions as appropriate. Develop effective communication skills within the department. Provide timely updates on progression of job responsibilities to leadership. Demonstrate the ability to work collaboratively within a team. What You'll Bring Demonstrate the ability to attain a general understanding of industry rules/regulations and Stifel policies that pertain to the department. Strong attention to detail skills to minimize risk to the firm. Initiative and self-direction when performing tasks with the ability to organize and prioritize effectively in order to meet deadlines. Able to handle large workloads that vary based on heavy volume periods. Ability to work autonomously throughout the day based on performance criteria provided by leadership. Education & Experience Minimum Required: Bachelor’s Degree or equivalent experience. Minimum Required: 1+ years professional business related experience.

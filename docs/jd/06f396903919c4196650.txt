Job Description: Job Title: Associate Location: Cary, NC Overview DB Global Technology, Inc. seeks an Associate in Cary, NC to design and implement high-quality, high-performance, and resilient technology solutions. Requires a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science or related field or equivalent and five (5) years of progressively responsible experience creating test plans and test cases for Functional Testing and Integration Testing; developing manual and automated test plans and test scripts; executing test cases for large and complex business processes; using HP ALM/QC for Test Planning, Test Execution and Defect Management; creating BDD tests using Cucumber; performing planning, scheduling and coordination of UAT activities with business users; executing UAT Scripts with business users and logging defects in HP ALM/QC; performing protractor testing for end-to-end test frameworks; and writing and executing SQL queries for back-end data validations. Salary range: $119,987.00 – $140,000.00/year. This position is eligible for Deutsche Bank's Employee Referral Incentive Program.

Department Overview Apprise by Walker & Dunlop , a wholly owned subsidiary of W&D, changes how our clients are served and delivers an elevated valuations experience. We drive faster, better valuation decisions and add value at every step of a deal. We combine industry-leading multifamily underwriting experience, data science, and analytics technology prowess. Our platform leverages the skills of the most trusted MAI-designated valuation experts in the U.S. and we are actively developing the appraisal tools of the future. The next generation of valuations experts manifests within Apprise, adopting and driving technology to become the nation's largest multifamily valuations company, while providing valuations service for many other specialties and sectors. The Impact You Will Have As an Apprise Director, you will play a key role in delivering high-quality appraisal and consulting services while supporting business growth and client relationships across a variety of markets. You will succeed by managing appraisal assignments from engagement through delivery, mentoring team members, and ensuring exceptional client service. You will collaborate with appraisers, research analysts, clients, and business leaders to deliver accurate valuations and drive operational excellence. Primary Responsibilities Assist in the production and review of appraisal and consulting assignments across multiple markets. Manage client relationships throughout the appraisal lifecycle, including engagement, due diligence, report delivery, and revision requests. Coordinate the procurement of due diligence materials required to complete appraisal assignments. Conduct property inspections, market research, and competitive analyses. Train, mentor, and review the work of team members, as needed. Provide guidance and support to staff appraisers and research analysts throughout the project lifecycle. Contribute to the enhancement of proprietary valuation software by providing user feedback and recommendations. Develop and maintain client relationships to support business growth nationwide. Communicate and collaborate effectively with teammates, clients, vendors, and other stakeholders, including travel as needed. Perform other duties as assigned.

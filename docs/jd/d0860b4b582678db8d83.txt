Provides leadership vital to implement Global EHSS programs. Develops, monitors, and coordinates Company EHSS programs in accordance with federal, state, and local regulations. Initiates and maintains extensive contacts with key managers and officials of other organizations and regulatory agencies, requiring skill in persuasion and negotiation of critical issues. The successful candidate will: Develop and manage EHSS risk management and integration into projects and programs, raising awareness and ensuring compliance with applicable safety codes, policies and regulations. Demonstrate functional expertise for EHSS programs including but not limited to, hazard communication, risk management, high-risk processes, hazardous gas, and chemical management. Drive continuous improvement of EHSS managed programs and work with clients to drive an actively caring safety culture. Identify hazards and apply practical, risk-based solutions to reduce exposure to serious injuries and high-consequence events. Develop and implement risk plans, procedures, and controls; verify that controls are effective and documented. Primary responsibilities include: Own and drive the execution of EHSS risk management ensuring effective implementation, compliance, and continuous improvement across operations. Implement and maintain site-level EHSS programs such as but not limited to, hazard communication, risk management, high-risk processes, hazardous gas and chemical management. Conduct site EHSS risk assessments and maintain the EHSS Risk Registers. Provide EHSS input to site projects, including EHSS participation in Process Hazard Analyses and design reviews. Maintain accurate records of risk reviews, safety data and provide periodic updates as required. Collaborate with Industrial Hygienist, operations and global EHSS Teams to review new products and processes. Investigate and implement continuous improvement processes required to improve EHSS in manufacturing operations Partners with clients to implement environmental, health and safety policies, practices, and requirements identified as part of risk assessments.

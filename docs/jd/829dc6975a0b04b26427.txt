Avery Dennison Corporation (NYSE: AVY) is a global materials science and digital identification solutions company. We are Making Possible™ products and solutions that help advance the industries we serve, providing branding and information solutions that optimize labor and supply chain efficiency, reduce waste and mitigate loss, advance sustainability, circularity and transparency and better connect brands and consumers. We design and develop labeling and functional materials, radio-frequency identification (RFID) inlays and tags, software applications that connect the physical and digital and offerings that enhance branded packaging and carry or display information that improves the customer experience. About the role: Avery Dennison’s North America Sales Early Career Program (SECP) has a single business focus to build talent for our world class organization. With a concentrated blend of experiential on-the-job learning and classroom and virtual training, the SECP serves as the talent pipeline for roles throughout the organization. We are seeking dynamic and agile individuals eager for a lifelong career in sales to join our Sales Early Career Program. SECP Associates will gain in-depth exposure through various assignments over 24-30 months. The initial 6-9 months will be spent in our Corporate offices in Mentor, OH, followed by rotations into sales regions across the East, West, or Mid-West. Key Responsibilities: Gain a strong sales foundation: Sales Associates will craft the key skills and proficiencies needed to thrive in the sales industry & develop technical product knowledge Work cross-functionally to gain business insights and be exposed to various aspects of the business Develop key skills: Build skills in areas such as pricing, relationship management, negotiation, time management, and strategic business planning Presenting products/services: Effectively communicating the value proposition to potential customers Closing sales: Negotiating deals and finalizing sales and transaction Bachelor’s Degree in Business, or related field required with Sales minor or certificate a plus. Previous sales internship or co-op experience required or relevant work experience Experience via student organizations, such as Alpha Kappa Psi, Association of Latino Professionals of America, Multicultural Business Association, etc. Volunteerism or similar activities Must have reliable transportation to and from work Ability and willingness to travel approximately 60–70% for business.

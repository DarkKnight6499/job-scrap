Join the Commercial & Investment Bank Finance & Business Management Cross-border Payments Business Management team as a Senior Associate, helping shape and execute our growth strategy for a critical international payments franchise. As a Senior Associate in the Commercial & Investment Bank Finance & Business Management Cross-border Payments Business Management team, you will optimize business performance by forwarding key initiatives. We act as trusted advisors and counterweights to Business/Group Heads. This high-impact position involves conducting robust analytics to support senior product leaders, ensuring transparency around business performance drivers, and managing end-to-end business activities. As trusted advisors, we identify, escalate, and mitigate business risks, making this role essential to our success. This role offers high visibility and the opportunity to work closely with a wide variety of stakeholders and senior business leads.

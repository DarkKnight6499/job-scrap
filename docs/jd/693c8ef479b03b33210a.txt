Your Team, Your Impact We are looking for a System Level Electrical Validation Engineer to join our Marvell Switch Group. In this role, you will be a key member of team that responsible for the validation of high bandwidth Ethernet switching ASICs. What You Can Expect Execute and develop comprehensive functional testing of high-bandwidth Ethernet switching silicon systems to verify proper behavior, adherence to required specifications, and performance across all spec-defined conditions. Drive the debug of complex switching silicon and system platforms with a strong emphasis on Layer-1 issues, including link-up flows, state-of-the-art SerDes, signal integrity, link stability, and analog front-end behavior. Work closely with software developers, hardware design engineers, architects, and operations teams to investigate system-level issues and accelerate root-cause identification. Stay current with modern automation tools, validation methodologies, and industry best practices, proactively identifying opportunities to enhance existing validation flows and efficiency. What We're Looking For Requirements: Bachelor's/Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Electro-Optics, or related fields. 10+ years of related professional experience. Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, Java, or C#. Experience with automation and data analysis tools. Strong understanding of high-speed SerDes and equalization techniques. 5+ years' experience with High-Speed I/O testing, debugging, and validation. Problem-Solving: Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to troubleshoot and resolve complex issues. Attention to Detail: High attention to detail to ensure accuracy and reliability of automated systems. Communication: Excellent communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with cross-functional teams. Advantages: Experience with Electro-Optic systems. Deep understanding of L1/L2 Ethernet layers and standards. Knowledge of Memory, PCIe, and Ethernet systems interfaces. Hands-on test and validation experience of analog/digital and signal/power integrity using lab equipment such as high-speed oscilloscopes, BERT analyzers, TDR/VNA, etc. Familiarity with board CAD tools, including Cadence and Allegro.

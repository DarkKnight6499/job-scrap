Responsibilities This role will be based in our Hamilton, NJ office. We offer a hybrid work schedule with 4 days in the office; and 1 day remote where it makes sense to do so. The Business Analyst (BA) for the Program Services Team is responsible for assessing and documenting the group’s workflow and systems needs to support the operations and growth of the business. BA will work with the various operating teams which interface with and support the Program Services Team and Stop Loss Division to identify workflow and data management needs, assess the impact of the change, capture and document requirements and then partner with each area’s operational leaders to ensure those requirements are delivered, all while supporting the Company through the implementation process. What you can expect: Culture of innovation, teamwork, supportive colleagues and leaders willing to invest in talent Internal mobility opportunities Visibility to senior leaders and partnership with cross functional teams Opportunity to impact change Benefits – competitive compensation, paid time off, comprehensive wellness benefits and programs, employer funded health savings account, profit sharing, 401k, paid parental leave, employee stock purchase plan, tuition assistance and professional continuing education We'll count on you to: Lead ongoing reviews of business processes and proposal/development of optimization strategies. Gather and document data management risks and needs of the business to investigate and propose solutions with business case rationale. Construct workflow charts and diagrams; studying business process and system capabilities. Gathers job specifications and business rules, and technical deliverables by directly interfacing with the business stakeholders upfront, then regroup to recommend solutions, and assist in the implementation and user training for roll-out of any new solutions. Translate stakeholder business requirements to technical documentation for implementation Responsible for supporting the business stakeholders in creating detailed reports to support business operations relating to projects, financial management, KPI’s and forecasting. Maintain current documentation as required for business continuity and management, including but not limited to data formats, requirements, specifications, project requests, project plans and related information. Directly assist with automation and/or digital transformation initiatives Ensure coordination with the IT team, compliance with corporate standards and procedures and manages any statement of work documents Responsible for participating with the business stakeholders in the management of any external vendor solutions or development resources related to proposed solutions.

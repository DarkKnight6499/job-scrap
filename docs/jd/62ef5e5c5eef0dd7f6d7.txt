About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking an Equipment finance Underwriting Manager, supporting the Dealer and Floorplan space within the Commercial Vehicle Group (CVG) . This role will focus on developing new business, growing existing client relationships, and partnering with credit and internal teams to structure and execute tailored financing solutions. The WFEF Commercial Vehicle Group (CVG) Underwriter is responsible for the underwriting and management of titled vehicle vendor, leasing or finance, and third party origination programs where there is high reliance on the program counterparty. This includes managing floorplan relationships with transportation equipment dealers.

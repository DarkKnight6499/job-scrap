Company : Highmark Health Job Description : JOB SUMMARY This job is a key role in the oversight of enterprise investment portfolios managed by external managers and advisors. The portfolios include insurance reserve assets, operating assets, pension plan assets, and savings plan assets that require an understanding of the unique cash flow, risk, legal and regulatory aspects of each portfolio to properly construct and monitor portfolio solutions. ESSENTIAL RESPONSIBILITIES Perform regular due diligence on external investment managers, investment products, and prospective investments. Monitor and evaluate existing portfolio cashflows and holdings and provide insights on performance and risks to various stakeholders Conduct fundamental research and analysis on investment opportunities and market developments Coordinate the efficient movement of capital when rebalancing, hiring or firing investment managers to minimize transaction costs Support treasury and investment reporting activities to Highmark Health executive management, Investment Committee of the Board, ratings agencies and other parties Continue to update and evolve key performance indicators and risk analytics used to evaluate investment portfolio performance Perform custodial account maintenance and fee payment processing duties Other duties as assigned or requested. EDUCATION Minimum Bachelor's degree in finance, economics, or mathematics or relevant experience and/or education as determined by the company in lieu of bachelor’s degree. Preferred None EXPERIENCE Minimum 5-10 years of relevant, progressive experience in the area of specialization Preferred Demonstrated experience evaluating and monitoring externally managed investment portfolios across one or more asset classes (e.g. public equity, fixed income, private equity, real assets) Proven experience preparing and presenting investment materials to governance bodies, including investment committees or executive leadership LICENSES/CERTIFICATIONS Minimum None Preferred Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) Certified Treasury Professional (CTP) Certified Public Accountant (CPA) SKILLS Strong written and oral communication skills Strong relationship building skills Client focused with strong business acumen Self-starter with the ability to work under pressure independently and as part of a team Ability to think strategically and act proactively to create strong trust and confidence with business units Strong innovative problem-solving capabilities SCOPE OF RESPONSIBILITY Does this role supervise/manage other employees?

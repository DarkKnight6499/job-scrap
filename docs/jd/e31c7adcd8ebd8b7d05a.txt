About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Vice-President level Portfolio Manager for the Technology, Media & Telecom (TMT) team within Wells Fargo Corporate & Investment Banking. This TMT Lead CIB Portfolio Manager will partner with Industry Coverage, IG Syndications, Leveraged Finance, Leveraged Underwriting, Macro, Global Payments & Liquidity, and Risk Management to oversee the execution of credit related functions and oversight on a portfolio of private and public companies. The TMT team manages relationships with leading global companies that operate within a wide variety of sub-sectors, including Software, Internet/Digital, Hardware, Semiconductor, Media & Telecom, Data & Analytics, and FinTech. In this role, you will: Manage and oversee credit aspects of a broad portfolio of public and private companies across the TMT sector Perform detailed credit, financial statement analysis, industry analysis and projection modeling to ensure accurate and timely monitoring, risk ratings, covenant compliance, watchlist updates, recognition of potential problems loans of leveraged credit portfolio Lead coordination and execution of loan booking, closing, legal documentation, compliance requirements, and final credit memos Partner with senior bankers to prepare and present portfolio updates to senior leadership, risk management and coverage partners Partner with Corporate Banking Relationship Management, Industry Coverage and Debt Capital Markets Bankers, and other product partners to support client coverage needs related to credit products. Lead credit analysis support and execution to grow relationships through products such as rates/fx/operating exposure for cash management. Engage with clients as needed On-going coordination and partnership with Industrials Independent Risk Management related to borrower and/or portfolio needs Assist with coordination and preparation for borrower/portfolio reviews and responses for internal and external exams Directly or indirectly manage, mentor and train junior team members Responsible for adherence to all Wells Fargo credit policies and procedures.

Associate Director, Advanced Manufacturing
Boston Dynamics
Location: Waltham, MA

Boston Dynamics is looking for an Associate Director, Advanced Manufacturing to join our Prototype Operations Team. . This role leads manufacturing engineering and advanced manufacturing for Prototype Operations, building the capabilities that turn new designs into working hardware. The Associate Director heads a team of manufacturing engineers and advanced manufacturing engineers who concept, design, implement, and sustain those capabilities - processes, tooling, fixtures, equipment, automation, and assembly and test stations - from quantity zero through prototype production volume. Because Prototype Operations sits within the Product & Technology organization, this team works shoulder to shoulder with design engineering. The Associate Director owns the loop between design and build: bringing DFx into designs early, feeding build learnings back quickly, and making sure products and processes hit their targets for the optimal balance of speed and quality to enable development. Beyond today's builds, the role prepares processes proven in prototype to scale or transfer to high-volume production. It also shapes the internal prototype operations capabilities roadmap, grows engineering talent, and informs long-term product and operational decisions. Boston Dynamics is a world leader in mobile robots, tackling some of the toughest robotics challenges. For years, our awe-inspiring viral videos on YouTube have shown the world what remarkably capable robots can do. Now we are quickly becoming a recognized leader in automation solutions for industrial applications and warehouse logistics. How you will make an impact: Provide strategic and technical leadership to manufacturing engineering and advanced manufacturing engineering teams across the full capability lifecycle: concept, design, build, qualification, and sustaining. This may include an industrial engineer and a packaging engineer too. Own build readiness for prototype production: process flows, work instructions, tooling, fixtures, equipment, line layouts, and capacity plans that meet build schedules and volume targets. Set and execute the advanced manufacturing roadmap; evaluate, pilot, and deploy new processes and automation (e.g., automated assembly, precision alignment, additive, laser welding, precision polishing) that improves in-house development speed for engineering. Embed manufacturing engineering in the design process: lead DFx reviews and provide manufacturability, assembly, test, and serviceability input at early product development gates. Close the loop between build and design - capture build issues, drive root cause and corrective action with Design, Quality, and Test, and manage the manufacturing impact of engineering changes. Define and drive manufacturing engineering processes, standards, and metrics (capacity, readiness, first-pass yield, cycle time, TAKT time, process capability) to support data-driven continuous improvement. Prepare processes for scale and transfer: lightweight PFMEAs, control plans, process validation, and documentation that allow prototype processes to move to high-volume production internally or at contract manufacturers. Manage external partners - integrators, equipment suppliers, and contract manufacturers - including SOWs, RFQs, design reviews, and acceptance. Develop resource plans, capital budgets, program priorities, and operational risk mitigation strategies to support business goals and product timelines. Represent Manufacturing Engineering in senior program reviews, build readiness reviews, operational forums, and business planning. Build, lead, and coach high-performing teams; hire key talent and provide development plans and career paths for managers, engineers, and technical leads. Influence long-term product and technology decisions by contributing manufacturing insight to roadmap and platform planning.

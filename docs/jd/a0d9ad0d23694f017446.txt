What You'll Be Doing The Benefits Associate supports the administration and delivery of the Firm's employee benefit program. The Benefits Associate serves as a key resource for associates regarding benefit plans, enrollment, eligibility and claims. The Benefits Associate is responsible for supporting day-to-day tasks and assists with the communication and facilitates effective administration for the Firm. What We're Looking For Explain the features of the Firm's benefit policies, plans, and enrollment procedures to employees. Communicate, verbally and in writing, all aspects of the benefits program and respond to basic questions from employees concerning plan content, including handling and researching Tier 2 benefits issues. Responsible for processing Tuition/Certification/Designation questions, requests, and payments. Assist with organizing, explaining, and distributing annual, or merger related, open enrollment materials and the enrollment process. Assist with wellbeing projects and events. Process benefit terminations, life events, and new hire benefits in HRIS. Process monthly benefit and other related vendor invoices. Assist with auditing enrollment and vendor eligibility/funding files for the benefit programs. Act as liaison to the health, welfare, and 401(k) third party administrators and work with vendors to provide notifications and coordinate meetings. What You'll Bring Experience with health and welfare day-to-day operations and benefit plan administration. Ability to communicate and present to all levels of the Firm. Proven ability to understand the causes, costs, and potential outcomes of issues and problems, and how to implement information or formulate corrective actions. Education & Experience Minimum Required: High School Degree or equivalent work experience. Minimum Required: 1+ years of benefit experience. Licenses & Credentials Minimum Required: None.

AECOM is looking for an experienced Associate Director to join our Auckland Buildings & Places team. This is an opportunity for a senior engineering or building-services professional who has moved into design leadership, project management and multidisciplinary delivery — and is ready to have a bigger influence on projects, clients and people. You'll lead some of the most complex building projects across healthcare, aviation, defence, data centres, education and commercial , bringing together engineers, architects and specialist teams to deliver integrated solutions. You'll have the scale of AECOM's New Zealand, Australian and global network behind you, while having genuine influence over how projects are delivered and how our people develop. What you'll be doing Lead multidisciplinary design and delivery on complex building projects. Bring together engineering, architectural and specialist disciplines to solve complex challenges. Lead design outcomes, programme, commercial performance, quality and risk. Work directly with clients and build long-term relationships. Lead, mentor and develop senior engineers and emerging leaders. Support major bids, proposals and business development. Connect clients and project teams with AECOM's wider technical expertise. What we're looking for You might currently be a Mechanical / Electrical / Building Services Engineering Manager, Principal Engineer, Design Manager, Project Manager, Project Director or Associate Director. What's important is that you've moved beyond your individual discipline and have experience leading people, coordinating multiple disciplines and taking ownership of complex project outcomes. You'll bring: Significant experience in a building-services, engineering consultancy or multidisciplinary environment. Experience leading complex building projects and multidisciplinary teams. Strong client, commercial and project management skills. The ability to influence senior stakeholders and bring people together. A genuine interest in developing people and growing a high-performing team. A relevant engineering or built environment qualification. Chartered status is advantageous. You don't need to tick every box. If you're a senior engineering leader who is ready to step into a broader role — or already operating at this level and looking for greater scale and opportunity — we'd like to hear from you.

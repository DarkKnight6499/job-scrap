listed below – Information Architecture Vision: Establish the Enterprise Information Architecture vision within the context of business strategy and in alignment with overall Technology & Strategy direction. Translate strategic requirements into a usable enterprise information architecture, which may include an enterprise data model, common business vocabulary, ontology, and taxonomies, to guide enterprise solution development and achieve consistency of information assets. Create an information architecture consistent with the business, application, and technical architectures. Facilitate the mapping and traceability of information assets as they flow from source datastores to targets, thus ensuring optimal transparency, quality, consistency, and controlled redundancy. Information Architecture Delivery: Work with and mentor delivery teams to provide data solutions that are in keeping with the enterprise information architecture. This may include: Assessing suitability of vendor solutions to meet enterprise information requirements. Construct, refine and maintain information models, as needed, to meet business requirements and drive data designs for operational and analytical solutions. Facilitate the definition of integrative and canonical views of data or information across multiple content types to support convergence of information asset migration planning or impact analysis. Ensure new development is consistent and well-integrated with the established enterprise information architecture, using metadata management and/or a repository to promote reuse and standards adoption. Review corporate data sources for new business information and better data sources. Assist in post-implementation improvement efforts to enhance performance and provide increased functionality. Participate in key project design reviews as part of the methodology process to ensure application designs adhere to enterprise information architecture guidelines. Provide oversight for the BI Data Warehouse. Design an enterprise-wide integrated Data Warehouse for Business Intelligence by modeling new and enhancing existing BIDW datamarts. Establish logical and physical models, maintain metadata, and guide integration engineers for the physical datamart schema and logical integration (ETL) processes. Assist, as required, with testing and issue resolution. Establish and enhance governance processes for the BI Data Warehouse (naming conventions, conformed dimensions and facts, meta-models, security configuration) to ensure a consistent implementation across all datamarts. On large implementation projects, deliver project artifacts including: Data Integration Inventory, 360 Data Integration Diagram, Core structures diagram, Data Integration Requirements document and Test Plans, Report Inventory, Reporting Gap Analysis and Strategy, and Historical Data Strategy Business Intelligence Team Leadership: Provide leadership, oversight, and quality assurance for BI Team and team deliverables. This may include: Manage and coach BI team members in delivery of high-quality business intelligence solutions. Assist to estimate effort / duration for new requests and change orders. Track work queue, assign and adjust priorities and due dates based on client requirements and DT Leadership direction. Provide BI team with visibility to DT projects and project pipeline. Provide a forum for BI team collaboration on BI and Data Warehouse design and technical challenges. Establish, document, and maintain best practices for Business Intelligence and Data Warehousing.

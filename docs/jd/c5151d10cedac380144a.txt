Job Summary “Depending upon the qualifications of the successful applicant, the hiring manager may elect to fill this position at a higher level within the job hierarchy.” This position is for an engineer, geologist, or environmental scientist who would provide technical and regulatory support to power generation facilities in Indiana and Kentucky, with a primary focus on groundwater, industrial waste, landfills, and coal combustion residuals (CCR). The position may be filled at the Associate EHS Consultant or EHS Consultant level based on the selected candidate’s education and experience. This is an excellent opportunity for a recent graduate or early-career engineer, geologist, or environmental scientist to gain hands-on experience with groundwater investigations, landfill and CCR compliance, permitting, data analysis, and environmental reporting. The successful candidate will work alongside experienced environmental professionals and may have opportunities to pursue Professional Engineer (PE) or Professional Geologist (PG) licensure. Students who will complete their degree before the anticipated start date and recent graduates are encouraged to apply.

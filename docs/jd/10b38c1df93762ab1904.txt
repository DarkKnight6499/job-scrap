SS&C is a leading provider of mission-critical, AI-powered technology and services empowering financial services and healthcare organizations to work smarter, faster, and securely. More than 23,000 financial services and healthcare organizations, from the world's largest companies to small and mid-market firms, rely on SS&C for expertise, scale, and technology. Job Description Job Title: Senior Associate, Operations Locations: Edina, MN (hybrid) or Evansville, IN (hybrid) About the Role Join our team and play a key role in delivering high-quality fund accounting and operational services to investment fund clients. In this role, you’ll oversee fund reconciliations, trade processing, valuations, NAV reporting, and client deliverables while serving as a trusted client contact and mentoring junior team members. Why Join SS&C SS&C combines proprietary technology with deep industry expertise to support complex financial and health care operations. Our teams design, implement, and operate solutions that help clients manage data, automate processes, and scale their businesses with confidence. You will work with industry experts, modern platforms, and evolving technologies, gaining exposure to real-world operational challenges and large-scale enterprise environments. How You Will Make an Impact Posting journal entries to complete required reporting Preparing and reviewing cash and investment position reconciliations Preparing and reviewing dividend, interest and expense reconciliations Processing trades, corporate actions and accrual entries Resolution of trade, position and cash breaks Pricing of securities and valuation reviews Preparing and review accurate and timely Net Asset Valuation packages Preparing and review income and performance allocation calculations Preparation of performance reporting for clients Communicate and respond to Client/LP/Audit requests Principle point of contact for clients on Operations team issues Mentoring junior team members Liaise with other offices and develop cross training processes and plans Participate in sales process and lead demo's of Operations systems Other general duties as determined by management Required Experience Bachelor’s degree in Accounting or relevant field 3-5+ years of experience within the fund administration industry (operations/MBO background a plus) Good understanding of GAAP accounting for investment funds Good understanding of trade settlement, collateral and treasury processes Being a Team Player with a positive attitude Strong Communication skills, oral and written Proficient with Advent Products, Bloomberg and Microsoft Office Products Understand the importance of excellent customer service The ability to work in a fast-paced, deadline driven environment Join SS&C, where innovation meets global opportunities.

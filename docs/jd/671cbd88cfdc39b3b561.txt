At Thermo Fisher Scientific, you’ll join a global organization at the forefront of clinical research and laboratory science. Join our collaborative team as a Portfolio Manager at Thermo Fisher Scientific, where you'll advance critical initiatives that enable our customers to make the world healthier, cleaner, and safer. In this role, you'll manage complex projects across multiple functions, overseeing technical implementations and process improvements. You'll coordinate with cross-functional teams to ensure successful project delivery across an assigned Strategic account. Working with stakeholders at all levels, you'll facilitate communications, monitor progress, and ensure alignment with organizational goals. What You’ll Do: Accountable for overall portfolio management, deliverables and client relationship and satisfaction​ Provides oversight and coordination of client portfolio for high value or pivotal client account studies. Provides oversight to internal departments and external clients to ensure the project requirements are understood, agreed and followed at all times, this requires negotiating and real-time decision making with the client. Facilitates the flow of technical information to all stakeholders. Consults and utilizes the scientific input of laboratory management and technical staff to manage portfolio deliverables Facilitate portfolio-wide meetings and attend program meetings for visibility and escalation. Collaborates with Business Development and Finance to develop and capitalize on new opportunities to expand client portfolios. Ownership of strategic governance meetings: prepare, lead and drive actions Participate in Tiered Accountability to support portfolio risk mitigation Ownership of large or complex portfolio contracts, contract renewals, and supports securing new opportunities for growth and expansion of the portfolio Oversight of revenue recognition for the portfolio Provide training and mentorship for Program Manager I, Program Manager II and Program Manager III.

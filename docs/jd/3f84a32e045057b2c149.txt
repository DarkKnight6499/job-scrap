Job Summary This role offers the opportunity to join a well‑established, growing Real Estate Institutional Capital Group team that is recognized for its leadership in commercial lending and lead Agent role in RE syndication market. The Senior Servicing Officer is responsible for servicing an assigned portfolio of commercial real estate loans, primarily supporting the Real Estate Institutional Capital Group Line of Business, including non‑owner occupied and interim loan structures. The role provides end‑to‑end servicing support, portfolio administration, and related client services for a sophisticated client base that includes public and private REITs, real estate private equity firms and advisors, and commercial real estate owners, with additional focus on specialized healthcare property financing for hospitals and senior housing owners and operators. This position independently manages all pre‑closing and pre‑boarding document reviews, risk management activities, and ongoing loan system processing, including loan boarding, maintenance, and data integrity. As a Senior Service Officer, this role additionally allows for the opportunity of leadership, training and mentoring other teammates. Our team partners closely with relationship managers, closers, and internal teams to deliver high‑quality service and expert guidance across revolver, term, borrowing base and bridge loan products, while balancing multiple priorities and varying levels of complexity. As the bank continues to expand its commercial real estate platform, this role provides meaningful exposure to complex transactions, strong collaboration across lending teams, and the opportunity to grow within a stable, relationship‑driven organization. Essential Functions Manage pre- and post-closing services for complex loan structures including ESOPs, Healthcare, Agribusiness, Real Estate, and more. Identify and resolve risk/exceptions, escalate as needed, and communicate with management and LOB. Maintain expert-level understanding of systems and ensure data integrity. Collaborate in-person with internal and external stakeholders to drive the loan process. Book loans in LoanIQ, confirm collateral, and manage documentation exceptions. Review and prepare documentation using Laser Pro Lending (LPL) and DocuSign. Lead projects and continuous improvement initiatives which may require in-person sessions. Conduct pre-closing quality verification and manage fiduciary responsibilities. Onsite training and mentoring of team members; serve as backup to manager. Participate in client meetings and sales team/pipeline discussions which includes onsite participation. Monitor and manage post-approval reporting. Ensure compliance with documentation, insurance, and legal standards. Act as a resource for documentation and compliance matters. Coordinate funding and booking of transactions, including wire call backs and approvals. Performs other duties as assigned; duties, responsibilities and/or activities may change or new ones may be assigned at any time with or without notice Complies with all KeyBank policies and procedures, including without limitation, acting professionally at all times, conducting business ethically, avoiding conflicts of interest, and acting in the best interests of Key’s clients and Key.

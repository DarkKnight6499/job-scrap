Markets | Spread Products Division | Residential Mortgage Whole Loan Transaction Management Group Business Overview Citigroup's Non-Agency Residential Mortgage Transaction Management Group, a unit within Spread Products, is seeking to add an Analyst to its existing team. The broader business focuses on loan trading and new issue securitizations of non-agency residential mortgage assets, serving institutional clients across the residential credit ecosystem. The desk touches a wide range of non-agency asset classes, including: New origination prime and near-prime loans HELOC and closed-end second mortgage loans Non-QM and DSCR mortgage loans Seasoned performing, re-performing, and non-performing loans Role Overview The Analyst will support the loan trading and new issue securitization functions of the business, gaining direct exposure to: Due diligence — Both pre- and post-settlement diligence coordination and review for loan trades and securitized transactions Contract negotiations — Supporting documentation and terms for loan sale/purchase agreements and securitization transactions Trade execution support — Assisting traders with bid lists, pricing analysis, and settlement coordination for whole loan trades Data analytics — Loan-level and pool-level analysis to support trade pricing, bid evaluation, and securitization structuring New issue securitization support — Assisting with deal structuring, collateral review, and execution of securitized product issuances Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Business, or a related field Strong analytical and quantitative skills, with proficiency in Excel (VBA/SQL a plus) Excellent written and verbal communication skills Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment Strong attention to detail, particularly in data analysis and documentation review Interest in residential mortgage markets, structured products, and fixed income 0–2 years of relevant experience (internship or full-time) preferred This job

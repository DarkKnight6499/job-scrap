Senior Financial Analyst – Ventures Analytics & Operations
Qualcomm
Location: San Diego, California, United States of America
Link: https://careers.qualcomm.com/careers/job/446718690061

Company: Qualcomm Incorporated Job Area: Finance & Accounting Group, Finance & Accounting Group > Finance General Summary: Exciting opportunity to join Qualcomm’s Ventures Finance team, supporting portfolio analysis, investment diligence, portfolio reporting and the development of next-generation reporting and AI-driven workflows. The position sits at the intersection of venture investing, financial analysis, data infrastructure, and AI-driven innovation, providing high visibility across investment managers, finance leadership, and cross-functional stakeholders. The successful candidate will play a key role in enhancing investment decision-making, improving portfolio insights, and building scalable systems that support Qualcomm Ventures' global investment activities. **This position is not eligible for Qualcomm immigration sponsorship.** **Position requires to be onsite in San Diego, 5 days a week** Key Responsibilities 1) Venture Financial Analysis & Investment Support Analyze portfolio company performance, valuation drivers, and return outcomes across stages Support investment committee (IC) processes, including financial modeling, return analysis, and ownership/dilution tracking Evaluate investment outcomes under various entry, follow-on, and exit scenarios Partner with investment managers on portfolio monitoring, company performance reviews, and investment-related analyses 2) Portfolio Reporting & Insights Develop and maintain portfolio dashboards, portfolio and management reporting Deliver executive-level insights on portfolio performance, key risks, valuation changes, and emerging trends Support quarterly portfolio reviews and valuation processes Enhance reporting frameworks to improve transparency, consistency, and decision-making across the Ventures organization 3) Data Systems & Infrastructure Own and enhance Ventures data ecosystem and reporting pipelines Maintain data integrity, consistency, and accessibility across investment and operational processes Design scalable reporting structures that transform investment data into actionable insights 4) AI & Automation Enablement Identify, design and implement AI-enabled workflows and automation solutions across Ventures processes Develop prototypes, agents, and automation tools to improve reporting efficiency and investment insights Partner with Finance and IT to scale automation across Ventures processes 5) Cross-Functional Collaboration Work closely with investment managers, Treasury, Accounting, Tax, Legal, and other Finance partners Support interactions with external partners, service providers, and portfolio companies as needed Contribute to strategic initiatives that improve the effectiveness and scalability of the Ventures organization Minimum Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Business, Data Analytics or related field 3-5 years of experience in Finance Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with demonstrated attention to detail Advanced Excel and financial modeling capabilities Preferred Qualifications MBA or Master’s degree in Finance, or a related discipline Experience in venture capital, growth equity, or investment portfolio analysis Strong financial modeling skills (valuation, IRR, ownership/dilution, scenario analysis) Experience with AI/automation tools or strong interest in applying AI to finance workflows Ability to translate complex analysis into clear, executive-level insights Profile / Success Characteristics Highly analytical with strong attention to detail Systems-oriented thinker who can improve processes, not just analyze outcomes Comfortable operating in an fast-paced, and evolving environment Strong communication skills with ability to influence cross-functional stakeholders Self-starter with a bias toward ownership, execution, and continuous improvement Curious about emerging technologies and motivated to leverage AI and automation to solve business problems Minimum Qualifications: • Bachelor's degree. • 3+ years of Finance, Accounting, or related work experience. *Completed advanced degrees in a relevant field may be substituted for up to two years (Master’s = one year, Doctorate = two years) of work experience.

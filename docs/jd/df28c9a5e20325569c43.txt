The Role
We are looking for a hands-on Employee Success (HR) Associate to support various business units within our North America teams throughout the entire employee lifecycle. As part of our global team, you will be responsible for delivering critical HR processes and services, while also taking on advanced responsibilities that contribute to strategic HR initiatives.
 What You'll Do Ensure operational excellence across the entire employee life cycle, including preparing contracts, reference letters, employee changes, off-boarding materials, and other related areas Provide first-line support for all employees within designated business groups Provide operational support across core Employee Success processes and programs Support onboarding activities for new hires Partner closely with Employee Success (HR) Business Partners to ensure successful implementation of key projects and initiatives Take ownership of our HRIS system’s data quality, aligning with global HR operational processes where needed Lead and execute process improvement initiatives to enhance HR service delivery Collaborate with cross–functional teams to develop and implement HR policies and procedures Requirements Bachelor’s degree with strong academic performance 0-2 years of professional experience Strong attention to detail and communications skills, both verbal and written Experiencing working with various HR tools and systems (e.g. Workday, Lever) A dynamic communication style with the ability to collaborate successfully with diverse stakeholders Can-do mentality to achieve the best results possible within a defined time frame Experience working at a scale-up or software company Ambition for professional growth and development Perks & Benefits Medical, dental, vision, and basic life insurance Flexible PTO and company paid holidays Retirement programs 1% charitable giving program Compensation Base pay: $50,000 - $70,000 The salary range listed here has been provided to comply with local regulations and represents a potential base salary range for this role.

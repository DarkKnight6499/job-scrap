Salary Range $25.00 - $26.00 Target Openings 1 What Is the Opportunity? Are you curious by nature? Do you enjoy managing relationships and employing your critical thinking skills? Underwriters perform a critical function of evaluating the acceptability of risk and determining appropriate pricing of insurance products. The Underwriting Apprenticeship Program provides students with the amazing opportunity to gain exposure to this critical function at Travelers. The semester long program provides qualified students with an excellent opportunity to gain first-hand insurance underwriting experience, receive valuable on-the-job training and learn more about the property & casualty insurance industry from industry professionals. It is a potential pathway to our 12 month Underwriting Professional Development Program. The intent of this position is to provide students the ability to apply to our 2024 Underwriting Apprenticeship opportunities which start in January. Applications outside of this audience will not be considered at this time. However, for summer internship opportunities, we encourage anyone to apply to our current 2024 Underwriting Professional Development Program Internship roles in various locations. What Will You Do? Within your assigned business unit, each apprentice will be given a designated coach and be expected to: Complete core assignments and training modules geared toward insurance and underwriting principles. Participate and complete self-paced professional development courses in time management, presentation skills and other key areas. Complete challenging and meaningful project work over the course of this program at the discretion of your manager. You may work independently or with other interns and will be asked to present your findings to local leadership. Sample projects include a territorial risk assessment to determine profitability and underwriting opportunities as well as a business process analysis to determine underwriting gaps. Learn about other areas at Travelers such as Risk Control, Claims, and related Corporate functions. Interns may also engage in Agency visits with Account Executives. Perform other duties as assigned. What Will Our Ideal Candidate Have? Because the Underwriting Apprenticeship Program is itself complex and dynamic, we welcome applicants that have a variety of majors, backgrounds, and experiences. We are most interested in students who are serious about entering the insurance industry and who are excited about the prospect of making underwriting their future career choice. Preferred cumulative GPA of 3.0 or above. Working knowledge of Microsoft Office. Strong verbal and written communication skills. Strong analytical skills. Legal eligibility to work in the United States.

JOB SUMMARY As part of the Enterprise Technology Development Program (ETDP), this role participates in a two-year, paid, early-career rotational development program within the Business Operations track. Associates gain hands-on experience across multiple teams while contributing to meaningful business and technology initiatives. The incumbent serves as a strategic business partner to assigned leadership teams, providing data-driven insights, operational support, and governance oversight to help drive organizational performance. This role analyzes operational data, monitors key business metrics, and identifies opportunities to improve efficiency, compliance, and business outcomes. Working closely with leaders and stakeholders, the incumbent translates complex information into actionable recommendations that support informed decision-making and operational excellence. Uses minimal judgement and discretion on projects while leveraging established processes, tools, and governance frameworks to deliver results. ESSENTIAL RESPONSIBILITIES Partner with leaders and stakeholders to support planning activities, operational reviews, process improvements, and business initiatives. Analyze operational, workforce, financial, and business performance data to identify trends, risks, opportunities, and actionable insights. Develop, maintain, and deliver recurring and ad hoc reports, dashboards, scorecards, and presentations for leadership teams. Assist and actively participate on project teams in various project roles, including stakeholder communications and project support activities. Research best practices, leading technologies, and emerging industry trends for consideration in design and improvement relating to rotations. Monitor operational compliance, data quality, governance standards, and business processes; identify and escalate issues as needed. Document processes, maintain operational procedures, and support continuous improvement efforts across the organization. Other duties as assigned or requested.

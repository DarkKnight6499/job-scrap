About the Role The Investor Solutions team brings together information, data, and expertise from across Baron Capital to address the due diligence and information needs of clients, prospects, consultants, and other key stakeholders. The team plays an important role in winning and retaining business by transforming Firm and product knowledge into accurate, consistent, compelling, and tailored investor communications. Its work includes requests for proposals (RFPs), due diligence questionnaires (DDQs), requests for information (RFIs), consultant databases, competitive analyses, Q&As, compliance-related questionnaires, and other investor information requests across the Firm’s sales channels. The team also serves as a steward of Baron’s institutional knowledge. Information developed through each assignment is evaluated, reconciled with existing and prior communications, and incorporated into the team’s content, data, systems, and processes so future responses are stronger, more efficient, and consistent over time. The team partners with subject-matter experts across Investments, Sales, Compliance, Legal, Marketing, Operations, Trading, Portfolio Risk and Data Analytics, and other functions, while maintaining central responsibility for integrating their input into client-ready responses. The Associate supports the development and delivery of investor information and due diligence materials while building knowledge of Baron Capital, its investment strategies, products, philosophy, processes, and operations. Under the guidance of more senior team members, the Associate researches and gathers information, drafts and updates content, maintains quantitative and qualitative data, coordinates assigned requests, and helps ensure that deliverables are accurate, responsive, complete, and submitted on time. Essential Responsibilities Manage assigned RFPs, DDQs, RFIs, consultant database submissions, and other investor information requests from initiation through completion, following established processes and communicating status, questions, and risks promptly. Research and draft clear, accurate, concise, and responsive content using approved materials, prior responses, internal systems, source documents, and input from subject-matter experts. Collaborate with colleagues across the Firm to gather data and insights; document source information and identify new or changed information that should be reflected in the team’s knowledge base and future responses. Develop a working knowledge of the Firm, investment strategies, products, investment philosophy, processes, operations, and service-provider relationships, and learn to articulate that information appropriately for sophisticated external audiences. Build an understanding of the purpose and audience for each request and learn to tailor responses to the specific question rather than relying on generic or boilerplate language. Maintain assigned quantitative and qualitative information in RFP software, consultant databases, trackers, and other team systems, with careful attention to accuracy, currency, consistency, and source support. Compare draft responses against approved content, public materials, prior communications, and available source documents; raise inconsistencies, gaps, ambiguities, or potentially outdated information for review. Apply Baron Capital’s writing standards, formatting requirements, review protocols, and recordkeeping practices to all assignments. Manage multiple deadlines and priorities, seek guidance when needed, and escalate issues early enough to protect quality and timeliness. Contribute to team projects, content updates, data reviews, and process improvements as assigned.

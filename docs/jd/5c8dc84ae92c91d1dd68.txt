Your role What you'll be doing What We Need Corpay is currently hiring a Portfolio Manager for our Private Markets line of business in Paris. In this role, you will be responsible for building a portfolio of high-value private capital market clients from the ground up through a blend of business development and prospecting activities. You will report directly to the Head of France and regularly collaborate with Strategists, Sales Managers and the wider Private Markets colleagues. Join a new Paris team backed by a renowned, publicly listed financial group, delivering a real alternative to banks on FX, rates, and investment products. If you're driven to help build the French market and want an intrapreneurial adventure inside a major group, this role is for you. How We Work As a Portfolio Manager, Corpay will set you up for success by providing: Assigned workspace in Paris. Monday - Friday office expectation. Company-issued equipment Formal, hands-on training, mentoring and development opportunities Role Responsibilities The responsibilities of the role will include: Researching and qualifying potential clients to build a database of leads Engaging and consulting with C-level executives of medium to large businesses Sourcing and initiating new sales opportunities Learning clients’ businesses and uncovering key problems and challenges Educating clients on Corpay solutions and value proposition Collaborating with analysts and strategists to build risk management solutions Developing trusted relationships with existing portfolio clients Retaining and growing portfolio revenue opportunities Managing business development and account management activities Learning to independently execute market transactions for clients This role will focus on targeting the venture capital, private equity, private debt, and real estate sectors as core client verticals Qualifications & Skills Fluency in English & French is essential Experience in business development, sales, account management, financial services or related environment preferred Bachelor degree preferred but not essential Strong communication skills Ability to engage senior stakeholders and decision makers Urgency and ability to take feedback onboard quickly Ambition and motivation to develop professionally Humility and high emotional intelligence Competitive mindset, driven by continuous self-improvement and a strong will to exceed targets Understanding of FX markets and interest rates (debt and investment instruments) is a plus Familiarity with spot and derivative products (forwards, swaps, options) is a plus Benefits & Perks Health insurance Work from home allowance whilst they are hybrid (58 Euros per month) 25 days of annual leave plus 10 days of RTT leave (35 days total) Pay Transparency The salary range for this position is €50,000 -70,000 gross per year.

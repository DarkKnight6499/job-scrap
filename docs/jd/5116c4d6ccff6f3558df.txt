L&G Asset Management – America is looking for an ambitious Multi Asset Trader to join its Global Trading Team in Chicago. Equity trading experience at a buy or sell-side institution is required with Derivative (Listed or OTC) or FX trading experience an advantage. Successful candidate should be enthusiastic, a proven self-starter with a strong willingness to develop trading skills across different asset classes, a genuine global team player and have a strong work ethic. Responsibilities: Manage and monitor the execution of trades ensuring it is in line with regulatory standards, LGIM(A)’s policies, procedures and risk management practices at all times. You'll advise investment teams with different investment styles on trade implementation while providing relevant market information that identifies and creates actionable investment opportunities. Maintain and leverage strong relationships with counterparties across different asset classes on a global level. Work effectively within a global team structure, building close relationships with our traders & portfolio managers in Chicago, London & Hong Kong. Challenge and upgrade current processes and procedures while mitigating risk for greater efficiency, adding new ideas and initiatives to the team. Work with our Trading Research team to analyse, process and interpret large trading data sets to continually improve client outcomes. Remain informed about the evolving market structure, methods of execution and products as well as maintain competency on all trading platforms. Where required, cross-train to achieve competency in Listed & OTC Derivatives and FX trading. 5+ years’ experience working in an Equity trading role, experience trading using algorithms required. Experience in trading Listed/OTC Derivatives or FX a strong advantage. Solid understanding of market structure & risk (impact, liquidity) as well successful experience in managing large numbers of orders. High level of attention to detail with a strong risk management mindset. Strong interpersonal skills and experience in a collaborative team-based environment. Ability to multi-task, trading different asset classes while working in a fast-paced environment. Willingness to adapt and learn new asset classes and products as needs arise. Strong analytical skills. Quantitative and programming skills will be additive. Strong work ethic, impeccable integrity.

Job Summary The Security Operations and Event Monitoring Center (SOEMC) is an industry-leading operations center that plays a vital role in keeping AEP safe and compliant. An Event Monitoring Center Rep Assoc will answer alarms and phone calls to help protect AEP assets, assist internal customers, and be the face of AEP Security. The SOEMC monitors alarms throughout AEP’s footprint at various facilities, answers the corporate Security hotline, and helps maintain NERC CIP compliance. The Event Monitoring Center Rep Assoc will oversee other SOEMC members and terminals to ensure AEP Security standards are met. Under direct supervision and using clearly defined procedures, responds to internal customers with security needs and addresses alarms using monitoring software. Handles basic level problems, uses existing procedures to solve routine or standard problems, and escalates other issues. Under minimal supervision and assisting with writing clearly defined guidelines, responds to internal customers with security needs and addresses alarms using monitoring software. Handles basic-level problems and provides solution options based on criticality. The role provides for the SOEMC and oversees the day-to-day operations of a 24/7 security monitoring and response function. Additionally, they lead efforts to detect, analyze, and respond to security incidents, ensuring timely resolution and minimal impact on the organization. Job Description What you’ll do: Respond to incoming alarms using AEP’s monitoring software. Handles basic problems. Answers the security hotline and assists internal customers with basic security needs. Creates incident reports following standard procedure. Troubleshoots fundamental security systems problems and provides technician support where needed. Assist callers with access control or badge questions. Helps AEP maintain North American Electric Reliability Corporation (NERC) Critical Infrastructure Plan (CIP) compliance following existing procedures. Assists other operators and terminals to ensure standards are being met Preferred Qualifications: Excellent decision-making and problem-solving skills, plus verbal and written communication skills to technical and non-technical audiences of various levels in the organization Previous experience in a call center or customer service role, demonstrating strong communication and problem-solving skills. Familiarity with security systems and incident reporting processes. Ability to remain calm and professional in high-pressure situations, ensuring customer satisfaction. Knowledge of access control systems and NERC CIP compliance regulations is a plus. Other Preferred Requirements: Rotating shift work. Ability to type 30 words per minute. What We're Looking For: Education requirements are listed below: Possess a high school diploma or equivalent Work Experience requirement listed below: Minimum of one (1) year of military, law enforcement, government, security, or related experience.

Job Description: Associate Director, Private Fixed Income - SLC Management What is in it for you: We are seeking an Associate Director, Private Fixed Income to join the Corporate Private Placement team in Wellesley, MA, contributing to the investment grade portfolio objectives of the Private Fixed Income (PFI) group at SLC Management. SLC's Private Fixed Income group is one of the largest investment grade private debt investors in North America. The team is responsible for the investment management of over $41 billion of private debt, invested and managed on behalf of Sun Life Financial's balance sheet and a growing third-party client base. The cross-border team of more than 50 investment professionals is organized across three verticals: Corporate Credit, Infrastructure Debt, and Structured Credit. The PFI team has an annual investment program in excess of $8 billion. PFI holdings are comprised of high-quality, investment grade transactions that are highly negotiated and customized, enabling SLC to achieve yields in excess of comparably rated public securities. The breadth and depth of Sun Life's private fixed income portfolio provide the organization with a meaningful competitive advantage. The PFI team's investment philosophy, disciplined approach, collaborative culture, and deep expertise have enabled it to achieve a leading market position. Our ideal candidate will contribute immediately to the continued growth of the Corporate Private Placement team's business through a focus on origination, strong credit underwriting, and portfolio management. Natural leadership skills and the ability to work independently while thriving in a team environment are key attributes for success. This role requires strong financial statement analysis and investment analysis skills to support management of the PFI portfolio through the underwriting of new investment opportunities and the monitoring of existing private debt investments.

Industry/Sector Insurance Specialism Guidewire Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Guidewire Configuration Consultant, Senior Associate you will specialize in developing and customizing applications using the Guidewire platform within our Business Application Consulting practice. As a Senior Associate you will analyze complex client needs, manage assigned workstreams, and contribute to solution design, coding, testing, and implementation efforts that support policy administration, claims management, and billing for insurance organizations. In this role at PwC, you will bring technical problem-solving, a client-focused mindset, and an ability to adapt to changing project needs while building meaningful client connections and supporting delivery across insurance technology engagements. Responsibilities - Designing and configuring Guidewire applications to support policy, billing, and claims workflows for insurance clients - Developing reusable code, rules, and configuration components aligned with project requirements and platform architecture - Testing application changes, tracing defects, and supporting issue resolution across development and deployment cycles - Translating client business needs into technical solutions that improve process flow and system usability - Interpreting functional requirements and converting them into configuration updates, integrations, and custom logic - Reviewing deliverables from junior team members and providing guidance on technical tasks and work planning - Collaborating with client stakeholders to clarify needs, track progress, and adapt to changing project priorities - Applying Guidewire tools, software development methods, and system development skills to deliver configured solutions What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 3 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for a Master's or Masters of Business Administration degree in Actuarial Science, Business Administration/Management, Business Analytics, Commerce, Computer and Information Science, Computer Applications, Computer Engineering, Computer Engineering & Accounting, Data Processing/Analytics/Science, Economics, Electrical Engineering, Engineering, Engineering and Business, Finance, Information Technology, Management Information Systems, Mathematics, Mechanical Engineering, Operations Management/Research, Risk Management, Software App, Statistics - Demonstrating Guidewire PolicyCenter configuration experience for insurance projects - Applying Guidewire ClaimCenter or BillingCenter knowledge in client delivery work - Using critical thinking to break down complex configuration issues - Interpreting data to inform recommendations for client teams - Building client relationships while guiding junior team members - Adapting to ambiguity and changing project priorities Travel Requirements Up to 60% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $77,000 -

Launch Your Career in Cyber Risk Analytics Are you interested in cybersecurity, data, and solving complex business challenges? Aon is looking for a Junior Cyber Risk Analyst to join our Cyber Threat Intelligence team within Cyber Reinsurance Solutions. This is an opportunity for recent graduates to build analytical, technical, and business skills while working with experienced professionals in a growing area of risk. In this role, you'll work with data, technology, and cyber risk insights to help organizations make informed decisions. Through hands-on experience, mentorship, and professional development, you'll build a strong foundation for a career in cyber risk analytics. Aon is in the business of better decisions At Aon, we shape decisions for the better to protect and enrich the lives of people around the world. As an organization, we are united through trust as one inclusive team and we are passionate about helping our colleagues and clients succeed. What is Cyber Threat Intelligence and Cyber Reinsurance Solutions? As a Junior Cyber Risk Analyst, you will work with experienced team members to analyze data, develop insights, and support projects that help clients better understand cyber risk. What You Will Do As a Junior Cyber Risk Analyst, you will gain hands-on experience working with cyber and insurance data while developing an understanding of how analytics can support real-world risk and business decisions. Support Data and Analytical Workflows Clean, organize, and validate data from multiple sources. Support analytical workflows and reporting processes. Assist with data matching, quality checks, and automation initiatives. Contribute to process documentation and continuous improvement efforts. Build Cyber and Threat Intelligence Knowledge Analyze cyber-related data, including vulnerabilities, incidents, and technology dependencies. Support research on emerging cyber threats and trends. Help develop insights that support cyber risk assessments and scenario analysis. Collaborate with cyber threat intelligence specialists and analytics professionals. Support Insurance and Reinsurance Analytics Create reports, dashboards, and data visualizations. Support portfolio and risk analyses. Help prepare materials for internal and client presentations. Work with colleagues across analytics, cybersecurity, and reinsurance teams. Develop Communication and Stakeholder Skills Communicate findings to technical and non-technical audiences. Participate in team discussions and cross-functional projects. Build relationships across Aon's global organization. Gain exposure to client-facing work and business strategy. Qualifications College Seniors graduating between Spring 2026 to Spring 2027 with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 Bachelor’s degree (BA/BS) in Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, Economics, or another quantitative, technical, or related field is preferred. Please Note: For this role you must be legally authorized to work in the United States without the need for employer sponsorship, now or at any time in the future. Business and professional development in Commercial Risk, Reinsurance, client service, communication, collaboration, problem solving and commercial awareness. Exposure to data, analytics and technology that support risk assessment, market analysis, workflow coordination and client-ready recommendations. Networking, mentorship and coaching opportunities to build relationships across Aon and develop your career path.

Summary: The Technical Engineering Lead is responsible for the technology strategy, architecture, delivery, and lifecycle management for analytics and AI capabilities supporting Clinical Operations — anchored today in GDO Workbench, with a remit that extends to analytical and AI needs surfacing elsewhere across Drug Development. This role serves as the senior technology leader partnering with Global Development Operations and BI&T leadership to deliver digital solutions that improve study execution, increase operational efficiency, enable AI-driven decision making, and accelerate the delivery of medicines to patients. The successful candidate will lead a matrixed team of architects, engineers, data specialists, and vendor partners while driving platform modernization, enterprise integration, and AI-enabled innovation across Clinical Operations. Key Responsibilities: Product & Technology Leadership: • Define and execute the technology strategy and roadmap for Clinical Operations Intelligence platforms. • Partner with business, product, and digital leaders to translate strategic priorities into technology solutions. • Drive innovation through analytics, AI, automation, and digital capabilities. • Ensure technology investments deliver measurable business value and user adoption. • Serve as the trusted advisor for platform capabilities, scalability, and future direction. Architecture & Platform Strategy: • Own the target-state architecture for GDO Workbench and related platforms. • Lead platform modernization and technology transformation initiatives. • Define architecture across applications, data, analytics, cloud, and integrations. • Drive decisions related to platform convergence, data architecture, and enterprise integration. • Ensure solutions align with enterprise technology, security, and data standards. Delivery & Engineering Leadership: • Lead cross-functional teams responsible for design, development, testing, deployment, and support. • Provide technical leadership across multiple product pods and development workstreams. • Establish delivery governance and release management processes. • Drive execution excellence through Agile product delivery and engineering best practices. • Balance innovation, stability, technical debt reduction, and operational efficiency. Data, Analytics & AI Enablement: • Partner with Data Products and enterprise data teams to deliver integrated data and analytics solutions. • Enable AI-driven insights, intelligent recommendations, automation, and decision-support capabilities. • Drive adoption of modern data platforms and scalable analytics architecture. • Support self-service analytics and next-generation digital user experiences. Stakeholder & Governance Leadership: • Build strong partnerships across Global Development Operations, BI&T, R&D, and enterprise technology teams. • Present technology roadmaps, architecture recommendations, risks, and investment opportunities to senior leadership. • Lead governance discussions supporting platform strategy, prioritization, and investment decisions. • Influence outcomes across highly matrixed organizations and external partners. Quality, Compliance & Risk Management: • Ensure all solutions meet BMS quality, security, privacy, compliance, and regulatory requirements. • Establish governance processes supporting architecture, development, release management, and operations. • Drive continuous improvement in engineering quality and operational excellence. • Identify and proactively mitigate technology and business risks. Qualifications Required: • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, Data Science, or related field. • 12+ years of experience leading technology platforms, digital products, or enterprise applications. • 5+ years of leadership experience managing cross-functional technology teams. • Proven experience developing and executing technology roadmaps. • Experience leading large-scale digital transformation initiatives. • Strong executive communication and stakeholder management skills. Preferred: • Advanced degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, or related discipline. • Experience in Pharmaceutical, Biotechnology, Clinical Development, or Life Sciences organizations. • Experience with cloud platforms, data platforms, analytics, and AI-enabled solutions. • Experience leading Agile product development organizations. • Experience managing strategic technology vendors and consulting partners. Leadership Competencies: • Strategic mindset with strong business acumen. • Ability to influence across complex matrix organizations. • Strong product and customer orientation. • Exceptional collaboration and communication skills. • Demonstrated ability to lead through ambiguity and organizational change. • Passion for innovation, talent development, and continuous improvement We hire for skills and capabilities, not just credentials – if this role excites you, but doesn’t perfectly match your resume, we encourage you to apply anyway.

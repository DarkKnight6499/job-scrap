Industry/Sector Health Services Specialism Operations Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Grant Administration Consultant, Senior Associate you will support client service engagements within our Operations Consulting practice, with a focus on provider business operations in the health services sector. As a Senior Associate you will analyze complex grant administration issues, manage assigned workstreams, guide junior team members, and build meaningful client connections while navigating changing priorities and varied stakeholder needs. In this role at PwC, you will apply the methods, tools, and business context needed to develop deliverables that support grant administration processes, interpret feedback, and contribute to solutions that align with broader project objectives. Responsibilities - Managing grant administration workstreams for health services clients, tracking deadlines, deliverables, and documentation across multiple projects - Analyzing grant requirements, compliance terms, and operational processes to support funding activities and program execution - Coordinating data collection, validation, and reporting using business data analytics and digital tooling - Reviewing grant workflows and process steps to identify gaps, reduce rework, and improve operational effectiveness - Preparing client-ready materials, status updates, and summaries of key issues using clear written communication - Applying analytical thinking to interpret performance metrics, monitor progress, and flag risks or delays early - Collaborating with client leaders and internal teams to organize priorities, address questions, and keep work moving forward - Supporting process improvement efforts by using operations management knowledge and continuous learning across assigned tasks - Mentoring junior team members through feedback, task review, and day-to-day guidance on grant administration activities What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 4 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for at least one of the following fields of study: Accounting, Actuarial Science, Analytics/Data Science, Business Administration/Management, Computer Science/Information Systems, Economics, Engineering, Finance, Government/Public Policy, Health Administration/Public Health, Mathematics/Statistics, Operations/Supply Chain, Organizational Management/Behavior, Risk Management/Insurance - Demonstrating grant administration or program support in health services settings - Applying analytical thinking to interpret data and prioritize deliverables - Utilizing business data analytics and process improvement methods -

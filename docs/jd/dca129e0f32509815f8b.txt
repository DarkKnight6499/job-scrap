Zurich Group Investment Management, Inc. (ZGIM), part of Zurich North America, is looking for an Investment Accounting & Reporting Associate Director to join our Investment Administration and Accounting (IAA) team in Parsippany, NJ. As the investment landscape grows more complex and technology moves faster, we rely on deep technical competence, strong analytical capability and a genuine commitment to excellence to deliver high-quality outcomes for the business. Because we understand investment processes, financial instruments and regulatory frameworks inside out, we adapt quickly, hold the line on rigorous standards, and give our partners and stakeholders support that means something. IAA is a global function within Zurich Investment Management, responsible for the accuracy, transparency and governance of investment accounting, valuation and reporting across the organization. We safeguard the integrity of investment data, keep us aligned with regulatory and audit requirements, and deliver insights that shape strategic portfolio and business decisions. Through this critical work, our team sits at the center of the reliability and credibility of investment information enterprise wide. We are also continuously improving how we work, enhancing and standardizing processes across regions, and using technology, automation and cross-team collaboration to drive efficiency and build operational resilience. As a trusted partner to Investment Management and Finance, we deliver the reliable, transparent, high-quality investment information that lets the company decide with confidence.

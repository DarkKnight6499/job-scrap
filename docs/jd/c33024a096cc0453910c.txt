About JLL – Lease Administration JLL's Lease Administration platform supports client needs ranging from lease abstraction and critical dates management to full financial management of landlord invoices and reconciliation audits — enabling clients to make strategic real estate decisions with confidence. We are seeking a Senior Lease Administration Associate & Abstractor to join our team. This role provides comprehensive lease administration services including abstraction, financial review, and client support. You will work with a high level of independence while collaborating closely with internal teams to deliver accurate, timely services that enable our clients to make strategic real estate decisions. What this job involves – Responsibilities “Being a trailblazer and breaking new ground” As a Senior Lease Administration Associate & Abstractor , you will be responsible for delivering high-quality lease abstraction services while managing key Lease Administration responsibilities across client portfolios. You will work independently with a strong attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment. This role requires a balance of technical abstraction expertise and broader lease administration competencies, including financial review, client relationship management, and process oversight. You will be a key player in ensuring accuracy and effective management of client lease portfolios, with exposure to the full breadth of our Lease Administration platform. To be successful in this role, you should be able to ensure accurate reporting and reconciliation of information, and foster critical relationships between various individuals, teams, and clients. As a Senior Lease Administration Associate & Abstractor , you will be responsible for comprehensive lease administration services including: Lease Abstraction Liaise with internal parties and transition/regional teams to obtain appropriate lease documentation Perform lease document matching and review for completeness prior to abstraction Abstract critical and key information from leases and other contract documentation into lease database systems (including CoStar and other platforms) Accurately record lease document information into Master Document Control Logs and Document Trackers Manage abstracts and system data; verify and review system reports and data for accuracy and completeness Prepare area, cost-schedule, and events variance reports for validation projects Work with internal stakeholders to resolve document and abstraction-related queries Maintain processes and procedures ensuring adherence to internal and external compliance policies Lease Administration Coordinate abstractions from transition and regional teams; manage project assignment and tracking of the double-check process Review CAM, other escalations, and real estate tax invoices Review data entry, validation, and management of lease documents Review rent variance analysis and rent payment processing Review subtenant account management, and billing/collections of third-party tenant rents Track and maintain critical milestone dates Conduct financial analysis and volume trend reporting Review landlord invoices for adherence to lease terms Prepare invoices and support invoice processing workflows Manage client, vendor, and landlord relationships with a "Customer First Mindset" Partner with outsource providers as needed Streamline current processes and drive efficiencies across the team Client/vendor/landlord relationship management Management of project abstraction services Partnership with outsource providers Invoice preparation Support, exposure and leadership of other special projects as assigned by manager Sound like you?

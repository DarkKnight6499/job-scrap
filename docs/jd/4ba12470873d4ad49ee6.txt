Brief Role Description: The Foreign Exchange E-Distribution sales desk with in the Fixed Income Division actively markets the Morgan Stanley suite of electronic FX products to institutional and corporate clients. We are looking for a dynamic Analyst or Associate to sell Morgan Stanley’s EFX offering to our client base. The E-Distribution sales professional will be responsible for outbound electronic FX sales, relationship management, and client wallet optimization. This person will focus on sales to institutional clients (hedge funds, asset managers, corporates) as well as the sales of any new product roll-out. Additionally, this individual will collaborate with various internal sales, trading, strat and support teams to actively manage ongoing client relationships. Goals are to increase wallet share, bring new relationships to Morgan Stanley, and improve profitability.

Associate - Portfolio Logistics Lead and LMS Consultant Position Overview Capital One’s Enterprise Learning Platform and Operations team is seeking a highly motivated, detail-oriented, and customer-focused individual to join our team as a Learning Program & Portfolio Ops Associate. In this role, you will own platform logistics in One Learn for closed session offerings across Learning & Development, manage operational intake tasks in Learning Planner, and consult enterprise learning professionals on LMS functionality optimization. Working closely with internal facilitators, external vendors, and portfolio owners, you will manage end-to-end course logistics, deliver actionable data insights, ensure well-managed operations, and identify opportunities to automate tasks and streamline processes. Responsibilities: Manage end-to-end One Learn platform logistics for closed session offerings across L&D, including course, session, and enrollments. Execute designated operational intake tasks within our intake platform, Learning Planner. Consult with enterprise learning professionals on LMS capabilities, best practices, and opportunities for optimization. Actively monitor course enrollments, waitlists, and completion trends; generate basic and recurring reporting in One Learn to guide business decisions around session capacity and availability. Provide high-touch support to facilitators on One Learn navigation, platform capabilities, Zoom invites, basic reporting, and roster reconciliation. Serve as a direct point of contact for learners regarding LMS navigation, course inquiries, and specific offerings. Act as a liaison between clients and product teams, managing and escalating issues through appropriate channels to achieve timely resolution. Maintain well-managed operational standards by thoroughly documenting processes, team responsibilities, best practices, and training resources. Drive continuous improvement of the One Learn platform by identifying, proposing, and implementing LMS functionality optimizations. The Ideal Candidate Will Be: An organized operational planner who excels at balancing complex, concurrent logistical schedules while maintaining high attention to detail and quality execution. A consultative relationship builder who collaborates effectively with LOBs, portfolio owners, learning consultants and facilitators to offer thoughtful recommendations and deliver seamless learning experiences. A proactive, solutions-oriented problem solver who digs into operational workflows to reduce friction, automate manual tasks, and optimize learning platforms. A customer-focused communicator passionate about delivering exceptional, high-touch support to facilitators, learners, and cross-functional partners. Basic Qualifications: High School Diploma, GED or equivalent certification At least 2 years of experience in LMS administration, learning operations, or client-facing program logistics Preferred Qualifications: 3+ years of experience managing corporate learning logistics or LMS operations in an enterprise environment Experience consulting on LMS functionality, learner experience, or learning gamification strategies Experience using Google Suite, Slack, Zoom, and platform reporting features Experience with process documentation and familiarity with operational risk or well-managed frameworks At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position.

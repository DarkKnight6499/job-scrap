Associate - Product Operations, Flights Inventory As a Product Operations Associate supporting Flights Inventory, you’ll help support the operational health, quality, and performance of Capital One’s Flights platform. You’ll work closely with Senior Associates within Product Operations, Customer Service, Product, Engineering, Commercial teams, booking providers, and internal stakeholders to help ensure reliable airline inventory, strong bookability, and exceptional customer experiences across Capital One Travel products. In this role, you’ll assist with day-to-day operational processes across the shopping and booking lifecycle, including monitoring inventory behavior, supplier configuration support, fare mapping assistance, and onboarding activities for new airline and provider integrations. You’ll also contribute to initiatives focused on shopping optimization, inventory quality, and ancillary product support. You’ll contribute to bug investigations, operational escalations, and operational issues across booking environments while partnering with cross-functional teams to support issue resolution and continuous improvement efforts. This role is ideal for someone who is analytical, detail-oriented, technically curious, data-driven, has a strong bias for action, and excited to grow within a fast-paced travel technology environment. As part of the team’s continued evolution, you’ll also gain exposure to modern automation and AI-powered operational tools used to improve monitoring, workflow efficiency, operational reporting, and issue investigation across the Flights platform. What You’ll Do Support operational health and day-to-day performance of airline inventory and bookings systems Monitor bookability, latency, shopping behavior, and operational metrics to help identify issues and trends Assist with bug triage, escalation tracking, and operational investigations across booking environments Support testing, validation, and ongoing QA of new features, supplier integrations, and configuration changes in staging and production environments Help maintain fare mapping, supplier configurations, and manage various tenant configurations across booking systems Support NDC integrations and supplier connectivity initiatives to improve inventory quality and coverage Assist with maintaining the Product Ops Intake Board and coordinating incoming operational requests.

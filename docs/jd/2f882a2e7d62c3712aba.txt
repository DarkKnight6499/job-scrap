Job Description Ares Management is seeking a highly motivated and detail-oriented Associate Vice President to join the Public Credit Financial Planning & Analysis team. This team is based in Boston, New York and Los Angeles and is a part of the firm’s larger Finance organization. The team is responsible for various projection models, fund forecasting and other corporate finance strategy, and works closely with numerous groups across the organization, including Credit investment professionals and senior management as well as investor relations, fund accounting, business operations, portfolio management and technology teams. The ideal candidate would have a fundamental understanding of alternative investing and/or private credit and would demonstrate an aptitude in financial modeling and forecasting solutions. The candidate would also partner closely with our technology team to support automation and AI integration of models and processes. The role requires a dynamic, adaptive, experienced hands-on professional to ensure best practices in a fast-paced, rapidly changing environment.

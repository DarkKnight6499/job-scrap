Job Description: Our Opportunity: Chewy seeks a motivated and independent Senior Financial Analyst to provide financial leadership to an exciting and fast-paced Fulfillment Center (FC). This is an essential position at one of the network’s fulfillment centers located in Phoenix, AZ. As the company grows, this role within the Finance organization represents a fundamental building block in Chewy’s enterprise performance management and profitability improvement efforts. As Senior Financial Analyst, you will be located at the fulfillment center, working with the leadership team on-site to improve performance and meet or exceed financial commitments.

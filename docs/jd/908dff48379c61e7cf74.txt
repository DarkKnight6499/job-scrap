In addition to daily responsibilities, participants will have the opportunity to participate in a series of structured activities designed to enhance their learning experience including: Co-Op New Hire Orientation Senior Leadership Speaker Series Social & Networking Events Presentation Challenges At the conclusion of this position, co-ops will have increased their knowledge of investing, the mutual fund industry, employee engagement, and a firm understanding of investment operations, data, and technology at an asset manager. MFS co-op positions are a 6-month commitment, working Monday – Friday (January 13th through June 25th, 2027), and work between 35-40 hours. Our program is designed for undergraduate students who are currently enrolled in a co-op program through their college or university and can meet our requirements. Job Summary The Enterprise Data Management (EDM) group includes teams focused on data management and quality, market data management, data governance, analytics support, and data technology that services the data needs and capabilities of the enterprise. This role will report to the Data Management & Quality organization and primarily perform data quality-focused activities and data management initiatives but will have the opportunity to gain exposure to all areas of EDM including the technologies utilized to support the Unified Data Platform. WHAT YOU WILL DO Work with the team to refine various procedures and activities that enhance data management and quality environment related to core investment data domains Work with Data Analysts and data domain owners to identify, build, and implement new data quality checks Execute daily and monthly data quality checks, identifying opportunities to strengthen and improve checks Opportunistically contribute to EDM data initiatives that require data analysis Assist with Investments and Distribution Triage daily/weekly oversight activities; ensuring metadata completeness for root causes, domain type, and accountability/ownership Assist with maintenance of EDM SharePoint site Other data-focused tasks and projects as needed WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR Self-motivated and self-starter with the ability to take initiative Ability to handle multiple tasks and projects simultaneously Interest in investment data, investment operations, and learning investment applications/technology Strong oral and written communication skills Experience with Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Microsoft CoPilot Experience with data visualization, SQL, Microsoft SharePoint helpful but not required Hourly Pay Rate: $21.00 - $25.00 If any applicant is unable to complete an application or respond to a job opening because of a disability, please contact MFS at 617-954-5000 or email talent_acquisition@mfs.com for assistance.

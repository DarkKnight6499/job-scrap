Our Team : The Global Securitized Products Data Team is responsible for researching and supporting a $12 trillion global securitized products market. The Americas ABS Securitized Products Team is responsible for creating and maintaining a large library of cash-flow models and data. The team manages the end-to-end workflows supporting the US ABS sector, including new issuance and bond data, cash-flow modeling, collateral performance data, periodic cash-flow reconciliation, and client support. What’s the Role? As a Team Leader, you will lead, empower, and develop a high-performing team of cash-flow modelers and financial data professionals responsible for delivering high-quality data and analytics across the US ABS market. You will combine people leadership with hands-on involvement in workflow execution, cash-flow modeling, client support, and the continued development of our securitized products data offerings. You will demonstrate strong market and product knowledge across US ABS, using your expertise to guide the team, solve complex data, modeling, and workflow challenges, and ensure the accurate and timely delivery of financial data and cash-flow models. You will establish and maintain consistent data management standards and best practices while fostering a culture of accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement. A key part of the role will be identifying opportunities to modernize and scale our workflows. You will champion automation, operational efficiency, and innovative approaches that improve the quality, timeliness, and scalability of our data products while enabling the team to focus on the highest-value work. You will partner closely with Sales, Market Specialists, Product, Engineering, and global counterparts to understand client and market needs, shape product and data priorities, and identify opportunities to strengthen our offerings and create new business opportunities.

The Impact you will have in this role: The Director, Portfolio Management is a senior team leader responsible for owning the enterprise “front door” advisory model that connects enterprise strategy, business priorities, portfolio planning, prioritization, investment trade-offs and value realization. This leader manages the Portfolio Management team and serves as a trusted partner to Business, Technology, Finance and Risk, ensuring portfolio priorities are clearly defined, investments are evaluated against enterprise objectives, and initiatives are positioned to deliver measurable outcomes. The Director is accountable for portfolio execution discipline, initiative outcomes, portfolio health visibility and executive-ready recommendations that help leaders make informed choices on funding, capacity, risk and value. Your Primary Responsibilities: • Lead the Portfolio Management team, including team priorities, operating rhythm, stakeholder coverage, delivery commitments, coaching, development and performance expectations. • Own the enterprise front-door advisory model for portfolio intake, planning, prioritization, investment trade-offs, value realization and senior leadership decision support. • Translate enterprise strategy into actionable portfolio priorities and recommendations, ensuring initiatives align to strategy, mission, risk appetite, capacity and financial objectives. • Be accountable for portfolio execution and initiative outcomes, including expected benefits, measurable value, portfolio health, delivery risks and realization of intended business results. • Drive and coordinate annual planning, including intake, evaluation, prioritization, demand shaping, funding alignment, resource capacity assessment and leadership review preparation. • Facilitate ongoing portfolio management routines that surface portfolio-level risks, issues, dependencies, resource constraints and decision needs for senior leaders. • Own portfolio metrics, including KPI and OKR design, operational and outcome measures, portfolio health indicators, value realization tracking and visibility into trends and performance insights. • Lead investment trade-off analysis across strategy, value, risk, capacity and funding, using data-driven criteria to recommend where to start, stop, scale, defer or reshape investments. • Provide review and challenge of plans, assumptions, risks, mitigation strategies and portfolio forecasts to improve decision quality, accountability and execution confidence. • Partner across Business, Technology, Finance, Risk and delivery teams on planning, demand, funding, capacity, prioritization and executive-ready insights, recommendations and decision materials.

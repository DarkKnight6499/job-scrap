Location: 4910 Tiedeman Road, Brooklyn Ohio Job Summary The Senior Data & Business Intelligence Associate is primarily responsible for the sourcing, manipulation, preparation, and validation of data for consumption – which may be in the form of production reports or as analytical data sets to answer questions or to perform predictive analysis. The Senior Data & Business Intelligence Associate uses queries and data mining to produce more complex reports and descriptive analysis that provide answers to questions such as “How do I measure?” and “What happened?”. Through self-managed data sourcing projects, the Senior Data & Business Intelligence Associate should liaise between technology partners and the business to engineer data into insightful business intelligence solutions, process improvements / automations, and reports. Success criteria include: leveraging critical thinking, technical expertise, and anticipation of business needs from technology and business partners to provide reporting and analysis or implement process improvements; leading conversations with business partners and effectively communicate with all stakeholders; and providing solutions based on an understanding of impacts / intersections with other business partners and lines of business.

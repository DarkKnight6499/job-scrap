Job Area: Engineering Services Group, Engineering Services Group > Support Engineering General Summary: We are seeking an Associate Support Engineer to provide Tier 1 support and maintain internally developed equipment across lab and production environments. This role involves hands-on hardware work, RF cabling, system troubleshooting, and IT infrastructure support. The ideal candidate will have foundational level technical ability, strong problem-solving skills, and the ability to operate independently while collaborating with cross-functional teams. Minimum Qualifications: • Bachelor's degree. OR Associate's degree and 1+ year of technical or related work experience. OR High School Diploma or equivalent and 2+ years of technical or related work experience. Key Responsibilities · Test Equipment Support & Maintenance o Install, configure, and maintain internally built test systems and platforms o Support system upgrades, modifications, and hardware integrations · Hardware & RF Work o Execute RF cabling, connector installation, and signal path verification o Perform hardware installations including installing system components in racks and hardware upgrades. o Troubleshoot RF and hardware-related issues to ensure optimal system performance · Troubleshooting & Diagnostics o Diagnose system issues using Linux command line tools o Support issue resolution across hardware, software, and network layers · IT Infrastructure Support o Assist with maintenance and troubleshooting of servers, network switches, and lab IT systems § Being able to determine if hardware is faulty and setting up replacement hardware o Support system connectivity, configuration, and network stability o Collaborate with IT teams for escalations and system improvements · Inventory & Asset Management o Track, manage, and maintain internal and external (commercial) test equipment inventory o Support equipment audits, lifecycle management, and asset documentation o Coordinate movement and allocation of lab assets as needed · Calibration & Site Support o Assist during calibration events and site-wide maintenance activities o Coordinate with calibration teams to ensure equipment readiness · Documentation & Communication o Maintain accurate records of work performed, issues, and resolutions through ServiceNow workorders and tickets. o Provide clear updates to stakeholders on system status and progress o Contribute to process improvements and standard operating procedures o Contribute to technical papers by adding to or creating Confluence pages. Preferred Qualifications · Experience with test equipment calibration processes · Familiarity with automated test systems or lab environments · Experience working with internal/custom-built hardware platforms · Basic scripting experience (Python, Bash, or similar) for troubleshooting/automation --- Physical Requirements · Ability to lift and move heavy equipment using appropriate tools and safety practices · Frequent bending, pulling, and handling of equipment and cables · Ability to work in lab and data center environments for extended periods --- Key Competencies · Strong troubleshooting and analytical skills · Hands-on technical aptitude · Clear and professional communication · Ability to work independently and within a team environment · Strong organizational and inventory management s .

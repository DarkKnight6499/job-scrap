About the Team: The IT Department at Relativity Space designs, builds, and maintains the technology ecosystem that powers our organization. At Launch Complex 16 (LC-16), our responsibilities are critical to mission success. We ensure seamless infrastructure operations across the Launch Control Center (LCC), the Horizontal Integration Facility (HIF), and the launch pad. We provide secure, high-performance computing environments that support mission control, vehicle integration, and engineering field operations. Our team safeguards critical systems and data through proactive security measures and compliance enforcement, ensuring mission readiness at every stage of the countdown. We deploy and support the hardware and software that enable our launch engineers to communicate efficiently and transfer data instantly across the Cape. With a focus on high availability, rapid response, and continuous improvement, we provide the foundation for launch cadence and technical excellence.

About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Senior Balance Sheet Management Analyst to join the Balance Sheet & Interest Rate Risk Management (BSIRRM) team, with a specific focus on supporting strategic projects and initiatives that enhance the balance sheet and net interest income forecasting process. This is an individual contributor role reporting into BSIRRM Governance and Oversight, Risk Management and Strategic Initiatives function. It requires nimble deployment of technical capabilities, alongside strategic thinking, risk management mindset, and IRR knowledge to drive forward complex initiatives, ad hoc executive requests, and technology transformation efforts. objectives Serve as the BSIRRM strategic initiatives analyst for forecasting process enhancement workstreams — requirements definition, development, UAT coordination, and post-implementation review across balance sheet and NII forecasting Support creation of executive analytics — enabling data flows, interaction with models, and synthesizing forecasting inputs and outputs into materials for senior leadership, committees, and ad hoc executive requests Translate strategic priorities into actionable plans and initiatives, ensuring alignment across the BSIRRM function Support change management items related to newly developed forecasting capabilities and ongoing process automation Monitor and report on progress, risks, and issues, providing regular updates to senior leadership

About the Team Invesco’s ETF Capital Markets team operates at the center of the ETF trading ecosystem, partnering closely with portfolio management, operations, authorized participants, market makers, exchanges and custodians. The team combines deep ETF market expertise with data and analytics to support trading and liquidity, identify and resolve market issues, and continually enhance the infrastructure supporting Invesco’s U.S. ETF platform. Team members work in a highly collaborative, fast-moving environment with significant exposure to both internal stakeholders and external market participants. About the Role The ETF Capital Markets Platform Lead, Americas is a highly visible role supporting the continued growth and evolution of Invesco’s U.S. ETF platform. This individual will oversee critical primary-market processes, lead secondary-market surveillance, and help ensure efficient trading and liquidity across the ETF platform. The role will work closely with ETF Portfolio Management, Operations and other internal partners while building strong relationships with authorized participants, market makers, exchanges and custodians. The successful candidate will bring strong ETF market knowledge, analytical capabilities and a solutions-oriented mindset to identify market trends, resolve complex issues and drive enhancements across the capital markets platform. The successful candidate will bring a strong combination of ETF capital markets expertise, analytical capability, relationship management and platform leadership. They will understand the mechanics of ETFs across both the primary and secondary markets, including creations and redemptions, basket construction, liquidity and trading dynamics, and will be comfortable using market data to identify trends, manage issues, and make informed decisions. They will also be an effective relationship builder who can confidently engage with authorized participants, market makers, exchanges and custodians, while partnering closely with Portfolio Management, ETF Operations and other internal stakeholders. The ideal candidate is highly organized, solutions-oriented and comfortable operating in a fast-paced market environment where they must balance daily execution with longer-term platform enhancements. Responsibilities of the Role Support day-to-day communications with institutional investors and consultants, ensuring timely, accurate, and professional responses to inquiries and information requests. Own daily coverage for the US ETF platform, including authorized participant (AP) and market maker (MM) orders, baskets, and creations and redemptions, and manage the business processes and operational requirements of the ETF Capital Markets Platform. Lead secondary-market surveillance of Invesco's US ETFs, using Bloomberg, custodian and proprietary data points to monitor secondary-market trading and liquidity and to identify issues for escalation. Develop fixed income platform capabilities and serve as a point of escalation for fixed income creation and redemption activity, working with ETF Portfolio Management and ETF Operations. Build and maintain relationships with exchanges, APs, market makers and custodians; collaborate with Portfolio Management, Operations and other key stakeholders to enhance information flow with the street and provide critical conflict resolution. Develop and maintain internal and external reports and associated capital markets data supporting platform and surveillance activity. Develop and maintain internal and external documentation related to the ETF Capital Markets platform. Coordinate execution of key initiatives and projects within the ETF Capital Markets platform across functional groups. Requirements of the Role Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Mathematics, Computer Science or a related discipline. Three (3) to five (5) years related work experience in one or more ETF secondary-market or market-making activity, fixed income ETF or primary-market activity, or an AP desk, ETF operations or custodian function. FINRA Series 7 and Series 63 required Strong understanding of ETF structure and mechanics, including creation and redemption, basket construction and the primary and secondary market ecosystem. Proficient understanding of financial markets, investment concepts and trading. Strong analytical skills to analyze and interpret various market data and apply such information to their day-to-day activities. Excellent verbal and written communication skills, including clear escalation and conflict resolution with external partners and internal stakeholders. Strong organizational skills and detail orientation. Ability to conduct complex analysis and present data in a meaningful way. Ability to work in fast paced dynamic environment, drive forward key initiatives and manage workflow on simultaneous projects. Self-directed; must be comfortable with establishing objectives and then working independently to achieve them, seeking managers’ input as needed. The capacity to rapidly learn the ETF issuer business, including development of a keen understanding of the drivers of success. Creative and self-motivated individual who works collaboratively in a high performance, team environment. Problem solving skills are necessary to identify trends and root issues and to recommend solutions that support the company’s business objectives. Preferred Skills Experience with AI tooling to drive process automation. Proficiency in Bloomberg and FactSet. Seed capital management experience.

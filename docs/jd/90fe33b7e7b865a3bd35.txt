Job Description As a valued colleague on our team, you will leverage your deep knowledge of the mortgage industry and TSP landscape to lead strategic initiatives and identify opportunities that strengthen Fannie Mae’s engagement with key technology and service providers across the Single-Family mortgage ecosystem. You will work closely with TSPs and internal product and business partners to align on priorities, share market insights, and support initiatives that advance Fannie Mae’s strategic objectives. THE IMPACT YOU WILL MAKE The SF Market Engagement - Customer and Partner Management - Lead Associate role will offer you the flexibility to make each day your own, while working alongside people who care so that you can deliver on the following responsibilities: Lead strategic initiatives and identify opportunities with mortgage technology and service providers that support business and product priorities across the Single-Family mortgage ecosystem. Serve as a subject matter resource on the mortgage TSP landscape, with a focus on point-of-sale and loan origination systems, appraisal and collateral solutions, and capital markets technology and service providers. Partner with Single-Family business, product, and technology teams to assess TSP capabilities, emerging solutions, and market developments and translate those insights into actionable recommendations. Lead technology and product-focused discussions with TSPs and internal stakeholders to drive alignment on strategic priorities, opportunities, and initiatives. Develop a strong understanding of TSP capabilities and their role within lender workflows to identify opportunities to support product adoption, market engagement, and customer needs. Gather and synthesize insights from TSPs, lenders, and internal customer-facing teams to identify market trends, technology needs, challenges, and opportunities across the mortgage ecosystem. Share TSP and technology insights across internal forums and with key stakeholders to strengthen organizational knowledge of the evolving mortgage technology landscape. Build effective relationships with external TSPs and internal partners while maintaining focus on strategic initiatives and business outcomes. THE EXPERIENCE YOU BRING TO THE TEAM Required Experiences: At least 4 years of experience in the mortgage industry with demonstrated knowledge of the mortgage origination lifecycle, lender workflows, and the technology and service providers that support the mortgage ecosystem. Experience working directly with mortgage technology TSPs, service providers, and lenders. Experience with one or more mortgage technology or service-provider segments, such as point-of-sale platforms, loan origination systems, appraisal and collateral solutions, or capital markets pricing and execution solutions. Experience working directly with mortgage technology or service providers, with demonstrated knowledge of TSP capabilities, lender workflows, and how TSP solutions integrate into the broader mortgage ecosystem. Experience partnering across business, product, technology, and customer-facing teams to advance strategic initiatives and align stakeholders around priorities. Experience leading discussions with internal and external stakeholders and communicating complex mortgage, technology, or product concepts to varied audiences. Experience gathering and synthesizing information from multiple sources to identify opportunities, inform recommendations, and support business decisions. Desired Experiences: Bachelor’s Degree or equivalent Experience working within a mortgage lender, mortgage technology provider, or mortgage-related service provider. Experience with appraisal and collateral technology or service providers, including knowledge of how appraisal, valuation, and collateral solutions support the mortgage lifecycle. Knowledge of the TSP landscape supporting Single-Family mortgage origination, collateral, and/or capital markets activities. Experience working with leading mortgage technology and service providers across loan origination, point-of-sale, appraisal, collateral, pricing, execution, or hedging solutions.

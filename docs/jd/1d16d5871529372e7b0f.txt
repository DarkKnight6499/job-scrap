Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Business Controls Management Level Senior Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As an Enterprise Risk Management/SOX Senior Associate you will support internal audit and control-focused client work within our Audit and Assurance practice, helping evaluate governance, risk management, and internal control processes. As a Senior Associate you will build meaningful client connections, manage assigned workstreams, and use critical thinking to break down complex concepts while mentoring junior team members and adapting to shifting client needs. In this role at PwC, you will contribute to enterprise risk and SOX activities by interpreting data, developing recommendations, and using technology-enabled methods to help clients strengthen control environments and respond to change. Responsibilities - Analyzing enterprise risk and internal control processes for client engagements - Assessing SOX and internal audit requirements across financial reporting and business operations - Developing workstreams for control design, testing, and remediation activities - Reviewing process documentation, control narratives, and risk matrices for accuracy and completeness - Interpreting audit findings and translating them into practical recommendations for control improvements - Coordinating with client stakeholders to gather information, discuss issues, and track follow-up actions - Applying auditing methodologies, risk assessment tools, and digital solutions to streamline delivery - Mentoring junior team members on assigned tasks, work products, and technical procedures What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 3 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for at least one of the following fields of study: Accounting, Analytics/Data Science, Business Administration/Management, Computer Science/Information Systems, Economics, Engineering, Finance, Financial Mathematics/Quantitative Finance, Health Administration/Public Health, Law/Legal Studies, Mathematics/Statistics - At least one of the following: Current pursuit of or an active Certified Internal Auditor (CIA), Certified Public Accountant (CPA), or Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA) certification(s) - Demonstrating internal audit, SOX, or controls testing coursework - Developing data analysis, reporting, or documentation skills - Interpreting business processes and control environments - Collaborating across teams while navigating ambiguity Travel Requirements Up to 60% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $77,000 - $202,000.

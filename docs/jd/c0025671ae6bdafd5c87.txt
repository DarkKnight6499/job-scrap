DESCRIPTION: The Financial/Credit Analyst III supports month-end close, government contract accounting, indirect rate calculations, revenue and billing reconciliations, and compliance reporting. The role works with finance, contracts, project, and operational teams to ensure contract costs and revenue are recorded accurately, calculations are well supported, and required submissions and audit materials are completed on schedule. This position has a significant role in Cost-Plus-Fixed-Fee (CPFF) contract accounting, including revenue calculations, indirect rate development, final rate variance invoicing, annual Incurred Cost Submissions, and rate audits. What you will do: Month-end close and contract accounting: Support month-end close activities, including revenue calculations and accruals, government contract labor and other cost allocations, fringe and G&A allocations, and monthly indirect rate calculations Prepare estimates of monthly receivables and liabilities for CPFF contracts Calculate CPFF revenue and prepare related journal entries Reconcile CPFF revenue to current billing amounts and investigate and resolve differences Calculate, reconcile, and support unbilled revenue balances and related entries Maintain accurate, complete, and well-documented accounting schedules and workpapers Rate calculations and invoicing: Support final indirect rate calculations and variance invoicing for CPFF projects Calculate final rate variances, prepare related variance invoices, and adjust or reconcile revenue to align with final invoiced amounts Assist with historical rate and variance work across multiple fiscal years Support annual provisional indirect rate calculations and related government submissions Review rate calculations for accuracy, consistency, and adequate supporting documentation Compliance, submissions, and audits: Support preparation of the annual Incurred Cost Submission (ICS), including calculations, reconciliations, schedules, and supporting documentation Coordinate and track work required to meet government submission deadlines Support rate audits by preparing calculations, reconciliations, and audit-ready documentation Respond to audit questions and requests by researching records and coordinating with internal stakeholders Follow applicable contract terms, company policies, and government accounting and compliance requirements Process and collaboration: Work with finance, contracts, project, and operational teams to obtain and validate accounting inputs Identify discrepancies, assess their impact, and communicate issues and proposed resolutions to management Help Strong Excel skills and experience working with accounting systems and large financial datasets Strong attention to detail, organization, analytical skills, and ability to manage multiple deadlines

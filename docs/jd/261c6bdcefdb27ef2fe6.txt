JPMorganChase is seeking an Analyst to join its Equity Capital Markets (ECM) – Syndicate desk. The Syndicate desk sits at the center of the firm's equity issuance franchise, responsible for the pricing, allocation, and distribution of equity and equity-linked offerings, including IPOs, follow-on offerings, block trades, and convertible bonds. As an Analyst in our (ECM) – Syndicate team, you will serve as a critical bridge between issuer clients, origination and coverage teams, institutional investors, and Equity Sales & Trading, the team manages real-time execution of equity offerings — translating client objectives and market conditions into pricing, timing, and allocation outcomes. This is a high-visibility, fast-paced role offering direct exposure to live capital markets transactions and the mechanics of bringing new equity issuance to market.

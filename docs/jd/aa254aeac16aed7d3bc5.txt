Audit Associate
Deutsche Bank
Location: New York, 1 Columbus Circle
Link: https://db.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/DBWebsite/job/New-York-1-Columbus-Circle/Audit-Associate_R0456585

Job Description: Job Title: Audit Associate Location: New York, NY Overview Deutsche Bank New York Branch seeks an Audit Associate in New York, NY to execute day-to-day audit work utilizing industry leading audit skills and techniques including Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Data Analytics, audit planning, execution of audit fieldwork and finding discussion and reporting in line with Group Audit Methodology and IIA Standards. Requires a Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics or related field or equivalent, and two (2) years of experience documenting process flows as part of audit walkthroughs, identifying risks including fraud, existence of controls, and identification of missing or deficient controls; utilizing process flows to develop testing procedures for testing of key controls using documented organizational policies and standards, regulations, data analytics tools, and Artificial Intelligence (AI) software; performing preliminary solutions for assessment of Management finding closure packs in coordination with audit leadership to meet internal standards as part of validation procedures; collaborating with internal teams both locally and globally to deliver audits and special projects including thematic reviews from regulatory or Management requests on key risk topics; conducting walkthrough meetings and discussions with stakeholders regarding processing risk and control environments; utilizing applications including Word, Excel, PowerPoint and data analytics tools to assess and document business and operational risks of processes. Salary range: $105,000.00 – $129,500.00/year. This position is eligible for Deutsche Bank's Employee Referral Incentive Program.

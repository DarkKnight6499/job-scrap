Essential Duties, Responsibilities & Accountabilities Support the Payments Technology Banking team to grow deposits and fee income for SouthState Bank. Develop strong working relationship with other members of the Corporate Financial Services teams. Meet or exceed annual budget. Maximize portfolio profitability through appropriate pricing and delivery of the Bank’s products and services to a nation-wide client base. Payments Technology Banking team services include complex treasury management services (ACH, Disbursement Services, Information Reporting, Card Sponsorship Programs, FBO Account structures) and other corporate banking services. Work with line of business head in Payments and Technology as well as risk management / credit administration / compliance partner(s) to develop new profitable Payments Technology Banking client relationships. Complete financial analysis and the underwriting and closing of new and existing client opportunities. Complete new and existing client ACH limit / credit memos in support of new business initiatives. Support new client implementation requirements including document collection, file management and view memos and coordinate with SouthState Operations / Risk Management and Compliance teams as required. Work with Payments Technology Banking team members to develop a strong referral / COI network and enhance the Bank’s reputation in the marketplace. Work with Treasury Services Officer to cross-sell SouthState’s treasury services products. Portfolio management responsibility to monitor client’s financial, operational and compliance status. Review portfolio with cross functional teams (risk management, credit, operations) on a regular basis. Ensure client financials and compliance requirements are received and reviewed in a timely manner. Support the bank’s strong relationship banking culture through on-going customer contact, call reports, quality customer experience and superior Perform industry analysis and participate in relevant industry related events. Regularly & efficiently, communicate successes and challenges to direct manager. In depth support for senior bankers for assigned clients, to include meeting prep, participation in client meetings, write call notes, assist with follow up items, among other items Comply with all the Bank’s compliance requirements including Bank Secrecy Act, OFAC and USA PATRIOT Act.

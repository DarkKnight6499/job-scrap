Job Summary As part of the Strategic Planning and Performance Management team, Manager, Strategy & Portfolio Optimization is responsible for implementing customer acquisition and retention strategic initiatives for various brands across the Residential line of business both within ERCOT and across our national footprint. This role works to provide thought leadership and lead discussions with senior management regarding residential market strategy, including evaluation of market positioning, existing portfolio management and growth opportunities.

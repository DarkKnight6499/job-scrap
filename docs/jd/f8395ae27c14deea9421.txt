About This Critical Role We are seeking a Quantitative Associate to join the SDG team in Denver, Colorado. The SDG Quantitative Team provides the analytical foundation for our practice, developing the financial models and debt structures necessary to fund large-scale infrastructure and real estate development. As a Quantitative Associate, you will work at the intersection of investment banking and financial analysis, delivering the data-driven insights required to structure complex transactions. About The Ideal Candidate The ideal candidate is passionate about financial modeling, thrives in a fast-paced environment, and possesses the ability to navigate multiple priorities with strong organizational skills. You must display the professional maturity required to interact effectively with senior partners, clients, and colleagues. Financial Structuring: Develop, refine, and stress-test sophisticated financial models that serve as the foundation for real estate-based public finance transactions. Analytical Rigor : Provide high-level numerical analysis and debt service modeling, ensuring accuracy and strategic alignment from deal inception through final execution. Process Innovation : Conceptualize and implement new modeling methodologies and technical solutions to improve operational efficiency and analytical precision. Strategic Collaboration : Partner directly with senior bankers to translate complex client objectives into viable financial structures. Transaction Management : Navigate multiple, overlapping transaction timelines with precision, ensuring that all quantitative deliverables meet the high standards of our practice. Technical Leadership : Contribute to the ongoing evolution of our quantitative systems, including the integration of advanced Excel automation and internal AI tools. Team Development : Collaborate with the Quantitative Team to share technical expertise, mentor junior members, and foster a culture of continuous improvement and high performance. Organizational Stewardship : Consistently model SDG’s values and contribute to the professional development and collaborative culture of the team.

Business Analyst – Allegro Location: Houston, Texas, United States | Practice Area: Business Consulting | Type: Permanent Help leading Energy organizations transform complex business challenges into technology-enabled solutions with Allegro. The Role Capco is a global technology and business consultancy, focused on the Financial Services and Energy sectors. For nearly 20 years, we have combined innovative thinking with deep industry expertise to deliver business consulting, digital transformation, and technology solutions that help our clients reduce risk, improve operational efficiency, and unlock new opportunities. As a Business Analyst specializing in Allegro, you will work closely with leading Energy clients to bridge the gap between business needs and technology solutions. You'll partner with stakeholders, functional teams, and technical specialists to analyze business processes, define requirements, and support the successful implementation and enhancement of Allegro solutions. This is an excellent opportunity to contribute to complex transformation programs while developing long-term client relationships in a collaborative consulting environment. What You'll Do Lead business analysis activities including stakeholder workshops, requirements gathering, process mapping, and documentation to support Allegro implementations and enhancements. Develop functional specifications, identify current and future state gaps, and translate business requirements into technology solutions that align with client objectives. Support solution delivery through testing, defect analysis, user acceptance testing, training, go-live activities, and post-implementation support. Produce high-quality consulting deliverables including business cases, proposals, presentations, process documentation, and reusable consulting assets. Collaborate with client and Capco teams to deliver successful transformation initiatives across Energy trading and risk management environments. What We're Looking For 5+ years of Business Analyst experience, including hands-on implementation and configuration of Allegro within Energy trading environments. Strong experience gathering business requirements, facilitating workshops, creating process maps, and producing functional specifications throughout the project lifecycle. Experience supporting testing, user training, production cutover, troubleshooting, and business user support during technology implementations. Strong communication, presentation, stakeholder management, and project delivery skills with the ability to work effectively in consulting environments. Bachelor's degree in MIS, Computer Science, Business, Finance, Economics, Financial Engineering, or a related discipline. Bonus Points For Experience working within a management consulting firm. Background in Energy, Power, or Commodities trading. Experience writing project proposals and supporting business development activities. Project management experience delivering enterprise technology initiatives. Advanced degree in a relevant discipline.

Associate Portfolio Manager (Equity)
Dimensional Fund Advisors
Location: Austin; Charlotte
Link: https://dimensional.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/DFA_Careers/job/Austin/Associate-Portfolio-Manager--Equity-_2026-8865

Job Description: Portfolio Management is responsible for the daily oversight and management of Dimensional’s funds and implements our investment philosophy across all our investment solutions. Portfolio Management interacts and collaborates closely with several departments including Trading, Research, Technology, Legal, Compliance, Operations, and the Global Client Group to ensure a superior investment experience for our clients. The Portfolio Management team ensures that Dimensional’s ETFs deliver consistent and efficient exposure to the dimensions of higher expected returns, as identified by the greatest minds in modern financial economics. Our portfolio managers achieve this exposure by implementing a daily and systematic portfolio management process. In addition to overseeing and enhancing this process, portfolio managers also interact with current and prospective clients. As an Associate Portfolio Manager, you will cover all aspects of managing ETFs and other types of portfolios, and will also be responsible for discussing our investment thesis and portfolios to a wide range of clients. Responsibilities Responsible for all aspects of managing ETFs and other types of portfolios. This will include tasks such as issuing buy and sell instructions in global markets, making ETF Create and Redeem baskets, trading foreign currency, electing on corporate actions, and evaluating the eligibility of securities. Monitor ETF characteristics and maintain portfolio structure following the process-driven approach of the firm, identifying buy and sell candidates in a systematic manner and partnering with trading teams globally to implement those decisions. Partner with Capital Markets to enhance ETF market quality and efficiently manage flows. Provide verbal and written explanations of Dimensional funds, performance, process, and other investment related issues internally and to both existing and prospective clients. Challenge current thinking and search out new opportunities to continually evolve Dimensional’s investment offerings. Collaborate with the Sales, Marketing, Consultant and Client Services teams to effectively respond to clients’ investment needs. Project work focused on making the investment process as efficient as possible.

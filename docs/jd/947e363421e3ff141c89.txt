Business Unit – Sr. Finance Analyst (Sr. Ops Finance Analyst) Position is located at our primary manufacturing and engineering plant in Billerica, MA. Job Responsibilities: Develop effective relationships with operations business partners (Mfg, Mat, Log, SC) Manage PBV (Period, Burden & Variance) spending Maintaining accurate factory spending & labor data for decision making (plan, analysis, close, reporting). Partner with Ops stakeholders so you are aligned closely with build plan, receipt, and PPV assumptions (business and accounting). Manage COGS & Inventory accounting for NEXX Maintain accurate COGS accounting (plan, analysis, close, reporting). Inventory Accounting (BS, BPR, E&O, reserves, GL Recon, Close & Reporting). Work with manager/team to define and manage core inventory data in a manner that allows for quick and accurate support of business decisions. Support Factory Acct Model Maintain accurate Factory Acct Model, prepare a comprehensive cost accounting analysis for mgmt. review which includes prior year actuals along with recommendations (activity rates & burden) for the upcoming year. Assist manager with BSR (Balance Sheet Review) and SOX/Compliance processes Lead and/or support adhoc projects assigned by manager Job Requirements: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Cost/Managerial Accounting, or related field of study 4-7 years of experience.

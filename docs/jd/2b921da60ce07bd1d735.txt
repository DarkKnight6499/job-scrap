THE OPPORTUNITY Nearwater's Risk Retention business provides asset-backed securitization sponsors (ABS, CLO, CMBS, RMBS) with financing for their retained risk retention interests, in compliance with Dodd-Frank and EU/UK Securitization Regulation requirements. As a Cross Product Associate, you will support origination and portfolio management across multiple asset classes and transaction types, working directly with senior originators and the Head of Risk Retention on live deal flow from initial screening through close and ongoing monitoring. WHAT YOU'LL DO Deal Origination & Structuring Support the Risk Retention team in executing financing transactions across multiple securitization asset classes, including ABS, CLO, CMBS, and RMBS marketing materials and presentation decks for meetings with global investment and PE firms, managers, and sponsors Draft term sheets, structuring memos, and investment committee materials Portfolio & Relationship Management Monitor the securitization market and provide timely insights to the team Participate in client discussions to support the origination process and ongoing client coverage

The Role At Kyndryl, we design, build, manage, and modernize the mission-critical technology systems that the world depends on every day. As our Market Intelligence & Price-to-Win Leader, you will serve as the strategic bridge between deep infrastructure technology and commercial market competitiveness. You will be the primary subject matter authority on global market pricing dynamics across Hybrid Cloud, server infrastructure, public/private cloud environments, and resilient backup and recovery services. In this high-visibility role, you will embed with strategic deal teams to translate complex market benchmarks, provider positioning, and competitive intelligence into actionable commercial strategies. Operating as both a technologist and a commercial strategist, you will decode market benchmarks, clarify delivery boundaries and bundled cost structures, and leverage cutting-edge AI analytical tools to sharpen Kyndryl’s competitive edge. Your expertise will directly influence executive pursuit reviews, optimize commercial constructs, and elevate win probabilities for transformative, enterprise-scale engagements. Key Responsibilities Market Pricing Authority: Serve as Kyndryl’s primary SME on market pricing structures across server infrastructure, private and public cloud platforms, backup and recovery services, and hybrid IT operating models. Competitive Intelligence & Benchmarking: Rigorously evaluate benchmarks from industry advisory firms; clarify market inclusions, exclusions, baseline assumptions, and bundled versus out-of-scope services to establish clear "in-market" positioning. Price-to-Win Leadership: Partner with pursuit leaders on multi-million-dollar transformational opportunities to architect winning pricing strategies, improve deal competitiveness, and balance aggressive market positioning with robust operating margins. Technical & Cost Synthesis: Collaborate with solution architects and cost advisors to align market comparisons with actual delivery architectures, evaluating core cost drivers across compute, storage, cloud consumption, and labor-based operational models. AI-Powered Analytics & Research: Harness advanced AI platforms and data analytics to accelerate competitive research, detect pricing trends, and automate repeatable intelligence methodologies. Executive Advisory & Asset Creation: Deliver high-impact briefings and recommendations for executive bid reviews, while authoring reusable market playbooks, pricing guidance assets, and training workshops for global solutioning teams.

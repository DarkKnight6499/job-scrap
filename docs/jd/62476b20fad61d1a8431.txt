Underwriting Coordinator, Facultative
Swiss Re
Location: Kansas City, MO, US

We’re looking for someone with an operations background who brings together technical expertise, process discipline, and a genuine enthusiasm for working with people. If you move easily between details and dialogue, processes and people, independent problem-solving and teamwork, we’d love to hear from you! About the Role This is a fantastic entry point for someone eager to build deep expertise in facultative reinsurance operations. In this role you will be an essential partner to our team of Facultative Underwriters supporting for all non–client facing activities, including but not limited to: Data entry of Submission risk clearance Policy tracking and client follow-ups for missing documents Provide administrative support regarding records management and processes for the business relationships in his/her area of responsibility Assists in the planning and reporting process including data gathering and data entry Enforces and maintains guidelines throughout all facultative risks including archiving and storage planning This role is ideal for someone who is equally comfortable diving into the details of a data set, identifying and resolving inconsistencies, organizing information in a clear and reliable way and picking up the phone to follow up with a colleague. A big part of the job is handling information flows, spotting gaps, and ensuring accuracy across systems and documents — so an analytical mindset and ease with structured tasks are as essential as engaging with colleagues, sharing ideas, asking questions, and building strong working relationships. About the Team We are a friendly and diverse group of individuals located in the U.S., Canada, and Mexico with a wide range of experience in the reinsurance industry. We support North America Property and Casualty Reinsurance teams and units in delivering on their core mandates. We are part of a global team called Reinsurance Business Operations, bringing together different perspectives, backgrounds, and expertise as we collaborate closely with our colleagues all over the world. About You Naturally curious, you enjoy getting into the technical complexities and understanding how processes and systems fit together, taking pride in accuracy and quality, all while thriving through conversation, collaboration, and being part of a team. You possess exceptional communication skills with strong customer focus and service orientation, especially when supporting underwriters and stakeholders. Comfortably working both independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment, you can support underwriters across different locations and time zones. Additional requirements include : Foundational understanding of the reinsurance industry and how it works, with an interest in developing deeper industry knowledge. Understanding of operational processes such as submissions handling, policy documentation and general reinsurance workflows Excellent attention to detail and ability to work with complex information accurately Apply sound judgment, logic, and common sense in decision-making Demonstrates willingness to share knowledge and act as a reference point for less experienced team members Comfortable working with data and maintaining organized and accurate information across multiple systems Ability to spot inconsistencies and resolve Experience identifying process gaps and contributing to operational improvements Strong sense of ownership and accountability for end-to-end task completion Our company has a hybrid work model where the expectation is that you will be in the office at least three days per week. This position is not eligible for full-time telework, relocation assistance or visa sponsorship. The estimated base salary range for this position is $52,000 to $78,000.

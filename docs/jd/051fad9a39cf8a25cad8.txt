KEY RESPONSIBILITIES This position is a unique opportunity to work collaboratively with a small team and play an active role in driving sustainability projects for the firm. The candidate will develop stakeholder communications for Bain Capital and contribute to Sustainability & Impact research and special projects. Communications / Reporting: Manage the Firm’s communications on sustainability, including annual Sustainability & Impact Report and UN PRI submission Collaborate with Investor Relations to prepare investor communications (primarily Global Private Equity) Lead additional firmwide Sustainability & Impact reporting and framework collaboration efforts Build the reporting vehicles to update key audiences on Bain Capital’s approach to Sustainability & Impact Strategy / Projects: Work closely with a wide variety of internal stakeholders spanning the firm’s business units and geographies, including with deal teams, portfolio teams and investor relations team Contribute to Sustainability & Impact integration across private markets investment strategies, identifying innovations and tools that help drive impact Research select Sustainability & Impact topics that are priorities for the Firm and business units Portfolio Management / Impact: Work with Global Private Equity and individual portfolio companies as needed to drive Sustainability & Impact performance Contribute to Sustainability & Impact measurement and tracking approach, including data collection process, analysis, and improvement initiatives Support development of events and forums on sustainability for internal and external stakeholders, including investors and portfolio companies This person will be based in Boston. Individual will collaborate daily with the investment team, portfolio group and portfolio companies, external partners and other members of the Bain Capital platform. We expect this team member to be a mission-driven individual with experience in project management, familiarity with impact and sustainability frameworks, and a passion for driving change in companies.

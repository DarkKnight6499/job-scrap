DOT IT Portfolio Management Analyst
Guidehouse
Location: US - DC, Washington; US - VA, Arlington

Job Family : Management Consulting Travel Required : None Clearance Required : Ability to Obtain Public Trust What You Will Do : Provide a broad range of technical services in support of DOT’s Information Technology (IT) portfolio, Capital Planning and Investment Control (CPIC) and investment management processes and functions, to include: • DOT-Wide IT Portfolio Management Support • Budget Formulation Support • Budget Execution Support • Business Process Reengineering You will also perform research on government-wide and DOT IT policy; validate IT budget formulation and execution IT data. Track progress on workflows Develop meeting minutes and track actions. Perform document control Support reporting of IT formulation and execution data Be responsive and proactive in responding to client needs. You will help respond to external (OMB) and internal (leadership) data calls. Assist in the various regular reporting; perform analysis on IT Spend Plan and individual procurements. Develop and maintain dashboard; prepare meeting materials. Support status reporting; assist maintenance of repository. What You Will Need : Must be able to OBTAIN and MAINTAIN a Federal or DoD "PUBLIC TRUST". Based on our contractual obligations, candidate must be located within the United States and US Citizen. Bachelor’s degree from an accredited college/university. Three (3) to Five (5) years of experience. Work on-site at DOT HQ in Washington DC. Must be responsive and proactive in responding to client needs. Understanding of how to prioritize tasks. Have background in financial management with the ability perform detailed analysis on IT budget formulation and execution data, attention to detail, work in a matrixed team. What Would Be Nice To Have : Experience with OMB IT portfolio management and CPIC data, processes, and reporting. The annual salary range for this position is $98,000.00-$163,000.00.

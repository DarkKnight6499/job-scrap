Treasury Asset Liability Analyst
United Community Bank
Location: Greenville, SC, US
Link: https://careers-ucbi.icims.com/jobs/2482/treasury-asset-liability-analyst/job

Overview The Treasury Liability Asset Analyst is responsible for supporting the institution’s asset-liability management (ALM) and treasury operations by analyzing interest rate risk, maintaining financial models, and ensuring regulatory compliance. This role requires a strong understanding of financial modeling, risk measurement, and data reconciliation, as well as the ability to support investment and derivatives activities. What You’ll Do Quantify and report the institution’s interest rate risk (IRR) position using the company’s ALM software model (the “Model”) Run ALM Model and have a thorough understanding of how the Model functions, the assumptions being used in the Model and how Model output is impacted by changes in the balance sheet, assumptions and interest rates Update, maintain, and document Model input assumptions Using the Model, produce monthly IRR measurements such as Earnings at Risk (“EAR”) and Economic Value of Equity (“EVE”) Reconcile the Model to actual balance (G/L) Be completely familiar with regulatory guidance, such as SR Letters and the Examiner’s Handbook, with respect to asset-liability management, and maintain copies of recent guidance documents for reference Ensure full documentation is maintained for all AL processes and procedures to ensure business continuity and to provide a basis for process auditing Assist with other Treasury department projects as needed Maintain Compliance with Model Policy Credit modelling of counterparties in internal credit model, and monthly counterparty reporting. Load, or assist Investment Portfolio Manager with loading, monthly bond data into bond analytics software, reconcile data as loaded to G/L. Load data from bond analytics software into ALM model, Assist with reporting from bond analytics software Derivatives marketing support Update customer derivatives pricing sheet from internal pricing model Requirements For Success Bachelor’s degree in finance, economics, mathematics or other related field required Banking or financial experience required Experience working with financial data and/or data modelling preferred Experience with database management preferred Experience in Asset Liability modelling preferred Excellent analytical and communication skills Proficient in Microsoft Office programs Ability to multitask under time constraints Participate in all required compliance training, including Bank Secrecy Act/anti-money laundering training, as well as internal and external training programs, online training, meetings, and seminars/conferences, etc. Conditions of Employment Must be able to pass background and credit check FLSA Status: Exempt SUPERVISORY RESPONSIBILITY This position does not manage employees.

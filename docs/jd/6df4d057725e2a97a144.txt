As an Agency MBS Strategist at Citi, you will lead prepayment strategy analysis for Agency Mortgage-Backed Securities and shape the relative value ideas that guide institutional client decisions. Sitting within Citi's Agency MBS team, this role combines deep market analysis with direct client engagement, giving you the opportunity to influence trading strategy and investment outcomes. You will work closely with sales, trading, and quantitative colleagues while mentoring junior talent within the team. Responsibilities Lead prepayment strategy analysis for Agency Mortgage-Backed Securities, identifying trends that inform trading decisions. Identify relative value trade opportunities by analyzing collateral characteristics and issuance trends. Analyze large datasets using quantitative methods to uncover patterns in prepayment speeds, issuance volume, and supply and demand. Evaluate the relative value of securities and recommend enhancements to existing valuation models. Produce written investment reports that translate data and market insights into actionable recommendations for clients. Build strong relationships with institutional clients and partner with sales, trading, and quantitative teams to address their questions and requests. Mentor and train junior analysts and associates, supporting their growth within the team. Evaluate risk in every decision, upholding compliance standards and safeguarding Citi's reputation, clients, and assets. Required Qualifications & Skills 6 to 10 years of experience in fixed income markets, with hands-on expertise in Agency Mortgage-Backed Securities. Ability to value fixed income securities and translate that analysis into practical trading recommendations. Proficiency in Python, Excel, and a database querying language such as SQL, applied with a methodical and organized approach to data analysis. Clear, effective written and verbal communication skills for producing client-facing investment reports. Strong interpersonal skills, a collaborative mindset, and a proactive approach to work. Active Series SIE, 7, and 63 licenses, or the ability to obtain and maintain them. Bachelor's degree. Beneficial Skills & Qualifications Master's degree in a related field. What We Offer This role gives you the platform to shape strategy for one of Citi's core fixed income businesses while building your profile with institutional clients and developing your expertise alongside experienced colleagues. Hybrid working model with three days in the office and two days working remotely. Exposure to a global network of institutional clients and cross-functional teams in sales, trading, and quantitative research. Autonomy to develop valuation frameworks and trade ideas while collaborating closely with experienced strategists. Learning and development resources to deepen your expertise in fixed income markets and strategy. Opportunity to mentor junior analysts and associates while growing your own capabilities. Benefits supporting financial wellbeing and work-life balance.

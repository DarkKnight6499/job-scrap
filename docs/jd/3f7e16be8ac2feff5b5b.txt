work with CRM’s internal team to ensure correct calculation and reporting of credit risk metrics Monitor and prepare report regarding adherence or breach to risk metrics; Maintain liaison with Head Office for effective communication and responsible for channeling loans that require HO approval Prepare credit risk reports to committees on monthly basis to Credit Risk Committee, to Risk Management and Internal Control Committee and the Board meeting quarterly Prepare portfolio report and analysis including but not limited to Key Risk Indicators, exposure, credit rating, concentration and exception management Provide periodic report to Head Office, including but not limited to asset quality report, credit risk report, credit counterparty risk report, major event report etc. Credit Risk Policies and Procedures Enhance existing credit policies and procedures in line with industry practice, regulatory guidance and guidance from Head Office Regulatory and Audit Liaison Assist in the Bank’s communication with regulators and/or auditors regarding credit related issues Coordinate internal/external audit or regulatory examinations, assist the Bank to prepare and respond to requests and questions Manage remediation process, including proposing action plans and track remediation progress Risk Committee Secretary Propose meeting agenda Prepare and compile meeting materials Prepare meeting minutes Qualifications Bachelor’s degree is required, preferably in Finance/Economics/Accounting major;

About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking an AI Product Area Business Analyst (Lead Product Manager) to join our Banking Operations Strategy and Transformation Department. In this role, you will: Manage product area strategy, aligning AI Business Implementation Change with Product Objectives Make decisions in complex and multi-faceted situations of global market dynamics, trends, and regulatory impact Oversee risk management to meet deliverables and drive new initiatives Collaborate and consult with business leaders, SMEs, product owners, business analysts, and Production managers Analyze AI usage patterns, feedback, and business outcomes to identify improvement opportunities. Optimize prompts, workflows, user experience (UX), and business rules to improve accuracy and adoption. Partner with Operations to gather feedback and convert insights into product backlog enhancements. Validate AI capability performance through testing, quality assurance, and outcome measurement. Measure realization of expected business benefits and provide ongoing performance reporting Required Qualifications: 5+ years of Product Management, product development, strategic planning, process management, change delivery experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education Desired Qualifications: Lead AI readiness for business adoption of technology changes, and provide AI adoption strategies for business lines within assigned product area Experience leading product strategy, roadmap development, and backlog prioritization within an Agile environment. Strong knowledge of product management principles, including Objectives and Key Results (OKRs), feature planning, and capacity management. Certification in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Agile methodologies, Product Management, or related disciplines. Ability to analyze complex products, services, and business requirements to identify solutions and drive outcomes. Proven experience partnering with technology, business, and operational teams to deliver product enhancements and process improvements. Strong understanding of Agile methodologies, Scrum practices, and product delivery lifecycles. Experience managing competing priorities and making sound decisions in a fast-paced, highly regulated environment. Knowledge of risk management, governance, and regulatory requirements impacting product development and delivery. Demonstrated ability to influence stakeholders, build consensus, and communicate effectively across all organizational levels. Experience leading cross-functional projects and mentoring or coaching team members. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and strategic thinking skills.

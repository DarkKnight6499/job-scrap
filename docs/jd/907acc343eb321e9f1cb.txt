Regular or Temporary: Regular Language Fluency: English (Required) Work Shift: 1st shift (United States of America) Please review the following job description: Provides enterprise-wide governance and strategic oversight of expense management, ensuring disciplined investment decisions aligned with financial priorities and strategic alignment ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Following is a summary of the essential functions for this job. Other duties may be performed, both major and minor, which are not mentioned below. Specific activities may change from time to time based on business needs. Support and help administer the enterprise Spend Approval process , ensuring consistency, transparency, and adherence to governance standards Provide independent financial analysis and challenge on material spend and investment requests Prepare executive-ready materials and summaries to support spend approval discussions and decisions Assess forecast impacts of approved spend and track actual outcomes versus expectations Partner with Line of Business (LOB) CFO teams and business leaders to ensure accurate, timely, and complete submission of spend approval requests Perform ad hoc analysis of operating and investment spend across the enterprise to identify trends, risks, and opportunities Track, analyze, and report on enterprise-wide expense efficiency initiatives Enterprise expense reporting to support LOB CFO teams and business Leverage and help develop automated reporting and analytical tools to improve process efficiency, data quality, and insight delivery QUALIFICATIONS Required Qualifications: The requirements listed below are representative of the knowledge, skill and/or ability required.

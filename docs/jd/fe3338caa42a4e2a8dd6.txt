At Allstate, great things happen when our people work together to protect families and their belongings from life’s uncertainties. And for more than 90 years, our innovative drive has kept us a step ahead of our customers’ evolving needs. From advocating for seat belts, air bags and graduated driving laws, to being an industry leader in pricing sophistication, telematics, and, more recently, device and identity protection. Job Description The Senior Associate processes, reviews, and investigates issues related to policies and/or systems by utilizing extensive knowledge of applications, using a broad level of discretion. The individual utilizes a wide variety of systems and media channels to investigate and resolve inquiries to improve customer loyalty and provide final resolution. Key Responsibilities • Identifies, validates, and analyzes system issues • May assist in defining code fix for systems • Leads root cause problem-solving, provides expertise and direction on process improvements and efficiency gains • Researches and responds to inquiries regarding rules and rates interpretations, contract, and/or products • May investigate and provide testimony in trials/depositions • May contribute to advanced analytic and metric research • May direct, lead, or consult on complex investigation efforts that may include internal/external audits or examinations • Under minimal direction, utilizes extensive knowledge of applications to resolve complex issues and may provide consultation in the design and impact of applications and/or support procedures Experience • 0-2 years experience (Preferred) Supervisory Responsibilities • This job does not have supervisory duties. Skills Compensation Compensation offered for this role is 19.76 - 30.33 per hour and is based on experience and qualifications.

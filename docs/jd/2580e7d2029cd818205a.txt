Job Description Summary The Deposits and Treasury Management Domain Enterprise Architect is responsible for defining and advancing the architecture that supports the Bank’s deposit and treasury management businesses. This role translates business and product strategies into actionable domain architectures and technology roadmaps, guiding long-term modernization of digital onboarding, treasury services, core banking integration, and client experiences. Working across business, Product, Engineering, Operations, Risk, and technology teams, the architect provides technical direction, strengthens architectural consistency, and helps the Bank deliver strategic change efficiently, securely, and at scale. Job Description Primary Functions of the Position: Develop and maintain current-state and target-state domain architectures, including business capabilities, platforms, applications, integrations, data flows, and key dependencies. Align domain architecture roadmaps and technology investment priorities with Deposits and Treasury Management business strategies, product roadmaps, and delivery backlogs. Shape long-term architecture direction for deposit onboarding, treasury and cash management services, core banking integration, digital client experiences, and automation. Serve as the primary domain architecture authority within enterprise standards, evaluating and challenging significant solution designs, guiding technical tradeoffs, and contributing directly to technical design and implementation as appropriate. Partner with business, Product, Engineering, Operations, Data, Risk, Security, and architecture peers to resolve cross-platform dependencies and enable effective execution of strategic priorities. Identify and prioritize architectural risks, technical debt, opportunities for simplification and reuse, and improvements to scalability, resilience, and operational efficiency. Develop and sponsor Requests for Comments (RFCs) and Architecture Decision Records (ADRs), participate in the Technical Design Forum (TDF), and elevate cross-domain decisions or exceptions through established governance. Ensure architectural direction reflects security, data integrity, operational resilience, auditability, and applicable regulatory expectations. Education and Experience Requirements: 7–10+ years of progressive technology experience, including 3+ years in enterprise, domain, or solution architecture. Demonstrated experience developing domain architecture views, target states, technology roadmaps, and technical direction across interconnected platforms. Deposits, Treasury Management, or banking platform experience strongly preferred; Fiserv core banking experience, particularly Signature, preferred. Bachelor’s degree in computer science, information systems, engineering, or equivalent practical experience. Essential Skills and Abilities: Deep technical understanding of enterprise applications, APIs, integration, cloud platforms, data architecture, security, and modern engineering practices. Ability to translate business capabilities, product strategies, and operational needs into coherent domain architecture and sequenced technology roadmaps. Strong technical judgment to assess complex designs, challenge assumptions, evaluate tradeoffs, and guide practical, sustainable architecture decisions. Understanding of banking technology modernization, platform integration, automation, and approaches that improve scalability, resilience, and efficiency. Ability to identify cross-platform dependencies, technical debt, architectural risks, and opportunities for simplification and reuse. Knowledge of architecture standards, decision documentation, security controls, operational resilience, and regulatory expectations. Strong communication, relationship-building, and influence across business, Product, Engineering, Operations, Risk, and technology stakeholders. Ability to connect architectural direction and decisions to business value, investment priorities, and effective execution of long-term strategic initiatives.

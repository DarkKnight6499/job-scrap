Job Description: This job is responsible for supporting data and technology requests, complex reporting and analysis, and modeling to manage risk and minimize credit and operating losses. Key responsibilities include reviewing and analyzing portfolio trends, supporting the development of more complex databases or queries to extract data, and supporting the production of data for internal and external reporting needs. Job expectations include working with Technology to implement changes necessary to support ongoing data needs. Responsibilities: Supports the development and execution of reporting to allow Risk and business partners to effectively identify and manage risk Analyzes trends and supports the data design, portfolio trends, loss trends, and portfolio dynamics to ensure alignment with business strategies Provides analytical support on various product strategic initiatives affecting multiple Risk functions and lines of business to ensure company goals are met Participates in the development of database production processes and performance reports for senior management, using data mining and other advanced analytical techniques to aggregate data Supports with responding to complex ad-hoc requests for information from both internal and external parties Provides support on cross-functional efforts to remediate data quality issues Monitors the outputs of controls and investigates alerts from the control checks Required Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field. 3+ years of experience in Risk Analytics, Risk Reporting, Decision Science, Credit Risk, Operational Risk, or a related analytical function. Advanced SQL skills with the ability to query and manipulate large relational databases. Experience with PowerCurve, Experian Decision Analytics, or comparable decision strategy platforms. Proficiency in Tableau for dashboard development and analytical reporting. Strong experience in report validation, data reconciliation, data quality testing, and controls. Experience performing UAT, parallel testing, or production validation activities. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and critical thinking skills. Excellent verbal and written communication skills. Demonstrated attention to detail and commitment to data accuracy. Desired Qualifications: Experience in Consumer Lending, Mortgage, Credit Card, or Enterprise Risk functions. Knowledge of decision strategies, and automated underwriting systems. Familiarity with Python, SAS, R, or advanced analytics tools. Experience working in Agile project environments. Knowledge of regulatory expectations related to model governance, data governance, and risk management.

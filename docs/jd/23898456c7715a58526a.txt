Your role General - Data Analytics - P3 - IND What you'll be doing What We Need Corpay is currently looking to hire a Senior Associate - BI Analytics & Reporting within our Payables division. This position falls under our Corporate Payments line of business and is located in Nagpur. You will report directly to the Associate Director BI in this role. How we Work As a Senior Associate - BI Analytics & Reporting, Corpay will set you up for success by providing: Assigned workspace in our Nagpur office Company-issued equipment Formal, hands-on training Role Responsibilities: The responsibilities of the role will include: Designing, developing, and maintaining the organization's business intelligence and reporting dashboards. Designing, developing, and maintaining data models and feeds for report development. Collaborating with business stakeholders to understand reporting and analysis needs, refine business questions, and translate them into technical requirements. Using Power BI and/or SQL expertise to understand business needs and deliver technical solutions. Preparing insights and conduct data analysis to answer business questions, identify key drivers, and support data-driven decision-making. Ensuring that data is accurate, validated, and easily accessible for reporting and analysis. Documenting report logic, KPI definitions, data sources, assumptions, and refresh cadence. Managing and prioritizing workload and identify opportunities to streamline processes and improve efficiency. Applying AI-enabled tools to improve productivity, quality, and efficiency across business intelligence and analytics activities. Supporting development and maintenance of management reports, executive views, and persona-based dashboards as needed. Following, learning, and sharing best practices for Power BI development. Ensuring compliance with security and data privacy policies and regulations. Keeping up to date with industry trends and emerging technologies and evaluate their potential impact on business intelligence and reporting. Qualifications & Skills Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field. Master’s degree or MBA preferred. At least 4 years of experience in business intelligence and reporting, with a focus on data visualization, dashboards, and reporting. Experience across requirement analysis, solutioning, design, modeling, implementation, testing, and end-user communication. Excellent communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams. Ability to adapt to changing reporting requirements, priorities, and project timelines. Demonstrated experience with Microsoft Power BI, with proficiency in complex DAX, Power Query, tabular data modeling, visualizations, RLS, composite modeling, Power BI Service, licensing, etc. Strong understanding of database systems, analytical processing, data modeling, and ETL/DWH frameworks. Experience with SQL development for BI and analytics use cases, including queries, views, stored procedures, and reusable data assets. Experience with cloud data platforms and data integration technologies such as Microsoft Fabric, Azure SQL, Azure Data Factory, AWS, or similar platforms is a plus. Experience using AI-assisted analytics, automation, or productivity tools, such as Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, Codex, or internal AI agents, for BI development, SQL, documentation, analysis, or workflow improvement is a plus. Experience with Microsoft Excel, SharePoint Online, Power Automate, Power Apps, Power BI administration/governance, REST APIs, PowerShell, DAX Studio, Tabular Editor, Python, or R is a plus. Experience in end-to-end implementation projects as well as maintenance and support projects is a plus. Experience in Banking or Financial Services industry is a plus. Experience in Agile methodologies is preferred. Work in diverse environments - geographical, cultural, and business - is preferred. Certification in Microsoft Power BI or SQL is a plus.

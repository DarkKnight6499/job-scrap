Position Purpose: The Sr Financial Analyst will support the financial planning, reporting, forecasting, and strategic decision-making processes for recently acquired businesses, including SRS Distribution, GMS, and Mingledorff's, while ensuring alignment with enterprise financial objectives and reporting standards. This role serves as a key finance partner responsible for managing core financial processes, supporting acquisition integration activities, and providing actionable insights to senior leadership. The position requires a strong combination of financial acumen, analytical rigor, and business partnership skills to drive forecasting accuracy, improve financial visibility, and support the successful integration of acquired entities into enterprise planning and reporting processes. Sr Financial Analysts regularly collaborate with executive leaders, accounting, operations, and FP&A teams to translate complex financial data into meaningful business insights and guide strategic decision-making. Utilize advanced financial modeling techniques to create detailed forecasts and provide variance analysis to explain key financial trends. Deliver clear, comprehensive reporting to senior leadership, highlighting critical insights and potential risks. Support month end close processes. 30% Preparing and Delivering Insights - Develop and present financial analysis both visually and verbally to business leaders. Complete financial modeling to project “what if” scenarios, and explain underlying causes of possible metric variations.

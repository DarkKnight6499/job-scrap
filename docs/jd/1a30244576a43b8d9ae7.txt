ROLE:

SentiLink builds the fraud detection and identity verification models much of the US financial system runs on. Each one has to pass review by our customers' model risk teams, regulators, and auditors without slowing how fast we ship. You'll own that domain end to end.

You'll lead a team of 3+ data scientists. You won't build our fraud products; your team measures, stress-tests, explains, and monitors them. It's a management role that stays hands-on: you'll review code, challenge methodology, and step in where needed.

The role includes real modeling work: assurance models that audit our production models, find gaps in how our products behave, and produce unbiased evaluation metrics.

We use AI in model risk today, but mostly in one-off projects. You'll make it a first-tier tool that scales what the team can do, putting you at the cutting edge of AI in model risk and governance.

You'll also work directly with model risk teams at some of the biggest banks and financial institutions in the space. Governance gates how fast they adopt our products, so this role is a direct lever on growth.

This is a remote, US-based role.

Technologies: Python, PostgreSQL, AWS (S3, Redshift, SageMaker), pandas, scikit-learn, XGBoost, MLflow, and modern LLM tooling.

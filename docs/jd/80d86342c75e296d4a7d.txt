Senior Financial Analyst - Specialty Printing Technology Solutions Description - Job Summary • This role is responsible for the E2E Finance support for WW business selling HP Ink cartridges to third parties who develop and sell their own print solutions. Responsibilities: Collaborate with business leaders to develop and present monthly flash, annual budget and long-range plans across multiple product categories Evaluating new investment opportunities Forecast, track and report division spend Review and approve sales comp plans and attainment Leading and advising on critical business initiatives, overseeing analysts across finance sub-functions and geographies/organizations. Drive process improvements, Represents the business unit/organization, and mentor lower-level employees, contributing to operational and strategic success. Perform highly complex financial analysis for a financial function or business, utilizing a unique understanding of the business to achieve objectives effectively. Provide information and counsel in the areas of controls, budgeting, and planning, return on investment decisions, and the development of overall business plans and strategies.

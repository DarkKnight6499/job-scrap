Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all . Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever. At Micron's Non-Volatile Engineering Group (NVEG), we are advancing the future of data storage technology. Our SSD Validation Engineers play a key role in ensuring our products deliver top-tier performance and reliability. Join our Enterprise Solid State Drives (eSSD) group and be part of a team committed to innovation and excellence. As a Staff Engineer, SSD Validation , you will own significant portions of the test automation infrastructure used to validate Enterprise Storage System interoperability. Working closely with test execution engineers and firmware developers, you will define automation architecture, raise the bar on test quality and efficiency, and mentor engineers across the team. This role is based in Longmont, CO, surrounded by stunning landscapes and a vibrant community. Responsibilities Architect and implement cross-platform storage APIs to control and characterize system behavior Design, document, and automate test cases validating SSD features, gross functions, and use cases Drive regression strategy and continuous improvement across a representative cross-section of systems Research, evaluate, and procure cutting-edge storage hardware platforms Provide technical direction, code review, and mentorship, partnering with firmware and product teams to align coverage with program priorities Minimum Qualifications Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or equivalent experience 5+ years in test automation development, validation engineering, or a closely related field Strong Python proficiency with emphasis on object-oriented programming and abstract interfaces Experience building cross-platform infrastructure across Windows/Linux/BSD and x86/ARM, including OS APIs, process control, memory management, and device driver interactions Working knowledge of enterprise storage products and protocols, plus AI-assisted development tools such as TabNine or Claude Code Preferred Qualifications Established track record building, owning, and scaling test automation frameworks Demonstrated ability to define and execute test strategy in a fast-paced, competitive environment Experience mentoring engineers or leading small technical efforts Strong communication skills, including conveying complex patterns through UML documentation Intense curiosity about the nature of complex computing systems The US base salary range that Micron Technology estimates it could pay for this full-time position is: $121,000.00 - $207,000.00 a year Additional compensation may include benefits, bonuses and equity.

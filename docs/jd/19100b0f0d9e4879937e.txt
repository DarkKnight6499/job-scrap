Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Cybersecurity & Privacy Management Level Director Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Cloud Security Consultant - Director you will help shape and elevate security strategies across client cloud environments, including IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS, within our Cybersecurity practice. As a Director you will set strategic direction, lead business development efforts, and guide executive-level client relationships while mentoring others and advancing new ideas that support business growth and responsible use of technology. In this role, you will bring deep cloud security knowledge, help identify vulnerabilities, conduct risk assessments and testing activities, and contribute to secure design choices across cloud service provider environments. Responsibilities - Leading cloud security advisory engagements across IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS environments for clients seeking improved security posture - Translating business needs into cloud security strategies, architectures, and implementation plans across one or more cloud service providers - Directing risk assessments, vulnerability testing, and security reviews to identify weaknesses across cloud platforms and workloads - Overseeing the design and deployment of security controls, monitoring capabilities, and incident response processes for cloud environments - Advising clients on cloud infrastructure, identity, access, encryption, and logging approaches that support secure modernization - Driving business growth by identifying new cloud security opportunities and shaping client engagement plans - Developing thought leadership, new service offerings, and practical methods that reflect emerging cloud threats and trends - Coaching managers and senior team members while creating opportunities for technical growth and leadership development - Building executive relationships across technology and risk leaders to influence direction and expand trusted client relationships - Validating adherence to professional standards, independence requirements, and the Firm’s code of conduct What You Must Have -

Help keep deals moving by combining precision contract review with responsive dealer support. In this role, your work ensures retail and lease contracts are accurate, complete, and ready to book—reducing delays and improving the dealer experience. You will solve issues in real time, balancing calls and production work in a high-volume environment. If you thrive on accuracy, speed, and problem-solving, this role is for you. As a Senior Specialist in Auto Finance Booking and Funding, you review and process retail and lease applications to confirm contracts meet credit decisions, program requirements, documentation standards, and funding validations prior to booking. You will also support dealership partners by phone, answering funding questions, clarifying requirements, and resolving issues that could prevent a deal from moving forward. You will help us deliver strong customer and dealer outcomes by maintaining high accuracy and consistent controls while meeting service level expectations. We work as a team, and you will partner with leaders and supporting teams to resolve issues and reduce downstream impacts.

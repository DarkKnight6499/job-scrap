Responsibilities The Product Consultant: Receivables serves as the product solutions architect for services used by Commercial clients to collect payments. As a receivables subject matter expert, this role is primarily responsible for advancing and prioritizing new sales opportunities through direct client engagement. The Product Consultant represents the bank’s Receivables solution set while actively listening for opportunities to support clients with ONB’s full suite of Treasury Management (TM) products and services. This position is responsible for setting pricing strategy on a per‑opportunity basis for Receivables solutions and works in close collaboration with Sales, Product Management, Implementation, and Service partners to drive profitable growth for the bank.

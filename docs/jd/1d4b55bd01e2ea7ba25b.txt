Industry/Sector Not Applicable Specialism Functional & Industry Technologies Management Level Associate Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a GIS Technical Consultant, Experienced Associate you will support client work in Esri consulting, using ArcGIS and other Esri tools to analyze, manage, and visualize geographic data for business needs across urban planning, environmental management, natural resource exploration, transportation, and other fields. As an Associate you will focus on learning and contributing to client projects, adapting to a fast-paced environment, and taking ownership of work that helps deliver value while building your technical skills and professional brand within our Advisory practice. In this role at PwC, you will support software implementation, training, and day-to-day client support, while developing a deeper understanding of how geographic information systems can help clients improve operational efficiency and use location-based insights in practical ways. Responsibilities - Supporting GIS consulting engagements by gathering client requirements and translating them into mapped business needs - Configuring ArcGIS tools and related geographic information system applications to organize, analyze, and visualize spatial data - Assisting in GIS data conversion, data cleanup, and validation across source systems and project deliverables - Developing maps, dashboards, and location-based visualizations that help clients interpret geographic trends and operational patterns - Collaborating on solution design for field service and operations modernization initiatives across planning, maintenance, and dispatch functions - Researching client environments and geographic datasets to identify issues, patterns, and opportunities for process improvement - Preparing technical documentation, project materials, and status updates that support delivery milestones and client communication - Applying digital tooling, AI fluency, and GIS architecture concepts to support implementation and ongoing solution support What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 1 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for a Master's of Business Administration or PHD degree in at least one of the following fields of study: Computer and Information Science, Computer Engineering, Computer Management, Computer Science, Engineering, Geography, Geological Engineering, Geophysics, Information Technology, Management Information Systems - Demonstrating GIS application experience in client support settings - Applying Esri mapping software across field service modernization projects - Gathering and analyzing spatial data from multiple sources - Adapting quickly across varied client and team priorities - Communicating ideas clearly while asking thoughtful questions - Learning technical standards and commercial awareness on engagements Travel Requirements Up to 60% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $63,000 - $140,000.

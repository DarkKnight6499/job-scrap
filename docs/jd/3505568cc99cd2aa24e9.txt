Back to search results Position Summary Deloitte is seeking a top-performing professional services Firm Enterprise Solutions Associate Director, Government Client Contracts Management and Contracts Group Lead to support our Government and Public Services Practice (GPS). In this role, you will collaborate cross-functionally within the Operational Excellence team and across Deloitte's Enterprise Solutions. This role is designed for a visionary leader who can manage day-to-day operations while inspiring and developing a team within Deloitte’s Government Contracts & Procurement Organization (GCPO). You will be aligned with a specific Account, leading a dedicated contracts team and serving as a trusted source of strategic contracting advice to give Deloitte a competitive advantage in winning Federal engagements. You will mentor and develop a team of contracts professionals, ensuring they align with Deloitte’s strategic objectives and values. By fostering a collaborative, efficient, and highly effective environment, you will enhance team performance, drive professional growth, balance workloads, and facilitate cross-functional initiatives. While all locations will be considered, this role offers a flexible hybrid scheduled with candidates based in Washington, D.C. or Arlington, VA (or nearby) preferred. Work You’ll Do As a Group Lead, you will direct a team of contracts professionals in all facets of Federal contract administration. You will manage complex, high-value, and strategic contractual matters that directly impact firm revenue generation and retention.

The Sustainable Investing team at JPMAM unifies the firm’s ESG efforts and sustainable investment strategies and partners with our global investment and distribution teams to develop dedicated sustainable investing research, thought leadership and solutions – by way of leveraging our differentiated investment platform, proprietary technology, data analytics, deep ESG expertise and culture of innovation – and offers innovative sustainable solutions to our institutional and retail clients. JPMAM is looking for a Sustainable Investing Research Analyst to contribute to all aspects of the team’s work. You will be part of a team led by the Global Head of Sustainable Investing Research Insights reporting to one of two Sustainable Investing Research Leads and working collaboratively with financial analysts and portfolio managers. Our preference is for candidates with strong, existing investment research experience in delivering sustainability insights – covering ESG risk assessment and attribution as well as implementing methodologies and investment frameworks across listed equity, credit and other asset classes. Our internal culture demands high standards but is friendly, respectful and places high value on diversity and inclusion. Job Responsibilities Research financially material sustainability issues across industries as well as related policies and methodologies in order to inform JPMAM investment strategies. Develop analytical capabilities and investment tools to assess sustainability risks and opportunities or select appropriate third-party solutions. Write research notes and presentations to educate and enhance the firm’s and its clients’ understanding of the nexus between sustainability and investment management. Collaborate and communicate with a global network of JPMAM portfolio managers, analysts and technology partners, with a focus on NY-based investors. Required qualifications, capabilities and skills 3+ years of Sustainable investing research experience Subject matter expertise in sustainability-related financial analysis Work is fast paced, with tight deadlines and high pressure for results. Strong ability to communicate both orally and in writing. Intellectually curious and driven by desire to build insight into a business through both quantitative and qualitative analysis.

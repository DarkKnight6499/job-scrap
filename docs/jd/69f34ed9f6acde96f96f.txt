The Crown Is Yours As a Discord Community Associate, you'll help build the most engaged, trusted, and exciting sportsbook community in the game. You'll be the voice of our brand inside our official Discord server, connecting with players who live and breathe sports, betting, and the biggest moments across the sports calendar. Working closely with the Community Lead, you'll create memorable experiences through real-time conversations, engaging content, and community events while bringing your passion for sports and gaming to every interaction. What you'll do Be an active presence in our Discord server, creating authentic conversations around live sporting events, betting trends, and the moments that matter most to our players. Build credibility with our community by engaging confidently with sports fans and sportsbook players while responding quickly, accurately, and thoughtfully to questions and feedback. Create and execute community-first experiences, including giveaways, Ask Me Anything sessions, live watch parties, prediction contests, and themed events that deepen engagement. Identify emerging conversations, player sentiment, and trends across sports, gaming, and community channels, then share actionable insights to help improve our app, features, and player experience. Champion the voice of the community across cross-functional teams while maintaining our brand tone and fostering a welcoming, engaging environment. Support community coverage during peak sports moments, including evenings, weekends, major sporting events, and championship games on a rotating schedule. What you'll bring At least 1 year of experience building, moderating, or growing online communities, preferably within sports, gaming, sports betting, esports, or consumer entertainment. Deep familiarity with Discord, including mod tools, roles, bots, and engagement features. Proven ability to create thumb-stopping content and conversations tailored to specific communities. Excellent written communication skills with the ability to adapt your voice in real time, whether you're celebrating a big win, answering player questions, or navigating sensitive situations. A strong sense of urgency and ownership — you’re fast, proactive, and solutions-focused. An understanding of Sportsbook or Daily Fantasy Sports products and can talk betting lines, big games, or prop picks without missing a beat. Experience moderating high-volume communities or social platforms during live events is a plus.

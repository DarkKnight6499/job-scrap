The Opportunity The Creativity & Productivity (C&P) Finance team is seeking a Senior Financial Manager to lead the ARR Consolidation function, reporting to the Director of FP&A. This role sits at the center of a fast-scaling business and is responsible for driving financial clarity, operating rigor, and decision velocity across ARR planning and reporting. You'll lead a team, own the ARR narrative end-to-end, and build the processes, tools, and partnerships that let the function scale. What You'll Do Lead and develop a high-performing ARR Consolidation team, operating as a player-coach who balances hands-on ownership with team growth Own end-to-end ARR forecasting, planning, and reporting Drive the Weekly Executive Team update, Quarterly Rolling Forecast (QRF), Quarterly Business Review (QBR), Quarterly Financial Review (QFR), and Annual Planning cycles Build and scale processes, tools, and automation for ARR reporting, partnering with Data Science and Finance Operations to drive efficiency and insight Serve as a key cross-functional partner across Route-to-Market, Geography, and Product Groups to align on one cohesive C&P narrative Deliver executive-ready insights and materials for senior leadership, Board, and investor-related requests Uphold strong financial guardianship — ensuring controls, consistency, and trust in ARR outputs while acting as an independent, structured voice What You Need to Succeed 8+ years of FP&A experience with SaaS/topline exposure (ARR, subscription metrics), including 3+ years of people management experience Proven track record building and developing high-performing teams Extensive hands-on experience with planning tools (TM1, Hyperion) and business intelligence tools (Power BI or similar) Strong business partnership skills — able to build trust and drive alignment across cross-functional finance and business teams Comfortable operating in ambiguity, with a structured, ownership-driven approach Excellent communication and storytelling skills, with strong executive presence About Adobe Adobe empowers everyone to create through innovative platforms and tools that unleash creativity, productivity and personalized customer experiences.

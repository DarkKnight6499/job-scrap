Role Summary The Distribution Finance team is responsible for the financial health of NLG’s life and annuity sales– providing financial leadership through insights and recommendations, monitoring trends and driving National Life Group to be a culture of fact-based decision makers. This position provides financial support to key business partners across the organization including financial planning and analysis, financial and operational metrics, and financial forecasting. This is a multi-faceted project and operational support role that focuses on National Life’s selling customers. To provide an outstanding experience to our customers, it is critical that we have timely, accurate, and impactful insights and analysis. We are seeking an analyst to generate and deliver value that will drive improvements, and enable smart decision making, in a pay for performance environment. In this role you will work with a broad set of data sources, analytical tools and visualization software to help the business solve real problems and drive desired results. A successful candidate will display strong analytical as well as interpersonal skills, and a deep interest in understanding our business. The ability to develop strong relationships with our internal and external customers, fully understanding their needs, and using data to drive business impact will be paramount. This position will work as part of a team on the day-to-day enterprise distribution finance processes and have the ability to work autonomously in a fast-paced and dynamic environment. This position currently offers an onsite hybrid work schedule, with the expectation that you are in the office four (4) days per week during onsite core days. Our current onsite core days are Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. The work schedule type and core days are subject to change with advance notification and manager discretion.

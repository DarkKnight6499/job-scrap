Overview This is a hybrid role with the expectation that time working will regularly take place inside and outside of a company office. We are seeking a dynamic, highly motivated Treasury Management Sales Associate to support Treasury Management Sales Advisors across Middle Market Banking, and/or Commercial Finance portfolios. The associate partners with advisors to drive portfolio growth, deepen client relationships, execute strategic sales initiatives, and deliver treasury solutions that meet client needs. This role combines strategic sales support, client engagement, independent sales execution, and overall treasury relationship support. The individual will collaborate with key internal partners to identify opportunities, execute sales strategies, and to support seamless execution and an exceptional client experience Responsibilities & Qualifications • Partner with Treasury Management Sales Advisors to develop and execute sales strategies, portfolio plans, and go-to-market initiatives. • Conduct client, prospect, industry, and competitive research to identify treasury opportunities and support revenue growth. • Prepare account structures, pricing proposals, fee comparisons, and client presentations. • Manage strategic client portfolios by supporting client retention, wallet share expansion, and treasury solution adoption. • Independently manage designated treasury sales opportunities from discovery through close, including inbound referrals and reactive opportunities. • Conduct treasury discovery conversations to understand client workflows, payment processes, liquidity needs, and fraud mitigation requirements. • Deliver consultative recommendations aligned with client business objectives and treasury best practices. • Partner with implementation and service teams to ensure client satisfaction and successful adoption of treasury services. • Proactively manage accurate pipeline, forecasting, proposal tracking, and reporting activities within applicable CRM, including clean opportunity submissions. • Stay current on payment innovations, fintech developments, treasury trends, banking regulations, and competitive offerings. • Provide onboarding assistance and best practice sharing for newer Sales Associates as appropriate.

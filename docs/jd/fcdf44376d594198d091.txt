JOB OPENING #: 9747 POSITION TITLE: Associate Financial Systems Administrator DEPARTMENT: Financial Planning and Analysis (FP&A) - POSITION SPECIFICATIONS: Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems, Computer Science, Finance, Data Analytics or related field required 0 - 2 years of experience in system administration, data analysis or financial systems Experience with MS Excel required Experience with Power BI or similar required Understanding of financial processes (forecasting, budgeting, reporting) is required Scripting or automation experience required Experience with Oracle EPM Cloud (FreeForm, Planning) is a plus MAJOR DUTIES: The Oracle EPM Cloud System Administrator is responsible for the administration, maintenance, and support of Oracle Enterprise Performance Management (EPM) Cloud applications, including Free Form, Planning, Enterprise Data Management (EDM), and Narrative Reporting. This role ensures system stability, performance, and availability while supporting business users and enabling efficient financial planning and reporting processes. The position requires strong technical expertise in EPM administration, data integration, and environment management within a cloud-based platform.

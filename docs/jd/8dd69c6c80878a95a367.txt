Cherry Bekaert’s Risk Advisory practice is evolving as quickly as the challenges our clients face, delivering innovative solutions and strategic guidance that help organizations scale with confidence. As a result of our continued growth, a position has been created for a Manager (Operational Transformation) to lead client-facing initiatives that improve critical processes, strengthen operational readiness, and help organizations translate transformational change into practical, sustainable ways of working. If you have demonstrated experience leveraging your project management skills and a problem-solving mindset at the intersection of business processes, technology, people, risk, and controls (particularly within manufacturing, transportation and logistics) … keep reading. As a Manager (Operational Transformation), you will: Lead operational transformation and process optimization engagements from planning through execution, translating complex business challenges into practical, scalable solutions Assess and redesign end-to-end business processes, including Procure-to-Pay, Order-to-Cash, Hire-to-Retire, and Record-to-Report, identifying opportunities to improve efficiency, controls, accountability, and scalability Build and manage comprehensive project plans that define workstreams, milestones, dependencies, risks, deliverables, and stakeholder responsibilities Help clients navigate the operational impact of technology transformation, including evaluating process and control implications, developing test plans, and preparing organizations for successful deployment and operational readiness Develop the tools that make transformation sustainable, including standard operating procedures, work instructions, job aids, change documentation, operational readiness assessments, and KPI frameworks Advise clients on organizational and operating-model improvements, identifying opportunities to optimize resources, clarify roles and responsibilities, and build processes that support continued growth Translate detailed analysis into executive-level insights, developing clear recommendations, presentations, and summaries for senior executives and boards while helping stakeholders understand business implications and recommended actions Lead client teams through ambiguity and change, looking beyond individual process gaps to identify broader solutions and opportunities for transformational improvement Manage engagement delivery and develop team members, balancing project quality, client expectations, budgets, timelines,

Division Overview Great American Financial Institution Services Division (FIS) provides risk management, fee income, and product enhancement programs to vehicle lenders and lessors, commercial equipment lenders, and mortgage lenders throughout the U.S. and Canada. FIS serves a diverse client base that includes commercial banks, credit unions, finance companies, buy-here pay-here dealers, and leasing and rental organizations. The division helps financial institutions effectively manage risk while supporting their business and customer needs. http://www.greatamericaninsurancegroup.com/about-us/business-operations/division/financial-institution-services Position Overview The FIS Division is seeking a Claims Intern (Spring 2027) to provide administrative and project support throughout the claims process. This opportunity is designed for a motivated college student interested in building practical business, insurance, or risk management experience while supporting the day-to-day operations of the Claims team. The intern will work a minimum of 16 hours per week during business hours. The position follows a hybrid schedule based out of the downtown Cincinnati office, with at least 50% of working time in the office; the initial training period will be predominantly in office. Responsibilities Provide project and administrative support to Claims staff to help facilitate efficient claims operations. Manage incoming document mailboxes and add supporting documentation and vendor reports to electronic claim files. Review, enter, and process vendor invoices for payment within established authority levels. Prepare and distribute correspondence to appropriate parties. Learn and use claims systems, databases, and other business applications to complete assigned work. Maintain accurate and organized claim documentation and records. Communicate effectively with team members and other stakeholders while supporting claims-related activities. Participate in special projects and additional assignments as needed.

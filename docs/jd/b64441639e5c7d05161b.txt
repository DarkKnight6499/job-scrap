Job Description SUMMARY Ares Management is seeking a highly motivated Senior Associate, AI Engineer to help build and operate the firm's enterprise AI platform and production AI capabilities supporting use cases across investment and corporate functions. This is a hands-on engineering role within the central Data & AI organization. The Senior Associate will work with experienced AI engineers, platform engineers, data engineers, product managers, and business-aligned technology teams to design and deliver production-quality AI services and applications. The successful candidate will combine strong software engineering fundamentals with practical experience developing Generative AI and machine learning solutions. They will contribute to shared platform capabilities such as model access, retrieval, agentic workflows, evaluation, APIs, observability, and developer tooling, while also helping product and application teams bring AI use cases from prototype through production. This role is well suited for an engineer who wants to work across modern AI technologies while developing deep experience in enterprise-grade architecture, security, governance, reliability, and software engineering.

Job Summary This role supports short- and long-term workforce planning through data analysis, forecasting, and strategic insights. The position partners with leaders across the organization to evaluate operational needs, analyze workforce trends, and develop recommendations that support operational and financial objectives. The ideal candidate can translate complex data into meaningful insights, communicate findings effectively to senior leadership, and influence business decisions through data-driven recommendations. Develop forecasts and analytical models to evaluate current and future workforce needs based on business demand, organizational growth, and operational priorities. Translate complex data and analysis into clear, actionable insights and recommendations, and communicate findings to senior leadership through compelling presentations and storytelling. Create and maintain reports, dashboards, visualizations, and presentations using Tableau and other Business Intelligence tools to support strategic and operational planning. Partner with Business Intelligence teams and cross-functional stakeholders to identify reporting and analytical needs, develop effective reporting solutions, and independently address business questions when needed. Support budgeting and financial planning by evaluating staffing requirements, forecasting workforce costs, and providing data-driven resource recommendations. Assess workforce impacts and planning requirements associated with onboarding new business groups, functions, or strategic initiatives, and collaborate with business leaders to align workforce plans with organizational goals.

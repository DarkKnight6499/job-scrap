Job Description Senior Implementation Consultant - Investment Management Locations : NY, NY / Boston, MA / Chicago, IL / Jacksonville, FL / San Francisco, CA | Hybrid Get To Know Us: SS&C is looking for a Senior Implementation Consultant to join the Investment Management (IM) Implementations team responsible for front-office components of the Genesis Platform, including Eze OMS and Genesis Portfolio Management. We are looking for an organized individual that can coordinate with a variety of internal/external stakeholders, communicate with project managers, and consult on solutions to best fit clients’ business use cases. If you enjoy working with financial technology and being a knowledgeable product expert in a collaborative team-based environment, we want to hear from you! Implementation Consultants play an essential role in nearly every phase of SS&C’s implementation project, from post-sales project design to client requirement delivery. Collaboration is an important part of the role, to ensure that the onboarding requirements are understood by internal/external stakeholders and implemented to plan, ultimately driving client satisfaction through effective project execution. In addition, Implementation Consultants are responsible for creating effective, scalable, and supportable configurations through testing and analysis of different workflows based off initial requirement gathering. We expect the individual to help develop and apply best practices with an eye on constantly improving the team’s effectiveness. Why You Will Love It Here!

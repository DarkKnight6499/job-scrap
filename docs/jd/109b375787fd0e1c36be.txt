SMBC Group is a top-tier global financial group. Headquartered in Tokyo and with a 400-year history, SMBC Group offers a diverse range of financial services, including banking, leasing, securities, credit cards, and consumer finance. The Group has more than 130 offices and 80,000 employees worldwide in nearly 40 countries. Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group, Inc. (SMFG) is the holding company of SMBC Group, which is one of the three largest banking groups in Japan. SMFG’s shares trade on the Tokyo, Nagoya, and New York (NYSE: SMFG) stock exchanges. In the Americas, SMBC Group has a presence in the US, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, and Peru. Backed by the capital strength of SMBC Group and the value of its relationships in Asia, the Group offers a range of commercial and investment banking services to its corporate, institutional, and municipal clients. It connects a diverse client base to local markets and the organization’s extensive global network. The Group’s operating companies in the Americas include Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corp. (SMBC), SMBC Nikko Securities America, Inc., SMBC Capital Markets, Inc., SMBC MANUBANK, JRI America, Inc., SMBC Leasing and Finance, Inc., Banco Sumitomo Mitsui Brasileiro S.A., and Sumitomo Mitsui Finance and Leasing Co., Ltd. The anticipated salary range for this role is between $150,000.00 and $200,000.00. The specific salary offered to an applicant will be based on their individual qualifications, experiences, and an analysis of the current compensation paid in their geography and the market for similar roles at the time of hire. The role may also be eligible for an annual discretionary incentive award. In addition to cash compensation, SMBC offers a competitive portfolio of benefits to its employees. Role Description SMBC's Global Financial Institutions Group (GFIG) is seeking an Associate to join its Insurance Client Coverage team. The role provides the opportunity to work closely with insurance companies and financial sponsors, supporting the origination of financing, liquidity, and risk management solutions, public and private capital raising transactions, and strategic advisory engagements. In addition, the team serves as a key lending partner to SMBC's insurance clients, delivering customized financing solutions across a broad range of transaction types. Role Objectives: Delivery Demonstrate a strong team-oriented mindset and the ability to collaborate effectively with colleagues across product groups, industry teams, and geographic regions. Manage multiple projects simultaneously while maintaining a high degree of accuracy, organization, and attention to detail. Partner with GFIG's Insurance Client Coverage team as an integral part of debt capital markets origination efforts, and collaborating with Banking, Advisory, and Global Markets professionals to deepen client relationships, identify opportunities, and drive new business development. Play a key role in the origination, approval, and execution of corporate lending transactions, including deal structuring, pricing, term sheet negotiation, financial modeling and analytics, credit and risk analysis, profitability assessment, capital allocation, legal documentation review, and ongoing portfolio monitoring. Develop an understanding of insurance company accounting, rating agency, and regulatory capital frameworks, including Statutory Accounting, U.S. GAAP, IFRS, Risk-Based Capital (RBC), Solvency II, the Solvency Modernization Initiative (SMI), and IAIS capital standards. Build expertise in insurance company capital structures and leverage considerations, with particular focus on rating agency methodologies and the treatment of debt, hybrid, and contingent capital instruments. Qualifications and Skills Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Accounting, or a related field.1-3 years of experience in corporate finance, corporate banking, debt capital markets, investment banking, ratings advisory, or a related field at a leading financial institution or rating agency.Strong financial modeling, analytical, and quantitative skills, with the ability to assess credit, capital, and liquidity profiles.Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to prepare client presentations, credit memoranda, and transaction-related materials.Experience within the insurance sector is preferred, although candidates with broader financial institutions or corporate banking experience will also be considered.Demonstrated interest in insurance markets, capital structures, and strategic financing solutions.

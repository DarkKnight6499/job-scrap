Play a key role in bringing innovative financial products to market through commercialization, launch readiness, and cross-functional execution. As a Financial Product Senior Associate in JPMorgan Chase, you will play a pivotal role in shaping and managing strategic product initiatives from development through market launch. You will lead end-to-end readiness efforts and partner across product, field & servicing, and marketing organizations to drive successful launches, adoption, and scalable growth. Your role will include defining readiness requirements, coordinating cross-functional execution, monitoring launch performance, and identifying opportunities to optimize and scale initiatives throughout the product lifecycle. Job Responsibilities Analyze market trends and customer data to inform product positioning and pricing strategies, ensuring alignment with business objectives. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to support the development and execution of product lifecycle management initiatives. Monitor and evaluate product performance metrics, identifying opportunities for improvement and implementing necessary adjustments. Assist in the preparation and execution of controlled tests to validate product hypotheses and drive data-driven decision-making. Analyze customer behavior and sentiment to increase engagement and optimize their experience. Ensure compliance with regulatory standards by designing and monitoring controls across customer-facing processes. Partner with internal and external stakeholders to coordinate launch activities, manage dependencies, and drive successful market expansion. Required Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills 5+ years of experience or equivalent expertise in financial product management, focusing on lifecycle and performance optimization. Demonstrate proficiency in analyzing market dynamics and customer data to guide strategic product decisions. Applies advanced analytical techniques to interpret financial models, evaluate returns, and inform investment recommendations. Employs structured ideation frameworks to design innovative, customer-centric solutions within budget, time, and regulatory constraints. Experience utilizing PMI/Agile methodologies to deliver customer-facing initiatives on scope, schedule, and budget while managing delivery and operational risks. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including executive-level presentations and leadership updates. Strong organizational and project management skills with attention to detail and execution rigor. Preferred Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills Experience supporting consumer deposit products, credit cards, lending products, or other consumer financial products.

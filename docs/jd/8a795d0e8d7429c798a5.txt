As an Ontada Sr. Associate Service Desk Agent you will provide advanced frontline support while serving as a product specialist capable of independently resolving more complex customer issues and supporting team effectiveness. Key Responsibilities Deliver white-glove customer experiences through proactive communication, case ownership, and issue resolution. Resolve moderate-complexity incidents and requests requiring deeper application and workflow knowledge. Serve as a subject matter resource for one or more assigned products, workflows, or support processes. Analyze customer-reported issues and perform intermediate troubleshooting using available support tools and resources. Identify patterns, trends, and recurring customer issues and recommend improvements. Assist B1 representatives with troubleshooting guidance and issue resolution. Participate in support readiness activities for product releases, enhancements, and workflow changes. Contribute to creation and maintenance of knowledge articles, troubleshooting guides, and support documentation. Support customer onboarding, education, and adoption activities when appropriate. Assist with queue management and workload balancing activities. Partner with peers and leadership to improve support processes and customer experiences. Demonstrate growing understanding of product integrations and downstream customer impacts. Additional Responsibilities Develop subject matter expertise in one or more products, workflows, or customer support processes. Assist with onboarding and training of new team members. Contribute to the creation of knowledge articles, troubleshooting guides, and customer-facing resources. Support product releases and readiness through knowledge acquisition and training participation. Provide guidance to B1 representatives on standard troubleshooting and support practices. Assist with queue management, workload balancing, and support coverage activities. Identify recurring customer issues and recommend process or documentation improvements. Participate in customer education and adoption activities as needed Demonstrate commitment to McKesson's i2CARE and LEADRx principles and customer-first culture.

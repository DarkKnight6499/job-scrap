Asset & Wealth Management Asset & Wealth Management (AWM) is the primary investing area within Goldman Sachs, bringing together traditional and alternative investment capabilities to help clients pursue their long-term goals. Acting as a fiduciary, the business invests on behalf of a broad range of clients, including pension plans, sovereign wealth funds, insurance companies, endowments, foundations, financial advisors and individuals. With approximately $3.6 trillion in assets under supervision, AWM combines global scale with a culture grounded in partnership, client service, integrity and excellence. The division offers candidates the opportunity to contribute to a business that is central to the firm and focused on delivering strong investment outcomes for clients. Public Investing The Public Investing team is part of the asset management business and focuses on public markets across asset classes. The organization includes investment teams across equities (both quantitative and fundamental strategies), fixed income and multi-asset allocation. The Performance Strategy team within Public Investing serves as a resource for the leadership of the Public Investing organization, with a focus on investment performance, investment process and strategic projects. The team works closely with investors, client teams and senior stakeholders, sitting at the intersection of many parts of the investing organization. This role offers a broad view of public markets and a unique opportunity to learn across asset classes, follow market developments, build relationships with investment professionals and contribute to projects that add value across the business. This is an opportunity for a motivated candidate to gain exposure to senior investment leadership, deepen their understanding of public markets and contribute to high-impact work across a global platform. Role Responsibilities The Associate will support performance monitoring and strategy across the Public Investing complex. Responsibilities will include reviewing fund performance, identifying exceptions, analyzing attribution and portfolio activity, and coordinating follow-ups with relevant stakeholders. The individual will help prepare performance-related materials for senior management, fund boards, key stakeholders and other relevant governance forums globally. The role will require clear communication, strong attention to detail and the ability to turn performance data into useful insights for decision-makers. In this role, the person will also contribute to projects that enhance the performance monitoring process, including developing and testing upgrades in collaboration with the engineering team, expanding competitive analysis, comparing outcomes to client expectations, evaluating the quality of returns and broadening the team’s coverage. The individual will be expected to identify process improvements and help strengthen the team’s overall operating model.

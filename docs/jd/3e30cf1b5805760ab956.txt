As an organization, we are united through trust as one inclusive team and we are passionate about helping our colleagues and clients succeed. What the day will look like Legal consultant to assist stakeholders with federal income tax and ERISA questions and issues related to the design and administration of benefit, compensation, and human resource programs. Colleagues use analytical and communication skills and technical depth to help internal and external clients and other colleagues address business considerations and legal risks. Legal consultants would be expected to draft defined benefit and defined contribution plan documents and amendments and address Section 409A compliance and correction issues. In addition, they will assist with IRS determination letter applications, evaluate noncompliance issues and prepare EPCRS filings, and draft summary plan descriptions and related plan disclosures. Finally, they will also research new and innovative ideas involving retirement and employee benefits generally. Technical consulting: Provide research involving tax and ERISA matters that support creative solutions to technical issues that strike an appropriate balance between technical and business considerations; serve as a firm-wide expert in one or more specific legal areas; ensure that consultants and clients value resourcefulness, creativity and well-reasoned risk taking. Legal developments: Monitor regulatory developments and their impact on clients and colleagues. Client leadership: Maintain thorough understanding of clients’ businesses; maintain strong personal relationships with clients and help other colleagues to develop client relationships; help bring consultants with different skills and attributes together to improve Aon’s responsiveness to client needs. Business leadership: Play a leading role in developing new consulting opportunities for legal and consultants within Wealth Solutions; recognize opportunities to make integrated offers that span business lines. Thought leadership: Provide strategic direction on topics which affect our business (either locally, nationally, or more broadly to the line of business); support and encourage new ideas; research/analyze information to send to clients; train and support other colleagues Skills and experience that will lead to success Eight plus years of experience in legal practice in ERISA/employee benefits, preferably at a large law firm (100+), smaller specialty law firm (e.g., ERISA, employment law), benefits consulting firm, or corporate counsel Hands-on experience working with retirement plans and related administration is strongly preferred Education: Juris Doctor degree from an accredited law school or equivalent years of industry experience.

About the Role Figure is hiring a Senior Financial Portfolio Analyst – Servicing to provide advanced financial analysis, portfolio performance monitoring, servicing cost modeling, compliance oversight, and forecasting for a servicing portfolio. This role supports cross-functional teams such as Servicing Operations, Capital Markets, Collections/Default, Investor Reporting, and Asset Management. What You’ll Do Servicing P&L Management — Maintain and enhance servicing profit-and-loss models, including revenue streams, ancillary fees, sub-servicing costs, advances, late fees, recoveries, and corporate allocations. MSR Valuation Support — Support mortgage servicing rights (MSR) valuation by modeling discount rates, CPR/prepayment speeds, delinquency curves, and cost-to-service assumptions. Portfolio Analytics — Build dashboards tracking static pool performance, delinquency migration, modification outcomes, foreclosure metrics, and collection effectiveness. Revenue & Cost Forecasting — Forecast servicing revenue based on portfolio balance, runoff, payoffs, and interest-rate conditions; develop models for default expenses, foreclosure timelines, claim recoveries, and REO performance. Risk & Compliance Monitoring — Provide financial performance analysis, risk assessment, and compliance monitoring for servicing portfolios. Escrow & Advance Modeling — Model escrow balances, interest earnings, advance recoverability, and servicing cost allocations by investor. Operational & Strategic Support — Contribute financial insights to operational efficiency initiatives. Reporting & Executive Presentations — Prepare monthly business review decks, variance analysis, margin analytics, and operational KPIs for senior leadership. What We Look For Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Statistics, Business, or related field. 4–7 years of mortgage FP&A, servicing analytics, or portfolio-analysis experience. Strong understanding of MSR economics , delinquency modeling, advance mechanics, and servicing cost structures. Experience with servicing systems such as MSP, Sagent, LoanServ , and data analysis tools such as SQL, Snowflake, Tableau, Power BI . Ability to work independently and manage complex workloads with strict deadlines. Preferred Qualifications Master’s degree (MBA or related). CPA or similar financial certification (preferred).

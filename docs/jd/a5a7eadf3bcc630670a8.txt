General Description The Business Analyst for the Enterprise Voice of the Customer (VoC) Program serves as a liaison between business and technology teams, supporting the collection, analysis, reporting, and continuous improvement of customer feedback programs. This role works closely with PR & VI stakeholders to gather and document business requirements, analyze customer experience data, identify technology capabilities, and support the design and implementation of executive customer insights reports that advance Popular’s vision of becoming the #1 bank for customers. Essential Duties and Responsibilities Data Architecture & Information Management Collaborate with Technology, Data Management, and Enterprise Architecture teams to design and support enhancements to customer feedback data repositories. Document data requirements, business rules, data models, and metadata standards to ensure customer feedback data is integrated, governed, and accessible across the enterprise. Support the design and ongoing management of a customer insights repository that consolidates feedback from surveys, complaints, claims, contact centers, social media, CRM systems, and other customer listening points. Ensure data quality, consistency, integrity, and traceability across customer experience data sources. Support data governance initiatives and compliance with enterprise data management standards. Systems Analysis & Integration Design Participate in business and functional analysis for new VoC capabilities, platforms, and enhancements. Document business requirements, functional specifications, process flows, use cases, and technical requirements for VoC solutions. Collaborate with technology teams to design system integration requirements among VoC platforms, CRM systems, case management tools, customer complaint systems, data warehouses, and business intelligence environments. Analyze system dependencies and recommend technology solutions that enhance customer insight collection, management, and reporting. Participate in solution design reviews, testing activities, and implementation efforts to ensure alignment with business objectives. Automation & Process Optimization Identify opportunities for process improvement and support automation initiatives for customer feedback ingestion, categorization, reporting, and escalation processes. Work with technology teams to leverage AI, machine learning, text analytics, natural language processing (NLP), and robotic process automation (RPA) solutions for advanced customer feedback analysis. Develop automated monitoring and alerting mechanisms to identify emerging customer experience issues and trends. Continuously optimize VoC processes to improve speed, scalability, and effectiveness. Reporting, Dashboard Development, & Business Intelligence Design, develop, and maintain enterprise-level dashboards and scorecards using Power BI, Tableau, SQL, Excel, and other business intelligence tools. Create executive, operational, and business-unit reporting that provides actionable insights into customer satisfaction, loyalty, complaints, customer effort, and journey performance. Support self-service reporting capabilities that empower business stakeholders to access customer insights independently. Assist in the creation and maintenance of automated reporting solutions that consolidate multiple data sources into a single source of truth for consistent customer experience measurement and performance tracking. Define and maintain key performance indicators (KPIs), key risk indicators (KRIs), and customer experience metrics to ensure consistency across enterprise reporting. Present analytical findings through intuitive visualizations, storytelling, and executive presentations. This Job has no supervisory responsibilities but in special circumstances may lead the work of others. Education Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Statistics, Data Analytics, Finance, Information Systems, or a related field. Experience At least three (3) to five (5) years of experience in data analysis, information systems, customer experience, reporting, business intelligence, or a related function. Preferred: Experience working with Voice of the Customer (VoC) programs, customer experience initiatives, surveys, claims, complaints, or other customer feedback sources.

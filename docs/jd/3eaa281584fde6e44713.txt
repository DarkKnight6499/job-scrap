To foster that innovation, New York Life is looking for a talented individual to join a new business unit focused on accelerating enterprise growth through new and differentiated offerings. New York Life has formed a new Client Growth and Retention business team to accelerate enterprise growth by developing and operating programs that help access new pools of customers, as well as programs that deepen customer relationships/ retention. Role Overview: The Prospecting Activation Manager is responsible for executing Client Growth & Retention’s consumer activation initiatives by ensuring strategic plans are implemented effectively within the field. Serving as the operational bridge between Home Office planning and field execution, this role partners closely with Agency, General Offices, field leaders, and enterprise stakeholders to ensure consumer activation activities are executed with excellence and continuously improved. This role is responsible for monitoring execution, identifying opportunities for optimization, resolving operational challenges, and capturing field insights that improve future consumer activations. Success is achieved by ensuring high-quality execution that creates meaningful opportunities for qualified prospective clients to engage with New York Life agents and advisors. What You'll Do: Primary Responsibilities · Execute approved consumer activation strategies across key segments. · Support execution of prospecting initiatives, conferences, seminars, webinars, partnerships, sponsorships, and other field activation activities. · Serve as the primary operational liaison between Client Growth & Retention and local General Offices during market activation initiatives. · Partner with Agency and field leadership to coordinate successful implementation of consumer activation activities. · Conduct on-site observations and execution reviews to ensure initiatives are delivered according to established operating standards. · Monitor execution quality and identify opportunities to improve the prospect experience, agent experience, and operational effectiveness. · Ensure consumer activation activities are appropriately staffed, coordinated, and equipped with the necessary resources. · Assist in identifying and coordinating participating agents for consumer activation initiatives. · Validate that operational components—including lead capture, technology, marketing materials, and event logistics—are functioning effectively. · Proactively identify operational issues and implement appropriate solutions while escalating broader concerns as needed. · Gather quantitative and qualitative feedback from the field to identify trends, opportunities, and recommendations for continuous improvement. · Track execution activities and provide timely reporting to support organizational visibility and informed decision-making. · Share field observations and implementation insights with Strategy, Planning & Implementation, and Content & Enablement teams to improve future initiatives. What You'll Bring: Required Skills Strong execution and operational management skills. Exceptional organizational abilities with outstanding attention to detail. Demonstrated ability to manage multiple initiatives simultaneously. Strong interpersonal and relationship-building skills. Professional presence with the ability to effectively represent the Home Office in field environments. Customer service mindset focused on creating exceptional experiences for agents, field leaders, and enterprise partners. Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Strong problem-solving and decision-making abilities. Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively across multiple organizations. Adaptability and sound judgment in dynamic operational environments. Ability to observe, evaluate, and recommend operational improvements. Strong business acumen with an understanding of sales enablement and field operations. Commitment to continuous improvement and operational excellence. Bachelor's degree or an equivalent combination of education and relevant professional experience. Success Profile The successful candidate is a highly collaborative operator who thrives in execution-oriented environments. They are energized by bringing strategic plans to life, ensuring initiatives are delivered with consistency, professionalism, and operational excellence. They possess a natural bias toward action while maintaining exceptional attention to detail. They understand that successful execution requires both disciplined adherence to established processes and the judgment to recognize when adjustments are needed in real time. They proactively identify opportunities to improve execution, strengthen partnerships, and enhance the experience of agents, field leaders, and prospective clients.
